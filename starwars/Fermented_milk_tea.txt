'''Fermented milk tea''' was a fermented variety of a beverage involving [[milk]] and [[tea]] that was sold at a [[Unidentified bar (Ryloth)|bar]] located in a [[Unidentified hub|hub]] near the [[Ryloth's equatorial forest|equatorial forests]] of<ref name="Closed Fist" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|ryloth|Ryloth}}</ref> [[Planet|world]] [[Ryloth]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Closed Fist" /> At some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]],<ref name="IsrenDate">The segment of "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" depicting a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi's]] [[death]] onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' takes place around [[379 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:ClosedFistJediDeathDate|here]]. [[Marda Ro]] visited the [[Unidentified hub|hub]] after the [[Jedi]]'s death; therefore, she must have met him no earlier than 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the [[Evereni]]<ref name="Closed Fist" /> founder of a group of [[pirate]]s who would eventually become the [[Nihil]]<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref>&mdash;visited the bar during<ref name="Closed Fist" /> her<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> [[Marda Ro's hunt|search]] for others of her [[species]] and ordered fermented milk tea, which she then drank. Ro later mentioned the beverage in a [[Marda Ro's journal|recording]] she made for her [[Ro family|descendants]].<ref name="Closed Fist" /> By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="ShallaDate">"The Lie" chapter in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], which includes [[Shalla Ro]]'s [[death]], features [[Asgar Ro]]'s ascension to [[Eye of the Nihil]], an event dated to [[252 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' establishes she introduced [[Marchion Ro]] to [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]], he must have listened to it by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> during his youth, Ro's [[Gender|great-great-grandson]] [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to the recording, including the message where fermented milk tea was mentioned.<ref name="Temptation" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fermented milk tea appeared in a flashback in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Closed Fist" />
