The '''Akti Amaugh''', also known as the '''Valley of Caves''', was a sacred valley in the [[Lowlands]] on [[Aldhani]]. The [[Nasma Klain]]&mdash;a river sacred to the [[Dhani]] people&mdash;used to flow through there and a temple called the [[Nasma Brani]] sat on its banks. When the [[Galactic Empire]] came to Aldhani<ref name="Aldhani" /> by [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of the episode "[[Aldhani (episode)|Aldhani]]" takes place shortly after "[[Kassa (episode)|Kassa]]," which dates itself to [[5 BBY]]. [[Vel Sartha]] states that the airbase was liberated thirteen years prior, therefore the airbase must have been constructed by [[18 BBY]].</ref> they seized the [[Alkenzi Air Base]] and claimed the land for [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] after discovering the unique storage possibilities in the caverns. The Empire later built a [[Aldhani dam|dam]] to block the Nasma Klain and stationed the [[Aldhani Garrison]] there. The newly constructed dam was used as a distribution hub to store weapons and supplies, including the Imperial quarterly payroll of the entire sector.<ref name="Aldhani" />

Once every three [[Standard year|years]], the Dhanis gathered at the Nasma Brani<ref name="Databank: Dhani">{{Databank|dhani|Dhani}}</ref> for the Mak-ani bray Dhani, a celestial event also known as the [[Eye of Aldhani]].<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref>
