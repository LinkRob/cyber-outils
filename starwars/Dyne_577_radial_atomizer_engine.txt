'''Dyne 577 radial atomizer engines''', also simply referred to as '''Dyne 577 sublight engines'''<ref name="Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]] ''p. 14''</ref>, were a variant of [[sublight engine]]s that were fitted and utilised onboard the [[starship]]s of the [[Judicial Forces]], the peacekeeping entity of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Judicial Department|Department of Justice]].<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> In total, three thrusters of this model<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> were integrated into [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class space cruisers]].

==Characteristics==
These robust and powerful engines served as the primary propulsion system for the starships of the [[Republic Diplomatic Fleet]],<ref name="O&A I">[[Obi-Wan and Anakin 1|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 1]]</ref> which consisted of ''Consular''-class space cruisers, such as the ''[[Radiant VII]]''.<ref name="TPM" /> The thrusters could propel Republic cruisers at great velocities within atmosphere, allowing the vessels to reach 900km/h.<ref name="SWJ1">''[[Star Wars Journeys: The Phantom Menace]]''</ref>
