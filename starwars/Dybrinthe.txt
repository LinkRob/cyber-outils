'''Dybrinthe''' were a [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] native to the [[Deep Core]] [[moon]] of [[Dybrin 12]]. Due to the moon's poisonous and highly pressurized atmosphere, Dybrinthe out of Dybrin 12 had to wear large helmets and [[Breath mask|breathing gear]] when they left their homeworld.

==Biology and appearance==
Dybrinthe were a bipedal [[humanoid]] [[species]] with [[Color|bright]] skin, wide [[eye]]s, and a gill-like respiratory system.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> They had hands with five digits.<ref name="TFW Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Outside [[Dybrin 12]], Dybrinthe were required to wear large [[Breath mask|breathing apparatus]]<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> that supplied them with processed [[atmosphere]] via hoses.<ref name="TFW Visual Dictionary" /> They also had to wear suits that simulated the higher pressure and [[temperature]] that they were use to.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==Society and culture==
[[File:Dybrinthe.jpg|thumb|left|180px|Athgar Heece and another Dybrinthe in Niima Outpost.]]
The [[species]] lived in clans, with each clan controlling its own territory. [[Fuel]], bubbling up from Dybrin 12's vast underground [[ocean]]s, was collected by mobile refinery-[[City|cities]], which would then process the fuel. It was commonplace for Dybrinthe clans to raid other clans, though this became less prevalent. However, rival clans would still tunnel underground and attempt to divert fuel to their own clans. Within clans, Dybrinthe society was ordered into castes, such as [[engineer]]ing castes.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />

==Dybrinthe in the galaxy==
During the [[High Republic Era]], the Dybrinthe [[Jedi Master]] [[Dijuka]] served on the [[Jedi High Council]].<ref name="TFW Visual Dictionary" />

Few Dybrinthe left their [[homeworld]], though most of those who did were [[scientist]]s drawn from the engineering castes of their clans.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />

A notable exception was [[Athgar Heece]], a Dybrinthe [[bounty hunter]]. During the [[Cold War]], Heece was one of few visitors to the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] who found the planet cool and pleasant, because Heece was used to operating in higher atmospheric conditions.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> Another Dybrinthe was also present with Heece at the same [[time]].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dybrinthe first featured in the [[2015]] film ''[[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens]]'' through Athgar Heece and another unnamed Dybrinthe.<ref name="Episode VII" /> For the film, Heece was portrayed by British artist [[Mel Pickup]].<ref name="Southport Visister">{{WebCite|url=https://www.southportvisiter.co.uk/whats-on/whats-on-news/star-wars-southport-couple-role-10610876|text=Southport couple's excitement at roles in Star Wars: The Force Awakens movie|author=Andrew Brown|date=2015-12-16|work=Southport Visister|archivedate=20180706133820}}</ref> Though not identified on-screen, Dybrinthe were first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TFW Visual Dictionary" />
