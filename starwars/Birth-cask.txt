'''Birth-casks''' were external wombs used by the [[Yevetha]] for [[Reproduction/Legends|reproduction]]. The birth-casks were nourished by [[Blood/Legends|blood]]; the blood of the [[Parent/Legends|mother]] was preferred but the blood of any Yevetha was sufficient. Serial twins could be born from the same birth-cask, and it reminded alive as long as it was supplied with a small amount of blood. Many Yevetha kept their birth-casks and returned to them at night to sleep.<ref name="CTD">''[[Cracken's Threat Dossier]]''</ref>
