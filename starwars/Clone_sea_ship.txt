The [[Grand Army of the Republic]] utilized a type of '''sea ship''' during the [[Clone Wars]]. Multiple [[Boat|ships]] participated in the [[Battle of Abrion Bridge]], alongside the [[212th Attack Battalion]], which was supplemented by a [[Mekedrix's company|Roonian company]], [[442nd Siege Battalion]] forces, and [[Republic Navy]] units. By the end of the battle, many water units were destroyed, and the Republic suffered a defeat.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />
