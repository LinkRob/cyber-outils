"'''Dissolution of Hope'''" is a story featuring [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] written by [[Torunn Grønbekk]] and illustrated by [[Klaus Janson]]. The story was published in the [[Darth Vader – Black, White & Red 1|first issue]] of the anthology miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Darth Vader – Black, White & Red]]'', that was published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[April 26]], [[2023]].
