A '''blurrg''' (plural: '''blurrg''' or '''blurrgs''') was a [[Sentience|non-sentient]], two-[[Limb|legged]] [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[Species|beast]] of burden found throughout [[the galaxy]]. Blurrgs were notably used by the [[Twi'lek Resistance]] and [[Free Ryloth Movement]] during the [[Clone Wars]] and the [[Imperial Era]]. They were also corralled by the [[moisture farmer]] [[Kuiil]] on [[Arvala-7]].

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:ChamSyndullasBlurrg-SL.png|thumb|left|250px|The Twi'lek Cham Syndulla stands beside a blurrg, providing a size comparison.]]
Blurrgs were a two-[[Limb|legged]] [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[Reptile|reptilian]]<ref name="Alien Archive" /> [[species]] that were used as beasts of burden on a number of worlds located across [[the galaxy]],<ref name="Blurrg">{{Databank|blurrg|Blurrg}}</ref> including [[Endor]] and [[Ryloth]]. Blurrgs had two short arms that had two fingers with claws, and a thick tail that helped them balance. The creatures possessed large mouths and many sharp teeth that could chew through most materials, including [[grass]]es and weeds,<ref name="Alien Archive" /> though they were not strictly [[Diet|herbivores]] and were known to consume other organisms, even members of their own kind.<ref name="Chapter 1" /> The hip joint that connected their stocky hind legs to their body was unusually high up on a blurrg's body.<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> They stood around 2.5 to 3 meters tall.<ref name="BMF 38" />

Blurrgs were typically dark in coloration, ranging from a dark blue, green, or gray, to brown and black, but also featured a lighter mottled pattern that could be shades of lighter blue or orange. Rebel leader [[Cham Syndulla]]'s blurrg had white markings on its head.<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> Depending on the breed, blurrgs would live for around thirty-five to forty [[standard year]]s.<ref name="DotRVG" />

Despite their awkward appearance, blurrgs could run very fast. During the [[Battle of Ryloth]], blurrgs easily outpaced the [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s used by the [[Galactic Republic|Republic's]] [[Lightning Squadron]],<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> which could top out at 75 [[Kilometer per hour|kph]] on flat terrain,<ref name="DS Incredibuilds">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Death Star Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]''</ref> as well as reportedly being able to match the velocity of Imperial speeders.<ref name="Alien Archive" />

==Behavior==
[[File:Blurrg-ToppsAuthentics.jpg|thumb|right|250px|One of Kuiil's blurrgs on Arvala-7]]
Stoic and strong, blurrgs were used for everything from agricultural labor and transportation to war.<ref name="Blurrg" /> They were very versatile for various kinds of service despite their poor intelligence. Blurrgs were also strong enough to carry large loads. Blurrgs were commonly found in an agricultural setting, employed by farmers to move equipment or clear weeds and grass.<ref name="Alien Archive" />

Some blurrgs could also have a hostile disposition when provoked. When one Twi'lek Resistance member invited a clone to mount his blurrg, the blurrg roared and bucked as the clone unsuccessfully attempted to control it.<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> Their viciousness when provoked made them useful in battle.<ref name="Alien Archive" /> [[Gender|Male]] blurrgs were eaten by females after the [[Reproduction|mating process]].<ref name="Chapter 1" />

==History==
{{Quote|No, you will need one. To ride. The way is impossible to pass without a blurrg mount.|Kuiil explains the difficult terrain on Arvala-7 to Din Djarin|<ref name="Chapter 1" />}}
During the [[Clone Wars]], when the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] invaded the [[planet]] Ryloth, the [[Twi'lek Resistance]], led by Cham Syndulla, had blurrgs in their care that they used as mounts. They set up a [[Twi'lek freedom fighters hideout|hideout]] inside a ruined Separatist [[C-9979 landing craft|ship]] and had several stables for their blurrg mounts inside.<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" />

[[File:Syndulla_charge.png|thumb|left|250px|Blurrgs carry Twi'lek freedom fighters into battle alongside the clones]]
When Syndulla and the Resistance met with in battle [[Jedi General]] [[Mace Windu]] and the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s Lightning Squadron, they agreed to work together to retake the capital city of [[Lessu City|Lessu]]. During the final offensive against Separatist forces in Lessu, several of the freedom fighters rode blurrgs that charged at [[Separatist Droid Army|droid troops]] alongside the AT-RTs utilized by the [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> During the [[time]] of the [[Mission to Zygerria]], some blurrgs were sold in the markets of [[Zygerria]].<ref name="Slaves">{{TCW|Slaves of the Republic}}</ref>

During the [[Imperial Era]], Syndulla's [[Free Ryloth Movement]] continued to use blurrgs. After the occupation of the [[Tann Province]], Syndulla and [[Numa]] rode blurrgs on a raid on an [[K79-S80 Imperial Troop Transport|Imperial Troop Transport]] alongside Syndulla's daughter [[Hera Syndulla|Hera]], eventually riding into Hera's ship, the ''[[Ghost]]'', to escape Imperial reinforcements.<ref name="Hera's Heroes">{{Rebels|Hera's Heroes}}</ref>

[[File:KuiilBlurrg-TMArtImagery.png|thumb|right|200px|Kuiil riding one of his blurrgs during the New Republic Era]]
By [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Mandalorian date">{{MandoDate|S1}}</ref> the [[Ugnaught]] [[Kuiil]] had a small [[Kuiil's moisture farm|moisture farm]] on the planet Arvala-7 where he kept several tamed blurrgs that he used for transportation across the difficult terrain. When the [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] [[Mission to Arvala-7|traveled]] to [[Arvala-7]] after taking a job from [[the Client]], he met Kuiil, who helped him learn to ride before loaning him a blurrg to use for travel.<ref name="Chapter 1" /> Later, when Djarin returned to Kuiil's farm to ask the Ugnaught that he accompany him to [[Nevarro]] on a mission, Kuiil agreed to join him when Djarin allowed him to bring his blurrgs along. The creatures would sit in the cargo hold of the ''[[Razor Crest]]''. During the mission, the blurrgs were killed when the group was attacked by several [[Nevarro reptavian|native reptavians]]. Later, Kuiil traveled with [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] back to the ''Razor Crest'' on the remaining blurrg, but was run down by [[scout trooper]]s, who killed Kuiil and his blurrg before taking Grogu back to the city.<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref>

Two Blurrgs were owned by Din Djarin near his [[Clan Mudhorn cabin|cabin]] on Nevarro. One rainy night, while modifying his [[Razor Crest 2.0|new ''Razor Crest'']] with the [[Anzellan Droidsmiths]], the two Blurrgs sensed [[Embo|two]] [[Keibu|intruders]] approaching the cabin, which altered Djarin, using his helmet's thermal vision and spotted a [[Kyuzo]].<ref name="The Mandalorian and Grogu">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Blurrg-Concept-Mandalorian.png|thumb|left|250px|''The Mandalorian'' concept art featuring blurrgs, by [[Christian Alzmann]]]]
In the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], blurrgs first appeared in "[[Liberty on Ryloth]]," an episode of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' that first aired in [[2009]].<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Blurrg/Legends|blurrgs]] first appeared in the [[1985]] television film ''[[Ewoks: The Battle for Endor]]'',<ref name="The Battle for Endor">''[[Ewoks: The Battle for Endor]]''</ref> and were based on a creature sketched for the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars Chronicles]]''</ref> They made their canonical live-action debut in "[[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian]],"<ref name="Chapter 1" /> the first episode of the [[Disney+]] television series [[Star Wars: The Mandalorian|''The Mandalorian's'']] [[The Mandalorian Season One|first season]], which premiered on [[November 12]], [[2019]].<ref name="Episode Amount">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/heat-vision/star-wars-new-mandalorian-details-footage-unveiled-1201931|text='The Mandalorian': Jon Favreau Teases Disney+ Series at Star Wars Celebration|author=Aaron Couch & Graeme McMillan|date=2018-12-10|archivedate=20191109001233}}</ref> However, their earlier Legends appearance in ''The Battle for Endor'' had also been in live action.<ref name="The Battle for Endor" />
