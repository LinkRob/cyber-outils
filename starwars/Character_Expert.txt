'''Character Expert''' was a [[Aki-Aki]] found at the [[Festival of the Ancestors]]. They were the expert of the characters in the story "[[The Rise and Fall of Panakin Slytalker]]" which was about a [[Jedi]] [[Knight]] named [[Qui-Gon Jinn|Bye-Gon Jim]] who met a young [[Anakin Skywalker|Panakin Slytalker]].<ref name="LEGO" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Character Expert" first appeared in a mission titled "Festival of the Ancestors" in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]]ical [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''.<ref name="LEGO" />
