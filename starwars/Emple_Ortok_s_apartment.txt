'''Emple Ortok's apartment''' was an apartment owned by the [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Nihil]] [[Emple Ortok]] in [[Eriadu City]],<ref name="CtM" /> the [[capital]] [[city]]<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> of the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]], during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="CtM" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|COC:CTM}}</ref> Ortok was on the balcony of his apartment when the [[Lucem]] former [[Jedi]] [[Ruu]] landed next to Ortok, surprising him. After a short confrontation, Ruu [[Death|killed]] Ortok and [[Thief|stole]] his [[identichip]] which he intended to use help him travel across the [[Stormwall]],<ref name="CtM" /> a [[hyperspace]] barrier [[Deployment of the stormseeds|created]] by the Nihil.<ref name="THRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Leaving the man dead on his balcony, Ruu departed from the apartment and traveled to a [[Unidentified public shipyard|public shipyard]] where he stole Ortok's private [[Unidentified vessel|vessel]].<ref name="CtM" />

==Behind the scenes==
Emple Ortok's apartment appeared in "[[Chronicles of Corellia: Cracking the Mask]]," the second installment of the ''[[The High Republic: Chronicles of Corellia]]'' short story series. The story was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published in the [[Star Wars Insider 228|228th]] issue of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="CtM" /> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref> on [[October 29]], [[2024]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/167-star-wars-insider-228|text=Star Wars Insider #228}}</ref>
