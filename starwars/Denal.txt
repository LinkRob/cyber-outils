'''Denal''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served the [[Galactic Republic]] as a [[soldier]] in the [[501st Legion]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Denal served under [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] during the war, including the [[Battle of Mimban]] and the [[Raid on Skytop Station|mission to Skytop Station]]. At the [[Battle of Devaron]], he was killed by [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]].

==Biography==
===The early war===
{{Quote|Those droids are getting closer.|Denal|<ref name="Duel of the Droids" />}}
In the early [[Standard month|months]] of the [[Clone Wars]], Denal served in the [[501st Legion]] under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]]. He fought in the [[Battle of Mimban]] alongside fellow [[Cloning|clones]] [[Hardcase]] and [[Jesse]].<ref name="501st Plus One">{{StoryCite|story=501 Plus One|book=Age of Republic Special 1}}</ref>

[[File:MissionToSkytopStation-TCWS01E07.jpg|thumb|left|260px|Denal and the rest of the squad that infiltrated Skytop Station.]]
When Skywalker's personal [[astromech droid]], [[R2-D2]], was held captive by the scavenger [[Gha Nachkt]] and scheduled to be handed over to [[General]] [[Grievous]] at [[Skytop Station]], Denal was assigned to be a part of the rescue [[squad]] on a [[Raid on Skytop Station|mission]] to save the R2 unit and destroy the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] spy station. Traveling to the station in the [[G9 Rigger-class light freighter|G9 ''Rigger''-class light freighter]] ''[[Twilight]]'', Denal and the others donned jetpacks and jumped from the ship and onto the outside of the station. Upon landing, Denal and the other troopers laughed at their [[Clone Captain|Captain]], [[Rex]], who clumsily tried to balance with their replacement astromech, [[R3-S6]], strapped to his torso.<ref name="Duel of the Droids" />

During this mission, he, [[Commander]] Tano, and the rest of the squad were tasked with planting the detonators while Skywalker rescued R2. However, they were hampered significantly by R3, who was actually a Separatist double-agent. At one point, R3 closed several [[blast door]]s on the group and also sent a squad of [[B1-series battle droid|B1 battle droids]] as well as [[B2-series super battle droid|super battle droids]] to their position. Surrounded, the group were pinned down until they used several [[Electromagnetic pulse grenade|EMP grenades]] to defeat the droids. Shortly after, the group were attacked by Grievous. After Grievous overpowered Tano, he set upon the clones, killing three of them and knocking Denal and Rex to the ground. Tano later saved Rex from being executed and led Grievous away. Denal awoke to being one of the only survivors of the attack. Rex contacted Ahsoka, who was hiding from Grievous, and she told the two clones to finish the mission and rendezvous at the landing bay as planned. Denal grabbed the backpack of charges and ran after Rex.<ref name="Duel of the Droids" />

After planting the [[bomb]]s, Denal and Rex met up with Skywalker and R2 in the station's landing bay. When Rex told Skywalker that Tano was engaging Grievous, Skywalker was distressed. Rex offered to lead him to their last position, but as he, Skywalker, Denal, and the [[Unidentified clone trooper pilot (Twilight)|clone trooper]] who had landed the ''Twilight'' began to run, they were then trapped there by [[R3-S6]], who revealed himself to be a Separatist [[spy]]. They then came under attack by [[B2-series super battle droid|super battle droids]] and [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]]. After setting off the explosions, Denal and the other survivors were able to board the ''[[Twilight]]'' before the station was destroyed.<ref name="Duel of the Droids" />

===Battle of Devaron===
{{Quote|Hey, Denal, good job frying that bounty hunter.|A clone trooper complements Denal, although it was actually Cad Bane wearing Denal's armor|<ref name="CotF">{{TCW|Children of the Force}}</ref>}}
[[File:Resolute_briefing_CoD.png|thumb|right|250px|The Battle of Devaron was the last mission for Denal, who was killed by Cad Bane.]]
Denal was sent with Skywalker, Tano, and Rex to Devaron to [[Battle of Devaron|stop]] bounty hunter [[Cad Bane]] from acquiring the [[kyber memory crystal]] from Master [[Bolla Ropal]] on [[Devaron]]. He and the others then boarded [[Cad Bane's Munificent-class star frigate|Bane's]] [[Munificent-class star frigate|command ship]]. Denal was part of the group that stormed the bridge and took control of the ship. Afterwards, Skywalker ordered Rex to lock down the escape pods so no one escaped, and Denal joined the group that went to carry out the orders. However, when one of the engines on the ship exploded, Skywalker changed the plan and instead lead their forces to locate Master Ropal. In the hangar, they encountered Bane and his battle droids. After fighting the battle droids to acquire a [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|''Sheathipede''-class transport shuttle]], Denal and another trooper, [[Koho]], were then sent by Rex to find Skywalker and Tano. They then found Bane and held him up, threatening him. However, Bane overpowered them, killing both Koho and Denal, though Bane himself was also wounded in the scuffle.<ref name="CoD" />

Bane switched armor with Denal, and faked his death.<ref name="CoD" /> At first, the clone troopers were impressed with Denal's apparent killing of Bane. However, Bane's disguise was uncovered by one of the clones and Tano, who saw the Duros' green blood, which had come from a wound sustained during the bounty hunter's fight against Denal, who discovered that "Denal" was in fact Bane after following him from the shuttle. Bane then made his escape in a [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]] and jumped into hyperspace.<ref name="CotF" />

==Equipment==
[[File:Denal-SWCT.png|thumb|right|175px|Clone trooper Denal]]
Denal has standard [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] with customized [[Color|blue]] markings on his helmet that were consistent with the [[501st Legion]]. He used a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]],<ref name="Duel of the Droids" /> which sometimes had an [[ascension cable]] attachment. He also sometimes had dark red macrobinoculars attached to his helmet.<ref name="CoD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Denal first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Duel of the Droids]]."<ref name="Duel of the Droids" /> Like all clones, he was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]]. Although not named in the story itself, a clone wearing Denal's armor also appeared in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] short "[[501 Plus One]]," a story contained within [[Age of Republic Special 1|''Age of Republic Special'' 1]]. [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Matt Martin]] later clarified on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] that the clone in the comic was indeed Denal.<ref name="First Matt Martin Denal tweet">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1085677268322508800|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=Sure looks like him.|archivefile=File:MattTalksDenal.jpg}}</ref><ref name="Second Matt Martin Denal tweet">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1085935295940354053|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=It's him.|archivefile=File:MattMartinSaysItsDenal.jpg}}</ref>
