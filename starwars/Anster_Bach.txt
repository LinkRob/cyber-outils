'''Anster Bach''' was a [[Quarren]] [[Gender|male]] who represented [[Canto Bight]] at the [[Galactic Dejarik Association Championship Tournament]]. [[Maz Kanata]] bet Bach one hundred thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] that she could play against [[Bilk Plessey]] in the final match and get him to play with his [[Limb|arms]] behind his back. Kanata lost the match, but won the bet, receiving the credits from Bach.<ref name="Win/Lose" />
