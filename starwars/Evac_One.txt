'''''Evac One''''' was an [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|Imperial ''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] originally known as '''Transport Ship 63378''' which was stolen from the [[Galactic Empire]] and later used as an [[evac ship]] by the [[Spectres]].

==Description==
{{Quote|That's not one of us!|TK-626, referring to Transport 63378 as it was being stollen by the Specters.|<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />}}
''Evac One'' was an [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|Imperial ''Gozanti''-class cruiser]]<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> manufactured by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]<ref name="Rebels Guide" /> for use in the [[Imperial Military|military]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> 63.8 [[meter]]s long, 32 meters wide and 14.7 meters high, the [[Starship|ship]] was armed with a dorsal twin [[laser cannon]] [[turret]] and a ventral heavy [[laser]] [[cannon]] turret. Like all Imperial ''Gozanti''-class ships, it could transport either four [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s,<ref name="Rebels Guide" /> four [[All Terrain Defense Pod]]s or even two [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s<ref name="Rebels Princess">{{Rebels|A Princess on Lothal}}</ref> on underside-mounted docking clamps.<ref name="Droids">{{Rebels|Droids in Distress}}</ref> Its hull was painted a light [[Color|gray]],<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> in keeping with the Empire's preference for muted colors.<ref name="newhero">''[[Star Wars Rebels: A New Hero]]''</ref> After it became a rebel ship under the name of ''Evac One'', its appearance remained mostly unchanged save for an orange [[Starbird (symbol)|starbird]] painted at the starboard side.<ref name="VA" />

==History==
{{Quote|The transport you stole will get us close to the fleet over Mustafar. We know they have Kanan on Tarkin's Star Destroyer.''"<br />"''And it's surrounded by a bunch of other Star Destroyers.''"<br />"''Uh, lots more. We'll need a distraction to cover our entry. Sabine?''"<br />"''Engine room's here, all the power for the ship. If we could get inside the docking bay, I could rig something, black 'em out. But our transport ship's not gonna fit in there.|Hera Syndulla, Garazeb Orrelios, and Sabine Wren|<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />}}
[[File:EvacOneApproachesSovereign.png|thumb|300px|left|The ship during the rescue of Kanan Jarrus]]
The ship was known to the Empire as Transport Ship 63378.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Fire Across the Galaxy]]" to [[4 BBY]].</ref> it was stolen by the [[Spectres]] to [[Action at Mustafar|rescue]] their leader, [[Kanan Jarrus]], from the [[Mustafar system]]. [[Sabine Wren]] made a distraction at a [[Lothal]] [[hangar]] while her fellow rebels absconded with the cruiser. They then added their previously [[Attack on Imperial Troop Transports|stolen]] [[Rebels TIE fighter|TIE fighter]] in order to enter the [[Star system|system]]. They rendezvoused with the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Sovereign]]'' for the rescue, leaving their droid [[C1-10P]] to [[pilot]] it. After rescuing Jarrus, they fled the Star Destroyer, but C1-10P had left with the [[transport]]. He then returned leading a [[Phoenix Cell|rebel fleet]], and together they escaped the system.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />

It was later used by the larger rebellion, seeing action as an [[Evac ship|evacuation ship]] under the callsign ''Evac One'', such as the [[Skirmish on Imvur|skirmish]] on [[Imvur]].<ref name="VA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Transport Ship 63378 first appeared in "[[Fire Across the Galaxy]]," the finale of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />

It was later confirmed to be the same ship as ''Evac One'' by the author [[Martin Fisher]].<ref name="Evac One">{{Twitter|tronprogram2|status/777717946416263168|[[Martin Fisher]]|quote=It was intended to be.|archivefile=File:Stolen Evac One.png}}</ref>
