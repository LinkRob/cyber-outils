'''''From Star Wars to Jedi: The Making of a Saga''''' is a [[1983]] documentary on the making of the [[Original trilogy|original ''Star Wars'' trilogy]]. It was broadcast on [[Wikipedia:PBS|PBS]] on [[December 3]], [[1983]].<ref name="This Week Gideon">{{ThisWeek|xCDs0meB6OY|Moff Gideon Enters the Battlefield, The Child Gets a Name, and More!|sw_url=video/moff-gideon-enters-the-battlefield-the-child-gets-a-name-and-more}}</ref>

==Release and reception==
The documentary was first released on home video in [[1986]] on VHS and Betamax from [[Wikipedia:Playhouse Video|Playhouse Video]],<ref name="SWoV Making Saga VHS 1986">{{WebCite|url=https://swonvideo.com/vhs/vvhsfromswtojedius.htm|text=From Star Wars to Jedi - The Making of a Saga, VHS, Playhouse Video, 1986|work=Star Wars on Video|archivedate=20251101033437}}</ref> and in Japan on laserdisc from [[Wikipedia:VAP, Inc.|VAP]].<ref name="SWoV Making Saga LD Japan 1986">{{WebCite|url=https://swonvideo.com/laserdisc/vldjpswtojedi.htm|text=From Star Wars to Jedi, Laserdisc, VAP Video, Japan 1986|work=Star Wars on Video|archivedate=20240911054446}}</ref> Later VHS versions were released in [[1989]] (US),<ref name="SWoV Making Saga VHS 1989">{{WebCite|url=https://swonvideo.com/vhs/vvhsswtojedius1989.htm|text=From Star Wars to Jedi, VHS, CBS/Fox Video, USA 1989|work=Star Wars on Video|archivedate=20251029084119}}</ref> [[1990]] (UK),<ref name="SWoV Making Saga VHS 1990">{{WebCite|url=https://swonvideo.com/vhs/vuk1990saga.htm|text=From Star Wars to Jedi - The Making of a Saga, VHS, CBS/Fox Video UK, 1990|work=Star Wars on Video|archivedate=20251031133951}}</ref> and [[1995]] (US),<ref name="SWoV Making Saga VHS 1995">{{WebCite|url=https://swonvideo.com/vhs/vvhsussaga.htm|text=From Star Wars to Jedi, VHS, Fox Video. USA 1995|work=Star Wars on Video|archivedate=20251030233639}}</ref> with variations released in original trilogy boxed sets in the US and UK in [[1992]]<ref name="SWoV Making Saga VHS 1992">{{WebCite|url=https://swonvideo.com/vhs/vtrilogylbxboxsaga.htm|text=From Star Wars to Jedi, VHS, 20th Century Fox Video USA, 1992|work=Star Wars on Video|archivedate=20251028112149}}</ref> and 1995.<ref name="SWoV Making Saga VHS UK 1995">{{WebCite|url=https://swonvideo.com/vhs/vvhsuk1995executortapefromswtojedi.htm|text=From Star Wars to Jedi, VHS, CBS/Fox Video UK, 1995|work=Star Wars on Video|archivedate=20251028204359}}</ref> It was also released on Laserdisc in Japan in 1995.<ref name="">{{WebCite|url=https://swonvideo.com/laserdisc/vldcolljapanswtojedi.htm|text=From Star Wars to Jedi: The Making of a Saga, Laserdisc, 20th Century Fox Home Ent. Japan 1995|work=Star Wars on Video|archivedate=20241112050847}}</ref>

As yet, there are no high-definition releases of the documentary and unlike its predecessors, ''[[The Making of Star Wars]]'', ''[[SP FX: The Empire Strikes Back]]'' and ''[[Classic Creatures: Return of the Jedi]]'', it was not included in the [[2011]] ''Star Wars'' [[Star Wars: The Complete Saga|Blu-ray]] boxset of all six films.<ref name="Complete Blu-ray">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''</ref>

In [[2014]], the documentary was made available for free on the official ''Star Wars'' YouTube channel.<ref name="Free">{{SWYouTube|F6Xa6CcShAI|From Star Wars to Jedi: The Making of a Saga (Part 1 of 9)|sw_url=video/from-star-wars-to-jedi-the-making-of-a-saga}}</ref>
