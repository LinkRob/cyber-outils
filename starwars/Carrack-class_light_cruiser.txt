The '''''Carrack''-class light cruiser''', sometimes identified as the '''''Carrack''-class Imperial Ship''' during the [[Imperial Era]], was a class of [[light cruiser]] that was introduced during the [[Clone Wars]] for the [[Galactic Republic]], and continued to serve under the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[New Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Characteristics==
===Design===
[[File:Carrack_bridge.jpg|thumb|left|240px|Part of the bridge of a ''Carrack''-class cruiser.]]
Designed and manufactured on the<ref name="BMF 52" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> [[Esseles]] by the [[Damorian Manufacturing Corporation]], the ''Carrack''-class [[light cruiser]] was intended to serve as an inexpensive alternative to larger [[capital ship]]s. Despite this and not being designed as ships of the line, the [[cruiser]]s were heavily armed for their size, and completely capable of combating larger enemies. The Carrack was 350 [[meter]]s in length, 72 meter in width, and 75 meters in height. The Carrack had a crew complement of 1,092 individuals in normal deployments alongside 142 [[soldier]]s, although because of the simple controls and its automation, it could be flown by only two [[pilot]]s. A second variant of the ''Carrack''-class cruiser was designated as the ''[[Carrack-A]]''.<ref name="BMF 52" /> Imperial Carracks were also identified as the ''Carrack''-class Imperial Ship.<ref name="Chopper Chase">{{Disney|subdomain=lol|url=games/star-wars-rebels-chopper-chase|text=Star Wars Rebels: Chopper Chase|int=Star Wars Rebels: Chopper Chase|nolive=1}}</ref>

The Carrack featured a dorsal [[rectenna]] amidships, and had a deceptive appearance. With the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] at the front of the vessel, the [[cockpit]] [[viewport]]s appeared to be that of a smaller ship, however, behind the windows were several bridge levels. In spite of its size, the ''Carrack''-class did not feature any internal [[hangar]] bays. To compensate, the cruisers were outfitted with [[starfighter]] racks or gantries on the outer [[hull]] late in the design process. Each rack was able to carry five [[TIE series|TIE fighter series]] starfighters that were used for patrols, reconnaissance, or courier missions. They were not commonly used, however, as the ''Carrack''-class normally relied on other vessels to provide defense in combat situations. The light cruiser had the capability of carrying cargo up to 3,500 [[metric ton]]s.<ref name="BMF 52" /> The interior of an Imperial Carrack featured many hallways and compartments, with consoles, [[computer]]s, stations and storage crates placed throughout the ship.<ref name="Chopper Chase">{{Disney|subdomain=lol|url=games/star-wars-rebels-chopper-chase|text=Star Wars Rebels: Chopper Chase|int=Star Wars Rebels: Chopper Chase|nolive=1}}</ref>

===Armament and engines===
[[File:CarrackLightCruiser-MF52 copy.png|thumb|300px|left|The ''Carrack''-class light cruiser had powerful engines.]] 
The ''Carrack''-class light cruiser was notable for its impressive array of armaments for its size. In one configuration, the ''Carrack''-class cruiser featured ten heavy [[turbolaser]]s for offense, twenty [[laser cannon]]s for defense, and five [[tractor beam projector]]s to capture and disable hostile ships. The cruiser's armament, paired with its maneuverability and speed, made it a highly versatile warship. As such, it was not uncommon for a fleet of ''Carrack''-class light cruisers to destroy a vessel ten times larger than themselves. For a ship of its class, the Carrack was fast, being able to achieve speeds of 80 [[Megalight per hour|MGLT]], similar to a [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]. At full throttle, the Carrack could intercept and attack most enemies, outrun most vessels that it could not defend against in open space, and break through a sizeable [[blockade]] fleet.<ref name="BMF 52" />

The ship was powered by an [[Ionization reactor/Canon|ionization reactor]] at the ship's aft and came in two engine configurations. One variant contained six engines in two columns of three. The other variant had sixteen total [[engine]]s in three rows of four engines and a single row of four smaller secondary engines at the base of the aft section. The Carrack was also equipped with a class 1 [[hyperdrive]] system.<ref name="BMF 52" />

===Hull and structure===
[[File:Carrack-hull.png|thumb|right|290px|The external dorsal hull of an Imperial ''Carrack''-class light cruiser.]]
The ''Carrack''-class cruiser's internal design featured a unique compartment system composed of high-density, overlapping [[armor]] plates along the forward and central [[hull]]. Despite using a large amount of space, these self-contained compartments with automatic hatches increased the survivability of the cruiser after experiencing a hull breach. It carried a forward and aft [[deflector shield generator]] which created a standard [[deflector shield]] grid.<ref name="BMF 52" />

The Carrack had several weaknesses, including the relatively light armor that protected the hull around its [[reactor]], making the ship vulnerable to a [[proton torpedo]] or a precise hit from a laser [[cannon]]. This could eliminate the ship's core reactor and cause the ship and its crew to become powerless and adrift. Despite this setback, the efficiency of the compartment system and deflector shield grid ensured that a ''Carrack''-class light cruiser could withstand several penetrating hits that may have destroyed a larger vessel. As such, it was not uncommon for afflicted light cruisers to be recovered in the aftermath of a battle with scores of survivors in its sealed compartments. Because of this, Carracks maintained a positive reputation, and became one of the popular postings of the [[Imperial Navy]].<ref name="BMF 52" /> The dorsal hull of a Carrack in Imperial service could also have multiple [[antenna]]s added, as well as a domed apparatus.<ref name="Chopper Chase" />

==Role==
[[File:Carrack-diagram.png|thumb|300px|left|A ''Carrack''-class light cruiser]]
The ''Carrack''-class cruisers initially served as escorts for larger ships within the [[Republic Navy|Galactic Republic Navy]]. Robust and reliable, the ''Carrack''-class cruiser was an important workhorse for the following Imperial Navy. Although [[Star Destroyer]]s were the most common symbol of Imperial [[Navy|naval]] power, the [[Galactic Empire]] enforced its rule through many smaller ships that formed the backbone of the Imperial Starfleet, which included the Carrack. Due to its characteristics, the ''Carrack''-class was used to transport personnel such as planetary [[governor]]s, [[admiral]]s, [[sector]] [[moff]]s, and other Imperial dignitaries.<ref name="BMF 52" />

It was not rare to encounter pairs of Carracks substituting for the more combat ready starfighters as system patrol ships, often being dispatched to calmer parts of [[Realspace|space]]. Imperial strategists quickly realized the cruiser's potential for serving in anti-starfighter roles over planets or in a [[fleet]] defense screen.<ref name="BMF 52" />

==History==
===Republic service===
[[File:CarrackAft-FF78.png|thumb|right|230px|A ''Carrack''-class light cruiser from Home Fleet Strike Group Five.]]
The ''Carrack''-class light cruiser were first introduced during the [[Clone Wars]] and served in the [[Galactic Republic]]. They were deployed as escorts for larger ships such as the [[Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser|''Dreadnought''-class heavy cruisers]]. Towards the later stages of the war, Carracks served in the Republic's [[Coruscant Home Defense Fleet]], with three forming the majority of [[Home Fleet Strike Group Five]]. The unit consisted of the Carracks [[Integrity (Carrack-class)|''Integrity'']], [[Indomitable (Carrack-class)|''Indomitable'']], and ''[[Perseverance]]'', with the ''Integrity'' serving as the [[flagship]] of the group under [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Lorth Needa]]. They were supported by the ''Dreadnought''-class [[heavy cruiser]] ''[[Mas Ramdar]]''. Home Fleet Strike Group Five participated in the [[Battle of Coruscant]], and trapped [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] aboard the flagship ''[[Invisible Hand]]'' and demanded its surrender.<ref name="BMF 52" />

Lieutenant Commander Needa did not believe Grievous' claims that he had Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] [[prisoner]] aboard his ship, and gave the Separatist general ten [[Standard minute|minutes]] to prove the claim before the ships of Group Five opened fire. Although Grievous did not surrender, Chancellor Palpatine was eventually rescued by [[Jedi General]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="BMF 52" />

===Imperial service===
At the end of the Clone Wars, the Republic [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] into the Galactic Empire and the Carracks were taken over and added into Imperial Navy service.<ref name="BMF 52" /> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="14 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> Imperial Moff [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] contacted an [[Unidentified Imperial colonel (Galidraan Station)|Imperial colonel]] stationed at [[Galidraan Station]] and asked what Imperial resources were in the area, as he suspected an imminent attack from [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|insurgents]]. Along with several other facilities, the [[colonel]] reported on the Imperial Navy's [[Belderone]] [[R/M Facility Four deepdock]], where two ''Carrack''-class light cruisers were parked, along with several [[CR90 corvette]]s and [[Victory-class Star Destroyer|''Victory''-class Star Destroyers]], and the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Liberator (Venator-class)|''Liberator'']]. Tarkin deployed the ''Liberator'' to [[Nam Chorios]], as that seemed the most likely target in the vicinity, although the insurgents ultimately chose to [[Attack on Lucazec|attack]] [[Lucazec]] next.<ref name="Tarkin" />

[[File:Carrack-interior.png|thumb|left|230px|During the Imperial Era, C1-10P infiltrated a ''Carrack''-class cruiser.]]
They were eventually deployed similarly to how the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] used CR90 corvettes, and were commonly dispatched to patrol [[star system]]s in the [[Core Worlds]] and the [[Corporate Sector]]. Due to their characteristics, Imperial Navy strategists were stubborn to retire them, allowing the ''Carrack''-class to remain in operation in one form or another.<ref name="BMF 52" /> Sometime between [[5 BBY]] and [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Date Note">{{ChopperChaseDate}}</ref> a [[Unidentified Carrack-class light cruiser (Spectres)|''Carrack''-class light cruiser]] experienced a technical mishap and was abandoned in the [[Kryton system]]. The [[Lothal]]-based resistance cell known as the [[Spectres]] [[Mission to tag a Carrack-class light cruiser|travelled]] to tag the cruiser with a [[Rebel Alliance starbird|rebel starbird]] in order to make the Empire believe that the Spectres were responsible for what occurred to the light cruiser. The group's [[droid]], [[C1-10P]], managed to board the ship and complete the mission.<ref name="Chopper Chase" />

===New Republic service===
In the [[Standard month|months]] following the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> in which the Empire faced a critical defeat in the [[Galactic Civil War]], the ''Carrack''-class light cruiser ''[[Oculus]]'' was used by the Alliance's successor, the [[New Republic]], in its [[New Republic Defense Fleet|naval branch]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Carrack_Cruiser_WEG_Imperial_Sourcebook.png|thumb|right|300px|The ''Carrack''-class originated from the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity.]]
The ''Carrack''-class light cruiser was first mentioned in [[canon]] in the [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> It first appeared in the [[2015]] novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], written by [[Chuck Wendig]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Carrack-class light cruiser/Legends|''Carrack'' class]] was first mentioned in ''[[The Star Wars Rules Companion]]'', a [[roleplaying game]] supplement to [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Greg Gorden]] and published in [[1989]].<ref name="Rules Companion">''[[The Star Wars Rules Companion]]''</ref> The vessel was visually depicted in the same year in Gorden's ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]'', a sourcebook for ''Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game'',<ref name="Imperial Sourcebook">''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]''</ref> before making its first appearance in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[1991]] novel, ''[[Heir to the Empire]]''.<ref name="Heir">''[[Heir to the Empire]]''</ref>
