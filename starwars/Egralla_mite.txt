'''Egralla mites''' were [[insect]] pests that fed on data storage devices. They could infest [[Library|libraries]] and archives, endangering their collections, and could require exterminators to remove. They could travel between systems on [[starship]]s, and preferred to make for high ground.<ref name="The Living Force" />

A few years before [[Jedi Master]]s [[Eeth Koth]] and [[Oppo Rancisis]] visited the library in the [[Jedi outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]], the caretaker [[Voh|Seneschal Voh]] had dealt with an infestation there. Voh theorized that it happened because the local [[spaceport]] was skimping on decontamination of incoming ships. He stated that the mites were to data devices what [[mynock]]s were to starships. Unable to deal with the infestation himself, Voh had hired an exterminator.<ref name="The Living Force" />
