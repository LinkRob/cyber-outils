A '''false eye''' was an anti-security device that mimicked the unique patterns of a person's [[eye]]. [[Spy|Spies]] and [[bounty hunter]]s used such items to bypass ocular-based [[Biometrics|biometric]] locks and gain entry to secured containers or buildings.<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>
