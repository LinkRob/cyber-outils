Following the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], [[Mon Mothma]] issued a '''declaration''' that formally established the [[New Republic]] and completed the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s transformation into a [[Politics|government]].<ref name="OTFL">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref>

{{Nr-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The declaration of a New Republic was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="OTFL" /> Before that, it first appeared in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] in the ''[[Heir to the Empire Sourcebook]]'', a [[1992]] reference book written by [[Bill Slavicsek]].<ref name="HTTE SB">''[[Heir to the Empire Sourcebook]]''</ref>
