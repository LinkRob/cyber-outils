'''Arbozoic trees''' were a [[species]] of [[tree]] that possessed attributes of [[planet]]-based and [[mineral]]-based species. They were found on the planet [[Aleen]], where their extensive root systems stretched into the [[Aleen Underworld]].

==Description==
[[File:Epguide405.jpg|thumb|left|200px|The roots of the arbozoic trees reached deep into the Underworld.]]
Arbozoic trees were a species of [[tree]] that dominated the ecology of the planet of [[Aleen]]. The strange glowing [[seed]]s of the arbozoic trees were [[crystal]]line in nature, and remained mysterious to researchers seeking to unlock their mysterious chemical composition. Growing in every climatic zone, the trees dotted the landscape of the surface of Aleen, while the root systems of the tree constituted over half of the living matter on and in the world. The [[Aleen Underworld|Underworld]] was largely carved by the roots of the arbozoic trees, which spread out into the crust of the planet for [[kilometer]]s. When a tree [[Death|died]], the roots rotted away into nothing from the inside out, leaving behind the caverns that comprised the habitat of the [[Kindalo]].<ref name="NoP" />
