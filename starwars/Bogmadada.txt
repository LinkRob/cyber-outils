'''Bogmadada''' was a location in [[the galaxy]] where the [[Two-Headed Toothvole]] was from. At least twenty [[cycle]]s<ref name="Outlaws" /> before [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[pirate]] [[captain]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] claimed to have killed the Two-Headed Toothvole of Bogmadada by shooting it between both sets of eyes on each head in the first draft of chapter 112 of his [[holobook]], "[[Hondo's Memoirs]]". Ohnaka left an excerpt of chapter 112 on a [[datapad]] at the [[Ohnaka Hideout]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] when the [[Ohnaka Gang]] abandoned the base.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
