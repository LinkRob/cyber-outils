'''Embertown''' was a [[mining]] location on the [[planet]] [[Burnin Konn]]. Originally known as '''Kind Caverns''', the place was renamed after the mine caught [[fire]]. Having survived the [[Great Jedi Purge]], the [[Jedi]] [[Khandra]] and [[Nuhj]] went into hiding there. Fighting broke out in Embertown with the arrival of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitor]], who attacked the two Jedi. That battle continued, and eventually ended up in a [[chromium]] mine off [[Wickridge]].<ref name="Uprising" />
