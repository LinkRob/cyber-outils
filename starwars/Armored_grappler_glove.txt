'''Armored grappler gloves''' were a type of [[armor]]ed [[glove]] consisting of a [[Color|brown]] fabric glove with striated yellow armor covering the back of the hand and part of the lower [[Limb|arm]]. The [[starship]] [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Viano Thag]] wore a pair of armored grappler gloves<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> while working on devices just inside the gate of<ref name="LostFound">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> the [[city]] [[Point Ueda]] on the [[planet]] [[Ueda]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> in [[132 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Armored grappler gloves first appeared in "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="LostFound" /> the first episode of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which aired on [[Disney+]] on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> Prior to the release of the episode, the gloves were revealed in a trailer exclusively shown at [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Celebration">{{CelebrationTrailer|''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''|Celebration Europe (2023)}}</ref> during the [[Lucasfilm]] Studio Showcase on [[April 7]], [[2023]].<ref name="CelebrationDate">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-lucasfilm-studio-showcase|text=SWCE 2023: 11 Highlights from Lucasfilm's Studio Showcase}}</ref> They were then identified in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
