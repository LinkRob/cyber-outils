'''Bek''' was a [[Mon Calamari]] from the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]] who served alongside [[Nien Nunb]] as a [[pilot]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. By [[34 ABY]], he was serving as a [[communications officer]] for the [[Resistance]], and during that [[Standard year|year]], he relocated to the Resistance's [[Resistance base (Batuu)|base]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]]. There, he helped prepare for the arrival of new recruits to the cause, joining [[Resistance High Command|Resistance Command]] and working with [[Ground Logistics Division|Logistics]] to prepare for an eventual evacuation while serving under [[Colonel]] [[Devik]]. He was reassigned to the [[Intersystem Transport Ship]] [[Cargo vessel TR-141]] alongside his friend Nunb when Command fell into need of qualified [[flight officer]]s.

As a [[lieutenant]] in the [[Resistance navy]], Bek helped brief a group of Resistance recruits after an evacuation of the base was ordered upon the approach of the ''[[Finalizer]]'', [[flagship]] of [[First Order]] [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]. With the recruits loaded aboard his [[transport]], the Mon Calamari co-piloted them into [[Realspace|space]] above Batuu before being [[Battle of Batuu|ambushed]] by [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|First Order TIE fighters]]. His [[Starship|ship]] was captured by the ''Finalizer'' using a [[tractor beam]], and Bek and all the recruits were taken [[prisoner]] by the First Order.

From an undisclosed location aboard the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]], Bek helped orchestrate a prisoner escape, guiding a pair of [[R5 series astromech droid]]s as they drove two [[First Order Fleet Transport]]s through the ship, narrowly dodging a [[probe droid]] before being recognized by [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] as they neared the [[turbolift]]s that would take them to the [[First Order Short-Range Evacuation Vehicle|escape pods]]. With their plan ruined, Bek redirected the [[droid]]s and led them through several dangerous encounters before finally escaping back to the surface of Batuu, where he crash landed within [[Savi & Son Salvage]]'s [[Savi's scrapyards|junkyard]].

==Biography==
===Always a rebel===
{{Quote|Lieutenant Bek, one of our top commanders, will lead you.''"<br />"''Affirmative. Transport is ready. Nien Nunb and I shall personally see them delivered.|audio=Bek-SeeThemDelivered-SWROTR.flac|Rey and Bek brief a group of Resistance recruits|<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" />}}
[[File:BekAndNunb.jpg|thumb|left|270px|Bek worked with his longtime friend Nien Nunb in both the Rebel Alliance and the Resistance.]]
Bek was a [[Mon Calamari]]<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" /> from<ref name="Staff" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Mon Cala]] who served as a [[pilot]] during the last [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]], at some point befriending fellow<ref name="Staff" /> [[Rebel Alliance Navy]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Nien Nunb]].<ref name="Staff" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the arrival of the [[Resistance]] on [[Batuu]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> during the [[First Order-Resistance War]], Bek, by then a sympathizer, [[lieutenant]], and [[communications officer]] for the [[Resistance]], relocated to the force's [[Resistance base (Batuu)|base]]<ref name="Staff" /> in the [[Black Spire Ruins]] on the Outer Rim planet [[Batuu]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> where<ref name="Staff" /> he<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" /> was instrumental in bringing the small operation together and making preparations for the arrival of more new recruits. As the number of recruits increased, Bek took on a role in [[Resistance High Command|Resistance Command]] coordinating recruit processing and working with [[Ground Logistics Division|Logistics]] on pack out in preparation for the base's eventual evacuation, serving as [[commanding officer]] [[Colonel]] [[Devik]]'s righthand.<ref name="Staff" />

As missions mounted up, however, Command found itself shorthanded when it came to qualified [[transport]] [[flight officer]]s. With his previous experience as a rebel pilot, Bek received an order assigning him to an [[Intersystem Transport Ship]] in the [[Resistance navy]], with the more junior Lieutenant [[Nasheer]] replacing him as Communications Officer. In his new position, Bek was paired with his [[Time|longtime]] friend Nunb.<ref name="Staff" />

===Evacuating the base===
{{Dialogue|First Order officer|Cargo vessel TR-141, identify yourselves and prepare to be boarded.|Bek|This is TR-141. We are a civilian vessel. By whose authority are we being detained?|Armitage Hux|By the authority of the First Order, Resistance scum. Now bring down your shields and prepare to be boarded.|attr=Bek attempts to trick Hux and a First Order officer|src=<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" />|audio=Bek-Officer-Hux-SWROTR.flac}}
[[File:BekUnderAttack.jpg|thumb|right|270px|Bek, Nunb, and the recruits are attacked by First Order TIE fighters.]]
After the arrival of the [[First Order]] and its [[Supreme Leader]], [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], on Batuu<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" /> less than a [[Standard month|month]] after Bek's arrival,<ref name="Staff" /> Resistance operative [[Finn]], along with a covert [[Finn's team|team]], infiltrated<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" /> Ren's [[flagship]],<ref name="TROS Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> the ''[[Finalizer]]'', and informed the Resistance that the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] was headed their way. With the evacuation of the Batuu base to a [[Pacara base|base]] on<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Pacara]] ordered, [[Jedi]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], along with Bek, Nunb, and [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]], briefed recruits on the evacuation plans. The recruits then loaded onto an Intersystem Transport Ship piloted by Bek and Nunb under the designation [[Cargo vessel TR-141]] and left the outpost for [[Realspace|space]], passing through Batuu's [[atmosphere]] and into the [[Planetary ring|ring]] of [[asteroid]]s surrounding the planet before being [[Battle of Batuu|attacked]] by [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s. Bek ordered the [[Deflector shield|shields]] be raised just as the [[starfighter]]s fired upon their [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] escort, but he soon discovered the origin of the [[TIE series|TIE fighters]]: a pair of [[Star Destroyer]]s.<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" />

The ''Finalizer'', one of the two Star Destroyers above Batuu, captured Bek's transport in a [[tractor beam]], leading Dameron to escape the battle to bring back reinforcements. The vessel was then contacted by a [[First Order officer]], whom Bek unsuccessfully attempted to deceive into believing they were a civilian cargo vessel. After being demanded by the First Order's [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] to lower their shields, and after the lieutenant reminded the recruits to keep the location of the Pacara base secret, Bek's transport was pulled into the ''Finalizer''{{'s}} [[hangar]], and all Resistance personnel were commanded by a member of the First Order to exit the transport and prepare for [[Torture|interrogation]]. During the interrogation, Bek and Nunb were separated from the recruits, who were interrogated by Ren and Hux before the latter received a call for the two to return to the [[Starship|ship's]] [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]].<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" />

===Prison break===
{{Quote|Your cover is blown! Find an alternate route! Who picked these two droids anyway?|Bek attempts to lead the R5 units to the escape pods|<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" />|audio=Bek-WhoPickedTheseTwo-SWROTR.flac}}
[[File:Star-wars-rise-of-the-resistance-hangar.jpg|thumb|left|270px|Bek and the recruits are captured by the First Order.]]
With the recruits unattended, members of Finn's covert team cut through the walls of the interrogation room and freed the [[prisoner]]s, loading them into two [[First Order Fleet Transport]]s piloted by two [[R5 series astromech droid]]s. Finn informed the recruits that the two [[R-series|droids]] would be taking them to the ship's [[First Order Short-Range Evacuation Vehicle|escape pods]]. Bek, transmitting from an undisclosed location aboard the ship, began guiding the recruits through the ''Finalizer'', helping them deceive another pair of R5 units into believing their breakout was a prisoner transfer. He then assisted in their evasion of a [[probe droid]] and led them to the [[turbolift]]s that would take them to the [[escape pod]]s. However, the recruits were recognized by two [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] and attacked. With their path to the turbolifts blocked, Bek commanded the R5 units to find an alternate route. With Bek's help, the [[Astromech droid|droids]] navigated their way around [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport]]s, the ship's bridge, and [[turbolaser]]s, being chased by Ren for most of their escape, only narrowly avoiding capture after a TIE fighter crashed through the [[hull]] behind the Supreme Leader.<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" />

[[File:BekIsAlive.jpg|thumb|right|270px|Bek survives a crash landing on Batuu.]]
Having reached their destination, the prisoners entered the escape pods and jettisoned themselves back toward Batuu, crashing into the [[Savi's scrapyards|junkyard]]<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" /> of [[Savi & Son Salvage]].<ref name="Staff" /> Finn made contact with the recruits and inquired worriedly about Bek's whereabouts, and much to his pleasure, the lieutenant was discovered in his own escape pod elsewhere in the junkyard. Bek then congratulated the recruits and the R5 units and thanked them for keeping the location of the Pacara base from the First Order.<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I have a bad feeling about this.|Bek, upon being captured|<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" />|audio=Bek-BadFeeling-SWROTR.flac}}
A Mon Calamari with [[Color|gray]] skin and yellow [[eye]]s,<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" /> Bek was a well-respected and disciplined [[officer]] who was as comfortable commanding a ship as he was in a military outpost. He was the epitome of grace under pressure,<ref name="Staff" /> though he had a bad feeling upon being captured by the First Order and showed frustration with the R5 units when their cover was blown during their escape. After their escape proved successful, he thanked the recruits for their efforts, though he was prompted to include the R5 units with which he had previously shown frustration.<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" />

==Skills and abilities==
Bek was a capable pilot.<ref name="Staff" />

==Equipment==
While assisting in the evacuation of the Batuu base, Bek wore a tan [[Clothing|shirt]] and brown pants with a thick, brown [[belt]], brown [[boot]]s, and a Resistance lieutenant's [[Resistance rank badge|rank badge]]. He co-piloted an Intersystem Transport Ship.<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" />
