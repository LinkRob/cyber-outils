The '''Coruscant underworld police recruitment test''' was the entrance exam for the [[Coruscant underworld police|underworld police]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The test measured an individual's ability to identify faces. By the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[bounty hunter]] [[Brand]] trained herself with versions of the recruitment test that could be found on the [[black market]], which enhanced her ability to remember faces.<ref name="Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Coruscant underworld police recruitment test was mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Twilight Company" />
