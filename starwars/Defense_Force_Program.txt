The '''Defense Force Program''' ('''DFP''') was a proposed military force for the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. The DFP was proposed by [[Senator]] [[Tia Toon]] of the [[planet]] [[Sullust]]. Prior to the [[Nihil]]'s [[Valo Atrocity|attack]] on the [[Valo]] [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]], the DFP was not popular enough to pass in the [[Galactic Senate]], despite multiple votes to establish it. Many officials viewed the DFP as Toon advancing the interests of the [[SoroSuub Corporation]] although Toon saw no conflict-of-interest advocating for his system's largest company and the Republic's security. Negative views of the program ranged from overly expensive to "unaligned with the vision" of [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]]'s administration.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

Toon, a [[Sullustan]], admired the [[Jedi Order]] but believed the Republic should not rely solely on the Order and the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] for protection. He worried what might happen if the [[Jedi]] were stretched too thin to protect the Republic or if something should happen to them. Toon also believed the RDC was too weak, disparate, and [[Republic Defense Coalition treaties|constrained]] to provide proper protection to the Republic. After the attack on Valo, Toon swore to support rebuilding efforts and praised the Jedi and the [[Royal Togruta Fleet]] for their timely aid. He proceeded to cite the attack as an example of why the DFP was necessary, as the Jedi and [[Togruta]] forces could have easily not made it in time to prevent much worse destruction. [[Jedi High Council]] member [[Stellan Gios]] reflected that Toon had been proven right about the need for the program.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

==Behind the scenes==
The proposed Defense Force Program was described in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''. By the end of that novel, it remained a proposal.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
