The '''GALAAR line''' was a line of [[Mandalorian]] [[blaster]]s, named after the [[galaar]], a common [[bird]] of prey [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]]. It included the [[GALAAR-15 blaster carbine]]. Like other weapons from the [[SHUKUR line|SHUKUR]] and [[WESTAR line]]s, the GALAAR line was never intended for export outside of Mandalore.<ref name="COTR">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref>
