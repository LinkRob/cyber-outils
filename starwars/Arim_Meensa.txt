'''Arim Meensa''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|V-wing starfighter]] [[pilot]] who held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Wing Leader]] in [[14 BBY]]. [[Gender|He]] and his pilots were loyal to [[Colonel]] [[Belkor Dray]], who governed [[Ryloth]] on behalf of [[Moff]] [[Delian Mors]]. During the [[Mission to Ryloth]], Belkor misled Meensa and his comrades into believing that Mors was part of a conspiracy to [[assassin]]ate [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Mors managed to sway Meensa and convince him of Belkor's treachery. He and his fellow pilots then helped Mors to locate the two [[Sith Lord]]s and destroyed Belkor's recon craft, [[Death|killing]] the renegade officer.<ref name="LOTS" />
