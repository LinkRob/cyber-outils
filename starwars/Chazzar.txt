'''Chazzar''' was an individual who served as a [[captain]] in [[Phoenix Squadron]]. Flying an [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]], she led the [[squadron]], including [[Mandalorian]] [[Journeyman Protector|Protector]] [[Fenn Rau]] and [[Cadet]] [[Kay Karno]], in [[Starfighter combat|combat]] against [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. When Karno recklessly left formation in pursuit of the enemy, Chazzar was left without cover. She barely escaped with her life, but piloted her damaged fighter back to [[Phoenix Home]].<ref name="D&C" />

Rau assumed temporary command of the squadron while she recovered from her wounds.<ref name="D&C" />
