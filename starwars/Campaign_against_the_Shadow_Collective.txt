During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Sith]] launched a '''campaign''' against the renegade [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]]'s [[Shadow Collective]]. The purpose of the campaign was to draw out the [[witch]] [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]] and [[Death|destroy]] her, a long time enemy of [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious|Sidious]]. Maul's intentions laid in a '''plot to destroy Darth Sidious''', his former master. After learning of Maul's escape from Sidious' capture and potential alliance with Dooku, the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Galactic Republic]] began '''pursuit''' of the two Sith.

The [[Separatist Droid Army]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Confederacy navy]] fought against the Collective, which included the [[Pyke Syndicate]], [[Black Sun]], Maul's [[Mandalorian super commando]]s, as well as a comradeship with the [[Nightbrothers]] of [[Dathomir]]. The Separatists suffered a loss in the [[Battle on Ord Mantell|Battle of Ord Mantell]], as the Separatist leaders of the campaign, [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and [[General]] [[Grievous]], were captured, but Dooku eventually freed Grievous, and he and Maul escaped from the Galactic Republic when they engaged the Shadow Collective during the [[Assault on Vizsla Keep 09]]. This alliance between the two was a ploy on both sides, and Maul planned to use it to bring back Talzin using Dooku's body, but were [[Second Battle of Dathomir|interrupted]] by Grievous and Darth Sidious himself. In the end, Sidious and the Confederacy defeated the Shadow Collective, as well as achieving [[Darth]] Sidious' goal of killing Talzin.

==Prelude==
In [[54 BBY]],<ref name="JMM 1">[[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 1|''Star Wars Jedi Master'' Magazine 1]]</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]], the [[Dathomirian]] [[Zabrak]] [[Nightbrothers|Nightbrother]] [[Maul]] was [[Reproduction|born]] to [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]] of the [[Nightsisters]].<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3" /> When Maul was a [[Youngling|child]], [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious|Sidious]] became an ally to Mother Talzin, visiting Dathomir and trading secrets of the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side of the force]] with her.<ref name="Unwilling Apprentice">{{StoryCite|story=An Unwilling Apprentice|book=Myths & Fables}}</ref> Sidious promised to make Talzin his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], however he took and trained her son Maul instead. Talzin swore to get her revenge someday.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3" />

Over the [[Standard year|years]], Sidious trained Maul to become a weapon of the [[Sith]], relentless against his enemies.<ref name="USW NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> During Maul's training, Sidious brought him to the Sith world of [[Malachor]] to breath in the ashes of his fallen Sith brethren, [[Great Scourge of Malachor|killed]] by the [[Jedi]] long before. This gave Maul a want for revenge against the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="DM 2">[[Darth Maul (2017) 2|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 2]]</ref> Eventually, in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="IoN Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|'Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], including the [[Invasion of Naboo]], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the Sith enacted their plan for revenge. In the [[Invasion of Naboo]], Maul engaged into a [[lightsaber duel]] with [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. Maul successfully [[Death|killed]] Jinn, but Kenobi sliced Maul's lower torso off, with Maul falling to his supposed death.<ref name="PM">{{Film|I}}</ref> Although having lost his apprentice Maul, Sidious continued his plans, gaining a reputation as a good [[senator]] for the people of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="SWEPalpatine">{{SWE|characters|palpatine|Palpatine}}</ref> However, Sidious was lacking an apprentice, and thus the [[Dark Jedi|Fallen Jedi]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] was made Palpatine's apprentice.<ref name="SWEDooku">{{SWE|characters|dooku|Count Dooku}}</ref> Dooku was the leader of an anti-Republic group known as the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. He publicly rejected any opportunity to talk with Palpatine, unbeknownst to the public that Dooku was Sidious' apprentice.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="First Battle of Geonosis Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], including the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] and start of the [[Clone Wars]], to [[22 BBY]].</ref> Dooku and his [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]] fought the Galactic Republic's [[Grand Army of the Republic|army]] of [[Cloning|clones]] on the planet [[Geonosis]] in a [[First Battle of Geonosis|battle]] that would mark the start of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="AotC">{{Film|II}}</ref> Two years into the Clone Wars, in [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Nightsisters Date">{{TCWcanondates|312}}</ref> Dooku was ordered by Sidious to assassinate his personal [[sith assassin]], [[Asajj Ventress]]. Although reluctant at first, Dooku followed through his master's orders. However, Ventress survived the attempt on her life, and returned to her [[homeworld]] of Dathomir. Ventress was once a Nightsister, but as a child the clan was forced to give her up to the [[Siniteen]] [[pirate]] [[Hal'Sted]] to protect the clan.<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref> Looking for revenge on Dooku, Ventress travelled to the [[Nightbrother village]] in order to find an assassin through the [[Selection]]. In an assassin, she found [[Savage Opress]],<ref name="Monster">{{TCW|Monster}}</ref> the brother of Darth Maul.<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref> Opress was to become the "apprentice" of Dooku as offered by the Nightsisters.<ref name="Monster" /> However, Opress betrayed both Ventress and Dooku, returning to Dathomir and learning that his long lost brother Maul, lived.<ref name="Witches of the Mist">{{TCW|Witches of the Mist}}</ref> Maul lived, stranded on the junk planet of [[Lotho Minor]].<ref name="Brothers" /> Through his hate and will to survive after being nearly killed by Kenobi back on Naboo, he used [[the Force]] to hang on to an airvent. He eventually made his was to a trash compactor, bringing him to Lotho Minor.<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>

Upon meeting his brother, Opress brought his brother back to Dathomir, in order to have Maul's mind restored by Mother Talzin. Talzin restored Maul's mind, as well as giving him a set of metal legs made from the wreckage of the [[battle droid]]s from a [[Battle of Dathomir|massacre of the Nightsisters]],<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref> enacted as revenge by Dooku.<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref> Maul and Opress went on a rampage in a quest for revenge on the Jedi.<ref name="Rampage">{{TBB|Rampage (episode)}}</ref> After a [[Skirmish on Florrum|battle]] against Obi-Wan Kenobi, Jedi Master [[Adi Gallia]], and the [[Ohnaka Gang]] of criminals, the brothers escaped in [[escape pod]]s.<ref name="Revival">{{TCW|Revival (episode)}}</ref> Floating barely alive, they were saved by the [[Mandalorian]] splinter group named [[Death Watch]]. They took them to the [[Death Watch camp]] on [[Zanbar]], giving Opress a prosthetic arm and Maul new metal legs. [[Pre Vizsla]] of Death Watch explained to Maul the malmanagement of the [[New Mandalorians|New Mandalorian]] government. Maul promised Death Watch the ability to retake Mandalore and kill Kenobi. Now an alliance, they travelled [[The galaxy|the Galaxy]] offering various criminal syndicates a part in the new criminal alliance. With the allegiance of the [[Hutt Clan]], [[Pyke Syndicate]], and the [[Black Sun]], the [[Shadow Collective]] was born.<ref name="Eminence">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref>

Having acquired the forces to make a sufficient army, the Shadow Collective successfully seized control of the Mandalorian city of [[Sundari]]. Eventually Death Watch betrayed the Collective, driving the criminals away and arresting Opress and Maul. They themselves claimed rule over Mandalore. Escaping from [[prison]], the Sith brothers sought a new ally in Mandalorian [[Prime Minister]] [[Almec]]. Maul stormed into the Mandalorian [[throne room]], challenging Pre Visla to a duel. Maul defeated Visla, beheading him with the [[Darksaber]], a [[Color|black-bladed]] [[lightsaber]] owned by the first Mandalorian Jedi [[Tarre Vizsla]]. Maul's victory split Death Watch into two with [[Mandalorian super commando|half]] staying loyal to Maul, and the other half rebelling against Maul led by [[Bo-Katan Kryze]]. Almec was reinstated as Prime Minister, and Maul had taken over Mandalore.<ref name="Shades of Reason">{{TCW|Shades of Reason}}</ref>

[[File:Stygeon_Prime_rescue.png|thumb|right|250px|Maul is broken out of the Spire by his super commandos.]]
However, scared of Maul's growing power, Sidious traveled to Mandalore in order to end Maul's rule. Maul, Savage, and Sidious dueled in the depths of Sundari. Sidious killed Opress, and disarmed Maul, sparing him as he needed Maul<ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref> to get rid of Talzin. Sidious kept Maul in [[Spire (Stygeon Prime)|the Spire]], a prison on the planet [[Stygeon Prime]]. Maul was [[Escape from the Spire|broken out]] of the prison by his Mandalorian super commandos led by [[Commander]] [[Gar Saxon]]. This would be mark the beginning of a war that would take place between the Confederacy of Independent Systems and the Shadow Collective.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 1" />

==The campaign==
===Battle of Zanbar===
{{Main|Battle of Zanbar}}
[[File:Armies-on-Zanbar.png|thumb|left|250px|The armies of the Separatist Alliance and Mandalorian traditionalists line up.]]
Following orders given by Almec, the super commandos brought Maul to the Death Watch camp on Zanbar to rendezvous with his forces. Maul ignited the Darksaber, rallying his army and promising them the war they had waited for their entire lives. At that note, a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Separatist navy]] fleet headed by Grievous began [[Battle of Zanbar|attacking]] the Shadow Collective forces as part of<ref name="Son of Dathomir 1" /> the new campaign against Maul.<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Kashyyyk and Other Planets in the Mid Rim}}</ref> The forces had followed Maul to the [[moon]]. [[C-9979 landing craft]]s flew in the skies, and Maul's army began to rain [[turret]] fire on the [[dropship]]s. When the ships landed, the might of the full Separatist droid army was unleashed upon the army, with various waves of [[droideka]]s, [[B1-series battle droid]]s, and [[B2-series super battle droid]]s.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 1" />

As Maul sliced through the battle [[droid]]s, Grievous stood on the landing bridge of his [[Grievous's Providence-class Dreadnought (Campaign against the Shadow Collective)|his]] [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence-class'' Dreadnought]]. Promising Maul that the end was soon to come, the General sent four [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s in an attempt to take the Zabrak out. As the battle roared around him, Maul made quick work of the MagnaGuards. One Mandalorian tried to take out Grievous by himself, but was quickly killed by the [[Kaleesh]] [[warlord]]. It was at this point that Maul and Grievous finally engaged in a lightsaber duel. Eventually, Grievous kicked Maul to the ground. Maul looked around as his army was massacred by the endless forces of droids. In retaliation, Maul had his [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|gauntlet fighters]] bomb the droid army. Having been picked up by Commando [[Rook Kast]], in his personal gauntlet fighter, the [[Nightbrother (Gauntlet fighter)|''Nightbrother'']]. They escaped as flames engulfed the Death Watch camp. Grievous reported to Dooku that he had made Maul and his forces flee.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 1" />

===Battle of Ord Mantell===
{{Main|Battle on Ord Mantell}}
{{Quote|This isn't a counter attack--it's a slaughter!|Ziton Moj|<ref name="Son of Dathomir 2" />}}
[[File:CIS_bombardment_SoD_part2.png|thumb|right|300px|The Separatists bombard Ord Mantell]]
Maul meditated while in [[hyperspace]]. Maul was met by Talzin via the [[spirit world]], a parallel dimension in Nightsister culture which few Nightsisters could travel. Talzin instructed Maul to bring his fleet to the planet [[Ord Mantell]] to gather more strength and forces. Entering the [[Bright Jewel system]], the Shadow Collective fleet landed in the [[Black Sun stronghold]] in [[Ord Mantell City]]. Maul discussed the war with [[Ziton Moj]] of the Black Sun and [[Fife]] of the Pyke Syndicate. Meanwhile, Confederate spies reported to Dooku and Grievous that Maul's fleet had landed on Ord Mantell. Dooku reminded Grievous not to attack until they had confirmation that Talzin was with them. As if on cue, [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] [[Tey-Zuka]] informed them that reconnaissance droids near Dathomir reported a single transport shuttle leaving the planet. Thus, Dooku and Grievous prepared to [[Battle on Ord Mantell|attack]] Ord Mantell.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 2" />

As the transport ship from Dathomir landed on Ord Mantell, a [[Viscus' clan|clan]] of Nightbrothers stepped out, headed by Brother [[Viscus]]. Maul met with his brethren, preparing for battle. The Separatist fleet entered out of hyperspace, firing down on the city at the command of Grievous. As Separatist forces landed, the two armies began fighting in the second battle of their war.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 2" />

Dooku landed his ship at the site of the battle, sending out MagnaGuards to find Maul and Talzin. At that same time, Grievous and Tey-Zuka contemplated where Maul's Mandalorian warships were. Then, Maul sprung a trap on Grievous, attacking his dreadnought and the rest of the droid fleet. As Dooku investigated, Viscus and his Nightbrothers took out his MagnaGuards. They then fought, with Dooku standing outnumbered.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 2" />

[[File:Battle_over_Ord_Mantell.png|thumb|left|275px|The orbital battle between the Shadow Collective and Separatists over Ord Mantell]]
Saxon, a Pyke, and a Falleen hid behind rubble as the seemingly never ending forces of droids continued their slaughter of the Collective. However, a [[B2 grapple droid]] punched through the rubble, taking hold of the Pyke. As the space battle continued around them, Maul and Saxon landed on Grievous' dreadnought, with Maul using the Darksaber to cut a whole in the side of the ship. The two entered the bridge of the ship, and [[Force push]]ed a group of droids to the side. Maul tackled Grievous and raised the Darksaber to Grievous' throat.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 2" />

Count Dooku stood his own against the Nightbrothers, sending endless volleys of [[Force lightning]] at them. As the droids surrounded the Shadow Collective, they were forced to surrender. However, Maul's takeover of the Providence gunship caused all the droids to deactivate, giving them time to rip apart the forces of the droid army. Dooku used the Force to slam Brother Viscus into the ground. However, before he was able to kill Viscus, he was captured by the survivors of the Collective.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 2" />

===Assault on Visla Keep 09===
{{Main|Assault on Vizsla Keep 09}}
{{Quote|General Kenobi-- Probes are launched and gunships are ready to scramble on your order. If Maul is hiding in there, we'll drag him out.|[[Commander]] [[Cody]] of the Galactic Republic|<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3" />}}
The events at Ord Mantell caught the attention of the Galactic Republic and Jedi Order. Via hologram, Jedi Master [[Mace Windu]] notified [[Chancellor]] Palpatine of the ongoing battles. A Jedi investigation group was sent to Ord Mantell after the battle, including Jedi Masters, Mace Windu, Obi-Wan Kenobi, [[Tiplee]], and [[Aayla Secura]]. Their investigation led them to the finding a [[Mandalorian armor|Mandalorian super commando helmet]], leading them to believe that the battle had taken place between Dooku and the Shadow Collective. The group then prepared to travel to a nearby Mandalorian [[Space station|supply outpost]] known as [[Vizsla Keep 09]].<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3" />

[[File:Dooku_Kills_Tiplee.png|thumb|right|300px|Jedi Master Tiplee is killed by Count Dooku]]
Meanwhile at Vizsla Keep 09, having captured Dooku and Grievous, Maul hologrammed Sidious showing him how he had captured the two. Speaking on account of Mother Talzin, Maul reminded Sidious of his history with the Dathomirian [[witch]]. Sidious told Maul to kill both of them as he had no use for either of them. Turning off his holocommunications, Maul and Rook put Grievous in the ship's brig while Maul would converse with the Count.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3" />

Using Maul as a vessel, Talzin appeared to Dooku through the spirit world. The witch explained her history with Sidious and how he took her [[Reproduction|son]] Maul. Talzin asked Dooku to join her and Maul, explaining how Sidious would eventually betray him. While conversing, Commander Saxon notified Maul of incoming Republic ships, with Maul commanding him to block their transmissions. However in the commotion, Dooku used the Force to press a button releasing Grievous from his cell.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3" />

The [[Republic Navy|Galactic Republic Navy]] fleet slowly approached the space station. As the troops prepared for an [[Assault on Vizsla Keep 09|invasion]], the Shadow Collective launched gauntlet fighters. As the rest of the ships engaged in combat, an [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] ship carrying Kenobi, Tiplee, and the [[212th Attack Battalion]] landed in<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3" /> the rings of [[Phelbos]].<ref name="VK9 Databank">{{Databank|vizsla-keep-09|Vizsla Keep 09}}</ref> During the commotion, Grievous slipped out of Visla Keep 09 through an escape pod.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3" />

[[File:Kast_missile.png|thumb|left|250px|Rook Kast fires a missile.]]
Maul handed back Dooku [[Dooku's lightsaber|his lightsaber]], teaming up with him in order to escape alive. The Jedi and Sith engaged in a standoff in which Dooku killed Master Tiplee. Rook Kast used the [[missile]] on her [[Z-6 jetpack]], rendering Secura and Windu unconscious. Maul, Dooku, Kast, and Saxon escaped in the ''Nightbrother'', living to fight another day. The [[clone trooper]]s arrested the Mandalorians left at the station and Supreme Chancellor Palpatine demanded to speak with the now conscious Windu. Windu believed that Dooku had been the master of Maul and that the Battle of Ord Mantell had been just a deception. However, Palpatine believed that the battle could spell disaster for the Republic.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3" />

===End of the campaign===
{{Main|Second Battle of Dathomir|Attack on the Pyke Syndicate|Attack on the Black Sun}}
En route to Dathomir, Maul was hologrammed by Moj and Fife. They believed that Maul's Collective was becoming his own crusade and named the casualties in which it had cost them. Maul reassured them that the galaxy would soon be theirs for the taking. Landing in the cliffside [[Nightbrother village]], Maul presented Brother Viscus with Count Dooku. Preparing for Mother Talzin's return, Maul escorted Dooku into a large building and placed his hand on a large stone panel where he began to recite a prayer to his mother. Talzin's reflection appeared in the stone and soon her spirit began to emanate from it, [[Possession|possessing]] the Count. Meanwhile, Sidious and Grievous stayed in orbit above the planet in the ''[[Scimitar]]'', a heavily modified [[Star Courier]] which Maul had piloted during his apprenticeship to Sidious. Having tracked Count Dooku's signal, Sidious ordered Grievous to jam their communications and bring the ship into cloaking mode. Viscus and the Nightbrothers left to prepare Talzin's chambers while Maul watched as the Count became possessed. Maul explained the process Dooku's body was going through. However, Grievous exploded his way through one of the walls of building, with Sidious in tow.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 4" />

During the events on Dathomir, Separatist armadas laid siege to Black Sun and Pyke Syndicate forces. Fife and Ziton Moj hologrammed Maul again, but were instead met by Rook Kast. The crime lords demanded to speak with Maul, but Kast instead instructed them to follow their orders. The Black Sun and the Pyke Syndicate both pulled out of the Shadow Collective, leaving Maul and the Death Watch by themselves.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 4" />

[[File:Droids-Second-Battle-of-Dathomir.png|thumb|right|200px|The Separatist forces destroy the Nightbrother village.]]
As Maul drew the Darksaber, Dooku's body had become fully possessed by Talzin. The two sides [[Second Battle of Dathomir|engaged]] in lightsaber combat, with Maul fighting Grievous and Talzin fighting Sidious. Sidious disarmed Talzin and used Force lightning to electrocute Dooku's body. Having finally gained the necessary energies, Talzin's spirit exited Dooku and she materialized. Meanwhile, a Separatist invasion force appeared in the [[Dathomir system]]. Maul force pushed Grievous out the whole in the building's wall. Talzin and Sidious each sent force lightning at each other, the two forces dispersing. Waking up, Dooku added a stream of lightning to help Sidious oppose Talzin. Maul put his hand on his mother's shoulder, offering her his strength. However, the Sith's strength overpowered that of the Dathomirians. Additionally, Grievous had crawled back into the building through the window. In the ''Nightbrother'', Kast and Saxon urged Maul to retreat. Talzin told Maul to leave, however Maul believed that they could both escape. Talzin force pushed Maul away, into his ship in a last stand to protect her son. The two Mandalorians held back Maul as he watched Grievous stab his mother in the heart. As the three escaped in the gauntlet fighter, the invasion fleet landed on Dathomir and destroyed the Nightbrother's village.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 4" />

==Aftermath==
Returning to Mandalore, Maul regrouped his Shadow Collective,<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref> adding the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate.<ref name="Together Again">{{TCW|Together Again}}</ref> Maul and his forces [[Siege of Mandalore|fought]] against the invasion force of the Bo-Katan's [[Nite Owls]] and the Galactic Republic's [[332nd Division]], led by [[Ahsoka Tano]], the former [[Padawan]] of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice" /> Maul was captured by Tano and imprisoned on the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tribunal]]''. While imprisoned on the ship, Darth Sidious ordered the activation of [[Order 66]],<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> a directive order in the [[behavioral modification biochip]] of clone troopers that would cause them to attack all the Jedi.<ref name="Fugitive">{{TCW|Fugitive}}</ref> In order to ensure her escape, Tano freed Maul from his cell as two [[Imperial shock trooper|shock troopers]] were about to execute him.<ref name="Shattered" /> Maul travelled to the ship's [[hyperdrive]], which he destroyed using the Force. Exiting hyperspace, the ship was set on a crash collision into a [[Unidentified moon|moon]]. In the ship's hangar, Maul piloted a [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class attack/transport shuttle]], escaping the vessel.<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> The activation of Order 66 marked the end of the Clone Wars and Sidious [[Proclamation of the New Order|reformation]] of the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> an event which Maul had foreseen.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], Maul continued his criminal operations under his Crimson Dawn<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> and as the secret head of the [[Five Crime Syndicates]] overall.<ref name="Crimson Climb">''[[Crimson Climb]]''</ref> While running Crimson Dawn, Maul traveled to the planet [[Damanos]] to search a [[Sith castle]] in search of Sith artifacts. Maul saw a vision of Grievous, who taunted him about killing his mother during the Second Battle of Dathomir.<ref name="Ghosts of Maul">{{StoryCite|story=The Ghosts of Maul|book=Stories of Jedi and Sith}}</ref>

Not long after the events of the Second Battle of Dathomir, the surviving Nightbrothers travelled to the [[Upper Strangled Cliffs]] where they built a [[Unidentified Nightbrother village|new village]] within the cliffs. During the activation of Order 66, the Jedi Master [[Taron Malicos]] crashed his ship into the [[Swamp of Sacrifice]]. Malicos was captured by the Nightbrothers, but escaped and killed Brother Viscus. Malicos usurped control of the Nightbrothers and worked in conjunction with one of the last Nightsisters [[Merrin]].<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFODate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]], who survived Order 66 as well, [[Mission to Dathomir (Cal Kestis)|travelled]] to Dathomir with his [[Stinger Mantis crew|crew]] aboard the [[luxury yacht]] ''[[Stinger Mantis]]''. With the help of Merrin, Malicos was buried alive. Merrin then joined the ''Mantis'' crew, leaving the Nightbrothers without a leader.<ref name="JFO" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|It was one of the arcs that I really believed in and I felt like there was a lot of story there to be able to tell. It wasn't just evil versus evil, it was like someone that we respected versus some characters that as bad as they are, we still love watching them in action and kind of scheming their machinations against one another.|[[Matt Michnovetz]]|<ref name="CWL">{{SWYouTube|vnH1JKr4o_8|Star Wars: The Clone Wars Legacy|sw_url=video/the-clone-wars-legacy}}</ref>}}
[[File:Assault_on_Visla_Keep-ConceptArt.png|thumb|right|250px|Concept art of Republic ships approaching Vizsla Keep 09.]]
The campaign against the Shadow Collective first appeared in [[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 1|first issue]] of the four-part [[Star Wars: Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir|''Star Wars: Darth Maul—Son of Dathomir'']] series,<ref name="Son of Dathomir 1" /> written by [[Jeremy Barlow]], and illustrated by penciller [[Juan Frigeri]], inker [[Mauro Vargas]], colorist [[Wes Dzioba]], and cover artist [[Chris Scalf]].<ref name="SoD TradePB">{{DarkHorse|url=Books/24-771/Star-Wars-Darth-Maul-Son-of-Dathomir-TPB|text=Star Wars: Darth Maul&mdash;Son of Dathomir TPB}}</ref> ''Son of Dathomir'' was an adaptation of unproduced scripts from ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', which was part of the television show's sixth production season, as part of [[The Clone Wars Legacy]] project. However, the episodes were ultimately canceled after [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] acqusition of [[Lucasfilm]].<ref name="CWL" /> The [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry for Count Dooku named the war as the "plot to destroy Darth Sidious."<ref name="DB">{{Databank|count-dooku|Count Dooku}}</ref>

In the original scripts for the ''Clone Wars'' episodes, the war would have included a [[gladiator]]ial pit battle against a [[shatterax]] monster.<ref name="Insider149">[[Star Wars Insider 149|''Star Wars Insider'' 149]]</ref> [[Daniel Wallace]] writer of the [[Star Wars Insider 149|''Star Wars Insider'' 149]] article in which this was reported, states that this plot point was ultimately cut from the final comic run.<ref name="Daniel Wallace Gladiator">{{Twitter|danwallaceMSP|status/524062233991872512|[[Daniel Wallace]]|date=2014-10-20|quote=It was in the rough story info that I received, must have been cut|archivefile=File:DanielWallace-Shatterax-Twitter.png}}</ref>
