'''Borbig Drob''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Houk]] who worked as a [[gladiator]] during the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s reign and was declared champion of the contest known as the [[Stormblade Bloodfest]] on nine occasions. He was invited to take part in the event known as [[Gladiator Night]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]], where he faced the [[Feeorin]] gladiator [[Warjak]], whom he had agreed to lose to for additional pay. The rigged match was interrupted, however, by the arrival of [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Jenkes]], who was being chased by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] and his companion [[Ezra Bridger]]. After Jenkes wounded him, Bossk requested that he fight the two gladiators in bare-handed combat, which Drob and Warjak agreed to, only for the bounty hunter to defeat them both.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Come on, fellas. Give a dead guy a break. Bare-handed means bare-handed.|Bossk asks Drob to drop his weapons before they fight|<ref name="EG8" />}}
The [[Gender|male]] [[Houk]] Borbig Drob worked as a [[gladiator]] during the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|rule]]. He participated in the gladiatorial event known as the [[Stormblade Bloodfest]] several [[time]]s, emerging victorious on nine occasions. Sometime after his ninth victory, Drob was hired by the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Jenkes]], who used the alias "the Commissioner," to take part in an event called [[Gladiator Night]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]].<ref name="EG8" /> Drob, and his opponent, [[Warjak]], were picked up on the planet [[Nyriaan]] by a [[transport]], which delivered them to the [[Capital City]] [[Capital City spaceport|spaceport]] on Lothal in secret, as Jenkes was operating the gladiatorial fights illegally. Once on Lothal, the pair were taken in an unmarked [[K79-S80 Imperial Troop Transport|Imperial Troop Transport]] to [[Monad Outpost]], a [[mining]] outpost refurbished for the fight.<ref name="EG5">''[[Ezra's Gamble]]''</ref>

[[File:Bossk-TCW.png|thumb|left|Drob fought the bounty hunter Bossk at Monad Outpost.|250px]]
At Monad Outpost, Drob was armed, before heading into the arena where he was introduced to the waiting crowd by Gladiator Night's [[Pa'lowick]] [[Unidentified Pa'lowick announcer|announcer]], garnering both cheers and boos from the audience of [[gambler]]s watching. Before the fight began, Warjak confirmed with Drob that the Houk would let him win, as the Commissioner had promised them a small fortune if they rigged the fight.<ref name="EG8" /> The fight then began, but was soon interrupted by the arrival of Jenkes in the arena, who was being chased by the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] and a local street urchin named [[Ezra Bridger]]. Drob and Warjak failed to notice the trio's arrival at first, but realized something was wrong when the cheering of the crowd ceased, causing them to stop fighting.<ref name="EG9">''[[Ezra's Gamble]]''</ref>

Jenkes, who Drob did not know was the Commissioner, wounded Bossk and then announced that he was only there to [[Death|kill]] Bossk and not to stop the fight, after which Bossk announced that if he were to die, it should be in combat against the gladiators. This earned the approval of the crowd, and Drob and Warjak prepared to face the Trandoshan, who asked that the fight be in [[unarmed combat]]. The two gladiators obliged and dropped their weapons, after which Bossk immediately launched himself at Drob, headbutting him in the face and driving his knee into the Houk's [[stomach]], causing him to collapse. Bossk then also defeated Warjak, before revealing that Jenkes was the Commissioner and that he had planned to steal the bets of every [[Gambling|gambler]] at the arena. Imperial forces then arrived at Monad Outpost, and Jenkes was arrested.<ref name="EG9" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Remember, the Commissioner will pay us a fortune if I win."<br />"I remember."|Warjak and Drob confer before beginning their rigged fight|<ref name="EG8" />}}
Borbig Drob was a capable gladiator who won the Stormblade Bloodfest on multiple occasions. However, he was willing to lose in a rigged match against Warjak for additional pay. When Bossk challenged him and Warjak to bare-handed combat, Drob assumed he could easily defeat the wounded Trandoshan and willingly dropped his weapons, only to be quickly beaten by the [[bounty]] hunter. The Houk was heavily muscled,<ref name="EG8" /> with huge forearms.<ref name="EG5" />

==Equipment==
At Monad Outpost, Drob fought bare-chested<ref name="EG5" /> with a heavy [[sword]] and a broad shield.<ref name="EG8" />
