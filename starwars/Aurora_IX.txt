The '''''Aurora IX''''' was a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]], a brand-new design from the [[Republic shipyards]] on [[Hosnian Prime]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. During a combined Republic and [[Jedi Order]] effort to save the [[Hetzal system]] from [[hyperspace]] debris, it was commanded by [[Captain]] [[Finial Bright]] and crewed by [[Petty Officer]] [[Innamin]] and [[Ensign]] [[Peeples]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
