The '''assault on the Stormwall''' was an attempt by the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] to breach the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Stormwall]] so they could reach the Nihil [[Occlusion Zone]]. After the assault destroyed several [[stormseed]]s creating a breach in the Stormwall, multiple Republic [[Starship|vessels]] made their way to the breached zone, intending to enter the Occlusion Zone. As they approached, however, the wall began to repair itself and the Republic vessels and sole [[Jedi Vector]] which approached were destroyed immediately.<ref name="Eye of Darkness" />

In response to the attack on the borders of their territory, the [[Eye of the Nihil]], [[Marchion Ro]], had the Occlusion Zone [[Expansion of the Stormwall|expanded]] by having the stormseeds moved, resulting in the Republic losing more [[sector]]s to the Nihil.<ref name="Eye of Darkness" />
