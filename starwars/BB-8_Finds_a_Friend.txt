'''''BB-8 Finds a Friend''''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' young-readers title in the [[World of Reading]] Level 1 series. It was written by [[Nate Millici]], illustrated by [[Pilot Studio]], and published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[October 25]], [[2016]]. The book's story adapts [[BB-series astromech droid|astromech droid]] [[BB-8]]'s first encounter with the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] from the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'']]. ''BB-8 Finds a Friend'' was released exclusively as part of [[World of Reading Star Wars Boxed Set: Level 1|World of Reading ''Star Wars'' Boxed Set: Level 1]].<ref name="Penguin" />
