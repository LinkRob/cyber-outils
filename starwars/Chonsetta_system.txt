The '''Chonsetta system''' was located in the [[Metatessu sector]]<ref name="Systems" /> within [[the Slice]] portion<ref name="Slice" /> of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. Situated at the coordinates R-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it contained at least one [[comet]].<ref name="BTC" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]]&mdash;consisting of the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'', the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]] ''[[Apailana's Promise]]'', and two [[X-wing starfighter]]s&mdash;was [[Battle in the Chonsetta system|attacked]] by around a dozen [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE interceptors]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], which had followed a [[hypermatter]] leak from the ''Thunderstrike''{{'s}} [[hyperdrive]]. The [[TIE series|TIEs]], having been hidden away in a comet trail, ravaged the starboard side of the [[corvette]] before the 61st escaped. After the attack, the rebel [[company]] discovered how the Empire had tracked its [[Starship|vessels]].<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chonsetta [[Star system|system]] first appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
