Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''battle''' was fought on<ref name="Vader 30" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> [[Fedalle]] between four former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaidens]] of [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The [[handmaiden]]s, consisting of [[Dormé]], [[Eirtaé]], [[Saché]], and [[Rabé Tonsort]], along with another, [[Sabé]], had previously [[Battle at Padmé's tomb|attempted]] to [[Death|kill]] Vader, as they believed him to be responsible for Amidala's [[death]]. The group were defeated, and Sabé eventually came into the service of Vader as he sought to bring order to [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Vader 30" />

The other handmaidens learned of Sabé's service to Vader and tried to rescue their former companion. They captured the [[assassin]] [[Ochi]], who also served Vader, and then infiltrated Vader's [[flagship]], the ''[[Executor]]'', where they learned of Sabé's present [[Mission to Brentaal IV (Sabé)|mission to kill]] the revolutionary [[Jul Tambor]]. After Dormé's cover was blown, Vader took her aboard a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|shuttle]] and they departed from the ''Executor''. The other handmaidens followed Vader's [[shuttle]] to Fedalle, where Dormé tried to inform them she had left the information on Sabé's location so they could track her. Saché then informed Dormé that she was not going to sacrifice her to ensure they saved Sabé, which Dormé countered by stating she had sacrificed them all instead. Vader drew his [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and the handmaidens attacked.<ref name="Vader 30" />

Vader was eventually able to overwhelm and defeat the four, and as he attempted to recruit them to serve him, the handmaidens revealed that they had the activation code to the [[self-destruct mechanism]]s of the ''Executor'', and that they were prepared to kill all aboard if they did not get Sabé. Vader told them to do it, but the situation was interrupted by Ochi, who presented Vader with a message from [[Admiral]] [[Firmus Piett]] informing them that Tambor had contacted them with a challenge for him to retrieve Sabé from him. Vader then asked Dormé what she wanted, to which she replied that if it was how they would save Sabé, then they would be at his service.<ref name="Vader 30" />
