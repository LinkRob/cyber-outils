The '''Fangs of the Hutt''' were a criminal gang that operated on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Ponemah Terminal]], utilizing heavy [[speeder]]s. They were one of the many criminal groups on Ponemah who attempted to [[Hunt for Dooku's Treasure|track down]] supposed lost treasure aboard the crashed [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[cruiser]] ''[[Obrexta III]]'', managing to board the [[Starship|vessel]] first. However, the Fangs of the Hutt were unaware that the ship's core had been leaking [[Poison|toxic]] [[durilliam]] [[gas]], and all [[Death|perished]] upon entering the wreck.<ref name="Crimson Corsair" />
