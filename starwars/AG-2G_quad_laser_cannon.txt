The '''AG-2G quad laser cannon''' was a vintage [[quad laser cannon]] model and the primary defensive weapons of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' when [[Finn]] and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] [[Escape from Jakku|commandeered]] the [[Starship|ship]] thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="TVD" /> AG-2Gs were manufactured by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]].<ref name="IncrediBuilds" />

It was favored for its rapid transverse movement and tight beam production that allowed long-range shooting. The four barrels fired one at a time in a pattern that could be selected by the gunner. Each barrel fired once every 1.32 [[second]]s.<ref name="OWM">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>

Prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Han Solo]] and [[Luke Skywalker]] also used the AG-2Gs to defend the ''Millennium Falcon'' from four [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] after escaping the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Episode IV" />
