'''Bo Rogiave''' was a [[Lacertilo]] member of the [[Resistance]]. As a member of an [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[species]], Rogiave had an advantage in low-[[oxygen]] areas due to his compound [[lung]]s. When the Resistance prepared to [[Battle of Exegol|attack]] the [[Sith Eternal military|forces]] of the [[Sith Eternal]] on [[Exegol]], Rogiave eagerly signed up for the ground assault team.<ref name="TROS VD" />
