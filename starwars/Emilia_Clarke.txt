'''Emilia Clarke''', full name '''Emilia Isabelle Euphemia Rose Clarke''', (born [[October 23]], [[1986]]<ref name="This Week Hype" />) is an [[Wikipedia:British people|English]] actress. She played the character [[Qi'ra]]<ref name="Vanity Fair Interview">{{WebCite|url=http://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2017/08/michael-k-williams-emmy-interview|text=Michael K. Williams Has a Story You Need to Read to Believe|author=Miller, Julie|date=2017-08-22|archivedate=20200519233434}}</ref> in the movie ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', which is part of the ''[[Star Wars Anthology Series]]''. Her role in the film has been stated to "round out a dynamic cast of characters that [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] will encounter on their adventures."<ref name="Cast">{{SW|url=news/emilia-clarke-joins-the-han-solo-stand-alone-film|text=Emilia Clarke Joins the Han Solo Stand-Alone Film}}</ref>

Clarke rose to fame in [[2011]] for her breakthrough role as [[Wikipedia:Daenerys Targaryen|Daenerys Targaryen]] in the [[Wikipedia:HBO|HBO]] series [[Wikipedia:Game of Thrones|''Game of Thrones'']]. She is also known for her starring roles as [[Wikipedia:Sarah Connor (Terminator)|Sarah Connor]] in the [[2015]] science fiction film [[Wikipedia:Terminator Genisys|''Terminator Genisys'']] and as Louisa Clark in the [[2016]] romantic drama film [[Wikipedia:Me Before You (film)|''Me Before You'']].
