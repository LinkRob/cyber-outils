'''Anadeen'''<ref name="Life Debt" /> was located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]'s [[Anadeen system]] at the coordinates O-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Systems" /> in [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It was the site of a [[Anadeen shipyard|shipyard]] that was controlled by the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[New Republic]]. The New Republic fought to gain control of the [[shipyard]]<ref name="Life Debt" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> which [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]] noted when he spoke of contested Imperial locations like Anadeen in a meeting with his new Imperial [[Shadow Council]]<ref name="Life Debt" /> that [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="Date" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> Anadeen lay in the [[New Republic]]'s territory.<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anadeen was first mentioned in the [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', the second installment of [[Chuck Wendig]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Life Debt" />
