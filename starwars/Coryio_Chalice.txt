The '''Coryio Chalice''' was an artifact in [[the galaxy]]. In [[3 BBY]], [[Davo Sculdun]], a wealthy [[Chandrilan]] business [[gender|man]], purchased the chalice from an [[unidentified art dealer|art dealer]] only to later find out that it was a [[Forger (profession)|forgery]]. As a result he ordered his assistant [[Mishko]] to have his entire collection re-appraised.<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" />
