'''Athul Taran''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Gormak]] who lived on [[Dalna]]<ref name="Vengeance" /> in [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Star Wars: Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> He argued with [[Path of the Open Hand]] member [[Xander Cran]] near the [[Ferdan]] landing strips. When [[Jedi]] [[Oliviah Zeveron]] and [[Matthea Cathley]] interceded, Taran informed them that the Path had been buying up land and buildings in Ferdan, accusing them of trying to force [[Dalnan]]s out of their homes. He considered the Path responsible for the [[Battle of Jedha|riots on Jedha]], calling Cran "[[Brikal-shell paint|brikal]]-head." Taran demanded to see what was in the crates the Path was offloading, and after some persuading by Cathley, verified that they contained Starbeam [[legume]]s and other food supplies. Although Cran stated that the food was to feed everyone on Dalna during the inevitable food shortages caused by the early rains, Taran said he would not be eating their "muck" before walking away.<ref name="Vengeance" />
