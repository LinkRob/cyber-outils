'''Fektrin's squad''' was a unit led by [[Sergeant]] [[Fektrin]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[61st Mobile Infantry]].<ref name="TC" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> the 61st [[Raid on an Imperial heavy freighter|raided an Imperial heavy freighter]]. The [[squad]] escorted a trio of engineers to obtain the supplies the unit needed for their ship, the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]''. During the fight, one of their [[Rebel trooper|troopers]], [[Cappandar]], took a dozen shots before hitting the floor. Fektrin led the rest of the squad to a rendezvous where the engineers got onto a terminal. When Imperial reinforcements arrived, the squads were ordered to get back to their pods and Fektrin was killed as his squad traveled back. The [[alien]] [[Mzun]] stepped in to take charge of the squad after.<ref name="TC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fektrin's squad appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="TC" />
