A '''corsair''' was a type of [[warship]] and [[capital ship]].<ref name="SecondEdition">[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles (second edition)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles'' second edition]]</ref> The [[Lioaoin Regime]] used a variety of [[starship]]s, including corsairs, which were used as a [[patrol ship]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /><ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> During the [[New Republic Era]], [[Pirate King]] [[Gorian Shard]]<ref name="Pirate">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 21: The Pirate}}</ref> owned a [[Gorian Shard's pirate Corsair|corsair]] of the [[Cumulus-class Corsair|''Cumulus''-class]], which he used to lead his [[Gorian Shard's pirate crew|gang]] of [[pirate]]s. Shard's corsair was equipped with fourteen ventral ball-shaped [[turret]] [[gunwale]]s. Shard's corsair had an anvil-shaped prow.<ref name="Apostate">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 17: The Apostate}}</ref>
