The '''Black Sun fortress''' was a fortified compound built into a cliffside on the [[Volcano|volcanic]] [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Mustafar]]. From within, a [[Black Sun ruling council|cabal]] of [[Falleen]] [[noble]]s led and operated the criminal syndicate known as the [[Black Sun]] during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Databank Black Sun" /> and beyond.<ref name="Hope & Courage">''[[Forces of Destiny: Tales of Hope & Courage]]''</ref> It was here that [[Maul]] and the [[Death Watch]] Mandalorians [[Mission to Mustafar (Death Watch)|confronted]] the Black Sun leadership with an ultimatum to join the [[Shadow Collective]], a criminal alliance set up and led by Maul.<ref name="SWE Black Sun">{{SWE|groups|black-sun|Black Sun}}</ref> Although Black Sun abandoned Mustafar during the time of the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> it continued to be used during the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="Species From A to Z">{{GermanFanCite|115|Database}}</ref> [[Pirate Queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] made reference to the fortress in her [[Maz Kanata's Journal|journal]] as the site from which the Black Sun leadership governed the syndicate.<ref name="Hope & Courage" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Black Sun fortress made its first appearance in "[[Eminence (episode)|Eminence]]," the fourteenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''. Although the installation was not named in its debut appearance,<ref name="Eminence">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref> the accompanying episode guide identified it as the Black Sun fortress.<ref name="Eminence Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/eminence-episode-guide|text="Eminence" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>
