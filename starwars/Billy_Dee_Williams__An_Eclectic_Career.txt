"'''Billy Dee Williams: An Eclectic Career'''" is an article which appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 58|''Star Wars Insider'' 58]]. It was written by [[Hellen Keier]] and [[Allen Varney]] and revolved around an interview with [[Billy Dee Williams]], who played [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando Calrissian]], at the Gen Con Game Fair in Milwaukee, Wisconsin in [[August]] [[2001]].
