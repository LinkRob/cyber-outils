A '''division''' was a type of military unit of varying size and type that was active throughout different factions in [[Galaxy|galactic]] history. Divisions ranged from [[Navy|naval]] to [[army]] divisions, and were different military unit types. The term division could also be used generically to mention a contingent of military units.

==Description==
===Law enforcement===
{{Expandsect}}
The [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]]'s sub-bureaus<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> were also referred to as divisions. The [[Coalition for Progress]], led by [[Minister]] [[Pitina Mar-Mas Voor]], was considered one such division of COMPNOR and served as the propaganda arm of the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Aphra 29">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 29|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 29]]</ref>

===Ground forces===
A land military division was part of an [[army]], and varied in size and type.<ref name="TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> The [[commanding officer]] of a division was referred to as a [[Commander|Division Commander]].<ref name="Encyclopedia Din">{{EncyclopediaCite|Din Djarin and Other Characters of the Underworld}}</ref> Variations of a land military division were the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[armored infantry division]], which utilized [[Tank|armor]] and [[infantry]] forces,<ref name="AAT Databank">{{Databank|aat-battle-tank|AAT Battle Tank}}</ref> and the armored division<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> which was also referred to as a tank division. Such divisions were deployed by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]],<ref name="AAT Databank" /> the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="Padawan Lost">{{TCW|Padawan Lost}}</ref> and the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref> Armored [[squadron]]s could form part of Republic tank divisions.<ref name="Padawan Lost" />

[[File:Hasbro-PhaseIIboxart.png|thumb|right|350px|Personnel of the GAR 332nd Division were part of the 501st Legion]]
Ground divisions in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] could be combined-arms units subordinate to a [[legion]] with aerial and ground vehicles<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref> and specialized reconnaissance divisions subordinate to a [[battalion]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> Republic divisions could also contain [[Company|infantry companies]] and had the capability to deploy [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s, [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]] and [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/carrier|LAAT carriers]]. Such a division could be led by a [[Clone Commander]].<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> Additionally, Republic armor divisions consisted of [[AT-series|all-terrain vehicles]] and [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]]s supported by [[clone sharpshooter]]s, [[clone trooper officer]]s, [[heavy weapons clone trooper]]s, and standard [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

In the Galactic Empire, a division could consist of 5,000 to 10,000 [[soldier]]s and could be led by a [[general]]<ref name="Chapter 15">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 15: The Believer}}</ref> or a [[colonel]].<ref name="Dark Lord 8" /> An Imperial armored division could consist of [[All Terrain Defense Turret]] [[artillery]] [[walker]]s, [[Imperial swamp trooper]]s, [[wet-weather gear stormtrooper]]s, [[Imperial medic]]s, and [[Imperial ground crew]], with logistical support from [[Y-45 armored transport hauler]]s and aerial support by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]].<ref name="TOG" /> Other armored divisions used [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|All Terrain Armored Transports]], [[All Terrain Defense Pod]]s, [[K79-S80 Imperial Troop Transport]]s, and [[stormtrooper]]s. Such a division could be attached to an Imperial numbered [[fleet]] and led by the fleet's commander.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

===Naval forces===
Naval divisions were part of the Galactic Empire's [[Imperial Navy]] [[Order of Battle]], and contained multiple ships. An Imperial Navy admiral had the authority to assemble multiple divisions.<ref name="No Good Deed">{{StoryCite|story=No Good Deed...|book=Darth Vader (2017) 1}}</ref> The [[Rebel Alliance Navy]] also utilized numbered naval divisions, which consisted of various types of different starships such as [[frigate]]s, [[corvette]]s, [[transport]]s,<ref name="Star Wars 1">[[Star Wars (2020) 1|''Star Wars'' (2020) 1]]</ref> and [[cruiser]]s.<ref name="Star Wars 15">[[Star Wars (2020) 15|''Star Wars'' (2020) 15]]</ref> In the Alliance Navy, a division could be led by a general,<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> a [[commodore]],<ref name="Star Wars 7">[[Star Wars (2020) 7|''Star Wars'' (2020) 7]]</ref> or a naval [[commander]]. Alliance divisions could have starfighter squadrons.<ref name="Star Wars 1" />

==History==
===Republic Era===
{{Quote|A division of Republic troops was to arrive yesterday, but the evacuations caused by the collapsing star in the Bunka system waylaid them.|Poli Dapatian|<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order 1">[[Jedi Fallen Order - Dark Temple 1|''Jedi Fallen Order - Dark Temple'' 1]]</ref>}}
While it was active in the [[Republic Era]], the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]] deployed [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tanks]] which formed the frontline of the [[Trade Federation]]'s armored infantry divisions. The [[Droid]] Army subsequently participated in the [[Invasion of Naboo]], deploying its vast [[Trade Federation military|military]], including Armored Assault Tanks.<ref name="AAT Databank" /> The [[Judicial Forces]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] served as the Republic's premier [[Law enforcement agency|peacekeeping force]] towards the end of the Republic Era.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> When the [[Ontothon government]] joined the Republic, a division of Republic [[soldier]]s was meant to arrive on the planet at the request of the local constabulary. The division was waylaid when a [[star]] collapsed in the [[Bunka system]] and they were needed to aid with evacuations.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order 1" />

===Clone Wars===
[[File:Christophsis_shield.png|thumb|left|280px|Separatist armored divisions participated in the Battle of Christophsis.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], both factions deployed ground divisions in the conflict. Pressed into Separatist service,<ref name="AAT Databank" /> [[tank]] divisions were deployed against the [[Jedi]] and [[Galactic Republic]] during the conflict,<ref name="Collapse" /> with these armored divisions containing Armored Assault Tanks that overpowered opposition on many Republic [[planet]]s,<ref name="AAT Databank" /> There were multiple divisions within the Republic's Grand Army.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

In the first year of the Clone Wars, [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> several Separatist armored divisions consisting of [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I]]s participated in the [[Battle of Christophsis]] during the invasion.<ref name="AAT Databank" /> The Separatist presence was under the overall command of [[General]] [[Whorm Loathsom]], and his units fought against Republic forces led by [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]]. Ultimately, Separatist forces were defeated.<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> That same year, the [[224th Imperial Armored Division|224th Clone Division]]<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> and its [[Mud Jumpers]] fought in the [[Battle of Mimban]] against Separatist forces alongside units of the [[501st Legion]].<ref name="501 Plus One">{{StoryCite|story=501 Plus One|book=Age of Republic Special 1}}</ref>

[[File:Clone-tank-divisions-Felucia.png|right|280px|thumb|Tanks belonging to clone tank divisions during the Second Battle of Felucia.]]
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> less than a [[standard year]] after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> that sparked the Clone Wars,<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> the [[212th Recon Division]], which was organized under<ref name="Battlefront II" /> the [[7th Sky Corps]]'<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> [[212th Attack Battalion]], was deployed alongside the 501st Legion, the [[41st Elite Corps]], and a [[Jet's battalion|special operations battalion]]. The 212th consisted of [[Advanced Recon Force trooper|Advanced Recon Force Scout Troopers]] and was deployed alongside All Terrain Tactical Enforcers from LAAT/i gunships and LAAT carriers.<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> Clone tank divisions participated in the [[Second Battle of Felucia]]<ref name="Padawan Lost">{{TCW|Padawan Lost}}</ref> in [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> and were supported by the [[104th Battalion]] and the 501st Legion. A [[Unidentified tank squadron|Republic tank squadron]] was part of one of these clone tank divisions.<ref name="Padawan Lost" />

A Republic division undertook a [[mission to Aleen]] after a series of [[groundquake]]s rocked the planet alongside the 104th Battalion. Secret [[agent]]s of Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] were among the medical staff.<ref name="Nexus">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref> Later, the [[Umbaran Militia]], which allied itself with the Separatists during the Clone Wars, also deployed tank divisions against the Galactic Republic during the [[Battle of Umbara]]. During the campaign, three [[Umbaran]] tank divisions protected an [[Umbaran airbase|airbase]] near the [[Umbaran capital city]], but were ultimately defeated when Republic forces conquered the base.<ref name="The General">{{TCW|The General (episode)}}</ref>

[[File:Rex-and-guards.png|thumb|left|300px|The 332nd was formed late in the Clone Wars and active under Clone Commander Rex.]]
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the Republic [[332nd Division]]<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> was created after the [[501st Legion]] was split into two forces. The 332nd Division participated in the [[Siege of Mandalore]], and consisted of the [[332nd Company]] and other units who were equipped with standard 501st [[color]]s and the standard white. The 332nd Division was led by [[Clone Commander]] [[Rex]]. It consisted of other clone troopers, including fellow [[clone trooper officer]]s, such as [[Clone Lieutenant]] [[Jesse]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Vaughn]], and an [[Advanced Recon Commando]] in the form of Jesse.<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref> Additionally, the Grand Army of the Republic deployed the [[181st Armor Division]] during the Clone Wars, which consisted of [[clone trooper]]s with green markings and vehicles. The 181st's arsenal allowed it to conduct safe [[transport]] in harsh environments and reinforce Republic [[regiment]]s that were stuck in remote locations.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

===Imperial Era===
====Early deployments====
[[File:224th-Han-Solo-group.png|thumb|right|300px|The 224th Imperial Armored Division participated in the Mimban Campaign.]]
Early during the [[Imperial Era]]<ref name="Dark Lord 8" /> in 19 BBY,<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Colonel]] [[Barokki]] led a [[Barokki's division|division]] which had poor performance. As a result, the [[Imperial officer]] was reprimanded directly by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Dark Lord 8" /> Sometime between 19 BBY and [[14 BBY]],<ref name="No Good deed date">{{NoGoodDeedDate}}</ref> [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] ordered [[Admiral]] [[Mummert]] with assembling naval divisions and stationing them at the [[Hortolo system]] before a deadline. Admiral Mummert was unable to deploy the [[starship]]s by the deadline, as gathering the proper divisions had taken longer than believed since the ships were scattered.<ref name="No Good Deed" />

The [[224th Imperial Armored Division]] participated in the [[Mimban Campaign]]<ref name="TOG" /> in [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> The division's forces there consisted of [[All Terrain Defense Turret]]s, and was supported by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and [[Y-45 armored transport hauler]]s. Its infantry forces consisted of [[Imperial officer]]s, [[Imperial swamp trooper]]s, [[wet-weather gear stormtrooper]]s, [[Imperial medic]]s, and [[Imperial ground crew]].<ref name="TOG" /> Around the same time,<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> the [[Vandor division]],<ref name="Imperial Troops">{{EncyclopediaCite|Imperial Troops}}</ref> located on the Imperial [[planet]] [[Vandor]], was active and consisted of [[range trooper]]s that defended Imperial [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport]]s that traveled between the [[mountain]]s and the frontier.<ref name="TOG" />

====Galactic Civil War====
During the [[Battle of Atollon]]<ref name="Zero Hour" /> in [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> an [[Unidentified armored division|armored division]] was deployed by the Imperial [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|7th Fleet]] and consisted of several [[heavy battalion]]s. These heavy [[battalion]]s consisted of All Terrain Armored Transports that were bolstered by All Terrain Defense Pods and supported by Imperial Troop Transports and stormtroopers.<ref name="Zero Hour" /> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Darth]] Vader alerted all nearby divisions shortly prior to a [[skirmish on Hradreek]] in an attempt to secure the second [[crossguard lightsaber]] belonging to the ancient [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Atrius]].<ref name="Annual 4">[[Star Wars Annual (2015) 4|''Star Wars Annual'' (2015) 4]]</ref> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] utilized divisions within their [[Alliance Army]] structure, with [[Alliance officer]]s commanding the units of ground-based [[troop]]s.<ref name="Lead">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref>

Following the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Rebel Alliance Navy]]'s structure included numbered [[Navy|naval]] divisions after the main force splintered. The divisions acted as [[Rebel cell/Canon|rebel cells]], and were different in their composition. The [[Fourth Division]] consisted of at least three [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]]s, at least four [[CR90 corvette]]s, at least two [[GR-75 medium transport]]s, and other support ships. The Fourth Division was led by [[Alliance officer]]s, as well as [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Commander]] [[Grek]]. Additionally, it was supported by [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]] of the [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps]].<ref name="Star Wars 1" />

[[File:Rebel_fleet_ESB.jpg|thumb|left|260px|The Alliance Navy Fourth Division after the Battle of Hoth.]]
The [[Sixth Division]] was destroyed by Imperial forces,<ref name="Star Wars 3">[[Star Wars (2020) 3|''Star Wars'' (2020) 3]]</ref> and consisted of at least one CR90 corvette, a GR-75 medium transport, and starfighters.<ref name="Star Wars 10">[[Star Wars (2020) 10|''Star Wars'' (2020) 10]]</ref> The [[Eighth Division]] was also active, but was destroyed at [[Malastare]].<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> Additionally, the [[Seventh Division]], which consisted of the [[MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80A ''Home One'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]] ''[[Home One]]'', at least one EF76 Nebulon-B escort [[frigate]], at least one CR90 corvette, and a number of [[starfighter]]s, was another such group.<ref name="Star Wars 8">[[Star Wars (2020) 8|''Star Wars'' (2020) 8]]</ref> The [[Eleventh Division]] consisted of another MC80A Star Cruiser.<ref name="Star Wars 15" />

In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> A [[Valin Hess's division|division]] under the command of General [[Valin Hess]] maintained between 5,000 to 10,000 soldiers, which included field operatives. Hess's division participated in the [[Destruction of Burnin Konn|attack on Burnin Konn]] under [[Operation: Cinder]], leading to the division's destruction.<ref name="Chapter 15" /> After the [[Battle of Endor]], defected pilot [[Yrica Quell]] believed that an ex-Imperial who arrived with another group at [[Traitor's Remorse]] was from one of the elite [[stormtrooper]] divisions, perhaps a [[death trooper]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> During the Galactic Civil War between the Rebel Alliance's successor state, the [[New Republic]], and the Empire, during the [[hunt for Shadow Wing]], the [[New Republic Intelligence]] [[Agent]] [[Caern Adan]] eventually had an entire division of personnel under his control.<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>

===New Republic Era===
[[File:FinnChewbaccaSoloCaptured-TFA.png|thumb|right|265px|Ren's stormtrooper division in the Battle of Takodana.]]
By the time after the fall of the Galactic Empire and its remnants were active, [[Mandalorian]]s maintained the formation of a division as part of their warrior organizational hierarchy. When Mandalorian [[Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] participated in a [[Assault on the Imperial Gozanti freighter|mission]] to steal weapons from the [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]],<ref name="Chapter 11">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref> in [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> she stated for the [[Tribus|captain]] of the [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|Imperial ''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] they were stealing if they could imagine what a division of Mandalorians could do.<ref name="Chapter 11" />

Like its Imperial predecessor,<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> the [[First Order]] also utilized infantry divisions in the [[First Order Army]]'s organizational structure. During the [[Battle of Takodana]], [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] ordered his [[Kylo Ren's division|stormtrooper division]] to withdraw upon the capture of [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and the arrival of the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref>
