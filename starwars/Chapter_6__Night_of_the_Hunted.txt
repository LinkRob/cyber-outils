"'''Chapter 6: Night of the Hunted'''" is the sixth episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'' and was released on [[April 20]], [[2026]] alongside "[[Chapter 5: Inquisition]]." Its title was revealed on [[March 17]], 2026 by the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' Twitter account.<ref name="Twitter" />

==Plot summary==
===Maul's lair===
The episode opens with a landscape shot of an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] hovering above [[Janix city]] before zooming into the lower levels of the metropolis. At [[Maul's safe house]], [[Maul]] practices with [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|his lightsaber]] when [[Rook Kast]] brings him news. In the command room, they view [[Spybot]]'s footage of the [[Inquisitor]] known as [[Marrok]]. 

===Calm before the storm===
Back at the Lawson apartment, [[Brander Lawson]] and [[Rylee Lawson]] finish having dinner with their [[Jedi]] guests [[Eeko-Dio Daki]] and [[Devon Izara]]. Daki thanks Rylee for inviting them for dinner but Brander says that they have to leave. He shuts the blinds. Daki sends Izara and Rylee out of the room. Brander warns Rylee to keep an eye on Izara. 

In private, Brander tells Daki that the two Jedi have placed him and his son in danger. Daki responds that the Empire would do the same to him and his Padawan. He introduces himself as a Jedi Master and explains that he and Izara barely survived the [[Great Jedi Purge]]. They were traveling through the planet Janix but the [[Galactic Empire]]'s arrival has complicated matters. Master Daki says that they are only looking for a place of refuge. 

When Brander says that the situation is difficult, Daki suggests that the lawman may be privy to smuggling routes, criminal passages and anything that could help them. Brander replies that he is no friend of the Empire but that he has a child to think about and apologizes. Meanwhile, Izara shelters in Rylee's room. Izara notices a picture of Rylee and his parents and asks about his mother [[Drea Lawson]]. Rylee explains that his parents are separated and that Drea got a job working for the Empire on [[Coruscant]]. Rylee explains that he stayed on [[Janix]] because of school and his [[botekin]] team. When Izara expresses puzzlement at botekin, Rylee explains that it is a popular local sport. Izara explains that she experienced a different childhood. 

===The Imperial raid and pursuit===
Meanwhile, Brander tells Master Daki that he was interrogated by an Inquisitor but did not reveal anything about Daki. He explains that the Imperials released him but that he doesn't know if the Inquisitor believed him. Daki's face motions as the Jedi Master senses a disturbance in [[the Force]]. Just then, a red [[lightsaber]] slices through the front door. Daki tells Izara they have to leave now. The Inquisitor [[Marrok]] hurls the door away with the Force. Daki and Izara draw their lightsabers in self defence and hold off the Inquisitor so that the Lawsons can escape through the window. 

Seeing a parked [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] (LAAT/i) gunship below, Lawson and his son head towards the fire escape. Daki sends Izara to protect the Lawsons while he holds Marrok off. He collapses a light panel on top of the Inquisitor while the apartment catches fire. Izara and the Lawsons head to the top of the apartment complex where they are pursued by an LAAT/i gunship carrying [[TK stormtrooper]]s, who demand they surrender. After arguing about their escape plans, Brander exchanges fire with the stormtroopers while Izara attacks them with her lightsaber. 

At Brander's prompting, Izara jumps onto the LAAT/i gunship's front canopy and hijacks the vehicle, throwing out the pilot. Daki joins them on the rooftop but is followed by Marrok. Daki and Marrok continue their lightsaber duel. Izara uses the gunship's guns and [[laser cannon]]s to strafe Marrok, allowing Daki to flee with them aboard the gunship. Despite her inexperience, Izara tries her best to fly the gunship. They are pursued by several Imperial LAAT/i gunships. At Brander's urging, Izara takes the ship down through a highway. One of the pursuing LAAT/i gunships is not able to pull up in time and crashes onto the highway. 

Inside the passenger compartment, Rylee asks why is the Empire after them. Brander tries to explain that the Empire is not what they think but their conversation is interrupted by gunfire. One of the pursuing gunships hits one of the left rear engines of their stolen gunship, causing it to list. Brander shoots at one of the pursuing LAAT/i gunships' pilots, causing his ship to crash into a structure and explode. The damaged gunship crashes on a nearby platform. The four flee through a door before their Imperial pursuers catch up with them. 

===Fugitives===
A contingent of stormtroopers and an [[All Terrain Armored Cannon]] (AT-AC) walker march outside the [[Janix Civil Defense HQ]]. Inside the building, [[Officer]] [[Reb]] and [[2B0T]] ("Two Boots") discuss the new Imperial support for the [[Tactical Defense Force]]. Stormtroopers accompany [[Tactical Defense Droid]]s. Two Boots is enthusiastic that the Imperials have offered them significant support and hopes that Captain Lawson will see his point about the benefits of Imperial rule. Their conversation is interrupted by several holographic wanted posters displaying Brander's face. A smug [[Lieutenant]] [[Blake]] informs Reb that Lawson is wanted for [[treason]] and warns him not to follow in his superior's lead. He also chastises Two Boots for his non-regulation boots and tells him to get rid of them. 

Meanwhile, the four fugitives hide in an alleyway while an AT-AC and a contingent of stormtroopers marches through the streets. Master Daki says that the Empire now knows that they are Jedi and will not stop hunting them even if they leave the city. Desperate, Brander takes his son and the Jedi to [[Rheena Sul]]'s nightclub in [[Section 5 (Janix)|Section 5]], seeking help in getting transport offworld. They find Sul serving a [[Devlikk]] patron, who eyes the group with interest. In private, Brander tells Sul that they need passage offworld while Daki adds somewhere without an Imperial presence. Sul says this is difficult due to the trouble the have stirred up. When Brander says that they only came to her because things are desperate, Sul replies that she will see what she can do. 

Elsewhere, Spybot monitors an Imperial [[Viper probe droid]] near Maul's hideout and relays this information to Kast and Maul. Despite Kast's concerns about the Imperial presence on Janix, Maul heads out to seek Izara, regarding the dangerous situation as a test of her resilience and worthiness. Back at the nightclub, Rylee explains to Izara that Sul was an informant who worked with his dad. When Daki asks Brander if Sul can be trusted, the lawman explains that Sul was a [[bounty hunter]] he had encountered on [[Catalor]]. Despite a bad start to their relationship, the two survived because of each other. Through the security cameras, Brander sees several stormtroopers entering the reception to the nightclub and warns Rylee that they need to leave. 

Sul tells them to wait upstairs while she handles the situation. When Sul goes out to greet the stormtroopers, their leader orders that she be detained. Sul struggles with her captor and a fight breaks out. Viewing the situation through a security camera, Brander tells the others to flee. Stormtroopers ascend up the [[turbolift]]. While Brander and Daki fight them with their [[blaster]] and lightsaber, Brander instructs the younglings to flee up the turbolift. Reaching the parking bay at the top, the two hide behind structures and vehicles while stormtroopers fan out and search the area. The stormtroopers are accompanied by Marrok. 

===The train ride and Maul to the rescue===
While the younglings head for a fence, Daki uses the Force to knock out three stormtroopers. He and Brander flee in a turbolift before more stormtrooper reinforcements arrive. Reaching the parking lot, they take out three stormtroopers guarding the turbolift bay and exchange fire with the rest of the squad. Daki tries to reassure Brander that Izara is well trained but Brander is anxious. The two men flee down an alleyway with stormtroopers in pursuit. Spybot hacks into the security camera system and discovers Izara and Rylee have fled into a subway terminal. Before they can board the train, Marrok catches with them up. Izara tells Rylee to flee while she fights off the Inquisitor. Rylee jumps onto an open air cargo carriage.

Izara duels with Marrok but is unable to match the older Force wielder. She retreats onto the train but the Inquisitor follows her and continues the duel there. The Inquisitor advances through the train carriages and corners Izara against one of the carriages. He demands to know where Maul is. Maul reveals himself and jumps down from a carriage. Marrok replies that he now knows Maul lives and the two men parry. Maul hits Marrok several times but the Inquisitor is relentless. Izara comes to the aid of Maul and manages to drive Marrok towards the end of the train. Maul aids her advance by hurling boxes of crates at the Inquisitor, who dodges and deflects them with the Force. 

When Marrok activates the spinning mode of [[Marrok's lightsaber|his spinning lightsaber]], Maul responds by igniting the second blade of his double-bladed lightsaber. Rylee helps Izara up while Maul throws his lightsaber into Marrok, with the latter blocking it and getting pushed on the other carriage. The trio regroup and Izara slashes through the connection system connecting the rail carriage that Marrok is standing on, leaving the Inquisitor behind. Izara turns to face Maul and raises her lightsaber in self-defense. Maul defuses the situation by deactivating his lightsaber and telling Izara that she has now seen the face of her true enemy. 

Later, Marrok returns to the hovering Star Destroyer and activates his holoproject. He speaks to an unidentified cloaked figure and confirms that Maul is alive and is in league with two Jedi. He asks his master how he should proceed.
