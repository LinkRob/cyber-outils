The '''Ezaraa''' were a [[species]] of warmongering [[Diet|carnivorous]] [[Sentience|sentients]]<ref name="Screaming Citadel" /> that were [[Homeworld|native]] to the the [[planet]] [[Ezaraa (planet)|Ezaraa]].<ref name="Aphra 10" /> They intended to overthrow the [[Galactic Empire]], only to replace it with their own [[Dominion of the Ezaraa|dominion]] and feed on the other species, which they deemed as lesser to them.<ref name="Screaming Citadel" /> Despite their wide reaching dreams of conquest, however, the Dominion was limited to a mere two [[Ezaraa continents|continents]] on Ezaraa. To arm their revolution, the dominion sent Ezaraa to take advantage of opportunities such as the [[Auction of Rur]].<ref name="Aphra 10" />

==Society and culture==
{{Quote|<nowiki></nowiki>''Hah''! Why pay when one can ''slay''? Then retrieve the bauble from its smoking chassis! It is the Ezaraa way!''"<br />"''For the glory of the Ezaraa Dominion!''"<br />[The Ezaraas take a single causality]<br />"Retreat!|Ezaraa warriors during the Rur Crystal incident|<ref name="Aphra 11">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 11|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 11]]</ref>}}
Described by the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] as "religious types,"<ref name="Smugglers">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> the Ezaraa made up the [[Dominion of the Ezaraa]], a warmongering faction that had control of two [[Ezaraa continents|continents]] on their homeworld of [[Ezaraa (planet)|Ezaraa]]. Despite that limited territory,<ref name="Aphra 10" /> the Dominion had dreams of overthrowing [[Galactic Empire]] and replace it as the ruling government of [[the galaxy]],<ref name="Screaming Citadel" /> with one Ezaraa further proclaiming that their ultimate [[destiny]] was to control the whole [[universe]].<ref name="Aphra 10" /> Once in power, they would feed on the other species, which they deemed as lesser to them.<ref name="Screaming Citadel" /> Enemies of the Ezaraa were regarded as heretics. One Ezaraa once mocked the [[human]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] as "quasi-[[Sentience|sentient]] flesh," but they offered a small degree&mdash;specifically 0.00001 percent&mdash;of their future earnings to Aphra and her next ten descendants should she provide them with the [[Rur Crystal]]. If she did not, the Ezaraa promised that her and the greater [[Aphra family]] would suffer within [[torture plant]]s. For her part, however, Aphra was not threatened and instead thought the Ezaraa were foolish for dreaming of universal conquest when they did not even control their whole homeworld.<ref name="Aphra 10" />

The Ezaraa way was defined by combat and the taking of life; during the incident with Aphra and the Rur Crystal, during which [[Eternal Rur]] intelligence [[Skirmish aboard the Sorca Retreat|managed to take over]] a [[Unidentified blue Cyban Front droid|droid body]], an Ezaraa remarked it was better to claim their prize from the droid's corpse, heralding it as the Ezaraa way. In the name of the Ezaraa Dominion and its supposed glory, that Ezaraa and two others quickly engaged the droid, only for one of their number to be cut down. In response, one of the two remaining Ezaraas quickly ordered a retreat.<ref name="Aphra 11" /> One of the survivors did attempt to engage the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] in combat, proclaiming their species was unstoppable before Vader slaid the Ezaraa.<ref name="Aphra 12">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 12|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 12]]</ref>

==Ezaraa in the galaxy==
{{Quote|The Ezaraa will rule the universe. It is our destiny. For delivering the Rur sentience to us, you will receive 0.00001% of our Imperial revenue, for you and your next ten descendants.|An Ezaraa, to Chelli Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 10" />}}
[[File:EzaraaDominion-2016DoctorAphra10.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The Ezaraa dreamed of spreading their Dominion across the stars.]]
The Dominion of the Ezaraa yearned to rule the universe and sought out anything that could arm them against their opposition.<ref name="Aphra 10" /> [[Unidentified Ezaraa|One]] of them visited the [[Citadel of Ktath'atn]] in an attempt to trade an oddity to the [[Unidentified Queen of Ktath'atn|queen]] there in exchange for a favor of the Dominion's choice. The attempt did not succeed, however.<ref name="Screaming Citadel" /> The Dominion of the Ezaraa also visited the [[Auction of Rur|auction]] of the conscious [[Rur Crystal|kyber crystal]] containing the [[Eternal Rur]], in the hopes of obtaining and weaponizing it.<ref name="Aphra 9" /> They promised the host of the auction, [[Doctor]] Chelli Aphra, a share of their Dominion if she granted them the crystal and they were ever successful in taking over the galaxy. Upon confirming with her [[protocol droid]] [[0-0-0]] that the Ezaraa did not even control all of their home planet, however, she was left unsure if they were more foolish for dreaming of universal conquest or if she was for inviting them in the first place.<ref name="Aphra 10" />

The [[kyber crystal]], however, was able to possess a [[Cyban Front]] [[droid]] and attack those gathered. In response and in the name of their government's glory, Ezaraa attacked with the aim of capturing the crystal from the droid's corpse, only for Rur to quickly slay one of the Ezaraa. Scared, the two survivors elected to retreat.<ref name="Aphra 11" /> After [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] summoned by 0-0-0 and Aphra's fellow droid servant [[BT-1]], both of whom were eager to break from her service and hoped to use the incident to do so, entered the battle, one of the Ezaraa attempted to engage the [[Sith]] [[Lord]] [[Darth]] Vader, who led the Imperial assault and quickly cut down the Ezaraa before moving onto Rur.<ref name="Aphra 12" />
