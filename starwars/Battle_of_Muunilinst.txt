The '''Battle of Muunilinst''' was a battle during the [[Clone Wars]] that took place on the [[planet]] [[Muunilinst]].<ref name="Build Falcon 45" /> The training of [[clone trooper pilot]]s in their handling of the [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]] postponed their deployment until this battle.<ref name="Starfighters">{{EncyclopediaCite|Republic, Rebel, and Resistance Starfighters}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the [[Battle of Muunilinst/Legends|Battle of Muunilinst]] appeared in the [[Clone Wars: Season One|first]] and [[Clone Wars: Season Two|second seasons]] of the television series, ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Clone Wars">''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]''</ref>
