'''Cor Tophervin''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who was installed as the [[governor]] of [[Inusagi]]<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> in [[6 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> He was a personal friend of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. He did not get to enjoy his new position for long, however, as he was [[Death|killed]] when [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[partisans]] [[Mission to Inusagi|stormed]] the [[sakoola blossom festival]] where Tophervin was to be formally invested as governor and massacred all of the guests.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
