'''Adi Gallia's Jedi Starfighter''' was a [[Red]] [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] flown by [[Jedi Master]] [[Adi Gallia]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

==History==
[[File:Foresight-SWZ.png|thumb|left|270px|Gallia pilots her Delta-7B interceptor during the Battle of Lola Sayu.]]
Around [[21 BBY]], Adi Gallia piloted her [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] during a [[Battle of Lola Sayu|mission]] to extract a group of [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Capture of Even Piell|prisoners]] and the team sent to rescue them after they escaped from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[prison]] known as the [[Citadel]] on the [[planet]] [[Lola Sayu]]. Together with [[Saesee Tiin]] and [[Kit Fisto]] in their [[Saesee Tiin's Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|respective]] [[Kit Fisto's Delta-7B starfighter|interceptors]], the three Jedi escorted one of [[Plo's Bros|Plo Koon's gunships]] to the planet's surface to help in the extraction of the prisoners. Gallia, with Tiin and Fisto, provided cover fire for [[Plo Koon]] while he successfully extracted the prisoners on his [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|gunship]].<ref name="Rescue">{{TCW|Citadel Rescue}}</ref>
