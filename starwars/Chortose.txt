'''Chortose''' was an astronomical object situated in the [[Rseik sector]]<ref name="THR 7" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Region" /> inhabited by the [[Chortose (species)|Chortose]] [[species]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Agricultural Hub 42]] was a [[factory]] situated on Chortose. The factory was [[Attack on Chortose|attacked]] by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] in the time leading up to the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] for its [[sprayer droid]]s. The [[Jedi Knight]] [[Keeve Trennis]] was sent to defend Chortose, while the [[Jedi]] [[Wayseeker]] [[Orla Jareni]] followed a [[distress signal]] to Chortose on her own, assisting Trennis when she was overwhelmed by the Nihil.<ref name="THR 7" />
