'''Dewback Snacks''' were a type of [[cracker]] that were shaped to look like various [[Species|creatures]]. It came in a [[Color|green]] container that resembled a [[dewback]], with "DEWBACK SNACKS" and "COSMIC CREATURE CRACKERS" written on the side in [[High Galactic]]. The beige-colored crackers resembled the faces of [[Gamorrean]]s, [[Mynock]]s, [[Rancor]]s, [[Tauntaun]]s, and [[Wampa]]s.<ref name="Dewback" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dewback Snacks featured on the card named "Dewback Snacks" from [[Star Wars: Card Trader]], debuting in the ''Galactic Feast'' card set on [[November 11]], [[2025]].<ref name="Dewback" />
