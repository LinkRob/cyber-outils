'''Electromagnetic winds''' were a weather feature on [[planet]]s such as [[Zeffo]], in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="SWJFO" />

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the Empire attempted to mine Zeffo for resources toward the construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]]. The electromagnetic winds in Zeffo, however, made this task impossible for the most part.<ref name="SWJFO" />
