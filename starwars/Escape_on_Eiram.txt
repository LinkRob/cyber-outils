The '''escape on Eiram''' was an escape plan carried out by the [[Drengir]] after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon]] to escape the [[Blight]]. The Drengir's original plan was to abduct [[Eiram]] [[Eirami|citicens]] and force them to build a [[starship]], but the Drengir were [[Incident on Eiram|stopped]] by the [[Jedi]] and the [[Republic Defense Coalition]].<ref name="Seeds of Starlight" /> Following those events, the [[Great Progenitor]] took the stage and devised a new plan to overgrow [[Barraza City]] with a [[Drengir seed pod|seed pod]] and to eventually lift the entire structure into the [[atmosphere]] by filling the pod with femented [[gas]]. The Jedi then [[Battle in Arovia park|confronted]] the Great Progenitor and suggested an alliance, offering her and her Drengir the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] research vessel ''[[Innovator]]'' to safely escape Eiram and fly to the uninhabited [[planet]] [[Floris]]. The Drengir accepted the offer, but before they could escape, a rogue Drengir [[Saxl Neth's army|faction]] lead by [[Saxl Neth]] attacked the Jedi and the Drengir. They were quickly defeated and the Drengir boarded the ''Innovator'' to escape to Floris. However, against their agreemant, the Drengir headed for the [[Wookiee]] homeworld [[Kashyyyk]] instead.<ref name="Haunted Starlight" />
