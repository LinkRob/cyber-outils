The '''Freetown Marshal's office''' was the law enforcement department that operated in the town of the planet [[Tatooine]] [[Freetown]] and [[Mos Pelgo territories]]. During the [[New Republic Era]], the agency was headed by the [[Marshal]] [[Cobb Vanth]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> and his right-hand man, [[Deputy]] [[Scott]].<ref name="C6FTDCAS">{{BOBF|Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger}}</ref>
