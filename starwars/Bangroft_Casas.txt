'''Bangroft Casas''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[ensign]] in the [[Galactic Empire]], who was stationed the [[tibanna]] [[gas]] [[mining]] [[Space station|station]] above the [[planet]] [[Pandem Nai]]. When [[Colonel]] [[Shakara Nuress]] of the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] took control of operations on the [[planet]], she chose Casas to be her [[pilot]] rather than waste the time of one from the 204th. One [[Standard day|day]], the ensign flew the Colonel from the settlement [[Induchron]], confirming their destination to be [[Orbital One]] and relaying news from the station on the arrival of the 204th's [[Squadron Four (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Four]]. After their [[shuttle]] reached the station's [[hangar]] Nuress left Casas to tend to the vessel.

==Biography==
===Shuttle pilot===
{{Quote|Colonel? Incoming message from Orbital One. Squadron Four has returned. They report no losses.''"<br />"''It can wait till I arrive.|Casas and Shakara Nuress|<ref name="Alphabet" />}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Bangroft Casas<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> was assigned to the [[tibanna]] [[gas]] [[mining]] [[Space station|station]] [[Orbital One]], located in the [[orbit]] of the [[planet]] [[Pandem Nai]]. There, he served as an [[ensign]] and a [[pilot]]. Several [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Alphabet" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], led by [[Colonel]] [[Shakara Nuress]], gained control of Pandem Nai and its orbital stations to establish a base of operations to restock and resupply [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]].<ref name="Alphabet" />

[[Colonel]] [[Shakara Nuress]], the 204th's [[commanding officer]], chose Casas fly her around for appearance purposes aboard a [[Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle|''Zeta''-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle]]. The colonel chose the ensign wishing not to waste the time of her [[fighter wing]]'s pilots. When Casas flew Nuress out of [[Induchron]], a settlement on Pandem Nai, one [[Standard day|day]], he asked if she wanted to return to Orbital One. The Colonel, who sat in the co-pilot's seat, told the pilot to take the long route to the station, specifying the route when Casas began to ask what she meant. The ensign acknowledged her details and proceeded to fly the [[shuttle]] along the route.<ref name="Alphabet" />

When a message reached the shuttle from Orbital One, reporting the return of the 204th's [[Squadron Four (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Four]] from a mission. Casas informed Nuress, adding that the squadron had taken no losses, but the colonel told the pilot that the news could wait. Some more time passed until the vessel reached the top of the [[atmosphere]], where the ensign notified the colonel of their descent towards the mining stations, asking her if she wanted to pilot the starship herself. Nuress declined, letting Casas fly the shuttle into Orbital One himself. One the vessel landed, the colonel left him to tend to the starship and traveled to her quarters.<ref name="Alphabet" />

===TIE duty===
Sometime after, the [[New Republic]]'s [[Alphabet Squadron]] [[Battle of Pandem Nai|attacked]] the 204th at Pandem Nai. Nuress was killed during the battle, with many of the fighter wing fleeing the [[Pandem Nai system|system]] as their base of operations was devastated. Casas traveled with the 204th, and was promoted to [[TIE series|TIE]] duty.<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> Around [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY" /> the fighter wing [[Attack on Cerberon|attacked]] New Republic [[Barma Battle Group|forces]], Alphabet [[Squadron]] among them, at the [[Cerberon system]], decimating the enemy [[fleet]]. As the 204th's [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] attacked New Republic forces on the planet [[Troithe]] after, Casas was killed in the fighting. Upon hearing of this news, Colonel [[Soran Keize]] reflected on the ensign's death, and remembered that he had meant to meet with every new addition to the 204th, but had lost track of time.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Descending now, Colonel. Would you, ah, like to do it yourself?''"<br />"''No need. I'm content to enjoy the view.|Casas and Shakara Nuress|<ref name="Alphabet" />}}
A [[human]]<ref name="USW" /> male ensign, Casas' had the impression of an errand boy to Shakara Nuress, who found his record to be unexceptional. The pilot was sure to inquire directives from the colonel and report any incoming news to her, even offering for her to fly the shuttle herself.<ref name="Alphabet" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bangroft Casas first appeared in the [[2019]] novel [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Alphabet" /> His first name, Bangroft, was identified in ''Alpahabet Squadron''{{'s}} sequel, ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', written by Freed and published in [[2020]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
