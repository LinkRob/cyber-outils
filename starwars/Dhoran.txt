'''Dhoran''' was a [[Wookiee]] [[Gender|male]] who was the lover of [[Oga Garra]], the [[Crime lord|crime boss]] of [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]. Garra caught Dhoran with one of her [[Waiter|waitresses]], [[Meeba]], and ordered him to leave the [[planet]] under the threat of [[death]]. When Dhoran dismissed Garra's demand, the crime boss shot and killed him.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />
