An '''Agamar gunslinger belt''' was a type of [[belt]] that was made on<ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[Agamar]]. It was designed to be simple as to not limit a being's mobility during a [[blaster]] fight. The [[Color|black]] belt connected in the front by large oval buckle along side a space to store two [[blaster module]]s. A [[holster]] that could carry a [[blaster pistol]] was attacked to the right side of the belt. On its back, it had an attached pouch as well as space to store [[bacta vial]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Twi'lek]] [[merchant]] [[Lalini Ledeno]] had at least one Agamar gunslinger belt while he operated from an alley in the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the Outer Rim [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] learned the "I'm a friend of Arlo" password that Ledeno used to restrict access to his [[black market]] shop. She later used the password to gain access to Ledeno's stock and purchase the belt for 700 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
An Agamar gunslinger belt first appeared in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Prior to the game's release on [[August 30]], [[2024]],<ref name="Story trailer">{{SWYouTube|DUyk6D5rgcE|Star Wars Outlaws: Official Story Trailer}}</ref> the belt was shown in the game's "Behind The Scenes &ndash; Navigating the Underworld" trailer released on [[July 25]], 2024.<ref name="Navigating the Underworld">{{UbisoftYouTube|BxyqrPk51KM|Star Wars Outlaws: Behind The Scenes &ndash; Navigating the Underworld}}</ref> In the game, the belt would restore the player's health when they defeat enemies using the "[[Adrenaline]] Rush" ability.<ref name="Outlaws" />
