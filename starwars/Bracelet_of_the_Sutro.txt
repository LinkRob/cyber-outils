The '''Bracelet of the Sutro''' was a metallic [[bracelet]] that was owned by the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> Around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], she wore the bracelet on the [[Standard day|day]] that the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] came to visit her in her [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]]. She wore the bracelet on the wrist of her right [[Limb|arm]].<ref name="VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The bracelet first appeared as a costume prop in the film {{film|VII}} which was released in [[2015]]. It was never identified in the movie, but received a name in ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released the day after the film.
