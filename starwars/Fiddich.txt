'''Fiddich''', also known as '''Patchwork Fiddich''', was a [[Gender|male]] [[pirate]] who served under Captains [[Carabba]] and [[Gunda Mabin]] as part of the [[Blazing Claw]]s. He attempted to lead a mutiny against Mabin during her search for [[Maz Kanata]]'s treasures but was killed by her [[quickfire]]. His corpse was mounted to a forward [[cannon]] of the ''[[Bloody Bones]]'' and disintegrated when the ship jumped to [[hyperspace]].
