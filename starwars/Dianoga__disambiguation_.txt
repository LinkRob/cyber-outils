A "'''[[Dianoga/Legends|dianoga]]'''" was a large, tentacled creature originally from the planet [[Vodran/Legends|Vodran]].

There were also several things named after the dianoga:

===Starships===
*'''[[Dianoga (starship)|''Dianoga'']]''', an attack ship operated by the space pirate [[Gir Kybo Ren-Cha]].
*'''[[Dianoga-class assault starfighter|''Dianoga''-class assault starfighter]]''', a starfighter than loosely resembled the creature.

===Food and beverages===
*'''[[Dianoga cheese]]''', a form of cheese that shared the name.
*'''[[Dianoga cream]]''', a type of cream used in [[dianoga cream-filled donut]]s.
*'''[[Dianoga pie]]''', a meat pie filled with dianoga meat.
*'''[[Dianogan tea/Legends|Dianogan tea]]''', a hot beverage derived from chemicals from a dianoga spleen.

===Other uses===
*'''[[9D9-s54 Dianoga spy droid]]''', a model of [[spy droid]].
*'''[[Azure Dianoga Cantina]]''', a bar located on [[Coruscant/Legends|Imperial Center]].
*'''[[Dargul Dianogas]]''', a [[Shockball/Legends|shockball]] team from the planet [[Dargul]].
