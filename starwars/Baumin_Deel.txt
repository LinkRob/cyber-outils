'''Baumin Deel''' was a [[human]] individual who served as a [[New Republic]] [[spy]] within the [[Kenner Loring's faction|Imperial remnant]] led by [[Grand General]] [[Kenner Loring]]. Deel spied on Loring as the Grand General spoke with [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] regarding a potential alliance between their forces. Following the end of the transmission, however, Loring exposed Deel as a traitor. Deel managed to send a final transmission to [[Agent]] [[Calder Behrens]] before he was shot down by one of Loring's [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Republic Under Siege 1" />
