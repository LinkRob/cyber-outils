The '''Challenge of the Body''' was one of the three challenges that an [[Alderaanian]] [[Prince|heir]] had to undertake to prove their worthiness to be formally declared heir to the [[Alderaan]]ian [[throne]], along with the challenges of the [[Challenge of the Mind|Mind]] and [[Challenge of the Heart|Heart]] which were announced at the heir's [[Day of Demand]]. Traditionally, the heir climbed [[Appenza Peak]], a famous [[mountain]] visible from the [[capital]] [[Aldera]], solo, although they could choose to bring along one companion.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

When she was heir to the Alderaanian throne, [[Breha Organa]] was severely injured in a [[The accident|fall]] while completing her Challenge of the Body. She would have [[Death|died]] if not for the royal guards supervising from a distance. This led to the replacement of her [[heart]] and [[lung]]s with [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] [[pulmonodes]]. Breha's daughter [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], for her Challenge of the Body, asked her [[Parent|mother]] to accompany her on her climb.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

Years later while on the planet [[Madurs]] during her honeymoon, Leia remembered her mother's fall as Leia recognised that the more she was celebrated the more people would seek her flaws, exploit them and use them to topple her image.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>
