'''Detonators''' were remote control devices that could trigger explosives. As the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] hijacked the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Renown]]'', the detonator is connected to hundreds of [[rhydonium]] canisters in the [[hangar]] to [[Carida incident|blow up]] the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Republic Navy|fleet]], even the [[space station]] [[Valor (space station)|''Valor'']] the site of the [[Republic strategy conference]] in the [[Carida system]].<ref name="Point of No Return">{{TCW|Point of No Return (The Clone Wars)}}</ref> The [[Free Ryloth Movement]] had detonators that could be attached to bombs that would rig them to explode.<ref name="Homecoming">{{Rebels|Homecoming}}</ref> During [[Beckett's gang]] [[Heist on Vandor|attempt to steal]] [[coaxium]] on [[Vandor]], [[Val]] used a detonator to trigger a bomb rigged to destroy a bridge.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> The rebel weapons expert [[Sabine Wren]] carried detonators in her pockets to trigger charges she had planted elsewhere.<ref name="Art Attack">{{Rebels|Art Attack}}</ref> The [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Inquisitor]] known as the [[Eighth Brother]] utilized [[S-Mine Detonator]]s.<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref>
