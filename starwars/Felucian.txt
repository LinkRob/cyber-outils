'''Felucians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the plains and [[grass]]lands of the [[planet]] [[Felucia]]. At the time of the [[Clone Wars]], many Felucians,<ref name="VE" /> including [[Casiss Midagatis]],<ref name="TCW CE">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> were peaceful [[farmer]]s, growing [[nysillin]] and other crops.<ref name="VE" />
