'''D.C. Barns''' is a [[Wikipedia:Denver|Denver]], [[Wikipedia:Colorado|Colorado]] resident who won the ''Star Wars: Force for Change'' fundraising competition, earning himself a role in the film ''[[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens]]'', released on [[December 18]], [[2015]]. He played a "[[Unidentified bar patron (Takodana Castle) 2|Bar Patron]]" in the film.
