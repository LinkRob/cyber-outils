'''Dagobah''' was a remote, [[Scout|unexplored]] [[planet]] in the [[Dagobah system]], and one of the purest places in [[the galaxy]] within [[the Force]]. A remote world of [[swamp]]s and [[forest]]s, it served as a refuge for [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] during his [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] after the [[Order 66|destruction]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. [[Luke Skywalker]] received advanced [[Jedi training|training]] in the ways of the Force under [[Jedi Master]] Yoda on Dagobah, and it was later the place of Yoda's [[death]] and [[Force spirit|transformation into the Force]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Yes, I'm sure it's perfectly safe for droids.|Luke Skywalker, to [[R2-D2]]|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
[[File:Dragonsnake_Bog.jpg|thumb|left|200px|The swamp-covered surface of Dagobah]]
Dagobah was a [[planet]] located in the [[Sluis sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> It was a [[Temperature|hot]] and humid world that was a hotbed of life,<ref name="VE" /> with the [[Tash]] [[species]]<ref name="Galaxy's Edge" /> and numerous creatures&mdash;such as [[bogwing]]s, [[dragonsnake]]s, [[butcherbug]]s, [[sleen]]s, [[vine snake]]s, and [[swamp slug]]s<ref name="SWCL" />&mdash;living on world. [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] would also make a home for himself on Dagobah during his [[Exile (punishment)|exile]].<ref name="Episode V" /> Dagobah was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, [[swamp]]y [[planet]], mostly covered in [[Dragonsnake Bog|swamps and bogs]], interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple [[Gnarltree|living caves]]. The continental and oceanic crusts of Dagobah were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of [[Volcano|volcanic]] activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the [[lahdia plant]], the [[yoghurt plant]], [[gnarltree]], and [[yarum seed]]. The planet was devoid of any cities.<ref name="SWCL" />

Among the purest locations in [[The galaxy|the entire galaxy]] within the energy field known as [[the Force]],<ref name="Voices">{{TCW|Voices}}</ref> it shared a mysterious [[time]]-related quality with such locales as [[Mortis]] and [[Ahch-To]].<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The [[Cave of Evil]] was a [[vergence]] aligned with the [[dark side of the Force]] on Dagobah.<ref name="Vergence short story">{{StoryCite|story=Vergence (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> The Cave was able to expand its influence across Dagobah until Yoda's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the [[light side of the Force]] that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the [[Imperial Era]], Dagobah's ecosystem saw balance until Yoda's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Dagobah once more.<ref name="Light Falls">{{StoryCite|story=The Light That Falls|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref>

Dagobah had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more.<ref name="There is Another" />

==History==
===Republic Era===
{{Quote|But again, research. And in an academic paper written during ''the High Republic'', I found a description of a world very strong in the Force.|The Archivist|<ref name="CR3">[[Crimson Reign 3|''Crimson Reign'' 3]]</ref>}}
During the [[High Republic Era]], an [[Unidentified academic paper|academic paper]] was written that described Dagobah as a world strong in the Force.<ref name="CR3" />

[[File:Dagobah-Voices.png|thumb|right|250px|Yoda traveled to Dagobah in 19 BBY at the behest of Qui-Gon Jinn.]]
During the [[Republic Era]], [[Halka Four-Den]] [[Halka Four-Den's Alpha Team|Alpha]] [[Alpha Team|Team]] was dispatched to the [[planet]] Dagobah in order to chart and map the extending borders of the [[Galactic Republic]] and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three [[Standard month|months]], Four-Den and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing [[plant]]s. No trace of the team was discovered.<ref name="BMF 20" />

Near the end of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] traveled to the planet Dagobah after hearing the voice of deceased [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]. He ventured into the [[swamp]], where he communed with Jinn, who appeared manifested as a group of fireflies. He told Yoda of how to communicate with the living after death through the connection between the Living Force and the Cosmic Force. Then, Yoda entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Jedi fighting the [[Clone trooper|clones]] and then the mysterious [[Sith Lord]].<ref name="Voices" />

===Imperial Era===
====Yoda's exile====
{{Quote|Into exile I must go. Failed, I have.|Yoda, to Bail Organa|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:YodaFingerSpark-UP.png|thumb|left|200px|Yoda lived on the planet Dagobah for the rest of his life.]]
After his failure to defeat the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] following the [[Declaration of a New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Yoda [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] himself<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> to Dagobah.<ref name="Episode V" /> Using an [[E3-standard starship lifeboat]], he began his exile, where he continued his studies of the Force. The lifeboat served as Yoda's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Dagobah's [[swamp]]y environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Yoda built a new [[Yoda's Hut|home]] for himself.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> He positioned it near to the dark side cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years.<ref name="FACPOV2">{{StoryCite|story=Vergence (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> However, the Dagobah wet season created a need for the Jedi Master to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.<ref name="FACPOV">{{StoryCite|story=There is Another|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>

At some point, former [[Sava]] [[The Archivist|Madelin Sun]] arrived on Dagobah in search of Yoda. After saving her from a [[Unidentified Dagobah creature|monster]], Yoda used the Force to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Yoda watched from afar.<ref name="CR3" />

====Training ground of Luke Skywalker====
{{Quote|You will go to the Dagobah system. There you will learn from Yoda, the Jedi Master who instructed me.|Obi-Wan Kenobi, to Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
[[File:Yodalukedagobah.png|thumb|right|250px|Traveling to Dagobah, Skywalker sought out Grand Master Yoda in order to complete his Jedi training.]]
[[Standard year|Years]] later, [[Luke Skywalker]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] traveled to Dagobah under the direction of late [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in hopes of finding Yoda and being trained in the ways of the [[Jedi Order]]. Arriving with his astromech [[R2-D2]], Skywalker crashed into a bog upon landing and happened upon the Jedi, though he was unaware it was, in fact, Yoda at the time. The old master took Skywalker back to his [[Yoda's Hut|hut]] for dinner, where he revealed his true identity to Skywalker. Initially reluctant to train Skywalker due to his age and lack of patience, Yoda was eventually convinced to teach him by the [[Force spirit]] of Kenobi.<ref name="Episode V" />

One night during Skywalker's training, an Imperial [[probe droid]] from [[Project Swarm]] arrived on planet. The droid's operator, [[Maela]], initially believed there was no reason to look further into the planet during the unit's patrol until she spotted<ref name="Eyes">{{StoryCite|story=Eyes of the Empire|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> Skywalker's X-wing in the swamp.<ref name="Episode V" /> Although she realized the outline of what she saw resembled an X-wing, she had realized the cruelty of the Empire after witnessing the aftermath of the [[Battle of Hoth]], so she deleted the footage before moving on to her next droid.<ref name="Eyes" />

During his training, Skywalker entered the [[Cave of Evil]] and [[Duel on Dagobah (3 ABY)|confronted]] a vision of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who he struck down, only to see his own face in Vader's [[Darth Vader's armor|mask]]. Later, Skywalker ended his training early, however, once he sensed his friends [[Han Solo]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] were in danger. Although Yoda and Kenobi attempted to reason with Skywalker to stay, the student left the planet, promising to return and finish his training.<ref name="Episode V" />

He would keep his word, and after learning Vader was his father during their [[duel on Cloud City]], saving Leia and Chewbacca from Vader on [[Cloud City]], and rescuing Solo from [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] on [[Tatooine]], he would return to Dagobah to complete his Jedi training. Upon arriving, he found Yoda sick and dying, who told him that his training was complete, but all he had to do was confront Vader one last time, and the old master became [[Force spirit|one with the Force]]. The ghost of Kenobi then revealed to Skywalker the truth that Leia was, in fact, a Skywalker and Luke's sister before Luke left the planet to confront Vader and the Emperor in hopes of turning Vader back to the [[Light side of the Force|light side]].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> The Cave of Evil, meanwhile, took advantage of Yoda's death to once again spread its power across Dagobah.<ref name="Light Falls" />

===New Republic Era===
{{Quote|The cave is power. And darkness. And you will enter.''"<br />"''What's in there?''"<br />"''Only what you've been too weak to bury.|Snoke and Kylo Ren|<ref name="Age of Resistance Snoke">[[Age of Resistance - Supreme Leader Snoke 1|''Age of Resistance - Supreme Leader Snoke'' 1]]</ref>}}
[[File:KyloVsVisionLuke-AORSnoke1.jpg|thumb|left|Ren dueling the vision of Skywalker.]]
Sometime after the [[skirmish on the Minemoon]], [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], nephew of Luke Skywalker, along with his master and [[Supreme Leader]] of the [[First Order]], [[Snoke]], came to Dagobah in order to face Ren's darkest fears. In the Cave of Evil, he experienced a vision of his uncle Luke, whom he dueled and killed, and a vision of his parents Leia and Han. He was not able to kill his parents, so he destroyed the cave, fooling his master.<ref name="Age of Resistance Snoke">[[Age of Resistance - Supreme Leader Snoke 1|''Age of Resistance - Supreme Leader Snoke'' 1]]</ref>

By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> herbs from Dagobah had made their way to [[Oga's Cantina]] on [[Batuu]], where they became a popular ingredient in the so-called [[Dagobah Slug Slinger]] alcoholic beverage.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge" /> During or after 34 ABY,<ref name="Date">"[[Who's Making That Noise in The Millennium Falcon?!]]" takes place after the events of {{Film|VIII}}, dated to [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[freighter]] ''Millennium Falcon'' landed on Dagobah. There, the [[Wookiee]] crewmember [[Chewbacca]] attempted to take some time to himself to rest aboard the ship, only for the droids [[BB-8]] and R2-D2<ref name="Noise">{{DroidDiaries|Who's Making That Noise in The Millennium Falcon?!}}</ref>&mdash;the very same droid who had accompanied Luke Skywalker to Dagobah<ref name="Episode V" />&mdash;discovered a [[Unidentified Kowakian monkey-lizard|Kowakian monkey-lizard]] aboard the ship.<ref name="Noise" />

==Inhabitants==
Dagobah was the homeworld of the Tash species, who grew herbs.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge" /> Jedi Grand Master Yoda made his way to Dagobah and adopted it<ref name="FACPOV" /> as his homeworld during the Imperial Era.<ref name="Episode V" /> Another being of sorts on Dagobah was the so-called Cave of Evil, a living Force vergence<ref name="FACPOV2" /> strong in the dark side,<ref name="Episode V" /> but confined to its place on the planet.<ref name="FACPOV2" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|A small escape pod hurls toward the swamp planet and disappears in the mist. The pod has landed, and YODA descends the ramp, surveying the unfamiliar terrain.|Description of Dagobah's deleted scene from Episode III|<ref name="Illustrated">[[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith Illustrated Screenplay|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith Illustrated Screenplay'']]</ref>}}
Dagobah first appeared in the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]].<ref name="Episode V" />

Slated to appear in one scene of the [[2005]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], the planet was cut from the final film, leaving it for the DVD release. The scene featured how Yoda started his exile. [[George Lucas]] removed the scene so that the film would not have "too many endings," despite producer [[Rick McCallum]]'s wishes.

In the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], an image of Dagobah was mistakenly used in the planet profile of [[Kashyyyk]]. The book also alleges that no civilization existed on Dagobah,<ref name="SWCL" /> but ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge]]'' later recanonized the Tash species.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge" />

The [[Kwazel Maw]] seen in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Bombad Jedi]]" was based off of an unused design by [[Ralph McQuarrie]] for a [[swamp]] [[slug]] creature on Dagobah.<ref name="Bombad Jedi Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/bombad-jedi-episode-guide|text="Bombad Jedi" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

There is a long-running fan debate about how long Luke Skywalker trained on Dagobah.<ref name="20 Easter Eggs">{{SW|url=news/lego-star-wars-terrifying-tales-easter-eggs|text=20 Eerie Easter Eggs from ''LEGO Star Wars Terrifying Tales''}}</ref> This debate was jokingly referenced in "The Lost Boy," a story told by [[Vaneé]], when Ben Solo asked if Skywalker trained for 20 [[Standard minute|minute]]s on Dagobah.<ref name="Terrifying Tales">''[[LEGO Star Wars Terrifying Tales]]''</ref>

Lucas' vision for Dagobah started out as a swampy and misty planet that would be similar to the setting of the Sherlock Holmes novel [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hound_of_the_Baskervilles The Hound of the Baskervilles], which was in Dartmoor, England.<ref name="Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back: The 40th Anniversary Special Edition">[[Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back: The 40th Anniversary Special Edition|''Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back'': The 40th Anniversary Special Edition]]</ref>
