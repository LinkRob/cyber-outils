'''Binogs''' were a [[species]] of [[amphi-mammalian]]s [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Bogano]]. During the [[Republic Era]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]] traveled to the planet and observed artistic depictions of a binog created by the ancient [[Zeffonian]] species. In [[14 BBY]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] visited the planet and observed a binog, taking notice of the various depictions of the creature. [[Standard year|Years]] later, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] visited Bogano in order to look for a suitable location for a base, encountering several binogs during their stay.

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Someone made camp here. They were excited to discover a giant creature that lives close by&hellip; A binog, they called it.|Cal Kestis upon discovering the camp of Jedi Master Eno Cordova|<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />}}
[[Homeworld|Native]] to<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Bogano">{{Databank|bogano|Bogano}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Bogano]], binogs were [[Reptile|reptilian]] and [[amphi-mammalian]]s&mdash;a type of [[Species|creature]] that exhibited the traits of both [[amphibian]]s and [[mammal]]s. They had a lifespan of several thousand [[Standard year|years]] and would only [[Reproduction|breed]] once during their lifetime.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

Binogs had a long, slender body that was useful for slithering in and out of the various holes of Bogano's landscape.<ref name="The Art of Fallen Order">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> They had [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]] and black [[eye]]s. A set of light-blue fins lined the amphi-mammalian's back and its long tail, ending with a tail fin. Each of its six [[Limb|legs]] had short webbed feet.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==Behavior==
{{Quote|That creature seems so at home here.''"<br />"''Boo bee boop bee beep.''"<br />"''Protector of Bogano? I thought that was you.|Cal Kestis, to BD-1, after commanding him to scan a binog|<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />}}
Binogs spent most of their [[time]] lying down and resting. When sensing danger, Binogs moved to a further resting spot.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==History==
{{Quote|I think we should go this way.''"<br />"''What, and risk waking up that sleeping binog? Why?|Luke Skywalker and Leia Organa|<ref name="Part 1">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=7|story=The Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 1}}</ref>}}
Due to their scarcity of offspring, very few binogs were left in [[the galaxy]] during the [[Republic Era]]. The ancient [[Zeffonian]] species, who had visited Bogano, created various artistic depictions of the creature. One such depiction included a mural in the [[Binog Mesa]] region of the planet, which shared its name with the binogs.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> Around [[1014 BBY]],<ref name="BinogSkeletonDate">In ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', the fossilized binog on [[Bogano]] was stated to have [[Death|died]] around 1000 [[Standard year|years]] before the events of the [[Star Wars video games|game]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[14 BBY]]. Therefore, the binog must have died around [[1014 BBY]].</ref> a binog was [[Death|killed]] near the [[Bogdo Sinkholes]], and years later, its [[skeleton]] had been encapsulated within the [[limestone]] structure of the planet. [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]] traveled to Bogano in that era, where he set up a [[Abandoned Workshop|workshop]] and studied the technology crafted by the Zeffo. Cordova also took time to observe the native wildlife, such as the binog. The Jedi Master observed the Zeffo depictions and hypothesized that the creature was a key factor in the Zeffo's decision to create a [[Bogano Vault|vault]] on the planet.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> Years later, in [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFO Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] journeyed to Bogano on his [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|quest to restore]] the [[Jedi Order]]. While in the [[Subterranean Refuge]], as Kestis remembered the [[Force power|force ability]] of wall running, a binog watched from above.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
While on Bogano, Kestis collected Cordova's observations about the creature. Kestis encountered the binog who watched him ealier residing in the Binog Mesa, and instructed his [[droid]] [[BD-1]] to scan the creature. He also found a binog mural made by the Zeffo. After encountering the fossilized binog, the Jedi had BD-1 scan it.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
{{EndGame}}

[[File:BinogSavesSkywalkerTwins.png|thumb|right|250px|A binog using its tail to whack away a stormtrooper who was about to kill Luke Skywalker and Leia Organa]]
Sometime between [[0 ABY]] and [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[The Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 1]]" take place between the events of {{Film|IV}} and {{Film|V}}, which corresponds to between [[0 ABY]] and [[3 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''.</ref> Jedi-in-[[Jedi training|training]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] undertook a [[Mission to Bogano (Galactic Civil War)|mission]] to Bogano in order to find a potential site for a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] base. While on the planet, Organa warned Skywalker not to go in the direction of a nearby binog out of fear of waking it from its [[sleep]].<ref name="Part 1" /> Later, when they were attacked by a group of [[stormtrooper]]s, a binog saved them from a [[Unidentified stormtrooper (Bogano)|trooper]] who was going to shoot them.<ref name="Part 2">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=8|story=The Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 2}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Binog-JFO.png|thumb|right|200px|Amy Fry's concept art of the binog]]
Binogs first appeared in the [[2019]] video game ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]]. One binog can be seen resting in the background on Bogano. When the [[Galactic Empire]] arrives on Bogano, the binog moves further away from the mainland, but it returns to a close position after the final mission has been completed.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

Concept art of the creature was created by [[Amy Fry]]. The binog skeleton was created as an intentional homage to the [[krayt dragon]] skeleton seen in the<ref name="The Art of Fallen Order" /> [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film,<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. The skeleton was implemented to convey the idea that nature is still dangerous even without the influence of the [[dark side of the Force]].<ref name="The Art of Fallen Order" />
