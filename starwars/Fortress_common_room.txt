The '''fortress common room'''<ref name="Destiny AD" /> was a room within the [[Brendok fortress]] used by the [[Brendok coven|coven]] of [[witch]]es led by [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Aniseya]] on the [[planet]] [[Brendok]]. It contained a semi-circle of stepped seating<ref name="Destiny" /> and was ajoined to the annex that housed the structure's [[power generator]].<ref name="Destiny AD" />
