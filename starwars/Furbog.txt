'''Furbogs''' were a trading commodity. The [[Besalisk/Legends|Besalisk]] cook [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster]] traded furbogs during his time as a trader along the [[Corellian Run/Legends|Corellian Run]], where he traded them for several unusual objects including [[Hyperdrive/Legends|hyperdrive]] attenuators, [[Sullustan gab-ripper]]s, [[silicon-pronged tack trim]] and a large quantity of [[Zoochberry/Legends|zoochberries]]. One furbog was worth up to ten times its weight in zoochberries.
