'''Dorvalla''' was a celestial body<ref name="Inbrief" /> in the [[Videnda sector]]'s [[Dorvalla system]],<ref name="Systems" /> within the [[Western Reaches]] portion<ref name="Western Reaches" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Systems" /> It shared its name with the [[Dorvallan necklace]].<ref name="Sanctuary" /> Between [[2 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">{{InbriefDate}}</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] took in a number of recruits from Dorvalla. The [[company]]'s [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]] soon noticed that the [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] [[Brand]], who was secretly a [[bounty hunter]], lingered around those new recruits. Evon cited this when Brand attempted to take a [[bounty]] on the captain during a [[Battle of Allst Prime|battle]] on the [[planet]] [[Allst Prime]]. He mentioned his point about the recruits from Dorvalla while trying to convince Brand to stay with the 61st.<ref name="Inbrief" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dorvalla was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the short story "[[Inbrief]]," written by [[Janine K. Spendlove]] and first published in [[Star Wars Insider 161|''Star Wars Insider'' 161]]<ref name="Inbrief" /> on [[November 10]], [[2015]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanMagazines|url=t/star-wars-insider/us/161|text=Star Wars Insider Issue #161}}</ref> The location was originally first mentioned in the [[1996]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] guide ''[[Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook]]'', which was written by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]],<ref name="SES">''[[Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook]]''</ref> and later made its first appearance in the [[2001]] short story ''[[Darth Maul: Saboteur]]'', written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="DMS">"[[Darth Maul: Saboteur]]"</ref>
