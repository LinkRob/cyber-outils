The '''Emperor's Royal Guard''', also named the '''Imperial Royal Guard''' and '''Imperial Guard''', alternatively identified as the '''Crimson Guard''', and known under the [[Galactic Republic]] as the '''[[Red Guard]]''', were the personal [[bodyguard]]s to the [[Galactic Emperor]]. In addition to strict requirements of size, strength, intelligence, and loyalty, only the most skilled [[soldier]]s in the [[Imperial Military]] qualified for duty in the Royal Guard. The Imperial Royal Guard were also occasionally assigned to provide protection to the [[Emperor's Fist]], [[Darth Vader]], and prominent members of the [[Imperial Ruling Council]], such as [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]].

The origins of the crimson-clad Royal Guard dated back to the [[time]] of the [[Separatist Crisis]], when the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], in his public guise as [[Supreme Chancellor]] Sheev Palpatine, crafted an order of protectors, named the Red Guard, for the [[Office of the Chancellor]], that stood apart from the cerulean-clad [[Senate Guard]] during the Galactic Republic's [[Republic Era|twilight]]. Over the course of the [[Clone Wars]], the Red Guard gradually supplanted the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] Guard and its elite sub-unit, the [[Senate Commando]]s, as the chancellor's bodyguards. By the end of the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars, Darth Sidious [[Proclamation of the New Order|proclaimed]] himself Emperor of the [[Galactic Empire]]. As a result, Senate Guards were completely phased out of service and replaced by the chancellor's Red Guards, who were henceforth known as the Royal Guard.

Darth Sidious sealed himself away from the populace throughout his [[Imperial Era|reign]]. On the rare occasions of his public appearances, he was always accompanied by members of the Royal Guard. In the [[Standard decade|decades]] that followed Sidious' [[death]] and the subsequent fall of the Empire at the [[Battle of Jakku]], a new order of protectors arose, the [[Praetorian Guard]] who wore ornate crimson armor, wielded an array of [[melee weapon]]s, and specialized in [[Unarmed combat|martial arts]], such as [[Teräs Käsi]]. Likewise, the reborn Darth Sidious revived his employment of crimson-clad guardsmen through the [[Sovereign Protector]]s, selected from the finest [[Sith trooper]]s the [[Sith Eternal]] had readied.

==Description==
[[File:Royal_Guard_RO.png|thumb|left|200px|Royal Guards wore red robes and armor, and were armed with force pikes.]]
The [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor's]] Royal [[Bodyguard|Guards]] were reputedly the most skilled warriors in the [[Imperial Military|forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]; out of millions of [[soldier]]s, [[Darth Sidious]]' guards were unsurpassed in skill and status.<ref name="Lando 3" /> During the [[Imperial Era|era of the Empire]], the Royal Guard consisted of conscripts and at least one [[clone trooper]].<ref name="Lords of the Sith" /> The qualifications for joining the Guard's ranks included strength, intelligence, and loyalty.<ref name="SWEroyalguard">{{SWE|groups|emperors-royal-guard|Emperor's Royal Guard}}</ref> Candidates for the Guard had to meet the basic height requirement of 1.83 [[meter]]s<ref name="EmpRoyGuard" /> and were all [[human]]s.<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" />

In addition to receiving advanced training in many forms of combat, Royal Guards were trained to be unquestionably loyal to their Emperor, Darth Sidious. The Emperor's word was considered law and his guards would obey his orders without question or hesitation, even if it meant [[Death|killing]] one of their own. The Guards were also among the few beings in [[the galaxy]] whom the Emperor trusted with the knowledge of his secret identity as a [[Sith Lord]], knowing they would speak of it to no one, not even among themselves.<ref name="Lords of the Sith" /> Trainees would spar against each other in the [[The Squall]]. Their final test was a battle to the [[death]] before the Emperor.<ref name="FACPOV">{{StoryCite|story=The Emperor's Red Guards|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> Though their primary duty was to the Emperor, in some cases the Royal Guards were also at the disposal of his [[Emperor's Fist|right hand]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]],<ref name="Rogue One" /> who acted as the [[Imperial Military]]'s informal [[Commander-in-Chief]],<ref name="Bust Collection">{{BustCollectionCite|30|Star Wars Universe}}</ref> and [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]].<ref name="Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2020) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 1]]</ref> Other upper-level figures who made of use of the Royal Guards included [[General]] [[Jylia Shale]],<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> as well as [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]].<ref name="Life Debt" /> Due to their little movement, they were sometimes believed to have been [[droid]]s instead of individuals.<ref name="Rise" />

[[File:Royal-Guard-Alt.png|thumb|right|200px|A Royal Guard with another set of equipment.]]
Despite the combat skills of the Royal Guard, the Emperor was confident in his [[Force power]]s to the extent that he did not believe he required a bodyguard unit. However, he found over the course of his reign that the crimson-clad guards served well to impress and intimidate those who saw them. Additionally, they served the purpose of defending the Emperor in situations where he did not wish to use his Force powers.<ref name="Lords of the Sith" /> As such, he never felt inclined to dispense with them as a unit.<ref name="beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> Sidious also made use of a [[Royal Guard droid|droid model]] that resembled his Royal Guards, sending the droids to summon [[Politics|politicians]] he needed to meet with.<ref name="Aphra 31">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 31|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 31]]</ref>

==History==
===Under the Republic===
{{Quote|Troubling is the treachery of the Senate guard, Captain Argyus. Reveled all around us our enemies are.|Yoda, on Senate Commando Captain Argyus' betrayal, an act which Palpatine used to expand the role of his personal Red Guards.|<ref name="Cloak of Darkness">{{TCW|Cloak of Darkness}}</ref>}}
[[File:RoyalGuard_EpII.jpg|thumb|left|200px|During the fall of the Republic, Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine was protected by a cohort of crimson-clad guards, the Red Guard.]]
During the [[Republic Era|decline]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], as [[Supreme Chancellor]] Sheev Palpatine, created<ref name="SWEroyalguard" /> the Red Guard<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>&mdash;a cohort of personal elite bodyguards that stood apart from the rest of his forces, including the cerulean-clad members of the [[Senate Guard]],<ref name="SWEroyalguard" /> from which they were handpicked.<ref name="Rise" /> Over [[time]], Palpatine came to favor his crimson-clad red guards over the blue guards of the [[Galactic Senate]] and even the elite [[Senate Commando]]s.<ref name="SWEsenateguard">{{SWE|groups|senateguard|Senate Guard}}</ref> Armed with [[force pike]]s,<ref name="Episode II" /> the red guards' flowing red robes and sleek, featureless scarlet masks blended in with the crimson-walled [[Emperor's office|office]] of the chancellor.<ref name="SWEroyalguard" />

The red-robed guards were formed prior to the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Episode II" /> and were in place by [[28 BBY]],<ref name="Founding date" /> with the newly-appointed [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] noting that they were a much more intimidating security force than the Senate Guard,<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref> though Palpatine retained the service of the Senate Guard<ref name="TZBSB">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast Strikes Back}}</ref> and [[commando]]s during the early period of the pan-[[Galaxy|galactic]] conflict.<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> After [[Captain]] [[Faro Argyus]] of the Senate Commandos revealed himself to be a [[Treason|traitor]], the Senate Guard's loyalty was put into question, and Palpatine took the opportunity to expand the Red Guard in the ensuing scandal. 

[[File:PalpatineRedGuard-TWJ.png|thumb|right|250px|As the Clone Wars continued, Palpatine expanded the roles of his Red Guard, with them entirely replacing traditional Senate Guards as his personal protectors by the war's height.]]
When Red Guards were out of uniform, they conducted official visits and covert operations in locations where the Supreme Chancellor was scheduled to visit. The Red Guard also scouted traveling locations, back up routes, handled logistics, and created evacuation plans. On [[Coruscant]], such events may have required thousands of [[Standard hour|hours]] of work. The Guard also investigated credible threats against Palpatine, whether they be simply graffiti to [[HoloNet]] broadcasts.<ref name="Rise" />

By [[19 BBY]], approximately three [[Standard year|years]] after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the Red Guard and [[Coruscant Guard]] [[Imperial shock trooper|clone shock troopers]] had all but supplanted the Senate Guard as the chancellor's protectors.<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref>

===Serving the Emperor===
[[File:Emperor_Sidious_Guards_SWTDS.png|thumb|left|250px|On rare occasions the Emperor could be seen in public, but always in the presence of his Royal Guards.]]
At the War's [[Mission to Mustafar|end]]<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> in 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Atlas" /> Darth Sidious [[Proclamation of the New Order|appointed]] himself Galactic Emperor and reorganized the Republic into the Empire.<ref name="Episode III" /> Under the [[New Order|regime]], the Emperor continued to favor the Royal Guard, formerly the Chancellor's Red Guard, as his personal security force which, in turn, led to the dissolution of the Senate Guard.<ref name="EmpRoyGuard" /> During the first [[Standard day|days]] of Sidious' reign, the bodyguards were responsible for Emperor Palpatine's security on Coruscant. Offworld, however, Emperor Palpatine was accompanied by a detachment of shock [[trooper]]s of the Coruscant Guard.<ref name="Episode III" /> During the Clone Wars, Sidious also secretly controlled the [[Thyrsus Sun Guard]] [[mercenary]] group, which fought for the Republic during the conflict. The Sun Guard helped to inspire his Royal Guard as Galactic Emperor.<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref>

As the years passed, Sidious sealed himself away from the populace, having discarded his facade as a friendly kind-[[heart]]ed politician<ref name="SWEroyalguard" /> in order to embrace his true identity as a [[Sith Lord]].<ref name="EmpRoyGuard" /> On rare occasions he would make a public appearance, but always in the company of the Royal Guard.<ref name="SWEroyalguard" /> During the early years of the Empire, a division of the Royal Guard was stationed in the [[Inquisitorius Headquarters]]&mdash;a [[skyscraper]] located in the [[The Works|Industrial District]] of Coruscant&mdash;which served as a training facility for Emperor Palpatine's [[Jedi hunter]]s known as the [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]].<ref name="Dark Lord 6">[[Darth Vader (2017) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 6]]</ref> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas" /> two Royal Guards accomplished Darth Sidious on a [[Rho-class transport shuttle|''Rho''-class shuttle]] to [[Tantiss Base]], where the Emperor was checking on [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock]] progress on [[Project Necromancer]].<ref name="Shadows of Tantiss">{{TBB|Shadows of Tantiss}}</ref> Later several Royal Guards [[Mission to Ryloth|accompanied]] Darth Sidious and his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], Darth Vader, to [[Ryloth]] where the [[Lord]]s of the [[Sith]] sought to lure the [[Free Ryloth Movement]] out of hiding. Most of the guardsmen were killed when Vader's [[Vader's shuttle (Mission to Ryloth)|shuttle]] crash-landed in the planet's [[Ryloth's equatorial forest|equatorial forest]].<ref name="Lords of the Sith" />

[[File:Starwarsrebelsroyalguard.png|thumb|right|300px|A trio of light-armored Royal Guards served aboard the ''Chimaera''.]]
Two guards, [[Sergeant]] [[Erstin Deez]] and a [[Unidentified Captain of the Royal Guard|clone Captain]] who once served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] as a [[Cloning|clone]] trooper, survived the crash along with Vader and his [[Sith Master]], Sidious. The captain was later killed by [[lylek]]s, but Deez was able to survive long enough for [[Moff]] [[Delian Mors]]' stormtroopers to rescue the two Sith Lords.<ref name="Lords of the Sith" /> A trio of Royal Guards, without their robes, were stationed aboard the ''[[Chimaera]]'', [[flagship]] of the [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|7th Fleet]] led by Grand Admiral [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], during the [[Liberation of Lothal|Liberation]] of [[Lothal]]. Emperor Palpatine commanded the men to kill [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]], having failed to use the young [[Jedi]] in his plan to control the [[World Between Worlds]]. Before his hologram fades, Palpatine orders the guards to destroy him. Bridger attempt to fight the trio off with a [[Force push]], but two guards were resistant to it. Using modified [[pike]]s with [[magnetic clamp]]s to subdue their [[Force-sensitive]] [[Great Jedi Purge|target]], the guards held Bridger still while a [[squad]] of stormtroopers attempted to shoot him. However, Bridger used [[the Force]] to hurl several boulders and rocks from a stolen piece of the [[Lothal Jedi Temple]] at his attackers, crushing them.<ref name="Family&Farewell">{{Rebels|Family Reunion &ndash; and Farewell}}</ref>

Although the Emperor's safety was the primary responsibility of the Royal Guard,<ref name="EmpRoyGuard" /> Sidious did not make exclusive use of them, loaning them also to Vader. By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="ROdate">{{FilmDate|RO}}</ref> two Royal Guards were stationed in [[Fortress Vader]] on [[Mustafar]]. They acted as sentinels protecting Emperor Palpatine's [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], usually while he rested in his [[bacta tank]].<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Royal Guards were often used in battle as [[assassin]]s,<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> armed with force pikes, vibroblades, and many other concealed weapons.<ref name="Lords of the Sith" />

[[File:RoyalGuards-Lando2.png|thumb|left|250px|Stationed aboard the ''Imperialis'', two Royal Guards were corrupted by the Mask of Lord Momin.]]
Following the [[Battle of Yavin]], two Royal Guards were stationed aboard Emperor Palpatine's luxury [[yacht]] ''[[Imperialis]]'' while it was undergoing a refit at [[Sienar Fleet Systems]]' [[Orbital Shipyard CC-24]] above the planet [[Castell]].<ref name="Lando 2">[[Lando 2|''Lando'' 2]]</ref> They guarded the [[Starship|vessel's]] central chamber which stored several [[Sith artifact]]s including the [[mask of Lord Momin]].<ref name="Lando 4">[[Lando 4|''Lando'' 4]]</ref> After [[Baron Administrator]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and his [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian's crew|companions]] [[Theft of the Imperialis|stole]] the ''Imperialis'', one of the Royal Guards stabbed [[Lobot]] with a vibroblade,<ref name="Lando 2" /> but he survived. The guards were then killed by the [[alien]] clone warriors [[Aleksin]] and [[Pavol]]. [[Korin Pers]] discovered that the guards were corrupted by the [[dark side of the Force|Dark Side of the Force]], as they were guarding the Mask of [[Darth Momin|Lord Momin]].<ref name="Lando 3" /> In the same year, a special variant was in service of [[Commander]] [[Karbin]] featuring a distinctive look. These variants wore [[armor]] fitted for combat in the front lines on [[Vrogas Vas]].<ref name="Star Wars 14">[[Star Wars (2015) 14|''Star Wars'' (2015) 14]]</ref>

[[File:AssassinationPalpatinesRoyalGuard-CrimsonReign2.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A group of Royal Guards killed to send a message to Palpatine.]]
During the secret [[war against the Sith]]<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" /> between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Crimson Reign 2|''Crimson Reign'' 2]], including the assassination of the [[Emperor's Royal Guard]], take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> Jedi hunter [[Ochi]] was employed by [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]] of [[Crimson Dawn]] to assassinate members of the Royal Guard in order to cause disruption in the Empire. Ochi and a [[ASN courier droid]] named [[ASN-909]] travelled to Coruscant and bought [[poison]] that would activate at a specific time. Activating a [[cloaking device]], the [[courier droid]] snuck into [[Inquisitorius Headquarters]] at [[the Works]]; converted into the living quarters for the guards, and poisoned the guard's breakfast with [[Ziroxin-12]] but discovered that one of the Royal Guards, Erstin Deez was not present. Ochi took it upon himself to assassinate Deez with [[Yurfloo]] poison. Afterwards while the Royal Guards were with Emperor Palpatine at his throne room in the Imperial Palace, all of them dropped dead due to the poison.<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" />

[[File:CrimsonTIEInterceptor-HiddenEmpire4.png|thumb|left|250px|Royal Guard's TIE/IN interceptor.]]
Sometime after the deaths of those Royal Guards, two others stood behind Darth Sidious after he summoned Vader to the Imperial Palace as he concluded there was a new enemy of the Sith. When [[Director]] [[Sliro Barsha|Barsha]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] was summoned to the throne room, he revealed Crimson Dawn orchestrated the ongoing [[Syndicate War]]. As Vader and Sidious walked through the palace and discussed Crimson Dawn's influence over the other recent activities such as the betrayal of the [[Grand Hutt Council]], the theft of the [[Screaming Key]] from Vader’s Fortress by the [[Knights of Ren]], and the defeat of [[Commander]] [[Ellian Zahra]]’s [[Hunter Fleet]] and her inability to finally destroy the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], a force of Royal Guards accompanied them as protection. Stopping in the hall along with the two Sith Lords, the Royal Guards remained as Vader revealed that Qi'ra, during their [[Duel on the Vermillion|duel]] aboard on the Crimson Dawn flagship [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']], had combat training that reminded him of Sidious' techniques, leading both to conclude she was trained by his first apprentice, [[Maul|Darth Maul]]. In response, the two men ordered the Imperial Military to track down the ''Vermillion''.<ref name="Crimson Reign 5">[[Crimson Reign 5|''Crimson Reign'' 5]]</ref>

To finally destroy Crimson Dawn, Royal Guards later fought alongside [[death trooper]]s and the two Sith Lords in the [[Battle at Amaxine Station]].<ref name="Hidden Empire 4">[[Hidden Empire 4|''Hidden Empire'' 4]]</ref> 

[[File:Imperial-formation-Vader-kneels.png|thumb|right|300px|Royal Guards escorted Emperor Palpatine to the Death Star II, where their sovereign would die.]]
In 4 ABY,<ref name="Atlas" /> several Royal Guards accompanied Emperor Palpatine to the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] orbiting the [[Endor|forest moon]] of [[Endor (planet)|Endor]]. Prior to the arrival of the Rebel Alliance, Emperor Palpatine dismissed the Royal Guards, which included [[Tauk]] and [[Unidentified Red Guard (Death Star II)|another guard]],<ref name="FACPOV" /> from his [[Emperor's Throne Room|throne room]], leaving him alone with Vader and the latter's son [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]]. As a result, the Royal Guards were not present to protect Emperor Palpatine when his apprentice turned against him in a last-ditch effort to save his son's life. With the deaths of Palpatine and Vader, coupled with the victory of the Alliance, the [[Battle of Endor]] was a turning point that marked the beginning of the end for the Empire.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> When one of the dismissed guards learned of the Emperor's death, they betrayed the Empire, shooting and killing their fellow guard Tauk, before fleeing the Death Star.<ref name="FACPOV" />

Royal Guards were stationed at the [[Palpatine Archive]] in the [[Coruscant system]], and attempted to stop the thieving Calrissian and [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki|Jaxxon]], who were after the [[Totem of Saglanost]] shortly after the Empire's debacle at [[Endor system|Endor]].<ref name="Hare-Brained Heist">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|annual=2019|story=Hare-Brained Heist}}</ref>

===Decline===
[[File:Battle_of_Jakku.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A Royal Guard fought and died on Jakku, the site of the last battle of the Galactic Civil War.]]
The Royal Guard continued to serve the Empire, acting as bodyguards for several high-[[Military rank|ranking]] [[Imperial officer]]s such as [[General]] [[Jylia Shale]] and Grand [[Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]]. Two Royal Guards acted as Shale's security detail at the [[Akiva Summit|emergency summit]] on [[Akiva]];<ref name="Aftermath" /> another two Guards escorted Sloane to the [[New Republic]] capital [[Chandrila]]<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to 5 ABY.</ref> where the Grand Admiral sought to negotiate an end to hostilities between the Empire and the Republic. To Sloane's surprise, the Empire's overtures of peace were a ruse for launching a surprise [[attack on Chandrila]], orchestrated by [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]]. During the attack, Sloane's Royal Guards attacked and killed several [[Senate Guard (New Republic)|New Republic Senate Guards]] who escorted the Imperial entourage to the [[Imperial–Republic peace talks|peace summit]].<ref name="Life Debt" />

Shortly after the attack on Chandrila, the Empire rallied to the [[Desert|barren]] world of [[Jakku]] under Rax's leadership as [[Counselor to the Empire]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> One of the soldiers in Rax's [[army]] was a [[Arliz Hadrassian's brother|Royal Guard]], who ultimately perished during the [[Battle of Jakku]]. The Royal Guard who fell at Jakku was survived by his sister, [[Arliz Hadrassian]], an [[Agent]] of the Imperial Security Bureau.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> 

===Legacy===
Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Mando Date">{{MandoDate}}</ref> the [[Shadow Council]], the ruling body of the Imperial Remnants,<ref name="Chapter 23">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 23: The Spies}}</ref> directed a unit of elite warriors known as the [[Praetorian Guard|Imperial Praetorian Guard]], whose red gear was a deliberate callback to the Emperor's Royal Guard.<ref name="Sixth Scale">{{HotToysCite|set=Television Masterpiece Series|packtype=Sixth Scale Figure|pack=#108: Imperial Praetorian Guard|link=collectibles/star-wars-imperial-praetorian-guard-hot-toys-912699}}</ref> However, they also possessed [[helmet]]s with a design similar to [[Mandalorian armor]].<ref name="Chapter 23" /> During the [[Cold War]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[First Order]], Agent [[Terex]] of the [[First Order Security Bureau]] had possession of a Royal Guard helmet aboard the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' that was displayed next to the helmets of a [[Coastal defender stormtrooper|shoretrooper]], an [[AT-AT pilot]], a [[death trooper]], and others, along with a custom set of armor.<ref name="Dameron 8">[[Poe Dameron 8|''Poe Dameron'' 8]]</ref> By [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas" /> Hadrassian sold her brother's Royal Guard helmet to Senator [[Ransolm Casterfo]], due to having [[Centrists|pro-Imperial]] views.<ref name="Bloodline" />

[[File:Praetorian_Guard_EW.png|thumb|right|225px|Inspired by the Emperor's Royal Guard, the Praetorian Guard served as personal bodyguards of Supreme Leader Snoke of the First Order.]]
The legacy of the Emperor's Royal Guard inspired the formation of the Elite Praetorian Guard,<ref name="Datapraetorian">{{Databank|elite-praetorian-guard|Elite Praetorian Guard}}</ref> a successor class to the Imperial Praetorian Guard<ref name="Chrome 2024">{{Topps|set=2024 Topps Star Wars Chrome|cardname=Praetorian Guard|link=https://www.ebay.com/itm/266955525298}}</ref> formed from it.<ref name="Comprehensive">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Like the Royal Guards who guarded Sidious during his reign, the Praetorians functioned as the bodyguards of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]], ruler of the First Order. Consisting of eight sentinels trained to protect Supreme Leader Snoke from any threat, the Praetorians were equipped with various [[melee weapon]]s and ornate crimson armor deliberately echoing the equipment used by the Royal Guard.<ref name="Datapraetorian" /> In [[34 ABY]], the Praetorians [[Battle of Oetchi|attacked]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] after the latter assassinated Snoke, resulting in a battle in which all eight guards were killed by the [[Ren|Master]] of the Knights of Ren and his new Jedi ally, allowing the former to become the new Supreme Leader.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

By [[35 ABY]],<ref name="TROS Timeline">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] to [[34 ABY]]. Because ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' places [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] one [[Standard year|year]] later, it must take place in [[35 ABY]].</ref> when Darth Sidious resurfaced on the hidden Sith world [[Exegol]], the [[Sovereign Protector]]s took the role as the Emperor's Royal Guard. The rank of these sentinels was solely bestowed upon the most proficient of [[Sith trooper]]s, zealous soldiers brought up by the Sith Eternal<ref name="IX Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> to help impose the Sith Lord's vision of a new [[Sith Empire]]. When Rey and Ben confronted Sidious during the [[Battle of Exegol]], the Sovereign Protectors attempted to protect their master, but were killed by the two Jedi.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

==Equipment==
{{Quote|What's your name? And why do you wear that&hellip;suit?''"<br />"''My name is Sergeant, and it's my honor to wear this armor.|Ryloth resident [[Drua]] questions Royal Guard Erstin Deez|<ref name="Lords of the Sith" />}}
[[File:ForcePike-SWBC30.png|thumb|left|250px|Royal Guard routinely wielded force pikes.]]
Royal Guards were completely covered in flowing [[blood]]-[[Color|red]] [[robe]]s and featureless scarlet masks that concealed their faces.<ref name="Episode III" /> There existed a variant of the uniform that lacked the robes and ceremonial helmet in favor of light armor, utilized by guards aboard the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''Chimaera''<ref name="Family&Farewell" /> and the guards accompanying the Emperor during the battle at the [[Amaxine Space Station]].<ref name="Hidden Empire 4" /> They wielded [[electrostaff]]s<ref name="Legion">{{Legion|set=Imperial Royal Guards Unit Expansion|cardname=Electrostaff Guard}}</ref> and [[force pike]]s, a [[staff]]-like weapon<ref name="DB Force pike">{{Databank|force-pike|Force Pike}}</ref> with a vibro-edged head.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> Additionally, their robes hid a [[heavy blaster pistol]], a [[vibroblade]], and various other weaponry.<ref name="Lords of the Sith" />

When on the battlefield, Royal Guards would abandon their traditional weapons in favor of an arsenal better suited for combat, including the [[T-21 light repeating blaster]], [[Smart Rocket]], and the [[homing shot]].<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref>

While off-duty, Royal Guards wore the standard [[Imperial officer's uniform]].<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" />

The Royal Guards flew in [[TIE/IN interceptor]]s, which had the same red [[color]] as them.<ref name="Hidden Empire 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Guard first appeared in the [[1983]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. Their design came from sketches by [[Nilo Rodis-Jamero]]. The conceptual drawings varied from centurion-like warriors to priest-like monks, but a common thread tying those designs together was the deep red hue.<ref name="Old DB">{{DB|organization|emperorsroyalguard|Emperor's Royal Guard}}</ref>

In the [[2016]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'', during the prologue's adaptation of ''Return of the Jedi'', the Royal Guards were not dismissed by the Emperor, and instead actively defended the Sith when Luke Skywalker and Darth Vader turned against him. However, the Royal Guardsmen were ultimately no match for the two, allowing for the literal overthrowing of the Sith, after which the Guards witnessed the redeemed Vader, who had sustained much injuries during the battle, express love in his final moments to his Jedi son, during which one Guard collapsed into tears to the emotional exchange, much to the annoyance of Luke Skywalker, as the other Guardsmen gave psychological support.<ref name="LEGO">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref>
