'''Coolant''' was a substance used to lower temperatures, often used on [[starship]]s. It could take a gaseous state.<ref name="Brain Invaders" /> It was possible for coolant to leak, which could cause problems. During the [[Battle of Cawa City|battle]] of [[Cawa City]], [[Shara Bey]] reported that she was leaking coolant after a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] fired on her [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]].<ref name="SE part 2" /> The [[Galactic Empire]] developed a type of [[enriched coolant]] and restricted its usage to the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
