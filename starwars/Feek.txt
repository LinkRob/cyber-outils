'''Feeks''' were a type of animal that inhabited the [[Temperature|frozen]] plains of the [[planet]]<ref name="Knights of Fate">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref> [[Dowut]], where they lived in subterranean dens that they used for [[Sleep|hibernation]]. The [[Diet|omnivorous]] [[Dowutin precursor species|herd animals]] that would eventually evolve into the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Dowutin]] species used their spiked [[Limb|digits]] and chin tusks to dig for hibernating feeks beneath the plains of their shared [[homeworld]].<ref name="AotG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Feeks were first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]'', a [[2016]] reference book written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="AotG" />
