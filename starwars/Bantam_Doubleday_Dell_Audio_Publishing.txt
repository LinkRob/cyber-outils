'''Bantam Doubleday Dell Audio Publishing''', formerly '''Bantam Audio Publishing''', was a subsidiary of [[Wikipedia:Bantam Books|Bantam Doubleday Dell]] (BDD). It was responsible for publishing audiobook cassettes of ''[[Star Wars]]'' novels published under the imprint [[Bantam Spectra]] during the 90's. With the acquisition of [[Random House]] in [[1998]] by the [[Wikipedia:Bertelsmann|Berterlsmann]] conglomerate, the owner of BDD, the company became a subsidiary of Random House.<ref name="Funding" /> BDD Audio ''Star Wars'' catalogue was later exploited under the imprint [[Random House Audio]].
