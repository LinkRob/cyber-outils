'''Arnot''' was a [[wood]]cutter on the [[planet]] [[Ison]] in a [[City|village]] called [[Haven (Ison)|Haven]]. In [[3 ABY]], a group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] sought refuge at the village, which resulted in [[Lesa Benex]], the leader of Haven, getting kidnapped by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Dengar]]. After the rebels rescued Benex and returned to Haven, Arnot went to help the injured Benex, whom she told to help the rebels load any provisions the village could spare onto a [[shuttle]] the rebels had stolen from the [[Galactic Empire]] so the group could rejoin the Rebellion. While Arnot was aiding the rebels, an Imperial [[Subspace transceiver|transceiver]] was found on the shuttle and alerted them of an upcoming [[Invasion of Ison|invasion]] by [[General]] [[Octavion Sorin]]. Arnot then left to find Benex and help the villagers seek refuge.

==Biography==
Arnot was a burly [[wood]]cutter who lived in [[Haven (Ison)|Haven]], a [[mountain]] [[City|village]] on the [[planet]] [[Ison]].<ref name="Legends" />

{{GameMechanics|option=1}}
Several [[Standard day|days]] after the [[Battle of Hoth]], in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">According to {{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/2/15/return-to-echo-base|text=Return to Echo Base}}, the events of the "Return to Hoth" campaign of ''[[Legends of the Alliance]]'' takes place several days after the [[Battle of Hoth]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> a group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] sought refuge at Haven. The [[bounty hunter]] [[Dengar]], who was searching for the group, tracked them to the village. While the rebels were off on a mission, Dengar kidnapped the leader of Haven, a [[Gender|woman]] named [[Lesa Benex]]. The rebels soon after confronted him on the [[moon]] [[Nar Shaddaa]] and rescued Benex.<ref name="Legends" />

When they returned to Haven, Arnot pushed forward from the amassing crowd to help. As he attempted to carry the injured Benex from a [[shuttle]] that the [[squad]] had stolen from Imperial [[General]] [[Octavion Sorin]], Benex told him to put her down and go help the rebels load any provisions they could spare so the squad could rejoin the Rebellion. While Arnot helped load the supplies onto the shuttle, the rebels found an Imperial [[Subspace transceiver|transceiver]] and discovered that Sorin knew their location and was preparing for an [[Invasion of Ison|invasion]]. The group of rebels convinced Arnot to return to Benex and help the villagers seek refuge in the mountains, so he left with a sense of urgency to find Benex.<ref name="Legends" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Arnot appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Assault]]'' companion app ''[[Legends of the Alliance]]'', in the "Return to Hoth" campaign,<ref name="Legends" /> made available for download in [[2019]].<ref name="Return">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/2/15/return-to-echo-base|text=Return to Echo Base}}</ref> This article assumes a specific path is taken in the campaign, where the players successfully rescue Lesa Benex from Dengar. If the players are unsuccessful at rescuing Benex, or choose to not rescue her, then Arnot will not make an appearance during the campaign.<ref name="Legends" />
