A '''communications tower''' was located on<ref name="Heroes" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Systems">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Foerost system]]</ref> astronomical object [[Foerost]].<ref name="Heroes" /> By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Drabatan]] [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek]] carried out a successful [[Demolitions job on Foerost|mission]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in which he destroyed the [[Comm tower|communications tower]].<ref name="Heroes" />

In 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> after the rebel group [[Rogue One]] arrived on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]], [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[pilot]]-[[Desertion|turned]]-rebel [[Bodhi Rook]] pointed out the [[Citadel Tower]], noting that all communications in and out of the [[Imperial Center of Military Research|base]]&mdash;which they planned to [[Battle of Scarif|infiltrate]]&mdash;passed through it. [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] vaguely recalled Pao's demolitions mission to destroy the tower on Foerost&mdash;noting it was similar to the one on Scarif&mdash;and made a note to ask the Drabatan about it.<ref name="Rogue One novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Foerost communications tower was first mentioned in [[Alexander Freed]]'s [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|novelization]] of the [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' released in [[2016]].<ref name="Rogue One novel" />
