'''Aktropaw''' was a [[Dathomirian]] [[Gender|female]] [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[witch]], and one of the [[Great Mothers]] who allied with [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]].<ref name="Part Six" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref> she and the other Great Mothers, [[Klothow]] and [[Lakesis]], welcomed [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Morgan Elsbeth]] and her [[Morgan Elsbeth's forces|forces]] to the [[planet]] [[Peridea]] after calling Elsbeth there via her [[dream]]s.<ref name="Part Six" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aktropaw first appeared in "[[Part Six: Far, Far Away]]," the sixth episode of the [[Star Wars: Ahsoka|''Ahsoka'' television series]], portrayed by [[Jeryl Prescott Gallien]]. While not identified in the episode, the character's name was given in its credits.<ref name="Part Six" /> In [[wikipedia:Greek Mythology|Greek Mythology]], the similarly named [[wikipedia:Lachesis|Atropos]] was one of three goddesses known as the [[wikipedia:Moirai|Fates]], the others' names being [[wikipedia:Clotho|Clotho]] and [[wikipedia:Lachesis|Lachesis]], who determined destiny itself. Atropos specifically was the one of the three who cut the threads of destiny and thus determined when an individual died.<ref name="Britannica">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/topic/Fate-Greek-and-Roman-mythology|text=Fate|quote=Fate, [&hellip;] in Greek and Roman mythology, any of three goddesses who determined human destinies, and in particular the span of a person's life and his allotment of misery and suffering. [&hellip;] From the time of the poet Hesiod (8th century BC) on, however, the Fates were personified as three very old women who spin the threads of human destiny. Their names were Clotho (Spinner), Lachesis (Allotter), and Atropos (Inflexible). Clotho spun the "thread" of human fate, Lachesis dispensed it, and Atropos cut the thread (thus determining the individual's moment of death).|archivedate=20230609102218}}</ref> This is similar to how Aktropaw and the other Great Mothers claimed to be able to read and know the threads of fate and destiny.<ref name="Part Six" />
