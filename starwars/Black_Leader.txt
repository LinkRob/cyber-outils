'''Black Leader''',<ref name="Front Lines" /> also known as '''Black One''',<ref name="Resistance Reborn">''[[Resistance Reborn]]''</ref> was a [[callsign]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Front Lines" /> and later the [[Resistance]]. The [[T-70 X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] used the title while leading [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]],<ref name="PD1" /> but also used it while commanding other units, such as [[Red Squadron (Resistance)|Red]] and [[Blue Squadron (Resistance)|Blue Squadrons]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> During [[Color|Black]] [[Squadron]]'s mission to gather the Resistance's allies and bring new [[Star system|systems]] into the Resistance, [[Temmin Wexley]] flew as Black Leader.<ref name="Poe Dameron 29">[[Poe Dameron 29|''Poe Dameron'' 29]]</ref>
