'''Bewil''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[captain]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] during its [[Galactic Civil War|war]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. In [[3 ABY]], he was part of the [[Imperial Military|forces]] serving under [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] that [[Escape from Cloud City|occupied]] [[Cloud City]] over the [[planet]] [[Bespin]] in order to capture the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan|trainee]] [[Luke Skywalker]]. Bewil informed Vader when Skywalker's [[Red Five (T-65B X-wing starfighter)|T-65B X-wing starfighter]] was sighted approaching the [[city]] and was present in Cloud City's [[carbon-freezing chamber]] when Skywalker's companion [[Han Solo]] was [[Carbon-freezing|frozen]] in [[carbonite]].

==Biography==
===Setting the trap===
{{Quote|Lord Vader. Ship approaching, X-wing class.''"<br />"''Good. Monitor Skywalker and allow him to land.|Bewil and Darth Vader|audio=BewilVader1.flac|<ref name="ESB" />}}
[[File:Bewil_and_Vader.png|thumb|left|250px|Bewil alerted Vader to the arrival of Luke Skywalker.]]
The [[human]] Bewil served in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Officer Corps]] as a [[captain]]<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2019 Topps Star Wars Black & White: The Empire Strikes Back|cardname=Milton Johns as Captain Bewil|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/196704/cid/12614796}}</ref> and [[soldier]]<ref name="novel" /> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="ESB" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|V}}, which includes Bewil's appearance, to [[3 ABY]].</ref> he<ref name="novel" /> was among the [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] led by [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] that occupied [[Cloud City]] over the [[planet]] [[Bespin]]. Vader aimed to capture the [[Heroes of Yavin|crew]] of the rebel [[YT-1300 light freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' and then use the rebels as bait as part of a larger trap for their comrade, the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan|trainee]] [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="ESB" />

Vader captured the ''Falcon''{{'s}} crew with aid from the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], [[Baron Administrator]] of Cloud City. The [[Sith Lord]] then viewed the [[city]]'s [[carbon-freezing chamber]], where he hoped to [[Carbon-freezing|freeze]] Skywalker in [[carbonite]] for transportation to the [[Galactic Emperor]], [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. Bewil approached Vader in the chamber and informed him that Skywalker's [[Red Five (T-65B X-wing starfighter)|T-65B X-wing starfighter]] had been spotted approaching Cloud City. Vader ordered that the [[Imperial officer|captain]] monitor the Jedi and allow him to land.<ref name="ESB" /> Bewil nodded and quickly walked away.<ref name="novel" />

===Carbon freeze===
[[File:BewilVader.png|thumb|right|250px|Bewil reported to Vader that Skywalker had landed.]]
Vader decided to test the [[carbon]]-freezing process before using it on Skywalker to ensure that it would not [[Death|kill]] the Jedi, and so ordered the ''Falcon''{{'s}} captain, [[Han Solo]], to be frozen first. Bewil stood by in the chamber and observed Solo being lowered and frozen. Once the process was complete and Calrissian had confirmed that Solo had survived the process, Bewil approached Vader and informed him that Skywalker had landed.<ref name="ESB" />

The [[Sith]] ordered Bewil to make sure that the Jedi found his way to the freezing chamber, and the [[Officer|captain]] left to carry out the orders. Skywalker was ultimately lured into the chamber while trying to rescue his friends, but managed to [[Escape from Cloud City|escape]] from Vader and leave Cloud City on the ''Falcon'' with its crew and Calrissian, who had betrayed the Empire.<ref name="ESB" />

==Personality and traits==
Bewil had [[Color|brown]] [[hair]], gray [[eye]]s, and light skin.<ref name="ESB" />

==Equipment==
Bewil wore a standard olive-gray [[Imperial officer's uniform]] with a [[Imperial kepi|cap]], one [[code cylinder]], and a [[rank insignia plaque]] showing two red bars above two blue.<ref name="ESB" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Kershner_and_Johns.png|thumb|left|180px|Milton Johns (right), on the set of ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]
Bewil first appeared in the [[1980]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], in which he was portrayed<ref name="ESB" /> by [[Milton Johns]],<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|147|Assistant of the Empire}}</ref> who was credited only as one of several "Other Officers."<ref name="ESB" /> Johns was hesitant to take such a small part, but after being booked initially for only a week of work, he ultimately was kept on for six weeks, delivering his first line on his second week and in his fourth week beginning to work on the cooling chamber set<ref name="Insider" /> on Stage 5 at [[Elstree Studios]].<ref name="Making Of">''[[The Making of The Empire Strikes Back]]''</ref> Further production issues meant his scenes were then not actually filmed until his sixth week,<ref name="Insider" /> with the scene of Solo being frozen being shot on [[June 21]], [[1979]].<ref name="Behind the Magic">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref>

In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Bewil's name was first used in the ''[[2019 Topps Star Wars Black & White: The Empire Strikes Back]]'' set of trading cards released by [[Topps|The Topps Company, Inc.]] in [[2018]], including cards signed by Johns himself.<ref name="Topps" /> The name was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity's ''[[Cloud City Limited]]'' set of cards, released for the [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]] in [[1997]].<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Cloud City Limited|cardname=Captain Bewil}}</ref>
