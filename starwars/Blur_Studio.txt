'''Blur Studio''', alternatively styled as '''BLUR Studio''' or '''Blur Studio Inc.''', is a visual effects, animation and design company that has produced a number of CGI trailers for ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars video games|video game]]s. Blur Studio produced the ''[[Deceived]]'' and [[Hope (cinematic trailer)|''Hope'']] cinematic trailers for ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', and also produced two commercials, [[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II#"Snow" & "Walls" (television commercials)|''Snow'']] and [[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II#"Snow" & "Walls" (television commercials)|''Walls'']], for ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II]]''.<ref name="Web" />
