The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''100 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3177 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''81 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7877''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*End of the [[High Republic Era]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
*[[Iakar]] begins to be visited for interstellar trade, with pharmaceutical corporations being drawn to the world's valuable organic compounds. {{Circa}}<ref name="Iakar trade">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' states that [[Iakar]] had been visited for [[Star|interstellar]] trade nearly a [[Standard century|century]] before the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]]. Therefore, Iakar began to be visited for interstellar trade around 100 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The [[2020]] reference book ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' states that the [[High Republic Era]] ended around 100 BBY.<ref name="HRE">''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' dates the High Republic between approximately 300 and 100 [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|IV}}, which corresponds to [[300 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, the High Republic Era must have occurred between approximately 300 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and 100 BBY.</ref> However, a later promotional video for the web novel ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' places the latter's events, which occur around [[82 BBY]], at the end of the High Republic Era.<ref name="TVoSD old date">{{WebCite|url=https://m.weibo.cn/status/4583274020349438|text=微博|author=''[[Star Wars]]''|date=2020-12-17|work=[[Wikipedia:Weibo|Weibo]]|archivedate=20201218020009}} states that the events of ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' occur about fifty [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|I}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]]. Therefore, the events of ''The Vow of Silver Dawn'' must take place around [[82 BBY]].</ref> ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' later moved ''Silver Dawn'' to [[200 BBY]] and confirmed that the High Republic Era ended around 100 BBY.<ref name="Timelines" />
