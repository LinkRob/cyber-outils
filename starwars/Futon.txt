A '''futon''' was an item that could be hung out to dry. When the former [[Sith]] known as "[[the Ronin]]" was [[Skirmish at the Osou spaceport|chased]] by [[Imperial troop]]s in the [[Osou]] settlement on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Genbara]], [[blaster]]fire was aimed at shadows that turned out to be merely a hung futon drying near an open [[Viewport|window]].<ref name="Ronin">''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A futon appeared in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]] novel ''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]'', written by [[Emma Mieko Candon]].<ref name="Ronin" /> In the real-world, a [[Wikipedia:Futon|futon]] ([[Wikipedia:Japanese|Japanese]]: 布団) is a [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] type of bedding consisting of a mattress and a duvet.<ref name="Jisho">{{WebCite|url=https://jisho.org/word/%E5%B8%83%E5%9B%A3|text=布団|work=Jisho.org|archivedate=20241202170537}}</ref>
