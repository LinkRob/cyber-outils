'''Adrafar'''<ref name="Falcon 71" /> was a [[planet]] located in the [[Abafar system]] of the [[Quelii sector]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="SWApp" /> The sister world of the [[desert]] planet [[Abafar]], Adrafar [[orbit]]ed the [[Color|yellow]] [[star]] [[Abafed]] on an identical orbit, though four [[Standard month|months]] behind. Despite the similarity, Abafar was the only inhabited world in the [[Star system|system]].<ref name="Falcon 71" />

==Behind the scenes==
Adrafar was introduced to [[canon]] in the [[Guide to the Galaxy]] section of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 71|seventy-first issue]] of the [[De Agostini]] magazine ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'', which was published in [[2016]].<ref name="Falcon 71" />
