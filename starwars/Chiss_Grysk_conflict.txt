{{Quote|You may call me Jixtus. Make good note of that name, Qilori of Uandualon. It's the name of he who will finally and completely destroy the Chiss Ascendancy.|Jixtus to Qilori of Uandalon|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
The '''Chiss–Grysk''' conflict was a war that consisted of a series of political and military conflicts between the [[Color|blue]]-skinned nation, the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] and their allies, and the [[Grysk]] warlike [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]'s nation of the [[Grysk Hegemony]] and their allies.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> The conflict began in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> between the Chiss Ascendancy and the Grysk-controlled [[Nikardun Destiny]], which attacked the Ascendancy's [[capital]], [[Csilla]], as well as attempting to kill and capture several Chiss, such as [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] ([[Core name]] "Thrawn"). However, the Nikardun Destiny was later defeated by Thrawn, who neutralized the Destiny's leader, [[General]] [[Yiv]] the Benevolent.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> After Yiv's failure to destroy the Chiss, the Grysk agent which had trained and manipulated the Nikardun, "[[Jixtus]]" employed another faction, the [[Agbui]], and their leader, [[Haplif]]. During their [[Agbui attempt to precipitate a Chiss Ascendancy civil war|attempt to precipitate a Chiss Ascendancy civil war]], they caused tensions between several of the Chiss' [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] and [[Great Families]], causing a [[Hoxim incident|battle]] between the [[Pommrio family]], the [[Erighal family]] and the [[Xodlak family]]. As Agbui agents had shared the location of a "[[nyix]] [[Mining|mine]]" with untapped resources to all families, causing all of them to arrive at the mine at the same time. However, Thrawn once again discovered the Agbui and Jixtus' plot, and destroyed the mine thanks to Jixtus' [[Watith]] agents that had been sent to kill Thrawn, which failed.<ref name="Greater Good" />

In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> Jixtus allied himself with the theocratic government of the [[Kilji]] species, the [[Kilji Illumine]] and its ruler, [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]]. Jixtus promised the Kiljis "nations and guidance" in their conquests of [[Kilji way of order and enlightenment|"enlightenment"]], while Nakirre&mdash;without him realizing&mdash;provided the Grysk transport aboard the Illumine's battle [[fleet]], the [[Illumine Kilhorde]], assuring the Chiss Ascendancy realized the Kiljis were not a threat, hiding their "true enemy," the Grysk Hegemony. As Nakirre and Jixtus traveled across the Ascendancy, brewing Chaos between families by showing recordings of the Hoxim incident, creating fake warships to simulate families attacking others, the Kilji Illumine lost. As Jixtus sent [[General]] [[Crofyp]] and [[Colonel]] [[Tildnis]] to [[Rapacc]], to [[Attack on Rapacc|retrieve]] [[refugee]]s, which failed, and later sent the one that survived the battle against Thrawn and the [[Paccian Governance]], [[Tildnis]] aboard his [[Kilji picket cruiser]], the [[Hammer (Kilji picket cruiser)|''Hammer'']] to [[Sunrise]], where it was [[Sunrise incident|boarded]] by Thrawn. During the attack on Tildnis, Thrawn and the Paccosh killed all Kiljis aboard, retrieving technical data and combat doctrines which he shared with the [[Paataatus Hiveborn]], as the Kilhorde had been sent by Jixtus to the southeast of the Ascendancy&mdash;and subsequently, to the north and northeast of the Hiveborn&mdash;to conquer several impoverished nations. The Paataatus, deeming the Kilhorde's operations near their borders unacceptable, [[Attack on the Illumine Kilhorde|destroyed]] the Kilhorde, freeing all nations and worlds it had conquered.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

Later, Thrawn [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand|fought]] Jixtus at Sunrise, after the latter had killed Nakirre aboard the Kilji's ''[[Whetstone]]'', causing Jixtus to [[Self-destruct mechanism|destroy]] himself and his [[Jixtus's fleet|forces]]. Ending the plot to destroy the Chiss Ascendancy.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> However, other [[Unidentified Grysk commander (Grysk task force)|Grysk agents]] resumed their operations against the Chiss, led by [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]], which ended catastrophically for the Grysks, as Ar'alani discovered the existence of the Grysk's [[homeworld]]s and destroyed many of their warships,<ref name="Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> as well as research facilities with the help of the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Grand Admiral]] Thrawn's help, as Thrawn had been exiled from the Ascendancy, and joined the Empire, becoming one of its highest-ranking officers.<ref name="Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref>
