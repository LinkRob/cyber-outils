The '''Commodities Enhancement Program''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] program introduced in [[9 BBY]] by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] that promised better market access [[galaxy]]-wide for [[Foodstuff|food]] and other organic raw materials. In reality, like many of the Emperor's promises, it was a lie designed to conceal other plans. Each world targeted by the program was hit hard, but the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Wobani]] had entirely collapsed after being targeted by the program.<ref name="Leia">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>
