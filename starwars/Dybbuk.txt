The '''Dybbuk Silencer'''<ref name="TAVG" /> also known as simply the '''Dybbuk''' was a fleshy,<ref name="Databank" /> star-shaped [[Color|yellow-green]] [[Parasite|parasitic]] creature used by the [[Galactic Republic]] to subdue violent criminals in the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Lost Found" /> The silencer was a [[Bioengineering|genetically modified]] from an [[Unidentified atmospheric glider|atmospheric glider]] native to [[Dybrin 12]] by [[Chiewab Amalgamated Pharmaceuticals]].<ref name="TAVG" /> Measuring 0.25 metres in width, the parasite would affect the user's mind by latching onto another organism's [[mouth]]<ref name="Databank" /> with its adhesive [[pseupod pad]]<ref name="TAVG" /> and induce an altered state of consciousness<ref name="Databank" /> while making a squelching sound,<ref name="Episode I AD">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> feeding off  the host’s neural energy while transmitting euphoric alpha waves. Some subjected to the silencer reported nightmares while under its effects which Chiewab [[scientist]]s denied.<ref name="TAVG" />

In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Timeline">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> aboard the Republic [[prisoner]] [[Prison ship|transport]] ''[[Palwick]]'' heading to the<ref name="Lost Found" /> [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[capital]]<ref name="Episode I AD" /> [[Coruscant]], the parasite was used to subdue a [[human]] convict, [[Melo Tolicus]]. During an [[Prisoner mutiny on the Palwick|escape attempt]] aboard the [[Starship|ship]] causing the ship to drop out of [[hyperspace]] near the [[Water|snowy]]<ref name="Lost Found" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Episode I AD" /> [[Carlac]], [[meknek]] [[Verosha Aniseya|Osha Aniseya]] used her [[Maintenance droid|repair droid]] [[Pip]] to electrocute the parasite latched to the convict. The electric shock caused the dybbuk to release its grip on the convict, freeing<ref name="Lost Found" /> him and leaving the dybbuk chittering<ref name="Episode I AD" /> while escaping the ship into [[Realspace|space]] before the ship entered Carlac's [[atmosphere]] and crash-landed.<ref name="Lost Found" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dybbuk appeared in "[[Lost / Found]]," the first episode of the live-action series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'',<ref name="Lost Found" /> which aired on [[June 4]], [[2024]] on [[Disney+]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> The episode was first screened in select theatres in the United States on [[June 3]], 2024.<ref name="First Screen">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-in-theaters|text=Watch the Mystery of ''The Acolyte'' Unfold in Select Theaters June 3}}</ref> The parasitic creature was designed by [[Wikipedia:Artist|artist]] [[Tom Blake]].<ref name="Blake Concept">{{WebCite|url=https://www.tomblakedesign.com/design/harrypotter-sts3m|text=THE ACOLYTE|work=[[Tom Blake]]'s [https://www.tomblakedesign.com official website]|archivedate=20250623015341}}</ref>
