'''BB-H20''' was a [[BB-series astromech droid]] that had a [[Color|red]] and white body, with a black [[sensor]]. The [[droid]] aided the [[Resistance]]<ref name="DF" /> along with [[R2-H25]]<ref name="R2-H25">{{DisneyToyCite|set=Star Wars: Droid Factory (toy line)|pack=R2-H25|link=r2-h25-holiday-droid-factory-figure-star-wars-418149062412.html|archivedate=20251012051054}}</ref> during their time on [[Crait]].<ref name="DF" />

==Behind the scenes==
This holiday-themed droid is a toy that was made available exclusively at [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] Parks in [[2020]] as part of the [[Star Wars: Droid Factory (toy line)|''Droid Factory'']] toy line.<ref name="DF" />
