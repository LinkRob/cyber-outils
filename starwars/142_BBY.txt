'''142 BBY''' or '''142<ref name="Date" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]]. By that year,<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Torbin]] had taken the [[Barash Vow]] and entered a silent state of [[meditation]] to atone for his role in an [[Mission to Brendok|incident]] on the [[planet]] [[Brendok]] in [[148 BBY]]. Ten years later,<ref name="Revenge" /> in [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a [[Cord Taunus|Jedi Master]] told Jedi Master [[Sol]], who had come to the [[Olega Jedi temple|Jedi temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Olega]] to speak with Torbin, that he had not spoken in over ten years.<ref name="Revenge" />

==Behind the scenes==
142 BBY was indirectly mentioned in "[[Revenge / Justice]],"<ref name="Revenge" /> the second episode of the live-action [[Disney+]] series [[Star Wars: The Acolyte|''The Acolyte'']], which released on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="guide">{{SW|url=series/the-acolyte/revenge-justice-episode-guide|text="Revenge / Justice" Episode Guide &#124; The Acolyte}}</ref>
