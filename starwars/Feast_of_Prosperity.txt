The '''Feast of Prosperity''' was an [[Standard year|annual]]<ref name="SWH" /> ancient event<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" /> that was celebrated by the [[Hutt]]s<ref name="SWH" /> on the [[moon]] of [[Nar Shaddaa]].<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" /> During the celebration, all Hutts were in agreement to show generosity.<ref name="SWH" /> The [[Purlah Soup]] was a dish whose origins dated back to the Feast of Prosperity. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for the Purlah Soup, as well information about the Feast of Prosperity, in his [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" /> published by [[35 ABY]].<ref name="TUC date">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'' establishes that the [[in-universe]] [[The Ultimate Cookbook|cookbook]] was published prior to the [[destruction of Kijimi]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[35 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Feast of Prosperity was first mentioned in the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]].<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" /> The Feast of Prosperity was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as an annual in-game event in the update 6.1.4 to the [[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Onslaught|''Onslaught'']] expansion of [[BioWare]]'s [[2011]] [[Star Wars video games|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]], ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''.<ref name="TOR Patch Notes">{{TORweb/Patch|6.1.4}}</ref>
