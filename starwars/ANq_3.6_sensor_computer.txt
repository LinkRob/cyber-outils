The '''ANq 3.6 sensor computer''',<ref name="SWCT X-wing">{{SWCT|set=Blueprints|cardname=X-wing Starfighter}}</ref> also known as the '''ANq 3.6 tracking computer''' or '''ANq 3.6 Computer Tracking System''', was a [[targeting computer]] that was installed on the [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]], [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]<ref name="SWCT" /> and [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]. It used a low powered [[targeting laser]] to fire continuously at a target. Using the range and vector data it collected, it could lock onto a target and provide a 97% probability of a hit. However, the laser would give away the location and approach vector of the starfighter so the targeting system was not used often. It could track up to 1,000 moving sublight objects, and acquire 20 possible targets. When programmed for extra sensitivity, the computer could track up to 120 specific sensor signatures.<ref name="RSOM">''[[Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>
