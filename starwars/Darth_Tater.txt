'''Darth Tater''' was a version of [[Wikipedia:Mr. Potato Head|Mr. Potato Head]], made to look like [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]]. [[Tag Greenly]] owned such a toy when he was still a baby. Bink's parents discovered his [[Force-sensitive/Legends|Force-sensibility]] when he let his toy float in the air. He was born on "Potatooine."

==Behind the scenes==
[[Darth/Legends|Darth]] [[Potato/Legends|Tater]] originally was a [[Wikia:c:swmerchandise:Darth Tater|real-life toy]] released by [[Hasbro]] in February [[2005]]. He came complete with lightsaber, cape, helmet, shoes, eyes, nose, and teeth. He retailed for $7.99 US.
