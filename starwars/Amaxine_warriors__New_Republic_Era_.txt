The '''Amaxine warriors''' were a paramilitary organization active during the [[New Republic Era]] commanded by [[Arliz Hadrassian]] and named after an [[Amaxine warriors|ancient warrior culture]]. They first made themselves known by [[Napkin Bombing|bombing]] the New Republic [[New Republic Senate|Senate]] [[New Republic senatorial complex|building]]. In response, [[Senator]]s [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Ransolm Casterfo]] undertook a [[Amaxine warrior crisis|campaign]] to investigate the Amaxines and their links to [[Rinnrivin Di]]'s [[Rinnrivin Di's cartel|cartel]]. In truth, the Amaxine warriors were a mere front for the [[First Order]], a secretive [[First Order military|military junta]] in the [[Unknown Regions]].

==Description==
The Amaxine warriors of the [[New Republic Era]] wore [[Color|black]] jumpsuits and carried various weapons including [[blaster]]s and [[force pike]]s, the weapons of the [[Emperor's Royal Guard]]. A substantial number of the Amaxine warriors were adolescent youths in their mid teens. Amaxine warriors spent most of their [[time]] training with weapons and flying with their [[starfighter]]s. The Amaxine warriors had at least half a dozen bases in [[Centrists|Centrist]] [[Realspace|space]]. Their main [[Sibensko complex|headquarters]] was an [[Water|underwater]] base on the [[Expansion Region]] [[planet]] of [[Sibensko]]. The Sibensko base contained a large ammunition depot and hosted several [[Starship|starfighters]] including [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wings]], [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wings]], [[B-wing heavy assault starfighter|B-wings]] and [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s. In addition, the Amaxines maintained a smaller base on the desert [[Planet|world]] of [[Daxam IV]]. The Daxam encampment boasted at least a thousand Amaxine warriors and had at least five [[squadron]]s of starfighters. While the Amaxines' resources and manpower paled in comparison to the "[[Galactic Empire|Old Empire]]," some contemporary observers like [[Senator]]s [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Ransolm Casterfo]] believed that they posed a significant threat to the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Bloodline" />

==History==
===Reappropriation===
In [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Timeframe" /> the [[Amaxine warriors]] became the name of a local paramilitary organization that controlled [[Smuggler|smuggling]] operations around Daxam IV. This organization had no connection to the original Amaxines but was merely a group of malcontents who had established themselves as a burgeoning paramilitary with the encouragement of [[Lady]] [[Carise Sindian]], a prominent [[Centrists|Centrist]] [[Senator]] who served as a clandestine agent of the [[First Order]], a [[Fascism|fascist]] [[First Order High Command|military junta]] in the [[Unknown Regions]]. From her position inside the Galactic Senate, Sindian sought out former [[Imperial officer]]s that had congregated within the various subcultures where sympathisers of the [[New Order]] had also gathered to since the [[Galactic Concordance|outcome]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]], helping establish contacts that linked these individuals to the [[Unknown Regions Imperial remnant|surviving remnants]] of the [[Imperial Navy|Imperial Starfleet]], which she and other like-minded [[Politics|politicians]] streamlined funds to through the Amaxines, financing its [[First Order Navy|reconstruction]]. With secret backing from Lady Carise &mdash; who envisioned the militants as shock troops for the First Order &mdash; these supporters reorganised themselves from simple dissidents into the Amaxine warriors, a militant organisation<ref name="Bloodline" /> that appropriated the martial traditions and culture of their historic namesake to promote the hateful ideology of the First Order among those most vulnerable to its alure.<ref name="Card">{{FFGXW2|set=Fugitives and Collaborators Squadron Pack|cardname=Amaxine Warrior}}</ref> After bankrolling the [[Nikto]] [[crime lord]] [[Rinnrivin Di]]'s [[Rinnrivin Di's cartel|cartel]], Lady Carise and her Centrist collaborators diverted funds into procuring weapons and training for the Amaxine warriors.<ref name="Bloodline" />

According to [[Joph Seastriker]], no record of the Amaxines existed on any New Republic public information grid. However, there were reports of Amaxine activity on several Centrist worlds clustered in one section of the galaxy. They generated considerable revenue from smuggling operations carried out by Rinnrivin Di's cartel, whose activities were hurting the offworld trade of the planet [[Ryloth]]. Rinnrivin hired the Amaxine warriors as pilots for his smuggling operations and raids on Ryloth's commercial shipping. On the surface, the Amaxines appeared to be the financiers and partners of Rinnrivin's cartel when in fact they were mainly proxies of the undercover Centrist agents of the First Order.<ref name="Bloodline" />

The Amaxines maintained between five and six bases including an encampment on the subarctic [[desert]] world of Daxam IV in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and a larger base on the watery planet of Sibensko in the Expansion Region. Their leader was [[Arliz Hadrassian]], a former [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]], who had ties to Lady Carise. While Hadrassian wanted the Amaxines to serve as the vanguard of the First Order's attack on the New Republic, Lady Carise regarded them as a distraction for sowing chaos in order to prepare the galaxy for the return of "meaningful authority" through the First Order.<ref name="Bloodline" />

The New Republic starfighter [[pilot]]s Joph and [[Greer Sonnel]] learned about the paramilitary organization during a visit to Sonnel's [[homeworld]] of [[Pamarthe]]. Joph and Greer discussed their findings with Senator Organa, who was spearheading an investigation into Rinnrivin's cartel. Realizing the two organizations were linked, Leia sent her colleague Senator Ransolm Casterfo and Greer to investigate the militia's base on Daxam IV. Shortly later, Hadrassian [[Napkin Bombing|bombed]] the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate's]] [[New Republic Senate conference building|conference building]] to sow confusion. This only drew Leia's attention to the Amaxines and Casterfo traveled to Sibensko to investigate. After befriending Hadrassian and gaining her trust, the two discovered that the Amaxine warriors were training had mustered a large fleet of starfighters and had thousands of personnel. Most of the Amaxines were adolescent youths who wore black jumpsuits. Casterfo also discovered their main base was on Sibensko.<ref name="Bloodline" />

===Destruction===
Later, Senator Organa and her entourage including [[C-3PO]] traveled to Sibensko. There, Threepio managed to download banking records showing that the Amaxines were funnelling billions of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] into Rinnrivin's cartel. Rinnrivin's cartel was channeling these funds to nameless sources on Centrist worlds. Leia and her team also discovered evidence that the Amaxines were marshalling starfighters and personnel for a war against the New Republic. Despite escaping the Amaxines, the underwater base was destroyed when a downed Amaxine starfighter collided with the ammunition store; creating an explosion that [[Death|killed]] many Amaxines and wiped out much of the evidence.<ref name="Bloodline" />

The [[Mission to Sibensko|skirmish]] on Sibensko dealt a heavy blow to the Amaxine warriors and wiped out most of their manpower and starships. Hadrassian was infuriated by the destruction of much of her organization and verbally attacked Lady Carise and her Centrist counterparts for refusing to secede from the Republic, pleading that she be allowed to take revenge on Leia Organa. Lady Carise retorted that Hadrassian's "Napkin Bombing" only drew attention to the Amaxines. Since the Amaxines had in her view outlived their usefulness, Carise withdrew her support from them. Lady Carise hoped that the events on Sibensko would cause the New Republic to cease investigating the Amaxines' links to the First Order. Despite this setback. C-3PO was unable to access data layers that linked the Amaxines directly to the First Order. As a result, most people in the New Republic Senate and media wrongly believed that the Amaxines had been the primary financiers and partners behind Rinnrivin's cartel.<ref name="Bloodline" />

After Ransolm spoke out in support of Leia's testimony on the Amaxines' activities, Lady Carise decided to discredit the dissident Centrist senator by arranging for Hadrassian to [[Tai-Lin Garr's assassination|assassinate]] the [[Populists|Populist]] [[Politics|politician]] [[Tai-Lin Garr]]. Hadrassian then [[Death|killed]] herself before she could be taken into custody. Lady Carise's [[slicer]]s then produced faux footage linking Casterfo to Hadrassian, who had taken the fall as the instigator behind the Napkin bombing and the Amaxine warriors. As a result, Casterfo was deported to his homeworld of [[Riosa]] for trial, sentencing, and [[Prison|imprisonment]]. In retaliation, Leia convinced the [[Elder Houses]] to strip Lady Carise of her royal titles. With the Senate unwilling to take action against the Amaxines and their true masters, Leia formed the [[Resistance]] to defend the New Republic.<ref name="Bloodline" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Amaxine warriors first appeared as minor antagonists in [[Claudia Gray]]'s [[2016]] novel [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']].
