'''Alpha Group''' was a [[Battle group|group]] within the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] under the command of [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]]. The group was present at the [[Mako-Ta Space Docks]], where it and other sub-groups were to disperse from the rest of the [[fleet]] until it was required to [[Galactic Civil War|fight]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Star Wars 50" />

As the groups were ready to launch into [[hyperspace]], a fault occurred resulting in the [[Starship|ships]] being unable to launch which was due to [[Trios|Queen Trios]] betrayal. Shortly after the launch failure, [[Death Squadron]] under the command of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Battle of Mako-Ta|arrived]]. The battle group's flagship was Admiral Ackbar's [[MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80A ''Home One'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]] ''[[Home One]]''.<ref name="Star Wars 50" />

During the battle, Luke Skywalker used his ship's light trail to signal Ackbar that his cruiser's bay doors were open. The admiral ordered the launch of all of his fighters, and for them to spread the word.<ref name="Star Wars 53" /> Once the fleet got the codes to bring its ships online, Ackbar ordered ''Home One'' to make the hyperspace jump.<ref name="Star Wars 55">[[Star Wars (2015) 55|''Star Wars'' (2015) 55]]</ref>
