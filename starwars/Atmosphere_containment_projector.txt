An '''atmosphere containment projector'''<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> was a device that maintained a spacecraft's internal [[atmosphere]] when bay doors or other openings were exposed to vacuum. It created an invisible barrier that prevents atmospheric loss while allowing solid objects and personnel to pass through freely.<ref name="A New Hope" /> These projectors were commonly used in [[hangar]] bays and docking ports on [[capital ship]]s like the ''[[Finalizer]]''<ref name="The Force Awakens">{{Film|VII}}</ref> and the [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']],<ref name="Rise of Skywalker" /> as well as [[battle station]]s like the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]<ref name="A New Hope" /> and [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star II]],<ref name="Return of the Jedi">{{Film|VI}}</ref> to facilitate operations in space without depressurization.<ref name="A New Hope" />
