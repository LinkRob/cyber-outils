The '''Durga system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Marzoon sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square I-16 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The system contained the astronomical object [[Durga (celestial body)|Durga]], which was ruled by a [[Durga royal family|royal family]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Wayseeker" />

The [[Mirialan]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] visited Durga while she was traveling [[the galaxy]] as a [[Jedi]] [[Wayseeker]]<ref name="Wayseeker" /> between around [[170 BBY]] and around [[160 BBY]].<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]'' take place around [[160 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:WayseekerDate|here]]. ''Wayseeker'' states that [[Vernestra Rwoh]] visited [[Durga (celestial body)|Durga]] during her travels as a [[Wayseeker]] which began a [[Standard decade|decade]] earlier, therefore meaning she traveled to Durga between around [[170 BBY]] and around 160 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> During her time on Durga, Rwoh met the [[Monarchy|royal]] family and mediated a [[Marriage|wedding]] contract.<ref name="Wayseeker" />
