A '''crown''' was an ornamental headdress worn by a [[Monarchy|monarch]] as a symbol of [[Gender|their]] sovereignty.<ref name="Prisoners">{{TCW|Prisoners}}</ref> By extension, the term also designated the state or government headed by a monarch.<ref name="DB Tandin">{{Databank|general-tandin|General Tandin}}</ref> The crown worn by the [[Monarch of Onderon|kings]] of [[Onderon]] was a [[Gold|gilded]] headband that resembled a wreath of interlocking leaves.<ref name="Front Runners">{{TCW|Front Runners}}</ref> The crown of the [[King of Mon Cala|kings]] of [[Mon Cala]] was beehive-shaped with a spiked circlet at the base.<ref name="Prisoners" /> The [[Boss]]es of the [[Gungan]]s wore a conical crown of rulership with a neck flap.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>
