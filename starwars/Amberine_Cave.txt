The '''Amberine Cave''' was a rock formation in the [[Lost Steppe]] region on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an entry from [[Jet Kordo]]'s journey about the [[Pyke Syndicate]] had been left behind a sealed portion of the cave along with a pair of [[Tosharan]] ruffian [[Clothing|pants]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|canon}}
During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] used a modification to her [[blaster pistol]] to blow open the sealed portion of the cave and retrieve the journal entry and clothing.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Amberine Cave appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The location with the loot was only accessible after the player get the power upgrade to Vess's blaster. The Tosharan ruffian pants found in the cave reduce the damage taken while sprinting and increase [[adrenaline]] from [[melee]] attacks. If they player has a full set of Tosharan ruffian clothing they have a chance to gain a free [[grenade]] when they are out.<ref name="Outlaws" />
