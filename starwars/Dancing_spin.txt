Found on<ref name="Survivor" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> [[Birren]], the '''dancing spin''' was one of several types of [[bird]] that the [[human]] [[Kata Akuna]] watched while living there with her [[Akuna family|family]]. Having left Birren and lost both her [[parent]]s, Akuna later joined the [[Stinger Mantis crew|crew]] of the [[luxury yacht]] ''[[Stinger Mantis]]''<ref name="Survivor" /> in [[9 BBY]], and in that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> she tried to list all the birds she remembered from Birren, including the dancing spin, while on board the [[yacht]]. The ''Mantis''{{'s}} owner, [[Greez Dritus]], overheard the list and asked what Akuna was doing, which Akuna explained, but when he asked for more information on the birds since he had never been to Birren, she put off the discussion till later.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
The dancing spin was mentioned in an ambient conversation in the [[2023]] [[Respawn Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Survivor" />
