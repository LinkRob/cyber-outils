The '''Bazaar Star Challenge''' was a [[podracing]] [[Racetrack|course]] that the [[Snivvian]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Coy Vrizh]] had won a race on by the [[time]] he took part in the [[Hydian-Hutta Podracing Circuit]]<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> around [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> The win was recorded on his downloadable [[Racer|racing]] profile.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Bazaar Star Challenge was mentioned in the [[2025]] [[Industrial Light & Magic]] [[Star Wars video games|virtual reality game]] [[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]].<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where the [[Bazaar Star Challenge/Legends|course]] appeared in "[[Shadows in Green]]," a [[2002]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' adventure written by [[Lee Pickler]] and published by [[RPGA]] for the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign.<ref name="LivingForce">{{LivingForce|Shadows in Green}}</ref>
