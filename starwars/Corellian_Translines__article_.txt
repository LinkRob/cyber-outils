'''Corellian Translines''' was an RPG supplement slated to appear in the cancelled [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 16|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 16]]. The article was supposed to elaborate upon [[Corellian Translines]], an interstellar transit company and introduce antagonists in the form of a [[Mon Calamari/Legends|Mon Calamari]] terrorist group by the name of [[Akkad]] and the [[Assassin/Legends|assassin]] [[Nova (assassin)|Nova]].
