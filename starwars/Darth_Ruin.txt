'''Darth Ruin''', formerly known as '''Phanius''', was an [[Umbaran]] [[Sith]] active some time before the [[Standard year|year]] [[990 BBY/Canon|990 BBY]]. Originally a [[Jedi Master]], Phanius left the [[Jedi Order]] and eventually became a Sith and adopted the title of [[Darth]]. Phanius was later remembered as the first member of the [[Lost Twenty]], a group of former Jedi Masters who for varying reasons had left the Order, and was remembered with a [[bronzium]] bust in the [[Jedi Archives|Archives]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. By 990 BBY, twelve more Masters had joined the ranks of the Lost.<ref name="SWcom" />
