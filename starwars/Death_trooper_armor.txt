'''Death trooper armor''' was the standard [[armor]] worn by the [[death trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Imperial Death Troopers" /> It was considered an improvement over the standard [[stormtrooper]] [[Stormtrooper armor|kit]]. The armor was worn over an environmentally sealed bodysuit. Characteristics of the armor included a cover of [[reflec]], a spray polymer which warped electromagnetic signals. The armor could have various objects attached, such as an [[IM-40 three-slot ammunition and tool pouch]] on the left shoulder guard, and a [[pauldron]] on the right denoting [[specialist]] [[Military rank|rank]] which appeared as plain black if not seen through the visors of death trooper helmets.<ref name=":0">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:ThrawnsUndeadTrooper-CGSWG.png|thumb|left|250px|A death trooper in Thrawn's forces whose armor has been reinforced and rebuilt with salvaged materials.]]
Death trooper [[armor]] was created by the [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research]] for [[death trooper]]s,<ref name="USW NE" /> the elite commandos<ref name="HC 80" /> of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[death trooper division]].<ref name="Imperial Troops" /> As such, the various death [[trooper]]s active across the Empire as guards<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> and elite [[soldier]]s<ref name="Death Troopers old" /> made use of the gear, although they still served as highly effective personnel without that armor.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

After the fall of the Empire in the [[Battle of Jakku]] in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> various death troopers who still subscribed to the Imperial ideology continued to use their armor as members of [[Imperial Remnants]]. For example, [[Gideon's Imperial remnant]] contained a [[Gideon's guard|formation]] of death troopers who continued to don their armor.<ref name="Chapter 8" /> Meanwhile, the [[Thrawn's forces|exiled forces]] of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn" contained two death troopers who maintained their broken armor with red ribbons and golden fill-in pieces. These two death troopers both died at some point but were resurrected by the [[magick]]s of the [[Great Mothers]], whom Thrawn had allied with.<ref name="Part Eight" />

Decades after the Empire's fall, during the [[New Republic Era]], the [[Knights of Ren|Knight of Ren]] [[Trudgen]] wore a patchworked helmet which included a fragment of the helmet of a death trooper he defeated in the past.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
