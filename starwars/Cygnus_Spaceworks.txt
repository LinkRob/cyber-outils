'''Cygnus Spaceworks''', also referred to as '''Cygnus Space Workshops''', was a [[starship]] manufacturing company active during both the [[Republic Era|Republic]] and [[Imperial Era]]s.<ref name="ROUVG" />

==History==
Before the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] came to [[Proclamation of the New Order|power]], Cygnus Space Workshops was coaxed by [[Republic Sienar Systems]] to develop the [[Abecederian line]].<ref name="ROUVG" /> It was one of many companies, including [[Theed Engineering]] and [[Sienar Fleet Systems]], who were involved in the construction of the starship ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', personal vessel of Imperial [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]. When a rebel cell stole the ''Carrion Spike'', a [[Mon Calamari]] member of the group named [[Artoz]] recognized Cygnus Space Workshops' part in outfitting the vessel.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Behind the scenes==
The company Cygnus Spaceworks originated in a [[1984]] fan-made material created by Lawrence Miller named ''[[Book of Imperial Shuttle Plans: Cygnus Spaceworks]]'', which described it as the company that made a series of ships very similar to the [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]]. The book was commonly mistaken for an official [[Lucasfilm]] product, and in [[1994]] the licensed [[Star Wars Technical Journal of the Imperial Forces|second volume]] of ''[[The Official Star Wars Technical Journal]]'' included the company. The [[2014]] [[James Luceno]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] then brought the company from the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' continuity into [[canon]].

On August 21, 2017 [[Fantasy Flight Games]] announced the [[Alpha-class Xg-1 Star Wing|''Alpha''-class Xg-1 Star Wing]] as part of its Wave XII for their ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Miniatures Game]]'', naming it as a product of Cygnus Spaceworks.<ref name="FFGweb-Bring on the Alpha Strike">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/8/21/bring-on-the-alpha-strike|text=Bring on the Alpha Strike}}</ref>
