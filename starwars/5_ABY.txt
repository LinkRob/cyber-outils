The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''5 ABY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3282 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7982''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*Six [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="Before">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref> [[Shara Bey]] and [[Kes Dameron]] retire from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="GA" />
*[[Kashyyyk]] is [[Liberation of Kashyyyk|liberated]] by the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Life Debt" />
*The [[Ryloth Insurgency]] comes to an end.<ref name="Life Debt" />
*[[Gallius Rax]] became [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] in effect but rejects the title and&mdash;in keeping with his intention to rule the [[Galactic Empire]] as the power behind the throne&mdash;proclaimed himself [[Counselor to the Empire]].<ref name="Life Debt" />
*After [[Invasions of Naboo|three consecutive sieges]], the New Republic is able to foil the Galactic Empire's attempt to regain control of [[Naboo]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />
*The New Republic and the Galactic Empire engage in one final [[Battle of Jakku|battle]] on and over the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Jakku]].<ref name="TFA VD" />
*The Galactic Empire signs the [[Galactic Concordance]] on [[Chandrila]],<ref name="Empire's End" /> following its defeat by the New Republic.<ref name="TFA VD" />
*The [[Galactic Civil War]] comes to an end.<ref name="TFA VD" />
*The [[Confederacy of Corporate Systems]] and the [[New Separatist Union]] were founded.<ref name="Empire's End" />
*The New Republic is formally founded.<ref name="BSI note 1">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that the [[New Republic]] was officially founded twenty-nine [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the New Republic's official founding must have happened in [[5 ABY]].</ref>
*The Galactic Empire is replaced by the New Republic officially<ref name="Rise and Fall" />
*Imperial rule on [[Coruscant]] is replaced by a [[Coruscant's provisional government|provisional government]] with [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] as its figurehead ruler.<ref name="Empire's End" />
*[[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] discontinues her [[Jedi training]] with [[Luke Skywalker]] on [[Ajan Kloss]].<ref name="BSI note 2" />
*Former [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] rallies a [[Unidentified Tusken Raider tribe|Tusken Raider tribe]] to victory over a [[Pyke Syndicate]] [[Tatooine repulsor train|repulsor train]].<ref name="Chapter 2">{{BOBF|Chapter 2: The Tribes of Tatooine}}</ref><ref name="Flashback date">{{InsiderCite|225|A Certain Point of View}} states that flashback events of "[[Chapter 2: The Tribes of Tatooine]]" took place five [[Standard year|years]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'' Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], corresponding to [[5 ABY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref>

==Births==
*[[Keldo]] on [[Parnassos]].<ref name="Keldo Birthdate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']] to [[34 ABY]]. ''Phasma'' establishes that Phasma is the same age as [[Siv]], who in a chapter set twelve years before the present day events, is stated to be sixteen. Therefore, Siv and Phasma were both [[Reproduction|born]] in [[6 ABY]]. As ''Phasma'' also establishes that Keldo was a year older than Phasma, he must have been born on [[Parnassos]] in [[5 ABY]].</ref>
*[[Ben Solo]] on [[Chandrila]].<ref name="Empire's End" />
*[[Chesille Sabrond]] on [[Exegol]].<ref name="Sabrond Birthdate">According to ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', [[Chesille Sabrond]] was thirty [[Standard year|years]] old one year after the "[[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]]." Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the Starkiller Incident to [[34 ABY]], Sabrond was [[Reproduction|born]] in 5 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref>
