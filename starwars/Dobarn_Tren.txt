'''Dobarn Tren''' was a [[Barbadelan]] [[Gender|male]] [[priest]] of the [[Sacred Way]]. He traveled to the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] to try and aid the poorest citizens of [[Coronet City]] but chose to leave the planet after [[Law enforcement agency|local law enforcement]] came to incorrectly suspect that he was involved in the [[black market]]. Tren was present in [[Coronet City Spaceport|Coronet Spaceport]] when the criminals [[Han Solo|Han]] and [[Qi'ra]] triggered a security alert in the [[Spaceport|port]] in [[13 BBY]].

==Biography==
The [[Barbadelan]] [[Gender|male]] Dobarn Tren served as a [[priest]] of the [[Sacred Way]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], he traveled to [[Coronet City]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]], hoping to alleviate the suffering of the [[city]]'s poorest citizens. His work drew the attention of [[Law enforcement agency|local law enforcement]], which came to incorrectly suspect that he was buying and selling wares illegally on the [[black market]]. Rather than face deportation, Tren regretfully chose to voluntarily leave Corellia.<ref name="SoloOG" />

In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' that take place on [[Corellia]] to [[13 BBY]].</ref> the Barbadelan was walking through [[Coronet City Spaceport|Coronet Spaceport]], where he joined a queue to pass through one of the [[spaceport]]'s [[Imperial Office of Customs|Imperial customs]] gates in order to leave the planet. However, before Tren could reach a gate, the criminals [[Han Solo|Han]] and [[Qi'ra]]&mdash;who were several places ahead of Tren in the queue&mdash;triggered a security alert in the port after Han breached the customs gate. The gate was then sealed while Tren still remained in the queue.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Tren had [[Color|tan]] skin, orange tendrils, and black [[eye]]s.<ref name="Solo" />

==Equipment==
While in the spaceport, Tren wore black [[Clothing|robes]], [[boot]]s, and [[glove]]s, as well as a cowl that attached to several satchels he was carrying.<ref name="SoloOG" />
