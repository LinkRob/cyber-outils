'''Ewok Pilot''', or simply '''E.P.''', was a character in [[Ewok Pilot (comic)|comics by the same name]], written and illustrated by [[Roan Novachez]]. He was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Ewok/Legends|Ewok]] who was a [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]] [[Pilot/Legends|pilot]]. He flew alongside [[Ewok Pilot's Counterparts|two other men]] who were training him. In later comics, he was joined by [[Jawa Pilot]]. Although they used to be enemies, partly due to Ewok Pilot being jealous of Jawa Pilot's popularity, they were later friends. His origins were explored in [[Ewok Pilot 1|''Ewok Pilot #1'']].<ref name="barnes1">''[[Jedi Academy: Return of the Padawan]]'' {{C|Barnes & Noble exclusive edition}}</ref>

For his noble efforts in bringing [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] [[Blagotine]] to [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], he was made a member of the [[Galactic Republic Defense Fleet]].<ref name="JA:ROTP" />
