The '''Death Star medal''' was a campaign [[medal]] that was awarded to [[Rebel pilot|starfighter pilots]] of [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]] and [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] who flew against the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]] during the [[Battle of Yavin]]. The medal had the highest rate of posthumous nominations of any Alliance decoration. It was often worn alongside the [[Defense of Yavin medal]].<ref name="Lead" />

The medal was characterized by a single [[silver]] pin depicting the first [[Death Star]] with a small [[Aurebesh]] number one at the center. It was worn below a [[ribbon]] rack on a dress uniform, or on the collar of a [[flight suit]]. There were a rare number of [[pilot]]s who wore the medal, and were seen at with awe and admiration by younger colleagues and subordinates. The award was more rare than the [[Death Star II medal]]. The only member permitted to wear both the Death Star medal and Death Star II medal was [[Wedge Antilles]].<ref name="Lead" />
