"'''A Droid's Eye View'''" is a short story that was published in ''[[Galaxy Guide 3: The Empire Strikes Back]]''. It was also included in ''[[The Movie Trilogy Sourcebook]]''. It told the story of [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker's]] training on [[Dagobah/Legends|Dagobah]] from the perspective of [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]].
