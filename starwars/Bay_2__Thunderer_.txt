'''Bay 2''' was a [[hangar]] aboard the [[First Order]] [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Rendezvous Point" /> ''[[Thunderer]]''.<ref name="Timelines" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|2|8}}</ref> the [[Resistance pilot]] [[Venisa Doza]] and her [[astromech droid]] [[Torch]] entered the bay, hoping to escape off of the [[Star Destroyer]]. Torch planted explosives on a nearby [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighter]] and detonated them, causing a [[fire]] to spread in the hangar.<ref name="Rendezvous Point" />
