The '''Drez system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Corva sector]] of both the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It fell within grid square P-4 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained [[Drez]], the [[homeworld]] of the [[Drezar]] [[Cabuck]], who was [[Reproduction|born]] during the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> around [[166 BBY]]<ref name="Dates">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' establishes that [[Cabuck]] was thirty-four [[standard year]]s old during the events of ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which take place in [[132 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:TheAcolyteDate|here]]. As such, Cabuck must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[166 BBY]].</ref> but lived on the [[planet]] [[Ueda]]<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> by [[132 BBY]].<ref name="Dates" />
