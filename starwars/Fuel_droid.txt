'''Fuel droids''', or '''refueling droids''', were a type of [[droid]]s used to refuel [[starship]] in place of an organic operator, to reduce the risk of explosion while manipulating explosive liquid metal [[fuel]].<ref name="MF 3D">''[[Star Wars: Millennium Falcon: A 3D Owner's Guide]]''</ref>

[[Theed Spaceport]] on [[Naboo]] was equipped with at least four building-sized refueling droids, which were painted [[Color|yellow]].<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fuel droids made a brief appearance in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> They were first [[canon]]ically identified as refueling droids in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], an updated version<ref name="CL" /> of a [[2005]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] book of the [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|same name]].<ref name="CL 2005">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
