'''Elkeenar''' was a rocky wasteland [[planet]] located in the [[Penagosis system]]<ref name="Free Fall" /> in [[Wild Space]], in grid square K-21 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> While a member of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]], [[Poe Dameron]] visited the [[Marimkes Mountains]] on Elkeenar with [[Tomasso]].<ref name="Free Fall" />

{{Planet-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The novel ''[[Poe Dameron: Free Fall]]'' places Elkeenar in the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Free Fall" /> though this placing was later changed by the "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix, which placed it in Wild Space.<ref name="Appendix" />
