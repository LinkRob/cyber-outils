The '''C-10 plasburst cannon''' was a model of [[plasburst laser cannon]] that could be equipped to [[starship]]s. The cannon would charge up and launch a powerful [[laser]]. Equipping an [[EML-850 light freighter]] with a C-10 plasburst cannon cost two thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and required a [[X-FP-C28 ship computer]], twenty units of [[quadanium steel]], and eight [[heuristic processor]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics}}
During [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] paid for the services of a [[starship]] [[Technician|mechanic]] to equip the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her EML-850 [[light freighter]], with a C-10 plasburst cannon as well as providing materials to install it.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}
