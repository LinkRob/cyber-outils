"'''Blood Feud!'''" is an article that appeared in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' [[Star Wars Insider 124|124]]. The article was written by [[Leland Chee]] and explores the history of the conflict between the [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiees]] of [[Kashyyyk/Legends|Kashyyyk]] and the [[Trandoshan/Legends|Trandoshans]] of [[Trandosha/Legends|Trandosha]].
