'''''Darth Vader and Son''''' is a [[Canon|non-canon]] picture book written and illustrated by [[Jeffrey Brown]]. It is comprised of one-page comic illustrations that show [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] parenting [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], and it was first published on [[April 18]], [[2012]] by [[Chronicle Books]]. A sequel, ''[[Vader's Little Princess]]'', was published in [[2013]].

==Publisher's summary==
''What if Darth Vader took an active role in raising his son? What if "Luke, I am your father" was just a stern admonishment from an annoyed dad? In this hilarious and sweet comic reimagining, Darth Vader is a dad like any other&mdash;except with all the baggage of being the Dark Lord of the Sith. Celebrated artist Jeffrey Brown's delightful illustrations give classic ''Star Wars'' moments a fresh twist, presenting the trials and joys of parenting through the lens of a galaxy far, far away. Life lessons include lightsaber batting practice, using the Force to raid the cookie jar, Take Your Child to Work Day on the Death Star ("Er, he looks just like you, Lord Vader!"), and the special bond shared between any father and son.''

==Plot summary==
[[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]], who is raising a four-year-old [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke]], teaches him how to play baseball with a [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]], but Luke accidentally slices the ball in half. Vader tells Luke not to make bubbles in his drink at the bar in [[Mos Eisley/Legends|Mos Eisley]], then teaches him how to ride a bicycle.

Vader asks Luke if he does not like the eggs he made, then Luke uses [[The Force|the force]] to make himself cereal instead.

Luke points out [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2/Legends|R2D2]] buried on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], but Vader is too distracted sunbathing to pay attention. Then, after [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia]] dresses up Luke's toys, he complains to Vader about not wanting a sister. Luke steals from the cookie jar with the force, and as punishment, Vader does not let him go to [[Tosche Station/Legends|Tosche Station]] after his nap.

Luke asks where babies come from and Vader does not know how to reply, and then the two of them and [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando]] see [[Bantha/Legends|Banthas]] at a zoo. Luke complains that [[Greedo/Legends|Greedo]] pushed him, and Vader asks if Luke pushed Greedo first.

Luke trick-or-treats as a [[Stormtrooper/Legends|Stormtrooper]] and is asked if he is too short, and then plays in the trash on the [[Death Star/Legends|Death Star]]. Vader asks his underlings if they want to babysit Luke, and then attends Luke's award ceremony.

Vader has trouble motivating Luke to get ready for school, then teaches him checkers. At the toy store, Luke wants a toy [[Gungan/Legends|Gungan]], but Vader says that it is not the toy he is looking for. Luke replies with, "Yes, it is".

Luke gets treated for a minor injury and then Vader threatens him with timeout if he does not put his toys away.

Luke is asked to shovel the snow but blows a droid up instead, then Luke plays in the rain and asks his father if he can have a treat after the two of them rule the galaxy.

Vader takes Luke to work, where he annoys him, and then Luke is scared of a [[Wookiee]]. He gives Vader a tie, who does not like it but accepts it to be polite. Luke then cries, rides on R2D2, and is a messy eater at the table.

Vader tells Luke not to walk on a beam as it is not safe, and then Vader is disturbed by Luke's impatience. Vader buys Luke an expensive birthday present, and on [[Endor/Legends|Endor]], Luke refrains from talking to the Stormtroopers because his father said not to talk to strangers. The Skywalkers get ice creams, but Luke drops his, so Vader gives his up.

Vader asks Luke for privacy when changing, and then Luke accidentally hits him in the crotch with a soccer ball. Luke does not want to go to bed, then asks to keep an alien. He falls asleep on Vader's arm on the couch, and then is nosy when Vader is building something.

Vader says that destroyers did not exist when he was Luke's age, and then tells Luke to accompany him because "it is the only way". Vader reads Luke a bedtime story about [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]], scaring Luke.

He then wants [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Ben Kenobi]] to tell Vader that he is old enough to ride in a pod. Luke listens to [[Ewok/Legends|Ewok]] music on a phonograph, to Vader's annoyance, and then pretends to be a [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi]] waving a stick.

Vader and Luke play hide and seek, and then Vader tickles Luke with the force. Luke draws the two of them, then stymies him by asking why it is called a "Death Star". Luke builds a spaceship out of LEGO and then tells Vader he loves him.
