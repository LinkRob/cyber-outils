'''Bay Three''' was a [[Hangar|docking bay]] in [[Niima Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. When the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] took [[BB-8]] to Niima Outpost, she told the [[droid]] there was a [[Merchant|trader]] in Bay Three named [[Horvins]] who may have been willing to give BB-8 a lift offworld.<ref name="TFAn" />
