'''Fruun''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served in the [[Galactic Empire]] in the [[Ryloth system]] five [[Standard year|years]] after the end of the [[Clone Wars]]. Fruun served as a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] in the [[Imperial Military]], under the command of [[Colonel]] [[Belkor Dray]]. He was one of hundreds whose loyalty was bought by Dray, either through blackmail or favors.<ref name="LOTS" />
