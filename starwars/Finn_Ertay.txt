'''Finn Ertay''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]] who served the [[Galactic Republic]] as a [[Jedi]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Ertay and a [[Unidentified Nikto Jedi|Kajain'sa'Nikto Jedi]] encountered the brothers [[Maul]] and [[Savage Opress]] on a [[Unidentified Outer Rim spaceport|spaceport]] somewhere in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The two Jedi engaged Maul and Opress in a [[lightsaber duel]], where Ertay was impaled by Maul while attempting to save the life of her comrade, who [[Death|died]] shortly afterwards.

==Biography==
Finn Ertay<ref name="GI Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode209.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Grievous Intrigue}}</ref> was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Jedi]] who served the [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date note 2">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Landing at Point Rain]]" to [[21 BBY]], and {{HelmetCollectionCite|76|Highlights of the Saga}} dates the [[death]] of [[Ponds]], as seen in the episode "[[Lethal Trackdown]]," to the same [[Standard year|year]]. Since {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}} places "[[Grievous Intrigue]]" between "Landing at Point Rain" and "Lethal Trackdown," its events, which include Ertay's attendance of the meeting, must take place in the same year.</ref> Finn Ertay was present at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple's]] [[Jedi Temple communication center|communication center]], where she watched a message from [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] alongside other Jedi. Ertay stood to the left of the [[Mon Calamari]] Jedi<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> [[Eekar Oki]],<ref name="GI Ep Guide" /> and watched as Grievous [[torture]]d [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeth Koth]]. She, Oki, and several others left the room soon after after [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] dismissed the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]].<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of ''[[Ghosts of Mortis]]'' and "[[Water War]]" to [[20 BBY]]. As the episode "[[Wookiee Hunt]]," which includes Ertay's appearance at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]], takes place between those two episodes according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, the episode's events must also take place in 20 BBY.</ref> Ertay was present at a [[Hangar|landing pad]] at the Temple's exterior standing next to Oki and several [[Wookiee]]s. There, she welcomed back [[Jedi]] trainees [[Jinx]], [[O-Mer]], and [[Ahsoka Tano]], who had been [[Battle on Wasskah|rescued]] from [[Wasskah]] by the Wookiee General [[Tarfful]] and returned to the [[Jedi temple|temple]] aboard the ''[[Halo]]'', the [[bounty hunter]] [[Sugi (bounty hunter)|Sugi's]] [[starship]].<ref name="WH">{{TCW|Wookiee Hunt}}</ref>

[[File:DuelAtSpaceport-Revival.png|left|thumb|220x220px|Ertay comes to the aid of her Nikto comrade, shortly before being killed by Darth Maul.]]
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date note 1" /> Ertay and a [[Unidentified Nikto Jedi|Nikto]] Jedi [[Duel at unidentified Outer Rim spaceport|dueled]] former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] and his brother [[Savage Opress]] at an [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Unidentified Outer Rim spaceport|spaceport]]. The [[Nikto]] was injured by Opress and Ertay rolled underneath Maul to aid her ally and block Opress' strike, however Maul impaled her through her exposed back and [[Death|killed]] her. Ertay's comrade was also killed shortly afterwards.<ref name="Revival" />

==Personality and traits==
Finn Ertay had [[Color|blue]] [[eye]]s and green skin. Ertay was in close proximity with high-ranking [[Jedi Master]]s such as [[Mace Windu]] and [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] when she was at the temple.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> When she saw her fellow Jedi in danger during their fight with the brothers, she quickly broke off her fight with Maul to defend him, which cost her her life when Maul stabbed her from behind.<ref name="Revival" />

==Powers and abilities==
Finn Ertay possessed skill in [[lightsaber combat]], managing to briefly [[Lightsaber duel|defend]] herself against Maul and Savage Opress. Ertay was somewhat athletic and fast, being able to slide in between Maul's legs to block a blow from Savage that would have killed her comrade, but her skill was not sufficient to save her life or defeat her opponents.<ref name="Revival" />

==Equipment==
Finn Ertay wielded a blue [[lightsaber]] while in combat, and wore brown [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]] in combat<ref name="Revival" /> and at the Jedi Temple. She also wore headgear which covered most of her head, save for her [[lekku]], but including her ear-cones.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

==Behind the scenes==
Finn Ertay first appeared, albeit unnamed, in "[[Grievous Intrigue]]," the ninth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> which first aired on [[January 1]], [[2010]].<ref name="Episode Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref> Ertay was identified by name in the episode's accompanying episode guide on [[StarWars.com]], in its "Trivia & Details" section.<ref name="GI Ep Guide" />
