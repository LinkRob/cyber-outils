A '''13-BSG-filament enhancer''' was a type of [[ZX1-capacitor]] tool modification. While disguised as a [[First Order technician]] aboard the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Titan]]'' during a [[Infiltration of the Titan|mission]] to [[Thief|steal]] a [[trans-binary deflector]]<ref name="STS" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|2|11}}</ref> the [[Resistance]] member [[Kazuda Xiono]] enthusiastically claimed to [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper 3 (Titan)|two]] [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper 4 (Titan)|stormtroopers]] that his ZX1-capacitor had a 13-BSG-filament enhancer attachment, successfully convincing the pair that he was a [[technician]].<ref name="STS" />

==Behind the scenes==
13-BSG-filament-enhancer was mentioned in "[[Station to Station]],"<ref name="STS" /> the eleventh episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[December 15]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney release">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref>
