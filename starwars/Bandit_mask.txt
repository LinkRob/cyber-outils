A '''bandit mask''' was a type of [[Clothing|mask]] wore in [[the galaxy]]. It was green in [[color]] with a narrow visor.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> one such mask had been stored in container behind an [[Ray shield|energy barrier]] at the abandoned [[Kerro's Speakeasy]] in [[Mirogana]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] visited the [[cantina|speakeasy]] after she upgraded her [[slicing unit]] with the help of the [[slicer]] [[Aila Bren]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}
