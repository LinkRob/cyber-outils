During the [[The galaxy|galaxy]]-wide [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] was a series of '''battles''' on a [[Unidentified rocky planet|rocky planet]]. In one of the battles, [[High Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], and their [[clone trooper]]s, including [[Sister]], fought Separatist [[battle droid]]s. Sister participating in burying her fallen brothers that had died in the fighting.<ref name="QH" />
