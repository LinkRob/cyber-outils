'''Cavo's''' was an establishment on<ref name="Kassa" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Blevin">{{Databank|supervisor-blevin|Supervisor Blevin}}</ref> [[Ferrix]] where drinks were served. In [[5 BBY]], when formulating an alibi to excuse his absence the previous [[Local day|night]], the [[human]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] collaborated with his friend [[Brasso]], establishing a false story that the two had been out drinking together. Andor told Brasso a detailed account of their alleged actions, saying that Brasso wanted to go to Cavo's for drinks before Andor refused due to the amount of patrons there that he owed [[Currency|money]].<ref name="Kassa" />
