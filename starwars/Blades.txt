'''Blades''' was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[cyborg]] and part of the [[M.A.R. Corps]]. Together with [[Tandorvo]], [[Chamdaka]] and [[Masakint]], they fought the [[Galactic Empire]] at [[Zee-Nine City Seven]]. During this [[Skirmish at Zee-Nine City Seven|skirmish]], they all were turned into unwilling [[soldier]]s of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] through [[droid brain]]s forcefully inserted into their bodies.<ref name="Vader 42" /> Blades was the only survivor of the [[Third Rebellion of Zee-Nine City Seven]], a key part of their identity.<ref name="Vader 49" />
