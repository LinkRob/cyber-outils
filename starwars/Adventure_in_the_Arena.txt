"'''Adventure in the Arena'''" is a [[2017]] short story published in the anthology ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories Strike Back]]'' that is a partial adaptation of the [[Star Wars saga|''Star Wars'' saga]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. It is written by [[S.T. Bende]] and illustrated by [[Pilot Studio]].
