'''Fleet''' was a possible winning hand in the "[[Corellian Spike]]" variant of [[Sabacc]]. Amounting to zero, it consisted of four cards of a kind (except a 10) and a zero card (e.g., +5, -5, +5, -5, and 0). The lower integer won (e.g., four 3s beat four 6s).<ref name="Galaxy's Edge Deck">[[Sabacc (Galaxy's Edge)|Sabacc at ''Galaxy's Edge'']]</ref>
