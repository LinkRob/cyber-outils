'''Aki Toyosaki''' ([[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]]: 豊崎愛生, ''Toyosaki Aki'')<ref name="JPN Cast">{{Disney|subdomain=starwars|domain=co.jp|url=news/20250926_01|text=日本語オリジナル版＜本予告＞が全世界解禁！『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』Volume3|date=2026-09-26}}</ref> is a Japanese voice actress who voiced [[Woopas]] in "[[The Song of Four Wings]]," a short film from the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 1|third season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]''.<ref name="SW">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-3-cast-announce|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 3 Cast Announced with New Trailer}}</ref> Toyosaki is known in Japan for her roles as Yui Hirasawa in [[Wikipedia:K-On!|''K-On!'']], Aoi Inuyama in [[Wikipedia:Laid-Back Camp|''Laid-Back Camp'']] and Yunyun in [[Wikipedia:KonoSuba|''KonoSuba'']].<ref name="JP" />
