'''Bree''' was a [[human]] [[Jedi Initiate]] who was part of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].

==Biography==
===Training on Starlight===
{{Quote|How can we be both afraid and brave?|Bree to Yoda|<ref name="Brave" />}}
Sometime around [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] Bree, [[Jon (Jedi)|Jon]], and [[Toko]] were [[Jedi training|training]] together in the [[Starlight Beacon's Jedi temple|Jedi temple]] on [[Starlight Beacon]]. During a particular exercise, the [[training droid]] [[8D-8D]] shared with the three younglings the possible adventures they may experience as [[Jedi Knight]]s. However, the younglings quickly became afraid rather than excited. When [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] became aware of their concerns, he aimed to teach them a valuable lesson on bravery with the help of of the [[Wookiee]] [[Padawan]], [[Burryaga Agaburry]]. After hearing Agaburry's earliest memory of overcoming fear, the three younglings accompanied Yoda and Agaburry aboard a [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] to learn this lesson firsthand.

[[File:BreeMeditating-JediBrave.png|thumb|200px|left|Bree meditating on Elphrona]]
After traveling to the [[planet]]s [[Geonosis]] and [[Mon Cala]], the Longbeam landed on [[Elphrona]]. While on the planet, Bree shared with Master Yoda that she feared being alone. Yoda shared that no one is truly ever alone and encouraged Bree to venture away from the Longbeam to meditate in solitude on the planet's surface. While meditating, Bree realized that her active imagination could give her strength and company when she was alone, realizing that she did not need to feel lonely at all. 

After all three younglings had learned a personal lesson on fear and bravery, Yoda brought them to [[Utapau]] to learn one last lesson before returning to Starlight Beacon. Here in the darkness of the planet's vast caves, Yoda taught them that Jedi carry [[Light side of the Force|the light]] with them at all times. The three younglings used this philosophy to guide them through the caves and back to their Longbeam. After returning to Starlight Beacon, the younglings could rely on their new lessons when they experienced moments of fear.<ref name="Brave" />

===The Guardian Protocols===
[[File:LightsaberTraining-THRSOS1.png|thumb|300px|right|Bree, Toko, and Jon training]]
After the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]] in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Jedi High Council]] called for all Jedi to return to [[Coruscant]] for their safety.<ref name="Edge of Balance 2">[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 2|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 2]]</ref> Bree was present in the [[Galactic Senate Chamber]] when members of the Jedi High Council announced the reimplementation of the [[Guardian Protocols]]. Bree shared with Jon and Toko her uncertainty over the announcement that Jedi younglings would begin to use real [[lightsaber]]s instead of [[training lightsaber]]s. However, in the weeks and months after the announcement, Bree commenced her lightsaber training in earnest.<ref name="SOS" />

Bree was sent to deliver a message to Yoda who was under the temple fighting a [[Blight]] infection. She felt scared but continued descending the stairs until she reached the bottom. She ran into [[Eve Byre]] and [[Stalwick]] who joined the group of Jedi holding back the Blight so Yoda could leave. Bree helped him as he walked away from the Blight and accompanied him back to the upper levels of the temple.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
