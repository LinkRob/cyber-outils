'''Fritzle Fries''' were a fried [[Foodstuff|snack]] that was crispy, [[Color|golden brown]] in color, and stick-shaped. [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] would have cravings for [[sandwich]]es with piles of the fries. According to Tuggs, the best in [[the galaxy]] were cooked by the [[Cooking droid|kitchen droids]] at his old friend [[Freerago]]'s [[satellite]] [[Restaurant|diner]],<ref name="Cookbook" /> [[Freerago's Satellite Diner and Motel]],<ref name="Last Shot" /> which orbited the [[planet]] [[Hosnian Prime]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

Around [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the flashback events of ''[[Last Shot]]'' that feature [[Han Solo]], including the events at [[Freerago's Satellite Diner and Motel]], to around [[3 BBY]].</ref> the [[human]] [[Han Solo]] consumed the fries along with other foods and beverages during a long, boring stakeout at the diner.<ref name="Last Shot" /> The [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the [[Hosnian system]] by the [[First Order]] in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> was presumed to have ended the snack's availability from Freerago.<ref name="Cookbook" /> Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date: Cookbook">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs included a recipe for Fritzle Fries in his [[Book|cookbook]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'' and recommended seasoning them with his [[Nerfsteak Seasoning]], dipping them in [[emulsauce]], or serving them as a side dish with his entrée [[Fried Endorian Tip-Yip]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fritzle Fries first appeared in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Last Shot]]'', written by [[Daniel José Older]].<ref name="Last Shot" /> They were first pictured with their recipe in ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]] and published in [[2019]]. The book classifies the [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe as meeting [[Wikipedia:Vegetarianism|vegetarian]] and [[Wikipedia:Gluten-free diet|gluten-free]] dietary considerations. It uses the ingredients [[Wikipedia:Tapioca balls|tapioca pearls]], [[Wikipedia:Yogurt|plain yogurt]], warm water, [[Wikipedia:Parmigiano-Reggiano|parmesan cheese]], [[Wikipedia:Cayenne pepper|cayenne pepper]], and [[Wikipedia:Rice flour|rice flour]], along with an oil such as [[Wikipedia:Canola oil|canola oil]] for frying.<ref name="Cookbook" />
