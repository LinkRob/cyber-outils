'''Balthamus''', also known as '''Number Ten''', was a [[medical droid]] in service of [[Fyzen Gor]]'s [[Brotherhood of Wire and Bone]], specifically of the [[Original Dozen]] created by Gor himself. He wore a hood and his droid hands had been replaced with two grayish, bloated human hands.<ref name="Last Shot" /> In [[7 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> he was in service on the moon [[Grava]] when [[Han Solo]] and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] arrived there. He brought them to [[Seven-Seven Dirgeos]] as they requested but was later destroyed when Lando severed his head from his body with his blaster and took his head with him. Lando later used a map from Balthamus' head to find the location of the [[Phylanx Redux Transmitter]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
