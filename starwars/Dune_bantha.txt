{{Quote|In addition to the common bantha, two other species are known to exist: the diminutive dwarf bantha, which dwells in the canyons and cliffs of Tatooine’s outermost deserts, and the less furry dune bantha, which is capable of surviving in the heat (and severe drought!) of the equatorial zone.|From the journal of Ardis San Tekka|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
The '''dune bantha''' was a [[species]] of [[bantha]], a type of [[Diet|omnivorous]] [[mammal]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the<ref name="Bestiary" /> [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. Dune banthas were less [[Hair|furry]] then the other two known bantha species&mdash;the [[Sentience|non-sentient]] common bantha and the [[dwarf bantha]]&mdash;and lived in the equatorial region of Tatooine where they had to survive extreme [[Temperature|heat]] and severe droughts. [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] [[Director of Creature Cartography]] [[Ardis San Tekka]] mentioned dune banthas in an entry on banthas in her [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]]<ref name="Bestiary" /> at some point after the [[Battle of Exegol]] in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BestiaryVol1Date}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], dune banthas were mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[S.T. Bende]].<ref name="Bestiary" /> The [[Dune bantha/Legends|species]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[The Wildlife of Star Wars: A Field Guide]]'', a [[2001]] reference book written by [[Terryl Whitlatch]] and [[Bob Carrau]] with illustrations by Whitlatch.<ref name="Wildlife">''[[The Wildlife of Star Wars: A Field Guide]]''</ref> The species then appeared for the first time in the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] ''[[Star Wars Galaxies]]'', which was developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment.<ref name="SWG">{{SWGcite|exp=AED|type=location|Deserts|Tatooine}}</ref>
