'''DIS-T81''' was a [[droid tri-fighter]] that served in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. At one point, DIS-T81 attacked a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor]] in [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="X-wing" /> In another instance, DIS-T81 flew near wreckage in space and a Republic [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|''Consular''-class Charger c70 light cruiser]] alongside other [[droid]] tri-fighters.<ref name="SWA">{{Armada|set=Separatist Fighter Squadrons Expansion Pack|cardname=DIS-T81}}</ref>
