'''''Artistically Done: The Life and Times of Grand Admiral Thrawn''''' was an analysis of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] [[author]]ed by [[Gre Halhg]].<ref name="Rise" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] referred to the analysis as groundbreaking and recommended it in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|But&hellip; it was so artistically done.|Thrawn's last words in Legends|<ref name="LastCommand">''[[The Last Command]]''</ref>}}
''Artistically Done: The Life and Times of Grand Admiral Thrawn'' was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, "But&hellip; it was so artistically done." were [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn's]] final words.<ref name="LastCommand" />
