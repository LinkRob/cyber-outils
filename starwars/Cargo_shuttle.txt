'''Cargo shuttles''' were a type of [[shuttle]] specifically designed to ferry cargo between locations. The [[Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle|''Zeta''-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle]] was a model of cargo shuttle manufactured by the [[Telgorn Corporation]] and used extensively by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="SWE CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>
