'''Bareen Krill''' was a [[Gender|female]]<ref name="HRA 8" /> [[Er'Kit (species)|Er'Kit]]<ref name="HRA 10" /> [[pirate]] who was a member of the [[Nihil]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Alongside her [[Sabata Krill's family|sister]] [[Sabata Krill|Sabata]], Bareen was a member of a [[Krix Kamerat's cell|cell]] led by [[Krix Kamerat]], a protégé of [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]]. The Krill sisters participated in an [[Battle of Takodana (Nihil conflict)|attack]] by the cell on [[Takodana]]. As the attack was beginning, Kamerat suspected one of the sisters of being the person he had overheard expressing concern about carrying out the attack because of the [[Jedi]] presence on the [[planet]], but the speaker did not identify themselves. After the Jedi [[Savina Besatrix Malagán|Sav Malagán]] repelled the first wave of the attack, she found the Krill sisters lying amidst the debris in the [[forest]], whereupon they asked for help.<ref name="HRA 8" />
