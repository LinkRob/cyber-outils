A '''bombardment''' occurred on the [[Tak-Beam complex]] on the [[planet]] [[Durkteel]] on the same [[Standard day|day]] as the [[Battle of Endor]] when the [[Galactic Empire]] dispatched three [[Star Destroyer]]s to Durkteel after it received rumors of a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] meeting occurring near the [[planet]]'s industrial center. After jamming transmissions, the Empire decided not to make mass arrests or demand surrender and instead, the [[Star Destroyer]]s launched an [[orbital bombardment]]. One of the many casualties was the rebel [[Hendri Underholt]]. Many [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Galactic Civil War]], a rebel artifact, ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', was recovered by the [[Planetary Governor|planetary governor]], [[Vaxvissh Kal Ness]], after an [[Excavation on Durkteel|excavation]] for a new [[comm tower]] revealed the artifacts. Eventually, seeing that the artifacts were old war-time rebel relics, intended for senior members of the Alliance, made their way to [[Major]] [[Heck Ensio]], [[Master Archivist]] [[Mordianius]], and to [[Commander]] [[Korr Sella]]. Sella then sent it to [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[Resistance]], as Organa was one of the few remaining senior members of the Alliance.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
