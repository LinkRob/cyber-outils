Sometime during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" /> by [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The flashbacks of [[Obi-Wan 4|''Obi-Wan'' 4]] take place after the [[Devaron massacre]] from "[[Monster]]," which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[20 BBY]], and before the end of the [[Clone Wars]], which ''Star Wars: Galactic Atlas'' also dates to [[19 BBY]].</ref> a '''Commerce Guild hub''' was established by the [[Commerce Guild]] on the [[planet]] [[Minntooine]]. Home to several residents, it spanned several structures and tiled flooring. Later, between 20 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[19 BBY]], the hub was attacked by the [[Death Wind]] execution [[squad]], led by rogue [[Roonian]] [[Commander]] [[Mekedrix]]. In the attack, the hub's residents were all [[Death|killed]], with the facility itself being set in flames, damaged from the attack.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />
