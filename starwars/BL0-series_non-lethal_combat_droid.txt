The '''BL0-series non-lethal combat droid''' was a model of [[Battle droid|combat droid]] created by [[Arakyd Industries]] for non-lethal enforcement. The BL0-series was designed to neutralize its targets rather than [[Death|killing]] them, using an array of non-lethal countermeasures including [[stun whip]]s. The [[droid]] scuttled on three [[Limb|legs]] and had robust hyperarticulated arms. Their effectiveness in combat led many of them to be repurposed as [[security droid]]s.<ref name="Disciples of Harmony" />
