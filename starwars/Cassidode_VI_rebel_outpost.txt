A tiny and ill-supplied '''rebel outpost''' was located on<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Cassidode system]]</ref> terrestrial astronomical object [[Cassidode VI]]. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[Rebel pilot|flier]] [[Farns Monsbee]] was the sole [[Snub-fighter|snubfighter]] [[pilot]]<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> protecting<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2018 Topps Star Wars Galactic Files|cardname=Farns Monsbee|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/170092/cid/11644227}}</ref> the outpost and flew the base's only [[starfighter]], an [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]],<ref name="HC75">{{HelmetCollectionCite|75|Databank A-Z}}</ref> for many solo flight [[Standard hour|hours]].<ref name="HC71">{{HelmetCollectionCite|71|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref>

At some point prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]]<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Monsbee was transferred to [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]]. His [[time]] on Cassidode VI meant he had logged more solo flight hours than anyone in his new [[squadron]],<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> with the long periods of isolation resulting in Monsbee becoming chatty in the recreation room.<ref name="HC71" />

==Behind the scenes==
The outpost was first mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" />
