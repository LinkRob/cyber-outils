The '''Boothi system''' was located in the [[Abrion sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was situated in grid square S-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. [[Boothi IV]]<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> and the [[planet]] [[Boothi XII]]<ref name="DoctorAphra1">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 1|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 1]]</ref> were both located in the Boothi [[Star system|system]].<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Boothi IV]]|system=the Boothi [[Star system|system]]}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Boothi system was first mentioned in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''.<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
