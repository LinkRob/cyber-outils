==Behind the scenes==
The Agbui attempt to precipitate a civil war in the Ascendancy served as the main plot of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', the second installment of Zahn's ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Greater Good" /> The plot was first alluded to via a promise from "[[Jixtus]]" to [[Qilori]] in ''Greater Good''{{'s}} [[2020]] prequel, ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
