The '''Cirilia system''' was located in [[Lannik Space]] within the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> The [[star system]] was situated in grid square R-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. The Cirilia system shared its name with<ref name="Appendix" /> the beverage [[Cirilian Sour Paste]] and the [[Salad|dish]] [[Cirilian Noodle Salad]], which were both created by the [[Gilliand]] [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza]], known as "Aunt Z."<ref name="LifeDayCookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Cirilia system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
