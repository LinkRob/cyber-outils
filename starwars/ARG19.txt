'''ARG19''', also known as "'''Aggie'''," was a mechanically simple, [[Color|yellow]]-plated [[droid]] who resided on the [[planet]] [[Mina-Rau]] around [[1 BBY]]. She became friends with the droid [[B2EMO]].<ref name="DB" /> In 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="JKE">{{Andor|Jedha, Kyber, Erso}}</ref> ARG19 and B2EMO raced alongside a table.<ref name="JKE AD">{{Andor|Jedha, Kyber, Erso}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref>
