During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and [[Galactic Empire]], the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] fought a '''battle''' against the Empire in the canyons of the astronomical object [[Xagobah]]<ref name="Battlefront" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> The battle came as part of [[Operation Ringbreaker]], where the 61st targeted a number of soft Imperial targets to force [[Imperial Military|military]] redeployments from the Empire's [[Kuat Drive Yards]] to weaken its security for a final attack.<ref name="Battlefront" />

The 61st secured a victory, sustaining some casualties between that conflict and one [[Battle of Naator|fought]] on the<ref name="Battlefront" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> [[Naator]]. The rebel unit, however, did not ultimately complete Operation Ringbreaker.<ref name="Battlefront" /> When the campaign was examined by the [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service]], [[General]] [[Crix Madine]] listed Xagobah among the targets in a report on Operation Ringbreaker's first phase, which was later included among an archive of non-electric documents called ''[[The Rebel Files]]'' by [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The battle of Xagobah was first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
