A '''bantha cheese hoagie''' was a type of [[sandwich]] sold in [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. One of its primary ingredients was [[bantha cheese]]. In [[1 BBY]], the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]] [[Buford]] bought a [[bantha]] [[cheese]] hoagie to [[Diet|eat]], though he accidentally lost it to his [[Candace's C.O.|commanding officer]]. He later reunited with the lost sandwich and ate it.

==Description==
{{Dialogue|Baljeet|Candace, did you get the socks?|Candace|Mission accomplished.|Buford|I got a hoagie!|attr=Baljeet, Buford, and Candace|src=<ref name="Phineas" />}}
A bantha cheese hoagie was a long [[sandwich]] filled with a variety of ingredients, including [[Color|green]] [[vegetable]]s and [[bantha cheese]] held between two slices of [[bread]]. It was [[Diet|edible]] by [[human]]s.<ref name="Phineas" />

==History==
{{Quote|My only regret is that I ate that hoagie.|Buford|<ref name="Phineas" />}}
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{PnFDate|1}}</ref> a [[bantha]] [[cheese]] hoagie was purchased by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]] [[Buford]] while he was on a [[Mission to buy socks for Darth Vader|mission]] to buy<ref name="Phineas" /> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] a new pair of [[Clothing|socks]] with fellow [[trooper]]s [[Baljeet]] and [[Candace]] at [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[desert]]<ref name="Phineas" /> [[planet]]<ref name="SWCL" /> [[Tatooine]]. The sandwich was kept in a [[paper]] bag and later accidentally given to [[Candace's C.O.|Candace's commanding officer]].<ref name="Phineas" />

[[File:VaderForceChokesCandacesCO-PhineasAndFerb.png|325px|thumb|left|Darth Vader Force chokes a commanding officer for handing him a sandwich instead of the socks he ordered.]]
Not long after, the [[commanding officer]] presented the paper bag to Vader aboard the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], thinking it was a pair of socks. Disappointing the [[Sith]] with his failure, the [[officer]] was [[Force choke|choked]] to [[death]] by Vader, and the sandwich was thrown away and ended up in<ref name="Phineas" /> [[garbage masher 3263827]].<ref name="SWCL" /> Soon after, the Imperial [[scientist]] [[Heinz Darthenshmirtz]] went into the [[garbage compactor]] and asked the [[dianoga]]<ref name="Phineas" /> [[Omi]]<ref name="The Baptist">{{StoryCite|story=The Baptist|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> to give him objects that Vader interacted with in the hopes of absorbing [[Dark side of the Force|Dark Force]] from them, including the bantha cheese hoagie. Darthenshmirtz later dropped the sandwich due to his altercation with the [[Platypus]] [[Perry (Platypus)|Perry]], a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[agent]].<ref name="Phineas" />

Eventually, Buford reunited with his sandwich when he found it on the floor aboard the [[Death Star]] and promptly ate it, even though it was wet due to previously going through the dirty [[water]]s of the garbage compactor. He later quickly regretted his decision when he tried to flee from the [[battle station]], rubbing his belly.<ref name="Phineas" />
