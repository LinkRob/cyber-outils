'''''Fortnite Battle Royale''''' is a [[Wikipedia:Free-to-play|free-to-play]] [[Wikipedia:Battle royale game|battle royale]] game mode within the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Fortnite]]'' developed and published by [[Epic Games]]. It was first released on [[September 26]], [[2017]].<ref name="BR EA" /> 

Several of its updates have included ''[[Star Wars]]'' content, beginning with the ''[[Fortnite X Star Wars]]'' event which occurred between [[December 14]], [[2019]] and [[January 7]], [[2020]] to promote [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker'']].

==Plot summary==
{{FortniteSummary|FNBR}}

===Chapter 2: Season 1 - New World===
{{Quote|At last the work of generations is complete. The great error is corrected. The day of victory is at hand. The day of revenge. The day of the Sith!|[[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] broadcast|audio=PalpatinesBroadcast.flac|<ref name="Fortnite X Star Wars">[[Fortnite X Star Wars|''Fortnite'' X ''Star Wars'']]</ref>}}
The [[w:c:fortnite:Zero Point|Zero Point]], an enigmatic being and the creator of all realities in the [[w:c:fortnite:The Omniverse|Omniverse]], reformed the [[w:c:fortnite:Athena|original Island]] into a [[w:c:fortnite:Apollo|new one]] after the original was destroyed by a [[w:c:fortnite:Black Hole|Black Hole]]. An event is soon held on this new Island at [[w:c:fortnite:Risky Reels|Risky Reels]], during which the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' appears. But the ''Falcon'' is not alone, as just when it is about to land, a [[First Order]] [[Star Destroyer]] [[fleet]] arrives from [[hyperspace]]. The ''Falcon'' is pursued by several [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]], but successfully destroys them and lands. The fleet later returns, however, and the ''Falcon'' must return to the air to fight them. [[Darth Sidious]] sends out a [[Palpatine's broadcast|broadcast]] announcing that it is the "day of the [[Sith]]."

===Chapter 2: Season 5 - Zero Point===
{{Quote|Landing was a little rough. Better check on the Razor Crest. Hm. Cargo went flying. Better track it down.|The Mandalorian just after crashing on the Island|<ref name="FNBR">''[[Fortnite Battle Royale]]''</ref>}}
With the Zero Point left exposed and unstable by severe damage done to [[w:c:fortnite:The Bridge|The Bridge]] by [[w:c:fortnite:Galactus|Galactus]], [[w:c:fortnite:Loopers|Loopers]] are able to escape the the captivity of the [[w:c:fortnite:Imagined Order|Imagined Order]]. To stop this from happening, [[w:c:fortnite:Doctor Slone|Doctor Slone]] orders [[w:c:fortnite:Agent Jones|Agent Jones]] to re-enter the Island, stop potential escapees, and re-seal The Bridge. As part of this effort, Jones hires several individuals including [[Din Djarin|the Mandalorian]], a [[bounty hunter]] from another reality. The Mandalorian arrives via the ''[[Razor Crest]]'', but crashes his [[gunship]] near the Island's center and loses his cargo. Doing battle with Loopers around the Island, the Mandalorian eventually finds his way to [[w:c:fortnite:Kit's Cantina|Kit's Cantina]].

===Chapter 3: Season 2 - Resistance===
{{Quote|Mission failure is not an option.|An unidentified Stormtrooper|<ref name="FNBR" />}}
The Island is flipped by [[w:c:fortnite:The Foundation|The Foundation]] in order to close the [[w:c:fortnite:Cube Queen|Cube Queen's]] rift to [[w:c:fortnite:The Last Reality|The Last Reality]], revealing a [[w:c:fortnite:Artemis|new landscape]] on the other side. Eventually the Imagined Order, still stuck on the other side of the Island, digs their way through and begins [[w:c:fortnite:The Collider|their plan]] to destroy [[w:c:fortnite:The Loop|The Loop]]. To stop them, [[w:c:fortnite:The Seven|The Seven]] rebuild the [[w:c:fortnite:Mecha Team Leader|Mecha Team Leader]]. Around this same time, a [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] appears in the sky, [[stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] are deployed across the Island, and several [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] checkpoints are erected. With the Zero Point again exposed, it shifts between realities, offering Agent Jones and The Foundation a glimpse of a [[lightsaber]] [[lightsaber duel|duel]] between [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his former apprentice, [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]], who has recently joined forces with the Imagined Order.

===Chapter 3: Season 3 - Vibin'===
{{Quote|The Relic is in the possession of a "Darth Vader." And our only option is to take it back by force.|AMIE|<ref name="FNBR" />}}
The Zero Point's exposure leads to the formation of the [[w:c:fortnite:Reality Tree|Reality Tree]]. The Galactic Empire, this time bringing [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] Darth Vader in a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class shuttle]], arrive to the Island for a second time, this time in search of [[Sith holocron]], simply known to the residents of the Island as "the Relic." This leads to [[w:c:fortnite:The Scientist|The Scientist]], a key member of The Seven assisted by his artificial intelligence [[w:c:fortnite:AMIE|AMIE]], believing that the [[Holocron]]'s appearance alongside Relic of others realities may be the Zero Point trying to communicate. Docking Bays [[Imperial Docking Bay Vega|Vega]], [[Imperial Docking Bay Alpha|Alpha]], [[Imperial Docking Bay Deimos|Deimos]], [[Imperial Docking Bay Eclipse|Eclipse]], and [[Imperial Docking Bay Radar|Radar]] are constructed to accommodate Vader's continued visits to the Island. Vader is defeated by The Looper but in their battle, the Sith Holocron was thrown into the ocean.

===Chapter 3: Season 4 - Paradise===
{{Quote|Do it for the rebellion.|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="FNBR" />}}
Vader and the Empire return to the Island after the defeat of the Imagined Order and the appearance of the [[w:c:fortnite:Chrome|Chrome]], but their intentions are not known this time around. [[Luke Skywalker]] guides Loopers to take on the Empire in the name of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]] and defeat Vader, Luke's father, once more.

===Chapter 4: Season 2 - MEGAメガ===
{{Quote|A new Empire has risen. We must prepare for what's next.|Unknown|<ref name="FNBR" />}}
After the previous Island's destruction by [[w:c:fortnite:The Herald|The Herald]], an agent of the Last Reality, a [[w:c:fortnite:Asteria|new Island]] is crafted by the Zero Point from different realities using [[w:c:fortnite:Zero Fusion|Zero Fusion]]. Some time after, [[clone trooper]] regiments of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] including the [[501st Legion|501st Legion's]] [[Torrent Company]] and the [[212th Attack Battalion]] arrive on the Island and set up [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] checkpoints to root out [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]], by order of [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. Anakin Skywalker, Obi-Wan Kenobi, and [[Maul]] appear in [[hologram]] form to invite Loopers into their reality through [[w:c:fortnite:Rift Gate|Rift Gates]] and teach them the ways of [[the Force]]. The [[Clone Wars]] begin, and later Anakin Skywalker turns to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] and becomes the fearsome Darth Vader. [[Order 66]] is enacted by Palpatine, who is secretly the Dadk Lord of the Sith Darth Sidious, and the clone trooper regiments across the Island turn hostile on the Loopers.

===Chapter 4: Season 4 - Last Resort===
{{Quote|This is a new day. A new beginning.|Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="FNBR" />}}
Some time passes, and [[Ahsoka Tano]] arrives from [[Seatos]] via a Rift Gate to teach new Force users. Her hologram can be found throughout the Island, leading Loopers to travel to Seatos and train in the Force.

===Chapter 5: Season 2 - Myths & Mortals===
{{Quote|The Emperor will be most pleased.|Darth Vader|<ref name="FNBR" />}}
[[w:c:fortnite:Kado Thorne|Kado Thorne]] uses a [[w:c:fortnite:Time Machine|Time Machine]] to go back to the time of the original Island. Agent Jones is tasked with altering the Black Hole which destroyed it to safely create new realities so that the future Island's inhabitants can take refuge in the before the inevitable collapse of the current and main reality, [[w:c:fortnite:Reality Zero|Reality Zero]]. Jones causes a second [[w:c:fortnite:Big Bang|Big Bang]] which effectively resets the universe and births many new realities, but destroys the Zero Point. The Big Bang creates many new "galaxies," each containing its own set of universes. In one of these galaxies, another new [[w:c:fortnite:Helios|Island]] by the name of Helios is born, with some of the former Island's peoples being placed there as refugees. The Galactic Empire and Darth Vader arrive to this new Island and blockade several roads throughout it. [[Chewbacca]] also arrives to fight back against the Empire's occupation.

===Chapter 6: Mini Season 1 - Galactic Battle===
{{Quote|We need all the bravery and skill your planet can provide. Once we start this war, there will be no turning back.|Leia Organa to the Looper|<ref name="FNBR" />}}
====Summary of previous events====
[[w:c:fortnite:Agent Jones|Agent Jones]], [[w:c:fortnite:The Looper|The Looper]] and [[w:c:fortnite:Hope|Hope]] travel to [[w:c:fortnite:Reality: 783|Reality: 783]] and arrives on the island named [[w:c:fortnite:Oninoshima|Oninoshima]] in order to investigate the latter's connection to [[w:c:fortnite:Pandora's Box|Pandora's Box]] and find a certain artifact. However, they were followed by a shard of the Zero Point, which has unleashed the [[w:c:fortnite:Demon Warriors|Demon Warriors]] from the [[w:c:fortnite:Spirit Realm|Spirit Realm]]. This leads to Jones, Hope and The Looper allying with Island natives [[w:c:fortnite:Kendo|Kendo]] and [[w:c:fortnite:Jade|Jade]]. However, Kendo and Jade's had an older brother named [[w:c:fortnite:Daigo|Daigo]], a mask maker who always followed a dangerous path for his ambitions but also to protect his siblings. 

After the kidnapping of Jade by the demons and a lot of adventures of Jones' group and Daigo to save her, the Mask Maker found the way to save her and discovered a secret entity, the Demons' true leader a terrible dark force. After a confrontation with the Shogun X, the very powerful Demon Warriors commander, Daigo saved Jade but became obsessed with fiding and confronting the "Dark Presence". Etablishing a plan to enter the [[w:c:fortnite:Spirit Realm|Spirit Realm]], Daigo allied with The Baron [[w:c:fortnite:Fletcher Kane|Fletcher Kane]], promising him power in exchange of his gold. Using the gold to repair a very old and very dangerous Mask of Power with Kintsugi technique, the duo headed to open a Spirit Realm Portal with the repaired mask, with Kane acting as Daigo's bodyguard for him to open the portal. However, once Kane was defeated, Daigo betrayed Kane and left, but was soon corrupted by the Dark Presence within the Spirit Realm, and transformed into a new demon. At the same time, the [[superweapon]] known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] appears in the skies of Oninoshima.

====Episode I: Imperial Takeover====
The '''Galactic Empire''', led by [[Darth Sidious|Emperor Palpatine]], appeared in the skies above the island in their [[Star_Destroyer#Cruisers|Star Destroyers]] and launched a full-scale invasion on Oninoshima. With overwhelming combat power, they encountered no resistance and were able to build their bases in the captured territories ([[w:c:fortnite:Demon's Dojo|Demon's Dojo]], which was completely destroyed and replaced by [[w:c:fortnite:First Order Base|First Order Base]], and the redesigned [[w:c:fortnite:Shogun's Solitude|Shogun's Solitude]]).

The Empire has set up outposts of its own and the [[First Order]] all over the [[w:c:fortnite:Oninoshima|island]]. The Empire has also taken over [[w:c:fortnite:Radio Stations|radio stations]], placing [[Death Troopers]] on guard duty. [[Death Star|The Death Star]] has settled on the horizon, biding its time.

[[w:c:fortnite:The Looper|The Looper]] discovers the Rebels' Data Log Entries, where they mainly learn how some of the rebels are discouraged by seeing how outgunned they are, while another log talks about rumors concerning a major counteroffensive move against The Empire. Later, infiltrating [[w:c:fortnite:Vader Samurai's Solitude|Vader Samurai's Solitude]], they discover Imperial Data Log Entries from its Stormtroopers, some of whom are alarmed by the number of people dancing on the island, others are worried about being stuck there, and one of them experienced and was terrified by their experience with one of the [[w:c:fortnite:Rift-To-Go|Rift-To-Go]]'s. However, one of the logs mentions that [[Emperor Palpatine]] is interested in the "junk" of Oninoshima. The Stormtroopers don't really know why this is the case, but if they find anything interesting they will report it to the Emperor as soon as possible.

[[General Grievous]] is on the hunt for Jedi Lightsabers collected from those that have fallen in battle, as well as meeting up with other favorable collectors. 

====Episode II: The Pull of the Force====
The Sith Lord [[Darth Maul]], as well as the Jedi Master [[Mace Windu]] both emerge and intervene in the conflict. [[General Grievous]] opposed Windu and his troopers, while Maul's involvement remains unclear.

The Looper meets up with Leia Organa. She sought after a rebel loyalist to formulate a plan on how to figure out why The Empire has taken a detour to Oninoshima, so they team up discover the truth. Through The Resistance's team of spies, they know to search for a hidden Empire cache, which the Looper was able to find by hotwiring a cannon into a mountain. Viewing them as an honorary rebel, Leia becomes even more determined to find the brave and skilled allies that this Island has after hearing what the Empire has planned.

During this time, [[Chewbacca]] begins to attack various Imperial Outposts, alongside providing battle assistance and [[w:c:fortnite:Wookiee Bowcaster|Wookiee Bowcasters]] to the Resistance.

====Episode III: Mandalorian Rising====
The Mandalorian Warriors expect honor, loyalty, and strength whether among themselves, but also from their allies. However, they suspect that their most recent allies and employer, the Galactic Empire, betrayed them and their agreement. Ironically, the mercenaries recruits another mercenary, The Looper, in an effort to find proof of this speculated betrayal. Infiltrating [[w:c:fortnite:Imperial Outpost|Outposts]] to dig into the Empire's data, The Looper found irrefutable proof of their betrayal, as well as a plan that the Mandalorians deemed completely dishonorable once informed by The Looper. 

Finding an honorable trust within The Looper, they break their contract with the Empire and turn against them, therefore joining [[w:c:fortnite:The Resistance (Galactic Battle)|The Resistance]]. Along with their allies, they freed [[w:c:fortnite:Outpost Enclave|Outpost Enclave]] from the imperial occupation.
However, the Empire has not said its last word, and the second-in-command of the is ready to retaliate against the rebels, elaborating plans involving Star Destroyers from his base Vader Samurai's Solitude.

==== Episode IV: Star Destroyer Bombardment ====
As per Vader's orders, a Star Destroyer was deployed to bombard The Resistance and their allies. In the meantime, Emperor Palpatine ordered the Death Star's superlaser to be charged to decimate the Oninoshima Island.

However, The Resistance has not said its last word and has every intention of retaliation. [[Leia Organa]] upgrades her Rebel Outpost with extra landing pads and rebel technology, with everybody preparing for the effort of infiltrating and sabotaging the Death Star itself from within, saving the Island from the Emperor's might for good.

====Episode V: [[w:c:fortnite:Death Star Sabotage|Death Star Sabotage]] ====
After a successful assault on Star Destroyers around the Death Star, [[w:c:fortnite:Hope|Hope]], [[w:c:fortnite:Vengeance Jones|Jones]], [[w:c:fortnite:The Looper|The Looper]] and their resistance allies successfully infiltrate the Death Star. Some disguise themselves as Stormtroopers, while others pose as prisoners, allowing them to slip past Imperial forces undetected. Once inside, they sabotage the tractor beam systems and other critical technologies, significantly reducing the Death Star's threat level—provided their mission succeeds.

Meanwhile, [[w:c:fortnite:Fishstick|Fishstick]] attempts to experiment with a [[w:c:fortnite:Rift-To-Go|Rift-To-Go]] in space—an untested maneuver. The device cause him to vanish from the group and makes it appear in space, outside. This sabotage effort serves as the core of the resistance's inside operation, while the [[Blue Squadron (disambiguation)|Blue Squadron]] maintains communication with Jones and Hope to coordinate their bombing run.

After disabling the Death Star's laser cannons, to allow [[Blue Leader]]'s Tie Fighter Squadron to do their move, the team is soon confronted by a pair of Stormtroopers. Jones tries to use Jedi mind tricks, but the attempt fails, forcing the group to neutralize the enemies with conventional weapons. This is followed by a trip to the Tie Fighter ships room, with a fight against the Imperial forces. Just as escape seems within reach, a TIE Fighter crashes into the bridge, separating Hope from the rest of the team.

Trapped in what appears to be a dead end, the group faces Emperor Palpatine himself. He effortlessly deflects their attacks, disarms them using the Force, and overwhelms them with Force Lightning, killing most of the members. With the team incapacitated and no options remaining, Jones prepares for the worst—until Hope returns just in time. She uses a Rift-To-Go to shield Jones from Palpatine's final strike. Though unsure if it would work, the gambit pays off: Jones, Hope and The Looper escapes moments before the Death Star explodes along with Fishstick and the few surviving members, falling toward the Island. However, the debris rains down on the Island, striking several places as well as the portal at [[w:c:fortnite:Predator Peak|Predator Peak]].

Colliding with the Spirit Realm portal, the fragment triggered a powerful energy wave that hits the reality barrier surrounding Oninoshima and sends a reality wave across the Island, hitting a Battle Bus with people inside and granting superpowers. At the same time, The Looper crashed on Oninoshima, unconscious, after being hit by the powerful wave. Just like the people of the Battle Bus, they also awakened powers of an unknown nature. In the meantime, seizing the chaos, [[w:c:fortnite:Mask Maker Daigo|Mask Maker Daigo]] breaks free alongside the [[w:c:fortnite:Demon Warriors|Demon Warriors]] and invades Oninoshima.
