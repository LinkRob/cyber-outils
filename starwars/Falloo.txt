'''Falloo''' was an [[Ewok]] [[Gender|female]] who was a member of the [[Bright Tree tribe]] on the [[forest]] [[moon]] [[Endor]]. Living in [[Bright Tree Village]], Eemee had a close relationship with the [[Dulok]] [[Eemee]], and the pair spent [[time]] together, along with Eemee's [[bordok]], in a decorated [[cave]] away from both their homes. When together, they picnicked, picked [[mushroom]]s, and created [[flower]] garlands.

Falloo's Dulok-fearing [[parent]]s eventually found the girls in the cave at around the same time that Eemee's parents discovered their daughter's whereabouts. The parents pulled their [[Youngling|children]] apart and began arguing, but Eemee and Falloo then held [[Limb|hands]] and forced the adults to make peace. The two families then shared a picnic together in the cave. The Bright Tree tribe's [[shaman]], [[Logray]], later told a tale about the pair during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Biography==
===Secret rendevous===
[[File:FallooParents-TheWoklingsTale.png|thumb|left|300px|alt=Falloo looks sheepish as she is caught leaving her home by her parents, who look irritated.|Falloo's parents feared Duloks.]]
Falloo was an [[Ewok]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Twitter" /> who was part of the [[Bright Tree tribe]], living with their [[parent]]s in [[Bright Tree Village]] on the [[forest]] [[moon]] [[Endor]]. The Ewok was close with the [[Dulok]]<ref name="Wokling" /> [[Eemee]],<ref name="Twitter" /> but as Falloo's parents feared Duloks, Falloo did not tell them about Eemee when leaving the village to see the other [[Youngling|girl]], instead mentioning a [[bordok]] companion, [[fruit]], and the [[Star|sun]]. Descending from the [[tree]]-top village on a [[rope]] with a basket of [[Foodstuff|food]], Falloo greeted the bordok on the forest floor and the pair then made their way to a decorated [[cave]] where Eemee awaited them.<ref name="Wokling" />

The Dulok gifted the pair [[flower]] garlands, which they donned, and Falloo and Eemee then sat down for a picnic. During the meal, Falloo asked about the Dulok's parents, who had been angry when Eemee left. The Dulok and the Ewok then went diving together in a pool of [[water]] and later went [[fungus]] collecting in the forest, although when Falloo tried to eat one of the [[mushroom]]s, Eemee became concerned and stopped the Ewok.<ref name="Wokling" />

===Parent problems===
[[File:FallooEemee-TheWoklingsTale.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Falloo places a yellow hood over Eemee's head while grinning. Eemee is smiling shyly and hiding two Ewok dolls behind their back. Behind the pair, snow is falling and their bordok has its tongue out to catch some.|Eemee and Falloo shared a close relationship, with Falloo gifting Eemee an Ewok hood.]]
Falloo gifted Eemee with an Ewok [[Clothing|hood]] at a later meeting between the pair, and using [[club]]s, they bashed a tree so that [[Plant|flowers]] would fall from it. Gathering the flowers, they used them to make another set of garlands back at the cave. However, Falloo's parents arrived while they played with the flowers, followed shortly after by Eemee's parents.<ref name="Wokling" />

The two couples pulled their respective daughters away from each other and shielded them as the adults began to argue with each other. Irritated at their parents, the two girls then clasped [[Limb|hands]] and pointed out a no anger sign in the cave. After making peace, the two sets of parents began looking at their daughters' cave decorations, then joined the two girls and the bordok for a picnic at which all of them wore garlands. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Bright Tree [[shaman]], [[Logray]], told a story about Eemee and Falloo to Ewoks gathered in Bright Tree Village.<ref name="Wokling" />

==Personality and traits==
Falloo did not tell their Dulok-fearing parents about visiting Eemee, with whom the [[Color|brown]]-[[Hair|furred]] Ewok had a close relationship. The pair held hands and stood defiant against their parents when the two couples found the girls, convincing them to make peace.<ref name="Wokling" />

==Equipment==
Falloo wore a blue hood and carried a brown woven basket. While spending [[time]] with Eemee, the Ewok also wore a garland of purple flowers on several occasions, using a [[wood]]en club while collecting flowers.<ref name="Wokling" />

==Behind the scenes==
Falloo appeared in "[[The Woklings' Tale]]," a story written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and illustrated by [[Paulina Ganucheau]], which was published in the one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] anthology [[Return of the Jedi – Ewoks 1|''Return of the Jedi – Ewoks'' 1]]<ref name="Wokling" /> on [[April 12]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103245/star_wars_return_of_the_jedi_-_ewoks_2023_1|text=Star Wars: Return Of The Jedi &ndash; Ewoks (2023) #1}}</ref> As the story has no text, the character was not identified in the comic,<ref name="Wokling" /> but Wong provided the Ewok's name and gender on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] following its release.<ref name="Twitter" />
