The '''assembly complex''' was a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Anaxes shipyards|shipyard]] on [[Anaxes]] seized by the [[Separatist Droid Army]] during the [[Clone Wars]], becoming their most fortified position during the [[Battle of Anaxes]]. [[Jedi General]]s [[Mace Windu]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] led the final attack to reclaim the site, with Windu disarming a bomb in the [[fusion generator]].<ref name="Unfinished Business" />
