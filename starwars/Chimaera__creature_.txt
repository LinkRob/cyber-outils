The '''chimaera''' was a three-headed monstrous<ref name="Rebel Recon #3.18">{{RebelsRecon|6Ez3y6BZGrk|#3.18: Inside "Secret Cargo" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-secret-cargo}}</ref> legendary creature<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|236|Darker Than Night}}</ref> known to the [[Unknown Regions]] [[Paccosh]] [[species]] and represented with arc-like [[spider]] [[Limb|legs]], a cluster of small snakes resembling stalked [[eye]]s rising from the center of the arcs, and two much larger [[Snake|serpentine]] heads perched atop long barbed necks curving up and around. The large snakes were described as having their heads and open [[mouth]]s pointing defiantly upward.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref>

[[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] [[flagship]], the ''[[Chimaera]]'', which was named for the creature, bore a [[Chimaera (symbol)|stylized depiction]] of a chimaera on its ventral surface.<ref name="Thrawn 4">[[Thrawn 4|''Thrawn'' 4]]</ref><ref name="Rebel Recon #3.15">{{RebelsRecon|netG0u3D2F0|#3.15: Inside "Trials of the Darksaber" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-trials-of-the-darksaber}}</ref> The officers, [[stormtrooper]]s and [[DT-series sentry droid|sentry droids]] of the Grand Admiral's [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|7th Fleet]] used a stylized representation of a Chimaera in their [[Imperial military uniforms|uniforms]] and [[pauldron]]s.<ref name="Rebel Recon #3.18" />
