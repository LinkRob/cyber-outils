The '''Challenge of the Mind''' was one of the three challenges that an [[Alderaanian]] [[Prince|heir]] had to undertake to prove their worthiness to be formally declared heir to the [[Alderaan]]ian [[throne]], along with the challenges of the [[Challenge of the Body|Body]] and [[Challenge of the Heart|Heart]]. The challenge was intended to test the heir's intelligence.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

[[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], for her Challenge of the Mind, served as her [[Parent|father]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa's]] aide in the [[Imperial Senate]] and represented Alderaan in the [[Apprentice Legislature]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />
