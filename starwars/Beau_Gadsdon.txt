'''Beau Gadson''' is an English actress who played eight year old [[Jyn Erso]] in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. In addition to portraying young Jyn, she is known for playing the recurring role of young [[Wikipedia:Princess Margaret, Countess of Snowdon|Princess Margaret]] in the historical drama series [[Wikipedia:The Crown (TV series)|''The Crown'']]. The young actress also appears in the music video for [[Wikipedia:Harry Styles|Harry Styles]]' 2017 song "[[Wikipedia:Kiwi (song)|Kiwi]]."
