The '''Czerka Stun Cuffs''' were a type of [[Stuncuffs|stuncuff]] restraint tool produced by [[Czerka Arms]].<ref name="SWGOH" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Czerka Stun Cuffs first appeared in the [[2015]] mobile [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'', developed by [[EA Capital Games]]. In the game they are used as "Gear" to upgrade characters and there exist five tiers, designated Mk 1 to Mk 4 and Mk 12, as well as "Salvage" and "Prototype" versions.<ref name="SWGOH" /> Like the other gear items, the Czerka Stun Cuffs were modeled by [[Brittany Hermann]],<ref name="HermannArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=naastika|name=[[Brittany Hermann]]|url=artwork/L3BvNv|text=Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes - Gear Icons|date=2021-01-13}}</ref> with additional help by<ref name="HermannForums1">{{EA|subdomain=forums|url=discussions/swgoh-strategy-and-tips-en/everything-you-never-needed-to-know-about-gear/4039897/replies/4039908|text=Reply|author=[[Brittany Hermann]]|date=2017-03-07}}</ref> [[Paul Martin]]<ref name="Martin1">{{ArtStation|username=morepaul|name=[[Paul Martin]]|url=artwork/8wwOxx|text=Star Wars Galaxy of Heroes - Gear Icons|date=2021-12-06}}</ref> and another artist.<ref name="HermannForums1" />
