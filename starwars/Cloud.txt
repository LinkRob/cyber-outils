'''Clouds''' were weather phenomena in the [[atmosphere]]s of [[planet]]s such as [[Bespin]]. The floating [[Cloud City]] was named for its position among the clouds.<ref name="TESB">{{Film|V}}</ref>

[[Cloud diary|Cloud diaries]] were a type of artistic journal used in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] in which the owner wrote in a stylized script which formed the details of drawings of clouds and other natural features.<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref>
