A '''unit''' of [[clone trooper]]s was led by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Aayla Secura]] during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] at the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]]. Following [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]]'s arrival on [[Geonosis]] to rescue the [[Jedi assault team]] in the [[Petranaki Arena]] with two [[Sector Army|battle armies]] of clone troopers, Secura took [[Commanding officer|command]] of a unit of clones after arriving on the front lines.<ref name="Ahsoka and Others" />

It was during this [[time]] when Secura demonstrated strong leadership and combat qualities, and how to masterfully manage military command. Secura proved her skills to the clones under her command, with who she fought shoulder to shoulder during the battle. Her [[Color|blue]] [[lightsaber]] was a standard for the clones who followed her. Following the battle, due to her leadership qualities, Aayla Secura was made a [[Jedi General]] and placed in command of the [[327th Star Corps]],<ref name="Ahsoka and Others" /> and was given her own [[Aayla Secura's task force|fleet group]].<ref name="HC 73">{{HelmetCollectionCite|73|Helmets}}</ref>
