== Biography ==
Baj was a young man who in [[3 BBY]], who embarked on the maiden voyage of the [[luxury train]] ''[[Exas Jewell]]'' on the [[planet]] [[Tyegin]]. On board the train, he took part in a high-stakes, invitation-only [[sabacc]] game with the [[Fosh]] [[D'Lina]], the [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[Rakan]], and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] who was impersonating the train's designer, [[Shen Kanton]].<ref name="Off the Rails" /> Calrissian, who had won the game, tried to depart with his winnings, but was stopped by Rakan and D'Lina who expressed surprise as Kanton was supposed to ride until the end of the trip. This discussion upset Baj, who demanded that they either play another hand or leave. However, the card game was interrupted by [[Pirate|pirates]], who stole Calrissian's winnings and valuables from the guests in the train car. The pirates also damaged the train before making their escape.<ref name="Off the Rails" />

With the train out of control, Calrissian led the other guests of the premier-class train car in unhooking the engine car and disrupting the magnetic link in order to slow it down using the furniture from the train. Baj tried to help by throwing the [[Unidentified dealer droid|dealer droid]] into the pile but was stopped by Calrissian. As the train's magnetic field was disrupted, the train jerked, throwing Baj from the front of the train. Calrissian caught his arm and pulled him back in. Eventually [[Tyegin's law enforcement]] responded to the situation and the guests were rescued.<ref name="Off the Rails" />

== Personality and traits ==
Baj was a small [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who had blonde hair, black eyes and tanned skin. He was a wealthy royal and wore a white dress suit, with a red sash and cummerbund and gold epaulettes. He was petulant and demanding.<ref name="Off the Rails" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baj appeared in "[[Off the Rails (comic story)|Off the Rails]]," a short comic written by [[Jeremy Barlow]] and illustrated by [[Bob Molesworth]].<ref name="Off the Rails" /> Although his name does not appear in the story proper, it is mentioned in the script.<ref name="Twit" />
