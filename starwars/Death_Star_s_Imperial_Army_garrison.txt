A '''garrison''' of the [[Imperial Army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] was stationed on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] and commanded by [[Chief of the Imperial Army]] [[General]] [[Cassio Tagge]]. The [[garrison]] was as fully equipped as other [[planet]]ary garrisons, and the garrison, along with its [[Imperial officer]]s and [[stormtrooper]] [[battalion]]s were not part of the Death Star's [[Battle Station Operations|BSO]] structure. The garrison was headquartered in the Death Star's southern hemisphere.<ref name="Dawn" />
