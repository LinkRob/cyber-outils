'''Diana Kent''' is a South African actress who portrays [[General]] [[Amret Engell|Engell]] in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. She played Kate Winslet's mother in Peter Jackson's psychological thriller ''Heavenly Creatures'' from 1994,<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://diana.dclough.org/?page_id=15|text=Film|archivedate=20200404190251}}</ref> and has played guest roles in British TV series such as ''Heartbeat'', ''Midsomer Murders'', ''New Tricks'', ''Agatha Christie's Poirot'' and the 2012 miniseries ''Titanic''.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://diana.dclough.org/?page_id=18|text=Television|archivedate=20200404190305}}</ref>
