'''D3-O9''', also spelled '''Deethree-Ohnine''',<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 2" /> was a [[droid]] who served aboard the [[Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''. Built [[Standard century|centuries]] prior to the [[First Order-Resistance War]] by the founder of [[Chandrila Star Line]], D3's primary role aboard the ''Halcyon'' was running ship logistics.<ref name="Starcruiser Characters" /> She also spoke with ''Halcyon'' guests on the [[droid link]] in the cabins and suites there.<ref name="PS">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
D3-O9 is a character created for the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] immersive experience at [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World|Walt Disney World Resort]]. She was first revealed with concept art on [[StarWars.com]] on [[November 17]], [[2021]].<ref name="Starcruiser Characters" /> She functioned as a virtual assistant that guests could communicate with through screens in their cabins.
