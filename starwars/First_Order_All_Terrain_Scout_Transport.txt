The '''First Order All Terrain Scout Transport (AT-ST)''' was a model of [[All Terrain Scout Transport]] [[walker]] manufactured by [[Kuat-Entralla Drive Yards]] and fielded by the [[First Order]]. Clad in next-generation reinforced composite [[armor]], this updated design's improved gyroscopic systems made it more stable than preceding AT-ST models used by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Incredible Cross-Sections" />

==Characteristics==
[[File:FOATST-TSWB.png|thumb|left|200px|A First Order AT-ST]]
Although it was based on the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]] design, the [[First Order]] All Terrain [[Scout]] [[Transport]] possessed several modest updates. The scout walker's gyroscopic systems were improved, which stabilized its balance, and, because of new breakthroughs in lightweight materials, the AT-ST's armored shell was upgraded, along with improved weapons systems.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

Unlike the AT-STs used by the [[Galactic Empire]], which had a separate viewport for each of the two crew members, the First Order AT-ST's cockpit had one long viewport across its front.<ref name="Incredible Cross-Sections" />

==History==
{{Quote|It will clear a path to the stronghold.|Captain Phasma, on the role of an AT-ST during the attack on Demir|<ref name="Age of Resistance" />}}
[[File:ATSTDemir-AORPhasma1.png|thumb|right|250px|An AT-ST fires its cannons during the attack on Demir.]]
When the First Order [[Attack on Demir|attacked]] the [[planet]] [[Demir]], the [[First Order Army|army]] deployed an AT-ST to clear a path to the [[Demirian stronghold]]. Once in position, [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] ordered for her [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] to stop their advance so the walker could fulfill its purpose. However, the [[Demirian]]s were able to take down the vehicle, forcing Phasma to employ a different plan.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />

[[BB-8’s AT-ST|One such AT-ST]] stored in the [[hangar]] of the ''[[Supremacy]]'' was hijacked by [[BB-8]] to rescue [[Finn]] and [[Rose Tico]] from stormtroopers.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> AT-STs, along with [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport]]s and [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport]]s, were also stored aboard the [[Thunderer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] commanded by [[Agent]] [[Tierny]] and [[Commander]] [[Pyre]].<ref name="The Escape">{{Resistance|The Escape (episode)}}</ref>
