The '''Imperial Base''' was an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] stronghold on the jungle [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Felucia/Legends|Felucia]]. The base was mainly made up of multi level bunkers similar to that of the [[Endor shield generator bunker|bunker]] on [[Endor/Legends|Endor]], with roads in between. There was also a path on the side one side of the base which led to a landing platform. It was seen around [[2 BBY/Legends|2 BBY]], when [[Galen Marek]], [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader's]] apprentice, stormed the facility, slaughtering all of the [[Imperial Navy commando|Navy commandos]], [[Imperial medic/Legends|medics]], and [[Imperial Heavy Trooper/Legends|Imperial heavy troopers]]. It is unknown what happened to the facility afterwards.

It was built in around several small cliffs. The Empire built the base so Imperial troops could operate on the planet and keep the native population of [[Felucian/Legends|Felucians]] from rebelling. Many soldiers assigned to the base noted that this was no easy task.
