{{Quote|The Cyclorrians are some of the most ingenious engineers in the known galaxy.|Jedi Nib Assek|<ref name="The Rising Storm" />}}
'''Cyclorrians''' were an [[insectoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] famed for their dedicated [[engineer]]ing skills. They were proud of producing advanced [[starship]]s at the [[Cyclor Shipyards]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]], which were second only to the [[Santhe Shipyards|Corellian shipyards]] during the [[High Republic Era]].

==Description==
Cyclorrians were a [[species]] of [[Sentience|sentient]] [[insectoid]]s who lived in hives alongside the rest of their swarm.<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[Homeworld|Native]] to the largely [[Color|green-and-brown]] [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Cyclor]]<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> and sharing their name with the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim's]] [[Cyclorria system]],<ref name="Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> they stood at about a [[meter]] tall and had a pair of long [[antenna]]e on their big round heads<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> for navigating their darkened hives and large pearlescent compound [[eye]]s. The species also had two shorter antennae near the small area under their eyes where they wore gray masks. Cyclorrians had beige and bluish gray skin, a pair of arms with three fingers each, and a pair of adhesive [[Limb|feet]], but they also wore [[Clothing|shoes]] and clothing covering their torsos and legs.<ref name="TROS VD" /> The people communicated in a [[language]] of chirrups and clicks, and their faces flushed purple when deeply stressed.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

The Cyclorrians of the [[Cyclor Shipyards]] worked in unison without needing to speak, perfectly doing their part without interrupting one another with an infectious enthusiasm; the Cyclorrians' work ethic allowed them to work non-stop for over twenty-four [[Standard hour|hours]] without complaint, producing some of the finest [[Starship|spacecrafts]] for the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

==History==
{{Quote|The locals have fought valiantly. Most of the Nihil raiders have been taken out along with the scav droids.|Jedi Porter Engle|<ref name="The Rising Storm" />}}
[[File:Cyclorrian-THRTrailOfShadows2.png|215px|thumb|left|A Cyclorian]]
Cyclorrians were known across [[the galaxy]] for their fine [[engineer]]ing capabilities and the advanced Cyclor Shipyards during the [[High Republic Era]], producing a [[fleet]] of [[Incom Corporation]]'s [[Z-29 Skyhawk]] [[starfighter]]s for worlds throughout the Republic and beyond. Considered second only to the [[Santhe Shipyards|Corellian shipyards]], they also built the state-of-the-art [[Elite-class vessel|''Elite''-class vessel]] ''[[Innovator]]'' following the [[Aqualish]] [[Vam Targes]]' design and strict supervision. When he visited the yet-to-be-commissioned ship's [[hangar]]<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] was as impressed with the ''Innovator'' as he was with its Cyclorrian shipbuilders. The [[Jedi Knight|Jedi]] [[Indeera Stokes]] also marvelled at the work being done at the shipyard.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

When the [[Nihil]] raiders [[First Battle of Cyclor|attacked]] the shipyard during the Jedi's visit, the Cyclorrians defended the pride of their work by [[pilot]]ing Z-29 Skyhawks with their [[glove]]d [[Limb|fingers]] and, in the breached hangar of the ''Innovator'', arming themselves with [[blaster]]s. While the swarm-mates were not [[soldier]]s, they also bravely fought Nihil [[scav droid]]s [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-manipulator-arm]], protected by their [[Environment suit|space-suits]]. Meanwhile, their two Jedi visitors engaged the Nihil with their own [[Jedi Vector|Vector fighters]].<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

Although the Nihil jammed communications to and from the shipyard with their [[scrambler droid]]s, the Cyclorrians engineered a way to broadcast some footage of the attack, its reach including the nearby [[Derra Outpost]] and the Republic [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]]'s [[flagship]], the ''[[Coruscant Dawn]]'', in the [[Core Worlds]]. Aboard, Jedi Knight [[Nib Assek]] concluded that the engineers must have bypassed the Nihil's jamming, prompting [[Vice Chair|Vice Chancellor]] [[Larep Reza]] to briskly state that they should pass on their knowledge to official channels. [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]] analyzed the footage and called on Derra Outpost to provide assistance, but Master [[Tera Sinube]] there had already sent a [[squadron]] of Jedi Vectors led by Master [[Porter Engle]].<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

Master Engle deemed the Cyclorrians valiant in battle, and the shipbuilders informed the Jedi that the Nihil's [[Sarn Starbreaker's Cloudship|lead ship]] had been destroyed, thanks to the efforts of Zettifar.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

One Cyclorrian swarm joined the [[Resistance]], including [[Vazzet Dipterz]] and [[Wizzich Mozzer]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
