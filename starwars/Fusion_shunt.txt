A '''fusion shunt''' was a type of secondary mode used for [[lightsaber]] blades. They were enabled when a [[Jedi]] modified the power output of their lightsaber, which amplified the power and profile of the blade when the fusion shunt was activated.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Fusion shunts were mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Rise" />
