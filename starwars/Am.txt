{{Quote|From the moment I was born, I was prepared to sacrifice everything for the dark side.|Am|<ref name="The Twins" />}}
'''Am''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Cloning|clone]] who, like her [[twin]] brother [[Karre]], was [[Reproduction|created]] through advanced cloning technology and the [[dark side of the Force]]. The two were raised from birth to become [[Dark Lord]]s of a [[Imperial Remnants|remnant]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] known as the [[TRIGGER Empire|T-Empire]]. At some point after the [[Battle of Exegol]] of [[35 ABY]], Am and Karre were on board a [[Gemini-class Star Destroyer|''Gemini''-class Star Destroyer]] as the final adjustments to its [[hyper cannon]] [[superweapon]] were being completed. When Am gave the order to fire the hyper [[cannon]], she learned that its [[power core]] had been [[Thief|stolen]], preventing it from charging. She quickly realized that Karre had stolen it and confronted him in a [[hangar]] as he tried to [[Escape from a Gemini-class Star Destroyer|escape]] with it. With the help of his [[droid]] [[R-DUO]], Karre stole a [[T-X-wing]] [[starfighter]], which he [[pilot]]ed out of the [[Star Destroyer]]. Before he could escape, Am exited to the surface of the Star Destroyer and [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|pull]] Karre and the power core back to her. Both twins used the Force to fight for control of the core, which eventually shattered to reveal the [[kyber crystal]] within it, which soon also shattered and exploded. 

In the aftermath of the explosion, Am and Karre both saw a [[Force vision|vision]] of Am's [[death]] as a result of her using the [[crystal]]. Karre revealed that he had previously seen the vision and explained that he had stolen the kyber crystal to save Am. Karre warned her not to use the crystal's power, but she ignored him and placed it into her [[Am's Dark Lord armor|armor]] before [[Lightsaber duel|attacking]] him with her [[Am's lightsabers|lightwhips]]. Karre [[Lightsaber combat|fought]] back, but Am knocked him off the Star Destroyer into [[Realspace|space]], where he was caught on his [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] by R-DUO. As a result of her attacks, Am's [[Dark armor|armor]] overloaded and she lost control of it. To save her, Karre used the power of the X-wing's [[hyperdrive]] and destroyed the kyber crystal with his [[Karre's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. After he jumped to [[hyperspace]], Am was left floating unconscious among debris from the Star Destroyer until she was rescued by her [[protocol droid]], [[B-2ON]]. 

==Biography==
===Creation===
{{Quote|This is our mission, as twins created through the power of the dark side.|Am, to B-2ON|<ref name="The Twins" />}}
[[File:AmKarreCloningTank-THETWINS.png|thumb|right|250px|Am and Karre were clones created through the dark side of the Force.]]
Am was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Cloning|clone]] who, like her [[twin]] brother [[Karre]], was<ref name="Art of Visions" /> secretly<ref name="Secretly">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-visions/episode-guide-the-twins|text="THE TWINS" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars: Visions}}</ref> [[Reproduction|created]] through advanced cloning technology and the [[dark side of the Force]]. The two were raised from birth to become [[Dark Lord]]s in<ref name="Art of Visions" /> the [[Imperial Army (T-Empire)|army]]<ref name="Movie Walker" /> of a [[Imperial Remnants|remnant]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] known as the [[TRIGGER Empire|T-Empire]],<ref name="Art of Visions" /> who planned for them to rule [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Meet the Heroes">{{SW|url=news/inside-star-wars-visions-meet-the-heroes|text=Inside ''Star Wars: Visions'': Meet the Heroes}}</ref> During the process of their creation, the twins were watched by [[Sith cultist]]s.<ref name="The Twins" /> Am and Karre were loyally served by the [[astromech droid]] [[R-DUO]] and the [[protocol droid]] [[B-2ON]],<ref name="Meet the Droids">{{SW|url=news/inside-star-wars-visions-meet-the-droids|text=Inside ''Star Wars: Visions'': Meet the Droids}}</ref> the latter of which also acted as a [[Nanny droid|nanny]], [[teacher]], and companion to Am.<ref name="Art of Visions" /> At some point while the twins were standing with a number of the cultists, Karre saw a [[Force vision|vision]] of Am's [[death]] as a result of her using the power of a [[kyber crystal]].<ref name="Manga">{{StoryCite|story=THE TWINS (manga)|book=Gangan Comics|bformatted=''Big Gangan'' Vol.09}}</ref>

===Betrayed===
{{Quote|Now let's surprise those peace-loving Republic fools. Ready the hyper cannon.|Am, to B-2ON|<ref name="The Twins" />}}
[[File:B2ONAndAm-THETWINS.png|thumb|left|250px|B-2ON reported to Am in her throne room.]]
At some point after the [[Battle of Exegol]] of [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|103}}</ref> Am and B-2ON were in her [[throne]] room aboard a [[Gemini-class Star Destroyer|''Gemini''-class Star Destroyer]]. The [[droid]] reported to her that the final adjustments to the [[Star Destroyer]] were being made and that they were waiting for her command. B-2ON expressed<ref name="The Twins" /> his<ref name="Art of Visions" /> joy that he would be able to witness Am and Karre complete their mission. Am responded that their journey had just begun and that she and Karre would bring order to the [[galaxy]]. B-2ON then reported that the [[Starship|Star Destroyer's]] [[planet]]-destroying [[hyper cannon]] would soon be operational and would be at full strength when it had finished charging. He then expressed his concerns about the effects that the twins' [[dark armor]]&mdash;which used the same [[dark energy]] as the hyper [[cannon]]&mdash;could have on their bodies. Am replied that she was prepared to sacrifice everything for the dark side and then ordered the hyper cannon to be readied to attack the [[New Republic]].<ref name="The Twins" /> 

However, before the [[superweapon]] could be fired, a [[T-Imperial crew]] [[Unidentified T-Imperial crewmember|member]] reported that the [[reactor]] core's power source had been stolen, preventing the hyper cannon from being charged. B-2ON was surprised, as only Am and Karre could access the reactor core chamber, but Am looked across the ship to see that Karre's throne was empty and realized that he had been the one who had stolen the [[power core]].<ref name="The Twins" />

===Stopping the escape===
{{Quote|Are you out of your mind? As you wish, you fool. If you won't hand it over, then I'll take it by force!|Am, to Karre|<ref name="The Twins" />}}
[[File:AmConfrontsKarre-THETWINS.png|thumb|right|250px|Am confronted Karre in the Star Destroyer's hangar.]]
Am ordered a group of [[T-Stormtrooper]]s to stop Karre, but he defeated them and made it into a [[hangar]] where he planned to [[Escape from a Gemini-class Star Destroyer|escape]]. Before he could, Am entered the hangar with B-2ON, a larger group of T-Stormtroopers, and two [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s. As she approached, Karre signaled for R-DUO, who was accompanying him, to sneak away. Am demanded that Karre return the power core, telling him she would forget what he had done if he did so. Karre refused, and removed his [[helmet]] and destroyed his [[Karre's Dark Lord armor|dark armor]]. Am inquired about Karre's motive, asking if he intended to give the power core to the Republic, but Karre refused to answer, saying Am would not understand. Am then decided to take the power core back by force, igniting two of her [[Am's lightsabers|lightsabers]] and preparing to attack Karre. Before she could, R-DUO powered on a stolen [[T-X-wing]] [[starfighter]] and fired on Am. She was able to project a [[Force barrier]], but the blasts deflected into her own forces and Karre was able to escape to the [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]].<ref name="The Twins" />

As Karre [[pilot]]ed his starfighter away, Am exited to the surface of the Star Destroyer and [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|pull]] the power core to her. She was eventually able to pull both Karre and the power core out of the X-wing and onto the surface of the Star Destroyer. As Karre fell, Am pulled the power core out of his grasp, but before it reached her, Karre recovered and began pulling it back toward him. Both twins struggled for control of the power core until it shattered, revealing the kyber [[crystal]] within it. Upon seeing the crystal, Am commented on its beauty and power and said that it would become a beacon of hope for the galaxy. Karre responded that it would only bring despair.<ref name="The Twins" /> 

[[File:The_Twins_duel.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Karre and Am fought for control of the kyber crystal.]]
Am and Karre continued fighting for the kyber crystal and both began striking it with [[Force lightning]], causing the crystal to resonate and then shatter, sending Am's lightning back at her and destroying her helmet. It then exploded and both twins were able to see the vision of Am's death that only Karre had previously seen. Karre explained that he had seen the vision and revealed that he had stolen the kyber crystal to prevent Am's death, saying he knew that she was too stubborn to have listened if Karre had told her what he had seen. Am responded by telling Karre she did not fear death and reminded him of the purpose of their birth.<ref name="The Twins" />

===Dueling Karre===
{{Quote|You think I fear death?! Did you forget? We are twins born from the dark side of the Force! We were made to bring back order to this galaxy with this power that transcends even death itself.|Am, to Karre|<ref name="Manga" />}}
[[File:AmLightwhips-THETWINS.png|thumb|right|250px|Am attacked Karre with her lightwhips.]]
Ignoring Karre's warning to not use the kyber crystal, Am placed a large fragment of it into her [[Am's Dark Lord armor|dark armor]]. Four mechanical [[limb]]s sprouted from her armor and emitted dark energy, forming long [[lightwhip]]s. She ignited her other two [[lightsaber]]s, which also became lightwhips, and then began attacking Karre, telling him that if he wanted to save her, then he should risk his life trying. As Am continued to attack Karre, he ran and took cover. Am launched another attack that destroyed his cover, but her lightwhips became wrapped around Karre's own [[Karre's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. He then sliced the energy beams of Am's [[weapon]]s and she returned all six to standard lightsabers.<ref name="The Twins" />

Karre then attacked Am by leaping and significantly extending the length of his lightsaber blade. She blocked the attack and kicked at him with a lightsaber-like weapon in her heel, which Karre dodged. Karre leaped at Am again, but she again blocked his attack and then forced him back until she was able to launch a dark energy-powered attack that knocked Karre off the surface of the Star Destroyer and into [[Realspace|space]], where he was caught on his X-wing by R-DUO.<ref name="The Twins" />

===Defeated and saved===
{{Quote|If this crystal is destroyed, our entire life's mission will be lost!''"<br />"''No, that isn't true!''"<br />"''How so?''"<br />"''Don't live for someone else! As long as you're alive, then you are free! And you're free to choose your own destiny!|Am and Karre|<ref name="The Twins" />}}
[[File:AmOverloaded-THETWINS.png|thumb|left|250px|Am released too much power from the kyber crystal, overloading her armor with dark energy.]]
As a result of using too much power from the kyber crystal, Am's armor overloaded and she lost control of it, causing her to be constricted by dark energy. Karre saw her and realized that if he did not destroy the kyber crystal, she would die, just as he saw in his vision. He was unsure if he had enough power to do so, but agreed to R-DUO's suggestion of using the X-wing's [[hyperdrive]]. R-DUO piloted the starfighter toward Am while Karre stood on the outside of it and ignited his lightsaber, causing several massive beams of various [[color]]s to appear and then coalesce into a single larger beam.<ref name="The Twins" />

As the X-wing approached Am, Karre's lightsaber cut through the Star Destroyer until it reached her. Despite what was happening to her, Am blocked Karre's blade and screamed at him that their life's mission would be lost if the crystal was destroyed. Karre replied that was not true, telling her not to live for someone else and that as long as she was alive, she would be free to choose her destiny. He then ordered R-DUO to activate the hyperdrive, which gave Karre enough power to push past Am's lightsabers. Karre's lightsaber briefly narrowed as it destroyed the kyber crystal before returning to its full size to finish cutting through the Star Destroyer as his X-wing jumped to [[hyperspace]].<ref name="The Twins" />

After Karre's X-wing made its jump, Am's armor was destroyed and she was left floating unconscious among the the debris from the Star Destroyer until B-2ON arrived to rescue her. Karre's X-wing later came out of hyperspace and crashed on<ref name="The Twins" /> the planet<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Art of Visions" /> After waking on top of his crashed starfighter, Karre told R-DUO that they would find Am and understand the purpose of their creation, and that he knew she was alive.<ref name="The Twins" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|However, even if I had told you about this, you would have never listened to me. I know you, and you are stubborn, Am.|Karre, to Am|<ref name="The Twins" />}}
Am was a human female clone with purple [[eye]]s, blond [[hair]], and light skin. She embraced her origin and the purpose of her creation, and her rage and the strength of her conviction gave her power.<ref name="Art of Visions" /> Am was stubborn and devoted to the dark side, willing to risk her life for it and her purpose.<ref name="The Twins" /> She struggled to find the right balance.<ref name="Meet the Villains">{{SW|url=news/inside-star-wars-visions-meet-the-villains|text=Inside ''Star Wars: Visions'': Meet the Villains}}</ref>

==Powers and abilities==
{{Quote|I can't! She's too strong in the Force!|Karre, on Am|<ref name="The Twins" />}}
Am was created with advanced cloning technology, allowing her to survive in outer space unaided, and was [[Bioengineering|scientifically designed]] to channel power, allowing her to use the Force.<ref name="Art of Visions" /> She was capable of creating a Force barrier and using telekinesis, being able to stop a starfighter with the Force. She could also use red Force lightning.<ref name="The Twins" /> Am was more powerful than Karre, but was still defeated by him. Although she [[Dark side adept|wielded]] the dark side, Am was not a [[Sith]] and was not trained in the Sith traditions.<ref name="Art of Visions" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|I am concerned about the effect this prototype dark armor could have on your physical body, as well as that of your brother.|B-2ON, to Am|<ref name="The Twins" />}}
Am wore a suit of prototype dark armor, which had a helmet, [[cape]], and four mechanical limbs that could wield lightsabers. It was powered by dark energy. She wielded six red-bladed lightsabers, which she could convert to lightwhips, and had another weapon in her heel. Am wore black [[clothing]] under her armor, which was ripped when her armor was destroyed. She also wore a black headband and black [[Piercing|earrings]]. Am had a throne room aboard the ''Gemini''-class Star Destroyer.<ref name="The Twins" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Development and design===
{{Quote|I don't consider whether the character is evil or good when I am desiging them. Instead, I visualize what the core of that particular character is.|Shigeto Koyama, on designing the twins|<ref name="Art of Visions" />}}
[[File:AmConceptArt-ArtOfVisions.png|thumb|right|220px|Concept art of Am]]
Am first appeared in "[[THE TWINS]]," a short film written by [[Hiromi Wakabayashi]], directed by [[Hiroyuki Imaishi]], and released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 1|first volume]] of the [[Canon|non-canon]] ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Trigger (animation studio)|Studio Trigger]],<ref name="The Twins" /> was released on [[Disney+]] on [[September 22]], [[2021]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-trailer-and-cast|text=Stunning New ''Star Wars: Visions'' Trailer Debuts}}</ref> Prior to the short's release, Am was introduced in a video uploaded to the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel on [[July 3]], 2021.<ref name="Special Look">{{SWYouTube|b9Gr9gKFxpE|STAR WARS: VISIONS &#124; SPECIAL LOOK &#124; DISNEY+|sw_url=video/special-look-star-wars-visions}}</ref> She was then identified in a [[StarWars.com]] article on [[August 17]] of that year. Am was voiced by [[Ryoko Shiraishi]] in the original [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]] version of the short and by [[Alison Brie]] in the English dub.<ref name="Stunning Trailer Debuts">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-trailer-and-cast|text=Stunning New ''Star Wars: Visions'' Trailer Debuts}}</ref> The [[Star Wars: Visions – THE TWINS (Original Soundtrack)|soundtrack]] for the short, which was composed by [[Michiru Ōshima]]<ref name="Soundtrack">[[Star Wars: Visions – THE TWINS (Original Soundtrack)|''Star Wars: Visions'' – THE TWINS Original Soundtrack]]</ref> and released for streaming on [[October 15]], 2021,<ref name="soundtrack announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-digital-soundtracks-are-here|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Digital Soundtracks Are Here}}</ref> included a track titled "Anthem of Imperial (Am's Theme)."<ref name="Soundtrack" />

During the auditions for the role, Imaishi thought it was important to choose someone who felt like an older sister. During her audition, Shiraishi was asked to scream multiple times, then came back and was asked to scream louder. She thought screaming loudly and showing power was important, so she worked hard on her screams in her performance, which she was exhausted by. While at the dubbing site, [[Junya Enoki]], who voiced Karre, heard Shiraishi screaming for a long time and thought it must have been difficult for her. He was impressed by her screams when watching the finished episode.<ref name="special program">{{DisneyPlusYouTube|video=7wv95d_OX4Q|text=【特別番組】スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ 全世界配信記念 &#124; Disney+ (ディズニープラス)|jp=1}}</ref>

[[File:VisionsFilmmakerFocus-TheTwins.png|thumb|left|250px|Promotional visual created by Wakabayashi and Koyoma]]
Am was designed by [[Shigeto Koyama]], who explained that while designing Am and Karre, he did not consider whether they were good or evil and instead visualized the core of the characters. Of the designs in "THE TWINS," Am was the least influenced by classic ''Star Wars'' designs. Instead, she was influenced by the energy and fashion of Trigger's other movies, such as their<ref name="Art of Visions" /> [[2019]]<ref name="Promare">{{WebCite|url=https://www.crunchyroll.com/news/latest/2022/5/17/promare-anime-film-heats-up-in-japan-again-with-revival-screenings|text=PROMARE Anime Film Heats Up in Japan Again with Revival Screenings|work=[[Wikipedia:Crunchyroll|Crunchyroll]]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/dfbsF}}</ref> film [[Wikipedia:Promare|''Promare'']],<ref name="Art of Visions" /> which was also directed by Imaishi,<ref name="Meet the Heroes" /> as well as [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] [[Wikipedia:Tokusatsu|''tokusatsu'']] heroes such as [[Wikipedia:Kamen Rider|Kamen Rider]] and [[Wikipedia:Ultraman|Ultraman]]. In addition to Trigger's aesthetic, Am's design combines elements from the [[Darth Vader's armor|armor]] used by [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and classic [[Wikipedia:Anime|anime]] details.<ref name="Meet the Villains" /> The [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] of Am's lightsabers were the inverse of Karre's [[hilt]], which itself was inspired by the [[Skywalker lightsaber|lightsaber]] used by<ref name="Art of Visions" /> the [[Jedi]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> Anakin Skywalker and his [[Skywalker family|son]] [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="Art of Visions" /> A promotional visual featuring Am and Karre's faces split down the middle was created by Koyama and Wakabayashi while the two were working on a proposal for the short, which they did in a conference room over around three days.<ref name="Visions Times">{{BookCite|author=Yamazaki, Gigan; Shide, Hidekuni; Ishii, Makoto|container=Visions Times|title=[[Wikipedia:Newtype|Newtype]]|publisher=[[Wikipedia:Kadokawa Corporation|Kadokawa]]|date=[[November]] [[2021]]|pages=insert poster|language=[[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]]}}</ref>

===Inspirations===
{{Quote|The sword fight I did all in my head! I do have a good stock of fighting scene ideas in my head. I just had to pick the best ones for the ''Star Wars'' theme and decide how to put it together.|translated=1|Hiroyuki Imaishi|<ref name="Focus">{{VisionsFocus|THE TWINS}}</ref>}}
[[File:AmConceptArt-EpisodeGuide.png|thumb|right|270px|Concept art of Am designed by Shigeto Koyama]]
The duel between the twins was inspired by the battle of Gojo Ohashi, a Japanese [[Wikipedia:Heian period|Heian period]] legend in which the [[Wikipedia:Sōhei|warrior-monk]] [[Wikipedia:Benkei|Saitō Musashibō Benkei]] met the young [[Wikipedia:Minamoto no Yoshitsune|Minamoto Yoshitsune]] on [[Wikipedia:Gojo Bridge|Gojo Ohashi bridge]]. Although Benkei was more powerful, Yoshitune won their battle. Similarly, Am was more powerful than Karre, but she was still defeated by him.<ref name="Art of Visions" /> Imaishi planned the fight in his head, saying that he had a stock of fight scene ideas in his head and picked the ones that were best for ''Star Wars''.<ref name="Focus" /> Am and Karre are similar to a flipped version of the twins [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]] and Luke. Twins are a classic ''Star Wars'' theme, but the short subverts the motif by following twins aligned with the dark side.<ref name="Meet the Heroes" /> Also like Luke and Leia, Karre and Am are of different genders.<ref name="JPN IGN">{{WebCite|url=https://jp.ign.com/star-wars-visions/54788/interview/the-twins|text=『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』監督インタビュー！ トリガー今石洋之監督『THE TWINS』|date=2021-09-29|work=IGN Japan|language=Japanese|archivedate=20250320134618}}</ref>

When Am's armor sprouted its mechanical arms, it was accompanied by a tone described by Sound Designer [[David W. Collins]] as a "[[Grievous]]/[[Ben Solo|Kylo]] cocktail", as it blended the effects that accompanied the lightsabers used by [[General]] Grievous and<ref name="Love Letter">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-feature-waugh-lopez-shirasaki|text="It's a Love Letter": The Producers of ''Star Wars: Visions'' Celebrate the Unique Anime Collection}}</ref> [[First Order]] [[warlord]]<ref name="Kylo Ren DB">{{Databank|kylo-ren|Supreme Leader Kylo Ren}}</ref> Kylo Ren. This information was included in a StarWars.com article published on [[September 21]], 2021, but the article incorrectly described the tone as accompanying Karre's armor.<ref name="Love Letter" /> The error was repeated in "[[Star Wars: Visions Season One Companion|''Star Wars: Visions'' Season One Companion]]," an article published in the [[Star Wars Insider 210|210th issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="Companion">{{InsiderCite|210|Star Wars: Visions Season One Companion|''Star Wars: Visions'' Season One Companion}}</ref> on [[May 3]], [[2022]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=insider/subscriptions|text=Star Wars Insider Subscription Information}}</ref>

Imaishi had never seen a story about two Dark Lords fighting and wanted to create one. The idea to make the characters twins came from Wakabayashi. Because a female Sith has never appeared in a ''Star Wars'' film, Imaishi wanted to depict one, explaining that because the female Jedi [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] appeared in<ref name="JPN IGN" /> the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film<ref name="Film Years">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''The Force Awakens'']], a female Darth Vader should also appear. He hoped that including one would allow female fans to confidently [[Wikipedia:Cosplay|cosplay]] as Darth Vader. Imaishi also said he wanted to include unashamedly anime-style scenes with beautiful girls.<ref name="JPN IGN" />
