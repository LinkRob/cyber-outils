'''FN-3181''' was a [[human]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who served the [[First Order]]. In [[34 ABY]], he was deployed to the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] as part of a First Order [[Attack on Tuanul|attack]] on the [[Sacred Villages|village]] of [[Tuanul]] in an effort to obtain part of a [[Map to Skywalker|map]] showing the location of vanished [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]].

After the stormtroopers [[Death|killed]] or captured the village's residents, [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], the leader of the [[Knights of Ren]], arrived and executed villager [[Lor San Tekka]]. This prompted the [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] to try and attack Ren, who used [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|freeze]] Dameron. FN-3181 and another [[trooper]] then dragged Dameron to Ren and searched the [[prisoner]], before carrying him aboard an [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport|Atmospheric Assault Lander]] to be taken away.

==Biography==
===Attack on Jakku===
[[File:Fn-3181.jpg|thumb|left|250px|FN-3181 (right) helped capture Poe Dameron during the First Order's attack on Jakku.]]
FN-3181<ref name="TFA" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> who served in the [[FN Trooper Corps|FN Corps]],<ref name="BTA" /> a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] unit in the [[First Order]]'s [[First Order military|military]] during its [[Cold War|conflict]] with the [[New Republic]]&ndash;backed [[Resistance]].<ref name="TFA" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VII}} to [[34 ABY]].</ref> he was was one<ref name="TFA" /> of eighty [[trooper]]s<ref name="TFAn" /> deployed from the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]''<ref name="ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> to the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] in four<ref name="TFA" /> [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport|Atmospheric Assault Landers]].<ref name="ICS" />

The [[soldier]]s were led by [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] and landed at [[Standard day|night]] outside the [[Sacred Villages|village]] of [[Tuanul]], which they had orders to [[Attack on Tuanul|attack]] in order to obtain part of a [[Map to Skywalker|map]] that led to the vanished [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]. The village's occupants fought back against FN-3181 and his comrades but were quickly overwhelmed, with those not [[Death|killed]] being captured and rounded up into the village's center.<ref name="TFA" />

===Capturing Poe Dameron===
{{Quote|Put him on board.|Kylo Ren orders FN-3181 to put Poe Dameron on an Atmospheric Assault Lander|audio=KyloRen-PutHimOnboard.flac|<ref name="TFA" />}}
[[File:FN-3181_escorts_Poe.png|thumb|right|225px|FN-3181 (foreground) escorted Poe Dameron onto an Atmospheric Assault Lander.]]
The [[Force-sensitive]] leader of the [[Knights of Ren]], [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], then arrived in the village in his<ref name="TFA" /> [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|''Upsilon''-class command shuttle]],<ref name="ICS" /> and village resident [[Lor San Tekka]], who had owned the map piece but was no longer in possession of it, was brought before him. The two briefly exchanged words before Ren killed the villager.<ref name="TFA" />

The [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]], who had been in the village to meet with San Tekka and had received the map piece, witnessed the man's death from his hiding place and opened fire on Ren in anger. Ren [[Telekinesis|froze]] both the [[blaster]] bolt and Dameron in place using the Force, after which FN-3181 and another trooper ran up to the [[pilot]] and grabbed him, each taking an [[Limb|arm]] and dragging Dameron before Ren.<ref name="TFA" />

Ren had FN-3181 search Dameron,<ref name="TFA" /> while the other trooper scanned him with a [[Portable scanner|personal scanner]].<ref name="TFAjn">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> After they found nothing on the pilot, the pair and another trooper escorted Dameron to one of the<ref name="TFA" /> Atmospheric Assault Landers<ref name="ICS" /> so that he could be taken back to the<ref name="TFA" /> ''Finalizer''.<ref name="ICS" /> However, Dameron no longer had the map piece, as he had given it to his [[BB-series astromech droid|astromech droid]], [[BB-8]]. The [[Astromech droid|droid]] escaped from Tuanul and eventually delivered it to the Resistance.<ref name="TFA" />

==Personality and traits==
When FN-3181 searched Poe Dameron, he did so with thuggish delight, smacking the pilot instead of patting him down. When the trooper found nothing, he was disappointed.<ref name="TFAjn" />

==Equipment==
FN-3181 wore standard [[First Order stormtrooper armor]] and carried<ref name="TFA" /> an [[F-11D blaster rifle]].<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|By the way, that stormtrooper on the right, in that uniform, umm, behind Poe, is Michael Giacchino, composer of fine film scores everywhere.|J.J. Abrams points out FN-3181 during his director's commentary of ''The Force Awakens''|<ref name="TFA" />}}
[[File:MGiacchino.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Michael Giacchino, who composed the musical score for ''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'', portrayed FN-3181 in ''The Force Awakens''.]]
FN-3181 first appeared in the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. The character was portrayed by [[Michael Giacchino]],<ref name="TFA" /> a long-time musical collaborator of the film's director, [[J.J. Abrams]]. Giacchino has provided the musical score for several of Abrams' other works,<ref name="SlashFilm">{{WebCite|url=http://www.slashfilm.com/star-wars-the-force-awakens-cameos|text='Force Awakens' Cameos Revealed: Michael Giacchino, Daniel Craig, and Radiohead's Nigel Godrich|author=Peter Sciretta|date=2015-12-18|archivedate=20170619120214}}</ref> as well as the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="RO">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> FN-3181 is not referred to by name in the film itself but is identified in the credits.<ref name="TFA" />

In the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|novelization of ''The Force Awakens'']], written by [[Alan Dean Foster]], FN-3181's actions are slightly altered. After the trooper searches Poe Dameron, the pilot sarcastically compliments him and winks, prompting FN-3181 to kick his legs out from under him.<ref name="TFAn" />

In [[2018]], Giacchino voiced a First Order stormtrooper in the [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] animated feature film [[Wikipedia:Ralph_Breaks_the_Internet|''Ralph Breaks the Internet'']] and later identified the trooper as FN-3181 on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]], commending director [[Wikipedia:Rich Moore|Rich Moore]] for inviting him to reprise the role.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|m_giacchino|status/1059931702540136448|[[Michael Giacchino]]|date=2018-11-06|quote=Pleased to announce that I got to reprise my @starwars Force Awakens role as @TheReal_FN3181 in the new @wreckitralph movie! Thanks @_rich_moore for inviting me along!|archivefile=File:Wreck_It_Ralph_FN3181.png}}</ref> In the film, FN-3181 and several other First Order stormtroopers discover the heroine Vanellope Von Schweetz in the Oh My Disney website. As Von Schweetz is not allowed on the site, they begin to chase her, but Von Schweetz touches FN-3181 with a video link and sends him to BuzzzTube, causing him to yell the [[Wilhelm scream]]. Von Schweetz then hides in a room containing all of the [[Wikipedia:Disney Princesses|Disney Princesses]] in order to escape the other stormtroopers.<ref name="RBtI">{{VisualMediaCite|title=[[Wikipedia:Ralph_Breaks_the_Internet|''Ralph Breaks the Internet'']]|director=[[Wikipedia:Rich Moore|Rich Moore]] and [[Wikipedia:Phil Johnston (filmmaker)|Phil Johnston]]|prod=[[Wikipedia:Walt Disney Animation Studios|Walt Disney Animation Studios]]|year=[[2018]]}}</ref>
