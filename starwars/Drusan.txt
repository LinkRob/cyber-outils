'''Drusan'''<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> was a [[racer]] from [[Rodia]] who participated in the [[Forty-eighth annual Dragonfire Air Rally|forty-eighth]] annual [[Dragonfire Air Rally]] on [[Coruscant]]. He flew a [[Recardian C-18]] in the race, placing second, after [[Thrar]] of [[Coachelle Prime]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
