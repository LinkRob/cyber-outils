'''Alchor Mirro''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[engineer]] who served in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> An elderly ground crew chief of Shadow Wing by the time of the [[Attack on Chadawa|Battle at Chadawa]], he had tried to retire three times before the [[Battle of Endor]] already. In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> he took part in the [[mission to Netalych]] with [[Yrica Quell]], [[Jeela Brebtin]], [[Fra Raida]], [[Nord Kandende]] and [[Agias Rikton]]. He argued for Quell's [[death]] after her comrades found out about her time in [[Alphabet Squadron]]. He returned to Shadow Wing aboard the ship Quell had found on a [[Unidentified planetoid (Cerberon system)|planetoid]] in the [[Cerberon system]] with only the [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] Jeela Brebtin. Shortly before [[Colonel]] [[Soran Keize]] left the 204th before the [[Battle of Jakku]], he visited Mirro and told him he'd never forgotten his promise to him.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
