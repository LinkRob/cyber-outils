A '''droid tank''' was a type of automated [[tank]] that were [[droid]]s themselves instead of needing a living [[pilot]] or even a droid. The tanks were used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref>
