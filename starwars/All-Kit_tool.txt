'''All-Kit tools''' were a type of all-purpose combination tool, and had [[torque wrench]], [[arc probe]], and [[fusioncutter]] functions.<ref name="ILMXLAB" /><ref name="TALESGALAXEDGE">''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge]]''</ref>

==Characteristics==
All-Kit tools were combination tools meant to be used across a broad range of situations, from making repairs on the fly, hotwiring [[blast door]]s, or cutting open [[cargo container]]s to name but a few.<ref name="ILMXLAB" /> [[Ty Yorrick]] was using an all-kit tool to repair the [[stabilizer]] of [[Rancor Rider|her]] [[starship]] when she was approached by two [[Safrifan]]s about a monster which was [[Death|killing]] their [[Youngling|children]]. Yorrick dropped the tool when she heard why they wanted her help.<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>
