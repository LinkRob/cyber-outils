The '''lightsaber''' wielded by the [[Zabrak]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Agen Kolar]] had the particularity of using dual [[kyber crystal]]s, which allowed the [[color]] of the [[plasma]] blade to be either green or blue.<ref name="ECUC" /> The second kyber [[crystal]] was from the [[lightsaber]] of his [[Padawan]], [[Tan Yuster]], who was [[Death|killed]] at the [[First Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="SWL" /> One time he used the weapon was when he [[Duel in Palpatine's office|died]] at the hands of [[Darth Sidious]] alongside fellow masters [[Saesee Tiin]], [[Kit Fisto]], and [[Mace Windu]].<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" /> It appeared to be the same design as [[Adi Gallia's second lightsaber]].<ref name="Adi Gallia SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Lightsaber Collection|cardname=Adi Gallia}}</ref>
