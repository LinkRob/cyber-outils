The '''"X-Wing" TechPlate''' is a rare fine art print produced by [[Acme Archives Direct]] that depicts the technical schematics of the [[T-65 X-wing starfighter/Legends|T-65 X-wing starfighter]]. It was first unveiled at [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World|Walt Disney World's]] [[Star Wars Weekends|''Star Wars'' Weekends]] in [[May]] [[2013]].
