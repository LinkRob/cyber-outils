The '''catacombs of Akiva'''<ref name="Star Wars 35" /> were located beneath [[Myrra]] on the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] utilized a [[Akiva production plant|droid factory]] in the catacombs during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> After they were forced to temporarily shutter [[Sunspot Prison]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] established a [[Rebel Alliance holding facility|holding facility]] in the catacombs of Akiva.<ref name="Star Wars 35" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Kay Vess]], [[Gedeek Obaz]] and [[ND-5]] traveled through the catacombs from the basement of [[Temmin Wexley]]'s [[Temmin's|shop]] in Myrra to the droid factory in an attempt to find a compatible [[compound regulator]] for ND-5's [[power core]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The catacombs of Akiva first appeared in the [[2015]] novel, [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] by [[Chuck Wendig]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> In [[2017]] they were identified as the "catacombs of Akiva" in the [[Star Wars (2015) 35|35 issue]] of the [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']] comic series.<ref name="Star Wars 35" /> It was later shown as part of "The Legacy" main quest in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
