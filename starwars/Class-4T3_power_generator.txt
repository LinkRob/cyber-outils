The '''Class-4T3 power generator''' was a [[power generator]] manufactured by [[Eksoan]], and used to power [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]]s. It had a tendency to overheat if it wasn't watched closely, especially if it was used to support [[Infantry|ground forces]]. It needed to be charged in advance, as its commissioning took up to fifteen [[Standard minute|minutes]].<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Class-4T3 power generator was first mentioned in the [[Imperial Sourcebook]], a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] sourcebook for [[West End Games]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', released in [[October]] [[1989]].<ref name="Roleplay">''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''</ref> The Class-4T3 power generator was first named in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[De Agostini|Altaya's]] "[[Blasters and Ranged Weapons]]" reference book, released exclusively in France on [[February 23]], [[2021]].<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" />
