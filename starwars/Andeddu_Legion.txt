The '''Andeddu Legion''', designated as the '''5th''', was a [[Sith trooper]] [[legion]] of the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith cult|cult]]. Consisting of five thousand [[trooper]]s within its ranks, the legion was active during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]] until the Sith Eternal's forces were defeated during the [[Battle of Exegol]] in [[35 ABY]]. As their history was hidden from the rest of [[the galaxy]], only the Sith Eternal's [[Sith cultist|cultists]] understood the significance of the legion's name: the [[Timeline of galactic history|ancient]] [[Sith Lord]] [[Andeddu]].

==Description==
[[File:SithTroopers-AoRTOCE.png|thumb|right|180px|The Andeddu Legion consisted of 5,000 Sith troopers (pictured).]]
The Andeddu Legion, identified numerically as 5th, was a [[legion]] within the [[Sith Eternal army|army]] of the [[Sith Eternal]], a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Sith cult|cult]] that worshipped the [[Sith]] and was headquartered on the [[Unknown Regions]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> Sith [[throneworld]]<ref name="DB Sith">{{Databank|sith|Sith}}</ref> [[Exegol]]. All Sith Eternal legions were numerically designated and given the name of an [[Timeline of galactic history|ancient]] [[Sith Lord]], which was [[Andeddu]] for the 5th. Only the Sith Eternal [[Sith cultist|cultists]] understood the significance of the names given to their legions, as their history was hidden to the rest of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

The legion had five thousand [[Sith trooper]]s and [[Sith Jetpack Trooper]]s<ref name="Encyclopedia" />&mdash;[[soldier]]s created from the offspring of cultists,<ref name="TROS VD" /> and [[Youngling|children]] conscripted into the ranks of the [[First Order]] who were identified and disappeared due to their potential.<ref name="TROS Expanded">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> The [[trooper]]s within the legion were further divided into [[squadron]]s of ten each, split between standard troopers and aerial soldiers.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> As the Andeddu Legion was comprised of Sith troopers, these troopers were equipped with [[Color|crimson red]] [[Sith trooper armor]] and could be armed with [[ST-W48 blaster rifle]]s or [[FWMB-10B repeating blaster]]s, while Sith Jetpack Troopers were equipped with [[NJP-900 integrated jetpack]]s and [[F-11ABA heavy blaster cannon]]s.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==History==
The Andeddu Legion was active by the time of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the First Order and the [[Resistance]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> which took place from [[34 ABY]] to [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The Sith Eternal and its [[Sith Eternal military|forces]] were defeated during the [[Battle of Exegol]] in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]<ref name="Note" /> when a joint Resistance force and a [[Citizens' Fleet]] from across the [[galaxy]] defeated [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]] and the entirety of his [[Final Order|forces]],<ref name="TROS Expanded" /> including the Andeddu Legion.<ref name="Note" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Andeddu Legion was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was released in [[2019]] and authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
