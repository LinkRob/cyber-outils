A [[Color|green]]-bladed '''lightsaber''' was the personal weapon of [[Jedi Master]] [[Even Piell]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="The Citadel" /> It was shorter than average to account for the [[Lannik (species)|Lannik]] Jedi Master's diminutive size, which would make wielding a full-size lightsaber impractical, and had a chrome-like sheen to its hilt.<ref name="Star Wars: The Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref>

Master Piell wielded his green-bladed weapon during his tenure as a member of the [[Jedi High Council]] and a General in the Clone Wars. After a mission where he recovered coordinates for a secret [[hyperspace]] [[Hyperspace route|lane]] called the [[Nexus Route]], Piell's [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|Jedi cruiser]] came under attack by a Separatist fleet and he was captured after [[droideka]]s boarded his ship and surrounded him. Piell was taken to the [[Citadel]], a prison on [[Lola Sayu]] that had once been intended to hold Jedi if they ever fell to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]], and [[torture]]d in an attempt to get the information out of him. A Republic task force led by [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] arrived to rescue him, however, with Kenobi returning Piell's lightsaber to him. Piell used the weapon several times during the course of the escape, destroying a [[Ringneck recon droid|probe droid]] and several [[LM-432 crab droid|crab droids]], but was [[Death|fatally]] wounded by an [[anooba]] and passed on his half of the coordinates to [[Ahsoka Tano]] before perishing. His lightsaber was returned to the Jedi Temple.<ref name="The Citadel" /><ref name="Citadel Rescue">{{TCW|Citadel Rescue}}</ref>
