'''Fatil''' was a blonde [[Gender|female]] who represented the<ref name="Bloodline" /> [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="TFA Beginner Game">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> world [[Orinda]] in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] of the [[New Republic]]. She admired the power of the [[Galactic Empire]], and attended a reception in which [[Senator]] [[Ransolm Casterfo]] displayed his collection of Imperial artifacts to a gathering of his fellow collectors, which he secretly hosted in order to determine if any of those he invited had ties to [[Rinnrivin Di]] and [[Rinnrivin Di's cartel|his cartel]].<ref name="Bloodline" />
