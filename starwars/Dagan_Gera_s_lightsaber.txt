'''Dagan Gera's lightsaber'''<ref name="Art of THR">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref> was a [[Color|yellow]]-bladed [[Split saber|split lightsaber]] wielded by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Dagan Gera]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. After being released from stasis during the [[Imperial Era]] in [[9 BBY]], Gera [[Bleeding|bled]] the [[kyber crystal]] and the blade turned red.<ref name="Survivor" />

After Gera's death at the hands of [[Cal Kestis]] and [[Bode Akuna]], Akuna secretly retrieved one of the blades and carried it as his own after he betrayed Kestis.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Description==
Constructed by the renowned Jedi Knight Dagan Gera, this weapon possessed an ornate hilt design indicative of the era the weapon was built in, and had yellow blades during Gera's time as a Jedi. However, during the Imperial Era, Gera bled the crystal, turning the saber's blades a deep crimson.<ref name="Survivor" />

It was a [[split saber]] that had the ability to be wielded in both single and [[double-bladed lightsaber|double-bladed]] forms.<ref name="Survivor" />

==History==
===High Republic Era===
Dagan Gera constructed his lightsaber at the height of the High Republic Era, a golden age of both the [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Galactic Republic]]. At one point, he dueled the [[Gen'Dai]] warrior [[Rayvis]] in single combat and defeated him, becoming the only warrior at the time to ever do so.<ref name="Survivor" />

Gera wielded the blade during the [[Nihil]] [[Attack on Tanalorr|invasion]] of the planet [[Tanalorr]], during which he led the Jedi's defense of the planet. After the [[Jedi Council]] ordered Tanalorr abandoned, an infuriated Gera murdered several of his fellow Jedi with the lightsaber.<ref name="Survivor" />

When Gera was critically injured and lost his right arm in an altercation with [[Santari Khri]], she placed half of the lightsaber in the [[Forest Array]]'s Rehabilitation Wing along with Gera himself, who was submerged in a [[bacta tank]] for preservation on Khri's orders.<ref name="Survivor" />

===Age of the Empire===
After two centuries of stasis, Gera was released from his prison from Jedi Knight [[Cal Kestis]], who informed him of the [[Order 66|destruction]] of the Jedi and the [[Declaration of a New Order|rise]] of a Galactic Empire from the ashes of the Republic. Kestis then returned Gera's lightsaber to him. After staggering to the nearest flat surface, Gera telekinetically disassembled his lightsaber and poured his anger and hatred for the Jedi Order, who he felt had betrayed him, into the crystal, [[bleeding]] it into a deep red.<ref name="Survivor" />
[[File:DaganGera_RedBladeshown.png|thumb|left|250px|Dagan Gera bled the kyber crystal of his lightsaber red after emerging from stasis.]]

Gera used the saber to duel Kestis multiple times during the fallen Jedi's quest to return to Tanalorr. After Gera's [[death]] at the hands of Kestis and his friend [[Bode Akuna]], Bode secretly retrieved half of the lightsaber.<ref name="Survivor" />

When Akuna betrayed Kestis and the [[Hidden Path]] to the Empire, revealed himself as a former Jedi, and attempted to reach Tanalorr on his own with his [[Kata Akuna|daughter]], he used Gera's blade to duel Kestis on two occasions. While he was victorious in their first encounter, Akuna was eventually defeated by Kestis in their [[Duel on Tanalorr|second encounter]], with the lightsaber knocked out of his hands. Kestis then killed Akuna when he refused to see reason. The saber was subsequently left behind in the temple on Tanalorr as Kestis and the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]'' [[Stinger Mantis crew|crew]] held a [[Funeral on Tanalorr|funeral]] for Akuna, [[Cere Junda]], and [[Eno Cordova]].<ref name="Survivor" />
