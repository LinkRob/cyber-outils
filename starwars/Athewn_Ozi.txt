'''Athewn Ozi''' was an individual who served as a [[captain]] in the [[First Order]] and was in [[Commanding officer|command]] of the [[Kijimi garrison|garrison]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]]. He instructed his subordinates to assign a [[lieutenant]] in charge of each area of [[Kijimi City]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> with one [[officer]] managing a portion of their occupational forces. One of the lieutenants serving under Ozi in the [[city]] was [[Lodent Cowell]].<ref name="GOH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref>

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref> when the [[Jedi]]-in-[[Jedi training|training]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] was being hunted by First Order [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], Ozi had one such lieutenant, [[Barok]], lock down a perimeter around the city.<ref name="TROS VD" /> The [[Sith Eternal]] later [[Destruction of Kijimi|destroyed]] Kijimi with a [[Derriphan|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer]] of the [[Final Order]] [[fleet]].<ref name="IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
