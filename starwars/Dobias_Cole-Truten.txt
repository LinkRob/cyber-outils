'''Dobias Cole-Truten''' was a [[human]] who made a meager living on [[Jedha]] trapping and selling [[silichordate]] [[worm]]s that other vendors would turn into meals. He required a [[rebreather]] due to his chronic respiratory condition, which he got from digging near [[Jedha's kyber mines]].<ref name="UVG" /> Cole-Truten had a reputation, at least with [[Baze Malbus]], of selling particularly wretched worms.<ref name="Guardians of the Whills" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Cole-Truten was in [[Jedha City]] when it was [[Destruction of Jedha City|targeted]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]],<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> and was [[Death|killed]] in the destruction.<ref name="Geektionary" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dobias Cole-Truten was first shown in the reel of promotional footage released for the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' at [[Celebration Europe (2016)|Celebration Europe]] on [[July 15]], [[2016]].<ref name="Celebration">{{SWYouTube|HUb_zpdyDpU|Rogue One: A Star Wars Story - Celebration Reel|sw_url=video/rogue-one-a-star-wars-story-celebration-reel}}</ref> He then went on to appear in the film itself which was released in North America on [[December 15]] later that year. Cole was not named in the film, but received identification in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was released the [[December 16|day after the film]].
