A '''double viol''' was a type of [[music]]al instrument. [[Axel Greylark]], a student at [[Reena University]], was proficient with it.<ref name="Queen's Bloom" /> Around [[396 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' establishes that [[Axel Greylark]] was eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old thirteen years prior to its events. As ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''Convergence'' to [[382 BBY]], he must have been [[Reproduction|born]] in [[413 BBY]]. Greylark is seventeen years old in "[[The Queen's Bloom]]," dating its events to around [[396 BBY]].</ref> he had been expected to perform as part of the [[university]]'s celebration of the [[Queen's Bloom Festival]], an [[Standard year|annual]] [[festival]] on the [[planet]] [[Reena]] that celebrated the blooming of [[plant]]s across the planet after a season of torrential rain. However, Greylark faked catching [[malongo pox]] to avoid participating in the university's festivities.<ref name="Queen's Bloom" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Tionne1.jpg|thumb|right|150px|A double viol as depicted in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]]
The double viol was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[The Queen's Bloom]]," a short story written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Queen's Bloom" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Double viol/Legends|double viol]] first appeared, albeit unidentified, in the [[1994]] novel [[Dark Apprentice (novel)|''Dark Apprentice'']], the second volume of [[Star Wars: The Jedi Academy Trilogy|''The Jedi Academy Trilogy'']], written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]].<ref name="Dark Apprentice">[[Dark Apprentice (novel)|''Dark Apprentice'']]</ref> It was first visually depicted and identified by name in the short story "[[Firestorm (short story)|Firestorm]]," also written by Anderson and published in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 15|fifteenth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'' by [[West End Games]] in [[November]] [[1997]]. The story's artwork was illustrated by [[Doug Shuler]].<ref name="Firestorm">{{StoryCite|story=Firestorm|book=Star Wars Adventure Journal 15}}</ref>
