'''C26-T''' was a [[sifter droid]] that was forced to work in the criminal [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s [[spice]] [[Spice mines of Kessel|mines]] on the [[planet]] [[Kessel]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on [[Kessel]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to nine years after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]], therefore the event on Kessel in ''Solo'' must take place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> C26-T was freed from [[slavery]] when its [[restraining bolt]] was removed during a [[Raid on Kessel|slave revolt]] in the [[Mining|mines]] started by the [[droid]] [[L3-37]].<ref name="Solo" />
