A '''Burtt acoustic signaler''' was an [[acoustic signaller]] that was mounted on the front of [[R2-series astromech droid]]s such as [[R2-D2]].<ref name="BR21">{{BuildR2Cite|1|Droid Directory}}</ref>

{{Tech-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Burtt acoustic signaler shares it's name with real-world sound designer [[Ben Burtt]], who created the [[binary]] sounds for [[R2-D2]].<ref name="guide">[[Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide (real-life book)|''Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide'']]</ref>
