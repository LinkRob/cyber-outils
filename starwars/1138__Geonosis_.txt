'''1138''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] that served the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] as part of a [[Geonosian droid army|droid army]] on the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]]. The B1's designation was written in [[Trade Federation Script]] on the back of their [[comlink booster pack]].<ref name="Episode II" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[B-series battle droid]] was among those deployed to the [[Petranaki Arena]] to [[First Battle of Geonosis|combat]] a [[Jedi assault team]] that had infiltrated the arena to rescue three [[prisoner]]s due for [[Death|execution]]: [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Episode II" />

As fighting broke out on the floor of the arena, 1138 was firing at opposing [[Jedi]] while [[Mace Windu]] deflected a blaster shot into a [[OOM command battle droid]].<ref name="Episode II" />

==Behind the scenes==
1138 first appeared in the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]]. The [[droid]]'s designation is written in [[Trade Federation Script]], but can easily be deciphered even without a guide;<ref name="Episode II" /> the numbers were officially identified in ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'', which gave a translation for B1 numerals.<ref name="Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
