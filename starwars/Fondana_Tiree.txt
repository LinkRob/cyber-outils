'''Fondana Tiree''' was the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Minister]] of [[Education Ministry|Education]] on [[Lothal]] during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]]. She was the [[Parent|mother]] of [[Kinera Tiree]], who attended [[Phelarion School]]. Due to her senior position, Fondana was well known to local government officials. While helping [[Zare Leonis]] to find his missing sister [[Dhara Leonis]], [[Merei Spanjaf]] impersonated Fondana's daughter Kinera during a [[Transportation Ministry data breach|data breach]] at the [[Transportation Ministry]]'s headquarters in [[Capital City]].<ref name="RitR" />
