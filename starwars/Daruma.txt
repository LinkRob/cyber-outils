'''Daruma''' was a [[droid]] of the [[Jedi Order]] and served as a companion to the [[Padawan]] [[Nakime]] and her [[Nakime's Jedi Master|Jedi Master]]. At some point, the group traveled to an [[Unidentified astronomical object (sacred tree forest)|astronomical object]] where they encountered the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Aman]] in a [[lightsaber duel]] atop a [[mountain]]. During the battle, Nakime performed a handspring off of Daruma as he used his [[Engine|thrusters]] to boost her jump. The attempted attack allowed for Aman to slash the Padawan's [[eye]]s, incapacitating and blinding her. Later, separated from her group, Nakime experienced visions featuring her master and Daruma.

==Biography==
A [[droid]] of the [[Jedi Order]], Daruma was the companion to a [[Nakime's Jedi Master|Jedi Master]] and his eager [[Padawan]] [[Nakime]]. [[Standard month|Months]] after a meeting between them, the trio traveled to a [[forest]]ed [[Unidentified astronomical object (sacred tree forest)|astronomical object]]. On the world, the group encountered<ref name="Paradise" /> the [[Sith apprentice]]<ref name="Focus">{{SWYouTube|series=Star Wars: Visions Filmmaker Focus|text=THE BIRD OF PARADISE|video=fSaJe2qO0J4}}</ref> [[Aman]] and engaged her in a [[lightsaber duel]] atop a [[mountain]]. The [[Sith]] used her [[Aman's lightsabers|lightsabers]] to force Nakime off of a shallow ledge before the [[Jedi Master]] fended off the attack. Daruma helped Nakime up back on to the mountain as the master took the offense. As the battle continued, the master advanced Aman further along the mountain as Daruma trailed behind, passing a disgruntled Nakime as she recovered.<ref name="Paradise" />

[[File:NakimeHandspring-BirdOfParadise.gif|thumb|right|300px|Nakime performing a handspring off Daruma's dome]]
After the master delivered a firm kick to Aman, she was sent reeling. Nakime took the opportunity to attempt a strike on Aman with her [[Nakime's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. She ran up behind Daruma and performed a handspring off of<ref name="Paradise" /> his<ref name="ParadiseAD" /> dome as he activated his [[Engine|thrusters]] to give her a boost. Nakime flipped over the master but was slashed by Aman's [[lightsaber]]s, blinding her and causing the [[Jedi]] to slide down the mountainside.<ref name="Paradise" />

Later, Nakime awoke at the bottom of the mountain and began to wander the [[sacred tree forest]]. On her second [[Standard day|day]], Nakime experienced a [[Force vision]] of Daruma that spoke in beeps and whistles. Nakime became annoyed with the vision, not understanding what his chirps meant. A vision of her master then formed, attempting to guide her in trusting the Force. Nakime yelled at the visions of Daruma and her master before they faded away. On the fourth day, while facing hunger, Nakime had another vision of the meeting between Daruma, her master, and Nakime herself months prior. The vision eventually faded away into an illusion of Aman, which challenged her [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]], which she later came to peace with in another vision.<ref name="Paradise" />

==Characteristics==
Daruma was a short spherical droid with a rounded dome for a head. He had treads attached to four [[Limb|legs]] that could be used as powerful boosters. A half-sphere that was capable of opening and closing was found on both sides of Daruma's [[Color|yellow]], white, and gray plated body. His head had eight purple [[Photoreceptor|sensors]] alongside five larger black sensors and two [[antenna]]s, one on each side.<ref name="Paradise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Daruma first appeared in "[[The Bird of Paradise]]," a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' animated series, produced by [[Polygon Pictures]] and released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Paradise" /> on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/why-we-love-star-wars-visions|text=Why We Love the Unique Stories of ''Star Wars: Visions''}}</ref> The droid was first pictured in a trailer released on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel on [[September 25]], 2025.<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|1lpOORwkbdE|Star Wars: Visions Volume 3 &#124; English Dub Trailer &#124; October 29 on Disney+}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> In concept art by [[Makiko Nanke]] created on [[June 28]], [[2023]], Daruma was portrayed as an [[R2-series astromech droid]] in Nakime's first Force vision.<ref name="Nanke">{{Twitter|username=@starwars|url=status/1985452313142800517|name=[[Star Wars]]|date=2025-11-03|quote=collect them all! Take a look at the stunning concept art behind Star Wars: Visions Volume 3 now streaming on @Disneyplus.|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/ABsOU?wr=false}}</ref> [[Satoshi Oshida]] and [[Takamichi Hasegawa]] created concept art of Daruma's finished design.<ref name="Concept3">{{SW|url=the-bird-of-paradise-concept-art-gallery-star-wars-visions-volume-3|text="The Bird of Paradise" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars: Visions Volume 3}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> Daruma shares his name with the real-world [[Wikipedia:Daruma doll|Daruma doll]], a type of traditional handmade wishing doll made in [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japan]].<ref name="Doll">{{WebCite|url=https://www.daruma.jp/about.html|text=What is a daruma doll?|work=[https://daruma-ito.co.jp daruma-ito.co.jp]|archivedate=20250207101649}}</ref>
