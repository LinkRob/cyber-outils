"'''All Our Sins Remembered'''" is the seventh adventure in ''[[The Bissillirus Campaign]]''.

==Plot summary==
The group of Alliance operatives investigating the creation of the [[Bissillirus Resupply Base]] travel to the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Entrus]] to report to [[Rebel Sector Command]].
