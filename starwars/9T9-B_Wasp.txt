The '''9T9-B ''Wasp''''', also known as the '''Voltec KT9 ''Wasp''''',<ref name="EV" /> was a [[podracer]] model manufactured by [[Keizar-Volvec|Voltec]]. The podracer was identifiable by its massive, twin turbine [[engine]]s. The [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[pilot]] [[Clegg Holdfast]] utilized a ''Wasp'' with [[Color|red]] detailing for a [[Boonta Eve Classic]] race. The podracer was destroyed during the second lap.<ref name="Episode I" />
