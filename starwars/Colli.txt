'''Colli''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Tholothian]] who lived on the [[Al'doleem|river moon of Al'doleem]].

==Biography==
===Early years and escape from Am'balaar City===
Colli was [[Reproduction|born]] to the [[Tholothian]]s [[Jogg]] and [[Mareena]]. By [[19 BBY]], he helped his [[parent]]s run the garage. When [[Jedi Master]] [[Kirak Infil'a]] came, they both greeted him and he told them the tale of how he [[Mission to Al'doleem|had]] [[Death|killed]] a [[Anakin Skywalker|Sith]] and that now he wanted to go kill the [[Galactic Emperor]] as well and restore light to [[the galaxy]]. Colli was amazed by this and Mareena told him that their family never believed that the [[Jedi]] were traitors and they started fixing [[Kirak Infil'a's Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Infil'a's ship]].<ref name="Darth Vader 4" />

Colli was shocked when Infil'a [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]], saying that even her [[Parent|father]] couldn't lift them without help. Infil'a replied that the Force made all things light, and told her of his plans to rebuild the Jedi and to come back and teach her the mysteries of the Force. Suddenly, Infil'a felt Vader coming and dropped the materials, with Colli running to protect both himself and [[Colli's sister|his sister]]. Colli gave the baby to her father and when they came out, Vader challenged Infil'a to a [[lightsaber duel]]. Even though the others were confused, Colli asked if Vader was the [[Sith]], but Infil'a told them all to go back to the [[Jogg and Mareena's garage|garage]] and fix the [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="Darth Vader 4" /> Colli and his sister managed to escape, though his parents were killed when Vader flooded the [[Am'balaar City|city]]. Years later he met [[Luke Skywalker]], whom he recognized as a Jedi. Colli told him the story of how Infil'a saved his life, and helped him find his way to [[Mount Pasvaal]].<ref name="Star Wars 19" />

==Personality and traits==
Colli had [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s and light skin. He wore a [[Tholoth headdress]]. Colli didn't believe that the Jedi were traitors and was on good terms with Kirak Infil'a. He was amazed when Infil'a used the Force. It is possible that Colli was [[Force-sensitive]] as Infil'a talked about teaching him the mysteries of the Force and rebuilding the order on Mount Pasvaal.<ref name="Darth Vader 4" />
