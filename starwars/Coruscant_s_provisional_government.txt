'''Coruscant's provisional government''' was established on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] in the [[Galactic Concordance|aftermath]] of the [[Galactic Civil War|collapse]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], forming from the [[New Order|regime's]] [[Coruscanti government|local authorities]] on Coruscant. [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] was designated to lead this transitional [[Politics|government]], who retained his position for his part in ending the Galactic Civil War, which [[New Republic]] observers were tasked with monitoring the provisional government.

Throughout the Empire's [[Imperial Era|reign]], Coruscant was the political and cultural [[Imperial City|center]] of [[the galaxy]]. With the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] in [[4 ABY]], however, the [[planet]] fell into a state of [[Coruscant civil war|civil war]] as [[Riot in Monument Plaza|rebellious]] [[Coruscanti|citizens]] fought against [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] across much of Coruscant. During the conflict, the Emperor's nominal successor, [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]], was [[Prison|imprisoned]] in the [[Imperial Palace]] by forces loyal to [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]], who seized power as the self-styled [[Counselor to the Empire]]. 

Following the [[Battle of Jakku]], the Galactic Empire was represented by Amedda at the signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]], a peace treaty that officially ended the war in [[5 ABY]]. The New Republic then allowed a provisional government to be formed on Coruscant with Amedda as its figurehead.

Throughout the [[Millennium|millennia]] [[the galaxy]]'s [[Politics|political]] power was concentrated on the [[Core Worlds|Core World]] [[Coruscant]],<ref name="Hosnian Prime DB">{{Databank|hosnian-prime|Hosnian Prime}}</ref> and the alleged [[homeworld]] of [[human]]ity<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> became a [[Galaxy|galactic]] hub of cultural diversity from the [[Republic Era|age]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] to the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Coruscant DB">{{Databank|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref> When [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] was [[Death|killed]] at the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the Empire splintered into various political and [[Imperial Military|military]] factions<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> such as [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]'s [[Ubrik Adelhard's Imperial remnant|domain]] in the [[Anoat sector]].<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> At the same [[time]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] capitalized on its victory at [[Endor]] by [[Declaration of a New Republic|reforming]] itself into the [[New Republic]].<ref name="New Republic DB">{{Databank|the-new-republic|The New Republic}}</ref> Meanwhile, the Empire's authority became increasingly diminished across the galaxy.<ref name="Shattered Empire 3">[[Shattered Empire 3|''Shattered Empire'' 3]]</ref>

[[File:Corsectrooper.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Chaos and civil war ensued across Coruscant in the aftermath of Emperor Palpatine's death.]]
Coruscant fell into a state of [[Coruscant civil war|civil war]] between Imperial forces and Rebel factions fighting for control of the [[Federal District]] as well as other government sectors.<ref name="Empire's End" /> [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] sought to negotiate his personal surrender to the New Republic. He secretly met with a Republic delegation on [[Velusia]] during the final [[Standard month|months]] of the war, seeking amnesty for his role in the Emperor's regime. Neither [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] nor [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] were willing to settle for Amedda alone, however, and demanded that he instead surrender the whole of the Galactic Empire. Their conditions left the Grand [[Vizier]] stunned; despite his legal authority, he did not have the power to force the Empire to capitulate.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Amedda returned to Coruscant where he contemplated [[Death|suicide]], knowing that the Republic would not accept a treaty unless he could give them the Empire's unconditional surrender.<ref name="Life Debt" /> As Amedda's influence waned, [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]] assumed ''de facto'' control of the regime as the self-styled [[Counselor to the Empire]]. His supporters on Coruscant seized custody of Amedda and placed him under [[Prisoner|house arrest]] in the [[Imperial Palace]], forcing the Grand Vizier to become Rax's figurehead for [[propaganda]] purposes.<ref name="Empire's End" />

===Formation===
{{Quote|Mas Amedda has come out of hiding and wants to sign a cease-fire. He wants to end it. All of it.|Mon Mothma, relaying the news of the Galactic Empire's surrender to the New Republic.|<ref name="Empire's End" />}}
[[File:Disintegrating-Imperial-Crest-RISEANDFALL.png|thumb|left|250px|A provisional government replaced the Imperial regime on Coruscant after the Empire's capitulation to the New Republic.]]
Within a [[Standard year|year]] of the Emperor's death, the Empire was decisively defeated by the Republic at the [[Battle of Jakku]], resulting in the death of Counselor Rax. Though a few Imperial remnants continued to persist in their resistance, the [[Galactic Civil War]] came to an end<ref name="Empire's End" /> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="GA" /> Following the collapse of the Galactic Empire, a provisional government on Coruscant was established under the transitional leadership of Mas Amedda. The Grand Vizier had represented the remaining Imperial government at the signing of the peace treaty, known as the [[Galactic Concordance]], on [[Chandrila]]. The New Republic in turn allowed Amedda to lead the provisional government on Coruscant, but only as a powerless figurehead.<ref name="Empire's End" />

The conditions of the Concordance required the transfer of Coruscant from Imperial control,<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> with a provisional government formed to rule in the interim. As such, New Republic observers were tasked with monitoring the provisional government.<ref name="Empire's End" />

Despite Amedda being designated<ref name="Empire's End" /> as provisional leader,<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> the people of Coruscant celebrated the New Republic's victory over the Galactic Empire and the official end of the Galactic Civil War. Unlike before, after the Battle of Endor, the Coruscanti were allowed to celebrate in public by the provisional government. The New Republic encouraged this festive atmosphere that spread across Coruscant and other planets throughout the galaxy, as a way to distinguish the [[New Republic Era]] from the Empire's reign.<ref name="Empire's End" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|The capital of the Old Republic was within the Core on a planet called Coruscant. The capital of the New Republic is currently on the planet Chandrila, which is also within the Core region.|[[CB-3DU]]|<ref name="Chapter 12: The Siege">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 12: The Siege}}</ref>}}
[[File:New-Republic-skyscraper.png|thumb|right|250px|Coruscant joined the New Republic but had lost its status as the galactic capital.]]
The provisional government was ultimately followed by Coruscant joining the New Republic, thereby allowing it to have representation in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] as a member world of the fledgling democracy. During the later years of the New Republic, Coruscant was part of a political bloc that advocated the adoption of a stronger military and centralized government, informally known as the [[Centrists]]. By the time of the [[Napkin Bombing]] in [[28 ABY]], [[Coruscanti]] political interests were represented by [[Senior Senator]] [[Arbo]] and [[Junior senator|Junior Senator]] [[Orris Madmund]], both of whom supported the goals of the Centrist faction. Senator Arbo was well known for his war hawk views and support of the [[New Republic Defense Force]].<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>

==Organization==
{{Main|Grand Vizier}}
[[File:GalacticConcordance-BattleOfJakkuLastStand4.png|thumb|left|250px|Coruscant's provisional government was a post-war government led by Mas Amedda and overseen by the New Republic.]]
Coruscant's provisional government was a powerless regime<ref name="Empire's End" /> led ''de facto''<ref name="TSWB" /> by Mas Amedda, the Grand Vizier of the former Galactic Empire. Although Amedda was allowed to continue his rule on the former Imperial capital, having played his part in surrendering the Empire and ending the Galactic Civil War, the New Republic's overseers on Coruscant ensured that he would only serve as a figurehead for the provisional government.<ref name="Empire's End" />

Coruscant was in a state of turmoil at the time of the provisional government's formation,<ref name="Empire's End" /> having experienced a [[Riot in Monument Plaza|decline in public order]]<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> and the planetary civil war that followed in the wake of Emperor Palpatine's death.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> With the signing of the Galactic Concordance, the provisional government lacked the power to suppress the Coruscanti who celebrated the Empire's downfall. Under the New Republic's oversight, the provisional government allowed the civilian population to openly celebrate in the streets of [[Imperial City]] without the threat of reprisal.<ref name="Empire's End" />
