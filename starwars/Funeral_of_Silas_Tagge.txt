Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''funeral''' was held for<ref name="Aphra 17" /> [[Silas Tagge]]&mdash;the former [[Head of Weapons Development]] in the [[Tagge Corporation]]<ref name="WOTBH Boushh">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]]</ref>&mdash;after<ref name="Aphra 17" /> he was [[Death|killed]] by his [[House of Tagge|sister]]&mdash;[[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]]&mdash;following his unsuccessful [[Skirmish aboard the Acquisitor|attempt]] to take over control of the company.<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" /> After<ref name="Aphra 17" /> he<ref name="Aphra 7">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 7|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 7]]</ref> recovered<ref name="Aphra 17" /> from being blown up by the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]],<ref name="Aphra 6">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 6]]</ref> [[Ronen Tagge]]&mdash;the nephew of Domina and Silas&mdash;caught up with news he had missed, including the [[funeral]]. He later discussed the funeral with his aunt, telling<ref name="Aphra 17" /> her<ref name="Aphra 6" /> he had heard it was fabulous, to which Domina responded that it was. As she thought back to killing her brother, Domina added that only the best would do for family.<ref name="Aphra 17" />

==Behind the scenes==
The funeral of Silas Tagge was mentioned in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 17|seventeenth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 17" /> on [[January 5]], [[2022]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/96840/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_17|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #17}}</ref>
