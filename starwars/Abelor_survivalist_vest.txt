A  '''Abelor survivalist vest''' was a type of durable [[Clothing|vest]] manufactured<ref name="Outlaws" /> on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Abelor]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> It was brown in [[color]] with a black vertical strap next to a silver colored circle with a semicircular symbol and a rectangle at the bottom on upper-right chest of the vest. The back of the vest featured a large orange [[Aurek]] symbol from the [[Aurebesh]] alphabet. The vest also included a pair of dark green fingerless [[Color|gloves]] and a grey undershirt with dark green cuffs.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a Abelor survivalist vest was for sale in [[Wesla's]], a shop on the Mid Rim planet [[Kijimi]] that was operated by the [[Twi'lek]] [[tailor]] [[Lani Wesla]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] outlaw [[Kay Vess]] purchased a Abelor survivalist vest from Wesla.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A Abelor survivalist vest appeared in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', a [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. When equipped by the player, the vest reduces damage taken from [[blaster]] and projectile [[weapon]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Iuliia Misiul]] created concept art of the Abelor survivalist vest and other outfits used by Vess.<ref name="Art Station">{{ArtStation|username=Iuliia Misiul|url=/artwork/4N9Ve4|name=[[Iuliia Misiul]]|text=Star Wars Outlaws &mdash; Kay's Gearsets}}</ref>
