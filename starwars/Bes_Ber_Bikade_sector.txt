The '''Bes Ber Bikade sector''' was a [[sector]] located in the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> It contained the [[Cymoon system]], which itself housed [[Weapons Factory Alpha]], one of the largest Imperial weapons factory in [[the galaxy]] located in the [[moon]] [[Cymoon 1]] and target of a [[Assault on Cymoon|rebel assault]] in [[0 ABY]].<ref name ="Skywalker Strikes Part I" /><ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The sector also contained the [[Megalox system]] wich contained the heavy [[gravity]] [[planet]] [[Megalox Beta]] and its [[prison]] [[Megalox]], target of a [[Resistance]] [[Mission to Megalox Beta|operation]] in [[32 ABY]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 4" /><ref name="Timelines" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bes Ber Bikade sector had its first appearance in [[canon]] in [[Star Wars (2015) 1|''Star Wars'' (2015) 1]], the first issue of the [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'' (2015)]].<ref name ="Skywalker Strikes Part I" /> However it was first identified as the Bes Ber Bikade sector in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]].''<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], it was first mentioned in ''[[Shield of Lies]]'', the second [[Book|novel]] of ''[[The Black Fleet Crisis]]'' trilogy, published by [[Bantam Spectra]] in [[September]], [[1996]].<ref name="Shield">''[[Shield of Lies]]''</ref>
