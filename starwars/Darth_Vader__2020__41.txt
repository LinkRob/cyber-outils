'''''Darth Vader'' 41''' is the forty-first issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]] and [[Federico Blee]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 20]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''MORE MACHINE THAN MAN! &ndash; A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! DARTH VADER has been taken over by THE SCOURGE! Or has THE SCOURGE been taken over by DARTH VADER? The Dark Lord's journey in the ''DARK DROIDS'' saga reaches its climax with a shocking twist that digs deep into Vader's very identity and future!''
