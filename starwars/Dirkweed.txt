'''Dirkweed''' was a fast-growing crystalline [[Plant|bramble]] with a a dull [[Color|slate-blue]] color that could be found on [[Narthex]], the [[moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Catadra]] in the [[Deep Core]]'s [[Cerberon system]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], after Narthex had been left uninhabited for nearly a [[millennium]], dirkweed had overrun much of its structures and terrain. The weed's hue appeared to tinge the moon's [[atmosphere]] as if it were obscured by [[Fire|campfire]] smoke.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
