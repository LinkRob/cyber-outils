'''Bondeeni''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Gran]] entrepreneur who did business with [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]], the [[Baron Administrator]] of [[Cloud City]], around [[1 BBY]]. He bought transfer codes for 10,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] after a failed negotiation attempt. Shortly thereafter, he asked the help of the [[Magnate Imperium]] and the head of a [[mining]] colony, [[Roz Fantanine]], about the Calrissian incident.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />
