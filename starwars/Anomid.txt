'''Anomids''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that were [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Yablari]]. Because they lacked vocal cords, they communicated with one another through a sophisticated and subtle combination of [[Anomid sign language|hand and body language]].<ref name="GotW" /> For the purpose of interacting with other species, they wore concealing [[vocoder mask]]s that transformed their subvocal harmonics into audible speech. During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], they were routinely searched for fear that their traditional headgear was disguising wanted fugitives. [[Silvanie Phest]], a [[Gender|female]] member of the species, was part of an Anomid colony converted to the [[Disciples of the Whills]] shortly before the [[Destruction of Jedha City|destruction]] of [[Jedha City]].<ref name="UVG" />

Under their distinctive masks, Anomids had wrinkled, bumpy heads with fin-like [[ear]]s that protruded from the sides of their heads, and two forward-facing [[eye]]s with no visible irises.<ref name="Alien Archive" />

An Anomid named [[Alxa Kress]] once served on the Jedi Council during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin">''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure]]''</ref>
