The article "'''Boba Fett: A Bounty Hunter's Journey'''" was written by [[Leland Chee]] as a feature in [[Star Wars Insider 117|''Star Wars Insider'' 117]]. The feature contains a brief history of [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett's]] character background development through the course of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga up through the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series. The article was reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2013|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2013]] and [[Star Wars: Rogues, Scoundrels, and Bounty Hunters|''Star Wars Insider: Rogues, Scoundrels, and Bounty Hunters'']].
