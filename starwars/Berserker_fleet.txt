The '''berserker fleet''' was a group of [[starship]]s which fought in [[Hutt Space]] several [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Alphabet" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> When the [[New Republic Intelligence]] [[agent]] [[Caern Adan]] explained the issue of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] operating at the [[planet]] [[Pandem Nai]] to [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]], the general agreed that the [[fighter wing]] was an issue, but cited the Imperial [[Moff]] [[Valco Pandion]] and the berserker [[fleet]]'s presence in [[Hutt]] [[Realspace|space]] as other problems for the [[New Republic]] that need to be addressed.<ref name="Alphabet" />

==Behind the scenes==
