A '''Jotaz''' with an abnormal hide lived in the [[Undercity Meats]] of [[Level 2046]] on [[Coruscant]]. During his [[Mission on Coruscant|mission]] to Coruscant<ref name="Jedi: Survivor" /> in [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] killed a [[Purge Trooper]] outside of the Frenzied Jotaz's lair. As Kestis left, the [[Jotaz]] attempted to break down the door between the Jedi and itself, but failed. Kestis decided that the Jotaz (not knowing what it was) would be a foe for another time. Later, Kestis returned to the Undercity Meats. After [[BD-1]], Kestis' [[BD explorer droid|exploration droid]], [[Electro dart|shocked]] the door panel that controlled the door which the Jotaz previously failed to destroy. In response to its freedom, the Jotaz began attacking the Jedi. However, Kestis was able to survive, and killed the Jotaz.<ref name="Jedi: Survivor" />
