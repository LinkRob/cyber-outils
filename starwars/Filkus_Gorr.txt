'''Filkus Gorr''' was a [[Melbu]] [[prospector]] who repaired [[B1-series battle droid]]s to sell to the [[Bedlam Raiders]] and their leader [[Rayvis]] on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. In [[9 BBY]], the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] encountered Gorr in [[Fort Kah'Lin|Fort Kah'lin]] and used [[the Force]] to [[Mind trick|persuade]] the Melbu not to sell the [[droid]]s and find them a new home instead. Gorr then gave Kestis materials that Rayvis had requested for upgrades.

==Biography==
{{Quote|You don't want to sell these droids to Rayvis.''"<br />"''I&hellip; don't?|Cal Kestis and Filkus Gorr|<ref name="Survivor" />}}
Filkus Gorr was a [[Melbu]] [[prospector]] active on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. Gorr worked with the [[Gen'Dai]] [[Rayvis]], leader of the criminal [[Bedlam Raiders]] group, repairing [[B1-series battle droid]]s in the Raider-controlled [[Fort Kah'Lin|Fort Kah'lin]] in order to sell them to Rayvis. The Gen'Dai also requested upgrades, and so the Melbu gathered materials and stored them in a chest behind a locked door in the fort.<ref name="Survivor" />

{{GameMechanics}}
[[File:FilkusGorr-Droid-JS.png|thumb|right|300px|Filkus Gorr repaired battle droids for the Bedlam Raiders.]]
In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] discovered Gorr working on a [[battle droid]] in one of the buildings in Fort Kah'lin. When the [[Jedi]] queried what the Melbu was doing, the prospector shushed him and then explained that the [[droid]]s needed to be awakened for the ascension of Rayvis, who Gorr referred to as the [[death]]less one. Realising that Gorr was working with the Raiders, Kestis warned the Melbu it would end badly, but the prospector was undetered, focusing on the profit that would be made from selling the droids.<ref name="Survivor" />

Kestis then used [[the Force]] to [[Mind trick|persuade]] Gorr not to sell the droids to the Raiders. Gorr told the deactivated droids that they would leave to find a new home once the work on the current droid was complete. The Melbu then opened the locked door and gave Kestis the material for upgrades since it was no longer needed. The Jedi used the material on parts of his [[Mountaineer]] [[Clothing|outfit]].<ref name="Survivor" />
{{EndGame}}

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Observe, little ones. We have a visitor.''"<br />"''What's all this?''"<br />"''Hush. We mustn't wake them. They need their rest.|Filkus Gorr and Cal Kestis|<ref name="Survivor" />}}
Gorr spoke to the deactivated droids and referred to them as "little ones," worrying Kestis would wake them from their "slumber" by speaking too loudly. The Melbu was willing to work with the raiders for profit, but Kestis was able to use the Force to manipulate the prospector into finding them a new home instead. Gorr had [[Color|reddish-brown]] skin and amber [[eye]]s.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|You. Rayvis requested upgrades. I obliged. I need them no longer.|Filkus Gorr gives Cal Kestis the Mountaineer material|<ref name="Survivor" />}}
Gorr wore a blue [[jumpsuit]] with black and brown [[glove]]s and a pair of brown [[boot]]s. The Melbu had a silver device linking the lower portion of their face to the protrusions above their head with black cables. While working on the droids, the prospector used a large green power tool and had materials that could be used on clothing as upgrades for Rayvis.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Your droids deserve a kinder master than Rayvis.''"<br />"''Yes&hellip; the little ones deserve better&hellip;|One of the two possible exchanges between Cal Kestis and Filkus Gorr after Kestis uses the Force| <ref name="Survivor" />}}
Filkus Gorr appeared as an optional encounter in the [[2023]] [[Respawn Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''. When using the Force to persuade Gorr, the player can convince the Melbu by either suggesting that the Raiders are cruel and that the droids deserve a better master or by suggesting the Raiders can not be trusted with Gorr's craftmanship. As the encounter must be completed to unlock the Mountaineer material collectable, this article assumes Kestis did convince Gorr not to sell the droids, but does not assume which of the two methods of persuasion he chose.<ref name="Survivor" />
