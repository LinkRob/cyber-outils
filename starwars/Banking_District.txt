'''Banking District''' was an area of [[Janix city]]<ref name="Dark Revenge" /> on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Shadow of Maul 1">[[Shadow of Maul 1|''Shadow of Maul'' 1]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Janix]].<ref name="Dark Revenge" /> During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] the [[Gran]] [[crime lord]] [[Nico Deemis]] operated a [[Nico Deemis' bank tower|bank]] within the district.<ref name="Dark Revenge" />  At some point between between [[17 BBY]] and around [[13 BBY]],<ref name="ShadowLordDate">{{ShadowLordDate}}</ref> a group of [[Mandalorian]] and [[Zabrak]] criminals [[Raid on a bank tower|raided]] the [[bank]] while a shipment of [[credit brick]]s were being delivered. One of the [[bank guard droid]] however manged to activate an alarm and seal off the rest of the vault. The alarms were reported to [[Janix Civil Defense]] which dispatched [[Tactical Defense Force]] units to the scene which arrived as the criminals were leaving in a stolen [[bank transport]].<ref name="Dark Revenge" />
