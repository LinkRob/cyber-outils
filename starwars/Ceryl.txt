'''Ceryl''' was a [[Jedi Knight]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> she [[pilot]]ed a [[Jedi Vector]] as part of a [[Jedi]] team who carried out an [[Assault on Naboo|assault]] on<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Naboo]] in order to liberate it from Nihil [[Occupation of Naboo|rule]]. During the assault, a [[Drift]] led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] which included Ceryl, launched an attack on the [[flagship]] of Nihil [[General]]<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Abediah Viess]],<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 4">[[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 4|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 4]]</ref> the ''[[Precipitate Fire]]''.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

When the flagship moved to fire on a Republic [[Tractate|ship]], Kriss called out for the Knight to move her [[Starfighter|Vector]]. Although Ceryl managed to dodge the [[cruiser]], her Vector was tapped by it, knocking her out of the Drift and spinning and careening out of control. Jedi Knight [[Dotdat San]] peeled off from the Drift to follow Ceryl's ship. Once Naboo was freed from the Nihil, most of the [[Jedi]] and [[Republic Defense Coalition]] forces left Naboo to return to [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Realspace|space]] on the [[Emissary-class Republic Cruiser|cruiser]] ''[[Third Horizon]]'' with Ceryl and San accompanying them.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
