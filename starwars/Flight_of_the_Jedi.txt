"'''Flight of the Jedi'''" was a two-part [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure published in the [[Valkyrie 19|nineteenth]] and [[Valkyrie 20|twentieth]] issue of the ''[[Valkyrie]]'' magazine. It was written by [[Phil Sidebotham]] and introduced [[Lilith]], a [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] trainee who betrayed the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]] when, during the [[Great Jedi Purge/Legends|Purge]], she changed sides to the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] and hunted down Purge survivors.

{{Magazine-stub|article}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Having eluded the Imperial forces on Lennax, the Jedi and their companions managed to get transport offworld. Now they approach their only hope of eluding the clutches of the Empire for good, the mysterious water-world of Arrakan. But the Empire does not give up that easily.....''<ref name="CB">{{WebCite|url=https://www.caliverbooks.com/Partizan%20Press/valk%20website/V20.HTM|text=Presenting ''Valkyrie'' 20|language=English|archivedate=20070526104103}}</ref>
