A '''commanding officer''',<ref name="Tarpals">{{Databank|general-tarpals|General Tarpals}}</ref> (shortened to '''CO'''),<ref name="Final Order">{{StoryCite|story=The Final Order|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> and also referred to as a '''commander''',<ref name="MSL">{{MSL|Chapter 5: Inquisition}}</ref> was a military [[officer]] that was in command, or had direct authority, over a particular military unit or formation. Military organizations such as the [[Gungan Grand Army]]<ref name="Tarpals" /> and the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] utilized commanding officers amongst its forces,<ref name="Warthog">{{Databank|warthog|Clone Pilot Warthog}}</ref> as did the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Commanding officers could hold varying degrees of [[military rank]]s, ranging from [[junior officer]]s with the rank of [[ensign]], to [[senior officer]]s ranked as [[general]]s.<ref name="Thrawn novel">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>
