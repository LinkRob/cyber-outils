According to legend, a '''battle''' occurred on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]] when a [[Serenno (individual)|member]] of [[House Serenno]] led a charge against the [[Sith Empire]], of which Serenno was allegedly part of. The event resulted in the [[Sith]] Empire being overthrown on Serenno. Prior to the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jenza]] told her brother, [[Dooku]], both of House Serenno, about the event according to legend and how her ancestor was the one who led the charge.<ref name="Dooku" />
