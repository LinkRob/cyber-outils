'''Bala Waleen'''<ref name="Script" /> was a [[Twi'lek]] [[spicerunner]] and merchant who staged a [[Insurrection on Aaloth|coup]] and usurped rule of the [[Ryloth]] colony [[planet]] [[Aaloth]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> He was the lover of [[Lourna Dee]],<ref name="Script" /> whose [[Parent|father]] [[Yudiah Dee]] was the [[Keeper of Aaloth]] prior to Bala's coup.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

Bala conspired with Lourna to depose her father as the world's Keeper, and the [[Lourna Dee's family's palace|family's palace]] was stormed by a mob of starving citizens. Waleen betrayed Lourna, selling her into [[slavery]] to the [[Zygerrian Alliance]] and having the rest of her [[Lourna Dee's family|family]] [[Death penalty|executed]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

Dee later [[Death|killed]] Bala in revenge in a [[raid on Aaloth]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" />
