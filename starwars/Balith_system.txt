The '''Balith system''' was located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]] and in grid square N-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. The [[star system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> contained the [[planet]]<ref name="Bug">{{StoryCite|story=Bug (short story)|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref> [[Balith]],<ref name="Assassin" /> which was experiencing a [[Balith's Civil War|civil war]] in [[21 BBY]].<ref name="AssassinDate">{{TCWcanondates|307}}</ref> After receiving a request for aid from Balith's people, the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Jedi High Council]] dispatched [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] to [[Commanding officer|command]] a [[Third Legion|legion]]<ref name="Assassin" /> of [[clone trooper]]s<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> and assist in the conflict.<ref name="Assassin" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
The Balith system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Balith system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
