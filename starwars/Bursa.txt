The '''bursa''' was a type of [[Species|animal]]. On at least one occasion, the [[hunter]] [[Tyro Viveca]] watched bursa-[[Hunting|baiting]] on [[Ohma-D'un]],<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> a [[moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> During the [[Imperial Era]], Viveca [[Writing|wrote]] about that and watching monster fights on the [[Planet|world]] [[Geonosis]] in the [[book]] known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', which he had owned and lost<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> by [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Date">The entry in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' mentioning the bursas is placed chronologically prior to an entry written by the criminal [[Tobias Beckett]], who ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' states [[Death|died]] in [[10 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Bursa-wosfg.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Bursas were bear-like creatures in the Legends continuity.]]
Bursas were first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> [[Bursa/Legends|Bursas]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were [[Bear/Legends|bear]]-like [[Species/Legends|creatures]] from [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] originally designed by [[Robin Pronovost]] for the reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Episode I]]'', which was to be written by Wallace.<ref name="Blog">{{Blog|danwallace|34|Lostwords: The Essential Guide to Episode I}}</ref>

Wallace had requested that Pronovost design the creatures after finding a reference from director [[George Lucas]] that stated that the [[Gungan]]s maintained an [[Gungan Grand Army]] due to past conflict with bear-like creatures. The ''Essential Guide'' was ultimately cancelled, but Pronovost's design for the creatures were used in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds]]'' and the reference book ''[[The Wildlife of Star Wars: A Field Guide]]'',<ref name="Blog" /> both released in [[2001]].<ref name="Battleground">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds]]''</ref><ref name="Wildlife">''[[The Wildlife of Star Wars: A Field Guide]]''</ref>
