A '''control momentum gyroscope''', abbreviated to '''CMG''' was a type of highly sensitive gyroscope utilized in [[starship]] [[gyrocomputer]]s. CMGs consisted of a spinning rotor, mounted to one or more tilting gimbals and placed inside a frame that was fixed parallel to the starship's "horizontal" position relative to a [[planet]]'s terrain. When the starship changed position in either of its axis, the gyroscope would maintain the same plane and tilt, changing its angular momentum and causing a gyroscopic torque. Data from the CMGs' movement was then fed to specialized [[sensor]]s in the gyrocomputer's exterior to assist the main [[navigation computer]]'s function.<ref name="BMF 25" />

==Behind the scenes==
Control momentum gyroscopes were first mentioned in current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 25|twenty-fifth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 25" /> published around [[June 24]], [[2015]]. The same issue also featured a diagram of their function.<ref name="BMF 25 publication date">{{BMFdates|25}}</ref>
