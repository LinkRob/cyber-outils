The '''Egg Lab''' was part of the [[cloning]] [[Tipoca City Military Complex|facility]] of [[Tipoca City]], [[Kamino]]. In that chamber, clone embryos were mass-produced. All of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[clone trooper]]s, who fought in the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], were grown there.<ref name="VE" />
