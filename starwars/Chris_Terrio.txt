'''Christopher "Chris" Terrio''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Wikipedia:Screenwriter|screenwriter]] and [[Wikipedia:Film director|film director]].

On [[September 12]], [[2017]], it was announced that Terrio would co-write the screenplay for [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] with [[J.J. Abrams]], who would also direct the film.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode IX">{{SW|url=news/j-j-abrams-to-write-and-direct-star-wars-episode-ix|text=J.J. Abrams to Write and Direct ''Star Wars'': Episode IX}}</ref>
