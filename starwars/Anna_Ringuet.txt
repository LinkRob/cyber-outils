'''Anna Ringuet''' worked as an environmental coordinator for the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> Then worked as a Studio Coordinator on the following four features; Rogue One: A Star Wars Story, Star Wars: Episode VIII, The Last Jedi, Solos: A Star Wars Story, Star Wars: Episode IX, The Rise of Skywalker. In 2021 she joined Lucasfilm Ltd as Manager of Physical Production, UK.
