'''''7189''''' was the name of a wall mural in the [[Capital City]] of [[Lothal]]. It featured the number "7189" written in [[Alternative Aurebesh numerals|alternate Aurebesh numerals]],<ref name="Empire Day">{{Rebels|Empire Day (episode)}}</ref> with the words "DELIVERIES IN THE BACK" written in [[Aurebesh]] beneath that.<ref name="SW">{{SW|url=series/spark-of-rebellion-concept-art-gallery|text="Spark of Rebellion" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 12}}</ref>
