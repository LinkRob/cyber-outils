'''Chief Imperial Inspector''' was a role within the [[Galactic Empire]] that was held by [[Lieutenant]] [[Hedge Devlin]] in the [[Chorlian sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Devlin was responsible for completing an intake form for a [[spacer]]'s strongbox that was confiscated by [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]] [[agent]] [[Paramiah Shrove]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

The [[inspector]] marked that the box should be returned to its owner despite the fact that it contained objectionable literature, including the [[book]] that would become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', as Devlin was focused on confiscating [[spice]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
The role of Chief Imperial Inspector was first mentioned on a supplemental item included in the deluxe edition of the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
