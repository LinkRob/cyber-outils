The '''Cantham House meetings''', also referred to as the '''Cantham House talks''', were meetings between members of the [[Delegation of 2,000]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The Delegation of 2,000 was composed of [[senator]]s in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] that began to counter [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] growing political powers.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> The meetings shared their name with [[Cantham House]], the location of [[Bail Organa's office]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="LPOA">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>

When the Galactic Republic was [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] into the [[Galactic Empire]] and several members of the Delegation of 2,000 were [[Senatorial arrests|arrested]] on charges of [[treason]] and conspiracy, [[Mon Mothma]] and [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] continued the meetings between themselves, in order to find ways to oppose Palpatine. After the [[Imperial Era|rise]] of the Galactic Empire and growing [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebellion]] against it, the Cantham House meetings continued to take place, now with [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] also participating.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
