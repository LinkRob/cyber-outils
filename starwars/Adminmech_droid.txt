An '''Adminmech droid''' was a type of [[droid]] similar to the [[Astromech droid]], but designed to be stationed on the ground.<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> Adminmechs were often placed by corporations on industrial areas given that the droids were driven by efficiency and they never questioned orders. Adminmechs were also known to be incapable of committing [[Thief|theft]].<ref name="Solo Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
