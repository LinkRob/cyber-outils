An '''aerial bombardment''' was a tactic utilized by sky-based [[Navy|naval forces]] against ground targets.

==History==
{{Quote|Shall we commence an aerial bombardment, sir?''"<br />&laquo;''Oh, well that ''does'' sound like the thing to do, doesn't it? Let's get right ''on'' that&hellip;eventually&hellip;''&raquo;|quoteend=0|[[Gak-Sixtoo]] and Bokku|<ref name="Vader 13" />}}
During the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], enormous [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship]]s had penetrated the upper [[atmosphere]] of [[Geonosis]], knocking out the [[Geonosian]] [[launch hive]]s with aerial bombardment under the command of [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]].<ref name="CL2016" /> In [[19 BBY]], sometime after the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]] from the [[Galactic Republic]], the Imperial government began conducting [[Attack on Kamino|operations]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]], which led to the acquisition of the [[Kaminoan]] [[species]]' [[cloning]] technology by the Empire. After their operations on Kamino had ceased, Imperial [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]] ordered a [[Edmon Rampart's fleet|fleet]] of three [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] to assume position and fire upon the [[capital]] of [[Tipoca City]]. As a result of the aerial bombardment, the [[city]] was destroyed and sank into the vast planetary [[ocean]]s,<ref name="Return to Kamino" /> while the [[clone commando]]s of the [[Desertion|deserted]] [[Clone Force 99]] and Imperial [[Clone Commander]] [[Crosshair]] survived the bombardment and escaped the planet.<ref name="Kamino Lost">{{TBB|Kamino Lost}}</ref>

[[File:ThrawnBombardsPeridea-Ahsoka.png|thumb|left|300px|Thrawn's bombardment on Peridea]]
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Vader 13 date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Darth Vader (2020) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 13]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Bokku]] [[Plot to assassinate Darth Vader|carried out]] an aerial bombardment at [[Zee-Nine City Seven]] in [[Hutt Space]], though the blasts were [[Lightsaber combat|blocked]] by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Vader 13" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Ahsoka Date">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref> [[Grand Admiral]]-in-[[Exile (punishment)|exile]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn" ordered his [[Star Destroyer]], the ''[[Chimaera]]'', to fire upon the attacking [[Force-sensitive]]s [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Sabine Wren]], [[Ezra Bridger]] as they approached the [[Great Mothers' Fortress]]. All three survived the attack.<ref name="Part 8" />
