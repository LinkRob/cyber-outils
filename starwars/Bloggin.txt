'''Bloggins''' were [[Bird|avian]] creatures that lived on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] noted in her [[Rey's survival guide|journal]] that bloggins may be the dumbest creatures in [[the galaxy]].

==Biology and appearance==
Bloggins were [[Bird|avians]] with [[eye]]stalks and long tails. Their eyestalks let them see predators from a long distance. However, their tendency to panic, run around in a circle squawking, and failing to fly away left them with little choices.<ref name="Survival" />

They traveled in flocks and provided [[Foodstuff|food]] for several of the local villagers. Bloggins regarded whatever land they were occupying as exclusively theirs and responded negatively to interlopers, protectively pecking at other beings that traveled through it.<ref name="Novelization" /> Their [[Bloggin-oil|oil]] was used in [[Bloggin-oil lamp|lamps]] by the villagers of [[Tuanul]].<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Additionally, the villagers raised them for meat and feathers.<ref name="Survival" /> [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] also used [[bloggin-leather]] on the strap of [[Rey's quarterstaff|her]] [[quarterstaff]].<ref name="Visual Dictionary" />

==History==
[[File:Bloggin_Survival.jpg|thumb|250px|Rey's drawing of a Bloggin]]
{{Quote|The Sacred Villagers raise bloggins for meat, feathers, and oil. And if you're in Niima, Bobbajo the Crittermonger will have some trussed for sale on his back.|Rey, in her survival guide|<ref name="Survival" />}}
A game existed called "[[whack-a-bloggin]]." One game stall in a festival on [[Serenno]] allowed you to play the game for two [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Dooku">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref>

[[Bobbajo]] the Crittermonger occasionally had some on his back for sale at [[Niima Outpost]].<ref name="Survival" />

The [[astromech droid]] [[BB-8]] encountered several bloggins on the Tuanul [[Sacred Villages|settlement]] during its secret [[Attack on Tuanul|mission]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]. They pecked at him as he passed, but BB-8 rolled around and avoided them.<ref name="Novelization" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bloggins first appeared in the [[2015]] film ''[[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens]]''.<ref name="TFW">{{Film|VII}}</ref> They were not identified on screen, and only identified as Bloggins in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''The Force Awakens'' novelization]], written by [[Alan Dean Foster]].<ref name="Novelization" />
