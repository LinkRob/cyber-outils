'''''Duel of the Fates''''' is an expansion card set of the ''[[Young Jedi Collectible Card Game]]'', produced by [[Decipher]] and released on [[November 8]], [[2000]].

==Gameplay==
This set contained new rules on "Dueling" and a "Hyperspace" pile for containing starfighters and transports. Many of the cards had game text (related to the dueling mechanic) and new versions of the characters. It also expanded on the "effect" cards released in the [[Battle of Naboo (YJCCG)]] set.
