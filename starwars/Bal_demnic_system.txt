The '''Bal'demnic system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Auril sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square R-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the late [[High Republic Era]], the system had yet to receive a name, and was designated as '''XA-1958JHR''' under a [[Columi]] cataloging system.<ref name="Acolyte VG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> By the era of the [[Galactic Empire]], the system and the [[Bal'demnic|planet]] within it had been named Bal'demnic.<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref>

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Bal'demnic system originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as the [[Bak'rofsen system]], and was recanonized when it was included in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
