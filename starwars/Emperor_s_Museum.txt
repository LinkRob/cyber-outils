The '''Emperor's Museum''' was a [[museum]] on [[Coruscant]], dedicated to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and also had a display on the [[Clone Wars]] and Palpatine's upbringing on [[Naboo]] titled "From Humble Beginnings". Whilst a part of the [[Apprentice Legislature]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and several other members were given a tour of the museum in [[3 BBY]] though it saddened her to see real footage and false history. An inaugural portrait of Palpatine was also shown to the group and Leia wondered what the point was of making him appear benevolent when in reality he was doing everything that proved he was cruel and a warmonger.<ref name="Leia" />
