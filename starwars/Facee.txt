'''Facee''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Twi'lek]] who worked with [[Kova (Twi'lek)|Kova]], [[Roz (criminal)|Roz]], and [[Hatto West]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], the quartet robbed [[Baron]] [[Serk]] with the help of the [[Toydarian]] merchant [[Nel Henol]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
