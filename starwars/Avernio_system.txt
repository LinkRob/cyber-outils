The '''Avernio system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Unknown Regions]]. It was situated at the coordinates G-5 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Avernio system was mentioned in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The system originated from the world [[Avernio Three]] that was introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''.
