A '''concussive baton''' was a type of [[baton]].

==Characteristics==
A concussive baton was a moderately sized baton that emitted a localized crack of sound when the concussive end was struck - compared to the bang of a [[slugthrower]]. The concussive force of the weapon was quite deadly, with strikes to the chin, ribs, and other vital areas being lethal, even against armored surfaces.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Concussive batons were the [[bounty hunter]] [[Mercurial Swift]]'s signature weapon, who owned a pair that had concussive and electrical [[stun]] functions.<ref name="Life Debt" />
