'''BB-19H''' was a [[BB-series astromech droid]] that had a gold, red, and translucent red body, with a black sensor. The [[droid]] spent time at [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on [[Takodana]] where he quietly observed the [[smuggler]]s, brigands, [[scout]]s, and [[Spy|spies]] there.<ref name="DF" />

==Behind the scenes==
This holiday-themed droid is a toy that was made available exclusively at [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] Parks in [[2019]] as part of the [[Star Wars: Droid Factory (toy line)|''Droid Factory'']] toy line.<ref name="DF" />
