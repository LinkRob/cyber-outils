The '''First Battle of Cyclor''',<ref name="Timelines" /> also known as the '''attack on Cyclor''', was a [[Nihil]] raid on the [[Cyclor Shipyards]] above [[Cyclor]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]. [[Sarn Starbreaker]], a [[Cloud (rank)|Cloud]] of [[Pan Eyta]]'s [[Tempest]], wanted to make a strike against the [[Galactic Republic]] by destroying the ''[[Innovator]]'', an [[Elite-class vessel|''Elite''-class vessel]] being built at the [[shipyard]]s which was to be a showpiece of the upcoming [[Valo Republic Fair]]. The [[Cyclorrian]] workers, alongside [[Jedi Knight]] [[Indeera Stokes]] and her [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]], fought back against the attackers. After the Cyclorrians managed to get a signal for help past the Nihil's [[scrambler droid]]s, a [[drift]] of [[Jedi Vector]]s led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Porter Engle]] arrived from [[Derra IV|Derra]] to reinforce them. Ultimately, Zettifar was able to destroy [[Sarn Starbreaker's Cloudship]] despite having been shot in the gut with a [[harpoon]], killing the Cloud amidst the defeat of all his forces.<ref name="Rising Storm" />
