'''Bynder Zebb''' was a corporate [[Twi'lek/Legends|Twi'lek]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Scout/Legends|scout]] who worked for [[Tagge Company/Legends|TaggeCo]]. He had visited worlds including [[Chandrila/Legends|Chandrila]] and [[Kal'Shebbol/Legends|Kal'Shebbol]], and was considered an expert in dealing with the creatures native to the planets he was scouting.<ref name="SE" /> Zebb was also a contributor and commenter on "[[Cynabar's Droid Datalog]]."<ref name="Droid">''[[Cynabar's Fantastic Technology: Droids]]''</ref>
