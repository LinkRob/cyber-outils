'''Aleena''' was one of the primary [[language]]s of [[Aleen]], as it was spoken by the native [[Aleena]] [[species]]. The Aleena were also capable of speaking [[Galactic Basic Standard]].<ref name="NoP" /> [[C-3PO]] could speak Aleena, and he encountered it when the [[Galactic Republic]] traveled on a mercy mission to Aleen.
