'''Bronson Webb''' (born [[February 20]], [[1983]]) is a British actor who portrays a [[Unidentified Rebel MP|Rebel MP]] in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Credits">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' credits</ref> He is also notable for playing the Slytherin student [[W:c:HarryPotter:Pike|Pike]] in the [[2004]] film ''[[Wikipedia:Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban (film)|Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban]]'', a man working for the Joker in the [[2008]] film ''[[Wikipedia:The Dark Knight (film)|The Dark Knight]]'', the [[W:c:pirates:cook (Queen Anne's Revenge)|cook]] punished by Blackbeard in the [[2011]] film ''[[Wikipedia:Pirates of the Caribbean: On Stranger Tides|Pirates of the Caribbean: On Stranger Tides]]'', and the Night's Watch deserter [[W:c:gameofthrones:Will|Will]] in the ''[[Wikipedia:Game of Thrones|Game of Thrones]]'' episode "[[W:c:gameofthrones:Winter Is Coming|Winter Is Coming]]" (with fellow ''Star Wars'' actors [[Ian Whyte]] and [[Spencer Wilding]] playing [[W:c:gameofthrones: White Walker (Winter Is Coming)|the White Walker]] terrorizing the character).

The actor spent eleven days filming his part, although even he has admitted that the character is hard to spot in the final product.<ref name="FB" />
