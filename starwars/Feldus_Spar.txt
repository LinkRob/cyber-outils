'''Feldus Spar''' was a [[Neimoidian]] [[Politics|politician]] of the [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]]'s [[capital]] [[city]], [[Zarra]]. Following the [[Cadesura disaster]]<ref name="Brotherhood" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] is set between the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the [[Clone Wars]] and the [[Battle of Christophsis]], as the novel's plot involves [[Anakin Skywalker]] becoming a [[Jedi Knight]], a rank he attained between those events. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates both events to [[22 BBY]], ''Brotherhood'' must be set in that year.</ref> in which an [[Cadesura district|entire district]] of the capital was destroyed, Spar oversaw relief efforts for survivors of the [[bomb]]ing. Spar later met in the [[Zarra capital complex]] with [[minister of defense]] [[Alluv Eyam]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] emissary [[Asajj Ventress]] to discuss an upcoming [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Kenobi and Skywalker)|diplomatic investigation]] by the [[Galactic Republic]] into the disaster.<ref name="Brotherhood" />
