From his northern post in Cochrane, Alberta (Canada), '''Bart Farkas''' lives with his three lovable children, and a of furry feline that helps to keep the family warm during the winter months. Bart can best be described as an elder-statesman of sorts when it comes to strategy guides. With over 100 print computer and video game strategy guides to his name, Bart knows strategy guides perhaps a little too well. Bart doesn't write many strategy guides now. But still occasionally writes other non-fiction works that fall outside the realm of gaming and technology. An avid amateur football official, Bart also worked with the Canadian Football League with Instant Replay officiating.
