'''Felines''',<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /> also referred to as '''felinoids''',<ref name ="A Bad Batch Novel">''[[Sanctuary: A Bad Batch Novel]]''</ref> were a type of creatures. [[Cat]]s,<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /> including [[tooka]]<ref name="Tooka in Databank">{{Databank|tooka|Tooka}}</ref> and [[Loth-cat]]s,<ref name="Loth-cat">Since the [[loth-cat]] [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry says they are members of the [[tooka]] family and the tooka databank entry says that tookas are felines, loth-cats are also felines.</ref><ref name="LC Databank">{{Databank|loth-cat|Loth-cat}}</ref> were considered to be feline.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /> The [[Nexu]] was a large feline, larger than a [[human]], that had a prehensile [[tail]].<ref name="swgoh">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> Some [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] were also classified as feline, such as [[Zygerrian]]s, [[Togorian]]s,<ref name="Survival Skills">''[[Star Wars: How Not to Get Eaten by Ewoks and Other Galactic Survival Skills]]''</ref> and [[Cathar|Cathars]].<ref name ="A Bad Batch Novel" /> Feline creatures are generally characterized by large [[ear]]s, short [[Mouth|snouts]] with sharp [[Tooth|teeth]], [[Hair|fur]], [[claw]]s, and tails.<ref name="Survival Skills" />
