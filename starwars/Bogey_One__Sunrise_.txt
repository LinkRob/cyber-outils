'''Bogey One''' was the codename of a [[Grysk]] [[Warship]] given to it by the crew of the ''[[Springhawk]]''. Bogey One participated in a [[Sunrise incident|battle]] against the ''Springhawk'' and the [[Aelos (frigate)|''Aelos'']]. Bogey One looked like a smaller version of a [[Shatter-class WarMaster|''Shatter''-class WarMaster]]. Bogey One employed the tactic of dibbers to protects its missiles. It escaped the battle after destroying the [[Kilji picket cruiser]] [[Hammer (Kilji picket cruiser)|''Hammer'']].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bogey One appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final volumine in the [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]] written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
