"'''A Perfect World'''" is an adventure seed contained in the ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]''. It offered a primary storyline for an adventure on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Gargolyn IV]] as well as possible alternate storylines that could be adapted by a gamemaster. It was written by [[Bill Olmesdahl]].
