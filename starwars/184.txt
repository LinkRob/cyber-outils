'''184''' was a [[stormtrooper]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Whilst [[Secret mission to Tatooine|attacking]] the [[Tantive IV]] under the command of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], 184 required a password to complete a mission for their squad. 184 asked a [[764|Stormtrooper]] they mistook for [[318 (Stormtrooper)|318]] if they remembered the password, only for the Stormtrooper to protest they were actually 764. To further confuse matters, 764 themselves had confused 184 for 318. The Stormtroopers were then encountered by [[Captain]] [[Raymus Antilles]] and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], having opened the door to the room the Stormtroopers were in by destroying the fuel cells powering the door. Leia and Antilles were able to bypass the Stormtroopers.<ref name="LEGO" />
