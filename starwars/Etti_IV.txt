'''Etti IV''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Master of Evil" /> located in the [[Etti system]] that served as the headquarters of the [[droid]] manufacturing company [[Cybot Galactica]] during the early [[Imperial Era]]. Cybot Galactica operated out of a [[city]]-headquarters known as the [[Arcoplex]],<ref name="Master of Evil" /> and in [[19 BBY]]<ref name="MoEDate">The events of ''Master of Evil'' begin during the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 5]] and then spans to the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 6]], which corresponds to [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> they were actively expanding the city by burning the surrounding [[forest]]s, clearing debris with [[construction droid]]s, and laying down [[gold]]en metal foundation.<ref name="Master of Evil" /> During the [[New Republic Era]], [[Sabodor's]] was an exotic pet shop located on Etti IV, and [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] ferried pets from there aboard their [[bulk freighter]] ''[[Eravana]]''.<ref name="TFAICS" />
