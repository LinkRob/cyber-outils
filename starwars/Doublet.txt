A '''doublet''' was a [[clothing]] item sometimes worn by [[noble]]s.

During a disastrous [[Grand Hunt]] on [[Pijal]], the [[droid]] that was the subject of the hunt was re-programmed to attack [[Princess]] [[Fanry]], who was soon to assume the role of [[queen]]. In the aftermath of the chaos, a noble whined about his torn doublet, which had ripped to reveal the entire [[gold]] satin lining and which was now felt to be too gaudy to be worn anywhere.<ref name="Master & Apprentice">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref>
