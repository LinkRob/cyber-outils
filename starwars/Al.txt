'''Al''',<ref name="Jooks and Al">{{WebCite|url=https://www.starwarsauthentics.com/iSynApp/productDisplay.action?sid=1102083&productId=1363987|text=JOOKS AND AL|quote=Mary Elizabeth McGlynn and Jonathan Lipow as Jooks and Al in ''Star Wars Resistance''.|archivedate=20181016063801}}</ref> known as '''Big Al''', was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who resided on the ''[[Colossus]]'' refueling station on [[Castilon]] in the later stages of the [[Cold War]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. He sported a bushy mustache and was a regular at [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza|Aunt Z's]] [[Aunt Z's Tavern|tavern]].<ref name="Recruit" />

==Biography==
When a brawl ensued during [[Kazuda Xiono]]'s game of darts against [[Grevel]], Al was the only bar patron who did not join in the fight but kept on drinking instead. The next [[Standard day|day]], before the race between Kazuda and [[Torra Doza]], Al was already waiting outside [[Aunt Z's Tavern]] before [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza|Aunt Z]] opened its door.<ref name="Recruit" /> When Kaz found himself in a precarious situation on the outside ledges of [[Doza Tower]], his plight was clearly visible from Aunt Z's Tavern. Al bet 50 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] that Kazuda would fall off.<ref name="High Tower">{{Resistance|The High Tower}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Al was a male human with [[Color|gray]] [[hair]] and light skin.<ref name="Recruit" />
