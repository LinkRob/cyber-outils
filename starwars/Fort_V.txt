The '''Fort V''' was a ground-mounted [[Autoturret/Legends|autoturret]] used by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. They were capable of detecting and blasting intruders with rapid fire.<ref name="FamiMaga">{{BookCite|author=|container=Star Wars|title=Family Computer Magazine|number=1987 #22|publisher=Tokuma Shoten|date=December 4, 1987|issn=|link=https://archive.org/details/zines_famimaga_1987_22}}</ref>

This model of autoturret was used by Imperial [[Stormtrooper/Legends|Stormtroopers]] in a hidden base on [[Hoth/Legends|Hoth]] around [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]].<ref name="sw87" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fort V appeared in the 1987 [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] [[Star Wars (1987 video game)|''Star Wars'' game]].<ref name="sw87" /> Their name was revealed in the [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese language]] guide of the game serialized in Family Computer Magazine, in which it was rendered as フォートⅤ (''Fōto V'') in [[Wikipedia:Katakana|katakana]] syllabary.<ref name="FamiMaga" />
