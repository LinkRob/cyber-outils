'''Droid workers''' were modified [[battle droid]]s created by the [[Geonosian]] [[queen]] [[Karina]] when she was [[Sterilization of Geonosis|sterilized]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] to continue the Geonosian [[species]]. She did not think of them as [[droid]]s, but as her own children.<ref name="Darth Vader 4" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Secret mission to Geonosis|stole]] the [[Portable droid factory|droid factory]] Karina used as her womb, preventing her from creating more droid workers.<ref name="Darth Vader 4" />

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|Interesting tweaks on the standard droid design, too. I suspect that she doesn't see these as droids.&hellip;|[[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]]|<ref name="Darth Vader 4" />}}
Modified [[battle droid]]s,<ref name="Darth Vader 4" /> [[droid]] workers<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> had a resemblance to the [[Geonosian]]s with their [[Color|brown]] plating, red [[photoreceptor]]s, and pair of functional wings on their backs. They carried [[E-5 blaster rifle]]s.<ref name="Darth Vader 4" />

==History==
During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Sterilization of Geonosis|sterilized]] the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]], [[Death|killing]] nearly all life on its surface. Karina, the last surviving Geonosian queen, was sterile and unable to repopulate her people. Desperate, she attached herself to a [[portable droid factory]] and produced droid workers, thinking of them as her children.

In 0 ABY, Darth Vader, rogue [[Archaeology|archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]], and assassin droids [[0-0-0]] and [[BT-1]] arrived at Geonosis to acquire the droid factory. Aphra sent the droids beneath the planet surface to map out the route. The two were attacked by numerous droid workers, but BT-1 easily destroyed them with its built-in weapons.

Vader and Aphra followed the droids deeper into the cavern, where they found Karina producing "eggs" using the droid factory, which "hatched" into droid workers. Vader quickly severed Karina from the factory with his [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. Karina ordered all of her droid workers to stop the four and retrieve the factory, but BT-1 and Vader destroyed any that got close. Vader then lifted the droid factory away using the [[Naboo Royal Starship]], preventing any more droid workers from being built.
