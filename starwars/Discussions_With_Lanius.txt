'''Discussions With Lanius''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[February 7]] [[2002]] and was written in the form of an interview with [[Lanius Qel-Bertuk]] by a local reporter covering the recent event in the [[Cularin system/Legends|Cularin system]].
