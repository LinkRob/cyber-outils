"'''Data File'''" or '''Technikfakten''' in the German issues, is a recurring two-page department of the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]]. It contains a spec sheet and an array of additional data related to a particular [[starship]] or weapon.
