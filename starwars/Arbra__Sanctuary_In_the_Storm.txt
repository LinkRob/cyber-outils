'''Arbra: Sanctuary In the Storm''' was a series of ''[[Planet Hoppers]]'' articles published by [[Wizards of the Coast]] featuring the planet [[Arbra/Legends|Arbra]] and other characters and settings appearing in [[Marvel Comics]]' [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'' series]]. It was written by [[Cory J. Herndon]] and published on [[Wizards.com]] in [[June]] [[2003]].

==Contents==
===Part 1: Hole in the Ground===
''&hellip;the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance's]] [[Kavel Mesa|Chief Logistics Officer]] offers an assessment of the [[Haven Base|new Rebel base]].''<ref name="Arbra" />

===Part 2: Judge Him Not By His Size===
''&hellip;[[Princess/Legends|Princess]] [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia]] supports the [[Hoojib/Legends|Hoojib]] leader [[Plif/Legends|Plif]] for a position in the [[New Republic Senate/Legends|New Republic Senate]].''<ref name="Arbra" />

===Part 3: The Darker===
''&hellip;[[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]], [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]], and [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] encounter an [[Darker|ancient horror]] that haunts [[Arbra/Legends|Arbra's]] underground.''<ref name="Arbra" />

===Part 4: Rebels in Disguise===
''&hellip;the Rebels on Arbra refit a [[Flying Bantha Squadron|flight wing]] of stolen [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter/Legends|TIEs]] to launch a daring hit-and-run [[Battle of the secret armada|attack]].''<ref name="Arbra" />
