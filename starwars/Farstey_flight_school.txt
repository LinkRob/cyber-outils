Around [[5 BBY]], a '''flight school''' was located on [[Farstey]] that was used to train [[rebel pilot]]s. In a message from [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]] of [[Phoenix Cell]] to [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]], Sato believed that the [[RZ-1T trainer]] was extremely helpful in certifying bush [[pilot]]s for [[A-wing starfighter]]s, and stated that if models were available, the Farstey and [[Homon flight school|Homon]] [[flight school]]s could use them.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
