A '''cloud flute''' was a type of [[flute]].<ref name="A Valiant Vow" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: A Valiant Vow]]''|nearly two years after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the [[Hutt]] [[Dahara Devirsivik Norri]] and her guards set up a camp on the [[planet]] [[Aricho]] that had various forms of entertainment, including an [[Unidentified cloud flute player|individual]] playing a cloud flute. At one point, when Norri took up her position on a raised dais, the [[Musician|cloud flute player]] began a [[Music|melody]] and two dancers began performing as well. Soon after, a [[Foodstuff|meal]] was served during Norri's meeting with her [[Devirsivik Clan|brother]] [[Churo]], the [[scientist]] [[Glenna Kip]], and Aricho's [[president]], [[Orenna Sala]]. Although the others ate in silence, Norri laughed at the dancers and cloud flute player, praised them, and requested new tunes. However, once Norri was ready to begin discussing the harvesting of [[glaka root]], she sent the entertainers away. Later, as Norri's servants packed up the camp, the Hutt sat on her [[throne]] with a bored expression as the cloud flute player performed nearby.<ref name="A Valiant Vow" />
