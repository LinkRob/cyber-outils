'''Amrosio''' was a [[Nothoiin (species)|Nothoiin]] [[Gender|male]] who lived in the [[Anoat sector]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Able to [[Force-sensitive|wield]] the power of [[the Force]], Amrosio became an acolyte of [[Xiath]], a respected [[Advisor|mentor]] to the reclusive [[Noble Court]]. By the [[time]] of the [[Iron Blockade]] enacted by Imperial [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] in the wake of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star's]] [[Battle of Endor|destruction]], Amrosio and his master Xiath resided on the [[planet]] [[Hosra]] in the [[Burnin Konn system]].<ref name="Uprising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Amrosio first appeared in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the multiplayer [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', created by the [[Kabam]] company and released on [[September 10]], [[2015]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms. Within the scope of the game, Amrosio acts as a companion to Xiath, one of several quest givers for the player character.
