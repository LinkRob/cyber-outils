'''Freedom of Naboo''' was a regular celebration held on the edge of the [[Lianorm Swamp]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] in the [[Standard year|years]] following the [[Gungan]] and [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] victory against [[Invasion of Naboo|occupying]] [[Trade Federation]] [[Trade Federation Droid Army|forces]] during the [[Battle of Naboo]]<ref name="SWCL" /> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> Prior to the battle, the [[swamp]] edges had been considered a no-man's land between the territories of the Naboo and the Gungans and were inhabited only by outcasts, but it was on the border between the two cultures that the native leaders met to plan out the battle.<ref name="SWCL" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Freedom of Naboo was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] in a section reprinted<ref name="SWCL" /> from the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]] and marked the [[Freedom of Naboo/Legends|celebration's]] first mention in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>
