The '''Amaxine Space Station''', also known as the '''Amaxine Station''', was an ancient [[space station]] in [[the galaxy]] that orbited a [[Unidentified star (Amaxine station)|dying]] [[star]] in a [[Amaxine Station system|star system]]. Built by the [[Amaxine warriors]] to facilitate their campaigns, the station was abandoned after it was infested by a dangerous [[Sentience|sentient]] [[plant]] [[species]], the [[Drengir]], which were in tune with the [[dark side of the Force]]. The [[Sith]] later [[Binding statues|sealed]] the Drengir there.

==Characteristics==
[[File:IntotheDark2-textless.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The overgrown environment of the Amaxine Space Station's interior]]
The Amaxine Space Station<ref name="Aphra 30">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 30|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 30]]</ref> was an ancient [[space station]] in [[the galaxy]]<ref name="ITD" /> that consisted of a central sphere encircled by a number of rings.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 1" /> It was located in a [[Amaxine Station system|star system]] where it orbited a [[Unidentified star (Amaxine station)|dying]] [[star]].<ref name="ITD" /> The station had several [[Amaxine relay|hyperspace relays]] that would travel through hyperspace to a predetermined location.<ref name="OotS">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref>

==Role==
The station was built by the [[Amaxine warriors]] to facilitate their campaigns.<ref name="ITD" />

==History==
===Ancient history===
The Amaxine warriors who built the station abandoned it after it was infested by the [[Drengir]], a dangerous [[Sentience|sentient]] [[plant]] [[species]] that were inherently in tune with the [[dark side of the Force]]. The Drengir were later [[Binding statues|sealed]] at the station by the [[Sith]].<ref name="ITD" />

===High Republic Era===
[[File:IntotheDark1-textless.jpg|thumb|right|370px|Pilot [[Affie Hollow]] and Padawan Reath Silas aboard the Amaxine Space Station]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], multiple [[starship]]s, including [[Vessel|one]] carrying [[Jedi Master]]s [[Cohmac Vitus]] and [[Orla Jareni]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Dez Rydan]], and [[Padawan]] [[Reath Silas]], were stranded at the station in the aftermath of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]. During their mission, Rydan was injured and was unintentionally transported through one of the hyperspace relays, leading his companions to believe him to be dead. Upon finding the Sith statues, the Jedi took them back to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]], unknowingly awakening the Drengir.<ref name="ITD" />

Returning to the station and putting the plants back to sleep, the Jedi were forced to again awaken the Drengir when the [[Nihil]] marauders, who hoped to use the station for their bloodily raids, arrived. In the end, Silas destroyed the station's hyperspace pods after rescuing Rydan, also sucking the Drengir and most of the Nihil aboard out into space.<ref name="ITD" /> However, the [[Great Progenitor]] survived. Additionally, disturbing her rest had awoken Drengir across the Outer Rim,<ref name="THR 4">[[The High Republic (2021) 4|''The High Republic'' (2021) 4]]</ref> beginning a [[Drengir crisis|crisis]] that would last until the [[Battle on Mulita|assault on Mulita]].<ref name="THR 8">[[The High Republic (2021) 8|''The High Republic'' (2021) 8]]</ref>

===Imperial Era===
Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Hidden Empire 4|''Hidden Empire'' (2023) 4]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> during [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]]'s [[War against the Sith|attempt]] to destroy the Sith as the [[Crime lord|leader]] of the criminal syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]], her associate "[[The Archivist]]" moved the [[Fermata Cage]] to the station to allow it to feed with its dark side energy. The Archivist's suspicions were correct and the Cage began consuming the Drengir plants, building up the energy necessary to open. Its power notified [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]]<ref name="Hidden Empire 4" /> and his [[Sith apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who both followed the [[disturbance in the Force]]<ref name="Hidden Empire 5">[[Hidden Empire 5|''Hidden Empire'' 5]]</ref> to the station on board the ''[[Executor]]''<ref name="Hidden Empire 4" /> with a force of [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyers]] backing them.<ref name="Aphra (2020) 30">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 30|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 30]]</ref>

[[File:PatchZircher-HiddenEmpire5-textless.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Vader aboard the Amaxine Space Station during the Crimson Dawn war]]
The two Sith had been led to believe an [[Unidentified Sith Lord (Fermata Cage)|ancient Sith Lord]] was trapped in the Cage, but Qi'ra hoped to draw them in to lock them away.<ref name="Hidden Empire 5" /> Qi'ra's [[Dawn Fleet]] arrived on the system and [[Battle at Amaxine Station|engaged]] the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|Star Dreadnought]] to buy the Archivist the time she needed, while the Dawn's [[starfighter]]s defended against the Imperial [[TIE series|TIEs]]. Nevertheless, the Emperor and Darth Vader landed to the station on board the Emperor's [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] accompanied by [[Emperor's Royal Guard|royal guards]] and [[death trooper]]s and engaged the Dawn's troops.<ref name="Hidden Empire 4" />

The [[Spark Eternal]], which was in possession of the body of the [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]], arrived at the station and was greeted by Qi'ra.<ref name="Aphra 28">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 28|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 28]]</ref> Vader and Sidious made their way deeper into the station and were greeted by the Spark, which Vader quickly handled and [[Force push]]ed out of Aphra.<ref name="Hidden Empire 5" /> Having made their way through the battle, [[Sana Starros' crew]] then saved Aphra from the Spark when it attempted to possess her again.<ref name="Aphra (2020) 30" /> When the two Sith encountered the open Fermata Cage, Qi'ra and the Archivist, both overlooking the incident from their own ships, activated the final stage of their plan to lock the two Sith inside. However, the [[Knights of Ren]] arrived in the system and fired on the station, collapsing the Cage in the name of proving themselves to the Sith.<ref name="Hidden Empire 5" />

The wave sent out by the collapse devastated the Dawn fleet, which the Empire targeted the remnants of while Qi'ra gave a retreat order. The Knights then boarded the station to meet the Sith, with Sidious torturing them with [[Force lightning]] but promising they would serve him. The Spark, meanwhile, took refugee in a disc left behind by the Fermata Cage,<ref name="Hidden Empire 5" /> where it would stowaway in a [[Unidentified KX-series security droid (Scourge)|KX-series security droid]] helping Imperial personnel clean up the station at Palpatine's request.<ref name="Dark Droids 1" />

===New Republic Era===
[[File:SnokeBenAmaxine-TheRiseOfKyloRen2.png|thumb|right|250px|The station was home to many exotic plants.]]
With Sidious having learned of the station's existence,<ref name="Hidden Empire 4" /> it would later become the homebase of [[Snoke]], the [[Supreme Leader]] of the [[First Order]]<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 1" /> and a pawn of Sidious,<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> during the [[New Republic Era|time]] of the [[New Republic]]. As Snoke resided in the space station, [[Ben Solo]], who had [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destroyed]] the [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker]] after believing he had killed [[Luke Skywalker]], visited the station to meet with Snoke.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Amaxine Space Station first appeared in the 2019 comic book issue [[The Rise of Kylo Ren 1|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1]], part of the comic book miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Kylo Ren]]'', which was written by [[Charles Soule]] and illustrated by [[Will Sliney]].<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 1" /> According to Soule, the space station was originally created for that comic, rather than being created during the planning for the multimedia project ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]''. Soule said it was [[Claudia Gray]]'s idea to use it<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|CharlesSoule|status/1395155218371862528|Charles Soule|quote=RoKR, and it was Claudia's idea to use it.|archivedate=20210520030910}}</ref> in her [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]'', which first identified it as the Amaxine station.<ref name="ITD" />
