'''Curried nuna-roll''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] made from [[nuna]].<ref name="ACS" /> Around [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[A Coruscant Solstice]]" to around [[232 BBY]].</ref> a curried-nuna roll stall was set up on [[Republic Avenue]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the crowded [[Solstice Tide]] festivities. While wandering through the crowded avenue, [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]] stopped by a protective rail near the stall to wait for the first flakes of [[holo-snow]] to fall and to breath in the stall's heady scents; however, he was interrupted when he sensed a disturbance in [[the Force]] caused by the [[thief]] [[Nittel Karkson]]. When Gios chased Karkson into Coruscant's lower levels he found the avenue's smells of curried nuna-roll and roasted [[mickelnut]]s were replaced with the scent of [[engine]] oil and despair.<ref name="ACS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Curried nuna-roll appeared in "[[A Coruscant Solstice]]," a short story written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in the [[2021]] ''[[Life Day Treasury]]'' anthology.<ref name="ACS" />
