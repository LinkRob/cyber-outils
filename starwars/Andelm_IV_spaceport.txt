The [[planet]] [[Andelm IV]] had a '''[[spaceport]]''' during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]]. Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the introverted tinkerer [[Sevox]] and his [[protocol droid]] [[I-7]] resided in a bunker near the spaceport. Later, the [[Galactic Empire]] sealed off the spaceport so that the local [[crime lord]] [[Jaum]] could deliver a shipment of [[dedlanite]] to [[Commander]] [[Kai]]'s [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]]. [[Zarro]] and her [[Wookiee]] friend [[Chewbacca]] fought with Jaum and Imperial forces at the spaceport.<ref name="SWC IV" />

During the skirmish, they managed to plant an [[astromech droid]] rigged with explosives called [[Boomer (astromech droid)|"Boomer"]] aboard Jaum's [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]].<ref name="SWC IV" /> Shortly later, Zarro and Chewie were taken into Imperial custody but managed to escape after convincing Kai that Jaum was a fraud and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[spy]].<ref name="SWC V">[[Chewbacca (2015) 5|''Chewbacca'' (2015) 5]]</ref>
