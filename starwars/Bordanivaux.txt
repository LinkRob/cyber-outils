{{Quote|Governor Bordanivaux is no patriot, and her decision to take Chadawa rogue was misguided at best. I have no love for the woman. But she gave us refuge when no one else would, and my people understand loyalty.|Madrighast, to Soran Keize|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
'''Bordanivaux''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[governor]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Being in charge of the [[planet]] [[Chadawa]], she split her [[Chadawan government|government]] from the Empire<ref name="Victory's Price" /> by [[5 ABY]]. Around that [[Standard year|year]]<ref name="Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}} ''Victory's Price'' also establishes that Bordanivaux allowed [[Madrighast]] to take refuge at [[Chadawa]] months before the events of the novel, placing the former events around that year.</ref> [[Colonel]] [[Madrighast]] and his [[Madrighast's unit|forces]] fled to Chadawa after taking many losses. Bordanivaux allowed the colonel's forces to take refuge at her planet. [[Standard month|Months]] later, the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], which served the Empire's loyalist forces, sought to destroy all civilization on Chadawa as punishment for the world going rogue. In the [[Attack on Chadawa|fighting]] that followed, the 204th was driven off after [[New Republic]] [[Barma Battle Group|forces]] intervened and Madrighast and his [[Unyielding|Star Destroyer]] were lost, but the Chadawan government remained in place, although under the threat of invasion by the New Republic.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bordanivaux was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
