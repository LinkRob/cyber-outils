'''Arina Ii''' is a [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice actress]] who voiced [[Rieve]] for the ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' video game<ref name="Spotlight" /> which was soft launched in [[2021]]<ref name="Soft launch">{{Twitter|ZyngaStarWars|status/1460656552734605317|[[Zynga]] Star Wars|quote=We are excited to announce Star Wars: Hunters is now entering soft launch and is available on Android for players in India, Philippines, Malaysia & Indonesia.|archivedate=20220625084625|archivefile=File:ZyngaStarWars-SoftLaunchDate-Twitter.png}}</ref> and later fully released in [[2024]]<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> She also narrated the [[Hunters: Battle for the Arena (audiobook)|audiobook]] for the tie in novel ''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]'' by [[Mark Oshiro]] in [[2023]].<ref name="Hunters BftA">[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena (audiobook)|''Hunters: Battle for the Arena'' audiobook]]</ref>
