'''Ak-rev's clan mother''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Weequay]] who led a [[Ak-rev's clan|clan]] of Weequays that lived on [[Tatooine]] as servants of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]].<ref name="Max Rebo 1" />

==Personality and traits==
Ak-rev's clan mother was loyal to Jabba, and would argue with the clan about if they should backstab the [[crime lord]], or if they should stay devoted.<ref name="Max Rebo 1" />
