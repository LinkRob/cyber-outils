'''Broadside''' was a [[clone trooper pilot]] who served in [[Shadow Squadron]], a [[Galactic Republic]] [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] during the [[Clone Wars]], as [[Shadow Three]]. Broadside also served as a member of the [[501st Legion]] and, with the dawn of the [[Galactic Empire]], was part of the [[Shadow Platoon]].

==Biography==
===Battle of Teth===
Broadside was a [[Gender|male]] [[clone trooper]] who served as a member of the [[501st Legion]]<ref name="Master of Evil" /> and the [[Shadow Squadron]] [[starfighter]] unit.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" /> While [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s forces prepared for the [[Battle of Teth]]<ref name="TCWFilm">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of the [[Battle of Christophsis]] and the early events of the [[Battle of Ryloth]] to [[22 BBY]]. As the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|film]] the Battle of Teth takes place within takes place between the events of the pieces of media the other events are attached to according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, this event must also take place in 22 BBY.</ref> Broadside walked alongside [[Matchstick]] on board Skywalker's [[Anakin Skywalker's Acclamator-class assault ship (Teth)|''Acclamator''-class]] [[assault ship]].<ref name="TCWFilm" />

===Attack on the ''Malevolence''===
[[File:Broadside-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|Broadside defends Kamino from Separatist attack]]
During the [[Malevolence Campaign|''Malevolence'' Campaign]], Broadside and [[Shadow Squadron]] were briefed by [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]] and [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] aboard the ''[[Resolute]]''. There, Skywalker informed Broadside and their fellow [[Clone trooper pilot|pilots]] that their mission was to attack the ''[[Malevolence]]'' by targeting its bridge and take out the [[commander]], [[General]] [[Grievous]]. Following the briefing, Broadside and [[Matchstick]] assured [[Jedi]] General [[Plo Koon]] that their [[squadron]] would be prepared for their mission, stating that they maintained minimal casualties and maximum effectiveness.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" />

Following Grievous' [[Battle of the Ryndellia system|attack]] on a medical convoy in the [[Ryndellia system]], Broadside, along with the rest of Shadow Squadron, Skywalker, and Koon took the [[Balmorra Run]] in order to reach the [[Kaliida Shoals Medical Center]] before the ''Malevolence''.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" />

Sometime later in the Clone Wars, Broadside and Shadow Squadron flew with Skywalker once again, this time to defend against Grievous' fleet in an [[Battle of Kamino|attack]] on the clone [[homeworld]] of [[Kamino]].<ref name="ARC">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Broadside was a brave clone pilot who was close with fellow Shadow Squadron pilot Matchstick.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" /> A [[Cloning|clone]] of [[Jango Fett]], Broadside was 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]]) tall,<ref name="TCW encyclopedia" /> had [[Color|graying]] black [[hair]], brown [[eye]]s and tan skin.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" />

==Equipment==
The pilot was equipped with white [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I]] [[clone trooper pilot]] [[armor]], and a [[helmet]] with the [[Republic crest]] on either side.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" />

==Behind the scenes==
Broadside first appeared in "[[Shadow of Malevolence|Shadow of ''Malevolence'']]," the third episode of [[The Clone Wars: Season One|Season One]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" /> Like all clones in the series, they were voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].
