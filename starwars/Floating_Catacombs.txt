The '''Floating Catacombs''' were a location in [[the galaxy]] where a long, long [[time]] before<ref name="Aphra 24" /> [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 24|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 24]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> [[Miril]], a [[Chadra-Fan]] [[Gender|female]] who led the [[Ascendant]], encountered the [[Darkseekers]], an organization of the [[Sith]] who hunted threats to the Sith.<ref name="Aphra 24" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and 4 ABY,<ref name="Date" /> after she was possessed by the [[Spark Eternal]], [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] became [[Memory Box|trapped]] within her memories. During one memory, Miril rescued Aphra and attempted to take her to a location but they ended up in the Floating Catacombs, where Miril once again came face to face with the Darkseekers. Aphra and Miril fought against the Darkseekers, and in an attempt to get Miril to sync with the Spark so they could escape the memory, Aphra pretended to betray Miril to the Darkseekers. The ploy worked and Miril synced together with the Spark Eternal, which she used to mimic a [[Thought Dowser|thought dowser]] and cause the Darkseekers to turn on each other and [[Death|kill]] each other. Once the Darkseekers were defeated, Miril and Aphra left the Floating Catacombs for the Ascendant's [[Unyielding Heart|headquarters]] on [[Bar'leth]].<ref name="Aphra 24" />
