'''Breacher Two''' was a [[breacher]] [[missile launcher]] used by the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]''. It was damaged during a [[Attack on Rapacc|battle]] against the [[Crofyp's task force|task force]] of the [[Kilji]] [[General]] [[Crofyp]],<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> in [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' takes place in [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> After the [[task force]]'s [[Space warfare|defeat]], [[Chiss]] [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Brisch]] reported the damage sustained during the battle to the ''Springhawk''{{'s}} [[commanding officer]], [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn."<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
