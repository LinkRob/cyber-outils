'''Arkax Station''' was a [[space station]]<ref name="WDSTKH" /> in the [[Inner Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], a [[Battle on Arkax Station|battle]] between [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[battle droid]]s and [[Jedi]] took place on Arkax Station. The [[parent]]s of [[Wuher]] were [[Death|killed]] during the battle, leaving him with a hatred for [[droid]]s.<ref name="WDSTKH" />
