A '''species''' of primitive creatures were [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Abednedo (planet)|Abednedo]], in the [[Colonies]] region of [[the galaxy]], where they lived in series of interconnected underground tunnels. Their [[mouth]] tendrils were sensory organs that helped them navigate in the dark. Eventually, they evolved into the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Abednedo]] [[species]].<ref name="AotG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The precursor species to the Abdenedos were first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[Jason Fry]]<ref name="AotG" /> and released on [[August 23]], [[2016]].<ref name="Amazon">{{StudioFun|url=books/star-wars-aliens-of-the-galaxy-9780794437312|text=Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy}}</ref>
