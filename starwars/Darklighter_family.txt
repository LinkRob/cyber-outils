The '''Darklighter family'''<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> was a [[human]] family who lived on the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Tatooine]] around the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The family owned a [[Darklighter farm|homestead]], and consisted of [[Biggs Darklighter]], a close friend of the legendary [[Luke Skywalker]] and a fighter who served both the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] as a [[pilot]],<ref name="ANH" /> as well as his [[Biggs Darklighter's mother|mother]].<ref name="Desert Son">{{StoryCite|story=Desert Son|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>
