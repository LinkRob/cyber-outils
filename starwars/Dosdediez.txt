'''Dosdediez''' was a [[roleplaying game]] magazine published by [[La Factoría de Ideas]] in [[Wikipedia:Spain|Spain]]. The magazine pubished the ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' article, "[[Infiltrado]]" by [[Darío Pérez]] and illustrated by [[Jaime Fabregat]], in the fourth issue of [[May]]/[[June]] [[1994]].<ref name="D4" />
