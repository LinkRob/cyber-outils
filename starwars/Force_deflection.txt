Using [[the Force]], the [[Jedi]]<ref name="Episode II" /> and the [[Sith]] had the ability to '''deflect''' energy-based attacks like [[blaster]] bolts<ref name="Episode V" /> and [[Force lightning]].<ref name="Episode II" /> A few even could block melee attacks, such as those performed with lightsabers.<ref name="BF2">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

==History==
In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="SWTHR date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Porter Engle]] fought a pair of [[Nihil]] on [[Elphrona]] when a party of the raiders attempted to [[Raid on Elphrona|kidnap]] the wealthy [[Blythe family]] for ransom. During the skirmish, Engle temporarily parted with his [[lightsaber]] when he threw it to eliminate one of the raiders. Before he could retrieve his weapon, the other raider fired at him, forcing Engle to deflect the shots with the Force.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="MAdate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' to [[40 BBY]].</ref> [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] contemplated employing this power, but ultimately rejected the idea as too hard, for plasma responded poorly to being commanded by the Force.<ref name="Master & Apprentice">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref>

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] confronted his former [[Padawan]] learner [[Dooku]], secretly the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Tyranus, in a hangar on [[Geonosis]] during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]. Tyranus, who had previously defeated [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], exclaimed his superiority over his former Master and fired a blast of Force lightning at Yoda. Yoda deflected the blast back at Tyranus, who in turn used the Force to deflect the blast into the ceiling of the hangar. The Jedi Master then absorbed another bolt of the Sith's lightning into his body without any harm, prompting Dooku to turn to his lightsaber for combat.<ref name="Episode II" />

In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Darth Sidious]] and Darth Tyranus used the [[dark illusion]] power on Yoda in an [[Mission to Moraband|attempt to corrupt]] the Grand Master. As a result, Yoda experienced a [[Force vision]] where he fought Sidious. During the vision, Sidious fired a blast of Force lightning at Yoda. Yoda was able to successfully deflect Sidious' Force power, sending the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] tumbling off of the structure they were situated on.<ref name="Sacrifice">{{TCW|Sacrifice (episode)}}</ref> Yoda also was able to deflect melee attacks.<ref name="BF2">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

[[File:FatherHoldsLightsaber-Overlords.png|thumb|The Father holding Anakin's lightsaber in his hand.|left]]
The Mortis Gods like [[Father (Mortis)|the Father]] and [[Son|the Son]] were so powerful that they not only able to block Force lightning but also lightsabers with their hands and arms.<ref name="Altar of Mortis">{{TCW|Altar of Mortis (episode)}}</ref>

[[File:MaulBendsBlasterBolts-TPA.png|thumb|300px|Maul deflects Kryze's blaster bolts with the Force.]]Sometime later, former Jedi Padawan [[Ahsoka Tano]] confronted the renegade Sith Lord [[Maul]] in the [[Grand Salon]] of the [[Sundari Royal Palace]] during the [[Siege of Mandalore]]. [[Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]], who was accompanying Tano at the time, charged at Maul while firing multiple blaster bolts at him. In response, Maul effortlessly veered the bolts with waves of his hand before briefly [[Telekinesis|levitating]] Kryze to dissuade further attacks.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref>

Shortly after the formation of the [[Galactic Empire]], Yoda [[Duel in the Galactic Senate|confronted]] Darth Sidious in the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]] on [[Coruscant]], intending to destroy the new [[Galactic Emperor]] and the Sith Order (along with Obi-Wan). Mirroring the dark illusion Sidious had previously subjected Yoda to, the Sith Lord fired a blast of Force lightning at the Jedi Grand Master. Just like before, Yoda was able to deflect Sidious' lightning as he lost [[Yoda's lightsaber|his lightsaber]], although he coalescing the energy of Sidious's Force lightning into a blue orb between the two of them, slowly redirecting the lightning back onto an astounded Sidious, who was slowly being pushed back by the strength of Yoda's counterattack. However, the combination of both of their powers caused an explosion of Force energy, blasting them both apart, he fell off of the [[repulsorpod]] he was standing on in the process.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

During the [[Invasion of Mon Cala]] in [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the Sith Lord [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] fought in the [[Battle of Dac City]]. In the battle, the [[Mon Cala planetary defense services]] fired upon Vader, who used the Force to deflect a blaster bolt.<ref name="Darth Vader 2017 14">[[Darth Vader (2017) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 14]]</ref> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Vader 18 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 18]] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] remarked that one of the reasons it was difficult to kill Vader was because he could deflect blaster fire with the flick of his wrist.<ref name="Darth Vader 2017 18">[[Darth Vader (2017) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 18]]</ref>

[[File:Ezra_and_Ahsoka_are_attacked_by_Sidious.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Ezra and Ahsoka deflect fiery energy.]]
Less than a year before the first Death Star was destroyed,<ref name="Star Wars: The Original Trilogy – A Graphic Novel">''[[Star Wars: The Original Trilogy – A Graphic Novel]]''</ref> [[Ahsoka Tano]] briefly employed this technique to defend herself and [[Ezra Bridger]] from the [[Fiery energy]] Darth Sidious employed in his effort to enter the World Between Worlds.<ref name="A World Between Worlds">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref>

Later in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> during the [[Battle of Scarif]], Vader boarded the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] cruiser ''[[Profundity]]'' in pursuit of the stolen [[Death Star plans]]. The Sith Lord received resistance aboard the [[MC75 Star Cruiser|star cruiser]] from the [[Alderaanian consular security]]. Vader cut through the soldiers, using Force deflection to redirect a blaster shot into a rebel, however the plans escaped aboard the [[CR90 corvette|corvette]] ''[[Tantive IV]]''.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

[[File:PalpatineForceDeflect-2015StarWarsAnnual1.png|thumb|right|200px|Darth Sidious deflects Eneb Ray's blaster bolts]]
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Lord Vader prepared an ambush for [[Han Solo]] and the crew of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' on the [[Tibanna]]-[[mining]] colony [[Cloud City]]. When the [[Administrator]] of the city [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] led Solo into the trap, the rebel [[smuggler]] immediately fired on Vader. Vader proceeded to block the blaster bolts with the Force and [[Telekinesis|telekinetically]] yank [[Han's heavy blaster pistol|Solo's blaster]] out of his hands.<ref name="Episode V" /> Following Vader's revelation that [[Luke Skywalker]] was his son and their subsequent [[duel on Cloud City]], the dark lord sought to learn about his son's past. His search eventually led him to the planet [[Vendaxa]], where he encountered a look-alike of his deceased wife [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. Upon learning that Vader was a servant of the Emperor, the look-alike, who was actually Amidala's former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|Handmaiden]] [[Sabé]], fired her blaster at the Sith. Vader deflected the [[laser]] with the Force and pulled the [[blaster rifle]] out of Sabé's hands.<ref name="Darth Vader 2020 2">[[Darth Vader (2020) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 2]]</ref>

In a display of this power being used against non-energy attacks,<ref name="Shattered Empire 4" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="SE4 date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Shattered Empire 4|''Shattered Empire'' 4]] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> [[Luke Skywalker]] deflected thrown grenades back at the Imperial soldiers who attacked him, killing them.<ref name="Shattered Empire 4" /> In 9 ABY,<ref name="Redemption date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Chapter 8: Redemption]]" to [[9 ABY]].</ref> [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] used this power to protect [[Din Djarin]] from a [[flamethrower]] by returning the flames back onto the Stormtrooper.<ref name="Mando8" />

In [[35 ABY]], [[Rey Skywalker]] used Force deflection to block the [[Sovereign Protector]]s' blaster bolts during her confrontation with her grandfather Darth Sidious during the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
