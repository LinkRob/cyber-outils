In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date note" /> a final '''[[lightsaber duel]]''' was fought between [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] and fallen Jedi [[Bode Akuna]] in the [[Jedi temple (Tanalorr)|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Tanalorr]]. The [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Merrin]] fought alongside Kestis against Akuna. The duel ended with Akuna's death and the acquisition of the [[Abyss compass|compass]] that showed the way to Tanalorr through the [[nebula]] [[Koboh Abyss]], which allowed the preparation of Tanalorr for use by the [[Hidden Path]] underground network.<ref name="Survivor" />
