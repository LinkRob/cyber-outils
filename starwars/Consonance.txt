The [[House of Organa|''RHA'']] '''''Consonance''''' was a [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|''Sphyrna''-class Hammerhead corvette]] in the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] that participated in the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> It survived the battle and escaped seconds before the arrival of the ''[[Devastator]]''.<ref name="Rogue One" /> Its [[communications officer]] was [[Lune Buscon]], who monitored [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] transmissions during the battle and was an [[Alderaanian consular security|Alderaanian fleet trooper]].<ref name="On the Front Lines" />
