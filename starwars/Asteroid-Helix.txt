The '''Asteroid-Helix''' was an [[asteroid field]] located near [[Karga]]. One of the [[asteroid]]s, [[Asteroid Helix 13-v]], was the location of an ancient [[The Force|Force]] cult temple, which received a number of [[pilgrim]]s over the [[Standard year|years]]. Long after the cult had disappeared, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] used Asteroid Helix 13-v as a temporary base, which was later investigated by the [[Galactic Empire]] following the rebels' departure.

==Description==
The Asteroid-Helix was an [[asteroid field]] located near [[Karga]], consisting of a number of [[asteroid]]s of varying shapes and sizes situated in a cylindrical spiral pattern. One of the asteroids in the Asteroid-Helix was [[Asteroid Helix 13-v]], and was large enough to build a sizeable structure within. Asteroid Helix 13-v had enough of an [[atmosphere]] for [[human]]s to survive on its surface.<ref name="Aphra 38" />

==History==
{{Quote|Signal the ''fleet''. Dispatch ''bombers''. The rebels are not ''here''. That is all the ''exploration'' I require.''"<br />"''B-but&mdash;but, Mister ''Vader'', you can't destroy this place! It's ''uni''&mdash;|Darth Vader and Korin Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 38" />}}
Sometime prior to the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Asteroid Helix 13-v, one of the asteroids in the field, was settled by a [[The Force|Force]] cult of the [[J'doon]] school, who turned the asteroid into a Force shrine. Over the shrine's history, it was visited by a number of [[pilgrim]]s, though was eventually abandoned.<ref name="Aphra 38" />

[[File:HelixShrineBombing-2016DoctorAphra38.jpg|thumb|left|320px|The Empire bomb Asteroid Helix 13-v.]]
By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 38|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 38]] takes place shortly before [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 39|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 39]], at which point [[Echo Base]] has been constructed on [[Hoth]], and prior to [[Empire Ascendant 1|''Empire Ascendant'' 1]], which in turn takes place before the [[Battle of Hoth]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' states that Echo Base was constructed in [[3 ABY]] and places the Battle of Hoth in the same [[Standard year|year]], meaning that the events of ''Doctor Aphra'' 38, including the appearance of the Asteroid-Helix, are set in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> Asteroid Helix 13-v had been used as a temporary base for a cell of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], who were preparing to travel to their next headquarters on<ref name="Aphra 38" /> the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]]<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> [[Hoth]].<ref name="Aphra 39">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 39|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 39]]</ref> While exploring the Asteroid-Helix, the [[scholar]] [[Korin Aphra]] encountered the rebel cell, who gave him supplies and sent him away. The rebels eventually departed the shrine,<ref name="Aphra 38" /> but left behind hidden [[camera]]s.<ref name="Aphra 39" />

After being captured by the Empire, Korin Aphra informed them of the rebel base, and the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] traveled to the Asteroid-Helix alongside Aphra and his daughter, the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]]. The two Aphras investigated the shrine, finding traces of the rebels' presence. After experiencing a [[Force vision]], Vader declared the expedition to be concluded, ordering [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE bombers]] to destroy the asteroid once they had left.<ref name="Aphra 38" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Asteroid-Helix first appeared in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 38|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 38]], written by [[Simon Spurrier]], illustrated by [[Caspar Wijngaard]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 38" /> on [[November 6]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/76030/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2016_38|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016) #38}}</ref> [[Jason Fry]], author of the "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix of [[star system]]s that was released alongside the "[[Star Wars Galaxy Map]]" map on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]], stated on his personal Substack that the Asteroid-Helix was among a number of systems that were intentionally unlisted due to being codenames or lacking defined coordinates.<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
