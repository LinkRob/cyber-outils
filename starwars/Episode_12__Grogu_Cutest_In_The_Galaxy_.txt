The '''twelfth episode''' of the animated web series ''[[Grogu Cutest In The Galaxy]]'' was released on [[August 26]], [[2025]]. It was posted by ''[[Star Wars]]'' Japan on [[Wikipedia:Instagram Reels|Instagram Reels]].<ref name="Instagram" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
[[Din Grogu]] spots a [[Marksman-H training remote]] on the ground, thinking it's just a ball. The remote is rolled around by Grogu, and then levitates, eventually shooting him. Grogu falls on his backside. The remote fires again, but this time Grogu jumps in time to avoid being hit.
