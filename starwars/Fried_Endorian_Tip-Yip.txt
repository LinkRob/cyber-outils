Maybe it ain't the traditional way that Ewoks cook up their feathered friend, but if you ask me, there's plenty of room on Endor for more than one type of tip-yip.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
'''Fried Endorian Tip-Yip''' was a dish comprised of fried [[tip-yip]] from [[Endor]], [[mashed chokeroot]], mixed [[vegetable]]s, and [[Gurreck Gravy]]. Recipes for the dish and its components were included by its creator, [[Strono Tuggs]], in his [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]. The dish was served at [[Tuggs' Grub]] whenever the mobile restaurant visited [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]].

==Description==
Fried Endorian Tip-Yip was a dish centered on fried meat from a [[tip-yip]], an avian [[species]] that originated on [[Endor]]. The traditional preparation of tip-yip by [[Ewok]]s was oven-roasted. [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] created his own version which was breaded and fried. He covered the fried tip-yip with [[Gurreck Gravy]], which was created by Ewoks and made with Endorian [[Plant|herbs]], and served it with a side of mixed [[vegetable]]s and [[mashed chokeroot]] stirred together. The dish could be consumed by Artiodacs,<ref name="Cookbook" /> [[human]]s,<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> and [[Togruta]]s.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

==History==
{{Quote|You should have seen how packed this place was when he first arrived. I might have snuck off work early just to get an order of fried Endorian tip-yip or seven.|Julen Rakab, about Tuggs' Grub|audio=RakabTipYip.flac|<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />}}
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date: Mandalorian">{{MandoDate|16}}</ref> the [[Mandalorian warrior]] [[Din Djarin]] traveled to [[Lafete]] as he sought help from fellow [[Mandalorian]]s [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] and [[Koska Reeves]] to rescue his [[foundling]], [[Din Grogu|Grogu]]. He located them at a [[Lafete diner|diner]] that served Endorian tip-yip.<ref name="The Mandalorian" />

Sometime after the [[Battle of Takodana]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Strono Tuggs wrote a [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]] that included recipes for Gurreck Gravy, mashed chokeroot, and fried tip-yip. He also began his mobile restaurant, [[Tuggs' Grub]], which he operated out of a modified [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|transport shuttle]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date: GE">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> he brought Tuggs' Grub to [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] and worked at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]], a building where rotating guest chefs brought mobile restaurants. On Batuu, he served Fried Endorian Tip-Yip in portions sized for adults or for [[youngling]]s.<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> Explorer and author [[Eloc Throno]] wrote in his guidebook ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'' that the hearty dish, which he referred to as Endorian Fried Chicken, was the best preparation of tip-yip he had eaten outside of Endor.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

In 34 ABY,<ref name="Date: GE" /> [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]] ate Fried Endorian Tip-Yip at Tuggs' Grub during its [[Standard month|monthly]] [[Standard week|week-long]] visit to Batuu.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> In that same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date: ACOF">The events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', including [[Julen Rakab]] and [[Izal Garsea]]'s visit to [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|Tuggs' Grub]], occur [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Tuggs served the [[farmer]] [[Julen Rakab]] and the [[smuggler]] [[Izal Garsea]] his dish along with a fiery [[Color|red]] [[sauce]] and [[Moof Juice]]. After Garsea left, the [[musician]] [[Neelo]] came to their table and ate from Rakab's dish. When Garsea returned, Tuggs prompted her to eat by questioning why she had not eaten her tip-yip yet; she enjoyed it even though it had grown cold. Rakab later gave a morsel of the tip-yip to a feral [[tooka]] cat.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:FriedEndorianTipYip-Cookbook.jpg|thumb|left|260px|Fried Endorian Tip-Yip]]
Fried Endorian Tip-Yip was first made available to guests in the restaurant Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], a themed attraction that opened at [[Disneyland]] Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> It later arrived at ''Galaxy's Edge'' in [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]].<ref name="DHS menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/dinner|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Dinner Menu}}</ref> It is served as an entrée or as a kids' meal called Fried Endorian Tip-yip Youngling Meal. In the past, the entrée's menu description read, "Crispy chicken, roasted vegetable potato mash, herb gravy." The entrée has since added another side option that originated with the youngling meal, becoming "Crispy chicken with choice of vegetable mashed potatoes with herb gravy or macaroni and cheese and roasted vegetables." Since its debut, the dish has been renamed more than once, including to Endorian Fried Chicken Tip-Yip<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref> and to Endorian Fried Chicken.<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> Another reconfiguration of the dish's sides was announced for Disney's Hollywood Studios on [[April 23]], [[2026]]. The new menu description reads "Crispy chicken served with house-made macaroni and roasted vegetables garnished with cauliflower leaf kimchi".<ref name="DPB2026">{{DPB|url=disney-experiences/disney-eats-may-the-4th-foodie-guide|text=Disney Eats: May the 4th Foodie Guide 2026}}</ref>

Recipes for the dish and its components are included in the [[2019]] recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The ingredients for the dish are boneless, skinless [[Wikipedia:Chicken as food|chicken]] breasts; an oil for frying such as [[Wikipedia:Vegetable oil|vegetable oil]]; [[Wikipedia:Flour|all-purpose flour]]; [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]]; [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]]; [[Wikipedia:Cayenne pepper|cayenne pepper]]; [[Wikipedia:Egg as food|egg]]; [[Wikipedia:Buttermilk|buttermilk]]; mashed chokeroot or [[Wikipedia:Mashed potato|mashed potatoes]]; mixed [[Wikipedia:Vegetable|vegetables]], such as [[Wikipedia:Pea|peas]], [[Wikipedia:Carrot|carrots]], and [[Wikipedia:Corn|corn]]; and Gurreck Gravy.<ref name="Cookbook" />
