The '''Dominus sector''' was a [[sector|subsector]] of the [[Lothal sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that contained the [[Dominus system]].<ref name="Appendix" />

{{Sec-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Lothal sector was created for the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' television series<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> and replaced the [[Dominus sector/Legends|Dominus sector]] as it existed in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, resulting in the Dominus sector being relegated to a subsector of the Lothal sector.<ref name="Appendix" />
