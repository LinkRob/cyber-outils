'''Cameron''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[501st Legion]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. During the [[Battle of Quell]], Cameron was one of several 501st [[soldier]]s, including [[Lucky]], who joined their superiors, [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Ahsoka Tano]], on a mission to rescue [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]] from the ''[[Liberty (Venator-class)|Liberty]]''. After boarding the ''Liberty'' and losing one member of their team, Cameron and the others would be joined by<ref name="JC" /> [[327th Star Corps]]<ref name="Destiny">''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Clone Trooper Mission]]''</ref> trooper [[Flash]] before finding Secura and [[Clone Commander]] [[Bly]].<ref name="JC" />

Cameron later [[Death|died]] along with Lucky and Flash to a [[mastiff phalone]] after a crash landing on [[Maridun]]. Cameron was choked to death by the deadly creature's claws.<ref name="JC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cameron appeared in "[[Jedi Crash]]" and was identified after their death, opening up questions as to which clone trooper they are before their demise. In the episode, three clone troopers<ref name="JC" /> from the [[501st Legion]]<ref name="Jedi Crash gallery" /> board the ''Liberty'' with Rex and Ahsoka Tano, only for one of those three troopers to be killed by enemy fire. By the time Tano and company reunite with Anakin Skywalker, however, a new third clone trooper has joined the team. Where this third trooper came from, such as whether they were a member of Secura's 327th stationed aboard the ''Liberty'' or boarded the Star Destroyer from one of the other 501st gunships, is not explained. All that the episode answers is that the three clones are named Cameron, Lucky, and Flash, leaving unclear as to who is who.<ref name="JC" />

In [[2019]], however, Flash would reappear in the audiobook ''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Clone Trooper Mission]]'', where Flash was established to be part of the 327th.<ref name="Destiny">''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Clone Trooper Mission]]''</ref> In [[2022]], the encyclopedia "[[The Grand Army of the Republic (reference book)|The Grand Army of the Republic]]" would be released, which incorrectly listed Cameron, Lucky, and Flash all as members of Secura's task force.<ref name="Grand Army Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Grand Army of the Republic}}</ref> In reality, at least two must be part<ref name="JC" /> of the 501st<ref name="Jedi Crash gallery" /> due to boarding with Tano and Rex.<ref name="JC" /> As Flash was independently confirmed to be part of the 327th,<ref name="Destiny" /> this article assumes the following: "The Grand Army of the Republic" is correct in describing Flash as a survivor of Secura's crew, thereby placing them as the third clone trooper whom Tano's team found along the way, but Cameron and Lucky must be Tano and Rex's two troopers who survived boarding. Therefore, Cameron and Lucky are part of the 501st, not Secura's 327th.
