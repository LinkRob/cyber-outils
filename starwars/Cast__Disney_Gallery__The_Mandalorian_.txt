"'''Cast'''" is the third episode of ''[[Disney Gallery: The Mandalorian]]'', a docuseries on the production of the [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''. The episode was aired on [[May 15]], [[2020]] on Disney+.<ref name="D23" />

==Official description==
''Pedro Pascal, Gina Carano, and Carl Weathers discuss the making of'' The Mandalorian.
