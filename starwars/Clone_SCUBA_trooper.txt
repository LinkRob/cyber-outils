'''Clone SCUBA troopers''', also known as '''clone dive troopers''', were [[clone trooper]]s from the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] who were outfitted for combat in [[Water|aquatic]] environments. During the [[Battle of Mon Cala]], these clone [[trooper]]s fought alongside [[Mon Calamari]] and [[Gungan Grand Army]] forces against [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] and [[Quarren]] forces led by [[Riff Tamson]] and [[Nossor Ri]]. In the end, the Separatists were defeated when the Quarrens turned against Tamson and joined forces with their former opponents.

==Description==
[[File:Clone-Scuba-Armor.png|thumb|left|130px|A clone SCUBA trooper]]
[[Clone trooper]]s who acted as [[Cloning|clone]] SCUBA [[trooper]]s wore a suit of [[Clone trooper armor|clone trooper gear]] known as [[Clone Dive Armor]].<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> A [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I version]] of SCUBA [[armor]] featured a [[Color|black]] [[helmet]] with an [[Breath mask|oxygen mask]].<ref name="Before the Dark">{{Acme|url=products/before-the-dark-times-james-silvani|text=Before the Dark Times}}</ref> During the days of [[Phase II clone trooper armor]], SCUBA troopers wore a set of [[Color|white]] armor<ref name="Prisoners">{{TCW|Prisoners}}</ref> that was fitted with six [[Water|underwater]] propulsion jets to aid their movement. To survive [[Water|underwater]], they carried a scuba [[backpack]] kit that was equipped with gill grills and a pair of breathing tubes which circulated [[oxygen]] into their [[helmet]]s. They wore white fins which could be attached to their boots.<ref name="Gungan Attack concept">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/gungan-attack-concept-art-gallery|text="Gungan Attack" Concept Art Gallery}}</ref> The armor could also be customized via coloration instead of remaining plain white.<ref name="Prisoners" /> 

Clone SCUBA troopers' breathing equipment and armor allowed them to survive for extended periods in the ocean depths of places such as [[Mon Cala]].<ref name="Prisoners" /> Some also rode the speedy [[One Man Submersible Devilfish|OMS Devilfish subs]].<ref name="Water War" /> The primary weapon of SCUBA troopers was the [[DC-12U beam rifle]], a double-barrelled firearm optimized for wet or underwater environments that also featured a high powered [[glowrod]] to provide light in the ocean depths.<ref name="Collapse" />

Like most clone troopers,<ref name="clonetroopers">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}}</ref> clone SCUBA troopers were 1.83 [[meter]]s or 6 [[Foot|feet]] tall. They specialized in underwater maneuvers and sub piloting. They were also trained in infiltration, armed and unarmed combat as well as survival tactics.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref>

==History==
Clone SCUBA troopers were [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] clone troopers who were outfitted for aquatic combat during the [[Clone Wars]]. They were an elite division of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], although standard clone troopers were sometimes fitted with their specialized gear.<ref name="SWECG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> A [[Monnk's company|company]] of Clone SCUBA troopers led by the [[Jedi General]] [[Kit Fisto]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Monnk]] traveled to the [[planet]] Mon Cala after [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and the [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] failed to mediate tensions between the [[Mon Calamari]] and [[Quarren]], and after the pro-[[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Quarren Isolation League]] attacked the Mon Calamari [[Coral City|capital]]. Clone SCUBA troopers aided the [[Mon Calamari Guard]] in the [[city]]'s [[Battle of Mon Cala|defense]] during the initial battle. Despite fighting off Quarren and [[AQ-series battle droid|aqua droid]] forces, the clones and their Mon Cala allies were forced to retreat after the Separatist [[Commander]] [[Riff Tamson]] unleashed [[Hydroid Medusa]]s.<ref name="Water War" />

[[File:Clone-SCUBA-Orange.png|thumb|right|240px|A Clone SCUBA trooper with orange markings]]
Some surviving Clone SCUBA troopers assisted the [[Jedi]] in their efforts to evacuate [[Prince]] [[Lee-Char]] and Senator [[Meena Tills]] offworld. When the escape attempt failed, two troopers accompanied the Jedi and their Mon Calamari allies into hiding in several underwater [[cave]]s. With the Republic unable to outfit a [[regiment]] of clone troopers for aquatic combat at a short notice, the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] instead appealed to the [[Gungan]]s of [[Naboo]] for help. A detachment of the [[Gungan Grand Army]] led by [[Junior Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] arrived in [[time]] to aid the fugitives but were overwhelmed by Tamson's [[Trident-class assault ship|''Trident'' ships]].<ref name="Gungan Attack" />

Several Clone SCUBA troopers were [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] in a [[prison]] camp with several Mon Calamari captives and civilians. Later, several of these troopers along with many Mon Calamari, Quarren, and Gungans attended the execution of Prince Lee-Char. Before Tamson could execute the Mon Cal prince, the Quarren [[chief]]tain [[Nossor Ri]] had a change of heart and successfully convinced his people to turn against Commander Tamson and the Separatist occupiers. The clone troopers along with the Quarren, Mon Calamari, Gungans, and Jedi then joined forces to expel the Separatist from Mon Cala.<ref name="Prisoners" />

During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Kier Domadi]] participated in Clone Wars-era battle reenactments as a scuba trooper when aquatic battles came up.<ref name="Leia">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>
