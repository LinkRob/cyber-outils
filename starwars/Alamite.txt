'''Alamites''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[humanoid]] [[species]] native to the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]], where they once resided in the deserted wastelands beyond the [[City|cities]]. After the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Great Purge of Mandalore|purge]] of Mandalore, some Alamites relocated to the ruins of [[Sundari]]. At some point, numerous Alamites attacked [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] and [[Din Djarin]] in the outskirts of Sundari.<ref name="Chapter 18" />

==Biology and appearance==
Alamites were an average-sized [[humanoid]] [[species]] possessing tusks and four [[eye]]s<ref name="Chapter 18" /> with bioluminescent retinas. They had powerful mandibles and prehensile toes.<ref name="Mando Visual Guide" /> According to [[Bo-Katan Kryze]], they were known to eat [[human]]s.<ref name="Chapter 18" />

==History==
Alamites were native to [[Mandalore]]. Before the [[Great Purge of Mandalore|Great Purge]], they lived in the Mandalorian wastelands in between the dome cities, but after the Purge, they migrated to the ruins of [[Sundari]].<ref name="Chapter 18" /> By this period, these scavengers wore primitive, tattered rags they belted in order to carry items, which included cudgels and hafted clubs made using [[trinitite]] stone glass.<ref name="Mando Visual Guide" />

[[Din Djarin]] was ambushed by<ref name="Chapter 18" /> savage Alamites in the layered Sundari ruins.<ref name="Mando Visual Guide" /> [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] subsequently fought off more Alamites while on a mission to rescue Djarin.<ref name="Chapter 18" />
