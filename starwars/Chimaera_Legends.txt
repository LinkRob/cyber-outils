The '''''Chimaera''''' {{Pronounced|kaɪˈmɪərə}} was an [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]] of the [[Imperial Navy/Legends|Imperial Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]] that served for over forty years and fought during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], the [[Yuuzhan Vong War]] and the [[Second Galactic Civil War]]. Under command of the famous [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]], it led the [[Chiss/Legends|Chiss]] [[Admiral/Legends|admiral's]] [[Thrawn's fleet (Galactic Empire)|fleet]] during his brilliant [[Thrawn campaign|campaign]] against the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]]. In the following years, it became the [[Flagship/Legends|flagship]] of the [[Imperial Remnant]] under [[Gilad Pellaeon/Legends|Gilad Pellaeon]]. The Remnant would lose the ''Chimaera'' following the Yuuzhan Vong War when it was severely damaged. The starship was eventually recovered by former Imperial Admiral [[Natasi Daala]], who made it the flagship of her private fleet, the [[Maw Irregular Fleet]].

==History==
===Galactic Civil War===
{{Quote|Captain Pellaeon is a competent commander, and he's made the ''Chimaera'' into one of the finest warships in the fleet.|[[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]] to [[Captain of the Line/Legends|Senior Captain]] [[Voss Parck/Legends|Parck]]|<ref name="CoF">{{StoryCite|story=Crisis of Faith|book=Heir to the Empire: The 20th Anniversary Edition}}</ref>}}
[[File:ChimaeraCCG.jpg|thumb|left|180px|The ''Chimaera'' at the [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor]]]]
Early in its service, an officer by the name of [[Gilad Pellaeon/Legends|Gilad Pellaeon]] was transferred to its command crew; he eventually became the ship's second in command. During this time, the ''Chimaera'' was upgraded to the new ''Imperial II'' standard, increasing its [[Deflector shield/Legends|shielding]] and armament.

At some point before the [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor]], the ''Chimaera'' was seen undergoing weapons testing along with the [[Inexorable (Imperial I-class)|''Inexorable'']], the ''[[Nemesis]]'' and the [[Stormhawk (Imperial I-class)|''Stormhawk'']] in the [[Carida system/Legends|Carida system]]. [[Ace Azzameen/Legends|Ace Azzameen's]] ''[[Otana/Legends|Otana]]'' [[Reconnaissance of mercenary base|mistakenly]] emerged from [[Hyperspace/Legends|hyperspace]] near the Star Destroyers but managed to escape.<ref name="XwA">''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Alliance]]''</ref>

Later, the Chimaera was recalled to Endor as part of [[Death Squadron/Legends|Death Squadron]], the fleet protecting the [[Death Star II/Legends|second Death Star]], under [[Admiral/Legends|Admiral]] [[Horst Strage]].<ref name="EGW" /> During the Battle of Endor, the Rebel pilot [[Ekelarc Yong/Legends|Ekelarc Yong]] crashed on the ship.<ref name="RotJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref><ref name="Insider105">{{InsiderCite|105|25 Years of Return of the Jedi!|25 Years of ''Return of the Jedi''!|reprint=1}}</ref>

[[File:Famous_Star_Destroyers_RotJ.png|thumb|left|250px|Ekelarc Yong crashes on the ''Chimaera'' during the Battle of Endor.]]
The ''Chimaera'' was next in the chain of command after the [[Command ship/Legends|command ships]] [[Executor/Legends|''Executor'']] and [[Pride of Tarlandia/Legends|''Pride of Tarlandia'']], and with the destruction of both, became acting command ship. After several hours of fighting and the loss of Admiral Strage, [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Gilad Pellaeon/Legends|Gilad Pellaeon]] became acting [[Commanding officer/Legends|commanding officer]] of the fleet and ordered a retreat<ref name="EGW" /> to the nearest [[Sector/Legends|sector]] [[Capital/Legends|capital]], [[Annaj/Legends|Cannij Barr]], in the [[Annaj system/Legends|Annaj system]].<ref name="TTB">''[[The Truce at Bakura Sourcebook]]''</ref><ref name="EGW" /> A quarrel broke out between the new commander of Death Squadron, who wanted to counterattack the Rebels, and two higher ranking officers: [[Admiral/Legends|Admirals]] [[Blitzer Harrsk|Harrsk]] and [[Adye Prittick]]. Harrsk quickly deserted to create his own warlord fief, and Pellaeon accepted to transfer command to Admiral Prittick, who ordered a retreat to the [[Yag'Dhul system/Legends|Yag'Dhul system]],<ref name="EGW" /> in order to protect the [[Core Worlds/Legends|Core Worlds]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> Admiral Prittick briefly commanded the fleet while at Annaj but did not remain in command for reasons still unknown; this was presumably at least in part due to the authority vested in ''Chimaera'' itself. ''Chimaera'' remained under Pellaeon's command during the tumultuous years that followed, and under Pellaeon's leadership, it came to be regarded as one of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire's]] most efficiently run Star Destroyers.

===Thrawn's flagship===
{{Quote|The ''Chimaera'' is fully at your command, Admiral.|[[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Gilad Pellaeon/Legends|Gilad Pellaeon]] to [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]]|<ref name="HttE" />}}
<div style="float:right">[[File:Chimaera.jpg|thumb|200px|''Chimaera'', as the flagship of [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]]]]</div>

This record for efficiency may have been what made [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]] choose the ''Chimaera'' to be his [[Flagship/Legends|flagship]]. Thrawn immediately left his mark on the ship, having a luxury entertainment suite once used by Pellaeon's predecessor turned into a meditation chamber/command room. Thrawn's reorganization of the fleet placed the ''Chimaera'' at the center of his personal armada, along with the Star Destroyers ''[[Death's Head]]'', [[Judicator/Legends|''Judicator'']], [[Inexorable (Imperial I-class)|''Inexorable'']], [[Stormhawk (Imperial I-class)|''Stormhawk'']], and ''[[Nemesis]]''.<ref name="TTSB">''[[The Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook]]''</ref>

Thrawn soon began training the crew to his own specifications, and when he ordered the ''Chimaera'' to [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] to [[Mission to Tatooine (Killik Twilight)|obtain]] the [[Alderaan/Legends|Alderaanian]] moss painting ''[[Killik Twilight]]'', they were put to the test by the unexpected complications caused by [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] and [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa Solo]].<ref name="TG">''[[Tatooine Ghost]]''</ref>

[[File:Thrawn_NEC.jpg|thumb|left|160px|[[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]] in his private command room aboard the ''Chimaera'']]

Soon afterward, Thrawn began [[Thrawn campaign|his campaign]] on the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] in earnest, starting with an information raid on [[Obroa-skai/Legends|Obroa-skai]]. The ''Chimaera'' remained his flagship throughout this devastating campaign, until his death at [[Bilbringi system/Legends|Bilbringi]] at the hands of his [[Noghri/Legends|Noghri]] bodyguard, [[Rukh/Legends|Rukh]].<ref name="TTSB" />

Pellaeon was forced to call a full retreat of the [[Imperial Navy/Legends|Imperial Starfleet]], which regrouped near the [[Unknown Regions/Legends|Unknown Regions]].<ref name="TLCSb">''[[The Last Command Sourcebook]]''</ref>

===New Republic capture===
Soon after, he was summoned to the [[Deep Core/Legends|Deep Core]] to serve the clone of [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]]. During [[Operation Shadow Hand]], Pellaeon was forced to abandon the ''Chimaera'' in the midst of the [[capture of Duro]], losing most of his senior staff in the process and nearly dying himself. Its burnt hulk was salvaged by the New Republic.<ref name="TNEC" />

The ''Chimaera'', now in New Republic hands, was nearly scrapped on several occasions. Finally, some years later, slicers working for the Imperial Remnant created a false disassembly order for the ship, sending it to the shipyards of [[Gravlex Med/Legends|Gravlex Med]], former homeworld of the [[Anx/Legends|Anx]]. Upon its arrival, its skeleton crew was overcome by [[Imperial commando/Legends|Imperial commandos]], who then blasted their way through New Republic pickets. [[Admiral/Legends|Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar/Legends|Ackbar]] himself commanded the forces that attempted to recapture the ''Chimaera'', but Pellaeon, now an Admiral in command of the entire Imperial Starfleet, nonetheless recovered his flagship.<ref name="TNEC" /> Pellaeon assigned Captain [[Ardiff]] to command the ship.<ref name="SotP" />

===Flagship of the Remnant===
{{Quote|I don't trust anyone off the ''Chimaera'' not to be in Disra's pocket.|[[Admiral/Legends|Admiral]] [[Gilad Pellaeon/Legends|Gilad Pellaeon]] to [[Commander/Legends|Commander]] [[Dreyf]]|<ref name="VotF">''[[Vision of the Future]]''</ref>}}
The fortunes of the Imperial Remnant soon took a turn for the worse. In a last ditch effort, Pellaeon had a [[Cloaking device/Legends|cloaking device]] and a [[Computerized Combat Predictor]] installed aboard the ''Chimaera'' for trials. Ultimately, the trials proved unpromising, and Pellaeon began seeking a treaty with the New Republic.<ref name="SotP" />

<div style="float:right">[[File:Gavrisompellaeon.jpg|thumb|250px|The signing of the [[Pellaeon–Gavrisom Treaty]] on board the ''Chimaera'']]</div>

Despite the scheming of recalcitrant elements within the Empire, Pellaeon succeeded in convincing the New Republic to declare peace, and Pellaeon and Acting [[Chief of State (New Republic)/Legends|Chief of State]] Ponc Gavrisom of the New Republic signed the [[Pellaeon–Gavrisom Treaty|peace accords]] aboard the ''Chimaera'', finally ending the decades-long war between the two governments.<ref name="VotF" />

===Yuuzhan Vong War===
{{Quote|With ''Chimaera'' still missing, the Moffs will assume that Gilad Pellaeon is dead.|[[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Arien Yage]] to [[Darth Caedus|Jacen Solo]]|<ref name="FH1">''[[The New Jedi Order: Force Heretic I: Remnant]]''</ref>}}
A fairly long period of peace was punctuated by the [[Yuuzhan Vong War]]. The ''Chimaera'' served valiantly at the [[Battle of Ithor]], then was recalled along with the rest of the fleet to defend the Imperial Remnant itself.<ref name="DT2" />

Eventually, [[Yuuzhan Vong]] forces, under Commander [[B'shith Vorrik]], appeared in the [[Bastion system/Legends|Bastion system]], destroying planetary defense systems with ease and catching the Imperial Navy off guard, as they were expecting the force to attack the [[Hand of Thrawn]] base at [[Nirauan/Legends|Nirauan]].<ref name="FH1" />

Only the ''Chimaera'' responded to the attack with suitable efficiency. Despite this, the Navy was able to hold its own, or so it appeared, until the Yuuzhan Vong pulled back to make way for swarms of thousands of [[grutchin]]s. The Navy responded to the grutchins with what Pellaeon considered to be appalling disorganization. The Remnant took serious losses from Vorrik's forces, and Pellaeon ordered the evacuation of Bastion. ''Chimaera'' and another ''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer, the ''[[Superior]]'', were harried by the Yuuzhan Vong all the way to a gas giant planet further out in the Bastion system, where the ''Chimaera'' took considerable damage before a [[Yorik-et|coralskipper]] rammed the bridge of the ship. Pellaeon was nearly killed, but a junior officer pulled him from the bridge and took him to a medical frigate. ''Chimaera'' was later able to limp into the [[Yaga Minor system/Legends|Yaga Minor system]], where it began undergoing repairs.<ref name="FH1" />

For the remaining duration of the war against the Yuuzhan Vong, Pellaeon commanded the fleet from the bridge of a different Star Destroyer, ''[[Right to Rule]]''.<ref name="FH1" /> As [[Supreme Commander (Alliance)/Legends|Supreme Commander]] of the [[Galactic Alliance Defense Force]], Pellaeon's flagship was the ''[[Megador]]''. It is possible that it was decided that the Alliance's flagship should not be one with such a distinguished history of Imperial service, especially with its commander being an Imperial as well.

===Second Galactic Civil War===
{{Quote|She was never confirmed destroyed.|[[Darth Caedus]] upon the arrival of the ''Chimaera'' at the Second Battle of Fondor|<ref name="Revelation" />}}
The ''Chimaera'' was later used by Admiral [[Natasi Daala]] as the flagship of her [[Maw Irregular Fleet]] during the [[Second Galactic Civil War]]. The ship had been refitted with unique weaponry, [[Metal-Crystal Phase Shifter]]s, remaining from Daala's command of the [[Maw Cluster/Legends|Maw Cluster]] where the Empire had developed new weapon technologies. These weapons made ''Chimaera'' a serious threat to all enemy vessels as that their shields could not repel the attacks.<ref name="Revelation" />

Daala made a theatrical arrival at the [[Second Battle of Fondor]] where the ''Chimaera'' enabled a decisive victory of the Imperial Remnant and the [[Alliance-in-exile]] against [[Darth Caedus]]'s [[Galactic Federation of Free Alliances|Galactic Alliance]].<ref name="Revelation" /> The ship later supported the [[Jedi Coalition]] at the [[Second Battle of Roche]].<ref name="Invincible">''[[Legacy of the Force: Invincible]]''</ref>

===Later history===
In the years following the Second Galactic Civil War, Daala became [[Chief of State (Galactic Alliance)|Chief of State]] of the Galactic Alliance but was quickly [[Plan Delta|deposed]] by the [[New Jedi Order]] in order to end her increasingly tyrannical rule.<ref name="Conviction">''[[Fate of the Jedi: Conviction]]''</ref> She eventually developed a plot to seize control of the Imperial Remnant. With his position threatened by Daala's ambition to become [[Empress/Legends|Empress]], [[Head of State (Imperial Remnant)|Head of State]] [[Jagged Fel]] mobilized a fleet of loyal supporters, including some [[Empire of the Hand]] forces, and moved to destroy Daala and her subversives with a pre-emptive [[Battle over Exodo II|strike]] in [[44 ABY]]. At [[Exodo II]], he engaged the Maw Irregular Fleet, once again led by the ''Chimaera''. [[Tors Remal]] served as the ship captain during the battle.<ref name="Ascension" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|In Greek myth, the Chimaera was a fantastical fire-breathing beast that combined lion, goat and snake. It was also held to be unconquerable, though it was eventually killed by Bellerophon. Nowadays, the word refers to something made up of disparate parts (usually as a result of grafting or genetic manipulation), or something wildly and grotesquely imaginary. All of those elements went into my decision to name Thrawn's flagship the ''Chimaera''. Disparate elements (human plus Chiss), considered imaginary (and hence not taken seriously by others until Thrawn was ready to move), and unconquerable.|Timothy Zahn|<ref name="HttE20">''[[Heir to the Empire: The 20th Anniversary Edition]]''</ref>}}
The ''Chimaera'' was created by [[Timothy Zahn]] for his seminal [[1991]] novel ''[[Heir to the Empire]]''. As the flagship of the famous Grand Admiral Thrawn and later the Imperial Remnant, it has became one of the most emblematic starships of the [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]], and its [[in-universe]] history now extends over 40 years. In [[1998]], the ''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]'' CD-ROM [[Retroactive continuity|retconned]] the ''Chimaera'' as one of the Star Destroyers seen in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="BTM">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref> [[Decipher]]'s [[Death Star II Limited|''Star Wars Customizable Card Game – Death Star II Limited'']] card set again placed the ship at the Battle of Endor in [[2000]].<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Death Star II Limited|cardname=''Chimaera''}}</ref> This was later confirmed in the ''[[Star Wars Insider 105]]'' article ''[[25 Years of Return of the Jedi!]]''.<ref name="Insider105" />

The vessel was named for the [[Wikipedia:Chimera (mythology)|three-headed monster]] of [[Wikipedia:Greek mythology|Greek mythology]], a beast made up of lion, goat and snake parts combined that was said to be unconquerable. In the present day, the word "chimaera" refers to something made of similarly disparate parts, often as a result of genetic manipulation. It also refers to things that are wildly and grotesquely imaginary. Author Timothy Zahn considered all three of these meanings when he chose to name Thrawn's flagship the ''Chimaera'': disparate elements to represent the Human and Chiss who manned the ship, considered imaginary and thus not taken seriously by opponents until Thrawn made his move, and an unconquerable enemy.<ref name="HttE20" />

''Chimaera'' was also the name of a server&mdash;or "Galaxy"&mdash;in ''[[Star Wars Galaxies]]''.
