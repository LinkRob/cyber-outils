The '''A280 blaster rifle''' was a type of [[blaster rifle]] mostly used by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel trooper|troopers]]<ref name="USW" /> and [[New Republic]] [[New Republic soldier|soldiers]],<ref name="Battlefront II" /> as part of the [[A-series]]. There existed two variants of the A280 blaster, the [[A280C blaster rifle|A280C]] and the [[A280-CFE convertible heavy blaster pistol|A280-CFE]], both of which were favored by [[Rebel Commando|commandos]] and [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces|special forces]] of the Rebel Alliance.

==Description==
[[File:A280BlasterRifle-EaW.png|thumb|left|250px|A Rebel soldier armed with an A280 blaster rifle.]]
The reliable A280<ref name="GEG">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref> was highly effective in piercing through [[armor]] and provided more power than other blaster rifles at long range. It featured a power charge system and integrated muzzle compensator.<ref name="USW" /> The [[A280-CFE convertible heavy blaster pistol|A280-CFE]] (covert field edition) was a modular version of the A280 that featured a core [[Blaster pistol|pistol]] that could be reconfigured into an [[assault rifle]] or [[sniper rifle]].<ref name="Launch Bay">{{YouTube|video=LWS1elNXu54|text=NEW Star Wars Launch Bay Rogue One Additions & Changes Update|channel=@TheDaniDiaries|name=The Dani Diaries}} {{C|at 6m35s}}</ref>

==History==
===Republic Era===
During the [[Galactic Republic]]'s final [[Standard year|years]], the A280 blaster rifles were commonly used by local [[planet]]ary [[Planetary defense forces|defense forces]] which made them readily available on the [[black market]], thus the Rebel Alliance acquired them with ease,<ref name="USW" /> and began to utilize it throughout their [[Galactic Civil War|fight]] against the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="TESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> who occasionally fought with them as well.<ref name="ROTJ" />

===Galactic Civil War===
During the [[Battle of Hoth]], many of the rebel troopers were armed with A280 blaster rifles<ref name="GEG" /> to hold off the incoming Imperial attackers until the [[GR-75 medium transport|evacuation transports]] fled away.<ref name="TESB" /> The [[bounty hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] used a customized A280 blaster rifle known as the [[Firestorm Rifle]],<ref name="Website">{{Hunters|url=characters/imara-vex|text=Imara Vex}}</ref> which she could even use as a [[sniper rifle]]. It possessed a black and red coloration with an orange design.<ref name="Crimson Reign 1" /> [[Major]] [[Lokmarcha]] used an A280 during [[Operation Yellow Moon]].<ref name="MovingTarget" /> The [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star's]] [[Overbridge (Death Star II)|overbridge]] had two A280s mounted onto one of the walls.<ref name="ROTJ" />

[[File:Capture_of_the_Glaciate.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Rebel troops equipped with A280s during the Battle of Jakku]]
The rifles were also heavily used by the Alliance in the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="GEG" /> where the Rebellion launched an all-out-assault on the second Death Star to destroy it and kill [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. In the ground phase of the battle, where the A280 saw use, members of the [[Special Forces Trackers Unit]] landed on Endor with the task of infiltrating and destroying the [[Death Star's shield generator]], thereby allowing the [[Rebel Alliance Navy]] to attack the Death Star itself.<ref name="ROTJ" /> Rebel soldiers like [[Musmuris Reetgeet]] wielded A280s when they captured the [[Immobilizer 418 cruiser]] ''[[Glaciate]]'' during the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref>

===Legacy===
The [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] later mentioned the A280 in his book ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]'' and stated it had been a common sight among the rebels on [[Hoth]] and Endor.<ref name="GEG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The A280 blaster rifle first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], a [[1980]] film as a weapon prop used by the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="TESB" /> Although the name was never given on-screen, the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'' published in [[April 28]], [[2015]] confirmed "A280 blaster rifle" as a [[canon]] name for the weapon.<ref name="USW" /> In ''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]'', it is referred to as a A-280. Parts of the A280 resemble the real life AR-15, specifically the receiver.

In ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'', the A280 uses the StG 44 model previously associated with the [[A280C blaster rifle]] and fires a three-shot burst, while the A280C uses the AR-15 model previously associated with the A280.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
