'''''Backstories: Luke Skywalker''''' was a canceled young readers reference book by [[Jason Fry]] which was to be published by [[Scholastic]]. It was never released after the previous book in the series, ''[[Princess Leia: Royal Rebel]]'', did not meet sales expectations in [[2016]].<ref name="Jason Fry post">{{JCFCite|url=posts/56622832|board=Literature|thread=Lostworlds & Lost Words: Cancelled Media|date=2020-05-01|time=12:35|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20200802152826/https://boards.theforce.net/threads/lostworlds-lost-words-cancelled-media.50053742|quote=Now that my memory's working a little better, my two Lostworlds. (Maybe there were others. The memory fades.) I wrote a Backstories: Luke Skywalker for Scholastic. The book was completely done (selfish aside: I got paid) but the previous titles in the series hadn't sold, so into the Maybe Not pile it went.|archivefile=File:JasonFryLostworldspost.png}}</ref>
