The '''E-XD-series infiltrator droid''', also known more simply as '''E-XD infiltrator droid''', was a model of [[Probe droid|recon droid]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[EXD-9]] was an Imperial infiltrator droid who infiltrated the [[Phoenix Cell]]'s [[Chopper Base]] on [[Atollon]].

==Description==
{{Quote|It looks like an older model of some type of protocol droid.|AP-5|<ref name="Warhead" />}}
[[File:E-XD_schematics.jpg|thumb|left|250px|E-XD infiltrator droid schematics]]
E-XD infiltrator droids were specialized recon droids designed to search for and infiltrate [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] bases. To facilitate this they possessed two modes- a "protocol" mode that outwardly resembled a [[RQ protocol droid|RQ series]] [[protocol droid]] and an attack mode, which extended its limbs, torso, and head, revealing built-in [[blaster]]s and a powerful pincer style grip. 

When in protocol mode, their [[sensor]]s were white. In attack mode, their sensors turned red and the droids were also capable of elevating their height. Infiltrator droids were capable of weathering extensive damage in combat, possessed considerable strength and could leap great heights. They were also capable of rapidly scanning various objects and lifeforms and identifying them, enabling at least [[EXD-9|one]] undercover unit to play the role of an inventory droid. 

Infiltrator droids could also cannibalize other droids for parts and drain their power to repair itself. These droids were programmed to pilot tactical infiltration pods and to operate [[transmitter]]s.<ref name="Warhead" /> Breaking Imperial protocol, the droids were fitted with a [[proton warhead]] that would [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destruct]] if the unit was compromised.<ref name="Rebels Recon #14">{{RebelsRecon|EfCuHRbbTZk|#3.14: Inside "Warhead" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-warhead}}</ref>

==History==
<div style="float:right">[[File:EXD-9_lands_on_Atollon.jpg|thumb|250px|EXD-9 lands on Atollon]]</div>
During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] commissioned the creation of E-XD infiltrator droids to scour the [[Outer Rim Territories]] for rebel bases. In [[2 BBY]], the Empire dispatched many infiltrator droids in [[X3-13|tactical infiltration pods]] to hunt for the [[Phoenix Cell]]'s [[Chopper Base|base]]. One of these units, [[EXD-9]], under the guise of an [[RQ protocol droid]], [[EXD-9's infiltration of Chopper Base|discovered]] [[Chopper Base]] on the planet [[Atollon]]. 

After being attacked by [[krykna]] spiders, he was adopted by [[Garazeb Orrelios]] and [[AP-5]], who believed that he would make a good inventory droid thanks to his rapid scanning functionality. However, [[Alexsandr Kallus]], an [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[agent]] who was serving as a rebel operative under the callsign [[Fulcrum]], alerted Zeb to the presence of the droids. Following a struggle, Zeb, AP-5, and the [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] overpowered a transformed EXD-9 temporarily disabled him. Unfortunately, this activated the droid's failsafe.

Unable to reset or remove the warhead, Zeb had AP-5 reprogram him into a improvised bomb- setting the droid's programming to return to its home base and then resume its countdown the moment it was tied into the Imperial network, hopefully destroying the droid and anything around it before it could reveal the exact location of the base. Though AP-5 was less than certain he had been successful in reprogramming, the plan worked, destroying an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] and all the other infiltrator droids aboard.

Despite the apparent failure of the effort, Thrawn was pleased with the results as he now knew that the Rebel base was on one of the planets that had been surveyed by the infiltration program. Though the exact location was not revealed, this narrowed his search considerably.<ref name="Warhead" />

After [[Mon Mothma]]'s rebellious speech against [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], E-XD units were spread across the [[Star system|system]] in tactical infiltration pods in an effort to locate her. One of these units was searching a destroyed [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[warship]], where members of the waiting ''[[Ghost]]'' crew destroyed it.<ref name="SC">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The E-XD infiltrator droid first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Warhead (episode)|Warhead]]," which first premiered on [[Disney XD]] on [[January 14]], [[2017]]. The design of the droid is based on an original design drawing for [[C-3PO]] by [[Ralph McQuarrie]].<ref name="Rebels Recon #14" /> The droid's explosive countdown is a nod to the [[Wikipedia:Predator (fictional species)|titular character's]] explosive countdown in the [[1987]] film ''[[Wikipedia:Predator (film)|Predator]]''.<ref name="Trivia Guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/warhead-trivia-gallery|text="Warhead" Trivia Gallery}}</ref>
