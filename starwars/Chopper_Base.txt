'''Chopper Base''', also known as '''Home Base''' was a base located on the isolated [[planet]] [[Atollon]]. After an extensive search for a suitable planet to house their base, [[Phoenix Cell]] at first decided that the [[Berzite's moon|moon]] of [[Berzite]] was a suitable location. However, thanks to the rebel [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P]] ("Chopper") and his recent acquaintance [[AP-5]], the rebels learned that the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] had recently set up a [[blockade]] around Berzite. AP-5 instead recommended the planet Atollon, where the rebels then built the base named in Chopper's honor. Chopper Base served as the headquarters of the [[Phoenix Squadron]] before being destroyed by [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] forces and the [[Bendu]] during the [[Battle of Atollon]] in [[2 BBY]].<ref name="Atollon Date">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'' places the events of "Zero Hour" to 17 [[Imperial calendar|AFE]], seventeen [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], which occurred in [[19 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, "Zero Hour" takes place in [[2 BBY]].</ref>

==Description==
[[File:Unlimited-ChopperBase.png|thumb|left|300px|Starships docked within Chopper Base]]
Chopper Base was built on the [[Atollon Coral Mesa]], a broad flat-topped elevation covered in huge [[coral]]line formations in [[3 BBY]] with the salvaged and looted parts of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Class four container transport]], [[Imperial Cargo Transport 241|Cargo transport 241]].<ref name="Dawn" /> The northern perimeter was near a nest inhabited by [[krykna]], large spidery creatures that devoured sentient beings. To ward off the krykna, a perimeter fence consisting of [[sensor beacon]]s was constructed in the northern perimeter. The base hosted runways for landing ships<ref name="Mystery of Chopper Base" /> and a command center that was equipped with [[hologram]]s for receiving and sending offworld transmissions.<ref name="Antilles Extraction">{{Rebels|The Antilles Extraction}}</ref> The base also held a supply depot that had their rations, explosives, and cargo. Most of the base's inhabitants appeared to reside in rebel ships like the [[Spectres]]' [[VCX-100 light freighter]] ''[[Ghost]]''.<ref name="Mystery of Chopper Base" />

==History==
[[File:Atollon_Coral_Mesa.png|thumb|right|250px|Chopper Base was built on the Atollon coral mesa.]]
In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Dawn" /> the [[Phoenix Cell]] discovered the uncharted planet of [[Atollon]] after receiving coordinates for a safe world from [[AP-5]], a former Imperial [[inventory droid]] that the rebel [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] had befriended.<ref name="The Forgotten Droid">{{Rebels|The Forgotten Droid}}</ref> To honor Chopper for his part in discovering Atollon, the base was named Chopper Base. After the krykna devoured [[Lieutenant]] [[Dicer]] and attacked the Spectres and [[Captain]] [[Rex]], the rebels constructed a fence consisting of sensor markers around Chopper Base's northern perimeter.<ref name="Mystery of Chopper Base" /> Rex was placed in charge of overseeing the base's operations.<ref name="Clone Tales">{{InsiderCite|187|Clone Tales}}</ref>

The former [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] rendezvoused with [[Kanan Jarrus]], his apprentice [[Ezra Bridger]], and Chopper for a [[Mission to Malachor|mission]] to [[Malachor]].<ref name="Mystery of Chopper Base" /> Tano was lost during the mission, but Jarrus, Bridger, and Chopper managed to return to Chopper Base in the shuttle ''[[Phantom]]''. There, they were received by [[Hera Syndulla]], the other crew of the ''Ghost'', Rex, AP-5, and the [[Phoenix Cell]]'s [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]].<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref>

A [[2 BBY|year later]], Chopper Base's command center hosted a visitor in the form of [[Hondo Ohnaka]], a [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] whom Bridger had [[Rescue of Hondo Ohnaka|rescued]] from the [[Naraka prison|Imperial prison]] on [[Naraka]]. During the briefing, Ohnaka informed the Spectres, Rex, and Commander Sato about an Imperial starship reclamation plant called [[Reklam Station]] in return for the rebels giving him a [[starship]] after the [[Mission to Reklam Station|recon mission]]. After Bridger's recon mission went awry, Jarrus, Syndulla, and the rest of Phoenix Squadron departed to rescue Bridger and his team.<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>

Later, the Spectres and Commander Sato attended a briefing in Chopper Base's command center where the rebels received a transmission from a [[Fulcrum]] informant that several cadets from the Imperial [[Skystrike Academy]] were wanting to defect. The [[Mandalorian]] weapons expert [[Sabine Wren]] was placed in charge of the [[Escape from Skystrike Academy|mission]]. After escaping with the cadets [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Derek Klivian]], Wren and the cadets were greeted by Commander Sato at Chopper Base's runway. He personally welcomed Antilles and Klivian into the Rebellion.<ref name="Antilles Extraction" />

During a [[Second mission to the third moon of Concord Dawn|recon mission]] to the [[Third moon of Concord Dawn|third moon]] of [[Concord Dawn]], Wren and her former adversary [[Fenn Rau]] worked together to prevent [[Gar Saxon]], the leader of the [[Imperial Super Commando]]s from taking the shuttle ''[[Phantom II]]'' and discovering the location of Chopper Base.<ref name="Imperial Super Commandos">{{Rebels|Imperial Supercommandos}}</ref> Following a successful mission to evacuate rebel sympathizers on [[Mykapo]], Commander Sato had an emotional reunion with his nephew [[Mart Mattin]], the leader of the [[Iron Squadron]], on Chopper Base's runway.<ref name="Iron Squadron">{{Rebels|Iron Squadron (episode)}}</ref>

During Bridger's [[Infiltration of the Imperial Armory Complex|undercover]] mission at the [[Lothal]] [[Imperial Armory Complex]], Syndulla, Wren, [[Garazeb Orrelios]], and Commander Sato corresponded with Bridger, Jarrus, and the [[Lothal]]ian rebel leader [[Ryder Azadi]] via hologram from the safety of the Chopper Base command center. As a result of Bridger and his team's mission, Phoenix Squadron learned that [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] was developing a new [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter|starfighter initiative]] and that the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] had become a Fulcrum operative.<ref name="An Inside Man">{{Rebels|An Inside Man}}</ref>

Using Bridger and his team's information, Syndulla began planning a strike on the Lothal Imperial factory. During a briefing in the Chopper Base command room, Bridger began experiencing [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] visions of the former [[Sith]] [[Maul]], whom he had previously encountered at [[Vizsla Keep 09]]. Despite insisting that he was fit, Syndulla was forced to relieve Bridger of his duties after the young Jedi almost killed a rebel trooper, whom he had mistaken for Maul. On the advice of Jarrus, Syndulla, and the other rebels proceeded with the mission while Bridger, Jarrus, and Wren stayed behind to confront Maul.<ref name="Visions and Voices">{{Rebels|Visions and Voices}}</ref>

Bridger agreed to follow Maul to the planet [[Dathomir]] after the latter threatened to activate a [[homing beacon]] that would alert the Empire to Chopper Base should he die. Wren managed to locate Bridger using a tracker she had planted on his [[wrist comm]]. Jarrus and Wren followed Bridger on the shuttle ''Phantom II''.<ref name="Visions and Voices" />

[[File:ChopperBasePort.png|thumb|left|250px|Starships based in Chopper Base.]]
Later, most of the Phoenix rebels and Spectres departed for a training exercise. Captain Orrelios, Chopper, AP-5, a communications officer, and some Rebel troops stayed behind to guard the base. During that time, an [[E-XD-series infiltrator droid]] designated [[EXD-9]] discovered the base but was knocked out by two krykna. Later, Orrelios and Chopper found him and brought him back to the base to be repaired. After repairing him, AP-5 set EXD-9 to work as an assistant inventory droid. After receiving a warning from Fulcrum about the infiltrator droid, Orrelios along with Chopper and AP-5 joined forces to [[EXD-9's infiltration of Chopper Base|overpower]] EXD-9 before it could alert the Empire. After reprogramming the droid, they sent it back as a rigged bomb to the Empire. EXD-9 destroyed an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] and managed to delay the Empire's efforts to locate Chopper Base.<ref name="Warhead">{{Rebels|Warhead (episode)}}</ref>

After Wren reluctantly agreed to learn how to wield the [[Darksaber]] in order to lead the [[Mandalorian]] people, Jarrus and Bridger took her to the wilderness to begin her training. Jarrus regarded Chopper Base as providing too many unnecessary distractions. Syndulla stayed behind at Chopper Base and corresponded with Jarrus via hologram. Later, Rau departed Chopper Base on a [[speeder bike]] to ferry supplies to the other rebels.<ref name="Trials of the Darksaber">{{Rebels|Trials of the Darksaber}}</ref>

Chopper Base's location was almost exposed on two occasions. With the help of the rogue [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]], Chopper managed to delete the base's location from Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]'s star chart.<ref name="TIE">{{Rebels|Through Imperial Eyes}}</ref> Later, a team of Imperial [[slicer]]s reprogrammed Chopper with the intention of finding the location of Chopper Base. Their plot was foiled by Syndulla and the Spectres following a [[Mission to recover Imperial codes|mission]] to [[Killun Station]].<ref name="DAD">{{Rebels|Double Agent Droid}}</ref> The former Imperial [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]]'s speech announcing the formation of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] was witnessed by several Phoenix rebels including Commander Sato, Rex, and AP-5 at Chopper Base.<ref name="Secret Cargo" />

After receiving messages from Jarrus' [[Kanan Jarrus' holocron|Jedi holocron]] and the [[Sith holocron (Malachor)|Sith holocron]] from [[Malachor]] indicating that [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was in danger, Bridger and Chopper managed to sneak off Chopper Base in a stolen [[RZ-1T trainer]]. Syndulla had forbade Bridger from leaving since she needed him to train for the upcoming [[attack on Lothal]]. They later returned to Chopper Base after Kenobi defeated Maul in combat and sent them back to help the Rebellion.<ref name="Twin Suns">{{Rebels|Twin Suns (episode)}}</ref> Despite the rebels' efforts to keep the base hidden, Thrawn discovered Chopper Base and send his [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]] to [[Battle of Atollon|attack]] the [[Atollon system]]. Thrawn's forces inflicted heavy casualties on the Phoenix Squadron and [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]]'s [[Massassi Group]], which had been massing for an attack on the Imperial [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter|TIE Defender]] factories on Lothal. Chopper Base was also destroyed by Imperial bombardment and the [[Bendu]], who summoned a storm that terrorized both sides.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>
