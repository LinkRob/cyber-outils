'''Darren''' was a [[human]] name. It could refer to:

==Legends==
*'''[[Darren]]''', [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]] [[Pilot/Legends|pilot]] and son of [[Korren Starchaser]]
*'''[[Darren Rakal]]''', [[Sergeant/Legends|sergeant]] in the [[Republic Army]] who served on [[Oricon]] during the [[Dread War]]
*'''[[Derran Takkar]]''', [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Moff/Legends|Moff]] who sought to sabotage the [[Marriage/Legends|wedding]] of [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and [[Mara Jade Skywalker|Mara Jade]]
*'''[[Darren T. Weeb]]''', [[Smuggler/Legends|smuggler]] and [[Captain/Legends|captain]] of the [[Freighter/Legends|freighter]] [[Arcadia (starship)|''Arcadia'']] during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]

==Real world==
*'''[[Darren Allanson]]''', author for the ''[[The Official Star Wars Fact File]]''
*'''[[Darren Gilford]]''', production designer for ''[[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens]]''
*'''[[Darren Hedges]]''', manager of international production for ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''
*'''[[Darren Johnson]]''', video game designer who worked on many [[LucasArts]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' games
*'''[[Darren Marshall]]''', sculptor and design artist who designed characters for ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|The Clone Wars]]''
*'''[[Darren Morfitt]]''', actor who portrayed a [[transport]] [[deck officer]] in ''[[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi]]''
*'''[[Darren Scott]]''', voice actor who portrayed [[Rhys Dallows]] in [[Star Wars: Starfighter (video game)|''Star Wars: Starfighter'']] and ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Starfighter]]''
*'''[[Darren Tan]]''', artist who provided artwork for the several ''[[Star Wars Galaxies Trading Card Game]]'' sets
*'''[[Darren White]]''', voice actor who supplied voices for additional characters in ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]''
