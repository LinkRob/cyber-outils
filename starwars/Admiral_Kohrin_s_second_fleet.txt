This '''fleet''' was the second [[Fleet/Legends|fleet]] of [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Admiral/Legends|Admiral]] [[Kohrin]]. Its [[Flagship/Legends|flagship]] was the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought/Legends|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] [[Enforcer (Executor-class)|''Enforcer'']]. The fleet was [[First attack on the Enforcer|ambushed]] by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] forces near [[Bespin/Legends|Bespin]], resulting in the ''Enforcer'' being severely damaged. As the ''Enforcer'' underwent repairs above [[Phaylenn]], the rest of Kohrin's fleet joined other Imperial [[Starship/Legends|ships]] in wiping out the Rebel presence in the [[Ahlenn system]].
