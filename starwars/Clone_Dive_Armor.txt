'''Clone Dive Armor''', also known as the '''Clone Dive Suit''', was a type of [[clone trooper armor]] that was worn by the [[clone SCUBA trooper]]s of the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

==Description==
As [[clone trooper]]s were as ill-suited for aquatic environments as any other [[human]], they were required to wear the [[Cloning|Clone]] Dive [[Armor]] to protect them from the [[ocean]]'s pressure levels on [[planet]]s such as [[Mon Cala]], while it also provided [[oxygen]] to breathe and assisted in their aquatic maneuverability. And by necessity, the Clone Dive Armor were constructed from light-weight materials to ensure that the wearer retained a certain degree of buoyancy. They were also equipped with an underwater propulsion system, fins, and sensors for better visibility.<ref name="Collapse" />

[[File:MonnkBeforeDive.png|thumb|left|300px|Commander [[Monnk]] in his Dive Armor]]
Clone Dive Armor could provide a twelve-[[Standard hour|hour]] supply of oxygen, and during that [[time]], the wearer also had a supply of fresh [[water]], nutrition supplements, and other biological necessities.<ref name="Collapse" /> [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I]] Dive Armor featured a black helmet.<ref name="Before the Dark">{{Acme|url=products/before-the-dark-times-james-silvani|text=Before the Dark Times}}</ref> By the period of [[Phase II clone trooper armor]], Clone Dive Armor was an all-white armor set that could be customized with different color markings by the trooper who wore it. Unlike contemporary Phase II gear, the helmet also featured a fin<ref name="Water War" /> like standard Phase I armor.<ref name="ATOC">{{Film|II}}</ref>
