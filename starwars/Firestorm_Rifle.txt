The '''Firestorm Rifle'''<ref name="SWH" /> was a customized [[A280 blaster rifle]]<ref name="Website" /> utilized by the [[human]] [[bounty hunter]] and competitive [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]],<ref name="SWH" /> both during her time as a member of the [[Orphans]],<ref name="Crimson Reign 1" /> as well as for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="SWH" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Within the mobile video game ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', the Firestorm Rifle was a "Base" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Imara Vex's blaster rifle, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added when the game's early access went live via update 0.11.0 on [[November 16]], [[2021]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[bounty hunter]]. This wrap was unlocked by default when Imara Vex became playable.<ref name="SWH" />
