'''Foris alloy''' was an alloy of a [[metal]] [[Mining|mined]] on a [[Rilias II's moon|moon]] of the astronomical object [[Rilias II]]. It allowed for an exact resonance with [[mineral]]s in [[perma-scatter cloud]]s, a type of [[cloud]] found on Rilias II. A funnel made of foris alloy was located in Rilias II's [[central water garden of Rilias II|central water garden]]. At sunrise and sunset, light filtered through perma-scatter clouds and hit the funnel, which constantly gathered thin trickles of [[water]] from the [[garden]]. The reverberation of the funnel then caused particles in the [[Atmosphere|air]] to vibrate into small rainbow stars, and also amplified certain audio frequencies.<ref name="TOTF" /> 

In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> when Rilias II was under the control of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]], the anti-Nihil operative [[Cair San Tekka]] took advantage of the interaction between foris alloy and the minerals in perma-scatter clouds to amplify a message recorded by [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]], which he broadcasted from within the funnel.<ref name="TOTF" />
