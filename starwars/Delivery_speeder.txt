A '''delivery speeder''' was a [[speeder]] used to deliver [[Foodstuff|food]] such as [[Corellian buckwheat noodles]]. The dining establishment [[Sakhet's Noodles]], owned by the [[Kupohan]] [[Sakhet]], located on the [[planet]] [[Denon]], utilized such a speeder when making its deliveries, which might be made at any [[time]] of the [[Standard day|day]].<ref name="HttJ" />
