'''Dresh Lipson''' was a [[Human]] [[Gender|male]] [[pilot]] and [[smuggler]]. He was an employee of the rich [[Tibanna]] gas tycoon [[Jai Cirri]]. He liked to [[Gambling|gamble]] and had romantic feelings for [[Jailyn Cirri]], Jai's daughter. When the [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] occupied [[Cloud City]], Lipson asked Jailyn escape with him from [[Bespin]].<ref name="FACPOV" />
