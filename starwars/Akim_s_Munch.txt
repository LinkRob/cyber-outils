'''Akim's Munch''' was a street café located in [[Mos Espa]], on the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Tatooine]]. The renowned [[Podracer (profession)|Podracer]] [[Sebulba]] regularly visited the café, where his fans could see him and pay him their respects.<ref name="SWCL" />
