The '''Afe clan''' was a clan of [[Cyphari]] on the [[planet]] of [[Cyphar]] that possessed territory near a [[human]] colony and a [[Unidentified Twi'lek settlement|Twi'lek settlement]] and was led by [[Chief]] [[Joko (Cyphari)|Joko]]. Over [[time]] however, the human [[colonist]]s began trespassing into Afe territory after the alleged discovery of [[mineral]]s, and the clan and colonists engaged in a [[Cyphar Incident|land dispute]]. Eventually the [[Imperial Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] were sent to aid the Afe clan and end the conflict. [[Commander]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] found the source of the dispute: a vein of [[Scarn (spice)|pre spice]] in Afe croplands that several of the colonists were eager to obtain. Thrawn ended the dispute by destroying the pre spice.<ref name="Thrawn" />
