"'''Commando Mission'''" is a short story written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and originally published in the ''[[Heir to the Empire Sourcebook]]''. It was later reprinted in ''[[The Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook]]''. Occurring briefly after the entries on [[Lieutenant/Legends|Lieutenant]] [[Judder Page/Legends|Page]] and the [[Katarn Commandos]], it follows a brief mission undertaken by Page and five of his [[Commando/Legends|commandos]] on the planet [[Hettsk]].

==Plot summary==
Lieutenant Page, [[Frorral/Legends|Frorral]], [[Idow]], [[Vandro]], [[Lilla Dade]], and [[Jortan]] are on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Hettsk]] following a [[Battle of Hettsk|battle]] on the [[Trans-Hydian/Legends|Borderland Regions]] world between the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] that resulted in an Imperial victory. Their mission is to retrieve [[Wing Commander/Legends|Wing Commander]] [[Greni]] and her [[Wingmate/Legends|wingmate]] [[Bross]], two members of [[Gold Squadron (New Republic)|Gold Squadron]] who were shot down during the battle. A group of Imperial [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] are bringing the downed pilots back to base, but make camp in the planet's dense forests at sunset. Page approaches their [[HAVr A9 Floating Fortress/Legends|HAVr A9 Floating Fortress]] and feigns surrender, and is then joined by his commandos in quickly disposing of the Imperials and rescuing the pilots.
