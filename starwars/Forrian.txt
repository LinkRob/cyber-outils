'''Forrians''' were small, [[Hair|furry]] [[mammal]]s that lived on the [[Planet|world]] [[Batuu]]. They harvested dried [[Berry|berries]] from the [[forest]] floor and had [[claw]]s that they used to grip [[tree]] [[Wood|bark]].<ref name="SotJ" /> Around [[379 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Shield of the Jedi]]" establishes that [[Rooper Nitani]] is around eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old during its events. In ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of to [[382 BBY]], Nitani is fifteen years old. Therefore, "Shield of the Jedi" must take place around [[379 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] encountered forrians when she climbed into the canopy of a forest on Batuu while searching for a [[Silandra Sho's shield|shield]] belonging to her [[Jedi Master|Master]], [[Silandra Sho]], who had tasked her with finding the [[Plasma shield|shield]] as part of her [[Rooper Nitani's Jedi Trials|Jedi Trials]]. Nitani's presence caused the forrians to become nervous, so she attempted to give off calming vibes in order to avoid the forrians attacking her with their claws.<ref name="SotJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Forrians appeared in the short story "[[Shield of the Jedi]]," written by [[George Mann]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="SotJ" />
