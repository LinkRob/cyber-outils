'''Aaron Giles''' is credited for additional UI programming on ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]''. He have also worked on several other [[LucasArts]] titles as a programmer, such as ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Mysteries of the Sith]]'', ''[[Star Wars: DroidWorks]]'', [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (video game)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'']], ''[[Star Wars: Rebel Assault II: The Hidden Empire]]'', [[Wikipedia:Afterlife (computer game)|''Afterlife'']], [[Wikipedia:Outlaws (1997 video game)|''Outlaws'']], [[Wikipedia:Indiana Jones and the Fate of Atlantis|''Indiana Jones and the Fate of Atlantis'']], [[Wikipedia:Indiana Jones and His Desktop Adventures|''Indiana Jones and His Desktop Adventures'']], [[Wikipedia:Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade: The Graphic Adventure|''Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade: The Graphic Adventure'']], [[Wikipedia:The Dig|''The Dig'']], [[Wikipedia:Loom (video game)|''Loom'']], [[Wikipedia:The Curse of Monkey Island|''The Curse of Monkey Island'']], ''[[Wikipedia:Maniac Mansion|Maniac Mansion]]' and [[Wikipedia:Full Throttle (1995 video game)|Full Throttle]]''.
