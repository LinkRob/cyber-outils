'''Bellis Da'lalo''' was a [[Musician|singer]] who was rumored to be coming out of retirement to perform for [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] as the headliner at the [[Standard year|annual]] [[Empire Day]] [[parade]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The unconfirmed rumor was covered on a broadcast of the [[HoloNet News]] by [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Dorna Parros]] while preparations for the event were well underway.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bellis Da'lalo was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
