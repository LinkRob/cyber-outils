'''Dismemberment''' was the loss of one or more [[limb]]s, such as the hand, foot, arm, or leg. Limbs could be lost intentionally, in battle, or accidentally. It was relatively common for such an injury to be inflicted during [[lightsaber combat]], as [[lightsaber]]s could cut through almost any substance. Individuals who had lost limbs were usually outfitted with [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] prosthetics.

The term was also used in reference to severing the limbs of [[droid]]s, or limbs that were already prosthetic.

==History==
{{Quote|It is only natural. He cut off your arm, you wanted revenge.|Sheev Palpatine, to Anakin Skywalker, referring to the latter's execution of Dooku|<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>}}
[[High Republic Era|High Republic]] Jedi [[Santari Khri]] cut off [[Dagan Gera]]'s right arm, when he attempted to take a [[Abyss compass]].<ref name="JS">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

During the [[Battle of Naboo]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] severed [[Darth]] [[Maul]]'s torso from his waist to avenge the [[death]] of his [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] at Maul's hands.<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref>

[[Bounty hunter|Bounty Hunter]] [[Zam Wesell]] had her right arm cut of by Obi-Wan after [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|trying]] to kill him.<ref name="Episode II" />

[[File:TisButAScratch-AotC.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Anakin Skywalker's right forearm was severed by Darth Tyranus during the First Battle of Geonosis.]]
During the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], [[Jedi Knight]] Obi-Wan Kenobi cut off the two front lower legs of a [[Acklay]] before killing it. [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] and [[clone trooper]] [[Clone template|template]] [[Jango Fett]] joined the battle to kill [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]]. Windu was able to cut his head off, killing the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[mercenary]]. Later, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]] severed Obi-Wan's Padawan [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s right forearm in a [[lightsaber duel]].<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>

During the [[Mission to Grievous's castle|Mission to Vassek 3]], [[Gor]]'s tail got cut of by Padawan [[Nahdar Vebb]] and his hands by Jedi Master [[Kit Fisto]], before being killed.<ref name="Lair of Grievous">{{TCW|Lair of Grievous}}</ref>

During the [[Battle of Saleucami]], [[Tactical droid]] [[TV-94]] attempted to kill Jedi Master [[Eeth Koth]], but had his right arm cut off and was destroyed by Anakin Skywalker.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue">{{TCW|Grievous Intrigue}}</ref>

Bounty hunter [[Twazzi]] had her right arm cut of by Anakin during the [[Plan to kidnap Chancellor Palpatine|kidnaping]] of Palpatine.<ref name="Crisis on Naboo">{{TCW|Crisis on Naboo}}</ref>

[[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC Trooper]] [[Echo]] lost his right forearm and both of his legs after getting caught in an explosion and captured by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] during the [[Battle of Lola Sayu]].<ref name="Counterattack">{{TCW|Counterattack}}</ref><ref name="A Distant Echo">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref>

[[Savage Opress]] lost his left arm to Obi-Wan and Maul's left lower leg to [[Jiro]] during a [[Skirmish on Florrum|skirmish]].<ref name="Revival">{{TCW|Revival (episode)}}</ref>

[[General]] [[Grievous]] ripped off the left arm of a [[Nightsister Zombie]], during the [[Battle of Dathomir]].<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref>

During the [[Battle of Anaxes]], Anakin cut of [[Admiral]] [[Trench]]'s left arms for the sequence to disarm the bomb in the [[Anaxes assembly complex]].<ref name="Unfinished Business">{{TCW|Unfinished Business (episode)}}</ref>

Three years after the First Battle of Geonosis, Skywalker exacted revenge by cutting off both Tyranus' hands with [[Skywalker lightsaber|his lightsaber]] during the [[Battle of Coruscant]]. Later decapitating him with [[Dooku's lightsaber|Tyranus' Lightsaber]] under the insistence of [[Darth Sidious]], under the alias of Chancellor Palpatine.<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:Grievousseveredhand.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Grievous's hand were severed during his duel with Kenobi on Utapau.]]
Grievous suffered multiple dismemberments during the [[Clone Wars]]. During the [[Raid on Skytop Station|Mission to Skytop Station]], Padawan [[Ahsoka Tano]] cut off his right hand while distracted to escape.<ref name="Duel of the Droids">{{TCW|Duel of the Droids}}</ref> During the [[Mission to Grievous's castle|Mission to Vassek 3]], Jedi Master Kit Fisto cut off his legs, and later his upper left hand.<ref name="Lair of Grievous" /> During the [[Battle of Dathomir]], [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Asajj Ventress]] cut off his upper right arm.<ref name="Massacre" /> During the [[Mission to Raxus Secundus]], [[Quinlan Vos]] cut off the set of his left hands.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> During the [[Third Battle of Mygeeto]], [[Depa Billaba]] cut off his lower hands,<ref name="Kanan 11">[[Kanan 11|''Kanan'' 11]]</ref> and in the [[Standard day|final days]] of the Clone Wars, during the [[Battle of Utapau]], Obi-Wan Kenobi cut off his lower hands.<ref name="Episode III" />

Following the issuing of [[Order 66]], Maul, now a renegade [[Sith]], engaged with multiple [[clone trooper]]s during a [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|skirmish]] aboard the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> ''[[Tribunal]]'',<ref name="TCW Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars The Clone Wars Character Encyclopedia: Join the battle!]]''</ref> having been a [[prisoner]] onboard the ship before Ahsoka Tano released him as a diversion for the brainwashed [[Cloning|clones]]. Encountering a squad in one hallway, Maul drove them back using the Force before [[Telekinesis|telekinetically]] pulling [[Unidentified clone trooper (332nd Company)|one trooper's]] right forearm through a closing [[blast door]], with the arm being severed when the door closed.<ref name="Shattered" /> This allowed Maul to steal the trooper's [[Comlink|wrist-comm]] and eavesdrop on the clones' transmissions.<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref>

After turning to the [[dark side of the Force]], Skywalker severed Mace Windu's right hand to save Darth Sidious when Windu tried to [[Duel in Palpatine's office|kill]] the latter.<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:VaderOperation-ROTS.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Vader lost his left arm and both lower legs when Obi-Wan Kenobi defeated him on Mustafar.]]
Skywalker later [[Duel on Mustafar|dueled]] his former Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi and lost both lower legs, as well as his left arm. The loss of all of his remaining limbs, [[Fire|burn]] injuries and subsequent reconstruction as a [[cyborg]] caused the fallen Jedi to all but fully embrace his new Sith identity as [[Darth]] Vader, Sith [[Lord]] and [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] Palpatine.<ref name="Episode III" />

As a [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], Vader oversaw the training of the [[Inquisitorius]]&mdash;a group of fallen Jedi who turned against the [[Jedi Order]] in the aftermath of [[Order 66]]. To teach his [[Jedi hunter]]s a lesson in the pain of loss, Vader dismembered the right hand of the [[Fifth Brother]] and the left forearm of the [[Sixth Brother]].<ref name="Dark Lord 7">[[Darth Vader (2017) 7|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 7]]</ref>

During the [[skirmish on Wayland]], Echo cut off the hand of an [[inventory droid]] and attached it onto his right arm under his [[TK stormtrooper armor|disguise as a TK stormtrooper]].<ref name="Flash Strike">{{TBB|Flash Strike}}</ref> [[Clone Commander]] [[Crosshair]] also had his right hand cut off by [[Clone X trooper]] [[CX-2]].<ref name="The Cavalry Has Arrived">{{TBB|The Cavalry Has Arrived}}</ref>

The [[Ninth Sister]] [[Skirmish in Bel City|lost]] her right lower leg to the [[Sixth Brother]],<ref name="Dark Lord 17">[[Darth Vader (2017) 17|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 17]]</ref> and her right [[Liberation on Kashyyyk|hand]] to [[Cal Kestis]].<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

The Jedi Knight [[Cal Kestis]] severed the limbs of many hostiles he encountered, including [[Second mission to Zeffo|cutting]] the arms off [[Jotaz]]s on [[Zeffo]] and the [[Nightsister Zombie|undead]] Nightsisters [[Mission to Dathomir (Cal Kestis)|on]] [[Dathomir]].<ref name="JFO" />

[[Greez Dritus]] [[Mission to Murkhana (Stinger Mantis)|lost]] his right upper forearm to the [[Fifth Brother]].<ref name="JBS">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]''</ref>

[[Chewbacca]] ripped the arms off a [[Pyke sentinel]] during the [[Raid on Kessel]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

After the [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]] threatened to cut of the hands of people in [[Anchorhead]] if they did not [[Search for Nari|turn]] in a [[Nari|Jedi]], she cut of the right hand of a [[Unidentified Anchorhead woman|Woman]] after she spoke out against the Empire.<ref name="Part I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref>

During Cal's [[Quest for Tanalorr]], he dismembered many hostiles including, [[Gorocco]]s, [[Mogu]]s, and [[Sutaban]]s.<ref name="JS" />

The [[Bedlam Raiders|Bedlam Raider]] leader [[Rayvis]] had his right forearm [[Mission to rescue ZN-A4|cut]] off by Cal, but was able to reattach it due to his [[Gen'Dai]] abilities. He then had both arms cut off, before being [[Second mission to the Shattered Moon|killed]] by Cal.<ref name="JS" />

The [[Eighth Brother]] threatened to cut off [[Ezra Bridger]]'s right hand and make him fall, if he didn't hand over a [[Sith holocron (Malachor)|Sith holocron]] during the [[Mission to Malachor]].<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref>

During the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Kenobi dismembered the [[Aqualish]] thug [[Ponda Baba]]'s right forearm in self-defense of [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

[[Hutt]] [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi]] had three lower legs of his twelve cybernetic legs cut [[Rescue of Luke Skywalker|off]] by [[Kreel]].<ref name="SW 12">[[Star Wars (2015) 12|''Star Wars'' (2015) 12]]</ref>

[[File:Lukenohandsma.png|thumb|left|250px|Luke Skywalker's right hand was severed in battle by his father, Darth Vader.]]
A [[Unidentified wampa|Wampa]] lost its right arm to Luke after attempting to eat him, later, Skywalker lost his right hand in a [[Duel on Cloud City|duel]] against his own father.<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref>

Skywalker fought Vader once more during the [[Battle of Endor]], and this [[time]] he emerged victorious after severing his father's right cybernetic hand.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

[[Krrsantan]] tore the left arm off a [[Unidentified Trandoshan gambler|Trandoshan]] in a [[Alcohol|drunken]] rage after watching him gamble.<ref name="GatheringStorm">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref>

In the [[New Republic Era|last]] [[Hosnian Cataclysm|day]] of the [[New Republic]], Chewbacca ripped off the right arm of the [[Crolute]] [[junk boss]] [[Unkar Plutt]] after he threatened the [[Jakku]] [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], the [[Wookiee]]'s newest friend.<ref name="awakensnovel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>

Sometime afterwards, when Rey was brought before [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] of the [[First Order]], the dark side [[Knights of Ren|warrior]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] took [[Battle of Oetchi|advantage]] of the situation to kill Snoke and usurp his [[throne]], cutting through Snoke's waist and wrists.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

==Cultural and species significance==
[[Wookiee]]s were known to pull people's arms out of their sockets when they lose.<ref name="Episode IV" />

[[Trandoshan]]s were able to regrow their limbs if they were severed.<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>

In some cultures in the [[the galaxy]], people were punished for theft by having a hand amputated.<ref name="Right-Hand">{{StoryCite|story=Right-Hand Man|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>

Although most species experienced cauterisation when a lightsaber amputated their limbs, Aqualish were known to bleed.<ref name="Episode IV" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Yet another in the series of ''Star Wars'' severed limbs. I've actually tried to talk to George [Lucas] about the whole reason for the severed limbs in these movies and he always just sort of smiles and chuckles and never gives me the real answer|Rob Coleman on the dismemberment gag of the saga films|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
So far, all ''[[Star Wars]]'' movies of the [[Star Wars saga|Skywalker Saga]] have featured dismemberment of either organic or [[Cybernetics|artificial]] limbs. In the commentary track of the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], [[Rob Coleman]] discussed that while he tried to talk with [[George Lucas]] about the reason of the gag, Lucas just smiled and chuckled at him without giving him the answer.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones">{{Film|II}} commentary track</ref>
