The '''freestanding subsectors''' was a term used to refer to unincorporated [[sector]]s and regions whose inclusion into the [[Galactic Republic]] or [[Galactic Empire]] was not yet ratified.<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|18|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> The [[Terrabe sector]], which notably housed the [[moon]] of [[Jedha]], was one of the freestanding subsectors.<ref name="RO World Locations" />
