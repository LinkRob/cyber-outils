The '''DSD1 Dwarf Spider Droid Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ61''') is an expansion to the tabletop game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. An updated version of the earlier [[DSD1 Dwarf Spider Droid Unit Expansion]], it was released on [[March 20]], [[2026]] as part of Atomic Mass Games' overhaul of the game.

==Publisher's summary==
''DSD1 Dwarf Spider Droids were favored by the Separatist Alliance for rugged durability and ease of manufacture. These four-legged, dome-shaped mechanical arachnids featured powerful, centrally mounted armaments capable of fulfilling a wide variety of battlefield roles.''

''The DSD1 Dwarf Spider Droid Unit Expansion includes 1 highly detailed miniature in hard plastic for 1 Support unit as well as unit and upgrade cards to support gameplay.''
