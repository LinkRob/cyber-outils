"'''Costume Theory'''" is an article by costume designer [[Michael Wilkinson]] for ''[[REBELIÓN: An Andor Zine]]'', focusing on the design concepts behind costumes featured in the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''. The piece explored the creative approach taken to develop specific looks for characters and cultures in the series, highlighting practical and thematic considerations behind costume choices. Among its main topics were: the [[Ghorman]] trench coat, [[Luthen Rael]]'s two contrasting personas ("Gallery Luthen" and "Natural Luthen"), and [[Chandrila]]n wedding costumes. Costume sketches featured in the article were illustrated by [[Andrei Riabovitchev]].<ref name="AndorS2Zine" />
