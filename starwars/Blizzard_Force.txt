'''Blizzard Force''', also designated as the '''Blizzard Unit''' was an elite [[Tank|armored]] unit within the [[501st Legion|501st Imperial Legion]] of the [[Imperial Army]] that specialized in cold-weather operations. It utilized multiple [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT]] [[walker]]s in which dozens of [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]] could be stationed inside of along with numerous armaments. The force itself was led by the ruthless [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]],<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> who had personally selected every [[AT-AT pilot]] in the unit.<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> Blizzard Force spearheaded the [[Battle of Hoth|assault]] of [[Echo Base]] on the [[Water|ice]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Hoth]] while the [[Death Squadron|Imperial Death Squadron]] loomed overhead.<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>

==Description==
[[File:FineArtPrint-TheLastHoldout.png|thumb|300px|left|The AT-ATs and snowtroopers of Blizzard Force advance against rebel forces.]]
Headquartered on [[Death Squadron]], the personal [[fleet]] of [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], Blizzard Force,<ref name="Twilight Company" /> or Blizzard Unit,<ref name="BC 21">{{BustCollectionCite|21|Star Wars Universe}}</ref> was an elite [[Tank|armored]] formation within the [[501st Legion|501st Imperial Legion]].<ref name="HC 29" /> Blizzard Force was under the command of [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]],<ref name="The Battle of Hoth" /> with fellow [[Imperial officer]]s consisting of [[Brigadier General]] [[Nevar]], [[Colonel]] [[Starck]],<ref name="CL 2016" /> [[Captain]] [[TS-4068]],<ref name="Duty" /> and [[Unidentified snowtrooper captain|another stormtrooper captain]].<ref name="Invasion of Echo Base" />

Blizzard Force consisted of at least one [[battalion]] of elite [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]],<ref name="The Battle of Hoth" /> as well as at least one [[armor]]ed [[squadron]] of [[walker]]s, the [[Thundering Herd]].<ref name="Vehicles New Edition" /> [[Blizzard Force unit two-two-eight-seven]] was a subunit,<ref name="Twilight Company" /> as was a [[TS-4068's squad|squad]] led by Captain TS-4068,<ref name="Duty" /> and another [[squad]] led directly by [[Lord]] Vader.<ref name="The Battle of Hoth" /> Blizzard Force consisted of at least twelve [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Galactic Civil War)|All Terrain Armored Transports]],<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> which had the callsigns of [[Blizzard 1]], [[Blizzard 2]], [[Blizzard 4]], [[Blizzard 6]], [[Blizzard 8]], and [[Blizzard 9]].<ref name="CL 2016" /> The AT-AT walkers were supported by [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s,<ref name="ESB" /> and the force could be deployed by [[Y-85 Titan dropship]]s or [[Gozanti-class Assault Carrier|''Gozanti''-class Assault Carriers]].<ref name="Twilight Company" />

==History==
{{Quote|Yes, Lord Vader. I've reached the main power generators. The shield will be down in moments. You may start your landing.|General Maximillian Veers to Darth Vader, regarding the Echo Base shield generator|<ref name="ESB" />}}
[[File:FineArtPrints-There-is-no-escape.png|thumb|left|300px|Darth Vader leads the Imperial assault on Hoth.]]
Upon discovering the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Echo Base|hidden base]] on the remote [[Planet|world]] of [[Hoth]], the [[Galactic Empire]] quickly mobilized an attack force, with the [[Death Squadron]] in [[orbit]] above the planet while the elite Blizzard Force led by [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]] would spearhead the assault on Echo Base below. Assembling a force of a dozen AT-AT walkers deployed by nimble [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruisers]] escorted by [[TIE series|TIEs]], Blizzard Force landed in the [[Moorsh Moraine]].<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> Blizzard Force promptly assaulted the base, attempting to disable the rebel's [[DSS-02 shield generator|shield generator]] and ultimately destroy the Rebellion once and for all.<ref name="GD">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref> The battle ended an Imperial victory, however not before Veers' [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT]] [[walker]] [[Blizzard 1]] was destroyed and several rebel [[GR-75 medium transport]]s and [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s escaped the [[Hoth system]].<ref name="ESB" />
