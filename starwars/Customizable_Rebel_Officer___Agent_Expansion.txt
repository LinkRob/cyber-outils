The '''Customizable Rebel Officer & Agent Expansion''' ('''SWQ13''') is an expansion to the tabletop game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. It contains a customizable miniature of a Rebel officer and Rebel agent, and was released on [[October 3]], [[2025]] as part of Atomic Mass Games' overhaul of the game.

==Publisher's summary==
''The Rebel Alliance Imperial Officer & Agent pack allows players to build two customized Rebel Alliance characters that can be used as either a Commander or Operative when building their Rebel Alliance armies.''

''This pack includes over 20 optional parts players can choose from when assembling their miniatures, allowing for a huge range of personal customization for each character. Players also customize their Rebel Officer and Agent’s in-game abilities using the 9 unique Upgrade cards included in this expansion. Each of these unique upgrades dramatically changes the battlefield role, abilities, and synergies the character brings to the army.''

==Contents==
*2 Miniatures
*2 Unit Cards
*9 Upgrade Cards
*2 Command Cards
