The [[Mandalorian]] [[artist]] [[Veraslayn Kast]] made a '''Death Watch poster'''. The [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] showcased [[soldier]]s of the [[Death Watch]], a Mandalorian [[terrorist]] group, in action. In the foreground, three [[armor]]-clad Mandalorians were on the ground while another using their [[jetpack]] took off into the air, with all four in front of a large [[fire]]. In the background was a [[city]] and a [[Color|red]] sky, as well as seven Mandalorians flying overhead with their jetpacks. This piece, as well as several others, were the first in ages to depict Mandalorians in battle.<ref name="SW Propaganda" />
