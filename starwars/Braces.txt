'''Braces'''<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> were a type of small device implemented by individuals on their [[Tooth|teeth]].<ref name="TPM" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Amee]] was the only [[youngling]] among her peers on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] who wore braces.<ref name="Geektionary" />

==Behind the scenes==
Braces first appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="TPM" /> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the device was identified in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''.<ref name="Geektionary" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Unidentified teeth implement|implement]] was not canonically identified but was given the name "braces" in a non-canonical graphic novel of that continuity, ''[[Jedi Academy: The Phantom Bully]]'', which was written by [[Jeffrey Brown]] and published in [[2015]].<ref name="Bully">''[[Jedi Academy: The Phantom Bully]]''</ref>
