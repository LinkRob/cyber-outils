The '''attack on ''Nechrim's Wake''''' began after a chaotic meeting between [[New Republic]] representative [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Fenril Consortium]] representative [[Kezrin Almovar]] clarified that the New Republic and its [[starship]]s were not welcome in the [[Fenril sector]] and would be met with force should they try.<ref name="SW1" />

Because of this, while entering the sector, the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Nechrim's Wake]]'' and other vessels<ref name="SW2" /> were attacked by multiple [[Zantarrk Gang]] starships<ref name="SW1" /> and the trading vessel was forced to crash land on [[Loradil]], where its crew was attacked by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Battle droid|battle droids]].<ref name="SW3" /><ref name="SW4" />

Leia and Mon Mothma discussed the attack afterwards on [[Chandrila]], Leia thinking it was an indication of their enemies getting "into position".<ref name="SW2" /> Even later, Mon Mothma contacted the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' with news of the attack in order to send New Republic heroes [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Han Solo]], [[Rynn Zenat]], and [[Beilert Valance]] to rescue the survivors on Loradil.<ref name="SW4" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on ''Nechrim's Wake first appeared in the first issue of the 2025 iteration of the flagship comic book series [[Star Wars (2025)|''Star Wars'']], written by Alex Segura, illustrated by Phil Noto, and published by Marvel Comics on May 7 of that year.<ref name="SW1 Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/119550/star_wars_2025_1|text=Star Wars (2025) #1}}</ref>
