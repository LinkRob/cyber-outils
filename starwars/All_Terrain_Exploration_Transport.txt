The '''All Terrain Exploration Transport (AT-ET)''', also commonly known as the "'''Strider'''," was a [[walker]] that was manufactured by [[Rothana Heavy Engineering]] and deployed for exploration and [[scout]]ing purposes. It was a smaller version of the [[AT-EST Exploration and Survey Walker]].

==Characteristics==
The All Terrain Exploration Transport (AT-ET), commonly known as the "Strider," was a small [[scout]] variant of the [[All Terrain Exploration and Survey Walker/Canon|All Terrain Exploration and Survey Walker]] that was manufactured by [[Rothana Heavy Engineering]] and cost 5,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. The AT-ET had a central chassis that was surrounded by six powerful multi-jointed mechanical [[Limb|legs]] with wide, pad-like feet. The segments of each leg could also be extended to safely travel deep rivers, marshes, snowbanks, and other terrain that could cause walkers to sink. Only the living quarters and stowage area in the rear of the chassis were enclosed in a tough, environmentally sealed fabric to repel the elements while the forward hull had an open [[cockpit]] and passenger area, allowing for excellent visibility and fields of fire when needed.<ref name="Savage Spirits" />

The Strider could carry for passengers, a [[pilot]], and a communications operator or navigator. In certain situations, the pilot could assume nav/comms duties to allow for an additional passenger. In emergencies, the vehicle could carry ten or more passengers in emergencies, using makeshift handholds and sideboards mounted on the hull. Due to this level of customization, the AT-ET was versatile and beloved in rougher terrains of [[the galaxy]]. Its chassis was continuously modified to carry more power [[sensor]]s, better passenger accommodations, stronger [[armor]], and [[Smuggler|smuggling]] compartments. With modifying the walker with hull-mounted weapons, owners could turn the Strider into an effective fighting vehicle that was better able to withstand the dangerous of hazardous terrain, as well as repel rival explorers.<ref name="Savage Spirits" />

==Role==
The Strider was ideal for short ventures into areas where the terrain was believed to be hazardous or simply unknown. It was more of a fight [[transport]] than combat walker.<ref name="Savage Spirits" />
