'''Athio III''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Dawn" /> in the [[Athio system]] of the [[Mayagil sector]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It fell within grid square N-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Following the [[Great Jedi Purge]]<ref name="Dawn" /> that began in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Velerie Tide]] hid on Athio III from the [[Galactic Empire]] and disguised herself as a local.<ref name="Dawn" />

By [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Kenobi date">{{KenobiDate|IV}} The episode shows [[Velerie Tide]] to already be [[Death|dead]].</ref> Tide had been revealed as a [[Force-sensitive|Force-user]] and was [[Death|killed]], with her remains being displayed in [[Fortress Inquisitorius]], headquarters of the Empire's [[Inquisitorius]] [[Jedi hunter|Jedi-hunters]], on the [[moon]] [[Nur]]. Athio III shared its name<ref name="Dawn" /> with the [[Athio Seven Hundred]] [[Podracing|podrace]].<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Athio III was mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'',<ref name="Dawn" /> within a section written by [[Emily Shkoukani]].<ref name="DawnAuthor">{{WookYouTube|-nqSqVOvfM4|Wookieepedia Interviews - Emily Shkoukani & Jason Fry}}</ref> The page mentioning the location was revealed prior<ref name="SDCC 2023 panel">{{SW|url=news/sdcc-2023-lucasfilm-publishing-panel|text=SDCC 2023: 10 Reveals from the Lucasfilm Publishing Panel}}</ref> to the book's [[November 2]], [[2023]] release<ref name="DK UK">{{DK|url=uk/9780241666371-star-wars-dawn-of-rebellion-the-visual-guide|text=Star Wars Dawn of Rebellion The Visual Guide}}</ref> at a [[Lucasfilm]] Publishing panel at [[Wikipedia:San Diego Comic Con|San Diego Comic Con]] and in an article on [[StarWars.com]] covering it on [[July 21]], 2023.<ref name="SDCC 2023 panel" />
