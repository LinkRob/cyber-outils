A '''battle''' between the [[Imperial Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] occurred in the [[Altyr system]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]'s [[Morshdine sector]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> as part of the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The Empire emerged victorious and destroyed a rebel [[Unidentified rebel capital ship|capital ship]]. [[Commander]] [[Folimar]] called the battle a brisk reminder of the Empire's superior force and was quoted by [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Dorna Parros]] during coverage of the battle on the [[HoloNet News]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
