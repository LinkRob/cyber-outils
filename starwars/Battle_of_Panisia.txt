The '''Battle of Panisia''' was a battle of the [[Galactic Civil War]] that was fought<ref name="Star Wars 23" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> at the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" /> [[planet]] [[Panisia]].<ref name="Star Wars 23" /> The battle began when the [[Hunter Fleet|forces]] of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Commander]] [[Ellian Zahra]] learned the location of a [[Second Division|division]] of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|rebel fleet]]<ref name="Star Wars 22" /> after the [[Crimson Dawn]] [[assassin]] [[Deathstick]] informed the [[Imperial Anti-Terrorism Watchline]] of the [[Temporary rebel cell base|rebel base]] there.<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" /> Zahra had her [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]], the ''[[Tarkin's Will]]'', travel to Panisia<ref name="Star Wars 22" /> where they began to [[Orbital bombardment|bombard]] the base and its [[Starship|ships]]. During the course of the battle, Deathstick was able to complete her own personal mission of capturing [[Cadeliah]], the heir to the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate]] and [[Unbroken Clan]] criminal syndicates, who had been taken in by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]].<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" />

Sometime after the arrival of the Imperial forces at Panisia, rebel reinforcements arrived after [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was given [[Galactic coordinates|coordinates]] by [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]], the leader of Crimson Dawn, in order to defeat Zahra and her forces.<ref name="Star Wars 22" /> After she gave a motivational speech to the crew of her ship, Zahra had the ''Tarkin's Will'' dive to Panisia, which encouraged the rebels to follow the ship into the planet's [[gravity well]], where the ''Tarkin's Will'' fired its [[ion cannon]]s and disabled the hit rebel ships which then began to fall onto the planet below. This left the rebels with a tough choice, to either fire on their own ships as they would not be able to evacuate them in [[time]] before they reached the base and allow time for the forces of the [[division]] based on Panisia to be evacuated, or to evacuate the crews of the of the disabled ships and risk them hitting the rebel base. The rebels ultimately decided to fire on their own ships. As the ''Tarkin's Will'' continued its dive to the base, it pulled out of it shortly before impact, which destroyed a number of the base's ships. Zahra then ordered for the ''Tarkin's Will'' [[Engine|thrusters]] to be turned on full, which incinerated the remaining ships and base as it made its way into [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Star Wars 23" />

When the ''Tarkin's Will'' reached space, Zahra ordered it to fire on the rebel flagship, ''[[Home One]]'', but unbeknownst to the commander, the ship had been infiltrated by rebel [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Pathfinders]] led by [[Sergeant]] [[Kes Dameron]] in an attempt to rescue his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Shara Bey]], who was being held prisoner aboard the ''Tarkin's Will''. After they rescued her, three of the Pathfinders destroyed the ships primary [[reactor]], which caused massive damage to the vessel, including taking down its [[Deflector shield|shields]]. The move allowed the rebel ships to fire on the ''Tarkin's Will'', and the vessel suffered catastrophic damage, though the crew attempted to repair the [[hyperdrive]] and made calls for reinforcements. Zahra ordered the ship be abandoned as it fell into Panisia, and she escaped aboard an [[escape pod]]. When she reached the surface, Zahra contacted Organa personally and requested the general come and hunt her where she was left for dead.<ref name="Star Wars 23" />
