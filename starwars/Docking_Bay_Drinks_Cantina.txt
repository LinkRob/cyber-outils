The '''Docking Bay Drinks Cantina''' was a location found towards the center of the [[Mos Espa Podrace]] battlefield of the [[Grand Arena]], utilized by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. It could be identified by a large horizontal sign with a raised [[Color|brown]]. The background of this sign was dark blue, with an orange streak running along the top. Below was three lines of text, each in [[Aurebesh]] and differing in color. The top white line read "[[Hangar|Docking Bay]]," the middle was light blue and said "[[Alcohol|Drinks]]," and the bottom in a big orange font displayed "[[Cantina]]," while to the far right of the sign was a neon illustration of a drinking glass filled with a bubbling liquid.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Docking Bay Drinks Cantina first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It was added to the game via the inclusion of the Mos Espa Podrace map.<ref name="Hunters" />
