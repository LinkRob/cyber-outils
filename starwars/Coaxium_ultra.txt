'''Coaxium ultra'''<ref name="Insider 224">{{InsiderCite|224|Launchpad}}</ref> was a formula of the [[Fuel|hyperfuel]] [[coaxium]] that was devised at a [[Unidentified coaxium refinery|refinery]] on the [[moon]] [[Ro Mira]] during the [[Republic Era]]. The [[human]] [[Gender|woman]] [[Azita Cruuz]] acquired a shipment of the coaxium which she offered in exchange for [[Galactic Credit Standard|Republic credits]], an offer she broadcasted far and wide. In an attempt to prevent the shipment from falling into the hands of groups like the [[Hutt Clan|Hutts]] and the [[Pyke Syndicate|Pykes]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] [[Mission to Ro Mira|dispatched]] [[Jedi]] [[Mace Windu]] to recover Cruuz and the shipment of coaxium she had promised.<ref name="Mace Windu 1" />
