'''B6-24''' was a [[protocol droid]] with [[Gender|feminine programming]] who hosted [[Droid Station Frequency 3-7-6-4-3-7]], a classified radio station where [[droid]]s helped droids. The station played in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="GEStaff" />

==Behind the scenes==
B6-24 was created for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at Disneyland Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]]. The droid is not present in the park, but her voice can be heard in certain areas.<ref name="GEStaff" />
