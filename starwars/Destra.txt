'''Destra''' was one of two [[moon]]s that orbited the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]] in the [[Batuu system]] of the [[Trilon sector]], the other being [[Valara]]. It was not visible from [[Black Spire Outpost]], the planet's largest [[City|settlement]].<ref name="GEStaff" /> The [[Ronto Roasters]] restaurant in Black Spire sold a beverage called the [[Destran Ice Cap]].<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ronto-roasters/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Ronto Roasters: Lunch and Dinner Menu}} {{C|Archive 16 November 2022}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Destra was first introduced as part of the backstory for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="GEStaff" /> a theme park land that opened to guests in [[Disneyland]] on [[May 31]], [[2019]] and in [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] on [[August 29]], 2019.<ref name="Opening Dates">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-opening-dates-revealed|text=''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge Opening Dates Revealed}}</ref> A beverage called the Destran Ice Cap was sold at Disneyland's [[Ronto Roasters]] for a limited time.<ref name="Menu" />

It was reported that [[August 14]]th, [[2015]], one day before the announcement of Galaxy's Edge, [[Lucasfilm]] filed trademarks for the names "Mon Destra" and "Valara," with no explanation for what they referred to. Though some fan sites suggested a connection to a fan fiction character named Darth Destra, and the name Valara already existed in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] as Jedi Master [[Valara (Jedi)|Valara]] and Force Adept [[Valara Saar]].<ref name="Slashfilm">{{WebCite|url=https://www.slashfilm.com/539509/valara-saar-lucasfilm-trademarks|text=Lucasfilm Trademarks Two Obscure Non-Canon 'Star Wars' Names|work=SlashFilm.com|archiveurl=https://archive.is/Dv2hM}}</ref>
