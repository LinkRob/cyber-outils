'''Fleet Command''' was the command structure of the [[New Republic Defense Fleet|naval branch]] of the [[New Republic]] 

==History==
===Galactic Civil War===
During the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Following a [[skirmish on Takodana]], [[Inferno Squad (Resistance)|Inferno Squad]] asked [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to approve a [[mission to Chinook Station]] to capture Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]] since [[fleet]] command may not have approved of the [[squad]] undertaking a personal mission. Organa sympathized, and approved the mission.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

===Peacetime===
{{Quote|Fulcrum, this is ''Home One'', do you copy, over?''"<br />"''Come in ''Home One''. This is Fulcrum, over.''"<br />"''Copy, incoming transmission, priority one, there's been an incident.|A [[Unidentified Home One comm officer|''Home One'' comm officer]] and [[Huyang]]|<ref name="Part One" />}}
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Ahsoka series date">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref> Fleet Command was stationed aboard ''[[Home One]]'' and contacted [[Ahsoka Tano]] and Huyang to inform them of the [[Attack on the Vesper|attack]] on the [[Defender-class cruiser|''Defender''-class cruiser]] ''[[Vesper]]''.<ref name="Part Five">{{Ahsoka|Part Five: Shadow Warrior}}</ref>
