'''Big String''' was an [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[alien]] who lived during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[Crime lord|crime boss]] in a [[Big String's fortress|fortress]] they constructed on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Amethia Prime]]. Some [[time]] later, an individual hired [[Chanath Cha]], a [[bounty hunter]], to destroy Big String's fortress and apprehend the crime boss. Cha did, and Big String was forced to flee. Cha caught up with String, however, and grabbed onto them and flew to cloud-level. Cha received a call from [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], so she dropped Big String from the tremendous height.

==Biography==
{{Quote|My fortress ''destroyed!'' Twenty cycles spent building my empire... ''Gone!'' Why are you doing this to me? Who are you?''"''<br />''"''My name is ''Chanath Cha''. As for ''why'', Big String...someone out there ''really'' doesn't like you.|Big String and Chanath Cha meet|<ref name="Lando 2" />}}
Big String was a [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[alien]] who lived during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], and worked as a [[Crime lord|crime boss]], engaging in various nefarious activities, which gained them a fortune in [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. String constructed a [[Big String's fortress|fortress]] on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[Planet|world]] [[Amethia Prime]] in a [[Color|purple]] [[ocean]] which was located on a series of small [[Volcano|volcanic]] islands. String built the [[fortress]] over the span of twenty [[Standard year|cycles]], and employed many beings who worked in the fortress, including at least three [[bodyguard]]s. However, some [[time]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]], an individual who disliked String hired prestigious [[bounty hunter]] [[Chanath Cha]] to destroy Big String's "empire" and capture the crime boss.<ref name="Lando 2" />

[[File:ChanathCarriesBigString-Lando2.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Cha carries Big String through the sky.]]
Soon thereafter, Cha [[Attack on Big String's fortress|invaded]] the fortress, [[Death|killing]] the majority of String's employees, and utilized her [[armor]] and [[rocket boot]]s to wreak havoc on the fortress. String fled in a [[boat]] with three guards to escape Cha. As they skimmed across the [[water]], the fortress exploded completely, wiping out String's "empire". One of the guards then inquired as to whether String wanted to return to the fortress to search for survivors, to which String replied: "Don't be and idiot. Drive." Cha appeared next to the boat underwater, keeping speed with her rocket [[boot]]s.<ref name="Lando 2" />

Before String could do anything, Cha shot up through the bow of the boat; overturning it, destroying its [[engine]] and throwing String and the guards into the water. They scrambled back onto the boat and Cha introduced herself to the crime [[boss]] and quickly overtook the three guards in [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]]. String, realizing he had little choice, surrendered to the [[bounty]] hunter and dropped their [[blaster rifle]]. Cha hoisted String up by their [[belt]] and ascended to cloud-level of Amenthia Prime's [[atmosphere]], where String pleaded with Cha that they could pay her more than she was being paid in exchange for freedom, but Cha had none of it and soon received a communication from [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]&mdash;the most recognizable man in [[the galaxy]]&mdash;who had a job for the bounty hunter. Therefore, Cha apologized to String and dropped the crime boss; not wanting to waste the Emperor's time.<ref name="Lando 2" />

==Personality and traits==
Big String was a crime lord and member amphibian species. String had [[Color|green]] skin, green [[eye]]s, a pudgy physique. They built a fortress on the oceanic world of Amethia Prime, which became the headquarters of their empire. During the Age of the Empire, String incurred the wrath of an individual who hired the bounty hunter Chanath Cha to destroy String's operations. String was able to wield a [[blaster]] but could not swim. The crime boss feared Cha and accepted defeated following a prolonged pursuit and skirmish. However, Cha dropped String into the ocean after she received a new assignment from Emperor Palpatine.<ref name="Lando 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Big String first appeared as a minor antagonist in the [[2015]] [[Marvel Comics]] story [[Star Wars: Lando (comic series)|''Star Wars: Lando'']]. He was written by [[Charles Soule]], drawn by [[Alex Maleev]], and colored by [[Paul Mounts]].
