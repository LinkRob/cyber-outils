The '''Estaria system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Indrexu sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square S-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the location [[Estaria]], which had [[orbit]]al [[Spaceport|ports]] known for their dense civilian traffic.<ref name="VP" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Estaria system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', which was written by Fry and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
