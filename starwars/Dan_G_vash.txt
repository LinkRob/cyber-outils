'''Dan G'vash''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]. G'vash traveled with his [[Jedi Master|master]] [[Tajin Crosser]] to investigate a [[disturbance in the Force]] on the [[planet]] [[Habo]]. After locating [[Habo village]], the two Jedi found a ship that Crosser believed looked like one that a [[Sith]] would use. While Crosser suggested they wait together at the ship for the individual to return, G'vash suggested he search through the [[mountain]]s surrounding the village while his master stayed with the ship. Crosser agreed to the plan, and when G'vash noticed a slain [[vamga]], he shortly after encountered an [[The Elder|elder individual]] wielding two small [[Color|red]] [[lightsaber]]s. G'vash was outmatched by his opponent, suffering a serious injury when he engaged the warrior in a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]], but he was later able to help his master finish off their opponent.<ref name="The Elder" />

==Personality and traits==
Dan was a determined Padawan apprentice with black hair, blue eyes, and dark skin. He was underwhelmed by his adventures in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], as he was hoping for more exciting action. When visiting [[Habo]] with his master, he was able to form a relationship with several local children when he spotted them looking at him, winking and smiling at them and later enlisting their help to explore the [[mountain]] areas near their village in his search for a possible [[Sith]] warrior. Despite recruiting the children to help him, G'vash felt that he was capable in mountainous terrain.<ref name="The Elder" />

Nonetheless, G'vash proved himself insightful and wise. He urged the children he enlisted to accompany him exploring the mountains to return to the village upon discovering a recently slain Vamga knowing that something was off. When he unexpectedly found himself confronting the Sith warrior in question&mdash;an elderly man who eager for strong opponents to&mdash;
and had to brandish his lightsaber in response of the latter's challenge, G'vash correctly deduced the danger his opponent posed and put up defensive stance instead of attacking head-on hoping to buy Tajin some time to arrive on his side albeit injured in the process. Despite this and the master-padawan duo's failure to learn more information about their enemy as the elderly Sith self-destructed his starship in his last moments, Tajin reminded the young padawan to not forget his training and his kindness and that time will make him stronger even if it would not be last.<ref name="The Elder" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dan G'vash first appeared in "[[The Elder (short film)|The Elder]]," a short film in the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. He is voiced by [[Yuichi Nakamura]] in Japanese version and [[Jordan Fisher]] in the English version.<ref name="The Elder" />
