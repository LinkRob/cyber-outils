The '''excavation on Durkteel''' occurred in [[34 ABY]] on the [[planet]] [[Durkteel]] when a construction crew were clearing the ground of the ruins of the [[Tak-Beam complex]] for a [[comm tower]]. Whilst undertaking the excavation, the crew broke into a chamber filled with dirt and a number of relics from the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Only one of these relics, a vault which contained ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', a compilation of information penned by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], was deemed to possess significant military or intelligence value.

Thus, ''The Rebel Files'' was then sent to the [[Planetary Governor|planetary governor]], [[Vaxvissh Kal Ness]], before the [[governor]] later sent it to the [[New Republic]], since one of its most prominent figures, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], was one of the few surviving high ranking Alliance members. Falling into the hands of [[Major]] [[Heck Ensio]], the artifact was again given away to [[Master Archivist]] [[Mordianius]], who would sent it to [[Commander]] [[Korr Sella]] of the [[Resistance]], so that she may give it to [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], leader of the Resistance.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
