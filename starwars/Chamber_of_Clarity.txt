The '''Chamber of Clarity''' was one of the [[Standard century|centuries]]-old [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Chamber|Meditation Chambers]] on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. This chamber was located atop a cliff overlooking the Untamed Downs near [[Rambler's Reach Valley]]. This chamber was part of the [[Jedi Trials|Jedi trials]] of Koboh, which collectively presented Jedi with trying, painful challenges that Jedi had not faced since their struggles as learners.<ref name="Survivor" />
