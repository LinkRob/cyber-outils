One [[Standard month|month]] before the [[Liberation of Kashyyyk|liberation]] of [[Kashyyyk]], forces of the [[New Republic]] '''attacked''' the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]]-held [[Hyborean Moon]], destroying the [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB's]] blacksite [[prison]] as well as its civilian complexes.

==Prelude==
During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], the [[Hyborean Moon]] was the site of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Hyborean Moon black-site prison|black-site prison]]. The [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Romwell Krass Junior]] regarded the Hyborean Moon as a cushy posting for [[Krass family|his family]]. Krass and his family lived in a comfortable home on the shore of a hot spring lake. He also arranged for his cronies to be transferred there.<ref name="LD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

==The Attack==
Following the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] became the [[New Republic]]. The New Republic dispatched a [[New Republic Defense Fleet|fleet]] to attack the Imperial presence on the Hyborean Moon. Krass' family was killed when New Republic [[starship]]s bombed the civilian structures. Krass barely managed to escaped on a starship as rebel forces stormed the prison.<ref name="LD" />

==Aftermath==
For a month, Romwell Krass Junior drifted through space grieving the loss of his family. He also became an alcoholic and took to drinking. Instead of reporting for duty to the Imperial [[capital]] of Coruscant, Krass traveled to [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on the planet [[Takodana]]. There, he became involved in a fight with a [[Unidentified New Republic pilot|New Republic pilot]]. The fight was broken up by Maz Kanata's droid [[ME-8D9]] who locked the two belligerents in Maz's dungeons. Maz allowed the two belligerents to leave but advised Krass to make peace with his grief.<ref name="LD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Attack on the Hyborean Moon was mentioned in passing in [[Chuck Wendig]]'s [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', the second book in [[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy|''The Aftermath Trilogy'']].
