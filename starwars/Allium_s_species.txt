This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. [[Professor]] [[Allium]] was a member of this species.<ref name="TFA" />

==Biology and appearance==
This [[species]] was distinguished by their [[Color|green]] skin, long fingers, tall necks, and enlarged craniums.<ref name="TFA" />

==Species in the galaxy==
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TFA Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> a member of this species, [[Professor]] [[Allium]], was present at [[Takodana Castle]] when the [[First Order]] destroyed the [[Hosnian system]] and invaded the planet [[Takodana]] in search of the [[droid]] [[BB-8]].<ref name="TFA" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|CO78's my favorite! I don't know why he's my favorite. Maybe it's got more to do with me. He's got my design sensibility.|''The Force Awakens'' senior sculptor [[Ivan Manzella]], on Allium|<ref name="TFA Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Allium_concept_art.png|thumb|right|200px|Castle Alien CO78 by Ivan Manzella]]
The species made their debut in the [[2015]] film {{Film|VII}}, directed by [[J.J. Abrams]].<ref name="TFA" /> They were originally designed by creature concept designer and sculptor [[Ivan Manzella]], with concept art of them being referred to as "Castle Alien C078."<ref name="TFA Art" />
