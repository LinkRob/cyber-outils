'''Blistmok''' were a type of [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[Diet|predator]] found on the [[Volcano|volcanic]] [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Mustafar]]. They [[Hunting|hunted]] in packs and were tireless, running down prey for [[Local day|days]] before attacking.<ref name="BMF33" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], blistmok were mentioned in "Wildlife of Mustafar,"<ref name="BMF33" /> an article written by [[Mark Newbold]]<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> and published in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 33|thirty-third]] issue of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF33" /> which was released around [[August 19]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|33}}</ref>

[[Blistmok/Legends|Blistmok]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in "Galactic Biological Survey Project – Mustafar," an article posted on the website for the [[Star Wars video games|massively multiplayer online role-playing games ]] ''[[Star Wars Galaxies]]'' on [[September 14]], [[2005]] as part of the pre-release material for the game's [[Star Wars Galaxies: Trials of Obi-Wan|''Trials of Obi-Wan'']] expansion.<ref name="Survey">{{SonyCite|url=en_US/players/content.vm?page=Friday%20Feature:%20Trials%20Of%20Obi-Wan%20Creatures&resource=features|text=Galactic Biological Survey Project &ndash; Mustafar}}</ref> The creatures then appeared for the first time in the expansion itself<ref name="Trials">''[[Star Wars Galaxies: Trials of Obi-Wan]]''</ref> when it was released on [[November 1]], 2005.<ref name="SWGMB">[[Star Wars Galaxies Memory Book|''Star Wars Galaxies'' Memory Book]]</ref>
