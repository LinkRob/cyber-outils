'''Battlegroup Echelon''' was a [[battle group]] within the [[Imperial Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Squadrons" /> By [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' establishes that the [[Galactic Empire]]'s hunt for [[Project Starhawk]] began and ended in [[4 ABY]]. According to ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]'', the [[ambush at the Zavian Abyss]] occurred during that time, and therefore must have also taken place in that [[Standard year|year]]. As ''Squadrons'' establishes that the Skirmish at [[Merisee]] occurred before the ambush, it must have taken place by that year.</ref> Battlegroup Echelon participated in the [[Skirmish at Merisee]]<ref name="Squadrons" /> in the [[Elrood sector]]<ref name="Temptation of the Force">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> where the formation's [[flagship]], led by their [[Unidentified admiral (Battlegroup Echelon)|admiral]], was captured and their supply lines cut. Despite the situation, the group's [[Imperial Military|forces]] regrouped and pushed back the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]], made repairs in enemy territory, and launched a rescue. When the unit returned, they defeated the [[Alliance Military|rebel forces]] and emerged victorious.<ref name="Squadrons" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Imperial [[Titan Squadron]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] [[Rella Sol]] recounted Battlegroup Echelon's efforts at the skirmish to fellow [[pilot]] [[Titan Three (Flight Baron)|Titan Three]] in a [[hangar]] after their vessel, the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Overseer (Imperial-class)|''Overseer'']], was heavily damaged in an [[ambush at the Zavian Abyss]] against the [[New Republic]]'s prototype ''[[Starhawk]]'' [[battleship]].<ref name="Squadrons" />
