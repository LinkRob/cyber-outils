'''Blue Squadron''' was a unit of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic's]] [[501st Legion/Legends|501st Legion]] that was made up of at least one [[Special Forces Clone Trooper (blue armor)|Special Forces Clone Trooper]] and under the command of a [[Special forces/Legends|Special Forces]] [[Commander/Legends|Commander]].<ref name="ILTM" /> Active during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]], a Special Forces [[Clone trooper/Legends|Clone Trooper]] from the [[Squadron/Legends|squadron]] attempted to pursue the [[Desertion/Legends|rogue]] [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] [[Jek-14/Legends|Jek-14]].<ref name="LegoWebsite">{{LEGOWeb|url=en-lu/product/jek-14s-stealth-starfighter-75018|text=Jek-14’s ™ Stealth Starfighter}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Squadron was first depicted in the "Jek-14's Stealth Starfighter" [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set through the inclusion of a Special Forces Clone [[Trooper/Legends|Trooper]] member of the squadron.<ref name="StealthStarfighter">{{LEGOCite|num=75018|name=Jek-14's Stealth Starfighter}}</ref> The unit was eventually identified as Blue Squadron in the ''LEGO: I Love That Minifigure'' LEGO encyclopedia, which established the trooper from the set to be part of the squadron.<ref name="ILTM" />
