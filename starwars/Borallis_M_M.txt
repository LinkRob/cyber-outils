'''Borallis M&M''' was a company that manufactured the [[Q-1041 Plasma cutter]], a model of [[molecular torch]] that was in use by the [[Galactic Empire]] in the [[Citadel Tower]] on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]]<ref name="ROUVG" /> by [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' establishes that the [[Q-1041 Plasma cutter]] was in used during the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Borallis M&M was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="ROUVG" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the company [[Borallis Metalworking & Materials]] was first mentioned in ''[[Galladinium's Fantastic Technology]]'', a [[1995]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' supplement published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="Galladinium">''[[Galladinium's Fantastic Technology]]''</ref>
