The '''Droideka Sharpshooter''',<ref name="Collapse" /> also known as the '''sharpshooter droideka''', the '''sniper droideka''', the '''sniper droid''', or the '''sharpshooter droid''', was a variation of [[droideka]] that specialized in [[Sniper|sniping]]. Unlike the original droideka, it was equipped with a single [[blaster rifle]] in the middle, and used its [[armor]]ed shell to cover its front while in combat, instead of [[deflector shield]]s.<ref name="Databank" /> Sharpshooter droidekas were part of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] forces commanded by the [[Zygerrian]] [[Darts D'Nar]] on [[Kiros]].<ref name="Kidnapped" />

==Description==
{{Quote|Their shields are too strong.|Ahsoka Tano shouting while being fired upon by a Droideka Sharpshooter|<ref name="Kidnapped" />}}
[[File:Droideka_Sharpshooter_CotR.png|thumb|left|200px|A Droideka Sharpshooter]]
[[Droideka]] Sharpshooters were a 1.87 [[meter]]<ref name="Databank" /> variation the [[Colicoid Creation Nest]] manufactured<ref name="Build R2 32">{{BuildR2Cite|32|Droid Directory}}</ref> droideka. They were armed with a finely calibrated long-barrel<ref name="Databank" /> heavy [[blaster rifle]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> The [[droid]] was [[armor]]ed with a [[Shield (physical armor)|shield]] that protected the droid from [[blaster]] fire, but left them open to attacks from either side, as they lacked the [[Deflector shield generator|shield projector]] of other droideka models. They could roll into position and could be used to take the enemy by surprise, and they were best placed in sheltered positions, where they could eliminate targets from long ranges.<ref name="Collapse" /> Droideka sharpshooters had combat and [[sniper]] programming, as well as exceptional speed.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> When placed strategically, the droideka was capable of eliminating important targets such as [[Jedi]], and their accuracy was far more superior to that of standard droidekas, as well as their single, high-powered [[rifle]] which could pierce through multiple targets in a single shot.<ref name="Collapse" /> The droid had orange and yellow markings at the base of the rifle.<ref name="Kidnapped" />

However, despite their effectiveness as a [[sniper]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] made less use of the Droideka Sharpshooter than alternative droidekas. This was largely due to the droid's limited versatility and that their use required more careful planning than other models.<ref name="Collapse" />

==History==
{{Quote|Ahsoka, they're weak from the side. I got an angle on your droid.<br>Ok, I got a view of yours too. Same time, ready? <br> Go!|Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano on taking down the sharpshooters|<ref name="Kidnapped" />}}
Two Droideka sharpshooters were amongst [[Zygerrian]] [[Commander]] [[Darts D'Nar]]'s forces during the [[Battle on Kiros]]. The droids were positioned to defend a [[bomb]] from [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. At first, the [[Jedi]] attempted to use their [[lightsaber]]s to deflect [[laser]] blasts back to the droidekas, but their front sections were shielded and armored, protecting the droids from the attack. As the droids continued to fire, Skywalker determined the droids were vulnerable from the side. He and Tano used their retreated positions to their advantage. They targeted each other's droid, causing the sniper droids to fire at their sides, allowing the Jedi to repel the droids' blasts and destroy them.<ref name="Kidnapped" />

==Behind the scenes==
Droideka Sharpshooters were designed during the initial production of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' by [[Kilian Plunkett]]. They initially remained unused in the series,<ref name="Art of TCW">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''</ref> but appeared in the [[The Clone Wars 1|first issue]] of the now [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (comic series)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' comic series]],<ref name="TCW cs 1">[[The Clone Wars 1|''The Clone Wars'' 1]]</ref> released on [[September 10]], [[2008]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/15-679/Star-Wars-The-Clone-Wars-1|text=Star Wars: The Clone Wars #1}}</ref>. They were later introduced in the main series in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|Season Four]] episode "[[Kidnapped]]"<ref name="Kidnapped" /> on [[November 25]], [[2011]],<ref name="Kidnapped EG old">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep411|text="Kidnapped" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide"|nolive=1}}</ref> which adapted the comic issue. The model of the Sharpshooter Droid for the episode was designed by [[Will Nichols]] in July [[2010]] and was sourced from the original droideka model. An additional texture was painted at the droid's torso at the request of supervising director<ref name="Kidnapped EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/kidnapped-episode-guide|text="Kidnapped" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> [[Dave Filoni]]<ref name="Kidnapped" /> on [[October 10]], 2010.<ref name="Kidnapped EG" />

The Sniper Droid's entry in the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] falsely features an image of a regular droideka<ref name="Databank" /> from the episode "[[Landing at Point Rain]]".<ref name="Landing">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref>
