An '''atlatl''' was a one-handed [[Gungan]] throwing device consisting of a short stick of non-conductive hardwood with a cradle in one of its ends. It acted as an extension of the [[Limb|arm]], enabling a [[booma]] to travel at a greater speed and force than possible with the unaided arm. It had optimal range of 30 [[meter]]s, and a maximum range of 100 meters.<ref name="USW" /> It could also be used without an energy ball, as a blunt weapon.

The atlatls used by the [[Gungan Grand Army]] were carved from a naturally isolating wood by the [[Otoh Gunga Defense League]].<ref name="USW" />

==Behind the scenes==
A real-life [[Wikipedia:Atlatl|atlatl]] is a tool used for throwing spears farther. This tool has been in existence for over 25,000 years.<ref name="spear">{{WebCite|url=http://www.worldatlatl.org/WhatisAtlatl.html|text=the World Atlatl Association|archivedate=20060616133611}}</ref>
