'''5389''' was the designation of a [[stormtrooper]] who served with a [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|remnant]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]], stationed at a [[Nevarro Imperial base|base]] on the [[Volcano|volcanic]] [[planet]] [[Nevarro]].<ref name="Chapter 12" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|12|the appearance of 5389}}</ref> some infiltrators [[Second skirmish on Nevarro|sabotaged]] the base's cooling system, which stopped the control of Nevarro's [[Magma|lava]] around the compound. 5389 and another stormtrooper, [[5387]], were called upon by one of their stormtrooper comrades to go with [[trooper]]. 5389 and 5387 followed suit, not realizing that the infiltrators had been watching from afar.<ref name="Chapter 12" />

==Behind the scenes==
5389 appeared in "[[Chapter 12: The Siege]]" of [[Jon Favreau]]'s [[2019]] [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', directed by [[Carl Weathers]]<ref name="Chapter 12" /> and released on [[November 20]], [[2020]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-november-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in November 2020}}</ref>
