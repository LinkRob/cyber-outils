'''Corellian bloodstripes''' were a series of [[color|red]] or gold horizontal bars that could be added to a pair of [[clothing|pants]] running down each leg.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> 

By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Han Solo]] had a pair of pants with gold [[Corellian]] bloodstripes that he wore when during a [[Raid on Kessel|heist]] of [[Coaxium]] from the [[planet]] [[Kessel]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> Han continued to wear pants with the bloodstripes including during a [[Royal Award Ceremony|ceremony]] when he was awarded a medal from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Film IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] found a pair of blue pants in a container aboard the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', an [[EML-850 light freighter]] that she stole from [[Sliro Barsha]]'s [[Westhill Palace]] in [[Canto Bight]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Corellian bloodstripes first appeared though unidentified on Han Solo's pants in the [[1977]] film, {{Film|IV}}.<ref name="Film IV" /> They were first identified in the [[canon]] continuity on the "Kessel runner pants" cosmetic that was exclusive to the Ultimate Edition of the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />  

In the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity, bloodstripes were a Corellian military award for conspicuous gallantry with red representing a first class award and gold representing a second class.<ref name="Corporate Sector Sourcebook">''[[Han Solo and the Corporate Sector Sourcebook]]''</ref>
