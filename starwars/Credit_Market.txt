The '''Credit Market''' was a structure that facilitated the automated trading of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the galactic market to sustain both the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[Galactic Civil War|war]] efforts.<ref name="Commander" />

The structure could be upgraded to allow for a greater credit procurement rate, credit storage capacity and structural integrity.<ref name="Commander" />

==Appearance==
The Credit Market comprised a tall, cylindrical fenced pedestal with two [[computer]]s. It was operated by a [[protocol droid]].<ref name="Commander" />
