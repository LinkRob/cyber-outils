'''Celwis's largest moon''' was located<ref name="Greater Good" /> in the [[Celwis system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> within the [[Unknown Regions]] and [[orbit]]ed the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[planet]] [[Celwis]], which was controlled by the [[Chiss]] [[Xodlak family]].<ref name="Greater Good" /> It was situated in grid square F-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The terrestrial [[moon]] had a cliff wall concealing the abandoned [[Chiss frigate]] ''[[Midsummer]]'', which had been left behind after the Xodlak family was demoted from its position of a [[Ruling Families|ruling family]] to that of one of the forty [[Great Families]].<ref name="Greater Good" /> Around [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> the ''Midsummer'' was reactivated as the Xodlak wished to use it to [[Hoxim incident|claim]] a [[Hoxim|world]] that contained a vast supply of the [[metal]] [[nyix]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Behind the scenes==
Celwis's largest moon was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', the second volume in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''The Ascendancy Trilogy'']].<ref name="Greater Good" />
