A '''Agamar gunslinger shirt''' was a type of [[Clothing|shirt]] woven<ref name="Outlaws" /> on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Agamar]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> It was designed to be simple as to not limit a being's mobility during a [[blaster]] fight. The shirt was [[Color|blue]] with a high collar and patterned shoulders. The upper-right chest of the shirt had a silver colored rectangle on top of a blue patch.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[human]] [[merchant]] [[Berek Keehro]] had at least one Agamar gunslinger shirt for sale while operating [[Sirei's Stall]] in the [[Market District (Mirogana)|Market District]] of the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] purchased an Agamar gunslinger shirt from Keehro for 1000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
An Agamar gunslinger shirt appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. When equipped, the shirt increases the [[adrenaline]] the player gains when they land a hit with [[Kay's blaster]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
