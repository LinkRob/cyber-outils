'''Epoxatal''' was a substance used to fill cracks in the [[hull]] of a [[starship]]. Under [[Han Solo]]'s ownership, the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' sported many epoxatal-filled cracks, which Solo did not bother to conceal.<ref name="Manual">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>
