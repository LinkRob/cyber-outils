In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''battle''' took place between the [[Paccian Governance]], the nation of the [[Paccosh]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]], and several members of the [[Grysk]] warlike species who had attempted to [[Slavery|enslave]] the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] [[Sunrise]]'s [[The Magys's people|inhabitants]]. The battle was a follow-up to the [[Paccian slaughter|Paccosh slaughter]] of most Grysks at Sunrise.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final installment in the ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]'' by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
