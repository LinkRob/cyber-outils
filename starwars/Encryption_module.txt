An '''encryption module''' (also known as a '''decryption module''') was a palm-sized encryption device used to encode messages sent over [[comlink]] or through subspace. [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]]'s [[D-Squad]] was sent on a [[Mission to steal a Separatist encryption module|mission]] to steal the module from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Secret Weapons" />
