[[General/Legends|General]] '''Chymoelaan''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] who served in the [[Imperial Army/Legends|Imperial Army]].<ref name="Lunch" /> [[Time/Legends|Sometime]] between [[0 ABY/Legends|0 ABY]] and [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]],<ref name="Lunch date">"[[Lunch Break]]" takes place in between when [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] learns of [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker's]] name and their [[Duel on Cloud City/Legends|duel on Cloud City]]. The former event occurs in [[Vader's Quest 1|''Vader's Quest'' 1]], which takes place zero [[Standard year/Legends|years]] after {{Film|IV}} and corresponds to [[0 ABY/Legends|0 ABY]], per ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''. ''The New Essential Chronology'' also dates the [[Lightsaber duel/Legends|duel]] on [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City]] to [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]]. Therefore, "Lunch Break" must have taken place in between those two [[Standard year/Legends|years]].</ref> Chymoelaan sent a pressing request of the presence of the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] in a meeting room.<ref name="Lunch" />

As that [[Standard day/Legends|night]] happened to be Vader's [[Birthday/Legends|birthday]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith/Legends|Sith Lord]] hoped that his [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] had planned a party for him and anticipated that they had set up the meeting to surprise him. Vader was dismayed to see only General Chymoelaan and realized by the meeting's end that no one had planned to do so. Later, he wrote about the incident in his [[Vader's diary|diary]].<ref name="Lunch" />

Chymoelaan had balding [[Color/Legends|black]] [[Hair/Legends|hair]] and a mole on their right cheek. As a general in service to the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]], they wore an [[Imperial officer's tunic/Legends|Imperial officer's tunic]] adorned with a [[Rank insignia plaque/Legends|rank insignia plaque]] on its left side.<ref name="Lunch" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chymoelaan appeared in a flashback in "[[Lunch Break]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] written and illustrated by [[Jonathan Adams]] and released in the [[Star Wars Tales 16|sixteenth]] issue of ''[[Star Wars Tales]]'',<ref name="Lunch" /> published on [[June 25]], [[2003]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/53994/star_wars_tales_1999_16|text=Star Wars Tales (1999) #16}}</ref>
