'''BT-9093''' was a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] of the [[First Order]]. Present during the First Order's [[Blockade of Kashyyyk|blockade]] of [[Kashyyyk]], BT-9093 and another stormtrooper were ordered to locate [[SN-1208]], who had failed to report in<ref name="Ghosts of Kashyyyk, Part 3" /> after being incapacitated by the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]].<ref name="Ghosts of Kashyyyk, Part 2">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=28|story=Ghosts of Kashyyyk, Part 2}}</ref> The two stormtroopers encountered the [[porg]] [[Terbus]], who led them into a trap set up by Chewbacca.<ref name="Ghosts of Kashyyyk, Part 3" />
