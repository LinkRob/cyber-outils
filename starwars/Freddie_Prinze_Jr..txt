'''Freddie Prinze Jr.''' (born [[March 8]], [[1976]])<ref name="This Week! Ep7" /> is an American actor. He voiced the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] character [[Kanan Jarrus]] in the animated series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', which aired on [[Disney XD]], and the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].

His wife, [[Sarah Michelle Gellar]], appeared on the ''Rebels'' show with him for the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Two|second season]], portraying the [[Seventh Sister]].
