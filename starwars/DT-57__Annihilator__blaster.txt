The '''DT-57 "Annihilator" blaster'''<ref name="SWVE" /> was a model<ref name="Beginnings" /> of [[blaster rifle]]<ref name="General Grievous" /> that could be wielded with one [[Limb|hand]], much like a [[blaster pistol]].<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" /> Once such Annihilator, named the [[Grievance Striker]],<ref name="Striker">{{Destiny|set=Convergence|cardname=Grievance Striker|link=http://swdestinydb.com/card/09169}}</ref> was owned by [[General]] [[Grievous]] during the [[Clone Wars]], though it was ultimately used by [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] to [[Death|kill]] the dreaded [[cyborg]] during the [[Battle of Utapau]].<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" />
