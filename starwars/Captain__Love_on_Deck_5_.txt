A fictional '''Captain''' in the [[holodrama]] ''[[Love on Deck 5]]''. The series released at least three parts<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> In the first part of the drama the Captain hears from the [[Musician|singer]] [[Bellis (Love on Deck 5)|Bellis]] that they always find themselves eating alone. To which the Captain respondes by inviting Bellis to dinner. Bellis accepted hoping to make the [[chief officer (Love on Deck 5)|chief officer]] jealous. Despite their efforts, things did not work out between Bellis and the chief officer. Bellis however booked another ticket to travel on the ship the following day and informed the Captain that they were going to bring the Captain on stage to play the [[valachord]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
