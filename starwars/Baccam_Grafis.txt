[[General]] '''Baccam Grafis''' was a [[human]] who was head of [[Ordnance and Supply]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and served as part of [[Alliance High Command]]. In [[1 BBY]], he was with [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] in the [[Great Temple]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]] when she was informed that there was [[Rogue One|rebel]] activity on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]].

In [[0 ABY]], Grafis wrote a report for Mothma on the net losses made by the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance fleet]] during a [[Skirmish in the Recluse's Nebula|skrimish]] in the [[Recluse's Nebula]]. The [[1 ABY|following year]], Grafis attended a meeting on the Alliance's [[Mako-Ta Space Docks]] where Mothma proposed that the rebels procure more [[Mon Calamari]] [[MC-series star cruiser|cruisers]] and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] proposed a plan for how to achieve that.

==Biography==
===Council Member===
{{Quote|These individuals will make up the rebel council. I know them well, which is why I know that putting them in the same room will be&hellip; messy.|A journal entry on Grafis and the other members of the rebel council written by Mon Mothma|<ref name="RebelFiles" />}}
[[File:RebelCouncilDocument-RF.png|thumb|right|250px|Baccam Grafis was selected as a member of the Rebel Council.]]
Baccam Grafis was a [[human]] [[general]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] who served as the head of [[Ordnance and Supply]] on [[Alliance High Command]]. He was part of the [[Massassi Group]] based out of the [[Great Temple]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]] and was responsible for the procurement of essential material for the Rebellion, pursuing lines of supply for [[starship]]-grade [[fuel]] and weaponry.<ref name="Visual Guide" /> Grafis was well known to [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] of the [[Imperial Senate]] and after she resigned from the Senate and made a [[Declaration of Rebellion]]<ref name="RebelFiles" /> in [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> he was picked to be part of the [[Alliance Cabinet|Rebel Council]] that would lead the alliance.<ref name="RebelFiles" />

Mothma wrote a journal entry stating that she assumed that putting the various council members in the same room would be messy based on what she knew of them. Grafis's inclusion on the council was also recorded on a classified intelligence form, with both the form and the journal entry coming to be included in the collection of documents known as ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="RebelFiles" />

===Scarif===
[[File:BaccamGrafis-RO.png|thumb|left|300px|Baccam Grafis was with Mon Mothma when she learned of the rebels on Scarif.]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Dates">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' and [[Star Wars (2015) 45|''Star Wars'' (2015) 45]] to [[1 BBY]] and [[1 ABY]] respectively.</ref> Graifs walked Mothma and a number of other members of Alliance High Command through the temple after the council failed to agree upon launching an [[Battle of Scarif|attack]] on [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Military|forces]] on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]] in order to obtain the [[Death Star plans|plans]] for the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]]. The group halted when<ref name="RO">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> [[Private]] [[Tenzigo Weems]]<ref name="Visual Guide" /> chased after them calling for Mothma.<ref name="RO" />

The private then revealed to them that they had intercepted Imperial reports of rebel activity on Scarif, which had been caused by [[Rogue One]], a group of rebels that had defied the cabinet's decision and gone to steal the plans alone. Weems also informed Mothma and the others that [[Admiral]] [[Raddus]] of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|rebel fleet]] was already preparing to aid them. The combined rebel forces then successfully obtained the plans for the [[Death Star]]<ref name="RO" /> and destroyed it at the [[Battle of Yavin]].

===Post Yavin===
[[File:RebelCouncil-2015StarWars45.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Baccam Grafis attended a meeting at the Mako-Ta Space Docks.]]
In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="RecluseDate">In ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', Grafis's report on the [[Skirmish in the Recluse's Nebula]] is placed between documentation covering the [[Assault on Cymoon]] and the [[Battle of Vrogas Vas]], both of which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[0 ABY]].</ref> Grafis filed a report for the attention of Mothma concerning a [[Skirmish in the Recluse's Nebula|skirmish]] between the Alliance and the Empire that had taken place in the [[Recluse's Nebula]]. In the report, he warned Mothma that the skirmish had been a net loss for the Alliance fleet and that the rebels would exhaust their naval resources within eighteen [[Standard month|month]]s, give or take three months, if they continued on their current trajectory. He then listed the losses from the skirmish and his departments accounting and associated adjustments for it. This document was also eventually added to ''The Rebel Files''.<ref name="RebelFiles" />

In [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Dates" /> Grafis attended a meeting at the [[Mako-Ta Space Docks]], held by Mothma. She proposed that the Alliance should procure more [[Mon Calamari]] [[Star Cruiser]]s for their cause. There, Grafis stood next to General [[Pitt Onoran]] as they watched [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] convey her plan to prompt a [[Mutiny on Mon Cala|mutiny]] of [[Mon Cala]]'s [[Mon Cala Mercantile Fleet|merchant fleet]]. Mothma approved of this<ref name="SW 45">[[Star Wars (2015) 45|''Star Wars'' (2015) 45]]</ref> and Organa was soon successful, obtaining many [[cruiser]]s for the Alliance.<ref name="SW 49">[[Star Wars (2015) 49|''Star Wars'' (2015) 49]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Grafis had light skin, [[Color|blue]] [[eye]]s, and was balding with a large white [[Hair|beard]]<ref name="Visual Guide" /> by 2 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].<ref name="Dates" />

==Equipment==
While in the Great Temple,<ref name="RO" /> Grafis wore a tan [[Clothing|shirt]] with a tan jacket under a [[tactician's overcoat]], which was an [[Alderaanian]] custom. His outfit also included a brown [[belt]], tan pants, and a rank plaque with five red pips, indicate his rank of general.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baccam Grafis first appeared in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="RO" /> The character was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].
