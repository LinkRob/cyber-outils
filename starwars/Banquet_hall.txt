The '''banquet hall''' of the [[Pijali monarchy]]'s [[Pijali palace|royal palace]] was located on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]. In [[40 BBY]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] announced his intention not to ratify the [[Governance Treaty]] initiative while in the banquet hall, over breakfast. The others present squabbled over his decision for over an [[Standard hour|hour]] after he left.

==Description==
The banquet hall was a section of the [[Pijali monarchy]]'s [[Pijali palace|royal palace]] on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]. It had a large table and several gilded chairs.<ref name="MA" />

==History==
{{Quote|We need to know whether the coronation and treaty ceremony are taking place as scheduled. And we need that information as soon as possible. Otherwise, I can't take proper security measures.|Deren, in the banquet hall|<ref name="MA" />}}
[[File:Qui-Gon-Jinn-Databank.jpg|thumb|left|240px|Qui-Gon Jinn announced his intention to not ratify the Governance Treaty while in the banquet hall.]]
In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' to [[40 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], visited Pijal not long before the [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]] of the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]], intending to ratify the [[Governance Treaty]] initiative on the [[Galactic Republic]]'s behalf. However, Jinn became queasy with fulfilling his [[Mission to Pijal|mission's]] mandate after discovering foul play by the parties involved and experiencing violent [[Force vision]]s of the upcoming coronation.<ref name="MA" />

Jinn visited the banquet hall during breakfast and informed Pijal's [[Jedi]] [[lord]] [[regent]], [[Rael Averross]], as well as [[Minister]] [[Orth]], [[Royal guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] [[captain]] [[Deren]], Kenobi, and Fanry of his decision not to ratify the treaty following the [[Gender|princess's]] coronation. As Fanry looked on in amusement, Orth and Averross argued with Jinn in earnest over his decision. Then, for over an [[Standard hour|hour]] after Jinn left the banquet hall, those present continued to argue over the Jedi Master's announcement. While Orth and Averross berated Kenobi for what they felt was his Master's "unstable" nature, Fanry only found the situation amusing. Eventually, at the others' urging, Kenobi left to speak further with Jinn about his decision.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The banquet hall appeared in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
