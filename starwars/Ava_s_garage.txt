'''Ava's garage''' also known as just '''Ava's''' was store operated by the [[starship]] [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Ava Wobin]] in the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The store consisted of a single building built into a rock formation near a landing pad at the [[Mirogana Spaceport]]. The building had a large curved opening that could be sealed with seven vertical shutters. Above the opening were three signs, all written in [[Aurebesh]], which read "Ava's Garage," "Ava's," and "Ship Services". Inside the building was a counter that Wobin conducted business from and an [[Raven 6]] [[arcade machine]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], [[Kay Vess]] utilized the shop's services to upgrade the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}
