A '''fire extinguisher''' was a tool that sprayed [[Color|white]] foam or gas with the ability to put out [[fire]]s.

During the [[Battle of Sullust]] of the [[Clone Wars]], [[R2-D2]] utilised his built-in fire extinguisher to rescue [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his [[astromech droid]] [[R4-P17]] by spraying its contents over [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|their]] scorched [[starfighter]].<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref> 

Members of [[Coruscant Rescue Ops]] utilized [[backpack]] [[suppression unit]]s which were portable tanks made from lightweight [[Metal|alloy]] that held fire retardant sprayed from handheld nozzles.<ref name="SWHC28">{{HelmetCollectionCite|28|Helmets}}</ref>

After [[Rescue of Princess Leia|escaping]] from the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first Death Star]] in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> R2-D2 made use of the tool to put out a small fire that was started on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' after it took several hits from pursuing [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="GA" /> he again made use of his built-in extinguisher while on [[Cloud City]]. This time it was used as a smokescreen to blind the [[stormtrooper]]s that were chasing them.<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In [[LEGO Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (Scholastic)|''LEGO Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith'']], after losing his [[Duel on Mustafar|duel]] with his former [[Jedi Master|master]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] on [[Mustafar]], [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]], saddened, sat too close to [[Magma|lava]] and caught his pants on [[fire]]. However, [[Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] arrived just in [[time]] to save him by putting out the fire with a fire extinguisher, stating that he liked his [[Sith Lord]]s on the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]], not burned to a crisp. Despite this, Vader was forced to henceforth wear [[Darth Vader's armor|armor]] due to his burns.<ref name="LEGO III">[[LEGO Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (Scholastic)|''LEGO Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith'' picture book]]</ref>
