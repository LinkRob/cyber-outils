The '''''Death Star Owner's Workshop Manual''''' was a [[Book|manual]] that helped with the construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]]. It was comprised of at least 327 parts, and the cover for part 327 was collected by [[Poe Dameron]] in a [[Unidentified book|vehicle guidebook]].<ref name="Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Death Star Owner's Workshop Manual'' was mentioned in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''.<ref name="Vehicles" /> Although the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] was created by the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> ''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]'' depicts the ''Death Star Owner's Workshop Manual'' with the emblem of the [[First Order]].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref>
