The '''Belnar Insurrections''' were a series of events<ref name="BTC" /> that took place sometime by the [[Standard year|year]] [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> taking place in the vicinity of the [[Colonies]]' [[Belnar system]].<ref name="Systems">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> During the events, the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[governor]] [[Everi Chalis]] punished the crew of the [[starship]] ''[[Mandible]]'', which had suffered an accident while carrying volatile cargo under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of a [[Alcohol|drunken]] [[Unidentified captain (Mandible)|captain]]. Rather than admit this act of incompetence on the part of one of its [[Imperial officer|officers]], the Galactic Empire instead credited the punishment to Chalis as to show the consequences for the accident. Chalis later mentioned the Belnar Insurrections to the crew of an [[Unidentified Imperial heavy freighter|Imperial heavy freighter]]<ref name="BTC" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> using the punishment of the ''Mandible''{{'s}} crew as a threat to make the [[freighter]] change course.<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Belnar Insurrections were mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
