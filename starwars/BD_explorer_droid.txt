'''BD explorer droids''', simply known as '''BD units''', were a model of [[explorer droid]].

==Characteristics==
BD units were companion [[droid]]s designed to assist with operations in remote and dangerous locations across the galaxy; programmed to be the ideal assistant to researchers or explorers operating alone in the field, they were designed to traverse all types of terrain.<ref name="Databank" /> As a result of their manufacturer collapsing, BD units were a relatively rare sight in the galaxy.<ref name="Game Informer" /> A type of similar<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> looking and similarly named droid, the [[BDX droid]], also existed but was much larger in scale.<ref name="Exploring Galaxy Edge">{{Twitter|username=starwars|url=status/1776413380393517073|name=[[Star Wars]]|date=2024-04-05|quote=Playful, curious, and cute. BDX droids are exploring Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge. Guests may encounter them around Black Spire Outpost in @Disneyland Park for a limited time during Season of the Force.|archivefile=File:BDXDroid-reveal.png}}</ref>

==History==
One such droid was [[BD-1]], who accompanied [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]], and later the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]]<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order" /> in [[14 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> Another BD droid was in a [[Mapuzo safehouse|safehouse]] on the planet [[Mapuzo]].<ref name="Designing the Galaxy">{{HomeVideoCite|set=Obi-Wan Kenobi: The Complete Series|featurette=Designing the Galaxy}}</ref> Another BD unit was [[TA-418]], who assisted [[Doctor]] [[Eustacia Okka]], former [[professor]] at the [[Shadow University]] institution, during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Aphra 1" /> [[BD-72]] was another BD unit who was under the ownership of [[Peli Motto]]<ref name="BOBFChapter5">{{BOBF|Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian}}</ref> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="DateBoba">{{BoBFDate|5}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The BD unit first appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''.<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order" /> It made its live action debut in "[[Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian]],"<ref name="BOBFChapter5" /> the fifth episode of the [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'', which was released on [[January 26]], [[2022]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-january-2022|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in January 2022}}</ref>
