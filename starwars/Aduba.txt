'''Aduba''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Bomber Command" /> located in the [[Aduba system]] in the [[Bheriz sector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> along the [[Triellus Trade Route]]<ref name="TFABG" /> on the edge<ref name="RO">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-rogue-one|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Rogue One''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Rogue One?}}</ref> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] in grid square U-11 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of ''[[Cobalt Hammer]]'' [[pilot]] [[Finch Dallow]]. Dallow flew crop-dusters and [[spotter rig]]s on Aduba before qualifying for a post in the [[New Republic Scout Service]].<ref name="Bomber Command" />
