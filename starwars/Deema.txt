'''Deema''' was one of the many people that the debtor [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] owed [[Currency|money]] to<ref name="Announcement" /> by [[5 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|107}}</ref> After fleeing his [[Andor household|home]] on the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]] as a fugitive, Andor took part in a [[Aldhani heist|heist]] on the planet [[Aldhani]], receiving a cut of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] payroll that was stolen. He then returned to Ferrix and gave twelve thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] from his cut to the [[Caleen Salyard|store]] owner [[Bix Caleen]] to pay all off of his outstanding debts, including what he owed to the lenders Deema, [[Brasso]], [[Nurchi]], and [[Xanwan]].<ref name="Announcement" />
