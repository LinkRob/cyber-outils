'''Brashan''' was a [[governor]] of the<ref name="Victory's Price" /> [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="Timelines" /> [[planet]] [[Fedovoi End]] and a member of the [[Yomo Council]], a breakaway faction from the [[Galactic Empire]] after its defeat at the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> With the Yomo Council occupying Fedovoi End, the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], a unit loyal to the Empire, was sent to [[Destruction of Fedovoi End|devastate]] the planet<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[5 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> as a response to the breakaway group's [[treason]] against the Empire. In a message to the world's population, [[Soran Keize]], the [[commanding officer]] of the [[fighter wing]], cited Brashan and [[General]] [[Tuluh]] as among the individuals the unit was going to erase from history during its attack. The 204th then proceeded to [[poison]] Fedovoi End and its inhabitants by opening up toxic [[gas]] pockets in the planet's [[Water|ice]] caps.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brashan was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the third and final volume in [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
