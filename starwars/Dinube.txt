'''Dinube''' was a [[Harch]] who was an [[Elder (Path of the Open Hand)|Elder]] in the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] and was present in the [[Path compound]] on [[Dalna]] before and during the events of the [[Night of Sorrow]]<ref name="POV" /> in [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> When the leader of the Path, a woman called [[Elecia Zeveron|the Mother]], fell ill, Elder [[Jichora]] sent for Dinube, who informed the Mother that she had experienced a seizure.<ref name="POV" />

Later, [[Yana Ro]] encountered Dinube in the nearby [[City|town]] of [[Ferdan]], standing on a [[Koyo fruit|koyo-fruit]] box and berating passers-by for ignoring what the Path believed was the plight of [[the Force]]. Yana had previously considered Dinube a moderate voice in the Path community, but saw this behavior as just one sign of changes in the Path's beliefs and methods. Back in the Path's [[Underground caverns|underground cavern]] system, Yana found Dinube checking over [[Detonite charge|detonite explosives]], which the Elder claimed were intended to open up further caves for Path use.<ref name="POV" />
