The '''Cophrigin system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Ash Worlds]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> to which [[viper probe droid]]s were deployed during<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> [[Project Swarm]],<ref name="Swarm">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 37|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 37]]</ref> an initiative by the [[Galactic Empire]] to discover the location of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Echo Base|hidden headquarters]] following the Imperial defeat at the [[Battle of Yavin]]<ref name="Odyssey" /> in [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cophrigin was mentioned in [[Jack Mitchell]]'s [[2021]] [[original trilogy]] adaptation, ''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''.<ref name="Odyssey" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Cophrigin system/Legends|Cophrigin system]] and the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Cophrigin V]] were introduced in the comic story "[[Extinction]]," which was written by [[Ron Marz]], illustrated by [[Claudio Castellini]], and published in two parts, with the first included in [[Star Wars Tales 1|Star Wars Tales #1]]<ref name="Extinction">{{Tales|issue1=1|issue2=2|Extinction}}</ref> on [[September 29]], [[1999]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/97-289/Star-Wars-Tales-1|text=Star Wars Tales #1}}</ref>
