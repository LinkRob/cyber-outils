'''Fathiers''' were a [[species]] of quadrupeds that resided in [[the galaxy]]. They were often used as mounts, and wealthy inhabitants of [[Kergans]] and [[Cantonica]] rode them in [[Fathier racing|competition]].

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Sounds like the feisty filly in the corner stall is having trouble sleeping. Did you do the endurance exercises with her in the double-gravity chamber today? You know fathiers need heavy exercise when they're cooped up on a ship like this.''"<br />"''Sorry. I had to clean the reflux combusters&mdash;''"<br />"''No excuses. Each of these fathiers is worth more than three years of your wages.|[[Ulina]] and [[Teal]]|<ref name="The Legends of Luke Skywalker">''[[The Legends of Luke Skywalker]]''</ref>}}
[[File:FathierLATimes.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A fathier head looking out of its stall in the Cantonica stables]]
A quadruped [[species]], fathiers were distinguished by their long [[Limb|legs]], [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]], and large [[ear]]s, which dissipated body heat. Fathiers could run at great speeds (up to 75 kilometers per hour) across various terrains, including beaches, fields, and cliff faces.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Their speed made them sought after by wealthy residents of [[the galaxy]], and the creatures were viewed as graceful and majestic.<ref name="SWME">''[[Star Wars Made Easy]]''</ref> On average, fathiers stood three [[meter]]s high at the shoulder; people seeing one in person for the first time were often shocked by their size.<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> When cooped up, fathiers required endurance exercises, so fathier [[transport]]s were equipped with [[double-gravity chamber]]s.<ref name="The Legends of Luke Skywalker" /> They were known for their stinky, spicy smell.<ref name="Rules of the Game">{{StoryCite|story=Rules of the Game|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref>

==Behavior==
[[File:Father-TLJTUG.png|thumb|right|250px|A fathier in the stables of Canto Bight]]
Fathiers could be domesticated, and they were used as mounts in competition. The inhabitants of the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]] exploited them in highly popular [[Fathier racing|races]] on the [[Canto Bight]] [[racetrack]]s, in which the animals suffered abuse.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==History==
In [[231 BBY]], a pair of fathiers were displayed at the [[Republic Fair]].<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref>
Around [[1 BBY]], the [[Spectres|Spectre]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]], while exploring the [[World Between Worlds]], came across a mysterious [[portal]] decorated with [[constellation]]-like images of fathiers. However, he did not examine nor enter it.<ref name="AWBW">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref>

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] used fathiers ridden by [[Tognath]] [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] in hit-and-run combat missions.<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref>

[[File:Fathier_rider_ARTWORK.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Tognath mercenaries ride fathiers.]]
A statue of a fathier could be found in the [[Lost Museum]] on [[Coruscant]]'s [[level 4]].<ref name="The Right Wrong Turn">[[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 25|''Star Wars Adventures'' (2017) 25]]</ref>

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Resistance]] operatives [[Finn]] and [[Rose Tico]] traveled to Cantonica in search of a [[Master Codebreaker]]. During their search, the two ran into trouble with the [[Canto Bight Police Department]], and they hid in the fathier stables at the Canto Bight racetrack. With the help of stable boy [[Temiri Blagg]], Finn and Tico let the fathiers loose, riding one to escape the [[Law enforcement agency|police force]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Fathiers in the galaxy==
{{Quote|I learned to hunt with bassa hounds. I learned to ride fathiers in competition. I hated all of it.|Lorica Demaris|<ref name="Join the Resistance">''[[Join the Resistance]]''</ref>}}
At some point in his life, the [[Corellian]] [[Mercurial Swift|Geb Teldar]] mucked the stall of a fathier for a living.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> [[Lorica Demaris]], a [[Zeltron]] from the planet [[Kergans]], learned to ride fathiers in competition, which she hated.<ref name="Join the Resistance" /> Fathiers were found across the galaxy, but their [[homeworld]] was considered a mystery.<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Fathier_BTS_D23.png|thumb|right|250px|A fathier in a stall in ''The Last Jedi'' behind-the-scenes footage from the D23 Expo]]<br />
Fathiers were created for the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], directed by [[Rian Johnson]]. They were first mentioned in the novel ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]].<ref name="Empire's End" />
