The '''Chrelythiumn system''' was a [[star system]] located within [[Wild Space]], far beyond the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. At some point during the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Mortis monolith]] appeared somewhere deep in the system and broadcasted an ancient [[Jedi]] [[distress signal]]. In response, the [[Jedi High Council]] sent [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] to investigate the source, where they were to rendezvous with a heavily armed [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|Jedi cruiser]] to defend against a possible [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] trick.

==Description==
Located far beyond the [[Outer Rim Territories]], the Chrelythiumn system was a [[star system]] situated in [[Wild Space]], at K-2 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. The [[Mortis monolith]], a giant, dark [[Color|red]] [[crystal]]line structure,<ref name="BMF 68" /> was located within the system, which provided access to the ethereal plane of [[Mortis]].<ref name="Overlords" />

==History==
[[File:Ghosts_of_Mortis_Rex_Venator.png|thumb|left|300px|A ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer after arriving within the Chrelythiumn system.]]
At some point, during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the Mortis monolith had appeared somewhere deep within the Chrelythiumn system.<ref name="Overlords" /> After its appearance, the [[Jedi Order]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] received an ancient [[distress signal]] that had not been used in over 2,000 [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="DB">{{Databank|mortis|Mortis}}</ref> In response to the distress signal, the [[Jedi High Council]] dispatched [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], and his [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], to the Chrelythiumn system to investigate the source. However, the Republic had sent a heavily armed [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] to assist the [[Jedi]] in the case it was a Separatist trick.<ref name="Overlords" />

Upon the [[starship]]s' arrival, the Jedi and the [[Star Destroyer]]'s personnel, including [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], could not pick up each other on their scopes, which confused both groups. Suddenly, the Jedi [[shuttle]]'s communications signals were cut off, and the Mortis monolith appeared to them, drawing them inside of it to the mysterious [[The Force|Force]] realm of [[Mortis]].<ref name="Overlords" /> After an [[Mission to Mortis|adventure]] that saw the [[death]]s of the three godlike [[Force wielder]]s that lived on Mortis, Skywalker, Kenobi, and Tano woke up back on board their shuttle inside the system. Regaining contact with Rex, they learned that to the outside [[universe]], the shuttle had apparently only been gone for a moment. After informing a confused Rex that they had been gone for a longer [[time]], Skywalker flew the shuttle to the Star Destroyer.<ref name="Ghosts of Mortis">{{TCW|Ghosts of Mortis}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Chrelythiumn system first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season three]] episode "[[Overlords]]."<ref name="Overlords" />
