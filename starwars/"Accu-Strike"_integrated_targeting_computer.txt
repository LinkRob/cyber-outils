The '''"Accu-Strike" integrated targeting computer''' was a device produced by [[Industrial Automaton]] and sold for 3,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] that combined a [[targeting computer]] and a [[Sensor array|sensor suite]], providing a constant stream of targeting data to its user. The information delivered by the device could be given to the user via audio or a heads-up display. More advanced versions of the device could incorporate [[Augmented vibro-motor|vibro-motors]] installed into the [[gauntlet]]s or [[glove]]s of [[armor]], connecting to the armor's [[helmet]] system. This feature could subtly adjust the user's [[Limb|hands]], improving accuracy.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Accu-Strike" integrated targeting computer was introduced in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
