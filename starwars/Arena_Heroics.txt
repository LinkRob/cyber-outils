The '''Arena Heroics''' was a pair of decorative [[K-16 Bryar Pistol]]s owned by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Clan Tal MT-97|standard blaster pistols]]. Sporting a [[Color|gold]], brown, and black color scheme, each of these [[Bryar pistol|Bryar]] [[blaster pistol]]s featured a raised golden insignia of [[Balada]] the [[Hutt]]s [[Hypernova-class control ship|''Hypernova''-class control ship]], the ''[[Hypernova]]'', on each side, as well as two curved golden points at the bottom of the grip.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind-the-scenes==
Arena Heroics was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Aran Tal's Clan Tal MT-97, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 1.0.0 update alongside Season 1 on [[June 4]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Arena Champion (Aran Tal)|Arena Champion]]. This wrap could be unlocked by reaching Tier 70 on the Season 1 Arena Pass' premium track.<ref name="Hunters" />
