'''G-90''', or '''Gee-Ninety''', was a [[Jayosbrand]] [[assassin droid]] who was part of the [[Assassin's Guild]] in the [[Imperial Era]]. The droid had a large spherical body with a head that protruded from the top.

==Biography==
[[File:VaderKillsG90-2020DarthVader21.png|thumb|left|200px|Darth Vader destroys Gee-Ninety for defying orders.]]
G-90,<ref name="Encyclopedia 2024" /> or Gee-Ninety, was hired to [[Assassination on Vincorba|assassinate]] a [[Unidentified pontiff|pontiff]], who had allied with the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate, alongside [[Chilla Zin]] and [[Loriach]] as a test by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] as [[Vader's crew|part]] of his quest to destroy the syndicate.<ref name="Vader 18" /> However, Vader later destroyed Gee-Ninety when the droid attempted to free [[prisoner]]s from [[torture]].<ref name="Vader 21" />

Rebuilt by [[Jul Tambor]] like many other [[droid]]s who had been slain by Vader,<ref name="Vader 30">[[Darth Vader (2020) 30|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 30]]</ref> Gee-Ninety became part of Tambor's [[Jul Tambor's forces|anti-Imperial organization]]. During the [[Mission to Brentaal IV (Sabé)|outreach mission]] on<ref name="Vader 29" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Vanguard Databank">{{Databank|vanguard-squadron|Vanguard Squadron}}</ref> [[Brentaal IV]], Gee-Ninety helped Tambor but warned him [[Sabé]] was a servant of Vader, which Tambor admitted he knew but was hoping to convince her to side with them.<ref name="Vader 29" /> After Tambor was discredited following a [[bombing of Kantiema]], his attempts to [[Uprising on Skako Minor|liberate]] [[Skako Minor]] collapsed. Gee-Ninety traveled to Skako Minor, where he was destroyed by Sabé after she had returned twelve [[Skakoan]] [[refugee]]s to the planet.<ref name="Vader 32">[[Darth Vader (2020) 32|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 32]]</ref>
