"'''Este Meza: Lucasfilm Events'''" was the eighth episode of the [[Disney+]] documentary series [[Wikipedia:One Day at Disney Shorts|''One Day at Disney (Shorts)'']], which included behind-the-scenes looks at the jobs of various [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] employees. The episode featured [[Lucasfilm]] Senior Events Manager [[Este Meza]], focusing on the aspect of his job where he operated [[BB-8]], planned character exhibitions, and planned ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Celebration]]s.<ref name="AppleTV" /> 

==Official description==
''Lucasfilm's Este Meza blurs the line between reality and fantasy for "Star Wars" fans through one-of-a-kind events and character appearances; Meza spends his days creating immersive experiences for fans of all ages around the world.''<ref name="AppleTV" />
