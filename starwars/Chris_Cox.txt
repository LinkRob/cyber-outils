'''Chris Cox''' has provided voices for several ''[[Star Wars]]'' video games.

==Works==
===Gameography===
*''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Jedi Academy]]'' &ndash; [[Wedge Antilles/Legends|Wedge Antilles]]
*''[[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron III: Rebel Strike]]'' &ndash; [[Admiral/Legends|Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar/Legends|Ackbar]], Imperial Officer 2, [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]], Wedge Antilles, Wingman 5
*''[[Star Wars: Battlefront]]'' &ndash; Alliance Infantry, CIS Infantry, CIS Officer, Gungan Infantry
*''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]'' &ndash; [[Gelesi]], Checkpoint Guard, [[Lootra]], [[Mandalorian]] Sentry, [[Peragus Mining Facility Dock Officer]], [[Galactic Republic]] [[Soldier]] (Credited as "Additional Voices")
*''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]'' &ndash; Rebel Trooper, X-Wing Pilot, Additional Voices
*''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' &ndash; Additional voices
*[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor|''Star Wars Jedi: Survivor'']] - Additional voices<ref name="Survivor"> [[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]</ref>
