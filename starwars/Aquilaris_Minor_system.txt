The '''Aquilaris Minor system''' was a [[star system]] located in grid square M-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] within the [[Grumani sector]]. The system was part of both the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western" /> The Aquilaris Minor system partly shared its name with both the [[Aquilaris system]] in the [[Sanbra sector]] of the Outer Rim<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Aquilaris Classic]] [[podracing]] [[Racetrack|course]], which the [[Neidorian]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Paghee'Dhe Dahglyn]] had won a race on by the [[time]] they participated on the [[Hydian-Hutta Podracing Circuit]]<ref name="BV">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> around [[10 BBY]].<ref name="BVDate">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Devil_Seven.jpg|thumb|right|300px|alt=The starfighter Devil Seven and another silhouetted starfighter blast off from the surface of Aquilaris Minor into the Aquilaris Minor system, where several other celestial bodies are visible in space.|The Aquilaris Minor system originated in the comic book ''Knight Errant: Deluge''.]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Aquilaris Minor system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Aquilaris Minor system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[Knight Errant: Deluge 2|second issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Knight Errant: Deluge]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book series]], which was written by [[John Jackson Miller]], illustrated by [[Iban Coello]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Deluge">[[Knight Errant: Deluge 2|''Knight Errant: Deluge'' 2]]</ref> on [[September 21]], [[2011]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/17-797/Star-Wars-Knight-Errant-Deluge-2|text=Star Wars: Knight Errant&mdash;Deluge #2}}</ref>

Although the comic identified the [[Star system/Legends|star system]] it depicted as the pre-existing [[Aquilaris system/Legends|Aquilaris system]],<ref name="Deluge" /> it was later [[Retroactive continuity|retconned]] into being a new separate system identified as the Aquilaris Minor system in an update to the StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Fry.<ref name="Essential Atlas Online Companion">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> Fry confirmed that this was due to mapping discrepancies between the placement of Aquilaris in ''The Essential Atlas'' and ''Knight Errant: Deluge''.<ref name="Atlas">{{JCFCite|url=posts/31618818|board=Literature|thread=The Essential Atlas and Galactic Cartography: Official Discussion|user=[[Jason Fry|jasonfy]]|date=2011-03-26|time=10:52 PM|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20191013233754/https://boards.theforce.net/threads/the-essential-atlas-and-galactic-cartography-official-discussion.21938930/page-163|quote=The new "Minor" worlds were invented to account for mapping discrepancies between the Atlas and Knight Errant.}}</ref>
