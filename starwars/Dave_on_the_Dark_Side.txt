"'''Dave on the Dark Side'''" is an interview with [[Dave Filoni]], executive producer of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', about the villains of the television series, such as [[the Grand Inquisitor]] and [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]]. It was conducted by [[Jonathan Wilkins]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 153|''Star Wars Insider'' 153]].
