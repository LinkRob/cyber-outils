'''Dave Wolverton''' ([[May 15]], [[1950s#1957|1957]] &ndash; [[January 14]], [[2022]])<ref name="Locus" /> was the author of the novel ''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]''. He also wrote a number of short stories, including ''[[Payback: The Tale of Dengar]]'', ''[[The Sand Tender: The Hammerhead's Tale]]'', and ''[[A Free Quarren in the Palace: Tessek's Tale]]'' for the ''[[Star Wars]]'' short-story anthologies ''[[Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina]]'', ''[[Tales from Jabba's Palace]]'' and ''[[Tales of the Bounty Hunters]]''. Wolverton also contributed to the ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Apprentice|Jedi Apprentice]]'' young-reader series, writing book one, [[Jedi Apprentice: The Rising Force|''The Rising Force'']], and co-writing book three, [[Jedi Apprentice: The Hidden Past|''The Hidden Past'']], with [[Jude Watson]]. He also wrote four of the [[Star Wars: Episode I Adventures|''Episode I Adventures'']], which include ''[[Episode I Adventures 5: The Ghostling Children]]'', ''[[Episode I Adventures 6: The Hunt for Anakin Skywalker]]'', ''[[Episode I Adventures 7: Capture Arawynne]]'' and ''[[Episode I Adventures 8: Trouble on Tatooine]]''. Wolverton also wrote eight books of the ''[[Star Wars Missions]]'' series.
