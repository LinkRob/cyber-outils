'''Besalisks''' were a [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] with four [[Limb|arms]] and an inflatable wattle that were [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Ojom]]. [[Dex's Diner]] owner and friend of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Dexter Jettster]], was a Besalisk, as was [[Pong Krell]], a [[Jedi Master]] who fell to the [[dark side of the Force]] after having a [[Force vision|vision]] of the [[Great Jedi Purge|Jedi's demise]] during the Clone Wars.

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|We're built sturdier than humans, with limbs to spare|[[Gadren]], following the loss of one of his arms at the [[Siege of Inyusu Tor]]|<ref name="BTC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Dexs_Diner_WotF.png|thumb|left|220px|Dexter Jettster owned Dex's Diner on Coruscant]]
Besalisks were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] of [[humanoid]]s with four [[Limb|arms]].<ref name="Besalisk" /> The superior arms dominated over the inferior ones.<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> They could average in height between 1.88<ref name="Dexter" /> and 2.36 [[meter]]s<ref name="Krell" /> and had green,<ref name="A New Dawn" /> orange, or brown [[color]]ed skin.<ref name="Princess Leia 2" /> Their large hands and feet both ended in four digits and large brown nails. Besalisks were thick-set, and could have bloated [[stomach]]s.<ref name="Krell" /> Besalisks also possessed a bony, three-pronged headcrest<ref name="A New Dawn" /> and an inflatable wattle.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> They had wide [[mouth]]s, and some Besalisks had large, pudgy lips,<ref name="Dexter" /> though not all. Besalisks could sport black<ref name="Krell" /> or brown<ref name="RR" /> [[hair]] on both their faces and their heads<ref name="Krell" /> and could have brown,<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters Alpha 1" /> orange,<ref name="Target Vader 2" /> or yellow [[eye]]s. At least one Besalisk was known to be [[Force-sensitive]].<ref name="Krell" />

==Society and culture==
Besalisks were known to be industrious and hardworking. A Besalisk saying was "If your neighbors trouble you, send your [[rodent]]s to their nest."<ref name="A New Dawn" />

==Besalisks in the galaxy==
{{Quote|Krell may do things differently, but he is effective in getting them done. He's a recognized war hero.''"<br />"''He may have had some victories, but have you see his casualty numbers? More troopers have been killed under his command than anyone else.|Rex and Fives discuss General Pong Krell|<ref name="Krell" />}}
Around the [[First Battle of Geonosis|start]] of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Dexter Jettster]] was a [[Coruscant]] [[Dex's Diner|diner]] owner and friend of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. During this [[time]], he successfully identified a [[Kamino saberdart]] to Kenobi, characterizing them by the cuts on their side due to his days as a [[prospector]]. He went on to inform Kenobi about the [[Kaminoan]]s who were reputable [[Cloning|cloners]] and kept to themselves but were welcoming to those who brought [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Attack of the Clones">{{Film|II}}</ref>

[[File:PongKrellDeadlyImposter-CM.png|thumb|right|220px|Pong Krell was a Besalisk Jedi whose unique physiology allowed him to wield two double-bladed lightsabers.]]
During the Clone Wars, [[Pong Krell]] was a [[Jedi Master]] known for his reputation as a successful [[general]] and for having the highest casualty rates of [[clone trooper]]s under his command.<ref name="Krell" /> Prior to the [[Battle of Umbara]], he [[Force vision|foresaw]] the [[Great Jedi Purge|demise]] of the [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]]. He immersed himself in the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] in order to survive and he attempted to [[Saboteur|sabotage]] the campaign, sending his clones on strategically inept missions, so he could become the [[Sith apprentice]] of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Head of State|leader]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. However, this was revealed by the clone [[trooper]]s of the [[501st Legion]] after he sent them against their fellow clones, tricked into believing they were [[Umbaran]]s disguised as clone troopers. He proceeded to cut down multiple clones with his [[double-bladed lightsaber]]s but was [[stun]]ned by clone trooper [[Tup]]. He was later executed by another clone, [[Dogma]].<ref name="Carnage of Krell">{{TCW|Carnage of Krell}}</ref>

By the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], a Besalisk subcommunity existed on the planet [[Gorse]]. This included [[Drakka]], owner of [[Drakka's Diner]], [[Lal Grallik]], enterprising chief of [[Moonglow Polychemical]], a [[mining]] and refinery company, and her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Gord Grallik]], the security chief. Both Gralliks were [[Death|murdered]] by [[Denetrius Vidian]] of the Empire.<ref name="A New Dawn" />

At the same time, several Besalisks worked for Vidian at [[Calcoraan Depot]] on the planet [[Calcoraan]].<ref name="A New Dawn" />

[[File:Galus_Vez.png|thumb|left|200px|Galus Vez was a starship parts dealer who owned the space station Osisis station.]]
Later during the Empire's reign, [[Galus Vez]] was a [[starship]] parts dealer who owned the [[space station]] [[Osisis Station]]. He worked with the [[Spectres]] from [[Lothal]] until he no longer wanted to be associated with them when Imperial inspections of his station increased. When they visited again, Vez refused to sell them the parts they needed and chose to challenge the groups [[pilot]], [[Hera Syndulla]], to a race. Should Syndulla win the race, Vez promised to hand over the parts, but should he win, then Vez would take the [[Ghost|rebels' ship]]. Syndulla raced him in the race and won, but Vez attempted to capture the rebels anyway. However, the Spectres anticipated this, and the group destroyed one of Vez's [[Transport|cargo vessels]] and used it as a distraction to disarm him and take out his men. They then forced Vez to hand over what they needed.<ref name="RR" />

[[Snes]] worked as muscle at [[Club Deeja]], an entertainment establishment in the [[city]] of [[Keren]] on the planet [[Naboo]], as well as at least two other Besalisks, one which was green skinned, and another whose skin was brown. Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the club's owner, [[Mul Sanaka]], tried to set a trap for the [[Alderaanian]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] within his club. Snes was given the job of disarming the princess and her companions before leading them inside. However, Snes accidentally revealed the trap to Organa, resulting in the princess attacking her and eventually defeating her and Sanaka.<ref name="Princess Leia 2" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Gadren]] was a Besalisk who served as a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[61st Mobile Infantry]]. During the [[Siege of Inyusu Tor]], he lost one of his four arms.<ref name="BTC" />

At some point before [[9 BBY]], a bearded Besalisk by the name of [[Wan'yek Whett]] served as the vile captain of a fishing crew aboard the starship ''[[Grapnel]].'' He was eventually tracked down and defeated by [[Skoova Stev]] on the [[third moon of Antine]], who excited a swarm of glumbrig eels into attacking the Dread Captain.<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Well, what would happen if you put this guy's helmet and put it on ''this'' guy?|[[George Lucas]] suggesting the combination of two alien models that would create Besalisks|<ref name="Attack of the Clones" />}}
[[File:DexterJettster-FF44.png|thumb|right|200px|The CGI model of Dexter Jettster used in ''Attack of the Clones''.]]
Besalisks first appeared in the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] through Dexter Jettster. They were never identified on-screen but were named in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] book ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]'', also from 2002, by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds (author)]]. For ''Attack of the Clones'', actor [[Ronald Falk]] was filmed for Dexter Jettster's scenes, digitally removed, and Jettster was replaced, made entirely with [[Wikipedia:Computer-generated imagery|computer-generated imagery]].<ref name="Attack of the Clones" />

Sometime during the production of ''Attack of the Clones'', the [[Lucasfilm]] art department had created multiple alien models in the hope one would go on to become Jettster. Inspected by director [[George Lucas]], he narrowed it down to two possible models. Asked for his opinion, Visual effects supervisor [[John Knoll]] preferred a different model, that looked similar to Besalisks, stating animation possibilities for its wattle. Lucas then suggested combining the top of one of the models he chose with the rest of the head of Knoll's preferred model. This became the Besalisk species. Many of the unused models became other species, such as [[Skakoan]]s.<ref name="Attack of the Clones" />

Concept art for [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]] exists depicting Besalisk Jedi Master [[Plun Kil]]. The artwork was created by lead character designer [[Kilian Plunkett]] and was intended for the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second]] season of the show, though it ultimately did not appear.<ref name="The General Episode Guide">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep408|text="The General" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}}</ref>
