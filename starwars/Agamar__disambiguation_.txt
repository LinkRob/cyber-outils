'''Agamar''' could refer to several things:

==[[Canon]]==
*[[Agamar]], a [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].
*[[Agamar system]], the [[star system]] of the planet [[Agamar]].
*[[Agamar garrison]], a [[Separatist holdouts|holdout]] of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[battle droid]]s located on the planet Agamar.

==[[Star Wars Legends|Legends]]==
*[[Agamar/Legends|Agamar]], an agriworld in the [[Mirgoshir system/Legends|Mirgoshir system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]].
*[[Agamar family]], a [[Human/Legends|Human]] family that lived on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] before the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].
*[[Agamar Resistance]], an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] resistance movement led by [[B'ante Hatcher]] on the planet [[Agamar/Legends|Agamar]] during the Galactic Civil War.
*[[Agamar slider]], a type of [[Diet/Legends|vegeterian]] [[Sandwich/Legends|sandwich]] from the planet Agamar.
*[[Gareth Agamar]], a [[TIE pilot]] of the Galactic Empire born to the [[Agamar family]].
