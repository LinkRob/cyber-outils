The '''Finkly Conference''' was a meeting that occurred<ref name="Axe" /> in [[5 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> When the [[ISB Supervisor]] [[Blevin]] needed to depart the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]] to oversee the [[takeover of the Preox-Morlana Corporate Sector]]<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> and the start of the [[Ferrix occupation]], he ended up missing the conference, resulting in a speech he was meant to give being agreed to be moved up. After returning to [[ISB headquarters office]] on Coruscant, Blevin spoke with several members of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] about what he had missed during his time away on the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]]. One of them informed the supervisor that he had missed the Finkly Conference and that his speech had been moved up.<ref name="Axe" />

==Behind the scenes==
