'''Dirjo''' was a type of cleaning substance that could be used to clean weapon parts. In [[3 BBY]], while [[Bix Caleen]] was cleaning a [[blaster pistol]] at their [[Axis network safehouse|safehouse]] on [[Coruscant]], [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] suggested that Caleen use Dirjo to remove the tarnish of the pistol. Caleen responded by stated there wasn't much left.<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dirjo was mentioned in "[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]" the fourth episode of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the live-action series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', which was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" /> on [[April 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref>
