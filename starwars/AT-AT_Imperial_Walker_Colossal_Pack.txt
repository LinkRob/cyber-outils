The '''''AT-AT Imperial Walker'' Colossal Pack''' is a Limited Edition scenario pack released on [[November 11]], [[2005]] for the [[Star Wars Miniatures]] Game, produced by [[Wizards of the Coast]]. This was the only miniature for the entire line that was released with the "colossal" size category. It came pre-assembled and painted, as was standard for Star Wars Miniature releases.

Contents include:
*AT-AT miniature (colossal)
*Counters
*Rules and Scenario booklet
*Double-Sided battle grid ([[Hoth]] maps)
