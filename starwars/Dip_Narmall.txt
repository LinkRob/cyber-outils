'''Dip Narmall''' was an individual who was present in [[Mirogana]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[coordinator]] at the [[Mirogana Spaceport]] made an announcement over the [[spaceport]]'s [[speaker]] system to notify Narmall that they were needed on [[B deck]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dip Narmall was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, they were mentioned in one of several ambient announcements that players can hear while at Mirogana Spaceport.<ref name="Outlaws" />
