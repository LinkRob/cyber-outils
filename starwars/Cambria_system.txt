The '''Cambria system''' was an astromonical object located in the [[Deep Core]]<ref name="BMF 36" /> and [[The Interior|Interior]] regions. It was situated in [[Standard Galactic Grid]] square L-10.<ref name="Location" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cambria was introduced in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 36|thirty-sixth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine series,<ref name="BMF 36" /> published around [[September 9]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|36}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Cambria system/Legends|Cambria system]] was mentioned in ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', a [[2009]] reference book authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
