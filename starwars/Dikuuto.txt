The '''Dikuuto''' ([[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]]: ディクート) were a crustacean species native to the snowy shores and sea ice of [[Hoth/Legends|Hoth]].<ref name="FamiMaga">{{BookCite|author=|container=Star Wars|title=Family Computer Magazine|number=1987 #22|publisher=Tokuma Shoten|date=December 4, 1987|issn=|link=https://archive.org/details/zines_famimaga_1987_22}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
These creatures first appeared in the [[1987]] [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] [[Star Wars (1987 video game)|''Star Wars'']] video game for the [[Wikipedia:Nintendo Entertainment System|Famicom]] home console.<ref name="Star Wars (1987 video game)" /> Their name was revealed in the [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese language]] guide of the game serialized in Family Computer Magazine, in which it was rendered as ディクート (''Dikuuto'') in [[Wikipedia:Katakana|katakana]] syllabary.<ref name="FamiMaga" />
