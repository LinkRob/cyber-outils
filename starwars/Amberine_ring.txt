'''Amberine rings''' were decorated with [[amberine]] gemstones that made them look more valuable they they really were.<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an amberine ring could be sold for thirty two [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Amberine rings appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the game, the ring could only be sold for credits, and did not have another function.<ref name="Outlaws" />
