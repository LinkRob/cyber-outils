The '''chow room''' was an area for [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunters]] and employees of the [[Grand Arena]] on [[Vespaara]] to eat. The chow room was located next to the backstage area of the Arena and contained plenty of food and beverage options, including [[caf]] and [[Nuna drumstick|deep-fried nuna legs]].

In [[9 ABY]], a public brawl happened in the chow room between Hunters [[Aran Tal]] and [[Imara Vex]]. The brawl caused a mess and property destruction in the chow room, even involving other Hunters [[Grozz]] and [[J-3DI]]. Fortunately Arena co-owner [[Daq Dragus]] came in and stopped the fight.

==Description==
The chow room was an area located next to the backstage area within the [[Grand Arena]] on the rocky [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] where [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunters]] and other personnel of the Arena went to eat. The chow room served plenty of [[Foodstuff|foods]], beverages, and other nutrition concerns. [[Caf]] and [[Nuna drumstick|deep-fried nuna legs]] were among the many foods and beverages served in the chow room.<ref name="BftA" />

==History==
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Hunters Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> as public tensions heated up between the [[Mandalorian]] Hunter [[Aran Tal]] and fellow Hunter [[Imara Vex]], the [[Arena News]] reported a brawl breaking out between the two in the chow room via eyewitnesses. Their fight involved use of their [[blaster]]s, which drew in other Hunters [[Grozz]] and [[J-3DI]]. However, the individual who started the blaster fire was unclear due to multiple conflicting reports. The brawl caused damage to tables, chairs, and decorative [[plant]]s before [[Hunters of the Outer Rim|arena]] co-owner [[Daq Dragus]] stepped in to pacify the combatants. Fortunately, no one was hurt in the crossfire.<ref name="ArenaNews">{{ArenaNews|url=47b94621fee5431384d0316302f36d0e-en-gb|text=Chow Room Brawl Gets Out of Control!}}</ref>

[[File:RieveNoxChowRoom-SHW.png|300px|thumb|left|Rieve and Nox talk at the chow room.]]
Later, in the same year,<ref name="Hunters Date" /> after the [[Zabrak]] Hunter [[Nox]]'s victory against rival Hunter [[Rieve]] in combat,<ref name="Victory for Nox">{{ArenaNews|url=746b46a167724989ae0f808381f01bf4-en-gb|text=Victory for Nox}}</ref> the two were found chatting together in the chow room. When fellow Hunter [[Diago Velaar]] approached the table, his comment about Nox making a friend was met with disproportionate hostility, as she threw down his food tray and nearly crushed a [[MSE series|mouse droid]] with her foot on her way out.<ref name="Chow Room Secrets">{{ArenaNews|url=7f43e105b9294a8e9fc0910d5cb71d7f-en-gb|text=Chow Room Secrets}}</ref>
