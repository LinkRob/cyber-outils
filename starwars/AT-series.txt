The '''AT-series'''<ref name="Starships and Speeders">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> were a line of [[walker]]s used by the [[Galactic Republic]], [[Galactic Empire]], and subsequently the [[First Order]]. The line included [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s (AT-AT), [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s (AT-ST), [[All Terrain Armored Cargo Transport]]s (AT-ACT) and [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s (AT-TE).<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

{{Veh-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The All-terrain vehicle first made its official appearance in the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] with the appearances of both the All Terrain Armored Transport and the All Terrain Scout Transport, both of which were seen in the opening scenes of the movie.<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref> However, the vehicle line was not officially named in the current [[canon]] until the release of the [[2017]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' published by [[Electronic Arts]].<ref name="New Battlefront">{{WebCite|url=http://www.pcgamer.com/star-wars-battlefront-2-will-release-in-2017|text=Star Wars Battlefront 2 will release in 2017|author=Prescott, Shaun|date=2016-05-10|archivedate=20160923000302}}</ref>
