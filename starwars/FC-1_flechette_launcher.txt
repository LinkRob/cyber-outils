The '''FC-1 flechette launcher''' was a model of [[flechette launcher]] produced by [[Golan Arms]]<ref name="Gadgets" /> that used a [[plasma]]-based firing mechanism. The weapon held six anti-personnel canisters containing hundreds of tiny, razor-sharp flechettes made of [[durasteel]]. When fired, its projectiles were capable of penetrating up to ten [[centimeter]]s, even through the [[Stormtrooper armor|armor]] of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> The design of the FC-1 inspired the creation of the [[DF-D1 Duo-Flechette Rifle]] at the hands of [[Salus Corporation]].<ref name="Gadgets" />

Following the [[Partisans]]' [[Mission to Inusagi|mission]] to [[Inusagi]], during which [[Saw Gerrera]] and members of [[Arane's partisans]] used FC-1 flechette launchers to [[assassin]]ate [[Cor Tophervin]] as well as [[Death|kill]] a number of Inusagians and Imperials alike, there was talk of banning such weapons from civilian use on that [[planet]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
