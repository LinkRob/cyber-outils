'''Badru''' was a [[gangster]] and member of the [[Sullustan]] [[Surat Nuat]]'s [[Surat Nuat's gang|gang]], which operated during the [[Imperial Era]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Nuat had formed an alliance with the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]], making the gang and its members an extension of the [[Hutt]]s' business on Akiva. As a result, the gang had access to a [[Hutt Cartel vault (Myrra)|vault]] within the [[Hutt Cartel District (Myrra)|Hutt Cartel District]] of Akiva's [[capital]] [[city]] [[Myrra]], with Badru having one of at least three [[keycard]]s required to unlock the vault.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> he left the card on a table outside the building containing the vault.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] learned of Badru's keycard and its approximate location from a [[Computer|data terminal]] near the sealed vault. She later stole the keycard from the table and used it and the two other keycards to deactivate the vault's [[ray shield]] and steal some of its contents.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Badru was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The intel quest specifies that Badru keeps the keycard on him at all times, however the player finds the keycard unaccompanied and none of the nearby patrolling Hutt Cartel guards are identified as Badru. Stealing the card is required to complete the optional "Hutt Cartel Vault" intel quest which rewards the player with 5000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], the [[Exhaustion]] [[Shift Token]], and the "Boonta Brawler" paint scheme for the player's [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
