A '''full-spectrum antigen''' was a type of decontaminant.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> after [[The Force|Force]]-[[Diet|consuming]] [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures escaped from containment aboard the [[Elite-class vessel|''Elite''-class vessel]] ''[[Innovator]]'', the [[Starship|ship's]] [[captain]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]], created an [[anesthetic]] by releasing a general full-spectrum antigen and two other decontaminants through vents into the ''Innovator''{{'s}} lower decks where the Nameless were. The anesthetic caused the Nameless to drowsily amble along until they reached the vessel's [[escape pod]]s, allowing Jomaram to seal them inside.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />
