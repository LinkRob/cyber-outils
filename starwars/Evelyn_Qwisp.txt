'''Evelyn Qwisp''' was a [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] who [[Author|wrote]] a [[book]] titled ''[[Meditations]]''<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> by [[231 BBY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> Jedi Master [[Cohmac Vitus]] requested a copy of the book from the [[Jedi Archives|archives]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. After he and his [[Padawan]], [[Reath Silas]], returned to the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] following a trip to<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Genetia">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Genetia system]]</ref> planet [[Genetia]], Vitus informed Silas he was going to visit Jedi Master [[Avar Kriss]]&mdash;the [[Jedi]] [[marshal]] of the station&mdash;to see if she had heard any news about his request for a copy of Qwisp's ''Meditations''.<ref name="Out of the Shadows" />

==Behind the scenes==
Evelyn Qwisp was mentioned in the [[2021]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
