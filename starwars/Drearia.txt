'''Drearia''' was a [[Planet|world]]<ref name="SkeletonCrew">{{SkeletonCrew|Very Interesting, As an Astrogation Problem}}</ref> located in the [[Drearia system]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It was situated in grid square L-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="TFABG" /> and the [[super-hyperroute]]<ref name="BMF 48">{{BuildFalconCite|48|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> known as the [[Hydian Way]] linked Drearia to the [[Paqualis system]] as well as<ref name="TFABG" /> the world<ref name="Lead">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> [[Champala]].<ref name="TFABG" /> Drearia shared its name with the company [[Drearian Defense Conglomerate]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>

In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoVGDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> Drearia fell within the borders of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="MandoVG" /> Around the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="9 ABY">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> while the [[astronomer]]<ref name="DB Khymm">{{Databank|khymm|Kh'ymm}}</ref> [[Kh'ymm]] was searching her archive of worlds on the [[Observatory Moon]] in order to determine the potential location of the lost planet [[At Attin]], Drearia's name was listed on her [[computer]] screen among other worlds.<ref name="SkeletonCrew" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drearia's name was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "The History of Neimoidia,"<ref name="BMF 52">{{BuildFalconCite|52|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> an article written by [[Mark Newbold]]<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> and included in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 52|fifty-second issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine<ref name="BMF 52" /> that was published around [[December 30]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMF52date">{{BuildFalconDates|52}}</ref> It was then first described as a distinct world in "[[Very Interesting, As an Astrogation Problem]],"<ref name="SkeletonCrew" /> the [[December 10]], [[2024]]<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1861409832349172011|''[[Star Wars]]''|date=2024-11-26|quote=Mark your calendars. 🗓️ On December 2, stream the two-episode series premiere of #SkeletonCrew, only on @DisneyPlus.|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/uxgSx|archivefile=File:StarWars-SkeletonCrewSchedule-Twitter.png}}</ref> third episode of the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]''.<ref name="InterestingEpGuide">{{SW|url=series/skeleton-crew/season-1-episode-3-episode-guide|text="Very Interesting, As an Astrogation Problem" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars: Skeleton Crew}}</ref> [[Drearia/Legends|Drearia]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
