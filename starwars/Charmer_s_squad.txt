'''Charmer's squad''' was a unit led by [[Sergeant]] [[Pol Andrissus]], known as Charmer, in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[61st Mobile Infantry]] shortly after the [[attack on Haidoral Prime]], where he was formally part of [[Hazram Namir]]'s [[Namir's squad|squad]]. During a [[raid on an Imperial heavy freighter]], the squad was attacking guard posts. When the squads evacuated, Charmer reported his squad's departure with one minute to spare, to Namir's delight. Sometime later, the Sixty-First was [[Attack on Mardona III|fighting]] on [[Mardona III]]. During the fight, Charmer's squad was attack by an [[Unidentified armored worm vehicle|armored worm vehicle]]. Charmer sacrificed himself for the squad but only [[Corbo]] made it out.<ref name="TC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Pol Andrissus's squad first appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="TC" />
