"'''Chameleon'''" is an adventure seed contained in the ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]''. A [[Changeling/Legends|shapeshifter]] murders [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] [[Commander/Legends|Commander]] [[Elmra]], leading to a hunt for the killer. Possible alternate storylines that could be adapted by a gamemaster were also included. It was written by [[Bill Olmesdahl]].
