The '''Cliffs of Dead Empire''', as named by [[Company 77]], was a cliffside on the coastline of [[Kef Bir]]'s principal [[ocean]]. Debris from the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]]'s destruction at the [[Battle of Endor]] landed in the ocean, and was visible from the cliffs.<ref name="TROS VD" />
