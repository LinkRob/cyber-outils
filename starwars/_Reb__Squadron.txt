'''"Reb" Squadron''' was a [[Resistance]] [[squadron]] of [[V-4X-D ski speeder]]s that fought against the [[First Order]] during the [[Battle of Crait]].<ref name="ICS" /> Confronted by the [[First Order military|armed forces]] of the First Order after taking shelter in an abandoned [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Crait outpost|outpost]], the Resistance attempted to delay the attack by destroying the enemy's [[superlaser siege cannon]]. [[Captain]] [[Poe Dameron]] led the makeshift sortie against the besiegers, but his squadron ultimately failed to destroy the [[cannon]]. Despite the [[Death|loss]] of [[Nodin Chavdri]] and [[Pammich Nerro Goode]], the rest of the squadron was evacuated on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' with help from the [[Jedi]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==History==
{{Quote|Why would you do that, huh? I was almost there! Why would you stop me?!"<br />"I ''saved'' you, dummy. That's how we're gonna win. Not by fighting what we hate. Saving what we love.|Finn and Rose, after Rose stopped Finn's suicide run|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:SuperlaserSiegeCanonAdvance-TLJ.png|thumb|left|250px|First Order prepares to attack the fleeing Resistance on Crait]]
Following the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation]] of [[D'Qar]]<ref name="Episode VIII" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Resistance]] fled to the [[planet]] [[Crait]], where they tried to hide in an old [[Crait outpost|outpost]] abandoned by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] many years prior, however they were followed by the [[First Order]]. The Resistance sent out a call for help to any allies able to help, but needed to buy some time, so the outpost's enormous main door was sealed shut and the Resistance forces prepared for battle. [[Captain]] [[Poe Dameron]] led a makeshift squadron of 13 [[V-4X-D ski speeder]]s, left behind by the Alliance, and piloted by the few remaining Resistance pilots.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Their plan was to destroy the [[superlaser siege cannon]] the First Order had brought to put a hole in the main blast doors. This was flanked by [[All Terrain MegaCaliber Six|AT-M6s]] and [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-ATs]], with air support from [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]].<ref name="Episode VIII" /> As the [[Battle of Crait|battle]] ensued, the Resistance suffered heavy losses from the combined ground and air assaults, including the [[Death|loss]] of the squadron's [[Nodin Chavdri]] and [[Ensign]] [[Pammich Nerro Goode]].<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

[[File:StarshipsSpeedersSpaceStations-Battle-of-Crait.png|thumb|right|250px|"Reb" Squadron's V-4X-D ski speeders battle it out on Crait]]
Unexpectedly, the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' arrived, piloted by [[Chewbacca]] and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], and drew the TIE fighters away from the battlefield. However, it was not enough as the First Order continued closer to overrunning the Resistance forces. As the siege cannon prepared to fire, Dameron ordered all speeders to fall back. [[Finn]] disobeyed and continued to fly his speeder towards the cannon in the hope of ramming it. At the last minute, [[Rose Tico]] crashed her speeder into Finn's, saving his life but injuring herself. With the base now vulnerable, the siege cannon fired at the main doors and created a hole. As the Resistance prepared for their final stand, [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] appeared out of nowhere and faced off against [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]. This bought valuable time for the remaining Resistance forces to find a way out of the base and escape on the ''Millennium Falcon''.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Behind the scenes==
Poe Dameron's makeshift squadron first appeared in the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]], however it did not receive a name in the film.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> The squadron was first identified as "Reb" Squadron in the ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]'' reference book written by [[Jason Fry]]<ref name="ICS" /> and published [[December 15]], 2017.<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/558521/star-wars-the-last-jedi--incredible-cross-sections-by-jason-fry|text=Star Wars The Last Jedi Incredible Cross-Sections}}</ref>
