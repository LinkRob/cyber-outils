Between [[12 BBY]] and [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''duel''' occurred between [[The Grand Inquisitor]] and several [[Jedi]] on the ''[[Voyager Dawn]]''. The Grand Inquisitor attacked the [[cruiser]] in order the learn the location of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Tensu Run]], whom he was hunting. After [[Death|executing]] Run's Master, [[Elan Rast]], the [[inquisitor]] turned to the remaining five [[Jedi]] who refused to give up the location. [[Gender|He]] engaged in [[lightsaber combat]] with the Jedi, fighting off each one. The Grand Inquisitor quickly killed each Jedi, before leaving the ''Voyager Dawn'' without the location to Run.<ref name="Inquisitors 1" />
