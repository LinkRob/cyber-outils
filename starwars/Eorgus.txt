'''Eorgus''' was an [[alien]] individual who lived in [[the galaxy]]. While gambling onboard the [[Casino Yego]], a [[casino ship]] which was stationed over [[Glee Anselm]], Eorgus was interrogated by the [[Seventh Sister]] and [[Ninth Sister]] for the whereabouts of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Tensu Run]], which he gave. After reciting his story, Eorgus was decapitated by the Seventh Sister.<ref name="Inquisitors 3" />
