'''Amethyst''' was a type of [[crystal]]. It was found on the [[planet]] [[Metagos]], where buildings in the wealthier areas of the [[city]] [[New Xaxxis]], including homes and utility structures, were crafted from a mix of amethyst, [[quartz]], and [[selenite]].<ref name="The Glass Abyss" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]]'s [[Mace Windu's lightsaber|lightsaber]] had a distinctive amethyst blade.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> The crystal shared its name with "Amethyst Apprentice" and "Amethyst Saber Pattern," a [[Clothing|costume]] and [[weapon]] respectively, which were used by the [[Grand Arena]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Rieve]]<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date2">{{HuntersDate}}</ref>
