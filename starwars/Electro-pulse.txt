An '''electro-pulse''' was a [[Limb|hand]]-held [[electromagnetic pulse]] device that disabled machines on contact. It was a [[Color|brown]], rectangular device that consisted of a number of buttons with a gold ring on top.<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> Upon landing on [[Heva]] during his [[Hunt for Vader|hunt]] for the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]<ref name="Target Vader 3">[[Target Vader 3|''Target Vader'' 3]]</ref> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[cyborg]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]] took an electro-pulse with him as he knew that Vader was a cyborg like himself, and the device could disable Vader's [[cybernetics]].<ref name="Target Vader 3" />

When he and his [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]] took on the [[Sith Lord]], Valance used the [[death]]s of the [[slicer]] [[Chio Fain]] and the tracker [[Honnah]] as an opportunity to close the distance and activate the device. While Valance managed to place the device on Vader's [[cape]], he failed to activate it in [[time]] when the [[Sith]] turned around and caught the [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] off-guard.<ref name="Target Vader 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
The electro-pulse was first mentioned in the [[Target Vader 3|third issue]] of the [[2019]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Target Vader]]'', written by [[Robbie Thompson]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 3" /> on [[September 25]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel-TV3">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/78926/star_wars_target_vader_2019_3|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #3}}</ref> The device made its first appearance in the [[Target Vader 4|fourth issue]] of the comic, which was also written by Thompson, illustrated by [[Stefano Landini]], and published by Marvel Comics<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> on [[October 9]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel-TV4">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/82434/star_wars_target_vader_2019_4|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #4}}</ref>
