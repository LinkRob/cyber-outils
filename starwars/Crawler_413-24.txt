'''Crawler 413-24''' was an [[ore crawler]] used by [[Captain]] [[Seevor]] of the [[Mining Guild]].<ref name="Crawler Commandeers" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Destruction Note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Crawler Commandeers]]" to [[1 BBY]].</ref> the [[Spectres]] [[Hijacking of Crawler 413-24|hijacked]] the crawler in order to use its long-range communications equipment. After the successful hijacking, the [[Lothal resistance group]] decided to keep the vehicle.<ref name="Crawler Commandeers" />
