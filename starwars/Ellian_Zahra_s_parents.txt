At least two individuals were the '''parents''' of the [[human]]<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Star Wars 21">[[Star Wars (2020) 21|''Star Wars'' (2020) 21]]</ref> [[Ellian Zahra]]. While Zahra was still young,<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> her [[parent]]s were [[Death|killed]],<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> along with the rest of her [[Ellian Zahra's family|family]], in a rebel [[terrorist]] attack long before<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> around [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> Believing that there was no fairness to life as a result,<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> Zahra was forced to survive on her own for some [[time]], eventually signing up at the [[Imperial Academy]] to prevent such a thing from happening to others.<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> Around 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Zahra mentioned the death of her parents to [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] after she passed the test to become his protégé.<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> She later mentioned the death of her family when [[Battle at Elessia|confronting]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Ellian Zahra's parents were first mentioned in flashbacks in the [[Star Wars (2020) 7|seventh issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars (2020)|''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series, written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> on [[October 7]] of that year.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/80516/star_wars_2020_7|text=Star Wars (2020) #7}}</ref>
