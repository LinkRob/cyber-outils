"'''Escaping Naboo'''" is a [[canon]] short story that appears in ''[[The Prequel Trilogy Stories]]''. It was written by [[Rebecca L. Schmidt]].<ref name="TPTS" /> It adapts the events of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']] from [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s landing on [[Naboo]] to their escape from the [[Trade Federation]] with [[Padmé Amidala]].
