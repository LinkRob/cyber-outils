'''Escort''' was one of many types of competitive sporting matches that could be played in the [[Grand Arena]]. In Escort, the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunters]] had to deliver a [[Escort Carrier (Grand Arena)|payload]] to the opposite side of a battlefield.<ref name="BftA" />
