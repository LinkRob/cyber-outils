'''Elliot Hawkes''' is a British stunt performer who portrays a Spice Runner in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. He also served as a stunt performer for the film.

Hawkes has worked as a stunt performer in well-known Hollywood movies such as the James Bond movie [[Wikipedia:Skyfall|''Skyfall'']]; [[Wikipedia:Maleficent (film)|''Maleficent'']] and its [[Wikipedia:Maleficent: Mistress of Evil|sequel]]; [[Wikipedia:Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them (film)|''Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them'']] and its 2018 [[Wikipedia:Fantastic Beasts: The Crimes of Grindelwald|sequel]], and the Marvel movies [[Wikipedia:Thor: The Dark World|''Thor: The Dark World'']] (starring fellow ''Star Wars'' actor [[Natalie Portman]]) and [[Wikipedia:Guardians of the Galaxy (film)|''Guardians of the Galaxy'']].
