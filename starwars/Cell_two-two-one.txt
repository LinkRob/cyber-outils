'''Cell two-two-one''',<ref name="Chapter 6" /> also called '''prison cell 2-2-1''',<ref name="Mando GN">[[The Mandalorian – The Graphic Novel of Season 1|''The Mandalorian'' – The Graphic Novel of Season 1]]</ref> was one of the [[prison]] cells inside a [[New Republic Correctional Transport]], the ''[[Bothan-5]]''. The [[Twi'lek]] criminal [[Qin]] was imprisoned in the cell after being arrested.<ref name="Chapter 6" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|1|[[Qin]]'s [[Bothan-Five incident|rescue]]}}</ref> Qin was rescued by a [[Ranzar Malk's crew|team]] of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]]. The team then immediately turned on one of their members, the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]], and trapped him in the cell. Djarin later escaped after attacking an [[N5 sentry droid]] from within the cell and using one of its arms to unlock the cell's door.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

==Behind the scenes==
