'''Eirriss Ryloth Defense Tech''', abbreviated as '''ERDT''', was an arms manufacturer which notably produced the [[Blurrg-1120 holdout blaster]]<ref name="The Visual Guide" /> and the [[Glie-44 blaster pistol]].<ref name="The Visual Dictionary" /> During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the company was associated with the [[Twi'lek]] [[Free Ryloth Movement]] on the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]]. The Twi'lek [[pilot]] [[Hera Syndulla]] used Eirriss' Blurrg-1120 during her [[time]] fighting the Empire on the planet [[Lothal]].<ref name="The Visual Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eirriss Ryloth Defense Tech was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Adam Bray]] and published in [[2014]].<ref name="The Visual Guide" />
