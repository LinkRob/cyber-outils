'''Frost''' was a [[Cathar/Legends|Cathar]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] serving the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]] and the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="TOR" /> Frost was stationed on [[Taris/Legends|Taris]] during the [[Cold War (Galactic Republic–Sith Empire)|Cold War]].<ref name="TOR" />

==Behind the scenes==
Frost was originally set to appear in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' but for some unknown reason his character was cut before release. Frost's file can be found on [https://swtor.jedipedia.net Jedipedia].<ref name="Jedipedia">{{WebCite|url=https://swtor.jedipedia.net/en/npc/jedi-master-frost|text=Jedi Master Frost|archivedate=20200823235152}}</ref>
