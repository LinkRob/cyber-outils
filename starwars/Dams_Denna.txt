'''Dams Denna'''<ref name="Naboo" /> was a diminutive [[human]]<ref name="TPM" /> from the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] who worked as an [[Animal trainer/Canon|animal trainer]].<ref name="Naboo" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}} to [[32 BBY]].</ref> they attended a [["Liberation Day" Parade|celebratory parade]] held in the streets of Naboo's [[capital]], [[Theed]], following the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] and [[Gungan]] victory over the [[Trade Federation]] during the [[Battle of Naboo]]. During the festivities, [[General]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] was among the Gungans that rode down Theed's<ref name="TPM" /> [[Palace Plaza]]<ref name="CompleteLocations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> escorting [[Boss]] [[Rugor Nass]].<ref name="TPM" />

Upon reaching the end of the plaza, Binks attempted to dismount from his [[kaadu]], but got his [[Limb|foot]] caught in a stirrup. Denna then moved forward and grabbed the kaadu's reigns,<ref name="TPM" /> helping Binks<ref name="Naboo" /> before the animal trainer led the kaadu away. At the event, Denna wore a [[Color|green]] [[Clothing|outfit]] including a tunic, pants, and [[boot]]s. They had light skin and brown [[hair]].<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dams Denna appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. The character was portrayed uncredited<ref name="TPM" /> by ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' publisher and Official ''Star Wars'' Fan Club president [[Dan Madsen]] as an Easter Egg,<ref name="Insider48">{{InsiderCite|48|I Was an Episode I Easter Egg!}}</ref> with the scene being filmed on an airstrip at [[Leavesden Studios]].<ref name="365Days">''[[Creating the Worlds of Star Wars: 365 Days]]''</ref> Madsen's cameo was revealed prior to the release of the film when he was pictured in costume in the article "[[When Artoo Met Wicket]]" in [[Star Wars Insider 39|''Star Wars Insider'' 39]].<ref name="Insider39">{{InsiderCite|39|When Artoo Met Wicket}}</ref>

In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Denna was was first identified in the English release of "[[The Crisis on Naboo]]," an entry in [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series<ref name="Naboo" /> that was originally released in Spanish on [[March 9]], [[2021]].<ref name="De Agostini">{{DeAgostini|url=libros-comics/enciclopedia-star-wars|text=Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref> The name [[Dams Denna/Legends|Dams Denna]], an anagram of Dan Madsen, originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used on a card in the [[2001]] ''[[Theed Palace Limited]]'' expansion set of the [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]].<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Theed Palace Limited|cardname=Dams Denna}}</ref>
