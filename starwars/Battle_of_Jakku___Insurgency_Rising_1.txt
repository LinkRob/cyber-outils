'''''Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising'' 1''' also stylized as '''''The Battle of Jakku &mdash; Insurgency Rising'' 1''', is the first issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] limited series ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising]]''. The issue was written by [[Alex Segura]], illustrated by [[Leonard Kirk]], [[Stefano Raffaele]], [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and [[Alex Sinclair]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 2]], [[2024]]. The issue also contains the story "[[The Rising]]."

==Plot summary==
===The War Isn't Over Yet===
In the [[Anoat sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing starfighter]] [[pilot]] [[Rynn Zenat]] finds herself being swarmed by [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter|TIE strikers]] in the [[standard hour|hours]] after the end of the [[Battle of Endor]]. She calls for backup and to her squadron mate and partner, [[Preeti Delmin]], certain her [[death]] is imminent. She makes an explosive sacrifice hoping to save her love, taking out many of the TIE strikers along with her own ship and, very likely, her life.

===Aftermath===
On the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] sees her [[marriage|husband]] [[Han Solo]] off as he embarks on a mission against the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]]. Meanwhile, on the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] [[Scepter (Imperial-class)|''Scepter'']], in the Anoat sector, [[Commander]] [[Bragh]] discusses the aftermath of a skirmish with Rebel [[starfighter]]s with [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]. They are approached by [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Kith Alaytia]], who asks them not to destroy the Rebel ships so that she can conduct an interrogation. Adelhard ignores her concerns and destroys the Rebels. She reminds the [[Moff]] that they need information following the recent battle at Endor. Adelhard does not let her mention the deaths of [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Bragh doubles down, reminding Alaytia that the Empire lives on indefinitely, as they will ensure it continues. Adelhard is nevertheless optimistic that he can maintain control of the sector and, eventually, the rest of [[the galaxy]]. He calls for [[Reyna Oskure]], an individual who may be of some use to him. 

Before Han's departure, at the victory celebration following the Battle of Endor, Leia slips away during the festivities. For her, the feeling of victory is tinged with the sharp and bitter taste of regret and potent anger. She approaches the [[Funeral of Anakin Skywalker|pyre of Darth Vader]], and says goodbye to her biological father. She cannot forgive him for his evil acts, only feels anger for all the people he hurt and left behind. She tosses his charred helmet aside and joins her chosen family.

===Machinations===
In an [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] stronghold on [[Devaron]], Reyna Oskure speaks with [[Yupe Tashu]], annoyed by the man's needlessly esoteric verbosity. Tashu asks Oskure to help the Acolytes squash the nascent [[New Republic]]. She is open to doing so, and informs Tashu she has been contacted by Adelhard. Tashu is pleased by this, and asks her to take good care of the Grand Moff, as Tashu has plans for him. Oskure leaves with a warning about the cyclical nature of institutions and governments. 

Back on Endor, [[Luke Skywalker]] approaches his sister Leia, hoping to talk with her about her visiting their father's pyre. He sensed her anger through [[the Force]] and worries that she may become driven by darkness. Leia rebuts, avowing that she is not like their father, that she is a person with feelings that cannot be buried, with trauma that has left scars. Luke then offers to train Leia in the ways of the [[Jedi]]. Before Leia can consider the offer, they are approached by a Rebel lieutenant who informs them that a badly damaged Rebel ship has arrived to their location demanding to speak with Leia. 

On the ''Scepter'', Kith Alaytia is approached by Commander Bragh, who asks about the news she supposedly has for him and Adelhard. She starts to explain, and Bragh realizes its importance and takes her directly to Adelhard. The Grand Moff reminds his two pillars of order and reason about their sacred duty to restore the Empire and harshly cuts off Alaytia as she begins to relay the news, asking her to be more to the point. She informs him that one of the Rebel B-wing pilots survived the attack.

The pilot who survived the attack is Rynn Zenat. She inexplicably made it all the way to Endor, unsure of how she survived or how she got to Endor. She starts to tell her story to Leia, how she thought the [[Galactic Civil War|war]] was over, but how things quickly changed when she engaged Adelhard's forces. She warns them all of Adelhard's ruthlessness and his goals to restore the Empire.
