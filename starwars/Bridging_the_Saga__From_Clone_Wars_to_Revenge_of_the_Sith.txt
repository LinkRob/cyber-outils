'''Bridging the Saga: From Clone Wars to Revenge of the Sith''' is a special featurette found on the home video release of [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume One|''Star Wars: Clone Wars'' Volume One]], released in [[2005]]. The featurette shows detailed interviews and behind the scenes clips of the crew as they try to make the series lead into the events that occur in [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].
