'''''500 Republica''''',<ref name="Ultimate" /> spelled as '''Five Hundred Republica''',<ref name="Into the Light">''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]''</ref> was the most prestigious and exclusive residential [[Skyscraper|tower]] located in the [[Federal District|Senate District]] of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. It was here that [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] maintained an expansive [[Palpatine's apartment|apartment suite]] in the building's upper levels that he had wired with discreet security and surveillance equipment.<ref name="Ultimate" /> [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]] stayed in ''500 Republica'' as Palpatine's guest during the [[Invasion of Naboo]].<ref name="TPM" /> ''500 Republica'' offered private [[turbolift]]s and security armaments to those rich and famous people who resided within. It also had the design of steps traveling upwards, and held fifty-three skydocks to accommodate large [[air taxi]]s to private [[starship]]s.<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> The [[Republic Futures Program|Futures Program]] graduate and former [[Senator]] who organized the Futures Program reunion [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]] attended had an apartment suite in the tower.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>
