Hoping to lure [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Bran Ath'Morath|Bran Ath' Morath]] to his death [[Darth]] [[Darth Caldoth|Caldoth]] destroyed the city of [[Pundith]] on the Planet [[Kizan]]. Sure enough Jedi ships soon appeared in orbit as Caldoth awaited in the ruins of Pundith. Four Jedi were sent but Caldoth quickly dispatched three of them with his red [[lightsaber]]. But Bran Ath' Morath and Caldoth were so evenly matched that their battle raged on and on. But as day became dusk both began to tire. Finally Caldoth lowered his blade and thrust himself forward, piercing himself with in the shoulder with Bran Ath' Morath's yellow lightsaber, however Caldoth's blade struck lower and killed Bran Ath' Morath and leaving Caldoth wounded but alive.
