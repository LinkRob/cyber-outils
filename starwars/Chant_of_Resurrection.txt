The '''Chant of Resurrection''' was an immensely powerful and sinister spell used to temporarily [[Zombie|reanimate]] the [[Death|dead]]. Of [[magick]] origin, it manifested as a greenish mist channeled from or through a glowing orb/crystalline object and was required to be spoken in a [[Dathomirian language|special]] [[language]]. Though powerful, the spell's effects would last only as long as the caster was active; should they cease maintaining it or be killed whilst using the spell, the undead would become immobile corpses once more.<ref name="Massacre" />

==History==
[[File:NightsisterZombieAttack-MalicosKestis.png|thumb|250x250px|Merrin brings dead Nightsisters as zombies to attack Cal Kestis and Taron Malicos.]]
The incantation was used by Old [[Daka]], the eldest of the [[Dathomir]]i [[Nightsisters]], to aid the Nightsisters' [[Army of the Dead|forces]] [[Battle of Dathomir|against]] the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. However, during the battle, Daka was killed by [[General]] [[Grievous]], causing the undead to return to their original state. The living Nightsisters, who lost most of their forces, were then quickly overwhelmed and massacred.<ref name="Massacre" /> Several [[Standard year|years]] later, [[Merrin]], one of the last surviving Nightsisters, used the spell after the former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] visited Dathomir.<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> Years after, after Merrin joined Kestis's team, the Mantis crew, she would use the Chant of Resurrection again to bring [[Qeris Lar]] back as a [[zombie]] to distract the [[Inquisitor]] known as the [[Fifth Brother]].<ref name="Battle Scars">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]''</ref>

[[File:Ahsoka8-ReanimatedStormtrooper.png|left|250px|thumb|A Night Troopers is reanimated by the Great Mothers.]]
The [[Great Mothers]] of [[Peridea]]'s [[Great Mothers' Fortress|Dathomiri fortress]] were able to use the spell to revive volunteers from [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]'s [[Night Trooper]] [[Thrawn's Night Trooper legion|legion]] in order to delay [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Sabine Wren]], and [[Ezra Bridger]] from interfering in their plans. The zombified [[stormtrooper]]s pushed on despite sustaining injuries and were only stopped when the trio of Jedi locked them behind heavy doors, which the troopers were forced to cut through. In the end, the zombies met their end when Thrawn, in a desperate attempt to hold back Tano and company, ordered the ''[[Chimaera]]'' to fire on the Dathomiri fortress. While Wren and Tano escaped death aboard their ship ''[[T-6 1974]]'', while Bridger had already safely boarded the ''Chimaera'', the undead Night Troopers were caught in the destruction.<ref name="Ahsoka Part 8">{{Ahsoka|Part Eight: The Jedi, the Witch, and the Warlord}}</ref>
