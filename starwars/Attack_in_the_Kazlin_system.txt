During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] '''attacked''' a [[Hutt Clan]] [[starship]] passing through the [[Kazlin system]] with a load of [[Vratixia renanicus|Vratixian barley]], [[Death|slaughtering]] the crew and stealing the cargo. The ship sent out a scrambled [[distress signal]] which was picked up by the [[Galactic Republic]] [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]], and a team of [[Jedi]] was dispatched to investigate. The Jedi discovered the now-derelict ship and investigated, coming into conflict with a [[Unidentified Nihil sniper|lone pirate]] left behind on the ship, who was killed by [[Jedi Master]] [[Sskeer]] after injuring [[Terec]]. Finding that the [[grain]] onboard had likely come from the [[planet]] [[Sedri Minor]], [[Marshal]] [[Avar Kriss]] sent Sskeer and [[Ceret]] to investigate.<ref name="THR 2" />
