'''Catta Dionize''' was an individual who served under [[Gender|Madame]] [[Travia Chan]], leader of the [[Atrivis Resistance Group]] during the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' dates the letter sent by Dionize to fourteen [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the proclamation to [[19 BBY]], the letter must have been sent in [[5 BBY]].</ref> Dionize sent a letter from [[Generis Station]] to [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] on behalf of Chan. The letter stated that she agreed to partner the Resistance Group with Mothma's [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], referencing the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Battle on Mantooine|victory]] over [[Armed rebellion in the Atrivis sector|resistance]] on the [[planet]] [[Mantooine]] earlier that [[Standard year|year]] as something the partnership would prevent from happening again. The letter was preserved in the collection of physical documents detailing the history of the Alliance known as [[the Rebel Files]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
Catta Dionize was first mentioned in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
