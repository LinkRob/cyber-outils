'''Aayla Secura's lightsaber'''<ref name="Mini book">''[[Star Wars: The Mini Book of Lightsabers]]''</ref> was the personal [[lightsaber]] of the [[Twi'lek]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Aayla Secura]]<ref name="Lightsabers" /> by the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Episode II" /> The [[lightsaber]] produced a [[Color|blue]]-blade when [[Plasma|ignited]] and had a [[durasteel]] hilt. The hilt itself was designed for durability during long missions and so that its inner workings were easily accessible if Secura needed to repair it in the field. The hilt's upper portion sported a ridged grip section and controls for adjusting the blade's length and intensity.<ref name="Lightsabers" />
