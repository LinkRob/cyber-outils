'''Bones''' were [[Color|white]],<ref name="SWE">{{SWE|technology|battledroid|battle droid}}</ref> rigid tissues that made up the internal [[Skeleton|skeletal structure]] of certain lifeforms,<ref name="dbGUN">{{Databank|gungan|Gungan}}</ref> including [[human]]s,<ref name="TRB">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref> [[Mirialan]]s,<ref name="ROTOM trivia">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/rise-of-the-old-masters-trivia-gallery|text="Rise of the Old Masters" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|slide 2 caption}}</ref> [[Iktotchi]],<ref name="Path of the Jedi">{{Rebels|Path of the Jedi}}</ref> [[Twi'lek]]s,<ref name="LotS">''[[Lords of the Sith]]''</ref> [[Feeorin]],<ref name="EG">''[[Ezra's Gamble]]''</ref> [[Lasat]],<ref name="ROR">''[[Rise of the Rebels]]''</ref> and [[krayt dragon]]s.<ref name="dbKRAY">{{Databank|tatooine|Tatooine}} {{C|Biography Gallery}}</ref> By contrast, the skeleton of the [[Gungan]]s was made of cartilaginous material rather than bone.<ref name="dbGUN" />

In [[humanoid]]s, the bone on each side of the face below the [[eye]], forming the prominence of the cheek, was called the zygoma.<ref name="TLJ:VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

The [[Nightsisters]] of [[Dathomir]] notably used bones, along with [[tree]] branches and animal skins, to create the strange structures that stood in their graveyards.<ref name="Massacre Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep419|text="Massacre" – ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}} {{C|slide 2 caption}}</ref>

Amongst the [[Kaleesh]] [[Soldier|warriors]] carved dangerous local [[Diet|predatory]] [[Species|animals]]' skulls, bones,<ref name="HC 8">{{HelmetCollectionCite|8|Helmets}}</ref> and teeth<ref name="Spanish HC 8">{{HelmetCollectionCite|8|Helmets}} {{C|Spanish version}}</ref> on their [[homeworld]], [[Kalee]], and wore their [[Kaleesh mask|terrifying masks]] into battle with smeared [[karabbac]]-[[blood]] war paint.<ref name="HC 8" />

[[File:Huttsplitter_blaster.png|left|thumb|Tasu Leech's "Huttsplitter," with a bone grip.]]
Some members of the criminal [[Kanjiklub]] gang incorporated pieces of bone into their jury-rigged [[blaster]]s. [[Topboss]] [[Tasu Leech]]'s [[Donderbus blaster pistol]] had a handle made from a [[Kintan strider]]'s ulna, while his [["Huttsplitter" blaster rifle]] featured a [[gundark]] bone grip, and [[Crokind Shand]]'s [[heavy bore rifle]] included a [[roggwart]] bone butt, and [[pirate]] [[captain]] [[Sidon Ithano]] also used [[Captain Ithano's blaster rifle| a visually identical weapon]].<ref name="Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

Some types of [[Foodstuff|food]] contained bones; when [[Peli Motto]] instructed her [[DUM-series pit droid|pit droids]] to fetch food for [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], she asked them to get something with bones in it.<ref name="Gunslinger">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 5: The Gunslinger}}</ref>
