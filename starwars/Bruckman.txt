[[Sergeant]] '''Bruckman''' was a [[Corellian]] [[Gender|male]] who was picked by [[General]] [[Han Solo]] to act as point man for an [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[Endor strike team|strike team]] of [[commando]]s that was [[Battle of Endor|deployed]] to the [[moon]] of [[Endor]] in [[4 ABY]]. The team's aim was to destroy a [[Death Star's shield generator|shield generator]] on the [[moon]] that was protecting the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star II]] [[superweapon]] above.

Bruckman was among the team members that entered the security bunker at the rear of the [[Deflector shield generator|generator]] where they began placing charges; however, they were captured by Imperial forces that had laid a trap for them. An [[army]] of local [[Ewok]]s then attacked the Imperials, allowing Bruckman and the others to also turn on their captors. Ultimately the Alliance destroyed the generator and the [[Death Star]].

==Biography==
===Strike team sergeant===
[[File:FlyingCasual-ROTJHD.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Bruckman and the strike team traveled to Endor on the ''Tydirium''.]]
Bruckman<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="ROTJ" /> [[Gender|male]] from the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], he served as a [[sergeant]] in the [[Alliance Military]]. In [[4 ABY]], [[General]] [[Han Solo]], a fellow [[Corellian]], chose Bruckman to act as point man for a [[Endor strike team|strike team]] of [[commando]]s he was leading during the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> in which the Alliance aimed to destroy the Empire's [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star II]] [[superweapon]] in the [[Endor system]]. The team's role would be to disable the [[Death Star's shield generator]], which was located on the [[moon]] of [[Endor]].<ref name="ROTJ" />

After the team deployed to Endor in the stolen Imperial [[shuttle]] ''[[Tydirium]]'',<ref name="ROTJ" /> they landed in a clearing some distance from the [[Deflector shield generator|generator]] and worked their way slowly through the dense undergrowth to the relative safety of the surrounding [[forest]]. Solo and some other leading members of the team became separated from the others, and the general ordered [[Major]] [[Bren Derlin]] to lead Bruckman and the others to regroup with them at the generator at 03:00 [[Standard hour|hours]] the next [[Standard day|morning]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> By the [[time]] they regrouped, Solo and the others had formed an alliance with a local [[Bright Tree tribe|tribe]] of [[Ewok]]s. Together, the Alliance forces and the Ewoks proceeded to a security bunker that acted as the generator's rear entrance, and after the Ewok [[Paploo]] drew away most of the guards, Bruckman and several other members of the team accompanied Solo inside.<ref name="ROTJ" />

===Capture before victory===
{{Quote|All right, move it!|One of the stormtroopers escorting Bruckman and the other rebels from the bunker|<ref name="ROTJ" />}}
[[File:RebelExitBunker-ROTJ.png|thumb|left|300px|Bruckman and the other rebels were captured in the bunker.]]
The Rebels worked their way through the bunker's labyrinth of corridors toward the control room, taking one [[Death|casualty]] in a battle with nine [[stormtrooper]]s en route.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> Inside the control room they forced the Imperials present to surrender and began laying charges; however, they had fallen into a trap. The Empire had expected the rebel attack, and concealed Imperial forces emerged and forced all of the Rebels in the bunker to surrender. Bruckman was lead outside with Solo and the others, where they found a large Imperial force waiting for them.<ref name="ROTJ" />

As all seemed lost, the uncaptured Rebel [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] called out from the nearby forest, luring several stormtroopers in after him. The army of Ewoks then attacked the Imperials, causing chaos in which Bruckman and the other captive rebels quickly turned on their captors as well. In the ensuing battle, the rebels eventually managed to get back inside the bunker and successfully destroyed it, allowing the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] to destroy the [[Death Star]] above.<ref name="ROTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bruckman appeared in the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the character was first identified in the English release of "[[The Battle of Endor and the Fall of the Empire]]," an entry in [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> that was originally released in Spanish on [[June 29]], [[2021]].<ref name="De Agostini">{{DeAgostini|url=libros-comics/enciclopedia-star-wars|text=Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref> The name [[Bruckman/Legends|Bruckman]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used on a card in the [[1999]] ''[[Endor Limited]]'' expansion set of the [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]].<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Endor Limited|cardname=Sergeant Bruckman}}</ref>
