The '''Coruscant cab''',<ref name="Orders concept">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/orders-concept-art-gallery|text="Orders" Concept Art Gallery}}</ref> was a model of [[air taxi]] operating on [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Orders" /> [[Jay Igno]] was a [[Nimbanel]] [[taxi]] driver<ref name="Orders Trivia">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/orders-trivia-gallery|text="Orders" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> who drove such a vehicle.<ref name="Orders" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Orders-Date">{{TCWcanondates|604}}</ref> Igno drove the rogue [[clone trooper]] [[Fives]] to the bar [[79's]] after the trooper discovered a [[Order 66|conspiracy]] surrounding [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and the clone's [[Behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chips]].<ref name="Orders" /> A Coruscant cab<ref name="DealND">{{TCW|Deal No Deal}}</ref> also stood in the [[Martez sisters' repair shop]] on [[Level 1313]] and others flew around the level.<ref name="GoneWT">{{TCW|Gone with a Trace}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:CoruscantCab-ConceptArt-RusselGChong.png|thumb|Illustration of the model for the Coruscant cab by Russel G. Chong|218x218px]]
This taxi model first appeared in the episode "[[Orders (episode)|Orders]]" of [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|the sixth season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', released in [[2014]].<ref name="Orders" /> The model was created by [[Russell G. Chong]] and finished on [[November 14]], [[2011]], and labeled "Coruscant cab".<ref name="Orders concept" />
