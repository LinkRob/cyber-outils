'''Efrain''' was a self-taught [[droid]] designer and [[Jarrod Kelvin]]'s mentor. He was an eccentric old [[Gender|man]] and [[Technician|mechanical]] savant who owned his own [[laboratory]] where he tested new droid designs. During the [[Imperial Era]], he was betrayed and his plans for a new droid were stolen and given to the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]]. In an effort to flee the empire, Efrain joined a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[convoy]] taking civilians to a hidden rebel [[Planet|world]]. But after the convoy was attacked, the transport he was on crash-landed onto the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Legends" />

==Biography==
[[File:Jarrod_Kelvin_vs._X-8_Droid.png|thumb|left|250px|Efrain's apprentice, Jarrod Kelvin, destroying an Imperial X-8 droid.]]
During the Imperial Era, Efrain created a powerful new prototype based off of the same droid line as [[J4X-7]]. Efrian was then betrayed and his life's work was stolen and given to the Empire. With Efrian's plans, the Empire prepared to mass-produce the [[X-8 blaster droid]]s in a facility on the planet [[Kuat]]. Jarrod Kelvin, Efrain's apprentice, was sent on a mission to the facility on Kuat to destroy all Imperial X-8 droids and reacquire Efrian's plans.<ref name="Heart of the Empire">{{SWIA|set=Heart of the Empire|mission=Test of Metal}}</ref>

During the [[Galactic Civil War]] at some point before [[1 BBY]]<ref name="Greedo Alive">''[[Legends of the Alliance]]'' has [[Greedo]] alive during this time, so it must take place prior to his death, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]].</ref>, he joined a rebel convoy transporting civilians to a hidden rebel world in an effort to escape the Empire. The Empire ambushed the convoy, and the transport he was on managed to jump to [[hyperspace]] and escape the battle. Eventually, the [[hyperdrive]] gave out near the planet Tatooine, where the damaged ship crashed. Efrain, along with the other survivors, set up a camp where they stayed before being attacked by [[Captain]] [[Terro]] and his forces.<ref name="Legends" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Jarrod, my boy, I may have solved the impact issue I'd been struggling with! Wait, where are we?|Efrain talking to Jarrod Kelvin|<ref name="Legends" />}}
Efrain was a smart man who taught himself how to build and design droids.<ref name="Legends" /> He mentored Jarrod Kelvin in droid design, which allowed Jarrod to build J4X-7.<ref name="Heart of the Empire" /> Efrain was often so engulfed in his work that he wouldn't notice what was going on around him.<ref name="Legends" />

==Behind the scenes==
Efrain was first mentioned unnamed in the ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Assault]]'' expansion set ''[[Heart of the Empire]]''.<ref name="Heart of the Empire" /> He was first named in the Star Wars: Imperial Assault companion app ''[[Legends of the Alliance]]'', in the "Jabba's Realm" campaign. In said campaign, Efrain has a chance of being one of the eight crash survivors present during the first mission, or guaranteed if the player chooses Jarrod Kelvin to be in the [[squad]]. Depending upon the actions and success of the player during the missions, Efrain's fate can vary. Efrain can end up being [[Slavery|enslaved]] by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]], eaten by a [[rancor]], left behind at Terro's camp, or successfully have escaped Tatooine with the squad.<ref name="Legends" />
