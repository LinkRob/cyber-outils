The '''''Absolution''''' was a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] in the [[First Order Navy|Navy]] of the [[First Order]] that [[Captain]] [[Archex|Cardinal]] interrogated a [[Resistance]] [[spy]], [[Vi Moradi]], for information about [[Captain]] [[Phasma]]'s past aboard. The ''Absolution'' was also where the younger half of the First Order [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] training took place, under the advisory of Cardinal. [[Brendol Hux]] had a grand and lavish suite as his living quarters on the [[officer]]'s deck. A decade after his death it was commandeered by his son, [[Armitage Hux]], when he visited the ship.<ref name="Phasma" /> After [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Resistance)|capturing]] [[Lor San Tekka]] on [[Cato Neimoidia]], [[Terex]] traveled to the ''Absolution'', where he met with Phasma to have the [[Cybernetics|cybernetic implants]] in his [[brain]] removed in exchange for Tekka's [[Great Forveen Nebula|location]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 24" />
