'''18 BBY''', or '''18 Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Events==
*[[Mon Cala]] is [[Invasion of Mon Cala|subjugated]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] after [[King of Mon Cala|King]] [[Lee-Char]] surrenders.<ref name="Dark Lord Mon Cala">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Invasion of Mon Cala]], as seen in issues [[Darth Vader (2017) 13|13]], [[Darth Vader (2017) 14|14]], [[Darth Vader (2017) 15|15]], [[Darth Vader (2017) 16|16]], and [[Darth Vader (2017) 17|17]] of [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2017)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'' (2017)]], to 18 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>
*The entire population of [[Raada]] is evacuated following an [[Raada Uprising|uprising]] of the local [[Raada resistance|resistance]] against the occupation of the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Ahsoka" />
*[[Ahsoka Tano]] joins [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] in his [[Bail Organa's resistance movement|fight]] against the Empire.<ref name="Ahsoka" />
*The [[Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill]] is passed.<ref name="TBB S2 Date note" />
*During a [[Rescue of Caleb Dume|battle in space]], [[Commander]] [[Grey (clone trooper)|Grey]], realizing that [[Order 66]] had altered his loyalty, sacrifices his life to save [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]].<ref name="Kanan 5 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Kanan 5|''Kanan'' 5]] to 18 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref><ref name="Kanan 5">[[Kanan 5|''Kanan'' 5]]</ref>
*Caleb Dume takes on the name "Kanan Jarrus."<ref name="Kanan 5 Date" /><ref name="Kanan 5" />
*Having already committed herself to establishing the political heart of the rebellion<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="19Date">{{MOFdate}}</ref> [[Mon Mothma]] begins to organize military and intelligence movements for an anti-Empire coalition known then as the "Alliance"<ref name="203Insider">{{InsiderCite|203|Rebel Rousers: Our Top Ten Rebels}}</ref> in 18 BBY.<ref name="Date">{{InsiderCite|203|Rebel Rousers: Our Top Ten Rebels}} states that [[Mon Mothma]] began organizing military and intelligence forces eighteen [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|IV}}, which corresponds to 18 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. Mothma must have began organizing the anti-Empire coalition in 18 BBY.</ref>
*The [[Nikardun campaigns]] to eliminate remnants of the [[Nikardun Destiny]] [[Yiv's battle forces|military]] are conducted.<ref name="Greater Good" /><ref name="Greater Good date" />
**The [[Skirmish above Nettehi|skirmish]] above [[Nettehi]] takes place, liberating the [[Paataatus]] [[Paataatus Hiveborn|government]] from the yolk of the [[Nikardun]].<ref name="Greater Good" /><ref name="Greater Good date" />
*The [[Grysk]] [[Jixtus's plan|plot to destroy the Chiss Ascendancy]] unfolds in the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="Greater Good" /><ref name="Greater Good date" />
**The [[Hoxim incident|skirmish]] above [[Hoxim]] takes place, and a civil war among the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] is prevented.<ref name="Greater Good" /><ref name="Greater Good date" />
*The [[Illumine Kilhorde]] is dissolved<ref name="Lesser Evil" /><ref name="Lesser Evil date" />
*The [[Galactic Empire]] liberates the [[Alkenzi Air Base]] on [[Aldhani]], and dammed up the sacred river, [[Nasma Klain]].<ref name="Aldhani (episode)">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref><ref name="Aldhani Date">The events of the episode "Aldhani" takes place shortly after "[[Kassa (episode)|Kassa]]," which dates itself to [[5 BBY]]. [[Vel Sartha]] states that the airbase was liberated thirteen years prior, therefore placing it in 18 BBY.</ref>
*[[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] brutally subjugates the population of the [[moon]] [[Antar 4]], in what becomes known as the [[Antar Atrocity]].<ref name="Tarkin" /><ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
*The Empire claims the [[planet]] [[Ilum]]<ref name="Timelines" /> and begins working on a [[Starkiller Base|massive terraforming project]].<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
*In his first mission for the Empire,<ref name="Comp Ency">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Alexsandr Kallus]] leads his [[Alexsandr Kallus' squad|squad]] to the [[Onderon insurgency|wartorn]] [[planet]] [[Onderon]], where his entire unit, save himself, is [[Attack on an Imperial patrol|wiped out]] by a [[Unidentified Lasat mercenary|Lasat mercenary]].<ref name="The Honorable Ones">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref>
*[[Sergeant]] [[Luthen Rael|Lear]] rescues the girl [[Kleya Marki]] and [[Desertion|defects]] from the Empire. {{Circa}}<ref name="Make It Stop">{{Andor|Make It Stop}}</ref><ref name="Break Down">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-interview-stellan-skarsgard-elizabeth-dulau|text=<i>Andor</i>'s Stellan Skarsgård and Elizabeth Dulau Break Down Luthen and Kleya}}</ref>
