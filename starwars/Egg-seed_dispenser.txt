An '''egg-seed dispenser''' was a device that dispensed [[egg-seed]]. The device was metallic [[Color|gray]] in color and included a vertical disc shaped base with a cylindrical protrusion extending from the center.<ref name="CL" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the [[Toydarian]] [[Watto]] had an egg-seed dispenser by his nest in the top of the [[Watto's junkshop|junkshop]] he ran in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] when a group led by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] visited in search of a [[T-14 hyperdrive generator]].<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], an egg-seed dispenser was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="CL" /> from the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]], illustrated by [[Richard Chasemore]] and [[Hans Jenssen]], and marked the first mention of the [[Egg-seed dispenser/Legends|device]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>
