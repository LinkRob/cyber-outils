The '''biological father''' of [[Candace]] and [[Phineas Flynn]] was a [[Gender|male]] individual who was [[Marriage|married]] to [[Linda Flynn-Fletcher]] and started a [[Flynn-Fletcher family|family]]. Linda was later remarried with [[Lawrence Fletcher]] sometime after Candace's departure to join the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the [[Battle of Yavin]], Phineas was in the process of explaining to his long lost sister Candace what had happened to their biological [[Parent|father]] when the destruction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]] cut the conversation short.<ref name="Phineas" />

==Behind the scenes==
The biological father of Candace and Phineas Flynn was mentioned in the crossover special ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]'',<ref name="Phineas" /> which premiered on [[July 26]], [[2014]].<ref name="Reporter">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/live-feed/phineas-ferbs-star-wars-special-711433|text='Phineas and Ferb's' 'Star Wars' Special Lands Premiere Date|author=Ng, Philiana|date=2014-06-12|work=The Hollywood Reporter|archivedate=20201205100409}}</ref> Flynn's biological father is an ongoing mystery throughout the main series ''[[W:c:PhineasandFerb:Phineas and Ferb|Phineas and Ferb]]'' that has sprouted many theories and discussions within the fan community. The creators of the animated series [[Dan Povenmire]] and [[Jeff "Swampy" Marsh]] have actively denied almost every theory surrounding it.<ref name="Dan YT">{{YouTube|video=OUuVIckowRQ|text=Dan Talks Phineas and Ferb Internet Theories|channel=@Dantible|name=Dan Povenmire|date=2022-02-07}}</ref>
