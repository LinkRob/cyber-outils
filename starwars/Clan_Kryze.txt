[[File:Satine-rescue-TL.png|thumb|Korkie Kryze, Bo-Katan and Duchess Satine in the Mandalore palace prison]]
'''Clan Kryze''' was a [[Mandalorian clan]] that was a part of [[House Kryze]].<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" /> It was based on [[Kalevala]].<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], it was a group of [[Mandalorian warrior]]s led by [[Bo-Katan Kryze]].<ref name="Clan Kryze">{{Databank|clan-kryze|Clan Kryze}}</ref> Following the [[Great Purge of Mandalore]],<ref name="Chapter 11: The Heiress" /> it was reduced to a shadow of its former self but remained under Kryze's leadership.<ref name="SWCEUE">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Clan Kryze consisted of Mandalorian warriors that donned [[Mandalorian armor]] with blue, white, and yellow color combinations. During the Siege of Mandalore, a smaller faction of Clan Kryze assisted Ahsoka and a fraction of the [[501st Legion]] in taking back Mandalore. Years later, a [[Unidentified Clan Kryze member|member]] of the clan pledged their allegiance to Bo-Katan alongside members of Clans [[Clan Eldar|Eldar]], [[Clan Rook|Rook]], [[Clan Vizsla|Vizsla]] and [[Clan Wren|Wren]], allowing for her to becoming [[Mandalore (title)|Mand'alor]].<ref name="Heroes">''[[Heroes of Mandalore|Star Wars Rebels: Heroes of Mandalore]]''</ref>
