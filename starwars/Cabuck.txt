'''Cabuck''' was a [[Drezar]] [[Homeworld|from]] [[Drez]] who was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[166 BBY]]. He served as owner, manager, and [[bartender]] of the [[Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop]] in [[Point Ueda]] on the [[Planet|world]] [[Ueda]] during the [[High Republic Era]] but resisted customizing it due to a superstitious belief that it was haunted by [[Lomi Usqi]], a previous owner.

In [[132 BBY]], he witnessed the [[assassin]] [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] enter the establishment and [[Mae's quest|confront]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Indara]], remaining behind the bar as the pair then fought throughout the [[noodle]] shop. He eventually accidentally drew their attention by making a noise, causing Aniseya to throw a [[Mae's knives|knife]] at him. Indara [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|stop]] the [[knife]] before it could hit Cabuck, but this distraction allowed Aniseya to throw another knife into the [[Jedi]]'s chest, [[Death|killing]] her.

Aniseya then approached a surrendering Cabuck at the bar and prepared to attack him until she saw Cabuck's three-[[Standard year|year]]-old [[Gender|son]], [[Cabuck Junior]], clinging onto the bartender's [[Limb|leg]], after which she left him unharmed and exited the establishment. While investigating the [[Indara's murder|murder]], the [[Jedi Order]] brought Cabuck to the [[Trade Federation]] [[WD-2550 Whydah-class Yield Conveyer|freighter]] ''[[Fallon]]'', where he misidentified Aniseya's [[twin]] sister, [[Verosha Aniseya]], as the murderer.

==Biography==
===Noodle provider===
[[File:Cabuck-LostFound.png|thumb|left|250px|alt=Cabuck, a bearded Drezar in a brown shirt, stands with his mouth open.|Cabuck owned the Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop.]]
Cabuck was a [[Drezar]] [[Homeworld|from]] [[Drez]] who was [[Reproduction|born]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> around [[166 BBY]].<ref name="Dates" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], he was owner, manager, and [[bartender]] of the [[Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop]],<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> a rundown [[Restaurant|eatery]]<ref name="Lomi Databank">{{Databank|lomi-usqi-noodle-shop|Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop}}</ref> that was the only establishment of its kind in [[Point Ueda]], the [[capital]] [[city]] on the [[planet]] [[Ueda]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> He served hot bowls of [[broth]] that he made to order fresh.<ref name="Drinks">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Drinks]]''</ref> Although he served as his own boss,<ref name="Quiz">{{SW|url=news/quiz-which-high-republic-job-is-right-for-you|text=Quiz: Which High Republic Job Is Right for You?}}</ref> Cabuck avoided re-naming or overly customizing the shop due to a superstitious belief that it was haunted by [[Lomi Usqi]], a previous owner from over a hundred years prior to the Drezar's birth.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

After the birth of his [[Gender|son]], [[Cabuck Junior]],<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> around [[135 BBY]],<ref name="Dates" /> he sometimes<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|cabuck|Cabuck}}</ref> brought the shy child to work with him. Cabuck stocked the Noodle Shop's bar with more than broth, meaning it became a welcoming public house for both locals and visiting travelers.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> The Drezar always kept the [[noodle]]s flowing for his customers<ref name="Databank" /> and employed the [[velospeeder]] rider [[Miow Joon]] to keep the shop's circular noodle-drying rack spinning during the athlete's training seasons in exchange for offering Joon a space to work out and free [[carbohydrate]]-packed [[Foodstuff|food]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

===Bartender bystander===
{{Quote|Master Indara.''"<br />"''Yes, my child?''"<br />"''We have unfinished business.|Mae-ho Aniseya challenges Indara in Cabuck's shop|audio=IndaraMae-Unfinishedbusiness-LostFound.flac|<ref name="Lost Found" />}}
[[File:CabuckMae-LostFound.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Cabuck watches the hooded Mae-ho Aniseya warily from behind the counter at the Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop.|Cabuck was at the bar when Mae-ho Aniseya entered his shop.]]
While Cabuck was thirty-four [[Standard year|years]] old<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> in [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Dates" /> the [[assassin]] [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] entered the noodle shop in [[Mae's quest|search]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Indara]].<ref name="Lost Found" /> As Aniseya surveyed the room, Cabuck [[eye]]d her with suspicion<ref name="Lost Found AD">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> while tending the bar, but went back to his work as Aniseya approached the table where Indara was sitting with several other patrons. Intending to [[Death|kill]] the [[Jedi]], Aniseya demanded that Indara fight her, attacking the other patrons sitting with her target when the Jedi refused.<ref name="Lost Found" />

Cabuck remained behind the bar and watched as Aniseya subdued the other patrons, after which Indara engaged the assassin. As the eatery's customers fled, Cabuck remained half-crouched behind the bar, rearing back when Aniseya leapt past him to the building's upper level, where she took<ref name="Lost Found" /> [[Kella Antro]], who delivered seasoning minerals to Cabuck's shop,<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> hostage. Indara pursued Aniseya and rescued Antro when Aniseya threw<ref name="Lost Found" /> her<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> from the shop's interior balcony. The pair then continued fighting until Cabuck accidentally drew their attention by making a noise from behind the counter.<ref name="Lost Found" />

===Witness to a murder===
{{Listen3|CabuckGasp-LostFound.flac|Cabuck gasps|Cabuck lets out a gasp as Indara stops a knife just before it hits him}}
[[File:CabuckDagger-LostFound.png|thumb|left|300px|alt=Cabuck stares in shock with his mouth open at a dagger suspended in air inches from his face.|Cabuck was saved from Mae-ho Aniseya's knife by Master Indara, who used the Force to freeze it in mid-air.]]
Cabuck looked up at the two combatants but did not flee as Aniseya hurled a [[Mae's knives|throwing knife]] at the bartender's head. Indara [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|halt]] the [[Knife|blade]] in mid-air, inches from Cabuck's face, but this distracted the Jedi and allowed Aniseya to throw another blade into Indara's chest, [[Indara's murder|fatally wounding her]]. As the Jedi Master collapsed, the blade in front of Cabuck fell from the air to the counter, causing the Drezar to gasp. After watching Indara's final moments, Aniseya approached the bar as Cabuck backed away from her with his [[Limb|hands]] raised<ref name="Lost Found" /> submissively.<ref name="Lost Found AD" />

Aniseya collected the blade she had thrown at Cabuck and then raised it to throw at the bartender but paused upon hearing whimpering from beneath the bar. Both Aniseya and Cabuck looked down to find<ref name="Lost Found" /> the three-year old<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> Cabuck Junior, who clutched at the barkeeper's leg and whimpered. Cabuck slowly placed his hand upon his child's head, while Aniseya hesitated and then turned away and left the noodle shop. The bartender looked up to see her go and then turned to tend to his child.<ref name="Lost Found" />

===A case of mistaken identity===
{{Quote|I'll know her when I see her.|Cabuck, to Tasi Lowa|audio=Cabuck-KnowHer-LostFound.flac|<ref name="Lost Found" />}}
[[File:CabuckAccusation-LostFound.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Cabuck stands in the doorway of Verosha Aniseya's bunk room pointing accusingly.|Cabuck mistakenly accused Verosha Aniseya of Indara's murder.]]
During the [[Jedi Order]]'s investigation of Indara's death, the Jedi mistakenly believed that Aniseya's identical [[Aniseya family|twin]], [[Verosha Aniseya]], had committed the murder based on the assassin's physical description, as Mae-ho was believed to have [[Mission to Brendok|perished]] years prior. Verosha was working as a [[meknek]] on the [[Trade Federation]]<ref name="Lost Found" /> [[WD-2550 Whydah-class Yield Conveyer|freighter]] ''[[Fallon]]'',<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Yord Fandar]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Tasi Lowa]] [[Mission to arrest Osha Aniseya|brought]] Cabuck onto the [[freighter]] in order to confirm the identification.<ref name="Lost Found" />

After the Jedi confronted Verosha at her [[Bunk 23|bunk]], Lowa fetched Cabuck and brought him to her.<ref name="Lost Found" /> Not having much choice in the matter,<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2025 Topps Star Wars Chrome|cardname=#110 - Cabuck|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/544247/cid/31223592/2025-Topps-Chrome-Star-Wars-110-Cabuck}}</ref> the bartender immediately pointed to Verosha and declared her Indara's killer, also complaining that she had ruined his bar. The Jedi then arrested Verosha for the murder and transferred her to<ref name="Lost Found" /> the [[DTE-CSA-17 Calaboose prison ship]] ''[[Palwick]]''<ref name="Palwick">{{Databank|palwick-prison-ship|Palwick prison ship}}</ref> to be taken to<ref name="Lost Found" /> the planet<ref name="Lost Found AD" /> [[Coruscant]] to face trial.<ref name="Lost Found" />

==Personality and traits==
Cabuck's superstitious belief in ghosts was strong enough to affect his business decisions, although he still brought his child to the shop.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> He did not flee from the fight in his eatery but surrendered<ref name="Lost Found" /> submissively<ref name="Lost Found AD" /> when Mae-ho approached. He later confidently misidentified her identical [[twin]] as Mae-ho.<ref name="Lost Found" /> The bartender had wideset [[Color|green]] eyes that allowed him to attend to multiple guests while watching out for his son at the shop and calloused brown skin that had been toughened by a lifetime of kitchen work<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> by 132 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].<ref name="Dates" /> His chest-length [[Hair|beard]]<ref name="Lost Found AD" /> was a graying brown. He stood 1.93 [[meter]]s (6 [[foot]] 4 inches) tall.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Equipment==
At the shop, Cabuck wore a black [[Clothing|shirt]], with black pants, black shoes, a black [[belt]], and a brown apron. Several containers including a secret [[Spice (seasoning)|spice]] shaker hung from his belt, and he also wore a brown satchel with a black strap.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Design===
{{Quote|That facial anatomy&mdash;the wide eyes, the descending eyebrows&mdash;wasa very good for setting the scene and showing their fear. He has a blind spot in the middle like a rabbit, which is why when the blade comes toward him, his child is much more shocked than he is. He actually didn't even see it. All these proportions, with his son holding onto the leg, played a part in the design. You're working toward one little vignette that will tell the moment.|Neal Scanlan|<ref name="ArtOf">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''</ref>}}
[[File:CabuckAndSon-ArtStation.jpg|thumb|right|300px|alt=Concept art of Cabuck and Cabuck Junior showing them with purpler skin and hats.|Costume concept of Cabuck and Cabuck Junior by Sophie Fretwell]]
Cabuck appeared in "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="Lost Found" /> the first episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which aired [[June 4]], [[2024]] on [[Disney+]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> Prior to the release of the episode, Cabuck was revealed in a trailer exclusively shown at [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Celebration">{{CelebrationTrailer|''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''|Celebration Europe (2023)}}</ref> during the [[Lucasfilm]] Studio Showcase on [[April 7]], [[2023]].<ref name="CelebrationDate">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-lucasfilm-studio-showcase|text=SWCE 2023: 11 Highlights from Lucasfilm's Studio Showcase}}</ref> Several early concept images of the noodle bar by concept artist [[Ferenc Norhof]] showed an [[Ithorian]] bartender instead of Cabuck while other concepts by concept artist [[Andrew Smith]] had it staffed entirely by droids.<ref name="ArtOf" />

[[Martin Rezard]] designed and sculpted Cabuck's head and hands, sculpting the head in [[Wikipedia:ZBrush|ZBrush]]. [[Brian Best]] then used ZBrush to create a 3D mould that was printed and reinforced in fibreglass before foam was added. The hands were a traditional plasticine sculpt.<ref name="RezardEmail">{{EmailCite|email=File:MartinRezard-AcolyteEmail.png|creator=Martin Rezard|date=2025-10-05}}</ref> Creature and Droid FX creative supervisor [[Neal Scanlan]] felt that the bartender's wide eyes and descending eyebrows helped set up the character's fear while also creating a blind spot in the center of his vision. This meant Cabuck would not have even seen the blade thrown at him by Mae-ho Aniseya, meaning Cabuck Junior would have been more frightened by the attack than his father.<ref name="ArtOf" />

The character's costume was designed by costume designer [[Jennifer Bryan]] with a concept illustrations of it drawn by illustrator [[Sophie Fretwell]]. The illustrations showed Cabuck with purple skin<ref name="ArtOf" /> and in one he is wearing a tall [[hat]].<ref name="Artstation">{{ArtStation|username=sophie_fretwell|url=artwork/8Bm5eR|name=[[Sophie Fretwell]]|text=The Acolyte - Crowd costume Illustrations}}</ref> Bryan chose a leather apron for the character to lean into the rugged nature of his work in the rough-and-tumble noodle bar.<ref name="ArtOf" />

In the first two episodes alone I had three speaking characters and worked on three additional non speaking characters. This picture depicts the three dialogue characters in episode one, where I provided animatronic lip-synch and on set voice acting (reference guide voices) with the principle cast. One was for the noodle bartender, another for Captain Blex, the Niemoidian and the third for the prison convict. Performing wonderfully in the actual costumes themselves were Liam Cook (noodle guy and convict) and James Henri-Thomas (Niemoidian). Eyes and brows expertly performed by Colin Purves and Adrian Parish. Beautiful work from all the CFX team as always! Everyone worked so damn hard on this show and I love how it's turned out. So happy for everyone involved! 🥳|archivedate=2024-06-19|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/5Qlxw}}</ref> The scenes in the shop were filmed at [[Shinfield Studios]],<ref name="Fantha Part I">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fanthatracks.com/news/the-acolyte-a-journey-to-the-filming-locations-part-1-madeira|text=The Acolyte: A journey to the filming locations Part 1: Madeira|archivedate=20241204220147}}</ref> [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="Julian Foddy">{{Lucasfilm|url=news/julian-foddy-acolyte|text=''The Acolyte'' Visual Effects Supervisor Julian Foddy}}</ref> ''The Acolyte's'' action designer and second unit director, [[Christopher Clark Cowan]], wanted the duel in the shop to show that Aniseya was testing Indara to see if the Jedi would protect the innocent,<ref name="Stunts-Noodleshop" /> with the assassin targeting bystanders like Antro and Cabuck.<ref name="Lost Found" /> The character was identified by an entry for the Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop in the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] section of [[StarWars.com]], which was released the day after the episode on [[June 5]], 2024.<ref name="Lomi Databank" />
