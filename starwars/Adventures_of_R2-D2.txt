'''''Adventures of R2-D2''''' was a board game by [[Kenner Products|Kenner]] and [[Parker Brothers]]<ref name="SW.com" /> in [[October]] [[1977]],<ref name="Cargo Bay" /> based on the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="SW.com" /> The game's objective was to "Follow [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2's]] adventures along a brightly colored path."<ref name="BGG" />
