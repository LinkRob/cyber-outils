The '''Batonn Defense Force''' were the local armed forces of the [[planet]] [[Batonn]] who answered to [[Governor]] [[Restos]]. They were opponents of the [[Batonn insurgents]], who were led by [[Nevil Cygni]]. The Defense Force took part in the [[siege of the Creekpath Mining and Refining complex]] alongside the [[Galactic Empire]] and made up half of the besieging forces.<ref name="Thrawn" />
