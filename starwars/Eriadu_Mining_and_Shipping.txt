'''Eriadu Mining and Shipping''', also known as '''Eriadu Mining''', was a corporation based on [[Eriadu]] that specialized in [[mining]] and shipping operations. For some time, the company was forced to deal with [[pirate]]s in their area of influence. Later, after the [[Trade Federation]]'s monopoly over the [[Outer Rim Territories]] was broken as a result of their failed [[Invasion of Naboo|occupation]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], Eriadu Mining found greater prosperity within the Outer Rim.<ref name="Tarkin" />
