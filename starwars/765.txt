However, 765 and their companions were destroyed by [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] crashing their [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptors]] on the ''Invisible Hand''<nowiki/>'s [[hangar]] deck.<ref name="LSS"/>
