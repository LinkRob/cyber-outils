In [[2 BBY]], [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[cadet]]s [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Derek Klivian]] were able to '''escape''' from [[Skystrike Academy]] with the help of [[Sabine Wren]] and the [[Spectres|rebels]] of [[Phoenix Cell]]. The escape began after Antilles, Klivian, and [[Rake Gahree]] expressed sympathies towards the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebellion]] against the Galactic Empire, which was reported to Phoenix Squadron by [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]&mdash;and secretly a rebel informant who operated under the codename [[Fulcrum]]. Phoenix Squadron tasked Wren, a former Imperial cadet herself, with infiltrating Skystrike Academy and making contact with the would-be defectors.

Wren entered the installation while two other rebels, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Ezra Bridger]], waited for a signal to help exfiltrate the cadets. Wren's mission encountered an obstacle when [[Arihnda Pryce]], [[Governor]] of the [[Lothal sector]], arrived with Agent Kallus to hunt the defecting cadets. During the first escape attempt, Rake was [[Death|killed]] while Wren and the cadets were captured. Wren managed to free herself and the two others, however, and made their way through the facility with the covert help of Agent Kallus. Wren and the cadets were picked up by the [[Jedi]] and returned to [[Chopper Base]] on [[Atollon]], where Antilles and Klivian joined Phoenix Squadron. Both went on to fight for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], with Antilles becoming a noted pilot in both the Rebel Alliance and the [[New Republic]].

==Prelude==
[[File:FulcrumContact-TAA.png|thumb|left|250px|The Lothal rebels and Commander Sato listening to Fulcrum]]
In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Imperial forces [[Massacre near Teralov|attacked]] a rebel convoy near [[Teralov]]. The Imperials ignored the rebels' pleas for mercy, and massacred the entire convoy. Around the same time, three disillusioned [[Imperial cadet]]s at the elite [[Skystrike Academy]], [[Wedge Antilles]], [[Derek Klivian|Hobbie]], and [[Rake Gahree]], decided to defect from the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]]. They sent a transmission through the auspices of a rebel agent named [[Fulcrum]], who relayed their message to [[Phoenix Cell]].<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" /> Unbeknownst to the [[Spectres]] and Phoenix Squadron, Fulcrum was actually [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]], a former long-standing enemy of the Spectres, who had become sympathetic to the rebellion after an encounter with Spectres member [[Garazeb Orrelios]] on the [[Geonosis|Geonosian]] [[moon]] [[Bahryn]].<ref name="The Honorable Ones">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref><ref name="An Inside Man">{{Rebels|An Inside Man}}</ref>

Reeling from the loss of the rebel convoy, [[Phoenix Leader]] [[Hera Syndulla]] dispatched [[Mandalorian]] former Imperial cadet [[Sabine Wren]] on a mission to infiltrate Skystrike Academy and extract the cadets. The former Imperial [[RA-7 protocol droid]] [[AP-5]] prepared fake identification for Sabine, who adopted the persona Ria Talla. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Kanan Jarrus]], his [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Ezra Bridger]], and the [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] were assigned to dispatch Wren to the Academy and to await picking her up.<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" />

==The mission==
===Going undercover===
[[File:Sabine_and_defectors_Antilles_Extraction.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Hobbie, Rake, Wedge, and Sabine]]
Wren managed to infiltrate Skystrike Academy with little problems. She met Antilles during a [[Simulator pod|simulator]] training session, and the two quickly befriended each other. Wren and Antilles shared an unease with the Academy's curriculum, which encouraged Imperial cadets to take no prisoners and to shoot unarmed, surrendering transports. Later, Wren overheard Antilles and his friends Hobbie and Gahree talking in secret about the message they'd sent, which gave her confirmation that they were the would-be defectors. While alone with Antilles, Wren disclosed that she was a rebel agent and reassured him that the rebellion had received his transmission. She told Antilles and his friends to be ready to leave at a moment's notice.<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" />

Wren's plan to extricate the defecting cadets was complicated by the arrival of [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] and Agent Kallus, who had received word about the defectors. Governor Pryce issued orders to [[Flight instructor|Instructor]] [[Goran]] to lock down the Academy while Agent Kallus questioned the cadets. When Kallus' investigation yielded no evidence of defectors, Governor Pryce and Instructor Goran set a trap by ordering the cadets to engage in a mock combat exercise in space. Pryce had secretly had [[kill switch]]es installed on the [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] the cadets would be flying.<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" />

Wren and the cadets took their chance to escape, attempting to rendezvous with Bridger's [[CR90 corvette]]. However, Governor Pryce then activated the kill switches, detaching the four cadets' TIE fighters' [[solar collector]] panels; leaving them stranded in space. She then dispatched [[Captain]] [[Vult Skerris]] and three [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE interceptors]] into the fray. Skerris destroyed Gahree's TIE fighter before leading an attack on Bridger's corvette, inflicting substantial damage to the hull. Despite Bridger's protests, Jarrus and Chopper withdrew the corvette from the engagement.<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" />

===Second escape===
[[File:Wedge_TIE_bomber.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Sabine and the Imperial cadets escaping in a TIE bomber]]
Wren, Antilles, and Hobbie were brought before Governor Pryce, who demanded to know which one of them was the rebel agent. To save Antilles from being tortured, Wren identified herself as the rebel agent. Antilles, worried about Wren, accidentally let her real name slip while he and Hobbie were led out of the cell. While Pryce prepared to interrogate Wren, Kallus led the other cadets into a holding cell. After identifying Wren as a former Imperial cadet and telling her that she'd come "home", Pryce ordered her [[stormtrooper]]s to fasten Wren to the torture device. However, the former cadet broke free of her captors and engaged Pryce in a fist fight. Despite Pryce putting up stiff resistance, Wren managed to knock her out by pushing her into the path of the torture device's stinger.<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" />

Wren then went to Antilles and Hobbie's holding cell. After knocking out two stormtrooper sentries, she freed the two cadets. Together, the three fled down a corridor while dodging stormtroopers and [[blast door]]s. They encountered Agent Kallus, who, to Wren's considerable surprise and suspicion, helped Wren and the cadets escape by directing them to [[Hangar]] Bay 24. By way of explanation for his actions, he told Wren to tell her [[Lasat]] friend Garazeb Orrelios that they were now even.<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" /> Due to Kallus' history with the Spectres, it did not occur to Wren that he might be Fulcrum, as Orellios had not told the others about what had happened on Bahryn.<ref name="An Inside Man" /> The trio managed to commandeer a [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE bomber]], chosen because it could take a hit, and fled into the atmosphere outside Skystrike Academy.<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" />

The cadets were pursued by Captain Skerris in his TIE Interceptor, which inflicted damage on the slow-moving bomber. Fortunately for Wren and the cadets, they were picked up by Bridger's CR90 corvette. After being safely evacuated aboard the corvette, the rebels and the defecting cadets escaped into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" />

==Aftermath==
At [[Atollon]]'s [[Chopper Base]], Wren presented Antilles and Hobbie to her commanding officer, [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]]. When Antilles asked whether the rebellion had been looking for pilots, Sato confirmed that this was the case and welcomed the two young men into the rebellion.<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" /> Antilles and Hobbie both became rebel pilots, and Antilles, in particular, became a famous war hero, flying against [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|both]] [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Stars]].<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref><ref name="Return of the Jedi">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

The Spectres and Phoenix Squadron did not find out that Kallus was Fulcrum until later, when Jarrus, Bridger, and Chopper [[Infiltration of the Imperial Armory Complex|infiltrated]] the [[Imperial Armory Complex]] on [[Lothal]]. When Jarrus and Bridger informed the rest of the team about Kallus' assistance, they were shocked and suspicious. Orrelios was as surprised as the others, reasoning that he had "accidentally" recruited the ISB agent, and was forced to admit that Kallus had been on Bahryn. Syndulla, suspicious that Kallus was playing a kind of long game, decided that they would use caution with their new ally.<ref name="An Inside Man" />

Wren's experience with kill switches became useful later, on the Spectres' [[Theft of the TIE Defender Elite|mission]] to Lothal the next year. She and Bridger wound up stealing the prototype [[TIE/D Defender Elite]], which was equipped with one as an anti-theft measure. While deactivating the starfighter's [[homing beacon]], Wren discovered the switch and immediately advised Bridger, who was piloting, to land. When the switch was activated, the crash was not as bad as it would have been because the Elite was already being brought in for a landing, allowing Bridger and Wren to escape with the fighter's [[flight data recorder]] and [[hyperdrive]].<ref name="Flight of the Defender">{{Rebels|Flight of the Defender}}</ref>

Antilles later recounted his experience many years after the [[battle of Endor]] to [[Vanguard Five (pilot)|Vanguard Five]] aboard the ''[[Temperance]]'' before taking over [[Victorum|ISD ''Victorum'']]. He and [[Lindon Javes]] have in common - flying for the Empire then defected to the rebellion. Antilles tells him that defecting is the smartest move he ever made and wished his friends would do the same.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The escape from Skystrike Academy first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[The Antilles Extraction]]," which premiered on [[Disney XD]] on [[October 8]], [[2016]].
