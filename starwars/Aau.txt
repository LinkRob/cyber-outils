'''Aau''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Korban]] [[Gender|girl]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Korba]] with her [[Parent|father]], [[Abat]]. On Korba, Aau washed [[Unidentified purple beast|beasts]] near the entrance of a [[mine]] shaft where Abat worked by [[19 BBY]]. After the [[Jedi Order|Jedi]] [[Kratu]] visited the pair's [[Aau's home|home]], they traveled to their respective workstations. Aau abandoned hers and followed her father to a tunnel, where she found a mine entrance in a [[mountain]].

She entered it to find a [[cave]] full of [[kyber crystal]]s and started to [[Musician|sing]], purifying the [[crystal]]s with her voice and destroying the cavern's spire, causing the mountain to collapse. Abat then dug a hole into the cave and rescued his daughter. However, the mountain's collapse cut a bridge the pair were using to escape; as a result, Aau and her father fell and were subsequently [[Telekinesis|saved]] from the fall by Kratu. While the [[Jedi]] used [[the Force]] to [[Force barrier|protect]] them from several falling stones, Aau sang and purified the mountains' kyber crystal. Shortly after, the girl and Kratu left Korba so that the Jedi could nurture Aau's ability to purify kyber.

==Biography==
===A curious mind===
{{Quote|You know that your voice near the crystal is dangerous. It frightens me to think what will happen when I'm not there to protect you. You must learn restraint, Aau. Do you understand? I just want you to be safe.|Abat, to Aau|<ref name="Aau" />}}
[[File:AauWindow.png|thumb|left|300px|Aau purified kyber crystals when she sang.]]
A [[Color|blue]]-skinned [[Force-sensitive]]<ref name="Aau" /> [[Korban]],<ref name="Filmmaker">{{VisionsFocus|Aau's Song}}</ref> Aau was the young [[Gender|daughter]] of the [[miner]] [[Abat]]. They lived on the [[planet]] [[Korba]], whose mountains were rich with [[kyber crystal]]s,<ref name="Aau" /> which the [[Jedi Order]] used to build [[lightsaber]]s, their [[weapon]]s.<ref name="Test">{{TCW|A Test of Strength}}</ref> She washed large, [[Unidentified purple beast|purple beasts]] at a [[Unidentified Korba settlement|settlement]] located near the entrance of a kyber crystal [[Mining|mine]] shaft and lived with her [[Parent|father]] at<ref name="Aau" /> a round [[Aau and Abat's dwelling|dwelling]].<ref name="AauAD">{{Visions|Aau's Song}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref>

At some point, they learned that the girl's voice could cause kyber [[crystal]]s to release energy when she [[Musician|sang]], which her father deemed dangerous.<ref name="Aau" /> By [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|209}}</ref> Aau climbed a cliff with a rope and looked at the landscape's spires, singing and watching as the [[Jedi]] [[Kratu]]'s [[Kratu's starship|starship]] traveled to her home, rushing there. Abat and his daughter then [[Diet|ate]] a [[Foodstuff|soup]] shortly before Kratu landed her [[starship]] near their home.

===With no sense===
{{Quote|You've grown. And so curious. Hmm. They ''call'' to you.|Kratu, to Aau|<ref name="Aau" />}}
Abat instructed Aau to keep her distance, and she left and lay on the ground near the Jedi starship. The [[Youngling|child]], however, disobeyed her father and listened in on his and Kraut's conversation from one of her home's windows. Abat showed the Jedi a dark kyber crystal, and Aau started to sing, alerting her father to her presence.<ref name="Aau" />

[[File:AauLunch.png|thumb|right|300px|Aau and Abat had lunch before Kratu arrived.]]
As a result, the girl fell from the window and her father and Kratu walked outside while Abat started to remind<ref name="Aau" /> his<ref name="AauAD" /> child that he had told her to keep her distance; however, the Jedi interrupted him, greeting Aau and noting that the crystals "called" to her. Kratu left, and Abat told his daughter that her voice near the kyber was dangerous. He expressed his fear for when Aau was on her own, instructing her to learn restraint. They then left on a [[speeder]] for the settlement where they worked.<ref name="Aau" />

===A purifying voice===
{{Quote|Aau.''"<br />"''It's okay, Abi. ''[sings]''<nowiki />|Abat and Aau|<ref name="Aau" />}}
[[File:AauSpires.png|thumb|left|300px|Aau purifies the spires' crystals.]]
At the settlement, Aau helped his father equip his mining equipment, and Abat wished her a good [[Standard day|day]] at work before departing. Aau then washed a purple beast with a brush and pried a [[Unidentified shelled insect|shelled insect]] off the creature's body with a thin paddle, placing the [[insect]] on the floor and chuckling as it entered a rock.<ref name="Aau" />

She left her station, retrieved a mining [[helmet]] from a wall, and jumped to a spire. She walked to another spire and climbed a cliff to a tunnel, where she encountered her father and the miner [[Attu]]. Ducking and turning her head to the right, Aau noticed another mining entrance and ran to the floor, screaming as she almost fell off a cliff. Attu, having heard her scream, looked down at the girl, who hid behind a rock, with the miner claiming that [[rock mouse]] had caused the sound.<ref name="Aau" />

Aau then entered the mine she had noticed, encountering a [[cave]]rn containing numerous kyber crystals and singing, purifying the crystals but interrupted by her father that causing the cave to collapse. Abat noticed that the spire was shaking, sliding down toward his child with a rope and bringing her out of the cave to its entrance.<ref name="Aau" />

===Into the stars===
{{Quote|You're the first of our kind to step out into the stars, Aau. I'm proud of you.|Abat, to Aau|<ref name="Aau" />}}
The pair and Attu then tried to escape via a [[wood]]en bridge; however, the cave's fall had collapsed the nearby spires, and the subsequent chasm detached one of the bridge's sides, and Abat fell. Aau tried to hold one of the poles holding the bridge, but she failed, crying and falling with her father. Kratu then jumped down moments before the pair slammed into the ground, using [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|stop]] their fall and creating a [[Force barrier|Force shield]] to protect them from the stones falling above them.<ref name="Aau" />

[[File:AauEmbrace.png|thumb|right|300px|Aau and Abat embrace before Aau leaves for the stars.]]
Closing her [[eye]]s, Aau sang, purifying the spires' crystals and stopping the spires' collapse. She buried her face in Abat's shoulder as he kneeled in<ref name="Aau" /> amazement.<ref name="AauAD" /> Kratu then told her that she could help Aau nurture her voice if she came with her, telling Abat that they could not choose where their "calling" took them, only whether or not they would answer.<ref name="Aau" />

Shortly after, Aau and her father sat on the edge of a cliff, sharing a<ref name="Aau" /> pod,<ref name="AauAD" /> which they ate. Abat told her she was the first Korban to leave for the [[star]]s, and she boarded Kratu's ship. She waved to his father as the vessel rose to the sky.<ref name="Aau" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Aau. You've grown. And so curious. Hmm. They ''call'' to you. And it takes courage to heed the call.|Kratu, to Aau|<ref name="Aau" />}}
Aau was a curious girl with green [[hair]], blue skin, and pink eyes. She cried Abat's name as he fell from the bridge; before leaving Korba, she told her father that she would miss him.<ref name="Aau" /> The girl longed to sing,<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-visions|text=SWCE 2023: ''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Trailer, Key Art, and Cast Revealed}}</ref> and was filled with a desire to connect with the kyber crystals through singing.<ref name="Visit">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-2-interview|text=With ''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2, Creators from Around the World Visit the Galaxy Far, Far Away}}</ref>

==Equipment==
Aau wore a blue [[Clothing|skirt]], a light jacket with a brown belt, which featured two pockets, a red collar, light brown trousers, and two brown shoes.<ref name="Aau" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|Your voice, Aau. Across the thousand stars I have traveled, never have I heard anything like it. It is a gift. And I can help you nurture it, but you have to come with me.''"<br />"''But her life is here.''"<br />"''We cannot choose where our calling takes us. Only whether or not to answer. Consider it.|Kratu, to Aau, and Abat|<ref name="Aau" />}}
Aau was able to purify kyber crystals by singing, which Kratu considered a gift. The Jedi had never seen anything like the girl's voice across the "thousand stars she had traveled."<ref name="Aau" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aau appeared in the [[Triggerfish Animation Studios|Triggerfish]] studio's short film "[[Aau's Song]]," the ninth episode of the anthology series ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]''{{'s}} [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|second season]], which released<ref name="Aau" /> on [[May 4]], 2023.<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-2|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Release Date, Studios Revealed}}</ref> Aau was voiced by [[Mpilo Jantjie]], and the character's singing was performed by [[Dineo Du Toit]].<ref name="Aau" /> Before the short film's release, Aau was pictured on a [[:File:Visions 2 poster.jpg|poster]] and first mentioned on a [[StarWars.com]] article published on [[April 10]], [[2023]] during the [[Celebration Europe (2023)|''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe]] convention.<ref name="Reveal" />
