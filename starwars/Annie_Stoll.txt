'''Annie Stoll''' is an award-winning designer, illustrator and art director. She has freelanced for [[Lucasfilm]] as a graphic designer creating designs for ''[[Star Wars]]'' merchandise. She has created illustrations as ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''{{'}} [[Graffiti/Legends|graffiti]] artist [[Sabine Wren]] for the book ''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]'' by author [[Daniel Wallace|Dan Wallace]].

==Biography==
Annie is a Grammy-nominated Art Director.

Annie was born in [[Wikipedia:Buffalo, New York|Buffalo, New York]] and studied design and illustration at [[Wikipedia:Daemen College|Daemen College]]. She moved to [[Wikipedia:New York City|New York City]], where she is currently an Art Director with [[Wikipedia:Sony Music|Sony Music Entertainment]]. She is the youngest woman to have been nominated for a Grammy for [[Wikipedia:Grammy Award for Best Recording Package|Best Recording Package]]. Her work has won many awards and been featured in magazines such as ''Communication Arts''. Her art style is controlled chaos with [[Wikipedia:anime|anime]] and street influences.{{Mediacat|imagecat=images by Annie Stoll}}
