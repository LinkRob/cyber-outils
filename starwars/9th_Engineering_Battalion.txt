The '''9th Engineering Battalion''' was an [[engineer]]ing [[battalion]] within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]. Later the 9th Engineering Battalion was part of the [[Galactic Empire]]. After serving as a [[Judicial]], [[Lira Hardin]] was a member of the battalion during the war and into the [[Imperial Era]] but left after she witnessed the [[massacre at Rampa I]].<ref name="Friends" />
