{{Quote|To give an example, on Coruscant, Pollux Hax&mdash;one of Palpatine's most ideological and nefarious officials&mdash;was very open in explaining the benefits of 'segregated areas of the cities' where non-humans were pushed together ostensibly to allow them to live their lives according to shared customs, but in practice to forcibly separate them from humans.|The information cited to ''An Imperial Guide to Coruscant'' by Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
'''''An Imperial Guide to Coruscant''''' was a travel guide to the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the [[Imperial Era]] [[author]]ed by [[Pollux Hax]], an official of the [[Galactic Empire]]. In it, he explained the supposed benefits of areas of the [[Imperial City|city]] on Coruscant where [[Alien|non-humans]] were forced to live together separate from [[human]]s, claiming that it was so that they could live according to their shared customs. This information from the guide was cited by [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
''An Imperial Guide to Coruscant'' was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Pollux Hax/Legends|Pollux Hax]] was created as the [[in-universe]] author for the [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] section of the [[1995]] reference book ''[[The Illustrated Star Wars Universe]]'', which was written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]].<ref name="Illustrated">''[[The Illustrated Star Wars Universe]]''</ref>
