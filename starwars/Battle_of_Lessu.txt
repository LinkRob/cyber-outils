The '''Battle of Lessu''', remembered as "'''The Hammer of Ryloth'''"<ref name="Homecoming" /> and also known as the '''liberation of Lessu''',<ref name="DawnofRebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> was the final engagement of the [[Battle of Ryloth]]<ref name="Liberty" /> fought in [[21 BBY]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Acting as the culmination of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]'s occupation of the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] and the [[Galactic Republic]]'s liberation effort, the Republic's [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps]] under [[Jedi General]] [[Mace Windu]] allied with the local [[Twi'lek Resistance]] under General [[Cham Syndulla]]. Together, Windu's and Syndulla's forces stormed the planetary [[capital]] of [[Lessu City]] and reclaimed it from [[Emir]] [[Wat Tambor]], who agreed to an unconditional surrender.<ref name="Liberty" /> The battle was celebrated across Ryloth<ref name="Homecoming" /> and became required study in military academies.<ref name="DawnofRebellion" />
