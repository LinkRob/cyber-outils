'''Dathomir''', nicknamed the "'''rancor planet'''," was a remote, neutral, and obscure [[planet]] in the [[Dathomir system]] of the [[Quelii sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]]. The [[dark side of the Force]] had an immensely strong presence on Dathomir, which allowed for the nurturing of many malevolent [[Force-sensitive|Force wielders]] native to the planet. Several [[witch]] [[Dathomiri clan|clans]] were prominent on the planet and practiced the [[The Force|Force-based]] abilities of [[magick]]. These clans included the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]], [[Blue Coral Divers Clan|Blue Coral Diver]], [[Singing Mountain (clan)|Singing Mountain]], and [[Howling Crag]] clans. Under the rule of the Nightsisters were the [[Nightbrothers]], a group of male Dathomirian warriors who were kept as [[Reproduction|breeding partners]]. The planet, bathed in blood-[[Color|red]] light by its [[Star|sun]] [[Domir]], had numerous continents that were overrun with vegetation, [[forest]]s, and [[swamp]]lands. It was also the homeworld of the [[Sentience|semi-sentient]] [[rancor]] species.

During the [[Clone Wars]], Dathomir would come under attack multiple times due to the machinations of [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]] and her rival [[Darth Sidious]], leading to the destruction of most of the Nightsisters and settlements on the planet by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] after conquering it. [[Asajj Ventress]], a Nightsister trained by [[Count]] [[Dooku]], returned to Mother Talzin after her master [[Battle of Sullust|betrayed]] her. This drew Dooku's attention to Dathomir, resulting in the slaughter of the Nightsisters. This left Talzin, Ventress, [[Merrin]], [[Shelish]], [[Jerserra]], [[Morgan Elsbeth]], [[Yenna]], and the three [[Great Mothers]] as the only known survivors.

In [[2 BBY]], [[Maul]] returned to Dathomir with the [[Jedi]] rebel [[Ezra Bridger]] to conduct a ritual that involved summoning the spirits of fallen Nightsisters in order to reveal fragments of a [[holocron]] vision. As a result, Maul and Ezra learned that the [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] held the key to destroying the [[Sith]] and that he resided on a [[Tatooine|planet]] with [[Tatoo I|twin]] [[Tatoo II|suns]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Dathomir is a dangerous world inhabited by a number of deadly creatures, such as the chirodactyl, an apex predator.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="The Secrets of the Sith" />}}
[[File:Nightfall-DC.png|thumb|left|250px|Being home to several predators, such as the rancor and the chirodactyl, Dathomir was an extremely dangerous place.]]
Dathomir was a remote and uninviting [[Planet|world]],<ref name="Databank Dathomir">{{Databank|dathomir|Dathomir}}</ref> although it was temperate and verdant, with a comfortable [[atmosphere]] despite its challenging ecosystem and dangerous [[Diet|predators]]. The [[Dathomirian]] subspecies of [[Zabrak]] was native to the planet.<ref name="VE" /> Perpetually bathed in a red mist, its terrain was one of contrasts, ranging from the murky [[swamp]]s the [[Nightsisters]] lived in, to the harsh, barren rocks where the [[Nightbrothers]] found residence.<ref name="Monster" /> Not many of Dathomir's residents left the planet, and those elsewhere in [[the galaxy]] rarely visited it.<ref name="II: TN">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-inside-intel-the-nightsisters|text=''Star Wars'' Inside Intel: The Nightsisters|int=Star Wars Inside Intel}}</ref>

Besides the [[rancor]], other native creatures included [[Bane Back Spider|bane back spider]]s, large and aggressive [[Spider|arachnids]] that spat corrosive [[acid]], and [[Lesser Nydak|nydaks]], bipedal predators with powerful [[Limb|arms]] that allowed them to easily traverse the landscape. Among the most distinctive creatures native to Dathomir, however, was the [[chirodactyl]], a species of gigantic [[bat]] that used its massive wings to fly to high heights and swoop down on unwary prey. One example of this species, [[Gorgara]], was known by the native Nightbrothers as "the winged daemon of the canyon" and was particularly feared by them.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> Dathomir also had [[Water|aquatic]] life, such as the [[Burra fish]].<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> [[Fishing]] families on the [[planet]] Batuu cooked and cultivated the foreign Burra fish for consumption.<ref name="GE Stories">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] has official backstory elements that, though not directly published for the public, are officially canon details that can be obtained verbally from cast members working in the land.</ref> Another aquatic creature, [[the Sleeper]], was an ancient [[mollusk]] with two large [[Color|lavender]] [[eye]]s, a carapace-covered body, [[antenna]]e, and four [[Limb|appendages]], and lived deep in the murky blue water within the [[Nightsister lair]]. The Sleeper was very important in Nightsister [[Nightsisters#Society and culture|tradition]] as it had an ingredient vital in the [[Water of Life]].<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

==History==
===Offworlder arrivals===
{{Quote|My friend, I have never been one to shy away from the pursuit of knowledge, but the shadow of the dark side lies heavy in this tomb. I've uncovered Kujet's legacy: a ruthless leader who destroyed the Astriums and lives of any who opposed the sage's rule. These Zeffo were once Kujet's enemies, brave rebels who stood against tyranny. F-forgive me. I've spent too many rotations on this planet. My mind is beginning to slip. I can go no further. I must return to Zeffo.|Eno Cordova|<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />}}
[[File:RiftValleyDathomir-WotF.png|thumb|left|250px|The Rift Valley, site of a battle that took place on Dathomir.]]
Hundreds of [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="CompEncyc">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> the six-[[Limb|armed]] [[Fromprath]] [[species]] lived on<ref name="Last Shot" /> the planet that one day would be named Dathomir,<ref name="CompEncyc" /> where they exploited the natural resources of the planet.<ref name="TDS">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side]]''</ref> However, the Nightsisters<ref name="CompEncyc" />&mdash;an ancient order of [[witch]]es from [[Peridea]] that had learnt [[extragalactic]] travel from [[Far galaxy|their]] [[galaxy]] to [[the galaxy]]<ref name="Far Far Away">{{Ahsoka|Part Six: Far, Far Away}}</ref>&mdash;came to arrive on the planet, dubbing it "Dathomir" in honor of their Dathomiri origins and forcing out the Fromprath to claim the planet for themselves.<ref name="CompEncyc" /> In order to drive out their rivals, the Nightsisters formed a symbiotic alliance with rancors.<ref name="TDS" /> Fromprath were successfully [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] from the [[planet]]<ref name="Last Shot" /> by the Nightsisters.<ref name="TDS" />

[[Standard century|Centuries]] before the [[Proclamation of the New Order|fall]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Zeffonian]]s, an ancient technologically advanced [[species]] native to the planet [[Zeffo]], were interested in the planet that one day would be named Dathomir. The [[Zeffo Sage]] [[Kujet]] built a large, imposing [[Tomb of Kujet|palace]] on the planet which acted as his secluded seat of power. While in power, Kujet was known as a ruthless and merciless ruler. He promised his followers power over the [[The Force|Life Wind]], what the Zeffonians called The Force, and in a bid to gain power over the Zeffonians, Kujet ordered the destruction of all [[astrium]]s apart from one astrium that only he would possess. He also staged a massacre of his enemies at his palace where he casted his enemies into pits below his throne room to ensure that no one could challenge him. When Kujet [[Death|died]], he was placed in a sarcophagus in his palace with one of the very few remaining astriums. At some point, the palace and the planet was abandoned by the Zeffonians leaving behind a shadow of the dark side.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

===Caldoth-Nightsister conflict===
According to legend, there was a [[Sith]] named [[Darth Caldoth]] who stole a Nightsister [[burial pod]] on the planet to learn the secrets of the Nightsister reanimation process. Because of this, the Nightsisters patiently waited and plotted till Caldoth was defeated at a Jedi Temple on [[Bathoris]]. After Caldoth's defeat by [[Jedi Master]] [[Bran Ath'Morath]], the Nightsisters called for a gathering on the planet at night time. During the gathering, six Nightsisters were chosen and bound to pillars while they were attacked by the spirits of dead Nightsisters. One Nightsister called [[Zeldin]] successfully managed to survive through the night. Then the very next night, the Nightsister coven gathered and added their voices to support Zeldin as she enacted a ritual to persuade and influence Caldoth into doing the Nightsisters bidding.<ref name="TGC">{{StoryCite|story=The Gilded Cage|book=Dark Legends}}</ref>

The ritual worked and Zeldin, while in a deep trance, slowly and carefully influenced Caldoth. The Nightsisters later enacted a ritual of prognostication for a glimpse into the future. Learning that Caldoth would take a male [[Twi'lek]] named [[Ry Nymbis]] as his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], Zeldin infiltrated Caldoth's mind in the hopes that Nymbis would betray and kill Caldoth. This failed however leading to the Nightsisters performing another ritual of prognostication on Dathomir. Because of this, the Nightsisters were aware of an old totem guarded by the guardian spirit of one of the ancient monks of a temple on the [[Unknown Regions]] moon [[Obsidia]]. Knowing that the Guardian spirit could obliterate Caldoth, Zeldin carefully extended her influence in Caldoths mind and pointed him towards an ancient tablet containing the location of the old totem. However once again, Caldoth managed to escape his demise by using the Nightsisters binding ritual to trap the Guardian spirit.<ref name="TGC" />

Annoyed, the Nightsisters performed a final ritual and gained insight into the future of Caldoth where Caldoth was seemingly killed by his enemy, Morath. Armed with this knowledge, Zeldin once again steered Caldoth into seeking revenge on Morath for beating him on Bathoris. The plan failed however and Morath was instead beheaded by Caldoth in the city of [[Pundith]] on the world of [[Kizan]]. Frustrated, the Nightsisters attempted to perform a ritual to turn Caldoth into a prisoner of the Nightsisters. Instead Zeldin, while attempting to break the mental barriers of Caldoth's mind, became trapped in Caldoth's mind. From that day onwards, the Nightsisters did not come to Zeldin's aid and instead put Zeldin's body in a Nightsister burial pod. It was later hung in the exact same spot as the previous burial pod taken by Caldoth. After the incident, Caldoth visited Zeldin time to time to hear her screams.<ref name="TGC" />

===Late Republic Era===

Prior to the [[Battle of Naboo|Battle]] of [[Naboo]], [[Sith]] [[Sith Lord|Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] went to Dathomir where he traded secrets of the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] of [[the Force]] with the Nightsisters' leader, [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]]. He took Talzin's oldest son, the Nightbrother [[Maul]] as his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]].<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3" /> One of Talzin's [[Asajj Ventress's mother|fellow Nightsisters]] also had a child, [[Asajj Ventress]], and was forced by Talzin to give her up to the criminal [[Hal'Sted]] in order to protect their coven.<ref name="Nightsisters" />

Prior to the [[Clone Wars]], [[OteroaCorp]] created their twentieth settlement on Dathomir. [[Oteroa Zero-Twenty]] was located in a [[jungle]] near the [[Nardithi Cluster]]. An OteroaCorp [[Starship|ship]] called the ''[[The Oteroan Sky]]'' [[Crash of The Oteroan Sky|crashed]] after it was sabotaged by the [[Nardithi Nightsisters]] and it crashed into the city, destroying it.<ref name="GoD" />

Sometime prior to [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order timeline">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]] explored the planet for relics of the Zeffo civilization and discovered the Tomb of Kujet. Supposedly overcoming a trial of some description, Cordova made it inside the Tomb.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

[[File:OpressWarriorTest-Monster.jpg|thumb|left|Savage Opress in the Selection|220x220px]]
Darth Maul's [[R3-series astromech droid]] [[R3-B0017]], was programmed to scavenge the planet.<ref name="R3B0017">{{DisneyToyCite|set=Star Wars: Droid Factory (toy line)|pack=R3-BOO17|altlink=https://jedibusiness.com/figureDetails.aspx?id=12151}}</ref> During the Clone Wars, Ventress returned to Dathomir and her coven's [[Nightsister lair|village]] following [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[Battle of Sullust|assassination attempt]] at the [[Battle of Sullust]] at the order of Darth Sidious as proof of his loyalty since her growing power caused a great [[Disturbance in the Force]] that threatened his life. Ventress was then healed and accepted the Nightsisters' help in [[Mission to Serenno (Nightsisters)|assassinating]] Dooku for revenge. Despite Ventress' failure, Talzin was able to reach out to Dooku and offer him a new [[assassin]] to replace Ventress as a Plan B to start her plan for revenge on Sidious by helping the former Separatist get vengeance on the Count of [[Serenno]].<ref name="Nightsisters" />

Dooku traveled to Dathomir to the Nightsisters' village where he proposed an alliance between his [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and Talzin's coven. Talzin refused his offer but agreed to supply him with a Nightbrother similar to Maul. Dooku accepted her offer and departed. Ventress traveled to the [[Nightbrother village]], where she chose several to participate in [[Selection|tests]] in their [[Nightbrother arena|arena]]. After killing and wounding several of the chosen Nightbrothers, Ventress chose Talzin’s younger son and Maul’s brother [[Savage Opress]] to be her replacement. Ventress then took Opress to the Nightsisters' village, where they used their [[magick]] to increase his strength and power. After Opress demonstrated his loyalty by killing his [[Feral|brother]], he was given to Dooku.<ref name="Monster" />

[[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] went to Dathomir to speak with the Nightbrothers about Opress' whereabouts. Brother [[Viscus]] told them one of Talzin's Nightsisters took Opress as a mate to their village. Kenobi and Skywalker went to the Nightsisters, where Talzin told them that Opress was [[Showdown at Toydaria|on]] [[Toydaria]]. After Opress' role was discovered, he returned to Dathomir. There, Talzin told him that his last surviving brother, Maul, was in exile somewhere in the Outer Rim. Opress left to find him, promising to return.<ref name="Witches of the Mist" />

[[File:BattleOfDathomir-Massacre.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Nightsisters summon their Army of the Dead to defend against the Separatists.]]
Ventress returned to Dathomir, where she was fully accepted into the Nightsisters. However, [[Darth]] Sidious and Dooku decided to send [[General]] [[Grievous]] and his [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]] to Dathomir to wipe the Nightsisters out for their attempts on Dooku's life and because they were a threat to Sidious’ new evil [[Galactic Empire|Empire]]. At first, the Nightsister and their [[Army of the Dead]] were able to [[Battle of Dathomir|hold out]] against Grievous' [[Confederacy military|forces]]. However, Dooku was able to subdue the Nightsisters when Grievous killed Old [[Daka]]. All the Nightsisters, except Ventress, Talzin,<ref name="Massacre" /> [[Merrin]],<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> [[Shelish]],<ref name="Uprsing">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> and Yenna were eradicated.<ref name="Bug" /> While Ventress became a [[bounty hunter]],<ref name="Bounty">{{TCW|Bounty (episode)}}</ref> Talzin continued to guide Opress to his brother from the village.<ref name="Brothers" /> Opress later returned to Dathomir with Maul. At the village, Talzin was able to restore Maul's sanity and gave him a new pair of [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] [[Limb|legs]] before she disappeared back to the spirit world.<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref> Ventress returned to Dathomir with undercover Jedi Master [[Quinlan Vos]] to teach him to use his emotions to strengthen his bond with the dark side.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

Maul returned to Dathomir with Dooku to the Nightbrothers' village. There, Talzin attempted to use Dooku in a ritual that would restore her physical form. However, Grievous, Sidious and their forces then [[Second Battle of Dathomir|arrived]] to Dathomir and proceeded to decimate the [[Shadow Collective]], with a newly restored Talzin perishing at Grievous' hands in the process of saving her son. Maul and what remained of his forces fled,<ref name="Son of Dathomir 4" /> and Dathomir was left to the conquerors.<ref name="Databank Dathomir" /> After Ventress<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> appeared<ref name="TBB article">{{SW|url=news/the-bad-batch-season-3-trailer|text=Watch the ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 3 Trailer}}</ref> to be [[Second battle of Christophsis|killed]], Vos and Kenobi returned her body to Dathomir for a [[funeral]], where her body was claimed by the waters of Dathomir to rest with her fallen sisters.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

===Imperial Era===
[[File:TaronMalicos-SWGoH.png|left|thumb|219x219px|Due to Dathomir's dark influence, Jedi Master Taron Malicos gave in to the dark side and became the leader of the Nightbrothers.]]
Prior to the end of the Clone Wars, an elite Separatist unit shot down [[Jedi General]] [[Weren Dal]] over Dathomir.<ref name="Savage">''[[Savage Spirits]]''</ref> [[Fel Trech]] crash landed on the planet, becoming mentally ill with survivor's guilt. He lived in a [[cave]] located in a [[mountain]] range and had a [[distress beacon]] that generated a detectable signal.<ref name="GoD" /> During the [[Great Jedi Purge]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Taron Malicos]] crash landed on the desolate planet. Though he survived the crash, the strong presence of the dark side of the Force drove him insane and he eventually usurped control of the surviving Nightbrothers by killing Brother Viscus.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

At some point, a [[Jerserra's master|female Inquisitor]] came to Dathomir, where she discovered [[Jerserra]], a survivor of the Battle of Dathomir, and trained her secretly to use as a weapon against the other Inquisitors. Before leaving, Jerserra took a shard of the [[Nardithi Cluster]]. Jerserra eventually killed her master and became a mercenary.<ref name="GoD" />

At some point before [[3 ABY]],<ref name="DA1 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 1|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 1]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] and [[Just Lucky]] traveled to the planet. Lucky was left behind on Dathomir by Aphra during the visit.<ref name="DA1">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 1|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 1]]</ref>

Around [[0 ABY]],<ref name="DH Date">{{ReturnVadersCastleDate}}</ref> [[Lieutenant]] [[Thom Hudd]] took [[Dathomiri spider-silk]], which were used as shrouds, from a burial site on Dathomir, not knowing it was cursed.<ref name="DH">[[Star Wars Adventures Free Comic Book Day 2019|''Star Wars Adventures'' Free Comic Book Day 2019]]</ref>

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order timeline" /> instructed by a [[hologram]] left by Cordova through his droid [[BD-1]], Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] arrived on Dathomir with his [[Stinger Mantis crew|companions]], former Knight [[Cere Junda]], Cordova's former Padawan, and Captain [[Greez Dritus]], their pilot. Kestis was confronted by Nightsister Merrin, one of the last of her kind. Merrin blamed the Jedi for the massacre that decimated her people, and did everything in her power to impede Kestis. On his way to the tomb, Kestis encountered a mysterious wanderer who warned him about the dangers the tomb posed. Ignoring him, Kestis proceeded onward, and after being forced to fight the undead Nightsisters brought back to life by Merrin, he eventually made it into the tomb. Within, he experienced a flashback where he remembered his Master [[Jaro Tapal]] sacrificing himself to protect him from the [[clone trooper]]s during the Purge, Kestis was attacked by a manifestation of Jaro's spirit, resulting in his [[Jaro Tapal's lightsaber|lightsaber]] being destroyed. Kestis soon learned that the mysterious wanderer he had encountered earlier was former Jedi Taron Malicos, who sought to learn the power of the Nightsisters and manipulated them against the Jedi. Malicos offered to teach Kestis how to handle the dark power of Dathomir against Merrin's wishes. Kestis refused and fled Dathomir when Merrin attacked.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

[[File:Cal_shows_Merrin_the_Astrium.jpg|thumb|220x220px|In 14 BBY, Merrin, a Nightsister who survived Grievous' attack, left Dathomir along with Jedi survivor Cal Kestis and his ship's crew to explore the galaxy.]]

After constructing a new [[Cal Kestis' second lightsaber|lightsaber]], Kestis returned to Dathomir to complete his trials. He faced Tapal's spirit again, defeating him by overcoming his feelings of guilt over his death. Malicos again attempted to sway Kestis to the dark side, and he attacked when he refused him. Merrin helped Kestis defeat Malicos by using her magick to bury him alive, and afterward she decided to join the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]'' crew.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

Maul led the criminal syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]]<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> from his room in the Nightsister fortress on Dathomir.<ref name="CR1">[[Crimson Reign 1|''Crimson Reign'' 1]]</ref> In [[10 BBY]], after the death of his minion [[Dryden Vos]], Maul summoned [[Qi'ra]], another of his underlings, to his homeworld, warning her they would be working more closely.<ref name="Solo" /> When Qi'ra arrived on Dathomir, Maul brought her to the fortress, explaining to her everything about the Sith and he planned to destroy them for revenge since took everything from him by killing his mother and his brother.<ref name="CR1" />

[[File:SabineKananPossessed-VisionsandVoices.png|left|thumb|240x240px|In search of flesh and blood to rebuild their clan, ghosts of the Nightsisters possessed Jedi Knight Kanan Jarrus and Mandalorian Sabine Wren.]]
Sometime later in [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Visions and Voices date">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'' dates the events of "[[Steps Into Shadow]]" seventeen [[Standard year|years]] after the end of the [[Clone Wars]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]; therefore, the events of ''Steps Into Shadow'' must take place in [[2 BBY]]. According to ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]'', [[Mon Mothma]]'s denunciation of the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance]] take place 2 years before the [[Battle of Yavin]] or [[2 BBY]]. As "Visions and Voices" is set between ''Steps Into Shadow'' and "Secret Cargo," it must also take place in 2 BBY.</ref> Maul would again return to Dathomir, this time accompanied by Padawan [[Ezra Bridger]]. There, Maul showed Bridger the remnants of his homeworld and finally, brought the young Jedi to the Nightsister village to partake in a ritual that would give each other the knowledge they sought from [[holocron]]s. However, when Bridger's companions Knight [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Sabine Wren]] were [[Possession|possessed]], Bridger left Maul to help them, causing Maul to leave Dathomir and dismiss his attempt to take Bridger as his apprentice as a failure.<ref name="Visions and Voices" />

By that time, Dathomir was a dead planet in terms of civilization: no sentient beings called the world home, due to many survivors living elsewhere. However, the Empire had apparently established a presence on Dathomir in the form of a [[Imperial Garrison (Dathomir)|garrison]]. Light cruisers and exploratory craft were known to have made secret visits and the Empire had taken a quiet interest in Dathomir, despite a lack of information on Imperial records. Despite the rumors of the Empire maintaining a garrison on Dathomir, there were no known logistics or personnel records to support the rumors.<ref name="DoR" />

During the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Force spirit|force ghost]] of Kenobi stated to Anakin’s son Jedi Knight [[Luke Skywalker]] that Dathomir, as well as many other planets, would be freed from the Empire's grip.<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref>

===New Republic Era===
{{Quote|Greetings from Dathomir, home of the rancor. Speaking of which, our friend over there could use a good old teeth-cleaning, wouldn't you say.|Aree|<ref name="Rancor">{{GoC|Rancor}}</ref>}}
[[File:Eye-of-Sion-Dathomir.png|thumb|200px|right|Bringing Thrawn and the Great Mothers, as well as the Star Destroyer Chimaera, the Eye of Sion went to Dathomir after returning from another galaxy.]]
In [[9 ABY]]<ref name="Ahsoka Date">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref>, the ''[[Eye of Sion]]'' and the [[Chimaera|ISD ''Chimaera'']] under [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] traveled to Dathomir from the Nightsisters’ ancient homeworld [[Peridea]]. There, they delivered coffins to surface for the [[Great Mothers]].<ref name="Part 8">{{Ahsoka|Part Eight: The Jedi, the Witch, and the Warlord}}</ref>

Between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook date">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the adventurous [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs|Strono "Cookie" Tuggs]] traveled to Dathomir during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took and unearthed the ingredients necessary to cook [[Dathomirian Noodles]]. Tuggs would later include the recipe for the Noodles, as well as a retelling of his journey to Dathomir, in his [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref> published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook date" />

During or after that year,<ref name="Date">{{GOCDate}}</ref> the [[safari droid]] [[SF-R3]] and his companion [[cam droid]] [[CAM]] were sent by [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] on a mission to Dathomir in order to research how to clean a [[rancor]]'s [[Tooth|teeth]]. However, Aree was [[Diet|eaten]] by the rancor. Waking up in the [[mouth]] of the rancor, Aree tickled part of the mouth, causing the rancor to spit out Aree. He used a [[bone]] to tickle the [[nose]] of the rancor, causing it to open it's mouth. Aree stuck the bone in the rancor's mouth, however, it was quickly snapped in half by the rancor. Aree detached his arm and stuck it in it's mouth, this time in success, and began to work on cleaning the teeth. The process was tedious, and by the end Aree had only cleaned one tooth. Disturbed by [[Unidentified blue Dathomir bird|bird]], Aree shooed it away, causing the rancor to sneeze the safari droid back again. The [[bird]]s then rushed to clean the rancor's teeth naturally in a quick and easy process. Aree came to the conclusion that the best way to clean the rancor's teeth is to let nature take its course.<ref name="Rancor">{{GoC|Rancor}}</ref>

==Locations==
===Nightsister lair===
{{Main|Nightsister lair}}
[[File:NightsisterFortress-Nightsisters.png|thumb|250px|Talzin's Nightsister village]]
The Nightsister lair was the [[stone]] fortress of the Nightsisters. Built into the side of a [[mountain]], the Nightsisters lived in the many stone structures inside. The opaque pools in the lair contain water vital to the Nightsisters [[magick]]s, as well as many life-forms: from tiny single-celled organisms to moderately sized fish and crustaceans, and creature known as The Sleeper.<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> The [[Hidden Cave]] inside the structure was the home of [[Daka]].<ref name="Collapse" /><ref name="Massacre" /><ref name="Dark Disciple" />

===Nightbrother villages===
{{Main|Nightbrother village|Unidentified Nightbrother village}}
[[File:Unidentified_Nightbrother_village_from_JFO.jpg|thumb|250px|The Nightbrothers second village|left]]
The [[Nightbrother village]] was the home of the Nightbrothers until its destruction in [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> during the [[Second Battle of Dathomir]].<ref name="Son of Dathomir 4" /> The village was out in the open in a natural defensive position on top of a mountain.<ref name="Collapse" /><ref name="Witches of the Mist" /> After the destruction of their first village, the Nightbrothers built [[Unidentified Nightbrother village|another village]], where they were reigned over by the fallen Jedi [[Taron Malicos]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

===Kujet's Tomb===
{{Main|Tomb of Kujet}}
[[File:DathomirRuins.png|thumb|right|206px|The Tomb of Kujet, a Zeffo Sage.]]
The tomb of the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Zeffo Sage]] [[Kujet]], the palace was located at the mouth of the [[Treacherous Approach]]. Designed for sacrifice and death, Kujet would cast his enemies into the pits below his Throne Room.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
