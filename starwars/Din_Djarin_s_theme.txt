"'''Din Djarin's theme'''" is the theme used for [[Din Djarin]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' TV show. It was composed by [[Ludwig Göransson]], who composed the whole soundtrack.
The theme was played with bass recorder during [[The Mandalorian Season One|season 1]] and with electric guitar during [[The Mandalorian Season Two|season 2]], to show his evolution from lonely bounty hunter to making a team with [[Din Grogu|Grogu]].<ref name="The Sounds of Season 2 of 'The Mandalorian'">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theringer.com/tv/2020/11/6/21551513/mandalorian-season-score-john-williams-ludwig-goransson|text=The Sounds of Season 2 of 'The Mandalorian'|date=2020-11-06|archivedate=20201109204220}}</ref>
