'''Escape Plan JGT-HB-4''' was a contingency escape plan issued by [[Cylo]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]]-turned-[[Treason|traitor]]. Shortly after the [[Shu-Torun War|War on Shu-Torun]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], aboard the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]'', located [[Cylo's whale fleet]] in the [[Crushank Nebulae]]. When Cylo spotted the Imperial [[Starship|ship]], he ordered [[Morit Astarte]] to initiate [[Escape Plan JGT-HB]], before switching to Escape Plan JGT-HB-4 instead.<ref name="DV21" />
