An '''archipelago continent''' was located on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. The continent was carpeted in a layer of [[Unidentified whitish stone|whitish stone]] that was often used as gravel on the world during the [[Republic Era]]. By that [[time]], the [[stone]] was considered a precious [[gemstone]] by the [[Rodian]] [[species]] due to the wavelengths it evoked.<ref name="MA" />

In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> gemstone [[Thief|thieves]] [[Rahara Wick]] and [[Pax Maripher]] visited the Alderaan continent to [[Smuggler|smuggle]] a load of the stone off-world to<ref name="MA" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Rodia]] aboard their [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]], the [[Meryx (starship)|''Meryx'']]. After departing, the pair reflected on the stone they had taken from the area.<ref name="MA" /> In [[1 BBY]], Alderaan was [[Destruction of Alderaan|annihilated]]<ref name="Destruction" /> by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The archipelago continent was mentioned in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
