'''Cactus Juice''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[fathier]] who was a competitor in the [[Canto Cup]], a [[Fathier racing|fathier race]] held in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. He<ref name="Outlaws" /> was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[2 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> and had [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]] and black [[eye]]s. Cactus Juice was 294 [[centimeter]]s tall and weighed 977 [[kilogram]]s by the [[time]] of the Canto Cup<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cactus Juice appeared in a hologram in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. He was one of the fathiers that can randomly feature in the optional Canto Fathier Racing activity. In most races, the six fathiers are assigned a random [[jockey]] and a random symbol. As such, a [[canon]]ical pairing has not been established.<ref name="Outlaws" />
