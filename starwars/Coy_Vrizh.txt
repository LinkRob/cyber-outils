==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|And a little trivia was that he originally started as a Gamorrean Guard, but we, in the end, needed a character that could talk English&mdash;|[[Stephen Zavala]], on Coy Vrizh's initial development|<ref name="Beyond Victory AMA">{{OfficialDiscord|event=MQHW Community Developer AMA - ILM - Star Wars: Beyond Victory|servername=[[Wikipedia:Meta Quest|Meta Quest and Horizon World]]|date=2025-10-24|messagelink=|eventlink=https://discord.com/events/1152286282941218907/1428800213862055986|quote=|archivefile=File:BeyondVictoryAMA-Discord.png}}</ref>}}
Coy Vrizh is a character created for the [[Wikipedia:Virtual reality|virtual reality]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]];<ref name="Beyond Victory Playset" /> however, the Snivvian first appeared in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] one-shot [[Beyond Victory 1|''Beyond Victory'' 1]],<ref name="Beyond Victory 1" /> which acts as a tie-in to the aforementioned video game.<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-beyond-victory-announce|text=''Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset'' from ILM &ndash; Exclusive Reveal}}</ref> Vrizh was originally planned to be a [[Gamorrean]], though the species was changed to a Snivvian after the character needed to speak [[Wikipedia:English language|English]].<ref name="Beyond Victory AMA" />
