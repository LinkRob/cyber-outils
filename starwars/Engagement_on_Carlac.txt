A '''battle''' was fought between the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s and the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Defense Coalition]] on<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|carlac|Carlac}}</ref> [[Carlac]]<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> in [[231 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> The engagement, which was fought as part of [[Operation: Counterstrike|Operation Counterstrike]]&mdash;the Republic and [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Nihil conflict|response]] to the Nihil's [[Valo Atrocity|attack]] on<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> the Outer Rim planet<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> [[Valo]] during its [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]]&mdash;ended with a Republic victory and the capture of several pirates as [[prisoner]]s.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

Afterward, the pirates were taken to the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] where they were [[Prison|imprisoned]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> Later in the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> at a briefing on Starlight to inform [[Jedi]] and members of the defense coalition on the status of Operation Counterstrike shortly before the [[Battle of Galov]], [[Avar Kriss]]&mdash;the Jedi [[marshal]] of the station&mdash;mentioned the Republic's battle against the Nihil on Carlac.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

==Behind the scenes==
