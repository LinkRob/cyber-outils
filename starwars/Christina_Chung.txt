'''Christina Chung''' ('''鍾書寧''')<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|username=christinaillos|name=Christina Chung|archivedate=20200927121904}}</ref> is a [[Wikipedia:Taiwanese people|Taiwanese]]-[[Wikipedia:Hongkongers|Hongkonger]]-[[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] artist living in [[Wikipedia:Brooklyn|Brooklyn]], [[Wikipedia:New York City|New York City]],<ref name="Website-about" /> who worked with author [[Amy Ratcliffe]] in illustrating ''[[Star Wars: Women of the Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: The Jedi Mind]]''.<ref name="SW.com" /> Chung also illustrated the "Explorer Evie" loading screen for ''[[Fortnite]]''.<ref name="FN">''[[Fortnite]]''</ref>
