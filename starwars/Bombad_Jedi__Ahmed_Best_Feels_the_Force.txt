"'''Bombad Jedi: Ahmed Best Feels the Force'''" is an article that was featured in [[Star Wars Insider 199|''StarWars Insider'' 199]], published [[December 15]], [[2020]]. It is an interview of [[Ahmed Best]] conducted by [[Mark Newbold]]. It was first previewed five days prior in a [[December 10]] [[StarWars.com]] article. The two discuss Best's experience of playing [[Jar Jar Binks]] in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]] as well as [[Jedi Master]] [[Kelleran Beq]] in ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Temple Challenge]]''.
