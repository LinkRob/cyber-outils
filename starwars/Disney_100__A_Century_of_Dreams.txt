'''''Disney 100: A Century of Dreams''''' is a documentary special that premiered on [[December 14]], [[2023]]. The special was released on [[ABC Television Network|ABC]] as part of the 100 Years of Wonder celebration. On [[December 22]], 2023, the special was released under the title '''''Disney 100: A Century of Dreams – A Special Edition of 20/20''''' on both [[Disney+]] and [[Wikipedia:Hulu|Hulu]].
