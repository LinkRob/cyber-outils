A '''deprivation ritual''' was a form of [[meditation]] where a [[Jedi]] was suspended in a liquid tank with a slowly decreasing air supply, which allowed them to slip into a deep meditative state to become one with [[the Force]] as much as possible without [[Death|dying]]. After being observed by the [[Consular Jedi]] and [[doctor]] [[Rig Nema]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] underwent a deprivation ritual during the [[Clone Wars]] after he was contacted by the [[Force spirit|spirit]] of [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]. Undergoing the ritual, he communed with Jinn once again, and was told to go to the [[planet]] [[Dagobah]].<ref name="TCW:Voices">{{TCW|Voices}}</ref>
