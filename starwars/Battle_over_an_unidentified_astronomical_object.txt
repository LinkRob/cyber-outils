In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Helmet Collection 33" /> during the<ref name="Secret Weapons" /> [[The galaxy|galaxy]]-wide<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], a '''battle''' erupted in [[Realspace|space]] over an astronomical object. The battle began when a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Separatist fleet]], consisting of six [[Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class light destroyers]] under a [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]], emerged from [[hyperspace]] near the astronomical object, above which six Republic [[Open Circle Fleet]] [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] were present.<ref name="Secret Weapons" />

As soon as the Confederate [[fleet]] emerged from hyperspace, both sides began to exchange fire, resulting in at least one Venator sustaining damage. The Separatist attack occurred amid fears that the Confederacy was preparing for a major offensive. Seeking to decode an intercepted transmission that spurred on those fears, the Republic launched a [[Mission to steal a Separatist encryption module|secret mission]] to secure a [[Encryption module|Separatist encryption module]] with which the Republic could decrypt the message.<ref name="Secret Weapons" />
