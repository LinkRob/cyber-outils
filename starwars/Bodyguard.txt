{{Quote|This is my decoy, my protection&hellip; my loyal bodyguard.|[[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], introducing [[Sabé]] to [[Rugor Nass]]|<ref name="Episode 1">{{Film|I}}</ref>}}
'''Bodyguards''' were responsible for the protection of individuals. Often, they were a part of a military or [[Law enforcement agency|security force]]. Organizations that served as bodyguards included the [[Emperor's Royal Guard]],<ref name="ImpRoyalGuard">{{Databank|imperial-royal-guard|Imperial Royal Guard}}</ref> the [[Senate Guard]],<ref name="SenateGuard">{{Databank|senate-guard|Senate Guard}}</ref> the [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guard]],<ref name="ElitePraetorianGuard">{{Databank|elite-praetorian-guard|Elite Praetorian Guard}}</ref> the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]],<ref name="NabooRoyalGuard">{{Databank|naboo-royal-guards|Naboo Royal Guards}}</ref> the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]],<ref name="MandalorianGuard">{{Databank|mandalorian-guard|Mandalorian Guard}}</ref> the [[Coruscant Guard]],<ref name="CoruscantGuard">{{Databank|clone-commander-stone|Clone Commander Stone}}</ref> and the [[Geonosian]] [[Royal guard (Geonosian)|royal guard]].<ref name="Legacy">{{TCW|Legacy of Terror}}</ref> [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s served as droid bodyguards for members of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], most notably [[General]] [[Grievous]].<ref name="Beginnings">''[[Star Wars Journeys: Beginnings]]''</ref> During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the [[Knights of Ren]] became [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren's]] personal bodyguards after he [[Battle of Oetchi|usurped]] [[Snoke]]'s [[Dictatorship|rule]] as [[Supreme Leader]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
