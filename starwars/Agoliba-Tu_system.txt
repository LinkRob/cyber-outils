The '''Agoliba-Tu system''' was a [[star system]] located<ref name="The Legends of Luke Skywalker" /> in the [[Veragi sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained the [[planet]]s [[Agoliba-Ado]] and [[Agoliba-Ena]]. An [[Exogorth (Agoliba-Tu system)|exogorth]] that was visited by [[Luke Skywalker]] and a [[Unidentified female biologist|female]] [[biologist]] was located on an [[asteroid]] in the Agoliba-Tu system.<ref name="The Legends of Luke Skywalker" />
