The '''coronation of Queen Amidala''' as [[Monarch of Naboo|monarch]] of [[Naboo]] took place on the [[Day of Ascendancy]], during the [[Republic Era|final]] [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. [[Padmé Amidala]] was [[Democracy|elected]] to the throne of the [[Royal House of Naboo]], becoming the [[queen]] of her [[Naboo (people)|people]] at the age of fourteen.<ref name="SWP" />

A [[Naboo Coronation|poster]]&mdash;designed by the [[artist]] [[Palo Jemabie]]&mdash;was used as a general invitation to all citizens of Naboo, encouraging the population to gather in the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Theed]] in order to witness the ceremony. The [[Painting|artwork]] also included a notice from the [[Governor]] of Theed and the [[Naboo Royal Advisory Council]], informing the Naboo that the [[Palace Plaza]] would close on the 34th of [[Grizm]], 835th [[Naboo calendar|Year of Kwilaan]] due to a marching parade hosted by the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]].<ref name="SWP" />
