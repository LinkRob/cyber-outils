An '''individual''' served as a member of the [[senatorial committee for RDC actions]] within the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] that oversaw and evaluated the actions of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> By [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the individual had become one of the contacts that served in the committee for [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the contact obtained permissions for Mann to enter an [[RDC research compound]] on<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] so he could speak with the [[scientist]] [[Avon Sunvale]]. As he stood outside the door of Sunvale's [[laboratory]] in the compound, the Jedi Master mentally recalled his contact's help in getting him to speak with the scientist.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

==Behind the scenes==
Elzar Mann's contact was mentioned in the [[2024]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>
