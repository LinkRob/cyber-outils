'''F's lightsaber''' was a [[Color|yellow]]-bladed [[lightsaber]] with a thin, curved blade that was used by the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[F]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], F used her lightsaber to [[Lightsaber combat|cut]] [[Dismemberment|off]] the [[Limb|hand]] of the [[Pirate|raider]] [[Izuma]], who was holding [[Haru]], a resident of [[Meevai Village]] on the [[planet]] [[Keelia]], at [[blaster]]-point.

When F later surrendered herself to the [[Galactic Empire]] after the [[Unidentified refugee ship|refugee ship]] she was aboard was intercepted by the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Oppressor (Imperial-class)|''Oppressor'']], the [[refugee]] [[Ron]] pretended to capture her and gave her lightsaber to an [[Unidentified Imperial officer (Oppressor)|Imperial officer]]. F was brought to meet her former [[Jedi Master|master]], [[Zero|Shad-Rah Varcanella]], who returned her lightsaber to her. F used her lightsaber to [[Duel on the Oppressor|duel]] him, and although he cut off her feet and one arm, F used a vial of [[carbonite]] to [[Carbon-freezing|freeze]] him.

==Description==
F's lightsaber was a [[Color|yellow]]-bladed [[lightsaber]] used by [[F]], a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Art of Visions" /> The lightsaber had a rectangular black [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]]. The base of the [[hilt]] and the [[Weapon guard|guard]] were gray. The hilt was decorated and had a small red circle near the top on both sides, beneath which was a blue pattern. When activated, the guard separated horizontally and produced a ring of [[plasma]] around it that was the same color as the blade. The single blade was thin and curved to a point.<ref name="Bride" /> The lightsaber was powered by a [[kyber crystal]].<ref name="kyber crystal" />

==History==
{{Quote|What are you? Some kind of a monster?''"<br />"''I am a Jedi.|Izuma and F, before she ignites her lightsaber|<ref name="Bride" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era]], F attended the [[wedding of Asu and Haru]], residents of [[Meevai Village]] on the [[planet]]<ref name="Bride" /> [[Keelia]].<ref name="Meet the Heroes">{{SW|url=news/inside-star-wars-visions-meet-the-heroes|text=Inside ''Star Wars: Visions'': Meet the Heroes}}</ref> The next [[Local day|day]], a [[Attack on Meevai Village|battle]] occurred after [[Haru]] gave herself up as a hostage to the [[Pirate|raider]] [[Izuma]]. After most of<ref name="Bride" /> his<ref name="Episode Gallery Slide 13">{{SW|url=the-village-bride-episode-gallery|text="The Village Bride" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 13}}</ref> [[Izuma's raiders|forces]] were defeated, Izuma grabbed Haru and held a [[blaster]] to her. F, who was standing some distance away, raised<ref name="Bride" /> her<ref name="Art of Visions" /> lightsaber and ignited it while facing Izuma. She then held the lightsaber so that its blade pointed behind her, and after a few moments, she rushed toward Izuma, using a device in her [[Clothing|shoe]] to propel her forward, and [[Lightsaber combat|cut]] [[Dismemberment|off]] his [[Limb|hand]] holding the blaster with her lightsaber. Izuma collapsed, and F remained crouched behind him and Haru while holding her lightsaber, which she deactivated soon after.<ref name="Bride" />

[[File:ShadRahVsF-TheLostOnes.png|thumb|right|300px|F used her lightsaber to block Shad-Rah's attack.]]
Later, F was aboard a [[Unidentified refugee ship|refugee ship]] along with [[refugee]]s she had rescued on the planet [[Inoli]]. When the [[Starship|ship]] was intercepted by the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Oppressor (Imperial-class)|''Oppressor'']], F surrendered herself and traveled to the [[Star Destroyer]] along with the refugee [[Ron]] aboard his [[Ron's Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|''Sheathipede''-class transport shuttle]]. Upon arriving in the ''Oppressor''{{'s}} [[hangar]], Ron handed F's lightsaber to an [[Unidentified Imperial officer (Oppressor)|Imperial officer]], pretending he had captured F for a reward. The [[Imperial officer|officer]] took the lightsaber and looked at it, then had [[stormtrooper]]s bring F to a [[Zero's room|room]] aboard the ''Oppressor'' to meet with [[Zero| Shad-Rah Varcanella]], her former [[Jedi Master|master]] who was working with the Empire under the name Zero. Zero asked F to rejoin him as his apprentice, and a pillar rose from the floor between them, with its top opening to reveal F's lightsaber. Zero again asked F to join him, demanding she take her lightsaber and give him an answer.<ref name="Lost Ones" />

Although F refused to [[Lightsaber duel|fight]] her former master, Zero ignited his own [[Shad-Rah's lightsaber|lightsaber]] to [[Duel on the Oppressor|strike]] at her, and F [[Telekinesis|Force-pulled]] her lightsaber to herself to block his attack. While holding her lightsaber in one hand, F evaded several more strikes from Zero and [[Force jump|leapt]] back without attacking. F again refused her former master's request and then held her blade poised behind her. The two charged each other, with F doing a flip before leaping into the air to strike at Zero,<ref name="Lost Ones" /> who anticipated the attack<ref name="Lost Ones AD">{{Visions|The Lost Ones}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> and cut off F's right arm and feet, causing her lightsaber to fall to the floor. However, F was able to place a vial of [[carbonite]] on his shoulder, which [[Carbon-freezing|froze]] him, and she was then rescued by Ron and escaped with him on his [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|shuttle]].<ref name="Lost Ones" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I'd say we nailed the lightsaber! It turned out perfect.|Takeshi Takakura|<ref name="Art of Visions" />}}
[[File:F-LightsaberProposals-ArtOfVisions.png|thumb|right|250px|Proposals for F's lightsaber]]
F's lightsaber first appeared in "[[The Village Bride]]," a short film directed by [[Hitoshi Haga]] and released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 1|first volume ]] of the non-[[canon]] ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Kinema Citrus Co.]], was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Bride" /> on [[September 22]], [[2021]].<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-trailer-and-cast|text=Stunning New ''Star Wars: Visions'' Trailer Debuts}}</ref> The weapon was identified in ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]'', a [[2022]] reference book by [[Zack Davisson]]. The lightsaber was designed by [[Takeshi Takakura]], who was proud of his design and stated that it turned out perfect.<ref name="Art of Visions" />

F's lightsaber has a distinct [[Wikipedia:Japanese sword|Japanese sword]] motif,<ref name="Art of Visions" /> inspired by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Darksaber]].<ref name="Animate Times">{{WebCite|url=https://www.animatetimes.com/news/details.php?id=1762831026|text=アソーカとは異なる未来に繋ぐ、もう一人の“パダワン”の物語――『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』Volume3『彷徨う者たち』垪和等監督インタビュー|date=2025-11-13|work=[https://www.animatetimes.com animatetimes.com]|language=Japanese|archivedate=20251114010008}}</ref> Unlike traditional lightsabers, it has a thin blade like a [[Wikipedia:Katana|katana]].<ref name="Art of Visions" /> Haga was also inspired by the image of [[Wikipedia:Ninjatō|ninja swords]] in the works of [[Wikipedia:Manga|manga]] artist [[Wikipedia:Sanpei Shirato|Sanpei Shirato]], including [[Wikipedia:Kamui (1964 manga)|''Kamui'']] and [[Wikipedia:Sasuke|''Sasuke'']], which resulted in the lightsaber being shorter and straighter than other Japanese swords.<ref name="Animate Times" /> The yellow color of the blade is similar to the [[Rey's lightsaber|lightsaber]] used by<ref name="Art of Visions" /> the Jedi<ref name="ROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] in<ref name="Art of Visions" /> the [[2019]] film<ref name="ROS" /> [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. At time of the short's release, yellow lightsabers were rarely depicted onscreen and were mostly wielded by [[Jedi Temple Guard]]s.<ref name="Art of Visions" /> Haga decided to have F use a yellow lightsaber because he thought she was in a similar position to a Jedi Temple Guard.<ref name="IGN Japan">{{WebCite|url=https://jp.ign.com/star-wars-visions/54787/interview|text=『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』監督インタビュー！キネマシトラス垪和等監督『村の花嫁』|date=2021-09-29|work=IGN Japan|language=Japanese|archivedate=20250322214529}}</ref> ''The Art of Star Wars: Visions'' noted that some say that yellow lightsabers represent a desire to seek balance between knowledge and action.<ref name="Art of Visions" /> Haga said that the lightsaber was there to support the story, but it was really about loving nature.<ref name="special program">{{DisneyPlusYouTube|video=7wv95d_OX4Q|text=【特別番組】スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ 全世界配信記念 &#124; Disney+ (ディズニープラス)|jp=1}}</ref>

Multiple proposals for the design of the lightsaber were created, including one with a [[Curved-hilt lightsaber|bent hilt]] and designs with different hilt patterns and colors.<ref name="Art of Visions" /> F's lightsaber also appeared in "[[The Lost Ones]]," a sequel to "The Village Bride" produced by Kinema Citrus and released on Disney+ as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of ''Visions''<ref name="Lost Ones" /> on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Lost Date">{{SW|url=news/why-we-love-star-wars-visions|text=Why We Love the Unique Stories of ''Star Wars: Visions''}}</ref> [[Shiori Tani]] created a piece of concept art for the lightsaber that was included in the short's accompanying episode guide.<ref name="Lost Guide">{{SW|url=the-lost-ones-concept-art-gallery-star-wars-visions-volume-3|text="The Lost Ones" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars: Visions Volume 3}} {{C|Slide 9}}</ref>
