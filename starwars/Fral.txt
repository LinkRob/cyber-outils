'''Fral''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] which could be served with baked [[cushnip]]. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] cooked baked cushnip with fral at the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]] around thirty [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="TFAVD" />

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fral first appeared as a prop in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], a film released in [[2015]]. It was not identified in the film, but was named in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released the day after the film.
