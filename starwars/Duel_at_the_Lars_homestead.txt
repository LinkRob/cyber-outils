{{Quote|We can do this for old times' sake. But I was a Padawan then&hellip; now&hellip; you won't heal clean!|Obi-Wan Kenobi, to Darth Maul|<ref name="Old Wounds" />}}
A '''duel''' took place in [[16 BBY/Legends|16 BBY]] on the [[Lars homestead/Legends|Lars homestead]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] between the [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and the [[Cyborg/Legends|cyborg]] former [[Sith apprentice/Legends|Sith apprentice]] [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]]. Kenobi had previously [[Battle of Naboo/Legends|bisected]] Maul on the planet [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] in [[32 BBY/Legends|32 BBY]], however the apprentice survived his injuries and spent the intervening [[Standard year/Legends|years]] tracking down the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] across [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] for revenge. 

Maul eventually found Kenobi at the Lars homestead, where the Jedi was secretly watching over the [[Youngling/Legends|child]] [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]]. Maul attacked Skywalker's [[Lars family/Legends|uncle]], [[Owen Lars/Legends|Owen Lars]], which drew Kenobi out from his hiding place underground, watched by Skywalker and his [[Gender/Legends|aunt]], [[Beru Whitesun Lars/Legends|Beru Lars]]. The combatants [[Lightsaber duel/Legends|dueled]] with their [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsabers]], with Kenobi knocking off several of Maul's horns and Maul knocking Kenobi onto a [[X-34 landspeeder/Legends|landspeeder]]. The Jedi ultimately had his [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] ready to deliver the [[Death/Legends|killing]] blow to Maul, but found himself unable to do so. Instead, Owen shot Maul in the head with his [[GRS-1 snare rifle/Legends|rifle]]. The [[Moisture farmer/Legends|moisture farmer]] then forbade Kenobi from returning to his [[Moisture farm/Legends|home]], and the Jedi took Maul's corpse to [[Fire/Legends|burn]] it in the [[Dune Sea/Legends|Dune Sea]].

==Prelude==
===Quest for revenge===
{{Quote|That day in Theed I fell so far from what I was. When I came to rest all I could see was you. But I rose from my ruin to find myself greater&hellip; and all the days since have been to your end!|Maul, after finally finding Kenobi|<ref name="Old Wounds" />}}
[[File:MaulObiNaboo.png|thumb|right|250px|Kenobi and Maul dueled on Naboo in 32 BBY, which ended with Maul legless and falling down an energy shaft.]]
In [[32 BBY/Legends|32 BBY]],<ref name="NEC">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' places the events of the {{Film|I}} in [[32 BBY/Legends|32 BBY]].</ref> the [[Zabrak/Legends|Zabrak]] [[Sith apprentice/Legends|Sith apprentice]] [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]] was sent by his [[Sith Master/Legends|master]], [[Palpatine/Legends|Darth Sidious]], to [[Death/Legends|kill]] the [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn/Legends|Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in the [[City/Legends|city]] [[Theed/Legends|Theed]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]]. In the ensuing [[Battle of Naboo/Legends|battle]], Maul fatally impaled Jinn, but was bisected by Kenobi and fell into an energy shaft.<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> Kenobi and Sidious believed Maul to be dead,<ref name="TPM novel">[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'' novelization]]</ref> but the apprentice survived<ref name="Old Wounds" /> and painstakingly rebuilt his lower half<ref name="Comics">''[[Star Wars: The Comics Companion]]''</ref> with a set of [[Cybernetics/Legends|cybernetic]] [[Limb/Legends|legs]]. Maul spent [[Standard year/Legends|years]] tracking down Kenobi<ref name="Old Wounds" /> across [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]]<ref name="Comics" /> in pursuit of revenge.<ref name="Old Wounds" /> In [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]],<ref name="Kamino date">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' places [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi's]] [[Mission to Kamino (Separatist Crisis)/Legends|mission]] to the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Kamino/Legends|Kamino]] and the [[First Battle of Geonosis/Legends|Battle of Geonosis]] in [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]].</ref> Maul followed the Jedi to [[Tipoca City/Legends|Tipoca City]] on<ref name="Old Wounds" /> the [[Wild Space/Legends|Wild Space]] planet<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> [[Kamino/Legends|Kamino]] two [[Standard day/Legends|days]] after the Jedi had [[Mission to Kamino (Separatist Crisis)/Legends|been there]], and also missed him by a day after the [[First Battle of Geonosis/Legends|Battle of Geonosis]] shortly afterward.<ref name="Old Wounds" /> 

Three years later, in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]],<ref name="Mustafar date">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' places the events of {{Film|III}} in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]].</ref> Sidious, partly through his guise as [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor]] Palpatine of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]], achieved his long held [[Grand Plan/Legends|plans]] by seducing Kenobi's former Padawan, [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]], to the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side of the Force]], [[Order 66/Legends|initiating]] the [[Great Jedi Purge/Legends|Great Jedi Purge]], and [[Declaration of a New Order|transforming]] the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. Jedi Master Kenobi survived the initial purge and [[Duel on Mustafar/Legends|defeated]] Skywalker, rechristened as Darth Vader, in a [[Lightsaber duel/Legends|duel]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]] planet [[Mustafar/Legends|Mustafar]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> After Kenobi left his former Padawan [[Fire/Legends|burning]] alive next to a [[Magma/Legends|lava river]], Maul arrived on Mustafar and found Vader shortly before<ref name="Old Wounds" /> he was rescued by Sidious.<ref name="ROTS" /> The Zabrak initially feared that Vader was Kenobi and was relieved to discover otherwise.<ref name="Old Wounds" />

===Hiding on Tatooine===
{{Quote|An old junk dealer in Mos Espa choked up a name before I separated him from his greed.|Darth Maul, explaining how he tracked down Obi-Wan Kenobi to the Lars homestead|<ref name="Old Wounds" />}}
Kenobi subsequently took Vader's [[Reproduction/Legends|pregnant]] [[Marriage/Legends|wife]], [[Padmé Amidala/Legends|Padmé Amidala]], to the [[Asteroid/Legends|asteroid]]<ref name="TEA" /> [[Polis Massa/Legends|Polis Massa]],<ref name="Old Wounds" /> where she gave birth to Vader's [[Twin/Legends|twin]] [[Youngling/Legends|children]], [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke]] and [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia]], and then died from losing the will to live. Kenobi then arranged to take Luke to<ref name="ROTS" /> the Outer Rim<ref name="TEA" /> [[Desert/Legends|desert]] planet [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], where he would be raised by<ref name="ROTS" /> Vader's [[Gender/Legends|step-brother]], [[Owen Lars/Legends|Owen Lars]], and his wife, [[Beru Whitesun Lars/Legends|Beru Whitesun Lars]]. The Jedi Master stayed on Tatooine in [[Exile (punishment)/Legends|exile]] to hide from the purge and watch over the boy in the hope that he could grow to help restore the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi]].<ref name="NEGTC">''[[The New Essential Guide to Characters]]''</ref> Maul eventually followed Kenobi's trail to Polis Massa, where he murdered several members<ref name="Old Wounds" /> of the [[Kallidahin/Legends|Kallidahin]] [[Species/Legends|species]],<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> which he found unsatisfying, and accessed their medical logs.<ref name="Old Wounds" />

[[File:Maul_running.png|thumb|left|300px|Maul runs toward the Lars homestead, seen through Owen's electrobinoculars]]
Maul ultimately arrived on Tatooine and wrought destruction across its countryside.<ref name="Comics" /> When he arrived in<ref name="Old Wounds" /> the city<ref name="TPM" /> [[Mos Espa/Legends|Mos Espa]], he extracted a name from<ref name="Old Wounds" /> [[Watto/Legends|Watto]],<ref name="Watto">{{Blog|holocron|22/comments|Movie Characters killed in the EU}}</ref> a [[Junk dealer/Legends|junk dealer]]<ref name="Old Wounds" /> who had previously [[Slavery/Legends|enslaved]] Anakin Skywalker in his youth.<ref name="TPM" /> Maul discovered that Vader's son lived with the [[Lars family/Legends|Lars family]] at their [[Lars homestead/Legends|homestead]], and reasoned that by endangering Luke, he would draw Kenobi out.<ref name="Old Wounds" /> In [[16 BBY/Legends|16 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the former Sith journeyed to the Lars [[Moisture farm/Legends|moisture farm]] and arrived on foot as Tatooine's [[Tatoo I/Legends|twin]] [[Tatoo II/Legends|suns]] were setting, observed by Owen and Luke. Owen heard Maul approaching in the distance and spotted him with his [[Model TD2.3 electrobinoculars|electrobinoculars]]. Unsure of Maul's identity or intentions, the [[Moisture farmer/Legends|moisture farmer]] asked Beru to bring him his [[GRS-1 snare rifle/Legends|rifle]]. She asked him if a [[Tusken Raider/Legends|Tusken Raider]] was approaching, which Owen doubted, and he ordered Beru to take Luke inside.<ref name="Old Wounds" />

==The duel==
{{Quote|Does Palpatine know?''"<br />"''No. There is no Palpatine. No Empire. No Jedi. There is no light. No dark&hellip; just you and I here now!|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Darth Maul|<ref name="Old Wounds" />}}
Owen pointed his [[Weapon/Legends|weapon]] at Maul and ordered him to stop. Maul kept running, and as the [[Farmer/Legends|farmer]] fired, the former Sith [[Force jump/Legends|leapt]] in the air, grabbed Owen's [[Blaster rifle/Legends|blaster rifle]], and used it to knock him down. Beru, still holding Luke, pleaded for Maul not to hurt them. Maul expressed surprise that he had found the son of Anakin Skywalker, and [[Force sense/Legends|sensed]] Kenobi's [[Heart/Legends|heart]] quickening through [[The Force/Legends|the Force]], realizing he was near. The Zabrak recounted his journey for revenge across the years and explained purpose in finding Luke. Kenobi [[Telepathy/Legends|telepathically]] asked if Palpatine was aware of his presence. Maul denied it, stating that neither his former master, the Empire, the Jedi, the [[Light side of the Force/Legends|light]] or the dark side of the Force mattered; only him and Kenobi. The Jedi burst from beneath the ground, and recalled that he had been merely a Padawan when he and Maul had last fought, and that the Zabrak would not heal cleanly from their upcoming duel.<ref name="Old Wounds" />

[[File:OldWoundsCharacters.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Kenobi and Maul rematch while Beru checks on Owen.]]
Maul removed his [[Sith apparel|hood]] and both combatants unsheathed their [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsabers]]. The Zabrak lunged first, grabbing Kenobi by the [[Throat/Legends|throat]] with his right cybernetic leg. As the Jedi blocked Maul's [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]], the [[Cyborg/Legends|cyborg]] spoke about his fall on Naboo, and how he had surpassed his status then and worked toward his goal of killing Kenobi. The Jedi Master escaped Maul's grip and they both locked sabers before Kenobi punched Maul in the head, dislodging several of his horns. The Jedi changed his grip and leapt toward his opponent while Beru crawled toward her husband to check on him.<ref name="Old Wounds" />

As Kenobi landed, his [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] severed Maul's left arm and cut his [[Double-bladed lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] in half. The Zabrak kicked Kenobi backwards onto an [[X-34 landspeeder/Legends|X-34 landspeeder]] and used the Force to pull half of his weapon back. Kenobi climbed off the [[Landspeeder/Legends|landspeeder]] and, as Maul strode forwards, the Jedi held his deactivated lightsaber at his head. Kenobi found himself unable to deliver the killing blow, experiencing a vision of those that he had lost from his life, including Jinn, Amidala, Skywalker, and Jedi Master [[Mace Windu/Legends|Mace Windu]]. His indecision was interrupted, however, by a blaster shot from Owen directly at Maul's head, killing the former Sith apprentice.<ref name="Old Wounds" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|I told you I'd keep him safe, and I will. Even if that means from you. You don't come back here. Understand?|Owen Lars to Obi-Wan Kenobi after the duel|<ref name="Old Wounds" />}}
Kenobi told Owen that he would take Maul's body out to the [[Dune Sea/Legends|Dune Sea]] to burn it, to which the moisture farmer assented. The Jedi then asked if Skywalker was okay, and Owen responded that he had vowed to keep his nephew safe, even if it meant from Kenobi. He then forbade the Jedi from returning to the homestead. As the Jedi Master carried Maul's corpse away, he telepathically communicated with Skywalker, assuring him that he would always be nearby, even if the boy could not see him.<ref name="Old Wounds" /> Kenobi and Skywalker would later reunite in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]].<ref name="TEA" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The Darth Maul story is non-continuity.|Leland Chee, on the canon status of "Old Wounds"|<ref name="SWBoards">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=125719&start=0675#10095628|thread=Event Timeline and Character Age Discussion|user=[[Leland Chee|Tasty Taste]]|date=2005-03-29|time=11:02 AM}}</ref>}}
[[File:MaulVsKenobi-Revenge.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Maul and Kenobi would later have multiple canonical rematches in ''The Clone Wars''.]]
The duel at the Lars homestead was created for the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story "[[Old Wounds]]," written and illustrated by [[Aaron McBride]] and published in the graphic novel anthology ''[[Star Wars: Visionaries]]''<ref name="Old Wounds" /> on [[April 2]], [[2005]]. The duel was first alluded to in a preview for ''Visionaries'' published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[March 15]] of that year.<ref name="Inside">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/comics/news20050315.html|text=Inside ''Star Wars: Visionaries''}}</ref> Shortly before its release, the events of "Old Wounds" were stated to be considered [[Canon|non-canon]] within [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] media by [[Holocron continuity database]] keeper [[Leland Chee]].<ref name="SWBoards" /> The plot of the comic follows<ref name="Old Wounds" /> on from events of the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']],<ref name="TPM" /> in which Maul was presumed to have been killed.<ref name="TPM novel" /> The rifle that Owen Lars wields during the duel resembles<ref name="Old Wounds" /> a GRS-1 [[Snare rifle/Legends|snare rifle]],<ref name="GRS-1">{{CCG|set=Dagobah Limited|cardname=Zuckuss' Snare Rifle}}</ref> although it has a larger [[Stock/Legends|stock]] and functions as a blaster rifle.<ref name="Old Wounds" />

''[[Star Wars]]'' creator [[George Lucas]] later decided to canonically reveal that Maul had survived Naboo in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="Insider 134">{{InsiderCite|134|Behind and Beyond the Battle Lines}}</ref> a decision that author [[Stephen J. Sansweet]] attributed to the popularity of "Old Wounds."<ref name="Action Figure">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Action Figure Collection]]''</ref> Maul's design in the series was inspired by his "Old Wounds" appearance.<ref name="Revenge trivia gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/revenge-trivia-gallery|text="Revenge" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> A rematch between Maul and Kenobi, motivated by the former's desire for revenge, [[Skirmish above Raydonia/Legends|first occurred]] above the planet [[Raydonia/Legends|Raydonia]] in<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref> the [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|season four]] episode "[[Revenge (episode)|Revenge]]," which aired on [[March 14]], [[2012]].<ref name="Revenge_W9">{{WebCite|url=http://www.programme-tv.net/programme/series-tv/r15334-star-wars-the-clone-wars/3306715-vengeance|text=Star Wars : The Clone Wars Saison 4 - Épisode 22: Vengeance - 26min|work=Télé-Loisirs|language=French|archivedate=20161225123825}}</ref> The two would continue to clash throughout the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]]<ref name="Revival">{{TCW|Revival (episode)}}</ref><ref name="Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref> [[Skirmish on Florrum/Legends|on]] the planets<ref name="Florrum">{{SWE|locations|florrum|Florrum}}</ref><ref name="TEA" /> [[Florrum/Legends|Florrum]]<ref name="Revival" /> [[Battle of Mandalore/Legends|and]] [[Mandalore/Legends|Mandalore]].<ref name="Lawless" /> Their storyline was ultimately resolved in the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Three|third season]] of the canon animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'',<ref name="Twin Suns">{{Rebels|Twin Suns (episode)}}</ref> beginning with "[[The Holocrons of Fate]],"<ref name="The Holocrons of Fate">{{Rebels|The Holocrons of Fate}}</ref> the season's third episode which aired on [[October 1]], [[2016]].<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|174|Star Wars Rebels Season Three Episode Guide|''Star Wars Rebels'' Season Three Episode Guide}}</ref> During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Maul]] discovered that [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Kenobi]] was still alive and on [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Holocrons">{{Rebels|The Holocrons of Fate}}</ref> and [[Mission to Tatooine (Imperial Era)|tracked]] him down, where the former [[Sith]] challenged him to a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] and was [[Death|killed]]. Kenobi also referred to Maul as an "old wound" before the duel.<ref name="Twin Suns" />
