'''Erdo''' was a mining [[planet]]<ref name="Wayseeker" /> in the [[Erdo system]] in the [[Kriz sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], situated in grid square K-19<ref name="Appendix" /> along the [[Lipsec Run]] [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="LipsecRun" /> Several [[Standard decade|decades]] before<ref name="Wayseeker" /> [[160 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{WayseekerDate}}</ref> Erdo's previously-prosperous mines shut down, leaving the fortunes of Erdo and other worlds along the route, such as [[Ruathi]], on the downturn.<ref name="Wayseeker" />
