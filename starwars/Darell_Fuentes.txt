'''Darell Fuentes''' was the concept sketch artist for the ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' television series.<ref name="Part I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref> Every single piece of the [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister's]] costume started out as sketches from costume designer [[Suttirat Anne Larlarb]], which went to Fuentes, and then it received more technical drawings.<ref name="Suttirat interview">{{WebCite|url=https://www.costumedesignersguild.com/obi-wan-kenobi-suttirat-larlarb|text=Interview with Suttirat Larlarb|author=Foss, Gary|date=2022-06-13|work=[[Wikipedia:Costume Designers Guild|Costume Designers Guild]]|archivedate=20220803191837}}</ref>
