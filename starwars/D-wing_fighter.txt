The '''D-wing fighter''' was a type of [[starfighter]] active during the [[First Order-Resistance War]]. A modified D-wing was used by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Seleno Chandro]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

{{Ship-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The D-wing appears in the [[2019]] saga film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].

It was created by [[Industrial Light & Magic]], who at the behest of director [[J.J. Abrams]] included thousands of ships in the final sequence. For the fighters, they went through the alphabet for letters that had not yet been used.<ref name="TROS Featurette">Target exclusive [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] ''[[A Final Alliance]]'' video featurette</ref>
