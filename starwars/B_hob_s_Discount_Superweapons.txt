'''B'hob's Discount Superweapons''' was a shop owned by an individual called [[B'hob]]. Various [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Superweapon/Legends|superweapons]] were sold at the shop, including the [[Galaxy Gun]], the [[Tarkin (superweapon)|Tarkin]], the [[Death Star/Legends|Death Stars]], and the [[World Devastator]]s. It also planned to have the [[Darksaber (superweapon)|DarkSaber]] and [[Centerpoint Station|Starbuster]] appear as products. In addition to its superweapon offerings, the vendor also sold [[Super Star Destroyer/Legends|Super Star Destroyers]], including the [[Eclipse (Eclipse-class)|''Eclipse'']] and the [[Executor/Legends|''Executor'']].
