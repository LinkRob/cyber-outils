"'''Cover Story: Carrie Fisher'''" is a magazine article that featured [[Carrie Fisher]] during the [[1983]] theatrical release of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. It was written by Robert Greenberger and published in the 71st issue of the science fiction magazine [[Starlog (magazine)|''Starlog'']].<ref name="Starlog 71" />
