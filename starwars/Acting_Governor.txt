'''Acting Governor'''<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> was a temporary title of [[governor]]ship. [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] designated [[Arihnda Pryce]] as acting governor of the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] before granting her the full title. The appointments for acting governor were supposed to be granted through the [[Imperial Palace]], yet Tarkin found no need to do so. Pryce would be scheduled to be in the position for at least one or two [[Standard year|years]], in order to spend [[time]] on [[Coruscant]] learning the details of the position. Pryce was eventually promoted to [[Governor of the Lothal sector|Governor of Lothal]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

Shortly after the [[Battle of Endor]], [[Fara Yadeez]] served as the Acting Governor of the planet [[Troithe]].<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>
