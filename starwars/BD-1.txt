'''BD-1''' was a [[BD explorer droid]] that accompanied [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]] during the [[Republic Era|last years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. After having a [[Force vision]] of the [[Jedi Order]] [[Great Jedi Purge|fall]], Cordova hid [[Eno Cordova's holocron|a holocron]] with a list of [[Force-sensitive]] [[Youngling|children]] and encrypted a series of messages about it inside BD-1, only revealed by someone the droid trusted. Following [[Order 66]] and the [[Imperial Era|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the droid befriended [[Cal Kestis]], a former [[Padawan]] who endeavored to [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|rebuild the Jedi Order]]. Afterwards he accompanied Kestis on his adventures and [[Stinger Mantis crew's crusade against the Empire|his crusade against the Empire]] and subsequent [[quest for Tanalorr]].

==Biography==
The [[BD explorer droid]]<ref name="Databank" /> designated BD-1 was an exploration [[droid]]. Prior to the end of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Era|reign]], the [[Programmer|programmed]] droid accompanied [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]] on the [[Jedi]]'s journey to explore the history of the [[Zeffo]]. Cordova later realized that they were in danger, and programmed BD-1's memory to be blocked, only revealed by someone the droid trusted.<ref name="JFO" />

BD-1 then remained on [[Bogano]] in the vicinity of the Zeffo [[Bogano Vault|Vault]] until<ref name="JFO" /> [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> when the [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]], a survivor of [[Order 66]], arrived on the [[planet]] at the behest of [[Cere Junda]] in order to find [[Eno Cordova's holocron|a holocron]] key to [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|rebuilding]] the [[Jedi Order]]. Kestis quickly gained the support of BD-1, and they entered the vault. Along the way, the droid's [[scomp link]] was broken by the local wildlife. Unable to unlock the deepest reaches, Kestis took the droid back to his starship and crew, the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]''.<ref name="JFO" />

They then traveled to other worlds on their quest to uncover the secrets of the Vault and that of the Zeffo. BD-1 scanned other Zeffo artifacts, showing recordings of Cordova to Kestis. When they were confronted on Zeffo by the [[Second Sister]], BD-1 activated a force field between the combatants, saving Kestis' life. During their journey, Kestis upgraded BD-1 with several different gadgets to assist them, and also repaired the scomp link.<ref name="JFO" />

During their exploration of Zeffo, Kestis was stunned by a [[Haxion Brood]] [[bounty hunter]],<ref name="JFO" /> [[Atticus Rex]],<ref name="Manga">[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order, Vol. 2|''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'', Vol. 2]]</ref> and both he and BD-1 were imprisoned on [[Ordo Eris]]. Kestis broke them both out, and they were rescued by the ''Mantis''.<ref name="JFO" />

[[File:CalKestis_BD1_EnoCordova_BoganoVault_JFO.png|left|thumb|250px|BD-1 showing a hologram of Cordova.]]
Following a [[Force vision]] on [[Dathomir]] that left the Jedi's lightsaber damaged, they traveled to [[Ilum]] for a new [[kyber crystal]]. After battling [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] guarding the [[Crystal Cave|cave]], Kestis finally acquired a new crystal, only for it to break in his hands. The Padawan almost gave in to despair, but with BD-1's support, rallied and completed his [[Cal Kestis' second lightsaber|new lightsaber]]. BD-1 was then able to unlock his memory, showing the recording of Cordova.<ref name="JFO" />

Kestis then returned to Bogano and gathered the [[holocron]], but it was stolen by the Second Sister, and the ''Mantis'' crew [[Mission to Nur|followed]] her to the [[Fortress Inquisitorius]]. BD-1 traveled underwater with Kestis to the fortress. They recovered the holocron, but were confronted by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. During the fight, when Kestis and Vader's [[lightsaber]] blades were locked, BD-1 climbed onto Vader's left shoulder and electrocuted the [[Sith Lord]]. Although Vader restrained BD-1 afterward, the droid created an opening for Kestis to attack Vader, striking his torso and slightly damaging his [[Darth Vader's armor|armor]]. They only escaped by breaking the tunnel and escaping in the onrush of water. Once back aboard their ship, Kestis decided to destroy the holocron to prevent the [[Galactic Empire]] from finding the young [[Force-sensitive]]s, and then asked the crew of their next destination.<ref name="JFO" />

===An inseparable duo===
{{Quote|Didn't I tell you to be more careful about who you scan?''"<br />"''Boop boop.''"<br />"''Come on. Should've left you in that rathtar nest.''"<br />"''Beep beep!|Cal Kestis and BD-1|<ref name="Survivor" />}}
[[File:CalKestisBD1-Survivor.png|thumb|right|250px|Even with the end of the attempt to rebuild the Jedi Order, Kestis and BD-1 continued as an inseparable duo in the fight against the Galactic Empire.]]
BD-1 continued to accompany the ''Mantis'' crew and after their separation, he accompanied Kestis on [[Stinger Mantis crew's crusade against the Empire|his crusade against the Empire]]. On one occasion, the two ended up in a [[rathtar]] nest, an event Kestis would later use to tease BD-1, telling him he should have left him in the nest. On Coruscant, Kestis was on a [[Mission on Coruscant|mission]] to retrieve military intel from [[Daho Sejan]]'s yacht, and BD-1 assisted in hijacking the yacht and freeing Kestis from his [[stuncuffs]]. BD-1 then accompanied Kestis to Koboh, where the droid accidentally discovered the [[Chamber of Duality]], kicking off Kestis' [[Quest for Tanalorr|quest to reach]] the mythical planet [[Tanalorr]] hidden in the [[Koboh Abyss]]. On Koboh, BD-1 acquired the ability to fire [[Koboh Matter]] as well as [[electroshock dart]]s. Kestis eventually traveled to [[The Archive]] on [[Jedha]], where BD-1 was reunited with Cordova. However, [[Bode Akuna]] later betrayed Cordova, killing him with a blaster. Enraged, BD-1 rushed towards Akuna, but Kestis grabbed the droid, and narrowly avoided an explosion which would have likely destroyed BD-1. After Kestis' mission for Tanalorr was complete, BD-1 continued to accompany Kestis.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|Beeps in the face of danger.|Cal Kestis, on BD-1|<ref name="Jedi: Battle Scars">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]''</ref>}}
[[File:BD-1_AG.png|thumb|left|220px|BD-1 was equipped with many helpful tools.]]
As a BD unit, BD-1 was bipedal, and had white plating with black sensors.<ref name="JFO" /> Designed to assist with exploration in remote and dangerous locations, BD-1 was enthusiastic about research and data collection, but often disregarded self-preservation.<ref name="Databank" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|The scomp link is busted, but this should get you moving for now.|Cal Kestis|<ref name="JFO" />}}
BD-1 was equipped with various tools, including scanners, a healing [[Stimpack|stim]] [[Stim Canister Storage|dispenser]], a thruster for hovering but not flying, a [[spotlight]], a [[holoprojector]], and a [[scomp link]]. He was also able to preset a holographic [[map]] of his scanned suroundings. During his travels with Cal Kestis, BD-1 was upgraded with a transformer to control Imperial machines, two processing devices to hack [[Viper probe droid|probe droids]] and [[KX-series security droid]]s, and a motor to carry Kestis up zip-lines.<ref name="JFO" /> BD-1 later gained the ability to fire Koboh matter and electro darts on Koboh.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|He's your best pal, and he's kind of going to experience the entire story with you, every step of the way.|Narrative Lead Aaron Contreras, on BD-1|<ref name="Contreras">{{SW|url=news/inside-star-wars-jedi-fallen-order-with-respawns-aaron-contreras|text=Inside ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'' with Respawn's Aaron Contreras}}</ref>}}
BD-1 was created for the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]].<ref name="JFO" /> The droid was first revealed at the panel for ''Fallen Order'' at [[Celebration Chicago]] on [[April 13]], 2019, which was subsequently covered in an article on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="SWCC">{{SW|url=news/swcc-2019-5-things-we-learned-from-the-star-wars-jedi-fallen-order-panel|text=SWCC 2019: 5 Things We learned from the ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'' panel|nolive=1}}</ref>

===Development===
[[File:BD-1Designs.jpg|thumb|right|260px|Various concept designs for BD-1, including the one that became RE-1]]
{{Quote|Part of the Jedi fantasy is being with a droid. You've got Luke and R2, you've got Rey and BB-8. It felt really natural to have a companion droid for our hero.|''Jedi: Fallen Order''{{'s}} Lead Level Designer [[Jeff Magers]]|<ref name="Shacknews">{{WebCite|url=https://www.shacknews.com/article/114596/star-wars-jedi-fallen-order-interview-jedi-combat-bd-1-and-the-dark-souls-comparison|text=Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order interview: Jedi combat, BD-1, and the Dark Souls comparison|author=Ozzie Mejia|date=2019-10-22|work=[[Wikipedia:Shacknews|Shacknews]]|archivedate=20250523150942}}</ref>}}
The design process for BD-1 took over two years, during which Respawn worked closely with [[Lucasfilm]] to determine his appearance, personality, and functionality. Initially, they were determined to pair Kestis with a droid, but had not settled on the nature of the character. They raised the possibility of giving BD-1 actual dialogue, though the final design instead utilized beeps to produce emotional cues.<ref name="Game Informer">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/feature/2019/06/08/the-true-power-of-the-force|text=The True Power of the Force|author=Andrew Rainer|date=2019-06-08|work=[[Wikipedia:Game Informer|''Game Informer'']]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/hiQLC}}</ref> The development team researched other iconic droids in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, such as [[BB-8]] and [[R2-D2]]. Initial designs drew inspiration from familiar objects, such as appliances. Narrative lead [[Aaron Contreras]] based BD-1 on his own dog,<ref name="SWCC-Panel">{{SWYouTube|5H6LQkxwVxc|The Galaxy-Wide Premiere of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order Live Panel at SWCC 2019}}</ref> while other inspirations included the characters of [[Wikipedia:Snoopy|Snoopy]] and [[Wikipedia:Woodstock (Peanuts)|Woodstock]], created by [[Wikipedia:Charles Schulz|Charles Schulz]].<ref name="Game Informer" /> The "BD" in the droid's name was derived from "Buddy Droid," Respawn's internal nickname for the character.<ref name="EAPlay" />

In the initial design phase, BD-1 was instead a kitbashed booster repair droid with large thrusters and welding arms, and could provide a boost to extend the player's jumps. His design was later distilled around his essential functions<ref name="ArtOfJFO">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> and the purpose he served in the game,<ref name="Contreras" /> scanning and creating a database, which resulted in his large lens eye and holoprojector. Early designs for Cal Kestis featured a backpack which was meant to be a retrofitted carrier and charging base for BD-1. Concept art for numerous designs was created by lead concept artist [[Jordan Lamarre-Wan]].<ref name="ArtOfJFO" /> Some earlier concepts included features reminiscent of R2-D2, with a large central eye, while others appeared more menacing.<ref name="ArtOfJFOInterview">{{SW|url=news/the-magic-within-the-art-of-star-wars-jedi-fallen-order|text=The Magic Within ''The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order''}}</ref> BD-1's final design was a mix between the bipedal stance and head-to-body proportions of a bird with the loyalty and friendliness of a dog. His vacuum-formed shapes drew inspiration from [[electrobinoculars]], while his legs are based on those of an [[R2-series astromech droid|R2 unit]] and his default color pattern and heat sink are similar to that of a [[snowspeeder]].<ref name="ArtOfJFO" /> BD-1 also had to be small enough to ensure he would not be in the way during combat and exploration.<ref name="ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=jroid|url=artwork/3o28YB|name=[[Jordan Lamarre-Wan]]|text=JEDI: Fallen Order BD-1|date=2020-02-20}}</ref> In order to allow dialogue and interaction with BD-1, the developers created the droid to emote with his head and antennae. BD-1 climbing onto the player's back was pulled from [[Luke Skywalker]] carrying [[Yoda]] during his training in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="DesigningBD-1">{{EASWYouTube|v1NULUOTwnw|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order &ndash; Designing BD-1}}</ref> Some of BD-1's early designs are reminiscent of the droid [[L0-LA59]] in the [[2022]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]''.<ref name="Part I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref>

BD-1 shares traits with BT-7274, the deuteragonist of [[Wikipedia:Titanfall 2|''Titanfall 2'']], another title developed by Respawn Entertainment. Both are the mechanical companions of player characters, Cal Kestis for ''Jedi: Fallen Order'', and Jack Cooper in ''Titanfall 2''. Their similar name schemes, BD and BT, are short for both "Buddy Droid" and "Buddy Titan" respectively.<ref name="Buddy">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gamesradar.com/uk/star-wars-jedi-fallen-order-droid-bd-1-details|text=Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order droid details confirm all the ways BD-1 will be your new best friend|author=Connor Sheridan|date=2019-06-09|work=[[Wikipedia:GamesRadar+|''GamesRadar+'']]|archivedate=20200526190403}}</ref>

===Portrayal===
{{Quote|Despite his diminutive stature, BD is one of the emotional cornerstones of Cal's journey. When writing scripts, we always talked through BD's reactions to crucial story beats. But many of BD's most charming, meaningful moments were the result of on set collaboration, and Gideon's keen eye as an actor.|Danny Homan|<ref name="DHoman">{{Substack|subdomain=dannyhoman|url=p/hope-is-a-droid-bd-1-in-star-wars|text=Hope is a Droid: BD-1 in Star Wars Jedi: Survivor|name=[[Danny Homan]]|username=dannyhoman|date=2023-05-03}}</ref>}}
BD-1 was physically portrayed and voiced on set for ''Jedi: Fallen Order''<ref name="VG247">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vg247.com/star-wars-jedi-fallen-order-bd1-cardboard-puppet-nose-flute|text=The actor who played BD-1 in Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order used a cardboard puppet and a nose flute|author=Kirk McKeand|date=2020-01-20|archivedate=20240521203432}}</ref> and it's sequel ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor|Jedi: Survivor]]'' by [[Gideon Emery]]<ref name="DHoman" /> and classic ''Star Wars'' sound designer [[Ben Burtt]] provided the audio for him that was used in the final game.<ref name="EAPlay">{{SW|url=news/ea-play-2019-star-wars-jedi-fallen-order|text=EA Play 2019: ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'' Details and Gameplay Revealed}}</ref> ''Fallen Order''{{'s}} performance director [[Tom Keegan]] had previously worked with Emery on ''[[Wikipedia:Wolfenstein: The New Order|Wolfenstein: The New Order]]''.<ref name="VG247" />

Emery used a cardboard hand puppet of BD-1 on set<ref name="VG247" /> when [[Cameron Monaghan]] was performing [[Wikipedia:Motion capture|motion capture]] for Cal Kestis to simulate BD-1's position on on Monaghan's back and movements as a reference for the actors<ref name="DHoman" /> and the animators.<ref name="VG247" /> Emery also occassionally acted with a more detailed plastic puppet of BD-1 with pin points for the motion capture tracking.<ref name="SWCC-Panel" /> Additionally Emery provided BD-1's beeps and boops on set using a [[Wikipedia:Nose whistle|nose whistle]] for the other actors to react to. In addition to the ones in the scripts, many of BD-1's lines and meaningful moments in the final game were also improvised by Emery.<ref name="DHoman" /> ''Jedi: Survivor''{{'s}} lead writer<ref name="Survivor" /> [[Danny Homan]] highlighted that Emery's portaly gave BD-1 a real presence on set and sense of being an equal member of the cast and cheered up the crew during the long shoots.<ref name="DHoman" />

===Gameplay===
In ''Fallen Order'', BD-1 is responsible for numerous gameplay functions; healing the player and helping in comabat, providing a holographic map of the areas, manipulating doors and other technology, acting as a flashlight in darkened areas, scanning objects or the environment to add entries to the in-game databank and tactic guide, and helping with puzzles. The player can unlock and upgrade abilities for BD-1 over the course of the gameplay.<ref name="JFO" />

===Other appearances===
On [[November 12]], 2019, the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' announced [[RE-1]], a droid mini-pet for players that was inspired by BD-1 and the then-upcoming release of ''Fallen Order''. RE-1 was derived from a piece of Lamarre-Wan's early concept art for BD-1 in order to ensure that its design was influenced by ''Fallen Order''{{'s}} character while also fitting within the [[Old Republic era]].<ref name="TORE">{{TORweb|url=info/news/article/20191112|text=Claim Your Free Mini-pet to Celebrate Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order's Launch}}</ref> An additional droid based on BD-1, the [[JR-4 Recon Droid]], was released for the game in [[2023]] to celebrate the release of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="swtor.com">{{TORweb|url=info/news/article/20230428|text=Celebrate the Launch of ''Star Wars'' Jedi: Survivor™ with a Free Mini-pet!}}</ref>

The [[2022]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set of BD-1 stated that his manufacturer was [[Behold-Urwar Droid Concepts]] and that he had a height of 0.43 [[meter]]s.<ref name="Lego">{{LEGOCite|num=75335|name=BD-1}}</ref> Behold-Urwar was then later first canonically mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''.<ref name="CGttSWG" />
