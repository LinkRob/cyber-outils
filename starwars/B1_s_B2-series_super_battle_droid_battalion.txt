A '''battalion''' of [[B2-series super battle droid]]s<ref name="B1" /> saw use in the [[Clone Wars]] and was left over after its [[Mission to Mustafar|conclusion]].<ref name="The Mutiny image one">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-resistance/the-mutiny-episode-guide|text="The Mutiny" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars Resistance}} {{C|Image 1 caption}}</ref> Coming into possession of the storage container holding the super [[droid]]s, along with a single [[B1-series battle droid]], the [[pirate]] [[Sidon Ithano]] sold them to another pirate, [[Captain]] [[Kragan Gorr]]. Gorr used them in his [[Mutiny on the Colossus|attempted mutiny]] on the [[supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]''. However, [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Neeku Vozo]] had reprogrammed the B1, simply nicknaming him "[[B1 (Colossus)|B1]]" as well, to serve as the new droid [[commander]]. With the [[battle droid]]s on their side, the crew of the ''Colossus'' forced Kragan and the rest of the [[Warbird gang]] to leave their [[Space station|station]].<ref name="The Mutiny" />
