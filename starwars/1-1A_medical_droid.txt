The '''1-1A medical droid''' was a model of [[medical droid]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Chadra-Fan]] [[doctor]] and [[Nihil]] member [[Kisma Uttersond]] utilized a rusty and old mechanical [[Cybernetics|prosthetic]] that was once the [[Limb|leg]] of a 1-1A medical droid. As a result of the prosthetic, Uttersond had to hobble at a pace as fast as the leg would allow.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
