'''Circarpous''' was a location within the [[Circarpous sector]]'s [[Circarpous system]] within the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="SWApp" /> The [[droid]]s [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] were once sent on a [[diplomat]]ic [[Mission to Circarpous|mission]] to this location, an event C-3PO would later recall to [[Resistance]] [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] [[Jessika Pava]].<ref name="Weapon of a Jedi">''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]''</ref> It shared its name with the [[Circarpous sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Solo TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
