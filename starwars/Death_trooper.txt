'''Death troopers''', also known as '''death soldiers''' or '''AWR Troopers''', were an elite variant of [[stormtrooper]]s specializing in stealth, espionage and lethality that served the [[Galactic Empire]]. Operating under the [[death trooper division]], they acted as a [[Bodyguard|protective detail]] for significant [[Imperial officer]]s and members of the [[Tarkin Initiative]], as well as special-assignment [[commando]]s for [[Imperial Intelligence]] or the general [[Imperial Military]]. They wore [[Color|black]] suits of body [[Death trooper armor|armor]] and specialized [[Armor|helmets]] with vocal scramblers, micro-motion sensors, and heads-up displays with data on enemy and friendly positions on the battlefield. Death [[trooper]]s were trained in unarmed combat, heavy weapons, demolitions, improvised weaponry, guerrilla warfare, and marksmanship. Experts at covering their tracks, death troopers left little to no evidence of their missions. Identifiable by their distinct black armor, death troopers struck fear in the heart of anyone who crossed the Empire.

==History==
===Age of the Empire===
[[File:Battle_Formation_SoR.png|thumb|left|250px|Death troopers operated in small groups.]]
Death troopers were named by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] himself to capitalize on rumors<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> about a legendary [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research]] [[Project Blackwing|project]] to revive necrotic tissue,<ref name="Visual Guide" /> which ultimately resulted in the creation of uncontrollable [[Undead Trooper]]s during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="SWCommander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> An Imperial who wanted to create especially effective [[stormtrooper]]s and [[battle droid]]s and thus launched several projects to such ends, [[Gideon]], who eventually became a [[moff]], was at the head of the death trooper project.<ref name="Databank Download" /> Operating under [[Imperial Intelligence]]<ref name="Death Troopers old" /> but also the general [[Imperial Military]] command structure,<ref name="In Service to the Empire">{{StoryCite|story=In Service to the Empire|book=Empire Ascendant 1}}</ref> death troopers operated as elite soldiers<ref name="Death Troopers old" /> and [[commando]]s<ref name="HC 80" /> who were filed under the [[death trooper division]] of the [[Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="Imperial Troops" /> Death troopers also had ties to the [[Tarkin Initiative]]<ref name="HC 80" /> and [[Imperial Security Bureau]].<ref name="Rix Road" />

Early during the [[Project Stardust|construction]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] above the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]], [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] was accompanied by a [[Wilhuff Tarkin's guard|squad]] of death [[trooper]]s to [[Scarif]] to confront a breach of security by the [[Emperor's Fist|Emperor's lieutenant]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. After [[Governor]] Tarkin confronted the [[cyborg]], [[Lord]] Vader was ordered by Emperor Palpatine to uncover who was responsible for several sabotage attempts on the Death Star's construction.<ref name="Annual 2" />

After arriving on Geonosis, [[Commander]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] greeted Lord Vader on a landing pad with a number of death troopers. An [[assassin]]ation attempt was made on Krennic and Vader, as an explosion sent a pile of rocks crashing down on the Imperials, crushing several death troopers. Despite the attempt, Vader investigated and eventually learned that a rogue [[Geonosian hive]] led by a [[Unidentified Geonosian queen|Geonosian queen]] were responsible. The [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] and a contingent of death troopers then fought in a [[Skirmish in the Korakanni Mound|skirmish]] in the [[Korakanni Mound]], [[Death|killing]] the [[saboteur]]s and the [[queen]].<ref name="Annual 2" />

In [[13 BBY]], a [[squad]] of death troopers accompanied the Imperial weapons division's [[Director]],<ref name="Director Orson Krennic">{{Databank|director-orson-krennic|Director Orson Krennic}}</ref> Orson Krennic, to [[Lah'mu]]. There, they demolished the [[Erso homestead]] and [[Capture of Galen Erso|took]] [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]] to complete his [[Death Star plans|work]] on the Death Star. One of the troopers killed Erso's [[Marriage|wife]], [[Lyra Erso|Lyra]], and the squad attempted to find his daughter, [[Jyn Erso]], with no success.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

===Growing rebellion===
[[File:ThrawnAndDeathTroopersOnAtollon-SWRS03E22.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A team of death troopers served as bodyguards for Grand Admiral Thrawn.]]
During the early [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], two Death Troopers accompanied ISB Supervisor [[Dedra Meero]] during her hunt for Andor and his adoptive mother's funeral at [[Ferrix]] in 5 BBY.<ref name="Rix Road" /> [[Thrawn's guard|Another squad]] of death troopers served under the command of the [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet's]] [[commander]], [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], where they assisted him in capturing former [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]], following the latter's discovery as a Rebel [[Fulcrum|spy]], and held him during the [[Battle of Atollon|Battle]] of [[Atollon]]. In the later part of the battle, the squad accompanied Thrawn to [[Chopper Base]] in an attempt to capture senior [[Alliance officer]]s such as [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]] and [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]]. However, the Rebels were able to escape when a mystical [[The Force|Force]] being called the [[Bendu]] attacked both the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] and [[Alliance Military|Rebel forces]]. On Thrawn's orders, the death troopers concentrated their fire at Bendu, wounding him and sending him crashing to the ground.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="In the Name of the Rebellion date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[In the Name of the Rebellion]]" to [[1 BBY]].</ref> a [[DT-F16's squad|squad]] under Commander [[DT-F16]] accompanied a [[kyber crystal]] shipment and several [[technician]]s aboard [[Freighter 2716]]. After departing from [[Faos Station]], DT-F16 and her squad came under [[Mission to Faos Station|attack]] by [[Partisans]]' leader [[Saw Gerrera]] and [[Spectres]] [[Ezra Bridger]] and [[Sabine Wren]]. DT-F16, her squad and other troopers were then left behind by the Spectres and were destroyed, along with Captain [[Slavin]]'s [[Slavin's Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] when the kyber [[crystal]] they were escorting exploded.<ref name="In the Name of the Rebellion" />

A death trooper, [[DT-L21]], was stationed at [[Jhothal]]. There, he commanded stormtroopers when they discovered that the Spectres had returned. He, his [[Viper probe droid]]s and troopers searched the sewers for the Spectres, to no avail.<ref name="The Occupation">{{Rebels|The Occupation}}</ref> The death troopers then accompanied Grand Admiral Thrawn and Governor Pryce to the [[Lothal Imperial airfield|Imperial airfield]] to watch a test flight of the new [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]] [[TIE/D Defender Elite|Elite]]. When Bridger revealed himself, the death troopers were ordered by Thrawn to secure the TIE/D Defender. However, they lost when Wren stole the fighter.<ref name="Flight of the Defender">{{Rebels|Flight of the Defender}}</ref>

[[File:HydanAndDeathTroopers-SWRS04E12.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Minister Hydan arrived on Lothal under the protection of the death troopers.]]
Several death troopers then assisted Governor Pryce and [[Rukh]] in pursuing Syndulla when [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Knight|Knight]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] [[Rescue of Hera Syndulla (Lothal)|rescued]] her from the Imperial complex. The death troopers were able to catch to Jarrus and Syndulla.<ref name="Jedi Night">{{Rebels|Jedi Night}}</ref>

Two death troopers were assigned to one of the [[Emperor's adviser]]s, [[Minister]] [[Veris Hydan]], as the Empire and [[Mining Guild]] were excavating Lothal's [[Lothal Jedi Temple|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="Wolves & a Door">{{Rebels|Wolves and a Door}}</ref> They assisted Hydan in interrogating Wren, striking her in the head. However, Wren was able to knock one out when Captain [[Garazeb Orrelios]] rescued her, stealing his blaster in the process.<ref name="A World Between Worlds">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref>

Several death troopers were stationed at the [[Imperial Complex (Lothal)|Imperial Complex]]. With Rukh, the death troopers fought the rebels and attempted to stop them from reactivating the shields. They were taken out by Captain Orrelios. The remaining death troopers of [[Admiral]] Thrawn's guard were with him aboard the ''Chimaera'' and were taken out by [[Commander]] Bridger before the ship was taken by the Purrgil to [[Peridea]].<ref name="Family Reunion - & Farewell">{{Rebels|Family Reunion – and Farewell}}</ref>

===War===
{{Quote|&hellip;And I've seen plenty of ''death troopers'' just like ''you'' live up to their ''name''...by ''dying''.|Cordo, instructing his death troopers|<ref name="In Service to the Empire" />}}
[[File:KrennicAndDeathTroopers-RogueOne.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A squad of death troopers was assigned to protect Director Krennic, head of the Advanced Weapons Research.]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="RO date">{{FilmDate|RO}}</ref> death troopers would continue to escort Director Krennic during the Death Star's initial [[Destruction of Jedha City|testing]] stage at [[Jedha]] and during his trip to [[Eadu]]. He later deployed his death trooper squad during the [[Battle of Scarif]] when they fought [[Rogue One|Rebel ground forces]] that had infiltrated the planet. They managed to annihilate the remnants of the Rebel strike team. However, the entire squad was killed by [[Baze Malbus]] and [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] shortly before Krennic's own death.<ref name="Rogue One" />

In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Yavin]], death troopers served aboard [[Hivebase-1]], a secret [[space station]] made by the [[Tarkin Initiative]], serving under Commander [[Yewl]].<ref name="Aphra 18">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 18|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 18]]</ref> Yewl's death troopers [[Infiltration of Hivebase-1|fought]] Dr. Aphra's crew and their prisoner, Rebel General Hera Syndulla, until they escaped. They were killed when Hivebase-1 was destroyed.<ref name="Dr Aphra 19">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 19|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 19]]</ref>

Several accompanied Lieutenant-Inspector [[Magna Tolvan]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Dr Aphra 21">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 21|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 21]]</ref> A [[Darth Vader's guard|squad of death troopers]] were deployed by [[Sergeant]] [[Cordo]] into the [[mission to destroy the rebel cannon]]. After Cordo's death and the loss of three of the squad, the three survivors were selected to be the personal guard of Lord Vader,<ref name="In Service to the Empire" /> [[Supreme commander of the Imperial fleet|supreme commander of the Empire's armed forces]].<ref name="RiseFall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> Following the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="Darth Vader 1" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the troopers accompanied him on his quest for revenge against everyone that had hidden his son, Luke Skywalker, from him.<ref name="Darth Vader 1" /> Another [[Sly Moore's guard|unit]] accompanied [[Administrator]] [[Sly Moore]] when she traveled to [[Mustafar]] to oversee a mission being undertaken by an [[assassin]] named [[Ochi]] of [[Bestoon]]. The squad surrounded Ochi with their blasters trained as Moore commanded him to verify his job, to assassinate [[Darth]] Vader, had been completed, even though the Sith Lord went to the [[Eye of Webbish Bog]].<ref name="Vader 8" />

Death troopers were sent to deal with the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] after she meddled in the Empire's affairs, but the young woman was able to escape them.<ref name="Outlaws" /> Several death troopers accompanied Darth Sidious and Lord Vader into [[Battle at Amaxine Station|battle]] at the [[Amaxine Station]], witnessing both Sith Lords utilising their crimson [[Sith lightsaber|lightsabers]] to dispatch soldiers from the [[Crimson Dawn]].<ref name="Hidden Empire 4">[[Hidden Empire 4|''Hidden Empire'' 4]]</ref> During the [[Scourge of Coruscant]], Vader's own death trooper guard, which by that time had been joined by an additional member, was ordered to attack and fire upon him by Emperor Palpatine so as to bring out Vader's anger, with Palpatine mocking his apprentice over their betrayal&mdash;as they were always going to obey his commands over Vader's own&mdash;and his lack of power over the Imperial Military without his blessing. While Vader quickly cut down all four death troopers, Palpatine used the event to question if Vader really controlled his hate, or if it controlled him. Thereafter, the [[Scourge]]&mdash;a rogue [[artificial intelligence]] attempting to take over droids and cyborgs&mdash;managed to take control over the death troopers' corpses and attempted to board a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] with their puppeteered bodies. Made aware of this development as he dueled his master, Vader used the Force to lift all four into the air and snap their necks, then collected the Scourge's [[spider-droid]] vectors from the troopers and threw them into [[Imperial City]]. However, Palpatine simply had portions of the city destroyed, devasting parts of Coruscant to stop the spread and finally revealing to his apprentice that his power grew with the hate others held towards him.<ref name="Vader 40" />

===After the Empire===
[[File:GideonRemnant-TMS01E07.jpg|left|thumb|264x264px|Death troopers and other Imperial forces on Nevarro.]]
[[9 ABY|Nine years]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="Date Note">{{MandoDate|7}}</ref> a squad of death troopers served in an [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]. The troopers opened fire on a [[Nevarro school|cantina]] on [[Nevarro]], killing "[[the Client]]" and several stormtroopers while also trapping "[[Din Djarin|the Mandalorian]]," [[Greef Karga]] and [[Carasynthia Dune]] inside.<ref name="Chapter 7" />

The standoff was interrupted shortly after, with [[IG-11]] arriving to assist the Mandalorian and his comrades. As the group fought their way out of the cantina, a fierce battle ensued, with two death troopers resorting to hand-to-hand combat after they were disarmed. With the fight raging outdoors, several death troopers circled around to breach the cantina, and attempted to flush Cara Dune out with saturated blaster fire. The remaining death troopers escorted Moff Gideon, as he gave the order to [[Incinerator stormtrooper|"burn them out."]]<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref>

[[File:SabineKillsDeathTrooper-Ahsoka.png|right|thumb|250px|Sabine Wren clashes with a zombified death trooper.]]
Around [[9 ABY]], a pair of death troopers were among Grand Admiral Thrawn's forces on the extragalactic planet [[Peridea]]. Despite dying during the course of the assault on the spire, they were [[Zombie|re-animated]] by way of [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[magick]], attacking Sabine Wren and Ezra Bridger during the [[battle of Peridea]]. Despite the challenge posed by their undead status, both Jedi managed to overcome their enemies, with Wren unlocking [[the Force]] within herself to claim [[Sabine Wren's lightsaber|her lightsaber]], which she used to take down her opponent.<ref name="Part Eight" />

==Training==
{{Quote|I've heard death troopers are the best the Empire have.|Kay Vess|<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Iakaru_Victorious_DoR.png|thumb|200px|It was difficult, yet possible, to defeat a death trooper.]]
Death troopers were elite [[stormtrooper]]s in the Galactic Empire's [[Imperial Military|military]]. Stormtrooper candidates who excelled at their training were reassigned to the advanced death trooper camp on Scarif. Death troopers were required to meet rigid physical and ideological standards, with height and weight standards that exceeded typical trooper averages. The candidates were also subjected to a battery of physical tests and classified surgical enhancements that drastically enhanced their physical and mental attributes such as their strength, speed, agility, reflexes, and stamina, pushing those attributes to "far beyond [[human]]" levels. Additionally, death troopers had to complete rigorous training in exotic environments to be stronger, faster, and more resilient than the norm. Other specialties included unarmed combat, heavy weapons, demolitions, improvised weaponry, guerrilla warfare, and marksmanship. In the field, death troopers operated in small groups, with each trooper specializing in a specific skill set.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

Shortly before the Battle of Scarif, General [[Sotorus Ramda]] requested to be returned two [[platoon]]s of death troopers, as [[Death trooper training camp|their training camp]] on Scarif needed more [[instructor]]s. Ramda preferred veteran death troopers with body augmentations at v1.01 or earlier.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

==Equipment==
===Specialized armor===
{{Main|Death trooper armor}}
{{Quote|I presume these death troopers are combat-capable even without their armor and heavy weapons?|[[Ar'alani]]|<ref name="Thrawn: Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Imperial_Death_Troopers_Unit_Expansion_art.png|thumb|left|200px|Death troopers were issued specialized armor and weapons.]]
Death troopers were equipped with [[Death trooper armor|advanced armor]] considered an improvement over the standard [[Stormtrooper armor|stormtrooper kit]]. The [[helmet]] featured a number of advanced [[sensor]] and [[Targeting computer|targeting systems]] to give total situational awareness of combat areas, enemies and allies. These various upgrades to the helmet systems included a [[Neuro-Saav macromotion monitor]], [[Multi-Frequency Targeting and Acquisition System|multi-frequency targeting and acquisition sensors]], and image-intensifying active pulse emitters, the latter of which was stored within the two green "dots" at the bottom front of the helmet. The helmets also included [[Vocal emulator implant|voice scramblers]] which allowed them to speak<ref name="Visual Guide" /> in [[death trooper encryption]],<ref name="Language">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref> which made their communications impossible for anyone except another death trooper to understand, along with adding to their intimidating appearance.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

Death trooper armor was covered by a spray [[polymer]] called [[reflec]], which warped electromagnetic signals commonly found in [[sensor array]]s. This made death troopers well suited for stealth operations. Underneath their armor, troopers wore an environmentally sealed bodysuit. Death trooper [[pauldron]]s on their right shoulder denoted rank as [[specialist]]s, but appeared black and featureless to individuals if not viewed through a stormtrooper visor.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

===Weaponry===
[[File:Hasbro-DeathTrooperboxart.png|thumb|right|350px|Also wearing full armor, death troopers were equipped with weaponry like the SE-14r and E-11D.]]
Death troopers were equipped with a variety of tools and weapons depending on their specialties. On their chest, death troopers strapped on an [[IM-40 three-slot ammunition and tool pouch]]. Weapons commonly used included the [[SE-14r light repeating blaster]], [[E-11D blaster carbine]],<ref name="Visual Guide" /> [[DLT-19D heavy blaster rifle]], [[DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle]], and [[Smart Rocket]]s.<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref> Grenades included the [[C-25 fragmentation grenade]], [[baradium]] [[thermal detonator]], a [[Sonic Imploder]], or a [[thermal imploder]].<ref name="Visual Guide" />
