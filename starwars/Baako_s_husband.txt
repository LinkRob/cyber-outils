A [[Gender|male]] '''individual''' was the [[Marriage|husband]] of [[Baako]], the brother of [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]]. The [[Marriage|couple]] lived together on<ref name="Black Spire" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="Pantora">{{Databank|pantora|Pantora}}</ref> [[Pantora]]. During a [[Battle of Batuu|mission]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Black Spire" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> while Vi thought&mdash;albeit incorrectly&mdash;that she would [[Death|die]] from a [[poison dart]], the spy thought of Baako and his husband, noting to herself that she had yet to meet the latter.<ref name="Black Spire" />

==Behind the scenes==
