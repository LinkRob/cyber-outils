{{Quote|Corrupt the kyber crystal. Teach it your pain. Teach it your anger. Hear it sing a hymn of darkness. Make it bleed.|Darth Sidious, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 5" />}}
'''Bleeding''' was the [[Force power|process]] that a [[dark side adept]] used by way of the [[dark side of the Force]] to bend a [[kyber crystal]] to their will, turning it [[Color|red]]. It was possible for some [[Light side of the Force|light side]] users to [[Purification|purify]] corrupted crystals, which would bring them back to the light side and change their color to white. During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Ahsoka Tano]] purified kyber crystals that had been bled to make her white lightsabers.

==Description==
{{Quote|Since kyber crystals are naturally attuned to the light side of the Force, those who serve the dark side must follow a different path. First, they must seize a pure crystal from the lightsaber of a Jedi they have vanquished in combat—for the saber of a Sith is not given. It is taken. Then, they must infuse that crystal with their pain, anger, and hatred, bending it to their will. This corrupts the crystal, aligning it with the dark side and making it bleed with unbridled crimson power. The bleeding process can yield unpredictable results. Some crystals shatter, making their energies unstable and unpredictable. Others resist their realignment, refusing to be bent toward the darkness. Once a crystal has been properly bled, the primal power it releases becomes not only a deadly instrument in the hands of a Sith Lord, but a symbol of their true strength in the dark side.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="Secrets of the Sith">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref>}}
[[File:VaderBleeding-2017DarthVader5.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Darth Vader pours his dark emotions into a kyber crystal, bleeding it]]
Bleeding was accomplished by pouring rage, hate, fear and pain into a [[kyber crystal]] by means of the [[dark side of the Force]], until the crystal changed, resulting in the [[Color|crimson]]-bladed [[lightsaber]]s<ref name="Star Wars Lightsabers">''[[Star Wars Lightsabers: A Guide to Weapons of the Force]]''</ref> associated exclusively with [[dark side adept]]s.<ref name="TSotJ">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref> Since kyber crystals were inherently aligned with the [[light side of the Force]], they could attempt to resist the forced corruption. When [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] first attempted to bleed a kyber crystal, he experienced intense light side [[Force vision]]s urging him to return to the light side.<ref name="Dark Lord 5" /> The procedure could also damage the crystal as was the case with the kyber crystal at the heart of Ben Solo's Jedi lightsaber; Solo's attempt at bleeding it resulted in the crystal cracking and forced Solo, now going by Kylo Ren, to modify his lightsaber into a crossguard model in order to vent the excess energy from the unstable crystal.<ref name="TRoKR4" />

Any kyber [[crystal]] could be bled by a dark side user, including one's own, for example, if they were a former light side practitioner and kept their lightsaber. However, some [[Sith]] believed that the proper method was to take a kyber crystal from a [[Jedi]]'s lightsaber and bleed it.<ref name="Star Wars Lightsabers" />

It was possible to "[[Purification|heal]]" the effects of bleeding on a kyber crystal, by using the light side of [[the Force]], thus changing the crystal's color again.<ref name="Super Graphic">''[[Star Wars Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]''</ref> The process typically resulted in a white crystal.<ref name="Ahsoka" /><ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref><ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />

The famous [[Jedi hunter]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] amassed a macabre collection of lightsabers and learned the subtleties of [[lightsaber combat]] from the Jedi-turned-[[Sith Lord]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. Grievous, however, didn't use the Force, the stolen blades glowed the same hue for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[cyborg]] as they had for their previous Jedi users, therefore there was no kyber-crystal bleeding in those instances. Similarly, the [[Galactic Empire]] harvested a vest supply of kyber crystals from [[Jedha]] during the construction of the [[Death Star]], a [[moon]]-sized [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|space station]], to boost its [[superlaser|primary laser]] into a [[planet]]-killing superweapon. As with Grievous' stolen blades, the Death Star's capabilities were used for destructive reasons, but the machine lacked the dark-side-induced corruption required for its kyber to bleed.<ref name="Your Questions Answered">{{InsiderCite|230|A Certain Point of View}}</ref>

On the other hand, [[Starkiller Base]]'s kyber-boosted firepower, historically frequented by the Jedi Order and formerly known as [[Ilum]], as well as the [[Sith Eternal]]'s [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer]]s, gleamed a brilliant red. Unlike the Death Star, these superweapons' internal mechanisms combined crystal and dark energies. It is unclear whether the crimson blasts emanated from Starkiller Base and the Sith Star Destroyers were a consequence of kyber bleeding.<ref name="Your Questions Answered" />

==History==
===Pre-Empire===
[[File:BleedingSolsCrystal-TheAcolyte.gif|thumb|250px|Osha bleeding the kyber crystal of Sol's lightsaber.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Jedi]] [[Jora Malli]] found an ancient Sith [[lightspear]] powered by a bled kyber crystal, which she healed&mdash;largely to see how it was done&mdash;but found herself tightly bound to the now-white crystal. Thus, she used the crystal in [[Jora Malli's lightsaber|her lightsaber]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> In [[132 BBY]]<ref name="132 Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> during the High Republic Era,<ref name="Cast reveal">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-original-series-cast-revealed|text=''The Acolyte'' Original Series Cast Revealed}}</ref> during a [[Duel on Brendok|skirmish on Brendok]], the [[Sol's lightsaber|lightsaber]] of the [[Jedi Master]] [[Sol]] became damaged, exposing a portion of the kyber crystal. [[Verosha Aniseya|Osha Aniseya]] picked up the saber after hearing her former master's confession about killing her [[Aniseya|mother]], in anger, she unknowingly bled the crystal.<ref name="The Acolyte">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''</ref>

===Imperial Era===
====Darth Vader====
Shortly after the formation of the Galactic Empire, [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] and [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]] explained the bleeding process to his [[Sith apprentice]] Darth Vader. Having lost his [[Jedi]] [[Skywalker lightsaber|weapon]] on [[Mustafar]], Vader was in need of a new lightsaber. Sidious tasked his apprentice with tracking down and killing a member of the [[Jedi Order]], as Sith tradition dictated that the kyber crystal of a [[Sith lightsaber]] must be taken from a Jedi they killed.<ref name="Dark Lord 1">[[Darth Vader (2017) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 1]]</ref> Vader, having accepted his mission, [[Mission to Al'doleem|located]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Kirak Infil'a]] on the [[Al'doleem|river moon of Al'doleem]]. After killing Infil'a, Vader took [[Vader's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] with him when he departed.<ref name="Dark Lord 5" />

On his master's orders, Vader traveled to Mustafar, where he had recently suffered his [[Duel on Mustafar|greatest defeat]]. Sidious wanted the Sith Lord to bleed Master Infil'a's crystal on the surface of the planet. Upon reaching the planet's surface, Vader entered a [[Sith cave|cave]] that was strong with the dark side. He dismantled the [[curved-hilt lightsaber]] and placed the Jedi Master's kyber crystal on a rock.<ref name="Dark Lord 5" />

[[File:VaderProfundity.png|thumb|left|250px|In accordance with Sith tradition, Darth Vader bled a kyber crystal from another Jedi's lightsaber, causing its lightsaber to produce a crimson blade.]]
Using the Force, Vader began subjecting the crystal to the bleeding technique. However, the crystal resisted, blasting Vader into the wall, and subjecting him to Force visions of what his life could be if he chose to abandon the dark. In a vision, Vader returned to the light side of the Force and killed his [[Sith Master]], before returning to his Jedi Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and begging for forgiveness.<ref name="Dark Lord 5" />

Vader, having been thrown against the wall of the cave by the living crystal, returned to his feet and refused to give in. Grasping the crystal with both hands, Vader unrelentingly subjected the crystal to his pain and regret. Vader screamed, as the cave erupted in a stream of Force energy. Volcanic activity spiked and powerful storms erupted across Mustafar, in response to Vader's pain. Eventually, the crystal's will was shattered by the Dark Lord, who subsequently returned to the planet [[Coruscant]] and presented his efforts to his master.<ref name="Dark Lord 5" /> Vader later traveled to [[Galen Erso's Office]] on Coruscant, where he found a stash of kyber crystals left behind by [[Galen Walton Erso|Erso]] and bled them.<ref name="Annual 2">[[Darth Vader Annual 2|''Darth Vader Annual'' 2]]</ref>

====Inquisitorius====
{{Quote|They used to be red. When the creature had them, they were red. But I heard them before I ever saw him on Raada, and knew that they were meant for me.|Ahsoka Tano, on the Sixth Brother's kyber crystals|<ref name="Ahsoka" />}}
[[File:OrderOfInquisitors-PromiseOfPower.png|thumb|right|250px|With corrupted crystals in their lightsabers, the Inquisitors became known as "Red Blades".]]
The kyber crystals installed in the [[double-bladed spinning lightsaber]]s wielded by members of the [[Inquisitorius]] had undergone the bleeding process. When the Inquisitor [[Sixth Brother]] traveled to the [[moon]] [[Raada]] after hearing of [[Raada Uprising|Jedi activity there]], the "Jedi" in question, former [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], had already fled, after ensuring her friends from the [[Raada resistance]] were in hiding. The Sixth Brother located and attacked the hideout, killing several rebels and taking Tano's friend [[Kaeden Larte]] hostage in order to draw her back to the moon. When Tano, who had by that point joined forces with [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], learned what had been going on, she prepared to return to Raada. However, she first traveled to the sacred planet [[Ilum]] in an attempt to acquire new kyber crystals, but was unable to land after she discovered that the Empire was [[Mining|strip-mining]] the world.<ref name="Ahsoka" />

Tano looked into the Force for a clue as to what to do next, and heard a song that led her back to Raada. Arriving, Tano informed the moon's residents of plans for the impending evacuation. During the battle, Tano confronted the Sixth Brother unarmed, and killed him when she pulled on the crystals in his [[Sixth Brother's lightsaber|lightsaber]] with the Force, which caused the weapon to explode. Claiming the crystals, Tano purified them, noting that they seemed familiar and suspecting them to have been looted from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]. After assembling [[Ahsoka Tano's white lightsabers|her new lightsabers]], Tano rescued Larte and covered the evacuees as they fled into the waiting ships. She later showed Organa her new weapons on board the ''[[Tantive IV]]'', telling the Senator about bleeding and purification.<ref name="Ahsoka" />

====The surviving few====
{{Quote|It was my discovery! My home, and they just expected me to throw it away!''"<br />"''Dagan, wait.''"<br />"''I refused to obey and was betrayed by the one I trusted most!''"<br />"''No, stop!|Dagan Gera and Cal Kestis|<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>}}
[[File:CalProtectsZeeFromDagan-Survivor.png|left|thumb|250px|Through the bleeding process, the fallen Jedi Dagan Gera turned the blades of his lightsaber red.]]
The [[High Republic Era|High Republic]] Era Jedi Knight [[Dagan Gera]] removed the kyber crystal from his [[Dagan Gera's lightsaber|lightsaber]] returned to him by [[Cal Kestis]] and bled it following his release from stasis<ref name="Survivor" /> in [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Survivor date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> This resulted in the normally yellow blade turning a red hue.<ref name="Survivor" />

After [[Luke Skywalker's yellow lightsaber|his lightsaber]] was damaged during a fight with [[Killdroid]]s, Luke Skywalker traveled to find someone who could repair his lightsaber. Skywalker meet kyber crystal expert [[Cuata]] and [[Gretta]]. Cuata agreed to fix Skywalker's weapon if he purified a red kyber crystal. He and Gretta explained the process of both bleeding and purification. Luke tried to heal the crystal, but he ended up experiencing a Force vision where he was brought to a hall. The [[Gol (Sith)|Sith]] who was sitting on a throne welcomed him and told him to approach and tell them his name. The individual then asked Skywalker how and why he had entered their hall. Skywalker was then transported to Mustafar, where he saw [[Fortress Vader]] and his father on top of the castle. Skywalker tried another crystal and spoke with Yoda. After waking up, Luke saw the blank crystal turned green.<ref name="SW 35">[[Star Wars (2020) 35|''Star Wars'' (2020) 35]]</ref>

===New Republic Era===
{{Quote|As a final step on his path to the dark side, he bled his lightsaber's kyber crystal, only to fracture it in the process.|Darth Sidious, on Kylo Ren|<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" />}}
[[File:KyloBleedsCrystal-TheRiseOfKyloRen4.png|thumb|right|220px|The power of the dark side flowed through the Skywalker heir, Ben Solo, as he bled his kyber crystal.]]
The former member of the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] named [[Komat]] had a lightsaber with a red blade. After Komat escaped from the Acolytes with the help of [[Luke Skywalker]] purified the kyber crystal in [[Komat's lightsaber]], leaving the blade white, and symbolizing the purging of evil from Komat's mind and [[soul]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref>

In [[28 ABY]],<ref name="TRoKR Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that the [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker]] was destroyed six [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the destruction must have taken place in [[28 ABY]].</ref> proceeding a [[skirmish on the Minemoon]] of [[Mimban]], the fallen Jedi and newly minted [[Ren|Master of the Knights of Ren]] [[Ben Solo]] was traveling in [[realspace]] aboard the ''[[Night Buzzard]]''. Having recently been seduced by the dark side of the Force, Solo removed his Jedi kyber crystal from [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|his former Jedi weapon]]. Firmly grasping the crystal in his left hand, Ben poured all of his frustration and hatred towards his family legacy into the crystal.<ref name="TRoKR4" />

The [[Knights of Ren]] watched in awe as the ''Night Buzzard'' was engulfed in charges of dark energy. Remembering the faces of his former loved ones, Ben screamed as the crystal burst into a torrent of fiery energy. Although the crystal was corrupted, turning a red-yellow hue, it cracked in the process. Solo placed the crystal in his old lightsaber and activated it, but the crack in the crystal made the weapon unstable, and it short-circuited. To counteract the instability of the crystal, Solo modified his lightsaber, making it into<ref name="TRoKR4" /> an unstable<ref name="POV230">{{InsiderCite|230|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> [[crossguard lightsaber]].<ref name="TRoKR4" />

===First Order Era===
The [[kyber crystal]]-powered [[superweapon]]s of [[Starkiller Base]] and the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] fired blasts of a vibrant red coloration. The [[Dark energy|dark energies]] seen in both machines may have played a role and bled the used kyber crystals, explaining the distinctive appearance.<ref name="POV230" />

When Kylo Ren, formerly Ben Solo, grandson of Darth Vader, found [[Vaneé]], his grandfather's former servant, at [[Fortress Vader]], they embarked on a journey to other worlds, such as [[Tatooine]] and [[Naboo]], so that Ren could understand his grandfather's past. Upon returning to the fortress, Vaneé, believing that Ren had not yet learned what was necessary to be Vader's successor, sent [[DT-series sentry droid]]s to attack him. Despite being wounded by the droids, Ren destroyed them with a lightsaber he found there. Due to the kyber crystal being exposed, Ren used the bleeding process to change the lightsaber blade from green to red, also making it unstable, as the crystal cracked during the color change itself. Vaneé's attack was successful because it made him realize that Ren had learned what Vaneé believed he needed to know: that he was already who he needed to be. However, Vaneé's actions led Ren to kill him.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 11">[[Legacy of Vader 11|''Legacy of Vader'' 11]]</ref>
