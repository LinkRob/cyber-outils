'''Benjamin Bratt''' is an [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]]. When [[Claudia Gray]] wrote the [[2019]] novel ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', she imagined [[Rael Averross]] as being portrayed by Benjamin Bratt.<ref name="Tumblr">{{Tumblr|subdomain=claudiagray|url=post/184227180172/im-just-now-leaving-chicago-after-celebration|name=Claudia Gray's Photo Blog|text=Claudia Gray|quote=Benjamin Bratt as Rael Averross}}</ref> Bratt later portrayed [[Bail Prestor Organa]], previously portrayed by [[Jimmy Smits]], in the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', which premiered in [[2025]].<ref name="What a Festive Evening" /> Smits was unable to return for the series due to his obligations on another project. Series creator [[Tony Gilroy]] believed that the character needed to appear within its story.<ref name="Collider News">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/andor-season-2-bail-organa-recast-benjamin-bratt-explained-tony-gilroy|text="You Have To Have Him": Tony Gilroy Reveals Why That 'Andor' Character Was Recast in Season 2|author=Rachel Leishman and Maggie Lovitt|date=2024-04-29|work=''Collider''|archivedate=20250430064431}}</ref> With Smits out of the picture, [[Lucasfilm]] President [[Kathleen Kennedy]] pointed to Bratt as an alternative actor for Organa, which Gilroy thought was "brilliant." Bratt was beloved and energetic on set.<ref name="EW News">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/andor-creator-recasting-bail-organa-star-wars-benjamin-bratt-11721954|text=Andor creator explains recasting major Star Wars character with Benjamin Bratt|author=Dalton Ross|date=2024-04-29|work=''Entertainment Weekly''|archivedate=20250430013900}}</ref>
