'''Arcana''' was a [[planet]] that held a stronghold constructed by the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[witch]]es of [[Dathomir]] thousands of [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Part One" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref> [[Ahsoka Tano]] [[Mission to Arcana|took]] a [[Arcana Star Map|map]] from Arcana which was believed to lead to the Imperial [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]].<ref name="Part One" />
