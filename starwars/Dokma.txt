'''Dokma''' were small, timid [[gastropod]]s<ref name="CargoBay">[[Star Wars: Cargo Bay|''Star Wars'': Cargo Bay]]</ref> native to [[Atollon]]. Often preyed upon by the [[spider]]-like [[krykna]], they had protective shells and two [[eye]]stalks. [[Garazeb Orrelios]] nicknamed them "[[Lasat (language)|'''bogans''']]."<ref name="The Mystery of Chopper Base" /> According to [[Volt Vescuso]], the amount of [[milk]] a dokma drank was not to be underestimated.<ref name="A Crash of Fate">''[[A Crash of Fate]]''</ref>

The dokma are considered timid animals. They are born underwater and lose their first shell after weeks of drinking the mineral-rich underground water until they finally crawl to the surface. Once they have found a suitable spot, they shed their shell and stick to a coral like a barnacle, from which they absorb food and eventually allow a new shell to grow. For further reproduction, dokma look for a suitable partner at another coral formation and bring their offspring into the underground waters, always having to watch out for the krykna.<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref>
