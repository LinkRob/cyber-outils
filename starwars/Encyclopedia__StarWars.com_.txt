The '''Encyclopedia''' was, from [[September 13]], [[2011]] to [[July 1]], [[2014]], the [[Databank (original)|Databank]]'s successor as the only official ''[[Star Wars]]'' information database made available to the public. It was replaced by another online encyclopedia called the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]]. It was available on [[StarWars.com]]. All entries featured a link to [[Wookieepedia]]. The Encyclopedia entries only contained information pertaining to the six ''Star Wars'' films and [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''The Clone Wars'']] series, and did not incorporate any [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] material, although they did include some extra background information not shown in the films or ''The Clone Wars'' themselves.

{{Website-stub}}

==Continuity==
When asked whether the Encyclopedia was considered [[canon]] or [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]], [[Pablo Hidalgo]] stated that when the Encyclopedia began, it drew material exclusively from "[[George Lucas]]' ''Star Wars''," and therefore it did not include Expanded Universe material. He added that he was not sure how its later stages of development were to be handled.<ref name=“Question”>{{Bluesky|username=infinata.bsky.social|url=post/3md72cbo7n22e|name=Pablo Hidalgo|date=2026-01-24|quote=When it started in 2011-2012, it only drew material from George Lucas' Star Wars, so that wouldn't include Legends. But after that, I'm not sure what its next steps of evolution would entail.|archivedate=20260125025344}}</ref>
