The '''flame-tree''' was a type of three-hundred-[[meter]] tall [[tree]]. It had [[Color|bright-blue]] [[Plant|branches]] that supported many long, fragrant, red and orange hanging [[vine]]s. In [[232 BBY]], several [[Fire|flame]]-trees grew in a [[garden]] module of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]], where each of them formed a distinct ecosystem harboring [[Bioluminescence|bioluminescent]] [[bird]]s and [[insect]]s. That [[Standard year|year]], [[Jedi Master]]s [[Avar Kriss]] and [[Elzar Mann]] took a walk through the garden module, with the flame-trees at times making the latter feel like he was standing inside a campfire and atop an inferno.

==Biology and appearance==
The flame-tree was a type of tall, thin, and elegant [[tree]] that could reach around three hundred [[meter]]s in height. It had [[Color|bright-blue]] [[Plant|branches]] from which hanged a multitude of [[vine]]s stretching from the tree's crown to the ground. The vines were in different hues of red and orange with graceful gradients throughout their length, and they had an [[incense]]-like fragrance.<ref name="LOTJ" />

==Flame-trees in the galaxy==
[[File:ElzarMann_LOTJ.png|thumb|right|250px|In a Starlight Beacon garden module, flame-trees made an impression on the senses of Elzar Mann.]]
In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="LOTJdate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> several [[Fire|flame]]-trees grew in one of the [[garden]] modules of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]]. At the base of the large [[transparisteel]] bubble that formed the module, they had been planted in the soil from [[Qualai]], a small, low-[[gravity]] [[planet]] situated on the edge of the Outer Rim. The trees reached all the way to the garden module's top, with [[Atmosphere|air]] currents stirring their vines so that they gently moved back and forth. Each tree formed its own ecosystem, with small [[Bioluminescence|bioluminescent]] [[bird]]s and [[insect]]s flying between the vines like sparks.<ref name="LOTJ" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="LOTJdate" /> during the [[dedication of the Starlight Beacon]],<ref name="LOTJ" /> [[human]]<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Jedi Master]]s [[Avar Kriss]] and [[Elzar Mann]] took a walk through the garden module with the flame-trees. They ascended a spiraling walkway that led through the module, and at the center of the structure, the combination of the trees' presence and open [[Realspace|space]] being visible through the enclosing transparisteel made Mann feel like he was standing inside a campfire and peering out into the [[Local day|night]]. Moments later, after Kriss had departed, leaving her fellow [[Jedi]] at the top of the spiraling pathway, Mann again looked out into space, with the churning, rustling, and whipping flame-trees below him invoking a sense of standing atop an inferno.<ref name="LOTJ" />
