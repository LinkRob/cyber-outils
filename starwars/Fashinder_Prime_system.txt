The '''Fashinder Prime system''' was located in the [[Primtara sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]] and situated in grid square P-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[star system]] contained the terrestrial celestial body [[Fashinder Prime]].<ref name="RogueOneNovel" /> Between [[5 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date2">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]] establishes that the [[Partisans]]' time on [[Fashinder Prime]] occurred following [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Mission to Tamsye Prime|abandonment]] of [[Jyn Erso]] and before the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which equates to between [[5 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]] spent time on Fashinder Prime.<ref name="RogueOneNovel" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fashinder Prime system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
