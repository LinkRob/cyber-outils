'''Aubadas''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="QFTHC" /> in the [[Aubadas system]], in the [[Tunka sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Double planet|twin planet]] of [[Gloam]]. It was originally a desolate, desert planet with limited resources, however overtime was transformed into a lush marshy planet with many valleys, gullies, and [[mountain]]s. In [[382 BBY]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Silandra Sho]] and her [[Republic Pathfinder]] [[Master Sho's Pathfinder team|team]] visited the planet in search of pathfinder team [[Outer Rim Expedition Two-Five-Two]] and made contact with the [[Katikoot]] [[species]] and entered their capital city of [[Diurna]]<ref name="QFTHC" />
