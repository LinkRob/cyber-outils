'''CO-BA countermeasures''' was a type of defense system that could be installed on a [[starship]] to deflect incoming [[missle]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an individual named [[Pillofa]] stashed CO-BA countermeasures in a locked crate on [[Renpalli Station]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Toshaal system]].

{{GameMechanics}}
During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] discovered the stash, took the countermeasure system, and installed it in her [[EML-850 light freighter]] ''[[Trailblazer]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
CO-BA countermeasures appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
