'''Chandrilan Waffles''' were a type of round, [[Color|brown]] [[waffle]] from the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]. The waffles were marked with the logo of the [[Chandrila Star Line]] and were consumable by [[human]]s. They were available to guests on board the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'', which was operated by Chandrila Star Line,<ref name="Cruiser" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> They were served on their own or with a [[Rolled Omelet]] and two [[Roasted Sausage]]s in the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]] as part of the breakfast buffet on board the [[starship]], which also had [[syrup]] available to add to the waffles.<ref name="Cruiser" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chandrilan Waffles were a dish served at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Cruiser" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref>
