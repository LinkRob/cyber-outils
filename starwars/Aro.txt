'''Aro''' was a [[Rodian]] who worked for the [[Devaronian]] criminal [[Coir Cion]] on the [[moon of Dawk]] during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" /> Between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> when the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]] [[Mission to the moon of Dawk|arrived]] on the [[moon]] to [[Death|kill]] Cion, Aro led a group to confront him. Maul defeated the other forces, and Aro surrendered, offering to lead Maul to Cion. However, learning that Cion had ordered Aro to bring him the intruder's head, Maul instead cut off the Rodian's head and delivered it to the Devaronian in the [[Korts Cantina]] before killing Cion and his associates.<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" />
