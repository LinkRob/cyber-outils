A '''field generator''' was a device used for protection from falls. In the case of sudden changes in velocity, it would project a small personal force field that would shield the wearer, preventing any falls, rockslides or collisions from being fatal, although they had limits. It was commonly used in [[pathfinding]]. They were typically box-shaped, and small enough to be worn on a [[belt]].<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan" />
