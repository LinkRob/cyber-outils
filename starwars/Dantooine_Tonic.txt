The '''Dantooine Tonic''' was a mixed [[alcohol]]ic beverage served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> The [[Color|orange]]-colored [[cocktail]] was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> Its name references the planet [[Dantooine]].

==Behind the scenes==
The Dantooine Tonic was an alcoholic beverage that was formerly sold to customers at the Oga's Cantina attraction in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]].<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> It was first served in [[2023]] but was removed from the menu by [[2024]]. The menu's description read, "Del Maguey Vida Mezcal, Passion Fruit Syrup, Ginger Beer, Lime."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/dinner|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Dinner Menu}}</ref>
