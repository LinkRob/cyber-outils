"'''Chicken in the Pot'''" is a song made for the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' anthology]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="SoloS" /> A duet, its [[Huttese]] lyrics show a conversation between the two singers about what [[Foodstuff|food]] to eat. Throughout the song, the female singer expresses her hunger, mentioning food such as [[Wikipedia:Hamburger|hamburger]], [[Wikipedia:Cucumber|cucumber]], [[Wikipedia:Bolognese sauce|Bolognese]], and [[Wikipedia:Mayonnaise|mayonnaise]].<ref name="Lyrics">Meaning based on established [[Huttese]] vocabulary and new Huttese words provided by [[Leland Chee]]: {{Twitter|HolocronKeeper|status/1448837622596395010|[[Leland Chee]]|date=2021-10-14|quote=hamborgo = hamburger<br />cucumbo = cucumber<br />bolonay = Bolognese<br />mayanay = mayonnaise<br />#huttese|archivedate=20220212235503}}</ref>

In the making of the song, composer [[John Powell]] was inspired by early concept art depicting the character [[Luleo Primoc]] as a chicken-like creature.<ref name="Powell">{{YouTube|video=Ujm6aLuN7yQ|text=INTERVIEW: 'Solo A Star Wars Story' Composer John Powell|channel=channel/UCueVxTNTWaBRtCLxTmOtxRQ|name=Star Wars News Net|archivedate=20210422134823}}</ref> Musician [[Wikipedia:Louis Prima Jr.|Louis Prima Jr.]] served as the alto temp voice during the song's production. The [[in-universe]] male singer is named Luleo Primoc, a [[tuckerization]] of Prima's name.<ref name="Primoc name">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/1014353451319361538|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2018-07-03|quote=I named Luleo Primoc and am glad the temp alto voice (which can be heard on the soundtrack album) came out deeper in the end film.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/DuBhC|nolive=1}}</ref>

While Prima initially sang in a language that Powell found on the Internet, a linguist converted the lyrics to another language and Prima re-recorded the song. In the final cut of the film, Primoc was performed with a lower pitch by another singer at director [[Ron Howard]]'s request.<ref name="Powell" /> While the film's initial soundtrack album release features an alto voice for "Chicken in the Pot," its deluxe edition features the low-pitched version.<ref name="SoloS" />

[[Baraka May]] performed the female voice of "Chicken in the Pot," named [[Aurodia Ventafoli]] in-universe, while [[Sema-Tawi Smart]] acted as Ventafoli in the film.

Expanding on the chicken-like Luleo Primoc singing "Chicken in the Pot" in-universe, author [[Adam Bray]] wrote in<ref name="Bray">{{Twitter|authoradambray|status/1316951811341897728|[[Adam Bray]]|date=2020-10-16|quote=In Solo, Luleo Primoc sings the song "Chicken in the pot." Expanding on the idea he looks like a cooked bird, I added to his playlist in Ultimate Star Wars with the songs "Carve Your Name in My Heart" and "Your Love is Gravy." But "Stuffed With Your Love" didn't make the cut.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/vDwUT}}</ref> the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' that Primoc also sang "[[Carve Your Name in My Heart]]" and "[[Your Love is Gravy]]."<ref name="USWN">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> An additional third title, "Stuffed With Your Love," was not included in the published book.<ref name="Bray" />
