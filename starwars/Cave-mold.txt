'''Cave-molds''' were organisms that were [[Farmer|farmed]] by the residents of [[Pau City]], the [[capital]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Utapau]]. Cave-molds were grown along with [[ocean-kelp]]s in [[Hydroponics|hydroponic]] greenhouses on the [[Produce Level]], one of the lowest of Pau City's eleven levels.<ref name="SWCL" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cave-molds were introduced to the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] in a section written by [[Kerrie Dougherty]],<ref name="SWCL" /> a reprint of the [[2005]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|book]] of the same name that originally introduced [[Cave-mold/Legends|cave-molds]].<ref name="SWCL2005">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
