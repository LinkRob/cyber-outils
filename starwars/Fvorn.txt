'''Fvorn''' was a [[Duros]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] who was taken at a young age to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] and [[Jedi training|trained]] in the [[Force-sensitive|use]] of [[the Force]]. During the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]<ref name="Inquisitor" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> he was a member of the [[Jedi assault team|Geonosis strike team]], tasked with rescuing Jedi [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. Fvorn was fried to [[death]] by a [[Geonosian]] wielding a [[static pike]].<ref name="Inquisitor" />
