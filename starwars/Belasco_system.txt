The '''Belasco system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Belasco sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It lay in grid square O-11 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] and was connected via<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> the [[Ootmian Pabol]]<ref name="Ootmian" /> [[hyperspace route]] to the [[planet]]<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> [[Gyndine]] and the [[Mid Rim Territories]] astronomical object [[New Apsolon system|New Apsolon]].<ref name="TFABG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Belasco was first depicted in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] boxed set for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="TFABG" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Belasco/Legends|Belasco]] was first mentioned in ''[[Jedi Apprentice: The Deadly Hunter]]'', a [[2000]] novel authored by [[Jude Watson]],<ref name="Deadly Hunter">''[[Jedi Apprentice: The Deadly Hunter]]''</ref> and later made its appearance in ''[[Jedi Apprentice: The Dangerous Rescue]]'', a [[2001]] novel authored by the same person for the [[Star Wars: Jedi Apprentice|''Jedi Apprentice'']] novel series.<ref name="Dangerous Rescue">''[[Jedi Apprentice: The Dangerous Rescue]]''</ref>
