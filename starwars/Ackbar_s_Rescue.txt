"'''Ackbar's Rescue'''" is a short narrative story published in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'' in [[1987]]. The story recounts [[Gial Ackbar/Legends|Gial Ackbar's]] time as a [[Slavery/Legends|slave]] serving [[Grand Moff/Legends|Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin/Legends|Wilhuff Tarkin]]. It was reprinted in the [[The Star Wars Sourcebook (Second Edition)|second edition]] of the book in [[1994]].

==Plot summary==
[[Gial Ackbar/Legends|Gial Ackbar]], once a respected leader in [[Mon Calamari/Legends|Mon Calamari]] society, finds himself as a [[Slavery/Legends|slave]] of [[Grand Moff/Legends|Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin/Legends|Wilhuff Tarkin]]. Learning from Tarkin and studying the tactics of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]], Ackbar is eventually rescued by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance]] and becomes one of their foremost naval [[Officer/Legends|officers]].<ref name="SWS" />
