'''Darth Vader's Tower''' was a building on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] in the [[Galactic City/Legends|Imperial City]]. The tower was a retreat for [[Dark Lord of the Sith/Legends|Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]], and he used the building to meditate and train in [[The Force/Legends|the Force]]. Vader kept [[Darth Vader's meditation chamber/Legends|one of his]] [[Meditation chamber/Legends|meditation spheres]] within the building, allowing him to remove portions of [[Darth Vader's armor/Legends|his armor]] safely. Following the [[Battle of Shumari]], Vader kept one of his secret students, [[Tao (Sith Apprentice)|Tao]], in the tower, away from his master, [[Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]]. After the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], Vader retired to his tower to meditate on the identity of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] [[Pilot/Legends|pilot]] who had destroyed the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]]. There he was confronted by Tao, and the two briefly sparred together.<ref name="Perfect Evil" />

Vader returned to the tower once again after he had discovered that [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], his lost son, was the pilot who had destroyed the Death Star. As Vader meditated about how to best reunite with his son, he was approached by Tao once again. Vader, knowing that his son was alive, had no use for Tao, and he told the boy to go his own way. Tao was furious, but he left the tower, saddened that Vader had decided to abandon him.<ref name="Perfect Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
This tower appears to be modeled after the facemask of Vader's armor. It is unknown how this building relates to [[Darth Vader's Palace]], a building which served in the same function as this tower.<ref name="Perfect Evil" />
