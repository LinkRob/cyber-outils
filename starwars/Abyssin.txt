'''Abyssins''' were a cycloptic (single-[[eye]]d), [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Species|race]] native to [[Byss (Abyssin)|Byss]]. They had the ability to [[Regeneration|regenerate]] lost [[limb]]s and could live for nearly 300 [[Standard year|years]]. Notable Abyssins included [[Myo]], a patron of [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in [[Mos Eisley]], [[Tatooine]], and [[Gor Koresh]], a [[gangster]] and [[gambler]] who hunted [[Mandalorian]] [[Nevarro Mandalorian covert|coverts]] for their [[beskar]] [[Mandalorian armor|armor]].

==Biology and appearance==
Abyssins were a sentient [[species]] of cycloptic [[Humanoid|bipeds]]<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> native to Byss.<ref name="M&A" /> Their physical characteristics included a swarthy-[[Color|greenish]]<ref name="A New Hope" /> or dark blue-black<ref name="Gone with a Trace" /> skin, green and white [[hair]], and a single-pupiled [[eye]] in the center of their forehead.<ref name="A New Hope" /> They also had the notable abilities of regrowing missing/amputated limbs and living to be 300 years old.<ref name="Shryo" /> Some Abyssins possessed three, claw-tipped digits on each hand,<ref name="A New Hope" /> while others had four, more [[human]]-like fingers.<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> According to [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], the Abyssin would beat their elderly members to [[death]] in order to conserve resources.<ref name="M&A" />

==Abyssin in the galaxy==
[[File:Myo-BaseSeries4.png|thumb|left|200px|The Abyssin Myo]]
During the late [[Republic Era]], the [[Count of Serenno]], [[Ramil]], hired a group of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] to [[Incident on Serenno|invade]] [[Serenno]], including [[Oster]] and [[Unidentified Abyssin guard|another Abyssin]] (both of whom were later killed by [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]]).<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref>

The [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] used a [[Jabba the Hutt's dais|large hoversled]] as a throne that was decorated with several statues of Abyssin heads.<ref name="RotJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

[[Jackar Bowmani]], a [[Gender|male]] Abyssin, worked in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] as a munitions expert during the [[Clone Wars]], and was secretly fed modified explosive [[NM-K reconstitutor]] nano-[[droid]]s by his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Letta Turmond]], to use him as a [[bomb]] against the Jedi. Bowmani later self-[[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|detonated]] inside of the Jedi Temple hangar, [[Death|killing]] six [[Jedi]] and numerous [[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s, among other hangar workers in the process, destroying all but one of his arms.<ref name="Sabotage" /> Another Abyssin named Myo<ref name="CL 2016" /> was a patron of Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina in Mos Eisley when [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] first met [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]], shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="A New Hope" />

During the [[New Republic Era]], an Abyssin gambler and gangster named Gor Koresh profited from underworld [[gladiator]] matches<ref name="KoreshDatabank">{{Databank|gor-koresh|Gor Koresh}}</ref> at his own [[Gor Koresh's Honky Tonk|arena]]<ref name="SWCEUAE">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> while also hunting [[Mandalorian]] [[Nevarro Mandalorian covert|remnants]] for their beskar armor. At one point, during a fight-to-the-death between two [[Gamorrean]]s. he met with Mandalorian [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]], who was in search of others of his kind, at his arena. Koresh apparently had information on where other Mandalorians could be found, but he attempted to trick Djarin into a bet which could scam him of his beskar armor. Djarin refused the offer, prompting Koresh to pull-out a gun and hold Djarin at gunpoint, along with three armed guards who were on standby. In the chaos, Koresh ended up shooting and killing one of the Gamorrean fighters in the ring, saying he didn’t want to lose a bet. Djarin concurred, and subsequently defeated Koresh's guards, who fled amidst the fighting. Djarin intercepted Koresh outside the arena in the dimly-lit streets, capturing him with a rope around the foot and hanging him upside-down from a streetlight. Djarin continued pressing Koresh for the information he desired. After Koresh gave in, Djarin walked off, saying that letting him go wasn’t "part of the deal". Instead, Djarin fired at the streetlight above Koresh, rendering it useless and thus darkening the surrounding street, which enticed a pack of ravenous, nocturnal [[wild dog]]s.<ref name="Chapter 9">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
An Abyssin first appeared in the [[1977]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="A New Hope" /> The name "Abyssin" itself was only coined in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] [[roleplaying game]] sourcebook ''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]'' in [[1984]].<ref name="Galaxy Guide 4">''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]''</ref> The first [[canon]] source to use the species's name was the reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], released in [[2016]]. Although the name was misspelled as "Abbysin" in that book,<ref name="CL 2016" /> author [[Jason Fry]] has confirmed this is merely a typographical error.<ref name="Abbysin Twit">{{Twitter|jasoncfry|status/784035205547954176|[[Jason Fry]]|quote=@LelalMekha Typo|nobackup=1}}</ref>

The Abyssin were also considered to fill the role of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]'s alien guards for [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], becoming a predecessor design to the [[Neimoidian Royal Guard]]s seen in the final film. Dubbed the "Separatist Alien Guard," the concept art of the Abyssin soldier was later included as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]'' collection.<ref name="Bluray">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]'', Disc 7, Star Wars Archives: Episode III Separatist Alien Guard Concept</ref>
