An '''apostate''' was a member of a religious order who was expelled for breaking the rules of their faith.<ref name="Return">{{BOBF|Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian}}</ref> The [[Sorcerers of Tund]] expelled those who embraced the [[dark side of the Force]], dubbing them apostates. Once such [[Unidentified Sorcerer of Tund|apostate sorcerer]] attacked and butchered a [[Vildar Mac's village|village]] on [[Kiffar]], generating a deep shame in the rest of his Order.<ref name="The High Republic 2022 7">[[The High Republic (2022) 7|''The High Republic'' (2022) 7]]</ref> A [[Mandalorian]] that belonged to the<ref name="Return" /> [[Children of the Watch]]<ref name="Chapter 11">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref> and broke the [[Mandalorian Creed]] would become an apostate. According to Creed, one may only be redeemed in the [[Living Waters]] beneath the [[mines of Mandalore]]. The Mandalorian [[Din Djarin]] removed his helmet and was considered apostate by [[Paz Vizsla]] and [[The Armorer]].<ref name="Return" /> Djarin also considered himself an apostate, although the [[Magistrate]] of [[Nevarro]] [[Greef Karga]] promised him that on Nevarro he is not considered apostate and could be landed [[gentry]].<ref name="Chapter 17" />

In order to remove his [[decree of exile]], Djarin went on a [[mission to the Living Waters]]. After being captured in the ruins of [[Sundari]] by a [[Spider Tank]], he was rescued by [[Bo-Katan Kryze]], who later helped him bathe in the Living Waters.<ref name="Chapter 18">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 18: The Mines of Mandalore}}</ref> They returned to the [[Mandalorian secret covert|covert]] and showed a proof from the Living Waters to the Armorer, who confirmed that they were redeemed and no longer apostates.<ref name="Chapter 19">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 19: The Convert}}</ref>
