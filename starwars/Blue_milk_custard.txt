'''Blue milk custard''' was a comfort food made using the [[blue milk]] produced by [[bantha]] [[species]] native to the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. It was served as a dessert at [[Old Jho's Pit Stop]], a [[cantina]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] run by the [[Ithorian]] [[Jho]]. The custard was one of the [[human]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Kanan Jarrus]]'s top five favorite desserts served in the establishment.<ref name="AEYNTK" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blue milk custard was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]'', a reference book released in [[2015]].
