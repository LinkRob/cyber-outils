'''''Carry Identification''''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]]. It was written in [[High Galactic]] and read "ATTENTION IMPERIAL SUBJECTS:
REMEMBER: ALWAYS CARRY YOUR [[Identichip|IDENTIFICATION]]. YOUR SAFETY DEPENDS ON IT." It also had a [[stormtrooper]] [[helmet]] emblazoned at the center of the [[Imperial crest]], plus four other Imperial crests around the corners of the poster. In [[Aurebesh]] there is text at the top stating "Failure to produce identification on demand is grounds for immediate imperial adjudication" and text at the bottom stating "By order of his royal highness, [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], [[Imperial Decree 3263827]]"<ref name="Propaganda" />
