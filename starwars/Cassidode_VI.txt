'''Cassidode VI''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> in the [[Cassidode system]] of the [[Aparo sector]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It fell within grid square R-3 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the location of a tiny, ill-supplied [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[Cassidode VI rebel outpost|outpost]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[rebel pilot]] [[Farns Monsbee]] served as the base's sole [[Snub-fighter|snubfighter]] [[pilot]] before transferring to [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]] at some point prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]]<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cassidode VI was first mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" />
