'''Class-Two''' was a class of [[starship]] that was deployed as part of the [[Dawn Fleet]] during [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]]'s [[War against the Sith|attempts]] to destroy the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lords]] [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. During the [[Battle at Amaxine Station]], the Class-Two and [[Class Three|Three]] vessels were ordered by [[Margo]] to destroy the long-range [[cannon]]s of the ''[[Executor]]''.<ref name="Hidden Empire 4" />
