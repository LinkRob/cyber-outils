The '''Aurodium Conqueror''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Bounty Hunter (Imara Vex)|standard costume]]. This ornate [[Clothing|outfit]] and [[armor]] featured a [[Color|white]] and gray coloration, with golden adornments, a glowing green visor on the helmet, and a triangular golden [[shoulder-mounted armament]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aurodium Conqueror was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Imara Vex, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 1.0.0 alongside Season 1 and the game's global launch on [[June 4]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Aurodium Champion]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching the rank Kyber I within ''Hunter's'' "Ranked" mode during the game's first season.<ref name="Hunters" />
