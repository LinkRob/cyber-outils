'''Clothes steamers''' were devices with a steam head and a [[water]] bulb that were used to steam [[clothing]]. Manufactured by [[Ayelixe Fabrico]], they were available during the [[High Republic Era]], during which the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Yord Fandar]] traveled with one as part of a full tailoring kit. In [[132 BBY]], he used it to steam his [[mission robes]] on board the [[Polan-717 Jedi transport]] [[Polan GX-8|''Polan'' GX-8]] before departing on a [[Mission to Carlac|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Carlac]].

==Description==
Clothes steamers were cylindrical [[Color|gray]] devices manufactured by [[Ayelixe Fabrico]] for steaming [[clothing]]. A curved handle attached to the top of the cylinder with two green and one red button beneath it and in the center of the cylinder was a [[water]] bulb. A red band ran around the clyinder above its base and a steam head and nozzle were attached just below the band by a gray ridged tube.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> 

==History==
{{Quote|Yord. Put your clothes on.|Jecki Lon chastises a shirtless Yord Fandar for using a clothes steamer|audio=Jecki-PutYourClothesOn-LostFound.flac|<ref name="LostFound">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref>}}
[[File:YordSteaming-LostFound.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=A shirtless Yord Fandar stands in the ''Polan'' GX-8 steaming his mission robes with a clothing steamer.|Yord Fandar traveled with a clothes steamer in his tailoring kit.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Yord Fandar]] traveled with a clothes steamer as part of a full tailoring kit, as he was so particular about his appearance, he needed the ability to iron out any imperfections.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> after being assigned to a [[Mission to Carlac|mission]] to arrest the former [[Jedi]] [[Verosha Aniseya]] on<ref name="LostFound" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> [[Carlac]], Fandar boarded the<ref name="LostFound" /> Jedi [[Polan-717 Jedi transport]] [[Polan GX-8|''Polan'' GX-8]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the<ref name="LostFound" /> planet<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> [[Coruscant]] and removed his [[mission robes]] to steam them with the clothes steamer.<ref name="LostFound" />

[[Jedi Master]] [[Sol]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Jecki Lon]], then came upon the shirtless Fandar in the process of steaming, causing the knight to quickly put the steamer's nozzle down and bow. Sol did not acknowledge Fandar's state of undress when speaking to him, but Lon chastised him for being shirtless.<ref name="LostFound" />

==Behind the scenes==
