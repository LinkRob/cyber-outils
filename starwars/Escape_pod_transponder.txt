[[Escape pod]]s were equipped with '''transponders''' that signaled their location so that rescuers could locate the pods. Some models of escape pod had a handheld transponder, so that occupants of the pod could take it with them if they had to leave the immediate vicinity of the pod. Transponders in [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] escape pods were designed so the signal could only be picked up by Imperial searchers.<ref name="The Honorable Ones" />
