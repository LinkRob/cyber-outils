A '''catch vest''' was a type of [[armor]] made from tightly-woven energy absorbing fibers that could partially diffuse a potentially lethal [[blaster]] bolt. The innocuous vests were indistinguishable from regular [[clothing]] and were an excellent choice for the many [[The galaxy|galactic]] travellers who wished to safeguard against the prevelance of blaster use in the galaxy without advertising that they had an extra layer of protection.<ref name="G&G" />

Catch vests cost three hundred [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and were easy to find for purchase during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. They were commonly worn by well-traveled [[Corellian]]s and saved the lives of many [[Merchant|free-traders]].<ref name="G&G" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], catch vests were mentioned in the [[2019]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="G&G" /> The [[Catch vest/Legends|vests]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were mentioned in ''[[Suns of Fortune]]'', a [[2014]] Fantasy Flight Games supplement for ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]''.<ref name="SoF">''[[Suns of Fortune]]''</ref>
