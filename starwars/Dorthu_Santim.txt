'''Dorthu Santim''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Ithorian]]. Around five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], he resided on [[Lothal]]. During the [[planet]]'s [[time]] under the [[Galactic Empire]], Santim was forced to operate in an [[All Terrain Missile Platform]] facility of the [[Politics#Empire|Empire]] by its [[stormtrooper]]s, before he was rescued by the [[Spectres]] of [[Kanan Jarrus]]. Following his rescue, the Ithorian went to live with other defenseless citizens in the [[Haven]], a secret rebel base.<ref name="Recon Misions" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I'm only alive because the Empire still has use for me. My days are numbered.|Santim|<ref name="Recon Misions" />}}
After being brought back to the Haven, Santim believed he was only still alive because the Empire still had use for him. Bridger visited him in the Haven and gave him hope, making him believe that he and his family were safe for the time being.<ref name="Recon Misions" />
