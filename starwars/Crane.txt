A '''crane''' was a type of [[bird]]. Types of cranes included the [[castua crane]]<ref name="Attack" /> and the [[marsh crane]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Leia Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] met the [[Gender|girl]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] during a [[pathfinding]] class on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] and mentally noted that Holdo's thinness and gawkiness reminded her of a marsh crane. Later, during another session of the class on the world [[Pamarthe]], Holdo tilted her head and Organa was reminded of a marsh crane looking for [[Foodstuff|food]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> 

By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Attack date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Join the Resistance: Attack on Starkiller Base'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[Resistance]] members [[Sari Nadle]] and [[Mattis Banz]] saw a castua crane on the planet [[D'Qar]].<ref name="Attack" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Attack date" /> while on the [[First Order]]'s [[Starkiller Base]], Nadle and Banze remembered occasions they saw birds on D'Qar, including when they saw castua cranes.<ref name="Attack" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Milestones===
[[File:Crane-ArtOfVisions.png|thumb|right|300px|A relief of a crane depicted on a lightsaber hilt.]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], a crane was first mentioned in [[Claudia Gray]]'s [[2017]] novel ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, a [[whooping crane]] was mentioned in ''[[Lando Calrissian and the Flamewind of Oseon]]'', written by [[L. Neil Smith]] as the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars: The Lando Calrissian Adventures]]'' series.<ref name="Flamewind">''[[Lando Calrissian and the Flamewind of Oseon]]''</ref> 

An image of a crane first appeared in "[[Lop & Ochō]]," a short film that was released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 1|first volume]] of the non-canon ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Geno Studio]], was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Lop & Ochō" /> on [[September 22]], [[2021]].<ref name=" Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-trailer-and-cast|text=Stunning New ''Star Wars: Visions'' Trailer Debuts}}</ref> Prior to the short's release, images of a crane were first depicted simultaneously in the original Japanese and English dub trailers for the series, which were both uploaded to the official ''Star Wars'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel on [[August 17]] of that year.<ref name="JP Trailer">{{SWYouTube|lle0NNmvIyU|Star Wars: Visions &#124; Original Trailer &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/original-trailer-star-wars-visions}}</ref><ref name="English Trailer">{{SWYouTube|jtAsl-0o3O0|Star Wars: Visions &#124; English Dub Trailer &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/english-dub-trailer-star-wars-visions}}</ref> The crane was identified in the [[2022]] art book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]'', written by [[Zack Davisson]].<ref name="Art of Visions">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]''</ref> In the real world, [[Wikipedia:Crane|cranes]] are a type of tall wading bird.<ref name="crane">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/animal/crane-bird|text=crane|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|''Encyclopædia Britannica'']]|archivedate=20250209180043}}</ref>

===Lop & Ochō===
[[File:LightsaberBoxTop-ArtOfVisions.png|thumb|left|300px|Concept art of a crane depicted on a box.]]
''The Art of Star Wars: Visions'' established a crane to be a type of bird with two [[Limb|legs]], two wings, and a [[beak]], and that a relief of a crane is depicted on the [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] of a [[Yasaburō family heirloom lightsaber|lightsaber]]<ref name="Art of Visions" /> that was passed down through the generations of the [[Yasaburō clan]], a notable family on the planet [[Tao]]. Images of cranes that are golden in [[color]] and have [[flower]]s in their [[mouth]]s are also depicted on the top and side of the box in which the [[lightsaber]] is stored.<ref name="Lop & Ochō">{{Visions|Lop & Ochō}}</ref>

The Yasaburō clan's lightsaber, including the relief of a crane on it, was designed by [[Ren Ishimori]], and concept art of the lightsaber was colored by [[Yuichi Kuboki]].<ref name="EP guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-visions/episode-guide-lop-and-ocho|text="Lop & Ochō"" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars: Visions}}</ref> In traditional paintings of the [[Wikipedia:Bird-and-flower painting|birds and flowers]] genre, the bird often holds a flower in its mouth, so a star-shaped flower was placed in the mouth of the bird depicted on the box for the Yasaburō clan's lightsaber.<ref name="Art of Visions" />

===The Song of Four Wings===
{{Quote|The legends of your kind talk about birds just like these, huh? A guardian deity.|Crane, to Woopas|<ref name="Four Wings">{{Visions|The Song of Four Wings}}</ref>}}
[[File:ThreePaperCranes-TheSongOfFourWings.png|thumb|right|300px|Paper cranes created by Woopas]]
Cranes appeared in the form of [[paper crane]]s in "[[The Song of Four Wings]]", a short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of ''Visions'' that was produced by [[Project Studio Q]]. The animated short released on Disney+<ref name="Four Wings" /> on [[October 29]], [[2025]]. Concept art of the paper cranes was revealed ahead of the short's release in a [[StarWars.com]] article published on [[April 25]] of that year.<ref name="First Look">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-star-wars-visions-volume-3-spinoff-announce|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 3 First Look and Spinoff Announced – ''Star Wars'' Celebration Japan 2025}}</ref>

In "The Song of Four Wings," the [[human]] [[princess]] [[Crane (princess)|Crane]] found the [[Gigoran]] [[Youngling|child]] [[Woopas]] in the ruins of a [[Unidentified village (Joetz)|village]] that had been destroyed by the [[Galactic Empire]] on the planet [[Joetz]]. Crane noticed several paper cranes<ref name="Four Wings" /> with four wings configured like an [[X-wing starfighter]]<ref name="First Look" /> that Woopas had made himself, and noted she was familiar with Gigoran legends that spoke of the birds as guardian [[Deity|deities]]. After Crane helped Woopas safely escape the planet, he played with a paper crane aboard a [[starship]], while Crane held and looked at another. While Crane spoke with [[Admiral]] [[Basil Kiucee]], Woopas [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|levitate]] his paper crane.<ref name="Four Wings" />
