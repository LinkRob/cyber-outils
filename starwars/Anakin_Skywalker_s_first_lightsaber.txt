'''Anakin Skywalker's first lightsaber''' was the first [[lightsaber]] owned and built by [[Anakin Skywalker]]. It had a [[Color|blue]] [[plasma]] blade, chunky controls, and thick ridges on the handle.<ref name="UFC">''[[Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars]]''</ref> While Skywalker was fighting his way through [[Poggle the Lesser]]'s [[battle droid]] [[Geonosis droid factories|foundries]] on [[Geonosis]], his lightsaber was destroyed by a mechanical cutting arm. Shortly thereafter, the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] occurred, effectively starting the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Episode II" /> Sometime prior to the [[Battle of Christophsis|Battle]] of [[Christophsis]], Skywalker built himself a [[Skywalker lightsaber|new lightsaber]].<ref name="TCWf">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>

==History==
===Construction===
Around [[29 BBY]] [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] traveled to [[Ilum]] where he found his [[kyber crystal]], which he used to then construct his first lightsaber.<ref name="Timelines" />

===Usage===
====Training sessions====
{{Quote|He may be good with a ''lightsaber'', but that doesn't mean he'll make a good ''Jedi''.|A Duros Jedi|<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin 1">[[Obi-Wan and Anakin 1|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 1]]</ref>}}
[[File:AnakinTrainingDroid-ObiWanAnakin1.png|thumb|300px|left|Skywalker dueling a reprogrammed training droid at the Jedi Temple.]]
Following the construction of his lightsaber, Skywalker would return to [[Coruscant]] where he would participate in saber training sessions. In one particular session, Skywalker would alter the appearance of a [[Jedi training droid]] to mimic the appearance of [[Maul|Darth Maul]] and his [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|double-bladed lightsaber]]. Utilizing all his skills, Skywalker would emerge victorious by bisecting the droid in half with his lightsaber.<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin 1" />

====Mission to Carnelian IV====
Skywalker had his lightsaber when he and his master were investigating a [[distress signal]].

====Inquiring about lightsabers====
Some time after his and his masters mission to Carnelian IV, Skywalker and Kenobi were about to spar in the [[Jedi Temple training ground]]s when Anakin asked his master why the Jedi use lightsabers as opposed to any other weapon powered by [[kyber crystal]]s. Upon hearing this question, Kenobi told him the same thing [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] had told Kenobi; that wielding a lightsaber shows intent and that the user put thought behind wielding the weapon before drawing it, enforcing the idea that Jedi are protectors as opposed to destroyers.

====Rescuing Yoda====
When [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] was captured from [[Glee Anselm]], Kenobi and Skywalker launched a [[Rescue of Yoda|rescue mission]] unbeknownst to them that it was actually a test for the two Jedi.<ref name="CYD">''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure]]''</ref>

====A memorable training session====
During Skywalker's training with Kenobi, the two engaged in a lightsaber skirmish in the Jedi Temple.<ref name="Part V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref>

====Separatist crisis & destruction====
{{Quote|Not again! Obi-Wan's gonna kill me.|Skywalker, upon his lightsaber's destruction|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
[[File:AnakinPadawan-TLC.png|thumb|right|200px|Anakin with his lightsaber ignited.]]
While chasing bounty hunter [[Zam Wesell]], the [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|attempted]] assassin of [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] through the skylanes of [[Coruscant]], Skywalker grabbed onto [[Koro-2 all-environment exodrive airspeeder|Wesell's airspeeder]]. He ignited his lightsaber into the cockpit of the speeder in an attempt to sabotage the controls before the weapon flew out of his hand. Fortunately, the weapon was caught by Obi-Wan Kenobi, who was closely following in the [[XJ-6 airspeeder|speeder]] Skywalker originally utilized for the chase. Once Skywalker caused Wesell to crash land outside the [[Outlander Club]], Kenobi returned the lightsaber to him, whilst imploring him to take better care of it, saying the weapon was his "life".<ref name="Episode II" />

After Skywalker's mother was captured by [[Tusken Raider]]s, he tracked her to the Tuskens' camp. As soon as he found [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|Shmi]], she died in his arms, which caused the Padawan to go on a murderous rampage. Wielding his lightsaber, he slaughtered the entire tribe, including the men, women and children.<ref name="Episode II" />

After Obi-Wan Kenobi was captured by [[Count]] [[Dooku]] on [[Geonosis]], Skywalker and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] launched a rescue attempt and infiltrated one of the [[Geonosian]] [[Geonosis droid factories|droid foundries]]. While fighting his way through the facility, Skywalker was knocked down and pinned beneath the machinery. He was able to free himself, but not before his lightsaber was cut in half by a descending mechanical blade. Skywalker was unaware the weapon has been destroyed until he attempted to activate it, remarking that Kenobi was going to kill him for not taking better care of the weapon.<ref name="Episode II" />

===Legacy===
At some point between his ordeal on Geonosis<ref name="Episode II" /> and the [[mission to Benglor]],<ref name="Battle Tales 1">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 1|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 1]]</ref> Anakin Skywalker built himself a new lightsaber.<ref name="Episode III" /> He would wield this lightsaber throughout the Clone Wars until his [[Duel on Mustafar]]. Despite the loss of his lightsaber, Skywalker, now the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] [[Darth Vader|Vader]], built his third and final lightsaber that was based off of his first one.

Upon being asked by [[Verla]] who Skywalker was, [[Ferren Barr]] ordered his [[droid]] [[Endee]] to display files 430 to 459. The droid displayed a hologram of several [[Aurebesh]] characters including Skywalker, as well as a video of Anakin fighting [[B1-series battle droid]]s with his first lightsaber.<ref name="DV14">[[Darth Vader (2017) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 14]]</ref>

While reflecting on memories of the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] at the [[Petranaki Arena]] decades later, Vader imagined himself fighting in the arena with his first lightsaber to defend Padmé from battle droids.<ref name="DVA2">[[Darth Vader Annual 2|''Darth Vader Annual'' 2]]</ref>

After recruiting [[Sabé]], Vader worked with her to fight through Governor [[Tauntaza]]'s security. During one of their battles, Vader remembered using his first lightsaber to kill the Tusken Raiders.<ref name="DV24">[[Darth Vader (2020) 24|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 24]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Anakin Skywalker's first lightsaber first appeared in the [[2002]] [[canon]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="Episode II" />

Sources are inconsistent regarding when Skywalker first built his lightsaber. He used his first lightsaber throughout the [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] mini-series ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan and Anakin]]'', when he was twelve years old.<ref name="Obi & Anakin 2">[[Obi-Wan and Anakin 2|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 2]]</ref> However, both ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]'' and its [[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded|updated]] edition state that he built the lightsaber on [[Ilum]] at age fourteen.<ref name="AEYNtK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref><ref name="AEYNTK U&E">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>
