A '''canary moth''' was a type of [[moth]] that had wings. While on<ref name="ALD" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]<ref name="ALD" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> the [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] [[Wedge Antilles]] walked through a [[Unidentified spaceport (Chandrila)|spaceport]] to meet with his colleague, the [[pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]]. As he made his way toward a [[shuttle]], he noticed [[sachi blossom]] petals flitting in the air and thought they looked similar to a canary moth's shape.<ref name="ALD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Canary moths were mentioned in ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', a [[2016]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the second installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="ALD" />
