"'''Anakin's Vow'''" is a [[canon]] short story that appears in ''[[The Prequel Trilogy Stories]]''. It was written by [[Elizabeth Schaefer]].<ref name="TPTS" /> It adapts [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] from [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Anakin's]] time on [[Naboo]] to the funeral of [[Shmi Skywalker Lars]].
