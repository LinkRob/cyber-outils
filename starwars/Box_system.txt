'''Box system''' was a colloquial term used to describe a [[star system]] with strong [[electromagnet]]ic fluxes wrapped around its outer edges, which interacted with solar wind to obstruct [[hyperspace]] travel. The [[Unknown Regions]]' [[Rapacc system]] was one such system, containing the [[planet]] [[Rapacc]].

==Description==
{{Quote|The typical box system is bounded for the most part by external flux patterns interacting with solar wind. These two points mark the system's two gas giant planets.''"<br />"''Planets which carve out small holes in the solar wind as they travel along their orbits.|Thrawn and Ar'alani|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
Box systems were marked by uncharacteristically strong [[electromagnet]]ic fluxes wrapped around the [[Star system|system's]] outer edges. These fluxes interacted with solar wind from the system's [[star]], obstructing [[hyperspace]] travel and the performance of [[navigator]]s, such as the [[Chiss]] [[sky-walker]]s. For a [[starship]] to enter the interior of a box system, it had to do so in [[Realspace|space-normal]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

[[Planet]]s, such as [[gas giant]]s, could carve holes into the solar wind that allowed easy access into and out of the system. Such holes were difficult to notice from outside the system without detailed planetary data, but could be more easily exploited from within. The nature of box systems was advantageous for defense and preventing outside [[starship]] traffic from passing through the area, making them optimal for maintenance facilities, supply depots, or hidden staging areas.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==History==
{{Quote|So we go in through Shadow Number One and exit through Shadow Number Two?''"<br />"''Exactly. And the ''Springhawk'' can subsequently leave through either.|Senior Captain [[Kiwu'tro'owmis]] and Thrawn|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
The [[Rapacc system]], located in the [[Unknown Regions]] and containing the [[Paccosh]] [[homeworld]] of [[Rapacc]], was an example of a box system. After the planet was [[blockade]]d by the [[Nikardun Destiny]], two Paccosh [[refugee]] vessels managed to leave the system with the help of [[Void Guides|Void Guide]] navigators, with [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] assuming they utilized shadows in the system's solar wind to exit. Thrawn used the same shadows himself when [[Mission to the Rapacc system|investigating]] the [[Nikardun]] alongside [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
