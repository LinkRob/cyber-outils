The '''17kv antipersonnel twin blaster''' was a type of twin [[blaster cannon]] equipped onto the [[Multi-Troop Transport]]s<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> of the [[Trade Federation]] and its private [[Trade Federation Droid Army|droid army]].<ref name="Episode I" /> Following the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]], in which the Multi-Troop Transport found its way into the droid armies of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the 17kv antipersonnel twin [[blaster]] was used by the Confederacy during the war.<ref name="Episode III" />
