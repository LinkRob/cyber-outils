'''Fondor Station''' was a wheel-shaped [[space station]] that served as a [[shipyard]] and dock for [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] vessels such as [[Star Destroyer]]s and belonged to the [[Fondor Shipyards]], a collection of several similarly shaped space stations above the [[planet]] [[Fondor]]. Shortly after the [[Battle of Endor]], [[Moff]] [[Derrek Raythe]] was docked at the station on the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] [[Dauntless (Imperial-class)|''Dauntless'']] to extract [[Climate disruption array|experimental weather satellites]] for [[Operation: Cinder]]. Not long after, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Attack on Fondor|attacked]] the station, but were defeated.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
