'''Caelli-Merced''' was a [[repulsorcraft]] vehicle manufacturer during the time of the [[Galactic Empire]]. They produced the [[Halberd-441]]<ref name="TOG" /> and [[Skyblade-330]], two [[swoop]] [[Speeder bike|bike]] models notably used by [[Enfys Nest]] and the [[Cloud-Riders]].<ref name="CYD" />
