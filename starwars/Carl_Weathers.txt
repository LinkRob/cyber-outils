'''Carl Weathers''' ([[January 14]], [[1940s#1948|1948]] &ndash; [[February 1]], [[2024]])<ref name="death" /> was an actor and director who appears as [[Greef Karga]] in the [[Disney+]] live-action TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''. He was also famous for his role as [[Wikipedia:Apollo Creed|Apollo Creed]] in the [[Wikipedia:Rocky (franchise)|''Rocky'' franchise]]. An [[Aurebesh]] sign above entrance to the arena in the [[2026]] film ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]'' reads "WEATHERS APOLLO" as an homage to the late actor.<ref>{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-mandalorian-and-grogu-trivia|text=''Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu'' Behind-the-Scenes Facts and Trivia - Update|date=2026-05-22|quote=A sign above the doorway leading to the dejarik arena says “Weathers Apollo” in an homage to the late Greef Karga actor Carl Weathers who famously portrayed Apollo Creed in the ''Rocky'' franchise.}}</ref>
