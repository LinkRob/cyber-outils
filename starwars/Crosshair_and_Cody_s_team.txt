'''Crosshair and Cody's team''' was a [[squad]] of [[Clone trooper|clone stormtroopers]] under the command of [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]] tasked with [[Mission to Desix|quashing]] a [[Desix Separatist holdout|Separatist holdout]] on the planet of [[Desix]]. Amongst the team's members was the [[clone commando]] [[Crosshair]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Without a ship, we'll have to infiltrate the city on foot. Half the squad's too injured to continue.''"<br />"''I've beaten clankers with far less.|Commander Cody and Crosshair|<ref name="Solitary" />}}
The [[squad]] was comprised of numerous [[Clone trooper|clone]] [[stormtrooper]]s and led by [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]]. The squad also included [[team two]], and utilized [[Imperial Shuttle 995]], a [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class attack/transport shuttle]] flown by [[clone trooper pilot]]s. The [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s were equipped with standard [[Phase II clone trooper armor]], and used [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s along with a few [[BT X-42 heavy flame projector]]s. One [[clone commando]], [[Crosshair]], was also a member of the squad.<ref name="Solitary" />

==History==
{{Quote|We have official Imperial orders.''"<br />"''Desix separated from the Republic years ago, Commander. We are an independent system. Once your government acknowledges this, Grotton and his soldiers will be handed over. Those are my terms''"<br />"''We're not here to debate politics with you.''"<br />"''If you did, you might see how unjust your actions are.''"<br />"''Your droid army attacked two Imperial convoys.''"<br />"''We were defending ourselves from a hostile occupation. Your forces are the only threat to my people.''"<br />"''We are not the enemy. The Empire seeks to establish peace and order throughout the galaxy.|Commander Cody and Governor Tawni Ames|<ref name="Solitary" />}}
At the conclusion of the [[Clone Wars]] and the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Solitary" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] stronghold on the [[planet]] [[Desix]] refused Imperial authority and kidnapped their assigned [[governor]], Governor [[Grotton]], and held hostage. As a result, [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]] assigned clone commando Crosshair to a squad led by Clone Commander Cody to rescue the governor and established Imperial control.<ref name="Solitary" />

[[File:TawniAmesHoldsGrottonHostage.png|left|thumb|250px|Grotton is held hostage by Ames.]]
The squad was transported to the planet in a ''Nu''-class attack/transport shuttle 995, but as they approached the main [[city]] were met with [[missile]] fire, resulting in several hits. The shuttle crashed unto the surface near the city's outer perimeter, with several troopers killed in the crash. After eliminating a unit of [[battle droid]]s sent to check for survivors, Cody, Crosshair, and the contingent of clone survivors moved in and attacked the stronghold. After sustaining several casualties destroying an [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I]] and a number of droids, the squad moved in to the main stronghold. The squad split up, with [[team two]] tasked with other objectives while Cody, Crosshair, [[Nova (clone trooper)|Nova]], and [[Wyler]] moved in to liberate Governor Grotton. After losing Nova to [[BX-series droid commando|commando droids]] and Wyler to a [[droideka]], Cody and Crosshair moved to Grotton's position, eliminating scores of droids. Once the [[Desix Separatist holdout|Separatist holdout's]] leader, Governor [[Tawni Ames]] was persuaded to peacefully surrender, she was executed by Crosshair on the orders of Governor Grotton. Team two finished off any surviving droid forces, and members of the squad were extracted off-world by the same ''Nu''-class shuttle that Grotton previously arrived on as Imperial forces of TK troopers arrived to occupy the planet.<ref name="Solitary" />

Unsure if the Empire was in the right, Cody abandoned the Imperial Military after the mission. Rampart, who had never bothered to learn Cody's name in the first place and only knew him as CC-2224, assigned Crosshair a new mission under [[clone trooper officer]] [[CC-1226]].<ref name="Solitary" />
