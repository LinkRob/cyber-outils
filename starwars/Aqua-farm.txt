An '''aqua-farm''' was an area of [[water]] devoted to the [[Reproduction|production]] of aquatic animals. Such [[farm]]s were well-maintained on the [[swamp]]y [[planet]] of [[Artiod Minor]], where mated pairs of the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Artiodac]] [[species]] spent their<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Standard century|centuries]]-long<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> lifespans raising schools of [[prengalli]].<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aqua-farms were mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />
