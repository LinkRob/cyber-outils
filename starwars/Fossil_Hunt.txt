"'''Fossil Hunt'''" is the second part of the [[Yoda Rescue / Fossil Hunt|twenty-first]] episode of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|second season]] the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''. It was released on [[Disney+]] on [[March 19]], [[2025]].

==Official description==
''Kai protects a fossil.''

==Plot summary==
===The dig site===
[[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]], and [[Nubs]] join their explorer [[droid]] friend [[0G-LC]] in riding [[dunnel]]s through a [[Tenoo tree forest|forest]] on [[Tenoo]]. They are traveling to an excavation site. Brightstar is eager that the dunnels will help them reach the [[skeleton|fossil]] dig site quickly, while Solay says that seeing fossils of extinct animals will be amazing. Solay thanks 0G-LC, who says that the fossils will be invaluable to studying the natural history of [[Tenoo]]. 

Consulting a [[datapad]], 0G-LC briefs the younglings about the [[Raka (creature)|raka]], an ancient creature which used its [[claw]]s to climb the tallest [[tree]]s on Tenoo. Brightstar expresses eagerness and they gallop towards the dig site. At the site, Brightstar sees several fenced-off pits. Brightstar is underwhelmed by the dig site, but 0G-LC reassures him that they are going to see raka skeletons. He briefs the Jedi younglings on the patient work of using a brush to dust soil and dirt off a skeleton. Solay and Nubs express eagerness, but Brightstar is impatient towards the slow pace of excavation. 

===The unscrupulous entrepreneur===
The Jedi soon spot a pair of [[scoop droid|quadrupedal droids with frontal scoops]], which begin flattening the ground and knocking over the fences. 0G-LC and Solay tell the droids to stop so they do not damage the fossilss. Beeping in [[binary]], the droids ignore them and continue shoveling and pummeling the ground. At Solay's suggestion, the younglings decide to restrain the two droids. Brightstar jumps on top of a droid's shovel while Nubs turns it off. 0G-LC and Solay work together to disable the second scoop droid. Some dirt lands on 0G-LC. 

While 0G-LC thanks Solay, they are interrupted by the real estate developer [[Ozon Nimbee]], who is familiar with the younglings. Solay feels that he likes to "build bigger and better things" where they do not belong. Nimbee retorts, saying that he plans to turn the "worthless bit of land" into a lucrative resort. 0G-LC and Solay rebut that it is a fossil site of historical importance. Nimbee thinks this is boring and purports to present a legal claim over the dig site on a datapad.

Nimbee explains that his droids were going to level the site so he could start building. Nubs protests, but Brightstar proposes a compromise between the two parties. Nimbee tells the younglings and 0G-LC that he plans to turn the dig site into a beautiful resort attracting tourists to Tenoo for luxury and natural beauty. He says he wants to make something for everyone to enjoy. Brightstar is open to Nimbee's plan, but Solay and 0G-LC are concerned that the resort will damage the fossils, causing precious pieces of Tenoo's history to be lost.

===Escaping the pit===
Ozon Nimbee gawks at the idea of an extinct [[lizard]] disrupting his plans. 0G-LC, however, explains that the dig site is littered with countless more fossils waiting to be discovered. 0G-LC decides to show Nimbee and the younglings a largely complete raka skeleton he has been working on excavating. Nimbee reluctantly agrees to let them continue. The younglings and 0G-LC repel down into a deep pit where 0G-LC has been excavating a raka skeleton hidden behind a large tarp. While the younglings are amazed at the raka fossil, Nimbee is bored. He ascends his cable and transects the others', trapping them in the pit.

On the surface, Nimbee celebrates trapping the Jedi, hoping that his droids can continue leveling the site without interference. 0G-LC panics that Nimbee will destroy the dig site. Brightstar apologizes for not appreciate 0G's hard work in excavating the skeleton. Solay proposes that they use their [[training lightsaber]]s to climb out of the pit. The younglings climb out with 0G-LC piggy-backing on Nubs.

===Fighting back===
Nimbee soon returns with a [[bulldozer]] equipped with two large arms. The two scoop droids flank the bulldozer. Nimbee tasks the droids with filling the smaller holes while he deals with the big one. Nimbee clamps the bulldozer's arm around a mound of dirt and bulldozes it into one of the pits. The droids begin flattening the ground. 0G-LC expresses alarm that Nimbee and his droids will destroy the fossils. Solay tasks herself and Brightstar to handle the bulldozer while 0G-LC and Nubs tackle the droids. 

Nubs jumps on a droid while 0G-LC deals with the second droid. Nimbee orders one of his droids to deal with 0G-LC. Brightstar gives Solay a [[Force jump|lift]] into the air using [[the Force]]. Solay lands outside the windshield of the bulldozer and tells Nimbee that the fossils and Tenoo's history are more important than his resort. She pleads with Nimbee to stop, but he instead swipes at her with the bulldozer's arms. She dodges a few blows but eventually gets caught, clinging onto the left arm as it flails about. Brightstar leaps onto the second arm. 

As the swerving bulldozer approaches a pit, Nimbee attempts to convince Brightstar to buy into his resort plan, but Brightstar is unmoved. Spotting a hard rock, Brightstar jumps from the bulldozer and hurls the rock into the bulldozer's roller wheel, causing Nimbee to lose control of his vehicle. The bulldozer careens towards the deepest pit. Nimbee manages to jump out of the vehicle before the bulldozer falls inside, barely missing the raka skeleton. Nimbee is left clinging to the edge of the pit. 

After climbing up, Nimbee is distraught that his resort plans have been thwarted. Brightstar tells Nimbee that he has lost and that the dig site will be preserved for historical research. Nubs and 0G-LC, who have disabled the two scoop droids voice their agreement. Nimbee concedes defeat but refuses to clean up his mess. He departs in [[Ozon's ship|his starship]] while the Jedi younglings celebrate. 0G-LC is worried about the clean-up, but Brightstar convinces 0G-LC to repurpose Nimbee's abandoned scoop droids for repairing the fences. Brightstar and his friends uncover a new fossil and alert 0G-LC while the scoop droids clean up the site.
