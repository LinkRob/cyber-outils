'''Escape Plan JGT-HB''' was a contingency escape plan created by [[Cylo]], a [[doctor]] and [[Treason|traitor]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="DV21" /> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Darth Vader (2015) 21|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 21]] to [[0 ABY]].</ref> when the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]'' found [[Cylo's whale fleet|Cylo's fleet]] in the [[Crushank Nebulae]], Cylo ordered [[Morit Astarte]], a former Imperial agent, to prepare Escape Plan JGT-HB before reconsidering and electing [[Escape Plan JGT-HB-4]] instead.<ref name="DV21" />

==Behind the scenes==
Escape Plan JGT-HB was mentioned in the [[Darth Vader (2015) 21|twenty-first issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2015)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Kieron Gillen]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="DV21" /> on [[June 8]], [[2016]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/55401/darth_vader_2015_21|text=Darth Vader (2015) #21}}</ref>
