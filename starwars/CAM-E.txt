'''CAM-E''' was a [[cam droid]] affiliated with the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] who was stationed on the [[Youngling Care Space Station]] alongside [[Safari droid]] [[M1-RE]]. CAM-E cataloged footage of M1-RE taking care of [[youngling]]s,<ref name="Wookiee" /> and the recordings were eventually broadcasted to the [[HoloNet]].<ref name="GoC Activity Book">[[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts Activity Journal]]</ref>
