'''Dai Danu''' was a [[Sullustan]] freighter technician in service with the [[SoroSuub Corporation]]. After discovering a plot to frame him to have him terminated, Danu committed murder and fled aboard a hijacked cargo vessel. Danu then became a professional [[shipjacker]]. Danu was later targeted by [[Malia Shariv]] as she attempted to regain control of her ship, the ''[[Gundark's Back]]''.<ref name="ND" />
