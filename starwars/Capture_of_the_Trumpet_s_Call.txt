{{Quote|It was the wounded crew, they were Imperials. They must have slaughtered everyone on the ''Trumpet's Call'' before coming to the flotilla.|The [[rebel trooper]] [[Brand]], to fellow [[soldier]] [[Pol Andrissus|Charmer]]|<ref name="Battlefront" />}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], an Alliance [[light freighter]] that operated under the name ''[[Trumpet's Call]]'' was '''captured''' by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]]. The rebel crew of the [[freighter]], including the [[Unidentified captain (Trumpet's Call)|captain]], either suffocated or burned to [[death]] during the engagement as the [[life support]] systems failed. An infiltration team, put together by an [[Unidentified Imperial Special Forces commander|Imperial Special Forces commander]] aboard<ref name="Battlefront" /> the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> ''[[Herald]]'', then hijacked the damaged ''Trumpet's Call'' and took the light freighter to an Alliance [[flotilla]] in the [[Elochar sector]]. Pretending to be rebel survivors from the battle, the Imperial [[Spy|spies]] [[Infiltration of the Thunderstrike|infiltrated]] the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'' but were all killed not long after the rebels became aware of their presence.<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Behind the scenes==
The capture of the ''Trumpet's Call'' was mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
