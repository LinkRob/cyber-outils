The '''Arrow-23 landspeeder'''<ref name="Forged in Battle">''[[Forged in Battle]]''</ref> was a combat-modified [[landspeeder]] manufactured by [[Aratech Repulsor Company]]. It could carry five men comfortably, and boasted a [[laser cannon]] and a [[concussion missile]] [[Concussion missile launcher|launcher]]. It saw use in the [[Alliance Army|ground forces]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
