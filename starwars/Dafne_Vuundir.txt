'''Dafne Vuundir''' was [[queen]] of the [[planet]] [[Vuundalla]]. She ruled over the planet with her husband, [[King]] [[Pychor Vuundir]]. The two leaders were generally apathetic towards their people, but interested in wider [[The galaxy|galactic]] politics, particularly in [[Starlight Beacon]]. She was the mother of [[Prince]] [[Cyrus Vuundir]], and was unaware of his alter ego as the [[pirate]] Taborr Val Dorn, believing his workshop was simply a place in which he played. Following a [[groundquake]] on Vuundalla, she was convinced by Cyrus to allow supplies intended to rebuild her family's secondary [[fathier]] stable to be used for rebuilding efforts in the [[City|village]] near the palace. Dafne Vuundir was present at the [[Dedication of the Starlight Beacon|dedication of Starlight Beacon]].<ref name="Aftershock" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dafne Vuundir first appeared in the final batch of season one episodes of ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'' and later in the season one finale. She was voiced by [[Sharon Muthu]], who performed alongside her husband, [[Noshir Dalal]], who voiced King Pychor.<ref name="Aftershock" />
