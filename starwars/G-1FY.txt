'''G-1FY''' or '''Iffy''' was an old [[protocol droid]] who worked on the [[Wookiee]] [[starship]] ''[[Namrelllew]]''<ref name="Queen's Hope" /> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Queen's Hope]]'' is set ten [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Invasion of Naboo]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]], meaning that the novel must be set in [[22 BBY]].</ref> Shortly after the beginning of the [[Clone Wars]], he encountered [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] when she went undercover on the ship on a [[Siege on Hebekrr Minor|mission]] for the [[Galactic Senate]]. Iffy was secretly reprogrammed by the [[Neimoidian]] [[Oje N'deeb]].<ref name="Queen's Hope" />
