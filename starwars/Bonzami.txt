'''Bonzami''' were large, four-[[Limb|legged]] beasts that could be found on [[Bahryn]], one of [[Geonosis]]' [[moon]]s. They had large, axe-shaped jaws, and were resistant to [[blaster]] bolts.

==Biology and appearance==
Bonzami were massive, quadrupedal creatures that thrived in frigid [[temperature]]s. They had plated spines, razor-sharp [[Tooth|teeth]], and armored heads with horned [[mouth]]s. Their bodies were mostly covered in [[Color|white]] [[Hair|fur]], which allowed them to blend in easily with their gelid environment in spite of their large size.<ref name="DB Bonza" /> Their hide was resistant to blaster bolts, which made them difficult to kill, although they could be driven away by a high enough rate of fire.<ref name="The Honorable Ones" />

==Behavior==
Bonzami were predators that [[Hunting|hunted]] both alone and in packs.<ref name="DB Bonza" />

==History==
[[File:Bonzami.png|thumb|right|250px|A bonzami on Bahryn]]
Bonzami could be found on [[Bahryn]], one<ref name="The Honorable Ones" /> of fifteen [[moon]]s<ref name="AEYNK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> that [[orbit]]ed the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Geonosis]].<ref name="The Honorable Ones" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="The Honorable Ones date">{{RebelsDate|217}}</ref> [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]], a member of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]], and [[Garazeb Orrelios]], an anti-Empire freedom fighter, found themselves trapped together in a cavern on Bahryn after the [[escape pod]] they were in crash-landed on the moon. While trying to overcome their enmity and find a way out, the two were attacked by a bonzami, which they successfully fended off using their [[bo-rifle]]s. Later, two bonzami emerged, but the pair managed to narrowly escape through the hole punched through the cave's ceiling.<ref name="The Honorable Ones" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bonzami first appeared in "[[The Honorable Ones]]," the fifteenth episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. The design of their horn and beak was inspired by that of a truck-mounted snowplow, while their name derives from "Zamboni," a brand of machines used to clean and smooth the surface of ice rinks.<ref name="Honorable Trivia">{{SW|url=the-honorable-ones-trivia-gallery|text="The Honorable Ones" Trivia Gallery}}</ref>
