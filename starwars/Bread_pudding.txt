'''Bread pudding''' was a [[foodstuff]] made by the [[Opaline Creed]], a [[Religion|religious]] faction on the<ref name="TC" /> [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Outer Rim">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'' establishes that [[Crucival]] is located within the [[Tion Hegemony]], a sector that ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'' places in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].</ref> [[planet]] [[Crucival]], made on their holy [[Standard day|days]] during the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Edible to [[human]]s, the pudding featured sweet [[fruit]]s and a charred crust which [[Pira Ten]]&mdash;a member of the Creed&mdash;adored, despite the fact that it made her itch the next morning.<ref name="TC" />

On the eve of the [[Hieroprince's Ascension]], an event marked by the Creed, [[Hazram Namir]], another Creed member who used the name [[Umu]] Seven, shared his bowl of [[bread]] pudding with Ten after she was punished by the Creed.<ref name="TC" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'' states that the [[Battle of the Tower]] takes place at least two [[Standard month|months]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]], which corresponds to [[1 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, the battle in 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] if it was fought at least two months beforehand.</ref> some [[time]] after the Creed's demise, Namir and Ten, in the middle of a [[Battle of the Tower|battlefield]], talked about the pudding shortly before the latter [[Death|died]] of injuries she had received during the battle.<ref name="TC" />
