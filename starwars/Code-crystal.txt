A '''code-crystal''' was a component used in [[fractal radio]]s<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" /> which allowed for highly-encrypted communications.<ref name="Databank fractal radio">{{Databank|fractal-radio|fractal radio}}</ref> Code-crystals could be made to only fit specific transmitters.<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" />

In [[3 BBY]], [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] gave the [[Ghorman Front]] a new code-crystal that was made one-of-a-kind to only fit their radio which would allow the group to stay in communications with [[Luthen Rael]]'s [[Axis network|rebel network]].<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" />

==Behind the scenes==
A code-crytal appeared in "[[I Have Friends Everywhere]]", the fifth episode of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', which was released<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" /> on [[April 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref>
