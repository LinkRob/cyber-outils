[[Duke]] '''Chanward''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Noble|aristocrat]] and a member of the [[Noble Court]] of the [[Anoat sector]].<ref name="DD" /> During the [[Iron Blockade]], in which the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] locked the [[sector]] down so that its occupants could not learn about [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] [[death]] during the Empire's crushing defeat at the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> [[Chanward's personal bodyguard]] was [[assassin]]ated, and suspicion fell on the duke's own staff.<ref name="DD" /> On a separate occasion during the [[blockade]], Chanward designed a bold plan to capture an [[Imperial communications station]] and send a message to the public.<ref name="IW">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Run: ''Information Warfare''</ref>

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
The individual known as [[the Smuggler]], an ally of the Noble Court, provided one of their crew to Chanward as a replacement [[bodyguard]] after his was killed.<ref name="DD" /> The [[smuggler]]'s crew also helped to make the duke's plan to capture the communications station a success.<ref name="IW" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Chanward was created for the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', which was developed by [[Kabam]]<ref name="Uprising" /> and shut down in [[2016]].<ref name="ENDSWU">{{Kabam|subdomain=community|url=forums/showthread.php?675751-Star-Wars-Uprising-is-Shutting-Down|text=Discussion Thread: Star Wars Uprising is Shutting Down|date=2016-09-22}}</ref> The character was mentioned in two optional Crew Run missions. As this article assumes 100% game completion it treats both missions as having been successfully completed.<ref name="Uprising" />
