'''Dagonet''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[senator]] in the [[Galactic Republic]].

==History==
A [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[senator]] from [[Wayyl]],<ref name="CGttSWG" /> Dagonet served the [[Galactic Senate]], representing his [[homeworld]],<ref name="Justice" /> [[Wayyl]].<ref name="CGttSWG" /> Despite his longstanding policies and neglectful rule, his people couldn't vote for another senator.<ref name="Justice" />

At some point, Senator Dagonet's [[Uriel Dagonet|son]] was kidnapped on their [[homeworld]],<ref name="Justice" /> Wayyl,<ref name="CGttSWG" /> and two [[Jedi]], [[Dooku]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], went to the [[planet]] to rescue the teenager. There, they discovered that the kidnappers had organized themselves in a method to protest against Dagonet's neglectful rule, something that his son had come to agree with.<ref name="Justice" />

Dagonet and his [[Dagonet's soldiers|forces]] arrived shortly after the Jedi and was ruthless with the civilians, demanding that they be punished. As Dagonet continued to threaten the kidnappers, [[Dooku]] decided to take matters in his hands and used [[the Force]] to [[Force choke|choke]] the arrogant [[senator]]. Alarmed, Jinn attempted to stop him, but Dooku threw him aside with a [[Force push]]. Dooku nearly [[Death|killed]] Dagonet, but Jinn and the belligerent senator's [[Gender|son]] were able to save him. Before Dagonet and his son left in a [[speeder]] driven by his forces, Dagonet's son promised to help the civilians.<ref name="Justice" />

==Personality and traits==
Despite officially representing his homeworld, Dagonet spent most of his [[time]] on the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[capital]] planet [[Coruscant]]. He was completely oblivious to the treatment of his people and did not care that they were mistreated and barely able to survive. To show his position of power and instill fear, he willingly opened fire on his rebellious people, in hopes that they could be made examples for others to obey his command. Dagonet did care deeply for his son Uriel, ordering his troops to ceasefire, when Uriel ran between his soldiers and the civilians that the Jedi were protecting.<ref name="Justice" />

==Equipment==
Dagonet wore a white uniform and a similar [[helmet]] to his soldiers. He also wielded a [[blaster pistol]].<ref name="Justice" />
