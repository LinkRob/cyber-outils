If these squishy little orbs ain't enough to win back her favor, I guess I'll just munch on 'em myself. '''Blutopia pearls''', also written as '''Blutopia Pearls''', were squishy [[Color|brown]] orbs from [[Blutopia]], the [[water]]y [[homeworld]] of [[Blutopian]]s. They were a favorite [[Foodstuff|treat]] of the Blutopian [[Oga Garra]] and the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] picked some up for her on Blutopia during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he made between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]], hoping to earn favor.

He also found the pearls used as an ingredient on the [[planet]] [[Giju]] in a variant of the beverage [[Giju Glug]] and on the planet [[Ifron]], where they were used in [[Thuris Caf]]. He then included recipes for the pearls and both beverages in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]''.

==Description==

I found a few different variations, but my favorite version included some Blutopia Pearls at the bottom for a bit of extra flavor and flare.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
Blutopia pearls, also written as Blutopia Pearls, were small and squishy [[Color|brown]] orbs from [[Blutopia]], the [[water]]y [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> [[homeworld]] of the [[Blutopian]] [[species]].<ref name="Travelers">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> Blutopians could eat the [[Diet|vegetarian]] pearls, as could members of the [[Artiodac]] species.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==History==
{{Quote|Much to my surprise, a Thuris Caf is actually a blend of flavors from multiple worlds&mdash;like an Ithor Float with Blutopia Pearls and caf-infused ice cubes, all merged in milk made from koja nuts.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook Ebook">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''{{C|Ebook version only}}</ref>}}
[[File:Oga_Garra_databank.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Blutopia pearls were a favorite treat of Oga Garra.]]
The pearls were a favorite [[Foodstuff|treat]] of [[Oga Garra]], a Blutopian who ran [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]] and held power over the rest of the outpost. After Garra became upset with the Artiodac [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] for publishing the recipe for the [[blurrgfire]] beverage sold at her [[cantina]] in his first [[Book|cookbook]],<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'',<ref name="Blackspire">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="CookDate">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs decided to try and win back her favor by bringing her Blutopia pearls and picked some up on Blutopia during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateBSOCookbookDate}}</ref>

During the tour, he also encountered Blutopia pearls on other worlds such as [[Ifron]], an Outer Rim planet where it was used as part of a blend of flavors from multiple worlds alongside [[koja nut]] [[milk]], [[caf]]-infused ice cubes, and an [[Ithor Float]] in the [[Thuris Caf]] served at the [[Bovris Tapcafe]]. He also found it used on the [[Colonies]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> planet<ref name="Weapon">''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]''</ref> [[Giju]], where a layer of the pearls was used as an ingredient in a variant of the beverage [[Giju Glug]] that included them for extra flavor and flare. Tuggs decided that this variant was his favorite version of the drink.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

In 35 ABY,<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateBSOCookbookDate}}</ref> he published the recipe for the pearls and both beverages in his third cookbook, ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]''. In the recipe for the pearls, he mused that if Garra was not pleased with the treats then he would eat them himself, although he would have to do so somewhere other than Batuu.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blutopia pearls were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe is for a [[Wikipedia:Bubble tea|boba tea]] and makes use of [[Wikipedia:Tapioca pearl|boba pearls]] and [[Wikipedia:Water|water]] to represent Blutopia pearls and then more water, [[Wikipedia:Brown sugar|brown sugar]], and [[Wikipedia:Vanilla extract|vanilla extract]] for the [[Wikipedia:Syrup|syrup]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
