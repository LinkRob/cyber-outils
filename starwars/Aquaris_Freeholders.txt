The '''Aquaris Freeholders''' were a group who were involved with the [[smuggler]]s [[Chewbacca]] and [[Han Solo]] on a job delivering [[spice]]. [[Silver Fyre]] was affiliated with them. During the job, the Aquaris Freeholders hijacked their spice cargo, which earned them Solo's ire. When Solo was planning a new job to pay off a debt he owed [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]], Chewbacca mentioned Silver Fyre and the Aquaris Freeholders as possible clients, which Solo dismissed. Solo recorded the information about the Aquaris Freeholders in the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

In between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]]<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref>, [[Strono Tuggs]]'s [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|food freighter]] was boarded by the Aquaris Freeholders who were looking for [[spice]]. When they realized he didn't have anything except for food onboard, they demanded he make them a gourmet meal.<ref name="UC">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref>
