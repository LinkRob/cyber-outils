'''Dwindun''' was a [[Ma'cella]] [[Gender|male]] who was a member of [[Blue (Devaronian)|Blue's]] [[Blue's partisans|rebel team]] alongside his brother. Blue and her group with [[Jyn Erso]], who was an undercover agent of the Empire, brought [[foodstuff]]s to the [[planet]] [[Watassay]]. After they arrived the planet, they were captured and arrested by [[Admiral]] [[Bethava Rocwyn]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
