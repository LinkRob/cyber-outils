The '''Atravis system''' was located in the [[Atravis sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="WesternReaches" /> It was also situated in the grid square L-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="TFABG" /> The [[super-hyperroute]]<ref name="BMF 48">{{BuildFalconCite|48|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> known as the [[Hydian Way]] connected the Atravis [[Star system|system]] to the [[Rutan system|Rutan]] and [[Shumavar system]]s.<ref name="TFABG" />

The Atravis system fell within the borders of the [[New Republic]]<ref name="MandoVG" /> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="MandoVGDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Starcruiserdate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> several [[Viewscreen|screens]] aboard [[Chandrila Star Line]]'s [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' displayed a partial [[Star map|map]] of [[the galaxy]] that indicated the Atravis system's position.<ref name="GS">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Atravis system was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[Galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' boxed set.<ref name="TFABG" /> The system's first [[in-universe]] appearance came in the form of being depicted on a map viewable in [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]], an immersive two-day roleplaying experience at [[W:c:disney:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]] in Florida<ref name="GS" /> that opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]]<ref name="31 Things">{{SW|url=news/31-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=31 Things We Learned in an Exclusive Preview of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser|nolive=1}}</ref> and was launched for the final time on [[September 28]], [[2023]].<ref name="GalacticStarcruiserClosure">{{WebCite|url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/travel/2023/05/18/star-wars-hotel-closing|text=Disney to close its $5,000 Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser hotel|author=Hannah Sampson|date=2023-05-18|work=[[Wikipedia:The Washington Post|''The Washington Post'']]|archivedate=20230519064024}}</ref> The system was eventually identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The [[Atravis system/Legends|Atravis system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> The [[Star system/Legends|system]] subsequently made its first appearance in ''[[The Clone Wars: Act on Instinct]]'', a [[Star Wars comic books|webcomic]] authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on StarWars.com<ref name="AoI">''[[The Clone Wars: Act on Instinct]]''</ref> over the span of the 2009<ref name="AoI announcement">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/news20091001comics/index.html|text=New ''Clone Wars'' Web Comic Tells Original Tale}}</ref>&ndash;[[2010]]<ref name="Lethal Trackdown Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode222.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Lethal Trackdown}}</ref> [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series.<ref name="AoI announcement" />
