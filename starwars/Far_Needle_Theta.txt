'''Far Needle Theta''' was the [[callsign]] attributed to a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[starfighter]] [[pilot]]. While flying near the jumping-off point to the [[Hyperspace route|hyperlane]] [[Terrabe Bypass]] to [[Jedha]], the pilot spotted an [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|unidentified construct]] defended by an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[task force]] of [[capital ship]]s and [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] pickets. Far Needle Theta broke off contact to avoid capture and loss of intelligence data.<ref name="Rebel Dossier" />
