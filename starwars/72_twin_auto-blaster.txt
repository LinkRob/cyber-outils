The '''72 twin auto-blaster''' was an [[auto-blaster]] manufactured by [[Gyrhil]]. [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing]] [[starfighter]]s had a single 72 twin auto-blaster under the cockpit,<ref name="SWCT B-wing" /> with some carrying a second pair in the optional mount next to it. The auto-blaster was capable of automatically firing on enemy targets, but many pilots chose to turn off this capability and directly control the weapon themselves.<ref name="Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual">''[[Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>
