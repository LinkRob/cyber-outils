Following the [[Battle of Mon Cala]] during the [[Clone Wars]], a '''coronation''' was held for [[Lee-Char]] as he was [[crown]]ed [[King of Mon Cala|king]] of the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]]. During the ceremony, Lee-Char approached the center of the room, and bowed, before [[Lemcke]] opened a [[shell]] and placed a crown upon Lee-Char's head. Lemcke then announced the new king, and all the attendees chanted his name.<ref name="Prisoners" /> Following the coronation, the King would remain a strong and determined leader throughout the remainder of the [[Clone Wars]] and into the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Darth Vader (2017) 14" />.
