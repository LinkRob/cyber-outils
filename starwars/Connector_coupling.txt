'''Connector coupling''' was a type of mechanical component that could be utilized to repair certain devices. [[Synara San]], a [[Warbird gang]] [[spy]], brought some connector couplets to [[Jarek Yeager's Repair Station]] in a ruse to investigate the ''[[Colossus]]''{{'}} [[targeting computer]] that [[Kazuda Xiono]], [[Tamara Ryvora]], and [[Neeku Vozo]] had been repairing. Xiono, unaware of San's deception, nervously accepted the couplets in an attempt to usher her out of the repair shop quicker.<ref name="SynaraScore">{{Resistance|Synara's Score}}</ref>
