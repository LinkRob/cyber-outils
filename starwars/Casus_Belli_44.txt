'''''Casus Belli'' 44''' was a French roleplaying magazine published in March [[1988]]. It contained many roleplaying articles, including two adventures for the [[West End Games]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|D6]] system. The articles were called "[[Star Wars: Le jeu de rôle de la Guerre des Étoiles]]", written by [[Christophe Réaux|CROC]] with [[Annabella Belli]] and "[[L'écume de l'espace]]," written by [[Jean Balczesak]].
