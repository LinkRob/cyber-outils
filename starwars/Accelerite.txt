'''Accelerite''' was a type of unstable [[starship]] [[fuel]] ingredient used in [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]]'s [["Firecaller" light flame projector]]. It allowed the weapon to spray less fuel than most other [[Flamethrower|flame projectors]] per burst.<ref name="Gadgets">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Where am I gonna get 10,000 credits? I-I'd have to&mdash;I don't know&mdash;Steal all the accellerite in Corellia Tower.|Han|<ref name="Chase">{{All-Stars|The Chase with Han}}</ref>}}
Accelerite was mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Accellerite/Legends|accellerite]] was first mentioned in the [[2002]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan: The Aurorient Express 2|''Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan: The Aurorient Express'' 2]].<ref name="Aurorient Express 2">[[Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan: The Aurorient Express 2|''Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan: The Aurorient Express'' 2]]</ref>

Accelerite was mentioned in the non-[[canon]] series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: All-Stars]]''. In the series, the young [[Corellian]] [[Han Solo|Han]] attempted to [[Thief|steal]] accellerite from an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] hold in [[Corellia Tower]].<ref name="Chase" /> The episode used the ''Star Wars Legends'' spelling of the chemical in subtitles.<ref name="ChaseSub">{{All-Stars|The Chase with Han}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref>
