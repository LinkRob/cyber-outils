'''Barkhesh''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Naboo">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Heroes of Naboo and Other Characters of the Galaxy}}</ref> located in the [[Barkhesh system]] of the [[Seitia sector]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western" /> It was located in grid square K-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Barkhesh was the [[homeworld]] of the [[human]] [[Antidar Williams]], a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Judicial]] who [[Death|perished]] in the [[Naboo system]] in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Naboo">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Heroes of Naboo and Other Characters of the Galaxy}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Barkhesh was mentioned in "[[The Heroes of Naboo and Other Characters of the Galaxy]]," part of [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series<ref name="Naboo" /> which was published on [[August 19]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Encyclopédie Star Wars}}</ref> [[Barkhesh/Legends|Barkhesh]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in an installment of "[[Scouts' Dispatch]]," a series of ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' articles written by [[Peter Woodworth]] and published by [[West End Games]] in the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]''. The installment was included in the [[May]] [[1995]] [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 6|sixth issue]] of the magazine.<ref name="Scouts">{{JournalCite|6|Scouts' Dispatch}}</ref> The [[Planet/Legends|planet]] then first appeared in the [[1998]] [[Factor 5]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], [[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (video game)|''Star Wars: Rogue Squadron'']].<ref name="RogueSquadron">[[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (video game)|''Star Wars: Rogue Squadron'']]</ref>
