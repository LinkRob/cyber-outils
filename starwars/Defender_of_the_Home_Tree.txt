'''Defender of the Home Tree''' was a title the [[Wookiee]]s of [[Kashyyyk]] bestowed upon [[Yoda]], [[Grand Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]], for assisting them in many tense negotiations with their [[Kashyyyk system|system]] neighbors, the [[Trandoshan]]s.<ref name="CL" /> Yoda also earned the honor for helping the Wookiees [[Defense of Kashyyyk|defend Kashyyyk]] from [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] raiders in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
