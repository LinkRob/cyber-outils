The '''Black Spire Outpost Obelisk''' was a stone obelisk located in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. It was carved by local artisans with patterns matching those found on the outpost's original buildings<ref name="Traveler's Guide"/> from before the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Lys" /> By the [[time]] of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], it had become tradition amongst visitors and locals of the outpost to touch the obelisk and say "Til the Spire" for good luck and in hopes of safely returning to the outpost in future.<ref name="GEStaff">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] has offical backstory elements that, though not directly published for the public, are officially canon details that can be obtained verbally from cast members working in the land.</ref>
