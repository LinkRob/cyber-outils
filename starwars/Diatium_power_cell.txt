The '''diatium power cell''',<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> also known as a '''diatium power core''',<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> was a power source. They were suitable to be used as the primary power source in [[lightsaber]]s. The [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's first lightsaber|first lightsaber]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] wielded as a [[Jedi]] used one, which was surrounded by the notched hand grip. [[Darth Vader's lightsaber]] used a high-output diatium power cell.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader's]] grandson, [[Ben Solo]], also used a [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|lightsaber]] with a bronze-[[color]]ed<ref name="TFA VD" /> diatium power cell cradled in its handle during his [[Jedi training]] with [[Luke Skywalker]]'s reborn [[Jedi Order]] and after his fall to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] as Kylo Ren. Other than lightsabers, power cells can be used in other weaponry, such as [[Jannah's energy bow]].<ref name="RoS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

If modified correctly, power cells could be overcharged to enhance the power of the blade. A hazardous and dangerous undertaking, modifying a diatium power cell risked unbalancing a near-perfect system. Unpredictable when overcharged, the lightsaber blade produced was prone to fluctuations in strength.<ref name="EV" />
