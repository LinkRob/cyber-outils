"'''An Imperial Advertorial'''" was a mock advertisement for the [[Death Star Owner's Technical Manual]], published in [[Star Wars Insider 145]].

==Plot summary==
[[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] arrives at the [[Death Star/Legends|Death Star]] and is greeted by an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Commander/Legends|Commander]]. However, Vader informs the commander that the Rebel Alliance has [[Operation Skyhook|stolen]] the [[Death Star plans/Legends|Death Star plans]]. The commander then reminds Vader that he does not have the plans for the Death Star and as he doesn't know how to operate the station, he is powerless. Vader then gives the commander the book, citing that it contained a full list of the station's functions and the commander studies it, noticing a glaring vulnerability&ndash;in the form of a [[Thermal exhaust port/Legends|Thermal Exhaust Port]].
