'''Captivator''' was a [[bounty hunter]] [[assassin droid]] tasked with tracking down [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]]. Captivator was hired by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] to hunt down Solo. [[Gender|He]] worked with a [[human]], a [[Gran]], and a [[Kubaz]] bounty hunter. Later he joined with [[Alecia Beck]] and the [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] to find Solo.<ref name="Smuggler's Run" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Captivator_by_Seeley.jpg|left|thumb|Concept art of Captivator by Dave Seeley]]
Captivator first appeared in [[Greg Rucka]]'s junior novel ''[[Smuggler's Run: A Han Solo & Chewbacca Adventure]]'', which was released as part of the [[Journey to Star Wars: The Force Awakens|Journey to ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'']] book series.<ref name="Smuggler's Run" /> In early pieces of concept art drawn by [[Dave Seeley]], Captivator is shown to be wielding a [[Clone Wars]]-era [[Z-6 rotary blaster cannon]] and an Imperial [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]].
