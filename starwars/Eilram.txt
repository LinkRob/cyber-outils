'''Eilram''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Zeffonian]], and one of three of their species who were revered as great [[Zeffo Sage|sages]].

==Biography==
Eilram was a Sage revered in Zeffo culture for being of great wisdom and insight. A master of the [[The Force|Life Wind]], they held a place of high honor within a proving ground and academy on the planet [[Zeffo]]. They were the first Zeffo to establish a relationship with the [[Wookiee]]s of [[Kashyyyk]] and loved the [[planet]]'s [[plant]]s.

Sometime long before [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Eilram [[Death|died]] and was [[Burial|buried]] within the [[Tomb of Eilram|halls]] of the great school on Zeffo. The burial chamber was adorned with wind chimes, and its domed roof was carved in the image of a mighty [[wroshyr tree]]. Eilram's sarcophagus was interred under the [[stone]] roots of the tree, a symbol of their mighty love for Kashyyyk.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
