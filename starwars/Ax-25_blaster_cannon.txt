The '''Ax-25 blaster cannon''' was a model of [[blaster cannon]] produced by [[BlasTech Industries]]. The [[C-PH patrol speeder bike]]s produced by the [[Aratech Repulsor Company]] for the [[Galactic Empire]] were equipped with a single forward fixed Ax-25.<ref name="SoloOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ax-25 blaster cannon was created for the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> and was revealed earlier in the year in the first trailer released for it.<ref name="Teaser">{{SWYouTube|dNW0B0HsvVs|Solo: A Star Wars Story Official Teaser|sw_url=video/official-teaser-solo-a-star-wars-story}}</ref> The weapon received identification in the reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in conjunction with ''Solo''.<ref name="SoloOG" />
