The '''Coruscant Home Defense Fleet''',<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> also known as the '''Coruscant defense fleet''',<ref name="HC 8">{{HelmetCollectionCite|8|Highlights of the Saga}}</ref> was a [[fleet]] that constituted the [[Planetary defense forces|planetary defense force]] of [[Coruscant]] in the last [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Following the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]], it was integrated into the [[Republic Navy|naval forces]] of the new [[Republic Military]]. It was under the command of [[Republic officer]] [[Commander]] [[Honor Salima]] and participated in the [[Battle of Coruscant]].<ref name="On the Front Lines" />
