A '''capo''' was a title held by high ranking members of the [[Pyke Syndicate]] and [[Crimson Dawn]]. [[Pyke]] criminal [[Quay Tolsite]], who administered the Pyke Syndicate's operations on [[Kessel]], held this title.<ref name="Quay LEGO" /> Only the most trusted capos in Crimson Dawn wore a [[Dryden Vos' signet ring|signet]] [[ring]] as a symbol of their status.<ref name="TOG" />
