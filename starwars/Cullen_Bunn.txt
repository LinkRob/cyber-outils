'''Cullen Bunn''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] writer. He is the writer of the [[Marvel Comics]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Darth Maul (2017)|''Star Wars: Darth Maul'']]<ref name="Maul announcement" /> as well as the writer for the [[Star Wars Annual (2015) 4|''Star Wars Annual'' (2015) 4]] special.

Outside of ''[[Star Wars]]'', Bunn is also known for writing various ''X-Men'' titles, as well ''Agent Venom'' and ''Deadpool'' for Marvel, in addition to writing comics for Oni Press, [[Dark Horse Comics]], Image Comics, Dynamite Entertainment, Aftershock Comics, and Action Lab, namely known for ''Harrows County'' (Dark Horse) and ''The Damned'' and ''The Sixth Gun'' (Oni Press).
