{{Quote|Similarly, Education was not designed to carry out active teaching but instead to try and bring various schools, colleges, and universities across the galaxy into line with recommended Imperial curriculums and research topics.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
'''Education''' was a department of the [[Coalition for Progress]], a part of the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]] within the [[Galactic Empire]]. The department was tasked with ensuring that learning institutions such as schools, colleges, and [[University|universities]] aligned with the Empire's recommended teaching curriculums and research topics. The department had jurisdiction across [[the galaxy]] and its role proved a balancing act at [[time]]s due to academics' tendencies to undertake projects with unclear outcomes and disappear down tangents. The [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned the department in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Education was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" /> The [[Education/Legends|organization]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]'', a [[1989]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Greg Gorden]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="Imperial">''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]''</ref>
