"'''For Every Action: Preemptive Strike'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year five of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the first adventure in the [[For Every Action trilogy]] and was written by [[Rob Edwards]] and edited by [[Ronald A. Heintz|Ron Heintz]] and [[Margaret van Poelgeest-Heintz|Margaret Heintz]].

==Plot summary==
The crime lord [[Riboga]] has returned to the [[Cularin system/Legends|Cularin system]], and the heroes are sent to try and prevent him from taking over crime there. They counter his efforts on [[Dorumaa]] and [[Tolea Biqua]], before stealing treasure from his [[Riboga's asteroid base|asteroid base]].
