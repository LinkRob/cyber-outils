'''Coralya''' was a [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Prostitute/Legends|prostitute]] who operated out of [[Providence (lair)|Providence]] during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].

==Biography==
Born on [[Dantooine/Legends|Dantooine]], Coralya chose the adventurer's life at a young age, which forged her strong nature. During the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], she was chosen as a chief and representative by her colleagues at the [[Pirate/Legends|pirate]] lair of [[Providence (lair)|Providence]].

==Personality and traits==
Coralya had a strong will and sense of command, and was able to impose herself on the brutal and bawdy men of the station. She would always enforce her rights, which earned her respect but also garnered hatred by those who didn't receive her favors or challenged her.

Few would stand before her, and her authority was undisputed in the [[Arcades (Providence)|arcades]].

==Behind the scenes==
Coralya was created by [[Jean-Michel Ringuet]] for the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] aid "[[Providence (Avalon)|Providence]]," which appeared in the French magazine ''[[Avalon 1]]''. She was illustrated by [[Jean-Marc Emy]].
