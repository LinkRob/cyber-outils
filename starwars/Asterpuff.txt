'''Asterpuff''' was a shimmering [[Color|gold-colored]] [[lichen]] from the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]] that caused [[hallucination]]s when ingested. In [[382 BBY]], asterpuff was sold on E'ronoh, and [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[The Force|Force]] [[religion]] member [[Binnot Ullo]] and [[terrorist]] [[Rev Ferrol]] ingested it.

==Description==
{{Quote|Suit yourself. Have to make this rock tolerable until we can clean up your mess.|Binnot Ullo explains why he uses asterpuff after Abda refuses to|<ref name="Convergence" />}}
Asterpuff was [[Color|gold-colored]] [[lichen]] that reflected light, making it look like it was shimmering. It was [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]], where it grew in canyons. When inhaled or otherwise ingested, it had [[Hallucination|hallucinogenic]] and soothing psychological effects.<ref name="Convergence" />

==History==
{{Quote|Is it like the happy lichen on E'ronoh?|Axel Greylark asks Phan-tu Zenn about an Eirami lichen|<ref name="Convergence" />}}
[[File:Eronoh-THRShow.png|thumb|right|250px|Asterpuff grew on E'ronoh's cliffs.]]
In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> while the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[The Force|Force]] [[religion]] member [[Serrena]] walked in a [[The Rook market|market]] in [[the Rook]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the planet E'ronoh, she heard a haggler call out that asterpuff was available at a bargain price, advertising that the substance would allow one to "[[dream]] the dream of the [[Death|dead]]." Later, the Path member [[Binnot Ullo]] used the lichen, inhaling a pinch and offering it to fellow Path [[Children of the Open Hand|member]] [[Abda]], who declined. Ullo explained that he needed some way to keep their [[time]] on E'ronoh tolerable as his [[eye]]s dilated from the substance.<ref name="Convergence" />

Sometime after, [[Prince]] [[Phan-tu Zenn]] of E'ronoh's [[Double planet|twin planet]] [[Eiram]] and [[Axel Greylark]], [[Greylark family|son]] of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Kyong Greylark]], noticed the lichen while on the [[sail barge]] ''[[Amaryliss]]'' [[Aid mission on E'ronoh|going]] through E'ronoh's canyons. Later, on Eiram, Zenn mentioned that the planet had a lichen that disrupted communications, causing Greylark to ask if it was like the "happy lichen" on E'ronoh, referring to asterpuff. Sometime after, when the [[E'roni]] [[Children of E'ronoh|terrorist]] [[Rev Ferrol]] was preparing to try to destroy the [[E'ronoh royal family]], he sniffed asterpuff.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
Asterpuff appeared in the novel [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" /> The novel was published on [[November 22]], [[2022]]. Prior to its release, the mention of asterpuff was included in a [[StarWars.com]] preview for the novel on [[October 4]] of that year.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-convergence-excerpt|text=A Mysterious Mission Begins in ''The High Republic: Convergence'' &ndash; Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
