A '''cooling unit''',<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|jira|Jira}}</ref> or '''coolth unit''',<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> was a piece of technology that cooled items.<ref name="Databank" />

The [[human]] [[Gender|woman]] [[Jira]] expressed her need for a cooling unit to the young [[Slavery|slave]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], who searched for the unit and gave it to Jira.<ref name="Databank" />  metallic coolth unit was installed in the wall of the [[Lars homestead|Lars moisture farm]].<ref name="SWCL" /> The [[Z-6 rotary blaster cannon]] used a cooling unit.<ref name="Blasters and Firearms">{{EncyclopediaCite|Blasters and Ranged Weapons}}</ref> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> while surveying the damage to the ''[[Trailblazer]]'' after its crash landing on [[Toshara]] the [[Rodian]] [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Waka]] noted that the [[starship]]'s cooling unit had been punctured.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
