'''Byno Doubton''' was a fervent loyalist to the [[Galactic Republic]] who, with the patronage of fellow [[Eriadu]] native [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], helped found the [[Commission for the Protection of the Republic]] (COMPOR). During the [[Clone Wars]], he produced at least two [[propaganda]] pieces for the Republic's unity communications campaign, the first of which was a [[Painting|poster]] titled "[[Jedi Unite for Peace]]" that never saw distribution due to the suggestion by [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] that the [[Jedi Order]] not be used in propaganda due to the [[Jedi]]'s discomfort at taking on the role of [[Republic Military|military]] [[Jedi General|leaders]] in the war.<ref name="Propaganda" />

His second piece was entitled "[[Unite]]," and as part of a collaboration between COMPOR and the [[Republic War Office]], replaced the Jedi with the [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] in order to bolster moral. Following the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|reformation]] into the [[Galactic Empire]] at the end of the war, Doubton became an instructor in the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]]'s [[Sub-Adult Group]] and eventually retired to his [[Doubton family estate|family estate]] back on Eriadu.<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
Byno Doubton was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on [[October 25]], [[2016]].
