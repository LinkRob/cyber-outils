{{Quote|I was alone and weak after the tragic events on Mandalore, and I came here to you, my old ally, seeking safe haven. But, you tried to kill me. You failed.|Maul, speaking to Marg Krim's corpse|<ref name="Chapter 4" />}}
Around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date note" /> Imperator [[Marg Krim]] of the [[Pyke Syndicate]] '''attempted to assassinate''' [[Lord]] [[Maul]], the [[dark side adept]] who controlled the [[Shadow Collective]] criminal alliance.<ref name="Chapter 4" /> Having grown upset with Maul's leadership over his operations,<ref name="Together Again">{{TCW|Together Again}}</ref> Krim took the opportunity to act when Maul ventured to his [[Pyke Palace|palace]] on [[Oba Diah]] in the aftermath of the [[Siege of Mandalore]],<ref name="Chapter 4" /> which had seen the collapse of the collective.<ref name="Much to Learn">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars: Much to Learn|text=Pyke Syndicate|video=KDGOFRHM2f4|nolive=1}}</ref> Maul, however, managed to escape and was left enraged at Krim's betrayal. As he worked to rebuild the collective<ref name="Chapter 4" /> between [[17 BBY]] and around [[13 BBY]],<ref name="ShadowLordDate">{{ShadowLordDate}}</ref> Maul [[Assassination of Marg Krim|returned to Oba Diah]] to enact his revenge upon Krim, whom he personally executed before installing [[Captain of the Guard]] [[Kalt]] as his new [[Pyke]] puppet leader.<ref name="Chapter 4" />
