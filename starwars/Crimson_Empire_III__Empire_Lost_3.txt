'''''Crimson Empire III: Empire Lost'' 3''' is the third issue of the ''[[Star Wars: Crimson Empire III: Empire Lost]]'' series of comics, and was released on [[December 28]], [[2011]] by [[Dark Horse Comics]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Kir Kanos strikes back at the Empire! Kidnapped and dragooned into a hard-liner Imperial plot, Kir Kanos, the last surviving member of Emperor Palpatine's Royal Guard, has had enough! He goes to war with one Imperial faction, even as Leia Organa Solo receives an invitation to meet with emissaries from another.''

''Treachery layered on treachery is revealed and Kanos answers it the only way he knows how - with violence!''

==Plot summary==
{{Plot|comic}}

==Continuity==
The shuttle used by Kir Kanos is a [[Delta-class JV-7 escort shuttle|''Delta''-class JV-7 escort shuttle]] at the end of this issue, but is a [[Nu-class attack shuttle/Legends|''Nu''-class attack shuttle]] in [[Crimson Empire III: Empire Lost 4|''Crimson Empire III: Empire Lost'' 4]].
