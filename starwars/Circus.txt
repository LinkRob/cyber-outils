It's a nice life: I get my name on the posters, in bigger font than anyone else's (I insisted on that), and kids love my act.|[[Lugubrious Mote]]|<ref name="The Legends of Luke Skywalker">''[[The Legends of Luke Skywalker]]''</ref>}}
A '''circus''' was a traveling company of entertainers that performed large public shows featuring clowns, acrobats, and trained animals. [[Preigo's Traveling World of Wonder]], led by a [[Dug]] named [[Preigo]], was one such company.<ref name="bound">{{TCW|Bound for Rescue}}</ref> When she was still a young [[princess]] in the making on [[Alderaan]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] wished she could run away to join a [[The galaxy|galactic]] circus rather than attending her etiquette lessons.<ref name="PSFB">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref>
