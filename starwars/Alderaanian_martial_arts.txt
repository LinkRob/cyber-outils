'''Alderaanian martial arts''' were a form of [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]] techniques from [[Alderaan]],<ref name="SWA 22">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=22|story=A Matter Of Perception}}</ref> a [[planet]] otherwise reputed for the pacifism of its [[Alderaanian|inhabitants]].<ref name="Leia 2">[[Princess Leia 2|''Princess Leia'' 2]]</ref> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of Alderaan was proficient in such martial arts. She notably put it to good use during a [[Mission to Nothoiin|mission]] to [[Nothoiin]], when she grabbed an [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]] from a [[stormtrooper]]'s hands and promptly used it to knock him out.<ref name="SWA 22" />
