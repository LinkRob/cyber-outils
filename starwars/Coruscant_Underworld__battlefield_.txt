'''Coruscant Underworld''' was a battlefield within the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. The design and structure of the battlefield were based on the [[skyscraper]]s and [[Coruscant Underworld|underbelly]] of the planet [[Coruscant]]. In [[9 ABY]], Arena co-owner [[Balada]] commissioned for the construction of the Coruscant Underworld battlefield to ensure that the [[Kubaz]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Glikkin]]&mdash;who was a newcomer at the [[time]]&mdash;felt right at home.

==Description==
The Coruscant Underworld was a battlefield built for the competitive sporting show [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] within the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="SWH" /> The battlefield was covered by bright advertising signs and [[color]]ful [[graffiti]] throughout as it was based on the [[Coruscant Underworld|underbelly]] of the planet [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Explore">{{Hunters|url=news/explore-the-coruscant-underworld|text=Explore the Coruscant Underworld!}}</ref> It also emulated the great heights<ref name="SWH" /> of Coruscant's [[skyscraper]]s.<ref name="Coruscant Underworld">{{HuntersCite|quest=Coruscant Underworld}}</ref> One interior section of the battlefield was designed around a [[cantina]] with advertisements for [[sabacc]] and [[Dex's Diner]]. The center platform of Coruscant Underworld was a recreation of an active crime scene&mdash;specifically the aftermath of a cargo heist&mdash;with two [[LAAT/le patrol gunship]]s hovering above and beaming their lights at it.<ref name="Explore" /> Additionally, various interconnected walkways and alleys were scattered throughout the battlefield. Unlike other battlefields, Coruscant Underworld was visibly darker as the skies of Vespaara were nowhere to be seen. However, lines of [[airspeeder]] traffics could be seen up above and around the battlefield. Audience who attended live matches were able to view them from a towering podium at the side of the battlefield.<ref name="SWH" />

==History==
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SWH Date" /> the Coruscant Underworld battlefield was commissioned by the Arena co-owner [[Balada]] the [[Hutt]] for the Hunters of the Outer Rim. It was designed specifically to ensure that the<ref name="Explore" /> [[Kubaz]]<ref name="SWH" /> [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Glikkin]]&mdash;who was the newest addition to the show's roster at the [[time]]&mdash;to feel right at home.<ref name="Explore" /> Later, following Glikkin's introduction to the Grand Arena, the battlefield was fully operational and in use for the competitive show.<ref name="SWH" />

Here's an overview of everything to come... #StarWarsHunters|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/ckELA|archivefile=File:ZyngaStarWars-Season5ReleaseDate-Twitter.png}}</ref> During the development of Coruscant Underworld, the map was initially named Locklands.
