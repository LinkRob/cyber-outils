An '''accelerator''' was a component in [[starship]]s, [[speeder]]s, and [[speeder bike]]s that allowed them to accelerate. They could be found in [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s and the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', both of which were utilized on separate occasions by the [[pilot]] [[Han Solo]]. They were also utilized for repairs in [[Jarek Yeager's Repair Station]] on the [[supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]''.

==Description==
{{Quote|Okay. It's like popping a regulator on a speeder. Maybe? Pump the accelerator, juice the motivator and then&hellip;|Han Solo|<ref name="Cadet2" />}}
An accelerator was a component of a [[starship]],<ref name="HS3" /> [[speeder]],<ref name="Cadet2" /> or [[speeder bike]] that allowed it to accelerate.<ref name="DozaDilemma" /> Accelerators could be found in [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s,<ref name="Cadet2">[[Han Solo - Imperial Cadet 2|''Han Solo - Imperial Cadet'' 2]]</ref> and they were also equipped on the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="HS3" />

==History==
{{Quote|Chewie, prime the accelerators. Hurry.''"<br />"''Arrgh.''"<br />"''Don't argue, furball. I know what I'm doing.|Han Solo and Chewbacca|<ref name="HS3" />}}
[[File:FalconUsingAccelerator-HanSolo3.png|thumb|right|250px|Han Solo and Chewbacca used the ''Millennium Falcon's'' accelerators during the Dragon Void Run.]]
In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Cadet2Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Han Solo - Imperial Cadet'' 2 to [[13 ABY]].</ref> [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] [[cadet]] [[Han Solo]] pumped his TIE/ln space superiority starfighter's accelerator during a traning exercise in order to prevent himself from crashing, surmising it would have the same outcome as doing the same thing in a [[speeder]]. This act was ultimately futile, though he was still saved by fellow cadet [[Kanina Nico]].<ref name="Cadet2" /> Solo would again use accelerators<ref name="HS3" /> in [[0 ABY]]<ref name="Solo3Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Han Solo'' 3 to [[0 ABY]].</ref> during the [[Dragon Void Run]] race. After briefly being apprehended by the Galactic Empire, Solo and the other [[pilot]]s were released and began the race once again. Though he briefly pondered leaving the race and completing his mission to rescue undercover [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[Spy|spies]], Solo instead decided to continue the race and ordered his partner, [[Chewbacca]], to prime the ''Millennium Falcon's'' accelerators.<ref name="HS3" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="DozaDate">{{ResistanceDate|1|14}}</ref> the [[Technician|mechanics]] [[Tamara Ryvora]], [[Neeku Vozo]], and [[Kazuda Xiono]] repaired a speeder bike with an accelerator in [[Jarek Yeager's Repair Station]] on the [[supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]'', which was itself present on<ref name="DozaDilemma" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Castilon]]. Vozo concluded that the accelerator's installation concluded the trio's work, though he commented that it could be upended should the accelerator explode.<ref name="DozaDilemma" />

==Behind the scenes==
Accelerators were first mentioned in the [[Han Solo 3|third issue]] of the [[2016]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo]]'', which was published on [[August 31]], 2016.<ref name="PREVIEWSworld">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/52698/han_solo_2016_3|text=Han Solo (2016) #3}}</ref> In real life, an accelerator, also known as a gas pedal or [[Wikipedia:Throttle|throttle]], is a device used to increase or decrease power&mdash;and thus acceleration&mdash;for a vehicle.<ref name="DriversEd">{{WebCite|url=https://driversed.com/driving-information/the-vehicle/accelerator|text=Accelerator|archivedate=20230528215549}}</ref>
