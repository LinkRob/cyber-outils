The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''20 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3257 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7957''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The [[Battle of Sullust]] occurs. During the battle, [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s [[flagship]] the ''[[Resolute]]'' is destroyed. Also, [[Count]] [[Dooku]], on orders from his master [[Darth Sidious]], betrays his [[assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]], having [[TJ-912]]'s [[TJ-912's Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class light destroyer]] fire on the [[Unidentified Providence-class carrier/destroyer (Battle of Sullust)|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] she is on. Ventress is presumed dead by Dooku, but she survives and attempts to exact revenge.<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref>
*[[Force wielder|The Ones]] cease to exist on the planet [[Mortis]]. [[Son|The Son]] stabs [[Daughter|the Daughter]] with the [[Dagger of Mortis]].<ref name="Altar of Mortis">{{TCW|Altar of Mortis (episode)}}</ref> [[Father (Mortis)|The Father]] then kills himself to allow Anakin Skywalker to kill the Son.<ref name="Ghosts of Mortis">{{TCW|Ghosts of Mortis}}</ref>
*The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] moves to [[Annexation of the Qiilura system|annex]] the [[Qiilura system]]<ref name="Mercy Mission">{{TCW|Mercy Mission (episode)}}</ref>
*During a [[Mission to Aleen|Republic mercy mission]], [[droid]]s [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] restore peace between the [[Aleena]] and their underground neighbors on the [[planet]] [[Aleen]] by re-sealing the [[Great Seal]]<ref name="Mercy Mission" />
*The [[Nightbrothers|Nightbrother]] [[Savage Opress]] [[Mission to Lotho Minor|locates]] his lost brother, the renegade [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]],<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref> who reenters the [[The galaxy|galactic]] stage in order to seize revenge on [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref>
*The eight hundredth and forty-seventh [[Festival of Light]] is celebrated on [[Naboo]]. The festival is interrupted by an [[Plan to kidnap Chancellor Palpatine|attempt]] to abduct [[Supreme Chancellor]] Palpatine.<ref name="Crisis on Naboo">{{TCW|Crisis on Naboo}}</ref>
*Sheev Palpatine inducts [[Gallius Rax]] into the [[Contingency]] and the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' sets [[Gallius Rax]]'s flashback interlude chapter in Part 5 roughly twenty-five years before the Battle of Jakku, which occurs in [[5 ABY]].</ref>
