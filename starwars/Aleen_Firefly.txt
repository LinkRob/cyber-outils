{{Quote|The Aleena don't seem to miss their old neighbors all that much, but they do miss the twinklin' fireflies that used to swarm up from the caverns at night, back before the Great Seal was put in place. This dazzlin' drink the Aleena created as a tribute to those luminous creatures seems like the next best thing.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
The '''Aleen Firefly''' was a cloudy, light-[[Color|green]] beverage filled with red, green, and blue cubes created by the [[Aleena]] [[species]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Aleen]]. It was made as a tribute to the [[Firefly|fireflies]] found in the [[Aleen Underworld]] which had previously flown to the surface where the Aleen lived prior to the placement of the [[Great Seal]], a barrier between the surface world of the Aleen and the subterranean world of their [[Kindalo]] neighbors.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] visited Aleen during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]]<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> and then included a recipe for Aleen Firefly, which he considered dazzling, in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Aleen Firefly was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the beverage makes use of chilled [[Wikipedia:Pineapple juice|pineapple juice]] and chilled [[Wikipedia:List of lemon-lime drink brands|lemon lime soda]]. The gelatinous cubes make use of [[Wikipedia:Strawberry|strawberry]] [[Wikipedia:Gelatin|gelatin]], [[Wikipedia:Raspberry|raspberry]] gelatin, [[Wikipedia:Lime (fruit)|lime]] gelatin, and warm [[Wikipedia:Water|water]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
