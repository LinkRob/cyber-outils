The '''Arms Market''' was a market located in<ref name="BH 13" /> [[Wormtown]],<ref name="Location" /> on the [[moon]] [[Nar Shaddaa]].<ref name="BH 13" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bounty Hunters 13|''Bounty Hunters'' 13]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[bounty hunter]]s [[Valance]] and [[Dengar]] went to the market, since Dengar assured that [[Devono Vix]], an arms dealer, could tell them why [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] put a [[bounty]] on his former favorite bounty hunter [[Boba Fett]]'s head. After meeting with Vix and his friends, Valance decided to neglect the delicacy recommended by Dengar and punched the arms smuggler. After a fight between the two bounty hunters and Vix's friends, Valance used his [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] enhanced [[Limb|arm]] to cause an explosion [[Death|killing]] several opponents. Understanding that he had no other chance, Vix tried to run away, chased by Valance and Dengar.<ref name="BH 13" />

Vix, however, was able to escape after Valance was intercepted by the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]], who was on Nar Shaddaa with his fellow [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] in search of Boba Fett and therefore [[Han Solo]], who they thought was with him.<ref name="BH 13" />
