The '''Defense Finance Committee''' was a subcommittee within the [[Imperial Senate]] during the early [[Imperial Era]]. It was dedicated in diverting the funds necessary for the operation of the [[Kamino]]an [[Tipoca City Military Complex|facilities]] housing the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[clone trooper]] [[Imperial Army|army]], and its members included [[Kaminoan]] [[Senator]] [[Halle Burtoni]].<ref name="TC" />

By [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|208}}</ref> [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]] began appropriating the Committee's funds to finance his secret [[Project War-Mantle]]; this was known to members of the Committee but they nevertheless failed to act against him. Eventually, the truth behind the conspiracy was uncovered by Senators [[Riyo Chuchi]] of [[Pantora]] and [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] when the former questioned Burtoni on the matter. Burtoni's testimony of the matter before the Senate resulted in Rampart's arrest.<ref name="TC" />
