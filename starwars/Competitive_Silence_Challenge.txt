The '''Competitive Silence Challenge''' was a [[Youngling|children's]] game whose aim was to see who could stay quiet the longest. If a player made any noise at all, they were out. The last person who had not made any noise, won. The orphans at [[Marn (Phirmist)|Marn's]] [[Orphan farm|farm]] on [[Durkteel]], including [[Mattis Banz]], would often play that game.<ref name="Join the Resistance" />
