"'''Chewie vs. Holochess - Let the Wookiee Win'''" is the tenth episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''. It premiered on the [[StarWarsKids.com]]<ref name="Star Wars Kids" /> and [[The Walt Disney Company|DisneyNOW]]<ref name="Disney Now">{{DisneyNOW|url=shows/star-wars-galaxy-of-adventures|text=Galaxy of Adventures}}</ref> websites on [[December 7]], [[2018]].<ref name="YouTube" />

==Plot summary==
The episode opens with a game of [[Dejarik]] aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. A [[Mantellian Savrip]] defeats a [[Kintan Strider]]. [[Chewbacca]] growls in rage. [[C-3PO]] counters that [[R2-D2]] made a fair move and that screaming about it can't help him. [[Han Solo]] tells the [[droid]]s to let Chewbacca have it because it is not wise to upset a [[Wookiee]]. The camera zooms in on Chewbacca's menacing face and eyes.

In several flashback and future scenes, Chewbacca growls into Han's face and later at a [[MSE-6 series repair droid|mouse droid]] aboard the [[Death Star]]. He also threatens a [[Rodian]], then a [[stormtrooper]], and manhandles [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] on [[Cloud City]]. There is also a scene of Chewbacca and Han Solo fighting stormtroopers in a corridor. Returning to the present, C-3PO sees Han's point and counsels R2-D2 to let the Wookiee win.
