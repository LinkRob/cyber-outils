'''Ellian Zahra''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Commander]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Both cunning and vengeful, Zahra served at the helm of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tarkin's Will]]''. Zahra rose through the Empire as the protégé of [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], becoming a skilled tactician under his guidance. After she failed Tarkin during a [[Mission to Ikkrukk|mission]], Zahra was dismissed by her mentor, though Zahra attempted to regain his favor. Before she could redeem herself, Tarkin [[Death|perished]] aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]] when it was destroyed by the Rebel Alliance during the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Zahra held a personal grudge against [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the Rebellion for Tarkin's death, intending to kill her to avenge her mentor.

Following the [[Battle of Hoth]] in [[3 ABY]], Zahra was tasked by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] in hunting down the [[division]]s of the scattered [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]]. She failed to prevent the rebel [[Fourth Division]] from [[Battle at Rendezvous Point Delta-Three|escaping]] due to the intervention of the rebels aboard the [[freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', including Organa. Zahra continued to hunt down other divisions, but desired to destroy the Fourth Division and Organa along with it. During a [[Battle at Elessia|battle]] against the Fourth and [[Seventh Division]]s at [[Elessia]], Zahra confronted Organa personally, wounding the rebel leader with her [[Ellian Zahra's blade|blade]]&mdash;an act Zahra believed would demoralize Organa.

==Biography==
===Service to the Empire===
====Becoming Tarkin's protégé====
{{Quote|The day the terrorists killed my parents, I understood that life has no interest in ''playing fair.'' Since that day, I have seen with my eyes, not my hopes.|Ellian Zahra|<ref name="Star Wars 7" />}}
The [[human]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Star Wars 21" /> Ellian Zahra<ref name="Star Wars 8">[[Star Wars (2020) 8|''Star Wars'' (2020) 8]]</ref> lived during the [[Imperial Era]]. Zahra's [[Ellian Zahra's parents|parents]],<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> along with the rest of her [[Ellian Zahra's family|family]], were [[Death|killed]] in a rebel [[terrorist]] attack while she was still young. Forced to survive on her own for some [[time]], Zahra eventually entered into the [[Imperial Academy]] to serve the [[Galactic Empire]] and ensure others would not suffer what she had.<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> She scored at the highest levels on the Empire's selection exams, and was one of several students selected by [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], [[Governor]]<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and [[Grand Moff]],<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> for her potential and lack of familial or other ties.<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> Tarkin taught Zahra much, providing guidance and support.<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> Under Tarkin's instruction and influence, Zahra rose through the Imperial Academy.<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

[[File:ZahraAndVeermok-2020StarWars7.jpg|thumb|left|310px|Zahra earned her place as Tarkin's protégé by defeating the veermok on Eriadu.]]
Around [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Tarkin brought Zahra, along with two other students of his, [[Beldin]] and [[Hannevik]], to the Outer Rim<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Eriadu]],<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> his [[homeworld]],<ref name="USWNE" /> for a final test to determine the one who would become his protégé. Zahra and her peers were tasked by Tarkin to [[Hunting|hunt]] down a deadly [[Albinism|albino]] [[Eriadu veermok|veermok]] in the [[forest]] of the [[Carrion Plateau]] and bring him the creature's head before he departed in his [[flagship]], the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', at dawn in twelve [[Standard hour|hours]]. By dawn, Zahra had killed the veermok, having caught the creature in the [[tree]]s while it believed it was safe. Additionally, having determined that Tarkin intended for the candidates to hunt one another, Zahra defeated Beldin and Hannevik. Although she had sustained injuries in the hunt, Zahra returned to Tarkin with the veermok's head, also informing him that her peers had been unworthy.<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

Having passed the test, Zahra was taken in as Tarkin's protégé. As an [[Imperial officer|officer]] in the [[Imperial Navy]], she accompanied Tarkin to meetings with individuals such as [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] and oversaw the [[Torture|interrogation]] of rebels alongside her mentor, rising to the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Captain]]. Zahra was introduced to<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]]<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] by Tarkin, who recommended her insight during an engagement with rebels. She eventually obtained the rank of [[Lieutenant Commander]], having distinguished herself during her [[Standard year|years]] with Tarkin.<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

====Tarkin's final mission====
{{Quote|A Tarkin looks their enemy in the eye and puts their blade in their throat. You did neither, and so you failed. I thought I knew who you were, Commander. I was mistaken. All I know is what you are not.|Tarkin, to Zahra|<ref name="Star Wars 7" />}}
Eventually, Zahra met with Tarkin on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> [[battle station]] and [[superweapon]],<ref name="Timelines" /> where her mentor gave her a [[Mission to Ikkrukk|mission]] to eliminate [[Burnium Ro]], a [[warlord]] operating on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] world [[Ikkrukk]] and utilizing imagery associated with the obsolete [[Nihil]] criminal organization. When Tarkin assigned Zahra a strike force and the ''Carrion Spike'', she questioned if such firepower was needed; her mentor's description of the Nihil as agents of chaos led her to understand that Ro was the antithesis of the Empire's order and that she needed to send a message. Zahra departed, with Tarkin requesting that she bring him Ro's head.<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

[[File:ZahraOnIkkrukk-2020StarWars7.png|thumb|right|320px|On Ikkrukk, Zahra incorrectly believed she had been successful in eliminating Burnium Ro.]]
On Ikkrukk, Zahra deployed a network of [[Viper probe droid|probe droids]], creating a mobile surveillance net to search for Ro while she waited aboard the ''Carrion Spike''. When one of the [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette|vessel's]] [[Imperial Navy Trooper|Navy Troopers]] questioned how she thought of the idea, Zahra explained that she considered everything as a resource using the [[water]] she was drinking as an example. The [[probe droid]]s detected the warlord in [[Grail City]], and Zahra&mdash;unaware that he was actually a [[Burnium Ro's decoy|decoy]] of Ro's&mdash;had the [[Cloaking device|cloaked]] ''Carrion Spike'' follow him. After arriving at Ro's [[Burnium Ro's stronghold|stronghold]], Zahra had the [[corvette]] deactivate its cloaking system and fire on the stronghold, destroying the base and killing "Ro" and the forces within.<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

Upon Zahra's return to the [[Death Star]], Tarkin questioned why she had not brought him Ro's head. Zahra insisted that she had destroyed Ro and his operation, but Tarkin, playing a [[shadowfeed]] broadcast of the warlord, informed her that the man she had killed was a decoy and that the real Ro was using her failure and his survival to elevate himself. She listened in silence as Tarkin expressed his disappointment, telling Zahra that his beliefs in her had been mistaken before departing. Although Tarkin ordered that Zahra report to the personnel office to be reassigned, she remained determined to locate Ro.<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

====The Battle of Yavin====
{{Quote|Governor Tarkin. I've set this message to be transmitted to you once I've already embarked upon my mission. Forgive my familiarity, but our relationship is&hellip;meaningful to me. I would not be who I am without you. You know I would never intentionally fail you. I hope you will give me the opportunity to prove your faith in me was not misplaced. I have taken a shuttle without authorization, but I will return it. I will come back to you soon, and I will hand you the head of&mdash;|Zahra's message to Tarkin, interrupted by the Death Star's destruction|<ref name="Star Wars 7" />}}
A [[Standard week|week]] after her failed mission, Zahra had obtained intelligence that revealed Ro's location. She returned to the Death Star, but her efforts to meet with Tarkin were denied by an [[Unidentified Imperial officer (Ellian Zahra)|officer]] under orders from her former mentor. Zahra ordered that her information be passed to Tarkin, but the [[officer]] reiterated the governor's orders. Relenting, Zahra asked the officer to tell Tarkin that she understood. Intending to kill Ro and regain Tarkin's favor, Zahra headed for a [[hangar]], unaware that the Death Star was currently engaged in<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> the [[Battle of Yavin]], having come under attack by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] in the Outer Rim's [[Yavin system]] as the [[Space station|station]] prepared to destroy the [[Great Temple|rebel base]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]].<ref name="USWNE" />

Zahra attempted to requisition a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]], but the [[Unidentified stormtrooper (Ellian Zahra)|stormtrooper]] guarding it asked for authorization, informing her of the rebel attack. Although Zahra was confident that the rebels posed no danger to the Death Star, the [[stormtrooper]] remained uncooperative, having been tasked to hold the vessel for the potential evacuation of high-ranking officers. Zahra knocked the [[trooper]] unconscious and commandeered the [[shuttle]].<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

[[File:ZahraShuttleYavin-2020StarWars7.png|thumb|left|350px|Zahra's shuttle departs the Death Star moments before its destruction during the Battle of Yavin.]]
Flying out of the hangar amidst the battle, Zahra recorded a message for Tarkin, setting it to be transmitted once she had embarked on her mission. She explained her hope that Tarkin would give her an opportunity to prove herself and that she had stolen a shuttle, but assured her former mentor that she would return with Ro's head. However, at that moment, the rebels destroyed the Death Star<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> while Tarkin was still aboard the station.<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> Sustaining injuries as her [[starship]] was thrown and damaged by the blast, Zahra looked on in dismay at the destruction.<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

====A stalled career====
{{Quote|He died thinking I was a ''failure,'' Princess. I no longer have a chance to show him I am not, thanks to you.|Commander Zahra to Leia Organa, regarding Tarkin|<ref name="Star Wars 8" />}}
[[File:MiningFacility81B-2020StarWars8.png|thumb|right|340px|Per one of Tarkin's final orders, Zahra was reassigned to command Mining Facility 81-B on Kessel.]]
As per one of Tarkin's final orders<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> and a note that the governor had placed in her file indicating she should be indefinitely assigned non-essential duties, Zahra, holding the rank of [[Commander]], was placed in charge of [[Mining Facility 81-B]] on<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> the [[spice]] [[mining]] planet<ref name="USWNE" /> [[Kessel]]. Zahra believed that Tarkin had intended her reassignment to serve as a lesson and that she would have been provided with an opportunity to prove herself if her mentor had lived. Having learned of the role played by [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the Rebel Alliance&mdash;who had been held aboard the Death Star shortly before its destruction in the rebel attack that she had also engineered&mdash;she believed that Organa's capture had been the reason why Tarkin had been aboard the station. Zahra held Organa responsible for Tarkin's death, vowing to kill her to avenge her mentor.<ref name="Star Wars 8" />

According to contemporary reports, Zahra attempted to lobby others in the [[Imperial Military]] in an effort to reclaim her career. Although some of Tarkin's close allies considered granting her request, others&mdash;who wished to distance themselves from what they believed to be Tarkin's failures&mdash;did not desire to elevate an heir to the governor's power. In particular, the newly-promoted [[Grand General]] [[Cassio Tagge]] kept Zahra at a distance.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

Tarkin's records of Zahra's progress were eventually stored in the [[Imperial Archives]] in the file "Commander Ellian Zahra Progress Reports" under the "Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin" section, while her failure at Ikkrukk was documented in the "Mission to Ikkrukk" file within the "Military Operations" section. Zahra's reassignment to Kessel was stored in the "Personnel Assignment Order #9337118q" file in Tarkin's section, and several communications made by Zahra during her time on Kessel were contained in a file titled "Commander Ellian Zahra #7132-7281" under the "Kessel Communications" section of the archives.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

===Hunting the rebel armada===
====A chance for redemption====
{{Quote|I am placing you in charge of the effort to hunt down the remnants of the rebel fleet. You will be given all necessary resources.''"<br />"''Lord Vader&hellip;I&hellip;I am honored. I will not fail you.''"<br />"''See that you do not. This assignment does not come solely from me. The Emperor knows your name. This is either your greatest opportunity or the worst moment of your life. Time will tell.|Darth Vader and Ellian Zahra|<ref name="Star Wars 8" />}}
Zahra was still stationed on Kessel<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> by the time of the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> which saw the Rebel Alliance defeated by the Empire and forced to scatter its [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] into numerous [[division]]s to regroup at a later point.<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> After Vader was ordered by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] to hunt down and destroy the the remnants of the rebel [[fleet]],<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> he<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> sought out Zahra, knowing of her record<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> and having previously been introduced to her.<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> While Zahra oversaw the operations of the mining facility, a [[Unidentified Imperial lieutenant (Kessel)|lieutenant]] approached to inform her of an incoming transmission; when she sarcastically guessed it was the Emperor, Zahra was taken aback to learn it was Vader.<ref name="Star Wars 8" />

[[File:ZahraVaderMeeting-2020StarWars8.jpg|thumb|left|340px|Zahra was tasked by Darth Vader with hunting down the scattered rebel fleet.]]
Answering Vader's transmission, Zahra confirmed the [[Sith Lord]]'s statement that she had been mentored by Tarkin. Although Vader noted Tarkin's order for her reassignment, Zahra explained her belief that her mentor had intended it as a lesson, which Vader agreed was typical of Tarkin. Vader subsequently placed her in command of the effort to hunt the remnants of the rebel fleet, allowing her to use any resources that were necessary. Zahra was honored, assuring Vader she would succeed; the [[Sith]] warned her against failure, telling her that the Emperor had been informed of her name.<ref name="Star Wars 8" />

Zahra was placed in command of an [[Battle group|assault group]]<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> known as the [[Hunter Fleet]].<ref name="Star Wars 21" /> With Vader offering her the choice of any vessel in the Imperial Navy to be her flagship, Zahra chose the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Tarkin's Will|''Fortitude'']], which had been near the Death Star during the battle and had suffered severe damage from the station's wreckage. Zahra named the vessel the ''Tarkin's Will'' and selected Imperials who had similarly lost individuals close to them in the station's destruction to serve as her crew. She did not wish to repair the damage, intending for the [[Star Destroyer]] to memorialize the loss of the Death Star and those aboard it;<ref name="Star Wars 3" /> the damaged sections were cordoned off, with the bodies of the dead still left where they had fallen.<ref name="Star Wars 21" /> In the [[Standard day|days]] before Zahra intended to embark on her mission, [[Lieutenant]] [[Gorr]] was assigned to the ''Tarkin's Will'' as her second-in-command.<ref name="Star Wars 3" />

====Escape of the Fourth Division====
{{Quote|We attacked both cells. One was completely destroyed. The other&hellip;escaped, although they suffered significant losses. I would consider that part of the operation a partial success.''"<br />"''Partial success does not exist, Commander Zahra. You succeed, or you do not.|Commander Zahra and Darth Vader|<ref name="Star Wars 1" />}}
[[File:ZahraAboardTarkinsWill-2020StarWars1.jpg|thumb|right|330px|Zahra watches the attack on the Fourth Division.]]
Managing to break the Rebellion's codes, Zahra used a signal analysis protocol of her own design to track down two divisions that attempted to communicate&mdash;the [[Fourth Division|Fourth]] and [[Eighth Division]]s. Zahra split her battle group, sending her [[Hunter Fleet's secondary strike force|secondary strike force]] to [[Battle near Malastare|attack]] the Eighth Division near the<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> planet<ref name="USWNE" /> [[Malastare]] while she led an [[Battle at Rendezvous Point Delta-Three|attack]] on the Fourth Division, which was attempting to regroup at [[Rendezvous Point Delta-Three]] in the Mid Rim. Zahra utilized a tactic she had previously devised but never tested, using her fleet's [[turbolaser]]s to form a cordon and [[blockade]] the rebels against a nearby [[Unidentified Mid Rim star|star]]. Unable to escape, the Fourth Division suffered heavy damage from Zahra's [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. From the ''Tarkin's Will'', Zahra admired the destruction and her tactic's success, discussing her fleet's two engagements with Gorr.<ref name="Star Wars 1" />

When one rebel [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing starfighter]] escaped the blockade, Zahra ordered a [[Unidentified Imperial technician (Tarkin's Will)|technician]] to transfer her manual control of a single turbolaser. The [[technician]] was hesitant but followed the order at Gorr's insistence, and Zahra took control of the turbolaser and destroyed the [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]], relishing in the rebel's failed escape. The [[YT-1300 light freighter|freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' soon arrived amidst the battle, carrying Organa and several other rebels. Zahra, recognizing that Organa was likely aboard, ordered Gorr to divert a [[Unidentified TIE squadron|squadron]] of [[TIE series|TIEs]] and any non-essential turbolaser batteries to target the ''Falcon'', wishing to eliminate Organa and the vessel that had repeatedly escaped the Empire. However, the ''Falcon''{{'s}} crew managed to destroy the turbolasers on one of Zahra's [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|light cruisers]], giving the survivors of the Fourth Division a gap to escape into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Star Wars 1" />

Furious, Zahra snapped at Gorr when the lieutenant reported the escape before ordering the fleet to stand down in resignation. Zahra later reported to Vader via [[hologram]], asserting the operation was a partial success; Vader disagreed, however, questioning her as to why she had failed. The commander noted the ''Falcon''{{'s}} disruption, but Vader reprimanded her for attempting to destroy the vessel&mdash;unknown to Zahra, the ''Falcon'' carried the [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]], Vader's [[Skywalker family|son]], who the Sith Lord wanted alive. Zahra was dismissed by Vader, but not before he ordered her to destroy what remained of the rebel fleet with a warning against further failure.<ref name="Star Wars 1" />

====Locating additional cells====
{{Quote|That's the last of them. All rebel vessels in this system have been destroyed. That said, Commander Zahra, for the moment we have no additional leads on locations for cells of the remaining rebel fleet.''"<br />"''That's all right, Lieutenant Gorr. We'll find them. I have many plans in motion.|Lieutenant Gorr and Commander Zahra|<ref name="Star Wars 3" />}}
[[File:ZahraAndGorr-2020StarWars3.jpg|thumb|left|330px|Zahra and Lieutenant Gorr following the destruction of the Sixth Division.]]
After locating the Alliance's [[Sixth Division]] in the Mid Rim, Zahra led the ''Tarkin's Will'' in an [[Destruction of the Sixth Division|attack]] on the division. Her forces eliminated all targets within the [[Star system|system]], though Gorr noted they had no leads as to the locations of the remaining cells. Zahra, however, was certain that her plans would succeed in finding them, quoting her former mentor. When the lieutenant, unaware of the history behind the ''Tarkin's Will'', suggested that repairs be made to the vessel, Zahra chastised Gorr. She informed the apologetic lieutenant that the ''Tarkin's Will'' and its damage served as a memorial to those who died aboard the Death Star, rallying the crew in a call to never forgetting the losses. In awe, Gorr gave Zahra sympathy for if she had lost someone aboard the station, and the commander confirmed she had, declaring her intent to exact her vengeance on Organa.<ref name="Star Wars 3" />

Zahra later intercepted transmissions from what believed was the Alliance Fleet's [[Seventh Division]]. Her forces continued to track the division's movements, waiting for it to contact&mdash;and subsequently reveal the location of&mdash;another cell. Zahra reported her progress to Vader, who commended her but soon became distracted when Skywalker activated a [[The Grand Inquisitor|trap]] the Sith Lord had set at a [[Jedi outpost (Tempes)|Jedi outpost]] on the<ref name="Star Wars 6">[[Star Wars (2020) 6|''Star Wars'' (2020) 6]]</ref> planet<ref name="Skywalker AFAW">''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]''</ref> [[Tempes]]. When Vader ordered his [[pilot]] to set a course for Tempes, Zahra offered to bring the ''Tarkin's Will'' to assist, but the Sith Lord insisted that he would handle the matter himself.<ref name="Star Wars 6" />

====The rebel trap====
{{Quote|My name is Commander Zahra of the Imperial Navy. I have deactivated the safety controls on your main reactor. This ship will explode in a matter of minutes. That, however, is not my reason for boarding this vessel. I am here for ''Princess Leia Organa.''|Commander Zahra|<ref name="Star Wars 8" />}}
The Fourth Division eventually responded to the Seventh Division's transmission, and Gorr reported the news to Zahra. Recognizing the Fourth as Organa's group, Zahra intended to eliminate the division herself and send her secondary strike force after the Seventh. Gorr, however, noted that the positions of the Imperial and rebel fleets made the inverse more efficient. The commander was displeased, but relented, ordering the ''Tarkin's Will'' to the Seventh Division's location near the<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> Mid Rim astronomical object<ref name="Galaxy Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> [[Elessia]].<ref name="Star Wars 9">[[Star Wars (2020) 9|''Star Wars'' (2020) 9]]</ref> Jumping to hyperspace, Zahra's fleet arrived at the Seventh Division's location, launching an [[Battle at Elessia|attack]] on the rebels.<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

As the battle continued, Zahra's forces were positioned to overwhelm their foes due to their greater numbers. However, when she asked Gorr about the Fourth, the lieutenant informed her that the secondary force had arrived to find the rebels absent. Zahra was about to reprimand Gorr when the vessels of the Fourth Division&mdash;having intended to trap the Imperials in a [[pincer maneuver]]&mdash;emerged from hyperspace behind her fleet. Zahra was unconcerned, however; intending to lead a boarding party, she ordered Gorr to prepare her shuttle.<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> As Zahra, equipped in her [[armor]], prepared to depart, Gorr questioned whether it was wise for her to lead a boarding party amidst the battle. Determined to face Organa, Zahra insisted it was a necessary risk and that nobody else could complete the mission. She noted that Gorr had been shadowing her since the start of their hunt for the rebels, instructing the lieutenant to follow her own methods as she departed.<ref name="Star Wars 8" />

[[File:CutAPath-2020StarWars8.png|thumb|right|340px|Zahra and her spacetroopers board the rebel frigate.]]
Zahra boarded her [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]] alongside a [[squad]] of [[spacetrooper]]s, launching from the ''Tarkin's Will''. Headed for the [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] that served as the Fourth Division's flagship, Zahra and her [[stormtrooper]]s boarded the [[frigate]], breaching its [[airlock]]. Zahra led the charge, cutting down [[rebel trooper]]s with her [[Ellian Zahra's blade|blade]] and [[blaster]], heading toward the vessel's [[engine]] room. Zahra disabled the safety controls on the main [[reactor]], with the vessel threatening to explode within [[Standard minute|minutes]] as a result. She contacted the frigate's commanders, informing them that the vessel's imminent destruction was not her objective; Zahra promised to reactivate the systems if Organa came alone&mdash;armed or otherwise&mdash;to the engine room. Commander [[Grek (commodore)|Grek]] questioned her intent, and Zahra claimed she merely wished to speak.<ref name="Star Wars 8" />

====Confronting Organa====
{{Quote|Leia knows I won't stop until I have her. This blade will be the last thing she thinks about before she goes to sleep and the first thing she sees when she wakes up. I did more damage to the Rebellion in that engine room than a thousand Star Destroyers could. In that moment, in the darkness, I took away her ''hope.''|Commander Zahra|<ref name="Star Wars 8" />}}
[[File:ZahraLeiaConfrontation-2020StarWars8.jpg|thumb|left|340px|Zahra confronted Leia Organa in the frigate's engine room, intending to exact her revenge.]]
Despite knowing Zahra's request was a trap, Organa complied; when the princess entered the engine room, Zahra shot out the door's control panel, blocking her exit. True to her word, Zahra restored the reactor and holstered her blaster, promising to let Organa go if she reached the exit on the opposite end of the room alive. Hidden in the shadows of the engine room with her [[Sword|blade]] drawn, Zahra began to recount her life&mdash;including the death of her family and becoming Tarkin's protégé&mdash;to Organa, who attempted to fire upon her unseen foe. Upon mentioning Tarkin's name, Zahra slashed Organa's [[Limb|arm]] with her blade. Zahra blamed the princess for the destruction of the Death Star and Tarkin's demise; as she described how her mentor had died believing she was a failure, Zahra cut Organa's hand with another swipe, causing the princess to drop her blaster. Although Organa had almost reached the exit, the commander stood in her path, intending to seek her revenge&mdash;and take her time in doing so.<ref name="Star Wars 8" />

However, Zahra was interrupted by a communication from Gorr, who informed her that the two rebel divisions were too much for the Imperial forces to defeat. Preparing the ''Tarkin's Will'' for [[lightspeed]], Gorr urged her to return to the Star Destroyer. At that moment, Skywalker&mdash;having defeated Zahra's [[trooper]]s&mdash;confronted her from behind, his [[Luke Skywalker's yellow lightsaber|lightsaber]] drawn. Telling Organa to remember her and her blade, Zahra warned that the princess would see both again and then threatened to kill Skywalker; however, having been ordered not to due to Vader's plans for the Jedi, Zahra instead distracted Skywalker by throwing her blade past his head before leaping over him and kicking him in the back. Zahra retrieved her blade and exited the room, shattering a [[viewport]] with a shot from her blaster. Using her [[jetpack]], Zahra escaped the frigate and flew into the [[hangar]] bay of the ''Tarkin's Will'' before it departed.<ref name="Star Wars 8" />

[[File:ZahrasBlade-2020StarWars8.png|thumb|right|340px|Although she had not slain her foe, Zahra was satisfied with having taken away Organa's hope.]]
Aboard the Star Destroyer, Zahra spoke to Gorr in her office. The commander was satisfied with her achievement, showing off her blade covered in Organa's [[blood]]. Gorr was surprised, as Zahra's intent had been the princess's demise, but Zahra explained that she had to kill Organa the right way. Zahra noted that the outcome of their confrontation aboard the frigate was better, confident that her nemesis would now be unable to forget her or her blade. Having taken away the princess's hope, Zahra believed that she had caused lasting damage to Organa's status as a symbol of the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> As Zahra had predicted, Organa indeed felt hopeless for some time following her encounter with the Imperial commander.<ref name="Star Wars 14">[[Star Wars (2020) 14|''Star Wars'' (2020) 14]]</ref>

===The Alliance strikes back===
====Starlight in the darkness====
{{Quote|Our TIE pilots have the rebel fighters boxed in, Commander Zahra. The X-wings can't escape, and the A-wings seem unwilling to abandon them. Should I give the order to shoot them down?''"<br />"''Let's not be hasty. The rebel squadron's starfighters bear unique insignia, Lieutenant Gorr. It suggests that they may have a particular purpose or mission. Activate ''tractor beams.''|Lieutenant Gorr and Commander Zahra|<ref name="Star Wars 11">[[Star Wars (2020) 11|''Star Wars'' (2020) 11]]</ref>}}
Despite the rebel victory at Elessia and the reunification of two of the Alliance Fleet's divisions, Zahra and her forces remained on the hunt. The rebels, intending to establish new encryption codes and [[Operation Starlight|reassemble]] their fleet, attempted to devise a system based on the extinct [[Trawak]] [[language]] using an ancient [[Autonomous Translator Module, Mark II|droid]].<ref name="Star Wars 9">[[Star Wars (2020) 9|''Star Wars'' (2020) 9]]</ref> The Rebellion dispatched its [[Starlight Squadron]] to the likely rendezvous points of the other divisions in order to distribute the new code. In deep [[Realspace|space]] near<ref name="Star Wars 10">[[Star Wars (2020) 10|''Star Wars'' (2020) 10]]</ref> the Outer Rim planet<ref name="USWNE" /> [[Felucia]], the rebels encountered Imperial probe droids<ref name="Star Wars 10" /> that Zahra had left as a trap<ref name="Star Wars 12">[[Star Wars (2020) 12|''Star Wars'' (2020) 12]]</ref> amongst the wreckage of the Sixth Division.<ref name="Star Wars 10 " /> Zahra, aboard the ''Tarkin's Will'', subsequently [[Ambush near Felucia|ambushed]] the [[squadron]].<ref name="Star Wars 11" />

[[File:TarkinsWillAttacksStarlightSquadron-2020StarWars11.jpg|thumb|left|320px|Zahra and the ''Tarkin's Will'' ambushed Starlight Squadron, though the rebels escaped.]]
In pursuit of the rebels, Zahra launched TIEs from the ''Tarkin's Will'' to box in her foes and prevent their escape. When Gorr asked whether to order the TIEs to shoot down the rebels, Zahra declined; having recognized the unique nature of Starlight Squadron's insignia, Zahra deduced that the starfighters had a particular purpose and instead commanded for the [[tractor beam]]s to be activated, intending to capture the rebels. Starlight Squadron accelerated into the beam, destroying the [[Tractor beam projector|generators]] once the ''Tarkin's Will'' had lowered its [[Deflector shield|shields]] to pull the fighters in. The rebels entered the Star Destroyer's bay, managing to take control of the hangar before the Imperials could muster their forces. Upon being informed by Gorr of the development, an irate Zahra commanded the lieutenant to pass on the order to retake the hangar.<ref name="Star Wars 11" />

While the rebels captured an Imperial [[astromech droid]] in order to replace their own lost droids and perform the hyperspace calculations necessary for their X-wings, Zahra's forces dispatched [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s to the hangar. Although the rebels succeeded and were able to escape,<ref name="Star Wars 11" /> [[Lieutenant]]<ref name="Star Wars 12" /> [[Shara Bey]], who had lingered to steal Imperial intelligence from the hangar's other astromechs, was left trapped aboard the ''Tarkin's Will'' when her [[Shara Bey's A-wing|A-wing]] was destroyed.<ref name="Star Wars 11" /> Reported to have died in the destruction,<ref name="Star Wars 21" /> Bey managed to evade capture, and, unknown to Zahra, remained in hiding aboard the Star Destroyer, using her situation to gather Imperial intelligence.<ref name="Star Wars 12" />

====The hunt continues====
{{Quote|We are reunited&hellip;but so many of us remain lost. Three divisions of ''the Rebel Fleet'' have been reunited after our scattering at ''Hoth,'' thanks to the bravery and skill of ''Starlight Squadron'' and so many others. That leaves many ships, many members of the Alliance, yet to be found, while Imperial Commander ''Ellian Zahra'' remains on the hunt.|Mon Mothma|<ref name="Star Wars 19">[[Star Wars (2020) 19|''Star Wars'' (2020) 19]]</ref>}}
Not long after the battle, an anomalous transmission from the ''Tarkin's Will'' was detected<ref name="Star Wars 21" />&mdash;a message sent by Bey to the Rebellion.<ref name="Star Wars 12" /> Zahra did not know the cause of the anomaly, but became suspicious.<ref name="Star Wars 21" /> At some point following her encounter with Starlight Squadron, Zahra gave a briefing in which she described the squadron as a special missions crew. The Imperial forces eventually discovered the rebels' [[Eleventh Division]], located at [[Rendezvous Point Lambda-Four]] on the Outer Rim planet [[Ab Dalis]]. [[Admiral]] [[Kalaxo]] of the Star Destroyer ''[[Ultima II]]'' was [[Battle of Ab Dalis|dispatched]] to eliminate the division. When Starlight Squadron, accompanied by Skywalker, arrived on Ab Dalis to assist the trapped division, Lieutenant [[Bonnard]] suggested sending a message to Zahra, having recognized the squadron from the commander's briefing.<ref name="Star Wars 15">[[Star Wars (2020) 15|''Star Wars'' (2020) 15]]</ref>

[[File:RebelsAlmostReunited-2020StarWars19.png|thumb|right|330px|Despite Zahra's ongoing hunt for the Alliance Fleet, the rebels managed to reunite an additional division.]]
Kalaxo, however, was confident in the Imperial ability to destroy the rebels, only intending to contact Zahra when victory was achieved. When the ''Ultima II'' managed to break through the shield protecting the rebels' forward ground command post, Kalaxo ordered Bonnard to contact Zahra regarding their imminent victory; however, the rebels were able to destroy the ''Ultima II'', rescuing the Eleventh Division, including Alliance [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|leader]] [[Mon Mothma]].<ref name="Star Wars 15" /> Although the rebel fleet had managed to reunite three of its divisions, Zahra remained on the hunt for the remaining cells.<ref name="Star Wars 19" />

====Catching a ghost====
{{Quote|You're a fool. An ''overconfident fool&mdash;''though I'm glad it worked out this way. Better this than you being dragged out of the bowels of my ship kicking and screaming. This, at least, has some ''dignity'' to it. And I'm pleased to meet you too. Surviving down there for all that time&hellip;it couldn't have been easy. I'm impressed. Truly. You're going to tell me all about it. And so many other things too.|Zahra, confronting Shara Bey|<ref name="Star Wars 21" />}}
Unknown to Zahra, Bey remained trapped aboard the ''Tarkin's Will'' for some time, sending Imperial intelligence back to the rebel fleet. The pilot hid in the damaged sections of the Star Destroyer&mdash;where the internal [[sensor]]s did not function&mdash;scavenging equipment from the fallen bodies and wearing [[stormtrooper armor]] as a disguise to move around. As the anomalous transmissions and unauthorized accesses of datahubs continued, however, Zahra's suspicions grew, and she began to question whether Bey had truly been destroyed with her [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wing]]. The commander kept a project on the anomalies, receiving reports from Gorr on the matter.<ref name="Star Wars 21" />

When Gorr informed Zahra of a discipline report filed by [[Warrant Officer]] [[Kronauer]] regarding a substandard stormtrooper whose designation&mdash;TK-510&mdash;had caused the lieutenant to check their records, Zahra guessed that no trooper of that designation served aboard the ''Tarkin's Will''. Although Gorr reasoned that it was possible the trooper had lied, Zahra correctly suspected the identity of their stowaway. Displaying holographic footage from the battle, Zahra explained her suspicions regarding the pilot's supposed death, further deducing that Bey had been stealing Imperial secrets and living in the vessel's damaged sections to avoid detection. Holding up her blade, Zahra ordered Gorr to begin a search, intending to hunt the pilot.<ref name="Star Wars 21" />

[[File:ZahraStabsShara-2020StarWars21.png|thumb|left|320px|Zahra caught Shara Bey after the rebel pilot lived in hiding for some time aboard the ''Tarkin's Will''.]]
Bey, realizing she would soon be found before the rebels could rescue her, commenced her own escape plan; she damaged a ''Lambda''-class shuttle so that it would be transferred for maintenance to the auxiliary hangar, where it would be easier to steal. Zahra, however, noticed the shuttle being unexpectedly scheduled for maintenance, connecting it to the stowaway. Equipped with her armor and blade, she deactivated the shuttle and lay in wait for Bey. When the rebel pilot attempted to steal the vessel, Zahra confronted her. The commander deflected Bey's blaster shots before slicing through the barrel of her weapon, remarking on the pilot's overconfidence but also commending her on surviving for as long as she did. Zahra stabbed Bey through the chest with her blade, declaring that the pilot would tell her about her time aboard the Star Destroyer&mdash;among many other subjects.<ref name="Star Wars 21" />

===Failure on Panisia===
{{Quote|This should not end this way. I will kill this thing, and then we will settle our own account like the warriors we are.|Ellian Zahra to Leia Organa, while the former is about to be attacked by an unidentified creature|<ref name="Star Wars 24" />}}
Zahra's efforts against the rebels ended in failure during the [[Battle of Panisia]], which saw the destruction of the ''Tarkin's Will''.<ref name="Star Wars 23">[[Star Wars (2020) 23|''Star Wars'' (2020) 23]]</ref> On the surface, Zahra again confronted Organa, who explained to the Imperial commander the differences between how the Empire and rebellion fought. Zahra was left for dead in a cave.<ref name="Star Wars 24">[[Star Wars (2020) 24|''Star Wars'' (2020) 24]]</ref> Left with scars across the right side of her face, neck, and at least a portion of her upper body, Zahra also lost her right arm and leg, which were both replaced with [[cybernetics]]. She joined the ranks of a [[Zahra's raider crew|raider faction]] and longed for revenge against Organa.<ref name="Star Wars 48" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|Despite this, there were some notable issues and individuals that Tagge had to navigate. In the years before his death at Yavin, Grand Moff Tarkin had been mentoring a young Imperial officer named Ellian Zahra. ''[&hellip;]'' Tagge seemed determined to keep Zahra at arm's length if for no other reason than she threatened the tentative balance he'd manage to achieve within the armed forces.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise and Fall" />}}
The [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] read several files in the Imperial Archives involving Zahra and eventually mentioned her in<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> his<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date 35">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> Briefly documenting Zahra's career during her time as Tarkin's protégé and the period following his death, Kin included her as one of the individuals who posed an issue for Grand General Tagge to navigate in the aftermath of the Battle of Yavin. Kin noted Tagge's apparent dedication to keeping her at a distance, surmising that he saw Zahra as a threat to the tentative balance he could hope to achieve in the Imperial forces.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

==Personality and traits==
===Servant of the Empire===
{{Quote|The Rebellion, through a combination of luck and sabotage, destroyed the Death Star, the Emperor's greatest effort to maintain peace in this troubled galaxy. Countless loyal Imperial subjects died that day. ''People.'' Murdered by ''Leia Organa's'' traitorious insurgency.|Commander Zahra|<ref name="Star Wars 3" />}}
[[File:EllianZahra-2020StarWars3.jpg|thumb|right|230px|Dedicated to the Empire and the order it brought, Zahra was vengeful and cunning.]]
A vengeful<ref name="Star Wars 3" /> and cunning<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> commander, Zahra was devoted to serving the Empire, despite her previous failures.<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> After losing her family in a rebel attack and being alone for some time,<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> Zahra had a cynical view on life, believing there was no inherent fairness in life and seeing things things as they were rather than with hope.<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> Zahra signed up to the Imperial Academy in order to prevent others from suffering as she had.<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> In serving the Empire, Zahra believed that the regime brought order, which subsequently made way for peace. She always saw her resources as broadly as possible, considering all the ways to utilize them effectively. Zahra trusted droids&mdash;which were incapable of turning against her&mdash;more than organic operatives.<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> During her time on Kessel after her reassignment, Zahra was jaded, believing that nobody had interest in someone like her.<ref name="Star Wars 8" />

Zahra saw the Death Star as the Emperor's greatest effort to maintain peace in a troubled [[The galaxy|galaxy]], believing its destruction to be a tragic massacre of loyal Imperial subjects. After being chosen to lead the hunt for the rebel fleet, Zahra honored those who perished on the Death Star by taking command of the ''Fortitude'', a Star Destroyer damaged in the superweapon's destruction, and recruiting a crew that consisted solely of those who had lost someone on the station. She intended the ''Tarkin's Will'' to be an instrument of vengeance, believing that the rebels would come to know and fear the vessel, and criticized Gorr for being unaware of the history and meaning of the Star Destroyer.<ref name="Star Wars 3" />

Zahra was willing to stop at nothing to destroy the Rebel Alliance,<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> which she saw as terrorists<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> and traitors. She felt that the rebels had only succeeded in defeating the Death Star due to luck and sabotage.<ref name="Star Wars 3" /> Zahra admired the destruction of the rebel fleet, considering the sight to be beautiful, and reveled in destroying a rebel who had attempted to flee.<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> She believed Luke Skywalker to be arrogant, though noted that he was surpassed by Shara Bey's futile attempt to escape the ''Tarkin's Will''. However, Zahra was impressed at Bey's survival and was glad to be able to catch the stowaway herself, believing it to be a more dignified outcome.<ref name="Star Wars 21" /> When she failed to prevent the survivors of the Fourth Division from escaping, Zahra grew angry at Lieutenant Gorr for stating the obvious, although confidently maintained to Vader that the operation was partially successful.<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> She did not wish to repeat her orders to her crew.<ref name="Star Wars 11" /> Zahra had [[Color|black]] [[hair]],<ref name="Star Wars 3" /> blue [[eye]]s, and light skin.<ref name="Star Wars 1" />

===Tarkin and Organa===
{{Quote|And Commander, please allow me to say that if you also lost someone close to you on the Death Star, you have my utmost sympathies.''"<br />"''I did. Not long from now, I will whisper the name in Leia Organa's ear. And then my blade will find her throat.|Lieutenant Gorr and Commander Zahra|<ref name="Star Wars 3" />}}
[[File:StarWars2020-24-clean.jpg|thumb|left|240px|Zahra was determined to seek vengeance on Organa for Tarkin's death.]]
As Tarkin's protégé, Zahra was close to her mentor and felt that their relationship was meaningful.<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> She was grateful for his support and guidance,<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> believing that she would not be who she was without Tarkin. Zahra was determined in never failing her mentor. When Tarkin scorned her after her failed mission on Ikkrukk, Zahra was initially unable to accept the rejection, intent on doing anything to prove herself, although she understood her mentor's disappointment.<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> She assumed that Tarkin had intended her to learn from his dismissal and that he would have offered her an opportunity to regain his faith. After losing Tarkin in the destruction of the Death Star, Zahra, no longer having a chance to redeem herself, was angered that her mentor had died thinking her to be a failure.<ref name="Star Wars 8" />

Zahra held Leia Organa solely responsible for the destruction of the Death Star and Tarkin's death, vowing to kill her to avenge her mentor. She had a true hatred for Organa, wanting the princess to feel the pain she had felt in her own life.<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> Wishing to cut Organa's throat in revenge,<ref name="Star Wars 3" /> Zahra became intent on hunting the princess specifically.<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> She studied the Empire's files on Organa extensively in order to learn much about her target.<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> Zahra was reluctant to give up a chance to hunt Organa down<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> and was willing to take risks to exact her revenge. She felt that her vengeance on Organa had to be done right, wishing to take her time with the act. Although Zahra was unable to kill Organa during their first confrontation at Elessia, she was satisfied with having stolen her hope, believing that the princess would be unable to forget her desire for revenge.<ref name="Star Wars 8" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|People on this ship talk about their commander&hellip;this woman ''Ellian Zahra&hellip;''like she's some kind of unstoppable hunter. Like she can't be beaten.|Shara Bey|<ref name="Star Wars 21" />}}
[[File:EllianZahra-2020StarWars1.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Zahra was a skilled commander.]]
Showing significant potential and results during her time in the Imperial Academy, Zahra grew to become a skilled tactician and commander as a result of Tarkin's instruction,<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> crediting her mentor for teaching her everything she knew.<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> Her crew aboard the ''Tarkin's Will'' praised her as an unstoppable hunter.<ref name="Star Wars 21" /> She developed tactics that she utilized in battle, such as the use of a turbolaser cordon to blockade an enemy fleet against a celestial body.<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> Zahra also devised a unique method of using probe droids to form a surveillance network.<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> She was willing to go against the standard procedures of the [[Imperial Tactical Manual|Imperial tactical manual]]. When encountering Starlight Squadron, she was able to deduce its importance based on its fighter's insignia.<ref name="Star Wars 11" /> Zahra claimed to know all that occurred aboard the ''Tarkin's Will'', and eventually determined the presence of Bey aboard her vessel.<ref name="Star Wars 21" />

Zahra was a skilled fighter with both her blaster and her blade,<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> the latter of which was her preferred weapon.<ref name="Star Wars 24" /> She was able to defeat both a deadly veermok and her fellow students of Tarkin during her mentor's test on Eriadu.<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> During her confrontation with Organa on the frigate, Zahra was able to strike with her blade from the darkness, unseen by her foe.<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> She was also able to take manual control of her Star Destroyer's guns, proving more effective than the automated targeting during the battle against the Fourth Division.<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> Zahra was capable of piloting starships, such as the ''Lambda''-class shuttle.<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|Remember me, Princess. Remember my ''blade.'' You will see both of us again.|Zahra threatens Organa|<ref name="Star Wars 8" />}}
[[File:ZahrasArmor-2020StarWars21.png|thumb|left|240px|Zahra clad in her armor and wielding her blade]]
Zahra possessed a set of black-colored armor that she utilized in battle. The armor&mdash;which featured a [[helmet]] with [[goggles]], a [[rebreather]], and a [[jetpack]]<ref name="Star Wars 8" />&mdash;was capable of deflecting blaster bolts.<ref name="Star Wars 21" /> Zahra wielded a blade, with which she intended to kill Organa. She also carried a blaster.<ref name="Star Wars 8" />

As an officer of the Galactic Empire, Zahra wore a standard green [[Imperial military uniforms|Imperial military uniform]], featuring a [[rank insignia plaque]] and [[code cylinder]]s.<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> During her early time as Tarkin's protégé, Zahra wore a plaque with two red squares above two blue; as a Lieutenant Commander and Commander, her plaque featured four red squares above four blue. She occasionally wore a [[Imperial kepi|cap]] with an [[officer's disk]] during her time under Tarkin's tuition, although did not as a commander. On Eriadu, Zahra wore a brown [[cloak]] over her officer's uniform. During the hunt, she fashioned a stone [[dagger]], as well as a wooden crutch. While on Ikkrukk, Zahra wore a black longcoat.<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

In her hunt for the rebel fleet, Zahra commanded the ''Tarkin's Will'', an ''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer formerly known as the ''Fortitude'', which had been severely damaged in the destruction of the Death Star. She did not wish to repair the damage the Star Destroyer had sustained, keeping it as a memorial of the attack.<ref name="Star Wars 3" /> Zahra kept an office aboard the ''Tarkin's Will'', in which she displayed numerous weapons, including her blade and the stone dagger<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> from Eriadu,<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> and other trophies, such as mounted creature heads.<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> During her mission to Ikkrukk, Zahra utilized the ''Carrion Spike'', Tarkin's vessel. She also commandeered a ''Lambda''-class shuttle shortly before the Death Star's destruction.<ref name="Star Wars 7" /> When boarding the rebel frigate at Elessia, Zahra utilized a ''Sentinel''-class landing craft.<ref name="Star Wars 8" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ellian Zahra was introduced in the [[Star Wars (2020) 1|first issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars (2020)|''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series, written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Jesús Saiz]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> on [[January 1]] of that year.<ref name="Marvel SW1">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/80507/star_wars_2020_1|text=Star Wars (2020) #1}}</ref> Her forename was provided in the series' [[Star Wars (2020) 8|eighth issue]], also written by Soule<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> and published on [[November 4]], 2020.<ref name="Marvel SW8">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/80517/star_wars_2020_8|text=Star Wars (2020) #8}}</ref> Prior to the release of the comic, Zahra was first mentioned in an article on Marvel's website that covered the series' announcement on [[October 2]], [[2019]].<ref name="NYCC 19">{{Marvel|url=articles/comics/coming-in-january-star-wars-1-and-darth-vader-1|text=NYCC 2019: Coming Next Year, 'Star Wars' #1 and 'Darth Vader' #1}}</ref> In the series' first issue, Commander Zahra's uniform is depicted with a plaque featuring three red and three blue squares,<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> although later issues depict her plaque with four squares of each color.<ref name="Star Wars 3" /><ref name="Star Wars 8" />
