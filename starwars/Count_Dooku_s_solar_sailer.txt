'''Count Dooku's solar sailer''', also known as '''Count Dooku's ''Punworcca 116''-class interstellar sloop''', was a [[Punworcca 116-class interstellar sloop|''Punworcca 116''-class interstellar sloop]] owned by Count Dooku after receiving it as a gift from the [[Geonosian]]s. He used it as a private [[transport]] throughout the [[Clone Wars]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:DookusSolarSailer-SWBC61.png|thumb|left|250px|Dooku's sloop with solar sail deployed]] 
[[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[solar sailer]] was 16.76 [[meter]]s long and originally a ''Punworcca 116''-class sloop manufactured by the [[Huppla Pasa Tisc Shipwrights Collective]]. It could attain a maximum speed of 1,600 [[Kilometer per hour|kph]] and accelerate at 1000G with its sail, and 30G if utilizing its backup repulsor [[engine]]s. Additionally, it contained a Class 1.5 [[hyperdrive]] and featured 84 narrow [[Tractor beam|tractor]]/[[Repulsorlift|repulsor]] beams for defense, and had enough room to hold ten people.<ref name="SWJB" /> Count Dooku instructed the [[Geonosian]]s to add a solar energy-collecting sail to allow the [[Starship|ship]] to fly without fuel. When the sail deployed, the absorbed energy pulled the ship through space at sublight speeds. Continuing the aesthetic of other Geonosian vessels, the ship featured two bow prongs that extended beyond the cockpit orb. A single [[FA-4 pilot droid]] flew the craft while Count Dooku enjoyed an onboard databook library in a luxuriously decorated interior.<ref name="Ships of the Galaxy" />
