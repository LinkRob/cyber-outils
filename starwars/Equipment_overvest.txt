'''Equipment overvests''' were a piece of [[clothing]] worn by the [[rebel pilot]]s of [[Gray Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gray Squadron]] over pressurised [[Environment suit|g-suit]] overalls. They contained an emergency [[Breath mask|breathing system]] that could be linked to the [[pilot]]'s [[starship]] or operate independently for a limited [[time]]. Pilots [[Ekelarc Yong]] and [[Gureni Telsij]] wore equipment overvests during the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="HC" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Equipment overvests first appeared in the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> They were first identified in the [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 26|twenty-sixth issue]] of ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'', which was released in [[2016]].<ref name="HC" />
