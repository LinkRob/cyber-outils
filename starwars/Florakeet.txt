{{Quote|Not that I could see&mdash;transport was attacked by the Uugteen. Such primitives, you know? They burned that thing good on the outside. Roasted it like a florakeet before plucking its feathers.|Temmin Wexley|audio=Temmin-Florakeet-Aftermath.flac|<ref name="Aftermath" />}}
A '''florakeet''' was a type of feathered creature that could be roasted and plucked. When being questioned by [[Surat Nuat's gang|thugs]] working for the [[gangster]] [[Surat Nuat]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Battle of Endor]] and the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the [[junk dealer]] [[Temmin Wexley]] claimed that a [[Unidentified transport (Surat Nuat)|transport]] belonging to Nuat had been roasted and plucked like a florakeet by members of the [[Uugteen]] [[species]] prior to Wexley [[Scavenger|claiming]] its contents, which he falsely told them meant he had not known who owned the [[starship]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Florakeets were mentioned in the [[2015]] novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
