'''Crakka''' was a [[Hutt]] who was the cousin of the fellow Hutt [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], leader of the [[Hutt Clan]]. Crakka became estranged from Jabba after threatening to [[Death|kill]] him. Several [[Standard decade|decades]] later, Crakka visited Jabba at [[Boba Fett's Palace|his Palace]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. During the visit Crakka brought Jabba a [[hunanetre]] for his menagerie; claiming it was an apology for the previous threat.<ref name="RTVC 4" /> 

By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Crakka had interfered with the [[B'omarr Order|B'omarr Monks]] that patrolled [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The result of which led to Crakka meeting a fate that other members of Jabba's retinue described as worse than [[death]] and Jabba's [[majordomo]] [[Bib Fortuna]] writting a warning to the denizens of the palace in which he said that Crakka's fate should serve as a cautionary tale to not disturb the B'omarr Monks.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> a [[thief]] named [[Kay Vess]] broke into Jabba's Palace in search of her [[Merqaal]] companion [[Nix]] who had been sold to Jabba. While at the palace Vess read a [[datapad]] which included Fortuna's warning about Crakka.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}
