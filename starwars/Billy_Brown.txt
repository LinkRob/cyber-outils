'''Billy Brown''' is an actor in the film and gaming industries. He voiced Master [[Zez-Kai Ell]], Admiral [[Cede]] and additional characters in ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]''. He also voiced [[Chop'aa Notimo]] and [[Kleef]] in ''[[The Force Unleashed (game)|Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]''.
