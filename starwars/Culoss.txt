'''Culoss''' was the name of a band of [[pirate]]s that were originally part of [[Q'anah's Marauders]]. They notably hijacked the [[freighter]] ''[[Dromedar]]'' to steal its cargo of [[tibanna]] [[gas]] and captured its crew. After being boarded by members of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Navy|Navy]] [[Starship|vessel]] ''[[Blood Crow]]'' led by [[Lieutenant]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], they pretended to be part of the ''Dromedar''{{'s}} crew and captured the Imperial boarding party. Most of the members successfully stole the tibanna gas and evacuated the freighter, while three members were captured and interrogated by Thrawn at an [[Unidentified Imperial base (Ansion)|Imperial base]] on [[Ansion]]. Using the information Thrawn gathered both directly and indirectly, Imperial forces were able to launch counter operations on [[Uba]] and [[Cartherston]], and after receiving intelligence, Imperial forces planned to launch an operation on [[Kril'Dor]].<ref name="Thrawn" />
