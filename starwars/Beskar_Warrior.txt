The '''Beskar Warrior''' was a set of [[armor]] worn by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Clan Tal Warrior|standard costume]].<ref name="Hunters" /> It consisted of a suit of [[Mandalorian rally master armor]], once associated with [[Mandalorian]] [[Rally master]]s, battlefield [[commander]]s who wore unique [[Mandalorian rally master armor|armor]] during the [[Wars of Mandalorian expansion|Mandalorians' wars of expansion]],<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> though unlike the traditional [[Color|crimson]] coloration, this set was instead gold, gray, blue, black, and red, being made at least partially of [[beskar]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Beskar Warrior was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Aran Tal, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 6.0.0 alongside Season 6 on [[April 15]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Beskar MT-97]]. This costume was unlocked immediately for all players at the start of Season 6.<ref name="Hunters" />
