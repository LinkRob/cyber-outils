Prior to [[46 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> there was a violent '''encounter''' between elements of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Judicial Forces]] and the [[pirate]] gang called [[Q'anah's Marauders]] in the [[Senex-Juvex]]. The battle led to the destruction of six Judicial [[Starship|ships]], which prompted the Republic to place a [[bounty]] on the head of pirate [[queen]] [[Q'anah (pirate)|Q'anah]], forcing her to relocate the group's activities to the [[Greater Seswenna]].<ref name="Tarkin" />
