During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], an [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|ST-series tactical droid]], [[Commander]] [[Aut-O]], commanded a large '''fleet''' deep within Separatist-controlled [[Realspace|space]]. The [[fleet]] consisted of one [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]], at least five [[Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class light destroyers]], and at least three [[Separatist Gozanti-class cruiser|Separatist ''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] variants.<ref name="Secret Weapons" /> In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">{{TCWcanondates|510}}</ref> the fleet was [[Mission to steal a Separatist encryption module|infiltrated]] by a [[D-Squad|squad]] of Republic [[droid]]s led by the [[Zilkin]] [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]], who were sent to steal an [[encryption module]] from the ''Providence''-class [[Dreadnought]] [[Aut-O's flagship|flagship]] to decipher an intercepted and encoded transmission of [[General]] [[Grievous]] and discover what the Confederacy was planning.<ref name="Secret Weapons" />
