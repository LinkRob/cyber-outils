'''Dyan-thread''' was a material that was used to construct [[fishing]] lines on<ref name="Empire's End" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="MT">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref> [[Chandrila]]. A Chandrilan fishing technique involved pulling up [[fish]] one at a [[time]] with lines made of braided dyan-thread.<ref name="Empire's End" /> By [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' include the [[Battle of Jakku]], which is dated to [[5 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Since [[fishing]] with dyan-thread was considered "old" during the events of ''Empire's End'', it must have been old by 5 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> the technique was considered old, and the use of dyan-thread was limited to organic-led fishing rigs.<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dyan-thread was mentioned in the [[2017]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', the third novel in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]'' by [[Chuck Wendig]].<ref name="Empire's End" />
