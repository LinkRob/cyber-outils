'''Calrissian Enterprises''', formerly '''Vylar Tech''', was a company that specialized in producing [[droid]]s based in [[Cloud City]] on [[Bespin]] that was led by [[Impresario]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. Following the [[Battle of Endor]], Calrissian Enterprises was responsible for a large percentage of all the droids in Cloud City. The [[Solo family]]'s [[Cooking droid|kitchen droid]] [[BX-778]] was produced by them, and because he was a household unit, they imbued him with a personality.<ref name="Last Shot" />
