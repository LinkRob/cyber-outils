'''Baroli''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> located in the [[Baroli system]] within the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Baroli sector]]. It was situated in grid square N-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was connected to the [[Chardaan system|Chardaan]] and [[Pax system]]s via the [[Hydian Way]]<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> [[super-hyperroute]].<ref name="Build the Millennium Falcon 29">{{BuildFalconCite|29|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>

The celestial body was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Barolian]]s [[Jano Brayson]] and [[Teff]], who had become [[pilot]]s in the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> by [[231 BBY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'' establishes [[Jano Brayson]] and [[Teff]] had become [[pilot]]s in the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] by the time of the [[Battle of Galov]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[231 BBY]].</ref> after the two Barolians reunited on the Republic [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]]<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Eiram system]],<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Teff commented that they were a long way from Baroli, which Brayson agreed with.<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baroli was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in flashbacks in ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', a [[2021]] audio drama written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The spelling of the astronomical object's name was later confirmed with the release of the audio drama's [[The High Republic: Tempest Runner (script)|script]] in [[2022]].<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> The [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia">{{CSWECite|I|337|Ghent, Zakarisz}}</ref> [[Baroli/Legends|Baroli]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[Heir to the Empire Sourcebook]]'', a [[1992]] sourcebook written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and published by [[West End Games]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Heir to the Empire Sourcebook">''[[Heir to the Empire Sourcebook]]''</ref>
