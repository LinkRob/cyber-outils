A '''[[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]]''' was placed under the command of [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Bane had been hired by [[Darth Sidious]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], to retrieve a crystal containing the names of every [[Force-sensitive]] child known to the [[Jedi Order]] from its keeper, [[Jedi Master]] [[Bolla Ropal]]. In order to facilitate his mission, Sidious, as the secret leader of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], ordered [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] member [[Nute Gunray]] to give Bane the ship and an army of [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="Cargo of Doom" />

When [[Galactic Republic]] forces led by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] infiltrated the ship, the [[self-destruct mechanism]] was initiated. The ship ultimately exploded, although the Republic forces were able to escape. Bane himself escaped after killing the [[clone trooper]]s [[Koho]] and [[Denal]], taking the latter's armor and sneaking aboard amongst the Republic survivors.<ref name="Cargo of Doom" />
