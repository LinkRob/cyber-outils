The '''Dark Saber Pattern''' was a custom [[lightsaber]] [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Rieve]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Sith Saber Pattern|standard lightsaber]]. It served as a replica of one of [[Darth]] [[Maul]]'s [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|lightsabers]] when not connected to form a [[Double-bladed lightsaber|double-blade]].<ref name="SWH" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dark Saber Pattern was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Rieve's Sith Saber Pattern, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.20.0 on [[March 28]], [[2024]] during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Sith Lord (Rieve)|Sith Lord]]. This wrap could be unlocked by earning ranks on the 0.20.0 Arena Pass' track.<ref name="SWH" />
