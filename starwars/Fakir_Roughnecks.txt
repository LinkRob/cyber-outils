The '''Fakir Roughnecks'''<ref name="Dawn" /> was a rebel cell active in the [[Fakir sector]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> during the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|period of early rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]] prior to the formation of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> proper in [[2 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The group was still active during the preceding [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Alliance and the Empire, and at that [[time]] was led by the [[parent]]s of the [[human]] [[Dynia Reekeene]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Fakir Roughnecks were first mentioned indirectly in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> The group was then identified in the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by Hidalgo and [[Emily Shkoukani]].<ref name="Dawn" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Reekeene's Roughnecks]] were a rebel group active in the [[Fakir sector/Legends|Fakir sector]] that first appeared in "[[Tests of the Godking]]," an adventure written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and published in the [[1988]] ''[[Star Wars Campaign Pack]]'' for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' by [[West End Games]].<ref name="Campaign">{{WEGCite|story=Tests of the Godking|book=Star Wars Campaign Pack}}</ref>
