'''Chott el Djerid''' ([[Wikipedia:Arabic language|Arabic]]: شط الجريد) is a dry salt lake in southwest [[Tunisia]].

The salt flats were used for the exterior of the [[Lars homestead/Legends|Lars homestead]] on [[Tatooine]]. The interior was filmed in [[Hôtel Sidi Driss]], which is located in Matmata, 300 kilometers away.

Lucasfilm and crew returned here to shoot the [[Tatooine]] sequences for [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Attack of the Clones'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']]. The sets from [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|the original movie]]&mdash;which had been taken down&mdash;were rebuilt in [[2000]] to the exact specifications from the original plans, on the exact same spot and with attention to detail to recreate the very same building as from twenty-five years earlier.

The site was renovated by fans between [[2010]] [[2011|and]] [[2012]]. The man-made craters are still there.
