'''Alo Kortessi''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Clawdite]] [[princess]]. In [[4 ABY]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] operative [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] impersonated her during a [[Mission to obtain Embersteel wings|mission]] to obtain [[embersteel]] wings. The real Kortessi was arrested by [[stormtrooper]]s, who mistook her for an impostor.

==Biography==

[[File:Freemakers_undercover_Embersteel_Blade.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Kordi Freemaker impersonates Kortessi]]
Alo Kortessi was a [[Clawdite]] [[princess]] and a member of the [[Clawdite royal family]]. In [[4 ABY]], she attended the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Auction on Mygeeto|auction]] on [[Mygeeto]], which was selling [[starship]]s owned by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] sympathizers. The rebel operative [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] impersonated her while her brothers [[Zander Freemaker|Zander]] and [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan]] disguised themselves as her entourage. Kordi managed to use her purported royal status to bully the guards into letting her through. When the real Kortessi arrived, the [[stormtrooper]]s mistook her for an impostor and arrested her.<ref name="The Embersteel Blade" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alo Kortessi first appeared in the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' [[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures Season Two|Season Two]] episode "[[The Embersteel Blade]]," which premiered on [[August 1]], [[2017]]. She was voiced by [[Kari Wahlgren]].
