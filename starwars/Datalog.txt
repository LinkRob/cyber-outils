'''Datalogs''',<ref name="SC 105" /> also known as '''logs''',<ref name="SC 107">{{SkeletonCrew|We're Gonna Be In So Much Trouble}}</ref> were a kind of data storage device utilized by [[pirate]] [[captain]]s.

A figure well-known in pirate lore, Captain [[Tak Rennod]], kept his own datalog, in which he hid the coordinates to the legendary [[planet]] [[At Attin]],<ref name="SC 105" /> a [[Jewels of the Old Republic|Jewel of the Old Republic]] yielding massive supplies of [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="SC 103">{{SkeletonCrew|Very Interesting, As an Astrogation Problem}}</ref> Before his [[death]], Rennod had left the datalog in [[Captain Rennod's Lair|his lair]] on [[Lanupa]].<ref name="SC 105" /> Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SCDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> the lair was penetrated by former pirate Captain [[Jod Na Nawood|Silvo]] and four [[youngling]]s from<ref name="SC 105" /> the [[Skeleton Crew]],<ref name="T19XifsgFS0">{{SWYouTube|T19XifsgFS0|Skeleton Crew &#124; Blast from the Past &#124; Streaming Dec 2 on Disney+}}</ref> who hailed from At Attin. With the [[droid]] [[SM-33]]'s help, Silvo accessed Rennod's datalog and obtained the coordinates to the planet.<ref name="SC 105" />

==Behind the scenes==
The datalog made its [[canon]]ical debut in "[[You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates]]," the fifth episode of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'',<ref name="SC 105" /> which was released on [[December 24]], [[2024]].<ref name="Premiere">{{Twitter|StarWars|status/1861409832349172011|[[Star Wars]]|quote=Mark your calendars. 🗓️ On December 2, stream the two-episode series premiere of #SkeletonCrew, only on @DisneyPlus.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/QwMYe}}</ref> 

Before ''Skeleton Crew'', datalogs were presented in the non-canon game ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'' as a form of physical media that could hold a written message. The [[astromech droid]] [[R2-R9]] was tasked with delivering a datalog to a spatula-wielding [[Gungan]] in the [[city]] of [[Otoh Gunga]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. R2-R9 discovered that the many slopes of the city did not agree with its treads and asked another individual to deliver the message, which was from the spatula's original owner and asked for its return so that they could cook [[fish]]. The message was delivered, and the Gungan agreed to return the spatula, which he had been using as a [[slug-beetle]] swatter.<ref name="LEGO">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''</ref>

In [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]], the [[Datalog/Legends|datalog]] is a merchant's electronic catalogue.
