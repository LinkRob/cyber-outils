'''Death magic''' was a branch of [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] magic practiced by the [[Presagers of Hakotei]].

==Description==
{{Quote|The Sith reborn.|[[Darth Skrye]] in Dooku's vision during the ritual|<ref name="Jedi Lost" />}}
The Presagers used living sacrifices, specifically their fresh [[blood]], to see [[Force vision|visions]] of the future. In order to use their magic, the Presagers needed a [[Vergence|nexus]] for the dark side of the Force, such as those found on [[Hakotei]] or [[Asusto]]. They also believed that a "conduit" would come along that would allow them to use their magic to see every possible future. When he came to Asusto with [[Jedi Master]] [[Lene Kostana]] and her [[Padawan]] [[Sifo-Dyas]], the Presagers believed a young [[Dooku]] was such a conduit. As a result of their ritual, he saw many potential futures, including futures in which the [[Jedi]] conquered the [[Galactic Republic]]. He also saw a vision of his future [[Sith Master|Master]], [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
Death magic first appeared in the [[2019]] audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', which was written by [[Cavan Scott]].<ref name="Jedi Lost" />
