'''Aksind''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[stormtrooper]] who held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[sergeant]] under [[Commander]] [[Kimmund]]'s [[Kimmund's unit|command]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. As a [[Trooper|member]] of the [[1st Legion]], the [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] personal [[legion]], Aksind and his fellow [[soldier]]s accompanied the [[Sith Lord]] on his [[Mission to Batuu (Galactic Empire)|mission]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Batuu]] where they captured a group of [[Grysk warrior]]s. Sergeant Aksind was [[Death|killed]] by the [[Grysk]] [[prisoner]]s while defending the First Legion's personal [[transport]], the ''[[Darkhawk]]'', when they [[Skirmish aboard the Chimaera|attempted to escape]] from [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]''.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />
