Around [[22 BBY]] during the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] '''attacked''' the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]-controlled [[planet]] [[Aargonar]]. A contingent of [[clone trooper]]s attacked a newly established [[Aargonar Separatist base|Separatist base]] and [[Death|killed]] [[Mina Bonteri's husband]], who had served as an [[officer]] in the [[Confederacy military]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|A year ago next week, ''[my husband]'' was setting up a base on Aargonar when clones attacked.|Mina Bonteri|<ref name="HoBS" />}}
During the [[the galaxy]]-wide [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the [[planet]] [[Aargonar]] was under the control of the Confederacy.<ref name="Timelines" /> Aargonar was selected to be the site of a [[Aargonar Separatist base|new base]], which was established under the oversight of a [[Mina Bonteri's husband|Separatist officer]]<ref name="HoBS" /> who had a vehement belief in the Confederate cause.<ref name="Lux Databank">{{Databank|lux-bonteri|Lux Bonteri}}</ref> The [[Separatist officer|officer]] also happened to be the [[Marriage|husband]] of Separatist [[Senator]] [[Mina Bonteri]], who<ref name="HoBS" /> shared<ref name="Lux Databank" /> his devotion to the Confederacy. Together, the two had a [[Reproduction|son]] named [[Lux Bonteri|Lux]] some [[Standard year|years]] before the war.<ref name="HoBS" />

==The attack==
Around [[22 BBY]], the first year of the war, the Republic targeted and attacked Aargonar.<ref name="Timelines" /> A contingent of [[clone trooper]]s attacked the yet to be completed base. Bonteri's husband defended himself and fought with merit, but he was ultimately killed<ref name="HoBS" /> by the [[Cloning|clones]].<ref name="Lux Databank" />

==Aftermath==
The death of Bonteri's husband left her a widow and Lux without his father.<ref name="HoBS" /> Much to Mina's concern, Lux grew increasingly distant after the death of his father.<ref name="Mina SWE">{{SWE|characters|minabonteri|Mina Bonteri}}</ref> Almost a full year since his passing, only missing the anniversary by one [[Standard week|week]], the Bonteris were visited by Republic Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], who hoped to reach a diplomatic solution to the war and arrived with [[Ahsoka Tano]], a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] and [[Jedi Commander|commander]] in the [[Republic Military]].<ref name="HoBS" />
