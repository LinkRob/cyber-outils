The '''Drualkiin system''' was located in the [[Kessel sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and [[the Slice]], in grid square T-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="OuterRim" /> It was connected by the [[Triellus Trade Route]] [[hyperspace route]] to the [[Ku'Bakai system|Ku'Bakai]]<ref name="Triellus" /> and [[Formos system]]s.<ref name="BTM">{{BuildFalconCite|29|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Drualkiin [[Star system|system]] was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "Major Trade Routes,"<ref name="BTM" /> an article written by [[Mark Newbold]]<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> and included in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 29|twenty-ninth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine<ref name="BTM" /> that was published around [[July 22]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMF29date">{{BMFdates|29}}</ref> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Drualkiin system/Legends|Drualkiin system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[The Truce at Bakura Sourcebook]]'', a [[1996]] sourcebook published for use with [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="TTaBS">''[[The Truce at Bakura Sourcebook]]''</ref>
