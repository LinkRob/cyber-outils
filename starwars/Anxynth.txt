An '''anxynth''', also known as the '''nightmare bloom''', was a type of [[plant]] [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Lokud 7]]. It had [[poison]]ous but non-[[Death|deadly]] thorns yet the poison had a hallucinogenic properties. When Vader was on Lokud 7, he brushed against these thorns and began having nightmarish visions.<ref name="Visions 5" />
