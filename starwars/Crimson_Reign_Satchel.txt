A '''Crimson Reign Satchel''' was a type of [[Clothing|satchel]] used by [[Spy|spies]] and agents with the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime [[syndicate]] to transport classified intel. It was red in [[color]] with a gold stripe that ran down the middle and connected at the front with a grey clasp.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] was gifted a satchel after she gained an excellent reputation with the criminal organization.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A Crimson Reign Satchel appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, it was a cosmetic that could be used to customize the appearance of Nix, Vess' [[merqaal]] companion.<ref name="Outlaws" />
