'''Davin Bryce''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[captain]] who commanded the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Stormbringer]]''.<ref name="TTaT 2" /> A [[battalion]] of [[stormtrooper]]s [[Mission to Tibrin|subdued]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and decided to take him to the ''Stormbringer'' so Bryce could interrogate him.<ref name="TTaT" /> When [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] staged a rescue, the ''Stormbringer'' was destroyed when Bryce failed to raise the sheild in time to counterattack the [[Ishi Tib fleet]].<ref name="TTaT 2" />
