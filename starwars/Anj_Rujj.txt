'''Anj Rujj''' was a [[Color|light]]-skinned, blond member of the gang of highly educated [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] known as the [[Thugs of Thule]] during the [[Republic Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="CL" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[22 BBY]].</ref> Rujj was in the [[Outlander Club]] in the [[Uscru District]] of [[Coruscant]], [[capital]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. While in the club, Rujj wore red [[Clothing|shoes]], yellow pants, a red shirt, and a yellow [[scarf]], and walked past the [[Rodian]] [[Tyyx]] while the [[Jedi]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|interrogated]] the [[bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell]] outside.<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Anj Rujj was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. The image including Rujj<ref name="CL" /> was illustrated by [[Hans Jenssen]]<ref name="Artist">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hansjenssen.co.uk/star-wars-locations|text=''Star Wars Locations''|author=[[Hans Jenssen]]|archivedate=20190517022701}}</ref> and was part of material reprinted<ref name="CL" /> from the [[2003]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]'', which was written by [[Simon Beecroft]] and [[Curtis Saxton]] and marked the first mention of the [[Anj Rujj/Legends|character]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]''</ref>
