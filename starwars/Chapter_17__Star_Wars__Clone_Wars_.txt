"'''Chapter 17'''" is the seventeenth episode of the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' animated television series, the seventh episode in [[Clone Wars: Season Two|Season Two]], and the seventeenth episode in [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume One|Volume One]]. Directed by [[Genndy Tartakovsky]], the episode originally aired on [[April 5]], [[2004]].

==Plot summary==
The ''[[Azure Angel]]'' drops out of [[Hyperspace/Legends|hyperspace]], and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] guides the [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]] in to land on the jungle [[Moon/Legends|moon]] of [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]]. As he heads off to investigate, Skywalker hears the sound of another ship. Back at the landing site, he sees the squad of [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]] sent by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] disembarking from their [[CR20 troop carrier/Legends|transport]]. As the clones fan out to search the jungle, they are taken out one-by-one by someone using [[The Force/Legends|the Force]]. Skywalker realizes something is wrong and returns to the landing site to see the transport and his starfighter explode, destroying [[R4-P22]]. The [[Droid/Legends|droid's]] charred dome flies out of the flaming wreckage to land at the [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan's]] feet.

[[Asajj Ventress/Legends|Asajj Ventress]] calmly walks out of the flames and reveals herself as she stands on the wreckage of Skywalker's ship.
