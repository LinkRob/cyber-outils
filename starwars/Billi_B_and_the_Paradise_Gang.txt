'''Billi B and the Paradise Gang''' was a [[band]] that had performed the [[Music|song]] "[[Astral Love]]"<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]]. By the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> "Astral Love" had added to the [[jukebox]] at [[the Broken Hoof]] [[Cantina|bar]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]] and the jukebox at the [[Bestine Cantina]] on the planet [[Tatooine]]. The cover art for "Astral Love" featured the band's name written across the bottom in [[Huttese (alternative writing system)|Huttese]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Billi B and the Paradise Gang was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] continuity in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]], published by [[Ubisoft]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> and released on [[August 30]], [[2024]].<ref name="Story trailer">{{SWYouTube|DUyk6D5rgcE|Star Wars Outlaws: Official Story Trailer}}</ref> In the game, the band's name is shown to players when they are selecting songs from the specific jukeboxes.<ref name="Outlaws" /> "Astral Love" was composed for the game by [[Cody Matthew Johnson]]<ref name="Songs from the Underworld">{{OST|Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)}}</ref> and released the same day as the game<ref name="qobuz">{{WebCite|url=https://www.qobuz.com/us-en/album/songs-from-the-underworld-cody-matthew-johnson/okblkfn26uyuc|text=Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)|work=[https://www.qobuz.com Qobuz]|archivedate=20240908212835}}</ref> as part of the soundtrack ''[[Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)]]'', which listed Billi B and the Paradise Gang as a "Featured Artist."<ref name="Songs from the Underworld">{{OST|Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)}}</ref>

[[Billi B and the Paradise Gang/Legends|Billi B and the Paradise Gang]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity; first being mentioned in the short story "[[Slaying Dragons]], which was written by [[Angela Phillips]] and published in [[February]] [[1996]] in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 9|ninth issue]] of the [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game|roleplaying]] magazine ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'' by [[West End Games]].<ref name="Slaying Dragons">{{JournalCite|9|Slaying Dragons|reprint=1}}</ref>
