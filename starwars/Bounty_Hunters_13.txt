'''''Bounty Hunters'' 13''' is the thirteenth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. It was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[June 9]], [[2021]]. It features the story "'''Showdown on Smuggler's Moon'''," in which [[Beilert Valance]] and [[Dengar]] encounter [[Chewbacca]] on [[Nar Shaddaa]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE WOOKIEE ALWAYS WINS! Hot on the trail of BOBA FETT, VALANCE and DENGAR run into a rather large obstacle&mdash;the mighty CHEWBACCA! Will T'ONGA escape the trap set by a mysterious organization out to upend the underworld? And who is the figure in the shadows hunting them all?!''
