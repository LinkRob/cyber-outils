'''Duraceph'''<ref name="Acolyte VG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="LF" /> [[Lobereki]] was a member of this species.<ref name="Acolyte VG" />

==Biology and appearance==
Duraceph was a bipedal [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] distinguished by their fleshy appearances, with [[Color|blue]] and tan skin, as well as curved horns that extend from the sides of the head and reattach at the cheeks, all covered in a thick layer of [[hair]].<ref name="LF" />

==Species in the galaxy==
In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> [[Lobereki]], a member of the species who worked as a courier,<ref name="Acolyte VG" /> was seated at a table in the<ref name="LF" /> [[Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop]] on the [[planet]] [[Ueda]]<ref name="ShopDatabank">{{Databank|lomi-usqi-noodle-shop|Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop}}</ref> with [[Jedi Master]] [[Indara]] and other patrons, before engaging in a fight with a [[Mae-ho Aniseya|mysterious assassin]] who ultimately bested them and killed their [[Jedi]] associate.<ref name="LF" />

==Behind the scenes==
The species made their debut in the [[Lost / Found|first episode]] of the [[2024]] television series, ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', directed by [[Leslye Headland]].<ref name="LF" /> Their name was revealed within the ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''<ref name="Acolyte VG" /> which released in select countries on [[June 5]], [[2025]].<ref name="DK UK">{{DK|url=uk/book/9780241710425-star-wars-the-acolyte-visual-guide|text=Star Wars The Acolyte Visual Guide}}</ref>
