The '''Committee of Democratic Implementation''' was a committee within the [[New Republic]] that received a report in the early [[Standard year|years]] of the state's existence that detailed how the New Republic needed to provide its citizens not just with new laws, but also an understanding of what the fair and consistent application of justice and law looked like after the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The report was stored in the "Legal Framework" section of the [[New Republic Archives]] and the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned the committee when citing it in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Committee of Democratic Implementation was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
