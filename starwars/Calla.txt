'''Calla''' was a [[human]], [[Gender|female]] from the planet [[Naboo]], who went to [[Canto Bight]] as a tourist, but soon lost all her money and, unable to leave, took a job as a hotel receptionist. Later she became the Grammus sister's servant after she lost a gamble against [[Rhomby Grammus]]. She helped the sisters do a business deal with [[Derla Pidys]], and afterwards got a new job in another hotel thanks to the sisters.<ref name="Wine in Dreams" />
