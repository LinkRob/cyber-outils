A [[human]] [[Gender|woman]] known as the '''"Data Server"''' was an operative for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. They received data contained in a camtono from [[Bespinite]] [[Willrow Hood]], while in [[Cloud City]] over the [[planet]] [[Bespin]]. After assuring Hood that the data would assure Rebels "got fed" with data, they started distributing [[Ice cream]] from a [[Color|pink]] and white stand outside the [[Administrator's Palace|Admin Building]].<ref name="TSS" />
