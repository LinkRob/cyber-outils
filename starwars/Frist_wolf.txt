{{Quote|Have to do this the old-fashioned way&mdash;as my ancestors hunted ''frist wolves''.|Sskeer|<ref name="The High Republic 2">[[The High Republic (2021) 2|''The High Republic'' (2021) 2]]</ref>}}
'''Frist wolves''' were a type of [[wolf]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Trandosha]]. They were [[Hunting|hunted]] by the native [[Trandoshan]] [[species]], whose culture was firmly rooted in hunting, often with the aid of [[Trandoshan hunting hound]]s.<ref name="Kashyyyk and Other Planets" />

While [[Attack in the Kazlin system|searching]] for a [[Nihil]] [[Unidentified Nihil sniper|sniper]] aboard a derelict [[Hutt Clan]] [[Starship|ship]]<ref name="The High Republic 2" /> in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic (2021) 2|''The High Republic'' (2021) 2]] to [[232 BBY]].</ref> Trandoshan [[Jedi Master]] [[Sskeer]] stated he would need to do so like his ancestors had hunted frist wolves. After he discovered the Nihil's [[Blaster cannon|blaster]] on the ship's floor, Sskeer referred to the [[sniper]] as a frist wolf who had dropped their [[blaster]].<ref name="The High Republic 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Frist wolves were first mentioned in the [[The High Republic (2021) 2|second issue]] of the [[2021]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']], released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="The High Republic 2" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[February 3]], 2021.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/90347/star_wars_the_high_republic_2021_2|text=Star Wars: The High Republic (2021) #2}}</ref>
