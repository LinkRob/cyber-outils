'''Arro''' was a [[human]] [[Kessel Sabacc]] [[gambler]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> they frequently played against the [[Melitto]] [[Mehdo]] the [[Pantoran]] [[Kolimaag]] in [[the Domak Refectory|Domak Refectory]] in [[Kijimi City|Kijimi city]]. Arro had [[Color|tan]] skin, brown hair and brown eyes.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arro first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Ubisoft]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. They were one of several sabacc players that could be played against by [[Kay Vess]] in Kessel Sabacc, an optional activity.<ref name="Outlaws" />
