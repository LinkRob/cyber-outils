{{Quote| After getting the role, I really went back to all of the Star Wars women and sort of aligned myself with them, if that makes sense. There is this willingness to risk that they all have: to fight for what they believe in, to take action for their beliefs. And there is a confidence too, a determination; not only that they will succeed, but that they will be heard... Or that they will make themselves heard, even in the face of great opposition. I love that about Steela. You totally get the sense that this girl can and will, literally, change her world.|Dawn-Lyen Gardner|<ref name="Steela Interview">{{SW|url=/news/interview-dawn-lyen-gardner-steela-gerrera-on-the-clone-wars|text=Interview: Dawn-Lyen Gardner -- Steela Gerrera on The Clone Wars}}</ref>}}
'''Dawn-Lyen Gardner''' is an [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice artist]] who voiced [[Steela Gerrera]] in four episodes of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated series.<ref name="War on Two Fronts" /> Prior to recorder she was friends with [[Andrew Kishino]], the voice of Steela's brother, [[Saw Gerrera]].<ref name="Steela Interview" /> Gardner is set to lend her voice in the [[2025]] [[Disney+]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Underworld]]''<ref name="Underworld">{{SW|url=news/tales-of-the-underworld-announce|text=Two Favorite ''Star Wars'' Bounty Hunters are Back for ''Tales of the Underworld''}}</ref>
