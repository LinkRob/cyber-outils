A '''battle baton''' was wielded by [[Gial Ackbar]] when he served as [[Captain]] of the [[Mon Calamari Guard]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

==History==
In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|401}}</ref> [[Captain]] [[Gial Ackbar]] used a battle [[baton]]<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> during the [[Battle of Mon Cala]]. Ackbar the baton while he led the [[Mon Calamari Guard]]. He used the built-in [[blaster]] in the weapon to destroy [[AQ-series battle droid|aqua droids]] and protect [[Prince]] [[Lee-Char]] from danger.<ref name="WaterWar" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The battle baton is based on an accessory of Ackbar's [[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (toy line)|''Return of the Jedi'']] action figure. "[[Prisoners]]" director [[Danny Keller]] remembered that as a child he imagined the baton was a blaster, and thus it was [[canon]]ized.<ref name="Prisoners Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/prisoners-trivia|text="Prisoners" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> The baton first appeared in "[[Water War]]," the first episode from the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth season]].<ref name="WaterWar" />
