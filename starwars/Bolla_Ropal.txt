'''Bolla Ropal''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Rodian]] who served as a [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="COD" /> and [[Jedi General]]<ref name="COD Old EG">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode202.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Cargo of Doom}}</ref> in the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

==Biography==
When he was a [[Jedi Initiate]], Bolla Ropal had a favorite spoon that the other younglings constantly stole from him as a game. After Ropal and his fellows moved up to the rank of [[Padawan]]s, the spoon ended up in [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s possession. Another time, Kenobi swapped out Ropal's [[wash cream]] for [[Naboo glitterpaste]]. According to Kenobi, Ropal shimmered for days. Another time as a youngling, Bolla attempted to slice a piece of cheese to see if the cheese would toast. Unfortunately, it did not; the experiment ended in a mess. When Ropal was assigned to a [[Jedi Master]], he spent extensive time in the Jedi Archives researching holocrons.<ref name="Padawan" /> In [[41 BBY]],<ref name="PadawanDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Padawan (novel)|''Padawan'']] to [[41 BBY]].</ref> Ropal was having a meal with his fellow Padawans when a baby [[demon squid]] hidden in Kenobi's soup attacked Kenobi. As Kenobi panicked, Ropal burst out in laughter as his prank succeeded. Confronted by Kenobi, Ropal explained that he had antidote for the venom and Kenobi was never in any real danger. He continued to laugh as Kenobi left with his master, [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], to go meditate. Later that day, Ropal was walking through the Jedi Temple when he almost collided with [[Count]] [[Dooku]] who was visiting the Temple. The Rodian returned to the Padawan dining hall where he met with [[Prie]], [[Siri Tachi]], and several other Padawans. When Kenobi arrived, Ropal brought up the rumor that Jinn might leave the Jedi Order which upset Kenobi. Ropal followed Kenobi out into the hall and let him know that Jinn hadn't chosen Kenobi as a Padawan but took him on as requested by Master Yoda. He explained that no one would hold it against Kenobi if his Master left the Order.<ref name="Padawan" />

[[File:BollaRopalDead-CoD.png|thumb|left|230px|Ropal's body, as discovered by Republic forces]]
Ropal acted as the keeper of the [[Kyber memory crystal]] during the war, a crystal which contained data on the identities of [[Force-sensitive]] [[Youngling|children]] across [[the galaxy]]. After [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|stole]] the [[holocron]] containing the crystal from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], he [[Battle of Devaron|captured]] Ropal on the planet [[Devaron]] and then [[torture]]d him in an attempt to force the Rodian to open the holocron. Ropal refused and [[Death|died]] during the torture, although Bane later managed to force the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] to open the holocron.<ref name="COD" />

==Personality and traits==
Bolla Ropal was a [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Databank" /> [[Rodian]] Jedi Master,<ref name="COD" /> who was 1.75 [[meter]]s (5 [[Foot|feet]], 9 inches) tall.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> He had [[Color|blue]] [[eye]]s and green skin.<ref name="COD" /> Ropal was very curious and mischievous. He liked to pull pranks on his fellow Padawans and was never happier than when he was examining a holocron.<ref name="Padawan" /> During the Clone War, he served as the guardian of a kyber memory crystal. After Cad Bane stole both the crystal and the holocron, Ropal was resistant to Bane's torturous threats in an attempt to force the Jedi to unlock the holocron. The Jedi sacrificed himself in hopes that Bane would be unable to learn the holocron's secrets.<ref name="COD" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a Jedi, Ropal was trained in [[lightsaber combat]]<ref name="Join the Battle" /> and was powerful in [[Force-sensitive|the Force]].<ref name="COD" /> He was skilled in leadership and as the keeper of the kyber memory crystal, had vast knowledge of [[Jedi]] lore.<ref name="Join the Battle" />
