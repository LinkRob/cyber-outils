The '''Dark Star Hellions''' were a rival biker group of the [[Cloud-Riders]] led by [[Enfys Nest]] during the [[Imperial Era|rule]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The Cloud-Rider's colors were boldly displayed on their [[swoop]] bikes to distinguish themselves from the Dark Star Hellions and the [[Nova Demons]].<ref name="Official Guide" />
