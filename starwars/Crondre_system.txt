The '''Crondre system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Dufilvian sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> Situated in grid square R-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> it contained [[Crondre]], the [[homeworld]] of the [[human]] [[stormtrooper]] [[TK-7919]],<ref name="MandoVG" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[22 BBY]]. In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Dates">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'' states that [[TK-7919]] was thirty-one [[Standard year|years]] old at the [[time]] of their [[death]] during the [[incident on Nevarro]], also placing [[Crondre]] within the borders of the [[New Republic]] at that [[time]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the incident to [[9 ABY]], TK-7919 must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[22 BBY]].</ref> the system fell within the borders of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Crondre system was first mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The [[Crondre system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned indirectly in ''[[The Last Command Sourcebook]]'', a [[1993]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Eric S. Trautmann]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="TLCS">''[[The Last Command Sourcebook]]''</ref> It then appeared for the first time in the [[1998]] [[Coolhand Interactive]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']], although the game misspelled its name as "Chrondre [[Star system/Legends|system]]."<ref name="Chondre">[[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']]</ref> It was then identified as the Crondre system in ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', a [[2009]] reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
