The '''Expansionist Oligarchy''' was a cabal of corporate conglomerates that existed several [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="SWGA" /> The group pushed for the right of [[Planet|world]] development in the [[Outer Expansion Zone]] region from the [[Galactic Republic]]. However, upon acquisition of their rights, the Oligarchy engaged in several cruel labor practices akin to [[slavery]], exploitation of natural resources, and suppression of labor uprisings. Eventually, the Oligarchy was shut down following intervention by the Republic.<ref name="Rise" />

Despite the Republic's intervention, the same organizations and market forces which formed the Expansionist Oligarchy went on to establish the [[Corporate Sector]] some two centuries later. Compromises between the Republic and the corporations resulted in further division in the Republic and the [[Galactic Senate]], pushing the Senate away from civilian interests and more towards the interests of the companies.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Expansionist Oligarchy was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Expansionist Oligarchy/Legends|Expansionist Oligarchy]] was introduced in ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', a [[2009]] reference book authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
