'''Cryogenic power cells''' were a component of both the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Delta-class T-3c shuttle|''Delta''-class T-3c shuttle]]. In the latter craft, the cells were located in the [[shuttle]]'s wings.<ref name="Complete Vehicles">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref>
