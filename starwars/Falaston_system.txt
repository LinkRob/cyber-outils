The '''Falaston system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Kallea sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square K-21 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Falaston]].<ref name="Origin" />

==Behind the scenes==
