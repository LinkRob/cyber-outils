"'''Attack over Tatooine'''" is a comic story written by [[Christian Hector]] that was published in the magauzine [[LEGO Star Wars 1|''LEGO Star Wars'' 1]] by [[Blue Ocean Entertainment]] on [[July 4]], [[2015]].<ref name="jedi-bibliothek" /> It adapts the events of the [[secret mission to Tatooine]] from {{Film|IV}}.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
