'''''Doctor Aphra'' 8''' is the eighth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. It was written by [[Alyssa Wong]]; illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]], [[Victor Olazaba]], and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]]; and first published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[March 24]], [[2021]]. It features the story "'''The Engine Job Part 3: Old Wounds'''," in which [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] and [[Sana Starros]] investigate an ancient battlefield on [[Dol'har Hyde]].

==Publisher's summary==
''OLD WOUNDS! After a narrow escape, APHRA and SANA's investigation takes them to the site of an ancient battlefield! But VUKORAH and the UNBROKEN CLAN are hot on their heels! What answers await them amid the devastation?''

==Plot summary==
===A tip on Dol'har Hyde===
[[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] and [[Sana Starros|Starros]] are in a bar on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Dol'har Hyde]], speaking to the bartender. He says he remembers seeing [[Remy (smuggler)|Remy]] because the man couldn't keep his mouth shut, constantly trying to impress the bartender about a network of buyers interested in his latest score. The bartender thought this was stupid to do in a room full of smugglers.

Remy claimed his score was recovered from an old ship. Outside the window, Aphra observes many ships crashed into the planet's landscape. The bartender explains that ancient marauders tore up the planet, but the forest ultimately "got the last laugh."

===Investigating the Nihil battleship===
Aphra and Starros climb a massive tree until they can see that it has grown around an old [[Nihil]] [[battleship]]. Aphra theorizes that, according to the bartender, Remy found the [[hyperdrive]] in one of these ships and sold it on [[Corellia]] to a buyer from [[Midarr]], whom she believes must be [[Beol De'Rruyet|Beol De'rruyet]]. Starros is skeptical, pointing out that the ships have been picked over for centuries and doubts Remy was truthful.

As they prepare to rappel down the middle of the tree, Aphra asks Starros if she's scared. Starros simply reminds Aphra that she was the one who fell back in university.

During the descent, Aphra explains that she read [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] records of Nihil ships doing "impossible things with [[lightspeed]]"&mdash;jumping close to planets and blinking across battlefields almost instantaneously, like magic. Starros realizes that kind of technology could change the face of the war between the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]] and wonders if De'rruyet thinks he can replicate it.

Suddenly, Aphra's rappel snaps. She falls, crashing through the old ship and landing on thick plant matter that has grown around and through the vessel. Starros follows, and Aphra says she believes they're on the bridge. Starros notes the ship is completely overgrown, with roots coming through the floor and panels, suggesting the technology is destroyed. Aphra, however, starts cutting into a panel to see if it works.

===Meanwhile, on Canto Bight===
At the [[Galanx Casino]] on [[Canto Bight]], [[Just Lucky|Lucky]] cuts a hole out of a window and slides a sniper rifle through it, pointing down at the tracks below. He uses the scope to spot a group of people, including [[Alfar Sholt]], watching the race. Lucky shoots Sholt, causing panic among the assembled guests.

Lucky packs up his rifle and departs, remembering that when he was a kid, he promised himself he'd leave as soon as he got the chance, but "something" was always holding him back. Walking down an alley, he is unhappy to be met by [[Ariole Yu]], a [[Sixth Kin]] [[lieutenant]], who informs him that [[Wen Delphis]] wants to see them.

===The hyperdrive secret===
Back on Dol'har Hyde, Aphra continues to work on the console. When Starros asks what happened to the [[droid]]s Aphra used to have, Aphra says they are "freelancing." Aphra manages to get the ship to start, noting that the interface is strange and old but she can still work it.

She discovers the hyperdrive she is seeing is much more complex than the one Remy is advertising. She realizes this one "makes Remy's fancy engine look like a rock wrapped in string." Aphra fears Remy might not know he only has one part, or that it won't work with the power coils he claims to have—a combination that could be explosive. She explains that [[De'Rruyet Industries|De'rruyet Tech]] is unveiling this dangerous engine at an expo on [[Midarr]].

===Vukorah's ambush===
Suddenly, an explosion rocks their position. They recover to find ships outside their location, led by [[Vukorah|General Vukorah]] of the [[Unbroken Clan]]. As more missiles hit the wreck, Starros urges Aphra to leave, but Aphra refuses to go without the engine diagram.

Aphra explains that she can't rappel as her harness snapped and notices that Starros is bleeding from shrapnel. Nearby, the bar's owner and clients are seen dead, likely killed by Vukorah and her team.

The pair attempt to use a [[grappling hook]] to swing away, but it loses its grip, and they plummet. Vukorah's ships fire on the location again, and she confidently believes no one could have survived the damage. Having heard from the bartender that the hyperdrive was sent to Midarr, Vukorah orders her ships to set a course for the planet immediately.
