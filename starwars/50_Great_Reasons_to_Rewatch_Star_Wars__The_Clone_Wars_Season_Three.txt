"'''50 Great Reasons to Rewatch ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Season Three'''" is an article written by [[Leland Chee]], [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Daniel Wallace]] that appeared inside the magazine [[Star Wars Insider 125|''Star Wars Insider'' 125]]. The article provided a brief overview of [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|Season Three]] of the TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.
