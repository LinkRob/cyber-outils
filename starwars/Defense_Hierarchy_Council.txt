The '''Defense Hierarchy Council''', also known as the '''Hierarchy Defense Council''',<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> was a military council of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] in the [[Imperial Era]]. After a [[Chiss–Grysk skirmish|skirmish with Grysk warships]], [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] of the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] informed [[Imperial Navy]] [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] that she had concerns about their current situation, before informing Thrawn the Council would be furious if what she were to tell him left the room they were in. After Thrawn assured Ar'alani what was said would stay between the two, she told him of potential dangers to [[Chiss]] [[sky-walker]]s [[Un'hee]] and [[Vah'nya]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

The [[Chiss Syndicure|Syndicure]] of the Chiss Ascendancy was not supposed to have any influence over the operations of the [[Chiss Defense Force]], which were ''de jure'' solely the purview of the Defense Hierarchy Council. However, in practice, this separation of branches of government was sometimes broken. The Syndicure could pass laws that regulated the Ascendancy's military, but the Defense Hierarchy Council had ultimate authority over the personnel of the Defense Force, including the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet|Expansionary Defense Fleet]]. In spite of this, the [[Aristocra]] often sought to interfere in Fleet operations. This competition between the Syndicure and the Council often led to members of the Council seeking to "remind" the civilian leaders that the Council had authority over the military.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
