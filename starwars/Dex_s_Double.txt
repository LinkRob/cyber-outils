'''Dex's Double''' was a type of [[Burger/Legends|burger]] including two patties and an oozing blue substance sold<ref name="fb" /> between [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]] and [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{WebCite|url=http://blogs.evtrib.com/nerdvana/events/star-wars-celebration-vi-day-2-announcements-galore/79141|text=Star Wars Celebration VI – Day 2: Announcements galore!|author=Deihl, Dustin|work=Nerdvana|date=2012-08-24|archivedate=20130605115546|nolive=1}} states the events of ''[[Star Wars Detours]]'' would have taken place between the events of {{Film|III}} and {{Film|IV}}, which corresponds to between [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]] and [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]] per ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''.</ref> at [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]],<ref name="fb" /> an [[Restaurant/Legends|eatery]] maintained by the [[Besalisk/Legends|Besalisk]] [[Chef/Legends|cook]] [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster]], located in the [[CoCo Town/Legends|CoCo District]]<ref name="SWE">{{SWE|locations|dexsdiner|Dex's Diner}}</ref> of the [[Core Worlds/Legends|Galactic Core]]<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].<ref name="SWE" /> At one point, a promotion sign stated that a Dex's Double would come for free with any purchase, although Jettster did not guarantee it would be fully cooked, made of actual meat, free of [[Parasite/Legends|parasites]], or even free in the first place.<ref name="fb" />
