The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''34 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3243 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''15 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7943''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*At the [[Battle of Malastare Narrows]], [[Admiral]] [[Trench]] leads a [[Corporate Alliance]] fleet and is presumed dead after Jedi intervention.<ref name="Cat and Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref><ref name="Timelines" />
*At age 12, [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Naberrie]] participates in the [[Legislative Youth Program]], in which she meets a slightly older boy named [[Palo Jemabie]], falling in love with him.<ref name="EpII">In [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] states that she was 12 years old during her participation in the [[Legislative Youth Program]] and her brief relationship with [[Palo Jemabie]]. {{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Padmé Naberrie - Handmaiden}} states that Amidala was 14 years old during the [[Invasion of Naboo]], dated to [[32 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Thus, basic math indicates that she participated in the Legislative Youth Program in 34 BBY.</ref>
*[[Queen]] [[Réillata]] of [[Naboo]] retires after one term, and Queen [[Sanandrassa]] is elected the [[planet]]'s new [[Monarch of Naboo|monarch]].<ref name="QP dating">''[[Queen's Peril]]'' takes place in the months leading up to, and during, the [[Invasion of Naboo]]. As the invasion is dated to [[32 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'', the events of the novel must also take place in that year. In the novel, it is stated that Sanandrassa was elected Queen of Naboo two years previously, and served for one two-year term, thus meaning she was elected, and Réillata retired, in 34 BBY.</ref>
*[[Anakin Skywalker]] begins to rebuild [[C-3PO]].<ref name="2nd Reading">''[[Star Wars Workbook: 2nd Grade Reading]]''</ref>
*The [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]] introduces the [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Introduced">{{HelmetCollectionCite|20|Databank A-Z}} states that the [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] was introduced "about 15 years before the rise of the Empire," which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]].</ref>
