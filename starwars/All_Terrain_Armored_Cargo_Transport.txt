The '''All Terrain Armored Cargo Transport''' ('''AT-ACT''') was a larger version of the standard [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] combat [[walker]] that featured a dedicated cargo bed for the transportation of heavy building materials or combat munitions.

==Description==
[[File:AT-ACT-Fathead.png|thumb|left|200px|A side view showing the AT-ACT large cargo door.]]
The All Terrain Armored Cargo Transport was deployed at major [[Galactic Empire]] construction projects, such as [[shipyard]]s and sprawling research installations.<ref name="Databank" /> Although the AT-ACT was not built for combat it was still capable of posing a formidable threat to infantry as discovered by [[Rebel trooper]]s during the [[Battle of Scarif]]. The [[walker]]'s taller profile gave it a faster stride than an AT-AT, although at the expense of structural stability. To give the knee joints extra strength when carrying cumbersome loads, an [[Magnet|electromagnetic]] [[tensor field]] kept the overstressed motive assembly in smooth alignment. The [[Magnetic seal|magnetically sealed]] cargo module housed within the AT-ACT's frame encompassed nearly 550 [[cubic meter]]s of space and was capable of carrying raw material or combat munitions. Loads were carefully managed by [[stevedore droid]]s who divided up partial holds of ultradense materials into manageable trips. Powerful [[engine]]s and tensor field-supported legs kept the massive AT-ACTs moving forward, from [[mining]] sites to processing facilites, carrying cargo in situations where [[Repulsor field|repulsorfields]] were unreliable or not suitable due to material incompatibilities. The AT-ACT's footpads distributed its weight. The articulated neck had an access tube leading to the [[cockpit]]. The cabin had a reinforced [[viewport]], ventilation grid, and ladder rungs leading to an emergency exit hatch. The AT-ACT was armed with two [[Taim & Bak]] [[MS-2 heavy laser cannon]]s.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

Despite the existence of [[AT-ACT driver]]s,<ref name="HasbroRapidFire">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: Rogue One (toy line)|pack=Rapid Fire Imperial AT-ACT|altlink=https://pulse.hasbro.com/news/star-wars-rapid-fire-imperial-act|nolive=1}}</ref> the AT-ACT's design did not boast a specialized [[driver]] corps, as it was not technically designated a battlefield assault vehicle. [[Imperial combat assault tank pilot]]s, [[AT-AT pilot]]s, and other [[Imperial combat driver]]s in the [[Imperial Military]] were all qualified to operate it.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

==History==
[[File:Acme-ScarifAttack.png|thumb|left|250px|An AT-ACT takes part in the defense of Scarif from the Rebel Alliance's raid.]]
A number of AT-ACTs were stationed at the construction site of [[Fortress Vader]].<ref name="Dark Lord 23">[[Darth Vader (2017) 23|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 23]]</ref> When an army of [[Mustafarian]]s [[Battle of Fortress Vader|attacked]] the newly built [[fortress]], the walkers defended it until a trio of [[Force-sensitive]] Mustafarians used [[the Force]] to flood the place with [[Magma|lava]]. [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was the sole Imperial survivor, using a fallen AT-ACT to escape the flooded [[Gahenn Plains|plains]].<ref name="Dark Lord 24">[[Darth Vader (2017) 24|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 24]]</ref>

At least four AT-ACTs were present on [[Scarif]] in a [[Battle of Scarif|battle]] against a [[Rogue One|squad]] of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[Rebel trooper|soldiers]]. When the Rebel incursion threatened the security of the [[Imperial Center of Military Research|Imperial security complex]], [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] ordered the beach secured, and the ground crews seized this initiative to move their AT-ACTs into action.<ref name="Visual Guide" /> They proved resistant to the weapons of the Rebel troops, but took losses when faced with [[X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] and [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wings]]. They, along with all the other Imperial and Rebel forces in the area, were destroyed when [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] ordered the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] to fire on Scarif.<ref name="Rogue One" />

[[File:ATACT-SaS.png|thumb|right|200px|An AT-ACT walker on a desert planet fires its heavy blaster cannons.]]
After the [[Battle of Endor]] between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], the walkers were among the forces deployed to defend [[Kuat Drive Yards]] during the [[New Republic]]'s [[Defense of Kuat|siege]] against the [[shipyard]]s.<ref name="Kuat" />

At least one walker was stationed on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet of [[Kashyyyk]] in the [[Black Forest]] before its [[Liberation of Kashyyyk|liberation]] by the New Republic. By two [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Jakku]], the walker had fallen and was covered in [[Fern (plant)|ferns]] and [[flower]]s, with [[plant]] stalks and small [[tree]]s sprouting from its blown-open metal belly.<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Part of the cargo walker's origin stems from the adage of 'create things as you remember not as they were'; turns out, most people remember the AT-ATs as being far taller than the 'real' scale works out to be, so the R1 walker ended up being a new model.|Pablo Hidalgo|<ref name="Pablo tweet 1">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1738409171299750062|Pablo Hidalgo|quote=Part of the cargo walker's origin stems from the adage of 'create things as you remember not as they were'; turns out, most people remember the AT-ATs as being far taller than the 'real' scale works out to be, so the R1 walker ended up being a new model.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/gNZvI}}</ref>}}
[[File:Baze_Malbus_shoots_an_AT-ACT.png|thumb|250px|left|The AT-ACT made its first appearance in ''Rogue One''.]]
The first footage of the AT-ACT was shown in the ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' teaser trailer on [[April 7]], [[2016]].<ref name="Teaser">{{SWYouTube|Wji-BZ0oCwg|ROGUE ONE: A STAR WARS STORY Official Teaser Trailer}}</ref> The walker was identified as AT-ACT in a preview of the canceled reference book ''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story: The Official Visual Story Guide'' on [[May 17]], 2016.<ref name="Edelweiss">{{LegacyEdelweiss|url=#sku=1942556411&page=1|text=Rogue One: A Star Wars Story: The Official Visual Story Guide}}</ref> The full name was first revealed in the third episode of ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'' on [[May 25]], 2016.<ref name="SW Show">{{StarWarsShow|-IVW0aIO2Lg|Chewbacca Mom, Rogue One Character Reveal, A Star Wars Birthday Party|sw_url=video/the-star-wars-show-episode-3}}</ref> Behind the scenes, the walker was simply known as the "cargo walker."<ref name="Pablo tweet 2">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1738409512112103578|Pablo Hidalgo|quote=Production wise, it was just 'cargo walker'. That's usually the case with these things. They typically don't get an in-universe acronym (AT-ACT) until the franchise folks turn them into products. I've come up with my share over the years.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/yevC9}}</ref> The walker became a different model from the AT-AT as a result of those working on the movie believing the regular walker to be taller than it really was, necessitating the AT-ACT become its own variant.<ref name="Pablo tweet 1" />
