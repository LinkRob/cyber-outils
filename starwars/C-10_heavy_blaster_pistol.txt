The '''C-10 heavy blaster pistol''', also known as the '''C-10 "Dragoneye Reaper,"''' was a model of [[heavy blaster pistol]]. It was popular with [[bounty hunter]]s and [[Mercenary|mercenaries]], especially larger individuals like [[Wookiee]]s.

==Description==
The C-10 heavy blaster pistol, which was also known as the C-10 "Dragoneye Reaper," was a model of [[heavy blaster pistol]] with a large [[Blaster carbine|carbine]]-like frame and [[barrel]]. These elements were nearly identical to those found in the [["Renegade" heavy blaster pistol]] manufactured by [[SoroSuub Corporation]] and the [[Model X]] manufactured by [[Caliban (manufacturer)|Caliban]]. The weapon had medium range and came with a [[stun]] setting.<ref name="G&G" />

==History==
Costing 1000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], the C-10 found popularity with [[bounty hunter]]s and [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], especially those of great size and strength, for whom it made the perfect sidearm. A particularly large following for the weapon formed amongst the [[Wookiee]]s of the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]], although they still preferred [[bowcaster]]s and other local designs. While there were other weapons that could provide the same energy output and flexibility in terms of customization, features that Wookiees particularly valued, the C-10 was a better fit for a Wookiee's [[Limb|paw]].<ref name="G&G" />
