"'''Dobra Doompa'''" was a [[Music|song]] performed at [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] by [[Shag Kava]],<ref name="Episode VII" /> a traveling band made up of the [[musician]]s [[Taybin Ralorsa]], [[Infrablue Zedbeddy Coggins]], [[Ubert Quaril|Ubert "Sticks" Quaril]], and [[Sudswater Dillifay Glon]].<ref name="Visual Dictionary" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Dobra Doompa" was written by the composer and actor [[Lin-Manuel Miranda]] for [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which was released in North America on [[December 18]], [[2015]].
