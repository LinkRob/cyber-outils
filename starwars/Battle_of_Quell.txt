The '''Battle of Quell''' was a battle that took place in [[21 BBY]]&mdash;also dated to 2 [[Imperial calendar|BFE]]&mdash;during the [[Clone Wars]] in [[orbit]] and later the upper [[atmosphere]] of the [[planet]] [[Quell]], where the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Aayla Secura's task force|fleet]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]] was assaulted by a [[TF-1726's squadron|squadron]] of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]] commanded by the [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] [[TF-1726]]. As her forces suffered heavy losses to the Separatist [[squadron]], Secura requested aid from fellow [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] alongside his [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], who traveled across [[the galaxy]] aboard his [[flagship]], the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Resolute]]'' to mount a rescue with little time to spare.

During the battle, Secura's two supporting [[Star Destroyer]]s had been destroyed by the Separatist [[capital ship]]s, while her flagship, the ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer [[Liberty (Venator-class)|''Liberty'']], succumbed to a boarding action undertaken by dozens [[B2-RP battle droid|B2-RP super battle droids]] as well as consistent heavy [[turbolaser]] fire from the Separatist [[frigate]]s. After Skywalker arrived, he proceeded to board the ''Liberty'' while a [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|''Consular''-class cruiser]] docked in the lower [[hangar]] in order to retrieve Secura and the others from the doomed [[Starship|vessel]]. However, while escaping Skywalker was severely injured after holding back an explosion with [[the Force]] while Secura, his Padawan, the [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] and [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Bly]] boarded the rescue frigate. Amid their departure from Secura's doomed flagship, the Republic frigate had suffered damage from Separatist cannon fire, which caused it to enter [[hyperspace]] at an unknown trajectory, causing Admiral [[Wullf Yularen]] to calculate the ship's trajectory in order to locate them after the end of the battle.

By the end of the fighting, while Secura was saved, the Republic had suffered heavy casualties among her [[327th Star Corps]] of [[clone trooper]]s as well as significant numbers of the [[Cloning|clone]] crews aboard the three Jedi cruisers, who were unable to escape the destroyed vessels prior to their impact on the planet's surface. The Separatist victory, although an unqualified one, also underscored how the Republic's ultimate triumph in the war was not certain.

==Prelude==
Throughout the [[Clone Wars]], a galactic war fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the confederate government regularly utilized its [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces']] vast superior numbers to its advantage in engagements against the Republic. As such, the Confederacy used such tactics during battles that took place within the [[Outer Rim Territories]], a region of [[the galaxy]] where separatism had flourished, including during an engagement at the [[planet]] [[Quell]].<ref name="Battles" />

==The battle==
===Battle in the skies===
[[File:Liberty_bridge.png|thumb|left|270px|The ''Liberty'' is outnumbered by TF-1726's Separatist squadron.]]
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Twi'lek]] [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]] and her [[Aayla Secura's task force|task force]] of three [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] [[Flagship|flagged]] by the Star Destroyer [[Liberty (Venator-class)|''Liberty'']] were engaged by a [[TF-1726's squadron|squadron]] of five [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]] [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by the [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] [[TF-1726]] from his [[TF-1726's Munificent-class star frigate|command ship]] in [[orbit]] of the planet Quell in the Outer Rim. During the battle, both the Republic and Separatist [[capital ship]]s had launched several units of [[starfighter]]s in opposition of one another, with Secura deploying several groups of [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s while the [[tactical droid]] sent out [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class droid starfighters]].<ref name="Jedi Crash" />

As the Separatist [[squadron]] lost a single of its [[frigate]]s, the remaining capital ships pressed intense turbolaser fire on the Jedi cruisers, eventually bringing them into the planet's upper [[atmosphere]] as the other two Venators were torn apart and sent crashing to Quell's surface in a fiery wreckage. With the destruction of its supporting Venators, the ''Liberty'' was left outnumbered while it attempted to maintain altitude in the atmosphere. However, as the ''Liberty'' attempted to return turbolaser fire to the attacking frigates, the Jedi cruiser continued to take heavy Separatist fire and eventually lost power to its [[deflector shield]]s as it was struck.<ref name="Jedi Crash" />

[[File:Battle_of_Quell.png|thumb|right|270px|TF-1726's command ship deploys dozens of B2-RP super battle droids to board the ''Liberty''.]]
Following the deactivation of the Republic flagship's shields, TF-1726 ordered dozens of [[jetpack]]-equipped [[B2-RP battle droid|B2-RP super battle droids]] from his [[command ship]] to board the heavily damaged Venator through the breaches in its [[hull]]. To support the rocket [[droid]]s, [[B1-series battle droid]]s were also sent aboard the ''Liberty'', forcing Secura to order her [[Clone Commander]], [[Bly|CC-5052]], otherwise known as "Bly," to repel the invaders. Complying to his orders, Bly led several groups of [[clone trooper]]s from the [[327th Star Corps]] to engage the boarding [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="Jedi Crash" />

===Arrival of Republic reinforcements===
[[File:Resolute_hyperspace.png|thumb|left|270px|The ''Resolute'' travels across the galaxy to aid in the Battle of Quell.]]
While her forces suffered heavy losses, fellow Jedi General [[Anakin Skywalker]] aboard his flagship, the ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer ''[[Resolute]]'', had received word of Secura's circumstances and traveled across the galaxy to aid her in the battle. As the ''Resolute'' entered the atmosphere after arriving in the [[Quell system]], Skywalker departed the Jedi cruiser alongside his [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex|CT-7567]], otherwise known as a "Rex," and clone troopers of the [[501st Legion]] aboard several [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] [[gunship]]s to board the heavily damaged ''Liberty'' and rescue its crew before its destruction, while [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]] remained aboard the ''Resolute''. After departing his flagship, Skywalker had also arranged for a [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|retrofitted]] [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class cruiser]] to dock with Secura's flagship and retrieve them.<ref name="Jedi Crash" />

[[File:ThisIsWhereTheFunBegins-SWQ157.png|thumb|right|250px|Anakin Skywalker leapt onto the the back of one of the rocket droids.]]
In their descent to Secura's Venator, Skywalker and his force of gunships were intercepted by several of the rocket droids that had been entering the ''Liberty''. As the droids fired their [[blaster]]s on the gunships, the Republic craft returned with [[laser cannon]] fire and [[missile]]s as one of the rocket droids latched onto the craft that was carrying Skywalker, his Padawan and Rex, and tore open the [[cockpit]] canopy in an attempt to kill its [[clone trooper pilot]], who exited into the troop compartment while Skywalker jumped out onto one of the droids, in which he then made his way on the ''Liberty'' while the gunship had lost control and crashed into the Jedi cruiser's [[hull]]. After crashing into the ''Liberty'', Tano, Rex and several clone troopers exited the gunship and engaged the invading rocket droids and B1 battle droids, eventually meeting up with Secura and Bly in a long hallway after destroying several of the droids that had boarded.<ref name="Jedi Crash" />

[[File:LibertyRescue-JC.png|thumb|left|270px|The Republic survivors make their way to the ''Consular''-class cruiser.]]
While aboard the ''Liberty'', TF-1726 aboard his frigate had grown impatient with the prolonged fighting and ordered his droid crew to open fire on the final Venator, despite the reminder that hundreds of their droids were still aboard and would be destroyed as a result. As Skywalker and Secura continued to fend off the battle droids within her cruiser, the two eventually met up with Tano and Rex and continued to press forward through the Jedi cruiser's corridors while still destroying Separatist droids along the way. In response to an inquiry as to how he planned to rescue them from the ''Liberty'', Skywalker explained that a Republic frigate was docking in the lower hangar in order to evacuate them. As the the frigate docked with the Jedi cruiser, the Republic survivors extended the gangway and boarded, but were temporarily halted by a wall of flames from the intense Separatist fire, which consumed all clone troopers and battle droids that crossed its path. After catching sight of the flames, Skywalker used the Force to push his allies into the frigate and held the flames back.<ref name="Jedi Crash" />

Skywalker was rescued, and the cruiser departed the doomed Republic vessel just as it was being destroyed. Due to Skywalker's wounds, Secura sought to dock with the ''Resolute'', which was engaging [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]]. Wullf Yularen warned about docking in the middle of the battle, but reluctantly allowed Secura and the cruiser to dock. However, during the docking procedure, the cruiser was attacked by vulture droids, which accidentally activated the ship's hyperdrive, forcing the cruiser into evasive action to avoid a catastrophic collision with the ''Resolute'' as it jumped into hyperspace.<ref name="Jedi Crash" />

Yularen ordered the last known trajectory of the cruiser be plotted so the missing ship could be located after the battle.<ref name="Jedi Crash" /> Ultimately, the battle was a Separatist victory.<ref name="Super Databank" />

==Aftermath==
The out-of-control ship nearly collided with a [[Maridun's star|star]] in hyperspace, but after it was forced out, the ship crashed on the planet [[Maridun]]. The stranded Jedi and clones found medical assistance for Skywalker at a [[Lurmen village|village]] of [[Lurmen]] colonists.<ref name="Jedi Crash" /> However, not long after their arrival, Separatist forces led by [[General]] [[Lok Durd]] arrived at Maridun, plotting to destroy the village as part of a test for Durd's experimental weapon, the [[Defoliator]]. The Republic group, including a mostly-recovered Skywalker, successfully [[Defense of Maridun|defended]] the village from Durd's forces, before capturing him just before the ''Resolute'' arrived at Maridun in search of them.<ref name="Defenders of Peace">{{TCW|Defenders of Peace}}</ref>

Later, the Battle of Quell was mentioned in an intelligence report by Clone Commander Bly describing B2-RP battle droids, which was included in the survival compendium "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]."<ref name="Official Magazine 118">{{GermanFanCite|118|Database}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BattleofQuellConcept.png|thumb|right|270px|Concept art of the Battle of Quell.]]
The Battle of Quell was first depicted in the episode "[[Jedi Crash]],"<ref name="Jedi Crash" /> the thirteenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated television series, which aired on [[January 16]], [[2009]].<ref name="Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode013.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Jedi Crash}}</ref> The battle was identified in the corresponding episode guide on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Jedi Crash Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/jedi-crash-episode-guide|text="Jedi Crash" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the battle was first identified as the [[Sky Battle of Quell]] in the entry for [[Ahsoka Tano]] on StarWars.com.<ref name="Ahsoka">{{SWE|characters|ahsokatano|Ahsoka Tano}}</ref>

In the initial script for "Jedi Crash," the Battle of Quell was originally planned as a space battle, but it was later moved to Quell's upper atmosphere during the episode's pre-production for dramatic effect. As a result of the changes made to the battle sequence, the altitude shift produced continuity errors regarding the operational capabilities of hyperdrives, which [[canon]] had already established possessed problems while operating close to planets.<ref name="Ep Guide" />
