'''Boolahs''' were a type of [[rodent]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]]. They possessed [[Color|white]] [[Hair|fur]] and had a long, slinky body. [[Pogee Shrag]] owned a number of boolahs that had been abandoned by residents of Kwenn who had left the planet.<ref name="The Living Force" />
