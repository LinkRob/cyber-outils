{{Quote|And as for that lightsaber. A curved hilt. I haven't seen one of those in a while. Do you know who else had a curved hilt? Darth Sakia. By all accounts, she was quite the swordswoman.|Lene Kostana, to Dooku|audio=Sakia.flac|<ref name="DJL" />}}
'''Darth Sakia''' was a fictional [[Gender|female]] [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] created by [[Jedi Master]] [[Lene Kostana]], hoping that it would make [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Dooku]] remember a [[Force vision]] that he had seen when he broke into the [[Archive of Forbidden Artifacts]], an artifact collection located in the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]'s [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="DJL" /> Around [[88 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 2|''Star Wars Jedi Master'' Magazine 2]] states that [[Dooku]] was [[Reproduction|born]] in [[102 BBY]]. The [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script]] of ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'' states that Dooku was fourteen [[Standard year|years]] old when he fought against [[Lene Kostana]], placing the event around [[88 BBY]].</ref> Dooku faced Kostana in a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]], convinced that the Master was secretly a [[Sith Lord]]. While fighting, Kostana said that, like Dooku himself, Darth Sakia was a skilled swordswoman who used a [[Curved-hilt lightsaber|curved hilt]] [[lightsaber]]. When [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] joined them on the [[contemplation balcony]] where they were dueling, he assured Dooku that Sakia was fictitious.<ref name="DJL" />

Dooku later told his [[House Serenno|sister]], [[Jenza]], about the events in a [[holo-letter]].<ref name="DJL" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="DJL-JournalsDate">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku gave several [[Datacard|data disks]] containing his messages to Jenza to his recently recruited [[Sith assassin]]&mdash;[[Asajj Ventress]]&mdash;for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into his sister's location, and she listened to the holo-disk that mentioned Sakia.<ref name="DJL" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Sakia was first mentioned in a flashback in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="DJL" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin-Download">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-cavan-scott/9780593102817|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> The spelling of Darth Sakia was confirmed by the audio drama's [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script release]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> on [[October 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin-Download" />
