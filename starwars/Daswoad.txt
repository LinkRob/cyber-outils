'''Daswoad''' was a substance that could be used to dye [[Clothing|fabric]]. During the [[Festival of the Ancestors]] on [[Pasaana]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[35 ABY]],<ref name="TROS date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] to one [[Standard year|year]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], the events of ''The Rise of Skywalker'' must occur in [[35 ABY]].</ref> the [[Aki-Aki]] [[Nambi Ghima]] wore [[robe]]s made of [[Color|greenish-blue]] daswoad-dyed [[clothing]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
