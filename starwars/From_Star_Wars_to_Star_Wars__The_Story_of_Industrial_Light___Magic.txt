'''''From Star Wars to Star Wars: The Story of Industrial Light & Magic''''', is a [[1999]] TV documentary film written and directed by Jon Kroll and produced by Film Garden Entertainment, In association with [[Lucasfilm]].<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.filmgarden.tv/specials/page/3|text=info from Film Garden's offical site|archivedate=20120830054353|nolive=1}}</ref> it aired on [[Wikipedia:Fox Broadcasting Company|FOX]] to promote the upcoming film; [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace]].

the documentary chronicles the history of the visual effects company; [[Industrial Light & Magic]], as it chronicles their origins and their work on their first film; [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|Star Wars]] (1977) all the way up to [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|The Phantom Menace]] (1999), hence the title; "From Star Wars To Star Wars". it is hosted by [[Samuel L. Jackson]].

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
The incredible evolution of George Lucas' special effects dream factory, including an inside look at "Star Wars: Episode One - The Phantom Menace."
