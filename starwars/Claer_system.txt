The '''Claer system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Sern sector]] of the [[Colonies]]<ref name="map" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Theinterior" /> The system was located in grid square L-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="map" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Claer system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="map" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Claer system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
