The '''Cassander sector''' was a [[sector]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that contained numerous systems, as well as two subsectors, the [[Tadrin sector|Tadrin]] and [[Tendrannan sector]]s. In [[34 ABY]], during the [[Cold War]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[First Order]], the sector fell in the [[Borderlands]] region between the First Order and the [[New Republic]], and the Resistance [[Missions against pirates in the Cassander sector|sent]] an attack group to repel [[Sheh Soahi's pirates|pirates]] that were attempting to take over the sector with the help of the First Order. After a [[Battle of Sheh Soahi|battle]] on Sheh Soahi, the Resistance defeated the [[pirate]]s and managed to gather information about the First Order's involvement with the pirates' attacks.

==Description==
The Cassander [[sector]] was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and contained the [[Altunna system|Altunna]], [[Arron Prime system|Arron Prime]], [[Caraxl system|Caraxl]], [[Cassander system|Cassander]], [[Forsen system|Forsen]], [[Kaller system|Kaller]], [[Mamkoda system|Mamkoda]], [[Ord Canfre system|Ord Canfre]], [[Peldon Minor system|Peldon Minor]], and [[Sheh Soahi system]]s. It also contained two subsectors, the [[Tadrin sector|Tadrin]] and [[Tendrannan sector]]s.<ref name="Appendix" /> Collectively, the three sectors were known as the [[Cassandran Worlds]].<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-09-15.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-09-15}}</ref>

==History==
===Early history and the First Order===
{{Quote|Pirates have troubled the star systems near the Kalki Nebula for centuries, using bases inside the nebula for hit-and-run attacks.|An excerpt from Paige Tico's journal|<ref name="Bomber Command" />}}
For [[Standard century|centuries]], [[pirate]]s had operated in the Cassander sector, using hidden bases inside the Kalki Nebula to organize hit-and-run attacks against nearby [[star system]]s.<ref name="Bomber Command" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> during the [[Cold War]]<ref name="Bomber Command" /> between the [[First Order]]&mdash;the successor of the [[Galactic Empire]]&mdash;and the [[Resistance]], a small paramilitary force created by [[New Republic]] [[senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]],<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> the Cassander sector was located between New Republic and First Order Space. The First Order, seeking to take over the sector, enlisted a group of [[Sheh Soahi's pirates|pirates]] to take control of it on its behalf. With equipment provided by the First Order, the pirates raided both Mamkoda and Caraxl and eventually took control of a [[Mamkoda spaceport|local spaceport]] on the former, turning it into a [[Sheh Soahi pirate base|base of operations]].<ref name="Bomber Command" />

===The Resistance campaign===
{{Quote|Sheh Soahi was a tough fight. The pirates sent everything up against us.|An excerpt from Paige Tico's journal|<ref name="Bomber Command" />}}
[[File:First_Order.svg|thumb|left|150px|The First Order wanted to take control of the Cassander sector with the help of pirates.]]
Once the Resistance learned about the threat of the First Order in the Cassander sector, they [[Missions against pirates in the Cassander sector|sent]] an attack group to repel the pirates. The group consisted of [[RZ-2 A-wing interceptor]]s and [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 Starfortress bombers]]<ref name="Bomber Command" /> along with their mobile base of operations, the [[Free Virgillia-class Bunkerbuster|''Free Virgillia''-class Bunkerbuster]] ''[[Ninka]]'' under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]].<ref name="Resistance Fighter">''[[The Last Jedi: Rose Tico: Resistance Fighter]]''</ref> The Resistance's first objective in the sector was to destroy the local pirate base on Mamkoda. During the [[Battle of Mamkoda|battle]], [[A-wing starfighter|A-wings]] led by [[Resistance pilot]] [[Tallissan Lintra]] and [[bomber]]s from [[Crimson Squadron|Crimson]] and [[Cobalt Squadron]]s managed to destroy the [[spaceport]] without taking any losses. Three [[Standard day|days]] after the battle on Mamkoda, a group of Resistance bombers were [[Mission to Caraxl|dispatched]] to Caraxl on a relief mission to deliver supplies. During that attempt, Lintra reported that First Order [[TIE series|TIE fighters]] were approaching their position. Since the Resistance attack group had orders to avoid any confrontation with the First Order that could cause problems in the [[New Republic Senate]], the Resistance called off the relief mission and returned to the ''Ninka''.<ref name="Bomber Command" />

[[File:Ninka_DB.png|thumb|right|250px|The Resistance forces were stationed on the ''Ninka'' during their campaign in the Cassander sector.]]
The Resistance attack group then traveled to Sheh Soahi after [[Resistance spy droid network|droid spies]] informed them that the main pirate base was located there. On Sheh Soahi, the Resistance launched an [[Battle of Sheh Soahi|assault]] on the pirate base. After a fight between both forces, the pirates damaged the MG-100 bomber ''[[Cobalt Scarab]]'' and destroyed two A-wing [[Starfighter|fighters]]. However, the Resistance ultimately emerged victorious after it destroyed the pirates' base, defeated their forces, and intercepted a transmission in which the pirates demanded help from the First Order, with the Resistance planning to present the communication to the New Republic Senate. With the defeat of the pirates and the acquisition of the evidence of the First Order's involvement, the Resistance ended their campaign in the Cassander sector.<ref name="Bomber Command" />

Some [[time]] after those missions, Resistance [[gunner]] [[Paige Tico]] wrote about the campaign in the Cassander sector in her [[Paige Tico's journal|journal]], which ended up in possession of her sister [[Rose Tico]] after the former's [[death]] during the [[Battle of D'Qar]].<ref name="Bomber Command" /> Rose would later also mention the campaign in her own [[Rose Tico's journal|journal]].<ref name="Resistance Fighter" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cassander sector was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2017]] replica journal book ''[[The Last Jedi: Bomber Command]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Bomber Command" /> The "[[Cassander subsector|Cassander sector]]" was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2004]] [[Wizards of the Coast]] sourcebook ''[[Geonosis and the Outer Rim Worlds]]''. In that book, the Cassander [[Sector/Legends|sector]], along with the [[Tadrin sector/Legends|Tadrin]] and [[Tendrannan sector/Legends|Tendrannan sectors]], was established as part of the "[[Cassandran Worlds/Legends|Cassandran sectors]]."<ref name="Geonosis and the Outer Rim Worlds">''[[Geonosis and the Outer Rim Worlds]]''</ref> The [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', however, depicted [[Star system/Legends|star systems]]<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> that ''Geonosis and the Outer Rim Worlds'' had previously placed in the three different "sectors"<ref name="Geonosis and the Outer Rim Worlds" /> as part of a single "[[Cassander sector/Legends|Cassander sector]]."<ref name="TEA" /> The [[StarWars.com]] [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to ''The Essential Atlas'' later clarified that the Cassander, Tadrin, and Tendrannan sectors were, in fact, subsectors of the larger Cassander sector.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
