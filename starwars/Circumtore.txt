'''Circumtore''' was a ring-shaped [[planet]]<ref name="TUC">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref> located in the [[Hutt Space]] region of [[the galaxy]]. It was connected to the [[Hutt Space]] worlds [[Rorak]], [[Nar Kaaga]] and [[Hollastin system|Hollastin]] by [[hyperspace route]]s as well as [[Daalang]] outside of the region. Circumtore was located at [[galactic coordinates]] S-12.<ref name="TFA Beginner Game" /> Despite the planet's unusual shape, the [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] was unsure if Circumtore was formed naturally or constructed artificially.<ref name="TUC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Circumtore was first mentioned in the current [[canon]] in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a roleplaying game released by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]].<ref name="TFA Beginner Game">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> The ring-shaped artificial planetoid originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[1998]] novel ''[[The Mandalorian Armor]]'', which was written by [[K. W. Jeter]].<ref name="Mandalorian Armor">''[[The Mandalorian Armor]]''</ref>
