A [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] '''warehouse''' was located on the [[Inner Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]] near the world's [[Pijal's capital city|capital city]] during the [[Republic Era]]. In [[40 BBY]], the facility was destroyed by [[blackguard]] [[soldier]]s secretly operating under the [[Blackguard campaign|orders]] of the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]]. [[Standard day|Days]] later, [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] were shown a [[Hologram|holographic]] recording of the warehouse's destruction during a [[Mission to Pijal|mission]] briefing.

==Description==
A [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] warehouse was located near the [[Pijal's capital city|capital city]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]] during the [[Republic Era]]. The warehouse had recording devices that surveilled the area surrounding the facility.<ref name="MA" />

==History==
{{Quote|Our royal audiences are available to everyone. We've repeatedly asked to meet with this Halin Azucca or other Opposition leaders, under a promise of absolute immunity. But no one appears. No one speaks. Instead we got pranks and stunts, and then vicious attacks.|Fanry, while talking about the attack on the warehouse and other establishments|audio=Pijal9.flac|<ref name="MA" />}}
[[File:PijalCover.jpg|thumb|left|260px|The warehouse was located on Pijal (pictured).]]
In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[blackguard]] [[soldier]]s secretly operating under the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]]'s [[Blackguard campaign|orders]] rigged a [[droid]] to explode and destroy the warehouse. The facility was left in [[Fire|flames]], which [[Firefighter droid|firefighting droids]] attempted to extinguish. Pijal's [[lord]] [[regent]], [[Rael Averross]], was informed of the attack when a recording of the event was displayed on a [[Viewscreen|holoscreen]]. He cursed [[Halin Azucca]], the leader of the [[Opposition]] performance troupe whom he believed responsible for the incident, and hurriedly left a [[Unidentified cantina (Pijal)|cantina]] to return to the [[Pijali palace|royal palace]].<ref name="MA" />

[[Standard day|Days]] later, [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] were shown a [[Hologram|holographic]] recording of the warehouse's destruction while being briefed about recent upheavals on Pijal. Fanry blamed the attack on the facility on the Opposition, claiming the group consisted of [[terrorist]]s unwilling to meet with the [[monarchy]] to find a peaceful solution. Afterward, the [[Jedi]] pair began [[Mission to Pijal|investigating]] the origins of several attacks in the [[Pijal system]] and acquired the recording of the warehouse's destruction from the Pijali [[Royal guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] [[captain]] [[Deren]] to assist their efforts. Jinn and Kenobi later discovered that Azucca and her group of Opposition artists were not involved in the facility's destruction, having been framed for incidents instigated by the blackguards.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Czerka warehouse appeared in a hologram in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
