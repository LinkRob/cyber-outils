'''''Blood Ties: Boba Fett is Dead'' 2''' is the second issue of the ''[[Star Wars: Blood Ties: Boba Fett is Dead]]'' miniseries, part of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Blood Ties]]'' from [[Dark Horse Comics]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Boba Fett is dead, and whoever hired the hit squad had better run&mdash;as fast and as far as possible!''

''A mysterious avenger is on the trail of Boba Fett's killers. Connor Freeman&mdash;a clone relation to Fett&mdash;has been drawn into the whodunit. Freeman, who feels like he could use some protection himself, has been recruited against his will to find and protect a particular bounty hunter on a distant world&mdash;in Mandalorian space!''
