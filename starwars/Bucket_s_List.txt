'''''Bucket's List''''' is a series of behind-the-scenes [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] videos produced by [[StarWars.com]] that looks at each ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' episode. The series is named after one of the series' main characters, the [[droid]] [[R1-J5]]. Season One was released on StarWars.com, [[StarWarsKids.com]], the [https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCDe7m0POuwkL1gwnQ-FVhMw Star Wars Kids YouTube channel], and [https://www.disneynow.com DisneyNOW], while Season Two was not released on StarWars.com. The videos have since been removed from StarWarsKids.com and the Star Wars Kids YouTube channel.
