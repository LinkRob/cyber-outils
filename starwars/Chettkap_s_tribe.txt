A '''Jawa tribe''' led by [[Chettkap]] was active on [[Arvala-7]]<ref name="Mandalorian2" /> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Mandalorian date">{{MandoDate|2}}</ref> The [[Jawa]] tribe dismantled and stole pieces from the ''[[Razor Crest]]'', a [[gunship]] used by a [[bounty hunter]] known as [[Din Djarin|the Mandalorian]]. After the bounty hunter began attacking them, the tribe fled into their [[Unidentified sandcrawler|sandcrawler]] and escaped. The tribe lost five members in the process, three being disintegrated by the bounty hunter's [[Amban phase-pulse blaster]] and the final two being yanked off the sandcrawler and thrown to their deaths, but managed to fend off the Mandalorian by pelting him with junk and then stunning him with their [[ion blaster]]s. When the Mandalorian later demanded the parts back, they agreed to supply them if he stole a [[mudhorn]] [[Mudhorn egg|egg]] and gave it to the tribe. The Mandalorian agreed and secured the [[egg]], of which the tribe promptly ate the contents.<ref name="Mandalorian2" /> 

Its members included Chettkap,<ref name = "Handbook">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Handbook]]''</ref> their elder,<ref name="Mandalorian2" />  [[Thweepi]], their scavenger, and [[Kettkot]], one of their hunters.<ref name="VisualGuideFrench">''Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide'' {{C|[[Wikipedia:French language|French]] edition}}</ref>
