The '''charter of Chancellor''' was the document that empowered the [[Chancellor (New Republic)|chancellor]] of the [[New Republic]], the position that served as the leader of the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]]. The charter gave the chancellery the same emergency powers once granted to [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], which he used to [[Proclamation of the New Order|form]] the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Mon Mothma]], however, intended to eliminate those powers when she was elected as the first chancellor of the New Republic, criticizing them for undermining [[democracy]].<ref name="Aftermath" />
