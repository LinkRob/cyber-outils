'''''Coruscant Nights''''' was a [[Painting|poster]] ad for the [[Coruscant Nights|source of entertainment]] of the same name, put up on [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. It was written in [[Aurebesh]] and showed the silhouette of two people against a [[Color|blue]] background, and said "Coruscant Nights" at the top<ref name="Lethal" /> and "[[Coruscant Ballroom]]" at the bottom.<ref name="Lethal Trackdown">In "[[Lethal Trackdown]]," the [[Aurebesh]] text "[[Coruscant Ballroom]]" can be read at the bottom of a copy of the [[Painting|poster]], glimpsed over the entrance to [[Moshi Bar]] as [[Plo Koon]] says, "You may be surprised what people reveal when they have been drinking." If you compare the design at bottom of this poster to the full poster seen ''inside'' the [[cantina]] (where only the "[[Coruscant Nights]]" parts can be deciphered), you can see that it is the same poster, meaning that it reads "Coruscant Nights" at the top and "Coruscant Ballroom" at the bottom. The full version of the poster ''over the cantina entrance'' can also be seen in the wide shot of the [[Jedi]] leaving the bar, but only from a distance.</ref> It could be found in a [[Unidentified massage parlor|massage parlor]] in [[Slum district G17]],<ref name="Lost">{{TCW|Lightsaber Lost}}</ref> [[Moshi Bar]],<ref name="Lethal" /> the [[Lower market districts]]<ref name="Evil">{{TCW|Evil Plans}}</ref> and the [[Refresher|latrine]] in the [[79's]] cantina.<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref>
