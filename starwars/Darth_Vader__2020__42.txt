'''''Darth Vader'' 42''' is the forty-second issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Adam Gorham]] and [[Federico Blee]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 3]], [[2024]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE RISE OF THE SCHISM IMPERIAL! In the wake of ''DARK DROIDS'', DARTH VADER explores his powers and takes on the most dangerous team of rebel heroes he's ever faced! Meanwhile, the greatest threat to the EMPEROR'S rule is rising &hellip; from within THE EMPIRE itself! Will the DARK LORD OF THE SITH destroy it &hellip; or join it? Featuring the comic-book debut of ENRIC PRYDE from ''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker''!''

==Plot summary==
In the [[Imperial Palace]] on [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] stands frustratedly outside of the [[Emperor|Emperor's]] [[Emperor's throne room (Imperial Palace)|throne room]]. [[Sub-Administrator]] [[Sly Moore]] approaches, startling the [[Chagrian]]. She takes the lead of the situation, entering the throne room and announcing to Emperor [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] that Amedda has a report, which the Emperor sensed due to the Chagrian's fear. 

Amedda informs the Emperor that the [[droid]] [[scourge]] had been destroyed but that [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] had devised a method to infect and control droids of his own. This represents Vader's ongoing efforts to defend himself and seek increasing power. Enraged by Amedda's failure to quell Vader's ambitions, Palpatine attacks his vizier with [[Force lightning]] and gives Moore an opportunity to try.

In [[Zee-Nine City Seven]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Enric Pryde]] of [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyers]] ''[[Steadfast (Imperial-class)|Steadfast]]'' and ''[[Impervious]]'' prepares a recording to Amedda. He explains that the Imperial [[garrison]] there is understaffed and is a prime target for [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels]]. As he records, however, a series of explosions erupts and [[X-wing starfighter]]s fly overhead. He looks up to see the ''Impervious'' crashing into the planet. Pryde has his [[Stormtrooper]]s prepare for battle. 

Rebel [[prisoner]]s mock the Lieutenant, and Pryde prepares to [[death|kill]] them when he hears voices threatening him to stand down. He refuses, and the [[cyborg|augmented]] soldiers of [[M.A.R. Corps]] burst in to fight the Imperials. They subdue Pryde and lock him in a cage. 

Twelve [[Standard hour|hours]] later, Darth Vader and [[ZED-6-7]] arrive to the [[city]] and locate Pryde. The Lieutenant informs them that it is a trap and informs them of the cyborgs' presence. Vader releases Pryde from the cells, and ZED-6-7 advises Pryde to evacuate. The Lieutenant, however, is unconvinced and remains worried for Vader's safety in facing the formidable Rebels.

Vader faces the four members of M.A.R. Corps, and they engage in a fierce battle, the Rebels buoyed by rooftop support. Pryde attempts to intervene by creating cover for Vader, only for the [[Sith]] to [[Force push|push]] him back with [[the Force]]. The knocked down Pryde sees several [[MSE-series]] droids approach the cyborgs. They deploy spikes loaded with Imperial [[droid brain]]s that interface with the [[cybernetics|cybernetic]] implants on the Rebels. This forces them into the control of Vader, and the four cyborgs start firing on their former Rebel comrades. 

One M.A.R. Corps member, [[Masakint]], starts to fight the droid brain and attempts to get her squad mates to fight theirs too. In spite of her pleas, [[Tandorvo]] primes a [[thermal detonator]] and throws it to Rebel ordinance caches, destroying them. Just then, another MSE droid re-implants the droid brain into Masakint, bringing her back into Vader's control.

Later, in [[Fortress Vader]] on [[Mustafar]], ZED-6-7 and some [[medical droid]]s tinker with the M.A.R. Corps members. Vader remains frustrated at the mental fortitude the Rebels are able to muster in fighting the droid brains. Alas, Vader's plan hinges on leveraging the lived experience and skills [[sentience|sentient]] soldiers possess, so they cannot simply remove the organic brains. 

Soon, Sly Moore arrives at the castle. She informs Vader that she has been sent by the Emperor to spy on him and asks some prying questions. Vader is unwilling to play her games and recommends she stops them. Moore, however, has other plans. She mentions that Vader needs to destroy Palpatine or be destroyed himself, as is the way of the Sith, and she offers Vader a way to help him get ready to win against his master. She informs him of the [[Schism Imperial]], a group of Imperials, including herself, [[Governor]] [[Tauntaza]], and [[Captain]] [[Corleque]] that have grown tired of Palpatine's caprice and failure. She works to try and convince him to align with the Schism, saying that doing so could give him significant power. Vader contemplates his answer.
