The '''Fennesa system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]]. It was situated in grid square K-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It contained the astronomical object [[Fennesa]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fennesa system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]],"<ref name="Appendix" /> published on the [[StarWars.com]] website as part of the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Galaxy">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
