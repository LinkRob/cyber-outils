The '''Baffia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Deep Core]]. It was located in grid square L-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin of<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Baffian marble]]|system=Baffia [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> [[Baffian marble]].<ref name="Out of the Shadows">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref>
