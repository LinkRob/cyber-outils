'''Eoin Gorcan''' was a [[human]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. While in the [[Galactic Senate Chamber]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] in [[229 BBY]], he witnessed the [[Jedi High Council]] implement the [[Guardian Protocols]] as the [[Nihil conflict|fight]] against the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] escalated. While in the planet [[Eriadu]]'s [[Arkorayal Cabaret Cantina|Republic outpost]] in [[228 BBY]], he attended [[Admiral]] [[Pevel Kronara]]'s planning meeting for the joint [[Republic Defense Coalition]] and Jedi Order [[Raid on the Korvix Vorn|mission]] to rescue [[prisoner]]s from the Nihil [[prison ship]] ''[[Korvix Vorn]]''. That [[Standard year|year]], as part of the [[Eriaduan resistance]], Gorcan helped his allies repel the Nihil [[Invasion of Eriadu|invading]] Eriadu, celebrating once they were defeated.

==Biography==
{{Quote|You've all been rigorously vetted, so listen up and speak freely.|Pevel Kronara, to Eoin Gorcan and the others in a meeting|<ref name="THRA 4" />}}
The [[human]] Eoin Gorcan<ref name="THRA 4">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4]]</ref> was a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] of the [[Jedi Order]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="SoS 1" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="SWTHRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> the Order assembled in the [[Galactic Senate Chamber]] on<ref name="SoS 1" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="TCoC">{{StoryCite|story=The Call of Coruscant|book=The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life}}</ref> [[Coruscant]] where the [[Jedi High Council]] implemented the [[Guardian Protocols]] in response to the escalation of the ongoing [[Nihil conflict|fight]] against the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]. When one of the Council members explained that Padawans of any age would become eligible to undergo the [[Jedi Trials]] to [[Knighting Ceremony|become]] a [[Jedi Knight]], Padawan [[Amadeo Azzazzo]] was worried. Gorcan assured<ref name="SoS 1" /> him<ref name="TCoC" /> that they would not undergo the trials soon, though another Padawan&mdash;[[Ady Sun'Zee]]&mdash;guessed that if the Nihil attacks continued, the Order would need to hasten their promotions.<ref name="SoS 1" />

[[File:EoinTaniKantam-2023THRA4.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Eoin Gorcan (center) attended the planning meeting for Republic and Jedi's ''Korvix Vorn'' mission.]]
In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date-THRA4">{{StarlightDate|[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4]]|one [[Standard year|year]] and two weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> Gorcan was present in the [[Arkorayal Cabaret Cantina|Republic outpost]] on<ref name="THRA 4" /> the planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Eriadu]].<ref name="THRA 4" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date-THRA4" /> after being vetted to attend,<ref name="THRA 4" /> he<ref name="Twitter" /> attended a meeting hosted for members of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] and the Jedi Order that was led by the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Admiral]] [[Pevel Kronara]] to plan a [[Raid on the Korvix Vorn|mission]] to rescue [[prisoner]]s from the Nihil [[prison ship]] ''[[Korvix Vorn]]''. The Padawan stood next to [[Jedi Master]] [[Tani Briwill]] as Kronara displayed a [[hologram]] of the [[Starship|vessel]] and explained its [[Weapon|armaments]]&mdash;[[laser cannon]]s and [[blister bomb]]s, engaging in a discussion with several Jedi about how to best conduct the mission. Shortly after the meeting's end, the Republic and Jedi carried out the rescue<ref name="THRA 4" /> and successfully freed the ''Korvix Vorn''{{'s}} prisoners.<ref name="THRA 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 5]]</ref>

Later in 228 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|The High Republic Adventures (2023) 19|one [[Standard year|year]] and a few [[Standard month|months]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> Gorcan joined his Jedi and [[Eriaduan]] allies as part of the [[Eriaduan resistance]] to repel the Nihil [[Invasion of Eriadu|invading]] Eriadu. The Eriaduan resistance defeated the Nihil on the world, with Gorcan celebrating with his allies afterward,<ref name="THRA 19" /> taking time to reminisce on the allies who had lost their lives during the Nihil conflict.<ref name="THRA 20">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 20|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 20]]</ref> The Nihil collectively surrendered to the Republic not long afterward.<ref name="Trials">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref> 

==Personality and traits==
[[File:PadawansGuardianProtocols-THRSOS1.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Eoin Gorcan (center) sitting with Amadeo Azzazzo and Ady Sun'Zee as they watched the Jedi High Council implement the Guardian Protocols.]]
During the Nihil conflict, Gorcan was loyal to the Jedi Order and the Republic during their joint fight against the marauders and was among several individuals rigorously vetted to attend the briefing meeting for the ''Korvix Vorn'' mission.<ref name="THRA 4" /> Upon the implementation of the Guardian Protocols, he was willing to reassure his colleague Azzazzo when the Council announced Padawans of any age could be eligible to complete the Jedi Trials. Gorcan had [[Color|red]] [[hair]] and light skin.<ref name="SoS 1" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a Jedi Padawan, Gorcan was a [[Force-sensitive]] individual [[Jedi training|trained]] to [[Force power|wield]] [[the Force]].<ref name="THRA 4" />

==Equipment==
During the announcement of the Guardian Protocols' implementation, Gorcan donned white-and-gold [[Jedi apparel|Jedi attire]] that included a white [[cloak]] and a brown [[belt]].<ref name="SoS 1" /> On Eriadu, the Padawan donned brown Jedi [[robe]]s featuring a lighter [[Clothing|undertunic]], cream-colored pants, a [[utility belt]], a brown cloak, and brown [[boot]]s. Under his utility belt, Gorcan wore a gold-colored [[Padawan sash|sash]]<ref name="THRA 4" /> that was the same color as his [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Collection">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' [[Padawan]]s</ref> While fighting during the invasion of Eriadu, Gorcan donned tan-colored Jedi robes with yellow tabbards, along with a brown utility belt and brown boots.<ref name="THRA 19" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|You haven't met him&hellip; yet.|Michael Siglain|<ref name="Twitter" />}}
[[File:Shadows-of-Starlight-1-Cover.jpg|thumb|right|180px|Eoin Gorcan debuted in the first issue of the comic series ''The High Republic: Shadows of Starlight''.]]
Eoin Gorcan first appeared, albeit unidentified in the [[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 1|first issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight|The High Republic: Shadows of Starlight]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="SoS 1" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Ibraim Roberson]], and released by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/112950/star_wars_the_high_republic_-_shadows_of_starlight_2023_1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic &mdash; Shadows of Starlight (2023) #1}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm]] Publishing's Creative Director<ref name="SiglainTwitter">{{Twitter|username=msiglain|name=Michael Siglain|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/R4Dcv}}</ref> [[Michael Siglain]] revealed the character's pronouns on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]].<ref name="Twitter" />

The character was later identified by name in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|fourth issue]] of the 2023 comic series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Elisa Romboli]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="THRA 4" /> on [[March 13]], [[2024]], for ''The High Republic''{{'s}} Phase III.<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-796/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-4|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #4}}</ref> Older later confirmed on Twitter that Gorcan was indeed the redhaired Jedi Padawan first seen in ''Shadows of Starlight'' 1.<ref name="Twitter Older">{{Twitter|djolder|status/1774872748717719605|[[Daniel José Older]]|quote=yep yep|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/BmWrz|archivefile=File:DanielJoseOlder-EoinGorcanSoS1-Twitter.png}} {{C|In response to: "Cara and I would actually know if Eoin and mystery baby boy from SOS#1 are the same person because we have immediately adopted him/them 🤲🏻🧡 @Mittheresabosen"}}</ref>
