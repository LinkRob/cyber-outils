'''Beka''' was a [[senator]] who served within the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="Jedi Knights 4" /> Between [[40 BBY]] and [[34 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Jedi Knights 4|''Jedi Knights'' 4]] take place between [[40 BBY]] and [[34 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:JediKnightsDate|here]].</ref> Beka was illegally holding funds for the [[Gangster]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] until the [[Robbery of the Galactic Bank of Coruscant|robbery]] of ten thousand IBC [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] carried out by the [[Quaraxian]] [[Phaedra]], which led the Senate to discover the scheme and put Beka in significant legal peril.<ref name="Jedi Knights 4" />
