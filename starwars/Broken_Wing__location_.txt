The '''Broken Wing''' was a specific location formed on the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]] when an [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Zeffo)|unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer]] crashed there after suffering large amounts of damage from a [[Providence-class Dreadnought|Separatist dreadnought]] during the [[Battle against a Separatist dreadnought]]. The Broken Wing location was one of 3 areas formed by the crash landing.

The Broken Wing location could be accessed by traversing the [[Crash Site]] to a hallway that led to a large main chamber containing a [[Hologram]] of an [[Unidentified Clone Commander (Zeffo)|Unidentified Clone Commander]] informing [[Jedi General]] [[Chiata]] of the condition of the ship. This large chamber contained a staircase that led to a large turbine that contained a [[Rabid Jotaz]] by<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> [[14 BBY]].<ref name="JFO Date2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}

In 14 BBY,<ref name="JFO Date2" /> [[Cal Kestis]] while coming across the area encountered the Rabid Jotaz that attacked him leading to Cal Kestis fighting and [[Death|killing]] the beast.{{EndGame}} A broken pathway surrounded the large turbine that could be traversed leading to a long-winded hallway that led to a large room. The large room contained an entrance that went to a flooded hallway which then went to an electric field that could be disabled. The electric field contained a [[Death|dead]] [[clone trooper]] who died due to the crash landing of the unidentified venator. The electric field area had an elevator that went to an observation deck close to the large turbine<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> (that contained the Rabid Jotaz by 14 BBY<ref name="JFO Date2" />) where the clone commander got a transmission from the [[Supreme Chancellor]] to execute [[Order 66]]. The Clone Commander would later be [[Death|killed]] by a [[lightsaber]] of one of the [[Jedi]] close by to the Broken Wing location.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> By 14 BBY,<ref name="JFO Date2" /> fauna such as the [[Skungus]] also inhabited the area.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

In 14 BBY,<ref name="JFO Date2" /> Cal Kestis came across the area while travelling to the [[Imperial Dig Site]] close to the area.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
