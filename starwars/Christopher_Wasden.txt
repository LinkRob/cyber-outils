'''Christopher Wasden''' was the Assistant Technical Director for every episode of the television show ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''<ref name="Credit">Every episode of ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' credits Wasden as Assistant Technical Director.</ref> and the Assistant Asset Technical Director for the [[The Bad Batch Season 1|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''.<ref name="Credit 2">Every episode of the [[The Bad Batch Season 1|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' credits Wasden as Assistant Asset Technical Director.</ref>
