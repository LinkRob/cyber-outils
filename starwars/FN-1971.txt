{{Quote|I remembered the comics from when I was a kid. FN-1971 would sneak them in. They weren't regulation, and they would have sent us to reprogramming if they'd known, but man, were they great.|Finn|audio=FN-1971.flac|<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />}}
'''FN-1971''' was the designation of<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /> a [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who was a member of the [[First Order]]'s [[FN Trooper Corps|FN Corps]]. FN-1971 would sneak in comics based on the animated program [[Kade Genti, Master of Section Nine]], and gave them to a young stormtrooper, [[Finn|FN-2187]], to read and enjoy. The two were aware that their actions were against regulation and would have led to [[Reconditioning|reprogramming]] if they were caught.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Resistance Reborn Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Resistance Reborn]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> FN-2187, by then a member of the [[Resistance]] who adopted the name "Finn," remembered reading the comics provided by FN-1971 and chose to use the name of the show's protagonist, [[Kade Genti]], as an alias when going undercover at an [[Mission to Corellia|auction]] hosted at [[Coronet City]] on<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Corellia">{{Databank|corellia|Corellia}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

==Behind the scenes==
FN-1971 was mentioned in ''[[Resistance Reborn]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Rebecca Roanhorse]].<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /> The character's designation corresponds with Roanhorse's year of birth, [[1971]].<ref name="Year">{{WebCite|url=https://locusmag.com/2018/09/rebecca-roanhorse-from-legend-to-fantasy|text=Rebecca Roanhorse: From Legend to Fantasy|date=2018-09-17|work=[https://locusmag.com Locus Online]|quote=REBECCA ROANHORSE was born in 1971 in Conway AR and grew up in Fort Worth TX.|archivedate=20200121072217}}</ref>
