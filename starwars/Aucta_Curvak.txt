'''Aucta Curvak''' was a [[Quarren]] [[merchant]] and [[scavenger]] who operated a stall in the [[city]] of [[Myrra]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>

==Biography==
Aucta Curvak was a [[merchant]] and [[scavenger]] who resided on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> During which they operated a stall in the [[Hutt Cartel District (Myrra)|Hutt Cartel District]] of [[Myrra]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of Akiva. Their stock included [[polycarbonate]], [[quadanium steel]], [[helicyclic gear]]s, [[direct-energy circuit]]s and [[enhanced durasteel hull]]s. During the same [[Standard year|year]], Curvak offered exclusive stock to members and allies of the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]]. This stock included the [[H-M engine power core]]s and a [[X-FP-C28 ship computer]]. Curvak had also stashed an artist's [[airbrush]] in a container near their stall in the [[Hutt]] district.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> a [[thief]] named [[Kay Vess]] discovered Curvak's stash and stole the stashed airbrush. Around the same time, Vess talked with a [[Unidentified retired battle droid|battle droid]] in [[The Alcazar]] [[cantina]] in Myrra who had grown dissatisfied with their work at the cantina as it did not offer enough chances to utilized their combat abilities. After agreeing to help the [[droid]] find a new place to work, Vess had to decide if she would approach Curvak or a merchant named [[Karpen Miltik]] in the [[Pyke Syndicate District (Myrra)|Pyke Syndicate District]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Personality and traits==
Curvak was a [[Quarren]] with pale skin and [[Color|blue]] [[eye]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

==Equipment==
While operating their stall, Curvak wore a green [[clothing|tunic]], white [[glove]]s, an orange waist [[sash]] with a brown pouch and brown shoulder strap.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
Aucta Curvak first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. Curvak was one option the player could choose during the "Retired Droid" intel quest, during which approaching Curvak will result in an increase in the player's reputation with the Hutt Cartel and if they approach Miltik they would receive the same increase with their reputation with the [[Pyke Syndicate]]. The airbrush found in Curvak's stash is a reference to [[Sabine Wren's airbrushes]] and can be used as a trinket to customize the appearance of the player's [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]] or the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', the player's [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
