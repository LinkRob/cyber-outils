A [[Gender|female]] '''individual''' was the [[Parent|mother]]<ref name="Ghosts I" /> of [[human]] male [[historian]] and [[Resistance]] member<ref name="Beaumont Kin DB">{{Databank|beaumont-kin|Beaumont Kin}}</ref> [[Beaumont Kin]]. During Kin's involvement in the [[First Order]]'s [[Blockade of Kashyyyk|blockade]] of the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]]<ref name="Ghosts I" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Ghosts of Kashyyyk, Part 1]]" to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Kin claimed to [[Resistance pilot]] [[Daz Crano]] that<ref name="Ghosts I" /> his<ref name="Beaumont Kin DB" /> mother and [[Beaumont Kin's father|father]] told him he was useless, which was why he refused to follow his promising future and instead join the Resistance in their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the First Order.<ref name="Ghosts I" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beaumont Kin's mother was mentioned in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story "[[Ghosts of Kashyyyk, Part 1]]," published in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 27|twenty-seventh]] issue of the [[2017]] anthology comic series [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|''Star Wars Adventures'']]. The comic was written by [[John Barber]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="Ghosts I" /> on [[October 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-adventures-27-exclusive|text=''Star Wars Adventures'' #27 Gives the Mighty Chewbacca a Wookiee Welcome - Exclusive}}</ref>
