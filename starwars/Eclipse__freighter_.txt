The '''''Eclipse''''', also known by the alias '''''Tirion''''', was a [[YT-209 freighter|YT-209]] [[freighter]] used by a small band of [[smuggler]]s in [[13 BBY]]<ref name="Chronology note">"[[Mercy Mission (short story)|Mercy Mission]]" takes place one year after ''[[Lords of the Sith]]'', which takes place five years after the foundation of the Empire. Thus, it can be deduced that the story takes place in 13 BBY.</ref>. The ship was used in a [[Relief mission to Ryloth|smuggling run]] to provide medicine for [[Twi'lek]] elders on the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]]. Its [[captain]], [[Krysiant Rheden]], employed a small [[Krysiant Rheden's crew|crew]] with [[Hera Syndulla]] as its main [[pilot]] and [[Eira Tay]] as the [[Starship|ship's]] [[engineer]].<ref name="Mercy Mission" />
