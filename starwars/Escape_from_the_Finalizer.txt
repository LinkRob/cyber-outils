The [[First Order stormtrooper]] [[Finn|FN-2187]] and the [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] carried out an '''escape from the ''Finalizer''''' during the [[Cold War]] in [[34 ABY]].

==Prelude==
Shortly after the [[First Order]]'s [[Attack on Tuanul|invasion]] of the [[Church of the Force|sacred]] village of [[Tuanul]] on the desert planet of [[Jakku]], [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] was locked up on the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]'' by [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]. There, he was tortured and interrogated by the latter for information about what he did with the [[Map to Skywalker|map]] that led to [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

==The escape==
[[File:Escape-the-Finalizer.png|thumb|left|250px|Finn and Poe Dameron's stolen TIE fighter flees from the ''Finalizer'']]
[[First Order stormtrooper|Stormtrooper]] [[Finn|FN-2187]], who had just decided to desert the First Order, later helped Poe Dameron escape. After sneaking through several chambers, the two stole a TIE fighter, though they learned, too late, that it was connected to a wire. [[Thanisson]] informed his colonel that they were trying to escape, but it was too late. Other stormtroopers began firing at it, but they did not succeed in bringing it down, as it fired on the observation tower and broke free of the wire. From there, the two fugitives were attacked by turbolasers. Poe brought the fighter into view, and FN-2187 fired, destroying them entirely. As they flew back to Jakku, Kylo Ren was informed of the situation by General [[Armitage Hux]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

Although Hux did not know which stormtrooper had turned, Kylo remembered FN-2187's behavior during the attack on Tuanul. He then ordered that missiles be deployed, but FN-2187 shot one of them down. There, FN-2187 was rechristened as Finn by Dameron, as the stormtrooper was never given a different name. Finn insisted they could not return to the planet, believing they both had to escape from the First Order, but Poe insisted he had to come back for his droid, [[BB-8]]. Finn claimed that no droid could be so important, but Poe insisted he had a map to Luke Skywalker. Finn was appalled, but before he could reply, their TIE fighter was shot down by missiles, bringing the escape to a sudden end.<ref name="Episode VII" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|General, They've been hit."<br />"Destroyed?"<br />"Disabled. They were headed back to Jakku – the fighter's projected to crash in the Goazon badlands."<br />"They were going back for the droid – send a squad to the wreckage.|A technician informing General Hux about the crash of Poe and Finn's fighter|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:Jakku_from_Space.png|thumb|right|250px|The stolen TIE is sent crashing towards Jakku.]]
The blast knocked Dameron unconscious. Whereas FN-2187, dubbed "Finn" by Dameron, ejected from the TIE shortly before it crashed on the planet's surface,<ref name="Episode VII" /> Dameron recovered consciousness just in time to set the craft down more or less intact, and got out of the [[cockpit]] before something blew, leaving his [[Resistance fighter jacket|jacket]] behind. Suffering from a concussion and a brief period of memory loss, he moved away from the crash. When he awoke, Dameron found himself alone and set off to find a ship and contact the Resistance, sure that he would be able to reunite with BB-8 with their help. During his journey, he encountered a Jakku native, the [[Blarina]] [[Naka Iit]], whom Dameron managed to impress with both his sheer audacity and his piloting skills, such that he agreed to get Dameron to a ship in [[Blowback Town]] that would get him back to the Resistance.<ref name="TFA-novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>

Meanwhile, Finn found Poe's jacket, but no other sign of the man, and the fighter sunk into the sand and exploded. Believing Dameron to be dead, he discarded his armor and eventually arrived at the nearest settlement, looking for BB-8 to carry on Poe's mission. Once at the settlement, he found BB-8 along with [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], a desert scavenger, after witnessing her save the droid from two thugs. Soon after, BB-8 pointed him out to Rey, identifying Dameron's jacket, prompting the woman to chase after him. Finn attempted to outrun her but Rey outflanked him. Held under questioning and accused of stealing Dameron's jacket, Finn falsely claimed to be with the Resistance to get them to trust him. The three were immediately targeted by First Order troops looking for Finn and BB-8, but they managed to escape Jakku aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="Episode VII" />

Finn and Poe were later reunited at the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]],<ref name="Episode VII" /> just prior to the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]].
