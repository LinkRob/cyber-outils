The '''Freya system''' was a [[star system]] located in [[Wild Space]]. It was located in grid square S-18 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|system=Freya [[Star system|system]]|other=[[Freyan creeper moss]]}}</ref> of [[Freyan creeper moss]].<ref name="RR">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref>
