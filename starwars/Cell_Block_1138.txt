'''Cell Block 1138''' was an alleged [[prison]] area aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]]. [[Luke Skywalker]] mentioned the location to [[Lieutenant]] [[Shann Childsen]] in [[Detention Block AA-23]] as part of an effort to conceal his identity and the identity of [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]]. The [[Imperial officer]] stated that he was not notified of the transfer from Cell Block 1138, and that he would have to clear it.<ref name="Episode IV" />
