'''Agrippa Aldrete''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who at one point served as the [[senator]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] in the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="HC 34" /> During the [[Invasion of Naboo|Naboo Crisis]]<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> he [[Alderaanian delegation|served]] as an [[aide]] to Senator [[Bail Antilles]] of Alderaan,<ref name="HC 34" /> alongside fellow aides [[Liana Merian]] and [[Stonroy Soma]].<ref name="VE" /> At one point, he sat in a [[repulsorpod]] with Antilles and Soma in the [[Imperial Senate Building|Galactic Senate Building]] on the Republic [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]].<ref name="HC 34" />

By the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], Aldrete was an elder statesman and Antilles had been replaced by [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] as senator for Alderaan. During the conflict, Aldrete served as part of Organa's team of aides, which was led by the [[Alderaanian]] [[Sheltay Retrac]]. Aldrete had light skin, [[Color|gray]] [[hair]], and blue [[eye]]s.<ref name="HC 34" /> He wore a white [[Clothing|shirt]], white pants, and black shoes.<ref name="VE" />

Agrippa Aldrete was created for the [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']],<ref name="Deleted">{{HomeVideoCite|set=Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga|scene=Vote of No Confidence}}</ref> which was released on [[May 19]], [[1999]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> The character featured in a scene filmed on B Stage at [[Leavesden Studios]] on [[June 30]], [[1997]].<ref name="Filming">''[[The Star Wars Archives: Episodes I–III, 1999–2005]]''</ref> The scene was ultimately cut from the final release of the film and consisted of Aldrete sitting with Merian, Stoma, and Antilles in a repulorspod in the Senate Building while [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] calls for a [[Vote of No Confidence]] in [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]]. Antilles, who during the film's production was named Bail Organa, then turns to Adrete, who nods, before the senator stands to second the motion.<ref name="Deleted" />

[[File:Possible_Aldrete-TCW.png|thumb|right|300px|Character models similar to Agrippa Aldrete appear in ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars''.]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the character was first mentioned in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="VE" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the character was first mentioned in the reference book [[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary|''Star Wars: Episode I The Visual Dictionary'']], written by [[David West Reynolds]]<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and released on [[May 26]], 1999.<ref name="Amazon">{{CollectorCite|6|Books with Bite}}</ref> According to the [http://news.uwgb.edu/featured/leading-learning/01/22/aldrete-inspires-students official news page] of the [[Wikipedia:University of Wisconsin–Green Bay|University of Wisconsin–Green Bay]], Agrippa Aldrete was named after the university's Professor Gregory Aldrete and his Labrador retriever Agrippa.<ref name="UWGB">{{WebCite|url=http://news.uwgb.edu/featured/leading-learning/01/22/aldrete-inspires-students|text=Award winner: Aldrete's scholarship, enthusiasm inspire students|author=Anderson, R. Terry|date=2010-01-22|work=[http://news.uwgb.edu Inside UW-Green Bay News]|archivedate=20150922125307}}</ref>

A character model very similar in appearance to Aldrete can be seen in the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episodes "[[Assassin (episode)|Assassin]]" and "[[Crisis on Naboo]];" however, [[Holocron continuity database]] manager [[Leland Chee]] has stated that the model is not Agrippa due to its differing beard, also pointing out that the model is duplicated in some shots.<ref name="FB" />
