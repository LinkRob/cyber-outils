'''The''' '''Bandits of Peridea''',<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|bandits-of-peridea|Bandits of Peridea}}</ref> were a group of [[pirate]]s that roamed the desolate [[alien]] wasteland of the [[extragalactic]] [[planet]] of [[Peridea]] in the distant barred spiral [[far galaxy]] that spoke the same [[Noti language|language]] as the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Noti]].

==Description==
The Bandits of Peridea,<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|bandits-of-peridea|Bandits of Peridea}}</ref> also known as Peridea bandits, preyed on unprotected caravans from the desolate hills of Peridea, and charging atop their [[howler]]s to swiftly attack any unsuspecting traveler.<ref name="Name">{{Getty|url=thewaltdisneystudios/allaccess/#!asset/tbsz8wc4x6fjpw8wfx4xmqx|text=STC-FF-002961|date=2023-10-02}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|The Enemy of our Enemy is our friend, for now.|[[Baylan Skoll]], to [[Shin Hati]]|<ref name="Part Six" />}}
The Bandits of Peridea attacked anyone who ventured off onto the [[alien]] surface of [[Peridea]]. In [[9 ABY]], they attacked the [[Mandalorian]] [[Mandalorian warrior|warrior]] [[Sabine Wren]] while on her search for [[Jedi]] [[Ezra Bridger]]. They wore red [[armor]] with a staff and a rifle as a weapon.<ref name="Part Six" /> They also rode [[Howler]]s into battle. Later, they also helped [[Baylan Skoll]] and [[Shin Hati]] find and attempt to kill [[Sabine Wren]] and [[Ezra Bridger]].<ref name="Part Seven">{{Ahsoka|Part Seven: Dreams and Madness}}</ref> After the Battle of Peridea, Shin Hati made her way to their camp and ignited her lightsaber, drawing their attention.<ref name="Part Eight">{{Ahsoka|Part Eight: The Jedi, the Witch, and the Warlord}}</ref>
