The '''barrel''' of a gun was the tube through which the ammunition or energy bolt being fired traveled before leaving the weapon. The barrel of the criminal [[Tasu Leech]]'s [["Huttsplitter" blaster rifle]] was modified with oversized [[amplifier]]s to increase the charge of the bolt inside when fired.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
