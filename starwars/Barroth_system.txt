The '''Barroth system''' was located in the [[Kriz sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> and [[Western Reaches]]<ref name="Western Reaches" /> in grid square K-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> It contained the terrestrial [[moon of Barroth]], which the [[human]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Silandra Sho]] and her [[Padawan]], the human [[Rooper Nitani]], visited for the latter's [[Jedi training|training]]<ref name="Quest" /> at some point by [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Quest for the Hidden City]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Barroth [[Star system|system]] was first mentioned in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> [[June 17]], [[2025]]<ref name="Phase III">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-lucasfilm-publishing-panel|text=Phase III of ''The High Republic'' And All The Lucasfilm Publishing News from ''Star Wars'' Celebration Japan 2025 – UPDATE}}</ref> reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' on a [[The galaxy|galaxy]] map authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Christopher Ibbitt]].<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> The [[October 14]], 2025 update to<ref name="SubstackUpdate">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref> "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]] of the same year&mdash;with both works being authored by Fry<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>&mdash;subsequently identified the system by name.<ref name="Appendix" />
