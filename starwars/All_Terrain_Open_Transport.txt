The '''All Terrain Open Transport''' ('''AT-OT''') was an eight-legged [[walker]] of the [[AT-series|All-terrain vehicle]] line designed for the [[transport]]ation of infantry used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. It was deployed during the [[Battle of Felucia (Outer Rim Sieges)|Battle of Felucia]] towards the end of the war.

==Description==
[[File:AT-OTGalacticDefense.png|thumb|left|220px|An AT-OT with a top and red markings]]
The All Terrain Open Transport was a [[walker]] of the [[AT-series|All-terrain vehicle]] line with eight legs,<ref name="Episode III" /> four [[laser cannon]]s, two forward-facing medium [[cannon]]s,<ref name="Starfighters" /> and two rear antipersonnel cannons, an open top, and space to fit several [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Episode III" /> Some AT-OT's did have a top.<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> The walker traveled at a maximum speed of 55 [[Kilometer per hour|kilometers per hour]].<ref name="Starfighters" />

==Role==
AT-OTs were used primarily for transporting [[infantry]] safely into the fray of battle.<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> During the [[Battle of Felucia (Outer Rim Sieges)|Battle of Felucia]], at least three AT-OTs were used by [[clone trooper]]s to cross the battlefield in an attempt to protect key treatment facilities and prevent [[Shu Mai]],<ref name="BTM">{{BuildFalconCite|21|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> [[Presidente]] of the [[Commerce Guild]] and member of the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]],<ref name="Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition]]''</ref> from contaminating [[Felucia]]'s [[water]] supply.<ref name="BTM" />

==History==
The AT-OT was developed for use in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during [[the galaxy]]-spanning [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the Battle of Felucia near the end of the war, at least three AT-OTs were part of a republic convoy behind [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]] when [[Order 66]] was issued and her [[clone trooper]]s [[Death|shot]] her down.<ref name="Episode III" />

==Behind the scenes==
AT-OTs first appeared in the [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] on the [[planet]] [[Felucia]].<ref name="Episode III" /> The walker was later identified in the [[2014]] mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''.<ref name="Galactic Defense" />
