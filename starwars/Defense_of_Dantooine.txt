The '''defense of Dantooine'''<ref name="Helmet Collection 72">{{HelmetCollectionCite|72|Databank A-Z}}</ref> was a battle of the [[Clone Wars]] that began in the [[Standard week|weeks]] following the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]<ref name="Brotherhood" /> but was still being fought during the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]]. [[Jedi General]] [[Mace Windu]] oversaw the [[Galactic Republic]]'s defense on the [[planet]] [[Dantooine]] against<ref name="Brain Invaders" /> the [[Separatist Droid Army]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Windu's forces withstood the enemy attack but required medical supplies as a result of their efforts.<ref name="Brain Invaders" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle's passing mention in "[[Brain Invaders]]" was intended as a reference to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' television series,<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/brain-invaders-episode-guide|text="Brain Invaders" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> which depicted Mace Windu fighting a droid army single-handedly in the [[Battle of Dantooine (Clone Wars)|Battle of Dantooine]].<ref name="CW12">{{CW|12}}</ref><ref name="CW13">{{CW|13}}</ref>
