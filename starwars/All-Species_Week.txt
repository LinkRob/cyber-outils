'''All-Species Week''' was a [[Standard week|week]]-long holiday that was celebrated on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The festival celebrated the diversity of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that inhabited the [[ecumenopolis]].<ref name="Catalyst" />

The [[Erso family]] used the celebrations as a pretext to escape the [[Project Celestial Power complex]] headquarters after learning that [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso's]] research into [[kyber crystal]]s was being weaponized.<ref name="Catalyst" />
