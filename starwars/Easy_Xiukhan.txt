'''Easy Xiukhan''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Zabrak]] who joined a crew run by a [[The Smuggler|smuggler]], assisting in all sorts of missions. The [[smuggler]] was part of the [[Uprising]], fighting against the [[Galactic Empire]] in the [[Anoat sector]] at the [[time]] of the [[Iron Blockade]]. Xiukhan was a stealthy, mastermind criminal, leading other law-breakers in large [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] heists against the [[Bank of Aargau]], [[The Falleen Emerald]], and [[Lady Fate Casino|The Lady Fate Casino]].<ref name="Uprising" />
