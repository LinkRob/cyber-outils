'''''Dooku Boot''''' was a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] used by the [[Galactic Republic]] that took part in the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[nose]] of the [[gunship]] was customized with a [[painting]] of [[Count]] [[Dooku]] getting booted in the backside by a [[clone trooper]]'s [[Clone trooper armor|armored]] [[Limb|leg]].<ref name="Factory" />
