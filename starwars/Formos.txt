{{Quote|A ship approaching Kessel typically does so from Formos, where you'll find all sorts of smugglers waiting for charters. Or at least that's what they say they're doing.|Midnight's book|<ref name="Tales">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]''</ref>}}
'''Formos''' was an [[Outer Rim Territories]] astronomical object lying on the [[hyperspace route]]s known as the [[Pabol Sleheyron]] and the [[Triellus Trade Route]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Formos served as a processing location for [[spice]] transported off of the [[planet]] [[Kessel]]. Following the [[Galactic Concordance|collapse]] of the Empire, heavy fighting between competitors in the spice [[Merchant|trade]] engulfed Formos.

==Description==
Formos was an astronomical object<ref name="Tales" /> located in the [[Formos system]] of the [[Kessel sector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> a part of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="TSWGalaxy" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It lay in grid square T-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Formos was connected to the [[Prishella system]] by the [[Pabol Sleheyron]] [[hyperspace route]],<ref name="BTM" /> while the [[Triellus Trade Route]] linked it to the [[Eadu system|Eadu]]<ref name="R1" /> and [[Ku'Bakai system]]s.<ref name="TSWGalaxy" />

==History==
[[File:KesselRunMap.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Formos played a role in spice trade operating out of Kessel (a map of the Kessel Run pictured).]]
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Formos served as a processing terminus for shipments of [[spice]] traveling out of<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Kessel]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> During that [[time]], Formos boasted of protection provided by the [[Mining Guild]] of the Empire.<ref name="Dameron">''[[Poe Dameron: Free Fall]]''</ref>

After the [[Galactic Concordance|collapse]] of the Empire<ref name="TROS VD" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas" /> Formos became an easy target for competing parties in the spice [[Merchant|trade]]<ref name="Dameron" /> and became heavily embattled. It was deemed to be unsafe to operate on Formos, leading to the eventual rise of the [[pirate]] gang known as the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|Most of the time when a pilot says, 'I'm waiting for a charter,' what he really means is, 'I'm playing sabacc and getting into trouble.'|Midnight's book|<ref name="Tales" />}}
At some point between [[10 BBY]] and approximately [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MidnightBookDate}}</ref> the [[Corellian]] [[Bartender|barkeep]] known as [[Tivoche Bilure|Midnight]] mentioned Formos in a [[Midnight's book|book]] they were writing. In the book, Midnight stated that [[smuggler]] [[starship]]s usually traveled to Kessel from Formos. Midnight also claimed that [[pilot]]s waiting there to be hired to make the run spent their downtime playing the card game [[sabacc]] and getting into trouble.<ref name="Tales" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Formos.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Formos in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]]
Formos was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "Major Trade Routes,"<ref name="BTM" /> an article written by [[Mark Newbold]]<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> and included in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 29|twenty-ninth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine<ref name="BTM" /> that was published around [[July 22]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMF29date">{{BuildFalconDates|29}}</ref> It was subsequently established as a distinct celestial body in Newbold's [[January 31]], [[2017]] [[StarWars.com]] article "[[Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Rogue One?|Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Rogue One''?]]."<ref name="R1" /> Originally introduced as a [[Planet/Legends|planet]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Formos/Legends|Formos]] made its first appearance in "[[Way of the Wookiee!]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story written by [[Archie Goodwin]], penciled by [[Carmine Infantino]] and [[Pablo Marcos]], and published in the [[Star Wars Weekly 94|ninety-fourth issue]] of [[Marvel Comics]]' ''[[Star Wars Weekly]]'' comic-book series on [[December 12]], [[1979]].<ref name="Way of the Wookiee">{{StoryCite|story=Way of the Wookiee!|book=Star Wars Weekly 94}}</ref>

''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 29 reuses a map<ref name="BTM" /> from the [[2009]] ''Star Wars'' Legends reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', in which Formos is depicted as the endpoint of the [[Kessel Run/Legends|Kessel Run]] [[Hyperspace route/Legends|hyperspace route]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> The [[2018]] canon book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]'' by [[Jason Fry]], however, establishes that the [[Kessel Run]] led to [[Oba Diah]] instead, which this article assumes is correct.<ref name="Tales" />

Additionally, ''Tales from Vandor'' places Oba Diah on<ref name="Tales" /> what ''Build the Millennium Falcon'' 29<ref name="BTM" /> and the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> depict as the Pabol Sleheyron route.<ref name="BTM" /> However, a [[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|map]]<ref name="Timelines" /> created for the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'',<ref name="TimelinesSWcom">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-lucasfilm-publishing-behind-the-page-panel|text=SWCA 2022: 7 Things We Learned from the Lucasfilm Publishing Behind the Page Panel}}</ref> designed by [[Christopher Ibbitt]],<ref name="Fry email">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-31}}</ref> and given out as a promotional poster at [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Celebration Anaheim]]<ref name="TimelinesSWcom" /> on [[May 27]], [[2022]]<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|dkpublishing|status/1530292772598534145|[[Dorling Kindersley|DK Books US]]|date=2022-05-27|quote=Just announced at #StarWarsCelebration! Get your exclusive map - only at booth #2525.|archivedate=20220527210041}}</ref> prior to the book's release during [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Celebration">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-europe-2023-exclusives|text=Check Out ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Exclusives from Hasbro, the LEGO Group, and More}}</ref> of [[April 7]] through [[April 10|10]], [[2023]]<ref name="CelebrationSchedule">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-panel-schedule|text=Plan Ahead with the ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Panel Schedule}}</ref> instead established Oba Diah as Formos's neighbor on<ref name="Timelines">{{GalaxyMapPoster}}</ref> the Triellus Trade Route. This article assumes the latter placement is erroneous.<ref name="R1" />
