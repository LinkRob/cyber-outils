The '''All the Way Squad Pack''' ('''SWP31''') is an expansion for [[Atomic Mass Games]]' miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: Shatterpoint]]''. It was released on [[May 2]], [[2025]] alongside the [[Deploy the Garrison Squad Pack]], and featured miniatures of [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], [[K-2SO]], and two Rebel troopers.

==Publisher's summary==
''The All The Way Squad Pack brings the Rebel characters from'' Rogue One: A Star Wars Story™ ''to'' Star Wars™: ''Shatterpoint. The incredibly quotable Cassioan[sic] Andor is Joined by K2S0 and the Rebel Pathfinders in this exciting and thematic squad pack.''

''Using a variety of abilities, these characters bring more gameplay options to players who enjoy the Rebel Alliance. Paired on release with SWP51 and Director Krenneric, this will build for an exciting gameplay addition to'' Star Wars: ''Shatterpoint.''
