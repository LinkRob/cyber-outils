'''Arek Emjon''' was a [[Bith]] [[Gender|male]] who lived in the settlement of [[Reestkii]] [[Standard decade|decades]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]]. 

==Biography==
When a group of [[Zygerrian]] [[Slavery|slavers]] took control of the [[Reestkii]] settlement, Emjon and all of the other residents were forced into the [[Reestkii town hall|town hall]] as captives. As they sat in wait in the hall, the [[Nu-Cosian]] crittermonger [[Bobbajo]] began telling a story to the [[Youngling|children]] present about him and his pets involvement in the destruction of the first [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. Part way through the story, a loud crash outside the hall drew the attention of many within, and Emjon began anxiously whispering with fellow villagers [[P'nll Vun]] and [[Thaddeeus Marien]]. Bobbajo then continued his tale in order to calm everyone down, but it was soon interrupted again by a series of explosions. Scared, Emjon declared that the whole building was going to collapse. Again Bobbajo continued his tale to keep things calm, and when the [[Chevin]] [[Jol Bengim]] interrupted to dispute the storytellers claims, the Bith told him to be quiet, wanting to here the rest of what he considered a good story. After Bobbajo finished his tale, the storyteller opened the town hall's doors and revealed that the Zygerrians had all been incapacitated by unknown means. Bobbajo then collected up his pets, which had been loose in the town, and left the townsfolk to claim the slavers riches for themselves.<ref name="ACGAS" />

==Personality and traits==
Emjon had large [[eye]]s and was [[hair]]less like all Bith.<ref name="ACGAS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arek Emjon first appeared in "[[All Creatures Great and Small]]," a short story written by [[Landry Q. Walker]] and released in [[2015]].
