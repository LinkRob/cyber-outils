The '''mother''' of [[Everi Chalis]] was a [[Gender|female]] individual who lived prior to the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] to power<ref name="TC" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date">The section of ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'' mentioning [[Everi Chalis]]'s [[Parent|mother]] is set 22 [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Clone Wars]], which [[Mission to Mustafar|ended]] with the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[19 BBY]], according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. The events mentioning Chalis's mother must have therefore occurred in [[3 ABY]].</ref> They resided in a bombed-out [[Unidentified paper mill|paper mill]] on a [[Unidentified planet (Everi Chalis)|planet]] with an undeveloped society, sharing a stained mattress with their daughter. When Chalis was six [[Standard year|years]] old,<ref name="TC" /> her [[Parent|mother]] tried to sell her to the [[Unidentified captain (Everi Chalis)|captain]]<ref name="WOTG">''[[Star Wars: Women of the Galaxy: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> of a [[Trade Federation]] exploratory [[Starship|vessel]], but Everi was too small. As a result, Chalis remained on the [[planet]]<ref name="TC" /> until she departed by enrolling into the [[Colonial Academy]].<ref name="WOTG" /> Everi later mentioned her mother to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]]<ref name="TC" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date" />

==Behind the scenes==
Everi Chalis's mother was first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="TC" />
