The '''Champalan Imperial Mineral Extraction Initiative''' was a [[mining]] company affiliated with the [[Galactic Empire]] that drilled the landmasses of the [[planet]] [[Champala]] extensively while competing with the [[Imperial Champalan Marine Resource Collective]]. Both companies [[poison]]ed the world and bribed [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] to ignore the destruction they caused.

==Description==
The Champalan Imperial Mineral Extraction Initiative was a [[mining]] company associated with the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Rise" />

==History==
===Chewing up Champala===
{{Quote|Together both companies poisoned the land and the sea of Champala while also squabbling over which had priority for the few spaceports, which became congested and polluted.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:Chagrian_female_LBE.png|thumb|left|150px|alt=A Chagrian female in an orange Rebel Alliance pilot's flight suit.|The initiative poisoned Champala, home of the Chagrians.]]
As early as [[18 BBY]] during the [[Imperial Era]], the Champalan Imperial Mineral Extraction Initiative and the [[Imperial Champalan Marine Resource Collective]] became the two major competing industries on the [[planet]] [[Champala]], a resort world<ref name="Rise" /> that was [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[Chagrian]] [[species]].<ref name="Lead">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> The initiative extensively drilled the world's landmasses for fossilized resources, resulting in the land becoming [[poison]]ed as the collective's [[fishing]] also poisoned the [[Ocean|seas]].<ref name="Rise" />

The two companies squabbled over which had priority in Champala's few [[spaceport]]s, which became polluted and congested during the companies' activity. The collective and the initiative also paid ongoing [[Currency|bribes]] and a significant percentage of their trading profits to [[Mas Amedda]], a native of Champala and former [[Senator|representative]] of the world in the [[Galactic Senate]] who had become [[Grand Vizier]] of the Empire. This persuaded Amedda to look the other way as his homeworld and people became victim to the corporate destruction caused by the competing industries. [[Levinous]], a major shareholder in the Champalan Imperial Mineral Extraction Initiative, had close ties to Amedda and was granted the Imperial [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Grand General]], one of the most prestigious ranks within the [[Imperial Army]]. As he never left the Imperial [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]], he became known as a "[[holotable]] [[general]]."<ref name="Rise" />

===Extractor exposed===
{{Quote|Why exactly Mas Amedda allowed for his own planet and people to be the victims of such corporate destruction might seem unclear, but he later admitted under New Republic interrogation that he had accepted ongoing bribes from both companies as well as significant percentages of their trading profits.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:Grand_Vizier_Amedda.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=A holographic image of Mas Amedda in a red and black robe holding the Speaker's Staff against a black background.|The Champalan Imperial Mineral Extraction Initiative bribed Mas Amedda while mining on his homeworld.]]
After the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Levinous was among those who declared themself [[Emperor]], although he gained little support and was swiftly arrested. After [[Galactic Concordance|surrendering]] the Empire to the [[New Republic]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Amedda revealed his ties to the Champalan Imperial Mineral Extraction Initiative during interrogation by the Republic. This information was recorded in [[Mas Amedda, Interrogation Note 254|Mas Amedda, Interrogation Note #254]], which was stored in the "Imperial Prisoners" section of the [[New Republic Archives]].<ref name="Rise" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] wrote about the Champalan Imperial Mineral Extraction Initiative in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''. He discussed the company's mining of Champala and cited the interogation note, later attributing Levinous's rank almost entirely to his connections with Amedda and the collective rather than any talent he held, citing it as an example of how counter-productive the Empire's decision making process was.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Champalan Imperial Mineral Extraction Initiative was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
