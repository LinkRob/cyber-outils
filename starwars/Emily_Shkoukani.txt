'''Emily Shkoukani''' was a casting production assistant on the two-part pilot episode of ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', "[[The Recruit]],"<ref name="The Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref> and they have since become a junior creative executive at [[Lucasfilm]].<ref name="Emily Shkoukani" /> They are a member of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] and a contributing writer of features on [[StarWars.com]], including the [[Star Wars Inside Intel|''Star Wars'' Inside Intel]] series that began on [[March 30]], [[2021]].<ref name="II:Padmé Name" /> In the first episode of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Show]]'', they answered fans' questions.<ref name="THR Show">{{SWYouTube|v5phi7oXDms|A Deep Dive Into Star Wars: The High Republic, Bringing A Younger Yoda to Life (in VR), and More!}}</ref>
