'''Daiyu marketplace'''<ref name="Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref> was a marketplace in [[Daiyu City]] on the [[planet]] [[Daiyu]]. [[Aurebesh]] signage of various [[color]]s, spelling out "market", were put up on buildings on order to attract potential customers.<ref name="Kenobi" />

==History==
{{Quote|Don't smell anything. Don't look at anything. Don't touch anything.|Obi-Wan Kenobi to Princess Leia|<ref name="Kenobi" />}}
After rescuing a young [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]] from kidnappers, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] lead her through the marketplace, buying a green [[cape]] and a pair of [[glove]]s for her at [[Chiu Mo Threads]] and telling her to pretend that they were a farming family from [[Tawl]]. While continuing to walk through the marketplace, Kenobi advised Leia to keep a low profile and told her not to touch anything.

However, Leia quickly became distrustful of Obi-Wan and ran away, fleeing back into the marketplace, diving under a lumbering [[Gigoran]] and navigating through an animal auction. She then ran into the path of a [[Unidentified spicerunner|masked spicerunner]] who was hoping to claim the [[bounty]] on Kenobi. The [[spicerunner]] demanded to know his whereabouts, but Kenobi shot them down and also fought a gray skinned [[humanoid]]. He then followed Leia, but she escaped him once again while a group of [[Inquisitor]]s lead [[stormtrooper]]s into the marketplace, looking for Kenobi.<ref name="Kenobi" />
