'''Caitlin Sullivan Kelly''' is a Lead Writer<ref>{{TORweb|url=info/news/article/20220718|text=Game Update – ''Star Wars'': The Old Republic}}</ref> at ''[[Broadsword Online Games]]'', working on ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''. She is the author of the ''[[Quite A Story To Tell]], [[Intelligence Report: Historical Analysis of Manaan]],'' and ''[[All That's Left]]'' short stories. She is a former Senior Quality Analyst<ref>{{TORweb|url=info/news/article/20191220|text=Happy 8-Year Anniversary from ''Star Wars'': The Old Republic}}</ref>.
