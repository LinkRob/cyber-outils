'''Ed Dunkley''' provided video services for the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' television series,<ref name="CW1">[[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume One|''Star Wars: Clone Wars'' Volume One]]</ref> as well as [[Wikipedia:Final Fantasy: The Spirits Within|''Final Fantasy: The Spirits Within'']], [[Wikipedia:The Mexican|''The Mexican'']], [[Wikipedia:Jay and Silent Bob Strike Back|''Jay and Silent Bob Strike Back'']], [[Wikipedia:40 Days and 40 Nights|''40 Days and 40 Nights'']], [[Wikipedia:Envy (film)|''Envy'']], [[Wikipedia:Shrek 2|''Shrek 2'']], [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], and [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']]. He was also a visual effects staff member for [[Wikipedia:Twister (film)|''Twister'']], [[Wikipedia:Jurassic Park|''Jurassic Park'']], [[Wikipedia:Casper (film)|''Casper'']], [[Wikipedia:Mars Attacks!|''Mars Attacks!'']], [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], and [[Wikipedia:Wild Wild West|''Wild Wild West'']].
