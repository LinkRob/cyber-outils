'''Exim Panshard''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Sith Lord]] who held the title of [[viceroy]] on a [[Unidentified planet (Exim Panshard)|long forgotten]] [[planet]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> Panshard wore a [[Mask of Viceroy Exim Panshard|mask]] made of meteoric [[metal]] which was strong in the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] of [[the Force]]. The mask contained the screams of a hundred innocents [[Death|slaughtered]] for the viceroy's pleasure<ref name="Empire's End" /> and to give him power, enabling his own spirit to cling onto life through the mask.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> By [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> [[Yupe Tashu]] had obtained the mask. After conferring with the spirits of fallen Sith, he gifted the mask to the [[Pantoran]] [[Acolytes of the Beyond|Acolyte]] [[Kiza]]. When her boyfriend [[Remi]] demanded the mask for himself, Tashu chastised him for angering the fallen specters. He also confiscated Remi's [[Kiza's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and gave it to Kiza. Under the influence of Panshard's mask, Kiza took part in an [[Attack on a New Republic outpost|attack]] on a [[New Republic]] [[Devaron New Republic outpost|outpost]] on [[Devaron]] and killed Remi when he tried to reclaim his [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

Panshard's spirit made Kiza into his pawn on the promise she would get power if she brought him to the Sith [[planet]] [[Exegol]], where he hoped he would be reborn to bring about a new era of Sith rule. However, Panshard's plans were ruined<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> in [[21 ABY]]<ref name="Death" /> after [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] sensed his growing darkness. The [[quest for Exegol]] ended in defeat for Panshard; after Kiza was killed and he took full control over her body, Skywalker destroyed his mask before it could claim him as its vessel, ending the threat of Panshard once and for all.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />
