The '''appraiser's eye''' was a model of [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] [[eye]] that was a combination of a [[scanner]] and a cross-reference tool. Each unit cost 700 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and allowed the wearer to quickly identify various products and record local commodity prices.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
The appraiser's eye was mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
