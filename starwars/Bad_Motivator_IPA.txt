'''Bad Motivator IPA''' was an [[alcohol]]ic beverage served at the [[Oga's Cantina]] bar in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> The drink was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> [[Eloc Throno]], author of the ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', considered it to be one of the most flavorful beers on tap at the cantina.<ref name="Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Bad Motivator IPA is an [[Wikipedia:India pale ale|India pale ale]] available on tap to customers at the Oga's Cantina bar in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened in [[2019]].<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> It was revealed as part of a large promotional event held on [[February 26]], [[2019]].<ref name="Nerdist">{{WebCite|url=https://nerdist.com/article/star-wars-galaxys-edge-food-drinks-cocktails-menu-disney-parks|text=What You Can Eat and Drink in STAR WARS: Galaxy's Edge|work=[[Wikipedia:Nerdist Industries|Nerdist]]|author=[[Amy Ratcliffe]]|date=2019-02-27|archivedate=20190228074254}}</ref> The drink is named for the "red" [[droid]] [[R5-D4]]<ref name="OC Register">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ocregister.com/2019/05/24/disneyland-turns-to-big-time-craft-breweries-for-intergalactic-beers-on-ogas-cantina-menu-in-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Disneyland turns to big-time craft breweries for intergalactic beers on Oga's Cantina menu in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|author=MacDonald, Brady|date=2019-05-24|work=The Orange County Register|archivedate=20201125151306}}</ref> that [[Owen Lars]] almost purchased in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. When the droid displayed a "bad [[motivator]]," Lars purchased [[R2-D2]] instead.<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

Bad Motivator IPA is brewed by [[Wikipedia:Sierra Nevada Brewing Company|Sierra Nevada Brewing Company]], an independent brewery based in [[Wikipedia:Chico, California|Chico, California]].<ref name="OC Register" /> It has an [[Wikipedia:Alcohol by volume|ABV]] of 6.0%, and its menu description reads, "Tropical India Pale Ale with galaxy and comet hops."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/ogas-cantina/menus/dinner|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Oga's Cantina: Dinner Menu}}</ref>
