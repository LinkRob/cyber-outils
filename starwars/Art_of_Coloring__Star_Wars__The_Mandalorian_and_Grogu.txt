'''''Art of Coloring: Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu''''' is an adult coloring book based on the film [[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu|''The Mandalorian and Grogu'']]. It was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[April 21]], [[2026]] as part of Disney's "Art of Coloring" series.<ref name="NYCC">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2025-lucasfilm-publishing-reveals|text=NYCC 2025: All the News and Reveals from the Lucasfilm Publishing Panel}}</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
''​A brand-new entry in the Art of Coloring series based on Star Wars featuring over 100 pages of art.''.<ref name="Edelweiss" />
