"'''The Force Unleashed'''" is a theatrical motif written by [[Mark Griskey]] for the [[Musical score|score]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]''. It was written in [[2008]]. The theme is used to represent the entire ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]'' project.
