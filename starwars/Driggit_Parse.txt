'''Driggit Parse''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who was a friend of the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Gavi Takitaken]]. When the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Attack on Valo|attacked]] the [[planet]] [[Valo]], Gavi was forced to leave her behind as he went into hiding with fellow [[youngling]]s [[Kildo]] and [[Tep Tep]]. Leaving his friend behind left Gavi with complicated feelings of guilt. Parse eventually became a member of the Nihil.<ref name="EfV" />
