'''Den Harrit''' was a [[Togruta]] [[Jedi Knight]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Night of Sorrow]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Harrit participated in an [[Night of Sorrow|attack]] on the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[The Force|Force]] [[religion]] on the [[planet]] [[Dalna]]. Fellow [[Jedi]] [[Enya Keen]] noticed Harrit fighting among relentless Path members alongside fellow Togruta [[Jedi Master]] [[Fliss Woora]] and Jedi Knight [[Meya Wuk]].<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Behind the scenes==
