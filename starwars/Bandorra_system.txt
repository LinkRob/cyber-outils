The '''Bandorra system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]. It was located in grid square N-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Bandorra system was first mentioned in the second iteration of the [[Star Systems of the Galaxy]],<ref name="Appendix" /> an appendix of star systems written by [[Jason Fry]] and released on [[StarWars.com]] on [[October 13]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] video game ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Alliance]]''.<ref name="XWing">''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Alliance]]''</ref>
