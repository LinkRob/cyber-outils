The '''Battle of the Kaliida Nebula''', also known as the '''Battle of Kaliida Shoals Medcenter''' took place near the [[Kaliida Nebula]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The battle was famous for being the destruction of the current [[flagship]] of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[General]] [[Grievous]], the ''[[Malevolence]]''. The ship had been destroying [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] fleets throughout the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] due to it's [[Ion cannon]], which could disable an entire [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] in a single blast. The battle was lead by General [[Anakin Skywalker]], who led a squad of [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]]s, after suffering heavy losses, the squad moved from attacking the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], to attacking the ion cannon itself. The squad launched it's torpedoes, which resulted in the ship suffering heavy damage. After this three ''Venator''-class Star Destroyers arrived to help finish the job.<ref name="Shadow" />

==Prelude==
[[File:Battle_of_Abregado.jpg|thumb|left|220x220px|General Grievous destroys a Republic fleet.]]
In the [[22 BBY|first year]] of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[General]] [[Grievous]] commanded the secret weapon of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the ''[[Malevolence]]'',<ref name="Rising Malevolence">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> a [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> was a [[starship]] armed with two [[mega-ion cannon]]s that, when fired, could disable all functions of entire [[fleet]]s, leaving the ships without power for [[Deflector shield|shields]], weapons or [[starfighter]]s, rendering it completely defenseless to a barrage of [[cannon]] fire from the ''Malevolence''. It was later dubbed the "Mystery Weapon" by the [[Galactic Republic]] as a result of it appearing without warning, destroying Republic fleets and killing any living witnesses who used [[escape pod]]s to escape, thus becoming a mysterious weapon. One such fleet that was destroyed was the [[Plo Koon's fleet|fleet]] commanded by [[Jedi Master]] [[Plo Koon]], which met its end at the hands of Grievous during a brief [[Battle of Abregado|incursion]] in the [[Abregado system]]. However, the ''Malevolence'' and its ion cannons were finally discovered by the Galactic Republic after Plo Koon survived the battle with the help of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], who both left for the [[Abregado-rae|Abregado]] [[Star system|system]] against the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council's]] orders and managed to find and rescue their fellow [[Jedi]], and reported the existence of the ''Malevolence'' to the Council.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

After being discovered by the Galactic Republic and [[Battle of the Ryndellia system|destroying]] a convoy of [[Pelta-class frigate|medical frigates]] escorted by a lone [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] near [[Naboo]], the Separatist [[Head of State]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] gave Grievous the ''Malevolence''{{'s}} next target, the Republic's secret [[Kaliida Shoals Medical Center|medical station]] near the [[Kaliida Nebula]], which more than sixty-thousand wounded clone troopers were being tended to by the [[Kaminoan]] [[doctor]] [[Nala Se]]. Dooku ordered his faithful general to destroy the station, knowing that if the station was destroyed, the war would turn in favor of the Separatists. Catching wind of the Separatist ambush in the Ryndellia system, [[Jedi General]]s Plo Koon, Anakin Skywalker, and Skywalker's Padawan Ahsoka Tano remembered that their medical station was located within the system, as well, and had suspicions that Grievous would launch an attack on it, and the three Jedi left for the medical station immediately with a [[Shadow Squadron|squadron]] of [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber|Y-wing bombers]].<ref name="Shadow" />
