'''''Darth Vader'' 49''' is the forty-ninth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]] and [[Federico Blee]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[August 14]], [[2024]].

==Plot summary==
On the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Endoviara Seven]], the members of the [[M.A.R. Corps]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] tinker with the immobilized body of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], implanting him with a [[droid brain]] as revenge for him doing so to them. They formally introduce themselves to Vader, but the [[Sith Lord]] cares not for their identities. Instead, he evokes the name of [[Luke Skywalker]].

Luke Skywalker is on [[Caranthanax B]] with [[Sabé]] and [[Warba Calip]], [[Sabé's H-type Nubian yacht|their starship]] in need of extensive repairs after Luke damaged key systems with [[Luke Skywalker's yellow lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. Before repairs can be completed, they see an [[V-82 dropship]] landing nearby. Luke does not sense Vader's presence, as the occupants are [[Administrator]] [[Sly Moore|Sly Moore's]] [[Schism Imperial]] and a legion of [[Slandarv's battle droid model|high-tech battle droids]] led by [[Agent]] [[Fabarian]]. The [[droid]]s march on the [[H-type Nubian yacht]]. Although they do not detect any life forms, they destroy the ship. 

To draw the droids away from his allies, Luke distracts them with a decoy before attacking up close with his lightsaber, taking out many of the droids. After Fabarian confirms Luke's presence, [[Governor]] [[Tauntaza]], wearing [[Tauntaza's armor|her armor]] and wielding [[Darth Vader's electrostaff]] imbued with the power of a [[color|red]] [[Kyber crystal]] and a [[power cell]] from [[Tauntaza's fortress|her Death Machine]], fires a massive energy beam on Luke's location from the loading ramp of a Schism ship. This destroys the droids, and Luke helps Fabarian dive to safety. Sensing the danger and fear, Vader hopes his son can unleash his hate. 

Aboard the Schism ship, a technical flaw in the makeshift electrostaff causes Tauntaza to be injured from her attack. Moore is upset at the setback, especially realizing Vader is aware of the attack.

Back on Endoviara Seven, the Rebel [[cyborg]]s inquire more about why Vader cares about Skywalker. He emphasizes the urgency of the situation, and since they are tapped into Vader's nervous system, they know he is truthful. [[ZED-6-7]] explains Skywalker's value as a [[Jedi]] and that an alliance with Vader is the only way for the cyborgs to potentially survive this situation. This is especially true as Vader starts to overcome the interference of the droid brain.

On Caranthanax B, Luke sees his allies continue to flee, but also notices that Fabarian lost his left leg in the attack. Luke helps Fabarian relocate to a new position. On the Schism ship, Moore receives the coordinates of Calip and Sabé, but decides to focus on Skywalker. With Tauntaza's injuries, however, she cannot fire again. This leaves Moore as the only person who can don the [[armor]]. Before any action can occur, the Death Machine arrives to the battlefield with [[Corleque]] at the controls. Moore then broadcasts a message to Luke threatening to activate the machine. She reveals the Schism Imperial to Luke, their plan to overthrow the [[Emperor]]. She then extends an offer for the two of them to potentially rule as [[Rule of Two|the two Sith]], the Schism joining the Rebel Alliance. 

Moore pushes that Luke needs to show himself, that being the only way to survive and protect the planet. Meanwhile, Sabé and Calip take out the droids guarding the Troop Transport. With all the [[sentience|non-sentient]] life on the planet, Luke decides to present himself to Moore as Vader arrives to the scene. Going against her word, Moore seizes the opportunity to aim her energy weapons at Luke, and Vader attempts to stop the attack with [[the Force]], to no avail. The weapons strike Luke, launching him backwards, gravely injured.
