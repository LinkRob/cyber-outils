An '''assault boat''' was a type of landing craft used for military operations. 

One assigned [[R-1]] was used to land [[Kyle Katarn]]'s troops prior to the [[Battle of AX-456]].<ref name="DFSE">''[[Dark Forces: Soldier for the Empire]]''</ref> The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] used an assault boat to breach the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Imperial orbital base|orbital base]] over [[Mantooine/Legends|Mantooine]] during [[Operation Cobolt]].<ref name="RASB">''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]''</ref>

Assault boats participated in the [[Battle of Fort Nowhere]].<ref name="Dark Forces: Jedi Knight">''[[Dark Forces: Jedi Knight]]''</ref>
