'''Episode IV Classic Moments''' was an image archive on [[StarWars.com]] that provided concept art and behind the scenes images from production of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. Accompanied by quotes from production staff, many of these rare images, which were first published in this feature, have yet to appear elsewhere.
