'''Durelium''' was a substance that was profitable for [[smuggler]]s. The smuggler [[Platt Okeefe]] recommended it as a good cargo to turn a profit in the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]]'s ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', along with [[meleenium]], [[hyperbaride]]s.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Druelium was introduced into the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] by the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> It originated in the [[1997]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] roleplaying game supplement ''[[Pirates & Privateers]]''.<ref name="Pirates">''[[Pirates & Privateers]]''</ref>
