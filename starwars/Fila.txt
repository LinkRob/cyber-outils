'''Fila''' was a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Song of Winter's Heart" /> During or after [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> she worked as a [[scout]] for the Rebel Alliance during a [[Battle on Shard|battle]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Shard]].<ref name="The Song of Winter's Heart" />

Partnered with the [[Alderaanian]] [[Rel (Alderaanian)|Rel]], the two were tasked to scout a [[Shard Imperial base|temporary base]] that the Imperials had erected. While on the mission, Fila was fatally shot by an Imperial [[sniper]]. Rel was unable to get to his partner's side as she lay [[Death|dying]] alone in the [[Water|snow]]. Two [[Standard day|days]] later, Rel was determined to finish the mission, believing that not completing it would mean that Fila's sacrifice would have been for nothing.<ref name="The Song of Winter's Heart" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fila was mentioned in "[[The Song of Winter's Heart]]," a short story written by [[Cavan Scott]] and [[George Mann]] for the ''[[Life Day Treasury]]'' anthology, which was published on [[September 7]], [[2021]]. Prior to the book's release, Fila was first mentioned in a preview for the short story in a [[StarWars.com]] article published on [[September 3]] of the same year.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/life-day-treasury-exclusive-excerpt|text=Two Friends Caught on Opposing Sides Reconnect in the ''Life Day Treasury'' - Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
