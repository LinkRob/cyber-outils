The '''diplomatic corps''' was a branch of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. When [[Luke Skywalker]] was sat in a [[BTL Y-wing starfighter]] after being assigned a [[mission to recover Imperial communication logs]], he noted that the two-seat configuration in the Y-wing suggested someone from the Alliance was going with him. Skywalker hoped it wouldn't be a member of the [[diplomat]]ic corps who'd spend the journey practising speeches and getting spacesick, before finding out his companion was [[C-3PO]].<ref name="Weapon" />
