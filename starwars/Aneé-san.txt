'''Aneé-san''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Sith]] who worked as a [[bodyguard]] for [[Anzellan]] [[gangster]] [[Que-Dama]], wielding a [[Color|red]]-[[Aneé-san's lightsaber|bladed]] [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Payback" /> She<ref name="Focus" /> spent her [[Currency|money]] on making her body more beautiful. At one point, she faced off against<ref name="Payback" /> the former Sith<ref name="Ronin">''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]''</ref> known as [[the Ronin]].<ref name="Payback" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aneé-san appeared in "[[The Duel: Payback]]", a non-[[canon]] short film directed by [[Takanobu Mizuno]] that released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Kamikaze Douga]] and [[ANIMA (company)|ANIMA]],<ref name="Payback" /> released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/why-we-love-star-wars-visions|text=Why We Love the Unique Stories of ''Star Wars: Visions''}}</ref> The character was first introduced at the ''Star Wars: Visions'' panel at [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]] on [[April 19]], 2025.<ref name="Day 3">{{SWYouTube|83cNchT1gdA|Star Wars Celebration - DAY 3 Replay|livestream=p20GM68wyC4}}</ref> The spelling of her name was confirmed in a [[StarWars.com]] article published on [[April 25]] of that year.<ref name="First Look">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-star-wars-visions-volume-3-spinoff-announce|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 3 First Look and Spinoff Announced – ''Star Wars'' Celebration Japan 2025}}</ref> She is voiced by [[Takako Honda]] in the original [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]] version and by [[Suzy Nakamura]] in the English dub. Concept art of Aneé-san was created by [[Takashi Okazaki]].<ref name="Payback" /> The character is an homage to [[Wikipedia:Onna-musha|''onna-musha'']], female [[Wikipedia:Samurai|samurai]] warriors.<ref name="Focus" /> A production brief for the series describes Aneé-san as a former Sith;<ref name="Getty">{{Getty|url=s/b66vps8kq5ts777tc8bbbp|text=VISIONS SEASON 3 Final Production Brief|date=2025-10-23}}</ref> However, other sources only describe her as a Sith.<ref name="Focus" /><ref name="Day 3" /><ref name="First Look">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-star-wars-visions-volume-3-spinoff-announce|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 3 First Look and Spinoff Announced – ''Star Wars'' Celebration Japan 2025}}</ref><ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-visions/the-duel-payback-episode-guide|text="The Duel: Payback" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars: Visions Volume 3}}</ref>
