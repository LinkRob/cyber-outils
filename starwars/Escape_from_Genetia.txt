The '''escape from Genetia''' was carried out by the [[Ro family]]<ref name="PoV">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> between around [[397 BBY]] and [[387 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> On the [[planet]] [[Genetia]], members of the [[Evereni]] [[Ro family]] were attacked in the night by townspeople who believed the family was to blame for their crops failing. [[Yana Ro's sire]] was killed in the attack, and [[Marda Ro's sire]] fought viciously to ensure his family would escape. [[Yana Ro|Yana]] and [[Marda Ro]]'s grandparents were also killed in the attack. [[Yana Ro's mother|Yana]] and [[Marda Ro's mother|Marda's mothers]] escaped with the two girls after killing a family of [[Theelin]]s and taking their ship.<ref name="PoD" />
