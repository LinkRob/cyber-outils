The '''DLT cannon''' was a custom [[blaster]] that was based on the DLT chassis but modified for greater firepower. It was a large cannon and was the length of an adolescent [[human]] [[Gender|boy]]. The [[Ithorian]] [[gangster]] [[Herf (Ithorian)|Herf]] wielded a DLT cannon during a confrontation with the teenage [[junk dealer]] [[Temmin Wexley]]. The cannon was destroyed during a fight with Wexley's [[B1-series battle droid]] bodyguard "[[Mister Bones]]," who sliced it into three pieces with his [[vibroblade]] arm.<ref name="Aftermath" />
