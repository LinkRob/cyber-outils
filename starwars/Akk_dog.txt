'''Akk dogs''' were a type of [[dog]] native to [[Anoat]], a [[planet]] in the [[Anoat sector]]. They were [[Limb|quadrupedal]], with [[Color|red]], scaly skin, four horns, a pair of blue [[eye]]s, and rows of pointed [[Tooth|teeth]]. At the [[time]] of the [[Iron Blockade]], akk dogs freely roamed the streets of Anoat.<ref name="Uprising" /> When obtained young, akk dogs actually made good companions and guardians.<ref name="Akk Pups">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Daily Opportunity: "Akk Pups"</ref>
