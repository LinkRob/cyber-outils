The '''Chasidron Shoals''' were a location<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> in the [[Bortele sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Resistance]] captured the ''[[Vigil]]'' in a [[Battle of Chasidron Shoals]].<ref name="ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> The [[pirate]] group [[Kanjiklub]] once fought at the Chasidron Shoals where, according to [[Razoo Qin-Fee]], they knocked their challengers stupid.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
