'''Curtek Homestead''' was a home built into the side of a cliff located in the [[South Jundland Wastes]] region of the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the homestead had been abandoned.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> [[Kay Vess]] visited the homestead and found a vintage [[chronometer|chrono]] there.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Curtek Homestead appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' published by [[Ubisoft]]. The vintage chrono found at the homestead can be equipped at as a charm which increased the super cooling window on the players blaster.<ref name="Outlaws" />
