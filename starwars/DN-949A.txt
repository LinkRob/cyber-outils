'''DN-949A''' was an outpost located on the [[planet]] [[Netalych]] that served as the [[Galactic Empire]]'s tertiary [[fuel]]ing post and chemical processing facility for its [[sector]]. It had an organic population of less than 500 and was worked by around 1600 [[droid]]s of [[Class five droid|fifth degree]] or higher. Six [[Standard day|days]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], the droid population overthrew the local military forces and claimed independence for the outpost and the right to self-governance. A [[Standard year|year]] after Endor, an Imperial strike team led by [[Yrica Quell]] of the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] visited the outpost so that Quell could discreetly meet with the droid known as the [[Surgeon]] and have the remains of one of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[Sentinel (Contingency)|Messenger droids]] analyzed.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
