'''Firaxan sharks''' were a type of [[shark]].<ref name="Inquisitor" /> By [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'' describes [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]]' [[Iskat Akaris' first lightsaber|first lightsaber]] as having a firaxan shark [[tooth]] hilt end immediately prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the to [[22 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] had a polished firaxan shark [[tooth]] attached to the end of her [[Iskat Akaris' first lightsaber|first lightsaber]]. She made use of the [[lightsaber]] throughout the [[Clone Wars]], but it was eventually destroyed by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] shortly after Akaris joined the [[Inquisitorius]].<ref name="Inquisitor" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the tooth of a firaxan shark appeared in [[Delilah S. Dawson]]'s [[2023]] novel ''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''.<ref name="Inquisitor" /> The [[Firaxan shark/Legends|sharks]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they first appeared in the [[2003]] [[BioWare]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''.<ref name="KOTOR">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref>
