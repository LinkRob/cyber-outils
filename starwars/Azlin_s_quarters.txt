'''Azlin's quarters''' was a living quarters<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" /> located in the [[Jedi Temple Detention Center]]<ref name="Edge of Balance V3" /> within the lower levels of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the former [[Jedi]] [[Azlin Rell]], who had been advising the [[Jedi Order]] about the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures, was moved to the quarters after he had caused the [[Attack on Travyx Prime|destruction]] of a [[Unidentified Travyx Prime city|city]] on [[Travyx Prime]]. The room was clean and sparsely furnished, and Rell&mdash;who was blind&mdash;kept its light off when not receiving visitors. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Reath Silas]] often visited Rell in his quarters while researching the Nameless.<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" /> After helping the Jedi during a [[mission to Banchii]], Rell was moved from the quarters to an upgraded [[Azlin's chamber|chamber]], which Silas felt had been earned.<ref name="Into the Light">''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]''</ref>
