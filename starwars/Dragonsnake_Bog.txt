'''Dragonsnake Bog''' was a murky lagoon located on the [[swamp]] [[planet]] of [[Dagobah]]. It was named after its most dangerous occupant, a giant [[snake]]-like beast called the [[dragonsnake]]. When the the [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] decided to go on a self-imposed exile on Dagobah after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], he settled his [[Yoda's Hut|mud hut]] not far from Dragonsnake Bog.<ref name="JTS" />

[[Standard year|Years]] later, following the [[Battle of Hoth]], [[Luke Skywalker]] accidentally landed [[Red Five (T-65B X-wing starfighter)|his]] [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] [[starfighter]] in Dragonsnake Bog. Skywalker's [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] plunged into the [[water]], and ran afoul of a [[Unidentified dragonsnake|hungry dragonsnake]] that resided in the lagoon. Finding R2-D2 inedible, the creature spat him out onto the muddy floor.<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref>
