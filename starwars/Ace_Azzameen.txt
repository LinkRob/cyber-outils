'''Ace Azzameen''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who was a member of the [[Azzameen family/Canon|Azzameen family]]. He was the youngest son of [[Tomaas Azzameen]], and owned a [[droid]] named [[MK-09]]. After the [[death]]s of Tomaas and one of Ace's siblings, [[Galin Azzameen|Galin]], Ace took possession of the family's [[YT-2000 light freighter|YT-2000]] [[Otana|''Otana'']], and traveled to the [[Anoat sector|Anoat Sector]] with his siblings to seek refuge among the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="BMF" />
