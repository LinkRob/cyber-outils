Between c. [[1100 BBY]] and c. [[1000 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Momin]] was guided by the [[dark side of the Force]] to select and '''destroy''' a [[Unidentified city (Momin)|city]]. Momin created a [[Darth Momin's superweapon|superweapon]], with the help of [[Darth Momin's acolytes|his acolytes]] that possessed the power to destroy the [[city]]. The [[superweapon]] began to slowly destroy the city as the inhabitants realized their [[Death|fates]]. But then several combat hardened [[Jedi]] infiltrated the superweapon, and even though his acolytes put up a formidable defense it was not enough. The presence of the Jedi interfered with Momin's concentration with the [[weapon]], causing him to lose control of the [[engine]]s. The superweapon fired and obliterated the city in an instant. The weapon blew up, killing Momin but his consciousness survived in his [[Mask of Lord Momin|mask]]. Thousands of [[Standard year|years]] later, Momin told his story to [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|It was a weapon, of course, powerful enough to burn the city to ash.|Darth Momin to Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />}}
[[Darth Momin]] was a [[Sith Lord]] and a [[sculptor]]. Momin was [[Prison|imprisoned]] for the horror displayed in his work but later freed by [[Lord|Sith Lady]] [[Darth Shaa]] who trained him. After [[Death|killing]] Shaa, Momin traveled to [[the galaxy]] and became engulfed with the idea of creating art that would satisfy the [[dark side of the Force]] itself. [[The Force]] reached out to Momin and told him to choose a [[city]]. Using resources he inherited from Shaa and the help of [[Darth Momin's acolytes|his acolytes]], Momin created a [[Darth Momin's superweapon|superweapon]] powerful enough to burn the city to ash.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

==Destruction==
{{Quote|The crucial moment passed. The pain and horror I wanted existed only for a blink, evaporating as quickly as the city itself.|Momin to Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />}}
[[File:MominsSuperweapon-2017DarthVader22.jpg|thumb|left|175px|Darth Momin's superweapon]]
Momin flew the superweapon over the city and began the destruction. Momin planned to pour the force into the [[engine]] in order to slow the flow of [[time]] and in doing so freeze the painful reactions of the inhabitants of the city for eternity like insects fossilized in amber. As the inhabitants looked on with fear, Momin's twisted art project was working as the engine drifted into position for the perfect stroke. But then two members of the [[Jedi Order]] arrived to put an end to his plan. Momin's acolytes fought the Jedi but were unable to put up a successful defense as they were cut down by the formidable knights.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

The presence of the [[light side of the Force]] interfered with Momin's concentration as he lost control of the engine as the superweapon lit up in [[Fire|flames]] whilst the opposing forces exerted their power. But the weapon still fired and obliterated the city in an instant. Momin however, was unable to capture the horror he wished to preserve in time with the Force. Momin's physical body died in the fire but his consciousness remained in his [[Mask of Lord Momin|mask]].<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

==Aftermath==
Over a thousand [[Standard year|years]] later, [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] took the Momin mask to Mustafar with him. Claiming a new [[Roggo|body]], Momin recalled the events of the destruction of the city to Vader. Vader then employed Momin as the architect for [[Fortress Vader]].<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

==Behind the scenes==
The destruction of the city first appeared in the [[2018]] [[canon]] comic [[Darth Vader (2017) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 22]] in a flashback. The comic was written by [[Charles Soule]] and illustrated by [[Giuseppe Camuncoli]].<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />
