The '''AAL-2100/9.5 atmospheric assault lander''', also known as the '''Sith Transporter''', was a [[troop transport]] utilized by the [[Sith Eternal]] for deploying [[Sith trooper]]s onto the battlefield. It was an updated version of the [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport]], used by the [[First Order]]. The ship had the same structure as the AAL-1971/9.1 [[Troop]] [[Transport]], with a disembarkation ramp and a troop compartment. It also featured a [[blaster cannon]] mounted by a [[Sith]] [[trooper]] on the top section of the craft. Unlike its predecessor, the AAL-2100/9.5 did not have a tall [[cockpit]], instead sporting a low one near the stern. It saw action at the [[Battle of Exegol]] during the end of the [[First Order-Resistance War]], deploying Sith troopers onto the hull of the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] to engage [[Resistance]] forces.<ref name="TROS VD" />
