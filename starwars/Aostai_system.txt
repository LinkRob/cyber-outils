The '''Aostai system''' was located in [[Wild Space]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="The Interior" /> It was situated in grid square I-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Aostai [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The system originated as the [[Aostai region]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in [[Clone Wars Gambit: Siege|''Siege'']], [[Karen Miller]]'s [[2010]] second entry in the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars Gambit]]'' novel duology.<ref name="Siege">''[[Clone Wars Gambit: Siege]]''</ref>
