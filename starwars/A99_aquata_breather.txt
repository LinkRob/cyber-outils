The '''A99 aquata breather''', also called a '''Jedi breathing device''', was a small [[Rebreather|breathing]] device consisting of a compact mouthpiece that could fit in a utility belt. It was designed to supply its wearer with a breathable [[gas]], such as [[oxygen]] in the case of [[human]]s. Thanks to its advanced filters, it could work underwater, in a [[vacuum]], or among certain [[Poison|noxious]] environments. Its miniature compressed air tanks could supply oxygen for up to two [[Standard hour|hours]]. [[Jedi Knight]]s commonly carried aquata breathers during field operations. There existed a number of different variants customized for use by non-Human [[species]] who breathed gases other than oxygen,<ref name="SWE" /> including the [[Antiox breath mask]] that was designed for the [[Kel Dor]] species.<ref name="Lightsabers and Jedi Equipment" /> [[Jedi]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] used aquata breathers when traveling to the [[Water|underwater]] [[city]] of [[Otoh Gunga]] on [[Naboo]].<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> Some time before the [[Galactic Civil War]], an old A99 fell into the possession of [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] the [[Hutt]], who kept it as part of his collection of Jedi artifacts.<ref name="SW9">[[Star Wars (2015) 9|''Star Wars'' (2015) 9]]</ref>
