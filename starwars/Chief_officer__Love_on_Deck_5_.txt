A fictional '''chief officer''' in the [[holodrama]] ''[[Love on Deck 5]]''. The series released at least three parts<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> In the first part of the drama the [[Captain (Love on Deck 5)|Captain]] invites the [[Musician|singer]] [[Bellis (Love on Deck 5)|Bellis]] to dinner. Bellis accepted the offer noting that the captain should make sure the chief officer since Bellis is trying to make her jealous. In part three the Captain notes that despite Bellis efforts, things did not work out between Bellis and the chief officer.<ref name="Outlaws" />
