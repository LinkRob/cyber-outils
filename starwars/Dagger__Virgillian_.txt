'''Dagger''' was a [[Virgillia system|Virgillian]] woman and representative of the [[Virgillian Free Alignment]] who met with [[Arvel Crynyd]] at [[The End of the World]] tavern on [[Koda Station]]. She had promised Crynyd a message from one of his pilots, which was revealed to be the death of his former student [[Raf (Rebel)|Raf]].  <ref name="FACPOV" />
