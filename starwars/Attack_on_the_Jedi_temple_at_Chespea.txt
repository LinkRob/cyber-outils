During the [[High Republic Era]] in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Nihil]] carried out the '''attack on the Jedi temple at Chespea'''. The [[Chespea Temple]] was destroyed by [[Nihil]] [[Starship|ships]], along with Jedi Master [[Imgree]], her [[Imgree's Padawan|Padawan]] and all the [[Jedi]] inside the temple. As the [[Jedi temple|temple]] was attacked by the [[Nihil]], Imgree told her [[Padawan]] to contact the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. The Padawan nodded before he questioned if she wanted him to tell Coruscant that they were under attack. His Master shook her head, and replied that she wanted him to inform them that they were gone. The Padawan hesitated momentarily before he bravely did what Imgree had asked him to do. After he had contacted Coruscant and told, Nihil fire blasted through the temple's golden roof, and incinerated Imgree, her Padawan, and everybody inside the Temple.<ref name="TFS" />
