The '''central water garden of Rillias II''' was a [[water]] [[garden]] located on the [[planet]] [[Rilias II]].<ref name="TOTF" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the anti-[[Nihil]] operative [[Cair San Tekka]] broadcasted a message recorded by [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] from the [[water]] [[garden]]. He chose the location because a funnel in the garden made of [[foris alloy]] amplified certain audio frequencies.<ref name="TOTF" />
