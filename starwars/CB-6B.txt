'''CB-6B''' was an [[Color|orange-and-silver]]&ndash;plated [[BB-series astromech droid]] with a black [[sensor]].<ref name="Funko" /> The [[droid]], like all of its model, was manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]] and stood 0.67 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" />

CB-6B originated from [[Black Spire Outpost]],<ref name="Funko" /> a settlement on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Batuu]],<ref name="Batuu">{{Databank|black-spire-outpost|Black Spire Outpost}}</ref> and it served beside [[Resistance]] [[pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] while his [[astromech droid]], [[BB-8]], was stationed elsewhere on a separate mission. CB-6B could serve as a copilot.<ref name="Funko" />

==Behind the scenes==
CB-6B was created for a [[Funko]] Pop! toy released in [[2020]].<ref name="Funko" />
