'''Breasts''' were an anatomical feature on some [[species]] in [[the galaxy]], such as the [[human]], [[Dowaaka]], and [[Askajian]] species.<ref name="Note">For the purposes of this article, features that appear to be breasts in ''[[Star Wars]]'' visual media will be conjecturally referred to as "breasts" based on visual resemblance, even though they are not identified as such in the sources cited.</ref> Breasts occurred in both [[Sentience|sentient]] and non-sentient species.

==Description==
[[File:ChestMaleHuman.png|thumb|left|150px|Anakin Skywalker, a human male, displays defined chest muscles beneath his breasts.]]
[[Human]]s were a [[species]] in [[the galaxy]] known to nurse their [[Youngling|young]]. The human [[Gender|female]] [[Lyra Erso]] would breastfeed [[Jyn Erso]], her daughter with [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen]], which sometimes led the infant to fall [[Sleep|asleep]].<ref name="Catalyst" /> Adolescent humans would experience breast development as they progressed towards adulthood.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> When Jyn was an adolescent, she preferred to show off the development of her bicep muscles instead; however, she was pleased to dress up in a fitted [[Clothing|robe]] and cosmetics when she participated in the [[Mission to Inusagi|infiltration]] of [[Inusagi]]'s [[sakoola blossom festival]] by [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]].<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref> Some human adults, such as [[Selbie]], had a notably ample bosom.<ref name="M&A">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref>

Some adult beings displayed increased development of the chest muscles beneath their breasts, including the male [[Jedi Master]] [[Kirak Infil'a]],<ref name="Darth Vader 2">[[Darth Vader (2017) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 2]]</ref> [[Nautolan]] male Jedi Master [[Kit Fisto]],<ref name="Water War">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref> and human males such as Jedi Knight [[Anakin Skywalker]],<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> former [[Jedi]] [[Taron Malicos]],<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> and [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> The female [[Inquisitor]] known as the [[Ninth Sister]], a member of the [[Dowutin]] species<ref name="DB Ninth Sister">{{Databank|ninth-sister|Ninth Sister}}</ref> that was known for its physical strength,<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref> wore fitted armor that exhibited her large muscles.<ref name="Darth Vader 11">[[Darth Vader (2017) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 11]]</ref>

Members of the [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[Dowaaka]] species had four large breasts.<ref name="Vow">''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''</ref> The [[Askajian]] female [[Yarna d'al' Gargan]], who was an [[Slavery|enslaved]] dancer<ref name="DB Yarna">{{Databank|yarna-d-al-gargan|Yarna d'al' Gargan}}</ref> at [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt's]] [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]], had six.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> [[Muun]]s, on the other hand, did not possess prominent breasts in either gender.<ref name="SC 105">{{SkeletonCrew|You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates}}</ref>

[[File:Femalemuun.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A topless [[Unidentified female Muun|female Muun]] in a [[mud bath]]]]
A human's visual exposure to the the breasts of a human individual of a different gender could spark embarrassment, such as when [[Verosha Aniseya|Osha]] interacted with a topless "[[The Stranger|Stranger]]"<ref name="TA 106">{{Acolyte|Teach / Corrupt}}</ref> in [[132 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> or when [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] saw a shirtless [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] through [[Force-bond|Force connection]]<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="TLJDate">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. ''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'' takes place immediately after the end of ''The Force Awakens''.</ref> Muuns of opposite genders, on the other hand, did not mind exposing their breasts in public.<ref name="SC 105" />

[[Prairie-fowl]] breasts were a [[Foodstuff|food]] eaten on [[Lothal]]. [[Merei Spanjaf]] brought them home with [[tuber]]s for her [[Parent|mother]], [[Jessa Spanjaf]], to cook.<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks">''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]''</ref>
