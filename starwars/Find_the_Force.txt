'''''Find the Force''''' was a limited-time augmented-reality event on the official [[Star Wars App]] as part of the [[Force Friday]] II event on [[September 1]], [[2017]]. During the event, users could find augmented-reality versions of characters from the upcoming film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] by scanning the event's logo at participating retail locations. Upon finding a character, users were provided with their name and a small related data entry.<ref name="Find the Force">{{SW|url=news/we-went-on-a-find-the-force-scavenger-hunt|text=We Went on a ''Find the Force'' Scavenger Hunt}}</ref>
