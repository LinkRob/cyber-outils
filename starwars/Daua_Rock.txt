'''Daua Rock''' was a ridge on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> wind shear on the ridge created the conditions for an extreme erosion event to occur, causing Daua Rock to be closed off to all non-local traffic. [[Journalist|Reporter]] [[Botto Fna]] warned listeners about the event and area closure in the wind report segment of a [[Holonet News Toshara]] broadcast.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Daua Rock was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
