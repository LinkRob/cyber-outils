'''Angel Unzueta''' is a [[Wikipedia:Spaniards|Spanish]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] artist who was, along with [[Phil Noto]], one of the primary artists for [[Marvel Comics]]' [[Star Wars: Poe Dameron|''Poe Dameron'']] series.

[[StarWars.com]] announced on [[September 15]], [[2015]] that he would pencil [[Star Wars Annual (2015) 1|''Star Wars Annual'' (2015) 1]], written by [[Kieron Gillen]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/marvels-star-wars-and-darth-vader-annual-covers-exclusive-reveal|text=Marvel's ''Star Wars'' and ''Darth Vader Annual'' Covers &ndash; Exclusive Reveal}}</ref> He also contributed to two issues of the [[Star Wars: Shattered Empire|''Shattered Empire'']] miniseries by drawing the [[Naboo]] storyline.<ref name="Shattered Empire">{{WebCite|url=http://www.bleedingcool.com/2015/08/28/star-wars-shattered-empire-gains-angel-unzueta-and-emilio-laiso-to-the-force|text=''Star Wars: Shattered Empire'' Gains Angel Unzueta And Emilio Laiso To The Force|author=Johnston, Rich|work=Bleeding Cool|archivedate=20151204152530}}</ref> He provided art for the [[Poe Dameron 7|seventh issue]] of the ''Poe Dameron'' series,<ref name="Marvelstore">{{Marvel|url=Poe-Dameron-2016-7/digital-comic/42803|text=Poe Dameron (2016&ndash;) #7|subdomain=comicstore|nobackup=1}}</ref> and has drawn every numbered issue since [[Poe Dameron 14|issue 14]].

Unzueta's work also includes issues of Marvel's [[W:c:marvel:Cable and X-Force Vol 1|''Cable and X-Force'']] and [[W:c:marvel:Captain America: Sam Wilson Vol 1|''Captain America: Sam Wilson'']] series', as well as [[Wikipedia:DC Comics|DC Comics]]' [[W:c:dc:Titans Vol 2|''Titans'']] and [[W:c:dc:Green Lantern Corps Vol 2|''Green Lantern Corps'']].
