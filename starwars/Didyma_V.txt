'''Didyma V,''' was a terrestrial [[Planet|world]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> that was located in the [[Didyma system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> in the [[New Territories]] region<ref name="Location" /> and [[Wild Space]], in grid square J-5 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Didynon]] [[Engi Golba]]. [[Lommite]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> a type of [[ore]] used in [[transparisteel]],<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> was [[Mining|mined]] on the world.<ref name="TROS VD" />

By [[35 ABY]],<ref name="TROS Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]]. As ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that Golba joined the Resistance after the [[annexation of Didyma V]], the world must have been annexed by that [[time]].</ref> the [[First Order]], the successor state of the [[Galactic Empire]], had [[Annexation of Didyma V|annexed]] Didyma V, taking control of the lommite production. Golba escaped from the mines after its annexation to join the [[Resistance]], a paramilitary group led by former [[New Republic]] [[senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Didyma V was first mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
