"'''Anakin's Dark Deeds'''" is a piece written by [[John Williams]] for the [[Star Wars Episode III: Revenge of the Sith (soundtrack)|Revenge of the Sith soundtrack]]. It represents the killing of the [[Separatist Council/Legends|Separatist Council]] by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], his emotional strugle with the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]], and to some extent [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformation of the]] [[Galactic Republic]] into the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was performed by the [[London Symphony Orchestra]] and the [[London Voices]] in [[2005]], and contains references to "[[The Imperial March]]." It appears again in the video game ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' when Darth Vader appears. It also appears in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' 11th episode of the 7th season called ''[[Shattered]]'' during ''[[Order 66]]''.

In the [[2011]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', "Anakin's Dark Deeds" plays when [[Anuli]] is killed by the [[Eidolon Security|forces]] of the [[Eidolon (assassin)|Eidolon]].

In [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'' Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] similar motif is heard subtly during the scene in which [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] asks [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] to join him after defeating of [[Snoke]] and his [[Praetorian Guard]] and later it's heard more prominently and louder when she attempted to call to herself [[Skywalker lightsaber]] by using [[The Force|the Force]] but Kylo managed to intercept it.
