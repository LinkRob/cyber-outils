A '''flight jacket''' was a special type of [[jacket]] worn by some [[pilot]]s. Flight jackets were manufactured with special materials that allowed them to withstand high [[temperature]]s, though they had their limits.<ref name="awakensjunior">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> [[Poe Dameron]], an ace [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] for the [[Resistance]] wore a [[Resistance fighter jacket]], a specialized type of flight jacket. After he [[Escape from the Finalizer|escaped]] with the [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn]] in a stolen [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter|Special Forces TIE fighter]], Finn claimed the jacket when the fighter crashed and he could find only the jacket, but not Dameron, whom he assumed [[Death|killed]] in the crash. He used the jacket for shade and later took to wearing it. When the two met up again, Finn tried to return it, but Dameron told him to keep it, as he felt it suited him.<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>
