'''''Credit Exchange''''' was the name of several<ref name="Spark">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref> [[graffiti]] murals<ref name="Sabine" /> in the [[Capital City]] of [[Lothal]].<ref name="Spark" /> The [[Spectres|rebel]] [[Sabine Wren]] was so impressed with the mural that she included it in a series of photographs that she took of various murals around the [[city]], and added it to her [[Sabine Wren's journal|sketchbook]].<ref name="Sabine" /> One half of the mural read "[[Galactic Credit Standard|CREDIT]]" in [[Aurebesh]], and "EXCHANGE" in [[Outer Rim Basic]]; while the other half spelled out "EXCHANGE" in Aurebesh, accompanied by the word "CREDIT" written in Outer Rim Basic on both sides of the word "exchange".<ref name="SW">{{SW|url=series/spark-of-rebellion-concept-art-gallery|text="Spark of Rebellion" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 12}}</ref>
