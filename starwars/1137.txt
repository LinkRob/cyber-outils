'''1137''' was a [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]] who served the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].

==Biography==
Once during a mission, 1137 and their fellow [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]], [[1139]] were forgotten on a desert [[Planet/Legends|planet]] after their unit miscounted and departed. Ultimately, this proved fortuitous, for while abandoned the two [[Soldier/Legends|soldiers]] learned of a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|CIS]] secret weapon project. As dutiful soldiers, 1137 and their companion resolved to destroy it. To aid in this endeavor, they built a [[TX-130 Saber-class fighter tank/Legends|TX-130]] fighter [[Tank/Legends|tank]], with which they destroyed the Separatist weapon. Soon thereafter, realizing their absence, a [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] [[Gunship/Legends|gunship]] returned to retrieve the duo.<ref name="Always count your clones" />

Having proven their competence, 1137, together with 1139, were then assigned to hunt the missing [[Astromech droid/Legends|astromech]] [[Droid/Legends|droid]] [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]], their squad ordered by [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] to find them before the Jedi. Travelling in a [[Nu-class attack shuttle/Legends|Nu-class attack shuttle]], their first stop was the sector where R2-D2 was last seen, taking directions from a group of [[Rocket battle droid/Legends|Rocket battle droids]] who were floating through space. Landing on an unidentified [[Planet/Legends|planet]] as their scanners picked up various droid signatures.<ref name="A droid in the hand">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-us/Comics/comick11.aspx|text="A droid in the hand is worth two in the asteroid belt"|int=A droid in the hand is worth two in the asteroid belt|nolive=1|nobackup=1}}</ref>. After struggling to find any droids, the clone squad was ambushed by various [[B1-series battle droid/Legends|B1]] and [[B2-series super battle droid/Legends|B2]] [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]], 1137's commander ordered their shuttle to give them cover fire while the other clones attacked the droids head on. The clone squad were able to quickly overwhelm them and steal the droid forces' [[Armored Assault Tank/Legends|Armored Assault Tank]],<ref name="Clones don't surrender">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-us/Comics/comicl12.aspx|text="Clones don't even know the meaning of the word surrender"|int=Clones don't even know the meaning of the word surrender|nolive=1|nobackup=1}}</ref>only to be bombed from above by the clone forces in the shuttle, who had mistaken them for the enemy.<ref name="Do clones dream">''[[Do Clones dream of Electric Mynocks?]]''</ref>

Knocked unconscious by the blast, 1137 dreamt of flying an [[TIE Advanced x1/Legends|Advanced TIE]] before being woken up by 1139. Going off to mope while their colleagues worked on rebuilding their arsenal,1137 was visited by [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker's]] [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano/Legends|Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Do clones dream" /> Ahsoka began debriefing 1137 until the two were pursued by a [[Hyena-class bomber/Legends|Hyena-class bomber]] that shot at them until Ahsoka prepared to engage them with [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers/Legends|her]] [[Lightsaber/Legends|Lightsaber]], at which point three Rocket battle droids inside the bomber exited and surrendered. 1137 and Ahsoka speculated they were the same Rocket battle droids that directed 1137's squad down to the planet earlier, something which the droids denied. This line of questioning made 1137 briefly wonder how their squad was doing, only for them to disregard this line of thinking and chase the three droids away with Ahsoka. They were then contacted via [[Comlink/Legends|Comlink]] by Palpatine, who commanded them to execute one of the [[Contingency Orders for the Grand Army of the Republic: Order Initiation, Orders 1 Through 150|contingency orders]]. 1137 cut Palpatine off, however, as he interpreted this order as [[Order 6]], the order to throw away their comlink as quickly as possible. 1137 then regrouped with 1139 as Ahsoka departed.<ref name="Clone trooper obeys orders">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-US/Comics/265916.aspx|text="A clone trooper always obeys orders"|int=A clone trooper always obeys orders|nolive=1}}</ref>

Their partnership with 1139 became momentarily contentious, however, when the latter expressed desire for names to supersede their numerical designation&mdash;a sentiment not shared by 1137.<ref name="By any other name">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-us/Comics/Player/2009-4.aspx|text="A clone by any other name"|int=A clone by any other name|nolive=1}}</ref> Nevertheless, their disagreement was quickly resolved, and their search continued. Eventually, it took them and their pilot to an ice planet, whereat their [[Starship/Legends|ship]] crashed. As with their previous abandonment, this, too, proved fortuitous, as it begat a chance encounter with none other than the prominent CIS leader, [[Nute Gunray/Legends|Nute Gunray]]. Deciding against direct assault upon the [[Neimoidian/Legends|Neimoidian's]] [[Shuttle/Legends|shuttle]], 1137 and their companions settled on a ruse. Feigning that they were selling Clone Scout [[Cookie/Legends|cookies]], 1137 and the pilot distracted Gunray while 1139 and their commander subdued him. This being accomplished, the clone troopers absconded with the shuttle.<ref name="Clones are famous">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-us/Comics/comico16.aspx|text="Clones are famous all across the galaxy"|int=Clones are famous all across the galaxy|nolive=1|nobackup=1}}</ref>

The next stop on the astromech-hunt was [[Endor/Legends|Endor]]. Mistaking the local flora as being indicative of hostile [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiees]], 1137 retreated into the shuttle. This action drew the attention of a local [[Ewok/Legends|Ewok]], who sought to establish contact with 1139. However, the Ewok's attempt ultimately resulted in the capture of the whole party. Taken to the natives' camp, 1137 found themselves with their comrades-in-arms being employed as percussion instruments and 1139 being roasted. Their dilemma proved temporary as soon Nute Gunray arrived, escorted by droids and tanks, seeking vengeance for his humiliation.<ref name="Clones are diplomats''">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-us/Comics/comicp17.aspx|text="Clone troopers are excellent diplomats"|int=Clone troopers are excellent diplomats|nolive=1|nobackup=1}}</ref> This intervention inadvertently allowed the troopers to free themselves, after which they quickly dispatched their foes. Dismayed by their ferocity, Gunray retook his shuttle and fled.<ref name="Clones come to an end">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-us/Comics/Player/2009-7.aspx|text="All good clones must come to an end"|int=All good clones must come to an end|nolive=1}}</ref>

Despite the rather chaotic turn of events, 1137 and their companions retained enough composure to search for another means of transportation, which ultimately proved to be three [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/Legends|BTL-B Y-wing starfighters]]. Leaving Endor, they were intercepted by a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]. Aboard was, to 1137's surprise, none other than Supreme Chancellor Palpatine. Extremely displeased with their performance, citing the fact that Anakin and Ahsoka had already recovered the missing R2-D2, the Republic leader discharged them from the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]]. Without further ado, 1137 and their comrades took a beach-side vacation.<ref name="Clones come to an end" />

==Behind the scenes==
1137 and [[1139]] are the numbers before and after [[1138 (easter egg)|1138]]. The name of the comic that in which 1137 and 1139 are introduced, "[[Always count your clones before take-off]]," derives from the plot point of the duo having been left behind.
