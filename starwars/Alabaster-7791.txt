'''Alabaster-7791''' was the name of a particular light shade of [[Color|gray]] in the [[Lantillian paintworks coding system]]. When the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' belonged to [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], its [[hull]] was painted in that hue, with [[crys-anoblue-7255]] highlights.<ref name="TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
