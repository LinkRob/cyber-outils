"'''Blasters for Hire,'''" subtitled "'''A Mercenary's Guidebook,'''" is an [[Roleplaying game|RPG]] source article written by [[Tony Russo|Anthony Russo]]. It was first published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 3|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 3]].  The article explores the role of [[Mercenary/Legends|mercenaries]] in the ''Star Wars'' universe and gives ideas for incorporating them into adventures for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. It also details the [[Red Moons]], a group of mercenaries who can serve either as models for creating new characters or as [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|NPCs]] in an adventure.
