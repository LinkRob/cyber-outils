"'''All Jedi Walk Their Own Path'''" is a short story written by [[Charles Soule]] as part of the anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]''. The story was published on [[September 5]], [[2023]].

==Plot summary==
===Unfinished business===
The story follows [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] as he searches for his friend and fellow Jedi [[Burryaga Agaburry]] in the aftermath of the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of [[Starlight Beacon]]. A month after the space station's collapse, Zettifar refuses to give up on his [[Wookiee]] friend, sensing that Agaburry is still alive through [[the Force]]. He vows to search the planet [[Eiram]] for his friend.

===Master and apprentice===
In flashbacks, Bell recalls an earlier [[Mission to Teriona|mission]] which he undertook with his master [[Loden Greatstorm]] to the war-torn planet of [[Teriona]]. The [[Galactic Republic]] had sent a team of negotiators to secure peace on the resource rich world. However, the warring factions had taken the negotiating team hostage. In response, the Jedi had dispatched Greatstorm and Bell to rescue them. Greatstorm had taught Bell that all Jedi must walk their own and let the [[light side of the Force]] guide them. Trusting in the Force, Greatstorm destroys their [[comlink]] prior to embarking on the rescue mission.

During their first day on Teriona, Bell and his master set up an improvised tent using supplies left by the transport that had evacuated the Republic negotiators to safety. Later that night, the two Jedi come under attack from attackers armed with [[blaster]]s. Using the Force and their [[lightsaber]]s, the two Jedi fend off their attackers' [[blaster bolt]]s and grenades. After their attackers retreat, Greatstorm rests while Bell keeps watch.  

===The search for Burryaga===
In the present, Zettifar meets with [[Administrator]] [[Gorta]] to hire a small search crew and a vessel to search the maritime debris from Starlight Beacon's fall with the hope of finding his friend. Gorta is unable to commit resources to help Zettifar due to the large scale devastation caused by the space station's fall and the public hostility against the Jedi. He advises Zettifar to discard his Jedi robes and apparel due to the public sentiment against the Jedi.

Walking to a nearby pier, Zettifar meets a crew of three fishermen (a [[human]] [[gender|man]], [[gender|woman]] and red-skinned [[Twi'lek]]) struggling with a [[spinefin]] that has been caught in their nets. Zettifar uses his Force ability to connect with animals to free the sea creature. Learning that one of their crew members [[Bettyjill]] had left the crew following the fall of Starlight beacon, Zettifar offers his services as a replacement crew member in return for helping him to find his lost Jedi friend. The Twi'lek captain reluctantly accepts him as a crew member on a four-week cruise. 

===Living by example===
In the past, Greatstorm and Zettifar travel to a town called [[Strop's End]] in order to replenish their supplies and get a lay of the land. They visit a local bar where Greatstorm offers to buy the patrons a round of [[beer]]. Since they lack enough [[credit]]s to pay for the drinks, Greatstorm and Zettifar offer to repair the bartender's roofs. The two Jedi later obtain supplies from a shopkeeper in return for digging out her sewage system. The two Jedi obtain fresh produce and grains, which they bring back to their camp. Greatstorm hopes to use their presence in Strop's End to inspire the locals to abandon their warlike ways. 

Later, the two Jedi are visited by six locals led by a woman named [[Hennah]], who teaches at Strop's End school. Two of her male companions bring baskets containing cheeses, bottles and freshly-baked bread. One of the men tells the Jedi that his wife [[Sveen]] added [[orberry|orberries]] into the bread]]. Hennah and a man named [[Argeel]] thank the Jedi for replacing the tavern's roof and unclogging the sewers. They credit the Jedi with improving their lives and reducing the number of deaths within their community. The locals hope to share what the Jedi have taught them with other communities. Having help bring peace to Teriona, Greatstorm decides that their time on the planet is over.

===Hope and rescue===
In the present, Zettifar spends the next six weeks aboard the fishing trawler ''[[Queen Xiri]]''. From the crew, he learnt how to haul and mend nets, and how to deal with marine creatures. Zettifar also befriends the [[Queen Xiri's crew|ship's crew]] including the Twi'lek [[Gorge (Twi'lek)|Gorge]], the droid [[9-09]] ("Ny-no"), the [[captain]] [[Okoro]] and the captain's wife [[Nelly]]. After befriending the crew, Okoro offers to help Zettifar find Burryaga and spread word among the fisherman. Okoro reports that fisherman have been reporting strange totems washing up near [[Pastor's Shore]]. Examining one of the totems, Zettifar recognizes the object as a bit of foam insulation attached to a braid of Wookiee fur. Zettifar is relieved to receive confirmation that his friend is alive. 

With the help of the crew of the ''Queen Xiri'', Zettifar was able to enlist the assistance of the local fisherfolk in gathering information about every location where the talismans had washed up. Zettifar also established a command post at a strategically-located inn. Based on the information from the captains, he deduced that the talismans originated at a large whirlpool known as the [[Maw of Shareen]]. Zettifar travels to the whirlpool in a two-seater submersible while the other crew remained aboard the ship. Captain Okoro, Nell and Gorge think traveling to the Maw is dangerous but wish the two all the best.

As the submersible dives into the maw, a pack of fish attempt to breach the hull. Zettifar fires the submersible's harpoon gun, killing one of the beasts. As the deep sea pressure grows, Zettifar uses the Force to hold the submersible together. Through the Force, Zettifar tracks Burryaga's location to an undersea cavern that contains an air pocket with luminous plants, seaweed and mosses. There, he encounters Burryaga, who had been ripping patches of fur from his body to make talismans. 

The two Jedi share a meal of raw fish, crabs and seaweed inside the cavern. Burryaga also serves his friend water collected from the mosses on the wall. Zettifar is relieved that his friend survived the destruction of Starlight Beacon and credits [[Elzar Mann]] with ensure that the fallen space station did not burn up during its descent. As the two return to the submersible, Bell reflects on learning that he did not fail.
