A '''double-towered building''' overlooked a major plaza in [[Chaleydonia]], the [[capital]] [[city]] on [[Christophsis]]. The complex was comprised of two towers, one facing the north and the other situated to the south.<ref name="Hidden Enemy" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> during the [[Battle of Christophsis]] early in the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], along with their [[clone trooper]]s, set up an ambush for [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] forces in the towers. However, the plan was leaked to Separatist commander [[Asajj Ventress]] by a [[Slick|traitor]] in the ranks, forcing Kenobi, Skywalker and their men to beat a retreat from the building.<ref name="Hidden Enemy" />
