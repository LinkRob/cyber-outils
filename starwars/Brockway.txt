'''Brockway''' was a [[Lieutenant General|lieutenant general]] in the [[New Republic army|army]] of the [[New Republic]] who led their [[New Republic Defense Force|ground forces]] during the [[Battle of Jakku|battle]] against the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]<ref name="Aftermath: Empire's End" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[5 ABY]].<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Brockway led in the place of [[General]] [[Tyben]], who remained with the Republic's [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] on the planet [[Chandrila]] during the battle so that the New Republic would not be left without military leaders if they lost at Jakku. Tyben, who remained on Chandrila, was kept up to date on the progress of Brockway's forces during the battle, and discussed them with [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] via [[hologram]], while Brockway led the strategy on the ground. The New Republic claimed victory but only after many sustaining [[Death|casualties]], with fighting continuing against [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] who refused to surrender for many [[Standard month|months]] on Jakku.<ref name="Aftermath: Empire's End" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brockway was first mentioned in the novel ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]] and released on [[February 21]], [[2017]].
