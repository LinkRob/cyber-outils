The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''6 BBY''',<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> also known as '''3271 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7971''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The [[Leonis family]] migrates to [[Lothal]].<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy" />
*The [[Westhills massacre]] occurs on Lothal.<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy" />
*[[Dhara Leonis]] is kidnapped by [[the Grand Inquisitor]] and forcibly inducted into [[Project Harvester]] due to her [[Force-sensitive|force sensitivity]].<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks">''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]''</ref>
*The [[Westhills skirmish]] occurs.<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy" />
*The Empire's [[mining]] activities on Jelucan led to the planet becoming polluted.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>
*Jelucan people started using similar clothes and house decorations, reducing the division between the first and [[second-wave]]rs.<ref name="Lost Stars" />
*[[Mon Mothma]] establishes her [[Mon Mothma's rebel cell|rebel cell]].<ref name="SWOTFL">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref>
*An [[Imperial masking unit]] is stolen from the Imperial [[Lozash naval yard|naval yard]] on [[Lozash]],<ref name="Nobody's Listening!">{{Andor|Nobody's Listening!}}</ref><ref name="6 BBY">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e8-trivia-gallery|text="Narkina 5" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 6}} establishes that the events of "[[Narkina 5 (episode)|Narkina 5]]" take place in [[5 BBY]], while the events of "[[Rix Road (episode)|Rix Road]]" take place in 7972 [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]], which corresponds to 5 BBY per the dating systems of ''[[Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. As the events of "[[Nobody's Listening!]]" take place in between these two episodes, dated to 5 BBY, and the episode states that the theft at [[Lozash]] took place a year prior, the theft of the Imperial masking device must take place in 6 BBY.</ref> one of many high-level thefts of Imperial technological items during the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> The masking device finds its way into the hands of [[Anto Kreegyr]]'s [[Anto Kreegyr's group|rebel group]].<ref name="Nobody's Listening!" />
*[[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[partisans]] are hired by [[Arane Oreida]] to [[Mission to Inusagi|infiltrate]] the [[Sakoola blossom festival]] on [[Inusagi]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
*[[Kuat Drive Yards]] designs the [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvette]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref>
*[[Gar Saxon]] becomes [[governor]] of [[Mandalore]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Timelines" />
*[[Sabine Wren]] leaves the [[Imperial Academy]] after she builds "[[Arc Pulse Generator|the Duchess]]." {{Circa}}<ref name="Timelines" />
*[[Chio Fain]] and his partner [[Rone]] undertook a job which involved slicing into property of the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]]. The two [[slicer]]s were caught, and Rone let [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] catch up to her, which allowed Chio to escape into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Target Vader 4">[[Target Vader 4|''Target Vader'' 4]]</ref>
*Rone is [[Death|killed]] by the Empire, which made Chio hold a hatred towards the Empire, and wanting to kill [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor's]] enforcer.<ref name="Target Vader 4" />
*The [[Dizonite|Dizonites]] of [[Dizon Fray]] are [[Massacre of the Dizonites|massacred]] using Sabine Wren's weapon.<ref>''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>
