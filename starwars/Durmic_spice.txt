'''Durmic spice''' was a type of cooking [[Spice (seasoning)|spice]] that could be found in [[Hanna City]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of<ref name="ALD" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]. During the [[New Republic]]'s [[Liberation Day (Coruscant)|Liberation Day]] holiday<ref name="ALD" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> food [[Merchant|vendors]] throughout the city prepared dishes with durmic spice. As he surveyed the event, [[New Republic pilot]] [[Wedge Antilles]] smelled the spice's scent wafting through Hanna City's streets.<ref name="ALD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Durmic spice was mentioned in ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', a [[2016]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the second installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="ALD" />
