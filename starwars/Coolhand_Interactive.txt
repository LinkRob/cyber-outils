'''Coolhand Interactive''' was a [[Star Wars video games|video game]] studio headed by Doug Mogica and based in [[Wikipedia:Palo Alto, California|Palo Alto]], [[Wikipedia:California|California]]. The studio developed the [[1998]] video game [[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']] in conjunction with [[LucasArts]].<ref name="Rebellion Site">{{LucasArtsCite|url=static/rebellion/rebellion.htm|text=''Star Wars: Rebellion'' Official Website}}</ref>
