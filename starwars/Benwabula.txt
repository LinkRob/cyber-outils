'''Benwabula''' was a location<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> in the [[Benwabula system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[flight GH-78]], which stopped at both Benwabula and the [[Marleyvane shipyards|Marleyvane yards]], was scheduled to depart from [[Mirogana Spaceport]]'s [[Gate E2]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. However it was cancelled after the moon's [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Governor]] [[Thorden]] closed down a section of the spaceport.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Benwabula was mentioned in [[Aurebesh]] on a sign that players can read in the "All In" quest as part of the "Wild Card" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref> 

In the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity the [[Benwabula system/Legends|Benwabula system]] was introduced in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
