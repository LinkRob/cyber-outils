'''Eastwind Manor''' was a countryside retreat on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] that was used by [[House Prestor]]. [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], then known as Bail Prestor, [[summer]]ed in the manor as a [[Youngling|child]]. It had columns and flowering scrollwork in the style of [[Alderaan's neo-classical period]].<ref name="MOF" /> Around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|69}}</ref> Organa returned to the manor.<ref name="MOF" />
