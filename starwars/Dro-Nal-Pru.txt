'''Dro-Nal-Pru''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Cerean]] [[marshal]] of an [[encampment on Salstan]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. At one point, she led a [[Dro-Nal-Pru's mercenary army|mercenary army]] that included the [[Mercenary|saber-for-hire]] [[Ty Yorrick]] and the [[Mythrol]] [[Gren]]. Later on, she would hire both Yorrick and Gren to hunt down a [[Xunji|dangerous creature]], although it was secretly a plot to get revenge. Yorrick would succeed in capturing the creature and would release it on Dro-Nal-Pru for luring her into a trap.<ref name="Undead" />
