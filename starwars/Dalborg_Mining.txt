'''Dalborg Mining''' was a [[mining]] company based on [[Cynda]], a moon that orbited the planet [[Gorse]]. The company was involved in blasting operations in the moon's [[Zone Thirty-Nine]]. One of the company's foremen was [[Tarlor Choh]]. The demolitions expert [[Skelly]] worked for the company before being dismissed prior to the [[Gorse Conflict]].<ref name="AND" />
