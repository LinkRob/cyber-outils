The '''Barsha family''',<ref name="Outlaws" /> or '''Barsha clan''',<ref name="Insider 236">{{InsiderCite|236|Launchpad}}</ref> was a [[human]] family that [[Homeworld|hailed]] from [[Corellia]], a [[planet]] located in the [[Core Worlds]] of [[the galaxy]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], they were a powerful and wealthy family that owned a [[Corellian]] [[Barsha Corporation|manufacturing company]]. When the Clone Wars ended, the family was set to secure a contract in the manufacturing of [[Star Destroyer]]s for the [[Galactic Empire]], which would have made them one of the most powerful companies on Corellia. However, [[Sliro Barsha]], an illegitimate son, sold the Barsha family out to the Empire in order to earn a job as a [[director]] in the [[Imperial Security Bureau]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
