'''Darth Vexus''' was a [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] who served the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|Sith Empire]] during the [[Cold War (Galactic Republic–Sith Empire)|Cold War]]. He was on [[Voss]] to hunt a group of dissident [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] but was defeated by [[Darth Nox]].

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Vexus was originally slated to appear in the mission "Allies" for the Sith Inquisitor class in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', but the Voss-bound version of the mission was ultimately cut from the game and replaced with a simple conversation with [[Ashara Zavros]].
