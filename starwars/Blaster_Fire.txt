'''Blaster Fire''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[fathier]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> They took part in the [[Canto Cup]] [[Fathier racing|fathier race]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> that took place in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> and was held in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. By the time of the race, Blaster Fire stood at 280 [[centimeter]]s tall, weighed 919 [[kilogram]]s. They had [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]], black [[eye]]s and was considered an absolute wild card during a race.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the fathier competed in a Canto Cup race that the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] [[Gambling|bet]] on while watching the competition via [[hologram]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Blaster Fire appeared in a hologram in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. They are one of the fathiers that can randomly feature in Canto Fathier Racing, an optional activity. In most races, the six fathiers are assigned a random [[jockey]] and a random symbol. As such, this article does not assume one pairing or symbol color to be [[canon]]ical.<ref name="Outlaws" />
