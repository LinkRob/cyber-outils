The '''''Bokrea''''' was a [[patrol cruiser]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[navy]] that was present at [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] last stand, having been invited to the battle by [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]]. During the battle, the ''Bokrea'' stayed side by side with the [[Chiss]] [[warship]] ''[[Parala]]'', facing a [[Grysk]] [[Prism-class BattleChief]].

Following [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand]], [[Senior Captain]] [[Irizi'in'daro|Ziinda]] suggested to [[Supreme General]] [[Ba'kif]] that [[Mid Captain]] [[Csap'ro'strob|Apros]] be promoted to Senior Captain, and given command of a patrol cruiser, mentioning the ''Bokrea'' as a possible fit.

==History==
[[File:GreaterGood-OOP-Art.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Senior Captain Thrawn controlled the ''Bokrea''{{'s}} actions during the battle over Sunrise.]]
After [[Mid Captain]] [[Ufsa'mak'ro|Samakro]] brought the [[Chiss]] [[heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]'' over the [[planet]] [[Sunrise]] to prepare for the upcoming battle against the warlike [[Grysk]]'s and the [[Kilji Illumine]], [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] of the [[Nightdragon man-of-war]] class warship ''[[Vigilant]]'' informed him that she was able to contact many of her old subordinates to join them in the looming battle, with the [[patrol cruiser]] ''Bokrea'' being one of them. During the battle, the ''Bokrea'' and the patrol cruiser ''[[Parala]]'' took on a Grysk [[Prism-class BattleChief]], until [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] ordered them both to break off so that Senior Captain [[Irizi'in'daro|Ziinda]] of the [[warship]] ''[[Grayshrike]]'' could make an attack run on the BattleChief, dropping [[breacher]]'s and [[plasma sphere]]'s into its hull. Following the maneuver, the ''Bokrea'' and the ''Parala'' resumed their attack on the Grysk warship. Following the Chiss victory at [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand]], Senior Captain Ziinda spoke to [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] [[Supreme General]] [[Ba'kif]] about promoting Mid Captain [[Csap'ro'strob|Apros]] to Senior Captain, as well as command of his own ship, mentioning the ''Bokrea'' as a possible fit for him.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Bokrea'' appeared in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[2021]] [[Book|novel]] [[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]], the final installment of the [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
