'''Andrithal Robb-Voti''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served as [[senator]] for the [[planet]] [[Taris]] in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> Andrithal shared part of her surname with the [[Clone Wars]] era senator of Taris, [[Kin Robb]].<ref name="Kin Robb">{{Databank|kin-robb|Kin Robb}}</ref> In [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Bloodline date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Bloodline (novel)|Bloodline]]'' to [[28 ABY]].</ref> Robb-Voti was on the planet [[Hosnian Prime]] while it hosted the Senate, and she was injured during the [[Napkin Bombing]].<ref name="BL" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Death date" /> Robb-Voti was [[Death|killed]] when the [[First Order]] [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroyed]] the entire [[Hosnian system]] using the [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]].<ref name="TFA" /> While on Hosnian Prime, Robb-Voti wore [[Color|purple]] [[robe]]s, the Tarisian color of administration.<ref name="TFAVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Andrithal Robb-Voti was created to appear in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']],<ref name="TFAVD" /> which was released on [[December 18]], [[2015]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> She is not visible in the final release of the film,<ref name="TFA" /> but appeared in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TFAVD" /> and released the same day as the film.<ref name="New Books">{{SW|url=news/a-galaxy-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens-books-coming-december-18-first-look|text=A Galaxy of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Books Coming December 18 - UPDATED!}}</ref>
