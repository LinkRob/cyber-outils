'''Figg Excavations''' was a [[mining]] company that operated on [[Tokmia]],<ref name="Easy" /> a world located in the [[Anoat sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Location">The game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'' allows the player to send members of their crew from within the Anoat sector to Tokmia during a point in the story where no ships can leave the sector due to the Iron Blockade. As such, Tokmia must be located within the Anoat sector for the crew members to reach it.</ref> and when the company abandoned it, they left behind [[crystal]]s protected by a still-active security system.<ref name="Easy" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
During the [[Iron Blockade]], which was established by the [[Galactic Empire]] in an attempt to prevent news of their crushing defeat by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] at the [[Battle of Endor]] from reaching the Anoat [[sector]]'s populace,<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> [[the Smuggler]] sent one of their crew members to get through the Figg Excavations security systems on Tokmia and find crystals.<ref name="Easy" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Figg Excavations was originally created for the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] web article "[[Galactic Gazetteer: Hoth and the Greater Javin]]," which was written by [[Jason Fry]], [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Craig Robert Carey]] for [[Wizards of the Coast]] and released in [[2004]].<ref name="Hoth and the Greater Javin">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/article/sw20040325web|text=Galactic Gazetteer: Hoth and the Greater Javin|int=Galactic Gazetteer: Hoth and the Greater Javin}}</ref> It became [[canon]] when it was mentioned in the mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', which was developed by [[Kabam]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms and released in [[2015]].<ref name="Uprising" />
