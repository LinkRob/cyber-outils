The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''36 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3241 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''17 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7941''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Orson Callan Krennic]] and [[Galen Walton Erso]] meet for the first time.<ref name="Timelines" />
*[[Réillata]] is elected for her first term as [[Queen]] of [[Naboo]].<ref name="Timelines" />
*[[Fanry]] is supposed to be released from jail. {{Circa}}<ref name="M&A">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]]. As [[Fanry]] was sentenced to four [[Standard year|years]] in jail at the end of the book, she would have been released around 36 BBY.</ref>
*[[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] invited [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to enter politics.<ref name="Timelines" /> At that time, [[Finis Valorum]] had been re-elected [[Supreme Chancellor]] against expectations, yet, barely a year into his term, discussions began swirl that he would not be able to maintain his post. Palpatine's name emerged in that time as a possible successor.<ref name="Trkn" />
*[[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] and [[Ar'alani|Ziara]] begin a [[Lioaoin pirate campaign|campaign]] against [[Lioaoi]]n [[pirate]]s. {{Circa}}<ref name="Timelines" />

==Births==
*[[Gallius Rax]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Rax">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' establishes that Gallius Rax joined the [[Naval Intelligence Agency]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to around [[16 BBY]], at the age of twenty. Therefore, Rax was [[Reproduction|born]] around 36 BBY.</ref>
*[[Ahsoka Tano]] on [[Shili]].<ref name="Timelines" />

==Deaths==
*[[Unidentified Prefect (assassination)|Unidentified Prefect]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Unidentified Prefect">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[skirmish in the Kinoss system]] to 36 BBY. As the [[mission to Bardram Scoft]], in which the [[Chiss]] sent an [[Unidentified Chiss ambassador|ambassador]] to [[Bardram Scoft]] to meet with the new [[Unidentified Prefect (successor)|Prefect]], took place just before the skirmish, it must have taken place around 36 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>
