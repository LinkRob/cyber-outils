The '''Death Plains''' was an inhospitable stretch of [[desert]] on the [[planet]] [[Carajam]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Zingo Gabnit]] and [[Turfitch]] were forced to cross the Death Plains whilst escaping from the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]]. Discovering they had insufficient [[water]] to survive the trip, Gabnit shot Turfitch with his [[blaster]], leaving him to [[Death|die]]. Fett later came across Turfitch, who told the bounty hunter of Gabnit's location before dying.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />
