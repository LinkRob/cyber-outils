'''Flitrude''' was a [[Color|green]] and brown colored [[moon]] that orbited the [[planet]] [[Iego]], which was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="SWTVE" /> at [[Standard Galactic Grid]] coordinates T-7.<ref name="Coord" /> Like the rest of [[Moons of Iego|rest]] of Iego's 1,000 moons, Flitrude was as mysterious as the planet itself.<ref name="SWTVE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flitrude was mentioned in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''. The art used for the moon in the ''Visual Encyclopedia''<ref name="SWTVE" /> originated in a piece of concept art title "Iego 1000 Moons" that was drawn by [[Jackson Sze]] on [[October 3]], [[2007]] for ''[[Mystery of a Thousand Moons]]'', an episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="EpisodeGuide">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep118|text="Mystery of a Thousand Moons" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}} {{C|concept art only}}</ref> The moon did not appear in the episode itself.<ref name="MofTM">{{TCW|Mystery of a Thousand Moons}}</ref>
