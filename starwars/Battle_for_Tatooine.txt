The '''Battle for Tatooine''' was a successful effort by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] to drive the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|military]] forces from [[Tatooine]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Prelude==
[[File:ATOMEDGE.jpg|thumb|left|150px|The freighter ''Atom Edge'', which was carrying supplies to rebel forces on Tatooine]]
The conflict between [[Tatooine rebel cell]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] on [[Tatooine]] started with an attack on the ''[[Atom Edge]]'', a [[freighter]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance]] carrying supplies for [[Saponza]]'s Gang. The freighter was destroyed and crashed in two separate sites, one secured by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] and the other by [[Mercenary|mercenaries]]. Saponza and his [[Saponza's partner|partner]] attacked the mercenaries to recover the supplies.<ref name="Commander" />

The Rebellion retaliated with a [[bomber]] attack on an Imperial Listening Post using [[X-wing starfighter|X-Wings]] and [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-Wings]]. Sometime afterwards, an Imperial Patrol attacked [[Saponza's homestead]], but were warded off by Saponza's Gang. The Rebellion followed up with raids on three different supply posts. Another Imperial Patrol then attacked the Homestead, but to no avail. [[Jennica Pierce]] then ordered an attack on an Imperial Operative in order to steal intel. After a successful attack, a final patrol attempted to siege the homestead.<ref name="Commander" />

Using the Intel, the rebellion destroyed an Imperial Base. They continued to attack the Imperials by freeing a small town, attacking an outpost, and raiding two alloy depots, and destroying another [[Tatooine listening station|listening station]]. After these attacks, Jennica thought that they were ready to perform larger scale attacks on the Imperial forces residing on Tatooine.<ref name="Commander" />

==The battle==
[[Alliance High Command]] then constructed a plan to secure Tatooine. Saponza's gang would attack a base on [[Dandoran]], where one of Saponza's [[Rebel trooper|soldiers]] would "defect" and feed the Imperials information regarding coming attacks. To get the Imperials to trust the mole, he warned them of an attack on an Imperial Operative on Tatooine, but not early enough for reinforcements to be sent. After the attack on the Operative, the Imperials attacked the homestead.<ref name="Commander" />

[[Han Solo]] helped drive the plan forward. He directed that a number of facilities on Dandoran should be attacked, so the Empire would spread their forces thin throughout the sub-sector. After the attacks on Dandoran, Imperial forces attempted to siege the Homestead.<ref name="Commander" />

Solo revealed that they were going to trick the Empire into thinking they were going to attack the main base on Dandoran, when really they were going to attack a holding cell containing the rogue [[senator]] [[Johhar Kessen]]. To convince the Empire that an attack on the main Dandoran base was imminent, the gang attacked a [[staging area]] on Dandoran, then an Imperial Operative, and finally an Imperial Starport on Dandoran.<ref name="Commander" />

The Rebellion was now ready to free Kessen. Solo, [[Chewbacca]], and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], along with some [[Rebel Marksman|Rebel Marksmen]] and [[soldier]]s, infiltrated the Detention Center that housed Kessen and freed him from the Empire. To gain Kessen's loyalty, Saponza and his gang had to help Kessen on his hunts. After defeating various big game ([[krayt dragon]]s, [[rancor]]s, etc), Kessen pledged his loyalty to the Alliance. With Kessen's help, many Imperial shipments were "lost in transit".<ref name="Commander" />

The Alliance attacked on [[Fort Sunfire]], one of the largest Imperial bases on the [[planet]]. This infuriated [[Captain]] [[Kosh]], the [[Imperial officer]] in charge of operations on Tatooine. He launched two separate attacks on the Homestead, one with the entire [[68th Legion]], and one with a [[squadron]] of mechanized forces, but he could not destroy the Homestead.<ref name="Commander" />

The final stage of the battle started with an attack on a Starship Depot that housed a number of [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruisers]] that Kosh had stolen from the Empire to use in his [[black market]] smuggling operation. They continued with two assaults on contraband smuggling facilities operated by Kosh and his 68th Legion, with the objective of seizing Kosh's contraband shipments and appropriating them for Alliance use. Kosh, however, retaliated with an attack on Saponza's homestead, deploying a considerable detachment against the fortified homestead. They were defeated by the homestead's defenses that had been provided by the Alliance.<ref name="Commander" />

With Kosh having exhausted his mobile forces, the Alliance again went on the offensive, [[Attack on the Reinforcement Facility|destroying a reinforcement facility]] to [[Attack on Fort Ironhand|prevent]] Kosh from reinforcing [[Fort Ironhand]], the site of the vast majority of the 68th Legion's forces and the most significant base on Tatooine aside from [[Fort Sunfire]].<ref name="Commander" />

The Alliance executed the assault on Fort Ironhand in three stages. The first assault took down the western [[garrison]], with the second on the eastern garrison. The final stage of the assault focused on Fort Ironhand itself, and a group of [[Jawa]]s participated in the assault alongside the regular forces. The combined Alliance forces, along with its allies, overpowered Fort Ironhand, ending the Imperial presence on Tatooine.<ref name="Commander" />

==Aftermath==
Shortly after the conclusion of the final assault on Fort Ironhand, Organa contacted Saponza's gang and congratulated them on their victory, while also asking them to continue fighting the Empire in other locations throughout [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Commander" />

The Empire later managed to regain its presence on Tatooine, but their occupation ended with word of the Rebel victory in the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Chapter 9">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref>

The Battle for Tatooine is the main conflict of Chapter 11 of the Rebel Alliance campaign in ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''.<ref name="Commander" />
