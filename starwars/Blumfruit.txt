'''Blumfruit''' was a type of [[fruit]] that could bruise. The [[alien]] [[Takvax]] claimed to bruise like a blumfruit when begging not to be hurt by the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] on the [[planet]] [[Rion]]<ref name="Swoop" /> shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]] that occurred in [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Date">According to {{IDW|url=product/star-wars-adventures-21|text=Star Wars Adventures #21}}, "Swoop Racers" takes place just after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[0 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Blumfruit was mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] "[[Swoop Racers]]" which was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 21|''Star Wars Adventures'' (2017) 21]]<ref name="Swoop" /> on [[May 8]], [[2019]].<ref name="Amazon">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2017-2020-21/digital-comic/753663|text=Star Wars Adventures (2017-2020) #21|nolive=1}}</ref> The [[Blumfruit/Legends|fruit]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in "[[Wicket's Wagon]]," an episode of the [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']] television series<ref name="Ewoks">{{Ewoks|Wicket's Wagon}}</ref> that aired on [[November 9]], [[1985]].<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|27|A Star Wars CELibration|A ''Star Wars'' CELibration}}</ref>
