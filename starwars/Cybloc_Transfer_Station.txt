The '''Cybloc Transfer Station''',<ref name="TCW CE" /> also known as the '''InterGalactic storehouse station''',<ref name="SWE Police">{{SWE|technology|policedroid|police droid}} </ref> was an [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]]<ref name="TCW CE" /> [[space station]] located in the [[Cybloc system]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Revival" />

==History==
The Cybloc Transfer Station was [[Raid on the Cybloc Transfer Station|attacked]] by the [[Sith]] brothers [[Maul]] and [[Savage Opress]]. The two brothers sliced through the many [[Spaceport police droid]]s, who were used as [[security droid]]s, on the station, raiding it in order to draw the attention of the [[Jedi Order]] and, specifically, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="Revival" />
