'''Area three''' was a part of the [[home of the Silver Knights]] settlement on the [[Aakaash system]] [[planet]] [[Wind]]. After the ''[[Silver Dawn]]'' rescued [[Sean]] from the [[Vermilion Birds]]' [[raid on Nan Heli's villa]] and arrived home, Mistress [[White (droid)|White]], deputy leader of the Knights, coordinated efforts at the settlement to alight supplies. During the process, White ordered the Knights to open a [[Mining|mine]] tunnel in area three with its new cutting equipment as planned. Later, White told the Knights that the ''Silver Dawn'' had taken five batches of emergency [[Ration pack|rations]], and that the top five area representatives of their lottery rankings should come forward to retrieve them.<ref name="Vow" />
