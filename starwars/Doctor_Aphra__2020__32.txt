'''''Doctor Aphra'' 32''' is the thirty-second issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[May 31]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''LUKE SKYWALKER: FRIEND OR FOE? After the fallout of ''HIDDEN EMPIRE'', DOCTOR APHRA is at a crossroads. Who better to help her with that than&hellip;LUKE SKYWALKER?!''
