'''Dormitz''' was an individual who served in the [[First Order Navy|navy]] of the militant political faction known as the [[First Order]], successor of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Holding the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Junior Lieutenant|junior lieutenant]],<ref name="TFA Beginner Game" /> Dormitz worked as a [[pilot]] and [[hypercomm]] specialist, at one point flying an [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|''Upsilon''-class command shuttle]] near a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] as well as over [[Starkiller Base]].<ref name="X-Wing 2">{{FFGXW2|set=First Order Conversion Kit|cardname=Lieutenant Dormitz}}</ref>

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TFA Beginner Game Date">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]] states that thirty [[Standard year|years]] have passed since the [[Battle of Endor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]]. Therefore, the events of the game are set in [[34 ABY]].</ref> Dormitz was stationed aboard the ''Resurgent''-class [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Silencer]]'', which had been dispatched alongside [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] to patrol a [[hyperspace]] transit point between the [[Unknown Regions]] and the [[Core Worlds]] in search of [[Resistance]] [[Spy|spies]].<ref name="TFA Beginner Game" />

{{RPG|[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Discovery on Jakku'']]|canon=1}}
While on patrol, the ''Silencer'' encountered the [[TUG-b13|quadjumper]] ''[[Sunstrider]]'' and towed the [[Starship|ship]] to one of its [[hangar]] bays, where two [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] brought the ship's crew of adventurers to Dormitz in the adjacent waiting area for inspection. Unwilling to expose that they had indeed obtained intel on [[Jakku]] that they planned to deliver to the Resistance, the adventurers overpowered Dormitz, retrieving their equipment and seeking to escape the ''Silencer''.<ref name="TFA Beginner Game" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dormitz was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]] released by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]].<ref name="TFA Beginner Game Release">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/8/3/who-will-you-become|text=Who Will You Become?|date=2016-08-03}}</ref> In one encounter, the players are captured by the [[First Order]] and held aboard the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Silencer]]'', and they must first defeat Dormitz&mdash;who is accompanied by two [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] if the players are suspected to be [[Resistance]] operatives&mdash;to escape. The game identifies the character by he/him pronouns;<ref name="TFA Beginner Game" /> however, an article published on Fantasy Flight Games' [https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/ website] in [[2018]] refers to Dormitz as she/her.<ref name="A New Order">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/10/8/a-new-order|text=A New Order}}</ref>
