'''Fried Sakiyan Gwayo with Herb-roasted Topato''' was an [[Foodstuff|entrée]] served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]], on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Docking" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> This [[gwayo bird]] and [[topato]] dish was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="Docking" />

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fried [[Sakiyan]] Gwayo with Herb-roasted Topato first appeared as a provision available to guests at the Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo restaurant in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]], exclusively for [[Star Wars Nite]]s in [[2023]].<ref name="2023 brochure">{{Disney|subdomain=cdn1.parksmedia.wdprapps|url=vision-dam/digital/parks-platform/parks-standard-assets/disneyland/events/star-wars-nite/Disneyland-After_Dark-Star_Wars-Nite-2023-Event_Guide.pdf|text=Disneyland After Dark: Star Wars Nite 2023}}</ref> The menu described it as "Crispy [[Chicken]], Roasted [[Potato]]es, [[Wikipedia:Chicken tikka masala|Tikka Masala]], Herb [[Wikipedia:Aioli|Aïoli]]."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref>
