'''Coarseweave''' was a fabric used in [[clothing]]. 

==Description==
Coarseweave was a type of fabric that could be used to fashion [[clothing]] in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" />

==History==
[[File:Windu_in_corseweave.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Mace Windu wearing a coarseweave tunic]]
By [[32 BBY]]<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] wore a [[Color|brownish gold]] tunic made from coarseweave. Windu continued to wear this during his time serving on the [[Jedi High Council]] including during the the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] at the start of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> Windu wore the tunic during his attempted arrest of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[Mon Calamari]] [[merchant]] [[Jonkah Rremset]] sold a unit of coarseweave for sixty five [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] while in [[Mirogana]] the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. During the same [[Standard year|year]], a [[human]] merchant [[Simrain Kanda]] sold coarseweave out of their shop in the [[Wonto District market]] in [[Mos Eisley]] on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] utilized coarseweave in an armored undershirt and to improve her footwear. She also used it to improve the pouches she used to store [[bacta vial]]s and [[grenade]]s. Vess was able to sell coarseweave during this time for 26 credits.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Coarseweave first appeared though unidentified in {{Film|I}} released in [[1999]]. It was later identified in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" />

In the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' it was used as a material to unlock various expert abilities such as the "armored undershirt" which increases the players health and "Scavenger's Footwear" which allowed the player to move faster while crouched.<ref name="Outlaws" />
