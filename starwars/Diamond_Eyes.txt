The '''Diamond Eyes''' was the fifth and final embellishment of the [[Kalidor Crescent]], the highest honor endowed by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], granted only by the [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Alliance Chief of State]] or by the [[Supreme Commander of the Alliance Armed Forces]] for the greatest acts of heroism and gallantry against an overwhelming force. Those eligible for the final embellishment required a living [[Alliance Military|military]] individual performing a sixth instance of such behavior, a nomination from a superior [[Alliance officer|officer]] or a representative from the [[Alliance Civil Government|Alliance's civilian government]], and to have been previously awarded the [[Bronze Cluster]], the [[Silver Talons]], the [[Silver Scimitar]], and the [[Golden Wings]] embellishments for their Kalidor Crescent.<ref name="Lead" />

The embellishment itself was a pair of small [[diamond]]s that were pinned to the [[ribbon]] on either side of head of the likened appearance of a [[kalidor]] in flight, an [[Bird|avian]] predator [[species]] from<ref name="Lead" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Davnar II]].<ref name="Lead" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Diamond Eyes embellishment was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying]] sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]''.<ref name="Lead" /> The [[Diamond Eyes/Legends|embellishment]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared simultaneously in [[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']], a [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|computer game]] released in [[1993]] by [[Totally Games]]<ref name="X-wing">[[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']]</ref> and the accompanying companion book ''[[The Farlander Papers]]'' authored by [[Rusel DeMaria]] that was released for the game's Limited Edition version.<ref name="Farlander">{{StoryCite|story=The Farlander Papers|book=Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)}}</ref>
