'''Agira''' was an [[Imroosian]] [[Gender|female]] from the [[planet]] [[Imroosia]] who served as a [[handmaiden]] of the [[House of Organa]] on the planet [[Alderaan]]. In [[9 BBY]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] convinced Agira to wear her [[clothing]] as a disguise, claiming that [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa]] would find it amusing. When the queen came to collect Leia in the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan]], she was initially fooled by Agira's disguise but ultimately saw through the ruse when she felt the scales on the Imroosian's [[Limb|hands]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|She's ready? Great.|Queen Breha Organa is fooled by Agira's disguise|audio=Breha-Ready-OBK1.flac|<ref name="KenobiI" />}}
Agira was an<ref name="KenobiI" /> [[Imroosian]]<ref name="Publishing" /> [[Gender|female]]<ref name="KenobiI" /> from the [[Volcano|volcanic]] [[planet]] [[Imroosia]]<ref name="Topps" /> who served the [[House of Organa]],<ref name="KenobiI" /> the royal house of the planet [[Alderaan]],<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> as a [[handmaiden]]<ref name="KenobiI" /> while she was young. Making Alderaan her home, Agira became a friend to [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="KenobiDate">{{KenobiDate|I}}</ref> persuaded Agira and three other handmaidens that her [[Parent|mother]], [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa]], would find it amusing if Agira was disguised as Leia<ref name="KenobiI" /> and acted as her decoy. The idea was inspired by the [[Royal House of Naboo|Royal House]] of [[Naboo]],<ref name="Publishing" /> a planet where [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|Royal Handmaidens]] acted as decoys for the [[Monarch of Naboo|monarch]].<ref name="USW" /> The other handmaidens then dressed Agira in the princess's [[clothing]] in one of the rooms of the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan]],<ref name="KenobiI" /> with a large hood concealing her face.<ref name="Publishing" />

[[File:AgiraInDisguise-KenobiI.png|thumb|right|300px|Agira's disguise as Princess Leia Organa fooled Queen Breha Organa.]]
The queen then entered the room and, believing that the handmaiden was her daughter, began briefing Agira on an upcoming meeting with Breha's sister, [[Celly Organa]], and her family. The queen took Agira's [[Limb|hand]] and began leading her toward the door, but she stopped when she felt the scales on the Imroosian's hands. Breha pulled back the hood concealing Agira's smiling face, after which the handmaiden revealed that Leia had come up with the ruse. The queen then left to find her daughter, who had snuck out to go [[Shipspotter|shipspotting]] in the nearby [[forest]].<ref name="KenobiI" />

==Personality and traits==
Agira was a loyal handmaiden and a friend of Organa.<ref name="Publishing" /> She had scaled [[Color|white]] skin, brown [[eye]]s, and brown [[hair]],<ref name="KenobiI" /> which was intricately braided as part of her disguise.<ref name="KenobiIAD">{{Kenobi|Part I}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref>

==Equipment==
While disguised as Organa, Agira wore a white blouse, pink pants,<ref name="KenobiI" /> a pair of brocaded [[velvet]] shoes,<ref name="KenobiIAD" /> and an embroidered pink tunic and matching hooded [[scarf]].<ref name="KenobiI" />

Agira was portrayed by [[Molly Miller]] in the [[Part I|first episode]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' television series,<ref name="KenobiI" /> which aired on [[May 27]], [[2022]].<ref name="Episode 1 Premiere">{{Twitter|obiwankenobi|status/1530019678562549760|''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]''|date=2022-05-27|quote=The time for #ObiWanKenobi has arrived&hellip;early. Start streaming the first two episodes on @DisneyPlus TONIGHT at 9PM PT.|archivedate=20220527053945}}</ref> Agira's name is not spoken in the episode but appears in the credits.<ref name="KenobiI" /> The character's makeup was done by [[Gabriel De Cunto]] and [[Scott Wheeler]] and took several hours to apply.<ref name="KenobiI" /><ref name="Instagram" /> Miller also served as a stunt double for young Leia Organa in the series, effectively making her Organa's double in two ways.<ref name="KenobiI" /> 

[[Emily Shkoukani]] wrote the page covering Agira in the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' and included the idea of Organa being inspired by the royals of Naboo and having Handmaidens based on the fact that the character was established to have been raised learning the heritage of her birth mother,<ref name="Secrets">{{SW|url=news/dawn-of-rebellion|text=5 Behind-the-Pages Secrets of ''Star Wars:'' ''Dawn of Rebellion''}}</ref> Queen [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="USW" />
