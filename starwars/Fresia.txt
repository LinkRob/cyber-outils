'''Fresia''' was a terrestrial [[planet]] in the [[Core Worlds]]' [[Torranix sector]] that was [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[Coromon Headhunter]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], the world's [[Galactic Republic]] [[senator]], [[Seti Ashgad]], simply vanished. At some point between [[19 BBY]] and [[5 BBY]], the [[partisans]] of resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]] conducted a [[Raid on an Incom warehouse|violent raid]] on an [[Incom Corporation]] [[Unidentified Incom Corporation warehouse|warehouse]] on Fresia and [[Thief|stole]] eight [[T-65 X-wing starfighter]]s. By [[1 BBY]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] had arrived on the planet and recruited the [[human]] Fresia native [[Jaldine Gerams]] as a [[Rebel pilot|pilot]].

==Description==
Fresia was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Geektionary" /> in the [[Fresia system]] of the [[Torranix sector]] in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It fell within grid square K-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was [[Homeworld|home]] to the fierce creature known as the [[Coromon Headhunter]].<ref name="BMF" />

==History==
[[File:GeramsCockpit-RO.png|thumb|left|300px|alt=Jaldine Gerams sits in the cockpit of an X-wing starfighter wearing a blue flight suit and a rebel flight helmet with a checkered blue and gray pattern.|Rebel pilot and computer specialist Jaldine Gerams was born on Fresia.]]
Around [[61 BBY]],<ref name="GeramsDates">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]'' states that [[Jaldine Gerams]] was around sixty [[Standard year|years]] old while serving in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]]. Therefore, she must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[61 BBY]].</ref> the [[human]] [[Jaldine Gerams]]<ref name="TUVG" /> was [[Reproduction|born]] a native of Fresia. She became a [[programmer]]<ref name="Geektionary" /> and [[computer]] specialist, authoring [[starfighter]] [[Flight simulator|simulator]] programs and logging countless [[Standard hour|hours]]<ref name="TUVG" /> while dreaming of flying a real starfighter.<ref name="HC71">{{HelmetCollectionCite|71|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], Fresia was represented in the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] by [[Senator]] [[Seti Ashgad]]; however, during the war there were increased [[death]] threats and [[assassin]]ation attempts on all senators and Ashgad simply vanished.<ref name="Collapse" />

The [[starship]] manufacturer [[Incom Corporation]] maintained a [[Unidentified Incom Corporation warehouse|warehouse]] on Fresia in which it stored [[T-65 X-wing starfighter]]s,<ref name="DRVG" /> also borrowing the name of the Coromon Headhunter for the [[Z-95 Headhunter]] starfighter the company produced with [[Sublights Products Corporation]].<ref name="BMF" /> At some point between [[19 BBY]] and [[5 BBY]],<ref name="RaidDate">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' establishes that the [[raid on an Incom warehouse]] took place between the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and the events of "[[Narkina 5 (episode)|Narkina 5]]." The book dates the Empire's formation and the events of "Narkina 5" to [[19 BBY]] and [[5 BBY]], respectively.</ref> the resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]] saw the value of [[X-wing starfighter]]s and [[Thief|stole]] eight from the warehouse on Fresia during a violent [[Raid on an Incom warehouse|raid]] carried out by his [[partisans]]. He then used the vehicles to form the [[Cavern Angels]] [[squadron]].<ref name="DRVG" /> By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="GeramsDates" /> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] had arrived on Fresia and encountered Gerams, who jumped at the chance to join up on the [[Standard day|day]] she encountered them so that she could fly a real starfighter as a [[rebel pilot]].<ref name="Geektionary" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Fresia.jpg|thumb|right|285px|alt=A planet with blue oceans and mountainous whie landmasses under long stretches of cloud stands against a black spacescape.|Fresia was first depicted in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity in ''Coruscant and the Core Worlds''.]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Fresia was first mentioned in the "Incom/SubPro Z-95 Headhunter" article in the "[[Starship Fact File]]" department of the [[De Agostini]] magazine [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 59|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 59]],<ref name="BMF" /> which was released around [[February 17]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|59}}</ref>

The [[Fresia/Legends|planet]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, in which it was first mentioned in the [[1987]] [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game (first edition)|first edition rulebook]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', which was published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="FirstEdition">[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game (first edition)|''Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game'']]</ref> The [[Planet/Legends|world]] was first pictured in the [[2003]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' supplement ''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]'', published by [[Wizards of the Coast]],<ref name="CatCW">''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]''</ref> and appeared for the first time in the [[2006]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]'', which was developed by [[Petroglyph]] and published by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="EaW">''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]''</ref>
