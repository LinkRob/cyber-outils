[[Lady]] '''Corvax''' was a [[Force-sensitive]]<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[humanoid]]<ref name="Vader VR 2 /> [[Gender|female]] individual who resided on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] along with her husband, [[Dorwin Corvax|Dorwin]]. When the latter fell in [[Attack on Mustafar|battle]], she attempted to resurrect him by using the Mustafarians' most sacred artifact, the [[Bright Star]]. Instead, the energies unleashed completely destroyed the surface of Mustafar. However, a [[Corvax descendant|descendant]] of hers destroyed the Bright Star and freed Dorwin, allowing Mustafar to slowly heal.

==Biography==
[[File:BlackBishop-VI.png|thumb|left|180px|Dorwin, the husband of Lady Corvax, after she failed to resurrect him]]
[[Lady]] Corvax was [[Reproduction|born]] before [[1032 BBY]].<ref name="Dates" /> Long before the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Lady Corvax and her [[Marriage|husband]], [[Dorwin Corvax]], resided on [[Mustafar]], which was then a lush planet thriving with life, and the couple lived in harmony with the [[Mustafarian|natives]]. One fateful [[Standard day|day]], however, Mustafar came under attack, and Dorwin [[Death|fell]] in [[Attack on Mustafar|battle]], [[sword]] in hand. Struck with grief, the woman stole the most sacred artifact of the Mustafarians, the [[Bright Star]] [[crystal]],<ref name="Vader VR 1" /> to power her [[Aeon Engine]], which she hoped could resurrect her love.<ref name="Vader VR 2" />

That attempt, however, utterly failed. Not only was Dorwin cursed to be trapped between the real world and what came after,<ref name="Vader VR 2" /> but the powerful energies that burst from the Bright Star [[Devastation of Mustafar|transformed]] the beautiful planet of Mustafar into a hellscape. Following the incident, Lady Corvax kept the artifact hidden in [[Corvax Fortress|her sanctum]], whose key could only be used by one of her descendants.<ref name="Vader VR 1" /> She hoped that one day, a descendant would recover the Bright Star, bring it back to the Mustafarians, and perhaps repair some of the damage she had done.<ref name="Vader VR 2" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], one of her descendants, a [[Corvax descendant|smuggler]] who was partially aided by Dorwin, destroyed the artifact, freeing Dorwin from his curse and allowing the planet to slowly heal.<ref name="Vader Immortal Ep III">{{VaderImmortal|III}}</ref>
