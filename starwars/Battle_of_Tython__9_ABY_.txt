The '''Battle of Tython''',<ref name="Timelines" /> also known as '''assault on Tython''', was a skirmish that took place on the [[planet]] [[Tython]] between a [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|remnant]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]], and the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] and his allies, [[Boba Fett]] and [[Fennec Shand]], during the [[New Republic Era]]<ref name="Chapter 14" /> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> 

==The battle==
The battle began with [[stormtrooper]]s disembarking off of a [[V-82 dropship]], and advancing up of the mountain where Boba Fett, Fennec Shand, and Din Djarin were located, in an attempt to capture [[Din Grogu|The Child]], who was at the top of the mountain. The stormtroopers quickly took heavy casualties as Fennec Shand and Boba Fett fired upon them. The [[Unidentified stormtrooper commander (Tython)|Stormtrooper Commander]] in charge of the assault ordered one of his troops to flank left and from above. Upon being told that there was too much fire, the commander called his subordinate an idiot, right before the subordinate was shot. Meanwhile, Boba Fett used [[Boba Fett's gaderffii stick|his gaderffii stick]] to attack several stormtroopers. At the same time, an [[artillery stormtrooper]] set up and deployed a mortar, attempting to use it on Fennec Shand. Meanwhile, stormtroopers set up a [[E-HOB heavy repeating laser cannon|E-HOB heavy repeating blaster]] and opened fire on Fennec, who retreated behind a large rock. Fennec pushed the rock downhill towards the stormtroopers who desperately tried to get away. Several were crushed, including the E-HOB gunner. The Stormtrooper Commander ordered his troops to advance, but was then ambushed and killed by Boba Fett, who retrieved his armor. Meanwhile, Din Djarin came to Fennec's aid, firing [[whistling bird]]s at the stormtroopers, who had been reinforced by a second troop transport. Boba Fett appeared with his armor, and killed several stormtroopers, prompting the rest to retreat. Boba then fired a missile at the retreating ships, destroying them both. However, a turbolaser blast came from the sky, fired from [[Gideon's light cruiser]]. Shortly after, Grogu was abducted by Gideon's [[Phase-III Dark Trooper|Dark Troopers]].<ref name="Chapter 14" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle appeared in "[[Chapter 14: The Tragedy]]" of [[Jon Favreau]]'s [[2019]] [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', written by Favreau and directed by [[Robert Rodriguez]]<ref name="Chapter 14" /> and released on [[December 4]], [[2020]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-november-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in November 2020}}</ref>

To visualize the skirmish between Din Djarin, Fennec Shand, and Boba Fett against Gideon's Imperial remnant, Rodriguez had his young sons dress with some [[Wikipedia:Halloween|Halloween]] costumes and used some ''[[Star Wars]]'' action figures to record a walkthrough of the planned sequence. He ended up turning a three-page battle scene into a nine-minute sequence, given that he had wanted to see Fett back since he was a kid but felt that it was necessary to have him feel and move different so he would not look like the Mandalorian, as he was unaware if Fett would be returning in further episodes at the time.<ref name="Skirmish Planning">{{DisneyGallery|Disney Gallery: The Mandalorian: Making of Season Two|Making of Season Two}}</ref>
