'''Ashley Eckstein''' (born '''Maria Ashley Drane''' on [[September 22]], [[1981]]) is an [[Wikipedia:Actor|actress]]; who had voiced [[Ahsoka Tano]] in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]<ref name="TCW film" /> and [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|television series]] from [[2008]]<ref name="Rising Malevolence">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> to [[2013]]<ref name="The Wrong Jedi">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref> and in [[2020]]'s revival season<ref name="Gone with a Trace>{{TCW|Gone with a Trace}}</ref>. She reprised the role in ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' from [[2015]]<ref name="Rebel Resolve">{{Rebels|Rebel Resolve}}</ref> to [[2018]]<ref name="A World Between Worlds">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref> and in ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (television series)|Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi]]''<ref name="Practice Makes Perfect">{{TOTJ|Practice Makes Perfect (episode)}}</ref>. Eckstein also narrated the audiobook edition of the novel [[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']], which focuses on the character.<ref name="Ahsoka novel">[[Ahsoka (audiobook)|''Ahsoka'' audiobook]]</ref>

In [[2010]], Eckstein founded the clothing manufacturer [http://heruniverse.com Her Universe]; which specializes in science fiction and fantasy-themed fashion for girls and women; including ''[[Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Year by Year" />

Beyond her work as Ahsoka, Eckstein also is passionate about mental health. She created, wrote, and hosted the [[2022]] [[StarWarsKids.com|Star Wars Kids]] series ''[[Star Wars Mindful Matters]]''.<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1521882343581003776|[[Star Wars]]|quote=Join Ashley Eckstein in our new Star Wars Kids short series Star Wars Mindful Matters as she teaches mindful lessons inspired by our favorite Star Wars characters and stories.|archivedate=20220504160104}}</ref>
