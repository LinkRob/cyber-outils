'''Coach Epsilon''' was a section of a [[train]] that transported citizens on the [[planet]] [[Milvayne]]. Shortly after the [[Skirmish aboard Accresker Jail|skirmish]] aboard [[Accresker Jail]], the criminals [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] and [[0-0-0]] traveled to the planet to have the [[proximity bomb]]s inside them removed. To reach [[South District Seven]], Aphra bribed the train driver to let them on without I.D.s. The driver referred Aphra to Coach Epsilon while telling 0-0-0 to stay in the rear-most wagon with the other droids. When 0-0-0 threatened him, he reported them to the [[Informahub]] and the two were forced into the [[City|undercity]] of Milvayne by the [[Milvayne Authority]].<ref name="Aphra 27" />
