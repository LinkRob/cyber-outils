'''Charles Howerton''' is a [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice artist]] who voiced [[Owen Lars]] in the [[1997]] ''[[A New Hope Read-Along]]''<ref name="ANH" /> and [[Tiaan Jerjerrod]] in the 1997 ''[[Return of the Jedi Read-Along]]''. The same audio of each was reused for the [[2015]] read-along storybook versions.<ref name="RotJ">''[[Return of the Jedi Read-Along]]''</ref>
