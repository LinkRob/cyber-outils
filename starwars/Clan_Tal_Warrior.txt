The '''Clan Tal Warrior''' was a set of [[Mandalorian]] [[armor]] was belonging to and worn by [[Aran Tal]], a competitor in the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]]. It was the ancestral [[Mandalorian armor|armor]] of [[Clan Tal]],<ref name="Aran Tal" /> and was made of [[beskar]] according to the [[Arena News]].<ref name="Arena News" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Clan Tal Warrior was a "Base" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Aran Tal's [[Mandalorian armor]], featured in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 1.0.0 alongside Season 1 and the game's global launch on [[June 4]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Clan Tal MT-97]]. This costume was unlocked by default when Aran Tal became playable.<ref name="Aran Tal" />
