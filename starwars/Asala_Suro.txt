'''Asala Suro''' was an [[Umbaran]] [[archivist]] [[Marriage|married]] to the [[Gender|female]] Umbaran [[Cosa Simo]]. While Suro was helping her wife trace her ancestry, Simo discovered a [[Unidentified familial line|familial line]] dating back over three [[Millennium|millennia]] before<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> [[132 BBY]]. In that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date"> [[Star Wars: The Acolyte|''The Acolyte'']]&mdash;and therefore "[[Revenge / Justice]]"&mdash;takes place in [[132 BBY]].</ref> Suro and Simo visited [[Olega City]] on the [[planet]] [[Olega]], to which Simo traced the line, hoping that Simo would be permitted to view ancient records stored in the [[Jedi Order]]'s local [[Olega Jedi temple|temple]].<ref name="AcolyteVG " /> Shortly after the [[Skirmish on Olega|murder]] of the temple's [[Jedi Master]] [[Torbin]], Simo and Suro were standing near the wall of a building in one of Olega City's streets.<ref name="RJ">{{Acolyte|Revenge / Justice}}</ref> Suro had [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s and light-sensitive white skin and, during her visit of Olega City, she wore a travel [[Shadowcloak (clothing)|shadowcloak]] and bundled clothing that protected her skin.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Asala Suro first appeared, albeit unidentified, in "[[Revenge / Justice]],"<ref name="RJ" /> the second episode of the live-action [[Disney+]] series [[Star Wars: The Acolyte|''The Acolyte'']], which aired on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> The individual was later identified as Asala Suro in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> The Olega scenes<ref name="Location">{{Lucasfilm|url=news/lucasfilm-on-location-the-acolyte|text=Lucasfilm on Location: ''The Acolyte''}}</ref> that featured Suro<ref name="RJ" /> were shot at [[Shinfield Studios]],<ref name="Location" /> [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="Julian Foddy">{{Lucasfilm|url=news/julian-foddy-acolyte|text=''The Acolyte'' Visual Effects Supervisor Julian Foddy}}</ref>
