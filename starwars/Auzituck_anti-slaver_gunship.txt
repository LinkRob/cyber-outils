The '''''Auzituck'' anti-slaver gunship''', also called the '''''Auzituck''-class gunship''', or the '''Wookiee gunship''', was a [[gunship]] manufactured by [[Appazanna Engineering Works]] and used by [[Wookiee]]s to battle [[Slavery|slavers]].<ref name="rebelsvisual" />

==Description==
[[File:Wookiee_Liberator_Auzituck_Gunship_XWM.png|thumb|left|250px|An ''Auzituck'' in space]]
''Auzituck'' roughly translated to "liberated one" in [[Shyriiwook]], the native [[language]] of the [[Wookiee]]s. The [[gunship]] was fast, well-[[armor]]ed and heavily armed with a narrow profile and large [[engine]]s. It had a dual-tiered, bubble-shaped canopy that housed the [[pilot]] in the upper, rear section with the copilot and [[gunner]] in the lower forward position.<ref name="DoR" /> The interiors were made from [[wroshyr tree]]s, with sap serving as glue.<ref name="TRB">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref>

==History==
During the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]], one such [[Starship|vessel]], serving as a freedom fighter against the [[Galactic Empire]], was contracted to meet with the crew of the ''[[Ghost]]'' by [[Cikatro Vizago]]. But by the [[time]] it arrived, it had been raided by [[Trandoshan]] [[enslaver]]s and left adrift, and the crew had been sold to the Empire.<ref name="TRB" />

<div style="float:left">[[File:Ghost_and_Auzituck.png|thumb|left|250px|An ''Auzituck'' gunship docked with the ''Ghost''.]]</div>
Another gunship later rendezvoused with the ''Ghost'' to recover the Wookiee crew they had [[Mission to free Wookiee prisoners|rescued]].<ref name="Spark">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref> One of the Wookiees that was rescued, [[Wullffwarro]], [[Wullffwarro's Auzituck anti-slaver gunship|uses]] one of the ''Auzituck''s as his personal ship.<ref>{{RebelsMagCite|27|A Time to Survive}}</ref>

The Wookiee [[bounty hunter]] [[Krrsantan]] also owned [[Black Krrsantan's Auzituck anti-slaver gunship|his own]] ''Auzituck'' gunship during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Vader 8">[[Darth Vader (2015) 8|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 8]]</ref>

Wookiee gunships joined the [[Citizens' Fleet]] during the [[Battle of Exegol]] in<ref name="TROS Junior Novel" /> [[35 ABY]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[Sith Eternal]].<ref name="TROS date">{{Film|IX}} events is dated to [[35 ABY]] as ''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary'' states its events took place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which took place in [[34 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Auzituck'' anti-slaver gunship first appeared in ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]'', the one-hour premiere for the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. The name "Auzituck" dates back to the [[May]] [[1973]] [[The Star Wars rough draft|rough draft]] of what ultimately became [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': ''Episode IV A New Hope'']]. In scene 135 of that script, which was set in the "Wookee [sic] camp," an early version of Chewbacca was greeted by his father, Auzituck, Chief of the [[Chewbacca's family/Legends|Kaapauku]] tribe.<ref name="JBSS">{{WebCite|url=http://www.starwarz.com/starkiller/2010/03/the-star-wars-rough-draft|text=The Star Wars&mdash;Rough Draft|work=The Jedi Bendu Script Site|archivedate=20111004132652}}</ref>

[[File:Auzituck_Gunship_DoR.png|thumb|right|200px|An ''Auzituck'' gunship firing its forward cannons.]]
The young readers book ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Head to Head]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and featuring various match-ups from the series, placed a patrol gunship against an [[Gozanti-class cruiser|Imperial freighter]], with the gunship damaging the freighter and escaping.

In the episode ''[[Peril on Kashyyyk]]'' of ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'', Wookiees use the gunships to raid a [[Trandoshan]] prison camp.<ref name="Freemakers">{{FreemakerAdventures|Peril on Kashyyyk}}</ref> Later in the season two episode ''[[A Perilous Rescue]]'', the rebels take cover in a crashed gunship before being [[Skirmish on Kashyyyk (Freemakers)|rescued]] by the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] flying the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']].<ref name="Freemakers rescue">{{FreemakerAdventures|A Perilous Rescue}}</ref>

The ship, particularly the distinctive bubble cockpits, resembles the real-life Mi-24 "Hind" helicopter gunship, much like the [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT]].
