[[Otoh Langua]], a small [[Gungan]] [[city]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], was '''attacked''',<ref name="SWOTFL" /> like most Gungan cities,<ref name="Episode I" /> by the [[Trade Federation Droid Army|droid army]] of the [[Trade Federation]] during the [[Invasion of Naboo]]. [[Oma Prumba]], a [[militiagung]] survivor of the attack, later participated in the [[Battle of Naboo]] and recounted her experiences.<ref name="SWOTFL" />
