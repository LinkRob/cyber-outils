'''Fuel computers''' were components of [[starship]] computer systems that kept track of when and where a ship had been [[Fuel|refuelled]]. The [[Jedi Order]] had a trick, primarily used to catch [[smuggler]]s, of cross-referencing locations someone was known to have visited with where they had refuelled so they could deduce where else the target may have gone.<ref name="Children of the Force" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]] used this on the ''[[Xanadu Blood]]'', a [[starfighter]] owned by [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]], to deduce the location of several [[Force-sensitive]] children he had been hired to kidnap, and found that he had taken the children to the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]]. This allowed them to set out on a [[Mission to Mustafar (Skywalker and Tano)|rescue mission]].<ref name="Children of the Force" />

==Behind the scenes==
A fuel computer first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|season 2]] episode "[[Children of the Force]]."<ref name="Children of the Force" />
