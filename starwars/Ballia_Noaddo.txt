'''Ballia Noaddo''' was a [[human]] executive of the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]] who resigned over the amount of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] influence at the company. In [[13 BBY]], she was one of many unhappy citizens of the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] trying to leave via the [[Coronet City Spaceport|Coronet Spaceport]].

After [[stormtrooper]]s disrupted the queue she was in, the criminals [[Han Solo|Han]] and [[Qi'ra]] cut in front of Noaddo and then caused a security alert once they reached the customs gate.

==Biography==
'''Ballia Noaddo''' was a [[human]] citizen of the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] who worked in an executive position at the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]. Displeased with growing [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] influence at the company, she resigned and joined many other unhappy citizens in trying to leave the planet.<ref name="Solo Guide" />

In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events on [[Corellia]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[13 BBY]].</ref> she was queueing in [[Coronet City Spaceport|Coronet Spaceport]] in [[Coronet City]] when Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s disurpted the queue to forcefully removed someone further up the line. She quickly moved to regain her spot, but not before the criminals [[Han Solo|Han]] and [[Qi'ra]] cut in directly in front of her. The queue then progressed up until the duo in front of Noaddo moved up to the security gate, at which point a security alerted was sounded in the [[Spaceport|port]] when Han breached the customs gate.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Noaddo had light skin, green [[eye]]s, and blonde [[hair]]. She was unhappy enough with the amount of Imperial influence at Corellian Engineering Corporation for her to resign her executive position there.<ref name="Solo Guide" />

==Equipment==
While at the port, Noaddo wore a puffy [[Color|brown]] sleeveless [[Clothing|jacket]] over gray robes with brown [[boot]]s. She carried a white [[shockproof travel case]] and wore her exit documentation [[data tag]] around her neck.<ref name="Solo Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ballia Noaddo appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Anthology'']] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" /> The character was first identified by name in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Solo Guide" />
