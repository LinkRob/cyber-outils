'''Casino cruisers''' were a type of [[cruiser]] used on the [[casino]] [[planet]] of [[Galator III]]. The middle decks of the cruisers were used for storage, a fact the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] used to escape from [[patrol ship]]s that were chasing his [[starship]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', as he carried out a [[Heist on Galator III|heist]] on the planet.<ref name="Han Chewie 1" />
