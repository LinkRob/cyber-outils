[[Senator]] '''Den Gane''' was a [[Politics|politician]] in the [[Imperial Senate]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], representing the world of [[Roona]].<ref name="Welcome" /> They had the same last name as [[Edcel Bar Gane]], a [[Roonan]] Senator in the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> who came from the same [[planet]].<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref>

Following the [[Ghorman Massacre]]<ref name="Welcome" /> in [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{Andor|Messenger}} dates the events of {{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}} to [[2 BBY]].</ref> the senate held a session to discuss the events. Gane was one of several senators who spoke within the [[Grand Convocation Chamber]] as part of the [[Standard day|day's]] agenda. Gane expressed frustration regarding the massacre's events and complacency of the Imperials to allow such an attack to take place.<ref name="Welcome" />

==Behind the scenes==
Den Gane appeared in the [[Welcome to the Rebellion|ninth episode]] of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''<ref name="Welcome" /> which aired on [[May 6]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed|date=2025-03-24}}</ref> In the credits, the character is listed as "[[Senator]] Gane"; the character's full name is provided by the [[Aurebesh]] caption that appears on the monitor as they speak, which reads "SEN. DEN GANE • [[Roona|ROONA]]".<ref name="Welcome" />

In the episode on [[Disney+]], the subtitles erroneously attribute Senator [[Nico]]'s name when Gane speaks, although the name is used correctly when Nico themselves speaks; however, Gane is explicitly identified by the previously cited Aurebesh. Complicating matters even further is that in the episode credits, which lists the characters in order of appearance, Gane is erroneously listed before Nico; but in the actual episode, Nico speaks before Gane. Both characters are performed by [[Caitlin Nicholas]] and voiced by [[Colin Purves]].<ref name="Welcome" />
