'''Bran''' was a [[human]] who worked as an intern for the company [[De'Rruyet Industries]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Aphra 9" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 9|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 9]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Bran and [[Unidentified human intern|another intern]] was just outside of the [[De'Rruyet Center]] on the<ref name="Aphra 9" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Aphra 8">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 8|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 8]]</ref> [[Midarr]]. The pair were tasked with getting the owner of the company, [[Beol De'Rruyet]], his [[caf]] delivery. As the two returned from a [[cafe]] with the caf at hand, the other intern complained about their current position and how the shifts were going to be awful.

Unbeknownst to the intern, Bran was taken down by the [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]], and before the other intern realized, they were also taken out by the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]]. Starros and Aphra then took their clothes and disguised themselves to enter the facility, pretending to be Bran and the other intern. Bran had [[Color|red]] [[hair]] and fair skin. They wore a brown uniform [[Clothing|vest]] with a white top underneath, a pair of gray [[boot]]s, a pair of tan pants, and a black [[belt]].<ref name="Aphra 9" />
