The '''Battle of Comantira''' was fought by the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Imperial Era]]<ref name="Vader 38" /> in the vicinity of the [[Comantira system]].<ref name="Battlenamia" /> Eventual Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Corleque]] served under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of fellow eventual Admiral [[Firmus Piett]] at the battle. During the battle, an evac [[transport]] was due to be launched, though the [[shuttle]] Corleque was on arrived to the transport three [[second]]s late. To Corleque's surprise, however, Piett ordered the transport's launch be delayed four seconds, which allowed Corleque's shuttle to reach it.<ref name="Vader 38" />

[[Standard year|Years]] later between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the now Admiral Corleque recalled the Battle of Comantira after<ref name="Vader 38" /> he<ref name="Vader 10">[[Darth Vader (2020) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 10]]</ref> was ordered to [[Attack on the Executor (Scourge crisis)|destroy]] the ''[[Executor]]''<ref name="Vader 38" />&mdash;the [[flagship]] of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]<ref name="Vader 38 solicit">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-september-2023-preview|text=Uncover the ''Dark Droids'' Crossover Event in Marvel's September 2023 ''Star Wars'' Comics &ndash; Exclusive Preview}}</ref> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] which Piett served as admiral aboard&mdash;after it was taken over by<ref name="Vader 38" /> [[Scourge crisis|scourged]] [[droid]]s.<ref name="Vader 38 solicit" /> Corleque intended to rescue Piett in return for his actions during the Battle of Comantira, though the droids were defeated after [[Droid uprising|forces]] led by Vader repaired and activated the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|vessel's]] fail-safe mechanism. Afterward, Corleque's [[flagship]], the ''[[Unbreakable]]'', came under attack by droids, comprimising his [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|ship]]. After being informed of the development, Piett, despite protests from Corleque, waited four seconds like he had at the Battle of Comantira, and had the ''Executor'' and [[Starship|ships]] in [[Corleque's fleet]] destroy the ''Unbreakable''.<ref name="Vader 38" />

The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Vader 38" /> on [[September 13]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103069/star_wars_darth_vader_2020_38|text=Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020) #38}}</ref>
