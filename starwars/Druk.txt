'''Druk''' was an exclamation of frustration<ref name="High Republic 2021 3" /> or worry.<ref name="Jedha script">[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha (script)|''The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha'' script]]</ref>	It could also be used as an intensifier, such as "who the druk are they?"<ref name="Vader 18">[[Darth Vader (2020) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 18]]</ref>

Druk was also used as a synonym for nonsense, e.g. "a load of [[bantha]] druk"<ref name="Convergence">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]''</ref> or "the druk that flows from that [[mouth]] of [[Zeetar|yours]]."<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> [[Archaeology|Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] referred to a [[Path engine]] replica that did not live up to [[Beol De'Rruyet]]'s claims as "full of druk."<ref name="Aphra 2020 8">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 8|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 8]]</ref> 

The term could also refer to something of low quality, such as "piece of druk."<ref name="Tempest Runner script">[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner (script)|''The High Republic: Tempest Runner'' script]]</ref> The term "drukhole" referred to an unpleasant place.<ref name="High Republic 2022 3">[[The High Republic (2022) 3|''The High Republic'' (2022) 3]]</ref>

{{Language-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The use of the word "druk" is similar to the real-world impolite word "shit," especially when used in phrases like "[[bantha]] druk,"<ref name="Convergence" /> which is similar to "bullshit"; "piece of druk,"<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> which is similar to the term "piece of shit"; "full of druk,"<ref name="Aphra 2020 8" /> which is similar to "full of shit," and "drukhole,"<ref name="High Republic 2022 3" /> which is similar to "shithole." It is also phonetically similar to the real-world vulgar swear word "f*ck."
