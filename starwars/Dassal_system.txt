The '''Dassal system'''<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|dassal-system|Dassal system}}</ref> was a [[star system]]<ref name="The Core Problem" /> located in [[Sector Epsilon 51-3]]<ref name="Sector" /> of the [[Unknown Regions]] of [[the galaxy]]. The system contained several [[planet]]s and [[planetoid]]s, including one planet, [[Najra-Va]], which had an inhabited [[Unidentified moon (Najra-Va)|moon]]. Upon [[Mission to the Dassal system|investigating]] [[First Order]] activity in the system, [[Kazuda Xiono]] and [[Poe Dameron]] learned that the [[Dassal system's sun|sun]] of the star system had disappeared and its planets had been cored out.<ref name="The Core Problem" /> At another point,<ref name="X-wing">{{FFGXW2|set=Major Vonreg's TIE Expansion Pack|cardname="Holo"}}</ref> A [[TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor|TIE/ba interceptor]] flown by the [[First Order Special Forces]] [[ace]] [[pilot]] [["Holo"]]<ref name="A Legend Reborn">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2020/1/8/legend-reborn|text=A Legend Reborn|date=2020-01-08}}</ref> participated in a skirmish within the Dassal system that involved other [[starfighter]]s of the [[TIE series|TIE fighter series]].<ref name="X-wing" />
