"'''Back with a Bang!'''" was an article written by [[Jonathan Wilkins]]. It was an interview with [[Joel Aron]] discussing his work as CG effects and lighting supervisor on the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' TV series. It was published in [[Star Wars Insider 120|''Star Wars Insider'' 120]] and later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2014|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2014]].
