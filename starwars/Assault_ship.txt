'''Assault ships''' were a type of [[starship]] used for combat. The [[Galactic Republic]] used [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class transgalactic military assault ships]] during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> while the [[Mandalore|Mandalorian]] [[Death Watch]] used [[Mandalorian gunship]]s, another model of assault ship.<ref name="Mandalorian Gunship">{{Databank|mandalorian-gunship|Mandalorian Gunship}}</ref> The [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]] was the [[Imperial Navy]]'s primary assault ship.<ref name="Starships and Speeders">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref>
