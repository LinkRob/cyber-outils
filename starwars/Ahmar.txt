'''Ahmar''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Ahmar's species|alien]] [[Jedi]] who survived [[Order 66]] and was brought to the [[moon]] [[Nur]] alongside [[Barriss Offee]] and [[Dante]] where he was offered the chance to become an [[Inquisitor]]. However, Ahmar refused to believe [[the Grand Inquisitor]]'s claims that the [[Jedi Order]] had betrayed the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] and planned to break out of the [[Fortress Inquisitorius]]. However, he was killed by the Grand Inquisitor while trying to make his attempt to escape.<ref name="Devoted" />
