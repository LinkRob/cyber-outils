The '''Anthan system''' was a [[star system]] located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Shadows and Secrets, Part VI" /> in the [[Braxant sector]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It contained the [[planet]] [[Anthan Prime]], which was orbited by many [[moon]]s. The closest moon [[Anthan 1]], [[orbit]]ed within the voluminous [[gas]]eous [[atmosphere]] of Anthan Prime itself, while [[Anthan 12]], [[Anthan 13]], and [[Anthan 14]] were considered the mid-moons.<ref name="Shadows and Secrets, Part VI" />
