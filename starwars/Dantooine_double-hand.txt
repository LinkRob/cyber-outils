'''Dantooine double-hand''' was a game<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> that shared its name with the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] [[Dantooine]].<ref name="Dantooine">{{Databank|dantooine|Dantooine}}</ref> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], members of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] [[Mission to Planet X (Path of the Open Hand)|traveled]] aboard the [[yacht]] ''[[Scupper]]'' to<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> the [[Wild Space]]<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> planet [[Sophros|Planet X]].<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />

In order to kill [[time]] during their journey, the cult members [[Shea Ganandra]] and [[Ort (Reesarian)|Ort]] played Dantooine double-hand on an upturned crate in one of the [[Starship|ship's]] holds, which was mentally acknowledged by the [[Evereni]] [[Guide of the Closed Fist|Guide of the Open Hand]] [[Marda Ro]]. After they reached [[The Veil (Sophros)|the Veil]] around the planet, the two were called to the ''Scupper''{{'s}} [[cockpit]] by the ship's owner, the [[prospector]] [[Radicaz Dobbs|Sunshine Dobbs]].<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dantooine double-hand was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2023]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Dantooine double-hand/Legends|Dantooine double-hand]] was a card game that appeared in ''[[MedStar II: Jedi Healer]]'', a [[2004]] novel written by [[Steve Perry]] and [[Michael Reaves]] as the second volume in the [[MedStar Duology]].<ref name="MedStar II">''[[MedStar II: Jedi Healer]]''</ref>
