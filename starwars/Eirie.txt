'''Eirie''', known as the '''Timekeeper Moon'''<ref name="Convergence" /> and the '''lost moon''', was a [[moon]]<ref name="Into the Dark" /> between the [[Double planet|twin planets]] [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]], in the [[Eiram system]] of the [[Dalnan sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Convergence" /> At some point in [[time]], the moon broke from its [[orbit]] and drifted between the two worlds, and the inhabitants of neither planet knew which it had originated from. As the moon was devoid of any value, Eiram and E'ronoh did not bother to fight over claiming it. A [[Eiram–E'ronoh crisis|crisis]] broke out after members of the [[Directorate]] kidnapped [[Monarchy|rulers]] from both Eiram and E'ronoh, holding them in [[cave]]s on the lost moon.<ref name="Into the Dark" />

One legend about the moon was that, billions of years before the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]], when [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]] were two cosmic beings that emerged from stardust, the moon was their shared heart, vital to both E'ronoh's winds and Eiram's tides.<ref name="Convergence" />
