The '''husband''' of [[Crys Taanzer]] was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Human/Legends|Human]] from [[Nadiem/Legends|Nadiem]]. He was [[Marriage/Legends|married]] to his wife during the waning years of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]], and together they [[Reproduction/Legends|had]] a son, [[Kennan Taanzer]]. He was [[Death/Legends|killed]] during the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Separatist]] [[Battle of Nadiem|invasion]] of Nadiem in [[22 BBY/Legends|21.6 BBY]]. He was survived by his wife and son, the latter of whom was taken by his [[Parent/Legends|mother]] to the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]] for [[Jedi training/Legends|training]].<ref name="Dark Times" />
