The '''Crystal Cave''' was located in the [[Jedi Temple (Ilum)|Jedi Temple]] on the planet [[Ilum]]. It was a sacred place where [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] would go find their [[Kyber crystal|lightsaber crystals]] in the ritual known as [[Gathering|the Gathering]].

{{Loc-stub}}

==History==
After [[Ilum]] was discovered tens of [[Millennium|thousands]] of [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Proclamation of the New Order|creation]] by a [[Unidentified Jedi scout|Jedi scout]], one of the later survey teams ultimately discovered the vast quantity of naturally-forming [[kyber crystal]]s such as [[Pontite crystal|pontite]] and [[Mephite crystal|mephite]]. The Jedi Order's [[Jedi High Council|High Council]] dispatched [[scholar]]s, [[artisan]]s and warriors to secure the planet.<ref name="Nexus" />

Following the discovery of the cave by the survey teams, which was considered to be the largest and richest cavern complex, the Jedi Council established a massive, baroque [[Jedi Temple (Ilum)|temple]] over the entrance.<ref name="Nexus" />

By [[18 BBY]], the [[Galactic Empire]] had smashed the waterfall entrance to the cave to gain entry, removing the cliff-side entryway the Jedi had used for generations.<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref>

In the year [[34 ABY]], [[Resistance]] pilot [[Poe Dameron]] [[Battle of Starkiller Base|destroyed]] [[Starkiller Base]], a [[superweapon]] built into Ilum's surface, destroying the planet, along with crystal cave.
