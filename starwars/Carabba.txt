{{Quote|You have an eye for treasure, Gunda, yet no stomach for slaughter.|Carabba's opinion on Gunda|<ref name="SG" />}}
'''Carabba''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[pirate]] [[captain]] who lead the [[Blazing Claw]] when the [[Aqualish]] [[Gunda Mabin]] served them as a cabin pup. At one point he shoved the pirate [[Fiddich|Patchwork Fiddich's]] cheek against the muzzle of a [[blaster]], peeling skin from flesh and scarring him in a ritual known as [[the Burning]]. Every [[Standard day|day]] the captain told Mabin that she had no [[stomach]] for slaughter, but a good [[eye]] for treasure, even long after she had risen to the rank of mate. She eventually [[Death|killed]] him as he was talking with a blade between the ribs, spilling his insides across the deck. Following his demise, Mabin donned Carabba's [[Color|black]] [[Hat|cap]] and took over as leader of the Blazing Claw.<ref name="SG" /> By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The entry in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' mentioning [[Captain]] Carabba is placed chronologically prior to an entry written by the criminal [[Tobias Beckett]]. ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' depicts Beckett's [[death]] on the [[planet]] [[Savareen]], and ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on Savareen in the film to nine [[Standard year|years]] after the [[proclamation of the New Order]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the proclamation to [[19 BBY]], Beckett's death must occur in [[10 BBY]], and so his entry in the guide and those chronologically before it must all be set prior to or during 10 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> she wrote about him in the [[book]] that would become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', admitting he had been right about her eye for treasure, but misjudged her capacity for slaughter.<ref name="SG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Carabba was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="SG" />
