"'''Drawn by the Force'''" was a regular department of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' written by [[Daniel Wallace]] that covered upcoming ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comics]], starting with [[Star Wars Insider 77|''Star Wars Insider'' 77]]. Previously, the comics department was named "Comics Link".

Following [[Star Wars Insider 92|issue 92]], the ''Drawn By The Force'' name was dropped and the comics column was rolled into the ''[[Jawa's Corner]]'' and later [[Blaster (department)|''Blaster'']].
