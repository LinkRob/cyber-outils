The '''AK-BK''' was an amalgamation of two [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] [[walker]]s modified to include buildings on top of it.<ref name="Day 3" /> The [[Anzellan]] [[Que-Dama]] operated a [[gambling]] hall in the AK-BK.<ref name="Focus" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|We actually call that AT-AT walker the "AK-BK." The reason is that we designed the AT-AT to resemble an ''akabeko''. So, we named it "AK-BK."|Takanobu Mizuno|translated=1|<ref name="AnimateTimes" />}}
[[File:AKBK-ConceptPayback.jpg|thumb|right|400px|AK-BK concept art]]
The AK-BK appeared in "[[The Duel: Payback]]", a non-[[canon]] short film that is part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Kamikaze Douga]] and [[ANIMA (company)|ANIMA]], was released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]]. The AK-BK was first mentioned at the ''Star Wars: Visions'' panel at [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]] on [[April 19]], 2025.<ref name="Day 3" />

According to director [[Takanobu Mizuno]], the AK-BK is designed to resemble and takes its name from [[Wikipedia:Akabeko|''akabeko'']],<ref name="AnimateTimes" /> a toy from [[Wikipedia:Fukushima|Fukushima]], [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japan]]  shaped like a red cow.<ref name="Jisho">{{WebCite|url=https://jisho.org/word/%E8%B5%A4%E3%81%B9%E3%81%93|text=赤べこ|work=Jisho.org|archivedate=20170923141709}}</ref> The buildings on the AK-BK are [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]]-style buildings inspired by those at the [[Wikipedia:Ginzan Onsen|Ginzan Onsen]]. It also has a sign based on the Kenbishi [[Wikipedia:Sake|Sake]] Brewing Company. It was designed by [[Takashi Okazaki]].<ref name="Day 3" />
