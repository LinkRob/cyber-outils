{{Quote|From the relic-strewn battlefields of Ruusan to Takodana Castle, Maz Kanata has etched a deep mark in the crossroads of the underworld.|Maz Kanata, ''Smuggler's Guide''|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
At some [[Time|point]] in [[The galaxy|galactic]] history, a series of '''battles''' were fought on [[Ruusan]], leaving behind relic-strewn battlefields that would remain years after.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> A room in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] was decorated with stained [[glass]] windows, one of which depicted one of the battles on Ruusan.<ref name="Lightsaber Lost">{{TCW|Lightsaber Lost}}</ref> The former [[Pirate Queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] mentioned the battles in the [[book]] that became known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Jedi_Temple_stained_glass_concept_art.png|thumb|right|250px|Jedi Temple stained glass concept art in ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Two'']]
One of the battles on Ruusan appeared in the artwork by [[Wayne Lo]] titled "Unused Jedi Temple mosaic" for the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series. It was included in the production journal of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Two]]''. The concept art also depicted [[Valenthyne Farfalla]] leading the [[Army of Light/Legends|Army of Light]], [[Jedi Lord|Lord]] [[Hoth (Jedi Lord)|Hoth]] and the [[Death/Legends|death]] of [[Pernicar]].<ref name="TCW CS2">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Two|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Two]]</ref> Parts of the mosaic can be seen, which includes the battle, in a [[Unidentified veranda (Jedi Temple)|room]] in "[[Lightsaber Lost]]," the eleventh episode of the second season of the series.<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" />
