'''Crispy-dried Green Beans''' was a [[Diet|vegetarian]] [[Foodstuff|dish]] that passengers on board the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' could order from the bar of the [[Starship|ship's]] [[Sublight Lounge]]<ref name="Cruiser" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The dish consisted of a plate of [[Green Bean]]s that had been dried until crisp, and was served with a [[Creamy Green Pearberry Dip]]. The [[Color|green]] [[Seed|beans]] and brown [[sauce]] could be consumed by [[human]]s.<ref name="Cruiser" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crispy-dried Green Beans was a dish available at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Cruiser" /> which was opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> The dish was last served at the hotel during the [[September 28]]&ndash;[[September 30|30]] voyage of [[2023]], the final voyage before the closure of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser.<ref name="Official website">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/overview|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Overview}}</ref>
