'''Barlon Hightower''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Hightower was recruited by [[Wedge Antilles]] into the rebuilt [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]] following the [[Battle of Hoth]]. He was [[Death|killed]] in an [[Attack on an asteroid mining station|attack]] on a nearby [[pirate]] base, that threatened the safety of the Alliance's rendezvous point, where he was assigned the [[callsign]] [[Red Ten]] and flew a [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]] as [[Lieutenant]] [[Wes Janson]]'s wingman.<ref name="FACPOV" />
