The '''Draxton DF-15 medium-yield fusion generator''', known simply as the '''DF-15 fusion generator''', was a type of lightweight [[fusion generator]]. Its design resembled a small cube roughly half a [[meter]] square with universal [[power coupling]]s on each side. The DF-15 was surrounded by a sturdy frame made out of bent alloy tubing that provided it with protection and handles for carrying. It was lightweight, quiet, efficient, and could provide clean power for up to ten [[Standard year|years]]. It cost 550 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and could recharge [[blaster]]s and other devices.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
