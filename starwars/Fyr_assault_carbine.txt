The '''Fyr assault carbine''' was a type of [[Slugthrower carbine/Canon|slugthrower carbine]] that was manufactured by [[Glunok]], an obscure [[Ando]]an arms manufacturer that offered a wide range of modern combat [[slugthrower]]s. It was manufactured as a response to the threat of possible [[Jedi]] invasions during the [[Clone Wars]] after many [[Aqualish]] aligned with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Gadgets" />

The Fyr assault carbine offered a high rate of fire when configured into a compact [[rifle]], which sold well enough to gain distribution deals with major weapon retail outlets such as [[Galladinium]] and [[Loose Cannon Arms]]. Its rate of fire depleted the weapon's ammunition quickly, which required several [[kilogram]]s of spare magazines. When fired, the carbine was capable of producing a severe recoil and muzzle climb that made it difficult for effective control and aim, and as a result, the Fyr became more popular with enthusiasts at target ranges than with actual battlefield combatants.<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fyr assault carbine was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Gadgets" /> It was originally mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[Dangerous Covenants]]'', a [[2014]] sourcebook supplement for Fantasy Flight Games' ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]].<ref name="Dangerous">''[[Dangerous Covenants]]''</ref>
