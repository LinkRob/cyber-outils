'''Casitine Loscar''' was a [[scout]] for the resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[partisans]] while they were based in the [[Catacombs of Cadera]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]]. Using a variety of face-concealing disguises, Loscar listened for valuable intel in the marketplaces of [[Jedha City]] and was present there in [[1 BBY]] when [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] agents [[Jyn Erso]] and [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] were there, ultimately helping to capture them with a group of other partisans following a partisan [[Battle on Jedha|ambush]] of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[convoy]] in [[Tythoni Square]].

==Biography==
===Market eavesdropper===
[[File:LoscarMarket-RO.png|thumb|left|300px|Casitine Loscar served as a scout for the partisans in the markets of Jedha City.]]
Casitine Loscar was a member of the resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[partisans]] during the group's [[Campaigns of Saw Gerrera's Partisans|campaigns]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. While Gerrera was based out of the [[Catacombs of Cadera]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]], Loscar served as a [[scout]] there and eavesdropped for valuable intel while navigating the crowded marketplaces<ref name="Guide" /> of [[Jedha City]] on the moon.<ref name="RO" />

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> he<ref name="Guide" /> was moving through a crowded street near one of the [[city]]'s marketplaces when he walked past<ref name="RO" /> the criminals [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]] and [[Ponda Baba]]<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> as Baba shouted at the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] agent [[Jyn Erso]] for bumping into Evazan. After Erso and her ally [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] moved away from the criminals, Loscar walked behind them into another busy market area and was stood looking at a stall next to the [[Guardians of the Whills|Guardian of the Whills]] [[Chirrut Îmwe]] when Îmwe called out to Erso. As Erso and Îmwe conversed,<ref name="RO" /> Loscar's fellow partisan [[Dafir Toogan]]<ref name="Guide" /> stood beside the scout.<ref name="RO" />

===Ambush and prisoner capture===
Loscar interacted with a container on the edge of [[Tythoni Square]] and then stood among crowds of onlookers in the square as an Imperial [[convoy]] carrying [[kyber crystal]]s passed through. The partisans had an [[Battle on Jedha|ambush]] set up in the square, and many emerged from the surrounding buildings and crowds to overwhelm the Imperials and steal the [[crystal]]s before more [[Jedha Imperial garrison|Imperial forces]] arrived and caused the surviving partisans to flee. Soon after the ambush, Loscar stood with an elderly [[human]] in a separate square in the city as a group of Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s questioned Erso, Andor, and their [[droid]] companion [[K-2SO]] nearby.<ref name="RO" />

[[File:LoscarUbrentoJoyo-RO.png|thumb|right|300px|Casitine Loscar witnessed Chirrut Îmwe attack a group of stormtroopers.]]
Îmwe then entered the square from behind Loscar and proceeded to attack the Imperials, causing Loscar and the other onlookers present to scatter. Loscar soon returned however with a large group of partisans led by the [[Tognath]] [[Benthic]], who took Erso, Andor, Îmwe, and Îmwe's partner, [[Baze Malbus]], captive, with some of the group escorting the [[prisoner]]s back to Gerrera at the catacombs. The Empire then [[Protocol 13|evacuated]] its [[Imperial Military|forces]] from Jedha before the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]] fired upon the moon, [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroying]] both Jedha City and the catacombs.<ref name="RO" /> Many partisans [[Death|perished]] in the blast, although Benthic and some others did survive.<ref name="SW39">[[Star Wars (2015) 39|''Star Wars'' (2015) 39]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Casitine Loscar had light skin.<ref name="RO" />

==Equipment==
While in the markets of Jedha City, Loscar wore a [[Color|brown]] [[poncho]] over two layers of brown [[Clothing|shirts]] and had several face-concealing disguises that he used including a blue and black [[helmet]]<ref name="Guide" /> that matched one worn by at least one other individual in the market.<ref name="RO" /> The scout's fellow partisans nicknamed this helmeted look "Tin Head."<ref name="Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:LoscarDuplicate-Together.png|thumb|left|300px|Additional footage of Casitine Loscar appeared in the "Together" TV spot.]]
Casitine Loscar was created for the [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="RO" /> which was released on [[December 16]], [[2016]].<ref name="YbY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> Concept art including Loscar titled "Jedha Extras Version 5A" was drawn for the film by costume concept artist [[Adam Brockbank]].<ref name="ArtOf">''[[The Art of Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> Prior to the film's release, Loscar was first revealed in the film's first full trailer,<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|frdj1zb9sMY|Rogue One: A Star Wars Story Trailer (Official)|sw_url=video/rogue-one-a-star-wars-story-trailer}}</ref> which was first aired on [[August 11]], 2016.<ref name="TrailerDate">{{SW|url=news/9-highlights-from-the-rogue-one-a-star-wars-story-trailer|text=9 Highlights from the ''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' Trailer|nolive=1}}</ref>

The "Together" TV spot for the film, released on [[November 11]], 2016, showed additional footage of Loscar not used in the film in which he is walking alongside the other individual wearing the same helmet.<ref name="Together">{{SWYouTube|zVJtP2qYMQ0|Rogue One: A Star Wars Story "Together" TV Spot}}</ref> The character was then identified in the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'',<ref name="Guide" /> within a section written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="DawnAuthor">{{WookYouTube|-nqSqVOvfM4|Wookieepedia Interviews - Emily Shkoukani & Jason Fry}}</ref>
