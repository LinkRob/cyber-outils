The '''Deploy the Garrison Squad Pack''' ('''SWP51''') is an expansion for [[Atomic Mass Games]]' miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: Shatterpoint]]''. It was released on [[May 2]], [[2025]], and will feature miniatures of [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]], two [[Coastal defender stormtrooper|shoretroopers]], and three [[death trooper]]s.

==Publisher's summary==
===Online summary===
''The Deploy the Garrison Squad Pack brings the elite forces of the Empire to the front lines. Director Krennic, made popular by'' Rogue One: A Star Wars Story™, ''is joined by the imposing Death Troopers and instantly iconic Shore Troopers.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

''Their gameplay brings exciting abilities to'' Star Wars™: ''Shatterpoint and provides Imperial players with long range and multiple support options. For players looking to grow their collection with the forces of the Empire, this is a fantastic addition.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

===Physical package summary===
{{Quote|Are We blind! Deploy the garrison!|Director Krennic|<ref name="Asmodee" />}}
''The Empire's grip on the galaxy grows tighter as the Death Star is constructed. With Director Krennic overseeing its completion, victory seems all but assured.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

''The ISB Director is joined by elite forces, with Death Troopers and Shoretroopers at his command. The Death Trooper Specialist guards his commander and leads the troops with deadly precision. Together this squad reigns down firepower from above to crush all rebel forces that oppose them.''<ref name="Asmodee" />
