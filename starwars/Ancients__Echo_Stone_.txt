{{Quote|The Echo Stone was so powerful that, long ago, the ancients locked it away inside the Dead Moon of Gothol, to prevent it from ever falling into the hands of the unworthy.|A [[Unidentified narrator|narrator]]|<ref name="Echoes of Fear 1" />}}
The '''ancients''' were a group of people that possessed the [[Echo Stone]]. To prevent the powerful artifact from falling into the wrong hands, the ancients his the stone inside the [[Dead Moon of Gothol]]. To further ensure the safety of the Echo Stone, the ancients encased the entire moon in a mechanism of interlocking metal plates forming a seemingly impenetrable puzzle.<ref name="Echoes of Fear 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ancients were mentioned in the [[The High Republic Adventures – Echoes of Fear 1|first issue]] of the [[2024]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Echoes of Fear]]'', which was written by [[George Mann]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Echoes of Fear 1" /> on [[September 4]], [[2024]]<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3014-600/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures--Echoes-of-Fear-1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures--Echoes of Fear #1}}</ref> as part of the [[Star Wars: The High Republic]] multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Echoes of Fear 1" />
