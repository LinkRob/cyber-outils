The '''Dug Paintwork''' was a bodily configuration owned by the [[droideka]] [[Team Slingshot droideka|member]] of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]] that she could swap for her standard [[Beloved Paintwork|frame]] during the the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This configuration [[Color|silver]] and dark gray frame, with coarsely painted orange and white plating. It also featured glowing orange [[sensor]]s and glowing purple interiors within the [[barrel]]s of its cylindrical [[Limb|arm]] [[cannon]]s. This configuration was designed to loosely resemble the [[Sebulba's Podracer|podracer]] used by the [[Dug]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[pilot]] [[Sebulba]], with the other member of team Slingshot, the [[Ugnaught]] [[Dizzy (Ugnaught)|Dizzy]], even toying with the idea of equipping it with a [[flamethrower]], an idea which he ultimately abandoned.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dug Paintwork was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dug Racer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
