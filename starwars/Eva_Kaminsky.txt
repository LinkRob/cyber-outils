'''Eva Kaminsky''' is an actress who provided her voice to several episodes of the English dubbed version of ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]''.<ref name="The Duel">{{Visions|The Duel (short film)}}</ref> She voiced Villager C in "[[The Duel (short film)|The Duel]]," an [[Unidentified female Jedi (F)|Unidentified female Jedi]] in [[The Village Bride]], [[Niizo]] in "[[The Ninth Jedi]]," and opening narration in "[[Lop & Ochō]]."
