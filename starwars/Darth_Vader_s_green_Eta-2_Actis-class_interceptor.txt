[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] used this green-painted '''[[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor]]''' during his early days as a [[Sith apprentice]], replacing the [[Anakin Skywalker's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|one]] he lost during the [[Battle of Coruscant]].<ref name="ROTS" />

==History==
After leading the [[Operation: Knightfall|massacre]] on the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]], the newly anointed [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] flew this [[starfighter]] from the Temple to [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[Padmé's Apartment|apartment]]. With [[R2-D2]] accompanying him, Vader then traveled to the volcanic [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] to eliminate the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] leaders. The vehicle rested on Mustafar for over a decade, after Vader's battle with [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] left him too wounded to [[pilot]] it off of the planet.<ref name="ROTS" />

[[File:VaderBattlesTIEs-2020DarthVader10.png|thumb|left|215px|Flying the Eta-2 he had last used over twenty years earlier, Darth Vader fought his way to Exegol.]]
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Darth Vader (2020) 8|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 8]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Darth Vader, after escaping from the [[Eye of Webbish Bog]] with a [[Vader's wayfinder|Sith wayfinder]], rested upon his old vehicle to recover. Shortly after, [[Ochi]] of [[Bestoon]] found the wounded Vader and expressed his surprise at Vader's survival.<ref name="Darth Vader 8">[[Darth Vader (2020) 8|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 8]]</ref> Fighting and defeating the [[Droid Crush Pirates of Bestoon]] before capturing Ochi, Vader repaired the Eta-2 using the droid pirates' parts. After Ochi plug in the coordinates, he trapped him in an [[escape pod]], where he [[magnet]]ically attached the said capsule to the bottom of the starfighter and used the [[Sith wayfinder|wayfinder]] to travel to the [[Red Honeycomb Zone]] in order to reach the world of [[Exegol]].<ref name="Darth Vader 9">[[Darth Vader (2020) 9|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 9]]</ref> Once there, he was attacked by forces of the [[Imperial Navy]] deployed to stop him, but Vader used his Eta-2 to take out all attacking [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and encountered a [[Unidentified summa-verminoth subspecies|summa-verminoth subspecies]]. Entering into the Red Zone by flying his Eta-2 into it, Vader experienced [[Force vision]]s of the past and possible future before making it through, crashing onto Exegol. There, he and Ochi, who had been inside a pod the Sith Lord attached to the bottom of the fighter, exited on to the planet's surface, where Vader took control of the mind of the [[summa-verminoth]] using [[the Force]].<ref name="Darth Vader 10">[[Darth Vader (2020) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 10]]</ref>
