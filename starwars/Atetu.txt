'''Atetu''' was a [[Jawa/Legends|Jawa]] who lived on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] during the reign of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. In [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]], shortly before the rescue of the captive former [[Smuggler/Legends|smuggler]] [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] from the [[Jabba's Palace/Legends|palace]] of the [[Crime lord/Legends|crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba the Hutt]], Atetu fellow Jawa [[Tweetu]] spent some [[Time/Legends|time]] with the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] in a run-down Tatooine [[Cantina/Legends|tavern]].

==Behind the scenes==
Tweetu was first mentioned in the revised draft script of the movie [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. However, the character was subsequently cut and did not feature in the film's final screenplay.
