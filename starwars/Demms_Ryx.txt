'''Demms Ryx''' was a [[Volpai]] [[Gender|male]] who [[Bartender|bartended]] at [[The End of the World]] [[Cantina|tavern]] on [[Koda Station]]. Around [[1 ABY]], he met [[flight instructor]] [[Arvel Crynyd]] there, Ryx comforting him about the [[death]] of [[Raf (Rebel)|Raf]], one of Crynyd's former students. Ryx eventually left Koda Station for an [[abandoned spacedock]] to join Crynyd in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. The Volpai served as a [[chef]] for the Alliance and trained to fly in an [[RZ-1T trainer|RZ-1T A-wing]]. He was present at the Alliance's [[Echo Base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]] and cooked for [[rebel pilot]]s on a nameless [[asteroid]] after the base [[Battle of Hoth|fell]] in [[3 ABY]]. He and Crynyd eventually became romantic partners and raised a daughter named [[Starr]]. By [[4 ABY]], the family kept a home on the planet [[Cerea]], safely removed from the [[Galactic Civil War|fighting]].

==Biography==
===First meeting===
{{Quote|I don't believe in anything.''"<br />"''I doubt that very much.|Crynyd and Ryx|audio=Crynyd-Ryx-DontBelieve-FACPOVROTJ.flac|<ref name="FACPOV" />}}
[[File:Arvel-crynyd.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Arvel Crynyd, Demms Ryx's eventual lover]]
During the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Galactic Civil War|war]] against the [[Galactic Empire]], Demms Ryx was a [[bartender]] at [[The End of the World]] [[Cantina|tavern]] on [[Koda Station]].<ref name="FACPOV" /> Around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date">"[[The Impossible Flight of Ash Angels]]" dates Demms Ryx and [[Arvel Crynyd]]'s meeting to around three [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Endor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> Ryx noticed bar patron [[Arvel Crynyd]], who was a [[flight instructor]] for the Rebel Alliance, storm out after learning that one of his former students, [[Raf (Rebel)|Raf]], had [[Death|died]]. Concerned, Ryx followed him outside of the bar. Because of the appearance of Ryx's [[Color|blue]] skin in the light streaming from the tavern, Crynyd told him that he looked like [[hyperspace]] come to life. Ryx laughed before holding out his [[Limb|hand]], drawing Crynyd in, and offering him a [[Alcohol|drink]].<ref name="FACPOV" />

Ryx politely sent [[Dagger (Virgillian)|Dagger]], a [[Gender|woman]] who Crynyd had learned of Raf's death from, on her way. He then had someone cover his shift so he could sit at a table next to Crynyd and talk to him, asking Crynyd about hyperspace. As Crynyd explained how the impossibility of hyperspace made people who willingly harmed each other hard for him to comprehend, Ryx pointed out that was a potentially rebellious rhetoric. Crynyd insisted he was not a rebel and only an instructor and got up to leave. Ryx placed his hand on Crynyd's shoulder and asked him to stay, convincing him to by asking him about his student who had died.<ref name="FACPOV" />

Ryx apologized to Crynyd for his loss, assuring him that [[Teacher|teaching]] Raf mattered and that teaching itself was an act of belief in the possibility of becoming better. Crynyd insisted he did not believe in anything, but Ryx doubted him. When Crynyd pointed out that Ryx was rebellious too, the bartender embraced it, with Crynyd admiring him for being sure of who he was. Though this unsettled Ryx, he joked that they had decided he was hyperspace come to life, Crynyd responding that he could see [[star]]s in his [[eye]]s. The [[Volpai]] told him to follow the stars but to come back to him when he wanted to talk. Ryx waited for Crynyd to return, smiling and offering him a drink when he finally did. Meeting up to have a drink and comfort each other became a habit between them until Crynyd left for an [[abandoned spacedock]] where [[rebel pilot]]s trained.<ref name="FACPOV" />

===Making a difference===
{{Quote|And I won't stay if it makes you uncomfortable. It's just&mdash;You make a difference. I serve drinks. I want to do more.''"<br />"''You don't. You don't make me uncomfortable.|audio=Ryx-Crynyd-Uncomfortable-FACPOVROTJ.flac|Ryx and Crynyd|<ref name="FACPOV" />}}
Ryx eventually hitched a ride to the [[Space station|spacedock]] with only a bag and found Crynyd, who recognized that Ryx had many questions. Ryx revealed that he wanted to plot his own course, explaining that he did not want to become a [[pilot]] but wanted to make a difference to both the cause and Crynyd. Crynyd assured Ryx his presence there did not make him uncomfortable and told him that they could use a [[chef]], joking that constantly [[Diet|eating]] [[Polystarch flour|polystarch]] [[ration pack]]s made him want to jump out of an [[airlock]]. Ryx agreed to the position, saying he could not lose Crynyd after just finding him.<ref name="FACPOV" />

[[File:RZ-1T_-_SW_Rebels_-_SW_Databank.png|thumb|left|260px|Crynyd taught Ryx to fly a RZ-1T trainer starfighter.]]
Crynyd eventually taught Ryx how to fly a [[RZ-1T trainer|RZ-1T]] [[A-wing starfighter]], though Crynyd later moved to the pilot's seat to show Ryx hyperspace. They continued to find each other and comfort each other as [[time]] passed, being together at the Alliance's [[Echo Base]] on<ref name="FACPOV" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Hoth]] and finding each other after the base [[Battle of Hoth|fell]]<ref name="FACPOV" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> After that, the two stayed together on a nameless [[asteroid]], where Ryx cooked for rebels while Crynyd ran supply missions and taught pilots. The two having fallen in love, they eventually had a daughter together named [[Starr]] and made a home together on<ref name="FACPOV" /> the planet<ref name="Cerean">{{Databank|cerean|Cerean}}</ref> [[Cerea]], away from the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|rebel fleet]].<ref name="FACPOV" />

Three [[standard day|days]] before the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="FACPOV" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Crynyd came to visit Ryx and Starr on Cerea. Starr wanted to know more about [[ash angel]]s, as Ryx had told her that they were the only featherless [[bird]]s in existence. Ryx explained that the birds' impossibility made them miraculous and worth holding onto while looking into Crynyd's eyes. Three days later,<ref name="FACPOV" /> Crynyd perished in the Battle of Endor.<ref name="Timelines" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You look like hyperspace come to life.|Crynyd, to Ryx|audio=Crynyd-HyperspaceComeToLife-FACPOVROTJ.flac|<ref name="FACPOV" />}}
Demms Ryx was a Volpai<ref name="FACPOV" /> male<ref name="Email" /> with four arms, blue skin, and stripes on his face and arms. Arvel Crynyd believed Ryx resembled hyperspace come to life and considered him headstrong and rebellious. Ryx was considerate and caring and believed deeply in the power of teaching. Though he self-identified as a rebel, his desire to do more and to matter to both the Alliance's cause and to Crynyd led him to join the Alliance formally. Ryx would always offer comfort and support to Crynyd and go out of his way to be with him, the two finding love and home with each other. Ryx believed that elements of life that seemed impossible were worth holding onto and made [[the galaxy]] miraculous.<ref name="FACPOV" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|I can cook. Quite well, actually. I like to make a difference, too. I want to feel like I matter.|Demms Ryx|audio=Ryx-Cook-FACPOVROTJ.flac|<ref name="FACPOV" />}}
Ryx was a skilled cook and capable bartender. Crynyd believed that Ryx made the best rations in the rebel [[fleet]] and once taught Ryx to fly a RZ-1T A-wing.<ref name="FACPOV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Demms Ryx appeared in flashbacks in the short story "[[The Impossible Flight of Ash Angels]]," written by [[Marieke Nijkamp]] and published in [[2023]] as part of the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="FACPOV" /> Nijkamp confirmed that Ryx's gender was male.<ref name="Email" />
