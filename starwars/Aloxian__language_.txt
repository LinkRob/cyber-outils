'''Aloxian''' was a [[language]] spoken by the [[Aloxian]] [[Gender|male]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Qort]]. When [[Battle at Dol'har Hyde|attempting]] to find the [[Nihil]] [[Krix Kamerat]] on [[Dol'har Hyde]], Qort mumbled his concerns about fellow Padawans [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]] and [[Farzala Tarabal]] in Aloxian to [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref>
