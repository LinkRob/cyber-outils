'''Dave Williams''' is an artist who made rough concept sketches for [[Applause, Inc.|Applause]] dolls as part of the ''[[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire]]'' multimedia project.

Part of his work included some storyboard with events from the saga, in order to be fine-tuned into select poses for the would-be resin figurines. Such proposed scenes and accompanied storyboards, included [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]]'s meeting with [[Xizor/Legends|Prince Xizor]]; [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke]] confronting [[Dash Rendar/Legends|Dash]] on [[Kile]] about [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia]] in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Gall]]. 

Pairings included figurines like "Spirit of Villainy" with Vader and Xizor; and Luke's and Dash's stand against [[Gizz's Gang]].
