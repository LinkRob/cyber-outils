'''Almakian apple pie''' was a type of [[pie]] made with [[Almakian apple]]s on [[Almak]], [[homeworld]] of the [[Leffingite]] [[species]]. It was served at the [[Restaurant|eatery]] known as [[Dex's Diner]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. An [[artist]] was aware of the [[Foodstuff|dish]] being available at [[Dexter Jettster|Dex's]] and included that information in an entry on Leffingites in a [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]] which was restored, expanded, and displayed at the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="Alien Archive" /> at some point no earlier than [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Almakian apple pie was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]'', which was written by [[Nicole Clubb]] and [[Katrina Pallant]].<ref name="Alien Archive" /> The [[Pie/Legends|pie]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was known as [[All-Almakian apple pie]] and was mentioned in "[[Dining at Dex's]]," a [[2009]] [[StarWars.com]] [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] exclusive article written by [[Gregory Walker]].<ref name="DaD">{{Hyperspace|url=fans/hyperspace/source/dexdiner/index.html|text=Dining at Dex's|int=Dining at Dex's}}</ref>
