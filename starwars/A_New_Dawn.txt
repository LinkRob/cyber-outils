'''''A New Dawn''''' was a pro-[[New Republic]] [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] created in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Endor]] by [[Janyor]] of [[Bith (planet)|Bith]]. With the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], the Republic endeavored to supplant the [[Imperial Era|rule]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] with a new era ushering in the restoration of [[democracy]]. To this end, the Republic utilized propaganda to promote public support for its cause.<ref name="Propaganda" />

The background depicted a [[Star|sunrise]] illuminating a [[Color|purple]]-colored horizon over a [[city]] on the Republic [[capital]] [[planet]] of [[Chandrila]]. At the top, the title "A NEW DAWN" was [[Writing|written]] in [[High Galactic]], and at the bottom of the poster, was the [[Sigil of the New Republic|sigil]] of the New Republic and a message&mdash;also written in High [[Galaxy|Galactic]]&mdash;that stated the following: "The fall of Emperor Palpatine brings about a new opportunity for peace." This was followed by a second message stating "JOIN THE NEW REPUBLIC IN CELEBRATING THE BEGINNING OF THIS GLORIOUS NEW ERA!"<ref name="Propaganda" />
