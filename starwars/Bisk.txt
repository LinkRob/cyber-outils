'''Bisk''' was a [[stormtrooper]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. By the [[time]] of the [[Iron Blockade]], Bisk was stationed at an Imperial outpost on [[Nar Hypa]], serving as a [[taskmaster]]. During the blockade, Bisk confronted a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] who had come to steal medical supplies from the outpost on [[Dapp|"Happy" Dapp's]] behalf. The taskmaster was ultimately [[Death|killed]] by the scoundrel.<ref name="Uprising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Taskmaster Bisk first appeared in the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''. He or she was the end boss of the story mission "The First of Four."<ref name="Uprising" />
