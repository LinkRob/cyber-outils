'''Far Linghe''' was a<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Gender|non-binary]]<ref name="Email" /> [[Cathar]] [[Jedi Knight]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. According to [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]], they were well-liked, and Kriss knew them by a reputation which centered on strong [[pilot]]ing and dedication. After the [[Guardian Protocols]] were implemented<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> in [[229 BBY]],<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> they partnered with fellow Jedi Knight [[Aviay]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> Linghe volunteered to be part of the Jedi ground team under Kriss who took part in an [[Assault on Naboo|assault]] on<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Naboo]] which was under [[Occupation of Naboo|occupation]] by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s in their [[Occlusion Zone]] region of [[Realspace|space]] that was behind the [[Stormwall]] barrier which prevented [[hyperspace]] travel.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

During the assault, the Cathar piloted a [[Jedi Vector]] and as the Jedi [[Drift]] reached the [[capital]] [[city]] [[Theed]], noted to the other pilots in the Drift that there were Nihil [[Starship|ships]] at point-six-two. After Aviay, who also took part in the aerial battle, was forced to [[Ejector seat|eject]] from his [[Starfighter|Vector]] after his ship was shot down, Linghe tracked his location within the city. The two later again took part in the aerial battle. After Naboo was freed from Nihil rule, the Cathar made their way to the [[San Tekka clan]] [[San Tekka compound|compound]] and drank a [[Soup|broth]] Master [[Porter Engle]] had made. Later, when the majority of the Jedi and [[Republic Defense Coalition]] forces left the planet, Linghe and Aviay remained on Naboo, believing the world needed a few Jedi to stay and that there was plenty for them to do within the Occlusion Zone.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

==Behind the scenes==
Far Linghe appeared in the [[2024]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> When asked about the usage of singular "they/them" pronouns for Linghe, the author stated that they had intended the usage to indicate a [[Wikipedia:Non-binary gender|non-binary]] gender for the character.<ref name="Email" />
