The '''3-MAL secure comlink''' was a type of versatile [[comlink]] system squeezed into a tiny, pill-shaped package. Popular among the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], the 3-MAL offered military-grade encryption and scanned civil defense, emergency services, and [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] frequencies. Like most comlinks on the market, the 3-MAL could easily synchronize with properly equipped [[droid]]s, [[computer]]s, [[sensor]]s, and [[cyborg]]s, and it could transmit through voice or data. The cost of the 3-MAL secure comlink was 250 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
