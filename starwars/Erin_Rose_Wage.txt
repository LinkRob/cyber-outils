'''Erin Rose Wage''' (born [[February 15]], [[1984]]) is the writer of ''Look and Find:'' ''[[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]'' as well as many more children's books featuring ''Star Wars'', Marvel, Disney, and other licensed characters. Her ''Star Wars'' books feature adaptations of the saga with illustrations by [[Art Mawhinney]], [[Brian Rood]], and others, plus sound effects and voices from the films.<ref name="Bibliothek">{{WebCite|url=https://jedi-bibliothek.de/datenbank/person/erin-rose-wage|text=Erin Rose Wage &mdash; Datenbank|work=Jedi-Bibliothek|dateaccessed=2017-06-27|archiveurl=https://archive.is/fpJ8o}}</ref>

Erin earned Bachelor and Master of Arts degrees in English from DePaul University, and spent a brief stint studying archaeology in Athens, Greece. She lives in Chicago with her spouse, Robert, and their cats, Linda and Motorcycle.
