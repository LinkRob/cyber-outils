'''Brendrak''' was an individual who served as an [[assassin]] in [[Commander]] [[Krix Kamerat]]'s [[Krix Kamerat's cell|cell]] for the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization during the [[High Republic Era]]. After Kamerat [[Desertion|defected]] from the Nihil in [[229 BBY]],<ref name="229 Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic Adventures: Crash Landing]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> he began to work with the [[Corellian]] [[Alys Ongwa|Crash Ongwa]] to [[Death|assassinate]] the [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]].<ref name="THRA 7" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="228 Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|The High Republic Adventures]]'' ([[2023]]) 4 takes place one [[Standard year|year]] and two [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' places in [[230 BBY]]. The implementation of the [[Guardian Protocols]] by the [[Jedi Order]], which ''Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia'' places in [[229 BBY]], took place one week after Starlight Beacon's destruction, according to [[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 1|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 1]]. As ''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4 takes place one year and one week after the implementation of the Guardian Protocols, it must take place in [[228 BBY]]. [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 7|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 7]] takes place shortly after ''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4, so it must take place around 228 BBY.</ref> Brendrak, who was still loyal to Kamerat, was considered to be a candidate to eliminate Ro, but was captured by the organization.<ref name="THRA 7" />

Later on<ref name="THRA 7" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref> [[Corellia]], Ongwa's [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection|crew]] and Kamerat had been notified that Brendrak had been apprehended, which was discussed amid the group. Despite the relief given from an individual over the loss, Ongwa informed the crew about how Brendrak was the last faithful Nihil to Kamerat who had a sliver of chance to terminate Ro. Kamerat then enlightened the crew about how the loss of Brendrak would never have occurred had they gone with his idea, which led the group into an argument. Kamerat departed from Corellia not long after<ref name="THRA 7" /> and eventually conducted his [[Assassination attempt on Marchion Ro|assassination attempt]] on Ro, which ultimately failed and led to his [[death]].<ref name="THRA CaB">''[[The High Republic Adventures: Crash and Burn]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Brendrak was mentioned in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 7|seventh issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="THRA 7" /> on [[July 10]], [[2024]]<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-800/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-7|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #7}}</ref> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia initiative's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="THRA 7" />
