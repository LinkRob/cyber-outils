'''Andrea Mutti''' is an [[Wikipedia:Italians|Italian]] artist. His ''[[Star Wars]]'' work includes interior art for the [[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic: War|''Knights of the Old Republic: War'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries and the ongoing [[Star Wars: Lost Tribe of the Sith—Spiral|''Lost Tribe of the Sith: Spiral'']] series, both from [[Dark Horse Comics]].
