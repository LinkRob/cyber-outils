'''AppSci SaberCats''' was a [[grav-ball]] team fielded by the [[Junior Academy of Applied Sciences]]. Center striker [[Zare Leonis]], fullback [[Beck Ollet]], and kicker [[Merei Spanjaf]] all played for the team.<ref name="Edge" />

The team was named after the [[sabercat]], a predatory [[feline]] which was found on [[Lothal]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>
