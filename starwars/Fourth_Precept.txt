The '''Fourth Precept''' was a [[Poet|poem]] that was featured in the ''[[Rammahgon]]'', one of the [[Sacred Jedi texts]]. The poem described [[Deity|gods]] fighting in a primeval [[universe]]. The front cover of the ''Rammahgon'' had an "[[eye]]" that was from the poem.<ref name="TROS VD" />
