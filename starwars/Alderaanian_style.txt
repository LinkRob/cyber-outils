The '''Alderaanian style''' was a distinct style of architecture that originated from the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]].<ref name="OR planets">{{EncyclopediaCite|Bespin and Other Planets in the Outer Rim}}</ref> It notably made heavy use of [[Color|white]] [[synthstone]].<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> The influence of the Alderaanian architecture could be seen on buildings everywhere across [[the galaxy]], including [[Bespin]], [[Coruscant]], [[Espirion]], [[Lothal]], and [[Naboo]].<ref name="HC 34">{{HelmetCollectionCite|34|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> [[Lord]] [[Ecclessis Figg]], whose [[Ecclessis Figg's wife|wife]] [[Homeworld|hailed]] from Alderaan, took inspiration from the Alderaanian style when he built [[Cloud City]].<ref name="OR planets" />
