'''Fraya''' was a [[Human]]. During the [[Cold War]], they were one of the candidates to join [[Han Solo]]'s [[Han Solo's shipping company|shipping company]] tested by him on the [[planet]] [[Azen]]. The test required recovering the ''[[Eravana]]'s'' [[hyperdrive]] from the top of a mountain. After Fraya's [[blaster]] got stolen by a [[Unidentified bird (Azen)|bird]], they were the first contestant to reach the hyperdrive, but was unable to stop in time and fell off the cliff. [[Varond Jelik]], who was right behind them, grabbed the crate containing the component and began heading back down, leaving them hanging from a branch.<ref name="LEGO" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fraya appeared in "[[The Solo Test]]," a comic written by [[Martin Fisher]] and featured in the magazine [[LEGO Star Wars 32|''LEGO Star Wars'' 32]].<ref name="LEGO" />
