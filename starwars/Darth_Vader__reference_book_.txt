"'''Darth Vader'''" is the first booklet of [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']], a series of [[canon]] reference booklets published in [[Wikipedia:French language|French]] by Altaya on [[August 5]], [[2020]]<ref name="Altaya" /> and published in [[Wikipedia:Spanish language|Spanish]] by [[De Agostini]] on [[August 25]], 2020.<ref name="De Agostini" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Formerly known as Anakin Skywalker, Darth Vader arises from the remains of the latter, rescued by Emperor Palpatine from the lava of Mustafar. Totally dominated by the dark side, Vader became the armed right arm of his master, sowing terror in the galaxy with his famous black armor and his terrible powers drawn from the Force. He quickly became the symbol of the tyrannical power of the Galactic Empire.''

==Continuity==
*The book gives inaccurate measurements for [[Darth Vader's armor|Vader's armor]]: The height is given as 2.02 [[meter]]s and the weight is given as 136 [[kilogram]]s; the correct height is 2.03 meters, and the correct weight is 120 kilos.<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>
