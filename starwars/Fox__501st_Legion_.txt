'''Fox''', also identified by his [[Cloning|clone]] designation '''CT-0000/1010''', was a loyal [[clone trooper]] who fought as a [[soldier]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] throughout the length of the [[Clone Wars]]. By [[19 BBY]], he was serving as a [[Clone Sergeant]] in the [[501st Legion]] on the Republic's [[capital]], [[Coruscant]]. After [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] issued [[Order 66]], Fox was deployed during [[Operation: Knightfall]].

Fox's [[Squad|unit]] was assigned to guard the temple's exit and entry points, and his [[commanding officer]], [[Clone Commander]] [[Appo]], ordered [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] to leave when the senator arrived to investigate. Before Organa could comply, the clones with Appo and Fox were attacked by [[Padawan]] [[Zett Jukassa]], who struck down Appo during his escape attempt. Fox's senior officer, [[Clone Lieutenant]] [[Bow (clone trooper)|Bow]], then [[Death|fatally]] shot the Padawan, but having grown weary of fighting during the war, Fox ordered his men to let Organa flee the scene.

==Biography==
===A clone on Coruscant===
{{Quote|There's been a rebellion, sir. Don't worry, the situation is under control.|CC-1119, to Bail Organa|<ref name="ROTS" />}}
[[File:Knightfall2.png|thumb|left|300px|CT-0000/1010 "Fox" served in Darth Vader's 501st Legion during the siege of the Jedi Temple.]]
CT-0000/1010 was the designation of a [[Gender|male]] [[Cloning|clone]]<ref name="Force Collection">{{ForceCollection|Clone Sergeant Fox|star=4}}</ref> of the [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], who was created on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] in order to serve as a [[clone trooper]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Clone Databank" /> He served throughout the entirety of the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] as part of the [[501st Legion]], spending most of the war stationed on [[Coruscant]],<ref name="Force Collection" /> the [[capital]] of the Republic.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>

By the [[Standard year|year]] [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|III}} to [[19 BBY]].</ref> CT-0000/1010 had earned the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Clone Sergeant]] and was known by the name Fox.<ref name="Force Collection" /> In that year, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] issued [[Order 66]] to all clone forces, tasking them with [[Death|killing]] all members of the [[Jedi Order]]. The [[sergeant]] was among the 501st forces led by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] in the the siege of the Jedi Temple on Coruscant, [[Operation: Knightfall]].<ref name="ROTS" />

===Sparing the senator===
{{Quote|I'm sorry, sir. It's time for you to leave.|CC-1119, to Bail Organa|<ref name="ROTS" />}}
[[File:Zett_Jukasa_Order_66.png|thumb|right|300px|Fox and his soldiers killed Padawan Zett Jukassa, whose death was witnessed by Senator Bail Organa.]]
Fox's [[Squad|unit]] was assigned the duty of guarding the entrances and exits to the [[Jedi temple|temple]].<ref name="Force Collection" /> The [[Standard day|day]] after the attack,<ref name="French">''[[Revenge of the Sith - The Film Novel]]''</ref> he was present with several other troopers as well as<ref name="ROTS" /> [[Clone Commander]] [[Appo]]<ref name="HC16">{{HelmetCollectionCite|16|Helmets}}</ref> and [[Clone Lieutenant]] [[Bow (clone trooper)|Bow]]<ref name="Master of Evil">''[[Master of Evil]]''</ref> on one of the temple's landing pads when [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] arrived in a [[landspeeder]] to investigate what was going on. Appo claimed the [[Jedi]] had betrayed the Republic and then swiftly ordered Organa to leave, but as the senator did so the Jedi<ref name="ROTS" /> [[Padawan]] [[Zett Jukassa]]<ref name="Force Collection" /> attacked Appo and his men.<ref name="ROTS" />

As the [[commander]]<ref name="Force Collection" /> and those around him were cut down, the remaining clones opened fire on the young Jedi and a bolt from Bow's [[blaster]] dealt the killing blow.<ref name="Master of Evil" /> Organa quickly fled the scene in his [[speeder]] as the clones prepared to fire on him too, but as Appo was wounded, the sergeant ordered his men to stand down.<ref name="ROTS" /> A year later in [[18 BBY]],<ref name="18 BBY">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']] takes place one [[Standard year|year]] after the events of [[Order 66]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, ''Ahsoka'' must be set in [[18 BBY]].</ref> Organa still experienced [[Dream|nightmares]] about Fox killing Jukassa. The dreams varied, with the senator sometimes saving the Padawan and sometimes being killed by the clones alongside him.<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Fox was a loyal soldier throughout the entirety of the Clone Wars, but by the war's end he had grown tired of the fighting. Having to kill Jukassa during Order 66 was the final straw, causing him to lose all desire for battle and to give the order to allow Organa to flee.<ref name="Force Collection" /> As a clone of Fett, Fox stood 1.83 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Clone Databank" />

==Equipment==
Fox wore [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] with the blue markings of the 501st Legion and used a [[DC-15A blaster rifle]].<ref name="ROTS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fox appeared in the [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref name="ROTS" /> which was released on [[May 19]], [[2005]].<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> His scene was filmed on a raised set against a blue screen on Monday, [[July 28]], [[2003]] on Stage 5 at [[Shepperton Studios]].<ref name="Making">''[[The Making of Star Wars Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref> Like all clones in the film, he was voiced by [[Temuera Morrison]]<ref name="ROTS" /> and generated using CGI.<ref name="Making" />

The sergeant was not named in the film,<ref name="ROTS" /> but was identified in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile card game]] ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'',<ref name="Force Collection" /> which initially launched in [[2013]]. Although the launch of ''Force Collection'' predates<ref name="Force Collection release">{{SW|url=news/announcing-star-wars-force-collection|text=Announcing Star Wars: Force Collection|nolive=1}}</ref> the ''Star Wars'' canon reset of [[2014]],<ref name="Legends">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Leland Chee]] confirmed that the game was kept updated to fit with canon.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|holocronkeeper|status/846807700482666496|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=I'm not gonna go through the trouble of changing them and I don't have any reason to believe currently that anyone would. [&hellip;] On the off-chance that we DID have a current need to change any of these, we would have for Force Collection.|archivedate=20210505080831}} &mdash; [[Leland Chee]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] has confirmed that [[in-universe]] information from ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' conforms with ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]].</ref> The designation and nickname originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first used in the article "[[Guide to the Grand Army of the Republic]]," which was written by [[Karen Traviss]] and [[Ryan Kaufman]] and published in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' [[Star Wars Insider 84|84]]<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|84|Guide to the Grand Army of the Republic}}</ref> on [[September 26]], 2005.<ref name="SW">{{SWArchive|url=community/fanclub/mag/news20050913.html|text=The Ultimate Clone Guide in ''Star Wars Insider'' #84}}</ref> Fox also appeared prior to the release of film in Legends in the [[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (novelization)|novelization]] of ''Revenge of the Sith'', which was written by [[Matthew Stover]]<ref name="Novel">[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (novelization)|''Star Wars Episode III: Revenge of the Sith'' novelization]]</ref> and released on [[April 2]], 2005.<ref name="Cargo Bay">{{RandomHouseOld|url=author/results.pperl?authorid=30059|text=Matthew Woodring Stover}}</ref>

Fox's card from ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' establishes that it was him who fatally shot Zett Jukassa,<ref name="Force Collection" /> however, the [[2025]] [[canon]] novel ''[[Master of Evil]]'' credits the killing blow to Lieutenant Bow.<ref name="Master of Evil" /> As the novel is the more recent source and has hierarchy over reference material, this article assumes that ''Master of Evil'' is the correct source.
