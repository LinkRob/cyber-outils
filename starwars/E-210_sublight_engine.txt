The '''E-210 sublight engine''' was a model of [[sublight engine]]. In [[3 ABY]], the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', an [[EML-850 light freighter]], was equipped with E-210 sublight engines when the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] stole it from the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Director]] [[Sliro Barsha]]'s [[Westhill Palace]] in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. Vess [[pilot]]ed the stolen [[freighter]] to the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] and carried out modifications on it there.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
Vess eventually upgraded the ''Trailblazer'' and replaced its E-210s with faster [[E-310 sublight engine]]s and then even faster [[E-410 sublight engine]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
E-210 sublight engines appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
