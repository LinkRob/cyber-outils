"'''Database'''" is a department within ''[[Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin]]'', a magazine published quarterly by [[Panini]] Verlag. The database is written by editors from [[Jedipedia]].<ref name="Magazine 84">[[Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin 84|''Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin'' 84]]</ref> Following the release of the physical issues, Jedipedia is allowed to host the articles its editors have written on its own website,<ref name="Jedipedia">{{WebCite|url=https://www.jedipedia.net/wiki/Jedipedia:OSWM|text=''Jedipedia'' in the ''Official Star Wars Magazine''|work=[[Jedipedia]]|archivedate=20240211223039}}</ref> albeit without images and the captions to those images<ref name="Jedipedia 104">{{WebCite|url=https://www.jedipedia.net/wiki/Jedipedia:OSWM_104|text=Groß machen Kriege niemanden|work=[[Jedipedia]]|archivedate=20240513123227}}</ref> seen in the physical release.<ref name="TOM 104">{{GermanFanCite|104|Database|Wars Not Make One Great}}</ref>
