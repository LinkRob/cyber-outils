"'''A Moonlight Stroll In The Country...'''" was a short vignette included in the [[1994]] sourcebook ''[[Creatures of the Galaxy]]'', published by [[West End Games]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. It was presented as an [[in-universe]] entry from the personal diary of [[Commander/Legends|Commander]] [[Lajar Weqill]].<ref name="Creatures">''[[Creatures of the Galaxy]]''</ref>
