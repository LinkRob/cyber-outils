A [[Gender|male]] individual was the '''uncle''' of the [[Rutian]] [[Twi'lek]] [[Jedi]] [[Aayla Secura]]. At some point, the uncle was [[Death|murdered]], setting Secura on a path towards the [[dark side of the Force]] as she hunted the murderer. However, thanks to her own reflection and the advice of [[Yoda]], she returned to the [[Light side of the Force|light side]].<ref name="Aayla Jedi Card" />
