A '''camera''',<ref name="Battle to the End">''[[Battle to the End]]''</ref> also known simply as a '''cam''',<ref name="EOW">{{StoryCite|story=End of Watch|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> was a type of device that captured light to generate images or visual footage. The [[Spectres]] rebel cell's [[VCX-100 light freighter|starship]] ''[[Ghost]]''{{'s}} [[holotransmitter]] was fitted with a camera. In [[4 BBY]], [[Ezra Bridger]] and his fellow Spectres delivered a speech using the holotransmitter's camera which they beamed to the [[Imperial Communications Center]] on [[Lothal]].<ref name="Battle to the End" /> [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[prison]] cells were often equipped with cameras to monitor activity.<ref name="secret">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref>
