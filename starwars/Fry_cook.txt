A '''fry cook''' was a type of [[Chef|cook]] who prepared [[Foodstuff|meals]] over a grill.<ref name="Way, Way">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}}</ref>

During the [[New Republic Era]], [[Unidentified fry cook|one such cook]] was present on [[Borgo Prime]] and tended to the [[Youngling|children]] [[Wim]] and [[Neel]].<ref name="Way, Way" /> Around the same [[time]], the [[Ardennian]] fry cook<ref name="Trailer article">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-mandalorian-and-grogu-trailer-key-art-2|text=Pedro Pascal Stars in New Trailer for ''The Mandalorian and Grogu''}}</ref> [[Hugo Durant]]<ref name="OST">{{OST|Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu (Original Motion Picture Soundtrack)}}</ref> ran a [[food pod]]<ref name="Roy Choi">{{SWYouTube|fwqguHvfG2Y|Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu &#124; Cooking with Roy Choi &#124; In Theaters May 22}}</ref> named [[Hugo's]]<ref name="Official Trailer" /> on [[Shakari]].<ref name="Shakari">{{WebCite|url=https://www.polygon.com/new-star-wars-planet-shakari-mandalorian-grogu-movie|text=The newest Star Wars planet is a 1920s gangster world ripped from a Martin Scorsese movie|author=Jake Kleinman|date=2026-04-25|archivedate=20260426071059}}</ref> Durant was approached by the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]], who was searching for information about a [[Hutt]].<ref name="Official Trailer" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fry cooks were introduced in the television series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'', specifically its second episode, "[[Way, Way Out Past the Barrier]]," which was released on [[Disney+]] on [[December 2]], [[2024]] as part of a two-episode premiere.<ref name="Early">{{SW|url=news/skeleton-crew-premiere-date|text=''Star Wars: Skeleton Crew'' Will Debut Early|date=2024-11-26}}</ref> An image released by [[The Walt Disney Company|The Walt Disney Studios]] on [[Wikipedia:Getty Images|Getty Images]] [[November 11]] that year first mentioned the role as "frycook" and "Frycook."<ref name="Getty" />
