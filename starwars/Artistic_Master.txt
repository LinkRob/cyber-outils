'''Artistic Master''' was a title.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> Around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' take place around [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Chiss]] [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," traveled to the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] [[Primea]]. On Primea, Thrawn claimed that he was the [[chief curator]] of the [[Nunech Art Collection]], using the alias of "Artistic Master Svorno."<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
The title of Artistic Master appeared in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
