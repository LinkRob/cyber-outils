The '''Cobaltia Sea''' was a [[Ocean|sea]] on<ref name="Glass" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Glass" /> Around [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]'' take place one [[Standard week|week]] after the [[Funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn|funeral]] of [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]].</ref> at a feast on the planet [[Metagos]], the [[gangster]] [[Chulok]]'s personal [[chef]] [[Ru Annlord]] served an [[Alderaanian Seafood Extravaganza]], which he explained was a platter of diverse seafood that had been caught in the Cobaltia Sea.<ref name="Glass" /> In [[1 BBY]], Alderaan was [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]].<ref name="Destruction" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Cobaltia Sea was mentioned in the [[2024]] novel ''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]'', written by [[Steven Barnes]].<ref name="Glass" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Cobaltia Sea/Legends|Cobaltia Sea]] was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars (2013) 2|second issue]] of the [[2013]] [[Star Wars (2013)|''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series, written by [[Brian Wood]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 2">[[Star Wars (2013) 2|''Star Wars'' (2013) 2]]</ref> on [[February 13]], 2013.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/53942/star_wars_2013_2|text=Star Wars (2013) #2}}</ref>
