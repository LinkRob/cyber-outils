This [[Gender|female]] individual was the '''mother''' of [[Enfys Nest]] and belonged to a long line of women who fought to protect the galaxy.<ref name="SoloStorybook">[[Solo: A Star Wars Story Read-Along Storybook and CD|''Solo: A Star Wars Story'' Read-Along Storybook and CD]]</ref> She was leader of the [[pirate]] gang known as the [[Cloud-Riders]] and wore a distinctive set of armor and a mask that she later passed down to her daughter.<ref name="Women of the Galaxy" /> At some point, she told Nest about a band of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] who went to a peaceful [[planet]] for a resource they coveted, they kept coming back until one [[time]], the people resisted and in retaliation the mercenaries cut off the [[tongue]] of every man, woman and child. Eventually the mercenaries became [[Crimson Dawn]], one of many criminal syndicates who committed unspeakable crimes across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Solo" />

After revealing herself to [[Han Solo]], [[Tobias Beckett]], [[Chewbacca]], and [[Qi'ra]], Nest told the group the tale her [[Parent|mother]] had told her. After being questioned by Beckett about what she would use [[Raid on Kessel|stolen]] [[coaxium]] from [[Kessel]] for, Nest said she would use it for what her mother would have used it for if she had survived, to fight back.<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
Enfys Nest's mother was first mentioned in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" /> Information regarding Enfys' mother was told by the film's directors [[Phil Lord]] and [[Christopher Miller]] to Enfys Nest actress [[Erin Kellyman]] on her approach to her character along other facts of her character's past so she could understand why Nest became the person she was in ''Solo''.<ref name="Erin's Interview Chicago">{{StarWarsShow|ga6Z0Xr-t68|Erin Kellyman Takes The Stage At SWCC 2019 &#124; The Star Wars Show Live!}}</ref>
