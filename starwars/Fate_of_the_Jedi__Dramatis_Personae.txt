'''''Fate of the Jedi: Dramatis Personae''''' is a downloadable reference book that serves as a preview to the [[Star Wars: Fate of the Jedi|''Fate of the Jedi'']] novel series. It was published by [[Del Rey]] books and made available on [[StarWars.com]] on [[March 18]], [[2009]] as a free PDF. Including a twelve-page excerpt from ''[[Fate of the Jedi: Outcast]]'', the first volume of ''Fate of the Jedi'', it is twenty-three pages long.
It was included in the paperback edition of ''[[The Old Republic: Deceived]]''.

The guide includes a brief synopsis of the state of [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] in [[43 ABY]] and short biographies of important characters from the forthcoming series. Licensed illustrations by various artists, all collected from previously published ''[[Star Wars]]'' works, are included throughout. The biographies are as follows:
