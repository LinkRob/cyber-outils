During the [[Imperial Era|age]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Utani Xane]] acted as the curator of [[Quarantine World III]], the location of the [[Triple-Zero protocol personality matrix]].<ref name="Darth Vader 3">[[Darth Vader (2015) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 3]]</ref>
