'''Douglass Bowerbank Robinson''' ([[February 8]], [[1930s|1930]] &ndash; [[December 16]], [[2021]])<ref name="Facebook" /> was an actor and stuntman who portrayed a [[Cold weather assault trooper|Snowtrooper]] and a [[Stormtrooper]] in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': ''Episode V The Empire Strikes Back'']].
