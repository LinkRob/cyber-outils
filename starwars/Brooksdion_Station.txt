'''Brooksdion Station''' was a [[New Republic]] [[space station]] in the [[Lost Souls system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Cademimu sector]]. The [[Mathieson's protocol droid|protocol droid]] of [[Admiral]] [[Mathieson]] was [[Thief|stolen]] from the station, and [[Rapier Squadron]], [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Poe Dameron]], were sent to find the culprit's [[starship]].<ref name="Age of Resistance" />
