The '''Clone Wars reenactment movement,''' or '''Clone War reenactment''', was the development of reenactments during the [[Imperial Era]] featuring battles from the [[Clone Wars]] of [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]]. The reenactments featured the armed forces of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Proclamation of the New Order|predecessor]], the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]], and its defunct enemy, the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederacy]], represented by [[Species|organics]] and [[droid]]s respectively.

The reenactments were particularly fashionable with wealthy beings from the [[Core Worlds]]. One [[planet]] from that region, the pacifist [[Alderaan]], was home to a surprising number of Clone Wars reenactors, including [[Kier Domadi]]. In [[35 ABY]], the historian [[Beaumont Kin]] referenced the reenactment movement and one of its [[Toln Yurak|contemporary supporters]] in his book ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', going on to discuss why the Galactic Empire tolerated such callbacks to the [[Republic Era]].

==History==
{{Quote|It was not a great leap to go from the Republic venerating the military during the Clone Wars to the Empire using it as cover to expand its armed forces while celebrating them at the same time.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise and Fall"/>}}
[[File:Beaumont Kin.png|left|thumb|Beaumont Kin wrote about the Clone Wars reenactment movement.]]
The Clone Wars reenactment movement,<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> or Clone War reenactment,<ref name="Leia">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> was the trend during the [[Imperial Era]] of amateurs reenacting famous battles from the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> of [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> This activity was particularly prominent within the [[Core Worlds]]. Participants would dress up as [[clone trooper]]s and engage in mock battle with de-militarized [[droid]]s, thus representing the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s predecessor, the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]], and the [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]] of its defunct enemy, the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederacy]], respectively. Despite [[Alderaan]]'s history of pacifism, the [[planet]] was home to a sizeable group of Clone Wars reenactors.<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> A notable example was [[Alderaanian]] [[Apprentice Legislator]] [[Kier Domadi]], who intended to become a Clone Wars [[historian]] and thus became involved in such reenactments<ref name="Leia" /> by [[3 BBY]].<ref name="LPoADate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> He typically played the role of a [[clone SCUBA trooper]] for aquatic battle recreations.<ref name="Leia" />

The reenactments were a rare instance of the Galactic Empire allowing Republic symbolism to be publicly displayed. In an interview with ''[[Clone Wars Quarterly]]'', [[Colonel]] [[Toln Yurak]] stated their belief that the reenactments provided young people with training that would be beneficial to them in serving the Empire.<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the historian [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned the reenactments in<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> his<ref name="TROS:TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> book ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''. While uncertain as to how prominent opinions like Yurak's were, Kin referenced the interview while discussing his thoughts on why the Empire tolerated the reenactments. He speculated that they were allowed because they helped translate public support from the [[Republic Military]] to the [[Imperial Army]] as well as keeping the resources and interest of the affluent upper classes engaged in a diversion that could not undermine the regime.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clone War reenactment was first mentioned in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young-adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="Leia" /> It was first identified as the Clone Wars reenactment movement in [[Chris Kempshall]]'s [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />
