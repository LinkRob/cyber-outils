'''''Coffret Star Wars 2 : Les Heros''''' is a box set containing two [[Wikipedia:French language|French]] young-readers books, ''[[Les Jedi]]'' and [[Luke Skywalker (children's book)|''Luke Skywalker'']], that were written by [[Sabine Boccador]]. It was published on [[October 14]], [[2016]] by [[Fleurus]] as part of the series ''[[La grande imagerie Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Amazon" />

==Publisher's summary==
''In this collection dedicated to the heroes of Star Wars, children will discover the followers of the light side of the Force, the Jedi and their allies. The approach to the different characters and situations will help them better understand the history of this famous saga. In this collection devoted to Star Wars characters, children will discover Luke Skywalker, the son of Anakin Skywalker. How will this young man, originally a simple farm boy, help save the galaxy from the Empire's terrifying dictatorship? That's what this book reveals, covering the adventures of Luke, his allies and his enemies.<ref name="Amazon" />''
