The '''Alderaan Trade Alliance''' was an organization active during the [[Imperial Era]] that shared a name with<ref name="Daughter" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|111}}</ref> [[Axis network|rebel]] leader [[Luthen Rael]], in order to disguise [[Luthen Rael's Fondor Haulcraft|his]] [[V-21.1 Chevlex light haulcraft|Fondor Haulcraft]] from an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Segra-Milo Imperial patrol|patrol]] that apprehended him above the planet [[Segra Milo]], used an Alderaan Trade Alliance [[Transponder code|transponder ID]]. Rael's [[Fondor Droid Mod]] generated the ID 12912505, which successfully passed a check performed by a [[Unidentified male pit crew member|crew member]] aboard the Imperial [[Cantwell-class Arrestor Cruiser|''Cantwell''-class Arrestor Cruiser]]. Although Rael's [[Starship|ship]] was identified as belonging to the Alderaan Trade Alliance, the Imperial [[Captain]] [[Elk]] nonetheless attempted to board the vessel, leading to Rael [[Skirmish above Segra Milo|battling]] the Imperial forces and escaping.<ref name="Daughter" />
