The '''Corson Prime system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Tharin sector]]'s [[Periphery]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square S-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Corson Prime]].<ref name="Origin" />

==Behind the scenes==
