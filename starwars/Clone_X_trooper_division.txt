A classified<ref name="Extraction">{{TBB|Extraction}}</ref> '''division''' of [[clone trooper]]s was active within the [[Advanced Science Division]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] early into its [[Imperial Era|reign]]. The [[division]] trained the [[trooper]]s into specialized operatives known as [[Clone X trooper]]s. During the process, their identities are erased and underwent conditioning, and the few that survived the process were fanatically loyal to the Empire. [[Clone X]], [[CX-1]], [[CX-2]], [[Sniper CloneX3]], [[Sniper CloneX4]], [[Sniper CloneX5]], and [[Unidentified Clone X trooper|another trooper]] were all part of the division. Rogue [[clone commando]] [[Crosshair]] was inducted, but their regimen proved unsuccessful due to Crosshair's unique clone deficiencies. The division of Clone X troopers were utilized for [[Royce Hemlock's deep undercover program]].<ref name="Infiltration" /> Eventually, a batch of modified and augmented clone assassins were created, [[CX squad]], and were deployed during the [[skirmish on Wayland]].<ref name="TCHA" />
