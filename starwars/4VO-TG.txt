'''4VO-TG''', or '''Teegee''', was a<ref name="Cataclysm" /> [[Gender|non-binary]]<ref name="Email" /> [[astromech droid]]. He was repaired by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Enya Keen]] in [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Cataclysm" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I did sneak in a nonbinary character into Cataclysm. The droid 4VO-TG (who is one of my absolute favorite characters in the book) is named after one of my children, who is nonbinary. I asked my child (who uses they/them pronouns) what they would like Teegee's pronouns to be, and they said "he/him" felt right.|Lydia Kang, about 4VO-TG|<ref name="Email" />}}
4VO-TG appeared in [[The High Republic: Cataclysm|''Cataclysm'']], a novel written by [[Lydia Kang]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Cataclysm" /> Phase II and published on [[April 4]], [[2023]]. Prior to its release, a mention of the droid was included in a [[StarWars.com]] preview for the novel on [[March 3]] of that year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-cataclysm-excerpt|text=The Jedi Arrive at Dalna in ''The High Republic: Cataclysm'' - Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref> Kang considered the droid one of her favorite characters in ''Cataclysm'', indicating that he was written to be [[Wikipedia:Non-binary gender|non-binary]]. When developing the character, the author asked her child&mdash;whom she named the droid after&mdash;what they would prefer 4VO-TG's pronouns to be, and they said using "he/him" felt right.<ref name="Email" />
