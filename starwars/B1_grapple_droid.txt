The '''B1 grapple droid''' was a type of [[B1-series battle droid]] used by the [[Separatist Droid Army]] during the [[Clone Wars]], a [[The galaxy|galactic]] conflict between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and the [[Galactic Republic]]. Like its [[B2 grapple droid|B2 counterpart]], the B1 grapple [[droid]] was armed with grapple [[claw]]s for [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]].<ref name="Galactic Defense" />

B1 grapple droids were [[Class four droid|fourth-class droids]]<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia note" /> crafted in the likeness of their [[Geonosian]] designers.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia note" /> The droid [[trooper]]s stood at a height of 1.93 [[meter]]s<ref name="Databank note" /> and possessed [[Color|white]]-and-green plating, black [[photoreceptor]]s,<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> [[comlink booster pack]]s,<ref name="Ultimate note" /> and a simple [[vocabulator]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia note" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Call in Melee troops to intercept the enemy.|Description of melee units in ''Star Wars: Galactic Defense''|<ref name="Star Wars: Galactic Defense">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Plasma.jpg|thumb|left|125px|B1 grapple droids resembled the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity's plasma battle droids.]]
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], B1 grapple droids appeared in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]'', which was released in [[2014]] and retired in [[2016]].<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[B1 grapple droid/Legends|B1 grapple droids]] first appeared in the [[2000]] video game ''[[Star Wars: Episode I Jedi Power Battles]]''.<ref name="Jedi Power Battles">[[Star Wars: Episode I Jedi Power Battles|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Jedi Power Battles'']]</ref> While ''Star Wars: Galactic Defense'' only referred to B1 grapple droids as "Melee troops,"<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> and the plating colors of the droids resembled those of the [[plasma battle droid]]s from ''Star Wars: Episode I Jedi Power Battles'',<ref name="Jedi Power Battles" /> the units were identified as B1 grapple droids in the ''Star Wars: Galactic Defense'' launch trailer, although the trailer mislabeled a graphic of an [[Imperial Heavy Trooper]] as such.<ref name="Trailer">{{SW|url=video/star-wars-galactic-defense-launch-trailer|text=Star Wars: Galactic Defense - Launch Trailer}}</ref>

In ''Star Wars: Galactic Defense'', B1 grapple droids were a type of melee unit that appeared in the "[[Dark side of the Force|Dark Side]]" missions. Players could deploy them to defend against waves of enemy units, such as [[clone trooper]]s and [[Jedi]]. The droids would be deployed in groups of three, and they could be upgraded into B2 grapple droids.<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> In the [[2018]] activity book ''[[Star Wars Droids Mad Libs: World's Greatest Word Game]]'', a word game prompt titled "Prepare For Battle" mentions B1 grapple droids and leaves spaces in a sentence for the player to write in words describing how the droids behaved in battles and used their grapples in combat.<ref name="Mad Lib">''[[Star Wars Droids Mad Libs: World's Greatest Word Game]]''</ref>
