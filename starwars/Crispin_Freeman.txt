'''Crispin Freeman''' is an actor who voiced [[Lieutenant]] [[Nolan]] in the [[The Bad Batch Season 2|second season]] of the [[2021]] [[Disney+]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''.<ref name="The Outpost" /> Freeman is best known for his work in the anime industry, with notable roles being Itachi Uchiha in Naruto, Shizuo Heiwajima in Durarara, Gyomei Himejima in Demon Slayer, and Friede in Pokémon Horizons: The Series.
