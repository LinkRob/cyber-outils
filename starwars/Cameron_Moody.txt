'''Cameron Moody''' is an American composer who provided additional music and conducting services for the [[2024]] [[Disney+]] original series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''.<ref name="Website" /> It was the composer's childhood dream to write music for ''Star Wars''.<ref name="InstaPost">{{Instagram|username=cameronmoody_|url=p/C7zU5wkPrsa/?img_index=1|name=[[Cameron Moody]]|date=2024-06-04|quote=One year, many hours, and thousands of notes later, today is #TheAcolyte day! I am SO excited for the release of the first 2 episodes of this show today! The biggest thank you goes to @_michaelabels for having me on the team, and fulfilling a childhood dream of writing music for Star Wars! 🤯🤯 Catch the premiere today on Disney+|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/hc4Rh}}</ref> He later attended [[Wikipedia:New York University|New York University]] to study music composition and screen scoring before becoming the youngest composer to ever become a fellow in the [[Wikipedia:NBCUniversal|NBC/Universal]] Composers Initiative at just 21 years old in 2024.<ref name="Website" />
