A '''fleet''' was under the command of [[Imperial Navy]] [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]] early during the [[Imperial Era]]. The [[fleet]] participated in the Imperial operation to withdraw all valuable assets from [[Kamino]], and later [[Attack on Kamino|destroyed]] major cities and facilities, including [[Tipoca City]]. The fleet consisted of more than three [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]],<ref name="Return to Kamino" /> including Rampart's [[flagship]], the Venator designated [[VZ-114]].<ref name="Truth and Consequences" /> The [[clone navigation officer]]s aboard Ramparts vessels sported the insignia of the [[Open Circle Fleet]].<ref name="Return to Kamino" />
