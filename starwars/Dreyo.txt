'''Dreyo''' was [[Moran (Koboh)|Moran's]] [[Smuggler|smuggling]] partner on [[Coruscant]]. Once the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] formed, Dreyo left Moran's smuggling operations because of the Empire's crack down on crime. Moran believed Dreyo was unaware he was alive<ref name="Survivor" /> by [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref>
