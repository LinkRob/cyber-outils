'''Colla IV''' was an [[Inner Rim Territories]] [[planet]] that was [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[insectoid]] [[Colicoid]]s, a violent warrior [[species]]. While the [[Galactic Republic]] failed to maintain a [[diplomat]]ic relationship with the world, the [[Trade Federation]] corporation successfully negotiated with the resident [[Colicoid Creation Nest]] manufacturing company, contracting them to create [[army]] [[droid]]s for them. The Colicoids subsequently created the [[droideka]] heavy duty [[Battle droid|combat droid]] in their own image and went on to make other droids and [[starship]]s that would be used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] against the Republic during the [[Clone Wars]].

==Description==
Colla IV,<ref name="AEYNTK" /> was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="CEUE" /> located in the fourth [[orbit]]al position<ref name="AEYNTK" /> of the [[Colla system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]], situated in grid square N-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> The [[toothspike raptor]] was a creature [[Homeworld|native]] to the world.<ref name="Rise" />

==History==
The [[Galactic Republic]] initially sent representatives, including [[Jedi Knight]]s, to Colla IV. However, the native [[Colicoid]]s' hostility towards outsiders prompted the Republic to quickly stop its efforts in order to end the protests against the [[species]]' excesses. Eventually, the [[Trade Federation]] corporation, looking for [[weapon]]s manufacturers that could create it a [[Trade Federation Droid Army|droid army]], successfully negotiated with the resident [[Colicoid Creation Nest]], a manufacturing company ran by the Colicoids, and awarded it a contract to create [[army]] [[droid]]s.<ref name="Battle droids">{{EncyclopediaCite|Battle Droids and Other Droids of War}}</ref>

[[File:Droideka-BYOR2D2-32.png|thumb|left|300px|The droideka was created on Colla IV.]]
The Colicoids subsequently designed the heavy-duty destroying machine known as the [[droideka]] in their own image, naming the model by combining the word "droid" and their [[language]]'s word for "mindless drone." The Trade Federation paid them in fifty [[transport]]s' worth of exotic [[Foodstuff|meats]], mostly still-living creatures. With a satisfactory arrangement for them, the Colicoids subsequently manufactured and sold the droidekas. The droideka designs were autonomous, which went against the Trade Federation's desires, as stated in their contract, for them to be controlled by a [[Central Control Computer|central computer]]. Nevertheless, the Colicoids did not expect the corporation to give much protest to that.<ref name="Battle droids" />

The Trade Federation became a key part of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], which was founded following the [[Raxus Address]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[24 BBY]].<ref name="24 BBY">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'' states that the [[Raxus Address]] took place two [[Standard year|years]] before the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]]. Therefore, the Raxus Address must have occurred in [[24 BBY]].</ref> Beside the droidekas,<ref name="CEUE" /> the Colicoid Creation Nest produced additional droids and vehicles for the Separatists,<ref name="CEUE" /><ref name="Battle droids" /><ref name="EOS">[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles (second edition)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles'', second edition]]</ref> including the [[Droideka Sharpshooter]],<ref name="Battle droids" /> [[Pistoeka sabotage droid]]s,<ref name="CEUE" /> and the [[Trident-class assault ship|''Trident''-class assault ships]]. The Colicoids designed the [[Droch-class boarding ship|''Droch''-class boarding ships]] with a [[hull]] loosely based on their homeworld's toothspike raptor.<ref name="Rise" /> They additionally manufactured the [[droid tri-fighter]] in cooperation with [[Phlac-Arphocc Automata Industries]], with the [[starfighter]]'s three-armed design mimicking the [[Skeleton|skull]] features of one of Colla IV's most ferocious [[Diet|predators]].<ref name="AEYNTK" /> Those droids and vehicles saw use by the Separatists against the Republic in the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="CEUE" /><ref name="AEYNTK" /><ref name="Battle droids" /><ref name="EOS" /> of [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> Colla IV was in [[New Republic]] territory.<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Inhabitants==
Colla IV was home to the Colicoids, a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[insectoid]] species known for having completely unfeeling and murderous ways.<ref name="CEUE" /> The two-[[meter]] warrior beings, who were covered in a dense [[chitin]]ous upper shell and had a diet that included meat, were violent among themselves but displayed particular hostility toward offworlders.<ref name="Battle droids" />

==Behind the scenes==
Colla IV was first introduced into the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] when it was mentioned in ''[[Star Wars Journeys: The Phantom Menace]]'', an [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]]<ref name="Journeys TPM">''[[Star Wars Journeys: The Phantom Menace]]''</ref> that was released on [[April 22]], [[2014]].<ref>{{Disney|subdomain=help|url=articles/en_US/FAQ/STAR-WARS-Journeys-The-Phantom-Menace?section=Apps|text=Disney Interactive support}}</ref> [[Colla IV/Legends|Colla IV]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, being first mentioned in the [[1999]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]].<ref name="Episode I VD">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
