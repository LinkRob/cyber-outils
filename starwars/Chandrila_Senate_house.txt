The '''Chandrila Senate house''',<ref name="Aftermath" /> also referred to as the '''Senate Building''',<ref name="Life Debt" /> was a building located in the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Hanna City]] on the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]. It served as the temporary headquarters of the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> The building had a main auditorium, secondary auditoriums,<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref> and endless scalloped balconies.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>
