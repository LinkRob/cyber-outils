'''''Bantha Tracks'' 17''' was the seventeenth issue of ''[[Bantha Tracks (department)|Bantha Tracks]]''. It features a brief profile of [[Admiral/Legends|Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar/Legends|Ackbar]], which includes the earliest reference to the [[Mon Calamari/Legends|Mon Calamari]] homeworld of "[[Dac/Legends|Dac]]."
