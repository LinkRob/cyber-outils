'''Baksan''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Rutian]] [[Twi'lek]] seen on [[Batuu]]. He used the title "[[Mayor]]."<ref name="Art" />

==Behind the scenes==
Concept art of Baksan, a character that potentially could have been a part of [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] themed land, was created by [[Andrew Domachowski]] and later appeared in ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge]]''.<ref name="Art" />
