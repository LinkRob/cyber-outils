'''Frank Henson''' was an actor, flight coordinator and stunt coordinator. He played various roles in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] such as a [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtrooper]], [[Skiff guard/Legends|skiff guard]], and rebel guard.
