The '''7th edition of ''Space and Humanity''''' was a [[book]] about [[starship]] design during the [[High Republic Era]]. It was written and published by Mr. [[Carl (engineer)|Carl]], the chief [[engineer]] of [[Star Bay Shipyards]].<ref name="TVoSD" />

In [[200 BBY]],<ref name="TVoSD date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Mostima]] and her [[Padawan]] [[Sean]] were sent on a [[Mission to Aakaash|mission]] to the [[Aakaash system]]. After they were seperated in an [[Attack on the Nan family villa|attack]], Sean joined the [[Silver Knights]], a local [[mercenary]] group lead by [[Li Yu]]. When Li asked Sean if the young [[Gender|boy]] was impressed by his [[battleship]], the ''[[Silver Dawn]]'', Sean noted that was clearly designed with the principles of the 7th edition of ''[[Space and Humanity]]''. The Jedi then added that it could be improved by retrofitting it to adhere to the [[9th edition of Space and Humanity|9th edition]], which included significant improvements in interior space utilization. The mercenary [[captain]] then got angry and told Sean that he didn't have the [[Land & Sky currency|money]] to buy every new edition let alone to modify his ship.<ref name="TVoSD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 7th edition of ''Space and Humanity'' was mentioned in the [[Wikipedia:Written Chinese|Chinese]] web novel ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''<ref name="TVoSD" /> written by "[[His Majesty the King]]," within its twenty-second chapter, which was released on [[December 29]], [[2020]]. The book's name was written in [[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|simplified Chinese]] as "《太空与人性》第7版" (《tài kōng yǔ rén xìng 》dì 7 bǎn).<ref name="Chapter 22">{{WebCite|url=https://www.qidian.com/chapter/1015950219/626238704|text=第22章 肖百万 _《星球大战：白银誓约》小说在线阅读|author=[[His Majesty the King]]|date=2020-12-29|language=[[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|Simplified Chinese]]|archivedate=20260526091655}}</ref>
