'''Armor-piercing grenades''' were oversized [[grenade]]s that were effective against armored vehicles. The grenades cost 100 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] per unit and could be set to detonate on impact, on a timer, or when it was affected by pressure.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
