A '''floodlight''' was a lighting unit for projecting a broad beam of artificial light. Such units were used on [[starship]]s and [[landspeeder]]s.<ref name="The Escape">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Escape]]''</ref> One known floodlight model was [[Gelieg]]'s [[20m-cp Strobe/C-Beam Lamp]], which was notably equipped on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="IncrediBuilds">''[[Star Wars: Millennium Falcon Deluxe Book and Model Set]]''</ref>
