A '''chauffeur droid''' was a type of [[droid]] that was used to drive a vehicle. During the [[Gorse Conflict]], a golden plated chauffeur droid operated a [[Speeder truck|hovertruck]] which the rebel [[Hera Syndulla]] and the freighter pilot [[Kanan Jarrus]] commandeered. The chauffeur droid tried to protest the hijacking but Hera flicked a switch on the droid's neck, switching him off. The two rebels then drove to the [[spaceport]] at [[Highground]]. She then threw the droid out of the vehicle, knocking out a [[stormtrooper]] sentry.<ref name="AND" />

==Behind the scenes==
The chauffeur droid first appeared as a minor character in [[John Jackson Miller]]'s [[2014]] novel [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]. He was first illustrated in the 2014 [[Star Wars Insider 152|''Star Wars Insider'' 152]] article "[[Exclusive Fiction|Rebels United]]," which contained a preview for the upcoming novel.<ref name="AND email interview">{{EmailCite|email=Wookieepedia:Email/John Jackson Miller (2016)|creator=[[John Jackson Miller]]|date=2016-06-13}}</ref>
