The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''28 ABY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3305 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''8005''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Admiral]] [[Enric Pryde]] is given command of a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] by [[First Order]] [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]. [[Gender|He]] names it the [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] in honor of the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Starship|vessel]] [[Steadfast (Imperial-class)|he served aboard]].<ref name="Pryde date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that [[Enric Pryde]] was made [[Commanding officer|commander]] of the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] 6 [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. From this it can be calculated that the event took place in 28 ABY.</ref>
*[[Senator]] [[Tai-Lin Garr]] is [[Tai-Lin Garr's assassination|assassinated]] by the [[Amaxine warriors (New Republic Era)|Amaxine warriors]].<ref name="Bloodline" />
*[[Carise Sindian]] is expelled from the [[Elder Houses]].<ref name="Bloodline" />
*[[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] resigns from the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] following revelations that her father is [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and forms the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Bloodline" />
*The [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker]] is [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destroyed]]<ref name="Luke temple Date" /> by [[Ben Solo]].<ref name="Encyclopedia: Kylo">{{EncyclopediaCite|Kylo Ren and Other Followers of the Dark Side}}. Original text: "Ben s'échappa des ruines complètement désespéré, persuadé qu'il avait tué son oncle et maître. Il s'abandonna au pouvoir du côté obscur et provoqua un orage qui mit le feu aux habitations et rasa le Temple Jedi, entraînant la mort de la plupart de ses camarades."</ref>
*After separating from his wife, [[Han Solo]] returns to smuggling with his life-long friend [[Chewbacca]].
*Ben Solo [[Death|kills]] [[Ren (human)|Ren]], becoming [[Ren|leader]] of the [[Knights of Ren]], and takes on his new name of Kylo Ren.<ref name="Rise of Kylo Ren 4">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 4|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 4]]</ref>
*Kylo Ren becomes [[Snoke]]'s apprentice and joins the [[First Order]].
*[[Luke Skywalker]] disappears from the galaxy, going into exile on [[Ahch-To]].

*[[Hy-Stepping]]<ref name="HSBirth">According to ''[[The Last Jedi: Rose Tico: Resistance Fighter]]'', [[Hy-Stepping]] was six when [[Rose Tico]] and [[Finn]] [[Mission to Canto Bight|visited]] [[Canto Bight]], as seen in [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']]. Since ''The Last Jedi'' continues directly from the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places in [[34 ABY]], it can be deduced that Hy-Stepping's year of birth is 28 ABY.</ref>
