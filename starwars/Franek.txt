'''Franek''' was an individual who served as a [[lieutenant]] in the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He had a [[Franek's husband|husband]]. At one point, Franek served in a [[Lexi Greylark's squad|squad]] led by the [[Greylark family]] member [[Lexi Greylark]].<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> he served as part of a Republic Defense Coalition force that accompanied several [[Jedi]] and [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Rina Greylark]] on a [[Mission to Mynos Three|mission]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Mynos III]].<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />
