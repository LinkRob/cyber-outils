'''Battle of Hoth: Early Storyboards for The Empire Strikes Back''' is an article written by [[Jonathan W. Rinzler]] that appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 114|''Star Wars Insider'' 114]] and continued in [[Star Wars Insider 115|''Star Wars Insider'' 115]]. The article discussed the [[Battle of Hoth/Legends|Battle of Hoth]] which was featured on [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], as well as showing concept storyboards from the early parts of the battle.<ref name="sw114" />
