{{Quote|So that's how you plan to get top rank? Butting your ship in where it doesn't belong?''"<br />"''By being better than ''you''. Don't know why you think you can call shots like&mdash;|Rac Syrmo and Bansu Ro|<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" />}}
'''Bansu Ro''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Reproduction|born]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] who became an [[Imperial cadet]] at the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Carida Academy|naval academy]] on the planet [[Carida]]. Training as a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]], he and fellow [[cadet]] [[Rac Syrmo]] competed for the top spot on their class's leaderboard until the Empire's [[Imperial Navy|navy]] commandeered their class around [[4 ABY]]. The Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] requested Ro and Syrmo to make up for losses in the [[fighter wing]]'s [[Squadron Five (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Five]].

Ro and Syrmo joined the 204th's Squadron Five, which was preparing for an attack on a mobile [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel recruitment base|recruitment base]]. When Squadron Five and a complement of [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE bombers]] were subsequently [[Attack on a rebel recruitment base|deployed]] to destroy the recruitment base, Ro engaged the defending [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s, saving Syrmo from one in the process. Squadron Five and the [[bomber]]s ultimately destroyed the recruitment base and its escorts. After the battle, the [[pilot]]s received news of the [[death]] of the [[Darth Sidious|Galactic Emperor]]. Squadron Five returned to the rest of the 204th, which continued to clash with the forces of the Alliance and its successor, the [[New Republic]], through many battles. The fighter wing was later stationed with the Empire's loyalist forces at the planet [[Jakku]].

During a social gathering with other 204th personnel, Ro fell under the influence of narcotics and grieved the victims of the 204th's genocides before he was taken to his [[bunk]]. The New Republic later [[Battle of Jakku|defeated]] the Empire at Jakku, and Ro fled the world, soon finding himself on the planet [[Freerock]] as an ex-Imperial. At the [[Rim's Edge]] [[cantina]] on Freerock, Ro was approached by the ex&ndash;[[New Republic pilot]] [[Nath Tensent]], who revealed what he knew of the former Imperial pilot. While prepared to draw a [[weapon]] as he did not trust his visitor, Ro listened as Tensent offered an invitation to join his [[Nath Tensent's crew|crew]] of freelancers, which already had taken in two other ex-204th pilots. Tensent left a bewildered Ro with an offer to speak again at the establishment [[Roderick's]].

==Biography==
===Training to be a pilot===
{{Quote|As the top two ranked in your class, you've been requested by the 204th. They've had some recent losses. For better or worse, you're on the same squadron as of now.|Yurib Nakan, to Bansu Ro and Rac Syrmo|<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" />}}
[[File:CaridaFlightTraining-TIEFighter4.png|thumb|left|300px|Bansu Ro and Rac Syrmo during flight training on Carida]]
Bansu Ro was [[Reproduction|born]] [[Homeworld|on]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Lothal]] and lived during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. He became estranged from his [[Bansu Ro's family|family]] after they sided with rebels against the Empire. Afterward,<ref name="Victory's Price" /> Ro joined<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> the [[Carida Academy|Imperial Naval Academy]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> on<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Timelines Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> planet<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Carida]] as an [[Imperial cadet]]. There, he trained as a [[pilot]] under [[Flight instructor]] [[Yurib Nakan]]. He also formed a rivalry with fellow [[cadet]] [[Rac Syrmo]], and both competed for the top rank<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> on the class leaderboard,<ref name="Imperial Cadet 2">[[Han Solo - Imperial Cadet 2|''Han Solo - Imperial Cadet'' 2]]</ref> outdoing the rest of their class.<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> However, the allegiance of Ro's family tainted his career, setting him on course to have a career making [[shuttle]] runs.<ref name="Victory's Price" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="4 ABY">The events of the [[TIE Fighter 4|fourth issue]] of the [[Star Wars: TIE Fighter (comic series)|''Star Wars: TIE Fighter'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]] take place between the [[mission to the Kudo system]] and the [[Battle of Endor]], both of which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> though, the Empire's [[Imperial Navy|navy]] chose to commandeer Ro's entire class for its latest war effort. The Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] specifically requested Ro and Syrmo, as the top two ranked of their class, to make up for their recent losses;<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> the [[fighter wing]] had been impressed by Ro's aptitude tests.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

Meanwhile, Ro and Syrmo flew [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] in a training exercise, where their target was a [[deflector shield generator dome]] marked with the [[Rebel Alliance starbird|insignia]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] against the Empire. During the exercise, both cadets flew at the dome together, each trying to make the killing shot on the [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]], but the pair fired on and destroyed it at the same [[time]]. After the training exercise, Ro and Syrmo disembarked from their [[TIE series|TIEs]] and argued over who had made the killing shot on the [[Deflector shield|shield]] generator dome.<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" />

[[File:RoSyrmoNakan-TIEFighter4.png|thumb|right|350px|Ro and Syrmo are told about their reassignment by Yurib Nakan.]]
As Ro bragged about his intentions to outperform his fellow cadet, Nakan interrupted the pair, criticizing their constant arguing. The flight [[instructor]] then told Ro and Syrmo about their transfer to the 204th. Upon realizing they were still the top-ranked in their class after their last exercise, Ro and Syrmo argued about who had been more pivotal in destroying the dome. Nakan interrupted, telling the pair how well they could fly together would matter more in their new assignment than their class rankings.<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" />

===Induction into the 204th===
{{Quote|There are a lot of officers with cadets under their command now. But we were the best in&mdash;''"<br />"''How many combat runs have you been on, cadets? How many kills have you racked up?|Ro and Teso Broosh|<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" />}}
[[File:NewRecruits-TIEFighter4.png|thumb|left|350px|Ro and Syrmo were sent to join Squadron Five, commanded by Teso Broosh.]]
Ro and Syrmo were ferried on a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] from<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> the [[Ryleel Imperial Base]] on Carida<ref name="Bounty Hunters 25">[[Bounty Hunters 25|''Bounty Hunters'' 25]]</ref> to a [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]] carrying the 204th's [[Squadron Five (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Five]]. Onboard the shuttle, Syrmo questioned if the 204th was supposed to be elite. Ro remarked that the unit was not elite enough to avoid casualties. When Syrmo mentioned his [[parent]]s being unable to see their graduation, Ro bounced off of his comment and rekindled their debate on who was ahead in their class at Carida. Ro and Syrmo's shuttle then docked into the [[cruiser]]-[[carrier]], and the pair exited to meet their new [[squadron]].<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" />

The [[squadron commander]], [[Second Lieutenant Commander]] [[Teso Broosh]], was displeased that his squadron was receiving two cadets to replace their two [[Death|fallen]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilots]], [[Lyttan Dree]] and [[Zin Graw]]. Ro and Syrmo explained that their whole class had been transferred to the Imperial Navy and that they were the top-ranked cadets, but Broosh only asked about their combat experience, which the pair lacked. The squadron [[Commanding officer|commander]] parted to fetch Ro and Syrmo [[datapad]]s with Squadron Five's formations.<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" />

[[File:SquadronFiveBriefing-TIEFighter4.jpg|thumb|right|350px|Ro and the rest of Squadron Five are briefed by Broosh on their upcoming attack.]]
Squadron Five's ''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier was en route to attack a mobile [[rebel recruitment base]], and Broosh held a briefing with his pilots, including Ro and Syrmo. The squadron commander laid out his plan to hit the Alliance forces fast and hard after arriving out of [[hyperspace]]. After Broosh mentioned Graw, whose failed attempt to defect to the Alliance had led the Empire to locate the recruitment base, Ro quietly asked Syrmo who Graw was, which his fellow cadet did not know. Their commander continued his brief, discussing the possibility of taking [[prisoner]]s with [[Flight officer|Flight Officer]] [[Ganem Kahi]]<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> and detailing tactics<ref name="TIE Fighter 5">[[TIE Fighter 5|''TIE Fighter'' 5]]</ref> before the squadron then exited the briefing room to begin their mission.<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" />

===Into battle===
{{Quote|In case you haven't noticed, we're outnumbered. Tighten up the formation.''"<br />"''I'm sorry, commander. Our fighters aren't as fast as&mdash;''"<br />"''A good pilot can compensate for a lesser ship, Bansu. You said you were top of your class. Prove it.|Broosh and Ro|<ref name="TIE Fighter 5" />}}
The cruiser-carrier exited hyperspace near the Alliance's recruitment base, a [[GR-75 medium transport]], [[Attack on a rebel recruitment base|deploying]] a complement of [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE bombers]] with Squadron Five escorting them. Ro's squadron flew [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE interceptors]] aside from Ro and Syrmo, who flew standard TIE fighters. The [[interceptor]]s targeted and disabled the [[transport]]'s [[hyperdrive]], blocking the recruitment base's ability to escape into hyperspace. The Imperial [[Imperial Military|forces]] were then met with many [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s, which outnumbered the TIEs. As Squadron Five moved to engage the [[X-wing starfighter|X-wings]], Broosh told Ro and Syrmo that the pair were lagging behind.<ref name="TIE Fighter 5" />

[[File:TIEsAttack-TIEFighter5.jpg|thumb|left|350px|Squadron Five makes an attack run on the rebels.]]
Ro stressed that his and Syrmo's TIEs were not as fast as the TIE interceptors, but Broosh advised him to compensate for his "lesser ship" and prove that he was top of his class. Squadron Five engaged with the X-wings to protect the TIE [[bomber]]s. During the dogfight, Ro was pursued by an X-wing that he could not shake off. He called to his squadron for help, and Syrmo moved in to assist and destroyed the rebel fighter. Squadron Five then returned to formation, and, after shooting down at last two X-wings, the pilots helped the bombers destroy the rebel recruitment base while Ro and Syrmo proclaimed it was "For the Empire." Victorious, the squadron returned to the cruiser-carrier.<ref name="TIE Fighter 5" />

With Syrmo disappointed that he had failed to save a bomber, Ro assured him it was just a mistake. Syrmo expressed doubt that he was ready for active duty, but Ro told him that he could continue to save others like he had saved his fellow cadet. The pair were then interrupted by a voice over the [[intercom]], which announced that a major [[Battle of Endor|battle]] between the Alliance and the Empire had caused the destruction of the Empire's new [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]] and the presumed death of [[Galactic Emperor]]<ref name="TIE Fighter 5" /> [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> Broosh told Ro and the other pilots to await further orders, reminding them that there was still a command structure and that they were still an intact squadron.<ref name="TIE Fighter 5" />

===On the run===
{{Quote|Think about when we talked to the 204th.|Wyl Lark recalls when he reached out to the 204th pilots|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
[[File:WylLark-VictorysPrice.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The New Republic Starfighter Commander Wyl Lark (pictured) reached out to Bansu Ro over a comm channel.]]
Squadron Five eventually returned to the rest of the 204th, which found itself on the run as Imperial worlds began to fall to the Alliance&mdash;which reorganized into the [[New Republic]]&mdash;in the wake of the [[Emperor]]'s death. The fighter wing took part in the genocidal campaign known as [[Operation: Cinder]] before eventually setting up a base of operations at<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> the Outer Rim's<ref name="Timelines Map" /> [[Pandem Nai system]]. After a New Republic [[Barma Battle Group|battle group]] drove the 204th from the [[Star system|system]] in a crippling [[Battle of Pandem Nai|attack]],<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> the fighter wing worked to rebuild again before [[Cerberon system campaign|battling]] the same [[battle group]] at the [[Deep Core]]'s [[Cerberon system]]<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="SFDate">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref>

Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="VPDate">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> the 204th carried out a string of genocides on behalf of the Empire's loyalist forces against rogue Imperial factions, using the [[bulk freighter]] ''[[Yadeez]]'' as a [[flagship]]. However, the New Republic [[New Republic Defense Force|forces]] from Pandem Nai and the Cerberon system pursued the fighter wing through a series of systems. At<ref name="Victory's Price" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="Timelines Map" /> planet [[Chadawa]], the 204th's next target, the fighter wing found itself enclosed under a cage of planetary mechanical rings that channeled [[radiation]]. In an effort to reach out to the pilots of the 204th, New Republic [[Starfighter Commander]] [[Wyl Lark]] began trying to speak to them over an open communications channel using names of 204th pilots that his side had learned of. Ro was the second pilot Lark tried to speak to, and the commander told a story in hopes that the TIE pilot was listening.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

===Fall of the Empire===
{{Quote|Look, you're in dire straits so you may as well hear my pitch. I'm offering a way to resolve all your current troubles.''"<br />"''And purchase new ones?''"<br />"''Exactly. You see, I'm putting a crew together. I'm no patriot or a true believer&mdash;just looking to profit and do some good.|Nath Tensent invites Ro to join his crew|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
The 204th eventually escaped the [[Chadawa system]] and rendezvoused with the amassing Imperial loyalist forces at<ref name="Victory's Price" /> the Inner Rim<ref name="Timelines Map" /> planet [[Jakku]]. There, the fighter wing's personnel occupied themselves by visiting friends and family in other units or taking part in war games. At one point, a number of personnel had a social gathering in the ''Yadeez''{{'s}} [[hangar]]. Ro, who had acquired narcotics, found himself under the influence of the drugs and began sounding his grief over the victims of the genocides the 204th had caused. [[Soran Keize]], the 204th's commanding [[Imperial officer|officer]] at the time, and [[Creet]], a TIE [[Technician|mechanic]], helped Ro into a [[bunk]], and Keize told the TIE pilot that there was no shame in serving with his comrades during the massacres.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

[[File:Nath_Tensent.png|thumb|left|250px|Former New Republic pilot Nath Tensent (pictured) found Ro on Freerock.]]
Shortly after, the New Republic [[Battle of Jakku|attacked]] and defeated the Imperial loyalist forces at Jakku. With the 204th defeated during the battle, Ro fled Jakku and soon found himself on<ref name="Victory's Price" /> the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Timelines Map" /> planet [[Freerock]], which had become a haven for ex-Imperials like him. Four [[Standard month|months]] after the Empire's defeat at Jakku, Ro, having found more problems for himself, sold his [[blaster]] and went to the [[Rim's Edge]] [[cantina]], sitting in an alcove. That was where he was approached by [[Nath Tensent]], a veteran [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] from the New Republic battle group that had repeatedly fought the 204th, who sat at his table. Ro turned down Tensent's offer to buy a drink, not recognizing his guest, who called Ro by his name. Ro was surprised Tensent knew his name, and the veteran summarized what he knew of the ex-Imperial's past.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

Realizing who Tensent was, Ro reached for where he used to keep the blaster he had sold and questioned whether the veteran had been sent to hunt him and if [[Yrica Quell]], a former TIE pilot who had betrayed the 204th, had given information on him. Tensent confirmed the second inquiry and revealed that he was no longer with the New Republic. Ro did not believe the veteran's latter claim, reaching for his [[vibroknife]]. The veteran then invited Ro to join his [[Nath Tensent's crew|crew]] of freelancers, revealing that he had already recruited Creet and the 204th pilot [[Nord Kandende]], whom Ro dismissed as an idiot. After Tensent's [[astromech droid]], [[T5]], interrupted him about some other business, Ro's visitor advised the ex-Imperial to consider the offer and visit the establishment [[Roderick's]] if he wished to speak more. Perplexed, Ro watched Tensent leave with the [[droid]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|It was a mistake, Rac. We all make them.''"<br />"''A mistake that got our people killed. Maybe we&mdash; Maybe we weren't really ready for duty. Maybe I wasn't cut out to be&mdash;''"<br />"''So next time you save them. Just like you saved me. You stay alive and keep flying until you feel like you've saved enough to balance it out.|Ro and Syrmo|<ref name="TIE Fighter 5" />}}
[[File:RoSyrmoDatapad-TIEFighter4.png|thumb|right|350px|Ro and Syrmo competed for the top-ranked cadet in their class at Carida Academy.]]
Bansu Ro was a [[human]]<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> with [[Color|tan-colored]] skin<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> and slick black [[hair]] that ran down his scalp. He gave a short and lean impression on Nath Tensent.<ref name="Victory's Price" /> Ro formed an intense rivalry with Rac Syrmo, competing for the glory of being the top-ranked cadet in his class. During an exercise, he attempted to push his fighter in front to get the winning shot on a shield generator dome and tried to take as much credit as possible for the victory after. With him and Syrmo still beating their class on the leaderboard, Ro was sure he had the top spot over his fellow cadet, repeatedly bragging about it to his classmate.<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" />

When Teso Broosh was dissatisfied that he was taking on two cadets into his squadron, Ro tried to downplay the concerns by assuring the squadron commander that he and Syrmo were the best in their class. However, the pair were speechless when asked about their combat experience.<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> Flying with Squadron Five, Ro learned about his duty to protect his comrades in the Empire and lectured such to Syrmo after the other cadet felt guilty about a mistake he made that caused the death of another pilot.<ref name="TIE Fighter 5" />

[[File:RoAndSyrmo-TIEFighter4.jpg|thumb|left|280px|Ro and Syrmo in their Imperial cadet uniforms]]
The influence of narcotics led Ro to express open grief over the people that the 204th Imperial Fighter Wing had killed during genocides. After fleeing Jakku and becoming an ex-Imperial, he tried to stave off unwanted company like that of Tensent and prepared to draw a [[weapon]] when he realized the side the veteran had been on, distrusting his visitor. Ro was also weary of his surroundings during his meeting with Tensent, scanning for other possible enemies. He was initially skeptical of Tensent's offers, believing that he was trading his problems for other problems, but he was confused when the New Republic veteran finished pitching his offer and left Ro in peace.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|My parents were so excted for graduation.''"<br />"''They'll just have to settle for knowing you were number two when you left.''"<br />"''You don't ''know'' you were number one. They didn't tell us the final rankings.''"<br />"''Like Nakan said, rank doesn't matter where we're going.|Syrmo and Ro|<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" />}}
A skilled pilot, Ro outdid almost all of his class at the [[Imperial Academy|flight academy]]<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> and impressed the 204th with his aptitude tests.<ref name="Victory's Price" /> During the attack on the mobile recruitment base, he struggled to keep up with TIE interceptors in a TIE fighter, unable to compensate for their speed differences.<ref name="TIE Fighter 5" />

==Equipment==
Ro wore a black TIE pilot [[flight suit]] with a [[control box]] when he flew a TIE fighter and white Imperial cadet [[Clothing|uniform]] when he was not.<ref name="TIE Fighter 4" /> At Freerock, he owned a [[Knife|vibroknife]] and a blaster, although he later sold the latter.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
