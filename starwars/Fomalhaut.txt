'''Fomalhaut''' was the primary [[Star/Legends|star]] of the [[Fomalhaut system]] and was orbited by at least one small [[Planet/Legends|planet]]. Fomalhaut was prone to seasonal [[Solar flare/Legends|solar eruptions]], which forced [[Spacer/Legends|spacers]] and [[Pilot/Legends|pilots]] to perform many arduous maneuvers before jumping to [[Hyperspace/Legends|hyperspace]].<ref name="DR17" />

==Behind the scenes==
The unlicensed ''[[Star Wars Legends|Legends]]'' star, Fomalhaut, was mentioned in ''[[L'Arche d'Alliance]]'' ("The Ark of Covenant"), a scenario published in the French RPG magazine ''[[Dragon Radieux 17|''Dragon Radieux'' #17]]''.<ref name="DR17" />
