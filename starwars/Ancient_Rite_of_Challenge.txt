The '''Ancient Rite of Challenge''' was a ritual of the [[Mon Calamari]] and [[Quarren]] [[Species|people]]. In order to settle debates, two individuals could instead fight in the [[Sacred Arena of Salka'a]].<ref name="Allegiance 3" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] fought [[Chadkol Gee]]'s [[security droid]], [[RK-9|Arkay-Nine]] after the [[Resistance]] was accused of starting a brawl with a Quarren mob. Rey deafeated the [[droid]] but the Resistance was force to exile from [[Mon Cala]] because [[Rose Tico]] helped Rey in the fight.<ref name="Allegiance 3" />
