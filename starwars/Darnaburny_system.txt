The '''Darnaburny system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Kanz sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> It was situated in grid square N-4 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

During the [[High Republic Era]],<ref name="Wayseeker">''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]''</ref> the vicinity of the system was the site of<ref name="Battlenamia">{{Battlenamia|event=[[Darnaburny Civil War]]|system=Darnaburny [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> the [[Darnaburny Civil War]], which the [[Mirialan]] [[Jedi]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] had participated in.<ref name="Wayseeker" /> By around [[160 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]'' take place around [[160 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:WayseekerDate|here]].</ref> the [[human]] Jedi [[Indara]] had learned of Rwoh's involvement in the Darnaburry Civil War and considered it a historical and tragic event.<ref name="Wayseeker" />
