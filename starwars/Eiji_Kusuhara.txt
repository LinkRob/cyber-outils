'''Eiji Kusuhara''' was a Japanese actor who portrayed a Y-Wing pilot in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. Several people, including [[Lucasfilm]]'s [[Leland Chee]] and Kusuhara's longtime friend Hilary Westlake, believe Kusuhara was the actor who played [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|Y-wing]] pilot [[Gureni Telsij]] in the film.<ref name="Diversity In Space Blog." />
