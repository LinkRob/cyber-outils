'''Dume''' was a mysterious and abnormally large [[Gender|male]] [[Loth-wolf]] who shared the [[Reproduction|birth]] name of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Kanan Jarrus]]&mdash;Dume. Following the [[death]] of Kanan during a [[Rescue of Hera Syndulla (Lothal)|mission]] to rescue [[Hera Syndulla]], "Dume" became the continuation of Jarrus' will, communing with Jarrus' apprentice [[Ezra Bridger]]. The wolf tasked Ezra with stopping the [[Galactic Empire]] from uncovering the secrets of the [[Lothal Jedi Temple]].

==Biography==
Following the [[death]] of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Kanan Jarrus]], Dume communed with Kanan's [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]] in a [[dream]]. Ezra had earlier been taken to the [[Loth-wolf|loth-wolves]]' camp by the [[White Loth-wolf]] and two smaller black wolves. In the dream, he approached Bridger and produced a [[Lothal Jedi Temple key stone|key stone]], an artifact from the [[Lothal Jedi Temple]]. Dume told Bridger that the [[Jedi temple]] had knowledge and destruction, and that Bridger would need to go there to "restore past, redeem future." After that, Dume seemingly devoured Bridger, only for Bridger to reawaken from his dream, yet discovering that the Lothal key stone was by his [[Limb|feet]].<ref name="DUME" />

Later, Ezra related his encounter with Dume with his fellow members of the [[Spectres]] rebel cell prior to the [[mission to the Lothal Jedi Temple]].<ref name="WaaD">{{Rebels|Wolves and a Door}}</ref> Ezra also related his encounter to his friend [[Ahsoka Tano]], a former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] and survivor of the [[Clone Wars]] and [[Great Jedi Purge]]. Dume was a [[The Force|Force]] manifestation of Kanan Jarrus' will<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> and upon learning of the wolf, and that it had identified itself as "Dume" from his real name Caleb Dume, Tano speculated that it may have manifested as a result of the [[Cosmic Force]].<ref name="World Between Worlds">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Dume had a white marking on his forehead, the same marking that Jarrus once had on his shoulder pad.<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/dume-trivia-gallery|text="Dume" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> Unlike the other loth-wolves, Dume was larger than his counterparts and was able to speak several words in [[Galactic Basic Standard]], a language spoken by the late Kanan and his apprentice Ezra. While Ezra was mourning for Kanan's death, Dume acted callous and accused him of "running"; nonetheless, he gave the grieving Padawan a listening ear and encouraged him that he was not alone, as was seen by his saying "fight together." Dume was concerned with safeguarding the Lothal Jedi Temple from the Empire and tasked Ezra with stopping the Imperials from plundering the temple's secrets.<ref name="DUME" />
