The '''medical library''' of the [[Docha Institute]] on<ref name="TRS" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> astronomical object [[Dunnak]] was considered one of the best in [[the galaxy]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="TRS" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] boarded the ''[[Innovator]]'', an [[Elite-class vessel|''Elite''-class]] research [[Starship|vessel]] with state-of-the-art facilities. While there, he compared the ship's medical [[library]] to Docha Institute's, mentally considering that while both were impressive, the latter was better.<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Docha Institute's medical library was mentioned in the novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TRS" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase I and published on [[June 29]], [[2021]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> Prior to its release, the library's mention was included in a [[StarWars.com]] preview for the novel on [[May 21]] of that year.<ref name="Excerpt">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-high-republic-the-rising-storm-exclusive-excerpt-3|text=Bell Zettifar is at War with His Emotions in ''Star Wars: The High Republic: The Rising Storm'' - Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
