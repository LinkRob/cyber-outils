The '''''Coruscant Daily Newsfeed''''' was a news publication associated with<ref name="Low Red Moon" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Low Red Moon" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{LRMDate|1|the Coruscant Daily Newsfeed}}</ref> [[Jaylen Vrax|Jaylen Barsha]], a member of the wealthy [[Barsha family]], had the [[protocol droid]] [[A1-A1]] provide him with [[datapad]]s filled with news each [[Local day|day]] during the family's exile on the [[moon]] [[Gus Treta]]. Jaylen searched the news in hopes of finding information on his missing half-[[Gender|brother]], [[Sliro Barsha]]. On one occasion, while Jaylen loaded up a copy of the [[Standard day|day's]] ''Coruscant Daily Newsfeed'' provided by A1-A1, the [[BX-series droid commando|BX commando droid]] [[ND-5]] brought him his breakfast while disguised as a [[Service droid|servant droid]].<ref name="Low Red Moon" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the ''Coruscant Daily Newsfeed'' appeared in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]'', a [[2026]] novel by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="Low Red Moon" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Coruscant Daily Newsfeed/Legends|''Coruscant Daily NewsFeed'']] was introduced in the "[[Galaxywide NewsNets]]" feature of the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 3|third issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'', written by [[Paul Sudlow]] and published by [[West End Games]] in [[August]] [[1994]].<ref name="Journal 3">{{JournalCite|3|Galaxywide NewsNets}}</ref> The capitalization of ''Coruscant Daily Newsfeed'' was used in "[[Hutt and Seek]]," a short story written by [[Chris Cassidy]] and [[Tish Eggleston Pahl|Tish Pahl]] and published in the [[1999]] anthology book ''[[Tales from the New Republic]]''.<ref name="Hutt and Seek">{{StoryCite|story=Hutt and Seek|book=Tales from the New Republic}}</ref> Chen used the free-to-edit, unofficial online ''Star Wars'' encyclopedia [[Wookieepedia]] to find prominent ''Star Wars'' newspapers to include in ''Low Red Moon''.<ref name="Interview">{{YouTube|video=E64Bv4R-a68|text=Mike Chen On Writing The Galaxy's Sexiest Droid In Low Red Moon - Star Wars Author Interview|channel=@StarWarsExplained|name=Star Wars Explained|date=2026-02-05}}</ref>
