'''Durril''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as an [[admiral]] in the [[Imperial Navy]] and was present during the [[Batonn insurgency]]. Admiral Durril commanded the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''[[Judicator]]'' of the [[One Oh Third Task Force]] during the [[Battle of Scrim Island]].

==Biography==
[[Admiral]] Durril was tasked with taking out the [[Batonn insurgents]], who had seized [[Scrim Island]]. [[Gender|He]] took up the "Standard Siege Tactic." After negotiating supposedly failed he ordered all [[Starship|ships]] to [[fire]] on the [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] covering the Island. This had no affect on the strength of the shield generator. Whilst Durril focused his efforts on the center of the shield the western and southern edges contracted and two [[ion cannon]]s fired into the ''[[Judicator]]''. He ordered his escort [[frigate]]s to fire at the edge but by the [[time]] they started the shield had expanded back over them. The shield then contracted in the north and fired some more ion shots whilst the [[One Oh Third Task Force]] fired on the western and southern emplacements (covered by a shield). As Durril's view changed to the North, eight freighters snuck out from under the shield. Seven went to other continents and one went back into [[Realspace|space]] and later jumped away.<ref name="Thrawn" />

The [[Battle of Scrim Island]] was a crushing defeat for Durril as he failed to recapture Scrim Island. He was forced into repair and was assigned elsewhere for Admiral [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] to complete the mission instead.<ref name="Thrawn" />
