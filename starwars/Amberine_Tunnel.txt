The '''Amberine Tunnel''' was a rock formation with exposed [[amberine]] in the [[Grazing Fields]] region on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|canon}}
During [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>, the [[Merqaal]] [[Nix]] led his companion [[Kay Vess]] through the tunnel to a buried part from a [[Clone Wars]] era [[Liquid-cable launcher|grappling spike launcher]]. Nix enjoyed fiddling with the part.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Amberine Tunnel appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The location was one of the areas in the game where the player follows Nix through the environment to find a collectible. In this case, the "Gun Piece" nix treasure. This along with other Nix treasures found on [[Toshara]] could be traded with [[Berek Keehro]] in [[Mirogana]] to unlock the amberine [[necklace]] cosmetic for Nix.<ref name="Outlaws" />
