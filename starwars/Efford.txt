'''Efford''' was a child born into [[slavery]], owned by [[Allehander Pso]] on the [[Five Points]] [[space station]] in the [[Five Points system]]. They were siblings with [[Laurose]]. After their mom's indentured service duty expired, Pso sold Efford and the other workers as slaves to the [[Galactic Empire]]. However, [[Jyn Erso]] captured the crew of the [[Starship|spaceship]] and rescued them.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
