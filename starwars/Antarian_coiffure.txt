An '''Antarian coiffure''' was a style of [[hair]] that was worn by the [[human]] [[Sebla Sarobia]] when she attended the [[Festival of the Ancestors]] on the [[planet]] [[Pasaana]]<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|IX|Sebla Sarobia and her coiffure's appearance}}</ref> [[Antar]] was an astronomical object in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]],<ref name="TFA Beginner Game">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> that was orbited by the [[moon]] [[Antar 4]],<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> home of the horned [[Gotal]] [[species]].<ref name="Gotal">{{Databank|gotal|Gotal}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
An Antarian coiffure first appeared in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']],<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> and was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
