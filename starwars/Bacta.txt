'''Bacta''' was a thick, gelatinous substance with near-miraculous healing properties. It helped the body regrow tissue, including nerves, skin, and muscles.<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>

==Description==
{{Quote|It ''is'' a form of barley&mdash;specifically ''Vratixia renanicus''. Originally cultivated on ''Thyferra'', it is a key ingredient of ''bacta'' alongside alazhi and kavam.|[[Estala Maru]]|<ref name="High Republic 2">[[The High Republic (2021) 2|''The High Republic'' (2021) 2]]</ref>}}
Bacta was a mixture of [[kavam]] and [[alazhi]] bacteria combined with [[ambori]] fluid<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE" /> and a type of barley known as ''[[Vratixia renanicus]]''.<ref name="High Republic 2" /> It made use of microbiotics that started to work upon contact with a wound.<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref>

Promoting rapid regeneration of [[Species|organic]] compounds, bacta could be used in a variety of both critical and noncritical medical situations. Described as being warm to the touch, the bacta liquid could aid in the healing of concussions, internal organs, and broken ribs. Furthermore, it could be placed in small dishes to help regrow fingernails, mend cuts, burns, and other injuries. Due to its "one-size-fits-all" use in medical applications, it was a highly prized and commonly used medical treatment for most, if not all, injuries.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> Bacta could also knit together broken bones.<ref name="Queen's Peril">''[[Queen's Peril]]''</ref>

Despite its highly regenerative properties, some individuals harbored negative feelings towards the substance, finding its viscosity somewhere between liquid and slime. Additionally, most bacta patients felt as if they were being swallowed alive upon being submerged in the substance. As such, most doctors injected sedatives first, while subsequent waves of relaxation would slowly overcome the patient, sending them into a meditative state. While most retained consciousness, the combination of drugs and bacta commonly led patients to feeling like they were drifting into another world, with some comparing it to being in a mother's womb. Upon emerging from the liquid, many would describe feeling worse for a few days due to the loss of serenity precipitated by the substance.<ref name="Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> However, some would find the opposite to be true, describing their bodies as feeling light and free from pain.<ref name="Bloodline" />

In order to allow a patient to retain normal functions, a breathing apparatus was provided to the individual along with specialized sensors that allowed the doctor to monitor their patients' bodily functions while submerged.<ref name="Bloodline" />

Bacta could be administered by [[Bacta tank]]s,<ref name="Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> [[Bacta pod]]s,<ref name="Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land">{{BOBF|Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land}}</ref> [[Flexpoly bacta suit]]s,<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> [[Bacta patch|Bacta patche]]s,<ref name="The Deserter">{{TCW|The Deserter}}</ref> [[Bacta spray]]s,<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> [[Bacta bomb]]s,<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref> bacta gels<ref name="SWGoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> and small dishes.<ref name="Bloodline" />

==History==
Bacta was invented by the [[Vratix]], an [[insectoid]] [[species]] from [[Thyferra]], and manufactured by two companies, [[Zaltin Corp]] and [[Xucphra Corp]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE" />

Being considered a new miracle drug, bacta came into use during the [[High Republic Era]] as a replacement for [[juvan]]. The agriworld [[Hetzal Prime]] attempted to grow bacta during this time; during the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Elzar Mann]] helped his fellow Jedi [[Telekinesis|move]] a liquid [[tibanna]] container in [[realspace]] while he was near a bacta facility, which could only produce the drug in limited quantities. After the disaster, [[Senator]] [[Izzet Noor]] incorrectly speculated it was caused by the [[Selkath]] in an attempt to halt the production of bacta to protect their own business.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Bacta tank]]s existed during the High Republic Era<ref name="Rising Storm" /> by [[231 BBY]].<ref name="231 Note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the event of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref>

Medical kits could include injectible vials of bacta and other substances.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

After a gruesome [[Sabotage of Grievous' shuttle|shuttle crash]]<ref name="Galaxy at War">''[[Star Wars: A Galaxy at War]]''</ref> that nearly [[Death|killed]] him,<ref name="HC 8">''{{HelmetCollectionCite|8|Helmets}}</ref> the [[Kalesh]] [[warlord]] [[Grievous]] was fatally injured<ref name="Galaxy at War" /> and was put in a bacta tank,<ref name="German Build X-Wing 47">{{BuildXWingCite|47|Starfighter Aces}} (German Edition)</ref> resulting in his transformation into a [[cyborg]].<ref name="Lair">{{TCW|Lair of Grievous}}</ref>

During the [[Clone Wars]], [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]] often recovered from battle injuries in bacta tanks.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE" /> After his commanding officer, [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], was injured during a [[Battle of Saleucami|battle]] on [[Saleucami]], [[clone trooper]] [[medic]] [[Kix]] dressed the captain's wound with a [[bacta patch]].<ref name="The Deserter" />

[[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] was placed in a bacta tank after the injuries she sustained in the [[Attack on the Executive Center]], having been caught in the blast of the former [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[assassin]] [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]] when she activated his self-destruct mechanism. Between her time in the bacta tank, which erased any visible scars or signs of the surgery, and being operated on atop surgical cots, she was not fully conscious for the first few days after the attack. After finally regaining consciousness, the confused Mothma did not understand that the bacta had erased any possible scars and started to panic, only to be put back to sleep by a droid for, in its words, "her own safety."<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

In the safety of his compound on [[Mustafar]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] would often have his [[Darth Vader's armor|suit]] removed and submerge himself in a bacta tank in order to heal the pain caused by his [[Duel on Mustafar|injuries]]. Vader also hoped that with continued bacta use, his lungs might be healed, freeing him from the breathing apparatus that he was forced to use.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

[[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was put in a bacta tank after being burned by Darth Vader, for revenge of his injuries sustained on Mustafar, during the [[Battle on Mapuzo]].<ref name="Part III">{{Kenobi|Part III}}</ref>

[[File:BactaVials-OutlawsVCG.png|thumb|left|250px|Vials of bacta]]
In the year 9 BBY, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] stumbled upon and reawakened [[Dagan Gera]], a fallen Jedi who had been kept in stasis in a bacta tank since the time of the High Republic. While the bacta kept him alive and young far beyond his natural lifespan and healed the lightsaber wound his former colleague [[Santari Khri]] inflicted on him, it did not regenerate his missing arm, despite being immersed for [[Great Hyperspace Disaster|224]] [[Standard year|years]].<ref>''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

When the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] was [[Echo Base|stationed]] on the frozen [[planet]] of [[Hoth]], [[Luke Skywalker]] had to be put in a bacta tank after being attacked and maimed by a [[wampa]] ice creature.<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> During this time, grenade-like [[bacta bomb]]s were used to heal soldiers on the front line.<ref name="Battlefront" />

During the [[Scourge crisis|scourge of the droids]], two [[DT-series sentry droid]]s infected by the [[Scourge]] attacked Vader's bacta tank with the element of surprise, killing the two [[Emperor's Royal Guard]]s standing vigilant and destroying the tank. However, Vader harnessed the [[Force power|power]] of the [[The Force|force]] to crush both units.<ref name="Dark Droids 3">[[Dark Droids 3|''Dark Droids 3'']]</ref> The tank was later repaired, from which Vader reached out to his [[Sith Master|master]] through the force after another [[Attack on Vader|skirmish on Mustafar]].<ref name="Vader 39">[[Darth Vader (2020) 39|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 39]]</ref>

Reprogrammed [[Bounty hunter]] [[droid]] [[IG-11]] used a [[bacta spray]] to help heal [[Din Djarin]]'s head after a severe injury from an explosion during the [[Clash on Nevarro]].<ref name="Chapter 8" />

[[Boba Fett]] used a [[bacta pod]] in his [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] to heal from injuries sustained in the [[Sarlacc]].<ref name="Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land" /> After a failed assassination [[Assassination attempt on Boba Fett|attempt]], Boba put [[Unidentified Gamorrean guard 1|one]] of his [[Gamorrean]] guards in his bacta tank, after being bitten by [[Krrsantan]] in the left shoulder.<ref name="Chapter 3: The Streets of Mos Espa">{{BOBF|Chapter 3: The Streets of Mos Espa}}</ref> [[Cobb Vanth]] would also later use the same tank after being shot by [[Cad Bane]].<ref name="Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor">{{BOBF|Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor}}</ref>

A [[flexpoly bacta suit]] was a special type of suit that could be worn by a patient to treat them with artificial bacta, an advancement over bacta tanks. The [[First Order]] defector [[Finn]] was equipped with one of these after being attacked with a [[lightsaber]] by [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] after joining the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Episode VIII" /><ref name="Last Jedi Visual">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The blend used by the Resistance was synthetic in nature, but was, nevertheless, still quite expensive.<ref name="Last Jedi Junior">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>
