'''Ascaal district''' was a region of the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] that included the [[Ascada River]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> unusually high [[Water|rainfall]] occurred during a [[hurricane]], causing authorities to predict flooding in the district a [[Standard week|week]] later. [[Holonet News Toshara]] wind report [[Journalist|presenter]] [[Botto Fna]] announced this forecast over a [[HoloNet News]] report and advised any listeners who were going to travel near the river to plan an alternate route instead.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ascaal district was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
