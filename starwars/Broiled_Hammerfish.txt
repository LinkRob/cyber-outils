'''Broiled Hammerfish''' was a [[Foodstuff|seafood]] dish created by the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] when he visited the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Glee Anselm]] during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> He created the dish to try and improve relations between the world's two [[Homeworld|native]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]&mdash;the [[Nautolan]] and the [[Anselmi]]&mdash;using fresh-caught [[hammerfish]] from the [[ocean]]s where the Nautolan dwelt and glazing it with a sweet [[sauce]] made using ingredients from the islands that the Anselmi inhabited.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

The Broiled Hammerfish proved popular with tourists on the beaches of Glee Anselm, but Tuggs admitted that it probably was not enough to reunite the feuding natives when publishing the recipe for the dish in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> He acknowledged, however, that it at least could not make things worse.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Broiled Hammerfish was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the fish makes use of cubed [[Wikipedia:Salmon|salmon]], [[Wikipedia:Soy sauce#Varieties|tamari]], [[Wikipedia:Honey|honey]] [[Wikipedia:Mirin|mirin]], [[Wikipedia:Sesame oil|sesame oil]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic cloves]], minced [[Wikipedia:Ginger|ginger]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], minced [[Wikipedia:Scallion|green onion]], and cooked [[Wikipedia:Rice as food|rice]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
