The '''B2-HA super battle droid''' was a heavy weapons variant of the [[B2-series super battle droid]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Ambush" /> even fighting in the [[First Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="Who's Who in the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Who's Who in the Galaxy: Character Storybook]]''</ref>

==Characteristics==
The B2-HA super battle droid was a more powerful variant of the [[B2-series super battle droid]], with one arm sporting a [[Missile launcher|warhead]] or [[torpedo]] launcher, instead of the standard built-in [[laser]] [[cannon]].<ref name="Join the Battle" /> The missile launcher was capable of firing [[Missile|homing rockets]].<ref name="Bombad Jedi" /> B2-HA super battle droids also had heavy [[weapon]]s and artillery, as well as thick [[armor]] and aggressive programming.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> They could be seen [[Commanding officer|leading]] droids like [[B1-series battle droid]]s,<ref name="Bombad Jedi" /> despite not being programmed for tactics or leadership.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Like the standard B2,<ref name="JtB corresponding">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[B2-series super battle droid]]s</ref> the B2-HA was 1.93 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]], 4 inches) tall,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> had [[Gender|masculine programming]] and [[Color|red]] [[photoreceptor]]s.<ref name="Ambush" />

==History==
{{Quote|Excessive force deployed.|A B2-HA droid of the [[Bedlam Raiders]]|<ref name="Survivor" />}}
Tasked with leading a patrol, a B2-HA was part of the [[Hissrich droid army|Hissrich droid unit]] led by the droid [[mercenary]] [[AD-W4]].<ref name="Mace Windu 1">[[Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu 1|''Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu'' 1]]</ref> One B2-HA led B1 [[battle droid]]s [[Mission to Rodia|on]] the [[planet]] [[Rodia]].<ref name="Bombad Jedi" />

Many B2-HAs were onboard the [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk-''class battleship]] ''[[Primacy]]'' when it crashed on [[Koboh]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], they were reprogrammed by the [[Bedlam Raiders]] on the planet along with the rest of the [[Core ship|core ship's]] complement. Amongst their forces was [[459]], [[388]] and a modified unit known as "[[Beetu Deetu]]."<ref name="Survivor" />
