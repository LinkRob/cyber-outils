{{Quote|Brings me back to my gonkrock days. I miss playing with my band, Corellia's Not Dead. We didn't always get the best reviews, but we had the most fun.|Rieve|<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>}}
'''Corellia's Not Dead''' was a [[gonkrock]] [[band]] that [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competitor [[Rieve]] once fronted. The four-member group was founded by [[Musician|singer]] Rieve and [[drum]]mer [[Yemar]] in [[Hutta Town]], [[Nar Shaddaa]]. The two met while Rieve, working as a [[courier]], was delivering to the cartel Yemar worked security for, and they quickly became friends. They further connected over a shared love for the new style of [[music]] gonkrock. Within the following year, the pair added the [[Gender|female]] [[Zabrak]] low-end instrument musician [[Ully]] and the female [[human]] [[seven-string hallikset]] player [[Boornan]]. The latter musician connected her hallikset to a [[GNK-series power droid|GNK power droid]] to create a unique sound. Per Rieve's suggestion, the group took on the name Corellia's Not Dead as a reminder to [[the galaxy]] that her homeworld would always survive.<ref name="BftA" /> 

Over time, the band's reputation grew, and they played numerous gigs around Nar Shaddaa. Some of the gigs they played were unsanctioned, resulting in their performance being shut down. They gained an obsessive following due to the legendary and violent nature of their shows, but stayed in local venues. Nevertheless, bootleg recordings of their music were widely distributed on [[audiochip]]s throughout the galaxy.<ref name="BftA" /> 

Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Corellia's Not Dead headlined at [[Yoshoyan's]], drawing in a massive, packed crowd. From their first song, the crowd erupted in enjoyment. However, a heckler grabbed the device that the sound system operator [[Gortri]] used to communicate with Rieve's [[Comlink|commbud]]. He shouted into Rieve's ear, causing her to falter in her performance. His words evoked some of Rieve's trauma on her time in [[Coronet City]], causing her to spiral. When Boornan approached the singer, Rieve used [[the Force]] to push her back. The heckler continued, and Rieve erupted, using the Force without control. She parted the crowd, approached the man, and began crumbling the structure of the building. Rieve was taken out of her rage by the sight of a terrified [[Jablogian]] fan. She fled from Yoshoyan's to her home, collected her things, then went to the [[spaceport]] to leave Nar Shaddaa.<ref name="BftA" /> 

Several months later, Yemar had joined a [[Unidentified gonkrock band|new gonkrock band]], this time as the singer. His band was performing at the [[Oasis Cantina]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] when he spotted Rieve in the audience. Yemar paused the show to invite his former bandmate and friend to the stage for a special reunion. The audience, many of whom were fans of Corellia's Not Dead, enjoyed the rendition of their song "[[Forever (song)|Forever]]." After the performance, Yemar's new band had to go off-planet, but he re-established his connection with Rieve.<ref name="BftA" />
