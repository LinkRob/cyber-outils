The '''Atalia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Varada sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square H-19 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the planet [[Atalia]].<ref name="Origin" />

==Behind the scenes==
