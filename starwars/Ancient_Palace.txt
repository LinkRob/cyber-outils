The '''Ancient Palace''' was a palace located on the surface of [[Hosra]], a [[planet]] of the [[Anoat sector]]. By the time of the [[Iron Blockade]], the secretive [[Advisers|mystic]] [[Xiath]] resided in the palace, where he trained the [[Nothoiin (species)|Nothoiin]] [[noble]] [[Amrosio]] in the ways of [[the Force]].<ref name="Uprising" />
