'''Dyyl''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] with fair skin who posted a request for help in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. He asked that someone help him locate a [[star map]] that would help him get to the planet [[Bespin]] as his [[navigation computer]] was not functioning properly.<ref name="Datapad" />

A traveler visiting Black Spire Outpost agreed to help and so Dyyl directed them to find the downed [[viper probe droid]] [[V0-RK]], which he'd heard might be carrying the data he needed. After the traveler successfully retrieved the map and sent it to Dyyl he thanked them and transferred them 150 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Datapad" />
