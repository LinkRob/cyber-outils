The '''Dameron homestead'''<ref name="Flight Log">''[[Poe Dameron: Flight Log]]''</ref> was a small ranch in a [[Wetyin's Colony|colony]] on [[Yavin 4]] that was built by [[Shara Bey]] and [[Kes Dameron]] after they left their service in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] around six [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]. The homestead had a [[Koyo (tree)|koyo]] grove as well as a [[The Force|Force]]-sensitive [[Planet|tree]] that was a fragment of the [[Great Tree (Coruscant)|Great Tree]].<ref name="Before the Awakening" />
