In [[4 ABY]], the [[Galactic Empire]] held an '''auction''' at the [[Mygeeto]] [[Mygeeto Auction House|Auction House]] to auction off [[starship]]s that had been expropriated from [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] sympathizers. The auction sold several starships including a [[H-2 executive shuttle|Theta-class H-2 executive star shuttle]], an [[E-wing starfighter|E-wing escort fighter]], a [[Koro-2 all-environment Exodrive airspeeder]], and an [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor]]. The auction was attended by several individuals including [[Ignacio Wortan]], [[Wick Cooper]], [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]] the [[Hutt]], and the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]]. The Freemakers were on a [[Mission to obtain Embersteel wings|mission]] to procure the Theta star shuttle's [[embersteel]] wings, which they needed to [[Quest to build the Arrowhead|build]] the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']], a starship capable of turning the tide of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. For that mission, Kordi impersonated the [[Clawdite]] princess [[Alo Kortessi]].

==Prelude==
In [[4 ABY]], the [[Galactic Empire]] held an auction on [[Mygeeto]] to sell off starships that had been seized from [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] sympathizers. Several items were on sale at the auction including a [[H-2 executive shuttle|Theta-class H-2 executive star shuttle]], an [[E-wing starfighter|E-wing escort fighter]], a [[Koro-2 all-environment Exodrive airspeeder]], and an [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor]]. Needing a pair of [[embersteel]] blades to [[Quest to build the Arrowhead|build]] the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']], [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] managed to convince the Alliance leadership to loan them 75,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to purchase a [[H-2 executive shuttle|Theta-class H-2 executive star shuttle]] with embersteel wings.<ref name="TEB" />

==The auction==
The auction on Mygeeto was attended by several wealthy individuals including [[Ignacio Wortan]], [[Wick Cooper]], and [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]] the [[Hutt]]. Graballa wanted to visit the auction in order to recruit investors for his [[Graballa's Beachside Resort and Buffet|planned beachside resort and buffet]]. Another guest was the [[Clawdite]] [[princess]] [[Alo Kortessi]]. Taking advantage of the Clawdite shape-shifting ability, Kordi disguised herself as the princess while her brothers [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan]] and [[Zander Freemaker]] pretended to be her servants. Kordi managed to use her royal status to force the [[stormtrooper]] sentries to let her and her entourage inside. When the real Kortessi arrived, the stormtroopers mistook her for an impostor and arrested her instead.<ref name="TEB" />

Per tradition, the nobles lounge in the luxury terrace while their servants carried out the actual bidding. The auction was run by a [[Cerean]] manager. In Kordi's absence, Zander was placed in charge of the [[Mission to obtain Embersteel wings|mission]]. Zander gave in to his impulses and bidded for the Jedi light interceptor, believing that the ship was worth 30 credits. To his chagrin, Zander learned that the ship was actually worth 30,000 credits, leaving them short of the money needed to purchase the ''Theta''-class shuttle. Meanwhile, Kordi was forced to ward off Graballa's advances. Graballa mistook her for "Kortessi" and sought to recruit her as an investor for his beachside resort.<ref name="TEB" />

Unable to return the light interceptor, the Freemakers' situation was complicated further when one of Graballa's [[bounty hunter]]s [[Raam]] accidentally bidded for the ''Theta''-class shuttle. Kordi was furious with Zander while Graballa was furious with Raam and Baash for their incompetence. Kordi later traveled to Graballa's starship ''[[Rancor's Fist]]'' in an attempt to swap the Jedi light interceptor for the shuttle. Believing that Princess Kortessi's parents were rich, Graballa instead kidnapped her. This led Zander and Rowan to undertake a rescue mission. Kordi managed to free herself by posing as Graballa's business adviser and managed to escape with the help of her brothers. The Freemakers also managed to obtain the shuttle but the Jedi interceptor was lost during a clash with Graballa's leading bounty hunter [[Dengar]].<ref name="TEB" />
