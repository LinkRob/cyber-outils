The '''BD-680 emergency shuttle''' was a model of emergency [[shuttle]] produced by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]. The [[JT-731 Broadhorn transport]]s of the [[Hidden Path]] carried BD-680 shuttles, and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] used one to lure [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] away from the Hidden Path's ships as they fled [[Jabiim]], flying it down to [[Unidentified barren moon|one of]] Jabiim's [[moon]]s and causing Vader to [[Fight on a barren moon|follow]] him.
