"'''Echo Support'''" was a [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] that served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Following the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]], they served as [[Echo Support]] as part of [[Echo Squadron]] and participated in a [[Battle at Fostar Haven|battle]] at [[Fostar Haven]], where they flew a [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]].<ref name="Squadrons" />
