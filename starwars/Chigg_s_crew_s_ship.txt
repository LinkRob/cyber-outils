A '''ship''' was operated by the [[Chigg's crew|crew]] of the [[Abednedo]] [[Chigg]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. At one point, the crew traveled to the [[planet]] [[Galjaero]] to acquire an exotic [[fruit]] but their [[Starship|ship]] got stuck in mud. With help from [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] from the planet [[Tenoo]], the crew were able to recover their ship and traveled with the [[Jedi]] to the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]].<ref name="Stuck in the Muck" />
