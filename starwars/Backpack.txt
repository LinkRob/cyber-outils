'''Backpacks''',<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref> also known as '''satchels''', '''knapsacks''',<ref name="AToC">''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]''</ref> or '''rucksacks''',<ref name="SC 103">{{SkeletonCrew|Very Interesting, As an Astrogation Problem}}</ref> were portable storage compartments, devices, or rigs with two straps that let them be worn on users' backs. Backpacks were used in such notable occasions such as when [[Luke Skywalker]] used his backpack during his [[Jedi training]] on [[Dagobah]].<ref name="Episode V" />

Backpacks were used also throughout the [[Clone Wars]] by [[clone trooper]]s and [[Jedi]], notably when [[Ahsoka Tano]] used a backpack as a way to store [[Rotta]] the [[Hutt]]let.<ref name="TCWFilm">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> Several backpacks like [[Crosshair]]'s<ref name="The Outpost">{{TBB|The Outpost}}</ref> and the [[Clone X trooper]]s stored their [[773 Firepuncher sniper rifle]]s on their backpacks by taking the [[scope]] and long [[barrel]] off.<ref name="Infiltration">{{TBB|Infiltration (episode)}}</ref> [[Bodhi Rook]] wore a cable-dispensing backpack during the [[Battle of Scarif]] when he tied the [[Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle|''Zeta''-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle]] ''[[SW-0608]]'' into the Imperial communications tower relay.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

The [[First Order Army|First Order's]] [[D-93 Incinerator flamethrower|D-93 Incinerator]] had a set of fuel tanks fitted as a backpack. Another example includes Heavy Weapons troopers, Imperial Cold Weather Assault troopers, and Stormtroopers which all utilized various survival backpacks.{{Fact}}

The [[Resistance]] had backpacks designed for scouting.<ref name="Last Jedi VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Finn]] used one with his [[EL-16 blaster rifle]] strapped to it.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>
