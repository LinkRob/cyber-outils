'''Fortombro''' was a [[pirate]] who resided in [[the galaxy]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Fortombro hijacked an entire shipment of Imperial [[mining droid]]s, and the pirate reprogrammed the [[droid]]s into [[Death|deadly]] fighters. Only one droid, [[RN-18]], survived the process, so Fortombro began renting the droid to assorted customers. At the [[time]] of the [[Iron Blockade]], Fortombro rented RN-18 to a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] who operated in the [[Anoat sector]].<ref name="RN-18">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "RN-18"</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Fortombro was created for ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', a [[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] released in [[2015]]. In ''Uprising'', the character was mentioned in the description for RN-18,<ref name="RN-18" /> a Crew Member added in a [[2016]] update.<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|KabamRPG|status/693156122727956481|[[Kabam]] RPG|quote=A potent addition to any crew, the deadly RN-18 is a mining droid with full combat protocols.|archivefile=File:RN18_Tweet.png}}</ref>
