'''''Evil Has Its Eyes On You''''' was a [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] created by [[Commission for the Protection of the Republic|COMPOR]] [[artist]] [[Donclode Onstruss]] for the [[Galactic Republic]]. It depicts the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] firebrand leader [[Count]] [[Dooku]] in the center of the poster alongside the superimposed image of the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander]] of the Confederacy of Independent Systems' [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]], the infamous [[General]] [[Grievous]]' [[Armor|helmet]] looming from behind in an ominous manner, as well as yellow [[High Galactic]] Font declaring the word "Evil" up top and "Has Its Eyes On You" at the bottom, as well as much smaller white font in [[Aurebesh]] with the [[Republic crest]] at the bottom.<ref name="Propaganda" />
