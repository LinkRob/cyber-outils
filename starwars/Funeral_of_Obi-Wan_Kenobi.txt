A faux '''funeral''' was held for [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] during the [[Clone Wars]], as part of the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council's]] plot to send him undercover as the [[bounty hunter]] [[Rako Hardeen]].<ref name="Deception" />

Amongst those in attendance was Kenobi's former [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], Skywalker's Padwan [[Ahsoka Tano]], Jedi Master [[Plo Koon]] and [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] of [[Mandalore]] who could be heard crying.<ref name="Deception" />
