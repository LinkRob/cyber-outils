'''Blowtorches''',<ref name="Zero Friends AD">{{SkeletonCrew|Zero Friends Again}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> simply known as '''torches''', were a handheld device that produced a flame.<ref name="Zero Friends" /> Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SC Date">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> the [[human]] [[youngling]] [[Wim]] helped another human youngling [[KB]] replace a corroded [[micro-fuse]] in her [[cybernetics|augs]] by using a blowtorch to melt [[servomotor]] wires in a cup to create a new one.<ref name="Zero Friends" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blowtorches first appeared in [[Canon]] in the novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]] and released in [[2015]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>

They first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends]] continuity as [[droid]] mounted weapons.<ref name="Kotor II">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]''</ref>
