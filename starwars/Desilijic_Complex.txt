The '''Desilijic Complex''' was a townhouse owned by the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] in [[Mos Eisley]], [[Tatooine]]. Located at the near the corner of [[Spacers Row]] and [[Paradise Road]], it had a dome-shaped entrance, and much of the complex was built over an extensive system of caves and grottoes.<ref name="CL 2016" />

==History==
During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the area surrounding the townhouse was controlled by the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] and known as the [[Hutt Cartel District (Mos Eisley)|Hutt Cartel District]]. During the same time period the [[merchant]] [[Shenn Balat]] and [[contract broker]] [[Clemah Mudoo]] operated out of the entry to the house. The townhouse had a vault on its lower levels which required at least [[keycard]]s to open.
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] stole the vault keycards for the cartel members [[Bib Fortuna]], [[Dokk Strassi]] and [[Garfalaquox]] and used it to open the vault. From the vault, Vess stole an "Extra Refund" [[Shift Token]] as well as the materials to apply a "[[Boonta]] Brawler" paint scheme to a [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Desilijic Complex was first mentioned in the [[canon]] continuity in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']].<ref name="CL 2016" /> It made its first appearance in canon in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', where it was simply identified as Jabba's Townhouse.<ref name="Outlaws" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends]] continuity the complex was first mentioned in the [[1992]] junior novel ''[[Zorba the Hutt's Revenge]]''.
