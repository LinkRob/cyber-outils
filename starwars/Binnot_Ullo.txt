'''Binnot Ullo''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Mirialan]] [[Gender|male]], who was a member of the [[Path of the Open Hand]]. Ullo hated his own Force-sensitivity and denied it, as per his beliefs as a member of the Path. In his youth, he was a friend of [[Axel Greylark]], with whom he developed a brother-like bond.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date2">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> he met with [[Abda]], one of the [[Elecia Zeveron|Mother's]] [[Children of the Open Hand|Children]], on the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]]. After Greylark briefly worked with the Path once more on E'ronoh<ref name="Convergence" /> but betrayed their plans, Ullo developed a hate for the man whom he had once called a brother. All the same, under the Mother's orders, he helped free Greylark from a [[Republic Correctional Facility]] and brought him to the [[Path compound]] on the planet [[Dalna]], where Greylark met with the Mother. As per the Mother's plans to install Greylark as a pro-Path galactic leader, Greylark sent a message to his birth mother, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Kyong Greylark]], which she responded to by stepping down as chancellor. Brought in to watch Kyong's response at Axel's request, Ullo took joy in hurting his former friend when the angered Mother allowed him to. He destroyed Greylark's beloved droid [[QN-1]] and forced him to fight as part of the Path in the [[Night of Sorrow|Battle of Dalna]]. Ullo joined [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Path of the Closed Fist]] for the battle.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

During the battle, Ullo gave Greylark a new chance to side with the Path by executing a young member who did not want to fight, handing over a blaster which he had secretly removed the cartridge from. Greylark instead tried and, due to the lack of a cartridge, failed to kill Ullo, who laughed at the attempt and prepared to shoot Greylark, whom he described as a failed soldier instead of a brother. Instead, an explosion erupted that seemed to kill Greylark for him, with Ullo angrily bidding him goodbye. In truth, Greylark survived and freed the captured [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]], with whom he confronted Ullo in the Path caves at the end of the battle. Seemingly trying to flee, Ullo displayed several emotions upon seeing Greylark&mdash;showing surprise, shock, confusion, and then settling on relief&mdash;and tried to act innocent in regards to the attempted murder. While Greylark saw Ullo's remarks as lies, Ullo tried to connect with him as a brother and claimed they could create a new future, which tempted Greylark until he thought of QN-1. In what he suspected would be his final moments as the caves collapsed, Ullo tried to murder Greylark, who surprised his supposed brother by punching him twice. Greylark pushed [[Binnot Ullo's poison glove]] into Ullo, killing the Path leader with his own weapon.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
