A '''tooka''' was owned by the [[Aphra family]] as a pet by [[18 BBY]]. The [[tooka]] often defecated on the carpet of the family's [[Aphra family home|home]] on the [[Second Moon of Thrinittik]]. That [[Standard year|year]], the [[cat]] [[Sleep|slept]] next to the [[Gender|girl]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] as she packed for a trip with her [[Lona Aphra|mother]]. Around [[1 ABY]], Aphra recalled her pet tooka while speaking with the [[crime lord]] [[0-0-0]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Son-tuul]] about the [[gene]]tically modified tooka [[Flufto]].

==Biography==
===Living on the Second Moon of Thrinittik===
{{Quote|So what do ''you'' want to do? Stay here and play with your tooka? Or go on an adventure with your dad?''"<br />"''Adventure!''"<br />"''Then adventure it shall be!|Korin and Chelli Lona Aphra|<ref name="Doctor Aphra">''[[Doctor Aphra: An Audiobook Original]]''</ref>}}
[[File:AphraFamilyTookaSleeping-2016DoctorAphra32.png|thumb|left|270px|The Aphra family tooka slept as Chelli (left) picked her things.]]
By [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The flashback in ''[[Doctor Aphra: An Audiobook Original]]'' in which the [[tooka]] is mentioned takes place when [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] is six [[Standard year|years]] old. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates Aphra's [[Reproduction|birth]] to [[24 BBY]], the scene must take place in [[18 BBY]].</ref> the [[Aphra family]] owned a [[tooka]] as a pet while they lived together in a [[Aphra family home|house]] within the [[University Quarter]] of the [[Second Moon of Thrinittik]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 32" /> in the [[Colonies]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Thrinittik system]]</ref> The [[cat]] was prone to defecating on the family's carpet in the house.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 32" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Gender|girl]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] overheard her [[Parent|father]] [[Korin Aphra|Korin]] arguing with her mother [[Lona Aphra|Lona]] as Korin wanted to take the girl with him to the [[Fifth Moon of Thrinittik]] to recover [[Ordu Aspectu records|records]] of the [[Ordu Aspectu]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra" />&mdash;a splinter cell of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> After her father asked her if she wanted to accompany him or stay at home and play with the tooka, Aphra chose to go with Korin.<ref name="Doctor Aphra" />

One evening<ref name="Doctor Aphra 32" /> later that year,<ref name="Date 2">The flashbacks of [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 32|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 32]] that take place on the [[Second Moon of Thrinittik]] occur on the [[Standard day|day]] that [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and [[Lona Aphra]] leave the [[moon]] for [[Arbiflux]]. ''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 32 also establishes the two lived on Arbiflux for two [[Standard year|years]] before Lona was [[Death|killed]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates Lona's passing to [[16 BBY]], the flashback scene on the Second Moon of Thrinittik must take place in [[18 BBY]].</ref> after Korin neglected to keep watch over Chelli, she cut her finger. Lona arrived and told<ref name="Doctor Aphra 32" /> her [[Marriage|husband]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra" /> what had happened and that he should have been watching over her. In response, Korin told Lona he could have found a way to restore the Ordu Apsectu, to which Lona sarcastically remarked that if they were restored, the Aspectu could stop the cat from soiling on the carpet. Afterward, Lona told Chelli to pack for a trip, and as she did, the family's tooka [[Sleep|slept]] next to her on the carpet.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 32" />

In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date 3">The events of ''[[Doctor Aphra: An Audiobook Original]]'' are presented as a recording [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] made beginning directly before the events of [[Darth Vader (2015) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 3]] and concluding during the events of [[Darth Vader (2015) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 25]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the aforementioned [[Star Wars comic books|comics]] to [[0 ABY]].</ref> now a rogue [[archaeologist]] and [[doctor]], Chelli created a recording for her former lover&mdash;the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]]&mdash;that she intended Starros to hear after her death. During the recording, Aphra recounted a number of events in her life, including when her father had offered her the choice of joining him on a mission to the Fifth Moon of Thrinittik or staying to play with the tooka.<ref name="Doctor Aphra" />

Around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date 4">{{Aphra2016Date|15}}</ref> while working for the [[droid]] [[crime lord]] [[0-0-0]], Aphra [[Cloning|cloned]] a tooka<ref name="Doctor Aphra 15" /> into a batch of sixty<ref name="Doctor Aphra 16">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 16|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 16]]</ref> [[gene]]tically modified cats<ref name="Doctor Aphra 17">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 17|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 17]]</ref> called [[Flufto]]. While on<ref name="Doctor Aphra 15" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra" /> [[Son-tuul]] carrying one of the Fluftos, 0-0-0 asked the archaeologist why she was carrying him. In response, Aphra informed the droid of her cloning and mentioned she had kept a tooka as a pet before. The mention of the tooka caused 0-0-0 to return to an earlier point he had made about obsessing over one's origins.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 15" />

==Personality and traits==
The Aphra family's [[Color|orange]]-pelted tooka had paler [[Hair|fur]] on their face, front [[Limb|legs]], and the tips of their [[ear]]s. The tooka had a tendency to defecate on the family's carpet.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 32" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Aphra family's tooka was first mentioned in the [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 15|fifteenth issue]] of the [[2016]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Kieron Gillen]] and [[Simon Spurrier|Si Spurrier]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 15">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 15|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 15]]</ref> on [[December 20]], [[2017]].<ref name="Marvel 15">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/65306/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2016_15|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016) #15}}</ref> The tooka later made its first appearance, in flashbacks, in the series' [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 32|thirty-second issue]]. The issue was written by Spurrier, illustrated by [[Caspar Wijngaard]], and published by Marvel<ref name="Doctor Aphra 32" /> on [[May 8]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel 32">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/71451/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2016_32|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016) #32}}</ref>
