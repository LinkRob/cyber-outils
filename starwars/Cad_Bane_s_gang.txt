A '''gang''' was active<ref name="TOTU 104" /> during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="Date" /> The group was originally led by [[Lazlo]],<ref name="TOTU 104" /> after whose [[death]]<ref name="TOTU 105">{{TOTU|A Good Turn}}</ref> [[Cad Bane]] emerged as the new leader.<ref name="TOTU 106>{{TOTU|One Good Deed}}</ref> Other gang members included [[Arin]],<ref name="TOTU 105" /> [[Unidentified Bith (Cad Bane)|a Bith]], [[Tay Grutty]], and [[Vak Lako]].<ref name="TOTU 105" />

Around between [[62 BBY]] and [[33 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Cad Bane was detained by<ref name="TOTU 105" /> his<ref>{{Databank|cad-bane|Cad Bane}}</ref> former friend<ref name="TOTU 106" /> [[Niro]], a local [[Unidentified police force|police]] officer.<ref name="TOTU 105" /> Niro subsequently became involved with Arin, Bane's love interest, and raised Bane's and her [[Youngling|offspring]] [[Isaac]] as<ref name="TOTU 106" /> his<ref name="TOTU 104" /> own. By the [[time]] Bane was released from [[prison]], Arin had passed away. During the ensuing confrontation between the now-released Bane and Niro, the former [[Death|killed]] the latter, sparing the life of his biological child. Before the [[Showdown in an unidentified city|showdown]], Lako and Grutty had been killed during a shootout as they covered Bane as his [[sniper]]s, and the [[Bith]] had been died some time prior, leaving Cad Bane as the only surviving gang member.<ref name="TOTU 106" />
