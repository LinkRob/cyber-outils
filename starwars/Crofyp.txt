'''Crofyp''' was a [[Kilji]] [[general]] who served the [[Kilji Illumine]], the Kilji [[species]]' nation, as the [[Commanding officer|commander]] of a [[Crofyp's task force|task force]] consisting of his [[flagship]], the [[Kilji war cruiser]] ''[[Anvil]]'', and [[Colonel]] [[Tildnis]]'s [[Kilji picket cruiser]] [[Hammer (Kilji picket cruiser)|''Hammer'']]. In [[18 BBY]], the [[Grysk]] operative known as "[[Jixtus]]" dispatched Crofyp's [[task force]] to the [[Unknown Regions]]' [[Rapacc system]], tasking the Kiljis with capturing a [[The Magys's people|group]] of [[refugee]]s hiding on the [[planet]] [[Rapacc]]. After the Illumine task force arrived at Rapacc, it was [[Attack on Rapacc|confronted]] by the [[Paccosh]]-[[Thief|commandeered]] [[Nikardun blockade frigate]] [[Aelos (frigate)|''Aelos'']]. Crofyp threatened [[Commander]] [[Uingali foar Marocsaa]], the [[blockade frigate]]'s [[captain]], to allow his forces to retrieve the refugees they sought. Marocsaa refused, however, prompting the general to prepare a cross fire attack on the ''Aelos''.

After the ''Anvil'' moved past the ''Aelos'' along its starboard and the ''Hammer'' did likewise along the [[frigate]]'s portside, the [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]'' arrived in the [[Star system|system]], firing two [[starship]]-disabling [[plasma sphere]] [[weapon]]s at Crofyp's flagship before joining the confrontation in the inner system. The [[Chiss]] commander, [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," commanded Crofyp to move clear of the ''Aelos'', angering the general, who had his vessels fire [[spectrum laser]] salvos at the ''Springhawk''; however, the plasma spheres disabled the ''Anvil'' and several Chiss [[breacher]] [[missile]]s destroyed the [[war cruiser]], killing Crofyp while the ''Hammer'' fled.

==Biography==
===Confronting the Paccosh&hellip;===
{{Quote|Enough. The Nikardun are defeated, Pathfinder, and will never again arise. Speak lies and soothings when it is only your time that you waste. We are the Kiljis, and we will take what we want. Move from our path, Nikardun, or die. Here is the enlightened truth. Yiv did not serve with Jixtus, but ''under'' Jixtus, as a slave serves under a master. Nor are the Kiljis ''associates'' of Jixtus, but his masters. We use him as a guide and adviser to our voyages, nothing more.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Then you do not in fact serve with Jixtus, as the Pathfinder said?''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''The Pathfinder told you what you wanted to hear. We use him as a guide and adviser to our voyages, nothing more. He identifies which of the ignorant peoples of this region require enlightenment. For now, we merely require that the refugees who fled from their world and now hide here among the Paccosh be handed over to us.|General Crofyp and Uingali foar Marocsaa|translated=1|audio=Crofyp2.flac|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
Crofyp was a [[Kilji]] who served the [[Kilji Illumine]], the nation of the Kilji [[species]], as a [[general]], [[Commanding officer|commanding]] a [[Crofyp's task force|task force]] that consisted of his [[flagship]], the [[Kilji war cruiser]] ''[[Anvil]]'', and [[Colonel]] [[Tildnis]]'s [[Kilji picket cruiser]] [[Hammer (Kilji picket cruiser)|''Hammer'']]. At some point, the general was enlightened to the [[Kilji way of order and enlightenment]], the Illumine's [[religion]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Grysk]] agent known as "[[Jixtus]]," who had allied himself with the Illumine's ruler, [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]], sent Crofyp's [[task force]] to the [[Unknown Regions]]' [[Rapacc system]], tasking the Kilji force with capturing a [[The Magys's people|group]] of [[refugee]]s that had taken refuge on the [[planet]] [[Rapacc]] and bring them to a rendezvous point Crofyp would specify. Jixtus also hired [[Pathfinders (Navigators' Guild)|Pathfinder]] [[navigator]]s [[Qilori]] and [[Sarsh]] to navigate the ''Anvil'' and ''Hammer'' respectively.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

[[File:Hyperspace-TFA.jpg|thumb|left|300px|After Pathfinder Qilori stopped the ''Anvil''{{'s}} hyperspace (pictured) journey, General Crofyp spoke with the Pathfinder.]]
During the Kiljis' [[hyperspace]] journey to the Rapacc [[Star system|system]], the [[picket cruiser]] slowed down, prompting Qilori to stop the [[war cruiser]]'s voyage to allow Tildnis's [[cruiser]] to catch up. Annoyed by the sudden stop, Crofyp asked why the navigator had ceased his flagship's voyage. The Pathfinder answered Crofyp's question and the Kilji deemed the answer incorrect and believed that Tildnis's cruiser would pass the ''Anvil'' by unnoticed; however, the ''Hammer'' appeared near the ''Anvil'' after fifteen [[Standard minute|minutes]], proving Qilori right. After the navigator resumed the Kiljis' journey under Crofyp's directive, the [[Starship|vessels]] arrived at the Rapacc system at the same [[time]], as Crofyp desired.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

The Kilji task force was confronted by the [[Paccosh]]-[[Thief|commandeered]] [[Nikardun blockade frigate]] [[Aelos (frigate)|''Aelos'']], which was [[orbit]]ing Rapacc. The ''Aelos''{{'s}} captain, [[Commander]] [[Uingali foar Marocsaa]], questioned Crofyp on his target and identity. The general introduced his task force and purpose in the Rapacc system, threatening the [[blockade frigate]]'s captain to allow his forces to continue to Rapacc so that he could capture the refugees they sought.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

However, the Paccosh did not move, prompting Crofyp to threaten to destroy the ''Aelos''. Seeking to end the conflict peacefully, Qilori, believing Marocsaa was a member of the [[Nikardun]] species, claimed that with the Kiljis' arrival, the Nikardun could arise once again with Jixtus's guidance. However, his Kilji general cut him off and remarked that Qilori's claim was false, confronting the Paccosh commander verbally and instructing Marocsaa to move aside or die. As the Paccosh did not comply with his demands, Crofyp had his [[Kilji vassal]]s&mdash;followers of the Kilji way&mdash;prepare a cross fire attack on the ''Aelos''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

===&hellip;and dying at the hands of the Chiss===
{{Quote|Unidentified ships, this is Senior Captain Thrawn of the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet warship ''Springhawk''. You stand near our prey. Move clear of the Nikardun blockade frigate ''Aelos'' or suffer the humiliation of becoming collateral damage.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Who are you to speak of prey? And who are you to give orders to warships of the Illumine?''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''I already gave you my name. Do you need me to repeat it?''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Your name will die with you, unenlightened fool.|Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo and General Crofyp|audio=Crofyp3.flac|translated=1|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
[[File:Chaos-Rising-OOP-Art.png|thumb|right|300px|General Crofyp faced Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo at Rapacc.]]
Under Crofyp's directive, [[Vassal One (Anvil)|Vassal One]] moved the ''Anvil'' along the [[frigate]]'s starboard, [[Vassal Four (Anvil)|Vassal Four]] signaled Tildnis to do likewise along the Nikardun vessel's portside, and [[Vassal Three]] prepared to destroy the Paccosh when they moved to attack. Once the Kilji ships were in position, the [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]'' arrived in the outer Rapacc system and fired two vessel-disabling [[plasma sphere]] weapons at the starboard of Crofyp's flagship. The [[Chiss]] vessel then performed a [[microjump]] and joined the confrontation in the inner system. The ''Springhawk''{{'s}} commander, [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], who was also known by his [[core name]] of "Thrawn," instructed Crofyp to move clear of the ''Aelos'', which Thrawn classified as his prey.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

Furious upon an unenlightened being's orders to move clear of the frigate, the general had his task force fire three [[spectrum laser]] salvos at the Chiss following his ships' turn to [[Unidentified maneuver (Nakirre)|face]] the newcomer. As the Chiss began to trade fire with the Kiljis, the plasma spheres slammed into the ''Anvil''{{'s}} starboard, disabling its weaponry, [[sensor]]s, and systems. Qilori attempted to warn Crofyp about the attack; however, the general did not see it.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

While Crofyp moved the ''Anvil'' to shield the ''Hammer'' from the Chiss attack, Qilori moved to escape the battle in an [[escape pod]]; however, the ''Anvil''{{'s}} commander noticed Qilori's movement and, while speaking with Tildnis via [[Comm unit|comm]], told the navigator that he would not flee as those enlightened to the Kilji way stood, fought, and prevailed. However, the Pathfinder did not care, calling Crofyp a fool and rushing to the escape pods while Crofyp pointed a large, black weapon at him to threaten the navigator for fleeing. Qilori escaped before the ''Anvil''{{'s}} [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] exploded when the ''Springhawk''{{'s}} [[breacher]] [[missile]]s hit it, [[Death|killing]] Crofyp. The ''Hammer'' fled to the Unknown Regions' [[Sunrise system|"Sunrise" system]], where it was [[Sunrise incident|destroyed]] by Thrawn and the Paccosh.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Are you deaf as well as unenlightened? Like all others, they walk in darkness. We are here to take them to a place where they can be enlightened.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Can you not enlighten them here? They are useful servants to us. We don't wish to lose them.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''The wishes of the unenlightened are of no importance. Do you move aside, or do you die?|General Crofyp and Uingali foar Marocsaa|translated=1|audio=Crofyp4.flac|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
[[File:Steadfast-Thrawn6.png|thumb|left|300px|General Crofyp believed that his forces should not flee after the Chiss heavy cruiser (one such vessel pictured) ''Springhawk'' disabled the ''Anvil''{{'s}} starboard side.]]
As an enlightened Kilji, Crofyp held great contempt for those that "walked in darkness," the unenlightened. He deemed their wishes unimportant and did not accept their opinion, treating them with disdain. According to Qilori, Crofyp had an overinflated opinion of himself, which prompted him to dismiss the unenlightened's concerns, such as Qilori's warning about the Chiss attack. While speaking to Marocsaa, the general claimed that the Kiljis were Jixtus's masters, not the other way around, which was false, as Jixtus commanded Nakirre and the Illumine. The general called the Paccosh commander deaf after Marocsaa asked what the Kilji wanted with the refugees.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

The Kilji was impatient and short-tempered, threatening Qilori with the Pathfinders' enlightenment when he attempted to tell the general that a cross fire attack on the ''Aelos'' could result in significant damage to his task force. He believed that instead of retreating after the ''Springhawk'' disabled the ''Anvil''{{'s}} starboard, the Kiljis should stand, fight, and prevail. The Kilji was short and had wrinkly, rubbery skin that Qilori considered utterly repulsive. When the ''Springhawk''{{'s}} [[Mid Captain]] [[Ufsa'mak'ro]] "Samakro" heard Crofyp's voice via comm, he noted that it was rich in fury and arrogance.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Skills and abilities==
During his attack on Rapacc, Crofyp spoke the [[Kilji language]] with the ''Anvil''{{'s}} [[Sensor officer|sensor operator]], [[Vassal Two (Anvil)|Vassal Two]]. When speaking to Qilori, the Paccosh, and Thrawn, he spoke the [[Taarja]] trade [[language]], chewing words like sour [[fruit]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
