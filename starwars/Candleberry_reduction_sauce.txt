'''Candleberry reduction sauce''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|sauce]] that was served with [[groat chop]] at the [[Bistro De Lothal]] [[restaurant]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]<ref name="ERJ" /> by [[5 BBY]].<ref name="Date">The final entry in ''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]'' describes the events of the first episode of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' television show, ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' establishes that the events of ''Spark of Rebellion'' take place in the [[Standard year|year]] [[5 BBY]], and so the rest of the journal must have been completed sometime prior to that point.</ref> The chop and sauce were available as the third course of a four-course meal of the [[Standard day|day]] that the eatery served for 125 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], the menu for which was advertised on fliers produced by the establishment. The young [[thief]] [[Ezra Bridger]] mentioned the sauce in his [[Rebel Journal|journal]],<ref name="ERJ" /> published no earlier than the same [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="Date" />

==Behind the scenes==
Candleberry reduction sauce was first mentioned in ''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and released in [[2014]].<ref name="ERJ" /> [[Candleberry|Candleberries]] were a type of [[fruit]] that was previously mentioned in ''[[Yoda: Dark Rendezvous]]'', a [[2004]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel by [[Sean Stewart]].<ref name="YDR">''[[Yoda: Dark Rendezvous]]''</ref>
