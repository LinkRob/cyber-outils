'''Cat Miin'''<ref name="CVD" /> was a [[Gossam]]<ref name="Episode II" /> who served as [[Presidente]] [[Shu Mai]]'s [[aide]]<ref name="CVD" /> during the [[Separatist Crisis]] and the subsequent [[Clone Wars]] as a member of the [[Commerce Guild]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Episode II" />

In [[22 BBY]], Miin accompanied Shu Mai to the Separatist bastion of [[Geonosis]]. There she witnessed her mistress dedicating the Guild's [[Battle droid|droid]] armies to the cause of the Confederacy under the [[Sith]] [[Lord]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. Three years later, in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas" /> Miin was [[Death|slain]] on [[Mustafar]] [[Mission to Mustafar|along with the]] [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], Dooku's killer and replacement.<ref name="Episode III" />
