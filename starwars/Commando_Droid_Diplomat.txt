'''Commando Droid Diplomats''' were specialized models of [[BX-series droid commando]]s specialized for [[diplomat]]ic occasions.<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> They differed from regular commando [[droid]]s with their brown, yellow and blue colored plating and they wielded [[E-5 blaster rifle]]s.<ref name="Friend in Need" />

==History==
[[File:CommandoDroidDiplomatEscort-AFiN.png|thumb|left|300px|Commando Droid Diplomats escort Lux Bonteri onto a Separatist landing craft.]]
A group of Commando Droid Diplomats were present at a [[Confederate–Republic peace conference]] on the [[Mandalore|Mandalorian]] [[capital]] [[Sundari]]<ref name="Friend in Need" /> in [[20 BBY]].<ref name="FINDate">{{TCWcanondates|414}}</ref> During the conference the [[junior senator]] [[Lux Bonteri]] accused the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederacy]]'s leader [[Count]] [[Dooku]] to be responsible for the [[Attack on Confederate people|murder]] of [[Mina Bonteri|his mother]]. On the order of the Separatist [[senator]]s, three [[BX-series droid commando|droids]] escorted him out of the [[Grand Salon|throne room]] to [[C-9979 landing craft|a starship]], where they were ordered by Dooku through [[hologram]] to [[Death penalty|execute]] Bonteri, but were defeated by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. The remaining [[Battle droid|droids]] chased Tano and Bonteri to the [[Sundari shipping docks]], but were stopped by [[Captain]] [[Taggart (Senate Guard)|Taggart's]] [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Senate Guard]]s. Shortly after, one captured Commando Droid Diplomat was among the [[droid]]s used for target practice at the [[Death Watch]]'s [[Carlac Death Watch camp|camp]] on the planet [[Carlac]] and another [[Monster droid|cobbled together droid]] also used the torso of the model. After Bonteri, Tano and [[Anakin Skywalker|her Master]]'s [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] arrived at the camp, the [[Mandalorian]]s ordered R2-D2 to repair the droids. Later after Tano and Bonteri had [[Battle on Carlac|been captured]] by Death Watch, the astromech convinced the droids to rise up against their masters to cover the trio's escape.<ref name="Friend in Need" />

Later that same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="FrontRunnersDate">{{TCWcanondates|503}}</ref> two Commando Droid Diplomats were stationed inside the [[Unifar Temple]], in the planet [[Onderon]]'s capital [[city]] of [[Iziz]]. The 
droids escorted [[Ramsis Dendup]], the former [[king]] of Onderon to Unifar Temple's [[throne]] to speak with Separatist puppet [[Sanjay Rash]], Dendup's successor as king of Onderon. Later, a group of Commando Droid Diplomats defended [[Iziz]]'s [[Onderon power generator|power generator]] against attacking [[Onderon rebels]].<ref name="Front Runners">{{TCW|Front Runners}}</ref>

In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="DDDate">{{TCWcanondates|DD}}</ref> several<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> Commando Droid Diplomats<ref name="Legacyvideo">{{SWYouTube|vnH1JKr4o_8|Star Wars: The Clone Wars Legacy|sw_url=video/the-clone-wars-legacy}}</ref> guarded [[General]] [[Grievous]] and Dooku during Dooku's acceptance speech for the [[Raxian Humanitarian Award]] on a balcony on [[Raxus Secundus]].<ref name="Dark Disciple" />
