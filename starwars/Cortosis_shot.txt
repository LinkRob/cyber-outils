A '''cortosis shot''' was a [[Slugthrower/Legends|slugthrower]] projectile that was made out of the metal [[Cortosis/Legends|cortosis]] and used by [[Cad Bane/Legends|Cad Bane]] in [[21 BBY/Legends|21 BBY]]. This bullet, upon striking a [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] blade, would cause the blade to short out, giving the shooter an advantage over the lightsaber wielder. However, Bane stated that the bullets were expensive and hard on the slugthrower's [[Barrel/Legends|barrel]].
