The '''Barayfe system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Alchenaut sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It was located in grid square L-16 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Barayfe]].<ref name="Origin" />
