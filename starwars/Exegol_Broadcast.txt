The '''Exegol Broadcast'''<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> was a transmission broadcasting of the voice of the [[Death|late]] [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], who was in truth the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious, was delivered across [[the galaxy]]<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> It was a message of revenge; no longer concerned with hiding his [[Sith]] identity following his [[Cloning|resurrection]],<ref name="TSWB" /> Sidious declared that the [[Standard day|day]] of the Sith was imminent, and that victory was near. At this [[time]], the [[galaxy]] was engulfed in a [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], led respectively by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] and [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="TROS EE" />

Ren subsequently undertook a [[Search for Exegol|search for answers]], leading him to a [[Mission to Mustafar (First Order)|mission]] to [[Mustafar]] in search of a [[Vader's wayfinder|path]] to the reborn [[Emperor]] to confront this threat and secure his power.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
Emperor Palpatine's broadcast was first heard in [[Fortnite X Star Wars|''Fortnite'' X ''Star Wars'']], a collaborative event that took place in the virtual world of ''[[Fortnite]]''.<ref name="Fortnite">{{WebCite|url=https://www.polygon.com/fortnite/2019/12/20/21031513/star-wars-the-rise-of-skywalker-fortnite-opening-crawl-palpatines-message|text=The Rise of Skywalker's opening crawl references an event you could only hear in Fortnite|author=Good, Owen S.|date=2019-12-20|archivedate=20200121143555}}</ref> It was later [[canon]]ically mentioned in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> While also featured in [[Rae Carson]]'s [[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition|novelization]], Carson stated that the broadcast was conceived by someone else, not by her nor the [[Wikipedia:Fortnite|''Fortnite'']] developers.<ref>{{Twitter|raecarson|status/1244021201493585921|[[Rae Carson]]|quote=Someone else conceived it, and it was used by both me and Fortnite.|archivedate=20200328223839}}</ref>
