The '''Bazaar of Esoteric Obscenities''' was a location which burned down a number of [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="Alphabet">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Major]] [[Soran Keize]], former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[pilot]] of the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], had been present at the location when it had burned down.<ref name="Alphabet" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bazaar of Esoteric Obscenities was first mentioned in the [[2019]] novel [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Alphabet" />
