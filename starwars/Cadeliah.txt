{{Quote|There's blood on my hands, but this youngling is heir to ''two syndicates''&hellip; If she can be protected until she comes of age, she can unite them and ''end'' this bloody crime war.|Nakano Lash|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" />}}
'''Cadeliah''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Reproduction|born]] to two heirs of rival crime syndicates&mdash;[[Krynthia]] of the [[Unbroken Clan]] and [[Khamus]] of the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate]]. Khamus attempted to [[Death|kill]] Krynthia before she gave [[Reproduction|birth]] to hide the fact that he was the [[Parent|father]], [[Mission to Corellia (Nakano Lash's crew)|hiring]] [[bounty hunter]]s led by [[Nakano Lash]] to escort him into the [[Unbroken Clan sanctuary]]. Lash killed Khamus upon learning his intentions, rescuing Krynthia, who died giving birth to Cadeliah [[Standard week|weeks]] later. Lash went into hiding to take care of Cadeliah, who the former [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] hoped would be able to unite the two rival syndicates. After Lash's death and at her request, [[Beilert Valance]] took responsibility for her. He got her to [[Lowik]], and after an [[Skirmish on Lowik|ambush]] by fellow bounty hunters [[Zuckuss]] and [[4-LOM]], Cadeliah was placed in the care of [[Yuralla Vega]].

==Biography==
====Corellia====
Cadeliah was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> [[Royal line of the Unbroken Clan|Daughter]] of [[Krynthia]] of the [[Unbroken Clan]] and [[Khamus]] of the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate]], Cadeliah was the heir to two rival criminal empires. In order to avoid the fact that he was the [[Parent|father]], Khamus prepared to [[Death|kill]] his unborn child by executing its mother just after the [[Mission to Corellia (Nakano Lash's crew)|Skirmish on Corellia]]. Before he could do this, however, [[Nakano Lash]], a [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] enlisted to help Khamus,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 1">[[Bounty Hunters 1|''Bounty Hunters'' 1]]</ref> killed him, saving the mother and her child. [[T'ongor]], part of Lash's crew, helped the two to escape, meeting up with the rest of the ensemble, [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], [[Boba Fett]], and [[Beilert Valance]]. During the struggle, T'ongor was killed by a stray sniper blast<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4">[[Bounty Hunters 4|''Bounty Hunters'' 4]]</ref> coming from Fett's blaster. This bolt was intended for Krynthia and the unborn Cadeliah, but Valance shoved Fett, preventing this from happening.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 5">[[Bounty Hunters 5|''Bounty Hunters'' 5]]</ref> [[Standard week|Weeks]] later, Krynthia passed away in childbirth, and Lash took it upon herself to raise Cadeliah until she was old enough to bring an end to the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate–Unbroken Clan war|crime war]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" />

====Ruusan====
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date 2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bounty Hunters 4|''Bounty Hunters'' 4]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Lash and Cadeliah had been living in hiding on the planet [[Ruusan]]. During this time, Lash had been secretly contacting Valance, asking him to come help her with the child.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" /> Lash resurfaced into the public eye, and a chain of investigations lead several bounty hunters to Ruusan. One day while Lash was out, the [[Thisspiasian]] bounty hunter [[Ooris Bynar]] infiltrated their home, and had Cadeliah at gunpoint as Lash returned.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3">[[Bounty Hunters 3|''Bounty Hunters'' 3]]</ref> After a quick conversation, Cadeliah and Lash struck back at the hunter, killing him and deciding to leave for good. Soon after, [[T'onga]], sister of T'ongor, and Valance found Bynar's body. After questioning Lash's friend in a nearby village, they made their way into Ruusan's orbit to find her.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" />

[[File:CadeliahCapturedByOorisBynar-BountyHunters3.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Cadeliah captured by the bounty hunter Ooris Bynar]]
Onboard Lash's ship, Valance betrayed T'onga's goal of eliminating her, as the [[restraining bolt]] she planted on him was damaged. T'onga was then shot in the chest and severely injured by Boba Fett, who followed them to Ruusan thanks to the confession of Bossk.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" /> Valance began a brawl with Fett, before being electrocuted and temporarily disabled, allowing Fett to attack Lash and Cadeliah. They engaged in a short fight with Fett, where he stabbed Lash in the chest. As the ship began to disintegrate and Fett fled the scene, Valance had to leave Lash to die after desiring to, yet failing to save his former mentor. Cadeliah was now in the care of Valance, and they escaped on Valance's ship, the ''[[Broken Wing]]'' just as the Unbroken Clan arrived to destroy Lash once and for all.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 5" />

====On the run====
Cadeliah accompanied Valance to [[Spur Orbiting Market|The Spur]] to get repairs for his damaged cybernetics. While stopping for food, the pair barely escaped an ambush from an [[Unidentified Arcona bounty hunter|Arcona bounty hunter]] before making it to [[Slade's Repairs]], where Valance could get his palm blasters repaired and improved. Earlier, however, the Unbroken Clan's general [[Vukorah]] has enlisted the help of bounty hunting duo [[Zuckuss]] and [[4-LOM]] to hunt him down. The hunters quickly reach the repair shop, and shoot down the technician finishing work on Valance. After a brief yet painful brawl with the two, Cadeliah and her protector narrowly escaped once more. Zuckuss soon reveals to his compatriot, however, that he's placed a tracking device on the ''Broken Wing''. A heavily damaged Valance and Cadeliah soon arrived on [[Lowik]], to visit Valance's old flame, [[Yuralla Vega]]. Cadeliah helps the man limp to the [[Rebel outpost (Lowik)|rebel outpost]], and he falls into Vega's arms while slowly bleeding out. In orbit, the ''[[Mist Hunter]]'' arrives for an even bigger score.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 6">[[Bounty Hunters 6|''Bounty Hunters'' 6]]</ref>

====Bounty hunter ambush====
Zuckuss and 4-LOM soon arrived on the forested world with a cache of [[B2-series super battle droid]]s. Meanwhile, Vega, Cadeliah, and other rebels set up the mess hall's table as a makeshift operating table for Beilert. The bounty hunter was repaired, and a group of rebel soldiers went out to [[Skirmish on Lowik|protect]] the compound from an unknown presence detected outside. Valance barricaded the door, allowing for Vega and Cadeliah to escape through a tunnel while he held off the droid bounty hunter.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7">[[Bounty Hunters 7|''Bounty Hunters'' 7]]</ref>

Outside, the super battle droids kept the rebel soldiers busy, as the bounty hunter Zuckuss ambushed Vega and Cadeliah, battering the former in the head while stunning Cadeliah. Valance rushed into the forest to find the two, finding Vega cradled by her [[Kondra|husband]], who confirmed that she was going to make it. With this reassurance, Beilert went off to retrieve Cadeliah from the bounty hunters. Meeting the duo at the ''Mist Hunter'', Valance made a deal. He traded them the rare [[fire ruby]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7" /> given to him by Vega<ref name="Target Vader 5">[[Target Vader 5|''Target Vader'' 5]]</ref> to buy time as the Rebellion evacuated their base, taking Cadeliah with them. Cadeliah was now in the care of Vega and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7" />

===Capture by Crimson Dawn===
Shortly after, Valance asked Kondra about Cadeliah, and Kondra told the bounty hunter that she was fine with the couple and their fleeing alliance.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 8">[[Bounty Hunters 8|''Bounty Hunters'' 8]]</ref> At the [[Temporary rebel cell base]]<ref name="BH16">[[Bounty Hunters 16|''Bounty Hunters'' 16]]</ref> on [[Panisia]],<ref name="CR2">[[Crimson Reign 2|''Crimson Reign'' 2]]</ref> Cadeliah played with Yura and Kondra.<ref name="BH16" /> When a [[Hunter Fleet|Imperial assault group]] under Commander [[Ellian Zahra]] attacked Panisia, she was told to hide by Yura. As she hid, Cadeliah was captured by the [[Crimson Dawn]] assassin, [[Deathstick]]. She was then taken to the [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']], where she was brought before Crimson Dawn's leader, [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]].<ref name="CR2" />

She then had dinner with Qi'ra, a couple of her guards, and [[Vicrul]]. Cadeliah took a knife, tried to escape but was stopped by Vicrul. When Qi'ra ordered Vicrul to let her go, Cadeliah went back to eating her dinner.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 22">[[Bounty Hunters 22|''Bounty Hunters'' 22]]</ref> 

[[File:QiraMessageCadeliah-HiddenEmpire5.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Cadeliah listens to Qi'ra's message.]]
After the [[Battle at Amaxine Station]], Qi'ra left Cadeliah with all the credits she had acquired, hoping to give the girl the freedom she had never had.<ref name="HE 5">[[Hidden Empire 5|''Hidden Empire'' 5]]</ref>

After meeting up with T'onga and Losha, Cadeliah demanded that if they want to be part of her bankroll to their new establishment, she wants to be train to be a bounty hunter from them. Her second demand was a sweet sand cookie stand in the cantina and book [[Max Rebo Band]], which the two agreed.<ref name="BH 42">[[Bounty Hunters 42|''Bounty Hunters'' 42]]</ref>
