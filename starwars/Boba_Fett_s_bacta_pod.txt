A '''bacta pod''' was used by the [[crime lord]] and former [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Stranger" />

Fett would mainly use the bacta pod to heal from his injuries inflicted upon the [[Sarlacc]] in [[4 ABY]]<ref name="TGS">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref> and was forcibly removed from the pod by [[Krrsantan]] under the orders of [[The Twins]], who wanted their cousin [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]'s territory.<ref name="The Streets">{{BOBF|Chapter 3: The Streets of Mos Espa}}</ref>

In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Mos Espa]], the bacta pod was used by [[Sheriff]] [[Cobb Vanth]] to heal from his injuries by the [[Duros]] [[Bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]].<ref name="In the Name of Honor" />
