The '''anti-Ghorman whispering campaign''' was a [[propaganda]] campaign designed by the [[Galactic Empire]] to affect galactic opinion on the [[Ghorman (people)|Ghorman people]]. It was run by [[Nisus Osar]] and [[Dee Shambo]] from the Galactic Empire's [[Ministry of Enlightenment]] as part of an overall plan for the [[Suppression of Ghorman|Imperial suppression]] on the [[planet]] [[Ghorman]] to allow for the [[gouge mining]] of its [[kalkite]] [[mineral]] deposits. Shambo and Osar detailed parts of campaign, including the supposed [[death]] of an [[Unidentified Imperial naval inspector|Imperial naval inspector]], to the attendees of an [[Ghorman project|Imperial meeting]] at the [[Maltheen Divide]] focused on the Empire's plans for Ghorman. During the meeting, [[ISB Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]] expressed her opinion that propaganda would only get them so far and that they would need to encourage insurgent acts against the Empire.<ref name="One Year Later" />
