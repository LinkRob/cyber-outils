An '''anchoring spike''', or just '''anchor''', was a device attached to a [[starship]] by a cable and shot out so as to burrow in a surface and restrict the craft's movement. The prototype [[scout ship]] ''[[Star Herald]]'' was equipped with one such device.<ref name="The Rescue">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Rescue]]''</ref>
