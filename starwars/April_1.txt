'''April 1''' is the 91st day of the year (92nd in leap years), with 274 days remaining.

In many countries, April 1 is known as [[Wikipedia:April Fool's Day|April Fool's Day]]. [[StarWars.com]] has often posted practical jokes on this day, including:
*[[2001]] &ndash; [https://web.archive.org/web/20080629163821/http://www.starwars.com/episode-v/bts/article/f20010401/401 April 1, 1979 Main Page], a "preview" of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']].
*[[2004]] &ndash; [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']] is titled ''The Creeping Fear'' in the site navigational menus.
*[[2006]] &ndash; [https://web.archive.org/web/20080624031742/http://starwars.com/databank/updates/401/news20060401.html The Shadow War Chronicles] [[Databank (original)|Databank]] update, based on the film [[Willow (film)|''Willow'']].
