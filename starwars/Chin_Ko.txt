'''Chin Ko''' is a concept and visual design artist for [[LucasArts]]. His work includes ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]'' and [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Lightsaber Duels|''The Clone Wars: Lightsaber Duels'']]. A layout of ''Force Unleashed'' concept art by Ko concludes the [[2009]] coffee table book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Vader]]''.
