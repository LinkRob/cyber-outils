'''Arch-Provost''' was a governing position on [[Rendili]]. After the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], during which the [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class assault ships]] were introduced, the Arch-Provost ordered industrial [[Spy|spies]] and [[starship]] designers to reduce [[Kuat]]'s lead for new contracts handed out by the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Arch-Provost was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' canon in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 49|forty-ninth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 49">{{BuildFalconCite|49|Starship Fact File}}</ref> published around [[December 9]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdates">{{BMFdates|49}}</ref>
