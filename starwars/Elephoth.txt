An '''elephoth''' was a massive, quadrupedal creature with a pair of [[Tooth|tusks]], [[Color|green]] and thick hide, small red [[eye]]s, and two greenish trunks, covered with moss and vines. The [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s used by the [[Galactic Empire]] were described by some to bear a resemblance to [[steel]] elephoths on stilts, with [[laser cannon]]s instead of tusks.<ref name="SYWtBaJ?" />

The [[Limb|legs]] of an adult elephoth were said to be as thick as the trunk of a [[hornbeam]].<ref name="SYWtBaJ?" />

During his [[Jedi training|training]] on [[Dagobah]], [[Jedi]] apprentice [[Luke Skywalker]], his mentor [[Yoda]], and the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] encountered a [[Parent|mother]] elephoth who attacked them to protect her child.<ref name="SYWtBaJ?" />

==Behind the scenes==
Although elephoths are said to have two large trunks in ''[[The Empire Strikes Back: So You Want to Be a Jedi?]]'',<ref name="SYWtBaJ?" /> the specimen shown in ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' has no trunk at all.<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>
