'''Aldera''' was the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] in the [[Core Worlds]]. A picturesque city surrounded by nature, it housed both the renowned [[University of Alderaan]] and the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan|Royal Palace]], the latter being the official home of the [[Alderaanian monarchy|monarch of Alderaan]] and [[House of Organa|their family]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], Aldera housed the [[Alderaan Refugee Conference]]. In [[1 BBY]], the capital was lost along with the rest of Alderaan when [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] ordered the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] to [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroy]] the planet.

==Description==
[[File:Home_among_the_mountains.png|thumb|left|250px|Aldera was surrounded by mountain ranges.]]
Located in a [[Unidentified region|region]] of Alderaan,<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> one of the so-called Core Worlds of [[the galaxy]],<ref name="Ultimate SW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Aldera sat on a large island on a lake,<ref name="Dawn" /> nestled by a range of [[Water|snow]]-capped [[mountain]]s,<ref name="KenboiI">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref> one of which was the [[Appenza Peak]]. The [[River Wuitho]] ran along the the outskirts of Aldera, as well as several smaller villages.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> A [[forest]] also grew some way outside the city.<ref name="KenboiI" />

A scenic city, Aldera featured a collection of gleaming, rounded, [[Color|white]] structures constructed in the architectural [[Alderaanian style]] that included the Royal Palace. It also held a [[Aldera spaceport|spaceport]], which was considered one of the best in the Core Worlds.<ref name="Dawn" /> Aldera had modern and well-maintained port facilities, including private landing platforms dedicated to the arrival of royal visitors and returning dignitaries.<ref name="DB Ald">{{Databank|alderaan|Alderaan}}</ref> The renowned University of Alderaan was located in Aldera,<ref name="HC 34">{{HelmetCollectionCite|34|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> as well as many galleries and [[music]] halls.<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref>

[[File:Avenues_of_Aldera.png|thumb|250px|The avenues of Aldera were wide and lined with trees.]]
The city had wide, [[tree]]-lined avenues with multiple small gardens plotted along their ways. In an effort to keep the view of the palace as intact as possible, traffic in the city center was mostly pedestrian, although air traffic increased further from the palace, especially when heading towards the shipping platforms.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />

==History==
In its earliest [[Standard day|days]], Aldera was a simple [[Wood|logging]] camp before growing into the planet's capital.<ref name="JS">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> By the time [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] became the [[Coronation of the Alderaanian heir|Crown]] [[Princess]] in the [[Standard year|year]] [[3 BBY]], Aldera and the oldest part of its Royal Palace had already been in existence for over a [[millennium]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

During the [[Clone Wars]], a pan-galactic conflict fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], representatives from across the galaxy congregated in Aldera to attend conferences and engage in negotiations concerning multiple important issues.<ref name="Dawn" /> One notable example was the [[Alderaan Refugee Conference]], which was meant to address the growing number of [[refugee]]s displaced by the war.<ref name="Assassin">{{TCW|Assassin (episode)}}</ref>

After the Clone Wars ended and the Republic was [[Proclamation of the New Order|replaced]] by the autocratic [[Galactic Empire]], Alderaan emerged as a crucible of covert resistance against the [[New Order|new regime]]. The royal couple, [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa]] and [[Viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]], organized secret strategy meetings under the guise of dinner parties taking place in the Royal Palace's formal dinning room,<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> effectively turning Aldera's most central building into a top-secret meeting place for rebel leaders and sympathizers.<ref name="Dawn" />

The capital city was lost&mdash;along with the rest of Alderaan&mdash;in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas" /> when Imperial Moff Tarkin used the Death Star [[battle station]] to destroy the planet, which Princess Leia was forced to witness.<ref name="A New Hope" />
