'''Darth Tanis''' was an ancient [[Sith Lord]] who created the [[Malachor superweapon|superweapon]] in the [[Malachor Sith Temple|Sith Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Malachor]]. Later, the [[Tanis Legion|17th Legion]] of the [[Sith Eternal]] was named after [[Darth]] Tanis.

==Biography==
===Superweapon architect===
[[Darth]] Tanis was an ancient [[Sith Lord]] who created the [[Malachor superweapon|superweapon]] within the [[Malachor Sith Temple|Sith Temple]] on the [[Sith]] [[planet]] of [[Malachor]]. Ancient [[Sith]] accounts from [[3966 BBY]] described [[Kyber crystal|kyber]] weaponry developed by Tanis on the [[planet]] [[Malachor]]. This was the<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[Malachor superweapon]],<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> a deadly machinery that laid at the heart of the [[Malachor Sith Temple]],<ref name="Twilight Trivia">{{SW|url=series/episodes/twilight-of-the-apprentice-trivia-gallery|text="Twilight of the Apprentice" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> a [[battle station]] and site of contest with the [[Jedi]]<ref name="TOTA">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref> which ultimately led to the [[Great Scourge of Malachor]],<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> where all [[Lightsaber combat|combatants]] perished at the activation of the [[superweapon]]<ref name="TOTA" /> including a [[Unidentified Sith witch|Sith witch]] from the [[Sith Empire]].<ref name="Note">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'' establishes that the [[Great Scourge of Malachor]] was fought between the [[Jedi]] and the forces of the [[Sith]] [[witch]]. [[Darth Maul (2017) 2|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 2]] establishes that the Sith forces were part of the [[Sith Empire]], so it can be deduced that the Sith witch was affiliated with the Sith Empire, as well.</ref>

===Legacy===
====Seeking the weapon====
{{Quote|My apprentice is activating the temple, or more precisely, this battle station, which I shall use to exact my revenge on all my enemies!|Maul, to Ahsoka, referring to the power of the Malachor Sith Temple's superweapon|<ref name="TOTA" />}}
Tanis' weapon could be activated with a [[Sith holocron (Malachor)|Sith holocron located on Malachor]], which was sought by the former Sith Lord [[Maul]], the [[Eighth Brother]] of the [[Inquisitorius]], and the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> The [[Sith holocron]] contained the [[Presence]], a [[Gender|female]] entity<ref name="TOTA" /> who seduced [[Padawan|Jedi apprentice]] [[Ezra Bridger]], to a limited degree, to the [[dark side of the Force]], before this was discovered by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Kanan Jarrus]], who gave the [[holocron]] to [[Bendu]].<ref name="Steps">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>

The [[Sith artifact]] was later retrieved by Bridger and Jarrus, the former wanting to partake in a [[holocron fusion]] with Maul, both in pursuit of [[Force vision]]s for their own desires. The connection was severed midway in the event, however, which resulted in the destruction of the holocron.<ref name="Holocrons of Fate">{{Rebels|The Holocrons of Fate}}</ref>

====The Sith Eternal====
The [[Tanis Legion|17th Legion]] of [[Darth Sidious]]' [[Sith Eternal]]'s [[Sith Eternal army|army]] was named after Darth Tanis. As with other [[legion]]s, its namesake was an ancient Sith Lord, the significance of which was known only to the [[Sith cultist|Sith Eternal loyalists]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Tanis was first mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''. In the book, Tanis is named as the creator of the ancient Malachor superweapon.<ref name="TROS VD" /> The superweapon was introduced in "[[Twilight of the Apprentice]]," the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Two|season two]] finale of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', which aired in [[2016]]. "Twilight of the Apprentice" also introduced the Presence, the spirit of an ancient Sith Lady contained inside a Sith holocron.<ref name="TOTA" />

In a 2016 interview with IGN, ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' executive producer [[Dave Filoni]] provided some insight into what he saw as the backstory of the Presence. In his mind, the voice of the Presence is the voice of the ancient Sith Lord who built the Malachor temple. Filoni said that he liked the idea of an ancient and powerful woman, who had domination that the audience has not yet heard of, who was creating the weapon when it was attacked by the [[Jedi Order]] and everyone is turned to stone. Filoni further said that he has a name for the character, but he could not yet reveal it.<ref name="IGN">{{WebCite|url=https://uk.ign.com/articles/2016/03/31/star-wars-rebels-dave-filoni-on-ahsokas-fate-mauls-return-and-much-more?page=1|text=''Star Wars Rebels'': Dave Filoni on Ahsoka's Fate, Maul's Return and Much More|author=Goldman, Eric|date=2016-03-30|work=IGN|archivedate=20200103221540}}</ref>

The [[2018]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'' further stated that a "Sith [[witch]]" led Sith forces during the Great Scourge of Malachor, thus petrifying all combatants.<ref name="DOR">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> However, any direct correspondence between Darth Tanis, the Presence and the Sith witch has not been confirmed by any official source.
