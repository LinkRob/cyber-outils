The '''bodhar''' were a [[species]] of creatures with [[bone]]s that could be used to make the tone keys for [[xyloxan]]s, a type of musical instrument. The [[musician]] [[Ubert Quaril]] used a xyloxan with bodhar-bone tone bars while playing at the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]] around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The bones of a bodhar first appeared as part of a prop in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], a film released in [[2015]]. The substance was not identified in the film, but was named in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released the day after the film.
