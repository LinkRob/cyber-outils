'''Big game hunters''' were individuals who [[Hunting|hunted]] and [[Death|killed]] everything from deadly creatures to [[Sentience|sentient]] beings.<ref name="Trandosha">{{Databank|trandosha|Trandosha}}</ref> Members of the [[Trandoshan]] [[species]] were traditionally big game hunters.<ref name="Hound's Tooth">{{SWE|technology|houndstooth|''Hound's Tooth''}}</ref> The [[Gender|female]] [[human]] named [[Gozetta]] was also a game hunter, like her [[Gozetta's father|father]] and her [[Gozetta's grandfather|grandfather]] before her.<ref name="The Nest" />
