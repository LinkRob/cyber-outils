The '''Barma sector''' was a [[sector]] in the [[Colonies]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[New Republic]]'s [[Barma Battle Group]] was based in the sector, led by [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]]. The sector had lacked a [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] leader who could consolidate the sector's forces, giving the New Republic an opportunity to retake territory.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>
