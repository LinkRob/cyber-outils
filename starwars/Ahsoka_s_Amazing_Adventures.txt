"'''Ahsoka's Amazing Adventures'''" is a [[Canon|non-canon]] animated [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short released on [[May 2]], [[2025]]<ref name="Ahsoka's Amazing Adventures" /> as part of ''[[LEGO STAR WARS: Celebrate the Season]]''.<ref name="SWKids" />

==Official description==
''A showcase of Ahsoka's journey from apprentice, to rebel, to Jedi master.''<ref name="Ahsoka's Amazing Adventures" />

==Plot summary==
The episode opens with [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] and her master [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] climbing aboard their [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor]]s. They fly their starships out of a hangar and take part in a space dogfight above a grey 
planetoid. The two join [[Republic Navy]] [[starfighter]]s in shooting down several [[Vulture-class starfighter|Vulture droid]]s. Tano experiences motion sickness and throws up before regaining control and flying beside Skywalker. 

Later, Tano flies with the crew of the ''[[Ghost]]'' during an aerial dogfight with [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]'s [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]]. She flies the ''[[Phantom II]]'' and takes out several [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. This allows [[C1-10P|Chopper]] to extinguish a hull fire on the ''Ghost''. [[Hera Syndulla]], [[Sabine Wren]] and [[Kanan Jarrus]] wave to Tano. 

Years later, Tano teaches Wren to use [[the Force]] on the planet [[Seatos]]. Wren tries to lift a rock but instead uproots a tree, causing it to hit [[Huyang]]. The droid is covered by flowers. 

Later, Tano duels with [[Morgan Elsbeth]] at the [[Unidentified henge (Peridea)|henge]] on top of the [[Great Mothers' Fortress]] on [[Peridea]]. Several [[Night Trooper]]s converge around the two belligerents to support Elsbeth. Wren comes to Tano's rescue and shoots several troopers, drawing them away. She hides behind a pillar and chuckles. 

Tano and Elsbeth continue duelling. Tano knocks Elsbeth's [[Blade of Talzin]] out of her hand. Elsbeth panics and flees. In the process, she falls off the henge to the ground far below. The Night Troopers panic and retreat while Tano and Wren celebrate their victory together.
