The '''Ewok&ndash;Dulok wars''' were fought between the [[Ewok]]s and [[Dulok]]s,<ref name="LEGO saga">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''</ref> two [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Endor|forest moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Endor (planet)|Endor]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> A [[human]] attending the [[Aki-Aki]] [[Festival of the Ancestors]] held on [[Pasaana]]<ref name="LEGO saga" /> in [[35 ABY]]<ref name="Film Date">{{FilmDate|IX}}</ref> told a [[Resistance]] party undertaking a [[Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)|mission to Pasaana]] that they had witnessed the Ewok&ndash;Dulok wars but still found the Festival to be one of the strangest collections of people they had ever seen.<ref name="LEGO saga" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Canon|continuity]], the Ewok&ndash;Dulok wars were mentioned in the [[2022]] non-canon [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''.<ref name="LEGO saga" />
