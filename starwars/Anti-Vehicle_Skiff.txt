'''Anti-Vehicle Skiff''' were [[Bantha-II cargo skiff|Bantha class skiffs]] that were outfitted by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] with weapons that could penetrate heavily armored [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] vehicles. The skiffs were lightly armored and able to move quickly.<ref name="Commander" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anti-Vehicle Skiffs made their first appearance in the [[2014]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''. They could be unlocked with a Level 6 factory if the player is aligned with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]].<ref name="Commander" />
