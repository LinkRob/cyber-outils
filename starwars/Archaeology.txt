'''Archaeology''' was the study of past [[Sentience|sentient]] life and culture by the recovery and [[Scientist|scientific]] analysis of artifacts (i.e. material remains of cultural importance). Specialists in archaeology were referred to as [[archaeologist]]s. One of the discipline's branches was archaeoxenobiology, the analysis and interpretation of biological remains from archaeological sites.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 1|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 1]]</ref>

The [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Ministry for Antiquity]] took part in archaeological work, including locating artifacts of importance to [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. It was involved in an [[excavation of the Lothal Jedi Temple]] under [[Minister]] [[Veris Hydan]].<ref name="Wolves">{{Rebels|Wolves and a Door}}</ref><ref name="A World Between Worlds">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref> The Empire was also known to have an [[Imperial Institute for Archaeological Exploitation]].<ref name="Smuggler">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>
