'''Beetles''' were a type of [[insect]] found throughout [[the galaxy]]. They included the [[piranha beetle]],<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> the [[night-beetle]],<ref name="LOP">{{StoryCite|story=The Levers of Power|book=The Rise of the Empire}}</ref> the [[slug-beetle]],<ref name="BSV">{{TCW|Blue Shadow Virus (episode)}}</ref> the [[Andelm beetle]],<ref name="Chewbacca">[[Chewbacca (2015) 1|''Chewbacca'' (2015) 1]]</ref> and the [[clip beetle]],<ref name="The Snare">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Snare]]''</ref> among various others.
