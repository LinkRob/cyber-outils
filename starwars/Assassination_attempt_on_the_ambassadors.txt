In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> an '''assassination attempt''' was conducted on [[Eirami]] [[Ambassador]] [[Lian Cerox]] and [[E'roni]] Ambassador [[Tintak]] while a [[Summit on Jedha|peace summit]] was being held on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] between their [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|warring]] [[planet]]s, [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]]. Bombs were planted by the [[Brotherhood of the Ninth Door]] at the direction of [[Tilson Graf]] near Cerox's quarters at the Second Spire and Tintak's shuttle. Cerox was protected by [[Jedi Master]] [[Creighton Sun]] and Tintak was protected by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Aida Forte]], but Tintak lost his left hand and his [[pilot]] was killed, and Cerox was still severely injured and taken to a temporary [[Second Spire medical station|medical station]] by Sun.<ref name="TBoJ" />
