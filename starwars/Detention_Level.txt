The '''Detention Level''' was an area on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] that held numerous [[Prison|detention blocks]] located on descending sub-levels. Sub-level 0 was the first sub-level, with other sub-levels extending downwards towards the battle station's southern pole. Detention levels were organized around a large, spherical central airway for their sector, from which [[turbolift]]s brought in new [[prisoner]]s and guards.<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> They were surveilled by multiple [[57C]] [[security camera]]s. [[Computer]]s and [[droid]]s were forbidden from accessing information on the detention blocks and access to the level was restricted.<ref name="Episode IV" />

Detention Levels were not meant to hold prisoners long-term, but rather serve as a stopgap measure until prisoners could be transferred to other Imperial prisons.<ref name="Ultimate SW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Prisoners were kept in the dark, and were then dragged to brightly lit interrogation rooms.<ref name="SWCL" />

==History==
[[Detention Block AA-23]] on sub-level five was reserved for political [[prisoner]]s. [[Cell 2187]] was located on AA-23, and was the location where [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was [[Prison|imprisoned]] by [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] for suspected [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] activities<ref name="Episode IV" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Episode IV Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> After [[Rescue of Princess Leia|rescuing]] Organa, Luke Skywalker, [[Han Solo]], [[Chewbacca]], and the princess herself were trapped in a [[Garbage masher 3263827|garbage masher]] on this level and nearly crushed to [[death]] before being saved by the [[droid]]s [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]]. Later, the Detention Level, along with the rest of the Death Star, was destroyed during the [[Battle of Yavin]] thanks to Luke.<ref name="Episode IV" />
