At some point during the [[Iron Blockade|lockdown]] of the [[Anoat sector]] by the [[Galactic Empire]], a '''battle''' was fought on the former [[Hutt jewel world]] of [[Mataou]] between the Empire and the resistance faction called the [[Uprising]]. The Imperials, having realized that the resistance had established a strong presence in the [[Hutt]] ruins of Mataou, sought to wipe them out.<ref name="SWU SB Mataou" />

==Behind the scenes==
This battle is a "Sector Battle,"<ref name="SWU SB Mataou" /> a game-wide event that earns the players powerful rewards and dictates the expansion of in-game content. The [[canon]]ical outcome of a Sector Battle is not known, since it is determined by the players' involvement.<ref name="SWUweb">{{WebCite|url=https://playstarwarsuprising.com/game-guide/gameplay/#sector-battles|text=Sector Battles|work=[https://playstarwarsuprising.com Star Wars: Uprising Official Website]|archivedate=20160809151309}}</ref> However, the motives of the battle were addressed in the in-game description.<ref name="SWU SB Mataou" />
