'''Frudeki''' was a fictional [[mayor]] on [[Coruscant]] in the [[holodrama]] ''[[Glarnok & Yabassi]]''. The series released at least forty five episodes<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>  

In the forty third episode, [[Yabassi]] traced a [[blaster]] to a non existent shop on [[Level 1420]] but was ultimately able to link it to Frudeki by using the revisions. When [[Glarnok]] and Yabassi approached Frudeki to arrest him, he tried to escape proclaiming his allegiance to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. To which [[Glarnok]] called him a traitor.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Frudeki's voice first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
