"'''Do You Know What Your Children Are?'''" was the sixth instalment of [[The Keeper's World]], published in [[Pizzazz 6]] in March [[1978]]. The entire story arc was reprinted in the UK in ''[[Star Wars Weekly 47]]&ndash;[[Star Wars Weekly 50|50]]'' in [[1978]]&ndash;[[1979|79]], and in the [[1999]] limited-release comic ''[[Star Wars 0]]''.

==Plot summary==
[[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]], [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]], and [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] escape [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] by teleporting into the [[Keeper's Temple]], where they are confronted by four mysterious children with the power to control the elements. Before they can question the children further, the stormtroopers manage to activate the [[teleportal]] and continue the chase.
