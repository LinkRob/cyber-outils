'''Bocce''' was a [[language]] that saw some use on the [[Star|suns]]-scorched [[planet]] of [[Tatooine]]. The adopted aunt of [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Beru Whitesun Lars]], once insisted on acquiring a [[translator droid]] that could speak that tongue. This prompted her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Owen Lars]] to buy [[C-3PO]], a [[protocol droid]] who affirmed that Bocce was "like a second language" to him.<ref name="ANH" />
