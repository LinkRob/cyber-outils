'''Black fizzer''' was a type of beverage that was sold at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] on [[Tatooine]]. Shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Cornelius Evazan]] ordered two black fizzers for himself and [[Ponda Baba]] from the [[bartender]], [[Wuher]].<ref name="WDSTKH" />
