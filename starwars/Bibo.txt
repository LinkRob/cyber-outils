'''Bibo''' was a juvenile [[Gender|male]] [[rokkna]]<ref name="Bucket's List" /> adopted by [[Neeku Vozo]] in [[34 ABY]]. After winding up on the ''[[Colossus]]'' after being caught inside a wrecked [[Z-95 Headhunter]]<ref name="Bibo" /> from the [[Karavian Trench]] of [[Castilon]]'s [[ocean]]<ref name="Bucket's List" /> that was salvaged by [[Synara San]], Vozo was taken with the creature immediately after seeing him and decided to adopt him as a pet and name him Bibo. However, this attracted the ire of [[Bibo's mother|his mother]], a full-grown adult of the species, who [[Bibo incident|threatened]] the entire platform while attempting to retrieve Bibo. After realizing Bibo was the creature's child, Vozo finally let Bibo go.<ref name="Bibo" />

After the [[Sabotage of the Colossus|sabotage of the ''Colossus'']], [[Kazuda Xiono]] asked Vozo who his friends were, causing the [[Technician|mechanic]] to mention a particular chair, [[Buggles]], and note how he loved Bibo. Vozo then pondered what Bibo was currently doing.<ref name="The Engineer">{{Resistance|The Engineer}}</ref>
