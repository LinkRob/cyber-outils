An '''attack on an Imperial outpost''' was launched<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> around [[14 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> in the [[Imperial Era]] by the resident [[Kashyyyk resistance|resistance]] of the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]], against the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Occupation of Kashyyyk|occupation]] of the world. During the attack, [[Wookiee]] [[Commander]] [[Choyyssyk]] was captured and [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] in Kashyyyk's [[Imperial Refinery]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> In 14 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> after [[Standard month|months]] of [[Prison|imprisonment]], Choyyssyk was [[Liberation on Kashyyyk|freed]] by the fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] and the [[Partisans]], an insurgent group led by [[Militia|militant]] freedom fighter [[Saw Gerrera]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
After the Kashyyyk resistance and Partisans successfully defeated [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]], the Partisans member [[Mari Kosan]] mentioned Choyyssyk's capture during the attack to Kestis.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
