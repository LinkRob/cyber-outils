'''Farmers''', also known as '''ranchers''' or '''farmhands''', were [[farm]] workers or owners. On [[desert]] [[Planet|worlds]] like [[Tatooine]], [[moisture farmer]]s harvested [[water]] from the [[atmosphere]].<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> During the [[Imperial Era]], farmers on [[Lothal]] were sometimes dispossessed of their land.<ref name="FF">{{Rebels|Fighter Flight}}</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
[[Luke Skywalker]] is described in the original [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'']] script as a "'''farm boy'''."<ref name="IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
