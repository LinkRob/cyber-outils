'''''Episode I Adventures 5: The Ghostling Children''''' is a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] junior novel written by [[Dave Wolverton]]. It is the fifth volume of the ''[[Star Wars: Episode I Adventures]]'' series, and it was published by [[Scholastic]] in [[January]] [[2000]], with an accompanying gamebook. A sequel, [[Episode I Adventures 6: The Hunt for Anakin Skywalker|''The Hunt for Anakin Skywalker'']], was published the [[February|following month]].

==Publisher's summary==
''The Ghostling children live on a beautiful, isolated world, safe from harm&hellip;until they are captured by an evil trader. Soon they find themselves on the harsh planet of Tatooine, about to be sold to the terrifying Gardulla the Hutt.''

''Their only hope of survival is a young slave named Anakin and his friends.''

''Can the Ghostlings be freed?''
