The '''charon''' were a type of creature that lived on the [[planet]] [[Gand (planet)|Gand]]. Prior to [[Zuckuss]]' undertaking of the [[Great Rite]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 32" /> at some point before [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date note">The events of [[Bounty Hunters 32|''Bounty Hunters'' 32]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> a number of sacred [[flower]]s known as the [[T'karra flower]] were spotted by [[Gand]] as growing in the nesting grounds of the charon. As part of their rite, hatchlings of [[Zuckuss' clan]], including Zuckuss, were to recover a flower from the nesting grounds and use their insticts to survive against the charon and return in order to pass the test. After the hatchlings made their way to the grounds, one of the Gand, [[Garkung]], took a flower, though they were [[Death|killed]] by a charon. Zuckuss attempted to fight back against the charon, and was eventually able to get it to fall off the edge of a cliff. With the charon defeated, Zuckuss returned to the [[Unidentified Master Findsman|Master Findsman]], and informed them of what had happened, though he had not recovered a flower. The [[Master Findsman]] told Zuckuss that was not a condition of the rite, and cast Zuckuss out of the clan for failing to complete the rite.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 32" />
