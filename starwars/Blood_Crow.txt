The '''''Blood Crow''''' was an [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|Imperial ''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] originally commanded by [[Captain]] [[Rik Virgilio]], and later by Captain [[Filia Rossi]]. It was the first assignment of [[Lieutenant]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] and [[Ensign]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]].<ref name="Thrawn" />
