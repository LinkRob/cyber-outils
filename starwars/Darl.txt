'''Darl''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Nikto]] that lived on the [[planet]] [[Palagosal]] along with her husband [[Marl (Nikto)|Marl]]. While living on the planet, she worked at her shop co-ran with her husband, [[Marl's Good Enough Eats]]. After being rescued from a cave after a [[Nihil]] [[Nihil raid on Palagosal|raid]] on the planet, she and her husband both found out that the planet has been evacuated and nobody can live there, which ultimately made her and her husband mournful.<ref name="BtN" />
