'''Dosmit Ræh''' was a [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] who served as a [[captain]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. She flew an [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing starfighter]] and was the leader of the [[Tierfon Yellow Aces]] [[starfighter]] [[squadron]].

==Biography==
[[File:DosmitRaehHelmet-Wired.png|thumb|left|200px|Dosmit Ræh's recovered helmet.]]
Dosmit Ræh was an individual who served as a [[captain]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], a rebel group that opposed the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="TFA VD" /> At some point during her service, she<ref name="Survival Guide" /> led the [[Tierfon Yellow Aces]],<ref name="Saga of Rey">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Saga of Rey}}</ref> a rebel [[starfighter]] [[squadron]], and piloted a [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]].<ref name="TFA VD" />

In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="jakku date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[Battle of Jakku]] to [[5 ABY]], which according to ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' the Tierfon Yellow Aces took part in.</ref> as part of the recently formed [[New Republic]], the Yellow Aces participated in the [[Battle of Jakku]], the final battle of the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> By [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Helmet Date">The events of "Galactic Tales: A Loose Connection" take place when [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] is thirteen [[Standard year|years]] old, and already owns Ræh's helmet. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates Rey's [[Reproduction|birth]] to [[15 ABY]], the events of "Galactic Tales: A Loose Connection" must take place in [[28 ABY]].</ref> [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], a young [[Human|human]] [[scavenger]] in the [[desert]]s of [[Jakku]], discovered Ræh’s old and badly damaged [[flight helmet]].<ref name="Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref> As a young [[Gender|girl]], Rey found inspiration in Ræh, and made up stories about what the Rebel captain was like and what [[planet]] she might have come from.<ref name="Survival Guide" />

[[File:Rey_AT-AT.png|thumb|right|200px|Rey kept Dosmit Ræh's memory alive.]]
Rey created a [[Alliance pilot doll|fighter pilot]] [[doll]] using [[Color|orange]] fabric from a [[flight suit]] she recovered from a [[cargo container]] and often wore Ræh’s [[helmet]]. Together, Rey and "Ræh" went on adventures, exploring Rey's home, the downed [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] ''[[Hellhound Two]]'', as well as the surrounding sands. Rey eventually grew out of playing with the doll, but she kept the memory of Ræh alive by keeping the doll and her helmet in her possession throughout her [[Standard year|year]]s on Jakku.<ref name="Survival Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:YellowLeader-LEGO.png|thumb|left|120px|Dosmit Ræh as she appears in ''LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga'']]
{{Quote|Hmph... Seems like ol' Yellow Leader's cowardice is catching on...''"<br />"''I HAVE feelings, Dreis!|Garven Dreis and Dosmit Ræh|<ref name="Skywalker Saga">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''</ref>}}
Although Ræh herself did not make an appearance in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], her flight helmet did.<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>

Ræh appears unnamed in the [[2022]] video game ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', where she is portrayed as a human with light skin stationed at the [[Great Temple|Rebel base]] on [[Yavin 4]]. In the game, she is depicted as the leader of [[Yellow Squadron]], and is shown having a friendly rivalry with [[Garven Dreis]], the leader of [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]], who teases her and her squadron for their perceived cowardice.<ref name="Skywalker Saga" />
