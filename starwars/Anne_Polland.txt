'''Anne Marie Polland''' (sometimes shortened as '''Annie Polland''') was a costume-maker who specialized in making costumes and theater wardrobes. She created clothing for the characters of several "[[Star Wars]]" films and Halloween disguises for the children of [[George Lucas]].

Anne died [[November 1]], [[2001]] in Fairfax. She was 54.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.sfgate.com/news/article/Anne-Marie-Polland-2858546.php|text=Anne Marie Polland|date=2001-10-11|work=[[Wikipedia:SFGate|''SFGate'']]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/wip/JT6og}}</ref>
