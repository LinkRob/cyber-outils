'''Briala''' was a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] during the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Mirialan]] [[Lylee Anaraku]] heard her calling [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]] [[walker]]s "stalkers" and caught onto using the name. Briala also obtained a deck of cards and Anaraku planned to play [[Lifters]] aboard the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'' with her following the 61st's next [[Battle of Allst Prime|assignment]] on the [[planet]] [[Allst Prime]]<ref name="Inbrief" /> between [[2 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{InbriefDate}}</ref> When Anaraku used the name "stalkers" on Allst Prime, the [[rebel trooper]] [[Brand]] questioned the term and the Mirialan explained that he had gotten it from Briala. He also brought up Briala's deck of cards, asking Brand if she wanted to play Lifters with them after the battle.<ref name="Inbrief" />
