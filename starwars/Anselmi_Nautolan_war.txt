The '''Anselmi–Nautolan war''' was a war that was fought between the [[Anselmi]] and the [[Nautolan]]s during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="Jango Fett 1" /> Around [[33 BBY]],<ref name="33 BBY" /> an uneasy peace had been established<ref name="Jango Fett 4">[[Jango Fett 4|''Jango Fett'' 4]]</ref> and a [[Anselmi–Nautolan treaty|peace treaty]] was crafted.
