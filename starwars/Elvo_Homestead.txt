'''Elvo Homestead''' was a [[moisture farm]] located just west of [[Mos Eisley]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an amount of [[Rippinnium]] laden [[starship]] [[fuel]] was stored at the homestead.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
The [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] repealed down to the homes central courtyard<ref name="Outlaws" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> and took the fuel.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Elvo Homestead appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. Fuel found at the homestead could be used to customize the appearance of the engine flares of the ''[[Trailblazer]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
