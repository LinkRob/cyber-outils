'''Boontaspice''' was a type of [[Spice (seasoning)|seasoning]] used in cooking that was [[Diet|consumable]] by [[human]]s<ref name="Outlaws" /> that shared its name with the [[Outer Rim Territories]] astronomical object [[Boonta]].<ref name="Beginner Game">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> Around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="BVDate">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> the [[Twi'lek]] [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Sornah Bocc]] had a supply list on a [[datapad]] in their [[Sornah's Garage|garage]] on the [[planet]] [[Malastare]] that included Boontaspice.<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref>

Later in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the spice was used to season [[Foodstuff|dishes]] served in [[the Broken Hoof]], a [[cantina|bar]] in the [[South Stretch Worker's District]] of the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the Outer Rim planet [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] tried to get a free meal from the bar's [[Unidentified Twi'lek cook|Twi'lek cook]] by suggesting that it smelled a little too heavy on the Boontaspice and offering to taste it for them. The [[Chef|cook]] rejected Vess's offer, telling her to freeload somewhere else.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Boontaspice was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Boontaspice/Legends|Boontaspice]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1996]] novel [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|''Shadows of the Empire'']], written by [[Steve Perry]].<ref name="SOTE">[[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'' novel]]</ref>
