'''Bonegnawers''' were a type of [[Diet|carnivorous]] [[bird]] of prey that lived on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. Large, airborne creatures, they possessed strong [[Mouth|beaks]] that could crush both rocks and [[bone]]. Bonegnawers targeted relatively large prey, and were known for stalking their target before consuming it on the spot. Bonegnawer [[parent]]s would then bring the prey's bones back to their [[Youngling|young]] at their nest, where they ate and shared the marrow with their offspring.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], bonegnawers appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" /> The [[Bonegnawer/Legends|creatures]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in the [[1976]] [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|novelization]] of the film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] by [[Alan Dean Foster]].<ref name="ANHN">[[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars: A New Hope'' novelization]]</ref>
