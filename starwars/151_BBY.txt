'''151 BBY''', or '''151<ref name="Date" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Bazil]], a [[Tynnan]] from the [[planet]] [[Tynna]] who became a tracker of the [[Jedi Order]]<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> by [[132 BBY]], was [[Reproduction|born]] around that year.<ref name="Date" /> [[Tasi Lowa]], a [[Zygerrian]] [[Jedi]] from the astronomical object [[Iliabath]] and [[Padawan]] of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Yord Fandar]],<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> was also born around that year.<ref name="Date" />

==Behind the scenes==
151 BBY was indirectly mentioned in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
