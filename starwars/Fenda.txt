'''Fenda''' was a member of the [[Gatherers]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]. After learning that [[Vi Moradi]], new recruit to the Gatherers, was a member of the [[Resistance]], [[Lin (Batuu)|Lin]] whispered to Fenda, and the two kept their distance from her, scared that her presence would attract the [[First Order]] to the [[planet]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />
