The '''"Subduer-9" Riot Blaster''' was a type of long-ranged [[Scattergun|scatterblaster]] manufactured by [[SoroSuub Corporation]], and was one of the most common law enforcement scatterblasters in [[the galaxy]]. Although it was heavier at six [[kilogram]]s and not as manageable as standard [[blaster rifle]]s, riot [[blaster]]s could subdue large groups of civilians. The Subduer-9 could emit a wide cone of [[stun]] blasts, which rendered targets unconscious and made it easy to place them in [[binder]]s and awaken in [[prison]] cells [[Standard hour|hours]] later. The cost of the Subduer-9 was 1,250 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets" />

Although many considered riot blasters to be appropriate peacekeeping tools, the [[Galactic Empire]] had a reputation of unleashing them against peacefully protesting citizens without provocation on backwater [[Planet|worlds]]. Populations that had seen the Subduer-9 in action rarely had a positive view of it and considered it a tool of tyranny.<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Subduer-9" Riot Blaster was mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets" />
