'''Friielan''' was a half [[Theelin]]<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Gender|non-binary]]<ref name="Email" /> individual who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. They lost their [[Friielan's family|immediate family]] when the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Valo Atrocity|attacked]] the [[Republic Fair]] on<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> [[Valo]] in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> and it caused them to develop a warm but sharply focused demeanor.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> they were partnered with [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] to undertake [[Missions across the Stormwall|missions]] across the Nihil's [[Stormwall]] barrier that prevented [[hyperspace]] travel, using the [[Sunvale device]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

Later, after the Republic and [[Jedi Order]] [[Assault on Naboo|liberated]]<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Timelines" /> [[planet]] [[Naboo]] from the Nihil, Friielan informed Kriss that they wanted to remain on Naboo as the world would need pilots, stating it was the [[Cosmic Force|will of the Force]]. Though the Jedi Master did not want anyone to remain in the Nihil's [[Occlusion Zone]], she understood and told Friielan to be careful.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

==Behind the scenes==
Friielan appeared in the [[2024]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> When asked about the usage of singular "they/them" pronouns for Friielan, the author stated that they had intended the usage to indicate a [[Wikipedia:Non-binary gender|non-binary]] gender for the character.<ref name="Email" />
