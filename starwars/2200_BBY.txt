The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''2200 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''1077 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''2181 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''5777''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*By this point, the citizens of [[Begamor]] had begun to live in peace.<ref name="Begamor date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that the citizens of [[Begamor]] had lived in peace for thousands of [[Standard year|years]], the [[time]] of peace must have began by around [[2200 BBY]].</ref>
*By this point, a local [[Land & Sky Corporation|corporation]] had begun their legitimate rule over the [[Aakaash system]].<ref name="Aakaash system">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that the [[Land & Sky Corporation]], which ruled over the [[Aakaash system]], had legitimately ruled for several [[Millennium|millennia]], their reign must have been began by [[2200 BBY]].</ref>
*The [[Fire (city)|Fire]] [[space station]] is constructed in the Aakaash system.<ref name="Fire station" /> It expands over a period of two thousand years, housing millions of permanent residents and becoming an integral transit point between the [[star system]]'s [[Land|political]] and [[Wind|economic]] centers. Fire eventually becomes famous to the wider galaxy for being a wholly artificial construct.<ref name="Vow">''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''</ref>
*A metal currency, the design of which was based on an ancient item of Aakaash system philosophy, is used on Fire. It is eventually phased out in favor of the standard [[Land & Sky currency]] as well as Republic [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] later on.<ref name="Vow" />
