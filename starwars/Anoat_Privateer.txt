'''Anoat Privateers''' were [[privateer]]s based in the [[Anoat sector]]. After the [[Galactic Empire]] suffered a [[Battle of Endor|crushing defeat]] at [[Endor]], the whole sector got [[Iron Blockade|locked down]]. Consequently, the Privateers started working for the [[Uprising]], harassing and seizing the shipping of those not allied with that rebellious movement.<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>
