A short-lived '''civil war''' occurred on the<ref name="Complete Locations" /> [[Wild Space]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Derenzil system]]</ref> [[swamp]] [[planet]] [[Derenzil]]. Members of the [[Geonosian]] [[species]] [[Arms dealer|supplied]] both sides of the conflict with highly specialized, [[Water|aquatic]] [[sonic weapon]]s that were manufactured in a [[Dooku's hangar|factory]] near the [[Stalgasin hive]] on the planet [[Geonosis]]. Due to the risk posed by the project, the [[factory]] was equipped with a [[Starfighter|fighter]] [[hangar]]; however,<ref name="Complete Locations" /> by [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the facility had been abandoned.<ref name="Complete Locations" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the civil war on Derenzil was first mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="Complete Locations" /> from the [[2003]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]'', which was written by [[Simon Beecroft]] and [[Curtis Saxton]] and marked the first mention of the [[Derenzil civil war/Legends|war]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]''</ref>
