The '''Echo Horn''' was a weapon created by [[Glenna Kip]] as a way to control the [[Zixon]], the [[Homeworld|native]] [[species]] of [[Minfar]] for the [[Galactic Empire]]. It had the ability to control any group that heard its call. After the fall of the Empire, the [[First Order]] traveled to Minfar to obtain the weapon, although it was successfully [[Mission to Minfar|destroyed]] by the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Spark of the Resistance" />
