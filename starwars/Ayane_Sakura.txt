'''Ayane Sakura''' ([[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]]: 佐倉綾音) is a Japanese voice actress<ref name="JP" /> who voiced the [[Sith]] [[Aman]] in the Japanese version of "[[The Bird of Paradise]]," a short film from the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 1|third season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]''.<ref name="SW">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-3-cast-announce|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 3 Cast Announced with New Trailer}}</ref> Sakura is known in Japan for her roles as Yotsuba Nakano in [[Wikipedia:The Quintessential Quintuplets|The Quintessential Quintuplets]], Ran Mitake in the [[Wikipedia:BanG Dream!|BanG Dream!]] franchise, and Aira Shiratori in [[Wikipedia:Dandadan|Dandadan]].<ref name="JP" />
