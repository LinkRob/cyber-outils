'''Bantha formation''' was a [[starship]] maneuver in which a group of ships flew in a single-file line. When [[Shadow Squadron]] attempted to pass the [[Kaliida Nebula]] during the [[Battle of the Kaliida Nebula]]<ref name="EpGuide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode003.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Shadow of ''Malevolence''}}</ref> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="date">{{TCWcanondates|103}}</ref> they stumbled upon a swarm of [[Neebray]]s. In order to evade the giant [[leviathan]]s, [[Anakin Skywalker]] ordered the [[Starfighter|fighters]] to fly in bantha formation.<ref name="EpGuide" />
