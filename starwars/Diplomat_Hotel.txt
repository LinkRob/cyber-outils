The '''Diplomat Hotel''',<ref name="CL" /> or simply the '''Diplomat''', was a large [[skyscraper]] located in the [[Federal District]] (formerly the Senate District) of [[Imperial City]], the [[Ecumenopolis|planet-wide]] [[city]] of [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Inferno Squad">''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]''</ref> Situated close to the [[Galactic Courts of Justice]] and the [[City Municipal Authorities Building]], the [[hotel]] catered to a [[The galaxy|galactic]] clientele, and offered its guests simulated [[atmosphere]]s to suit their individual preferences.<ref name="CL" /> With a floor at [[level 5120]]<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> and its peak visible above [[Level 5127|5127]], the current surface level of its time,<ref name="CL" /> it spanned at least seven levels.<ref name="CL" /><ref name="Inferno Squad" /> At one point, [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Security Bureau]] booked a suite for the members of the freshly minted [[Inferno Squad]]. The suite included a kitchen, and the rooms had controls to wake its occupant with gradual light, with an illumination range from one to ten. Some rooms light up automatically when entered, and the lights could be turned off, when the guest spoke a verbal command. [[Senior Lieutenant]] [[Iden Versio]] encountered all of these features when staying at the Diplomat.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
