"'''Frog Man'''" was the [[Marriage|husband]] of [[Frog Lady]] who lived during the [[New Republic Era]]. He was a [[Gender|male]] [[Querm Rybet]]<ref name="SWE" /> who found a home on the [[moon]] of [[Trask]] where he told his wife to come.<ref name="Chapter 10" /> When [[Din Djarin]] succeeded in transporting his wife to Trask, Frog Man was very grateful and shook Djarin's hand. Later, Frog Man and his wife looked after [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] as a favor to Djarin while he went on a [[Assault on the Imperial Gozanti freighter|mission]], at their [[Frog Lady and Frog Man's home|new home]].<ref name="Chapter 11" />
