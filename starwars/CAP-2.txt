The '''CAP-2''' was a one-person two-legged [[walker]] that was used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. It saw little use during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> In [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> a CAP-2 was used by [[bounty hunter]]s that worked with [[Ochi]] to carry the [[Skirmish on Torrenoteer Minor|captured]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] to a [[Starship|ship]] on the [[planet]] [[Torrenoteer Minor]], though he was saved by [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />
