The '''Bon'nyuw-Luq sector''' was a [[sector]] situated within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It contained the [[planet]] [[Arbra]], the [[homeworld]] of the [[Hoojib]] [[species]], as well as the [[Sharlissia system]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], in [[228 BBY]], the Bon'nyuw-Luq sector partially fell within the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders']] [[Occlusion Zone]] territory after the [[Expansion of the Stormwall|expansion]] of its [[Stormwall]] barrier.

==Description==
{{Quote|This is our destination, a small planet largely covered in woodland.|Counselor Fry, regarding Arbra|<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref>}}
The Bon'nyuw-Luq [[sector]] was situated in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Where" /> and [[Trailing Sectors]] regions.<ref name="Trailing" /> It bordered the [[Garis sector|Garis]], [[Grumani sector|Grumani]], [[Mayagil sector|Mayagil]],<ref name="THRA 6" /> [[Sanbra sector|Sanbra]],<ref name="Where" /> [[Tamarin sector|Tamarin]], and [[Toblain sector]]s.<ref name="THRA 6" /> The Bon'nyuw-Luq sector was the location of the [[Crait system]], containing the remote [[mineral]] [[planet]] [[Crait]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> The sector also contained [[Arbra]],<ref name="Where" /> a small [[Forest|woodland]] planet, the [[tree]]s of which were inhabited by [[bird]]s.<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> The [[Sharlissia system]] was located on the borders of the Bon'nyuw-Luq and Toblain sectors.<ref name="THRA 6" />

==History==
{{Quote|So, we're on Arbra. Anyone know where that is?|''Restitution'' prisoner [[Ola Hest]]|<ref name="Tempest Runner" />}}
[[File:OcclusionZoneBefore-2023THRA6.png|thumb|left|270px|Bon'nyuw-Luq (top center) and its surrounding sectors, prior to the expansion of the Occlusion Zone]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], Arbra was inhabited.<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> By [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date 231">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> the planet's [[Arbra's main settlement|main settlement]] was the target of a [[Raid on Arbra's main settlement|raid]] by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] that resulted with most of its buildings [[Fire|burning]] down. Not long after the raid,<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> in that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date 231" /> the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Prison ship|correctional vessel]] [[Lourna Dee (prison ship)|''Restitution'']] visited Arbra in order for its [[prisoner]] workforce to assist in the relief efforts. Several work parties&mdash;including [[Work Party Three|one]] under the supervision of [[Counselor]]s [[Fry]] and [[Jon Wittick]] that included the incognito former Nihil [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]]&mdash;landed on Arbra in [[shuttle]]s, meeting with [[Administrator]] [[Lalutin]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

The prisoners were scheduled to work over the course of five [[Local day|days]]; however, while on Arbra, Lalutin&mdash;previously injured by Dee when the two attended [[Carida Academy]]&mdash;sought to reveal her true identity. Lalutin was [[Death|killed]] by Dee's fellow prisoner [[Tasia]], working alongside former Tempest Runner [[Pan Eyta]] Eyta, who also desired revenge against Dee. In the chaos caused by [[Pan Eyta's crew|Eyta's crew]], eight prisoners were killed and three escaped, while Eyta himself disappeared after being supposedly defeated by Dee. As a result of the incident, the ''Restitution'' departed Arbra for its next stop.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date 228">{{StarlightDate|[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4]]|one [[Standard year|year]] and two weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}} Since the events of [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 6|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 6]] are set immediately following the fourth issue's events and depict the [[expansion of the Stormwall]], the latter event must take place in the same year.</ref> the Nihil [[Expansion of the Stormwall|expanded]] their [[Occlusion Zone]] territory in response to a failed [[Assault on the Stormwall|assault]] by the Republic on its [[Stormwall]] barrier. Following the expansion, the edge of the Occlusion Zone bisected the Bon'nyuw-Luq sector, a portion of which was thus trapped behind the Stormwall.<ref name="THRA 6" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|Ah, sweet Arbra. It's good to be home.''"<br />"''Yeah&hellip; it's just a pity about your ''neighbors.''|The Hoojib [[Plif]] and [[Han Solo]]|<ref name="The Hoojib Menace" />}}
The world Arbra in the Bon'nyuw-Luq sector was [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[Hoojib]]s, a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] of small [[Hair|furred]] beings that possessed [[Telepathy|telepathic]] abilities. The Hoojibs [[Diet|fed]] on power sources, feasting on [[Energy crystal/Canon|energy crystals]] in their planet's [[cave]]s.<ref name="The Hoojib Menace" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Bon'nyuw-Luq sector was first mentioned in "[[Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?|Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?]]," an article written by [[Mark Newbold]] for [[StarWars.com]] on [[January 29]], [[2016]].<ref name="Where" /> The [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' later placed Crait in the sector.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> Crait first appeared in the [[2017]] young-adult novel ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', making that novel the Bon'nyuw-Luq sector's first appearance.<ref name="LPoA">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>
