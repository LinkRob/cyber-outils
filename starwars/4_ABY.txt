The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''4 ABY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> (Four Years "After [[Battle of Yavin]]") also known as '''3281 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" />, '''23 AFE'''  in the [[Imperial calendar|Imperial Calendar]] and Year '''7981''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Maz Kanata]] helps [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] acquire the [[Ubese]] armor for her [[Boushh]] disguise.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
*[[Han Solo]] is [[Rescue of Han Solo|rescued]] from [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]]. During the rescue, Leia Organa [[Death|kills]] the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] aboard his own [[sail barge]].<ref name="Episode VI" />
*[[Boba Fett]] is eaten by the Sarlacc Pit of Carkoon, but escapes.
*[[Bib Fortuna]] takes Jabba the Hutt’s throne.
*Having kept the promise he made to [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Yoda]], [[Luke Skywalker]] returned to [[Dagobah]] to complete his [[Jedi training]], but Yoda dies of old age before he could do so. Before dying, Yoda told Luke that he must confront [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to become a [[Jedi Knight]].<ref name="Episode VI" />
*[[Gallius Rax]] promoted to the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[admiral]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>
*The [[Galactic Empire]] began its fragmentation<ref name="Aftermath" /> after the first death of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] over [[Endor]]. The [[Battle of Endor]] also sees the destruction of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]] and redemption of Vader, with the latter becoming Anakin Skywalker once more after returning to the [[Light side of the Force|light side]] and killing Sidious.<ref name="Episode VI" />
*Most remaining Imperials surrender or flee, starting the [[Imperial Remnant]].
*Luke Skywalker holds a [[Funeral of Anakin Skywalker|funeral]] on Endor to honor his father.<ref name="Episode VI" />
*[[Riot in Monument Plaza|A riot]] breaks out in [[Monument Plaza]], beginning the [[Coruscant civil war]].<ref name="Aftermath" />
*A power vacuum opens up on [[Tatooine]] as a result of the death of Jabba<ref name="Aftermath" /> and withdrawal of the [[Imperial Army]]. The [[Mining Collective]] seeks to take advantage of the vacuum and occupies [[Freetown|Mos Pelgo]], though they are soon [[Liberation of Mos Pelgo|single-handledly]] defeated by<ref name="Chapter 9">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref> [[Sheriff]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> [[Cobb Vanth]].<ref name="Chapter 9" />
*[[Mas Amedda]] acted as Emperor in all but name and politics, while holding little real influence beyond Coruscant.<ref name="Life Debt" />
*The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] is [[Declaration of a New Republic|reorganized]] into the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Aftermath" />
*As part of his [[Contingency]] plan, the Empire follow Palpatine's last order via [[Operation: Cinder]].<ref name="Shattered Empire 2">[[Shattered Empire 2|''Shattered Empire'' 2]]</ref>
*[[Mon Mothma]] is elected the first [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] of the New Republic.<ref name="Aftermath" />
*The [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] is restored on [[Chandrila]].<ref name="Aftermath" />
*[[Iden Versio]] and [[Del Meeko]] [[Skirmish on Vardos|defect]] from the Galactic Empire and join the Rebel Alliance/New Republic.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>
*With the help of [[Lieutenant]] [[Shara Bey]] and [[R2-D2]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] travels to [[Vetine]] to [[Mission to Vetine|retrieve]] the remnants of two [[Great Tree (Coruscant)|Great Trees]] that were stolen from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] by [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="SE4">[[Shattered Empire 4|''Shattered Empire'' 4]]</ref>

==Deaths==
*[[Boushh]]<ref name="Boushh Death Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that [[Maz Kanata]] helped [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] acquire the [[Ubese]] [[armor]] for her Boushh disguise on [[Ord Mantell]] thirty [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore the events on Ord Mantell must take place in [[4 ABY]]. {{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Boushh - Bounty Hunter}} states that Boushh died prior to Leia using his armor during [[rescue of Han Solo]], which the ''Galactic Atlas'' dates to 4 ABY. As Boushh was still alive during the events on Ord Mantell, as shown in "[[Bounty Hunted]]," he had to have died sometime in 4 ABY, between the events on Ord Mantell and the rescue of Han Solo.</ref>
