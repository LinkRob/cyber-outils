'''Blane''' was a worker's [[Youngling|child]] of [[Allehander Pso]] on the [[Five Points]] [[space station]] in the [[Five Points system]]. After her mom's duty expired, Pso sold her and his other workers as [[Slavery|slaves]] to the [[Galactic Empire]]. However, [[Jyn Erso]] captured the crew of the spaceship and rescued them.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
