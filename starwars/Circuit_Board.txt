A '''Circuit Board''' was a component used in [[computer]] technology. They could be made out of [[carbonite]].<ref name="GoH" />

{{Tech-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Circuit Boards first appeared in the current [[canon]] in the mobile [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''. In the game carbonite Circuit Boards are used to upgrade the "Relic Amplifiers" of characters,<ref name="GoH" /> which were added to the game [[October 10]], [[2019]].<ref name="Relics">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/213464/title-update-9-10-2019|text=Title Update 9/10/2019}}</ref>
