{{Quote|We just need someone with a datapad and binoculars.|[[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], to [[Luke Skywalker]]|<ref name="Hyperspace Stories 2">[[Hyperspace Stories 2|''Hyperspace Stories'' 2]]</ref>}}
A '''datapad''',<ref name="DB" /> also known as a '''tablet''',<ref name="Choices">{{TOTJ|Choices}}</ref> was a hand-held electronic device used for inputting, storing, and displaying information. They typically featured a [[Hologram|holographic]] screen, and often an input mechanism<ref name="DB" /> such as an array of dials and buttons.<ref name="Voyage of Temptation">{{TCW|Voyage of Temptation}}</ref>

Datapads had a [[datascreen]]<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref> that displayed data for its users, such as displaying a [[cargo manifest]] for a [[starship]].<ref name="Voyage of Temptation" /> At least some datapads had reflective screen surfaces. [[Merei Spanjaf]] once saw a dim reflection of herself when staring at her datapad in consternation.<ref name="ranks">''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]''</ref> 

Some datapads came with a built-in [[locator]]<ref name="justice">''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]''</ref> and a [[Scanner (disambiguation)|scanner]].<ref name="Reunion">{{TBB|Reunion}}</ref> Some datapads also had a built-in [[holoprojector]] that could project holographic images, such as an identification hologram of a [[bounty]] target.<ref name="The Children from Tehar">{{Resistance|The Children from Tehar}}</ref> Additionally, some included a built-in [[distress beacon]] that sent a [[distress signal]] in emergency situations,<ref name="The Missing Agent">{{Resistance|The Missing Agent}}</ref> while others featured secure transceivers to transmit/receive secured information like encrypted coordinates<ref name="No Place Safe">{{Resistance|No Place Safe}}</ref>

Datapads, such as those used by the [[Advanced Science Division]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], could hold access codes, and were used as keys to open locked doors inside secured facilities.<ref name="Shadows">{{TBB|Shadows of Tantiss}}</ref> A [[training datapad]] was a type of datapad used for training by aspiring [[cadet]]s of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Academy|Academy]].<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> Datapads could be used by a technically proficient user to [[Slicer|slice]] and gain access to other secured devices.<ref name="The Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref> They could also act as a [[holodiary]].<ref name="Hunt for Ziro">{{TCW|Hunt for Ziro}}</ref> {{Tech-stub}}
