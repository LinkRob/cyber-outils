The '''Coruscant Patrol''' was a patrol organization that shared its name with the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The organization was active during the [[High Republic Era]] and had a number of [[starfighter]] [[cadet]]s. At some point, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Reath Silas]] met some of the Coruscant Patrol's cadets and felt that they spoke with an aura of firm conviction. While on a [[Mission on the Amaxine station|mission]] to the ancient [[Amaxine Space Station]]<ref name="ITD" /> in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Silas met the young [[Nihil]] [[Nan Hague|Nan]]&mdash;who had been previously disguised as a [[refugee]]. As Nan told him details about her life and how she was proud to be a Nihil, the Jedi reflected that the sureness in her voice was comparative to some Coruscant Patrol starfighter cadets he had encountered, as well as some other Padawans.<ref name="ITD" />
