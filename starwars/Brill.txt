'''Brill''' was a [[Tarsunt]] [[engineer]] who was a [[Lohgarra's crew|crew member]] of the ''[[Mighty Oak Apocalypse]]'' at the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]], working for the [[Wookiee]] [[captain]] [[Lohgarra]]. Ex-[[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[pilot]] [[Thane Kyrell]] was a fellow crew member of hers for a few [[Standard month|months]] following the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Eventually Brill and her crew mates reunited with Kyrell, who was now a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Together, they participated in the [[Battle of Endor]] and were present during the celebrations that took place on the [[Endor|forest moon]] afterwards.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

==Biography==
Brill joined the crew of the ''Mighty Oak Apocalypse'' at some point prior to [[3 BBY]] as a [[navigator]] for Logharra's crew. During 3 BBY, she and the crew accepted a job from [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to transport her to the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Yet, as they approached the destination Leia had originally given them, the senator ordered Brill to change the course of the ''Mighty Oak Apocalypse''. To Brill's shock, Leia had the [[freighter]] diverted to [[Pamarthe]], a [[planet]] which the Empire was preparing cut off from all space-traffic. Regardless of the unorthodox destination, Brill and her crew mates dropped Senator Organa off at an island [[Hangar|Docking Bay]] on Pamarthe as requested.<ref name="Princess">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>

Seven months after the [[Battle of Yavin]], Brill helped fellow crewmate, Thane Kyrell, deliver constructor [[droid]]s to the perpetually-winter world of [[Zeitooine]]. It was also on Zeitooine that Brill was tasked alongside her crew to find a new [[power cell]] for the ''Mighty Oak Apocalypse’s'' aft sensor array; despite searching several second-hand parts vendors, Brill was unsuccessful. Brill later accompanied the crew to [[Dinwa Prime]], [[Arieli]] and [[Ivera X]], becoming a permanent member of team.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

===Mercy Mission to Oulanne===
{{Quote|You realize if you can’t sync your movements with the autonav, you’ll rip us in two.”<br />I’ve got it.|Brill warning Thane Kyrell before he pilots the ''Mighty Oak Apocalypse''|<ref name="Lost Stars" />}}
After a devastating earthquake on the planet [[Oulanne]]’s megacontinent left its population in ruin, Brill assisted the crew of the ''Mighty Oak Apocalypse'' in smuggling medical supplies to the Oulannists. During this run, a megahurricane threatened to destroy the freighter as it entered Oulanne’s atmosphere; with Thane taking over as pilot, Brill watched in bafflement as her crew mate managed to work in tandem with the ship’s autonav to bring the ship safely into a docking bay located on the southern peninsula of Oulanne’s megacontinent. The crew of the ''Mighty Oak Apocalypse'' praised Thane for saving the freighter, though the skeptical Brill attributed his success to him being very “lucky” rather than a credit to his flying skills.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

The following day, Brill helped her crew, and the crew of another smuggling freighter led by [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Wedge Antilles]] unload their medical supplies and emergency rations for the people of Oulanne. After hours of work, Brill had learned that Wedge Antilles was a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and that he had attempted to recruit Thane. While Lohgarra discussed the possibility of joining the Rebel Alliance with the rest of the crew, Brill decided that, while Thane should join, the joining the Alliance was too much of a commitment for her.<ref name="Lost Stars" /> 

===Joining the Rebel Alliance===
{{Quote|We refitted the whole ship. New shields, new dampeners. She’s toting guns from more kinds of fighters than you can count on both hands. Or claws. Tentacles. Whatever you have!"<br />"Ready for action, huh?|Brill telling Thane about the ''Mighty Oak Apocalypse’s'' upgrades|<ref name="Lost Stars" />}}
Despite her reservations, Brill and the rest of the ''Mighty Oak Apocalypse'' joined the Rebel Alliance in [[4 ABY]], arriving on the Rebel-controlled world of [[5251977]] alongside a few [[Braha'tok-class gunship|Braha'tok gunships]]. Upon being reunited with Thane Kyrell aboard the [[MC80 Star Cruiser]], ''[[Liberty]]'', Brill proudly told him about all the upgrades she and Lohgarra had made on the ''Mighty Oak Apocalypse'' to make it combat-ready.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

Just prior to the [[Battle of Endor]], Brill shook Thane’s hand, wishing him good luck as he piloted a [[T-65 X-wing starfighter]] into battle. Thane was later reunited with Brill and the rest of the ''Mighty Oak Apocalypse'' on [[Endor]], all of whom survived the battle. Brill helped Thane’s squadmate [[Yendor Brethen]] repair his [[Astromech droid]], [[JJH2]], before they all joined in a party celebrating the Rebel Alliance’s victory.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

==Behind the scenes==
In [[Claudia Gray]]'s ''[[Lost Stars]]'' novel, Brill was introduced as a new member of the ''Mighty Oak Apocalypse'', having joined the crew some time similar to Thane Kyrell around [[1 ABY]].<ref name="Lost Stars" /> Yet this was [[Retroactive continuity|retconned]] by Brill's second appearance in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', wherein Gray depicted Brill as having been a member of the ''Mighty Oak Apocalypse'' as far back as [[3 BBY]].<ref name="Princess" />
