'''Crait''' was a small salty [[mineral]] [[planet]] located in a remote [[sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]], in grid square N-17 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. Covered with a layer of [[Color|white]] [[salt]] over its red soil, the planet once hosted a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Crait outpost|outpost]] during their [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Many [[Standard year|years]] later, the uninhabited planet became a haven for [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] [[Resistance]], which resulted in a [[Battle of Crait|battle]] between the Resistance and the [[First Order]] on the planet's surface.

==Description==
{{Quote|Not exactly the hottest holiday spot in the galaxy, is it? Though I guess if you really want to get away from it all, this is the place.|Ress Batten|<ref name="LPoA" />}}
[[File:Crait_crystal_creature.png|thumb|left|140px|A Vulptex on the salt-covered surface of Crait]]
Crait was a small, desolate,<ref name="LPoA" /> and uninhabited<ref name="Databank" /> [[planet]] located in a remote [[star system]] of the [[Crait system|same name]],<ref name="LPoA" /> in grid square N-17 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> The planet was rich in [[mineral]]s<ref name="Databank" /> and [[orbit]]ed [[Crait (star)|one]] [[star]]. A thick crust of [[Color|white]]-colored [[salt]] covered nearly the entire planet surface,<ref name="LPoA" /> with red-colored [[rhodochrosite]] base beneath.<ref name="EW">{{WebCite|url=http://ew.com/movies/2017/04/14/star-wars-rian-johnson-last-jedi-planet|text=Rian Johnson reveals details of new planet in ''The Last Jedi'' trailer|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2017-04-14|work=[[Entertainment Weekly Books|Entertainment Weekly]]|archivedate=20170424125855}}</ref> Though dominated by large salt flats, the planet also featured [[mountain]]s, canyons, and deeply sunken [[water]]ways&mdash;which were possibly home to aquatic creatures. Among these subterranean water reserves was a "briny" ocean. The planet had an [[atmosphere]] breathable to [[oxygen]]-breathers, such as [[human]]s, which was fed by the dissolution of underground reserves of solid oxygen and nitrogen-suspending crystals.<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> The white salt-covered surface reflected the sunlight, which would be blinding at midday. The [[sodium]]-containing salt also made the planet surface slippery, hard to travel; with gusts of wind sending up flumes of white salt.<ref name="LPoA" /> The planet was also inhabited by [[Vulptex|Vulptices]], [[Fox (creature)|fox]]-like creatures<ref name="Alien Archive" /> with white, [[crystal]]line [[Hair|fur]]<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> that lived within the burrows and tunnels beneath the surface of Crait.<ref name="EW Vulptex">{{WebCite|url=http://ew.com/movies/2017/11/20/star-wars-the-last-jedi-crystal-foxes|text=Luminous Beings: How ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi''{{'s}} crystal foxes came to life|author=Anthony Breznican|date=2017-11-20|work=[[Entertainment Weekly Books|Entertainment Weekly]]|archivedate=20171120173933}}</ref> Occasionally, large [[crystalstorm]]s would break out.<ref name="Visual Dictionary" />

==History==
===High Republic era===
[[Arkoff]] visited the planet while in the [[Jedi crèche]]. He later told the story to [[Azlin Rell]] about seeing a [[vulptex]] and wanting to take it home as a pet.<ref name="ItL">''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]''</ref>

===Imperial Era===
{{Quote|I find you in the middle of some kind of&hellip;insurgent camp, and ''I'm'' the one who has to answer questions?|Leia Organa, to Bail Organa|<ref name="LPoA" />}}
[[File:Crait_Base_Databank.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Crait was home to both the Rebel Alliance and the Resistance.]]
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the early rebel movement&mdash;a loose network of rebel cells led in secret by [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] of [[Alderaan]]<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref>&mdash;established a secret [[Crait outpost|outpost]] on the [[Crait's northern continent|northern continent]] of the planet. In the [[Standard year|year]] [[3 BBY]], one of these rebel groups [[Attack on Calderos|attacked]] [[Calderos Station]], a large Imperial facility used as a deep-[[Realspace|space]] waypoint, to weaken Imperial control and cover rebel shipment tracks to Crait. Although the rebels presumed that all data about Calderos Station was erased, Bail's adopted daughter, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], managed to find some old space traffic data which linked Crait and Calderos Station. To discover the identity of the attacker, Leia Organa and her guard [[Lieutenant]] [[Ress Batten]] traveled to Crait aboard the ''[[Polestar]]''. After the [[Starship|ship]] landed on the planet's surface, Leia was detained by rebel [[soldier]]s as she refused their proposal to leave Crait immediately. Upon being taken to the base, Leia found out that it was her father who was in charge of this facility, and her [[parent]]s were secretly organizing a rebel movement against the Empire.<ref name="LPoA" />

The rebel movement eventually turned into a more organized [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], and the Crait outpost was [[Evacuation of Crait base|abandoned]] some [[time]] before the [[Battle of Scarif]] in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="UVG" />

Later in the [[Galactic Civil War]], Rebel heroes Leia, [[Han Solo]], and [[Luke Skywalker]] led the Rebellion to Crait while [[Mission to Crait|searching]] for a location for a new Rebel base. During the search, they encountered [[Trusk Berinato]], a [[Gender|male]] individual who employed some [[mining droid]]s to work in the [[Mines of Crait]]. As Berinato showed the site to Leia and Solo, the Empire's [[Task Force 99|SCAR Squad]] had arrived and fought Leia's team. However, SCAR squad was forced to limp away and Leia knew that Crait was not a viable location for a new base.<ref name="Storms of Crait">[[The Last Jedi – The Storms of Crait 1|''The Last Jedi – The Storms of Crait'' 1]]</ref>

===First Order–Resistance War===
{{Quote|The mineral planet, Crait. An uncharted hideout from the days of the Rebellion.''"''<br />''"''That's a Rebel base?''"''<br />''"''Abandoned, but heavily armored with enough power to get a distress signal to our allies scattered in the Outer Rim.|Larma D'Acy and Poe Dameron|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:Battle_of_Crait.jpg|thumb|left|270px|V-4X-D Ski Speeders fighting the First Order forces on Crait.]]
Many years later, some time after the [[Battle of Starkiller Base|destruction]] of the [[Starkiller Base]] in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Crait became a haven for the fleeing forces of the [[Resistance]],<ref name="Databank" /> a small military force founded by Leia Organa to combat the [[First Order]].<ref name="Resistance DB">{{Databank|the-resistance|The Resistance}}</ref> As a result, the planet became a [[Battle of Crait|battleground]] between the two factions, which involved [[V-4X-D ski speeder]]s and the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' facing off with [[All Terrain MegaCaliber Six]] [[walker]]s, [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport|First Order AT-ATs]], [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE/fo]], and [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]s.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Locations==
[[File:Crait-TheStormsOfCrait1.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Crait during the Rebellion's time on the planet.]]
{{Quote|Are there any life signs?''"<br />"''Some movement in the canyons, possibly aquatic creatures, but on the surface&mdash;we've got one tiny scratch of a settlement up here on the northern continent.|Leia Organa and Ress Batten|<ref name="LPoA" />}}
Crait was a desolate<ref name="LPoA" /> and uninhabited world<ref name="Databank" /> which had neither [[Space station|orbital stations]] nor [[spaceport]]s. Prior to 3 BBY, the early rebellion against the Empire established a secret outpost on the northern continent of the planet.<ref name="LPoA" /> The outpost was still there and became a haven for the Resistance<ref name="EW" /><ref name="Databank" /> some time after 34 ABY.<ref name="GA" /> Additionally, Crait hosted [[Mining|mines]] to extract the planet's minerals.<ref name="EW" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|It's way out there. It's very remote. It's uncharted. It's a mineral planet and so there are mines on it.|Rian Johnson|<ref name="EW" />}}
Crait was created and developed for the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="EW" /> The planet was first mentioned in the [[2016]] reference guide ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="UVG" /> Crait made its first appearance in the [[2017]] young-adult novel ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="LPoA" />

First footage of Crait was revealed in the very first teaser trailer for ''The Last Jedi''.<ref name="Teaser">{{SWYouTube|zB4I68XVPzQ|Star Wars: The Last Jedi Official Teaser|sw_url=video/star-wars-the-last-jedi-official-teaser}}</ref> The real-world location for Crait was [[Salar de Uyuni]], the world's largest [[Wikipedia:Salt pan (geology)|salt flat]], located in [[Wikipedia:Bolivia|Bolivia]].<ref name="Worlds">{{SWYouTube|zIauywtvcjI|Star Wars: The Last Jedi &#124; Worlds of The Last Jedi|sw_url=video/worlds-of-the-last-jedi}}</ref> According to the film's writer and director, [[Rian Johnson]], Crait would "play a key role in the movie."<ref name="EW" />

[[File:LegoLastJedi.png|thumb|left|260px|The red salts of Crait was used for merchandising art, including [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO]] sets]]
Crait was also included in ''[[Star Tours: The Adventures Continue]]'', [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] [[Wikipedia:Simulator ride|simulator ride]] [[Wikipedia:Amusement park|theme park]] attraction. [[Anthony Daniels]] reprised his role as [[C-3PO]] for the new sequence,<ref name="Star Tours">{{YouTube|video=e-Lxh51eGW0|text=Star Tours Disney Parks Panel &#124; Star Wars Celebration 2017|channel=channel/UC7SjQGMropNWcVKwtwVQkbg|name=The DIS}}</ref><ref name="Daniels tweet">{{Twitter|ADaniels3PO|status/865504741693857792|[[Anthony Daniels]]|quote=Extremely thrilling! Yesterday I flew to Crait. One day soon you will be able to fly there too because the adventures continue to continue.|archivefile=File:Star Tours Crait.png}}</ref> which debuted at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] on [[November 17]], 2017.<ref name="Star Tours release">{{DPB|url=2017/10/new-adventure-from-star-wars-the-last-jedi-coming-to-star-tours-this-fall|text=New Adventure from ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Coming to Star Tours This Fall}}</ref> Coincidentally, the cavern scene from the film was inspired by a sequence [[Star Tours (real-world)|the original version of the attraction]], where the [[StarSpeeder 3000|Starspeeder]] flew through a [[comet]]<ref name="Starspeeder">{{WebCite|url=https://www.laughingplace.com/w/blogs/disney-buzz/2018/03/14/star-tours-inspired-design-crystal-caverns-last-jedi|text=Star Tours Inspired the Design of the Crystal Caverns in "The Last Jedi"|archivedate=20180314204601}}</ref> Additionally, [[Electronic Arts]] made Crait a DLC for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' as a "Galactic Assault" map on [[December 13]], 2017, coinciding with the release of ''The Last Jedi''.<ref name="EA">{{EA|url=games/starwars/battlefront/star-wars-battlefront-2/news/the-last-jedi-season-is-coming|text=Choose a Side: The Last Jedi Season Is Coming}}</ref>
