'''Comet''' was one of the many [[Reindeer/Legends|reindeer]] owned by<ref name="Christmas" /> the [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]]<ref name="SantaStarWars.com">{{SWArchive|url=welcome/about/news/f20061222/indexp11.html|text="''Star Wars'': The Best of 2006" - Kick Some Brick: LEGO Star Wars II}}</ref> [[Santa Claus/Legends|Santa Claus]], alongside [[Dasher]], [[Dancer (reindeer)|Dancer]], [[Prancer]], [[Vixen]], [[Cupid]], [[Dunder]], and [[Blixem]]. Comet pulled Claus' [[Sled/Legends|sleigh]] on the [[Standard year/Legends|annual]] holiday [[Christmas]], to assist him in his delivery of presents to [[Youngling/Legends|children]] throughout [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]]. After he became unable to deliver all the presents by himself, Santa enlisted the help of his son [[S. Claus]], who would also ride in his [[Parent/Legends|father]]{{'s}} sleigh. On one occasion while working in a [[Droid/Legends|droid]] toy [[Factory/Legends|factory]], the [[Protocol droid/Legends|protocol droid]] [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]] watched Santa, whom he mistook for S. Claus, load presents onto his sleigh and command Comet and the other reindeer to take him away.<ref name="Christmas" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
Comet was mentioned in ''[[Christmas in the Stars]]'',<ref name="Christmas" /> a [[Canon|non-canon]] [[1980]] [[Wikipedia:Christmas|Christmas]] album produced by [[Wikipedia:RSO Records|RSO Records]] that featured ''Star Wars''&ndash;themed Christmas songs.<ref name="Trick Question">{{SW|url=news/when-it-comes-to-christmas-music-is-there-anything-better-than-christmas-in-the-stars-trick-question|text=When it Comes to Christmas Music, Is There Anything Better Than ''Christmas in the Stars''? (Trick Question!)}}</ref> The track in which Comet is featured, "A Christmas Sighting," is a reference to the real-world [[Wikipedia:Christmas|Christmas]] song, [[Wikipedia:A Visit from St. Nicholas|The Night Before Christmas]],<ref name="Christmas" /> which introduced the Christmas legends of [[Wikipedia:Santa Claus's reindeer|Santa Claus's eight reindeer]].<ref name="TNBC">{{BookCite|author=[[Wikipedia:Clement Clarke Moore|Moore, Clement Clarke]] |title=[[Wikipedia:A Visit from St. Nicholas|A Visit from St. Nicholas]]|publisher=[[Wikipedia:Troy Sentinel|Troy Sentinel]]|date=1823-12-23|link=https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Troy_Sentinel/1823/12/23/Account_of_a_Visit_from_St._Nicholas}}</ref>
