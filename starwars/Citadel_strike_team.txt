An '''elite strike team''' of the [[Galactic Republic]] was led by [[High Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] to rescue [[Jedi Master]] [[Even Piell]] from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[Citadel]] [[prison]] on [[Lola Sayu]]. The team consisted of Kenobi, Skywalker, [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]], [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], two [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s&mdash;[[Echo]] and [[Fives]]&mdash;a [[R2-D2's battle droid squadron|battle droid squadron]], and a number of [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="The Citadel" />
