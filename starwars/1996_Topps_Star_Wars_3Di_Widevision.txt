'''''1996 Topps Star Wars 3Di Widevision''''', also simply known as '''''Star Wars 3D''''', was a [[Topps]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' trading card set. The 63-card set featured images from {{Film|IV}} using Topps' patented 3Di (3 Dimensional Imagery) digital technology.<ref name="SWGM 9" /> It was released in [[December]], [[1996]].<ref name="SWGM 9 ad" />
