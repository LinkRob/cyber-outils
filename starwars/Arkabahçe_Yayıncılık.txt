'''Arkabahçe Yayıncılık ve Reklamcılık Ltd. Şti.''', formerly known as '''Arka Bahçe Yayınları''' and '''Büyük Mavi''', is a publishing company that translated various [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novels into [[Wikipedia:Turkish language|Turkish]]. As the license holder they published a number of trilogies like ''[[Star Wars: Darth Bane Trilogy]]'', ''[[Star Wars: The Thrawn Trilogy]]'', and ''[[Star Wars: The Jedi Academy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Arka Bahçe">{{WebCite|url=https://www.arkabahce.com.tr.tr/arkabahce-yayinlari-tr|text=Arka Bahçe Yayınları|language=Turkish|archiveurl=https://archive.is/jyZhS}}</ref>
