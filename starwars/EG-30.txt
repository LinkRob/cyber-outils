'''EG-30''' was a [[GNK-series power droid]] who befriended the [[Jawa]] [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Jitt]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The two maintained a close friendship until the droid was sold to a family of [[moisture farmer]]s in [[1 BBY]]. Decades later, EG-30 worked at the edge of [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]], in an area controlled by the [[Resistance]]. He worked alongside fellow [[power droid]] [[EG-127]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Jitt and EG-30 were inseparable. They were ''never'' apart&hellip;work time&hellip;play time&hellip;nap time&hellip;free time&hellip;vacation time&hellip;and sometimes&hellip;just time.|Emil Graf|<ref name="Gonk" />}}
[[File:EG_and_Jitt.jpg|thumb|left|200px|EG-30 and Jitt nap together.]]
EG-30 was a [[GNK-series power droid|GNK-series]] [[power droid]] who was captured by a [[Nebit's tribe|tribe]] of [[Jawa]]s<ref name="Gonk" /> on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="DBTa">{{Databank|tatooine|Tatooine}}</ref> Several [[Standard month|months]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="Dating">[[Tales from Wild Space: Gonk!]] partially depicts the auction of [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]], which occurs shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. As "Gonk" states that Jitt and EG-30 had spent a few months with each other prior to this, their time together must happen before this event.</ref> he was brought aboard their [[sandcrawler]], where he met the Jawa's [[Technician|chief mechanic]], [[Jitt]]. The two instantly formed a connection, and would spend the coming months sharing nearly all their [[time]] together.<ref name="Gonk" />

Knowing that her fellow Jawas would eventually want to sell EG-30, Jitt installed a device inside her friend that would blow his primary [[motivator]], as no one would desire to buy a faulty [[droid]]. When the Jawas put EG-30 up for sale to a [[moisture farmer]], Jitt was prepared to activate the device, but had a change of heart when she saw the farmer's daughter embrace the droid with a hug. She allowed the Jawas to sell the droid, wanting her friend to be a part of a family.<ref name="Gonk" />

Decades later, during the time of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]],<ref name="Tales Date">"[[The Lost Stories, Part 1]]" establishes that the frame narrative of ''Tales from Wild Space'' takes place some time during the events of the [[First Order-Resistance War]].</ref> the [[cartographer]] [[Emil Graf]] would tell the story of the droid and the Jawa to his companions aboard the ''[[Star Herald]]''.<ref name="Gonk" /> EG-30 himself would eventually find his way to [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the Outer Rim world [[Batuu]]. A few months after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]<ref name="Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> he operated in an area controlled by the Resistance alongside another GNK power droid, [[EG-127]], a veteran of the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="GE" />

==Characteristics==
EG-30 shared a close relationship with his companion Jitt, working alongside the Jawa in both her work duties and her leisure hours. The droid partook in activities not often seen among GNK units; he was known to play against his friend in [[dejarik]] as well as compete in sledding races on the dunes of Tatooine.<ref name="Gonk" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:16-SK_A4-30_SWGE.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A third droids stands with EG-30 and EG-127 in Hollywood Studios.]]
EG-30 first appeared in "[[Tales from Wild Space: Gonk!]]," the secondary story of [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 8|''Star Wars Adventures'' (2017) 8]]. The story was written and illustrated by [[Otis Frampton]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="Gonk" /> on [[March 28]], [[2018]].<ref name="PREVIEWS8">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2017-2020-8/digital-comic/623159|text=Star Wars Adventures (2017-2020) #8|nolive=1}}</ref> The issue erroneously refers to the droid as "RM-30" on two occasions.<ref name="Gonk" />

The droid later appeared as a prop within the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="RS">{{GEAttr|Resistance Supply}}</ref> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The prop is capable of emitting various sound effects.<ref name="RS" /> The droid simultaneously appeared in the [[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]] application launched alongside the themed land, where it was identified as EG-30,<ref name="Datapad" /> although ''Galaxy's Edge'' staff were told to refer to it as A4-30.<ref name="RS" /> The Hollywood Studios version of the Datapad identifies EG-127 as the green GNK-series droid next to the [[X-wing starfighter]], which leaves EG-30 as the gray droid.<ref name="HS Datapad">{{DatapadCite|EG-127|EG30EG16-GEDatapad.png|EG-127HSMenu-GEDatapad.jpg|EG127p1-GEDatapad.png|EG127p2-GEDatapad.png}}</ref> In Disney's Hollywood Studios, a third unnamed GNK-series power droid stands with the pair.<ref name="RS" />
