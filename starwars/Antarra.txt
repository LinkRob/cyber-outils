The '''''Antarra''''' was a stolen [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] used by [[Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] to go on a [[Mission to Vetine|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Vetine]] to recover a [[Great Tree (Coruscant)|Great Tree]].

{{Ship-stub}}

==History==
After the [[Battle of Theed]], caused by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Operation: Cinder]],<ref name="Shattered Empire 3">[[Shattered Empire 3|''Shattered Empire'' 3]]</ref> [[Lieutenant]] [[Shara Bey]] was called by [[Luke Skywalker]] to pilot a stolen [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] named ''Antarra''. Skywalker needed to reach the [[planet]] [[Vetine]] to recover something. The target was a [[Great Tree (Coruscant)|Force-sensitive tree]] kept in an [[Imperial base (Vetine)|Imperial laboratory]], and it was everything remaining of the tree in the heart of [[Coruscant]] [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]].

Bey was asked to impersonate Imperial [[Commander]] [[Alecia Beck]] of [[Imperial Security Bureau]], while Skywalker would have been her bodyguard and [[R2-D2]] her droid. The deception succeeded when Commander [[Hurron]] pretended to believe Beck was there for an inspection; but once Bey and Skywalker entered the Imperial base, they were attacked by Hurron [[Stormtrooper|troops]]. However, they managed to collect two trees—although Skywalker thought only one was in Imperial hands–and escaped killing Hurron and his stormtroopers, leaving Vetine on the ''Antarra''.<ref name="Shattered Empire 4" />
