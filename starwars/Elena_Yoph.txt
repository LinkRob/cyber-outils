'''Elena Yoph''' was a member of the [[Guardians of the Whills]] [[Religion|religious group]] during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="QuiGon" /> At some point between [[68 BBY]] and [[58 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Qui-Gon Jinn|Qui-Gon Jinn's]] time as [[Dooku|Dooku's]] [[Padawan]] as occurring from c. [[68 BBY]] to c. [[58 BBY]]. ''[[Hyperspace Stories: Qui-Gon]]'' depicts Qui-Gon as Dooku's Padawan in the flashback in which [[Elena Yoph]] is featured.</ref> Yoph began remotely corresponding with the [[Jedi]] [[Dooku]] to collaboratively discover the location of the ruins of the underground [[Brotherhood of the Ninth Door temple|temple]] of the defunct [[Brotherhood of the Ninth Door]] located in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] of [[Jedha]]. After Dooku arrived to Jedha City with his [[Padawan]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], the allies met in person to explore the ruins created during the [[Battle of Jedha]].<ref name="QuiGon" />

Yoph considered calling off the search for the ruins after [[Minakatso|a survivor]] of the Brotherhood revealed himself to be vying for access to the temple, but was overruled by the Jedi. The trio eventually did locate and enter the abandoned temple, wherein they were confronted by the Brother, whose adept attempt at confusion involved hassling Yoph in order to goad Jinn into running into the Guardian of the Whills, knocking them both over.<ref name="QuiGon" />
