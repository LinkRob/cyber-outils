The '''Dock Worker''' was a [[Clothing|costume]] owned by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sprocket]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Prodigal Programmer|standard costume]]. This costume consisted of a dusty [[Color|red]] knitted sweater, with muddy black [[boot]]s and slick black [[Jumpsuit|overalls]] featuring a white upper rim and white shoulder straps. It also featured a large brown belt, which housed various pouches, hooks, straps, ropes, and cords.<ref name="Kay" /> The outfit resembled those worn by the [[Opolito|dock workers]] employed on the [[Water|estuary]] [[moon]] of [[Trask]].<ref name="Chapter 11">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Concept art for this Sprocket "Costume" was created by [[Anna Kay]]<ref name="Kay" /> for the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', though it never made it into the final version of game,<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> only revealed after ''Star Wars: Hunters''' cancellation<ref name="Kay" /> on [[October 1]], [[2025]].<ref name="Goodbye Hunters">{{Hunters|url=news/sunset-on-vespaara|text=Sunset on Vespaara}}</ref> It was intended to serve as a set with the [[Dock Worker Chair]] "Weapon Wrap."<ref name="Kay" />
