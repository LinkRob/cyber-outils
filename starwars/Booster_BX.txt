The '''''Booster BX''''' was a custom [[light freighter]] owned by the [[Broken Horn Syndicate]] that served as a raiding vessel during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]].<ref name="Dawn" />

==Characteristics==
The ''Booster BX'' was a custom [[light freighter]] manufactured by [[Beysh Custom Craft]]. It was considered to be a fast [[starship]], and was equipped with a class 2 and a class 14 [[hyperdrive]], had a sophisticated [[computer]] array, a short [[sensor]] range, specialized [[Slicer|slicing]] systems, [[magnet]]ized hull platings, and a [[navigation computer]]. It was armed with a [[turret]]-mounted twin light [[laser cannon]], a turret-mounted twin light [[ion cannon]], and a light [[Tractor beam projector|tractor beam]] turret. It had consumables that could last one [[Standard month|month]] and was crewed by a [[pilot]], a copilot, three [[gunner]]s, and an [[engineer]], with enough room for two passengers. It cost 225,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. Due to its sophisticated systems, the ''Booster BX'' was difficult to operate.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Role==
The ''Booster BX'' was used as a raiding vessel due to its abilities and equipment, as well as slash and grab operations on weapons depots.<ref name="Dawn" />

==History==
During the [[time]] the [[Broken Horn Syndicate]] was active on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]], [[crime lord]] [[Cikatro Vizago]] acquired the ''Booster BX''. The ''Booster BX'' was based out of a [[Ryder's camp|camp]] on Lothal, and served the syndicate by attacking [[Smuggler|smuggling]] ships and then selling the collected data to the [[Galactic Empire]]. At one point, the [[Spectres]] sought to use the ''Booster BX'' to capture an Imperial [[Courier droid|messenger droid]] in Lothal's orbit.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Booster BX'' was first mentioned in the [[2018]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] roleplaying sourcebook ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''.<ref name="Dawn" />
