'''Fondue'''<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=news/andor-trivia-guide-week-1|text=Secrets from the Set of ''Andor'' Season 2: Week 1 Trivia}}</ref> was a dish in which, with a specific type of fork, various sorts of food could be dipped in a pot filled with sauce before eaten. It could be enjoyed and shared among people in a social setting.<ref name="Harvest" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fondue was depicted in "[[Harvest]]," the third episode of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the [[Disney+]] live-action series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', which was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Harvest" /> on [[April 22]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref> The dish name was later revealed to be the same as the real-world [[Wikipedia:fondue|fondue]].<ref name="Trivia" />
