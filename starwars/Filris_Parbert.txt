'''Filris Parbert''' was raised on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] as a war orphan of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], he traveled the conflict's battlefronts as a [[holographer]]; however, after sustaining a traumatic injury he was left dependent on [[cybernetics]]. The injury solidified his view that the Alliance was a destructive force, and following the [[Battle of Endor]] he executed on a concept put forward by Imperial [[chief informational officer]] [[Ferric Obdur]] to create the [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] ''[[Who's Pulling the Strings?]]''. The poster implicated the [[New Republic]] the Alliance had formed into as collaborators with the criminal [[Hutt Clan]].<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
Filris Parbert was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on [[October 25]], [[2016]].
