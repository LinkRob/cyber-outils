{{Quote|Many Bothans died to bring us this information.|[[Mon Mothma]], before the [[Battle of Endor]]|<ref name="Episode VI" />}}
'''Bothans''' were a group known for operating a [[Bothan Spynet|spy network]].<ref name="Heir">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref> Bothans were known to communicate with each other in a series of growls.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>

Bothan [[Spy|spies]] were credited with [[Bothan Spynet intelligence operation|obtaining]] information concerning the location of [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]], despite sustaining heavy [[Death|casualties]], thereby enabling the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] to plan an attack on the unfinished [[space station]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> The Bothans also provided the Rebel Alliance with other useful information, such as the door code for the [[Death Star's shield generator|shield generator bunker]] on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]].<ref name="beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> They forged false [[chain code]]s that were implanted into [[Aqualish]] by [[Nautolan]] doctors.<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|It's pretty easy to confirm: we are purposely trying not to show them to leave all possibilities for when that story gets told. The dude in Resistance is based on the EU design of a Bothan that the Animation art team used as reference for a background alien.|[[Matt Martin]]|<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1157374965257105408|[[Matt Martin]]|date=2019-08-02|archivedate=20221012204527}}</ref>}}
[[File:Canon-Bothans.jpg|thumb|right|250px|How ''Star Wars: How Not to Get Eaten by Ewoks and Other Galactic Survival Skills'' depicts Bothans]]
Bothans were first mentioned in the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']],<ref name="Episode VI" /> and first [[canon]]ically appeared in the [[2016]] novel [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']].<ref name="Bloodline" /> Individuals resembling the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] depiction of the Bothan species<ref name="NEGAS">''[[The New Essential Guide to Alien Species]]''</ref> have appeared in various canon visual media, such as ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'',<ref name="S&H">{{Resistance|Secrets and Holograms}}</ref> ''[[Legends of the Alliance]]'' in the form of [[Mak Eshka'rey|Mak Eshkra'rey]],<ref name="Legends">''[[Legends of the Alliance]]''</ref> ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]'',<ref name="WOTBH Jabba">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Jabba the Hutt 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Jabba the Hutt'' 1]]</ref> ''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]'',<ref name="Sabine Sketchbook">''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]''</ref> and ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'',<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref> but whether these creatures are in fact Bothans has yet to be confirmed.

[[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Pablo Hidalgo]] joked that the reason why Bothans are never seen is because they are such good spies.<ref name="Good Spies">{{Bluesky|infinata.bsky.social|post/3lryy6ztfm22h|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=It's pretty much a recurring bit now. It's how you know they're good spies - you never see them.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/RM6SD}}</ref> Because Bothans were first mentioned in ''Return of the Jedi'', Lucasfilm decided that a live-action production should be the first to establish what they look like.<ref name="Production">{{Bluesky|infinata.bsky.social|post/3lryzy6dt2s2f|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=A decade ago, we figured "they're a production concept; production should define them." No takers yet.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/5Eq50}}</ref>

The reference book ''[[Star Wars: How Not to Get Eaten by Ewoks and Other Galactic Survival Skills]]'' also "pictures" Bothans by showing silhouettes of bothans from the Star Wars Legends continuity with red X's marked over them implying that those silhouettes are not what Bothans look like.<ref name="Eaten">''[[Star Wars: How Not to Get Eaten by Ewoks and Other Galactic Survival Skills]]''</ref> According to [[Matt Martin]] The [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] made the decision to hold off on picturing Bothans.<ref name="Twitter" />
