The '''Coruscant Freighter'''<ref name="By the Numbers">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Ship in the Star Wars Sequel Trilogy|video=bb3vhWXClTY|nolive=1}}</ref> was a model of cargo [[freighter]]<ref name="Episode Gallery" /> named for the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], where it was commonly used during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Senate Murders" /> One Coruscant Freighter was docked within the [[Borgo Prime]] [[shadowport]]<ref name="Barrier">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}}</ref> around [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], at least one of these freighters served as part of [[Chewbacca]] and [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]]'s [[Citizens' Fleet]] at the [[Battle of Exegol]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[Sith Eternal]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
