'''Collor Pondrat''' were a corporation that produced the [[Plug-F Mammoth Split-X engine|Plug-F Mammoth Split-X]] model of [[podracer]] [[engine]]<ref name="USW" /> and sponsored [[podracing]] events. By [[33 BBY|7944]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]], it was long suspected of laundering funds in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and at this [[time]] [[Inspector]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tanivos Divo]] of the [[Coruscant Security Force]] included information on the corporation in a case file that he maintained on podracing. The case file was later published by his grandson [[Exantor Divo]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], which includes the appearance of [[Sebulba's Podracer]], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the [[Dug]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Sebulba]] used Plug-F engines on his vehicle at the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] race on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="USW" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Collor Pondrat was first mentioned in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''.<ref name="USW" /> In the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]], the name is mis-spelt as "Collar Pondrat".<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> The company originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Episode I Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', a [[1999]] reference book written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]].<ref name="TPMIG">''[[Star Wars: Episode I Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
