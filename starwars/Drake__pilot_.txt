'''Drake''' was a [[human]]<ref name="USW" /> [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] who flew for the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy]]. Around [[2 ABY]], during the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Avenger]]''{{'s}} [[Attack on a deep space installation|attack]] on a [[Unidentified deep space installation|deep space installation]] fellow pilot [[Amara Kel]] heard someone screaming over the [[Comm unit|comm]]. The voice sounded like Drake, and Kel thought that Drake's [[death]] would be unpleasant for Kel because Drake owed Kell thirty [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Kel's Rules" />
