The '''709th Legion''', also known as the "'''Red Fury'''," was an elite [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[legion]] in the [[First Order military|military forces]] of the [[First Order]] under the command of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]. The legion's ranks were chosen by Ren [[Gender|himself]], and included specialized units, such as [[First Order heavy trooper|megablaster heavy assault troopers]], consisting in total of eight thousand [[infantry]] [[trooper]]s of the [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]]. [[Lieutenant]] [[Agnon]] and a [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper sergeant|stormtrooper sergeant]] served as staff [[officer]]s of the 709th. The [[TIE/es assault shuttle]] was the legion's [[starship]] and main [[transport]].

The 709th [[Battle of Batuu|occupied]] the [[Black Spire Outpost]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Batuu]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the First Order and the [[Resistance]]. Part of their mission was to seek out and destroy Resistance forces on Batuu; as such, they took a coercive approach when dealing with members of the local population, such as [[Dok-Ondar]] who was warned against aiding the First Order's enemies by the [[sergeant]] in the 709th. During the occupation of Black Spire, a [[squad]] of 709th stormtroopers attempted to capture the [[Clawdite]] fugitive [[Remex Io]].

==Description==
{{Quote|We don't know. But Kylo Ren is on his way, along with the Seven Hundred and Ninth Legion. Those are his elite stormtroopers, chosen by Kylo himself. We're sending help, but&mdash;|[[Green Leader]], to [[Vi Moradi]]|<ref name="Black Spire" />}}
Nicknamed the "[[Color|Red]] Fury," the 709th [[Legion]] was an elite [[infantry]] legion in the [[First Order]] [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]],<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" /> At full strength, the 709th consisted of eight thousand [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]]<ref name="TLJ cross note">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]'' states that [[legion]]s in the [[First Order]] had 8,000 [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]]. Since the 709th Legion was in the First Order, then it must have 8,000 [[trooper]]s.</ref> chosen by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] himself and under his [[Commanding officer|command]].<ref name="Black Spire" /> It utilized several stormtroopers&mdash;both regular [[trooper]]s<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" /> and variant units, such as [[First Order heavy trooper|megablaster heavy assault troopers]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" />

The [[TIE/es assault shuttle]], also known as a [[Twin ion engine|TIE]] echelon, was a [[First Order military]] [[Starship|vessel]] that the 709th utilized for [[transport]]ation. The design of its [[cockpit]] was inspired by [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren's]] [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|''Upsilon''-class]] [[Kylo Ren's command shuttle|command shuttle]] and the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|TIE Advanced x1]] [[pilot]]ed by his [[Skywalker family|grandfather]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="EW 2">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/movies/2019/02/28/disney-parks-galaxys-edge-star-wars-storyline|text=How Disney theme parks made Galaxy's Edge part of the Star Wars storyline|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2019-02-27|work=Entertainment Weekly|archivedate=20191013192631}}</ref>

==History==
===Deployment to Black Spire===
{{Quote|I expect nothing less, Sergeant. And before we are done, Batuu will never be the same.|Lieutenant Agnon|<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" />}}
[[File:TIEEchelonBatuu-GalaxysEdge1.png|thumb|left|300px|A detachment of the 709th Legion "Red Fury" arrived at the Black Spire Outpost, located on the planet Batuu, via a TIE/es assault shuttle.]]
The 709th Legion was active during the [[Galaxy|galactic]] [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the First Order and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timeline tweet">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> following the [[Battle of Oetchi|ascension]] of [[Supreme Leader]] Kylo Ren,<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> the 709th were deployed to the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]]; their [[Battle of Batuu|mission]] at the [[time]] was to occupy the [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] and eliminate Resistance forces in the area, Arriving at the outpost in the TIE echelon, a [[Squad|sub-unit]] of the 709th operated under the command of a [[First Order officer]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Agnon]], as well as a [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper sergeant|stormtrooper sergeant]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" />

The 709th were dismissive of the residents of Black Spire, referring to them as "Batuuan rats" and the outpost as a "rats' nest." However, they did enjoy the fear that the locals felt in the presence of the 709th. Fervently loyal to the First Order, the 709th despised the Resistance and were prepared to destroy all of Black Spire in order to draw out and [[Death|kill]] any insurgents in the vicinity. Such measures were proposed by the [[sergeant]] and supported by his [[commanding officer]], Agnon, who expected nothing less from the [[soldier]]s under his command.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" />

===Accosting a shopkeeper===
{{Quote|Consider this a friendly warning to report any contact with the Resistance. It would be a pity&hellip;if all your rare relics were reclassified as contraband and ordered destroyed.|A stormtrooper sergeant, to Dok-Ondar|<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" />}}
[[File:709thLegion-GalaxysEdge2.png|thumb|right|300px|Tasked with hunting Resistance spies, the 709th began interrogating the population of Black Spire.]]
The First Order's search for Resistance [[Spy|spies]] led the 709th to the [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|Den of Antiquities]], where they interrogated the shop's owner, [[Dok-Ondar]]. While questioning the [[Ithorian]] shopkeeper, the sergeant inspected one of the relics&mdash;[[Ki-Adi-Mundi's lightsaber|the lightsaber]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], who fought in the [[Clone Wars]] until he was [[Order 66|betrayed]] and [[Great Jedi Purge|executed]] by his [[clone trooper]]s. Dok-Ondar interpreted the sergeant's questions as veiled threats and claimed he was nothing more than a humble proprietor, but the sergeant had heard of his reputation for selling valuable relics for the right price, which led him to believe that the Resistance would seek out Dok-Ondar in order to acquire the [[Jedi]] [[lightsaber]]. Before the stormtroopers could leave, having warned Dok-Ondar to report any contact with the Resistance or suffer the confiscation and destruction of property, the Ithorian took note of the sergeant's ignorance of [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]] and felt the need to explain how he procured the lightsaber.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" />

The sergeant found the story neither interesting nor relevant to the 709th's mission. Dok-Ondar's point was that the lightsaber was indeed a powerful symbol; the weapon of a [[Jedi Knight|true warrior]], it outlasted not only its owner but also survived throughout the ages, including the [[Republic Era|fall]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Imperial Era|rise and fall]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], and the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]]. In all likelihood, the lightsaber would outlast even the First Order, according to Dok-Ondar. Furthermore, he warned the sergeant against resorting to strong-arm tactics because the cooperation of influential citizens was essential to the 709th Legion's control of the Black Spire Outpost, which was tenuous at best. The sergeant grudgingly walked away, having received reports of possible Resistance activity in the [[Merchant Row (Batuu)|marketplace]], but warned the Ithorian that he would return.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" />

===Shoot to kill===
{{Quote|Didn't say anything about taking him back "whole," though. If he keeps making me run he'll suffer for&mdash;''"<br />"''Open fire! Do not let him get off another shot. I take back what I said about bringing him in alive.|Stormtroopers of the 709th Legion|<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 3">[[Galaxy's Edge 3|''Galaxy's Edge'' 3]]</ref>}}
[[File:709thLegion-GalaxysEdge3.png|thumb|left|300px|Though ordered to detain Remex Io, the 709th was prepared to kill the scoundrel.]]
Due to [[Moon of Avedot|past events]],<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" /> the First Order issued a warrant for the arrest of [[Remex Io]]&mdash;the [[Clawdite]] member of [[Kendoh Voss]]' [[Kendoh Gang|crew]]&mdash;after discovering the fugitive's location in Black Spire with the help of Dok-Ondar's associate, [[Varg]]. Confronted by a squad of the 709th Legion, Io fled through the marketplace dodging [[blaster]] fire as the stormtroopers pursued him. Despite their orders to take Io alive, the troopers opened fire on their target as they pursued him through the streets, resulting in the death of a civilian who was caught in the crossfire. One of the stormtroopers implied that this was because their orders technically never said anything about bringing him back "whole."<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 3" />

During the pursuit, one of the stormtroopers was shot by Io, causing his squadmates to abandon their efforts to detain him in favor of simply killing the Clawdite. After losing sight of their target, a bystander told the stormtroopers that the fugitive was running in the direction of the [[spaceport]]. They thanked the civilian for assisting the First Order, unaware that it was Io using his [[species]]' shape-shifting ability.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 3" />

===Engaging the Resistance===
{{Quote|Attention all Batuuans. The 709th Legion of the First Order requests your cooperation here at Black Spire Outpost. We value all First Order Loyalists and it would be wise to aid in our efforts.|[[RF-1634]]|<ref name="Star Wars: Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]]</ref>}}
Eventually, the Red Fury encountered two individuals on Batuu that were suspected to be Resistance spies and attempted to apprehend them. However, they ended up engaging the two in a lightfight that proved difficult enough that they were forced to call in their sergeant to provide backup, with the sergeant having to prematurely depart from a briefing with his superior, Lieutenant Agnon. The sergeant was not pleased with his unit having difficulty against only two Resistance spies, especially having a stormtrooper being killed by them, but nevertheless managed to intercept and ultimately kill them via a [[125-Z treadspeeder bike|speeder]] he had brought along. However, it turned out the two individuals had no ties to the Resistance due to their falling easily before him when cornered. The unit was ultimately updated regarding three persons of interest being spotted near the spaceport, with their ultimately leaving the area due to there being nothing of interest in the vicinity, as well as having an informant follow them in case they leave the planet.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 4">[[Galaxy's Edge 4|''Galaxy's Edge'' 4]]</ref>

Eventually, the Red Fury, alongside their sergeant, encountered the Kendoh Gang and trapped them in the Den of Antiquities, with [[RS-2112]], at the sergeant's orders, promptly whacking [[Wooro]] across the face with his baton in a form of intimidation against Voss. However, Voss managed to escape by using a lightsaber to destroy a baby sarlacc's containment field, killing [[ZJ-7199]] in the process. A stormtrooper alerted his comrades about Voss, ordering them to set up a perimeter to trap her. When they received an anonymous tip about their location, they were led off the chase. Dok-Ondar had tricked them again, helping the Kendoh Gang escape as payment for his acquisition of the now completed [[Sword of Khashyun]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 5">[[Galaxy's Edge 5|''Galaxy's Edge'' 5]]</ref>
