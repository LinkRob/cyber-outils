The '''C-PH patrol speeder bike''', also known as the '''Imperial patrol speeder'''<ref name="TOG" /> or as the '''interceptor speeder bike''',<ref name="DB-old">{{Databank|imperial-patrol-troopers|patrol troopers|oldversion=20180416225309}}</ref> was a model of [[speeder bike]] used by [[patrol trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]] to patrol shipyards, cities and other industrial facilities useful to the Imperial cause.<ref name="DB" /> One example of their use was in [[Coronet City]] on [[Corellia]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> [[Imperial security trooper]]s also made use of the speeders.<ref name="Axe Forgets">{{Andor|The Axe Forgets}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
[[File:PatrolTrooperSpeeder-SWBC56.png|thumb|left|200px|A patrol trooper riding a C-PH patrol speeder bike]]
The C-PH patrol [[speeder bike]] was designed to convey power over agility, although it possessed both due to its advanced built-in [[repulsorlift]] maneuverability systems. Its sturdy shape was capable of withstanding any high-speed collisions with other vehicles. In addition, it had onboard sophisticated electronics that alerted the [[Pilot|driver]] of any incoming traffic. Unlike other [[speeder bike]]s, the C-PH lacked outrigger steering vanes, instead making use of a forward-facing bank of repulsorfield vector guides for maneuvering. Its main armament was a forward fixed [[BlasTech Industries]] [[Ax-25 blaster cannon]]. Other features included a retractable illuminator lamp on an articulated mount, control handle rail slots on the sides of the bike, rocker-pivoted foot pedals that controlled the speed and altitude, an acceleration pad with positive traction field near the seat, and the power cell access panel near the rear.<ref name="TOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The C-PH patrol speeder bike first appeared in the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Solo" /> and were identified in the accompaniying reference book''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TOG" />

The speeder's nature was a big friendly debate within the film's design department. While concept artist [[Vincent Jenkins]] wanted it to be another "car" like the other [[landspeeder]]s in the chase sequence as he felt that it was more realistic, comparing  it to the film ''[[Wikipedia:The Cannonball Run|The Cannonball Run]]'', producer [[Simon Emanuel]] always pitched it to be a speeder bike. Emanuel ultimately "won out" and Jenkins felt that it was the right decision. A collage of 25 different speeder bike designs were created by [[Patrick Faulwetter]] with the title "Trooper Car Version 01"; many of which were<ref name="ArtofSolo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> similar to the [[74-Z speeder bike]]<ref name="UltimateSW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> used by the [[scout trooper]]s in ''[[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> Atleast nine different landspeeder designs were created by [[James Clyne]], Jenkins, [[Alex Hutchings]],<ref name="ArtofSolo" /> and [[Jon McCoy]]<ref name="McCoy">{{WebCite|url=https://jonmccoyart.com/blog/vehicles-solo-a-star-wars-story|text=Solo &#124; Vehicles|work=[[Jon McCoy]]'s website|archivedate=20240106154403}}</ref> with Hutchings design&mdash;with the title title "Speeder Model 24"&mdash;featuring "wings" shaped similar<ref name="ArtofSolo" /> of those on a [[TIE Advanced x1]]<ref name="UltimateSW" /> and some of McCoy's designs had closed cabins.<ref name="McCoy" /> Two different bike designs close to the final version were drawn by [[Julian Caldow]] and Clynce after multiple other iterations. Clynce called the design a "very simple slab" and said that the artists had tried to design the simplest shape, reminiscent of [[Rey's speeder]]<ref name="ArtofSolo" /> from ''[[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens]]'',<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> but as an Imperial version with Clynce describing it as [[Brutalist (architecture)|brutalist]]. Clynce got the idea for one of the speeder design during a flight from [[Wikipedia:Fuerteventura|Fuerteventura]] in the [[Canary Islands]]<ref name="ArtofSolo" />&mdash;which served as the location for the film's scenes on [[Savareen]]<ref name="Kasdan3">{{Twitter|JonKasdan|status/1040529511513616385|[[Jon Kasdan]]|date=2018-9-14|quote=continued|archivefile=File:Jon-Kasdan-SoloFacts-September-14-2018.png|archivedate=20181002054217|nolive=1}} {{C|post 3/4}}</ref>&mdash;and drew it as a sketch with only his finger in the [[Wikipedia:Notes (Apple)|Apple Notes]] app, which he found difficult and amused his colleagues. A drawing of the speeder with it's final design was also drawn by Clyne with the title "Trooper Speeder Version 14".<ref name="ArtofSolo" />
