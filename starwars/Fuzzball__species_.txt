'''Fuzzballs'''<ref name="Way Out AD">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> were a type of exotic [[Species|creature]] in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Way Out TG">{{SW|url=season-1-episode-2-trivia-gallery-skeleton-crew|text="Way, Way Out Past the Barrier" Trivia Gallery &#124; Skeleton Crew}} {{C|slide 3}}</ref> During the [[New Republic Era]], a number of Fuzzballs were captured by [[pirate]]s and caged on [[Borgo Prime|Port Borgo]], including [[Fuzzball|one member]] of the species that was also named Fuzzball.<ref name="Fuzzball DB" /> Although eventually freed by a [[Skeleton Crew|group of lost children]],<ref name="Way Out AD" /> that individual Fuzzball<ref name="TMAG Trivia">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-mandalorian-and-grogu-trivia|text=''Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu'' Behind-the-Scenes Facts and Trivia - Update}}</ref> would later be imprisoned again, that being at the hands of [[Lord]] [[Janu Coin]], who kept the winged creature caged within the [[Salt Bar]] on the [[moon]] [[Shakari]].<ref name="TMAG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref>
