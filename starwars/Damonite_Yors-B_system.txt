The '''Damonite Yors-B system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Meridian sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square R-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Damonite Yors B system originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as the [[Damonite Yors-B system]], and was recanonized when it was included in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[October 13]] update to the appendix restored the original spelling of the system's name.<ref name="Substack 2">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref>
