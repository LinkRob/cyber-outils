'''Dava Cassamam''' was a [[Elnacon]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Official Guide" /> from the [[Homeworld|world]] of [[Nacon]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE" /> who was a deep cloud-[[miner]] on [[gas giant]] [[planet]]s and was a keen [[sabacc]] player.<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> She was present during [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Han Solo]]'s first [[sabacc]] game.<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|There's nothing particularly female about her. But that's aliens for you, who knows what sex some of them are? On a side note, there's a sort of default assumption to aliens and creatures that they are male unless they're obviously female — curvy, sexy, or have a feminine costume. In the case of her, why shouldn't she be female? She doesn't need all these tropes or cliches to make her female.|[[Jake Lunt Davies]] on Dava Cassamam's gender|<ref name="LDava Cassamam's Design">{{SW|url=news/solo-a-star-wars-story-designs|text=Inside the Designs of ''Solo: A Star Wars Story''}}</ref>}}
Dava Cassamam was created for the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Solo" /> and was first revealed in the film's first trailer.<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|jPEYpryMp2s|Solo: A Star Wars Story Official Trailer|sw_url=video/official-trailer-solo-a-star-wars-story}}</ref> The character first received identification in the reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="Official Guide" />

In an interview with [[StarWars.com]], creature concept designer [[Jake Lunt Davies]] revealed that given Dava's attributes, the audience would not associate her as a female character, given the assumption that creatures and aliens are male unless they are depicted curvy, sexy or with a feminine costume, so he doesn't see why Cassamam can't be female nor why she needs all these cliches or tropes to make her female. As far Davies recalls, however, Dava started off as another character, including the possibility that her design was intended for [[Dryden Vos]] at one point of development.<ref name="LDava Cassamam's Design" />
