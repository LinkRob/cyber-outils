'''Dotdat San''' was a [[Jedi Knight]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> San was part of a [[Jedi]] team who carried out an [[Assault on Naboo|assault]] on<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Naboo]] in order to liberate it from Nihil [[Occupation of Naboo|occupation]]. During the assault, she [[pilot]]ed a [[Jedi Vector]] within a [[Drift]] led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] in an attack on the ''[[Precipitate Fire]]'', the [[flagship]] of Nihil [[General]]<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Abediah Viess]].<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 4">[[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 4|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 4]]</ref> While engaging [[Starfighter|fighters]] deployed from the ''Precipitate Fire'', the wing of Jedi Knight [[Ceryl]]'s [[Starship|Vector]] tapped the Nihil flagship, knocking her out of the Drift and spinning and careening out of control. In response, San followed after Ceryl.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

After the planet was liberated, San was present in the [[San Tekka clan]] [[San Tekka compound|compound]], with Kriss noticing she was on her second helping of a [[soup]] cooked by Master [[Porter Engle]]. Not long after, San and Ceryl accompanied Kriss and multiple other [[Jedi]] as they left Naboo for [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Realspace|space]] on the [[Emissary-class Republic Cruiser|cruiser]] ''[[Third Horizon]]''.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
