'''Alderaanian shimmersilk''' was a type of [[shimmersilk]] that shared its name with the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. During the [[Republic Era]], the fabric was expensive and was used to make traditional [[Pijali court tunic]]s on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]].<ref name="MA" />

For the [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]] of Pijal's [[Princess]] [[Fanry]]<ref name="MA" /> in [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' to [[40 BBY]].</ref> Pijal's [[Jedi]] [[lord]] [[regent]] [[Rael Averross]] wore a [[Color|black]] Pijali court ensemble, pairing its tunic with a black [[cape]] featuring cerulean blue lining. Averross was unused to the fancy [[clothing]]'s styling, as he usually preferred to present an unkempt appearance, but allowed a [[Unidentified WA-2G droid|WA-2G droid]] to finish preparing him for the event. Several individuals seated for the coronation noticed Averross's choice of clothing when they attended the event not long afterward.<ref name="MA" />
