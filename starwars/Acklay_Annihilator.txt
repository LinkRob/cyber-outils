The '''Acklay Annihilator''' was a modified [[blaster rifle]] utilized by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Firestorm Rifle|standard rifle]]. This [[blaster]] [[rifle]] consisted of a [[Color|black]], gray, and silver frame, with a [[Bone|bony]] fragment covered in spikes, potentially once belonging to an [[acklay]], strapped to the top.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Acklay Annihilator was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Imara Vex's Firestorm Rifle, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 0.18.0 within the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Acklay Slayer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
