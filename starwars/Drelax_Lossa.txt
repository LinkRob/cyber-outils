'''Drelax Lossa''' was a wealthy individual who was a sympathizer of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during its [[Galactic Civil War|war]] with the [[Galactic Empire]]. They provided an endless supply of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who used the [[Currency|funds]] to buy [[Starship|ships]] for the Alliance.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'' to [[4 ABY]].</ref> Organa saw Lossa in the [[Atrium (Halcyon)|Atrium]] of the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' as the former was having her honeymoon aboard the vessel.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drelax Lossa appeared in ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'', a [[2022]] novel written by [[Beth Revis]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />
