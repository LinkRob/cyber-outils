A '''Chakino eel''' was a type of [[Color|green]] [[eel]] served frozen along with [[Duradan Soup]] in [[Kijimi City]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>
{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 ABY, the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] and her [[Merqaal]] companion [[Nix]] share a meal of Duradan Soup with [[Chakino eel]]. Nix got his [[tongue]] stuck on the [[water|ice]] encasing the eels before they had thawed and were ready to eat.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Chakino eel first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. They were served with [[Duradan Soup]], which was one of the galactic street foods featured in the game that gave equipable perks to Nix. The Duradan Soup perk in ''Star Wars Outlaws'' allowed Nix to distract multiple targets but reduced the distraction range.<ref name="Outlaws" />
