'''''Blood Oath''''' was a novel set to be written by [[Elaine Cunningham]] for [[Lucasfilm]] and [[Del Rey]], with an original release date of [[April]] [[2009]]. It would star the [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] [[Zekk]] encountering adventure, romance and [[Politics/Legends|political]] intrigue in the [[Hapes Consortium/Legends|Hapes Consortium]]. Due to a prolonged sickness, however, Cunningham was unable to complete the book or submit a manuscript. After several deadline extensions, the book was ultimately canceled.

Set in [[41 ABY]], the novel would have featured Zekk encountering a [[Unidentified pirate captain (Zekk)|pirate captain]], being sent on a mission by [[Hapan]] [[Queen Mother]] [[Tenel Ka Djo]], and teaming up with Djo's cousins [[Trista Zel|Trista]] and [[Taryn Zel]] to halt a brewing conflict. [[Jagged Fel]], the newly-appointed [[Head of State/Legends|Head of State]] of the [[Imperial Remnant]], would have been the focus of another plot that dealt with him settling into his role. However, ''Blood Oath''{{'s}} cancellation rendered its events non-[[canon]].

''Alongside Jaina Solo, he confronted the forces of Darth Caedus&mdash;the most powerful of the Sith Lords&mdash;in a battle that nearly destroyed him. But his story isn't over. For Zekk, the future holds much more&hellip;<ref name="firstlook" />

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|I enjoyed the contrast of Zekk coming into his own against the background of this very strong matriarchal society.|Elaine Cunningham|<ref name="Elaine Cunningham interview" />}}
[[File:Zekk.png|thumb|left|150px|Zekk, the would-be star of ''Blood Oath'']]
Author [[Elaine Cunningham]] described her novel ''Blood Oath'' as an adventure story that also contained [[Politics/Legends|political]] intrigue, humor, and romance.<ref name="hopeful">{{WebCite|url=https://www.tapatalk.com/groups/unjoe/hopeful-futures-facts-for-millennium-falcon-and-be-t3607.html|text=Hopeful Futures: Facts for Millennium Falcon and beyond (06/29/10)|work=Beyond the New Jedi Order|archivedate=20191111091050|nolive=1}}</ref> It was set to feature the [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] [[Zekk]],<ref name="vault" /> and the book would begin with Zekk spinning in his [[StealthX]] [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]]<ref name="blog2" /> following [[Battle of Uroro Station|events]] of the novel [[Legacy of the Force: Invincible|''Invincible'']] that saw him go missing.<ref name="invincible">''[[Legacy of the Force: Invincible]]''</ref> It would then cover his quest to rejoin the [[New Jedi Order]].<ref name="blog2" />

Desperate to contact the Order, Zekk would find himself at the mercy of a [[Unidentified pirate captain (Zekk)|pirate captain]] and in the middle of a volatile conflict that he would work to halt.<ref name="firstlook" /> Along the way, he would be drawn into [[Hapan]] politics and go on a mission for [[Tenel Ka Djo]], the [[Queen Mother]] of the [[Hapes Consortium/Legends|Hapes Consortium]].<ref name="Elaine Cunningham interview" /> He would also encounter Djo's cousins [[Trista Zel|Trista]] and [[Taryn Zel]], whom had first appeared in ''Invincible'',<ref name="coffeespill">{{SWBoards|url=message.jspa?messageID=17427002#17427002|thread=Books, Comics, & Television VIPs|user=[[Sue Rostoni]]|date=2008-11-06|time=10:24 AM}}</ref> and some slight romance would emerge.<ref name="Elaine Cunningham interview" />

Djo would also be the subject of an [[Assassin/Legends|assassination]] attempt by the [[Imperial Remnant]]'s [[Council of Moffs/Legends|Council of Moffs]], a group that had uncovered an ancient secret capable of [[Death/Legends|destroying]] all of the Hapan people. Meanwhile, [[Jagged Fel]], the newly-appointed [[Head of State/Legends|leader]] of the Remnant, would step into his role and make efforts to reign in the [[Moff/Legends|Moffs]] and their nest of intrigue.<ref name="firstlook" />

==Development==
{{Quote|Blood Oath and Boba Fett. No, it's not good news. Both are cancelled.|Sue Rostoni on ''Blood Oath'' and an [[untitled Boba Fett novel]]|<ref name="axed" />}}
After debuting in the [[1995]] young adult novel ''[[Young Jedi Knights: The Lost Ones]]'',<ref name="The Lost Ones">''[[Young Jedi Knights: The Lost Ones]]''</ref> Zekk went on to play a sizeable role in the [[Star Wars: The New Jedi Order|''New Jedi Order'']], [[Star Wars: The Dark Nest Trilogy|''Dark Nest'']], and [[Star Wars: Legacy of the Force|''Legacy of the Force'']] book series.<ref name="invincible" /><ref name="sbs">''[[The New Jedi Order: Star by Star]]''</ref><ref name="tuf">''[[The New Jedi Order: The Unifying Force]]''</ref><ref name="tjk">''[[Dark Nest I: The Joiner King]]''</ref><ref name="betrayal">''[[Legacy of the Force: Betrayal]]''</ref> ''Legacy of the Force'' wrapped up in [[2008]],<ref name="invincible" /> the same year that [[Lucasfilm]] extended an existing publishing contract with [[Del Rey]] books, granting Del Rey the rights to publish forty-five ''[[Star Wars]]'' books from [[2009]] to [[2013]].<ref name="extension">{{SWArchive|url=vault/books/news20080912.html|text=Del Rey ''Star Wars'' Books Continue Through 2013}}</ref> One of the novels slated for Spring 2009 was ''Blood Oath'', which veteran ''Star Wars'' author Elaine Cunningham was hired to pen.<ref name="vault" /> The story would take place in [[41 ABY]].<ref name="date" />

Prior to [[StarWars.com]]'s official announcement of the contract extension, a listing for ''Blood Oath'' appeared on [[Random House]]'s online catalog, in early [[September]] 2008.<ref name="random">{{SWBoards|url=message.jspa?messageID=17356896#17356896|thread=Books, Comics, & Television VIPs|user=S1th'ari|date=2008-09-05|time=10:06 PM}}</ref> The announcement was made one week later,<ref name="extension" /> and ''Blood Oath'' received a press release on [[September 18]].<ref name="vault" /> At the end of the month, [[Sue Rostoni]], the Executive Editor of [[Lucas Licensing]], gave a release date of [[April 28]], 2009.<ref name="blog1">{{Blog|eeusuestornii|11|A bit about the new novels.....}}</ref>

[[File:Elainecunningham.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Elaine Cunningham, the author of ''Blood Oath'']]
Cunningham's task was to write a standalone adventure set between the two big story arcs of ''Legacy of the Force'' and [[Star Wars: Fate of the Jedi|''Fate of the Jedi'']]. She was excited to take Zekk, a perennial sidekick, and give him a tale of his own&mdash;as he had always been defined by his relationship to [[Jaina Solo Fel|Jaina Solo]], Cunningham enjoyed the chance to have him rise above his past and become his own man, in a story arc that could contrast with the background of a strong, matriarchal society.<ref name="Elaine Cunningham interview" />

While writing the book, Cunningham underwent a long stretch of bad health, which included anemia and a very low resistance in her immune system. She was constantly sick for several years, and she found it very difficult to stay awake, focus, or hold a story in her head. She has said that Del Rey were very supportive and were willing to give her numerous deadline extensions,<ref name="Elaine Cunningham interview" /> and in [[December]] 2008, Rostoni revealed a new release date of December 2009. Lucasfilm and Del Rey had hoped for the novel to be released concurrently with ''Fate of the Jedi''{{'s}} [[Fate of the Jedi: Outcast|first volume]] in [[March]] 2009,<ref name="pushback1" /> but ''Blood Oath''{{'s}} new release date ended up being [[December 29]].<ref name="pushback1">{{SWBoards|url=message.jspa?messageID=17480399#17480399|thread=Books, Comics, & Television VIPs|user=[[Sue Rostoni]]|date=2008-12-18|time=12:25 PM}}</ref>

On [[January 6]], 2009, [[StarWars.com]] released a publisher's summary and [[Artist/Legends|artist]] [[Dave Seeley]]'s cover art for the book.<ref name="firstlook" /> Cunningham was actively blogging about the book, but the deletion of her blog and the removal of ''Blood Oath'' from the Random House catalog in [[June]] led to fan speculation that the novel had been canceled.<ref name="blogdelete">{{SWBoards|url=message.jspa?messageID=17653340#17653340|thread=Books, Comics, & Television VIPs|user=S1th'ari|date=2009-06-10|time=5:07 PM}}</ref> Rostoni revealed that the manuscript had yet to be turned in due to events beyond Cunningham's control or wishes, and that although Lucasfilm Ltd. wanted to keep the novel in their lineup, ''Blood Oath'' would indeed be cancelled if it was not submitted soon.<ref name="juneupdate">{{SWBoards|url=message.jspa?messageID=17661356#17661356|thread=Books, Comics, & Television VIPs|user=[[Sue Rostoni]]|date=2009-06-22|time=3:09 PM}}</ref>

Pending a manuscript submission, the book now had a projected release date of April [[2010]].<ref name="juneupdate" /> By [[August]] 2009, however, the manuscript had not been submitted, and ''Blood Oath'' was officially canceled.<ref name="axed" /><ref name="comiccon">{{WebCite|url=http://www.njoe.com/2009/08/14/blood-oath-and-fett-novel-officially-canceled|text=Blood Oath and Fett Novel Officially Canceled|work=New Jedi Order Encyclopedia|archivedate=20100919124452}}</ref> According to Cunningham, she, Lucasfilm and Del Rey had jointly decided that it was time to let the novel go.<ref name="Elaine Cunningham interview" />

==Continuity==
{{Quote|What was going to happen in it didn't really happen. Not like any of this really happens&hellip; but you know what I mean.|Sue Rostoni|<ref name="not-canon" />}}
''Blood Oath''{{'s}} cancellation deemed the events that would have happened in it non-[[canon]].<ref name="not-canon">{{SWBoards|url=message.jspa?messageID=17704276#17704276|thread=Books, Comics, & Television VIPs|user=[[Sue Rostoni]]|date=2009-08-14|time=9:35 AM}}</ref> The 2010 ''Fate of the Jedi'' novel [[Fate of the Jedi: Backlash|''Backlash'']], however, featured Zekk and Taryn Zel in minor roles. Set two [[Standard year/Legends|years]] after ''Invincible'', it established that Zekk had re-emerged six [[Standard month/Legends|months]] after his disappearance, romantically involved with Zel, and that neither of them had answered any questions regarding where they had been.<ref name="backlash">''[[Fate of the Jedi: Backlash]]''</ref> The two went on to appear in several other volumes of ''Fate of the Jedi'',<ref name="Vortex">''[[Fate of the Jedi: Vortex]]''</ref><ref name="Apocalypse">''[[Fate of the Jedi: Apocalypse]]''</ref> as did Jagged Fel and the Imperial Moff [[Drikl Lecersen]], both of whom played prominent roles.<ref name="backlash" /><ref name="Abyss">''[[Fate of the Jedi: Abyss]]''</ref><ref name="Ascension">''[[Fate of the Jedi: Ascension]]''</ref>
