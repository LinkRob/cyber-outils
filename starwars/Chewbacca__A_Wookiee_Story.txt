"'''Chewbacca: A Wookiee Story'''" is a short story that was published in the [[K-Zone Volume 19, Number 5|May 2018]] issue of the Australian magazine ''[[K-Zone]]''. It takes the form of a log entry made by [[Chewbacca]]. Each entry is written in [[Shyriiwook]] with an [[Galactic Basic Standard|English]] translation provided immediately below each entry.<ref name="K-Zone" />
