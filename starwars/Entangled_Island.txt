The '''Entangled Island''' was an island in [[Lake Amari]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. It was rumored among locals to be a drop-off point for smugglers.<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>  the materials required to apply a [[Color|violet]] [[Brezak]] paint scheme to a [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]] were stored in a container on the island.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During the same year,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> a [[Weequay]] [[Unidentified Weequay mechanic (Sashin)|mechanic]] in [[Sashin]], a [[city|village]] in the [[Karaba Foothills]], challenged [[Kay Vess]] visited the island noting that they hadn't been able to make to find out if the rumors were true. Vess later traveled to the island after installing a [[hydro-repulsor]] in her speeder bike and recovered the paint materials.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Entangled Island first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The location contained a paint scheme that players could use to customize the appearance of their speeder bike.<ref name="Outlaws" />
