'''4724''' was the designation of a [[droid]] who served in the [[Separatist Droid Army]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Galactic Republic]]. In [[21 BBY]], the droid was deployed to the [[Planet|world]] of [[Maridun]] as part of the [[Lok Durd's battalion|battalion]] under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[General]] [[Lok Durd]], who had arrived to test the [[defoliator]] weapon against the planet's [[Lurmen]] [[colonist]]s.

Following his initial test to prove it would not harm mechanical beings, Durd left with most of his force toward the [[Lurmen village]], leaving 4724 behind as part of the small droid compliment tasked with keeping their [[Separatist outpost (Maridun)|outpost]] secure. Unknown to the droids, three [[Jedi]] and two [[clone trooper]]s from the [[Republic Military]] were also on Maridun and raided the base in the night. 4724 was destroyed with the rest of the compliment left behind at the outpost.

==Biography==
{{Quote|First we'll take out the droids' communication station.|General Anakin Skywalker declares that the base where 4724 is stationed needs to be taken out|<ref name="DoP" />}}
4724 was a [[droid]] who was active in the [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] waged between the Confederacy and the [[Galactic Republic]]. 4724 was part of the [[Confederacy military|military]]<ref name="DoP" /> [[Lok Durd's battalion|battalion]]<ref name="DoP AD">{{TCW|Defenders of Peace}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> that served under a [[Neimoidian]] [[general]]<ref name="DoP" /> and weapons designer<ref name="DDT tank">{{Databank|ddt-tank|DDT tank}}</ref> named [[Lok Durd]].<ref name="DoP" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> 4724 and many other droids under Durd's command traveled to the [[planet]] [[Maridun]]<ref name="DoP" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Ultimate SW New">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> to test the [[defoliator]], an [[Species|organic]]-destroying weapon<ref name="DoP" /> Durd had conceived of.<ref name="DDT tank" /> 4724 was stationed at the [[Separatist outpost (Maridun)|outpost]] the general's forces established as a communications station.<ref name="DoP" />

After Durd's initial test of the weapon, which proved the defoliator would not harm [[battle droid]]s, the general led most of his [[troop]]s away to test the defoliator on a [[Lurmen village|village]] of [[Lurmen]] [[colonist]]s. 4724 was left behind at the base as part of its small security compliment to ensure Durd was not greeted with any issues when he returned. 4724 was tasked with keeping the base's west end secure. Unknown to the Separatists, a group of Republic combatants&mdash;[[Jedi General]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Aayla Secura]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Clone Commander]] [[Bly]], and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]]&mdash;were marooned on Maridun<ref name="DoP" /> after their [[Unidentified Consular-class cruiser (Quell)|''Consular''-class cruiser]] sustained damage during the [[Battle of Quell]] and crashed.<ref name="Jedi Crash">{{TCW|Jedi Crash}}</ref>

[[File:B1-in-command-center.png|thumb|right|250px|A B1 battle droid (pictured) asked for an update from 4724 on the security of the west end.]]
After [[Standard day|night]] had fallen, while 4724 was busy at the outpost's west end, the [[Jedi]] and [[clone trooper]]s moved to take out the outpost and steal a [[Unidentified Sheathipede-class transport shuttle (Maridun)|''Sheathipede''-class transport shuttle]], so as to reach the village before the incoming attack. As the Jedi and [[Cloning|clones]] quietly took out the droid guards, a [[Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Maridun)|B1 battle droid]] stationed within the outpost's [[Separatist command center|command center]] ordered 4724 to provide him with an update on the west end's security; instead of receiving an answer, the [[B1-series battle droid|B1 unit]] was then cut down by [[Skywalker lightsaber|Skywalker's lightsaber]].<ref name="DoP" /> 4724 and every other droid in the base was taken out,<ref name="Date" /> allowing the Republic combatants to steal the [[shuttle]] and return to the Lurmen. There, the Republic triumphed in battle over Durd, as the Jedi and clones took out his battle droids and destroyed the defoliator.<ref name="DoP" />
