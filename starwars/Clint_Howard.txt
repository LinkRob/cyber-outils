'''Clint Howard''' (born [[April 20]], [[1950s|1959]])<ref name="Tribute" /> is an American actor. In [[2012]], he appeared as [[Hurst Romodi/Legends|Hurst Romodi]] in a commercial for the video game ''[[Kinect Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Slashfilm">{{WebCite|url=https://www.slashfilm.com/clint-howard-in-han-solo|text=Clint Howard Cast in {{'}}Han Solo{{'}}; Here Are All of The Times Clint Appeared In Ron's Movies|accessdate=2020-06-16|author=Ben Pearson|date=2017-08-03|archivedate=20190114080345}}</ref> Six years later, he had a brief role as [[Ralakili]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. He is the younger brother of director [[Ron Howard]] and the uncle of [[Bryce Dallas Howard]].<ref name="EW">{{WebCite|url=http://ew.com/movies/2018/04/21/han-solo-star-wars-clint-howard-character|text=Ron Howard reveals brother Clint Howard's Solo: A Star Wars Story character|author=Romano, Nick|date=2018-04-21|archivedate=20180627134349}}</ref>
