{{Quote|Wait a minute! This is a Hosnian Prime ship! ''[&hellip;]'' Most Hosnian ships keep a data log backup of any transmissions sent and received from the ship. It's a feature that only comes with luxury ships like this.|Kazuda Xiono, to BB-8 about the data log backup|<ref name="AABR" />}}
A '''data log backup''' was a type of communications equipment used in luxury [[Racing starfighter|racers]] that recorded all transmissions sent and received from a [[Starship|ship]]. By [[34 ABY]], a [[Unidentified pilot (Hosnian Prime)|pilot]] from the [[planet]] [[Hosnian Prime]] had one installed on their [[Unidentified backup racer|backup racer]]. That [[Standard year|year]], the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] borrowed the [[Starfighter|racer]] to participate in the [[All Aces Battle Royale]] [[Sky race|race]] and recovered the [[First Order]] operative [[Marnia Un'la'na]]'s transmission from the vessel's backup.

==Description==
A type of communications equipment, a data log backup was often used in luxury [[Racing starfighter|racers]] and recorded all transmissions sent and received from a [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="AABR" />

==History==
[[File:BoltShattererKRacer-AABR.jpg|thumb|right|285px|Kazuda Xiono retrieved a First Order transmission from a backup racer's data log backup.]]
By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|SWA|All Aces Battle Royale}}</ref> many ships from the [[planet]] [[Hosnian Prime]] used the devices, including a [[Unidentified backup racer|backup racer]] owned by [[Unidentified pilot (Hosnian Prime)|one]] of the world's [[Homeworld|native]] [[pilot]]s.<ref name="AABR" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the pilot brought their [[Starfighter|racer]] to the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]'' to participate in the [[All Aces Battle Royale]] [[Sky race|race]].<ref name="AABR" />

The [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] borrowed the vessel to participate in the event and used it to intercept a data cache sent by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Information broker|informant]] [[Marnia Un'la'na]] by recording it with a [[Republic encryption recorder]]. However,<ref name="AABR" /> the [[ace]] pilot [[Hype Fazon]]'s ship&mdash;the ''[[Green Ace]]''<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>&mdash;collided with the racer, forcing the Resistance spy to crash land. Though Xiono was initially saddened about the recorder's destruction, after contemplating, he realized that the data log backup of his borrowed racer would have stored Un'la'na's cache and told the [[BB-series astromech droid|astromech droid]] [[BB-8]] of his revelation. The Resistance spy then recovered her transmission, wiped the ship's internal systems, and returned the vessel to its owner's [[hangar]].<ref name="AABR" />

==Behind the scenes==
A data log backup appeared in "[[All Aces Battle Royale (comic story)|All Aces Battle Royale]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story written by [[Kevin Burke]] and [[Chris "Doc" Wyatt]] and illustrated by [[Valentina Pinto]]. The story was published by [[IDW Publishing]] in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 16|sixteenth issue]] of the [[2017]] [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] comic series,<ref name="AABR" /> which was released on [[December 19]], [[2018]].<ref name="Amazon">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2017-2020-16/digital-comic/713512|text=Star Wars Adventures #16|nolive=1}}</ref>
