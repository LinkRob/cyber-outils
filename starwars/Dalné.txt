'''Dalné''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Reproduction|born]] around [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> who served as the [[Monarch of Naboo]]<ref name="Leia" /> in [[3 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Although the [[king]]s and [[queen]]s of the past actually ruled, Dalné's role was mostly ceremonial, as [[Naboo]]'s concerns now rested in the hands of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[governor]], [[Moff]] [[Quarsh Panaka]]. Though the queen resented this, she was unable to countermand or resist it. She was around the same age as [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]], who visited her<ref name="Leia" /> in 3 BBY.<ref name="Timelines" /> Frustrated by her lack of power and ability to actually rule, Dalné leapt at the chance to do something useful when Organa offered her a place on her relief mission to distribute supplies.<ref name="Leia" />

Learning of the corruption and ill-treatment of the [[miner]]s, the two women resolved to meet with Panaka and attempt to convince him to act, with the queen lending Organa a Naboo [[jubilation dress]] from her personal wardrobe to replace her soot-stained gown. Though Dalné was half-convinced that Panaka would not listen, she was both elated and confused when he did, and even more so when he began asking Organa personal questions regarding her birth [[parent]]s. Both women were unaware that Panaka had noticed Organa's resemblance to one of Dalné's predecessors, the late [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], who was in fact Organa's birth mother, and that he was intending to inform the Emperor of his idea.<ref name="Leia" />

Both women then bore witness to Panaka's [[Strike on Onoam|assassination]] by the [[Partisans]], narrowly escaping [[death]] by the skin of their [[Tooth|teeth]] when the Moff's [[Quarsh Panaka's chalet|chalet]] was destroyed by a bomb. Though shaken by Panaka's murder, Organa encouraged Dalné to take the opportunity, before another [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] governor was appointed, to enforce measures to protect miners on Naboo's [[moon]] [[Onoam]]. Though the young queen was skeptical of actually succeeding, she nonetheless resolved to do her best.<ref name="Leia" />
