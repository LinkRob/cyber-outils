The '''drowan''' was a [[forest]] [[Species|creature]] found on the [[planet]] [[Öetrago]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the forest habitat of the drowan and other creatures of Öetrago was [[Forest fires on Öetrago|ravaged]] by [[fire]]s, and [[Jedi Master]] [[Sskeer]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Keeve Trennis]], were dispatched to the planet. The two [[Jedi]] held back the flames so that the creatures could escape, but one drowan calf became trapped. Sskeer left his Padawan to hold the blaze on her own while he rescued the drowan.<ref name="The High Republic 3" />
