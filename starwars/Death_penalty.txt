The '''death penalty''', also known as '''execution''' or '''capital punishment''', was the practice of imposing the punishment of [[death]] for people convicted of certain serious crimes. A crime worthy of the death penalty was known as a '''capital offense'''.

==Description==
[[Planet|Worlds]] with the death penalty usually counted [[treason]] as a capital offense.<ref name="Bloodline" /> The [[Yavin Code]] declared that all [[prisoner]]s set to be executed were to be blindfolded, but the code's regulations largely fell out of use as the [[Standard century|centuries]] went by.<ref name="the Big Bang">{{TCW|The Big Bang}}</ref> The death penalty was often utilized by [[pirate]]s, who were infamous for tossing their convicts out of [[airlock]]s.<ref name="SC 101">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}}</ref> 

==History==
===High Republic Era===
By time of the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] did not make use of the death penalty.<ref name="Into the Light">''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]''</ref>  The [[Nihil]] marauders made use of execution to punish failure<ref name="Jedi Light">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> and treason. During the [[Nihil conflict]], [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]] pointed out to her mother, the former [[Senator]] [[Ghirra Starros]], the difference in punishment between the Republic and Nihil as proof she was serving the wrong side.<ref name="Into the Light" /> 

===Clone Wars===
{{Quote|Do not insult me with false deliberations. Are you not soldiers now? Is my punishment not to be death?|Prosset Dibs, to the Jedi High Council|<ref name="Mace Windu 5">[[Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu 5|''Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu'' 5]]</ref>}}
In the early [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Tenth Brother|Prosset Dibs]] was found guilty of [[treason]] for attempting to murder fellow [[Jedi]] Master [[Mace Windu]] during the [[operation on Hissrich]]. Dibs, who had come to regard the Jedi as tyrants, expected his punishment to be [[death]], but he was instead sentenced to rehabilitation at Windu's request.<ref name="Mace Windu 5" />

[[File:Rexecutioner-CoK.png|thumb|left|300px|Jedi General Pong Krell was executed for treason during the Battle of Umbara.]]
In the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], insurrection was a [[court-martial]] offense punishable<ref name="PoD">{{TCW|Plan of Dissent}}</ref> by arrest<ref name="The Hidden Enemy">{{TCW|The Hidden Enemy}}</ref> or even death. During the [[Battle of Umbara]], [[Jedi General]] [[Pong Krell]] ordered the arrest of [[clone trooper]]s [[Fives]] and [[Jesse]] for disobeying their [[commanding officer]], knowing that they would be court-martialed, found guilty and consequently executed for treason.<ref name="PoD" /> Later, however, he ordered the immediate execution of both [[Cloning|clones]] after [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] tried to intercede. The [[captain]] initially complied with Krell's orders, since as a [[general]], he had the authority to issue the death sentence during wartime. The execution was to be carried out by a firing [[squad]], but the clones refused to comply. Krell was subsequently arrested by his [[troop]]s for tricking the [[501st Legion]] and the [[212th Attack Battalion]] into fighting each other. Krell confessed to collaborating with the [[Umbaran]]s, intending to become an apprentice to [[Count]] [[Dooku]], and was ultimately executed as a traitor to the [[Galactic Republic]] by his most loyal [[trooper]], the clone known as [[Dogma]].<ref name="Carnage of Krell">{{TCW|Carnage of Krell}}</ref>

Later during the war, Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] was expelled from the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Trial of Ahsoka Tano|tried by a Republic court]] on charges of murder and treason. Tano had been framed in the [[bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar]], and the prosecutor, [[Admiral]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] vowed to ensure Tano's conviction and advocated to apply the death penalty for the former Jedi, much to the latter's shock. Ultimately, the real traitor, [[Barriss Offee]], was exposed before the court could render its decision on Tano. Tano was released from Republic [[Prison|custody]], but chose to reject the Jedi Order's offer to rejoin their ranks because, with the exception of [[Anakin Skywalker]], they had not stood by her.<ref name="tcwwrong">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref>

During Anakin Skywalker and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s [[mission to Utapau]], Kenobi was taken [[prisoner]] by General [[Grievous]] aboard [[Grievous' DH-Omni Support Vessel]]. Two [[OOM-series security droid]]s told Kenobi they were taking him from his prison cell to the "execution room," leading the Jedi Master to ask in surprise whether they had an entire room dedicated to conducting executions. Kenobi then informed the droids that the [[Yavin Code]] required that all prisoners must be blindfolded prior to execution, stalling for time as Anakin came to save him.<ref name="the Big Bang" />

The Jedi Master [[Quinlan Vos]] fell to the [[dark side of the Force]] after being sent to [[assassin]]ate [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] leader Count Dooku. Faking reform, he returned to the Jedi Order and committed treasonous acts. As the [[Jedi High Council]] discussed his proposed punishment, Mace Windu floated the possibility of execution, which would have been an unprecedented step in the history of the Jedi Order. The possibility was seriously considered, but ultimately rejected, largely because of the arguments of Obi-Wan Kenobi. However, after a second betrayal, execution was considered all but certain, but ultimately events conspired to allow Vos to reform.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>

===Imperial Era===
{{Quote|You'll hang for this."''<br />''"Seven years serving you? I deserve worse than that.|Jayhold Beehaz and Gorn|<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref>}}
During its [[Imperial Era|reign]], the [[Galactic Empire]] often carried out capital punishment for major and minor offenses, with many convicts being [[Hanging|hanged]].<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] lost his adoptive father, [[Clem Andor]], when he was hanged.<ref name="Reckoning">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref> When [[Commandant]] [[Jayhold Beehaz]] discovered his subordinate, [[Lieutenant]] [[Gorn]], had joined a [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|rebel infiltration team]], he declared that Gorn would be hanged for his treachery, but Gorn remarked that his years of carrying out Beehaz's orders had earned him a worse punishment.<ref name="The Eye" />

In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Leonis Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates {{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}} and ''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]'' to [[4 BBY]]. As this event occurs between {{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}} and ''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]'', it must also occur in 4 BBY.</ref> the [[Imperial cadet]] [[Zare Leonis]] was made to attend a [[Tribunal of Zare Leonis|tribunal]] for suspected treason against the [[Galactic Empire]]. He pleaded guilty to all counts against him and was sentenced to be executed, but escaped before the penalty could be carried out and conducted a [[Rescue of Dhara Leonis|rescue]] of [[Dhara Leonis|his sister]].<ref name="The Secret Academy">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref>

By the [[secret mission to Tatooine]], [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]] had the death sentence on twelve [[star system]]s, and he told this to [[Luke Skywalker]] in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

[[File:Conference_room1.png|thumb|right|300px|Grand Moff Tarkin, after learning that the rebel base on Dantooine was abandoned, planned to have Princess Leia Organa killed.]]
After [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was captured by the Galactic Empire while ferrying stolen [[Death Star plans]] to [[Tatooine]], [[Grand Moff]] Wilhuff Tarkin signed an order for her execution. [[Darth]] Vader, however, kept her alive in order to [[torture]] her for information regarding the stolen plans and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Great Temple|base]]. After she was discovered to have given false information that the [[Dantooine base|Rebel base]] was on [[Dantooine]], Tarkin called for her immediate execution, but, after the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' arrived on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], Vader instead chose to permit her escape because he believed she would lead the Empire to the base.<ref name="ANH" />

The [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] executed those who provoked his prejudice by feeding them to the [[sarlacc]]. During the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Han Solo]], and [[Chewbacca]] were sentenced to that fate, but managed to overpower the guards set to carry out their executions.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

In like manner, Rebel pilots were under orders to kill any Imperials who had served on the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]].<ref name="Shattered Empire 1">[[Shattered Empire 1|''Shattered Empire'' 1]]</ref>

===New Republic Era and after===
After the [[Galactic Civil War]] and the conversion of the Galactic Empire to the [[New Republic]], some Imperial officials were executed for war crimes. Agent [[Gideon]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] escaped that execution.<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> However, after Gideon's arrest on his cruiser by [[Marshal]] Carasynthia Dune, it was said that the [[New Republic Tribunal]] was likely to sentence him to death again for his crimes.<ref name="BobaChapter 5">{{BOBF|Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian}}</ref>

Around [[9 ABY]],<ref>{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> while [[Silvo's pirate raid|plundering]] a [[Unidentified bulk freighter (New Republic Era)|New Republic bulk freighter]], Captain [[Jod Na Nawood|Silvo]] had the [[starship]]'s [[Unidentified merchant captain|captain]] thrown into [[Realspace|space]] after<ref name="SC 101" /> he<ref name="SC 101 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> refused to cooperate with the invaders.<ref name="SC 101" /> Sometime later, Silvo<ref name="SC 106">{{SkeletonCrew|Zero Friends Again}}</ref> himself<ref name="Databank-Captain Silvo">{{Databank|captain-silvo|Captain Silvo}}</ref> was sentenced to death by airlock by his successor<ref name="SC 106" /> [[Brutus]].<ref name="SC 106 AD" /> Brutus, however, altered the verdict following<ref name="SC 106" /> [[Chaelt]]'s<ref name="SC 106 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|Zero Friends Again}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> intervention, whereby<ref name="SC 106" /> she<ref name="SC 106 AD" /> insisted on respecting the [[Pirate's Code]] by allowing the defendant the [[Right of Last Appeal]]. Despite temporarily sparing his life, Brutus warned Silvo that he would be executed in the event that [[At Attin]]—an isolated [[planet]] rumored to provide infinite supplies of Old Republic [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]—yielded no treasure, unlike the latter promised.<ref name="SC 106" />

In [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Timeframe">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/652893780236988420|[[Del Rey]]|quote=So excited to have @claudiagray writing a #StarWars novel with us. SW: New Republic: Bloodline coming 2016. Set 6 years before TFA.|archivefile=File:New Republic Bloodline Twitter Post.jpg}} establishes that the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] take place six [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|VII}}. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], so ''Bloodline'' must take place in [[28 ABY]].</ref> [[Senator]] [[Ransolm Casterfo]] lent his support to a campaign to reinstate the death penalty on his [[homeworld]] of [[Riosa]]. At the [[New Republic Era|time]], more [[Centrists|Centrist]] [[star system]]s were leaning toward it. Ironically, Casterfo ended up facing capital punishment after he was framed for the [[Tai-Lin Garr's assassination|assassination]] of [[Tai-Lin Garr]].<ref name="Bloodline" /> Casterfo's execution, however, was never carried out and he was eventually rescued by the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Resistance Reborn">''[[Resistance Reborn]]''</ref>

[[File:Stormtroopers_on_the_Supremacy.png|thumb|left|300px|Traitors in the First Order were put to death by stormtrooper executioners.]]
Execution was the prescribed punishment for traitors within the ranks of the [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]] of the [[First Order]]. This task was carried out by those within the position of [[First Order stormtrooper executioner|executioner trooper]]. These specialized [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] were rotated via random duty shifts, rather than being a position held by any single group of individuals. As such, any stormtrooper could be assigned this duty, but it was never known who, as assignments were kept anonymous.<ref name="TLJ Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Finn]], a First Order defector once known as FN-2187, dreaded this task and spent his shifts on it fervently hoping the day would end without him having to perform any executions. His own defection later caused him and fellow Resistance operative [[Rose Tico]] to end up facing this fate, but the two were saved when the Resistance [[flagship]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] crashed into the ''[[Supremacy]]'', instantly killing their executioners.<ref name="TLJ Expanded">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
