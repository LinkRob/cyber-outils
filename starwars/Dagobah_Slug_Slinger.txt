{{Quote|Despite its decidedly floral notes, the Dagobah Slug Slinger isn't nearly as swampy as you might expect from a drink that originated on Dagobah. That's because it's got a tart citrus base that provides the ideal balance to the slug's sweet, herbaceous ooze.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
'''Dagobah Slug Slinger''' was a [[cocktail]] with a strong [[alcohol]]ic base. It could be [[Sugar|sweetened]] with either the [[slime]] of its namesake, the [[Dagobah]] [[swamp slug]], or with an imitation [[syrup]], [[Dagobah Slug Syrup]]. It was served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] in [[34 ABY]]. The [[human]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]] drank the beverage there before she attempted to recruit for the [[Resistance]].

The same [[Standard year|year]], [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included recipes for the cocktail and syrup in his [[Book|cookbook]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''. At some point no earlier than [[35 ABY]], the drink was mentioned in a [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]] by [[Ardis San Tekka]], the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]]' [[Director of Creature Cartography]].

==Description==
{{Quote|The strong flavors also do a nice job of smoothin' out the equally strong portion of booze it contains. But don't be fooled&mdash;this one has more kick to it than a master of Teräs Käsi!|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
The Dagobah Slug Slinger was a [[Color|green]]-colored [[cocktail]] named after [[swamp slug]]s,<ref name="Cookbook" /> a [[Homeworld|native]] [[species]] of the [[swamp]] [[planet]] [[Dagobah]].<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> The large [[Slug (creature)|slugs]] secreted [[Sugar|saccharine]] [[slime]] with fragrant notes from the [[Plant|herbs]] they had [[Diet|digested]], and the slime was used to sweeten [[Foodstuff|desserts]] and drinks. The cocktail's combination of sweet and herbaceous slime plus a tart [[Fruit|citrus]] juice base masked its strong [[alcohol]]ic content, which was said to have a stronger kick than a master of the [[Unarmed combat|martial art]] [[Teräs Käsi]]. The drink was garnished with a sprig of an herb.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==History==
{{Quote|Swamp Slug slime is a key ingredient in the Dagobah Slug Slinger&ndash;one of the signature drinks at Oga’s Cantina on Batuu. Ardis sampled the verdant concoction on one of our visits, while I traded stories with DJ Rex. I think I got the better deal&hellip;|B8-T5's annotation, in Ardis San Tekka's journal|<ref name="Bestiary">''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]''</ref>}}
The cocktail was served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events of [[Oga's Cantina]] within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The [[cantina]] sweetened the drink with slime from swamp slugs that lived<ref name="Traveler's Guide to Batuu">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> in tanks along the bar back.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> One night in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="GE:BS date">Events depicted in the novel ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' are set in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Vi Moradi]], a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] and [[Resistance]] [[spy]], visited the cantina with her [[Batuuan]] coworkers after her first [[Standard day|day's]] work for [[Savi & Son Salvage]]. Following her coworker [[Ylena]]'s recommendation, Moradi started the night by imbibing a Dagobah Slug Slinger. After she bought the second round of drinks, she unsuccessfully attempted to recruit her new acquaintances for the Resistance.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />

[[File:Strono_Tuggs_VD.png|thumb|right|220px|Strono Tuggs (pictured) included a recipe for Dagobah Slug Slinger that used imitation syrup in his cookbook.]]
[[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] created an imitation [[syrup]], [[Dagobah Slug Syrup]], to use as a slime substitute when lacking a supply of slugs.<ref name="Cookbook" /> In 34 ABY,<ref name="Cookbook date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs published his [[Book|cookbook]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which included recipes for his imitation syrup and the Dagobah Slug Slinger.<ref name="Cookbook" /> The same year,<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]] included a photo of Dagobah Slug Slinger in Batuu guidebook, ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', listing it as one of Oga's Cantina's offerings.<ref name="Traveler's Guide to Batuu" />

At some point no earlier than 35 ABY,<ref name="BestiaryDate">{{BestiaryVol1Date}}</ref> [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]]' [[Director of Creature Cartography]], [[Ardis San Tekka]], and her [[safari droid]] [[B8-T5]] visited Batuu while studying [[Sentience|non-sentient]] creatures throughout [[the galaxy]]. While in Oga's Cantina, San Tekka tasted a Dagobah Slug Slinger while her [[droid]] conversed with the cantia's [[DJ (occupation)|DJ]], [[R-3X]]. Later, B8-T5 mentioned his owner consumption of a Dagobah Slug Slinger in an annotation in her [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]], within a section about swamp slugs. In the same publication, San Tekka noted that the decadence of the swamp slugs' slime had led to smaller specimens of the species being exported from Dagobah and kept in captivity for chefs to harvest from them for dishes and drinks.<ref name="Bestiary" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Ogas-Dagobah_Slug_Slinger.jpg|thumb|right|200px|The drink at a real-world Oga's Cantina]]
Dagobah Slug Slinger is one of the alcoholic beverages sold to customers in the [[In-universe|real-world]] Oga's Cantina bar in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. It was officially announced to the public on [[February 27]], [[2019]] in an article on the website [[Wikipedia:D23 (Disney)|D23: The Official Disney Fan Club]],<ref name="D23 Everything You Need to Know About SWGE">{{D23|url=galaxys-edge-announcements|text=Everything You Need to Know About ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> and debuted for guests to purchase when the land opened in [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in 2019.<ref name="DPB Foodie Guide">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The menu's description reads, "[[Wikipedia:Tequila Herradura|Herradura]] Reposado [[Wikipedia:Tequila|Tequila]], [[Wikipedia:Bols (brand)|Bols]] [[Wikipedia:Curaçao (liqueur)|Blue Curaçao]], [[Wikipedia:Citrus#Culinary|Citrus Juices]], [[Wikipedia:Ginger|Ginger]], Herbs, and [[Wikipedia:Bitters|Bitters]]."<ref name="DL Oga's Cantina menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/dinner|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Dinner Menu|oldversion=20210423170549}}</ref> On [[November 18]], [[2024]], the drink was removed from the menu at Disney's Hollywood Studios,<ref name="BlogMickey">{{WebCite|url=https://blogmickey.com/ogas-cantina-review-2024-new-menu-drinks-food|text=Oga’s Cantina Review: November 2024 Menu Refresh Brings New Drinks & Food|date=2024-11-19|work=[https://blogmickey.com/ BlogMickey]|archivedate=20241209190557}}</ref> while the drink was retired from the Disneyland version's menu sometime after [[April 23]], 2021.<ref name="DL Oga's Cantina menu" />

An out-of-universe recipe for the cocktail is included in the 2019 recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. It is made with the ingredients Dagobah Slug Syrup, [[Wikipedia:Lime (fruit)|lime juice]], [[Wikipedia:Orange juice|orange juice]], blue curaçao, tequila, and water. They are combined and shaken, then poured over ice and garnished with a sprig of [[Wikipedia:Rosemary|rosemary]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

The [[2020]] [[Canon|non-canon]] video game expansion ''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]'' includes Dagobah Slug Slingers as a drink sold to the player's [[Wikipedia:The Sims|Sims]] at the Oga's Cantina vendor in the Black Spire Outpost neighborhood. Upon consuming the drink for the first time, the Sim automatically learns the recipe and can prepare it at mixology bars in their home neighborhood.<ref name="Sims">''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]''</ref>
