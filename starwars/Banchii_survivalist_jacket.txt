A '''Banchii survivalist jacket''' was a type of resistant and insulated [[jacket]] that was gray in [[color]] and was made on<ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Banchii]]. A bronze colored circle with a semicircular symbol and a rectangle at the bottom was attached to the upper-right chest of the jacket. The jacket also included a pair of white [[Color|gloves]], a brown [[belt]], and a turquoise [[Clothing|shirt]] that was worn underneath it.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a Banchii survivalist jacket was for sale in [[Wesla's]], a shop on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet [[Kijimi]] that was operated by the [[Twi'lek]] [[tailor]] [[Lani Wesla]]. A [[Banchii survivalist belt]] and [[Banchii survivalist pants]] were also stocked by Wesla.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[human]] outlaw [[Kay Vess]] purchased a Banchii survivalist jacket from Wesla for 1,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A Banchii survivalist jacket appeared in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', a [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. When equipped by the player, the jacket reduces damage taken from [[blaster]] and projectile [[weapon]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />
