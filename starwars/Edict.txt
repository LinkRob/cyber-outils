The '''''Edict''''' was an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] that operated during the [[Imperial Era]]. In the closing stages of the [[Galactic Civil War]], it fell under the control of the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]].

==History==
The ''Edict'' was an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] of the [[Imperial Navy]]. By [[4 ABY]], it was considered a [[Starship|vessel]] past its prime, assigned to training missions and war games. Its equipment was stripped for other purposes so that eventually only the core components remained. Its [[starfighter]] complement was also reduced to [[TIE series|TIE]] [[TIE training drone|drone fighters]]. Consequently, it was seen as a relatively low-importance asset in the [[Standard month|months]] immediately following the apparent [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] at the [[Battle of Endor]], escaping engagement with [[New Republic]] forces.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

At some point, the ''Edict''{{'s}} [[hyperdrive]] malfunctioned, and its crew was unable to repair it without replacement parts—including a replacement generator, reactant agitator injector, [[coaxium]] suspension element, and twenty-eight Imperial-standard sealing bolts—that were impossible to obtain due to the Empire's fracturing. For several [[Standard week|weeks]], the crippled Star Destroyer floated through the [[Pormthulis system]]; the [[New Republic]] would capture the vessel, but they too did not effect repairs, instead allowing it to continue drifting through the system under [[starfighter]] escort.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

Sometime after the [[Battle of Pandem Nai|Attack on Pandem Nai]], the ''Edict'' was found by the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], which drove off the Republic escorts, recaptured the vessel, and freed its Imperial skeleton crew. The [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|cruiser-carrier]] ''[[Allegiance]]'' was sacrificed for parts so that the Star Destroyer would live on, serving under the 204th. [[Soran Keize]] suggested a celebration to welcome the ''Edict'' and its crew as the 204th's newest members; however, celebrations were disrupted by [[pilot]] [[Nord Kandende]], who announced the news that [[Andara]] had fallen to the [[New Republic]] and questioned why they were celebrating instead of fighting. Following the sacrifice of the ''Allegiance'', its [[commanding officer]], [[Captain]] [[Rogart Styll]], was given command of the ''Edict''.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
