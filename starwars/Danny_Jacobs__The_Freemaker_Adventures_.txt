'''Daniel Charles''' "'''Danny'''" '''Jacobs, Jr.''' provided the voice of [[Raam]] in ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]''.<ref name="SW Show">{{StarWarsShow|ewg2ZpaLsL0|We Unearth Super Rare Star Wars Toys, Talk with John DiMaggio and Danny Jacobs, and More!}}</ref> His other projects have included most of the [[Wikipedia:Madagascar (franchise)|''Madagascar'']] franchise (in which he is best known as the voice of King Julien), [[Wikipedia:Phineas and Ferb|''Phineas and Ferb'']], and [[Wikipedia:Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2012 TV series)|''Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles'']].<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.stuff.co.nz/entertainment/tv-radio/74727042/madagascars-king-julien-finds-a-new-voice|text=Madagascar's King Julien finds a new voice|author=Hinds, Julie|date=2015-12-08|work=[[Wikipedia:Stuff (website)|Stuff]]|archivedate=20181121203722}}</ref>

Jacobs and his co-star [[John DiMaggio]], who voiced [[Baash]], also appeared as guests on ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'' on [[September 20]], [[2017]].<ref name="SW Show" />
