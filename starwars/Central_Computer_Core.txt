The '''Central Computer Core''' was a [[computer]] in [[Killun Station]], an [[Imperial Security Bureau]] base located on [[Killun 71]]. It contained clearance codes for the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] that were eventually stolen by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]].<ref name="Double Agent Droid" />
