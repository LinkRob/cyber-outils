'''''Dark Lord''''' is an original German-language audio drama adaptation of ''[[Dark Lord: The Rise of Darth Vader]]''. It is a full-cast scripted audio play based on the German version of the novel, similar to the [[Star Wars (radio)|English-language radio drama versions of the original trilogy]]. 

The audio drama was released by [[WortArt]] in [[2008]] as part of a two-set series, along with ''[[Labyrinth of Evil (German audio drama)|Labyrinth of Evil]]'', adapting [[James Luceno]]'s novels that follow [[Anakin Skywalker]] and Darth Vader immediately before and after the events of {{Film|III}}.
