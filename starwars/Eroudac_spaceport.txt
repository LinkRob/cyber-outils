A '''spaceport''' was located on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Eroudac]]. At some point after leaving the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]]<ref name="ACOF" /> in [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]]. The novel establishes that [[Izal Garsea]] left the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] when she was five [[Standard year|years]] old, and as the novel takes place on the [[Standard day|day]] before and on Izal Garsea's eighteenth [[birthday]], she must have left the planet in [[21 ABY]].</ref> the [[Garsea family]] moved to Eroudac. While on Eroudac, [[Izal Garsea]] spent most of her time at the world's [[spaceport]], burning the [[fuel]] of her family's [[starship]]&mdash;the [[Meridian (light freighter)|''Meridian'']]&mdash;on "solo runs to nowhere" and eventually becoming friends with the port's guards. Following her return to Batuu<ref name="ACOF" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Garsea told her [[Youngling|childhood]] friend, the [[farmer]] [[Julen Rakab]], about the Eroudac spaceport.<ref name="ACOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
The spaceport was mentioned in the [[2019]] young-adult novel ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]].<ref name="ACOF" />
