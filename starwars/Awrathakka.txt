'''Awrathakka''' was a [[Wookiee]] [[city]] built around a giant [[Wroshyr tree]] and located on the Wookiee [[homeworld]] of [[Kashyyyk]]. During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces [[Slavery|enslaved]] the city's inhabitants and relocated them to [[Camp Sardo]].<ref name="Life Debt" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> a team of rebels led by [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] infiltrated Awrathakka and used it as a vantage point to launch their assault on Camp Sardo; which led to the [[liberation of Kashyyyk]] and the enslaved Wookiees from Imperial rule.<ref name="Life Debt" />
