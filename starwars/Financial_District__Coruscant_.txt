The '''Financial District''', also known as the '''financial zone''' was a location that spanned 30 square [[kilometer]]s in the [[Imperial City|Galactic City]] of [[Coruscant]]. It was adjacent to the [[Federal District]] and [[The Works]] and housed many [[bank]]s and financial institutions as well as the [[Greth Lan-Dwu Corp. electrical power-generating plant]].<ref name="SWCL" />
