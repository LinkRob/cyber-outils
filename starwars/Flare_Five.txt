'''Flare Five''' was the [[callsign]] assigned to the fifth [[pilot]] of the [[New Republic]]'s [[Flare Squadron]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] was designated this callsign during a [[Attack on Chadawa|battle]] with the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] over the [[planet]] [[Chadawa]], but was killed when their [[X-wing starfighter]] was destroyed.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
