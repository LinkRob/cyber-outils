'''Aurek-14''' was an excavation site on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]]. Located on the tropical world's southern hemisphere, this site was operated by the [[Galactic Empire]] during its occupancy of Scarif for the development of top-secret [[Imperial Military]] projects including construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="UVG" />
