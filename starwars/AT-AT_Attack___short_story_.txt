"'''AT-AT Attack!'''" is a short story published in ''[[The Original Trilogy Stories]]''. It was written by [[Andy Schmidt]].<ref name="Original" /> It adapts [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back]] from the arrival of the [[Imperial Navy]] over [[Hoth]] to [[Luke Skywalker]] flying to [[Dagobah]].
