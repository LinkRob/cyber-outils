'''''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 1''' is the first issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege]]''. The issue was written by [[Alex Segura]], illustrated by [[Stefano Raffaele]], [[Jethro Morales]], [[Jim Campbell]], and [[Alex Sinclair]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 20]], 2024. The issue also contains the story "[[Consolidation]]."

==Publisher's summary==
''STILL REELING FROM THE EVENTS OF INSURGENCY RISES, LUKE MUST SEEK OUT A RELIC THAT COULD HIDE CLUES TO THE FUTURE OF THE JEDI ORDER! LUKE SKYWALKER and a new friend find themselves cornered! Can two Imperial powerhouses find common ground against the nascent New Republic? Where are Han and Chewbacca? The heroes find themselves saved in the nick of time &mdash; by a fan-favorite guest star and ally!''

==Plot summary==
On the [[swamp]] [[planet]] [[Lehanis]], located in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[New Republic]] [[pilot]] [[Rynn Zenat]] emerge from a quest to find [[Ky Narec|Ky Narec's]] [[Ky Narec's Compendium|Compendium]], a [[Jedi artifact]] Luke realized was being sought after by the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[religion|cult]] the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] and their collaborator, the rogue [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]. Luke and Rynn find themselves surrounded by Acolytes, and they prepare for the imminent fight. As Acolytes lunge at the pair with their [[electrostaff]]s, Skywalker and Zenat fight back. Eventually, Luke gains enough purchase to step away from the melee. He approaches the Acolyte [[Arch-Mage]] [[Yuan Howzell]], who gloats about how the Acolytes use time to their advantage. While Luke has been preoccupied securing the Compendium, Howzell claims they were playing a different game in which they have won in his distraction. 

On the New Republic's provisional [[capital]], [[Chandrila]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] contemplates her shifting perspectives on the [[Galactic Civil War|war]] and her hopes for its ending. She realizes that the finality she sought is unrealistic, that nothing is ever easy. Leia's musings are interrupted by an incoming [[hologram]] from the [[Representative]] of [[Kashyyyk]], [[Kerrithrarr]]. The [[Wookiee]] delivers her information, albeit incomplete, of recent whereabouts of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. Leia promises to provide as much support as possible to the Wookiees. 

After ending the call, [[Mon Mothma]] joins Leia, reminding her that resources are scarce, meaning Kashyyyk will not necessarily be able to get the support Leia just promised. They discuss Luke's current mission before shifting their conversation to the topic of Adelhard. He has become a large threat. In the face of such danger, the women discuss their often conflicting goals of securing [[the galaxy]] as a fledgling government while staying true to their ideals.

In the [[Anoat sector]], Grand Moff Ubrik Adelhard oversees a [[navy|naval]] victory over New Republic forces, proud of his own might in what he hopes will be the revival of the Empire. He is soon approached by [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Kith Alaytia]] and [[Commander]] [[Bragh]], bringing with them a New Republic [[prisoner]] by the name of [[Pessin]]. He demanded an audience, surrendering to try and preserve his life. As leverage, he starts to reveal that Adelhard has a New Republic [[spy]] in his midst. Alaytia, secretly the spy Pessin is about to out, bludgeons the pilot with her [[blaster pistol]], knocking him unconscious. She justifies her actions as taking offense to the pilot thinking they are gullible enough to believe such a lie. 

Adelhard's contact in the Acolytes of the Beyond, an [[Anzati]] mystic named [[Reyna Oskure]], is suspicious of the uncharacteristic fervor of Alaytia's actions, but simply compliments the burning emotion and brutality. Adelhard inquires as to Oskure's reasons for coming to his [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]]. She answers with an unwelcome kiss, which Adelhard quickly pulls away from. Oskure asks Adelhard why he did so, wonders if he fears the temptation of what he does not understand. Adelhard claims he rejected her because of it being the wrong context. He recently resolved to embrace the dark side of [[the Force]] as a tool, but tells Oskure that he is still figuring out how best to use it.

In the [[Realspace|space]] above [[Malastare]], a holding station for the [[Kenner Loring's faction|Imperial forces]] of [[Grand General]] [[Kenner Loring]], New Republic [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|Y-wing]] pilot [[Brink]] finds himself cornered by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. Despite his [[squadron|squardon's]] best attempts, he [[death|dies]], and his squad mates attempt to flee. They are unsuccessful. The Grand General receives a transmission from Moff Adelhard. Loring informs Adelhard of the laughable [[Akiva Summit]] in which factions of the [[Imperial Remnant]] attempted to unite under [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]]. Adelhard works to secure an alliance with Loring. The Grand General is open to the idea, especially in light of rumors of an [[Battle of Jakku|upcoming final stand]] against the Republic, but is wary of Adelhard's association with Reyna Oskure. After ending the transmission, he pointedly calls out a lurking [[officer]], [[Baumin Deel]]. The New Republic spy flees down a hallway as [[stormtrooper]]s give chase. He makes it to a comms relay and gets just enough of a transmission out to inform [[Agent]] [[Calder Behrens]] of Loring and Adelhard combining forces before he is slain by one of the stormtroopers. 

Back on Lehanis, Luke tells Zenat of the trap they are in and to start retreating to their [[starship]]s. The Acolytes, however, are maintaining the fight. Zenat's quips catch the attention of Howzell, who manages to fling her satchel away from her, the Compendium inside. Before she can truly harm Rynn, Luke grazes Howzell in the back with [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. He orders her to stand down, but Howzell lurches at him with a jumping attack, knocking Luke to the ground as the mage continues her cryptic threats of time running out. 

Luke shuts Howzell up with a swing of his [[lightsaber]], taking the advantage. He interrogates her at lightsaber-point. Rynn joins in to bolster threats of violence if Howzell does not answer. Before they can get any information, though, Zenat sees someone swipe her satchel and run towards [[Red Five (T-65B X-wing starfighter)|Luke's X-wing]]. Skywalker realizes he knows the [[thief]]. It is the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Dr. Aphra]].
