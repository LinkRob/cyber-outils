The '''Ferrix Bell Tower''' was a bell [[tower]] located on the one end of [[Rix Road]] in the [[Ferrix]] [[Gavalox Communications|business center]], opposite to the [[Fountain Square]]. On its top there was a large [[beskar]] anvil used by the [[Time Grappler]] to sign the start and end of each day, as well as important events such as upstanding citizens' public [[funeral]]s.<ref name="Episode 12" />
