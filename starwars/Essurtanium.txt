'''Essurtanium''' was a substance with rare properties, potentially including [[The Force|Force]]-reactivity, a vein of which was believed to have been found by [[miner]]s on the [[planet]] [[Elphrona]] in [[232 BBY]]. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Indeera Stokes]], who was stationed at the planet's [[Elphrona Outpost|Jedi outpost]], planned to fly to the discovery site and obtain a sample of the substance; however, the plan had to be scrapped due to news of an ongoing [[Nihil]] [[Raid on Elphrona|raid]] on a local [[Blythe homestead|homestead]].

==Description==
Essurtanium was a substance that purportedly exhibited particularly rare properties, potentially including a measure of reactivity to [[the Force]].<ref name="LOTJ" />

==History==
[[File:IndeeraStokes-Vector.png|thumb|right|300px|Indeera Stokes was interested in studying a sample of essurtanium.]]
At one point in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="LOTJdate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> a group of [[miner]]s in the southern hemisphere of [[Elphrona]], an [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] the surface of which was dominated by [[mountain]] ranges of [[iron]] and other ferrous [[mineral]]s, believed they had discovered a vein of essurtanium. Information about the miners' claim reached<ref name="LOTJ" /> [[Jedi Knight]]<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Indeera Stokes]], who was stationed at the planet's [[Elphrona Outpost|Jedi outpost]].<ref name="LOTJ" />

Wishing to acquire a sample of essurtanium&mdash;which Stokes herself had never seen&mdash;to bring back and study, the Jedi Knight informed the other [[Jedi]] present at the [[Jedi outpost|outpost]] of her intent to take one of their [[Jedi Vector|Vector]] [[starfighter]]s to travel to the discovery site. Shortly thereafter, however, that plan was canceled when the Jedi at the outpost received information about an ongoing [[Raid on Elphrona|raid]] by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] on the Elphrona [[Blythe homestead|homestead]] of the [[Blythe family]].<ref name="LOTJ" /> [[229 BBY|Three years later]],<ref name="ChroniclesDate">{{ChroniclesoftheJediDate}}</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Harli Cogra]] [[Author|wrote]] a [[book]] titled ''[[Chronicles of the Jedi]]'', which detailed the Jedi of the [[High Republic Era]]. In the book, Cogra noted Stokes' interest in studying the properties of essurtanium.<ref name="Chronicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
