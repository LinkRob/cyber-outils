'''Cortosis''' was a [[metal]] that could be found on the [[planet]]s [[Dinzo]], [[Mokivj]], and [[Bal'demnic]], where the [[Sith]] known as "[[the Stranger]]" resided.

==Description==
{{Quote|It's also soft and frangible, useless for building into armor or other protective materials. ''[&hellip;]'' It appears they've found a method for weaving the cortosis into a network within a protective matrix. An energy impact is therefore dissipated across the entire network and throughout the entire droid armor shell.|Thrawn, on the normal reality of cortosis versus that seen in an armor|<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />}}
[[File:Cortosis_mine-TWC.png|thumb|left|250px|A cortosis mine on the planet Dinzo.]]
Cortosis was an extremely rare [[metal]] that had a very high energy absorption rate and transmission coefficients, which allowed even the energy of a [[blaster]] bolt to be dissipated upon contact. One of the strongest minerals in [[the galaxy]], cortosis could even withstand the blow of a [[lightsaber]] and briefly short out its blade. It could also redirect the heat of explosions, as discovered when [[Jedi General|General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] attempted to collapse the entrance of a [[cortosis mine]], and the resulting [[Mission to Mokivj|explosion]] breached through [[Mokivj]]'s crust.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> Apart from its physical properties, cortosis could also affect a [[Force-sensitive]]'s ability to use [[the Force]], as helmets made from the material isolated the wearer's thoughts and prevented others from sensing them.<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" />

Despite these advantages, cortosis was very fragile in its natural state,<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> being porous and brittle,<ref name="Official Magazine 118">{{GermanFanCite|118|Database}}</ref> making it normally useless as armor. However, it was possible to, with effort, turn cortosis into an armor. When faced with an example of cortosis armor, the [[Chiss]] tactician [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] ("Thrawn") theorized that the cortosis was woven into the armor network as part of a defense matrix, thereby ensuring any incoming energy weapon&mdash;such as a blaster shot or the swing of a lightsaber&mdash;was dissipated throughout the whole of the armor network. Nonetheless, cortosis armor had difficulties with prolonged energy attacks, such as those of [[lightning gun]]s, which would overload the material,<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> as well as brute force strikes that could shatter it.<ref name="Night">{{Acolyte|Night}}</ref>

Many of the best suits of [[Mandalorian armor]] utilized [[cortosis weave]],<ref name="No Disintegrations">[[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']]</ref> an energy-resistant mesh composed of cortosis [[ore]]. Cortosis could also be forged into a [[cortosis staff]] or [[cortosis sword]], melee weapons that retained the defensive properties of cortosis, while cortosis weave was additionally used in the creation of the [[cortosis shield]].<ref name="GaG">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref> Cortosis was stronger than similar materials like [[phrik]].<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />

==History==
===Old Republic Era===
[[File:Cortosis_Bar.png|thumb|right|150px|A bar of cortosis]]
During the [[Era of the Sith]], in the time of the Old Republic, the [[Ascendant]] created the [[Null Blade]] with a cortosis alloy as part of their battle with the [[Sith]] Order. The Null Blade could briefly short out lightsabers. However, the blade was not reinforced and thus could still be cut by a lightsaber. The blade was never completed before the Ascendant were [[Slaughter of the Ascendant|slaughtered]] by the Sith.<ref name="Aphra 25">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 25|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 25]]</ref> The usefulness of cortosis as a defensive metal would spread as rumors and stories that reached the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

===High Republic Era===
{{Quote|Cortosis. Handy against lightsabers, but also a sensory deprivation headpiece. Like we used as younglings.|The Stranger, on his helmet, to Osha Aniseya|<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" />}}
[[File:CortosisEffect-Night.png|thumb|left|350px|Cortosis was used as part of the Stranger's armor in a lightsaber battle against Jedi.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Sith]] known as "[[the Stranger]]"<ref name="Night" /> wore a [[Cortosis gauntlet|gauntlet]] and [[The Stranger's helmet|helmet]] forged from cortosis.<ref name="Qimir DB">{{Databank|qimir|The Stranger (alias "Qimir")}}</ref> The mask enabled him to keep his identity hidden from others, including his apprentice, [[Mae-ho Aniseya]]. In a [[Mission to Khofar|lightsaber duel]] on the planet [[Khofar]], the Stranger incorporated his cortosis gear into his unconventional combat style and used it to great effect against several [[Jedi]], who found their lightsabers briefly shortcircuited upon contact with their enemy's gear. The Stranger's helmet also protected against [[telepathy]], stating that removing it would allow [[Jedi Master]] [[Sol]] to read his thoughts. Despite its effectiveness in disrupting lightsabers, the Stranger's cortosis armor was vulnerable to more direct physical blows, as [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]] was able to smash his helmet with several blows from the hilt of her lightsaber while [[Yord Fandar]] successfully destroyed his cortosis gauntlet with a similar attack. While the Stranger would briefly lose his helmet in the struggle, revealing his public identity of Qimir to his foes, he reclaimed the mask before making his way to Mae's unconscious sister, [[Verosha Aniseya]].<ref name="Night" />

===Clone Wars===
{{Quote|My skin graft is a cortosis mesh&ndash;a holdover from the days when I advised manufacturers in the field late in the Clone Wars. I can assure you, young lady&ndash;every bolt you fire against me will carry directly into your friend.|Count Denetrius Vidian reveals his skin graft is cortosis mesh|<ref name="AND" />}}
During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Grysk]]s, who used cortosis for energy management, told [[Count]] [[Dooku]] of an effective way to use cortosis against the [[Galactic Republic]]. By specially weaving cortosis fibers within a protective matrix, it was possible to create armor that was both durable and immune to blaster fire. The cortosis [[armor]] also redirected the energy of a lightsaber back into itself, causing the lightsaber to temporarily shut down upon contact. [[Duke]] [[Solha]] was placed in charge of creating a [[C-B3 cortosis battle droid|series]] of [[B2-series super battle droid]]s equipped with the armor and, under the supervision of [[Darth Sidious]], [[clone trooper armor]] that could be used for [[Order 66]]. This was discovered by [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], Anakin Skywalker, and the Chiss Mitth'raw'nuruodo, known as "Thrawn", who proceeded to destroy the [[Cloning|clone]] trooper armor and sabotage the [[battle droid]]s so they would overheat [[Standard minute|minutes]] after activating. However, desperate to ensure the CIS could not use its mines, Skywalker rigged them to explode with Mokivj's lava flows. Instead, the cortosis in the ground redirected the heat across the world, devastating it.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

Also during the Clone Wars, the [[Cybernetics|cyborg]] business analyst Count [[Denetrius Vidian]] had a cortosis mesh skin graft installed on his body. [[Standard year|Years]] later in the [[Imperial Era]], when Vidian was attacked by the [[Jedi]]-in-hiding [[Kanan Jarrus]] and the [[Twi'lek]] [[pilot]] [[Hera Syndulla]], the Count was able to utilize the graft to conduct energy from a blaster bolt fired by Syndulla into Jarrus, who Vidian was holding at the time.<ref name="AND" />

===Imperial Era===
{{Quote|But look at your lightsaber.''"<br />"''<nowiki/>''Whoa''. That sword ''neutralized'' it?''"<br />"''That's the power of ''cortosis alloy'' for you.|Miril and Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 25" />}}
At some point, the Jedi Master [[Lodaka]] created a [[Master Lodaka's Lightsaber|lightsaber]] immune to the effects of cortosis. It was stored inside the [[Lothal Jedi Temple]] after Lodaka's [[death]], where it remained during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="NoP">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref> Cortosis could be found on the planet [[Dinzo]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]], where the [[Galactic Empire]] established a secret [[Cortosis mine (Dinzo)|mining operation]]. During the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early days]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="TWC" /> around [[3 BBY]],<ref name="3 BBY Date">{{Twitter|Jeremy_Barlow|status/838612089543376901|[[Jeremy Barlow]]|nolive=1|nobackup=1}} places "[[The Wrong Crowd]]" shortly after the events of "[[Homecoming]]," which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[3 BBY]].</ref> a [[Tonk's crew|rebel crew]] under a man named [[Tonk]] led a mission to destroy the mine, defying rebel leader [[Hera Syndulla]]'s advice to use the raw cortosis against the Empire as Tonk believed the Empire would simply dispatch its [[Imperial Navy|naval might]] to reclaim the mine.<ref name="TWC" />

[[File:SparkEternalVsVader-HiddenEmpire5.png|thumb|right|300px|The Spark Eternal-possessed Aphra briefly battles Vader with the Null Blade.]]
The [[Tagge Corporation]] used cortosis to produce the [[Tagge protoblade]], a [[Plasma cutter|plasma blade]] weapon intended to replicate a lightsaber,<ref name="WotBH Boushh">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]]</ref> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="WotBH Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> The protoblade took the form of a [[gauntlet]] from which a blade extended and was energized with plasma. The gauntlet had a cortosis core to protect the weapon's wielder, but the core could also cause the blade to lose power and cease to function as a plasma weapon. Nevertheless, the unpowered Tagge protoblade remained lethal as an edged melee weapon.<ref name="WotBH Boushh" /> At that time, the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime syndicate was interested in using the metal cortosis.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> Around the same time, the Ascendant's [[Spark Eternal]] [[artificial intelligence]] was unleashed back into the galaxy, during which time it possessed the body of [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]]. Trapped within her memories and the memories of [[Miril]] by the Spark Eternal, Aphra was shown the Null Blade by Miril in her memories. When she used a [[curved-hilt lightsaber]] to strike the blade, she discovered it could neutralize a lightsaber, though only temporarily, because of the cortosis.<ref name="Aphra 25" /> Later, the Spark Eternal used the Null Blade&mdash;which by that time was able to stand up to lightsaber strikes&mdash;against [[Darth]] Vader in the [[Battle at Amaxine Station]] and succeeded in shorting out his lightsaber. Vader, however, responded with a [[Force push]] that removed the Spark from Aphra's body.<ref name="Hidden Empire 5">[[Hidden Empire 5|''Hidden Empire'' 5]]</ref>
