During the [[Clone Wars]], a '''secret hangar'''<ref name="Sacrifice EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sacrifice-episode-gallery|text="Sacrifice" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> was located in the [[Inquisitorius Headquarters|Coruscant tower]] and was used by [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]], known publicly as [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] Sheev Palpatine and [[Count]] Dooku, respectively.<ref name="Sacrifice">{{TCW|Sacrifice (episode)}}</ref> Shortly after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], Dooku met Sidious in the [[hangar]],<ref name="AOTC" /> and in the closing stages of the war the hangar was used again when Dooku was summoned by Sidious after sensing that [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] was on [[Moraband]], the ancient [[homeworld]] of the [[Sith]].<ref name="Sacrifice" />
