The '''Creveld-4 radial ion drive''' was a model of [[ion engine]] of [[Nubia Star Drives, Incorporated|Nubian]] make. It was equipped on the [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnoughts]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], most notably the ''[[Invisible Hand]]''.<ref name="VE" />
