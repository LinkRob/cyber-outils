'''Frostfire crystal shards''' were a type of charm consisting of a small shard of the [[crystal]] [[nunsteyn]] imbued with the essence of [[frostfire]] from the [[Water|icy]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]]. The shard was hung from a string and believed to grant the wearer resilience.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[smuggler]] [[Neva Yaranu]] had a frostfire crystal shard available to purchase for one thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] in [[Kijimi City]] on Kijimi, although they would discount or increase the price by ten percent for those in good or poor standing with the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate respectively.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
A frostfire crystal shard appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The item is used as a minor charm in the game and causes the player to take reduced damage from explosions when equipped.<ref name="Outlaws" />
