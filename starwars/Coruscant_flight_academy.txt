A '''flight academy''' was located on the [[Galactic Republic]] capital world of [[Coruscant]] early during the [[Clone Wars]]. After the outbreak of the war at the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], [[Davijaan]], due to his status as a [[Clone Commander]], was given additional training in the flight academy on Coruscant, despite his [[Kaminoan]] training under their [[clone trooper pilot]] program. There, Davijaan learned tricks and tactics, and passed them on to the [[pilot]]s under his command in [[Clone Flight Squad Seven]]. This significantly improved their effectiveness.<ref name="HC 45" />
