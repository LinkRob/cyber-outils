The '''Bronzium Racer''' was a costume owned by [[Dizzy (Ugnaught)|Dizzy]], the [[Ugnaught]] member of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]], that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Wild Racer|standard costume]]. It featured an [[Color|orange]], red, brown, and black color scheme, and included dark purple and [[bronze]]-colored [[goggles]].

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bronzium Racer was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Bronzium Paintwork]].<ref name="Hunters" />
