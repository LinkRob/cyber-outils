'''Enyak bobs''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] made from [[Enyak/Canon|enyaks]]. They were edible to [[human]]s and [[Latero]].<ref name="Survivor" />

{{GameMechanics}}
During a conversation with the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]]<ref name="Survivor" /> in [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> the Latero [[Greez Dritus]] listed enyak bobs as one of several foods that he would cook to celebrate once they reached the planet [[Tanalorr]]. Dritus suggested he would get enyak bobs, [[Mygeeto burger]]s, and [[icefish]] as long as their human companion [[Bode Akuna]] did not have any allergies.<ref name="Survivor" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], enyak bobs were mentioned in an optional conversation in the [[2023]] [[Respawn Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Survivor" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the dish [[seasoned enyak bobs over teerlop-zikody]] was mentioned in "[[The Farrimmer Cafe]]," an article written by [[Jean Rabe]] and [[J. Allan Fawcett]] and published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 11|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 11]] in [[November]] [[1996]].<ref name="Farrimer">{{JournalCite|11|The Farrimmer Cafe}}</ref>
