The '''Bento Experience''' was a the second [[Foodstuff|course]] of dinner at [[An Evening with Gaya]] in the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]] on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''. The course consisted of three spiral [[dumpling]]s and four [[sauce]]s, as well as a [[Flora Noodle Salad]]. Among the three [[color]]ful dumplings, one contained sweet-and-sour [[tip-yip]], one had [[bantha]] beef, and the third was [[vegetable]]-filled. The dumplings were paired with four interplanetary dipping sauces: [[Kaadu Cream]], [[Melahnese curry]], [[Batuu Spice]], and a [[Barbecue-style]] sauce.<ref name="Cruiser" /> It was served in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> and was safe for [[human]]s to consume.<ref name="Cruiser" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bento Experience was available in the [[In-universe|real world]] at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Cruiser" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> It was last served at the hotel during the [[September 28]][[September 29|&ndash;]][[September 30|30]] voyage of [[2023]], the final voyage before the closure of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser.<ref name="Official website">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/overview|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Overview}}</ref>
