'''Chips''' where a type of [[Foodstuff|snack]] that came in bags. In the [[New Republic Era]], [[Karr Nuq Sin]] bought a bag of chips in [[Kijimi City]] for a couple of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. He thought they tasted mostly like [[salt]] and [[silicon]], but that they were better than nothing.<ref name="Force Collector">''[[Force Collector]]''</ref>
