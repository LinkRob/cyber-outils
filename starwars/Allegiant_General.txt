'''Allegiant General''' was a [[Military rank|rank]] in the [[First Order military]] that held power over both [[First Order Army|ground]] and [[First Order Navy|space-based]] forces, making it an equivalent to that of a [[Grand Marshal]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[Historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] further theorized that the rank had something to do with instilling loyalty within those under the holder's command and maintaining the [[Neo-Imperialism|Neo-Imperial]] ideology of the First Order.<ref name="Rise Fall" />

The rank was held by [[Admiral]] [[Enric Pryde]]<ref name="FO">{{EncyclopediaCite|The First Order}}</ref> during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. General [[Armitage Hux]] believed that [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] had promoted Pryde to allegiant general as a way to mock him, and also believed that all [[First Order officer|officers]] and [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] present on the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] knew it as well.<ref name="TROS Junior Novel">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> As a result of the alliance between the First Order and the [[Sith Eternal]], Allegiant [[General]] Pryde ascended to command the [[Sith Eternal military|armies]] of [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="DarthSidiousBioGal28" />
