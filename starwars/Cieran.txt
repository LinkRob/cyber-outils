'''Cieran''' manufactured [[Nivex-class starfighter|''Nivex''-class starfighters]]. During the [[Republic Era]], the [[Gemstone|jewel]] [[thief]] [[Pax Maripher]] owned one of the [[starfighter]]s, naming it the [[Facet (starfighter)|''Facet'']]. Over time, he modified it extensively for personal use.<ref name="MA">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' to [[40 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] inspected the ''Facet'', recognizing it as Cieran made but also acknowledging the [[Starship|vessel]] been modified.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cieran was mentioned in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
