The '''Aparian Wastes''' was a rocky area on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Serenno]]. Just before the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Spike (Serenno)|Spike]], a vast underground [[prison]], was constructed there by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] to hold highly valuable [[prisoner]]s that [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] wanted to interrogate himself.<ref name="COTR" /> Another [[Dooku's prison|prison]] with a similar purpose was located below [[Castle Serenno]] on the same planet.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The region was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 70|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 70]] magazine published by [[De Agostini]].<ref name="BMF 70">{{BuildFalconCite|70|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> However, the region was first explored in the [[2019]] sourcebook, ''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''.<ref name="COTR" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the location's first appearance was in the [[Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets 3|eighth]] issue of the ''[[Star Wars: Agent of the Empire]]'' comic series published by [[Dark Horse Comics]].<ref name="AotE - HT3">[[Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets 3|''Agent of the Empire – Hard Targets'' 3]]</ref>
