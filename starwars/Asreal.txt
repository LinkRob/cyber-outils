'''Asreal''' was a [[human]] [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Pathfinder]] who served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Star Wars 21" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Asreal was selected by [[Sergeant]] [[Kes Dameron]] to accompany him as he attempted to rescue his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Shara Bey]] from the Imperial [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tarkin's Will]]''. Asreal and [[Frell]] perished when they blew up the generator of the Star Destroyer.<ref name="Star Wars 21" />
