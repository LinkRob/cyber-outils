The ''''Dangerous' Dai Llewellyn Ward: Series Bites''' was a ward located on the first floor of [[St Mungo's Hospital for Magical Maladies and Injuries]].<ref name="ootp22"/> The ward was named after the famous [[Wales|Welsh]] [[Quidditch]] player, [[Dai Llewellyn|"Dangerous" Dai Llewellyn]] who was eaten by a [[Chimaera]].<ref name="FB"/>

The  Dai Llewellyn ward was where [[Arthur Weasley]] recovered from being bitten by [[Nagini]] after he was [[Attack on Arthur Weasley|attacked]] in the [[Department of Mysteries]] in [[December]] [[1995]].<ref name="ootp22" />

==Description==
Located on the [[Creature Induced Injuries]] corridor of [[St Mungo's Hospital for Magical Maladies and Injuries]]' first floor, the 'Dangerous' Dai Llewellyn Ward: Serious Bites<ref name="ootp22" /> was named in honour of [[Dai Llewellyn|"Dangerous" Dai Llewellyn]], a [[Wales|Welsh]] [[Quidditch]] player famous for his risky tactics, who died when he was eaten by a [[Chimaera]] while on holiday in [[Mykonos]], [[Greece]].<ref name="FB">{{FB|R}}</ref> 

In [[1995]], the ward's staff consisted of [[Healer]]-in-Charge [[Hippocrates Smethwyck]] and Trainee Healer [[Augustus Pye]]. The ward was small and dingy, the walls were oak panelled with only one window that was located opposite the door. It was mainly illuminated by shining crystal bubbles clustered in the middle of the ceiling. A [[portrait]] of [[Urquhart Rackharrow]], [[Spell creation|inventor]] of the [[Entrail-Expelling Curse]], was hung on one wall.<ref name="ootp22" />

==History==
{{Quote|Arthur Weasley? Yes, first floor, second door on the right, Dai Llewellyn Ward.|A witch directing Molly Weasley to the ward|Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix}}

In [[December]] [[1995]], [[Arthur Weasley]] was admitted to the ward after being [[Attack on Arthur Weasley|attacked]] by [[Nagini]] in the [[Department of Mysteries]]. He shared the ward with two other patients; a [[Unidentified werewolf|wizard]] who was bitten by a [[werewolf]] and a [[Bitten St Mungo's patient|witch]] who refused to tell the Healers what had bitten her.<ref name="ootp22" /> During Arthur Weasley's stay, [[Trainee Healer]] [[Augustus Pye]] was interested in [[Non-magic people|Muggle]] medicine and attempted to help Arthur by using stitches on his wounds, although they did not work as [[Nagini's venom]] was too powerful.<ref name="ootp23">{{OOTP|B|23}}</ref>
