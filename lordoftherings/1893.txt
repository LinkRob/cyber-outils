TITLE : 1893

== Events ==
=== Summer (southern hemisphere) ===
* [[J.R.R. Tolkien]] spends the cool hours in the family garden usually watching his father plant vines or trees. 
* Ronald is bitten by a [[Spiders|tarantula]] and runs away in terror, his nurse grabs him and sucks the poison out. 
* He shows interest in drawing, usually scribbling with pencil and paper. 
* He is bothered by teething and his health suffers in the heat.

=== April ===
* '''[[April 8|8]]''' - [[Vincent Trought]], a member of the [[Tea Club, Barrovian Society]], is born.
* '''[[April 20|20]]''' - [[Christopher Wiseman]], the only of Tolkien's friends that survived [[World War I]], is born.

=== October ===
* '''[[October 25|25]]''' - [[Robert Quilter Gilson]], childhood friend of Tolkien, is born.
