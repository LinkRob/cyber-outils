TITLE : "Something Has Gone Crack": New Perspectives on J.R.R. Tolkien in the Great War

{{Infobox Book|image=71yR5kXZJ1L.jpg|editor=[[Janet Brennan Croft]] &<br>Annika Röttinger|pages=414|media type=Paperback|released=September 21, 2019|publisher=Walking Tree Publishers|ISBN=3905703416|ISBN2=978-3905703412}}{{PublicationYearsBox}}

'''''"Something Has Gone Crack": New Perspectives on J.R.R. Tolkien in the Great War''''' examines many perpectives on the impact of the [[World War I|First World War]] on [[J.R.R. Tolkien]]'s [[Legendarium|Middle-earth legendarium]] and writing in general. It was edited by [[Janet Brennan Croft]] and Annika Röttinger, and published by [[:Category:Publications by Walking Tree Publishers|Walking Tree Publishers]] in September 2019.

*Chronology, by Annika Röttinger

*Glenn E. Peterson, "Strategic Blunders in the First Age Great Battles: Tolkien's Commentary on World War I"
*Annika Röttinger, "The Great War in Middle-earth – or: Where to Find Modern Warfare in ''The Lord of the Rings''"
*Tal Tovy, "Aspects of Total War in ''The Hobbit'' and ''The Lord of the Rings''"
 
===Biography: The Personal Becomes Art===

*Michael Flowers, "Tolkien in East Yorkshire, 1917-18: A Hemlock Glade, Two Towers, The Houses of Healing and a Beacon"
*Wilhelm Kuehs, "Two Poets at War: J.R.R. Tolkien and Ernst Jünger in the Battle of the Somme, a Semiotic Approach"
