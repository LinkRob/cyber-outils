TITLE : 1892

== Events ==
=== January ===
* '''[[January 3|3]]''' - [[J.R.R. Tolkien|John Ronald Reuel Tolkien]] is born to [[Arthur Reuel Tolkien|Arthur]] and [[Mabel Suffield|Mabel Tolkien]] at Bank House, Maitland Street, in [[Wikipedia:Bloemfontein|Bloemfontein]], [[Salkinórë|South Africa]].
* '''[[January 4|4]]''' - [[Arthur Reuel Tolkien]] sends a letter to his mother about his new son.
* '''[[January 31|31]]''' - [[J.R.R. Tolkien]] is christened in the Anglican Cathedral in Bloemfontein.

=== November ===
* '''[[November 15|15]]''' - Arthur, Mabel, two household servants, and the nurse holding Ronald pose for a picture in the garden at Bank House. The photograph is used in Tolkien Christmas cards sent to family and friends. The image is reproduced in ''[[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography]]'' and ''[[The Tolkien Family Album]]''.
