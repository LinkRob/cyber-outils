TITLE : 1894

== Events ==
=== February ===
* '''[[February 17|17]]''' - [[Hilary Tolkien|Hilary Arthur Reuel Tolkien]], younger brother to [[J.R.R. Tolkien]], is born in [[Wikipedia:Bloemfontein|Bloemfontein]], South Africa.

=== October ===
* '''[[October 18|18]]''' - [[Geoffrey Bache Smith]], childhood friend of Tolkien, is born.

=== November ===
* [[Mabel Tolkien|Mabel]], [[Hilary Tolkien|Hilary]], and [[J.R.R. Tolkien|Ronald]] take a train to the coast near Cape Town so Ronald can be in the cooler air.
* After they return home a trip to England is decided as [[Mabel Tolkien|Mabel]] dislikes the hot climate and the Bloemfontein lifestyle, even though [[Arthur Reuel Tolkien|Arthur]] is happy with it.

=== Christmas ===
* Tolkien later described his "first Christmas memory" being "of blazing sun, drawn curtains and a drooping eucalyptus" in [[Letter 163]].
